Big idea · 一句話看懂

A castle was not only a fortress. It was the home of a lord and the center of a small society: the lord's household, soldiers, craftspeople, and servants, and the peasants of the nearby villages, all tied together by land and by duties to one another. Castles changed as weapons and times changed.
城堡不只是要塞。它是領主的家，也是一個小社會的中心：領主的家人、士兵、工匠、僕人，以及附近村子的農民，靠土地和彼此的義務連在一起。城堡跟著武器和時代一起改變。

Unit 12: What Was Life Like in a Castle?

Britannica describes the castle as a medieval stronghold that was generally the home of the king or lord of the land around it. Strongholds with the same purpose were built in many parts of the world, including Japan and India. This lesson looks mostly at castles in western Europe, where they developed rapidly from the 9th century, and then at great stone buildings elsewhere in the same centuries.

Britannica 說，城堡是中世紀的要塞，通常是國王或那一帶領主的家。同樣用途的堡壘，在世界許多地方都有，包括日本和印度。這一課主要看西歐的城堡（從九世紀起很快發展），再看同一個時代別處的大型石造建築。

The first castles were often made of earth and timber. A mound, the motte, was ringed by a ditch and topped by a tower, and a fenced yard, the bailey, lay at its foot. After the Norman Conquest of England in 1066, well over 500 castles were raised in twenty years to control the conquered land. English Heritage notes that local people were forced to help build them.

最早的城堡常常是用泥土和木頭蓋的。一座土丘（motte）四周挖了壕溝，上面蓋一座塔；土丘腳下是一個用柵欄圍起來的院子（bailey）。1066 年諾曼人征服英格蘭以後，二十年內蓋了五百多座城堡，用來控制征服來的土地。English Heritage 指出，當地人被強迫來幫忙蓋。

Timber could burn and rot, so lords rebuilt in stone. The keep became the strongest part of the castle, with a well, private rooms, and stores. Wooden fences were replaced by stone curtain walls with towers, and gates were guarded by drawbridges and portcullises. Sieges were common: in 1215 attackers dug under a corner of Rochester's keep, and in 1216 Dover Castle held out for four months.

木頭會燒、會爛，所以領主改用石頭重蓋。主塔成了城堡最堅固的部分，裡面有井、私人的房間和存糧。木柵欄換成有塔樓的石造城牆，城門有吊橋和吊閘守著。圍城很常見：1215 年，攻擊的人在羅徹斯特主塔的一角底下挖地道；1216 年，多佛城堡撐了四個月。

A castle was also a household and a place of work. The National Archives lists constables, masons, blacksmiths, and servants among the people who kept a castle running. Most castles in England were granted by the king to his loyal lords. The lord's living quarters included a great hall, and records of 1396 at Portchester Castle list a new hall, kitchen, chambers, and offices.

城堡也是一個家，也是一個工作的地方。英國國家檔案館列出城堡總管、石匠、鐵匠和僕人，都是讓城堡運作的人。英格蘭大部分的城堡，是國王賜給忠心的領主的。領主的居住區有大廳；1396 年波特切斯特城堡的紀錄，列出新蓋的大廳、廚房、臥房和辦事的房間。

Most people did not live in castles. Most were farmers in villages, and in much of western Europe many villages were part of a lord's manor, with peasant houses, a church, a mill, and the lord's manor house, surrounded by fields, meadows, and woods. Peasants paid the lord in labor, money, and produce. Serfs were bound to their land and needed the lord's permission to leave, marry, or change jobs; they had little protection in law and passed to a new lord along with the land. From the 14th century serfdom faded in western Europe, but in eastern Europe it lasted into the 1800s.

大部分的人不住在城堡裡。大多數人是住在村子裡的農民；在西歐許多地方，這些村子屬於領主的莊園，有農民的房子、教堂、磨坊和領主的宅邸，四周是田地、草地和樹林。農民用勞動、錢和農產品繳給領主。農奴被綁在自己的土地上，要離開、結婚或換工作，都要領主同意；他們在法律上很少能對抗領主，還會連同土地一起轉給新的領主。從十四世紀起，西歐的農奴制度逐漸消失，可是東歐的農奴制一直延續到十九世紀。

Great stone buildings rose in other lands too. In Syria, the Hospitaller knights, a European crusading order, built the Crac des Chevaliers from 1142 to 1271, and the Mamluks, Muslim sultans of Egypt and Syria, added to it later. In southern Africa, ancestors of the Shona built the stone walls of Great Zimbabwe, the center of a large state, between about 1100 and 1450, without mortar; scholars think those walls showed the rulers' authority rather than being built for war. In Europe, the age of the medieval castle ended when guns could knock down walls quickly, as French armies showed in Italy in 1494.

別的地方也有大型的石造建築。在敘利亞，歐洲十字軍的醫院騎士團從 1142 年到 1271 年蓋了騎士堡，後來馬木路克（統治埃及和敘利亞的穆斯林蘇丹）又加蓋。在非洲南部，紹納人（也譯修納人）的祖先在大約 1100 到 1450 年間，替一個大國的中心大辛巴威，不用灰漿蓋起石牆；學者認為這些牆主要是展現統治者的權威，而不是用來打仗。在歐洲，大砲能很快打倒城牆以後，中世紀城堡的時代就結束了；1494 年法國軍隊在義大利就證明了這一點。

Questions · 閱讀理解

Why did many lords rebuild their castles in stone?

Timber could burn and rot. Stone lasted longer and was harder to attack.

Name three kinds of people, besides the lord, who lived or worked in a castle.

Constables, masons, blacksmiths, servants (any three).

How was a serf's life different from a free person's?

A serf was bound to the land, needed the lord's permission to leave, marry, or change jobs, paid the lord in labor, money, and produce, and had little protection in law.

How Do We Know? · 我們怎麼知道？

Records, pictures, and the ground 紀錄、圖畫，和土地本身

Castles left many kinds of evidence: royal records, embroidered and painted pictures, and the ruins themselves.
城堡留下了很多種證據：王室的紀錄、繡出來和畫出來的圖，以及廢墟本身。

📜

Royal records王室的紀錄

In 1086 William I ordered a survey of England, later called the Domesday Book. The English crown also kept accounts and licenses: in 1291, for example, a license allowed the owner of Stokesay to fortify it.
1086 年，威廉一世下令調查英格蘭，後來叫做《末日審判書》。英格蘭王室也留下帳目和許可：例如 1291 年，一份許可准許斯托克賽的主人替它加上防禦。

Its limits · 它的限制Royal records show what the king and his officials cared about, such as land, money, and permission. They say little about servants or peasants as people.王室的紀錄表現的是國王和官員在乎的事，例如土地、錢和許可；很少寫到僕人或農民這些人本身。

Britannica, “Domesday Book”; The National Archives, “Medieval castles” ↗
🧵

Pictures圖畫

The Bayeux Tapestry, an embroidered linen band about 68 meters (224 feet) long with more than 70 scenes, tells the story of the Norman Conquest of 1066.
貝葉掛毯是一條長約 68 公尺（224 英尺）的刺繡麻布，有七十多個場景，講 1066 年諾曼征服的故事。

Its limits · 它的限制Pictures were made by someone with a point of view. Britannica says it may have been commissioned by William's half-brother, Bishop Odo, and embroidered by English needleworkers about 1092.圖畫是有立場的人做的。Britannica 說，它可能是威廉同母異父的弟弟奧多主教訂製的，大約 1092 年由英格蘭的繡工繡成。

Britannica, “Bayeux Tapestry” ↗
⛏️

Digging and ruins挖掘和廢墟

Archaeologists study castles that still stand and dig where only mounds remain. At Totnes, excavation on the motte found the base of a watchtower, probably built in the 11th century; a stone shell keep was built there in the 1320s.
考古學家研究還立著的城堡，也在只剩土丘的地方挖掘。在托特尼斯，挖掘在土丘上找到一座瞭望塔的基座，大概是十一世紀蓋的；1320 年代那裡才蓋了石造的殼形主塔。

Its limits · 它的限制Timber rots away, so the earliest castles leave only faint traces in the soil.木頭會爛掉，所以最早的城堡只在土裡留下淡淡的痕跡。

English Heritage, “History of Totnes Castle” ↗

Different Accounts · 不同的說法

Three questions historians discuss 歷史學家討論的三個問題

Even familiar words like “feudal system” are debated. Here are three questions, with the views set side by side.
連「封建制度」這樣熟悉的詞，都有爭論。下面是三個問題，各種說法並列。

Was there a “feudal system”?真的有一個「封建制度」嗎？

  • The classic picture經典的說法

    Textbooks often describe lords who gave land, called a fief, to vassals, who owed military and other services and loyalty in return.
    教科書常常這樣描述：領主把土地（采邑）分給封臣，封臣回報以軍事和其他的服務，以及忠誠。

    Britannica, “Vassal” ↗

  • A label invented later後來發明的標籤

    Britannica calls feudalism a “historiographic construct”: the terms were invented long after the Middle Ages. The historian Elizabeth A. R. Brown argued in 1974 that the idea of one “feudal system” hides how different the arrangements were from place to place.
    Britannica 說，封建制度是一個「史學建構」：這些詞是中世紀過去很久以後才發明的。歷史學家伊莉莎白・布朗在 1974 年主張，「一個封建制度」的想法，掩蓋了各地安排有多麼不同。

    Britannica, “Feudalism”; Brown, American Historical Review (1974) ↗

Where things stand · 目前的情況Both views agree that lords, land, and duties mattered. The question is whether one neat “system” describes all of medieval Europe.兩種說法都同意領主、土地和義務很重要；問題在於，是不是有一個整齊的「制度」可以描述整個中世紀歐洲。

What were castles for?城堡是做什麼用的？

  • To control land控制土地

    English Heritage says the Normans' intensive program of castle building controlled newly conquered land, and Edward I built castles to control newly conquered north Wales.
    English Heritage 說，諾曼人密集蓋城堡，是為了控制剛征服的土地；愛德華一世蓋城堡，也是為了控制剛征服的北威爾斯。

    English Heritage, “Medieval Warfare” ↗

  • To live in and to run an estate居住和管理領地

    Britannica describes the castle as generally the residence of the king or lord, and records show halls, kitchens, chambers, and offices, the rooms of a household.
    Britannica 說，城堡通常是國王或領主的住所；紀錄裡也有大廳、廚房、臥房和辦事的房間，都是一個家需要的房間。

    Britannica, “Castle”; The National Archives ↗

Where things stand · 目前的情況One way to see it: many castles did both, and which purpose mattered more could change from castle to castle and from war to peace.一種看法是：很多城堡兩種用途都有；哪一種比較重要，要看是哪一座城堡、是戰時還是平時。

What ended the age of castles?城堡的時代是怎麼結束的？

  • Guns大砲

    Britannica says firearms developed so fast in the 15th and 16th centuries that military architecture had to change; French troops in Italy in 1494 took castle after castle with astonishing speed.
    Britannica 說，十五、十六世紀火器發展得很快，軍事建築不得不改變；1494 年法國軍隊在義大利，以驚人的速度攻下一座又一座城堡。

    Britannica, “Castle” ↗

  • Homes and forts went separate ways住宅和要塞分家

    Britannica also says that from the Renaissance, military and domestic buildings separated: forts were run by the monarch's army, and lords lived in unfortified palaces and manor houses.
    Britannica 也說，從文藝復興開始，軍事建築和住宅分開了：要塞由國王的軍隊管理，領主住在沒有防禦工事的宮殿和宅邸。

    Britannica, “Castle” ↗

Where things stand · 目前的情況One way to see it: the two answers fit together, since once guns made high walls weak, a lord had less reason to live inside them.一種看法是：兩個答案可以放在一起看，大砲讓高牆變得不堪一擊以後，領主就比較沒有理由住在牆裡面了。

Notice when a word was invented, and by whom.留意一個詞是什麼時候、被誰發明出來的。

Taiwan at That Time · 那時候的台灣

Villages, fields, and a fort that came later 村落、田地，和後來才有的城堡

While castles rose in Europe, the peoples of Taiwan lived in villages by farming, hunting, and fishing. A European-style fort came to Taiwan centuries later.
歐洲蓋城堡的時候，台灣的人群住在村落裡，靠農耕、打獵和捕魚生活。歐洲式的城堡，是好幾百年以後才來到台灣的。

🏝️

Archaeologists in Taiwan give dates as “years ago.” About 1,000 years ago is roughly the 11th century, the age of the motte-and-bailey castle.
台灣的考古學家用「距今幾年」說年代。距今大約一千年，差不多是十一世紀，也就是土丘城寨的時代。

  • Shisanhang culture, north北部的十三行文化

    The museum says the Shisanhang culture arose about 2,000 years ago; its people built wooden houses raised on posts, grew rice, and still relied on hunting, fishing, and gathering shellfish.
    史前館說，十三行文化的「房屋為木造干欄式建築，以種稻為生，但狩獵、捕魚、採貝仍很重要」。

    國立臺灣史前文化博物館 考古學習資源 ↗

  • Jingpu culture, east東部的靜浦文化

    On the east coast, the Jingpu culture lasted from about 1,500 to two or three hundred years ago, the whole age of European castles.
    在東海岸，靜浦文化「距今約一千五百年到二、三百年前」，涵蓋了歐洲城堡的整個時代。

    國立臺灣史前文化博物館 考古學習資源 ↗

  • Fort Zeelandia, 1624–1634熱蘭遮城，1624–1634

    The Dutch began building a fort at Tainan in 1624, named it Zeelandia in 1627, and finished it in 1634. It was a military, trade, and government center.
    荷蘭人 1624 年開始在台南蓋城，1627 年命名為熱蘭遮城，1634 年完工。它是軍事、貿易和行政的中心。

Stories Behind the Evidence · 證據背後的故事

A knights' castle, walls without mortar, a license, and a long siege 騎士的城堡、不用灰漿的牆、一份許可，和一場漫長的圍城

Four short stories about great stone buildings on three continents.
四個小故事：三大洲的大型石造建築。

1142–1271 · Syria · 敘利亞

Many builders, one fortress許多建造者，一座堡壘

The Crac des Chevaliers was built by the Hospitaller knights, a European crusading order, from 1142 to 1271, and Mamluk rulers added to it later. Also in Syria, Qal'at Salah El-Din, named for Saladin, shows Byzantine work from the 10th century, Frankish work, and additions by Saladin's Ayyubid dynasty layered on one another. UNESCO says the two castles show how builders of different peoples influenced each other.
騎士堡由歐洲十字軍的醫院騎士團從 1142 年蓋到 1271 年，後來馬木路克的統治者又加蓋。同樣在敘利亞、以薩拉丁命名的薩拉丁堡，有十世紀拜占庭的工程、法蘭克的工程，以及薩拉丁的阿尤布王朝加蓋的部分，一層疊一層。聯合國教科文組織說，這兩座城堡顯示了不同民族的建造者怎麼互相影響。

1100–1450 · Southern Africa · 非洲南部

Walls without mortar不用灰漿的牆

At Great Zimbabwe, builders fitted granite blocks one on top of another without mortar, each layer set back a little so the wall leaned inward and stayed stable. The Great Enclosure's walls reach about 11 meters high. The Metropolitan Museum says scholars doubt the walls ever served a military purpose; they showed the rulers' authority and kept the royal family apart.
在大辛巴威，建造者不用灰漿，把花崗岩石塊一層疊一層，每一層往內縮一點，讓牆往內斜、站得穩。大圍場的牆高到大約 11 公尺。大都會博物館說，學者懷疑這些牆曾經用在軍事上；它們展現統治者的權威，也讓王室和一般人隔開。

1291 · Stokesay · 斯托克賽

Permission to fortify加上防禦的許可

In England, the crown granted licenses to fortify buildings. In 1291 such a license was granted for Stokesay, and the document survives in the royal records.
在英格蘭，王室會發「加上防禦」的許可。1291 年，斯托克賽就拿到這樣一份許可；這份文件留在王室的紀錄裡。

1216 · Dover · 多佛

Four months四個月

When French forces besieged Dover Castle in 1216, its defenders held out for four months. A year earlier, at Rochester, attackers had dug a tunnel under one corner of the keep to bring it down.
1216 年法國軍隊圍攻多佛城堡，守軍撐了四個月。前一年在羅徹斯特，攻擊的人在主塔的一角底下挖地道，想讓它倒塌。

Myth vs. Fact · 常見迷思

Four things people get wrong 四個常見的誤會

✗ Myth · 迷思Castles were only for fighting.城堡只是用來打仗的。

✓ Fact · 事實Castles were generally the homes of kings and lords, with halls, kitchens, and private rooms, and many people worked in them.城堡通常是國王和領主的家，有大廳、廚房和私人的房間，也有很多人在裡面工作。

✗ Myth · 迷思All castles were made of stone.城堡都是石頭蓋的。

✓ Fact · 事實Many early castles were earth and timber. Most of the ones that survive are stone, because timber burned and rotted.很多早期的城堡是泥土和木頭蓋的。留下來的大多是石造的，因為木頭會燒、會爛。

✗ Myth · 迷思A dungeon was always an underground prison.dungeon 一直都是地下的牢房。

✓ Fact · 事實Donjon and dungeon first meant the castle keep, the lord's tower. The prison meaning came later.donjon 和 dungeon 原來指城堡的主塔，也就是領主的塔。「地牢」的意思是後來才有的。

✗ Myth · 迷思Great Zimbabwe must have been built by outsiders.大辛巴威一定是外來的人蓋的。

✓ Fact · 事實In the 1800s some European travelers and colonizers, stunned by its grandeur, credited it to foreign powers. Archaeology in the early 20th century confirmed its African origin: the Metropolitan Museum says it was built by Bantu-speaking ancestors of the Shona, and UNESCO says it was built by Bantu/Shona people.十九世紀有些歐洲旅人和殖民者，被它的宏偉震懾，就把它歸功於外來的勢力。二十世紀初的考古確認了它是非洲人蓋的：大都會博物館說，它是說班圖語的紹納人祖先蓋的；聯合國教科文組織也說是班圖／紹納人。

Classroom Activity 1 · 課堂活動一

A Day at the Castle 城堡的一天

You need · 準備材料

  • Role cards: lord, constable, mason, blacksmith, cook, servant, peasant, serf角色卡：領主、總管、石匠、鐵匠、廚師、僕人、農民、農奴
  • The 3D model in this lesson這一課的 3D 模型

Steps · 步驟

  1. Each student draws a role card and finds where that person would spend the day, in the model or on the manor.每個學生抽一張角色卡，在模型裡或莊園上，找出這個人一天會待在哪裡。
  2. Write three sentences: “I live …,” “I work …,” and “I owe …”寫三句英文：「I live …」「I work …」「I owe …」
  3. In groups, compare: who had the most choice about their life, and who had the least?分組比較：誰對自己的生活最有選擇？誰最少？
  4. Share one thing that surprised you.分享一件讓你驚訝的事。

Keep the discussion about duties and choices, not about cruelty. Remind students that life varied a great deal from place to place.
討論的重點放在義務和選擇，不要放在殘酷的細節。提醒學生：各地的生活差別很大。

Classroom Activity 2 · 課堂活動二

Great Stone Buildings Compared 大型石造建築比一比

You need · 準備材料

  • Pictures of a motte-and-bailey castle, a concentric castle, the Crac des Chevaliers, and Great Zimbabwe (from museum or UNESCO websites)土丘城寨、同心圓城堡、騎士堡和大辛巴威的照片（取自博物館或聯合國教科文組織網站）

Steps · 步驟

  1. In pairs, list what each was built of and roughly when.兩人一組，列出每一個是用什麼蓋的、大約在什麼時候。
  2. Find one thing they share and one thing that is different.找出一個共同點和一個不同點。
  3. Write two sentences with “was made of” and “was surrounded by.”用 was made of 和 was surrounded by 寫兩句英文。

Avoid ranking them. Castles and the Crac des Chevaliers were built for defense, while Great Zimbabwe's walls probably showed the rulers' status and kept them apart. Ask students why people build great walls.
不要替它們排名次。城堡和騎士堡是為了防守而蓋的；大辛巴威的牆大概是為了展現統治者的地位、和一般人隔開。問學生：人為什麼要蓋大牆？
