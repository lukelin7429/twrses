World History · 世界歷史

World History 世界歷史

Turning points that changed how many people lived, one question at a time: a 3D history globe, a timeline that shows what was happening in other places at the same moment, and an honest look at how we know.
改變了很多人生活的轉折點，一課問一個問題：3D 歷史地球儀、看得到「同一個時間，別的地方在做什麼」的時間軸，還有老實交代「我們怎麼知道」。

World history is too big to finish. This series does not try. It picks turning points, the moments that changed how many people lived, and asks one question in each lesson: Why did people start farming? What traveled along the Silk Road? Why did ships sail around the world?
世界歷史太大，講不完。這個系列不打算講完，只挑轉折點——那些改變了很多人生活的時刻——每一課問一個問題：人為什麼開始種田？絲路上走的是什麼？船為什麼要繞地球一圈？

Every lesson has a parallel timeline with seven lanes, so you can see what was happening in other parts of the world at the same time, and four cards that never change: How do we know? Different accounts. History English. And Taiwan at that time, which ties the wider world back to this island.
每一課都有一條分成七條線的平行時間軸，讓你看到同一個時間世界其他地方在做什麼；還有四張固定的卡：「我們怎麼知道？」「不同的說法」「歷史英語」，以及把世界接回這座島的「那時候的台灣」。

The lessons are written in English with a Chinese translation beside every paragraph, for upper elementary and junior high students, for teachers of bilingual classes, and for anyone who is curious. The series stops around the year 2000; what came after is still too close to see clearly.
課文用英文寫，每一段旁邊都有中文翻譯，給國小高年級到國中的學生、雙語課的老師，以及所有好奇的人。全系列寫到西元 2000 年前後為止；之後的事離現在太近，還看不清楚。

4 of 28 lessons are ready so far; more are on the way.
目前完成 4 課（共規劃 28 課），持續增加中。

How Did Humans Spread Across the World?

人類怎麼走到世界各地？Pull a time slider and watch people spread from Africa across a 3D globe. Why Did People Start Farming?

人為什麼開始種田？Follow one river valley through a year: foragers who move with the seasons on one bank, an early farming village on the other. Then see on a map how farming began separately in many parts of the world, and weigh what people gained and what they gave up.跟著一座河谷過一年：一岸是跟著季節搬家的採集狩獵者，另一岸是早期的農村。再從地圖上看農業怎麼在世界上好幾個地方各自開始，並且想一想人們得到了什麼、又放棄了什麼。Start the lesson · 開始上課 →Lesson 3 · 第三課入門 · Beginner

Why Did People Invent Writing?

人為什麼發明文字？Press number signs into a clay tablet in 3D, the way a scribe in ancient Mesopotamia kept accounts. Then find on a map the few places where writing began on its own, and discover the trick that let pictures write down names and speech.在 3D 泥板上壓出數字記號，像古代美索不達米亞的書吏那樣記帳。再從地圖上找出文字各自開始的幾個地方，並發現讓圖畫能寫下名字和說話的那個訣竅。Start the lesson · 開始上課 →Lesson 4 · 第四課入門 · Beginner

Why Did the First Cities Grow Beside Rivers?

最早的城市為什麼都在河邊？Raise the Great Pyramid course by course in 3D and test four ideas about how its blocks were lifted. Then find on a map the rivers where the first cities grew, and sort out what a river gave a city, and what trouble it brought.在 3D 裡一層一層蓋起大金字塔，試試四種「石頭怎麼運上去」的想法。再從地圖上找出最早的城市長在哪些河邊，分一分河帶給城市什麼、又帶來什麼麻煩。Start the lesson · 開始上課 →

How This Series Is Written · 這個系列怎麼寫

Six promises 六個約定

History is easy to bend. These are the rules every lesson follows, so that you can check us against them.
歷史很容易被寫歪。下面是每一課都遵守的約定，寫出來，讓你可以拿它來檢查我們。

  • ⚖️

    Facts with sources, and no side taken只寫查得到出處的事實，不替立場說話

    Each lesson lists where its facts come from. Where historians disagree, the accounts are set side by side and the lesson does not pick a winner.
    每一課都列出事實的出處。歷史學家看法不同的地方，把說法並列，不替誰下結論。

  • 🌍

    No center, and no ranking沒有中心，也不排名

    Africa, Asia, the Americas, Europe, and Oceania all have lessons of their own. No people are called advanced or backward; each lesson asks how people answered the place and the problems they had.
    非洲、亞洲、美洲、歐洲、大洋洲都有自己的課。不說哪一群人「先進」或「落後」，只問每個地方的人怎麼回應自己的環境和難題。

  • 🕊️

    Religions are told as history宗教當作歷史來寫

    The lessons keep three things apart: what believers hold, what tradition says, and what written and archaeological sources show. Every faith is treated with respect, and none is compared with another.
    課文把三件事分開：信徒相信的、傳統上說的、文獻與考古顯示的。每一種信仰都受到尊重，彼此不比較高下。

  • 🕯️

    Hard history is included困難的歷史也寫

    Slavery, colonial rule, world wars, and genocide are part of the story. The lessons say what happened, why it matters, and how people remember it, without graphic detail. Where estimates differ, they are shown together with their sources.
    奴隸貿易、殖民統治、世界大戰、大屠殺都是歷史的一部分。課文講發生了什麼、為什麼重要、後來的人怎麼記得，不描寫血腥細節；數字有不同估計時，連同出處一起並列。

  • 🗺️

    Maps are approximate, and say so地圖只畫大約，而且會說明

    Old empires and old routes had no sharp edges. The globe shows rough extents with their dates and sources, and never draws today's borders onto the past.
    古代的帝國和路線沒有清楚的邊。地球儀上只畫大約的範圍，標明年代與依據，不把今天的國界畫進古代。

  • 🔎

    The story can change故事會被修改

    New finds overturn old dates. The lessons say “about,” “researchers estimate,” and “the evidence so far” because that is the honest state of what is known.
    新的發現會推翻舊的年代。課文寫「大約」「研究者估計」「目前的證據顯示」，因為這才是我們所知道的真實狀態。
