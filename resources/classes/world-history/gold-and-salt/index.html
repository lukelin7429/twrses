Big idea · 一句話看懂

West Africa had gold; the Sahara had salt. Camel caravans linked the two, and the kingdoms of Ghana, Mali, and Songhai grew rich and powerful by controlling and taxing that trade. On the east coast, Swahili city-states such as Kilwa grew rich from the trade of the Indian Ocean.
西非有黃金，撒哈拉有鹽。駱駝商隊把兩者連起來；迦納、馬利、桑海這些王國靠控制、抽稅這門貿易而富強。在東海岸，基爾瓦這些斯瓦希里城邦，靠印度洋的貿易致富。

Unit 13: How Did Gold and Salt Build Kingdoms in Africa?

In the lands along the upper Niger and Senegal rivers, people mined gold. Far to the north, in the middle of the Sahara, salt was dug from the ground. Caravans of camels crossed the Sahara between the two, carrying salt south and gold north, and towns grew up where the routes began and ended.

在上尼日河和塞內加爾河一帶，人們開採黃金。遠在北方的撒哈拉沙漠中間，人們從地下挖鹽。駱駝商隊在兩者之間穿越撒哈拉，把鹽往南運、把黃金往北運；路線的起點和終點，就長出了城鎮。

The first great trading empire was Ghana, called Wagadu by its rulers. Its Soninke people acted as middlemen between the salt traders of the north and the producers of gold and ivory to the south, and the king taxed the trade, especially gold. Its last capital, Kumbi, had two towns: one for the king and one for Muslim traders. Before 1240 it was the greatest city of western Africa.

第一個大的貿易帝國是迦納，統治者自稱瓦加杜（Wagadu）。迦納的索寧克人，在北方的鹽商和南方出產黃金、象牙的人之間當中間人；國王向貿易抽稅，尤其是黃金。它最後的首都昆比有兩個城區：一個是國王的，一個是穆斯林商人的。1240 年以前，它是西非最大的城市。

About 1235 Sundiata Keita founded the empire of Mali. Much of what we know about him comes from oral traditions kept by griots, who were hereditary historians, poets, and spokespeople for the king. Mali reached its height under Mansa Musa, whose pilgrimage to Mecca in 1324, with a caravan of some 60,000 people and great amounts of gold, made Mali famous in North Africa and Europe.

大約 1235 年，松迪亞塔・凱塔建立馬利帝國。我們對他的了解，很多來自格里奧（griot）保存的口傳歷史；格里奧（吟遊詩人史家）是世襲的史家、詩人和國王的發言人。馬利在曼薩・穆薩的時候最興盛：他 1324 年去麥加朝覲，帶著大約六萬人的商隊和大量的黃金，讓馬利在北非和歐洲出了名。

Mali's cities became centers of trade and learning. At Timbuktu, where the desert meets the Niger, merchants exchanged gold and enslaved people for salt, North African cloth, and horses. Enslaved people were bought and sold across the Sahara, and some served in the king's administration; historians study this too. In 1352 the traveler Ibn Battuta crossed the Sahara from Sijilmasa and described Taghaza, a village whose houses were built of blocks of salt.

馬利的城市成為貿易和學術的中心。在沙漠和尼日河交會的廷巴克圖，商人用黃金和被奴役的人，交換鹽、北非的布和馬。被奴役的人在撒哈拉兩邊被買賣，有些還在國王的行政裡工作；歷史學家也研究這一部分。1352 年，旅行家伊本・巴杜達從西吉爾馬薩穿越撒哈拉，描述了塔加扎：一個房子用鹽塊蓋的村子。

In the 15th and 16th centuries, the Songhai empire, centered on Gao, became a great trading state of West Africa. Timbuktu reached the height of its trade and scholarship under the Askia rulers, from 1493 to 1591. In 1591 an army from Morocco took Timbuktu, and the city declined.

十五、十六世紀，以加奧為中心的桑海帝國，成為西非重要的貿易國家。廷巴克圖在阿斯基亞王朝（1493 到 1591 年）的時候，貿易和學術最興盛。1591 年，摩洛哥的軍隊攻下廷巴克圖，這座城從此衰落。

On the east coast, Swahili towns such as Mogadishu and Kilwa traded across the Indian Ocean with ports in Arabia, Persia, and India, and Chinese porcelain reached them too. Kilwa grew rich by controlling the trade with Sofala, where gold arrived from the lands near Great Zimbabwe. In 1505 Portuguese ships seized Sofala and briefly held Kilwa, trying to take over the gold trade.

在東海岸，摩加迪休、基爾瓦這些斯瓦希里城鎮，橫越印度洋和阿拉伯、波斯、印度的港口做生意，中國的瓷器也運到了這裡。基爾瓦控制了和索法拉的貿易而致富；大辛巴威一帶的黃金就是運到索法拉。1505 年，葡萄牙的船佔領了索法拉，也短暫佔領了基爾瓦，想要接管黃金貿易。

Questions · 閱讀理解

Why did people in West Africa trade gold for salt?

The south had gold; the Sahara had salt. Each side traded what it had for what the other had.

How did the kings of Ghana and Mali become rich?

They controlled the trade routes and the gold-producing lands, and they taxed the trade, especially gold.

Name two kinds of sources historians use to learn about Mali.

Oral traditions kept by griots; Arabic writers such as Ibn Battuta and al-ʿUmarī; archaeology (any two).

How Do We Know? · 我們怎麼知道？

Spoken histories, Arabic writers, and the ground 口傳的歷史、阿拉伯文作者，和土地本身

Historians of these kingdoms put together three very different kinds of evidence.
研究這些王國的歷史學家，要把三種很不一樣的證據拼起來。

🎶

Griots and oral tradition格里奧和口傳歷史

In Mali, griots were hereditary keepers of the empire's history and spokespeople for the king. Much of what we know about Sundiata comes from the stories and songs they passed down.
在馬利，格里奧是世襲的帝國史家，也是國王的發言人。我們對松迪亞塔的了解，很多來自他們一代一代傳下來的故事和歌。

Its limits · 它的限制Britannica notes discrepancies between the oral and written accounts. In general, oral traditions keep the memory and meaning of events but rarely give exact dates.Britannica 指出，口傳和文字的記載之間有出入。一般來說，口傳歷史保存了事件的記憶和意義，但很少有確切的年代。

Britannica, “Mali” ↗
📖

Arabic writers阿拉伯文作者

Geographers and travelers writing in Arabic described these lands: al-Bakrī described Ghana's capital in the 11th century, and Ibn Battuta visited Mali in 1352–53 and gave exact dates for his journey.
用阿拉伯文寫作的地理學家和旅人描述了這些地方：十一世紀的巴克里描述了迦納的首都；伊本・巴杜達 1352 到 1353 年到過馬利，還寫下旅程確切的日期。

Its limits · 它的限制Britannica notes that these sources, valuable as they are, are not infallible. Travelers also wrote as outsiders, and the 1929 English translation of Ibn Battuta uses outdated racial terms that this lesson does not repeat.Britannica 指出，這些資料雖然寶貴，但不是不會錯。旅人也是用外來者的眼光在寫；伊本・巴杜達 1929 年的英譯本還用了過時的種族稱呼，本課不沿用。

Britannica, “Mali” and “Ghana”; Ibn Battuta, trans. Gibb ↗
⛏️

Ruins and digging遺址和挖掘

Archaeologists have excavated ruins at Koumbi Saleh, which Britannica says have been tentatively identified as Ghana's capital. At Kilwa, extensive ruins of the Great Mosque and the Husuni Kubwa palace remain.
考古學家在昆比薩利赫挖掘遺址；Britannica 說，它暫時被認定是迦納的首都。在基爾瓦，大清真寺和胡蘇尼庫布瓦宮留下了大片的遺址。

Its limits · 它的限制Ruins show buildings and objects, but not the names, beliefs, and stories of the people who used them.遺址能看到建築和東西，但看不到使用它們的人的名字、信仰和故事。

Different Accounts · 不同的說法

Three questions historians discuss 歷史學家討論的三個問題

New evidence, from Arabic texts to ancient DNA, has changed some old stories. Here are three questions, with the views side by side.
從阿拉伯文的文獻到古代的 DNA，新的證據改寫了一些舊故事。下面是三個問題，各種說法並列。

Who founded Kilwa?基爾瓦是誰建立的？

  • Settlers from overseas從海外來的移民

    Britannica says Kilwa was founded in the late 10th century by settlers from Arabia and Persia. The Kilwa Chronicle, written in the 16th century, tells of Shirazi founders from Persia, though Britannica adds that whether they were really Persian is “somewhat doubtful.”
    Britannica 說，基爾瓦是十世紀後期由阿拉伯和波斯來的移民建立的。十六世紀寫成的《基爾瓦編年史》說，建立者是從波斯來的設拉子人；不過 Britannica 也說，他們是不是真的來自波斯，「有點可疑」。

    Britannica, “Kilwa” and “Eastern Africa” ↗

  • An African town that joined a wider world一座和外面世界連起來的非洲城鎮

    The Metropolitan Museum describes Kilwa as first a fishing and weaving community of the 8th or 9th century, and the Swahili towns as primarily African communities. A 2023 study of ancient DNA estimated that people of African and Asian origin were mixing along the Swahili coast by about 1000 CE; before about 1500 the Asian ancestry was mostly Persian-related, which fits part of the chronicle.
    大都會博物館說，基爾瓦最早是八或九世紀的一個捕魚、織布的社群，斯瓦希里的城鎮主要是非洲人的社群。2023 年一項古代 DNA 的研究推估，大約西元 1000 年，斯瓦希里海岸上非洲和亞洲來源的人已經在混居、通婚；1500 年以前的亞洲血統主要和波斯有關，這和編年史的一部分相符。

    Metropolitan Museum of Art, Timeline of Art History; Brielle et al., Nature (2023) ↗

Where things stand · 目前的情況Both views can fit: an African town on the coast, with newcomers from across the ocean who married into it. The Nature study notes that Swahili is an African Bantu language that took in words from Asian languages.兩種說法可以同時成立：一座海岸邊的非洲城鎮，有從海外來、和當地人通婚的新住民。那篇 Nature 研究也指出，斯瓦希里語是吸收了亞洲語言字彙的非洲班圖語。

Did the Almoravids conquer Ghana in 1076?穆拉比特人（阿爾摩拉維德人）在 1076 年征服了迦納嗎？

  • The Almoravids brought Ghana down阿爾摩拉維德人讓迦納衰落

    Britannica says Ghana began to decline in the 11th century with the rise of the Almoravids, a Muslim movement of Saharan Amazigh peoples. It says Abū Bakr, leader of their southern wing, took Audaghost in 1054 and seized Kumbi in 1076, though their rule over Ghana lasted only a few years.
    Britannica 說，迦納在十一世紀開始衰落，是因為阿爾摩拉維德人（撒哈拉阿馬齊格人，也就是柏柏人的穆斯林運動）興起。它說，他們南路的領袖阿布・巴克爾 1054 年攻下奧達戈斯特，1076 年奪取昆比，不過他們統治迦納只有短短幾年。

    Britannica, “Ghana” and “Almoravids” ↗

  • A conquest that never was從來沒有發生的征服

    In 1982 the historians David Conrad and Humphrey Fisher re-read the external Arabic sources and argued that none of them clearly describes an Almoravid conquest of Ghana in 1076.
    1982 年，歷史學家康拉德和費雪重新讀了外部的阿拉伯文資料，主張其中沒有一份清楚記載阿爾摩拉維德人在 1076 年征服迦納。

    Conrad and Fisher, History in Africa 9 (1982) ↗

Where things stand · 目前的情況Most historians accept that the Almoravids changed the trade of the western Sahara. The question is whether that happened by one conquest or by slower pressure.多數歷史學家接受，阿爾摩拉維德人改變了撒哈拉西部的貿易；問題在於，那是一次征服，還是比較慢的壓力。

Which sources tell Mali's story best?哪一種資料最能說出馬利的故事？

  • The griots格里奧

    Griots kept the history of Mali from the inside, in the words of its own people, including the story of Sundiata.
    格里奧從裡面保存了馬利的歷史，用的是馬利人自己的話，包括松迪亞塔的故事。

    Britannica, “Mali” and “Sundiata Keita” ↗

  • The written records文字紀錄

    Arabic writers such as al-ʿUmarī, Ibn Battuta, and Ibn Khaldūn recorded names, dates, and descriptions that oral traditions often leave out.
    烏馬里、伊本・巴杜達和伊本・赫勒敦這些阿拉伯文作者，記下了口傳歷史常常沒有的名字、年代和描述。

    Britannica, “Mali” ↗

Where things stand · 目前的情況Britannica says the oral and written sources are both valuable but not infallible, and that they sometimes disagree. Historians compare them.Britannica 說，口傳和文字的資料都很寶貴，但都不是不會錯，有時還彼此矛盾。歷史學家會把它們互相比對。

Ask who wrote each source, and when.問一問：每一份資料是誰寫的？什麼時候寫的？

這八句幫你描述貿易怎麼讓王國富起來。先聽、再跟著唸，然後用粗體的字自己造一句。

Taiwan at That Time · 那時候的台灣

Gold on a different island 另一個島上的黃金

Taiwan was not on these routes, but gold and trade goods appear in its story too.
台灣不在這些路線上，但黃金和貿易品也出現在台灣的故事裡。

🏝️

Archaeologists in Taiwan give dates as “years ago.” The Shisanhang culture began about 2,000 years ago; its Tang and Song coins point to overseas contact in the centuries of Ghana and Mali.
台灣的考古學家用「距今幾年」說年代。十三行文化大約兩千年前興起；遺址裡的唐宋古錢，說明了它在迦納和馬利那幾百年和海外的往來。

  • Shisanhang culture, north北部的十三行文化

    The museum says Shisanhang sites held gold ornaments, glass bracelets, and Tang and Song coins, which show overseas trade.
    史前館說，從出土的唐宋古錢及金飾、玻璃手環等，「可知他們有海外貿易的情形」。

    國立臺灣史前文化博物館 考古學習資源 ↗

  • Jingpu culture, east東部的靜浦文化

    On the east coast, the Jingpu culture, from about 1,500 to a few hundred years ago, had copper and gold ornaments and agate and glass that came from overseas.
    在東海岸，大約距今一千五百年到兩、三百年前的靜浦文化，有銅、金的裝飾品，以及來自海外的瑪瑙和琉璃。

    國立臺灣史前文化博物館 考古學習資源 ↗

  • Gold dust on “Liuqiu”「琉球」的沙金

    In the 14th century, at the time of Mali's greatest wealth, the Yuan traveler Wang Dayuan wrote that an island called Liuqiu, very near Penghu, produced gold dust, sulfur, and the hides of deer and leopards, among other goods. Scholars still debate whether it means Taiwan (Lesson 9).
    十四世紀，正是馬利最富有的時候，元朝旅行家汪大淵寫道，「自彭湖望之甚近」的「琉球」出產「沙金、黃荳、黍子、硫黃、黃蠟、鹿、豹、麂皮」。這是不是指台灣，學者還在討論（第九課）。

    汪大淵《島夷誌略》「琉球」 ↗

Stories Behind the Evidence · 證據背後的故事

A town of salt, the keepers of memory, a two-town capital, and a palace by the sea 鹽蓋的村子、記憶的守護者、兩個城區的首都，和海邊的宮殿

Four short stories about the people and places of the gold and salt trade.
四個小故事：黃金和鹽的貿易裡的人和地方。

1352 · Taghaza · 塔加扎

A village built of salt鹽蓋的村子

Ibn Battuta found that at Taghaza even the houses and mosques were built of blocks of salt, roofed with camel skins. The salt was dug by enslaved workers of the Massufa, and the trade there was worth huge amounts of gold dust.
伊本・巴杜達發現，在塔加扎，連房子和清真寺都是用鹽塊蓋的，屋頂鋪駱駝皮。鹽由馬蘇法人手下被奴役的人挖掘，那裡的生意值大量的金砂。

13th century → today · Mali · 馬利

Keepers of memory記憶的守護者

Griots were hereditary keepers of Mali's history and spokespeople for the king. Through them the story of Sundiata, who founded the empire about 1235, has been told for centuries.
格里奧是世襲的馬利歷史守護者，也替國王說話。靠著他們，大約 1235 年建立帝國的松迪亞塔的故事，被講了好幾百年。

Before 1240 · Kumbi · 昆比

A capital in two parts分成兩區的首都

Ghana's capital had two towns: one for the king and one for Muslim traders. With more than 15,000 people, it was the greatest city of western Africa before 1240.
迦納的首都有兩個城區：一個是國王的，一個是穆斯林商人的。它有一萬五千多人，1240 年以前是西非最大的城市。

13th–14th century · Kilwa · 基爾瓦

A palace by the sea海邊的宮殿

The great palace of Husuni Kubwa at Kilwa was built of coral stone in the 13th–14th century. The Met describes Swahili, Arab, Persian, and Indian influences there, combined into a style distinct to the Swahili coast.
基爾瓦的胡蘇尼庫布瓦大宮殿，在十三到十四世紀用珊瑚石蓋成。大都會博物館說，那裡融合了斯瓦希里本地、阿拉伯、波斯和印度的影響，成為斯瓦希里海岸獨有的風格。

Myth vs. Fact · 常見迷思

Four things people get wrong 四個常見的誤會

✗ Myth · 迷思Africa had no kingdoms or written history before Europeans arrived.歐洲人來以前，非洲沒有王國，也沒有寫下來的歷史。

✓ Fact · 事實Ghana was wealthy by the 8th century, and Mali and Songhai followed. Their history survives in oral traditions, in Arabic writings, in archaeology, and in writings by Africans themselves, such as the Kilwa Chronicle of the 16th century.迦納在八世紀就已經富有，接著是馬利和桑海。它們的歷史保存在口傳、阿拉伯文的著作、考古，以及非洲人自己寫的著作裡，例如十六世紀的《基爾瓦編年史》。

✗ Myth · 迷思The Sahara cut Africa off from the rest of the world.撒哈拉把非洲和世界隔開了。

✓ Fact · 事實Caravan routes crossed it for centuries. Sijilmasa, founded in 757, became the leading center of trans-Saharan trade in the western Maghrib.商隊路線穿越它好幾百年。757 年建立的西吉爾馬薩，成為北非西部橫越撒哈拉貿易最重要的中心。

✗ Myth · 迷思Salt was too common to matter.鹽太普通了，不重要。

✓ Fact · 事實Ibn Battuta saw salt used as money south of the Sahara. Even the English word salary comes from the Latin for “salt money.”伊本・巴杜達看到撒哈拉以南的人把鹽當錢用。連英文的 salary（薪水）都來自拉丁文的「鹽錢」。

✗ Myth · 迷思The Swahili towns were foreign colonies.斯瓦希里城鎮是外國的殖民地。

✓ Fact · 事實The Met describes them as primarily African communities. Swahili is an African Bantu language that took in words from Asian languages, and an ancient-DNA study estimates that African and Asian families were mixing on the coast by about 1000 CE.大都會博物館說它們主要是非洲人的社群。斯瓦希里語是吸收了亞洲語言字彙的非洲班圖語；一項古代 DNA 研究推估，大約西元 1000 年，海岸上非洲和亞洲的家族已經在混居、通婚。

Classroom Activity 1 · 課堂活動一

The Salt and Gold Market 鹽和黃金的市場

You need · 準備材料

  • Cards for gold, salt, cloth, horses, and ivory; a few “tax” tokens for the king黃金、鹽、布、馬、象牙的卡片；幾個國王收稅用的代幣

Steps · 步驟

  1. Split the class into northern traders (with salt, cloth, horses) and southern traders (with gold and ivory). One student is the king of Ghana in the middle.全班分成北方商人（有鹽、布、馬）和南方商人（有黃金、象牙），中間一個學生當迦納國王。
  2. Traders must pass through the king, who takes one tax token for each trade.每一筆交易都要經過國王，國王每一筆收一個稅的代幣。
  3. After five minutes, count: who became richest, and why?五分鐘後清點：誰最富有？為什麼？
  4. Discuss in English: “The king grew rich from …” and “Salt was traded for …”用英文討論：「The king grew rich from …」和「Salt was traded for …」

Do not include people as trade cards. If students ask, explain plainly that enslaved people were traded too, and that this is part of the history, not part of the game.
不要把人當成交易卡。學生問的時候，清楚地說明：被奴役的人也被買賣，這是歷史的一部分，不是遊戲的一部分。

Classroom Activity 2 · 課堂活動二

Follow the Traveler 跟著旅人走

You need · 準備材料

  • The 3D globe in this lesson這一課的 3D 地球儀
  • A blank map of Africa (land only)一張只畫陸地的非洲空白地圖

Steps · 步驟

  1. Follow Ibn Battuta's journey on the globe and draw it on the map.在地球儀上跟著伊本・巴杜達走，畫在地圖上。
  2. At each stop, write one thing he saw or did.每一站寫下他看到或做的一件事。
  3. Then follow Mansa Musa. Write two sentences comparing the two journeys.接著跟著曼薩・穆薩走，用兩句英文比較這兩段旅程。

Remind students that Ibn Battuta was an outsider, and that the people of Mali told their own history through the griots.
提醒學生：伊本・巴杜達是外來的人，馬利人用格里奧講自己的歷史。
