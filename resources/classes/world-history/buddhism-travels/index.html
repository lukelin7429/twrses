Big idea · 一句話看懂

Buddhism began in northern India about 2,400 to 2,500 years ago, with a teacher called the Buddha; scholars disagree about his exact dates. Emperor Ashoka's edicts, carved on rocks and pillars, are the earliest datable written evidence. Monks, traders, pilgrims, and rulers then carried the teaching south by sea and north and east over land, and each place made it its own.
佛教大約在兩千四、五百年前，從印度北部一位被稱為佛陀的老師開始；他確切的年代，學者看法不一。阿育王刻在岩石和石柱上的詔令，是最早可確定年代的文字證據。後來，僧人、商人、朝聖者和國王，把教法往南走海路、往北往東走陸路帶出去，每一個地方都把它變成自己的。

Unit 8: How Did Buddhism Begin and Travel?

About 2,400 to 2,500 years ago, in the plains of the Ganges River in northern India, a teacher named Siddhartha Gautama began to gather followers. They called him the Buddha, “the awakened one.” He taught that suffering has causes and that a path of ethical living, meditation, and wisdom can bring it to an end. Scholars do not agree on when he lived: an older view puts his life at about 560 to 480 BCE, while many now think he died around 400 BCE.

大約兩千四、五百年前，在印度北部恆河的平原上，一位名叫悉達多・喬達摩的老師開始有了追隨者。他們稱他為「佛陀」，意思是「覺悟的人」。他教導：苦有它的原因，而一條包含正當的生活、禪修和智慧的道路，能讓苦止息。他生活在什麼年代，學者看法不一：比較早的看法是大約西元前 560 到 480 年，現在很多學者則認為他大約在西元前 400 年前後去世。

For centuries, the Buddha's teachings were not written down. Monks and nuns learned them by heart and recited them together. Sri Lankan tradition says the Pāli scriptures were first written down on the island in the 1st century BCE. The oldest Buddhist manuscripts we actually have, written on birch bark, come from Gandhara in today's Pakistan and date from about the 1st century BCE to the 3rd century CE.

好幾百年裡，佛陀的教導沒有寫下來。比丘和比丘尼把它背起來，大家一起誦念。斯里蘭卡的傳統說，巴利文的經典在西元前一世紀第一次在島上寫成文字。我們手上真正有的最古老的佛教寫本，寫在樺樹皮上，來自今天巴基斯坦的犍陀羅，年代大約在西元前一世紀到西元三世紀之間。

The earliest datable written evidence for Buddhism comes from Ashoka, who ruled most of South Asia from about 270 to 232 BCE (some sources give 265 to 238). In one of his own edicts, he says he conquered the land of Kalinga, that 100,000 people were killed, and that he felt deep remorse. He had edicts carved on rocks and pillars across his empire, most in the Brahmi script and a few in other scripts and languages, including Greek and Aramaic. One edict speaks of his faith in the Buddha; another asks people to respect the followers of all religions.

佛教最早可確定年代的文字證據來自阿育王。他大約從西元前 270 年到 232 年統治南亞的大部分地方（也有資料寫西元前 265 到 238 年）。在他自己的一篇詔令裡，他說他征服了羯陵伽，有十萬人被殺，他深深地懊悔。他在帝國各地的岩石和石柱上刻下詔令，大多用婆羅米文字，也有少數用其他文字和語言，包括希臘文和亞蘭文。有一篇詔令說到他對佛陀的信心；另一篇則要人尊重所有宗教的信徒。

From India, Buddhism traveled south and east by sea. Sri Lankan chronicles say Ashoka's son Mahinda brought it to the island in the 3rd century BCE. Later, ships carried monks and traders across the Bay of Bengal. In 671 CE the Chinese monk Yijing studied in Srivijaya, on Sumatra, before sailing on to India, and in the 8th and 9th centuries the great stone monument of Borobudur rose on Java.

佛教從印度出發，走海路往南、往東傳。斯里蘭卡的編年史說，阿育王的兒子摩哂陀在西元前三世紀把佛教帶到島上。後來，船隻載著僧人和商人橫越孟加拉灣。西元 671 年，中國僧人義淨在前往印度以前，先在蘇門答臘的室利佛逝學習；八到九世紀，巨大的石造建築婆羅浮屠在爪哇蓋了起來。

Buddhism also traveled north and east over land. In Gandhara, artists carved some of the first images of the Buddha in human form in the 1st century CE. By 65 CE a Han prince in China was making offerings to the Buddha, and from about 148 CE, according to a later catalog of translations, monks such as An Shigao were translating texts into Chinese. Chinese pilgrims walked to India to find more: Xuanzang left in 629 and came home in 645 with 657 texts. The teaching reached Korea in the 4th century, Japan in the 6th, and Tibet in the 7th and 8th, according to each land's histories.

佛教也走陸路往北、往東傳。在犍陀羅，藝術家在西元一世紀刻出了最早的一批人形佛像。到了西元 65 年，中國漢朝的一位王子已經在供養佛陀；依後來的譯經目錄，從大約西元 148 年起，安世高等僧人開始把經典翻成中文。中國的朝聖者走路到印度去找更多的經典：玄奘在 629 年出發，645 年帶著 657 部經論回來。依照各地自己的史書，佛教在四世紀傳到朝鮮半島，六世紀傳到日本，七、八世紀傳到西藏。

Each place made Buddhism its own, in its own language, art, and buildings. In India itself, Buddhism slowly declined, and the great monastery of Nalanda was sacked around 1200. Today about 324 million people, roughly 4 percent of the world, are counted as Buddhists, and almost all of them live in the Asia-Pacific region. The teaching traveled with monks, traders, pilgrims, and rulers who chose to support it, over about 1,500 years.

每一個地方，都用自己的語言、藝術和建築，把佛教變成自己的。在印度本地，佛教慢慢衰落，偉大的那爛陀寺大約在 1200 年遭到洗劫。今天，大約有 3 億 2,400 萬人被算作佛教徒，大約是全世界的百分之四，幾乎全部住在亞洲及太平洋地區。這個教法跟著僧人、商人、朝聖者，以及選擇支持它的國王，旅行了大約一千五百年。

Questions · 閱讀理解

Why do we have to say “about” when we give the Buddha's dates?

The teachings were passed on by memory for centuries, and the written sources give different dates, so scholars disagree. An older view is about 560–480 BCE; many now think he died around 400 BCE.

Name one way Buddhism traveled by sea and one way it traveled over land.

By sea: to Sri Lanka, and across the Bay of Bengal to Southeast Asia, where Yijing studied in Srivijaya. Over land: through Gandhara and Central Asia to China, then on to Korea and Japan, partly by sea; also over the mountains to Tibet.

What is the difference between a date that tradition gives and a date that evidence shows? Give one example of each.

A traditional date comes from a story written down much later, such as Mahinda reaching Sri Lanka or Buddhism reaching Japan in 552. Evidence comes from close to the time, such as Ashoka's pillar at Lumbini or the Han edict of 65 CE.

How Do We Know? · 我們怎麼知道？

Stones, birch bark, and travelers' notes 石頭、樺樹皮和旅人的筆記

The earliest Buddhist teachings were memorized, not written, so historians have to look hard for evidence.
最早的佛教教導是背下來的，不是寫下來的，所以歷史學家得很用心地找證據。

🪨

Words carved in stone刻在石頭上的字

Ashoka's edicts survive on rocks and pillars across South Asia, most in the Brahmi script. They tell us about his conquest of Kalinga, his regret, and his ideas of right living, in his own words.
阿育王的詔令留在南亞各地的岩石和石柱上，大多用婆羅米文字寫成。它們用他自己的話，告訴我們他征服羯陵伽、他的懊悔，以及他對正當生活的想法。

Its limits · 它的限制The edicts tell us what Ashoka wanted people to read. They do not tell us how ordinary people lived or what they believed.詔令告訴我們阿育王希望人們讀到什麼，卻沒有告訴我們一般人怎麼生活、相信什麼。

Pilgrims' travel records朝聖者的遊記

Chinese pilgrims wrote down what they saw on the way to India. Faxian left around 399–400; Xuanzang traveled from 629 to 645 and described more than a hundred places in his Record of the Western Regions.
中國的朝聖者把去印度路上看到的寫了下來。法顯大約在 399 到 400 年出發；玄奘從 629 年走到 645 年，在《大唐西域記》裡描述了一百多個地方。

    很長一段時間，學者把他的一生定在大約西元前 560–480 年。台灣教育部的《重編國語辭典》到今天還寫西元前 566 年出生。

Where things stand · 目前的情況This lesson says “about 2,400 to 2,500 years ago” so that it fits the main scholarly views. The early sources were memorized, so no single date can be proved.這一課寫「大約兩千四、五百年前」，好讓它符合主要的學術看法。早期的資料是背下來的，所以沒有一個年代能被證明。

  • Yes, in his own words是，他自己這樣說

    In an edict found at Bairat, Ashoka tells the monks how great his faith in the Buddha, the Dhamma, and the Saṅgha is.
    在拜拉特發現的一篇詔令裡，阿育王告訴僧人，他對佛、法、僧的信心有多大。

    Dhammika, The Edicts of King Asoka ↗

  • A king for everyone所有人的國王

    Britannica notes that in his public edicts Ashoka spoke of no particular creed; another edict asks people to respect all religions. A 2024 book presents him as a ruler, a Buddhist, a moral philosopher, and someone who reached across religions.
    Britannica 指出，阿育王在公開的詔令裡沒有提倡某一種教義；另一篇詔令要人尊重所有的宗教。2024 年的一本書把他描寫成一位統治者、一位佛教徒、一位道德哲學家，也是一位跨越宗教的人。

    Britannica, “Ashoka”; Olivelle, Ashoka: Portrait of a Philosopher King (2024) ↗

Where things stand · 目前的情況Both can be true: he could be a Buddhist himself and still speak to all his people in broader terms.兩者可能都對：他自己可以是佛教徒，同時用更廣的話對所有的人民說話。

Where was the first image of the Buddha made?第一尊佛像是在哪裡做的？

  • Gandhara犍陀羅

    In the 1st century CE, one of the first places to show the Buddha in human form was India's northwestern frontier, in Gandhara.
    西元一世紀，最早把佛陀做成人形的地方之一，是印度西北邊境的犍陀羅。

    Metropolitan Museum of Art, “Buddhism and Buddhist Art” (2007) ↗

  • Mathura秣菟羅

    At about the same time, artists in Mathura, in north India, made a different-looking Buddha image.
    差不多同時，印度北部秣菟羅的藝術家，做出了樣子不一樣的佛像。

    Metropolitan Museum of Art, “Buddhism and Buddhist Art” (2007) ↗

Where things stand · 目前的情況Scholars still debate which came first. Before that, for centuries, artists showed the Buddha only through symbols such as footprints or an empty seat.學者還在討論哪一邊比較早。在那之前的好幾百年，藝術家只用腳印、空的座位這類象徵來表現佛陀。

When did Buddhism reach Japan?佛教什麼時候傳到日本？

  • 552 CE西元 552 年

    The Chronicles of Japan, finished in 720, say that the king of Baekje sent a gilt-bronze Buddha and sutras in the 13th year of Emperor Kinmei, 552 CE.
    720 年完成的《日本書紀》說，欽明天皇十三年（西元 552 年），百濟的聖明王送來金銅的釋迦佛像和經論。

    《日本書紀》卷十九 ↗

  • 538 CE西元 538 年

    Other old records give 538 CE for the same gift. Britannica gives both years: “in 538 or 552.”
    另外一些古老的紀錄，把同一份禮物記在西元 538 年。Britannica 兩個年份都寫：「538 年或 552 年」。

    Britannica, “Japanese religion” ↗

Where things stand · 目前的情況Both dates come from texts written long afterward. Either way, Buddhism reached Japan from Korea, and Japan's oldest temples were built soon after.兩個年代都來自很久以後寫的書。不管哪一個，佛教都是從朝鮮半島傳到日本，日本最古老的寺院也在不久以後蓋了起來。

Notice that these disagreements are about dates and evidence, not about whether Buddhism is right or wrong. History cannot answer that question.留意這些分歧是關於年代和證據，不是關於佛教對不對。那個問題，歷史回答不了。

Taiwan at That Time · 那時候的台灣

Far from the routes, for a long time 離路線很遠，很長一段時間

When Buddhism reached China, about 2,000 years ago, Taiwan was home to several prehistoric cultures. The first Buddhist temples we know of in the records came about 1,600 years later.
佛教傳到中國的時候，大約距今兩千年，台灣有好幾個史前文化。紀錄裡最早的佛寺，要再過大約一千六百年才出現。

🏝️

Archaeologists in Taiwan give dates as “years ago.” About 2,000 years ago is roughly the time of the Han edict of 65 CE.
台灣的考古學家用「距今幾年」說年代。距今大約兩千年，差不多就是西元 65 年漢朝那道詔書的時候。

  • Shisanhang culture, north北部的十三行文化

    Around 2,000 years ago, the Shisanhang culture rose in the Taipei Basin area.
    大約二千年前，十三行文化在臺北盆地附近興起。

    Around the same time, the Fengbitou culture in the south ended after about 1,500 years, and the Beinan culture in the east was passing the end of its peak, about 3,500 to 2,000 years ago.
    差不多同時，南部的鳳鼻頭文化（距今約三千五百到二千年前）結束；東部卑南文化極盛的年代（大約三千五百到二千年前）也在這時告一段落。

Stories Behind the Evidence · 證據背後的故事

A king's regret, four lions, a hidden library, and 657 texts 一個國王的懊悔、四隻獅子、一個藏起來的圖書館，和 657 部經論

Four short stories about the objects and journeys that tell Buddhism's history.
四個小故事：告訴我們佛教歷史的那些器物和旅程。

About 260 BCE · Kalinga · 羯陵伽

A king who said he was sorry一個說對不起的國王

Ashoka's thirteenth rock edict counts the cost of his war: 150,000 people taken away, 100,000 killed, and many more who died. Then he writes that he feels deep remorse, and that the best conquest is conquest by Dhamma. It is a rare thing to find in a royal inscription.
阿育王的第十三篇岩石詔令，算出了這場戰爭的代價：十五萬人被帶走，十萬人被殺，還有更多人死去。接著他寫道，他深深懊悔，而最好的征服，是用「法」來征服。在國王的銘文裡，這是很少見的。

3rd century BCE · Sarnath · 鹿野苑

Four lions, back to back背靠背的四隻獅子

Ashoka set up a pillar at Sarnath, where tradition says the Buddha first taught. On top, four lions sit back to back. That Lion Capital, now in the museum at Sarnath, is the basis of India's state emblem today.
阿育王在傳統上說佛陀第一次說法的鹿野苑，立了一根石柱。柱頂是四隻背靠背坐著的獅子。這個獅子柱頭現在放在鹿野苑的博物館，也是今天印度國徽的根據。

1900 · Dunhuang · 敦煌

A library behind a wall牆後面的圖書館

In 1900, a small cave at Mogao was found sealed behind a wall. Inside were tens of thousands of manuscripts and paintings, many of them Buddhist texts, in several languages.
1900 年，莫高窟有一個小石窟被發現封在一道牆後面。裡面有好幾萬件寫本和畫，很多是佛經，用了好幾種語言。

629–645 CE · Chang'an to India · 長安到印度

Sixteen years and 657 texts十六年和 657 部經論

Xuanzang left Chang'an in 629 and returned in 645 with 657 Buddhist texts. He spent the rest of his life translating them, and his travel record is still used by archaeologists to find ancient sites in India.
玄奘在 629 年離開長安，645 年帶著 657 部佛教經論回來。他用後半生翻譯它們，他的遊記到今天還被考古學家拿來尋找印度的古代遺址。

Myth vs. Fact · 常見迷思

Four things people get wrong 四個常見的誤會

✗ Myth · 迷思Buddhists believe the Buddha is a god.佛教徒相信佛陀是神。

✓ Fact · 事實In an early sutra (AN 4.36), a brahmin asks the Buddha whether he is a god, a spirit, or a human. The Buddha says he is none of these and asks to be remembered as a Buddha, “one who is awake.” Buddhist traditions differ in how they honor him.在一部早期的經典（AN 4.36）裡，有位婆羅門問佛陀是不是神、是精靈，還是人。佛陀回答都不是，請他記住自己是「佛陀」，也就是覺醒的人。各個佛教傳統禮敬他的方式不一樣。

✗ Myth · 迷思Buddhism began in China.佛教是從中國開始的。

✓ Fact · 事實Buddhism began in northern India. The earliest reliable record of it in China is from 65 CE, centuries after Ashoka's edicts.佛教從印度北部開始。中國最早可靠的佛教記載是西元 65 年，比阿育王的詔令晚了好幾百年。

✗ Myth · 迷思There have always been statues of the Buddha.一直都有佛像。

✓ Fact · 事實For centuries, artists showed the Buddha only through symbols such as footprints, an empty seat, or a tree. Images in human form appear in the 1st century CE.好幾百年裡，藝術家只用腳印、空的座位或一棵樹這類象徵來表現佛陀。人形的佛像到西元一世紀才出現。

✗ Myth · 迷思All Buddhists are vegetarian.所有的佛教徒都吃素。

✓ Fact · 事實An early Pāli sutra lets monks eat meat they did not see, hear, or suspect was killed for them. In China, Emperor Wu of Liang ordered monks and nuns to give up meat and alcohol, around 519–523 CE. Practice still differs from place to place.一部早期的巴利文經典說，僧人可以吃沒有看到、聽到或懷疑是為他們而殺的肉。在中國，梁武帝大約在西元 519 到 523 年之間，要求僧尼斷酒肉。各地的做法到今天還是不一樣。

Classroom Activity 1 · 課堂活動一

Pass the Teaching 把教法傳下去

You need · 準備材料

  • A short teaching of four lines, written on one card一張卡片，上面寫四行短短的教導
  • Paper and pencils for each team每一組紙和鉛筆

Steps · 步驟

  1. Divide the class into two teams. Show the card only to the first student in each team.把全班分成兩組。卡片只給每一組的第一個學生看。
  2. Team A whispers the lines from student to student, one at a time. Team B recites them together, out loud, as a group, each time someone new joins.A 組一個傳一個地悄悄說。B 組則是每加入一個人，大家就一起大聲誦念一次。
  3. At the end, both teams write down what they heard. Compare with the card.最後，兩組都把聽到的寫下來，和卡片比一比。
  4. Discuss in English: “Reciting together helps because …” and “Writing it down helps because …”用英文討論：「Reciting together helps because …」和「Writing it down helps because …」

Early monks recited the teachings together, which helps explain how they were kept for centuries without writing. The game also shows why historians still ask how much changed.
早期的僧人一起誦念教法，這有助於說明為什麼沒有文字也能保存好幾百年。這個遊戲也說明了，為什麼歷史學家還是會問：中間改變了多少？

Classroom Activity 2 · 課堂活動二

Date Detectives 年代偵探

You need · 準備材料

  • The 3D globe in this lesson這一課的 3D 地球儀
  • A worksheet with three columns: Place, Date, How do we know?一張學習單，分三欄：地方、年代、我們怎麼知道？

Steps · 步驟

  1. In pairs, choose six places on the globe and read their cards.兩人一組，在地球儀上選六個地方，讀它們的卡片。
  2. Fill in the three columns. Mark each date T (tradition) or E (evidence).填寫三欄。每個年代標上 T（傳統說法）或 E（證據）。
  3. Find the place whose source is furthest in time from the event. How many years apart?找出資料和那件事相隔最久的地方。差了幾年？
  4. Write one sentence with “According to …” and one with “… shows that …”.用 According to … 寫一句，再用 … shows that … 寫一句。

Remind students that a traditional date is not “wrong.” It is a different kind of source, and it tells us what later people believed.
提醒學生：傳統說法的年代不是「錯的」。它是另一種資料，告訴我們後來的人相信什麼。
