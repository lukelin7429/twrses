Big idea · 一句話看懂

Long before Europeans arrived, peoples of the Americas built great cities, calendars, and roads. The Maya carved stone cities and a full writing system; the Aztecs built Tenochtitlan on a lake; the Inca linked the Andes with more than 30,000 km of roads. They did it without carts or draft animals. The Maya, Nahua, and Quechua peoples still live there today.
早在歐洲人來以前，美洲的人們就蓋出了偉大的城市、曆法和道路。馬雅人用石頭蓋城市，還有完整的文字；阿茲特克人把特諾奇提特蘭蓋在湖上；印加人用超過三萬公里的道路把安地斯山連起來。他們沒有車子，也沒有拉車的牲口，還是做到了。馬雅人、納瓦人、克丘亞人今天都還住在那裡。

Unit 14: What Did the Maya, Aztecs, and Inca Build?

By about 1500 BCE, Maya people in southern Mexico and Central America were living in villages and growing corn, beans, and squash. By 200 CE their ceremonial centers had become cities with temples, pyramids, palaces, ball courts, and plazas. They cut limestone blocks with tools of harder stone, such as chert. In the Classic Period, from about 250 to 900, there were more than 40 Maya cities, among them Tikal, Copán, and Calakmul.

大約西元前 1500 年，墨西哥南部和中美洲的馬雅人已經住在村子裡，種玉米、豆子和南瓜。到了西元 200 年，他們的祭典中心已經變成城市，有神廟、金字塔、宮殿、球場和廣場。他們用比較硬的石頭（例如燧石）做工具，切下石灰岩的石塊。在大約 250 到 900 年的古典期，馬雅有四十多座城市，例如提卡爾、科潘和卡拉克穆爾。

The Maya developed a full writing system; Britannica calls it the only true writing system of the Americas before Europeans arrived. They carved inscriptions on stone monuments and painted books, called codices, on paper made from fig-tree bark. They counted in twenties, with a sign for zero, and kept careful calendars and tables of the Moon and Venus. After about 900, many cities of the southern lowlands emptied, while cities in the Yucatán, such as Chichén Itzá, went on. The Maya people did not disappear: more than five million people speak some 30 Mayan languages today.

馬雅人發展出完整的文字系統；Britannica 說，這是歐洲人來以前美洲唯一真正的文字系統。他們把文字刻在石頭紀念碑上，也畫在用無花果樹皮做的紙上，做成書，叫做抄本（codex）。他們用二十進位，有零的符號，還有精細的曆法，以及月亮和金星的表。大約 900 年以後，南部低地的許多城市沒有人住了，猶加敦半島上的城市，例如奇琴伊察，卻繼續興盛。馬雅人並沒有消失：今天有五百多萬人說大約三十種馬雅語言。

The Aztecs, who called themselves the Mexica, spoke Nahuatl. Their tradition says they wandered until they saw an eagle on a cactus on a small island in Lake Texcoco, and there, about 1325, they founded Tenochtitlan. They built causeways to the shore and farmed chinampas, narrow fields built up from lake mud that gave several harvests a year. By 1519 the Aztecs and their allies ruled some 5 to 6 million people, who paid tribute to the capital.

阿茲特克人自稱墨西加人，說納瓦特語。他們的傳統說，他們四處遷移，直到在特斯科科湖的一座小島上看到一隻停在仙人掌上的老鷹；大約 1325 年，他們就在那裡建立了特諾奇提特蘭。他們修了通往岸邊的堤道，在浮田（chinampa）上耕作：浮田是用湖底的泥堆成的狹長田地，一年可以收好幾次。到了 1519 年，阿茲特克人和盟友統治了大約五、六百萬人，這些人要向首都進貢。

The Inca made their capital at Cusco, high in the Andes. From 1438, under the ruler Pachacuti and his successors, they conquered an empire that stretched from what is now Ecuador to central Chile. They had no writing in the European sense; officials kept records on khipus, cords with knots that stood for numbers. They built a road network of more than 30,000 km, partly on older roads, with rope bridges over the gorges, and relay runners carried messages along it.

印加人以安地斯山高處的庫斯科為首都。從 1438 年起，在帕查庫蒂和他的繼承者手下，他們征服了一個從今天的厄瓜多延伸到智利中部的帝國。他們沒有歐洲那種意思的文字；官員用 khipu（結繩）記錄，繩子上的結代表數字。他們蓋了超過三萬公里的道路網（部分建在更早的道路上），用繩橋跨過峽谷，接力的信差沿著道路傳遞消息。

These peoples built without wheeled carts and without horses or oxen. Wheels existed in Mesoamerica, but only on small clay figures. People carried loads themselves, and in the Andes llamas carried them too. The Maya cut limestone with tools of harder stone, and Inca builders cut stone so precisely that their walls still stand without mortar.

這些民族蓋這一切，沒有用有輪子的車，也沒有馬或牛。中美洲有輪子，但只出現在小小的陶土像上。東西靠人自己背；在安地斯山，駱馬也幫忙馱。馬雅人用比較硬的石頭做工具切石灰岩；印加的工匠把石頭切得非常精準，石牆不用灰漿，到今天還立著。

In 1519 Spanish soldiers led by Hernán Cortés reached Mexico. With thousands of Indigenous allies who resented Aztec rule, and with diseases the people had never met, they took Tenochtitlan in 1521. In 1532 Francisco Pizarro captured the Inca ruler Atahuallpa at a time of civil war, and the Spanish took Cusco in 1533. Many of the first written accounts of these peoples were made by the conquerors and missionaries, so historians read them carefully, next to archaeology and the peoples' own records.

1519 年，科爾特斯帶領的西班牙士兵來到墨西哥。數千名不滿阿茲特克統治的原住民盟軍和他們一起作戰，加上當地人從來沒有碰過的疾病，他們在 1521 年攻下特諾奇提特蘭。1532 年，皮薩羅趁印加內戰的時候，俘虜了印加的統治者阿塔瓦爾帕；1533 年，西班牙人攻下庫斯科。關於這些民族最早的文字記載，很多是征服者和傳教士寫的，所以歷史學家讀的時候很小心，會和考古以及這些民族自己的紀錄對照。

Questions · 閱讀理解

Name one thing each of the Maya, the Aztecs, and the Inca built.

Maya: stone cities with pyramids (or writing, or calendars); Aztecs: Tenochtitlan on a lake, causeways, or chinampas; Inca: roads, rope bridges, or stone walls without mortar.

How did the Inca keep records without writing in the European sense?

Officials kept khipus, knotted cords whose knots stood for numbers.

Why do historians read the first Spanish accounts of these peoples carefully?

Many were written by conquerors and missionaries, who were outsiders and had their own purposes.

Read and Write Maya Numbers 讀一讀、寫一寫馬雅的數字

The Maya wrote numbers with just three signs: a dot for one, a bar for five, and a sign for zero. Numbers of 20 and more use places, like ours, but counted in twenties: the top place counts twenties and the bottom place counts ones. First read some numbers, then write some. There is no timer.
馬雅人寫數字只用三種符號：一點是 1，一橫是 5，還有一個零的符號。20 以上的數字和我們一樣用「位」，不過是二十進位：上面那一位數「幾個二十」，下面那一位數「幾個一」。先讀幾個，再自己寫幾個。不計時。

How Do We Know? · 我們怎麼知道？

Carved stone, painted books, and knotted cords 刻字的石頭、畫的書，和打結的繩子

這幾個民族各自留下不一樣的紀錄，每一種都有限制。另外還有西班牙人寫的記載。

🗿

Maya inscriptions and codices馬雅的銘文和抄本

Maya writing goes back at least to 300–200 BCE. Since the 1950s scholars have learned to read a substantial number of inscriptions, and they turned out to be mainly histories: events in the lives of rulers and their families, such as births, deaths, and captures.
馬雅文字至少可以追溯到西元前 300–200 年。1950 年代以來，學者讀懂了相當多的銘文；結果發現，它們主要是歷史：統治者和他們家族一生中的事，例如出生、死亡和被俘。

Its limits · 它的限制Most Maya books were destroyed as “pagan” by Spanish priests; in 1562 Diego de Landa had books and statues burned at Maní. Only four codices survive.大部分的馬雅書被西班牙神父當成「異教」毀掉了；1562 年，蘭達在馬尼燒掉書和神像。今天只剩四本抄本。

Britannica, “Maya hieroglyphic writing” and “Diego de Landa” ↗
🎨

Painted books by Nahua scribes納瓦人書記畫的書

Aztec scribes, called tlacuilos, painted records in pictures and glyphs. In 1542 they painted the Codex Mendoza, with the history of the rulers, the tribute of 38 provinces, and daily life.
阿茲特克的書記叫做 tlacuilo，用圖畫和符號作紀錄。1542 年，他們畫了《門多薩抄本》，裡面有統治者的歷史、38 個省的貢品，以及日常生活。

Its limits · 它的限制The Codex Mendoza was made two decades after the conquest, likely for the king of Spain, with notes in Spanish. Other Nahuatl writings of the 16th century use the alphabet that Spanish priests brought.《門多薩抄本》是征服之後二十年才畫的，大概是要給西班牙國王看，還加了西班牙文的註記。其他十六世紀的納瓦特語文獻，用的是西班牙神父帶來的字母。

Metropolitan Museum of Art, “Paying the Man”; Britannica, “Aztec” ↗
🪢

Khipus and the ground結繩和土地本身

Inca officials at every level kept khipus. Knots stood for numbers: a figure-eight knot for 1, a long knot for 2 to 9, and single knots for tens and hundreds, with a space for zero. More than 600 khipus have been found, and roads, bridges, and buildings still stand.
印加各層級的官員都用 khipu。結代表數字：8 字結是 1，繞 2 到 9 圈的長結是 2 到 9，單結代表十、百，空著不打結就是零。目前已經找到六百多條 khipu，道路、橋和建築也還在。

Its limits · 它的限制Scholars can read the numbers, but khipus that may record words have not been deciphered. Much of Inca history comes to us through Spaniards who wrote down what Inca officials told them.學者讀得懂數字，但可能記了話語的 khipu 還沒有解讀出來。印加的歷史，很多是透過西班牙人記下印加官員的口述才傳下來的。

Britannica, “quipu” and “Inca” ↗

    Britannica, “Maya” ↗

  • Broken trade, cut forests, and drought斷掉的貿易、砍光的森林和乾旱

    Discoveries in the 21st century led scholars to suggest more causes. One likely cause was wars that disrupted river and land trade routes; deforestation and drought may also have played a part.
    二十一世紀的新發現讓學者又提出別的原因：一個很可能的原因是戰爭讓河上和陸上的貿易路線中斷；森林被砍伐和乾旱也可能有影響。

    Britannica, “Maya” ↗

Where things stand · 目前的情況Britannica lists several possible causes together and does not say which mattered most. Cities emptied, but the people did not vanish: Maya cities in the Yucatán went on for centuries, and Maya communities live in the region today.Britannica 把幾個可能的原因並列，沒有說哪一個最重要。城市空了，人卻沒有消失：猶加敦的馬雅城市又興盛了好幾百年，今天馬雅人的社區也還在那一帶。

What was Machu Picchu?馬丘比丘到底是什麼地方？

  • The “lost city of the Incas”「印加失落之城」

    After 1911, Hiram Bingham claimed it was Vilcabamba, the last refuge of the Inca rulers, and later suggested it was a sanctuary for women chosen to serve the Sun. Britannica says many sources still call it the “lost city,” wrongly.
    1911 年以後，賓漢說它就是印加統治者最後的避難所比爾卡班巴，後來又說它是侍奉太陽的女子住的聖所。Britannica 說，很多資料到今天還把它叫做「失落之城」，這是錯的。

    Britannica, “Machu Picchu” ↗

  • A royal palace and retreat王室的宮殿和休憩地

    Its building style and other evidence suggest it was a palace complex of the ruler Pachacuti, and its remains suggest a royal retreat. Britannica adds that the reason it was abandoned is also unknown.
    從建築風格和其他證據看，它可能是統治者帕查庫蒂的宮殿群；遺物也顯示它可能是王室休憩的地方。Britannica 補充說，它為什麼被放棄，也不知道。

    Britannica, “Machu Picchu” ↗

Where things stand · 目前的情況Vilcabamba was later identified with another site, Espíritu Pampa. And Machu Picchu was never lost to the Quechua-speaking people who lived nearby: one of them guided Bingham there.比爾卡班巴後來被認定是另一個遺址，埃斯皮里圖潘帕。而且，馬丘比丘對住在附近、說克丘亞語的人從來不是「失落」的：帶賓漢上去的，就是其中一位。

Ask who wrote each source, and when.問一問：每一份資料是誰寫的？什麼時候寫的？

Taiwan at That Time · 那時候的台灣

An ocean between, and then a link 隔著一個大洋，後來連了起來

No contact between Taiwan and the Americas is known from the centuries of this lesson, but after the Spanish conquest, ships tied the two sides of the Pacific together.
在這一課的那幾百年，沒有台灣和美洲往來的紀錄；可是西班牙人征服美洲之後，船隻隔著太平洋把兩邊連了起來。

🏝️

When Tenochtitlan was young, a Chinese traveler was writing about an island near Penghu. Nearly three centuries later, the Spanish, who by then ran the Manila galleon trade across the Pacific, founded a settlement in northern Taiwan.
特諾奇提特蘭剛建立不久的時候，一位中國旅行家正在寫澎湖附近的一座島。將近三百年後，已經在太平洋上經營馬尼拉大帆船貿易的西班牙人，在台灣北部建立了聚落。

  • The 14th century: “Liuqiu”十四世紀：「琉球」

    In the century when Tenochtitlan was founded, the Yuan traveler Wang Dayuan wrote of an island called Liuqiu, very near Penghu. Scholars still debate whether it means Taiwan (Lessons 9 and 13).
    在特諾奇提特蘭建立的那個世紀，元朝旅行家汪大淵寫到一座「自彭湖望之甚近」的「琉球」。這是不是指台灣，學者還在討論（第九、十三課）。

    汪大淵《島夷誌略》「琉球」 ↗

  • 1565 onward: the Manila galleons1565 年起：馬尼拉大帆船

    From 1565 to 1815, a Spanish galleon sailed each year between Acapulco in Mexico and Manila. It carried Chinese silk to the Americas and brought back huge quantities of Mexican silver.
    從 1565 到 1815 年，每年有一艘西班牙大帆船在墨西哥的阿卡普爾科和馬尼拉之間往返，把中國的絲綢運到美洲，再運回大量墨西哥的白銀。

    Britannica, “Manila galleon” ↗

  • 1626: the Spanish at Keelung1626 年：西班牙人到基隆

    In 1626 the Spanish founded a settlement, San Salvador, on today's Heping Island in Keelung. Archaeologists found the foundations of a church or convent and a cemetery; early findings suggest the people buried there most likely came from Europe, Asia, and possibly Africa.
    1626 年，西班牙人在今天基隆的和平島建立聚落「聖薩爾瓦多」。考古學家挖到教堂（或修道院）的地基和墓地；初步結果顯示，葬在那裡的人很可能來自歐洲、亞洲，也可能來自非洲。

    University of Konstanz, “A new perspective on the European colonisation of Asia” (2017) ↗

Stories Behind the Evidence · 證據背後的故事

A bridge rebuilt every year, gardens on a lake, books that were burned, and a stairway of words 每年重建的橋、湖上的田、被燒掉的書，和一道寫滿字的階梯

Four short stories about what these peoples built and how it has lasted.
四個小故事：這些民族蓋了什麼，又怎麼留了下來。

Every year · Q'eswachaka · 克斯瓦恰卡

A bridge woven every year每年重新編的橋

Over a gorge of the Apurímac River, four Quechua-speaking communities rebuild a rope bridge each year in three days. Families twist grass into thin ropes; bridge builders braid them into six great ropes; the communities weave the bridge from both ends and then hold a festival.
在阿普里馬克河的峽谷上，四個說克丘亞語的社區每年花三天重建一座繩橋。各家先把草搓成細繩；造橋的師傅把它們編成六條大繩；社區的人從兩端一起編橋，最後辦一場慶典。

1325 → today · Xochimilco · 霍奇米爾科

Gardens on a lake湖上的田

Chinampas are narrow islands, some 6 to 10 meters wide and 100 to 200 meters long, built from layers of plants, earth, and mud. The lake waters and fertilizes them. Britannica says the technique is still used in Xochimilco, in Mexico City.
浮田是狹長的人工島，大約寬 6 到 10 公尺、長 100 到 200 公尺，用一層層的植物、泥土和湖泥堆成，湖水會替它澆水、施肥。Britannica 說，墨西哥城的霍奇米爾科到今天還在用這種方法。

1562 · Maní · 馬尼

The books that were burned被燒掉的書

The Franciscan friar Diego de Landa had some 5,000 Maya statues and many books destroyed at Maní, calling them the devil's work. Later, on trial in Spain, he wrote one of the main early accounts of Maya life. Only four Maya codices survive today, in Dresden, Madrid, Paris, and Mexico City.
方濟會士蘭達在馬尼毀掉大約五千尊馬雅神像和許多書，說那是魔鬼的東西。後來他在西班牙受審時，卻寫了一本最重要的早期馬雅生活紀錄。今天只剩四本馬雅抄本，分別在德勒斯登、馬德里、巴黎和墨西哥城。

Copán · 科潘

A stairway of words寫滿字的階梯

At Copán, a stairway leading up to a temple carries some 1,260 glyphs on its 63 remaining steps. Reading such inscriptions since 1975 has revealed much of the city's political and dynastic history, with at least 16 kings.
在科潘，一道通往神廟的階梯，留下來的 63 級上刻了大約 1,260 個字符。1975 年起，學者讀懂這類銘文，知道了這座城大部分的政治和王朝歷史，至少有十六位國王。

Myth vs. Fact · 常見迷思

Four things people get wrong 四個常見的誤會

✗ Myth · 迷思Columbus discovered America.哥倫布發現了美洲。

✓ Fact · 事實Millions of people already lived there, and around 500 CE Teotihuacan was one of the largest cities in the world (Lesson 1 tells how people first arrived). In 1492 Columbus reached the Caribbean; Europeans met the Americas, they did not discover them.那裡早就住了好幾百萬人；大約西元 500 年，特奧蒂瓦坎是世界上最大的城市之一（人最早怎麼到美洲，見第一課）。1492 年哥倫布到了加勒比海；是歐洲人遇見了美洲，不是發現了美洲。

✗ Myth · 迷思The Maya vanished.馬雅人消失了。

✓ Fact · 事實Many southern cities emptied after 900, but cities in the Yucatán went on, and more than five million people speak Mayan languages today. Nahuatl and Quechua are spoken today too.900 年以後，南部的許多城市空了，但猶加敦的城市繼續興盛；今天有五百多萬人說馬雅語言。納瓦特語和克丘亞語今天也都還有人說。

✗ Myth · 迷思Without wheels or iron, they could only build small things.沒有輪子和鐵，只能蓋小東西。

✓ Fact · 事實Teotihuacan's Pyramid of the Sun rises about 66 meters, Inca walls of precisely cut stone stand without mortar, and the Inca roads covered more than 30,000 km. Wheels existed in Mesoamerica only on small figures; people and llamas carried the loads.特奧蒂瓦坎的太陽金字塔大約 66 公尺高，印加精準切割的石牆不用灰漿也立得住，印加的道路總長超過三萬公里。中美洲的輪子只出現在小陶像上；東西靠人和駱馬搬運。

✗ Myth · 迷思The Maya were peaceful stargazers.馬雅人是一群愛好和平、只看星星的人。

✓ Fact · 事實Britannica says scholars of the mid-20th century thought so, mistakenly. Once the inscriptions could be read, they told of kings, wars, and captives, alongside the calendars and astronomy.Britannica 說，二十世紀中期的學者是這樣想的，但想錯了。等到讀得懂銘文，才發現上面除了曆法和天文，也記了國王、戰爭和俘虜。

Classroom Activity 1 · 課堂活動一

Pass the Khipu 傳遞結繩

You need · 準備材料

  • Pieces of string or yarn, one per team每組一條繩子或毛線
  • A card with a number for each team每組一張寫了數字的卡片

Steps · 步驟

  1. Show the khipu code: single knots near the top for tens, a long knot (wrapped 2 to 9 times) at the bottom for ones.先說明 khipu 的記法：上面的單結代表幾個十，最下面繞 2 到 9 圈的長結代表個位。
  2. The first student in each team ties the number on its card. No talking and no writing.每組第一個學生把卡片上的數字打成結。不能說話、不能寫字。
  3. Pass the cord down the line like relay runners. The last student reads the number aloud.像接力的信差一樣，把繩子一個一個傳下去，最後一個學生大聲讀出數字。
  4. Discuss in English: “The message was carried along …” and “The knots stood for …”用英文討論：「The message was carried along …」和「The knots stood for …」

Real khipus used a figure-eight knot for 1; for this game, any knot will do. Remind students that khipus that may record words are still not deciphered.
真正的 khipu 用 8 字結代表 1；遊戲裡打什麼結都可以。提醒學生：可能記了話語的 khipu 到今天還沒有解讀出來。

Classroom Activity 2 · 課堂活動二

Who Wrote This? 這是誰寫的？

You need · 準備材料

  • The three “How Do We Know?” cards in this lesson這一課「我們怎麼知道？」的三張卡

Steps · 步驟

  1. In groups, list the kinds of sources: Maya inscriptions, the Codex Mendoza, khipus, and Spanish accounts.分組列出史料的種類：馬雅銘文、《門多薩抄本》、khipu，以及西班牙人的記載。
  2. For each, answer: Who made it? When? For whom?每一種都回答：誰做的？什麼時候？給誰看的？
  3. Share one thing each source can tell us and one thing it cannot.說出每一種史料能告訴我們的一件事，和不能告訴我們的一件事。

Keep the focus on evidence. If students ask about sacrifice or the conquest, answer plainly and briefly, and point out who wrote each account.
重點放在證據。學生問到人祭或征服時，簡短、平實地回答，並指出每一份記載是誰寫的。
