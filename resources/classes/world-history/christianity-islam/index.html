Big idea · 一句話看懂

Christianity began in the 1st century in Judea, at the eastern edge of the Roman Empire; Islam began in the 7th century in Mecca and Medina, in Arabia. Both started with a small group of people. They spread along imperial roads, trade routes, and sea lanes to places far away, and people in each place took them up in their own languages and cultures.
基督宗教在一世紀從羅馬帝國東邊的猶太地區開始；伊斯蘭教在七世紀從阿拉伯半島的麥加和麥地那開始。兩者都從一小群人開始，沿著帝國的道路、貿易路線和海路傳到很遠的地方，各地的人用自己的語言和文化接受它們。

Unit 10: How Did Christianity and Islam Begin and Spread?

Today more than half of the world's people are Christians or Muslims: Pew Research Center counted 28.8% Christians and 25.6% Muslims in 2020. This lesson tells how both religions began and spread, as history. Where something is a matter of faith, we say “Christians believe” or “Muslims believe”; where it comes from a later tradition, we say “tradition says”; and where it can be checked, we say “the evidence shows.” We study the two together because their stories touch the same lands and the same routes, not to compare them.

今天全世界一半以上的人是基督徒或穆斯林：皮尤研究中心統計，2020 年基督徒占 28.8%，穆斯林占 25.6%。這一課把兩個宗教怎麼開始、怎麼傳開，當作歷史來講。遇到信仰的事，我們說「基督徒相信」或「穆斯林相信」；來自後來的傳統，我們說「傳統上說」；可以查核的，我們說「史料顯示」。把兩個宗教放在一起講，是因為它們的故事發生在同一些土地、同一些路上，不是要比較它們。

Christianity began with Jesus of Nazareth, a Jewish teacher from Galilee. Around 30 CE, possibly in that very year, he was executed by crucifixion in Jerusalem under the Roman governor Pontius Pilate. Christians believe he rose from the dead. His followers, led by apostles such as Peter and Paul, carried his message to Jewish and non-Jewish communities around the eastern Mediterranean, to Antioch, Alexandria, and Rome. The Roman roads and sea routes, and the Greek language that many people shared, helped them travel.

基督宗教從拿撒勒人耶穌開始，他是來自加利利的猶太教師。大約西元 30 年（可能就是那一年），他在耶路撒冷被羅馬總督本丟・彼拉多釘死在十字架上。基督徒相信他從死裡復活。他的追隨者由彼得、保羅等使徒帶領，把他的信息傳給地中海東岸的猶太人和非猶太人群體，傳到安提阿、亞歷山卓和羅馬。羅馬的道路和海路，以及許多人共通的希臘語，幫助他們旅行。

For almost three centuries Christians were a small group, and at times they were punished. In Rome, after a great fire in 64 CE, the emperor Nero blamed them and had many killed. Later emperors sometimes ordered persecutions too. Then in 313 the emperor Constantine made the persecution of Christians illegal, and in 380 the emperor Theodosius I made Catholic Christianity the official religion of the empire. By then it had also spread beyond Roman rule, to Armenia and to Aksum in Ethiopia. Later, along the Silk Roads, a Christian mission reached Chang'an in 635.

將近三百年的時間，基督徒是一小群人，有時候還受到懲罰。在羅馬，西元 64 年的一場大火之後，皇帝尼祿把罪名推給他們，殺害了很多人。後來的皇帝有時候也下令迫害。到了 313 年，皇帝君士坦丁讓迫害基督徒變成違法的；380 年，皇帝狄奧多西一世把大公教會的基督宗教定為帝國的正式宗教。那時它也已經傳到羅馬的統治之外，傳到亞美尼亞和衣索比亞的阿克蘇姆。後來，沿著絲路，基督教的傳教團在 635 年到了長安。

Islam began in the early 600s in Mecca, a trading town in Arabia. Muslims believe that from 610 the angel Gabriel brought God's words to Muhammad, a man of the city, and that these words were gathered as the Quran. Facing opposition, Muhammad and his followers moved to Medina in 622, a journey called the Hijra; the Islamic calendar counts its years from it. By the time Muhammad died in 632, much of Arabia had accepted his leadership.

伊斯蘭教在 600 年代初期，從阿拉伯半島的貿易城鎮麥加開始。穆斯林相信，從 610 年起，天使吉卜利勒（加百列）把真主的話帶給城裡的穆罕默德，這些話後來彙集成《古蘭經》。在麥加受到反對，穆罕默德和追隨者在 622 年搬到麥地那，這次遷移叫做「希吉拉」；伊斯蘭曆就從這一年開始算。到 632 年穆罕默德去世的時候，阿拉伯半島大部分已經接受他的領導。

Within about a hundred years, Muslim rule had spread as far west as Spain and as far east as India, and the caliphs ruled from Damascus and then Baghdad. But ruling a land is not the same as converting its people. Historians such as Richard Bulliet argue that in places such as Iran most people became Muslim only gradually, over about three centuries. Islam also spread along trade routes, carried by merchants and scholars, to West Africa, Southeast Asia, and the ports of China, while in northern India it came with conquest.

大約一百年之內，穆斯林的統治範圍就西到西班牙、東到印度，哈里發先後在大馬士革和巴格達統治。但統治一片土地，不等於那裡的人都改了信仰。布列特等歷史學家主張，在伊朗等地，大多數人是在大約三百年間，慢慢成為穆斯林的。伊斯蘭教也沿著貿易路線，由商人和學者帶到西非、東南亞和中國的港口；在印度北部，則是隨著征服而來。

Both religions lived side by side and met in many ways. In Baghdad a Christian scholar translated Greek books for the caliphs, and in Muslim-ruled Córdoba Christians kept their own communities. There were also wars, such as the Crusades from 1095, when armies from western Europe fought to take Jerusalem, and the Ottoman capture of Constantinople in 1453, which ended the Byzantine Empire. Each side remembered these events differently. By 1500, both religions were spoken of in dozens of languages, across Asia, Africa, and Europe.

兩個宗教並存，也以許多方式相遇。在巴格達，一位基督徒學者替哈里發翻譯希臘文的書；在穆斯林統治的科爾多瓦，基督徒保有自己的社群。也有戰爭，例如從 1095 年開始的十字軍，西歐的軍隊為了奪取耶路撒冷而作戰；還有 1453 年鄂圖曼攻下君士坦丁堡，拜占庭帝國就此結束。雙方對這些事有不同的記憶。到了 1500 年，這兩個宗教已經用幾十種語言，傳遍亞洲、非洲和歐洲。

Questions · 閱讀理解

Why does this lesson say “Christians believe” or “Muslims believe” for some statements, but “the evidence shows” for others?

Matters of faith, such as the resurrection of Jesus or revelations to Muhammad, cannot be tested by historical evidence. Other things, such as the date of a coin or a letter, can be checked.

Name two things that helped Christianity spread in its first four centuries, and two that helped Islam spread after 632.

Christianity: Roman roads and sea routes, the shared Greek language, missionaries such as Paul, and from the 300s the support of emperors (any two). Islam: conquests, trade routes across the Sahara and the Indian Ocean, merchants and scholars (any two).

Why do historians say that conquering a land is not the same as converting its people?

Rulers could change quickly, but many people kept their old religions for a long time. Historians such as Bulliet argue that in places such as Iran most people became Muslim gradually, over about three centuries.

The oldest manuscripts最古老的抄本

A scrap of papyrus from Egypt, now in the John Rylands Library in Manchester, holds a few lines of the Gospel of John (18:31–38) and is often dated to around 125 CE. Two parchment leaves of the Quran in Birmingham were radiocarbon-dated to between 568 and 645 CE.
一小片來自埃及的莎草紙，現在收藏在曼徹斯特的約翰・萊蘭茲圖書館，上面有《約翰福音》第 18 章的幾行字，常被定在大約西元 125 年。伯明罕收藏的兩頁《古蘭經》羊皮紙，經過放射性碳定年，在西元 568 到 645 年之間。

Its limits · 它的限制Dating by handwriting is uncertain: the estimate of about 125 allows 25 years either way, and other scholars prefer about 140. A radiocarbon date tells when the animal lived, not exactly when the words were written.用字體來定年代並不確定：大約 125 年的估計，前後各可差二十五年；也有學者認為大約是 140 年。放射性碳定年說的是那隻動物活著的時候，不完全等於寫字的時候。

Biblical Archaeology Society, “Earliest New Testament Fragment”; University of Birmingham (2015) ↗
🏛️

What outsiders wrote外人怎麼寫

Roman writers who were not Christians mentioned the new group: the governor Pliny the Younger around 112 CE, and the historian Tacitus a few years later. Britannica notes that a Syriac chronicle and an Armenian history, written within the first few decades after Muhammad's traditional death date, also mention him.
不是基督徒的羅馬作家提到了這個新的群體：大約西元 112 年的總督小普林尼，以及幾年後的史家塔西佗。Britannica 指出，在穆罕默德傳統上的去世年代之後的頭幾十年內寫成的一部敘利亞文編年史和一部亞美尼亞史書，也提到了他。

Its limits · 它的限制Outsiders often misunderstood what they saw, and some disliked the people they described. Their value is that they were close in time and had no reason to invent the new group.外人常常誤解他們看到的事，有些人也不喜歡他們描述的人。他們的價值在於離那個時代很近，也沒有理由捏造出這個新的群體。

Pliny, Letters 10.96; Tacitus, Annals 15.44; Britannica, “Muhammad” ↗
🪙

Coins and inscriptions錢幣和銘文

Rulers announced their faith on things that could not easily be changed later. King Ezana's coins from Aksum switched from a disk and crescent to a cross. The Dome of the Rock in Jerusalem is dated by a dedication inscription to the Islamic year 72, which is 691–692 CE.
統治者把自己的信仰刻在後來很難改的東西上。阿克蘇姆國王埃扎納的錢幣，從圓盤和新月改成十字架。耶路撒冷的圓頂清真寺有一段奉獻銘文，記的是伊斯蘭曆 72 年，也就是西元 691 到 692 年。

Its limits · 它的限制Coins and inscriptions show what rulers wanted people to see. They tell us little about what ordinary people believed.錢幣和銘文表現的是統治者想讓人看到的樣子，很少告訴我們一般人信什麼。

Metropolitan Museum of Art, “Foundations of Aksumite Civilization and Its Christian Legacy”; Archnet, Qubba al-Sakhra ↗

Different Accounts · 不同的說法

Four questions historians still discuss 歷史學家還在討論的四個問題

These are questions about history, not about which beliefs are true. On each, historians give more than one answer.
這些是歷史的問題，不是哪一種信仰對不對的問題。每一題，歷史學家都有不只一種答案。

Why did Constantine support Christianity?君士坦丁為什麼支持基督宗教？

  • He said he believed他說自己相信

    Constantine himself credited his success to the Christian God, and claimed to have won a battle because of a vision he received beforehand. He paid for churches, Bibles, and church councils.
    君士坦丁自己把他的成功歸功於基督徒的上帝，也說他在一場戰役之前得到一個異象，因而打贏了。他出錢蓋教堂、抄寫《聖經》、召開教會會議。

    Britannica, “Constantine I” ↗

  • It was also politics也有政治的考量

    Some have argued that his conversion was politically motivated. Britannica notes that he was not baptized until shortly before his death.
    有些人主張，他改信是出於政治的動機。Britannica 也指出，他直到臨死前不久才受洗。

    Britannica, “Constantine I” ↗

Where things stand · 目前的情況The two views can both be partly true: a person can act for more than one reason. What is not in doubt is what he did: he paid for churches, copies of the Bible, and church councils.兩種看法可以都有一部分是對的：一個人做事可以有不只一個理由。沒有疑問的是他做了什麼：他出錢蓋教堂、抄寫《聖經》、召開教會會議。

How fast did people become Muslim?人們多快成為穆斯林？

  • Rule came fast統治來得很快

    Under the Umayyad caliphs (661–750), Muslim rule expanded rapidly, as far west as Spain and as far east as India, spreading both Islam and the Arabic language.
    在伍麥亞王朝哈里發的時候（661–750），穆斯林的統治快速擴張，西到西班牙、東到印度，伊斯蘭教和阿拉伯語也跟著傳開。

    Britannica, “Umayyad dynasty” ↗

  • Conversion was slow改信是慢慢的

    The historian Richard Bulliet, studying the names of people in old biographies, argued that in Iran and other lands most people became Muslim gradually over about three centuries.
    歷史學家布列特（Richard Bulliet）研究古代傳記裡人名的變化，主張在伊朗等地，大多數人是在大約三百年間，慢慢成為穆斯林的。

    Bulliet, Conversion to Islam in the Medieval Period (Harvard University Press, 1979) ↗

Where things stand · 目前的情況The two views answer different questions: how fast rulers changed, and how fast ordinary people did. Bulliet's numbers are estimates drawn from the names of people who appear in books.兩種說法回答的是不同的問題：統治者換得多快，以及一般人變得多快。布列特的數字是從書裡出現的人名推估出來的。

When did Christianity reach India?基督宗教什麼時候傳到印度？

  • 52 CE, with Thomas西元 52 年，多馬傳來

    The Thomas Christians of Kerala hold that the apostle Thomas landed near Kodungallur in 52 CE and founded churches in seven villages.
    喀拉拉的多馬基督徒認為，使徒多馬在西元 52 年於科頓格勒爾附近登陸，在七個村子建立了教會。

    Britannica, “Thomas Christians” ↗

  • By the 2nd or 3rd century最晚二或三世紀

    Britannica says the story of Thomas is not verified, but that evidence suggests Christians have lived on the Malabar Coast since the 2nd or 3rd century.
    Britannica 說多馬的故事沒有得到證實，但證據顯示，從二或三世紀起，馬拉巴爾海岸就有基督徒。

    Britannica, “Thomas Christians” ↗

Where things stand · 目前的情況Either way, Christianity reached India very early, by sea, long before Europeans sailed there.不管哪一種說法，基督宗教都很早就從海路傳到印度，比歐洲人航行到那裡早得多。

How should we understand the Crusades?該怎麼理解十字軍？

  • As the crusaders saw them十字軍自己的看法

    Western European Christians saw the expeditions as a way to retake the Holy Land and to check the spread of Islam. Britannica notes that many who took part saw them as a way to make up for their sins and win forgiveness.
    西歐的基督徒把這些遠征看成奪回聖地、阻止伊斯蘭教擴張的方法。Britannica 指出，很多參加的人把它看成補贖罪過、得到赦免的方法。

    Britannica, “Crusades” ↗

  • As Muslims at the time saw them當時穆斯林的看法

    The historian Carole Hillenbrand shows that Muslim writers of the time usually called the crusaders “Franks” and saw them as invaders, one of several threats of the age; driving them out came to be described as jihad, struggle in God's cause.
    歷史學家希倫布蘭德（Carole Hillenbrand）指出，當時的穆斯林作家通常把十字軍叫做「法蘭克人」，把他們看成入侵者，是那個時代好幾個威脅之一；把他們趕出去，後來被說成「吉哈德」，也就是為主道而奮鬥。

    Hillenbrand, The Crusades: Islamic Perspectives (Edinburgh University Press, 1999) ↗

Where things stand · 目前的情況The same wars look different from each side. Historians today read Latin, Greek, Arabic, Armenian, and Hebrew sources together, and they also study the motives of land, wealth, and power that mixed with faith.同樣的戰爭，從兩邊看起來不一樣。今天的歷史學家會一起讀拉丁文、希臘文、阿拉伯文、亞美尼亞文和希伯來文的資料，也研究和信仰混在一起的土地、財富與權力的動機。

Notice that the views often fit together: people can act for more than one reason at once.留意這些說法常常可以同時成立：一個人做一件事，可以同時有好幾個理由。

Taiwan at That Time · 那時候的台灣

Taiwan's own beliefs, and later arrivals 台灣自己的信仰，和後來才到的宗教

Between 1 and 1500 CE, neither religion had reached Taiwan. The peoples of Taiwan had their own beliefs and customs. Christianity arrived in the 1600s; a large mosque was built in Taipei in the 20th century.
從西元 1 年到 1500 年，這兩個宗教都還沒有傳到台灣。台灣的人群有自己的信仰和習俗。基督宗教在 1600 年代傳到台灣；臺北的大清真寺則是二十世紀才蓋的。

🏝️

Archaeologists in Taiwan give dates as “years ago.” About 2,000 years ago is roughly the 1st century CE.
台灣的考古學家用「距今幾年」說年代。距今大約兩千年，差不多是西元一世紀。

  • Shisanhang culture, north北部的十三行文化

    The Shisanhang people, from about 2,000 years ago, buried their dead lying on one side with the legs bent. Customs like this show beliefs about death of their own.
    大約距今兩千年的十三行文化，「流行側身屈肢葬」。這樣的習俗，顯示他們對死亡有自己的看法。

    國立臺灣史前文化博物館 考古學習資源 ↗

  • Niaosong culture, southwest西南部的蔦松文化

    The museum says the Niaosong culture's small black pottery jars and bird-headed vessels were probably used in rituals.
    史前館說，蔦松文化的小黑陶罐和鳥頭狀器，「應該都和祭祀有關」。

    國立臺灣史前文化博物館 考古學習資源 ↗

  • Christianity arrives, 1627基督宗教傳來，1627 年

    On May 4, 1627, the Dutch minister Georgius Candidius arrived in Taiwan, and by 1628 he was preaching in the Siraya village of Sinkan.
    1627 年 5 月 4 日，荷蘭牧師甘迪留斯來到台灣，1628 年已經在西拉雅族的新港社傳教。

    Campbell, Formosa under the Dutch (1903); 國家教育研究院《原住民族重大歷史事件補充教材：麻豆社事件》 ↗

    In the north, the Spanish held a colony at Keelung in the same years. Archaeologists on Heping Island have found its church, Todos los Santos, and its cemetery.
    在北部，同一個時期西班牙人在基隆有一個據點。考古學家在和平島找到了它的教堂，諸聖教堂，以及墓地。

    University of Konstanz (2017) ↗

  • A new way of writing一種新的文字

    Missionaries taught the Siraya to write their own language with the Roman alphabet. The Siraya went on using it for contracts long after the Dutch left; the last known document dates from 1818.
    傳教士教西拉雅人用羅馬字母拼寫自己的語言。荷蘭人離開以後，西拉雅人還繼續用它寫契約，留下的「新港文書」最晚的一件寫於 1818 年。

Stories Behind the Evidence · 證據背後的故事

A governor's question, a coin, a translator, and a caravan of gold 總督的疑問、一枚錢幣、一位譯者，和一支載滿黃金的商隊

Four short stories about people who helped the two religions travel, or who wrote down what they saw.
四個小故事：幫助兩個宗教旅行的人，或把看到的事寫下來的人。

About 112 CE · Bithynia · 比提尼亞

A governor asks for advice總督請示皇帝

Pliny the Younger did not know what to do about the Christians in his province, so he wrote to the emperor Trajan. Trajan replied that Christians should not be hunted down and that unsigned accusations should not be accepted, though those found guilty could still be punished. The letters show how unsure Roman officials were about this new group.
小普林尼不知道該怎麼處理他的行省裡的基督徒，就寫信問皇帝圖拉真。圖拉真回答：不要去搜捕基督徒，沒有署名的控告也不要接受；不過被證實有罪的人，還是可以處罰。這些信顯示，羅馬的官員對這個新的群體有多麼不確定。

4th century · Aksum · 阿克蘇姆

A cross on a coin錢幣上的十字架

Aksum linked African, Mediterranean, and Asian trade, and its gold coins traveled with its merchants. When King Ezana became a Christian, his coins began to carry a cross instead of the old disk and crescent. Anyone who handled the money could see the change.
阿克蘇姆連起非洲、地中海和亞洲的貿易，它的金幣跟著商人旅行。埃扎納國王成為基督徒以後，他的錢幣上就不再是古老的圓盤和新月，而是十字架。任何拿到這些錢的人，都看得到這個改變。

9th century · Baghdad · 巴格達

A Christian translator for the caliph替哈里發翻譯的基督徒

Hunayn ibn Ishaq, a Christian of the Church of the East, was chief physician to the Abbasid caliph. He traveled to Syria, Palestine, and Egypt to collect Greek manuscripts and, with his students, translated works of Galen, Plato, and Aristotle. Most of the Greek originals of Galen's works are lost, so these translations are especially important.
侯奈因・伊本・易斯哈格是東方教會的基督徒，擔任阿拔斯王朝哈里發的首席御醫。他到敘利亞、巴勒斯坦和埃及收集希臘文的抄本，和學生一起翻譯蓋倫、柏拉圖和亞里斯多德的著作。蓋倫著作的希臘文原本大多已經失傳，所以這些譯本特別重要。

1324 CE · Cairo · 開羅

A pilgrim with a caravan of gold帶著黃金商隊的朝覲者

Mansa Musa of Mali crossed the Sahara on his way to Mecca. Chroniclers of the time reported that his caravan had some 60,000 people and so much gold that, after he spent and gave it away in Cairo, the value of gold there fell for years. Back home, he helped make Timbuktu a center of trade and Islamic learning.
馬利的曼薩・穆薩穿越撒哈拉沙漠去麥加。當時的編年史家記載，他的商隊大約有六萬人，帶了非常多的黃金；他在開羅花掉、送掉之後，那裡的金價好幾年都下跌。回國以後，他幫助廷巴克圖成為貿易和伊斯蘭學術的中心。

Myth vs. Fact · 常見迷思

Four things people get wrong 四個常見的誤會

✗ Myth · 迷思Christianity began in Europe.基督宗教是從歐洲開始的。

✓ Fact · 事實It began in Judea, in western Asia. Early Christian centers included Antioch and Alexandria, and Christianity reached Armenia, Ethiopia, India, and, along the Silk Roads, Chang'an.它是從西亞的猶太地區開始的。早期的基督教中心包括安提阿和亞歷山卓；基督宗教也傳到亞美尼亞、衣索比亞、印度，並沿著絲路傳到長安。

✗ Myth · 迷思Islam spread only by the sword.伊斯蘭教只靠武力傳播。

✓ Fact · 事實Early Muslim rule did expand by conquest, but historians such as Bulliet argue that most people converted gradually, over generations. In Southeast Asia, the first Muslim kingdoms were harbor towns on the trade routes, and Timbuktu became a center of trade and Islamic learning.早期穆斯林的統治確實是靠征服擴張的，但布列特等歷史學家主張，大多數人是經過好幾代，慢慢改信的。在東南亞，最早的穆斯林王國是貿易路線上的港口；廷巴克圖也成為貿易和伊斯蘭學術的中心。

✗ Myth · 迷思Christians and Muslims have always been enemies.基督徒和穆斯林一直都是敵人。

✓ Fact · 事實There were wars, such as the Crusades, but also long periods of living side by side: a Christian scholar was chief physician to the caliph in Baghdad, and Christians kept their own communities in Muslim-ruled Córdoba.有過戰爭，例如十字軍；但也有很長的時間彼此並存：一位基督徒學者在巴格達當哈里發的首席御醫，基督徒也在穆斯林統治的科爾多瓦保有自己的社群。

✗ Myth · 迷思Year 1 CE is the year Jesus was born.西元 1 年就是耶穌出生的那一年。

✓ Fact · 事實The year count was worked out in 525 by a scholar in Rome, Dionysius Exiguus, and historians think he got the year of Jesus' birth wrong: Britannica dates the birth to about 6–4 BCE. The Islamic calendar does not start with Muhammad's birth either, but with the Hijra in 622.這套紀年是 525 年羅馬的學者狄奧尼修斯（Dionysius Exiguus）推算出來的，歷史學家認為他把耶穌出生的年代算錯了：Britannica 把耶穌出生定在大約西元前 6 到 4 年。伊斯蘭曆也不是從穆罕默德出生開始算，而是從 622 年的希吉拉開始。

Classroom Activity 1 · 課堂活動一

Faith or Evidence? Card Sort 信仰還是證據？卡片分類

You need · 準備材料

  • Twelve statement cards about the two religions (six from each), made from this lesson十二張關於兩個宗教的句子卡（每個宗教六張），從這一課取材
  • Two labels: “Believers hold / tradition says” and “Evidence shows”兩張標籤：「信徒相信／傳統上說」和「史料顯示」

Steps · 步驟

  1. In groups of four, sort the cards under the two labels.四人一組，把卡片分到兩張標籤底下。
  2. For each “evidence” card, name the kind of evidence: a coin, an inscription, a manuscript, or an outsider's letter.每一張「史料」卡，說出是哪一種證據：錢幣、銘文、抄本，還是外人的信。
  3. Rewrite two cards as fair sentences, using “Christians believe …,” “Muslims believe …,” or “According to tradition …”把兩張卡改寫成公平的句子，用「Christians believe …」「Muslims believe …」或「According to tradition …」
  4. Discuss: why does a historian not need to decide whether a belief is true?討論：為什麼歷史學家不需要判斷一個信仰對不對？

Remind students that sorting a statement as a belief is not saying it is false. Classmates may hold these faiths, so keep the language respectful.
提醒學生：把一句話分到「信仰」，不是說它是假的。班上可能有同學有這些信仰，用詞要尊重。

Classroom Activity 2 · 課堂活動二

Follow the Route 跟著路線走

You need · 準備材料

  • The 3D globe in this lesson這一課的 3D 地球儀
  • A blank map of Asia, Africa, and Europe (land only)一張只畫陸地的亞洲、非洲、歐洲空白地圖

Steps · 步驟

  1. In pairs, choose one place on the globe, such as Aksum, Kyiv, Timbuktu, or Pasai.兩人一組，在地球儀上選一個地方，例如阿克蘇姆、基輔、廷巴克圖或巴賽。
  2. Trace the line back to where it started. Draw it on the map and write the year at each end.沿著線往回找到起點。把它畫在地圖上，兩端寫上年代。
  3. Write three sentences with “spread through,” “converted to,” and “according to tradition” or “the evidence shows.”用 spread through、converted to，以及 according to tradition 或 the evidence shows 寫三句英文。
  4. Share with another pair: who carried the religion to your place: traders, missionaries, soldiers, or pilgrims?和另一組分享：是誰把宗教帶到你們選的地方？商人、傳教的人、軍隊，還是朝聖者？

The globe shows approximate routes and dates. Encourage students to say “about” and to check the place card for how we know.
地球儀上的路線和年代都是大約的。鼓勵學生說「大約」，並且看小卡上「怎麼知道」那一段。
