Home Economics · 家政

Life Skills at Home 家政

Measure a cup of rice, read a laundry tag, sew on a button, keep track of a week of pocket money. Each lesson ends with one thing you can do at home tonight.
量一杯米、看懂一張洗標、縫一顆釦子、記一週的帳。每一課結束，你都帶走一件今天晚上就能在家做的事。

In Taiwan's curriculum, home economics belongs to the learning area called Integrative Activities. In junior high school that area has three subjects: home economics, scouting, and guidance. This series is written for upper elementary and junior high students, and for anyone who wants to learn everyday skills in two languages.
在十二年國教課綱裡，家政屬於「綜合活動」領域；國中階段，這個領域包含家政、童軍、輔導三個科目。這個系列寫給國小高年級到國中的學生，也寫給想用兩種語言學生活技能的每一個人。

Every lesson has a model you can touch, a short reading in English, and two cards you will see every time: eight sentences of everyday English for the kitchen, the laundry, or the store, and one small task to try at home tonight.
每一課都有一個可以動手的模型、一篇英文短文，還有兩張每一課都會出現的卡片：八句在廚房、洗衣間或商店用得到的生活英語，以及一件今天回家就能做的小任務。

All the hands-on cooking in this series uses plant foods: everyone can eat them, and they are the safest to handle, because there is no raw meat to deal with. When a lesson explains official knowledge, such as the food groups or the allergens on a label, it reports it as it is. If you have a food allergy, ask an adult at home and read the label first.
這個系列的實作都用蔬食：人人能吃，也最安全（不必處理生肉）。講到官方的知識（例如食物的分類、標示上的過敏原）時，一律照實介紹。如果你對某些食物過敏，請先問家裡的大人，並且看清楚標示。

Steps that use a knife, a stove, hot oil, bleach, or gas always say so, and they need an adult at home beside you. Every family does things a little differently, so each lesson shows one common way. Ask the people you live with how they do it.
用到刀、火、熱油、漂白水或瓦斯的步驟，課文都會寫明，並且要有家裡的大人在旁邊。每一家的做法都不太一樣，所以每一課介紹的是「一種常見的做法」；也請你回家問問一起住的人，他們是怎麼做的。

1 of 28 lessons is ready so far; more are on the way.
目前完成 1 課（共規劃 28 課），持續增加中。

Lesson 1 · 第一課入門 · Beginner

Why Does an American Recipe Say “Cups”?

美國的食譜為什麼寫「幾杯」？Pour water, flour, sugar, rice, and oil into the same measuring cup and set it on a scale. The cup is full every time, but the grams are not the same. Then change a recipe from cups to grams, and make it bigger or smaller.把水、麵粉、糖、米、油倒進同一個量杯，再放上秤：每次都是滿滿一杯，公克數卻不一樣。接著把一份食譜從「杯」換成「公克」，再放大、縮小。Start the lesson · 開始上課 →
