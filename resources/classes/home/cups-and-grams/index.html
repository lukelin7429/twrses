Why Does an American Recipe Say “Cups”?美國的食譜為什麼寫「幾杯」？

Pour water, flour, sugar, rice, and oil into the same measuring cup and set it on a scale. The cup is full every time, but the grams are not the same. Then change a recipe from cups to grams, and make it bigger or smaller.
把水、麵粉、糖、米、油倒進同一個量杯，再放上秤：每次都是滿滿一杯，公克數卻不一樣。接著把一份食譜從「杯」換成「公克」，再放大、縮小。

Big idea · 一句話看懂

There are two ways to measure food: by volume, with cups and spoons, and by weight, in grams. One cup of flour and one cup of sugar do not weigh the same, so to change cups into grams you have to know what is in the cup.
量東西有兩種方法：量體積（杯、匙）和量重量（公克）。同樣一杯，麵粉和糖不一樣重，所以把「杯」換成「公克」之前，要先知道杯子裡裝的是什麼。

Unit 1: Why Does an American Recipe Say “Cups”?

Open an American cookbook and you will not find many grams. You will find cups, tablespoons, and teaspoons. “Add 1 cup of flour and 2 tablespoons of oil,” the recipe says. In Taiwan, a recipe is more likely to give grams and milliliters. Neither way is wrong. They are two different answers to the same question: how much?

翻開一本美國的食譜，你找不到幾個「公克」，看到的是杯（cup）、湯匙（tablespoon）和茶匙（teaspoon）。食譜上寫著：「加 1 杯麵粉和 2 湯匙油。」在台灣，食譜比較常寫公克和毫升。兩種寫法都沒有錯，它們是用兩種方法回答同一個問題：要放多少？

The first way measures volume, which is how much space something takes up. A cup, a tablespoon, and a teaspoon are all units of volume. In the United States, one cup is about 237 milliliters. Three teaspoons make one tablespoon, and sixteen tablespoons make one cup. In the kitchen, people round a teaspoon to 5 milliliters and a tablespoon to 15.

第一種方法量的是體積，也就是東西占了多少空間。杯、湯匙、茶匙都是體積的單位。在美國，一杯大約是 237 毫升。三茶匙等於一湯匙，十六湯匙等於一杯。在廚房裡，大家把一茶匙當作 5 毫升，一湯匙當作 15 毫升。

The second way measures weight, which is how heavy something is. A kitchen scale shows it in grams. Here is the surprise. Fill the same cup with water, then with flour, then with sugar, and weigh it each time. The cup is full every time, but the scale shows about 237 grams of water, about 125 grams of flour, and about 200 grams of sugar.

第二種方法量的是重量，也就是東西有多重。廚房的秤用公克顯示重量。有趣的事情來了：用同一個杯子，先裝水，再裝麵粉，再裝糖，每次都秤一秤。杯子每次都是滿的，秤上卻是大約 237 公克的水、大約 125 公克的麵粉、大約 200 公克的糖。

Why are they different? Flour is a fine powder with a lot of air between its tiny pieces, so a cup of it is light. Sugar crystals pack closer together. Even the same flour can change. Spoon it in loosely, and a cup is about 125 grams. Pack it down, and the same cup can hold up to about 160 grams. This is why many bakers weigh their flour.

為什麼不一樣？麵粉是很細的粉，一粒一粒之間有很多空氣，所以一杯麵粉很輕；糖的結晶排得比較緊密。就算是同一種麵粉，也會變：鬆鬆地舀，一杯大約 125 公克；把它壓實，同一個杯子最多可以裝到大約 160 公克。所以很多做烘焙的人用秤來量麵粉。

Recipe English has its own short forms. “Tsp” means teaspoon, and “tbsp” means tablespoon. People read “1/2 cup” as “half a cup” and “1 1/2 cups” as “one and a half cups.” “A pinch of salt” is the tiny amount you can pick up between your thumb and one finger. The sign °F means degrees Fahrenheit, so an oven at 350°F is at about 177°C.

食譜的英文有自己的縮寫。tsp 是茶匙，tbsp 是湯匙。「1/2 cup」唸作 half a cup（半杯），「1 1/2 cups」唸作 one and a half cups（一杯半）。「a pinch of salt」（一小撮鹽）是拇指和一根手指捏得起來的那一點點。°F 是華氏的度數，所以烤箱的華氏 350 度大約是攝氏 177 度。

So how do you change a recipe? For water and other thin liquids, multiply the cups by about 237 to get milliliters. For dry things, you must know what is in the cup, because each food has its own number of grams per cup. Remember, too, that a cup is not the same size everywhere. A cup in Australia is 250 milliliters, and the cup that comes with a rice cooker is only about 180. Before you cook, ask one question: whose cup is this?

那麼，食譜要怎麼換單位？水和其他稀的液體，把杯數乘以大約 237，就是毫升。乾的材料就要先知道杯子裡裝的是什麼，因為每一種食物「一杯幾公克」都不一樣。還要記得：每個地方的「一杯」不一樣大。澳洲的一杯是 250 毫升，電鍋附的量米杯只有大約 180 毫升。下廚之前先問一句：這是誰的一杯？

Questions · 閱讀理解

A recipe says “1 cup of sugar.” Your friend says, “That is 237 grams, because one cup is 237 milliliters.” What is wrong with this idea?

Milliliters measure space and grams measure weight. Only water weighs about 1 gram for each milliliter. A cup of sugar takes up the same 237 milliliters, but it weighs about 200 grams.

Why do many bakers weigh flour instead of measuring it with a cup?

The amount of flour in a cup changes with how you fill it: about 125 grams when it is spooned in loosely, and up to about 160 grams when it is packed down. A scale gives the same amount every time.

Your family's rice cup holds about 180 milliliters. An American recipe asks for 1 cup of water. What will happen if you use the rice cup, and how could you fix it?

You will add too little water, because 180 milliliters is only about three quarters of a US cup. Measure 237 milliliters with a cup that has milliliter marks, or use one rice cup and then about one third of a rice cup more.

Two Systems · 兩套單位

Why does the United States still use cups?美國為什麼還在用「杯」？

The United States uses both systems. Since 1893, its customary units, such as the pound and the gallon, have been defined by metric units. US law calls the metric system the preferred system for trade, but using it is voluntary. So scientists use metric, food packages show both, and home cooks keep their cups.
美國其實兩套單位都在用。從 1893 年起，美國的慣用單位（例如磅、加侖）就是用公制單位來定義的。美國法律把公制稱為貿易上「優先採用」的制度，但是否使用由各界自願。所以科學家用公制，食品包裝兩種都標，而家裡做菜的人繼續用他們的杯子。

A Label Rule · 一條標示規定

240, 15, and 5240、15 和 5

An exact US cup is 236.588 mL, an awkward number. For nutrition labels, the US food rules say that a cup means 240 mL, a tablespoon means 15 mL, and a teaspoon means 5 mL. That is why a drink label can say “1 cup (240 mL).”
精確的美制一杯是 236.588 毫升，很不好記。美國的食品法規為了營養標示，規定一杯算 240 毫升、一湯匙算 15 毫升、一茶匙算 5 毫升。所以飲料的標示上會寫「1 cup (240 mL)」。

A Rice Cup · 一個量米杯

The cup that came from the gō從「合」來的杯子

Japan has an old unit for measuring rice called the gō (合), about 180 mL, and rice cookers there often come with a cup of that size. That is only about three quarters of a US cup, so never swap one for the other. Rice cups are not all alike, so measure the one you have at home.
日本有一個量米的舊單位叫「合」，大約 180 毫升；日本的電子鍋常附一個這麼大的量杯。這只有美制一杯的四分之三左右，所以兩種杯子千萬不要互相代替。每個量米杯不一定一樣大，量量看你家的那一個。

A Pinch · 一小撮

How big is a pinch?一小撮是多少？

A dictionary says a pinch is as much as you can take between your finger and thumb. There is no agreed standard for it. Some makers of measuring spoons call it one sixteenth of a teaspoon, and others give different sizes. A pinch is a reminder that cooking also uses your senses.
字典說，一小撮就是手指和拇指之間捏得起來的量。它沒有公認的標準：有些量匙的廠商把它定為十六分之一茶匙，也有別的說法。「一小撮」提醒我們，做菜也要靠自己的感覺。

Myth vs. Fact · 常見迷思

Four things people get wrong 四個常見的誤會

✗ Myth · 迷思A cup is the same size everywhere.全世界的「一杯」都一樣大。

✓ Fact · 事實A US cup is about 237 mL, a metric cup is 250 mL, a Japanese cup is 200 mL, and a rice-cooker cup is about 180 mL. Check where a recipe comes from before you measure.美制的一杯大約 237 毫升，公制的一杯是 250 毫升，日本的一杯是 200 毫升，電鍋的量米杯大約 180 毫升。動手量之前，先看食譜是哪裡來的。

✗ Myth · 迷思One cup of anything weighs the same.不管裝什麼，一杯都一樣重。

✓ Fact · 事實A cup measures space, not weight. One US cup holds about 237 g of water, 218 g of oil, 200 g of sugar, 185 g of uncooked rice, or 125 g of flour.杯子量的是空間，不是重量。美制一杯可以裝大約 237 公克的水、218 公克的油、200 公克的糖、185 公克的生米，或 125 公克的麵粉。

✗ Myth · 迷思The United States does not use the metric system.美國不用公制。

✓ Fact · 事實It uses both. The US measurement institute, NIST, calls the idea that the country never adopted the metric system untrue. US customary units have been defined by metric units since 1893.美國兩種都用。美國國家標準與技術研究院（NIST）說，「美國沒有採用公制」的說法並不正確；美國的慣用單位從 1893 年起就是用公制單位來定義的。

✗ Myth · 迷思An ounce is always a weight.盎司一定是重量的單位。

✓ Fact · 事實There are two kinds. An ounce (oz) is a weight, about 28 g. A fluid ounce (fl oz) is a volume, about 30 mL. A cup of flour is 8 fluid ounces of space, but it weighs only about 4.4 ounces.盎司有兩種。盎司（oz）是重量，大約 28 公克；液量盎司（fl oz）是體積，大約 30 毫升。一杯麵粉占了 8 液量盎司的空間，重量卻只有大約 4.4 盎司。

Kitchen units in four lines 四句話記住廚房的單位

Cups for space, grams for weight杯量空間，公克量輕重

A cup tells you how much room something takes. A scale tells you how heavy it is.
杯子告訴你東西占多少空間；秤告訴你它有多重。

Three and sixteen三和十六

Three teaspoons make one tablespoon. Sixteen tablespoons make one cup.
三茶匙是一湯匙；十六湯匙是一杯。

5, 15, 2405、15、240

Round numbers for the kitchen: a teaspoon is about 5 mL, a tablespoon about 15 mL, and a US cup about 240 mL.
廚房裡好記的整數：一茶匙大約 5 毫升，一湯匙大約 15 毫升，美制一杯大約 240 毫升。

Minus 32, times 5, divided by 9減 32，乘 5，除以 9

That changes Fahrenheit into Celsius. 350°F minus 32 is 318. Times 5 is 1,590. Divided by 9 is about 177°C.
這樣就把華氏換成攝氏。350 減 32 是 318，乘 5 是 1,590，除以 9 大約是攝氏 177 度。

Before You Start · 動手之前

Kitchen reminders for this lesson 這一課的廚房提醒

  • The activities in this lesson only pour and weigh. They use no knife and no stove. If you bake the cookies, the oven step needs an adult at home beside you.這一課的活動只有倒和秤，不用刀，也不用火。如果要烤餅乾，用烤箱的那一步要有家裡的大人在旁邊。
  • Do not eat or taste raw flour or raw dough. The US Food and Drug Administration reminds cooks that flour is a raw food.不要吃、也不要試吃生的麵粉或生的麵團。美國食品藥物管理局（FDA）提醒：麵粉是生的食物。
  • Food allergies are different for everyone. Before you touch or taste an ingredient, ask an adult at home and read the label.每個人的食物過敏不一樣。碰或吃一樣食材之前，先問家裡的大人，並且看清楚標示。
  • Wash your hands first. Wipe up spilled water or oil right away so nobody slips, and keep the scale dry.先洗手。水或油灑出來要馬上擦乾，免得有人滑倒；也別讓秤碰到水。

Classroom Activity 1 · 課堂活動一

How Many Milliliters Is Your Cup? 你的一杯是幾毫升？

You need · 準備材料

  • A measuring cup with milliliter marks一個有毫升刻度的量杯
  • Three different cups, for example a rice cup, a teacup, and a paper cup三個不一樣的杯子，例如量米杯、茶杯、紙杯
  • Water, a tray, and a towel水、一個托盤、一條抹布
  • Paper and a pencil紙和鉛筆

Steps · 步驟

  1. Guess first: how many milliliters does each cup hold? Write your guesses down.先猜猜看：每個杯子可以裝幾毫升？把猜的數字寫下來。
  2. Fill the first cup to the top with water. Do it over the tray.在托盤上，把第一個杯子的水裝到滿。
  3. Pour the water into the measuring cup. Put the measuring cup on the table, bring your eyes down to the level of the water, and read the mark.把水倒進量杯。量杯放在桌上，眼睛蹲到和水面一樣高，再讀刻度。
  4. Do the same with the other cups. Which one is closest to a US cup, about 237 mL?其他的杯子也這樣做。哪一個最接近美制的一杯（大約 237 毫升）？
  5. Talk about it: if a recipe just says “one cup,” what do you need to find out first?討論：如果食譜只寫「一杯」，你要先弄清楚什麼？

For the teacher: reading the mark from above gives a number that is too high or too low. Let students try it both ways and compare. Use the water for the plants afterward.
給老師：從上面往下看刻度，讀到的數字會偏高或偏低。可以讓學生兩種看法都試，再比較。用過的水拿去澆花。

Classroom Activity 2 · 課堂活動二

Scoop the Same Cup Three Ways 同一杯麵粉，舀三次

You need · 準備材料

  • A bag of flour, a spoon, and a chopstick for leveling一包麵粉、一支湯匙、一根用來刮平的筷子
  • One measuring cup, or any small cup, used for all three rounds一個量杯（或任何小杯子），三次都用同一個
  • A kitchen scale that shows grams, and a large bowl一台顯示公克的廚房用秤，和一個大碗
  • Paper and a pencil紙和鉛筆

Steps · 步驟

  1. Put the empty cup on the scale and set the scale to zero. Now the scale will count only the flour.把空杯放上秤，按歸零。這樣秤就只算麵粉的重量。
  2. Round one: spoon flour gently into the cup until it is heaped, and level it off with the chopstick. Weigh it and write the number down. Empty the cup into the bowl.第一次：用湯匙輕輕把麵粉舀進杯子，堆到冒出來，再用筷子刮平。秤一秤，記下數字，然後把麵粉倒進碗裡。
  3. Round two: dip the cup straight into the bag, lift it out, and level it off. Weigh it.第二次：把杯子直接插進袋子裡舀起來，刮平，再秤。
  4. Round three: fill the cup, press the flour down with the back of the spoon, add more, and level it off. Weigh it.第三次：把杯子裝滿，用湯匙背把麵粉壓實，再補滿、刮平，然後秤。
  5. Compare the three numbers. How many grams apart are the lightest and the heaviest? What does this tell you about recipes that say “1 cup of flour”?比較三個數字。最輕和最重差了幾公克？這對寫著「1 杯麵粉」的食譜有什麼啟示？

For the teacher: the flour stays clean, so put it back in a closed container and use it for cooking later. Raw flour is not for tasting. If anyone in the class is allergic to wheat, use rice or sugar instead. They change less, which is worth discussing too.
給老師：麵粉沒有弄髒，請放回密封的容器，留著之後煮東西用；生麵粉不能試吃。班上如果有人對小麥過敏，改用米或糖來做；它們的變化比較小，這一點也值得討論。

Try It at Home Tonight · 今天回家做

Measure Your Family's Cup 量一量你家的那個杯子

One small task to finish before the next lesson.
下一課之前完成的一件小事。

You can do this on your own · 可以自己做No adult needed. This task uses only water. Ask before you borrow the kitchen tools.不需要大人陪。這個任務只用到水；借用廚房的用具之前，先問一聲。

  1. Find the cup your family uses for measuring. It may be the rice cup that came with the rice cooker.找出家裡用來量東西的那個杯子，可能就是電鍋附的量米杯。
  2. Fill it with water and find out how many milliliters it holds. Use a cup or bottle with milliliter marks. If you have a kitchen scale, you can weigh the water instead: 1 gram of water is about 1 milliliter.把它裝滿水，查出它是幾毫升：用有毫升刻度的杯子或瓶子來量。如果家裡有廚房用的秤，也可以改成秤水的重量：1 公克的水大約是 1 毫升。
  3. Write the number down and bring it to class. Is your cup bigger or smaller than a US cup, about 237 mL?把數字記下來，帶到課堂上。你家的杯子比美制的一杯（大約 237 毫升）大，還是小？

Ask at home · 回家問一問Ask your family: how do you measure the rice and the water when you cook rice? Every family has its own way.問問家人：煮飯的時候，米和水是怎麼量的？每一家都有自己的做法。
