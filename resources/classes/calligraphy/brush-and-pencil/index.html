Does Brush Writing Help Your Pencil Writing?毛筆字和硬筆字

A brush line swells and thins, and a pencil line stays the same width. So what do the two have in common? Watch a brush and a pencil write the same character side by side in 3D, pull a slider to take the thickness away, find the stroke that is off, and write with both tools on one practice pad. This is the last of the fifteen lessons.
毛筆的線有粗有細，鉛筆的線從頭到尾一樣粗。那兩種字有什麼是一樣的？在 3D 裡看毛筆和鉛筆並排寫同一個字，拉動滑桿把粗細拿掉，找出寫歪的那一筆，再用同一個練字板兩種筆都寫寫看。這是十五堂課的最後一課。

Big idea · 一句話看懂

A brush and a pencil make different lines, but they build the same character. Take away the thick and thin of the brush, and what is left is the structure: the same strokes, in the same order, in the same places. We could not find a study that proves brush lessons make pencil writing better, so this lesson does not promise that. What it shows is the part the two tools share.
毛筆和鉛筆寫出來的線不一樣，可是搭起來的是同一個字。把毛筆的粗細拿掉，剩下來的就是結構：一樣的筆畫、一樣的筆順、一樣的位置。我們找不到研究能證明「練毛筆會讓鉛筆字變好」，所以這一課不這樣保證。這一課給你看的，是兩種筆共用的那一部分。

Unit 15: Does Brush Writing Help Your Pencil Writing?

For fourteen lessons you have followed the brush. But on most days you write with a pencil or a pen. Taiwan's curriculum calls this kind of writing 硬筆字, hard-pen writing, because the tip does not bend the way brush hairs do. So here is a fair question for the last lesson: does brush writing help your pencil writing?

前面十四課，我們一直跟著毛筆走。可是平常寫字，你用的多半是鉛筆或原子筆。台灣的課綱把這樣寫的字叫做「硬筆字」，因為筆尖不像筆毛那樣會彎。所以最後一課要問一個很實際的問題：練毛筆字，對鉛筆字有幫助嗎？

Start with what is different. A brush is soft, so its line swells when you press and thins when you lift. A pencil is hard. Its core is a thin rod of graphite mixed with clay, and it leaves a line of almost the same width from beginning to end. In our model, the brush line in 永 changes from 0.3 to 2.5 centimeters wide. The pencil line stays at 0.3.

先看不一樣的地方。毛筆是軟的，一按線就變粗，一提線就變細。鉛筆是硬的。筆芯是石墨混合黏土做成的細條，寫出來的線從頭到尾幾乎一樣粗。在我們的模型裡，毛筆寫「永」，線寬從 0.3 公分變到 2.5 公分；鉛筆的線一直是 0.3 公分。

Now look at what is the same. In the model, a brush and a pencil write 永 at the same moment. Both make five strokes. Both follow the same stroke order. Both travel the same path, and every stroke lands in the same position on the grid. Take the width away from the brush character, and you get the pencil character. It looks like a skeleton.

再看一樣的地方。模型裡，毛筆和鉛筆同時寫「永」。兩個都是五筆，筆順一樣，走的路線一樣，每一筆落在格子裡的位置也一樣。把毛筆字的粗細拿掉，就得到鉛筆字。它看起來像一副骨架。

Writers have a name for this skeleton: structure, or 間架結構 in Chinese. It means where each stroke starts and ends, how long it is, and how the strokes balance one another. Taiwan's curriculum asks students in Grades 3 to 6 to learn this structure and to write correct, neat hard-pen characters. It also says that in those grades hard-pen writing comes first, and the brush is the helper.

寫字的人給這副骨架取了名字：結構，也叫「間架結構」。意思是每一筆從哪裡開始、到哪裡結束、有多長，以及筆畫和筆畫之間怎麼互相平衡。台灣的課綱要三到六年級的學生學會間架結構，寫出正確、工整的硬筆字；課綱也說，這幾個年級「習寫以硬筆字為主，毛筆為輔」。

So, does the brush help? Here is an honest answer. We could not find a study that proves brush lessons make pencil writing better, so we will not promise that. What we can say is what the model shows: the two tools share stroke order and structure. A brush character is also big. The curriculum suggests squares of 8 to 12 centimeters for large brush characters, and 1.5 to 2 centimeters for hard-pen writing in Grades 1 and 2, so a stroke in the wrong position is easier to see.

那麼，毛筆到底有沒有幫助？老實說：我們找不到研究能證明「練毛筆會讓鉛筆字變好」，所以不這樣保證。我們能說的，是模型給你看到的：兩種筆共用筆順和結構。還有一點，毛筆字很大。課綱建議大楷毛筆字的格子是 8 到 12 公分見方，一、二年級硬筆字的格子只有 1.5 到 2 公分，所以哪一筆的位置跑掉了，在大字裡比較容易看出來。

Now try it yourself. Pull the slider and watch the thickness disappear. Find the stroke that is off in the game. Then write with both tools on the pad. The curriculum's advice is simple: aim for correct first, then upright, then neat, and beautiful last. It also says not to practice too much. Good writing is a habit, and you can build it with any tool.

現在換你試試。拉動滑桿，看粗細慢慢消失；在小遊戲裡找出寫歪的那一筆；再到練字板上兩種筆都寫寫看。課綱的建議很簡單：先求正確，再求端正，然後工整，最後才是美觀；課綱也提醒，不要練習過度。把字寫好是一種習慣，用哪一種筆都可以養成。
Read aloud · 課文朗讀

Questions · 閱讀理解

What is the biggest difference between a brush line and a pencil line?

The width. A brush line gets thicker and thinner as the hand presses and lifts. A pencil line stays almost the same width from beginning to end.

When a brush and a pencil write the same character, what stays the same?

The structure: the number of strokes, the stroke order, and the position and length of every stroke.

Does this lesson promise that brush practice will make your pencil writing better? Why or why not?

No. We could not find a study that proves it. The lesson only shows what the two tools share, which is stroke order and structure.

Pencils and Taiwan · 鉛筆與台灣

A pencil with no lead in it, two letters on its side, and writing in Taiwan's schools 沒有鉛的鉛筆、筆桿上的兩個字母，還有台灣學校裡的寫字課

Two things about the pencil from an encyclopedia and a pencil maker, and two about writing from Taiwan's own documents.
兩件關於鉛筆的事，來自百科全書和鉛筆製造商；兩件關於寫字的事，來自台灣自己的文件。

The “Lead” · 筆芯

No lead in a pencil鉛筆裡沒有鉛

An encyclopedia explains that a pencil is a thin rod of graphite, often called the “lead,” though it contains none of that metal. Modern pencils still use a mixture of graphite and clay. Taiwan's Ministry of Education dictionary says the same: a core made of graphite powder and clay.
大英百科說：鉛筆是一根細細的石墨條，大家常叫它「鉛」，其實裡面完全沒有鉛這種金屬；現在的鉛筆仍然用石墨和黏土的混合物。教育部辭典的說法一樣：「石墨粉和黏土製成的筆心」。

H and B · H 和 B

The letters on the side筆桿上的字母

One pencil maker explains the letters this way: H is for hard and B is for black, so HB means hard black, a medium-soft grade that the maker calls ideal for writing. The more clay in the core, the harder the pencil. The more graphite, the softer and blacker the line.
一家鉛筆製造商這樣解釋筆桿上的字母：H 是 hard（硬），B 是 black（黑），所以 HB 是「又硬又黑」，屬於中等偏軟的等級，製造商說它最適合寫字。筆芯裡黏土越多，鉛筆越硬；石墨越多，線就越軟、越黑。

Taiwan's Classrooms · 台灣的教室

Hard pen first, brush as helper硬筆為主，毛筆為輔

Taiwan's Mandarin curriculum says Grades 1 and 2 practice hard-pen writing, and Grades 3 to 6 also learn the brush. Its teaching notes describe four steps for both tools: tracing over a red model, copying from a model beside you, writing on your own, and using your writing in real life.
國語文課綱說：一、二年級以習寫硬筆字為主，三到六年級兼習毛筆字。課綱的教學說明還提到，硬筆和毛筆的寫字教學都有四個階段：描紅、臨摹、自運、應用——照著紅字描，看著範本寫，自己寫，最後用在生活裡。

The National Contest · 全國語文競賽

Fifty characters in fifty minutes五十分鐘，五十個字

In the writing event of Taiwan's National Language Competition, every group writes regular script with a traditional brush: 50 characters in 50 minutes. Students write characters 7 centimeters square. Half of the score is for brushwork. The other half is for structure and layout.
全國語文競賽的「寫字」項目，各組一律用傳統毛筆寫楷書：50 分鐘寫 50 個字。學生組的字是 7 公分見方。評分一半看筆法，另一半看「結構與章法」。

Myth vs. Fact · 常見迷思

Four things people get wrong 四個常見的誤會

✗ Myth · 迷思If you practice the brush, your pencil writing will surely get better.練了毛筆字，鉛筆字一定會變好。

✓ Fact · 事實We could not find a study that proves this. What the two tools share is stroke order and structure. Getting better with a pencil still takes practice with a pencil.我們找不到研究能證明這件事。兩種筆共用的是筆順和結構；鉛筆字要進步，還是得用鉛筆練。

✗ Myth · 迷思The dark core of a pencil is made of lead.鉛筆的筆芯是鉛做的。

✓ Fact · 事實It is graphite mixed with clay. It contains no lead at all.筆芯是石墨混合黏土做的，裡面完全沒有鉛。

✗ Myth · 迷思Good handwriting means beautiful handwriting.字寫得好，就是寫得漂亮。

✓ Fact · 事實Taiwan's curriculum puts correct first, then upright, then neat. Beautiful comes last.課綱的順序是：先正確，再端正，然後工整，最後才是美觀。

✗ Myth · 迷思The more times you copy a character, the better.同一個字抄越多遍越好。

✓ Fact · 事實The curriculum says writing practice should be about quality, and it warns against practicing too much.課綱說，寫字的練習「應以質為主要考量，避免過度練習」。

In the Classroom · 教室提醒

Before you pick up a brush 拿起毛筆之前

  • A sharp pencil has a point. Walk with the point down, and never point it at a classmate.削尖的鉛筆有尖端：拿著走路時筆尖朝下，不要對著同學。
  • Do not chew your pencil or put it in your mouth.不要咬鉛筆，也不要把鉛筆放進嘴裡。
  • Sit up, and keep your eyes 35 to 45 centimeters from the paper. That is the distance Taiwan's government suggests for children.坐正，眼睛離紙 35 到 45 公分。這是政府給小朋友的護眼建議。
  • After 30 minutes of close work, rest your eyes for 10 minutes. Make sure the light is bright enough.近距離用眼 30 分鐘，就休息 10 分鐘。光線要充足。
  • If you use a real brush for the activities, wear old clothes and cover the desk with newspaper.活動如果用真的毛筆：穿舊衣服，桌上先鋪報紙。

Classroom Activity 1 · 課堂活動一

Big and Small 一大一小

You need · 準備材料

  • A brush with clean water and newspaper (or a thick marker) for each pair每兩人一枝毛筆、清水和舊報紙（或一枝粗的麥克筆）
  • A sheet with one 10-cm square and one 2-cm square, each with a cross inside, and a pencil一張畫了 10 公分方格和 2 公分方格的紙（格子裡都畫十字），還有鉛筆

Steps · 步驟

  1. With the brush, write 永 in the big square. Watch the cross: which strokes touch the center line?用毛筆在大格子裡寫「永」。看著十字線：哪幾筆碰到中線？
  2. With the pencil, write 永 in the small square, in the same stroke order.用鉛筆在小格子裡寫「永」，筆順要一樣。
  3. Mark the start and the end of every stroke with a dot, in both characters.在兩個字上，把每一筆的起點和終點各點一個點。
  4. Compare with your partner. Are the dots in the same parts of the two squares?和同伴比一比：兩個格子裡，那些點是不是落在同樣的地方？

The two characters do not have to be beautiful. You are checking positions, not beauty.
兩個字都不必漂亮。這裡要檢查的是位置，不是美不美。

Classroom Activity 2 · 課堂活動二

Find My Mistake 找找我哪裡寫歪了

You need · 準備材料

  • Squared paper, a pencil, and an eraser for each student每人一張有格子的紙、鉛筆和橡皮擦
  • A model character on the board, such as 山, 水, or 春黑板上的一個範字，例如「山」「水」「春」

Steps · 步驟

  1. Copy the model once, as correctly as you can.先照著範字寫一次，越正確越好。
  2. Copy it again, and this time change one stroke on purpose: make it too short, too long, or move it.再寫一次，這次故意把其中一筆改掉：太短、太長，或移個位置。
  3. Swap papers. Circle the stroke your partner changed, and say what is wrong with it in English.和同伴交換。把他改掉的那一筆圈起來，用英文說它哪裡不對。
  4. Useful sentences: “This stroke is too short.” “This stroke is in the wrong position.”可以用的句子：“This stroke is too short.”、“This stroke is in the wrong position.”

Small changes are harder to find. Start with big ones.
改得越少越難找。先從明顯的開始。

  14. 14How Is Chinese Calligraphy Different from Western Calligraphy?中文書法和西洋書法有什麼不同？A brush changes its line by pressure; a broad-edge pen, by direction.毛筆的粗細看輕重，平頭筆的粗細看方向。
