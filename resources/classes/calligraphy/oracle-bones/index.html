Big idea · 一句話看懂

The earliest Chinese writing that survives on real objects was carved on tortoise shells and animal bones more than 3,000 years ago. Many of those characters began as pictures of things. Over many centuries, the shapes became simpler and more even: carved oracle bone script, bronze script, and then small seal script, the standard of the Qin dynasty.
現存有實物可證的最早漢字，是三千多年前刻在龜甲、獸骨上的甲骨文；其中許多字一開始是東西的圖畫。經過很多個世紀，字形越來越簡單、線條越來越勻稱：刀刻的甲骨文、青銅器上的金文，再到秦朝的標準字體小篆。

Unit 5: Where Did Chinese Characters Come From?

Before there was paper, people in China wrote on hard things. More than 3,000 years ago, in the late Shang dynasty, people carved characters on tortoise shells and animal bones, mostly the shoulder blades of cattle. We call this writing oracle bone script. Taiwan's Ministry of Education calls it the earliest Chinese writing that survives on real objects.

在有紙以前，中國人把字寫在硬的東西上。三千多年前的商代晚期，人們把字刻在龜甲和獸骨（大多是牛的肩胛骨）上，我們叫它甲骨文。台灣教育部的辭典說，甲骨文是中國有實物可證的最早文字。

Why carve on shells? The Shang kings asked many questions about what would happen. Diviners cut hollows into the back of a shell or bone and pressed the red-hot end of a wooden stick into them. Cracks appeared on the front, shaped a bit like the character 卜, which means to divine. Then they carved the record beside the cracks: the date, the question, and the answer. Nobody today knows exactly how they read the cracks.

為什麼要刻在龜甲上？商王對未來有很多問題。占卜的人在龜甲或獸骨的背面挖出凹洞，再用燒紅的木條末端去燒，正面就出現裂紋，形狀有點像「卜」字，「卜」就是占卜的意思。接著，他們把紀錄刻在裂紋旁邊：哪一天、問什麼、得到什麼答案。古人到底怎麼判讀裂紋，現在已經不可考了。

Many early characters began as pictures of things. 日 was the sun, 月 a crescent moon, 山 three mountain peaks, 水 a stream with drops of water, 人 a person seen from the side, and 馬 a horse standing on its tail, with a big eye and a mane. About 1,900 years ago, the scholar Xu Shen called this way of making characters 象形, drawing the shape of a thing, and he gave 日 and 月 as his examples.

許多早期的字是從東西的圖畫來的：「日」是太陽，「月」是彎彎的月亮，「山」是三座山峰，「水」是一道水流和兩旁的水滴，「人」是側面的人，「馬」是一匹站起來的馬，有大眼睛和鬃毛。大約一千九百年前，學者許慎把這種造字方法叫做「象形」，也就是照著東西的樣子畫出來，他舉的例子正是「日」和「月」。

Writing was also cast or carved on bronze. In the Shang and Zhou dynasties, words were put on bronze vessels such as the ding, a pot with three legs. These lines are thicker and rounder than carved lines. To study them on paper, people make rubbings: paper is pressed onto the writing and patted with ink, and the sunken characters stay white. The Mao Gong Ding in the National Palace Museum in Taipei holds about 500 characters in 32 lines.

字也鑄或刻在青銅器上。商周時代，人們把文字放在鼎這類青銅器上；鼎是有三隻腳的鍋子。這些線條比刻的線粗、也比較圓。為了在紙上研究，人們做拓片：把紙貼在字上、拍上墨，凹下去的字就留白。台北國立故宮博物院的毛公鼎，裡面有 32 行、大約 500 個字。

In 221 BCE, the state of Qin united China. The Shiji, a famous history, records that the First Emperor made the writing the same everywhere. Li Si and other officials simplified the older script into a standard form called small seal script, with smooth lines of even width.

西元前 221 年，秦統一天下。著名的史書《史記》記載，秦始皇讓全國「書同文字」。李斯等官員把舊的字體加以省改，成為標準字體，叫做小篆，線條圓滑、粗細一樣。

After seal script came clerical script, flat and wide (Lesson 6), and then regular script, the way we write today (Lesson 7). In this lesson, crack and carve a shell in 3D, make a rubbing from a bronze ding, and watch a brush write seal script. Then pull the slider to see one character change over 3,000 years.

小篆之後是字形扁扁的隸書（第六課），再來是我們今天寫的楷書（第七課）。這一課，在 3D 裡讓龜甲裂開、刻一個字，從青銅鼎拓下銘文，看毛筆寫小篆；再拉動滑桿，看一個字三千年來怎麼變。

日 · sun日：太陽

Pictograph · 象形

A sun with a mark in the middle. Carved on bone, the circle is made of straight lines and looks almost square. Cast in bronze, it is round again, with a fat dot. In seal script, the dot becomes a line across.
中間有一點的太陽。刻在骨頭上，圓圈用直線刻，看起來幾乎是方的；鑄在青銅上又變圓了，中間是一個胖胖的點；到了小篆，那一點變成一橫。

月 · moon月：月亮

Pictograph · 象形

A crescent moon. The oracle bone form is a curved slice; later forms add lines inside, and the crescent slowly turns into the 月 we write today.
彎彎的月亮。甲骨文是一片彎彎的月牙；後來裡面多了線條，月牙慢慢變成今天寫的「月」。

山 · mountain山：山

Pictograph · 象形

Three peaks standing on the ground, with the middle one the tallest. The peaks became three vertical strokes on a base.
三座山峰站在地上，中間那座最高。山峰後來變成三豎，站在一條底線上。

水 · water水：水

Pictograph · 象形

A stream in the middle with drops of water on both sides. In regular script, the drops became the strokes on the left and right.
中間一道水流，兩旁是水滴。到了楷書，水滴變成左右兩邊的筆畫。

人 · person人：人

Pictograph · 象形

A person seen from the side, bending forward with an arm hanging down. Two strokes are left in 人.
從側面看的人，身體往前彎、手垂下來。到了「人」字，只剩下兩筆。

馬 · horse馬：馬

Pictograph · 象形

A horse standing on its tail: a big eye at the top, a mane along the back, and legs on the left. The Shuowen says 馬 shows the horse's head, mane, tail, and four legs.
一匹站起來的馬：上面是大眼睛，背上有鬃毛，腿在左邊。《說文解字》說「馬」字畫的是馬的頭、鬃毛、尾巴和四隻腳。

Stories of Ancient Writing · 古文字的故事

A medicine with writing, fifteen digs, the longest inscription, and an old dictionary 藥材上的字、十五次發掘、最長的銘文，和一本古老的字典

Four short stories about how we found, kept, and explained the oldest Chinese writing.
關於最古老的漢字怎麼被發現、被保存、被解釋的四個小故事。

Discovery · 發現

A story about medicine藥材的故事

Oracle bones came from Xiaotun village near Anyang, Henan, and 1899 is usually given as the year they were discovered. A famous story says the scholar Wang Yirong was ill, and writing was spotted on a piece of turtle shell bought as medicine. Historians have shown that this story cannot be trusted; another account says an antique dealer brought Wang twelve pieces. Wang died in 1900, and in 1903 Liu E published the first book of oracle bone rubbings.
甲骨出自河南安陽附近的小屯村，後人通常把 1899 年當作甲骨文發現的年份。有個有名的故事說，學者王懿榮生病，有人在買來當藥的龜版上看見了字；學者已經證明這個故事不可信。另一種說法是古董商帶了十二片甲骨給王懿榮看。王懿榮 1900 年就去世了，1903 年劉鶚出版了第一本甲骨拓片的書《鐵雲藏龜》。

Taiwan · 台灣

Fifteen seasons of digging十五次發掘

From 1928 to 1937, Academia Sinica's Institute of History and Philology dug fifteen times at Yinxu, the ruins of the late Shang capital near Anyang. Today the institute in Taipei holds more than 25,000 pieces of oracle bones, and its museum shows some of them. In 2006, Yinxu became a UNESCO World Heritage Site.
1928 到 1937 年，中央研究院歷史語言研究所在安陽附近的殷墟（商代晚期的都城遺址）進行了十五次發掘。現在位在台北的史語所收藏了兩萬五千多片甲骨，所裡的歷史文物陳列館可以看到其中一些。2006 年，殷墟列入聯合國教科文組織的世界遺產。

The Longest Inscription · 最長的銘文

The Mao Gong Ding in Taipei台北故宮的毛公鼎

The Mao Gong Ding is a bronze ding from the late Western Zhou dynasty. Inside its bowl are 32 lines of writing, about 500 characters. The National Palace Museum in Taipei calls it the longest bronze inscription known so far. The Ministry of Education's dictionary counts 497 characters, so we say about 500.
毛公鼎是西周晚期的青銅鼎，鼎腹裡面鑄了 32 行字，大約 500 字。台北國立故宮博物院說它是目前所知最長的青銅器銘文。教育部辭典算的是 497 字，所以我們說「大約 500 字」。

An Old Dictionary · 字典

Xu Shen's Shuowen Jiezi許慎的《說文解字》

Around the year 100, the scholar Xu Shen finished the Shuowen Jiezi. It explains 9,353 characters, written in small seal script and sorted into 540 groups. It describes six ways characters were made; drawing the shape of a thing (象形) is one of them, with 日 and 月 as examples. It also says 卜 shows the shape of a heated shell's crack.
大約西元 100 年，東漢學者許慎完成了《說文解字》，用小篆解說 9,353 個字，分成 540 部。書裡說造字的方法有六種（六書），「象形」是其中一種，例子是「日」和「月」；書裡也說「卜」字像燒過的龜甲上的裂紋。

Myth vs. Fact · 常見迷思

Four things people get wrong 四個常見的誤會

✗ Myth · 迷思Every Chinese character is a picture.每個漢字都是一幅畫。

✓ Fact · 事實Only some began as pictures. Xu Shen described six ways characters were made. Many characters join a part for meaning with a part for sound, like 江 and 河, his own examples.只有一部分是從圖畫來的。許慎說造字有六種方法；很多字是「表示意思的部分」加上「表示讀音的部分」，像他舉的「江」和「河」。

✗ Myth · 迷思One man, Cangjie, invented Chinese writing.漢字是倉頡一個人發明的。

✓ Fact · 事實That is a legend. As the Ministry of Education's dictionary says, writing could not be invented by one person; it grew little by little over a long time.那是傳說。教育部辭典說，文字不可能是一個人獨創的，而是先人一點一點累積而成。

✗ Myth · 迷思Scholars can read every oracle bone character.學者已經看得懂所有的甲骨文。

✓ Fact · 事實Not yet. A 2016 UNESCO report counts about 4,400 different characters, and only about 1,400 of them match characters we use today. Other counts differ a little, but many are still a puzzle.還沒有。2016 年聯合國教科文組織的報告說，甲骨文大約有 4,400 個不同的字，其中大約只有 1,400 個對得上今天用的字。各家的數字有些不同，但還有很多字是謎。

✗ Myth · 迷思Ancient characters look nothing like today's.古文字和今天的字完全不像。

✓ Fact · 事實Many keep their shape. 山 still has three peaks, 水 still has a stream in the middle, and 人 still has two strokes. Pull the slider and look for what stays the same.很多字保留了原來的樣子：「山」還是三座山峰，「水」中間還是一道水流，「人」還是兩筆。拉拉看滑桿，找找什麼沒有變。

In the Classroom · 教室提醒

Before you pick up a brush 拿起毛筆之前

  • Carve with a blunt wooden stick or a dull pencil on clay or a foam tray. Never use a real knife in class.用鈍的木棒或沒削尖的鉛筆，在黏土或保麗龍盤上刻；上課時絕對不要用真的刀。
  • Do not try to crack shells or bones with heat. The model shows how it was done; we do not do it in class.不要自己用火燒龜甲或骨頭。模型示範古人怎麼做，課堂上不做。
  • Crayons and ink can stain. Cover the desk and wear old clothes or an apron.蠟筆和墨都會弄髒：桌上先鋪報紙，穿舊衣服或圍裙。
  • Wash your hands after working with clay, crayons, or ink.玩過黏土、蠟筆或墨之後，記得洗手。

Classroom Activity 1 · 課堂活動一

Carve an Oracle Bone 刻一片甲骨

You need · 準備材料

  • A flat piece of clay for each student (or a clean foam tray)每人一塊壓平的黏土（或一個洗乾淨的保麗龍盤）
  • A blunt wooden stick or a dull pencil一根鈍的木棒，或沒削尖的鉛筆
  • A sheet with the six oracle bone characters from this lesson一張印好這一課六個甲骨文的學習單

Steps · 步驟

  1. Shape the clay like a shell or a bone, and press it flat.把黏土捏成龜甲或骨頭的形狀，再壓平。
  2. On the left, carve a crack shaped like 卜.在左邊刻一個像「卜」的裂紋。
  3. Beside the crack, carve one of the six oracle bone characters using only straight lines.在裂紋旁邊刻六個甲骨文中的一個，只能用直線。
  4. Trade with a classmate. Can they read your character? Can you read theirs?和同學交換：他看得出你刻的是什麼字嗎？你看得出他的嗎？

Ask the class: was it harder to carve a circle or a straight line? Then look at 日 in oracle bone script.
問問全班：刻圓圈和刻直線，哪個比較難？再看看甲骨文的「日」。

Classroom Activity 2 · 課堂活動二

Make a Rubbing 做一張拓片

You need · 準備材料

  • A clean foam tray一個洗乾淨的保麗龍盤
  • A pencil, thin paper, and a crayon with the paper peeled off一枝鉛筆、一張薄紙，和一枝剝掉包裝紙的蠟筆

Steps · 步驟

  1. Press hard with the pencil to draw a character into the foam, so the lines sink in.用鉛筆用力在保麗龍盤上寫一個字，讓筆畫凹下去。
  2. Lay the paper on top and hold it still.把薄紙蓋在上面，按好不要動。
  3. Rub the side of the crayon across the paper. The sunken lines stay white.用蠟筆的側面在紙上來回塗，凹下去的筆畫就留白了。
  4. Compare your rubbing with the one in the 3D model.把你的拓片和 3D 模型裡的拓片比一比。

Try 日 in bronze script: a circle with a fat dot in the middle.
試試金文的「日」：一個圓圈，中間一個胖胖的點。
