Yan Zhenqing and Liu Gongquan both wrote regular script in the Tang dynasty, yet you can tell them apart at a glance. Watch two brushes write the same character side by side in 3D, compare their press-and-lift curves, guess who wrote each character on real stone rubbings, and find out whose lines yours are closer to.
顏真卿和柳公權都是唐朝人，寫的都是楷書，可是一眼就分得出來。在 3D 裡看兩枝毛筆並排寫同一個字、比一比提按曲線，到真的拓本上猜猜每個字是誰寫的，再看看你的線條比較像誰。

Big idea · 一句話看懂

Every calligrapher writes the same characters with the same strokes, but each one presses, lifts, and shapes them in a different way. Yan Zhenqing's lines are thick and full; Liu Gongquan's are lean and firm. Later writers summed it up in four characters: 顏筋柳骨, the muscle of Yan and the bone of Liu.
每一位書法家寫的是同樣的字、同樣的筆畫，可是下筆的輕重、提按的方式、字的形狀，每個人都不一樣。顏真卿的線條又粗又飽滿，柳公權的線條又瘦又挺。後人用四個字來形容：「顏筋柳骨」。

Unit 9: Strong Muscle, Hard Bone: Why Does Every Calligrapher's Writing Look Different?

Ask two friends to write the character 大. They will use the same three strokes in the same order, but the two characters will not look the same. Calligraphers are like that too. Two of the most famous, Yan Zhenqing and Liu Gongquan, both lived in the Tang dynasty and both wrote regular script, yet people can tell their writing apart at a glance.

請兩位同學各寫一個「大」字。他們用的是同樣的三筆、同樣的筆順，可是寫出來的兩個字不會一樣。書法家也是這樣。顏真卿和柳公權是最有名的兩位：都是唐朝人，寫的都是楷書，可是大家一眼就能分出誰是誰。

Yan Zhenqing was an official in the 700s. His lines are thick and full, and his characters look broad and steady. He was also known for his courage. When the general An Lushan rebelled, most cities in the north fell, but Yan had quietly repaired the walls of his city, Pingyuan, and stored food, so it was ready. In his seventies he was sent to speak with another rebel, who killed him because he would not give in.

顏真卿是西元八世紀的官員。他的線條又粗又飽滿，字看起來寬闊、穩重。他也以勇敢聞名：安祿山叛變的時候，北方的城池大多失守，只有他守的平原城，因為早就悄悄修好城牆、存好糧食，所以守得住。七十多歲時，他奉命去勸說另一個叛將，因為不肯屈服而被殺害。

Liu Gongquan was born about seventy years later. His lines are leaner, with sharp corners and a tight center, like a strong, thin frame. One day the emperor asked him how to use the brush well. Liu answered, “Using the brush depends on the heart. When the heart is upright, the brush is upright.” The emperor understood that Liu was really giving him advice about how to rule.

柳公權比顏真卿晚出生大約七十年。他的線條比較瘦，轉角銳利，字的中心收得很緊，像一副又瘦又結實的骨架。有一次，皇帝問他怎樣才能把筆用好，他回答：「用筆在心，心正則筆正。」皇帝聽懂了——柳公權其實是在勸他好好治理國家。

Almost two hundred years after Liu died, the Song dynasty writer Fan Zhongyan praised a friend's calligraphy with four characters: 顏筋柳骨, “the muscle of Yan and the bone of Liu.” The words stuck. Muscle stands for Yan's full, powerful lines; bone stands for Liu's hard, clean ones. Both are praise: the phrase means writing that is strong.

柳公權去世後將近兩百年，宋朝的范仲淹用四個字稱讚朋友的書法：「顏筋柳骨」。這四個字就這樣流傳下來。「筋」說的是顏真卿飽滿有力的線條，「骨」說的是柳公權堅硬俐落的線條。兩個都是稱讚：意思是字寫得很有力。

Where does the difference come from? Not from the characters, and not from the stroke order. It comes from how hard the brush presses, where it lifts, how each stroke begins and ends, and how the strokes are spaced. In our model, when both write the same character at the same size, Yan's lines are 1.2 to 1.5 times as thick as Liu's.

差別是從哪裡來的？不是字不一樣，也不是筆順不一樣，而是毛筆按得多重、在哪裡提起來、每一筆怎麼開始和結束、筆畫之間怎麼安排。在我們的模型裡，兩個人把同一個字寫成同樣大的時候，顏真卿的線條是柳公權的 1.2 到 1.5 倍粗。

For more than a thousand years, students have learned regular script by copying masters such as these two, stroke by stroke. People often name four great masters of regular script together: Ouyang Xun, Yan Zhenqing, Liu Gongquan, and Zhao Mengfu. In this lesson, watch two brushes write side by side, guess who wrote each character, and then try both styles yourself.

一千多年來，學楷書的人都是照著像他們這樣的名家，一筆一筆臨摹。大家常把四位楷書名家合在一起說：歐陽詢、顏真卿、柳公權、趙孟頫。這一課，看兩枝毛筆並排寫字，猜猜每個字是誰寫的，再自己兩種寫法都試試看。

The Phrase · 出處

Who first said 顏筋柳骨?「顏筋柳骨」是誰說的？

In 1043 the Song dynasty statesman Fan Zhongyan wrote a farewell to his dead friend Shi Manqing. One line says, “Manqing's brush: the muscle of Yan, the bone of Liu.” So the phrase began as praise for a third person whose writing had the strengths of both. Later it came to describe Yan and Liu themselves.
宋仁宗慶曆三年（1043），范仲淹為過世的朋友石曼卿寫了一篇祭文，裡面說：「曼卿之筆，顏筋柳骨。」（蘇軾引用的寫法）所以這四個字一開始，是稱讚「第三個人」的字兼有兩家的長處；後來才用來形容顏真卿和柳公權本人。

Advice · 筆諫

“When the heart is upright, the brush is upright”心正則筆正

The Old Book of Tang says that Emperor Muzong once asked Liu Gongquan how to use the brush perfectly. Liu replied, “Using the brush depends on the heart; when the heart is upright, the brush is upright.” The emperor's face changed: he knew Liu was using the brush to give him advice.
《舊唐書》記載：唐穆宗「嘗問公權筆何盡善，對曰：『用筆在心，心正則筆正。』上改容，知其筆諫也。」——皇帝的臉色變了，因為他知道柳公權是藉著談毛筆來勸他。

Taiwan · 台灣

A draft full of grief, in Taipei台北故宮的〈祭姪文稿〉

In 758 Yan Zhenqing wrote a draft to mourn his nephew Jiming, who was killed in the rebellion. He crossed out and changed words as he went: an old note on the scroll counts 234 characters plus 34 crossed out. The Yuan dynasty collector Xianyu Shu called it “the second-best running script under heaven.” It is in the National Palace Museum in Taipei and is a National Treasure.
乾元元年（758），顏真卿為在戰亂中遇害的姪子季明寫了一篇祭文的草稿。他邊寫邊塗改：卷後的舊題記數過，一共 234 個字，另外塗掉 34 個字。元代的鮮于樞稱它「天下行書第二」。這件作品收藏在台北的國立故宮博物院，是國寶。

Fame · 名氣

A name worth gold「此購柳書」

The Old Book of Tang says that when a great family set up a memorial stone and the writing was not by Liu Gongquan, people thought the children had failed in their duty to their parents. Envoys from other lands even brought a separate sum of money, marked “for buying Liu's calligraphy.”
《舊唐書》說：「當時公卿大臣家碑板，不得公權手筆者，人以為不孝。外夷入貢，皆別署貨貝，曰此購柳書。」——大官家裡立碑，如果不是柳公權寫的，別人會覺得子孫不孝；外國使者來進貢，還特別另外準備一筆錢，寫明是「買柳公權的字」用的。

Myth vs. Fact · 常見迷思

Four things people get wrong 四個常見的誤會

✗ Myth · 迷思Yan Zhenqing's writing was always thick and heavy.顏真卿的字一直都又粗又重。

✓ Fact · 事實The Duobao Pagoda Stele, which our model follows, is his earliest surviving work, written when he was 44. The National Palace Museum notes that it is cleaner and leaner than the grand, heavy style of his middle and later years.我們模型參考的〈多寶塔碑〉是他傳世最早的作品，寫的時候四十四歲。故宮說這件作品「風格清勁，與中晚期的偉岸沉雄略有不同」——他後來的字更粗、更厚重。

✗ Myth · 迷思“Muscle” is praise and “bone” is a complaint that Liu's writing is too thin.「筋」是稱讚，「骨」是嫌柳公權的字太瘦。

✓ Fact · 事實Both words are praise. The dictionary explains that the writing of Yan and Liu is strong and vigorous, and that is why people say 顏筋柳骨.兩個字都是稱讚。教育部辭典解釋：「唐代顏真卿、柳公權的書法，筆力遒勁，故稱為『顏筋柳骨』。」

✗ Myth · 迷思The history books say Liu Gongquan learned from Yan Zhenqing.史書說柳公權是跟顏真卿學的。

✓ Fact · 事實The Old Book of Tang says only that he first studied Wang Xizhi and then looked widely at recent writers before forming his own style. It was the Song writer Su Shi who thought Liu's writing came out of Yan's.《舊唐書》只說他「初學王書，遍閱近代筆法，體勢勁媚，自成一家」。說「柳少師書，本出於顏」的是宋朝的蘇軾——那是蘇軾的看法。

✗ Myth · 迷思Good handwriting proves that someone is a good person.字寫得好，就證明這個人是好人。

✓ Fact · 事實Old writers such as Su Shi liked to say that writing resembles the person, and Yan Zhenqing's life fits the idea well. But that is a way of looking at art, not a rule. Handwriting gets better with practice.蘇軾這些古人喜歡說「凡書象其為人」，顏真卿的一生也很符合這個說法。不過那是欣賞書法的一種看法，不是定律。字是練出來的。

Yan and Liu in a sentence 一句話記住顏筋柳骨

In the Classroom · 教室提醒

Before you pick up a brush 拿起毛筆之前

  • Ink stains clothes and is very hard to wash out. Wear old clothes or an apron, and roll up your sleeves.墨沾到衣服很難洗掉：穿舊衣服或圍裙，袖子捲起來。
  • Cover the desk with old newspaper, and keep the ink in a heavy, steady dish so it will not tip over.桌上先鋪舊報紙；墨要裝在重一點、放得穩的碟子裡，免得打翻。
  • Press gently. Crushing the brush against the paper squashes the hairs and can split or break them.輕輕地按就好：把筆用力壓在紙上，筆毛會擠扁，也可能分岔或折斷。
  • Do not put the brush, the ink, or the ink stick in your mouth, and do not lick the brush tip to shape it.毛筆、墨汁、墨條都不要放進嘴裡，也不要用嘴巴舔筆尖。
  • After writing, wash the brush in clean water until the water runs clear, gently shape it into a point, and hang it tip-down or lay it flat to dry. Wash your hands, too.寫完後用清水洗筆，洗到水變清為止；輕輕把筆毛理成尖形，筆尖朝下掛起來或平放晾乾。也要洗手。

Classroom Activity 1 · 課堂活動一

One Character, Two Ways 一個字，兩種寫法

You need · 準備材料

  • A brush and clean water, with newspaper or a water-writing cloth毛筆和清水，舊報紙或水寫布
  • This page's side-by-side model, on a screen這一頁的並排模型（用螢幕看）

Steps · 步驟

  1. Watch the two brushes write 十 in the model.先看模型裡兩枝筆寫「十」。
  2. Write 十 the Yan way: press down more and move slowly.學顏真卿寫一個「十」：按重一點、慢慢寫。
  3. Write 十 the Liu way: press less, keep the line thin, and stop firmly at the ends.學柳公權寫一個「十」：按輕一點、線條細細的，收筆穩穩停住。
  4. Do the same with 人 and 大. Show a partner and ask which is which.「人」和「大」也各寫兩種。給同伴看，請他猜哪個是哪一種。

Use water first. When you can make the two look different, try it with ink.
先用清水練。兩種寫得出差別以後，再用墨寫。

Classroom Activity 2 · 課堂活動二

Handwriting Detectives 筆跡小偵探

You need · 準備材料

  • Small cards and pencils or pens小卡片和鉛筆或原子筆
  • A box to collect the cards一個收卡片的盒子

Steps · 步驟

  1. Everyone writes the same three characters, 大 人 十, on a card, with no name.每個人在卡片上寫同樣的三個字「大、人、十」，不要寫名字。
  2. Mix the cards. Each group takes five and guesses who wrote each one.把卡片混在一起。每組抽五張，猜猜各是誰寫的。
  3. Say what clue you used: thick or thin lines? Big or small? Leaning or straight?說出你用的線索：線條粗還是細？字大還是小？歪的還是正的？
  4. Talk about it: what makes each person's writing their own?討論：每個人的字，是哪些地方讓它有自己的樣子？

Describe the writing, not the writer: every style has its strengths.
只描述字，不評論人：每一種風格都有它的長處。
