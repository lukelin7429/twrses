Big idea · 一句話看懂

Chinese calligraphy needs only four tools: a brush, ink, paper, and an inkstone. Each one has its secrets: brush hair can be soft or springy, ink is made from soot, Xuan paper drinks in ink, and the inkstone is where you grind the ink.
書法只要四樣工具：筆、墨、紙、硯。每一樣都有學問：筆毛有軟有硬、墨是煙灰做的、宣紙會吸墨、硯台用來磨墨。

Reading · 英文閱讀

Unit 1: What Are the Four Treasures of the Study?

Calligraphy is the art of beautiful writing. In Chinese calligraphy, people write characters with a soft brush and black ink, and they need only four tools: a brush, an ink stick, paper, and an inkstone. Together these are called the Four Treasures of the Study, because for centuries they sat on the desk in a scholar's study. People have written with brushes for a very long time. The oldest brush ever found came from a tomb near Changsha and is more than 2,200 years old.

書法是把字寫得美的藝術。寫中國書法要用柔軟的毛筆和黑色的墨，而且只需要四樣工具：筆、墨條、紙和硯台。這四樣合稱「文房四寶」，因為幾百年來它們就擺在讀書人書房的桌上。人們用毛筆寫字已經很久很久了：目前找到最古老的毛筆，出土於長沙附近的一座墓，已經有兩千兩百多年。

The brush is a bundle of animal hair tied into a point and fixed to a bamboo handle. Soft white goat hair holds a lot of ink and makes round, gentle lines. Springy brown hair from the tail of a weasel makes sharp, strong lines. In Chinese it is called “wolf hair,” but it usually comes from a weasel. Many brushes mix the two kinds. A good brush has four virtues: its tip is sharp, its hairs are even, its body is round, and it springs back after you press it.

毛筆是一束動物的毛，紮成尖尖的形狀，裝在竹子做的筆桿上。軟軟的白色羊毛（羊毫）吸得住很多墨，寫出來的線條圓潤柔和；黃鼠狼尾巴上有彈性的褐色毛，寫出來的線條銳利有力。這種毛中文叫「狼毫」，其實通常來自黃鼠狼。很多毛筆把兩種毛混在一起（兼毫）。好的毛筆有四個條件：筆尖要尖、毛要齊、筆肚要圓、壓下去要能彈回來（尖、齊、圓、健）。

An ink stick looks like a small black bar, but it is made from soot. Makers burn pine wood or oil, collect the fine black soot, mix it with glue, and press it into molds to dry. To make liquid ink, you put a few drops of water on the inkstone and rub the stick in slow circles. Bit by bit, the soot comes off, and the water turns black. Grinding takes time, so many people today use bottled ink, but grinding is also a calm way to get ready to write.

墨條看起來像一根黑色的小棒子，其實是用煙灰做的。做墨的人燒松木或油，收集細細的黑色煙灰，加上膠拌勻，壓進模子裡晾乾。要變成墨汁，就在硯台上滴幾滴水，拿墨條慢慢畫圈磨：煙灰一點一點磨下來，水就變黑了。磨墨很花時間，所以今天很多人用瓶裝的墨汁；不過磨墨也是寫字前讓心安靜下來的好方法。

Calligraphers often write on Xuan paper, which is named after Xuanzhou in Anhui Province, China. It is made by hand from the bark of the blue sandalwood tree and rice straw. Raw Xuan paper drinks in water, so a drop of ink spreads out with soft edges, and the paper shows every move of the brush. Copy paper is different. It is sized, or coated, so the water cannot soak in, and the ink sits on top. In Taiwan, the town of Puli has made handmade paper since the Japanese era.

書法家常寫在宣紙上。宣紙的名字來自中國安徽的宣州，是用青檀樹的樹皮和稻草手工做成的。生宣紙會把水吸進去，所以一滴墨會暈開、邊緣柔柔的，筆的每一個動作都看得出來。影印紙就不一樣了：它上過膠，水滲不進去，墨就停在表面。在台灣，埔里從日治時代就開始做手工紙。

The inkstone is a flat piece of fine, hard stone. Its surface is smooth but a little rough, just enough to grind the ink stick quickly. The flat part where you grind is called the hill, and the deep part at one end is called the sea, where the finished ink collects. Some of the most famous inkstones are Duan inkstones, carved from purple stone near Zhaoqing in Guangdong Province, an area that was called Duanzhou in the Tang dynasty.

硯台是一塊扁平、細緻又堅硬的石頭。它的表面光滑，卻又帶一點點粗，剛好能把墨條很快磨下來。磨墨的平面叫「硯堂」，英文叫它「山丘」（the hill）；一頭深下去的地方叫「硯池」，英文叫它「海」（the sea），磨好的墨就流到那裡。最有名的硯台之一是端硯，用廣東肇慶附近的紫色石頭雕成；這一帶在唐代叫端州。

Calligraphy is not only for experts or for grandparents. In Taiwan, students begin writing with a brush in third or fourth grade, and many people write spring couplets for the Lunar New Year. You do not even need ink to start: a brush dipped in clean water can practice on old newspaper or on a water-writing cloth, where the marks fade as they dry. In this lesson, first watch the 3D brush write the character 一 (one) in slow motion. Then try writing it yourself on the practice pad.

書法不是只有專家或爺爺奶奶在寫。在台灣，學生在三、四年級就開始學寫毛筆字，過農曆年時也有很多人寫春聯。開始練習甚至不需要墨：毛筆蘸清水，就能在舊報紙或水寫布上練習，水痕乾了就消失。這一課，先看 3D 毛筆用慢動作寫出「一」，再到練字板上自己寫寫看。

Questions · 閱讀理解

What are the Four Treasures of the Study, and why do they have that name?

They are the brush, the ink stick, paper, and the inkstone. They have that name because for centuries they sat on the desk in a scholar's study.

How do you turn an ink stick into liquid ink?

Put a few drops of water on the inkstone and rub the ink stick in slow circles until the soot comes off and the water turns black.

Why does a drop of ink spread on raw Xuan paper but not on copy paper?

Raw Xuan paper drinks in water, so the ink spreads. Copy paper is sized, or coated, so the water cannot soak in and the ink stays on top.

Four Places · 四個產地

Where the famous treasures come from四寶的故鄉

The best-known treasures are named after the places that made them famous. Hu brushes come from Huzhou in Zhejiang, where the town of Shanlian still makes them by hand. Hui ink comes from old Huizhou in Anhui, and She County still makes it. Xuan paper is named after Xuanzhou in Anhui and is made in Jing County; in 2009 the craft was added to UNESCO's list of the Intangible Cultural Heritage of Humanity. Duan inkstones are carved near Zhaoqing in Guangdong, which was called Duanzhou in the Tang dynasty.
最有名的四寶，常用產地來稱呼。湖筆出自浙江湖州，善璉鎮至今還在手工製筆；徽墨出自安徽古徽州，歙縣至今仍在製墨；宣紙因安徽宣州得名，產在涇縣，2009 年宣紙傳統製作技藝列入聯合國教科文組織的人類非物質文化遺產代表作名錄；端硯雕自廣東肇慶附近，肇慶在唐代叫端州。

The Name · 這個說法

Where “the Four Treasures of the Study” comes from「文房四寶」這個說法

Wenfang (文房) means a scholar's study, the room for reading and writing. In the late 900s, Su Yijian, an official of the Song dynasty, wrote a whole book about the four tools, the Wenfang Sipu (Four Records of the Study): two parts on brushes, and one part each on inkstones, ink, and paper. Another Song writer, Ye Mengde, wrote down a saying of his time: “Shezhou has the Four Treasures of the Study,” meaning brush, ink, paper, and inkstone. In 1121, Shezhou was renamed Huizhou, the home of Hui ink.
「文房」是讀書人的書房，讀書寫字的地方。北宋官員蘇易簡在十世紀後半寫了一整本講這四樣工具的書《文房四譜》：筆兩卷，硯、墨、紙各一卷。宋代的葉夢得也記下當時的說法：「世言歙州有文房四寶，謂筆、墨、紙、硯也。」歙州在 1121 年改名徽州，也就是徽墨的故鄉。

Made in Taiwan · 台灣的紙

Handmade paper from Puli埔里的手工紙

Puli, near the geographic center of Taiwan, is known for its clean water, and papermakers need clean water. Handmade papermaking skills from Japan and China came to Puli during the Japanese era, and after World War II, through the 1960s and 1970s, Puli's handmade paper enjoyed a short export boom. From the 1980s the industry shrank, as many mills closed or moved to mainland China or Southeast Asia, and after the 1999 earthquake even fewer remained. Some of the mills still working now welcome visitors, who can watch paper being made and try making a sheet themselves.
埔里位在台灣地理中心附近，以水質純淨聞名，而造紙最需要好水。日治時期，日本和中國的手工造紙技術傳進埔里；二戰後到 1960、70 年代，埔里的手工紙曾短暫外銷興盛。1980 年代起產業逐漸萎縮，許多紙廠關門，或搬到中國大陸、東南亞設廠；1999 年九二一地震後，紙廠又更少了。還在做紙的紙廠，有些開放參觀，可以看師傅抄紙，也能自己動手做一張。

A Saying · 一句成語

Three fen into the wood: 入木三分入木三分

An old story says that Wang Xizhi, the great calligrapher of the Eastern Jin dynasty, once wrote on a wooden board for a ceremony. Later, when workers shaved the board to write on it again, they found his ink had soaked three fen deep into the wood (a fen is a small old unit of length). Today 入木三分 describes powerful brushwork, and also a comment or description that goes deep and gets right to the point.
相傳東晉書法家王羲之曾在祭祀用的木板上寫字，後來工人要把木板削掉重寫，發現墨跡滲進木頭有三分深（分是古代很小的長度單位）。今天「入木三分」用來形容筆力強勁，也比喻評論深刻中肯、描寫生動逼真。

Myth vs. Fact · 常見迷思

Four things people get wrong 四個常見的誤會

✗ Myth · 迷思You need bottled ink to write calligraphy.寫毛筆字一定要用墨汁。

✓ Fact · 事實For centuries, ink was ground fresh from an ink stick on an inkstone. Bottled ink is a modern shortcut that many classes use, and beginners can even practice with plain water on newspaper or a water-writing cloth.幾百年來，墨都是拿墨條在硯台上現磨的。瓶裝墨汁是現代的方便做法，很多課堂都用；初學的人甚至可以只用清水，在報紙或水寫布上練習。

✗ Myth · 迷思Xuan paper is just ordinary white paper.宣紙就是普通的白紙。

✓ Fact · 事實Xuan paper is made by hand from tree bark and rice straw. Raw Xuan paper is not sized, so it drinks in water and makes ink spread; copy paper is sized and smooth, so ink sits on top. The English name “rice paper” is misleading, too: the fibers come from bark and straw, not from rice grains.宣紙是用樹皮和稻草手工做的。生宣紙沒上膠，會吸水、讓墨暈開；影印紙上過膠又光滑，墨停在表面。英文常說的「rice paper（米紙）」也容易誤會：纖維來自樹皮和稻草，不是米粒。

✗ Myth · 迷思The softer the brush, the easier it is to write.筆毛越軟越好寫。

✓ Fact · 事實Soft goat hair holds lots of ink, but it bends a lot and springs back slowly, so it is hard to control. Springier weasel-hair or mixed-hair brushes are often easier for beginners.軟的羊毫吸墨多，可是彎得多、彈回得慢，比較難控制。有彈性的狼毫或兼毫，對初學者常常比較好寫。

✗ Myth · 迷思Only old people write calligraphy.書法只有老人家在寫。

✓ Fact · 事實In Taiwan, students start writing with a brush in third or fourth grade as part of their Chinese lessons, and people of all ages write spring couplets for the Lunar New Year.在台灣，學生三、四年級就在國語課學寫毛筆字；過農曆年寫春聯的，也是男女老少都有。

Remember It · 記憶口訣

The Four Treasures in a sentence 一句話記住文房四寶

Brush, ink, paper, stone筆、墨、紙、硯

Four tools on one desk: the Four Treasures of the Study.
四樣工具一張桌：文房四寶。

Sharp, even, round, springy尖、齊、圓、健

A good brush comes to a point, lines up evenly, stays round, and springs back.
好筆四個條件：筆尖尖、毛整齊、筆肚圓、有彈性。

Grind slowly, in circles磨墨畫圓，慢慢來

A few drops of water, small circles, and patience make dark ink.
幾滴水、畫小圓、有耐心，墨就黑了。

Raw paper drinks, copy paper doesn't生宣會喝水，影印紙不喝

On raw Xuan paper, ink spreads; on sized paper, it stays put.
墨在生宣紙上會暈開，在上過膠的紙上停在原地。

In the Classroom · 教室提醒

Before you pick up a brush 拿起毛筆之前

  • Ink stains clothes and is very hard to wash out. Wear old clothes or an apron, and roll up your sleeves.墨沾到衣服很難洗掉：穿舊衣服或圍裙，袖子捲起來。
  • Cover the desk with old newspaper, and keep the ink in a heavy, steady dish so it will not tip over.桌上先鋪舊報紙；墨要裝在重一點、放得穩的碟子裡，免得打翻。
  • Do not put the brush, the ink, or the ink stick in your mouth, and do not lick the brush tip to shape it.毛筆、墨汁、墨條都不要放進嘴裡，也不要用嘴巴舔筆尖。
  • Inkstones are heavy stone. Carry them with two hands, and set them down gently.硯台是石頭，很重：用兩隻手拿，輕輕放下。
  • After writing, wash the brush in clean water until the water runs clear, gently shape it into a point, and hang it tip-down or lay it flat to dry. Wash your hands, too.寫完後用清水洗筆，洗到水變清為止；輕輕把筆毛理成尖形，筆尖朝下掛起來或平放晾乾。也要洗手。

Classroom Activity 1 · 課堂活動一

Practice with Water: Horizontal Lines 用清水練橫線

You need · 準備材料

  • A brush of any size一枝毛筆（大小都可以）
  • A cup of clean water一杯清水
  • Old newspaper or a water-writing cloth舊報紙或水寫布
  • A paper towel一張紙巾

Steps · 步驟

  1. Dip the brush in the water, then wipe it on the edge of the cup so it is wet but not dripping.毛筆蘸水，在杯緣刮一刮，讓它濕濕的但不會滴水。
  2. Hold the brush straight up, like the brush in the 3D model.像 3D 模型裡的毛筆一樣，把筆桿拿直。
  3. Write a row of horizontal lines from left to right. Press down at the start, move steadily, then press and lift at the end.由左到右寫一排橫線：起筆往下按、中間穩穩地走、最後按一下再提起。
  4. Write one line slowly and one line quickly. Which is thicker? Watch the water marks fade as they dry, then write again in the same place.一條慢慢寫、一條快快寫：哪一條比較粗？看水痕乾掉慢慢消失，再在同一個地方重寫。

Water writing is clean and cheap, and you can repeat it as many times as you like, so it works well for a whole class. Newspaper soaks up water a bit like raw Xuan paper, so it also shows how ink would spread.
用清水練字乾淨又省錢，想寫幾次就寫幾次，很適合全班一起練。報紙吸水的樣子有點像生宣紙，也看得出墨會怎麼暈開。

Classroom Activity 2 · 課堂活動二

The Ink-Grinding Race: How Many Circles Make Black Ink? 磨墨比賽：磨幾圈水才夠黑？

You need · 準備材料

  • For each team: an inkstone, an ink stick, and a water dropper or a small spoon每一組：一個硯台、一條墨條、一個水盂或小湯匙
  • Strips of white paper (raw Xuan paper if you have it)白紙條（有生宣紙更好）
  • A record sheet and a pencil一張紀錄表和一枝鉛筆
  • Old newspaper to cover the desks舊報紙，鋪在桌上

Steps · 步驟

  1. Every team puts the same amount of water on the grinding surface, for example five drops.每一組在硯堂上放一樣多的水，例如 5 滴。
  2. One student grinds in small, slow circles, pressing gently, while a teammate counts the circles.一個人輕輕地、慢慢地畫小圓磨墨，隊友數圈數。
  3. Every 20 circles, dab a little ink onto a paper strip and write the number of circles beside it.每磨 20 圈，在紙條上點一點墨，旁邊寫上圈數。
  4. Stop at 100 circles. Line up the strips from every team and compare: whose ink got darkest? Did grinding faster or pressing harder help?磨到 100 圈就停。把各組的紙條排在一起比較：誰的墨最黑？磨得快、壓得重有沒有幫助？

An old saying goes: grind ink as gently as a sick man, and hold the brush as firmly as a strong man. Pressing hard makes rough, gritty ink. After grinding, wipe the ink stick dry so it does not stick to the stone or crack.
有句老話說：「磨墨如病夫，執筆如壯士。」用力壓反而磨出粗粗的墨。磨完要把墨條擦乾，免得黏在硯上或裂開。
