How Does a Brush Make Thick and Thin Lines?一枝毛筆怎麼寫出粗細？

A brush's tip is soft: press down for a thick line, lift up for a thin one. Control the pressure of a 3D brush yourself, compare the center tip with the side tip, slow down every part of 十 (ten), and see how your own press-and-lift curve compares.

Big idea · 一句話看懂

The biggest difference between a brush and a ballpoint pen is the soft tip. Press down, and the hairs spread for a thick line; lift up, and they gather for a thin one. The beauty of calligraphy lives in the rhythm of pressing and lifting, and it starts with holding the brush upright.
毛筆和原子筆最大的不同：筆鋒是軟的。往下按，筆毛散開線就粗；往上提，筆毛收攏線就細。書法的美就在提與按的節奏，而這要從把筆拿直開始。

Unit 2: How Does a Brush Make Thick and Thin Lines?

Draw a line with a ballpoint pen, and it is the same width from beginning to end. Draw a line with a brush, and it can be as thin as a hair in one place and as wide as your finger in another. The secret is the soft tip. When you press the brush down, the hairs spread out, and the line gets thicker. When you lift it up, the hairs come back together, and the line gets thinner.

用原子筆畫一條線，從頭到尾都一樣寬。用毛筆畫一條線，有的地方細得像頭髮，有的地方寬得像手指。祕密就在軟軟的筆鋒：把筆往下按，筆毛散開，線就變粗；往上提，筆毛收攏，線就變細。

Calligraphers call these two movements pressing and lifting. A good line is not pressed the same way from end to end. In a horizontal stroke, the brush presses down at the start, moves a little lighter through the middle, and presses again near the end. This rhythm of pressing and lifting gives a stroke its life, a bit like the beat in a piece of music.

書法家把這兩個動作叫做「按」和「提」。好的線條不會從頭到尾壓得一樣重。寫一筆橫的時候，起筆往下按，中間稍微輕一點地走過去，快到終點時再按一次。這種提與按的節奏讓筆畫有了生命，有點像音樂裡的拍子。

Every stroke has three parts: the start, the middle, and the finish. In regular script, the tip often starts by moving a little in the opposite direction, so that it is hidden inside the stroke. This is called hiding the tip. At the end, the brush can turn back on itself, or it can lift slowly until the line ends in a fine point, like a needle hanging down.

每一筆都有三個部分：起筆、行筆、收筆。寫楷書時，筆尖常常先往相反的方向走一點，讓筆尖藏在筆畫裡，這叫「藏鋒」。到了終點，毛筆可以往回收（回鋒），也可以一邊走一邊慢慢提起，讓線條收成細細的尖，像一根懸著的針（懸針）。

Watch where the tip goes. When the handle stays upright, the tip runs along the middle of the line. This is called the center tip, and it makes lines that look round and full. When the handle leans, the tip slides along one edge, and the line turns flat, with one sharp side and one rough side. This is the side tip.

注意筆尖走在哪裡。筆桿直立的時候，筆尖走在線的中間，這叫「中鋒」，寫出來的線條圓潤飽滿。筆桿斜了，筆尖就貼著一邊滑過去，線條變得扁平，一邊整齊、一邊粗糙，這叫「側鋒」。

To keep the tip in the middle, hold the brush upright. A traditional grip, often traced to Lu Xisheng of the Tang dynasty, gives each of the five fingers a job. The thumb, the index finger, and the middle finger hold the handle from both sides, the ring finger pushes back from behind, and the little finger supports the ring finger. Keep your fingers firm but your palm hollow, as if you were holding an egg.

要讓筆尖保持在中間，就要把筆拿直。有一種傳統的拿法，常說是唐代陸希聲傳下來的，讓五根手指各有工作：大拇指、食指、中指從兩邊拿住筆桿，無名指從後面頂住，小指托住無名指。手指要實，掌心要虛，好像手裡握著一顆蛋（指實掌虛）。

Speed matters, too. When the brush moves slowly, more ink soaks into the paper and the line grows thicker. When it moves quickly, the line becomes thinner and drier. In this lesson, slow down the 3D brush to watch pressing and lifting up close. Then write 十 (ten) on the practice pad: first the horizontal stroke, then the vertical one. The pad will compare your press-and-lift curve with the brush's.

速度也有關係。毛筆走得慢，比較多的墨滲進紙裡，線就變粗；走得快，線就變細、也比較乾。這一課，先把 3D 毛筆放慢，近看提和按；再到練字板上寫「十」：先寫橫，再寫豎。練字板會把你的提按曲線和毛筆的拿來比較。

Questions · 閱讀理解

Why can one brush make both thick and thin lines?

Its tip is soft. When you press it down, the hairs spread and the line gets thicker; when you lift it up, the hairs gather and the line gets thinner.

What are the three parts of a stroke, and what does hiding the tip mean?

The start, the middle, and the finish. Hiding the tip means the tip first moves a little in the opposite direction, so it stays hidden inside the stroke.

What is the difference between the center tip and the side tip?

With the center tip, the handle is upright and the tip runs along the middle of the line, so the line is round and full. With the side tip, the handle leans and the tip slides along one edge, so the line is flat, with one sharp side and one rough side.

Hold the Brush · 執筆

Five fingers, one upright brush 五根手指，一枝直立的筆

A traditional grip gives each finger one job and names it with one character. It is often traced to Lu Xisheng of the Tang dynasty and is known as the five-character method.
傳統的執筆法讓每根手指負責一件事，每件事用一個字來說，常說是唐代陸希聲傳下來的「五字執筆法」：擫、押、鉤、格、抵。

Presses the handle from the inside, tilted slightly upward.大拇指上節貼住筆桿內側，稍微往上斜。

押押 yā

Presses from the outside, so the thumb and index finger hold the handle between them.食指從外側壓住筆桿，和大拇指一內一外夾住。

鉤鉤 gōu

Middle finger中指

Curls like a hook around the outside and pulls inward.中指彎成鉤，鉤住筆桿外側往內用力。

格格 gé

Ring finger無名指

Pushes back from the inside with the edge of the nail, balancing the middle finger.無名指以指甲與肉相接處從內側頂住，擋住中指往內的力。

Sayings About the Brush · 關於筆的成語

An upright heart, iron strokes, strength through paper, and dancing dragons 心正筆正、鐵畫銀鉤、力透紙背、筆走龍蛇

Four sayings about writing with a brush, and the stories behind them.
四句形容寫字的成語，和它們背後的故事。

A Story · 一個故事

An upright heart, an upright brush心正筆正

The Old Book of Tang records that Emperor Muzong once asked the calligrapher Liu Gongquan how to use the brush perfectly. Liu answered, “Using the brush depends on the heart. When the heart is upright, the brush is upright.” The emperor's face changed: he understood that Liu was giving him advice through calligraphy. Today 心正筆正 also means that an honest heart leads to honest actions.
《舊唐書》記載，唐穆宗問書法家柳公權，筆要怎麼用才能寫到最好。柳公權回答：「用筆在心，心正則筆正。」穆宗臉色一變，明白柳公權是藉著書法在勸他。今天「心正筆正」也比喻內心公正，做事就正直。

Iron and Silver · 鐵與銀

Iron strokes and silver hooks鐵畫銀鉤

The Tang calligrapher Ouyang Xun wrote in his essay On Using the Brush that good strokes are “firm like iron strokes, graceful like silver hooks.” A children's classic, Youxue Qionglin, later used the words for Wang Xizhi's writing. Today 鐵畫銀鉤 praises calligraphy that is both strong and beautiful.
唐代書法家歐陽詢在〈用筆論〉裡說，好的筆畫「剛則鐵畫，媚若銀鉤」：剛勁像鐵、柔美像銀。後來的童蒙書《幼學瓊林》用它來形容王羲之的字。今天「鐵畫銀鉤」用來稱讚書法剛柔並濟。

Strength · 力道

Strength through the back of the paper力透紙背

The saying comes from Yan Zhenqing's notes on the brush methods he learned from Zhang Xu: when the writing is strong, its force seems to reach right through to the back of the paper. Today 力透紙背 praises powerful calligraphy, and also writing whose ideas are deep and strong. Strength here means control, not crushing the brush.
這句話出自唐代顏真卿記錄張旭（張長史）筆法的〈張長史十二意筆法記〉：寫字時運筆的力量好像穿透到紙的背面。今天「力透紙背」形容書法遒勁有力，也形容文章立意深刻。這裡的「力」是控制，不是把筆壓扁。

Movement · 動感

The brush runs like dragons and snakes筆走龍蛇

The Song poet Shi Xiaoyou praised a friend with the line “筆走龍蛇” in one of his ci poems: the brush moves as freely as dragons and snakes. Today it describes writing or calligraphy that flows freely and skillfully. You will see this kind of movement in running and cursive script later in this series.
宋代詞人石孝友在〈滿庭芳．上張紫微〉裡用「筆走龍蛇」稱讚對方：筆像龍蛇一樣自在地遊走。今天用來形容文筆揮灑自如、書法優美熟練。這個系列後面的行書、草書，就看得到這種動感。

Myth vs. Fact · 常見迷思

Four things people get wrong 四個常見的誤會

✗ Myth · 迷思Thick lines need a thick brush.粗的線要用粗的筆。

✓ Fact · 事實One brush can make both. Press it down and the hairs spread; lift it and they gather. In this lesson's model, the same brush makes lines from under 2 millimeters to about 3 centimeters wide.同一枝筆就做得到：往下按，筆毛散開；往上提，筆毛收攏。在這一課的模型裡，同一枝筆畫得出不到 2 公釐、到大約 3 公分寬的線。

✗ Myth · 迷思Pressing harder makes stronger calligraphy.越用力壓，字就越有力。

✓ Fact · 事實Strength comes from control: pressing and lifting at the right moments. Crushing the brush squashes the hairs, makes blobs, and can split the tip.有力來自控制：在對的時候按、在對的時候提。把筆壓扁，筆毛會擠成一團、墨跡變成一坨，筆尖也可能分岔。

✗ Myth · 迷思You hold a brush the same way as a pencil.拿毛筆和拿鉛筆一樣。

✓ Fact · 事實A brush is held upright, often with all five fingers, and the palm stays hollow. A pencil-style grip tilts the handle, so the tip slides to one side and the line turns flat.毛筆要拿直，常用五根手指，掌心是空的。像拿鉛筆那樣斜斜地拿，筆尖會偏到一邊，線就變扁。

✗ Myth · 迷思A stroke is just a line from one end to the other.一筆就是從這頭畫到那頭的線。

✓ Fact · 事實Every stroke has a start, a middle, and a finish. The tip can hide at the start, run in the middle, and turn back or lift to a point at the end.每一筆都有起筆、行筆、收筆：起筆可以藏鋒，行筆走中鋒，收筆可以回鋒，或是提成尖。

In the Classroom · 教室提醒

Before you pick up a brush 拿起毛筆之前

  • Ink stains clothes and is very hard to wash out. Wear old clothes or an apron, and roll up your sleeves.墨沾到衣服很難洗掉：穿舊衣服或圍裙，袖子捲起來。
  • Cover the desk with old newspaper, and keep the ink in a heavy, steady dish so it will not tip over.桌上先鋪舊報紙；墨要裝在重一點、放得穩的碟子裡，免得打翻。
  • Press gently. Crushing the brush against the paper squashes the hairs and can split or break them.輕輕地按就好：把筆用力壓在紙上，筆毛會擠扁，也可能分岔或折斷。
  • Do not put the brush, the ink, or the ink stick in your mouth, and do not lick the brush tip to shape it.毛筆、墨汁、墨條都不要放進嘴裡，也不要用嘴巴舔筆尖。
  • After writing, wash the brush in clean water until the water runs clear, gently shape it into a point, and hang it tip-down or lay it flat to dry. Wash your hands, too.寫完後用清水洗筆，洗到水變清為止；輕輕把筆毛理成尖形，筆尖朝下掛起來或平放晾乾。也要洗手。

Classroom Activity 1 · 課堂活動一

Thin, Thick, Thin with Water 用清水練提按

You need · 準備材料

  • A brush of any size一枝毛筆（大小都可以）
  • A cup of clean water一杯清水
  • Old newspaper or a water-writing cloth舊報紙或水寫布
  • A paper towel一張紙巾

Steps · 步驟

  1. Dip the brush in the water and wipe it on the edge of the cup so it is wet but not dripping.毛筆蘸水，在杯緣刮一刮，濕但不會滴。
  2. Draw a horizontal line at an even speed: start light, press down slowly until it is thick, then lift slowly until it is thin again.用平均的速度畫一條橫線：先輕輕地，慢慢按到最粗，再慢慢提起來變細。
  3. Draw a wave line: press, lift, press, lift. Then draw a vertical line that ends in a fine point, like a hanging needle.畫一條波浪線：按、提、按、提。再畫一條豎線，收尾提成細細的尖，像懸著的針。
  4. Trade with a partner and guess where they pressed and where they lifted. Write again after the water dries.和同學交換，猜猜對方哪裡按、哪裡提。水乾了再寫一次。

Water marks fade as they dry, so students can try again and again without wasting ink or paper.
水痕乾了就消失，學生可以一次又一次地試，不浪費墨也不浪費紙。

Classroom Activity 2 · 課堂活動二

An Egg in the Palm: The Five-Finger Check 掌中握蛋：五指執筆檢查

You need · 準備材料

  • A brush for each student每人一枝毛筆
  • A ping-pong ball or a small ball for each pair每兩人一顆乒乓球或小球
  • A cup of water and old newspaper一杯清水和舊報紙
  • A checklist with the five characters: 擫、押、鉤、格、抵寫著擫、押、鉤、格、抵的檢查表

Steps · 步驟

  1. Hold the ball loosely in your palm, then pick up the brush with all five fingers so the ball can stay there.掌心輕輕握著球，再用五根手指拿起毛筆，讓球還能留在掌心裡。
  2. Your partner checks each finger against the checklist, and that the handle stands upright.同學照檢查表一根一根看手指，也看筆桿是不是直立。
  3. Put the ball down, keep the hollow palm, and write 十 with water: the horizontal first, then the vertical.把球放下，掌心保持空空的，用清水寫「十」：先橫後豎。
  4. Switch roles. Talk about it: what changes when the handle leans?交換角色。討論：筆桿斜了，線條有什麼不一樣？

The ball is only a reminder of a hollow palm; take it away before writing for real. Left-handed students can mirror every step.
球只是用來提醒「掌虛」，真正寫字前要拿走。用左手的學生，每一步左右相反就好。
