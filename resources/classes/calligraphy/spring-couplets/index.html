Before the Lunar New Year, red paper goes up beside the door: two matching lines, a short line above, and a diamond with 春 or 福. Watch the brush write 春 and 福 in 3D, decide which line goes on which side of the door, play the upper-line game, and trace the two characters yourself.

Big idea · 一句話看懂

Spring couplets are lucky words written on red paper and put up by the door at the Lunar New Year. A couplet has two matching lines. The upper line ends in an oblique tone and the lower line ends in a level tone, and by custom the upper line goes on the right as you face the door.
春聯是農曆新年時寫在紅紙上、貼在門邊的吉祥話。一副對聯有兩句互相對仗的句子：上聯的最後一個字是仄聲，下聯的最後一個字是平聲；照習慣，面對大門時，上聯貼在右邊。

Unit 11: How Do You Write Spring Couplets?

A few days before the Lunar New Year, something red appears on doors all over Taiwan. Families put up strips of red paper with lucky words written in ink. These are spring couplets.

農曆新年前幾天，台灣到處的門上都出現了紅色。家家戶戶貼上紅紙條，上面用墨寫著吉祥話。這就是春聯。

The custom is very old. Long ago, people hung two boards of peach wood by the door at the New Year to keep bad things away. Later they wrote lucky lines on the boards. The history of the Song dynasty tells of a king of Shu, Meng Chang, who wrote his own pair of lines on the boards one New Year's Eve in the tenth century. In time, red paper took the place of wood.

這個習俗非常古老。很久以前，人們過年時在門邊掛兩塊桃木板（桃符），用來避邪。後來有人在木板上寫吉祥的句子。《宋史》記載，十世紀時後蜀的國君孟昶，有一年除夕親自在桃符上題了一副句子。再後來，紅紙取代了木板。

A couplet is a pair of lines. The two lines have the same number of characters, and their words match each other one by one. One line goes on each side of the door. A short line, the horizontal scroll, goes above the door. Many families also hang a diamond of red paper with just one character, such as 春 for spring or 福 for good fortune.

一副對聯有兩句。兩句的字數一樣，用字一個對一個。門的兩邊各貼一句；門的上方再貼一條短的，叫橫批。很多人家還會貼一張菱形的紅紙，上面只寫一個字，例如「春」或「福」。

Which line goes on which side? Listen to the last character. The upper line ends in an oblique tone, and the lower line ends in a level tone. In Mandarin, the first and second tones are mostly level, and the third and fourth tones are oblique. By custom, the upper line goes on the right as you face the door.

哪一句貼哪一邊？聽聽最後一個字。上聯的末字是仄聲，下聯的末字是平聲。用國語來說，一聲、二聲大多是平聲，三聲、四聲是仄聲。照習慣，面對大門時，上聯貼在右邊。

There is one trap. A few characters, such as 福, are second tone in Mandarin today, but they belong to an old group of short, clipped sounds that count as oblique. That is why some people check a tricky line by saying it in Taiwanese or Hakka.

有一個陷阱。少數幾個字，例如「福」，現在國語唸二聲，可是它們屬於古代一種又短又急的聲調（入聲），算仄聲。所以遇到拿不準的句子，有人會改用台語或客語唸唸看。

You can write your own. Fold the red paper lightly to mark a square for each character, write from top to bottom, and let the ink dry flat. Every winter, calligraphers at museums and halls around Taiwan write couplets for visitors. This year, try writing one yourself.

你也可以自己寫。把紅紙輕輕摺出每個字的方格，從上往下寫，寫完平放晾乾。每年冬天，台灣各地的博物館和紀念館都有書法家為民眾寫春聯。今年，試試看自己寫一副。

Questions · 閱讀理解

What did people hang by the door at the New Year long ago, before paper couplets?

Two boards of peach wood, to keep bad things away.

How can you tell the upper line of a couplet from the lower line?

Listen to the last character: the upper line ends in an oblique tone, and the lower line ends in a level tone.

Why is the character 福 a trap for people who use Mandarin tones to check a couplet?

Because 福 is second tone in Mandarin today, but it belongs to an old group of sounds that count as oblique.

Stories and Taiwan · 故事與台灣

Peach-wood boards, a king's two lines, an emperor's order, and the couplets of today 桃木板、國君的兩句話、皇帝的命令，和今天的春聯

Two things that old books record, one famous story, and where to see couplets being written in Taiwan.
兩件古書記下來的事、一個有名的傳說，還有在台灣哪裡看得到現場寫春聯。

Before Paper · 桃符

Boards of peach wood門邊的桃木板

The dictionary explains that people long ago set two peach-wood boards by the door at the New Year, with the names or pictures of two gods who could catch ghosts. A Qing dynasty book about Beijing says simply, “Spring couplets are peach-wood charms.”
教育部辭典解釋「桃符」：古代相傳有神荼、鬱壘二神能捉百鬼，所以新年時在門旁設兩塊桃木板，寫上二神的名字或畫上圖像，用來驅鬼避邪。清朝的《燕京歲時記》說：「春聯者，即桃符也。」

A King · 孟昶

“The new year brings blessings to spare”新年納餘慶

The History of Song says that every New Year's Eve the king of Shu had a scholar write words on the peach boards beside his bedroom door. One year he did not like them, so he wrote his own: 新年納餘慶，喜節號長春. A Qing scholar called this pair the oldest of its kind. Other books give slightly different words, and some scholars point to still earlier lines.
《宋史》記載，後蜀國君孟昶「每歲除，命學士為詞，題桃符，置寢門左右」；有一年他嫌學士寫得不好，「自命筆題云：『新年納餘慶，喜節號長春。』」清朝的紀昀說這一聯「最古」。不過各書記的字句略有不同（常見的寫法是「嘉節號長春」），也有學者指出還有更早的例子。

A Story · 傳說

Did an emperor order every door to have one?明太祖下令貼春聯？

A famous story says the first Ming emperor ordered every household in his capital to put up a spring couplet on New Year's Eve, and then walked the streets in plain clothes to look at them. The story comes from a Qing dynasty book of anecdotes, written long after his time, so treat it as a story.
有個有名的故事：明太祖定都金陵後，「於除夕忽傳旨：公卿士庶家，門上須加春聯一副」，還微服出去看。這個說法出自清朝的筆記《簪雲樓雜說》（後人轉引），離明太祖的時代已經很久，所以只能當作傳說。

Taiwan · 台灣

Couplets written while you wait現場揮毫送春聯

Each winter the Office of the President gives out a New Year couplet; for 2026, the Year of the Horse, the words were 七喜春來, written by the calligrapher Yu Tianci. The National Palace Museum has invited calligraphers to write couplets and diamond papers for visitors, and the Sun Yat-sen Memorial Hall has held a New Year writing event since 2005.
總統府每年冬天都會發送新年春聯；2026 年（馬年）的賀詞是「七喜春來」，由書法家余天賜揮毫。故宮辦過新春揮毫活動，請書法老師「為民眾揮毫致贈春聯、斗方」；國父紀念館則從民國 94 年（2005）起，每年春節前都舉辦迎春揮毫。

Myth vs. Fact · 常見迷思

Four things people get wrong 四個常見的誤會

✗ Myth · 迷思The two lines can go on either side of the door.兩句貼哪一邊都可以。

✓ Fact · 事實A couplet has an order. The upper line ends in an oblique tone and the lower line in a level tone, and by custom the upper line goes on the right as you face the door.對聯有上下之分：上聯末字仄聲、下聯末字平聲。照習慣，面對大門時上聯貼右邊。

✗ Myth · 迷思Third and fourth tone are always oblique; first and second tone are always level.三聲、四聲一定是仄聲，一聲、二聲一定是平聲。

✓ Fact · 事實Mostly, but not always. The dictionary notes that the old entering tone no longer exists in Mandarin: its characters are now spread across all four tones. So 福 is second tone today but still counts as oblique.大部分是，但不是全部。教育部辭典說：「現國音中已無入聲，入聲字分別歸入陰平、陽平、上聲、去聲四聲之中。」所以「福」現在唸二聲，仍然算仄聲。

✗ Myth · 迷思Spring couplets began with an order from the first Ming emperor.春聯是明太祖下令才開始有的。

✓ Fact · 事實That is a story from a Qing dynasty book. The History of Song already records lines written on New Year peach boards in the tenth century, about four hundred years earlier.那是清朝筆記裡的故事。《宋史》已經記載十世紀時在桃符上題句，比明太祖早了大約四百年。

✗ Myth · 迷思The character 福 must always be hung upside down.「福」字一定要倒著貼。

✓ Fact · 事實Some people do turn it over, because 倒 (upside down) sounds like 到 (to arrive). But it is a play on words, not a rule, and customs differ from family to family.有人把它倒過來貼，因為「倒」和「到」同音，代表「福到」。不過這是諧音的趣味，不是規定；每一家的習慣不一樣。

In the Classroom · 教室提醒

Before you pick up a brush 拿起毛筆之前

  • Ink stains clothes and is very hard to wash out. Wear old clothes or an apron, and roll up your sleeves.墨沾到衣服很難洗掉：穿舊衣服或圍裙，袖子捲起來。
  • Cover the desk with old newspaper, and keep the ink in a heavy, steady dish so it will not tip over.桌上先鋪舊報紙；墨要裝在重一點、放得穩的碟子裡，免得打翻。
  • Press gently. Crushing the brush against the paper squashes the hairs and can split or break them.輕輕地按就好：把筆用力壓在紙上，筆毛會擠扁，也可能分岔或折斷。
  • Do not put the brush, the ink, or the ink stick in your mouth, and do not lick the brush tip to shape it.毛筆、墨汁、墨條都不要放進嘴裡，也不要用嘴巴舔筆尖。
  • After writing, wash the brush in clean water until the water runs clear, gently shape it into a point, and hang it tip-down or lay it flat to dry. Wash your hands, too.寫完後用清水洗筆，洗到水變清為止；輕輕把筆毛理成尖形，筆尖朝下掛起來或平放晾乾。也要洗手。

Classroom Activity 1 · 課堂活動一

Write a Diamond of Spring 寫一張「春」字斗方

You need · 準備材料

  • A square of red paper, about 20 to 30 cm on each side正方形的紅紙，邊長大約 20 到 30 公分
  • A large brush, ink, and old newspaper to cover the desk大一點的毛筆、墨汁，鋪桌子的舊報紙
  • Scrap paper for practice練習用的廢紙

Steps · 步驟

  1. Fold the square corner to corner both ways, then open it. The folds cross at the middle.把紅紙沿兩條對角線各摺一次再打開，摺痕交叉的地方就是正中間。
  2. Turn the paper so that one corner points up.把紙轉成一個角朝上。
  3. Practice 春 on scrap paper first, in the right stroke order. Use the practice pad on this page if you like.先在廢紙上照筆順練習「春」。也可以先用這一頁的練字板。
  4. Write 春 on the red paper, with the middle of the character on the cross. Let it dry flat.在紅紙上寫「春」，讓字的中心落在摺痕交叉的地方。寫完平放晾乾。

Load the brush well, but wipe off the extra ink first so it does not drip.
筆要蘸飽墨，但下筆前先把多餘的墨刮掉，才不會滴下來。

Classroom Activity 2 · 課堂活動二

The Classroom Door 教室的大門

You need · 準備材料

  • Six couplets from this page, each line on its own strip of paper這一頁的六副對聯，每一句寫在一張紙條上
  • Tape, and the classroom door膠帶，和教室的門

Steps · 步驟

  1. Mix up all twelve strips. Each pair of students takes one strip and finds the partner who holds the matching line.把十二張紙條混在一起。兩人一組拿一張，去找拿著另一句的那一組。
  2. Say the last characters out loud and decide which line is the upper line.把兩句的末字唸出來，決定哪一句是上聯。
  3. Stand facing the door and hold the two lines on the correct sides. The class checks.面對教室的門，把兩句舉在正確的兩邊，請全班檢查。
  4. Challenge: write your own pair of four-character lines in English or Chinese.挑戰：用英文或中文，自己寫一副四個字（或四個詞）的對句。

An English couplet can match too: “Flowers open, wealth arrives; bamboo speaks, peace stays.”
英文也可以對仗，例如把「花開富貴，竹報平安」譯成兩句字數相當的句子。

Dates, names, places, and facts on this page were checked against these sources (October 2026). · 本頁的年代、人名、地名與事實依下列資料查證（2026 年 10 月）。

  1. Spring couplets: lucky lines written on red paper at the Lunar New Year and put up on both sides of the door春聯：「農曆新年時，以紅紙書寫吉祥的聯語，張貼在門的兩旁。」教育部《重編國語辭典修訂本》「春聯」 ↗
  2. Couplet: two lines with the same number of characters whose words match對聯：「字數相同，用字互相對仗的兩個句子。」上聯：「對聯的前一半。」橫批：「與對聯搭配的橫幅。」教育部《重編國語辭典修訂本》「對聯」 ↗
  3. Peach-wood charms and the two door gods; “Spring couplets are peach-wood charms”桃符：「新年時於門旁設兩塊桃木板，上面書寫二神之名或畫上其圖像，用以驅鬼避邪」；《燕京歲時記》：「春聯者，即桃符也。」教育部《重編國語辭典修訂本》「桃符」 ↗
  4. Diamond paper (doufang): a square paper for lucky words, pasted on the door斗方：「用以書寫吉祥文字，貼在門上的方形紙。」教育部《重編國語辭典修訂本》「斗方」 ↗
  5. Level and oblique tones: level is the level tone; oblique is the rising, departing, and entering tones平仄：「平，四聲中的平聲。仄，四聲中的上、去、入三聲。」平聲：「今國音分化為陰平、陽平二類，即第一聲、第二聲。」教育部《重編國語辭典修訂本》「平仄」 ↗
  6. The entering tone no longer exists in Mandarin; its characters are spread across the four tones入聲：「現國音中已無入聲，入聲字分別歸入陰平、陽平、上聲、去聲四聲之中。」教育部《重編國語辭典修訂本》「入聲」 ↗
  7. Meng Chang wrote his own lines on the peach boards: 新年納餘慶，喜節號長春「每歲除，命學士為詞，題桃符，置寢門左右。末年，學士幸寅遜撰詞，昶以其非工，自命筆題云：『新年納餘慶，喜節號長春。』」維基文庫《宋史》卷四百七十九 ↗
  8. Ji Yun: Meng Chang's pair is the oldest; the story of the first Ming emperor's order, quoted from a Qing book of anecdotes「楹帖始於桃符，蜀孟昶『餘慶』『長春』一聯最古」；引《簪雲樓雜說》：「春聯之設，自明孝陵昉也…於除夕忽傳旨：『公卿士庶家，門上須加春聯一副。』」維基文庫 梁章鉅《楹聯叢話》卷一 ↗
  9. Upper line ends in an oblique tone, lower line in a level tone「對聯必有一上聯(或稱右聯)與一下聯(左聯)」「上聯最後一字必為仄聲，下聯最後一字必為平聲，更可以此分辨上下聯，以免左右掛反」臺北市立麗山高中 徐茂瑋〈閒話對聯〉 ↗

  11. Turning 福 or 春 upside down for the pun “has arrived”; Meng Chang's lines called the earliest spring couplet「更有人把這些字都倒過來貼，代表『福到』、『春到』…」農業部兒童網〈春聯〉 ↗
