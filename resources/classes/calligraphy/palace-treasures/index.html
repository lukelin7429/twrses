Chinese Calligraphy · Unit 3 · Lesson 10 · 書法 單元三 第十課

What Treasures of Calligraphy Can You See in Taiwan?故宮裡的書法國寶

A letter about snow, a draft full of grief, a monk's wild cursive, a book about writing, and two poems from exile: five National Treasures of calligraphy are kept in Taipei. Walk through a 3D gallery, find out why the lights are so low, match details to the works, and look back over all ten lessons.
一封談雪的短信、一篇滿紙悲傷的草稿、一位和尚的狂草、一卷談寫字的書、兩首貶謫中寫的詩——五件書法國寶就收藏在台北。走進 3D 展廳，看看燈光為什麼這麼暗，用局部圖配對作品，再回顧這十堂課。

Big idea · 一句話看懂

Some of the most famous works of Chinese calligraphy are kept in the National Palace Museum in Taipei, and many are National Treasures. Paper and silk are harmed by light, and the harm adds up, so the museum keeps the lights low, shows each work for only a short time, and then lets it rest.
許多最有名的書法作品收藏在台北的國立故宮博物院，其中不少是國寶。紙和絹怕光，而且光造成的傷害會累積，所以博物館把燈光調暗、每件作品只展出很短的時間，展完還要讓它休息。

Unit 10: What Treasures of Calligraphy Can You See in Taiwan?

Where can you see the real writing of the masters in this series? One answer is Taipei. The National Palace Museum there keeps more than 3,700 works of calligraphy, and some of them are among the most famous in the world.

這個系列裡的書法家，他們真正寫的字要到哪裡看？其中一個答案是台北。台北的國立故宮博物院收藏了三千七百多件書法作品，有些是全世界最有名的。

The museum began in 1925 in the old palace in Beijing. In 1933, as war came closer, more than thirteen thousand crates of its treasures were sent south for safety. In 1948 and 1949, a selection of the finest pieces was shipped to Taiwan, and in 1965 a new museum building opened in Taipei.

故宮博物院 1925 年在北京的紫禁城成立。1933 年戰火逼近，一萬多箱文物被運到南方避難。1948、1949 年，其中挑選出來的精品用船運到台灣；1965 年，台北的新館落成開放。

This lesson looks at five works. Two were written by people you have already met: a short letter by Wang Xizhi, kept as a fine Tang dynasty copy, and Yan Zhenqing's draft for his nephew. Two are in cursive script: Sun Guoting's long scroll about how to write, and the wild cursive of the monk Huaisu. The last is by the Song dynasty poet Su Shi.

這一課看五件作品。其中兩件的作者你已經認識：王羲之的一封短信（留下來的是唐代的精摹本），和顏真卿為姪子寫的草稿。兩件是草書：孫過庭談「怎麼寫字」的長卷，和和尚懷素的狂草。最後一件是宋朝詩人蘇軾寫的。

In 1080 Su Shi was sent away from the capital to live in Huangzhou. During his third Cold Food Festival there, in 1082, it rained and rained, and he wrote two sad poems. On the scroll, the characters begin small and grow larger and heavier as his feelings rise. The lower edge of the scroll is marked by fire. It was once taken to Japan, and the museum bought it in 1987.

元豐三年（1080），蘇軾被貶離京城，到黃州居住。元豐五年（1082）他在那裡過第三個寒食節，雨下個不停，他寫了兩首悲傷的詩。在這卷字上，字一開始比較小，隨著情緒越來越激動，越寫越大、越寫越重。卷子的下緣有火燒過的痕跡。它曾經流落到日本，1987 年由故宮買回。

You cannot see these works every day. Paper and silk are fragile, and light harms them a little at a time. The harm adds up and cannot be undone. So the museum keeps paintings and calligraphy at no more than 50 lux, which is far dimmer than daylight. Its most protected works may be shown for no more than 42 days, and then they rest for at least three years.

這些作品不是天天看得到。紙和絹很脆弱，光會一點一點地傷害它們；傷害會累積，而且無法復原。所以故宮規定書畫的照度不高於 50 lux——比白天的日光暗得多。最需要保護的那些「限展」書畫，一次最多展出 42 天，展完至少要休息三年。

All five are National Treasures. After ten lessons, you can read them in a new way: you know the scripts, the strokes, and how a brush makes thick and thin lines. If you visit, walk slowly, look closely, and never use a flash.

這五件都是國寶。上完十堂課，你可以用新的眼光來看它們：你認得字體、認得筆畫，也知道毛筆怎麼寫出粗細。如果去參觀，請慢慢走、仔細看，而且絕對不要開閃光燈。

A Journey · 流傳

The scroll that survived fire逃過火災的〈寒食帖〉

Later owners wrote the scroll's story on the scroll itself. One note says it was marked by fire when the Summer Palace was burned in 1860. It later went to Japan, where its owner carried it out of the fires after the great Tokyo earthquake of 1923. After the Second World War it was bought back, and in 1987 the museum bought it.
後來的收藏者把這卷字的遭遇寫在卷子後面。一則題跋說，1860 年圓明園被焚時它留下了燒痕。後來它流落到日本；1923 年關東大地震引起大火，收藏者冒險把它搶救出來。第二次世界大戰後它被買回，1987 年由故宮收購。

Practice · 苦練

Ten thousand banana plants懷素種芭蕉

A Tang dynasty life of Huaisu says he was too poor to buy paper, so he planted more than ten thousand banana plants at home to write on. When that was not enough, he wrote on a lacquered tray and a lacquered board, over and over, until he wore them through.
唐朝陸羽寫的〈僧懷素傳〉說：懷素「貧無紙可書，嘗於故里種芭蕉萬餘株，以供揮灑。書不足，乃漆一盤書之，又漆一方板，書至再三，盤板皆穿。」——沒有紙，就種芭蕉來寫；還不夠，就寫在漆盤和漆板上，寫到都磨穿了。

An Emperor · 皇帝

The emperor's favorite乾隆最愛的一封信

The Qianlong Emperor treasured Wang Xizhi's snow letter. The museum notes that he praised it as “matchless under heaven, rarely equaled in any age,” and kept it with two other works as his “Three Rarities.” You can still see his writing and seals all around the four lines.
乾隆皇帝非常珍愛〈快雪時晴帖〉。故宮說他「譽之為『天下無雙，古今鮮對』」，並把它和另外兩件作品合稱「三希」。直到今天，那四行字的周圍還看得到他題的字和蓋的印章。

Taiwan · 台灣

Before you visit去故宮之前

The museum is at No. 221, Section 2, Zhishan Road, Shilin District, Taipei. These works are not always on show, so check the museum's website first. Flash, extra lights, tripods, and selfie sticks are not allowed. The galleries are kept at 20 to 24 degrees Celsius, so bring a jacket.
故宮在臺北市士林區至善路二段 221 號。這些作品不是隨時都在展出，出發前先上故宮的網站查。展場拍照「禁止使用閃光燈、補光器材、腳架、自拍器」。展廳溫度維持在攝氏 20 到 24 度，記得帶件外套。

Myth vs. Fact · 常見迷思

Four things people get wrong 四個常見的誤會

✗ Myth · 迷思The museum's most famous works are always on show.故宮最有名的作品隨時都看得到。

✓ Fact · 事實Most paintings and calligraphy are shown for no more than three months and then rest for at least eighteen months. The seventy most protected works are shown for no more than 42 days at a time.一般書畫「展出期間以不超過三個月為原則，每次展出後須歸庫養護至少十八個月」；七十件限展書畫精華，一次最多展 42 天。

✗ Myth · 迷思Timely Clearing After Snowfall is in Wang Xizhi's own hand.〈快雪時晴帖〉是王羲之親筆寫的。

✓ Fact · 事實The museum describes it as a fine tracing copy made in the Tang dynasty. As Lesson 8 told, the original of his Lanting Xu is lost too.故宮的說明是「本幅為唐代精摹本」。第八課說過，他的〈蘭亭序〉原蹟也已經失傳。

✗ Myth · 迷思Ancient critics ranked the Cold Food scroll “the third-best running script under heaven.”古人把〈寒食帖〉評為「天下第三行書」。

✓ Fact · 事實We could not find any old source for that ranking. What the records do have is the Ming dynasty artist Dong Qichang, who called it the best of Su Shi's writing. Grouping it with the Lanting Xu and Yan Zhenqing's draft as the three great works of running script is a later habit.這個排名找不到古人的出處。有記錄的是明朝董其昌稱它為「蘇書甲觀」。把它和〈蘭亭序〉、〈祭姪文稿〉合稱三大行書，是後人的說法。

✗ Myth · 迷思The lights are low because black ink fades quickly.燈光暗，是因為黑色的墨很容易褪色。

✓ Fact · 事實Carbon ink is one of the materials least harmed by light. What light harms more are the paper, the silk, and their colors. That is why our test strip in the model uses dyes, not ink.碳做的墨是最不怕光的材料之一。比較怕光的是紙、絹和上面的顏色。所以模型裡的試紙用的是染料，不是墨。

In the Classroom · 教室提醒

Before you pick up a brush 拿起毛筆之前

  • Ink stains clothes and is very hard to wash out. Wear old clothes or an apron, and roll up your sleeves.墨沾到衣服很難洗掉：穿舊衣服或圍裙，袖子捲起來。
  • Cover the desk with old newspaper, and keep the ink in a heavy, steady dish so it will not tip over.桌上先鋪舊報紙；墨要裝在重一點、放得穩的碟子裡，免得打翻。
  • Press gently. Crushing the brush against the paper squashes the hairs and can split or break them.輕輕地按就好：把筆用力壓在紙上，筆毛會擠扁，也可能分岔或折斷。
  • Do not put the brush, the ink, or the ink stick in your mouth, and do not lick the brush tip to shape it.毛筆、墨汁、墨條都不要放進嘴裡，也不要用嘴巴舔筆尖。
  • After writing, wash the brush in clean water until the water runs clear, gently shape it into a point, and hang it tip-down or lay it flat to dry. Wash your hands, too.寫完後用清水洗筆，洗到水變清為止；輕輕把筆毛理成尖形，筆尖朝下掛起來或平放晾乾。也要洗手。

Classroom Activity 1 · 課堂活動一

The Sunlight Test 陽光實驗

You need · 準備材料

  • Two sheets of colored construction paper (the same color)兩張同樣顏色的色紙
  • A piece of thick card and some tape一塊厚紙板和膠帶
  • A sunny windowsill and a drawer曬得到太陽的窗台，和一個抽屜

Steps · 步驟

  1. Tape the card over half of one sheet and put it on the sunny windowsill.用厚紙板蓋住其中一張色紙的一半，放在窗台上曬太陽。
  2. Put the other sheet in a dark drawer.另一張放進黑暗的抽屜。
  3. After two weeks, take off the card and compare the three: sunlit, covered, and drawer.兩個星期後拿掉紙板，比較三個地方：曬到太陽的、被蓋住的、抽屜裡的。
  4. Talk about it: which museum rules does this explain?討論：這個實驗可以解釋博物館的哪些規定？

Take a photo on the first day so you can compare.
第一天先拍一張照片，之後才好比較。

Classroom Activity 2 · 課堂活動二

Be a Curator 我是策展人

You need · 準備材料

  • This page's five works, on a screen or printed這一頁的五件作品（用螢幕看或印出來）
  • Cards and pens for labels寫說明牌的卡片和筆

Steps · 步驟

  1. Each group chooses one of the five works.每組從五件作品裡選一件。
  2. Write a museum label in English: who, when, which script, and one sentence about why it is special.用英文寫一張說明牌：誰寫的、什麼時候、哪一種字體，再用一句話說它特別的地方。
  3. Decide how long your work may be on show, and how you will protect it from light.決定你的作品可以展多久，以及要怎麼保護它不被光傷害。
  4. Hold a five-work exhibition and give a one-minute tour.辦一場五件作品的小展覽，每組導覽一分鐘。

Good labels are short. Try to use fewer than forty words.
好的說明牌很短。試試看不超過四十個英文字。

  9. 9Strong Muscle, Hard Bone: Why Does Every Calligrapher's Writing Look Different?顏筋柳骨：為什麼每個書法家的字都不一樣？Yan's muscle and Liu's bone: same strokes, different pressure.顏筋柳骨：筆畫一樣，輕重不同。
  10. 10What Treasures of Calligraphy Can You See in Taiwan?故宮裡的書法國寶The treasures are in Taipei, resting in dim light.國寶就在台北，在暗暗的燈光下休息。
