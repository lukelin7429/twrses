Big idea · 一句話看懂

Stroke order is a shared agreement about which stroke comes first. Taiwan's Ministry of Education sums it up in 17 basic rules, such as left to right, top to bottom, and horizontal before vertical. When everyone follows the same order, writing is easier to teach and to learn, and it follows the flow of the brush from one stroke into the next.
筆順是大家約好的「先寫哪一筆」。教育部把它歸納成 17 條基本法則，例如先左後右、先上後下、先橫後豎。大家照同一套筆順寫，教和學都容易，也順著毛筆從這一筆走到下一筆的筆勢。

Unit 4: Why Do We Write Strokes in Order?

Stroke order is simply the order in which the strokes of a character are written. In Taiwan, the Ministry of Education sets a standard order for thousands of characters. Its handbook of standard characters sums up the order in 17 basic rules, and its stroke-order website, first opened in 2008, now shows animations for 6,063 characters that anyone can look up.

筆順，就是一個字的筆畫先後書寫的順序。在台灣，教育部為幾千個國字訂出標準筆順：《常用國字標準字體筆順手冊》把筆順歸納成 17 條基本法則；教育部的筆順學習網 2008 年開始試用，現在有 6,063 個國字的筆順動畫，任何人都能上網查。

Six of the rules are easy to see. Write from left to right, as in 川 (river), and from top to bottom, as in 三 (three). Write a horizontal stroke before a vertical stroke that crosses it, as in 十 (ten), and a left-falling stroke before a right-falling stroke that meets it, as in 人 (person). When a vertical stroke stands alone in the middle, like the one in 小 (small), write it first. For a box like 日 (sun), write the outside, then the inside, and close the bottom last.

其中有六條很容易看懂：從左寫到右，像「川」；從上寫到下，像「三」；橫和豎相交時先寫橫，像「十」；撇和捺相接時先撇後捺，像「人」；中間的豎不和其他筆畫相交時先寫，像「小」；四面圍起來的字，先寫外圍、再寫裡面、最後封口，像「日」。

If the finished character looks the same, why does the order matter? First, it is shared. Teachers, textbooks, and the ministry's website all teach the same order, so everyone can learn from everyone else. Second, the order follows the flow of the brush. The ministry explains that the second stroke of 日 ends with a small hook because the brush is already turning toward the next stroke inside the box.

寫出來的字看起來一樣，為什麼還要管筆順？第一，它是共同的標準：老師、課本和教育部的網站都教同一套筆順，大家可以互相學。第二，筆順順著毛筆的筆勢。教育部就解釋過：「日」的第二筆最後帶一個小鉤，是因為毛筆已經轉向，準備寫框裡的下一筆。

Order also helps with structure. In 日, writing the middle stroke before the bottom line lets you see the space you are dividing, and the bottom line closes the box neatly at the end. In 三, writing from the top down lets you compare each line with the one above it, so you can make the middle one short and the bottom one long.

筆順也幫助結構。寫「日」時，先寫中間的橫、最後才寫底下的橫，就看得見要分割的空間，最後再整齊地封口。寫「三」時，從上往下寫，每一橫都能和上面那一橫比一比，才能讓中間最短、最下面最長。

Practice paper helps, too. A nine-square grid divides each square into nine smaller squares, like the character 井, so you can check where each stroke begins and ends. The 田 grid and the 米 grid do the same job. Long ago, children started school by tracing red characters in a copybook that began 上大人, 孔乙己, chosen because the characters have very few strokes.

練習用的格子也有幫助。九宮格用「井」字形把一大格分成九小格，方便看每一筆從哪裡起、到哪裡收；田字格、米字格的功用也一樣。從前的孩子入學時，用描紅的習字簿練字，第一頁就是「上大人，孔乙己」，因為這些字的筆畫很少。

Questions · 閱讀理解

What is stroke order, and who sets the standard order in Taiwan?

It is the order in which the strokes of a character are written. In Taiwan, the Ministry of Education sets the standard order.

Name two of the rules in this lesson, with an example character for each.

For example: left to right, as in 川; top to bottom, as in 三; horizontal before vertical, as in 十; left-falling before right-falling, as in 人; the middle vertical first, as in 小; outside, inside, then close, as in 日.

Why is the bottom line of 日 written last?

Because the rule for boxes is outside first, then inside, then the bottom line to close the box. Closing it last leaves room to place the inside stroke well.

Stories of Stroke Order · 筆順的故事

Seventeen rules, a tiny hook, a grid like 井, and the first page of an old copybook 十七條法則、一個小鉤、井字格，和舊習字簿的第一頁

Four short stories about how people learn to write in order.
關於「照順序寫字」的四個小故事。

The Standard · 標準

Seventeen rules and a website十七條法則與一個網站

Taiwan's Ministry of Education sums up stroke order in 17 basic rules in its handbook of standard characters. Its stroke-order website opened for trial use in 2008 with the 4,808 common characters. In 2020 it grew to 6,057 characters and dropped the word “common” from its name, and in 2024 it reached 6,063 characters.
教育部在《常用國字標準字體筆順手冊》裡，把筆順歸納成 17 條基本法則。教育部的筆順學習網 2008 年試用，收錄 4,808 個常用字；2020 年擴充到 6,057 字，網站名稱也拿掉了「常用」兩個字；2024 年再擴充到 6,063 字。

A Tiny Hook · 小鉤

Why 日 has a hook and 口 does not「日」為什麼有鉤，「口」沒有？

The ministry's website answers this question. In 口 the second stroke turns without a hook and leans inward, ready to meet the closing stroke, because there is nothing inside. In 日 the second stroke ends with a small hook, because the brush is already turning toward the next stroke inside the box. The hook shows the flow of the brush.
教育部的網站回答過這個問題：「口」裡面沒有其他筆畫，第二筆是「橫折」不帶鉤，折筆往內斜，準備接最後一筆封口；「日」框裡還有筆畫，第二筆是「橫折鉤」，鉤起來是因為毛筆已經準備寫框裡的下一筆，是筆勢使然。

Practice Grids · 格子

A grid shaped like 井井字格：九宮格

The ministry's dictionary describes the nine-square grid as practice paper for copying models: each large square is divided like the character 井 into nine smaller squares, so learners can match the model and place every dot and line. The 田 grid and the 米 grid do the same job.
教育部辭典說，九宮格是臨帖用的界格紙：每一大格用「井」字形分成九小格，方便臨摹時對照範本字形、掌握點畫的部位。另有田字格、米字格，功用相當。

An Old Copybook · 習字簿

上大人, 孔乙己: the first page上大人，孔乙己

Long ago, children starting school traced red characters in a copybook that began 上大人，孔乙己，化三千，七十士. These characters were chosen because they have very few strokes. Tracing red models like this is called 描紅, the same idea as the faint red characters on this lesson's practice pad.
從前學童入學時，用描紅的習字簿練字，上面寫著「上大人，孔乙己，化三千，七十士」，因為這些字筆畫簡少，方便學習。照著紅色範字一筆一筆描，就叫「描紅」，和這一課練字板上淡紅色的範字是同一個道理。

Myth vs. Fact · 常見迷思

Four things people get wrong 四個常見的誤會

✗ Myth · 迷思If the finished character looks right, stroke order does not matter.字寫出來看起來對，筆順就不重要。

✓ Fact · 事實The result can look similar, but stroke order is a shared standard that teachers, textbooks, and the ministry's website all use. It also follows the flow of the brush, and later, when you write faster, strokes begin to join in the order you write them.寫出來可能看起來差不多，但筆順是老師、課本和教育部網站共同使用的標準；它也順著毛筆的筆勢；以後寫得快的時候，筆畫會照書寫的順序連起來。

✗ Myth · 迷思Stroke order is just one rule: top to bottom, left to right.筆順只有一條規則：由上而下、由左而右。

✓ Fact · 事實The ministry lists 17 basic rules. Some go against “top to bottom” at first glance: in 小 the middle vertical comes first, and in 日 the bottom line comes last.教育部列了 17 條基本法則。有的乍看不是「由上而下」：「小」要先寫中間的豎，「日」的底下那一橫最後寫。

✗ Myth · 迷思The box of 日 is drawn as one stroke.「日」外面的框是一筆畫完的。

✓ Fact · 事實The box takes three strokes: the left vertical, the top and right side in one turning stroke, and the bottom line, which is written last to close the box.外框要三筆：左邊的豎、一筆寫上面和右邊的橫折鉤，還有最後才寫、用來封口的底下那一橫。

✗ Myth · 迷思Stroke order only matters for brush calligraphy.只有寫毛筆字才需要筆順。

✓ Fact · 事實The standard order belongs to the character, not to the tool, so it is the same for pencils and pens. In Taiwan's curriculum for grades 3 and 4, children write mainly with pencils and pens, with the brush as a supplement.標準筆順跟著字走，不跟著筆走，鉛筆、原子筆也一樣。台灣國語文課綱第二學習階段（三、四年級）的寫字，以硬筆為主、毛筆為輔。

In the Classroom · 教室提醒

Before you pick up a brush 拿起毛筆之前

  • Ink stains clothes and is very hard to wash out. Wear old clothes or an apron, and roll up your sleeves.墨沾到衣服很難洗掉：穿舊衣服或圍裙，袖子捲起來。
  • Cover the desk with old newspaper, and keep the ink in a heavy, steady dish so it will not tip over.桌上先鋪舊報紙；墨要裝在重一點、放得穩的碟子裡，免得打翻。
  • Press gently. Crushing the brush against the paper squashes the hairs and can split or break them.輕輕地按就好：把筆用力壓在紙上，筆毛會擠扁，也可能分岔或折斷。
  • Do not put the brush, the ink, or the ink stick in your mouth, and do not lick the brush tip to shape it.毛筆、墨汁、墨條都不要放進嘴裡，也不要用嘴巴舔筆尖。
  • After writing, wash the brush in clean water until the water runs clear, gently shape it into a point, and hang it tip-down or lay it flat to dry. Wash your hands, too.寫完後用清水洗筆，洗到水變清為止；輕輕把筆毛理成尖形，筆尖朝下掛起來或平放晾乾。也要洗手。

Classroom Activity 1 · 課堂活動一

Writing in the Air 空中寫字

You need · 準備材料

  • Enough space for everyone to stretch out an arm每個人都能伸直手臂的空間
  • The six example characters on the board: 川、三、十、人、小、日黑板上寫好六個例字：川、三、十、人、小、日
  • Later: brushes, water, and old newspaper之後用：毛筆、清水、舊報紙

Steps · 步驟

  1. Everyone stands and writes a character in the air with the whole arm, as big as possible, counting the strokes out loud: one, two, three.全班站起來，用整隻手臂在空中把字寫得越大越好，一邊大聲數筆順：一、二、三。
  2. The teacher watches for anyone moving in a different order and stops to ask which rule applies.老師看誰的順序不一樣，停下來問：這個字用的是哪一條法則？
  3. Repeat with your eyes closed. Can you still feel where each stroke starts?閉上眼睛再寫一次：還感覺得到每一筆從哪裡開始嗎？
  4. Finish by writing the same characters with water on newspaper, saying the numbers as you go.最後用清水在報紙上寫同樣的字，邊寫邊唸數字。

Big arm movements make the order easy for the teacher to see, and students remember movements better than lists.
手臂動作大，老師一眼就看得出順序；而且動作比條列的規則更容易記住。

Classroom Activity 2 · 課堂活動二

Stroke-Order Relay 筆順接力

You need · 準備材料

  • A large sheet of paper with a nine-square grid for each team, taped to the wall每組一大張畫好九宮格的紙，貼在牆上
  • A thick marker or a brush with water for each team每組一枝粗的麥克筆，或一枝蘸清水的毛筆
  • A tablet or computer with the Ministry of Education's stroke-order website一台能開教育部筆順學習網的平板或電腦

Steps · 步驟

  1. The teacher names a character. The first student in each team runs up and writes only the first stroke.老師說一個字，每組第一位同學跑上前，只寫第一筆。
  2. The next student writes the second stroke, and so on, until the character is finished.下一位寫第二筆，依序接力，直到把字寫完。
  3. Check the order with the stroke-order animation on the ministry's website. Each correct stroke earns a point.用教育部筆順學習網的動畫核對順序，每一筆寫對得一分。
  4. Talk about the strokes that teams got wrong. Which rule would have helped?討論大家寫錯的那幾筆：哪一條法則能幫上忙？

Start with the six characters from this lesson, then try 田 and 山 from the ministry's examples.
先從這一課的六個例字開始，再試試教育部例字裡的「田」和「山」。
