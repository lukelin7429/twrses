Why Do People Copy Sutras by Hand?抄經：書法與修行

Before printing, every book was copied by hand, and Buddhist texts called sutras were copied with special care. People still copy them today, slowly, one character in each square. Watch a brush copy a line of the Heart Sutra in 3D, compare slow writing with hurried writing, find the right square, and copy five characters yourself.
還沒有印刷術的時候，每一本書都是用手抄的；佛教的經典抄得特別用心。直到今天還有很多人抄經：慢慢地，一格一個字。在 3D 裡看毛筆抄〈心經〉的一句，比較「慢而勻」和「趕著寫」，找出下一個字該寫的格子，再自己抄五個字。

Big idea · 一句話看懂

To copy a sutra is to write out a Buddhist text by hand, slowly and carefully, one character in each square, from top to bottom and from right to left. Long ago this was how books were made. Today many people still do it, as a way to show respect and to practice paying attention.
抄經就是用手把佛教的經典一個字一個字抄下來：慢慢地、仔細地，一格一個字，由上到下、由右到左。很久以前，書就是這樣做出來的。今天還有很多人抄經，用來表達恭敬，也練習專注。

Unit 13: Why Do People Copy Sutras by Hand?

Imagine a world with no printers and no copy machines. If you wanted a book, someone had to write out every word by hand. For hundreds of years, that is how the Buddhist texts called sutras were passed on. Trained copyists wrote them on long rolls of paper with ruled columns, one small, neat character after another.

想像一個沒有印表機、也沒有影印機的世界。想要一本書，就得有人用手把每一個字抄下來。好幾百年裡，佛教的經典就是這樣流傳的。受過訓練的抄寫人（經生）把經文寫在畫了直行界線的長卷上，一個接一個，又小又整齊。

Even after printing was invented, people went on copying sutras by hand, and many still do. The sutras themselves speak of writing them out as a good thing to do. For the people who copy, it is a way to show respect for the words, and a way to practice paying attention.

印刷術發明以後，人們還是繼續用手抄經，直到今天。經典裡就把「書寫」經文說成一件值得做的事。對抄經的人來說，這是對經文表達恭敬的方式，也是練習專注的方法。

The rules are simple. Sit up straight. Write one character in each square. Start at the top of the column on the right, write down to the bottom, and then move one column to the left. Do not hurry. Try to make every character the same size, in the middle of its square.

規矩很簡單。坐正。一格寫一個字。從最右邊那一行的最上面開始，往下寫到底，再往左換一行。不要趕。每個字盡量一樣大，寫在格子的正中間。

The most copied text is probably the Heart Sutra. It is very short: the translation by the Tang dynasty monk Xuanzang has only 260 characters. Its best-known line has eight characters: 色即是空，空即是色, “form is emptiness, emptiness is form.” People have thought about that line for more than a thousand years. In this lesson, we simply write it.

最多人抄的大概是〈心經〉。它非常短：唐朝玄奘法師翻譯的本子只有 260 個字。其中最有名的一句是八個字：「色即是空，空即是色。」一千多年來，人們一直在思考這句話。這一課，我們只是把它寫下來。

One famous copyist was Master Hongyi. Before he became a monk in 1918, he was an art and music teacher named Li Shutong. As a monk he gave up most of his arts, but he kept on writing sutras and short sayings to give to people. He said that what his writing shows is calm and quiet.

弘一大師是很有名的一位抄經人。1918 年出家以前，他叫李叔同，是美術和音樂老師。出家以後，他放下了大部分的藝術，卻繼續寫經、寫字送給人結緣。他說自己的字要表現的是平淡、恬靜。

You do not have to be Buddhist to try the method. Monks in old Europe copied books by hand too, and so did scribes in many other traditions. Copying any text you care about, slowly and neatly, is good practice for the hand and for the mind.

不是佛教徒，也可以試試這個方法。古時候歐洲的修士同樣用手抄書，許多別的傳統也有抄寫經典的人。把一段你珍惜的文字慢慢地、整齊地抄下來，對手和對心都是很好的練習。

Questions · 閱讀理解

Before printing, how were sutras passed on?

Copyists wrote them out by hand on long rolls of paper with ruled columns.

On a sutra page, where does the first character go, and which way does the writing move?

At the top of the column on the right. The writing goes down each column, and the columns go from right to left.

What did Master Hongyi keep doing after he became a monk?

He kept on writing sutras and short sayings to give to people.

History and Taiwan · 歷史與台灣

Where the custom comes from, and where you can meet it today.
這個習俗從哪裡來，今天又在哪裡遇得到。

Before Printing · 印刷以前

A job called “sutra copyist”一種叫「經生」的工作

A study from National Taiwan University explains that before printing was invented, old books all depended on clerks and copyists, and that Buddhist sutra copyists in the Northern Wei period were called jingsheng. The Diamond Sutra itself praises “writing out, keeping, reading, and reciting” the text.
臺大的一篇研究說：「印刷術發明以前，古代的典籍都有賴書吏或經生抄寫」，佛教的寫經生在北魏時期就叫「經生」。《金剛經》裡也稱讚「書寫、受持、讀誦、為人解說」這部經。

Dunhuang · 敦煌

A cave full of hand-copied scrolls裝滿寫本的石室

The dictionary records that a stone chamber at Dunhuang, on the old Silk Road, was found in 1900. Inside were a great many Buddhist texts hand-copied from the Six Dynasties to the Five Dynasties. Many were taken abroad. Taiwan's National Central Library keeps 142 Dunhuang items today.
教育部辭典記載，絲路上的敦煌石室在清光緒二十六年（1900）被發現，裡面「藏六朝、隋、唐及五代人手寫佛教經典及其他藝術品甚富」，後來許多被帶到國外。今天台灣的國家圖書館藏有敦煌文獻 142 號。

A Monk · 弘一大師

From Li Shutong to Master Hongyi從李叔同到弘一

Li Shutong (1880–1942) taught art and music. In 1918 he became a monk at Hupao Temple in Hangzhou and took the name Hongyi. The record of his life kept by a society in Taiwan lists sutra after sutra that he copied, and says that in his last year he was still writing for students. His last four characters were 悲欣交集, “sorrow and joy together.”
李叔同（1880–1942）原本是美術和音樂老師。民國七年（1918）他在杭州虎跑寺出家，號弘一。弘一大師紀念學會的年表記著他抄過一部又一部的經，直到最後一年還「寫字與晉江中學學生結緣」。他最後寫的四個字是「悲欣交集」。

Taiwan · 台灣

Sutras in the museum, and rooms for copying故宮的寫經，和抄經堂

The National Palace Museum keeps a hand-copied scroll labeled “sutra scroll by a Tang copyist”; the museum notes its seventeen characters to a column, the same layout as Dunhuang sutras. Some temples in Taiwan have quiet rooms for copying. One describes the practice as writing out a sutra with the most respectful heart: sit quietly first, and then copy with a calm mind.
台北故宮藏有一卷〈唐人寫經〉；館方指出它「每行書寫十七字」，「與敦煌寫經格式相通」。台灣有些道場設有安靜的抄經堂。佛光山這樣介紹：抄經「即是用最虔敬的心來抄寫佛經」，先靜坐，再「靜心虔誠抄經」。

Myth vs. Fact · 常見迷思

Four things people get wrong 四個常見的誤會

✗ Myth · 迷思Copying a sutra is a handwriting contest: the prettiest page wins.抄經是比賽誰的字漂亮。

✓ Fact · 事實The point is care and attention, not beauty. Neat, even, and complete matters more than fancy.重點是用心和專注，不是漂亮。工整、平均、不漏字，比花俏重要。

✗ Myth · 迷思A sutra page is written from left to right, like this sentence.抄經和這行字一樣，由左寫到右。

✓ Fact · 事實It is written in columns, from top to bottom, and the columns go from right to left.抄經是直的寫：每一行由上到下，行由右到左。

✗ Myth · 迷思The Heart Sutra is a very long book.〈心經〉是一部很長的書。

✓ Fact · 事實It is one of the shortest sutras. We counted the main text in Xuanzang's translation: 260 characters. The dictionary says it has “only a few hundred characters.”它是最短的經典之一。我們數過玄奘譯本的正文：260 個字。教育部辭典也說它「僅數百字」。

✗ Myth · 迷思Only Buddhists copied holy books by hand.只有佛教徒才用手抄經典。

✓ Fact · 事實Many traditions did. Monks in medieval Europe copied books in a writing room called a scriptorium, Jewish scribes write Torah scrolls with a quill on parchment, and for most of Islamic history the Qur'an was kept in manuscript.許多傳統都有。中世紀歐洲的修士在「抄寫室」裡抄書；猶太教的抄寫人用羽毛筆在羊皮紙上寫妥拉；伊斯蘭教的古蘭經，在大部分的歷史裡也是靠手抄本流傳。

In the Classroom · 教室提醒

Before you pick up a brush 拿起毛筆之前

  • Ink stains clothes and is very hard to wash out. Wear old clothes or an apron, and roll up your sleeves.墨沾到衣服很難洗掉：穿舊衣服或圍裙，袖子捲起來。
  • Cover the desk with old newspaper, and keep the ink in a heavy, steady dish so it will not tip over.桌上先鋪舊報紙；墨要裝在重一點、放得穩的碟子裡，免得打翻。
  • Press gently. Crushing the brush against the paper squashes the hairs and can split or break them.輕輕地按就好：把筆用力壓在紙上，筆毛會擠扁，也可能分岔或折斷。
  • Do not put the brush, the ink, or the ink stick in your mouth, and do not lick the brush tip to shape it.毛筆、墨汁、墨條都不要放進嘴裡，也不要用嘴巴舔筆尖。
  • After writing, wash the brush in clean water until the water runs clear, gently shape it into a point, and hang it tip-down or lay it flat to dry. Wash your hands, too.寫完後用清水洗筆，洗到水變清為止；輕輕把筆毛理成尖形，筆尖朝下掛起來或平放晾乾。也要洗手。

Classroom Activity 1 · 課堂活動一

Five Quiet Minutes 安靜的五分鐘

You need · 準備材料

  • Squared paper (squares of about 1.5 cm) and a pencil or a fine brush pen方格紙（每格大約 1.5 公分）和鉛筆或小楷筆
  • A short text: this lesson's eight characters, or a line the class chooses一段短短的文字：這一課的八個字，或全班選的一句話
  • A timer計時器

Steps · 步驟

  1. Sit up straight, feet on the floor. Take three slow breaths.坐正，腳踩在地上。慢慢呼吸三次。
  2. For five minutes, copy the text without talking: one character in each square, starting at the top right.五分鐘內不說話，把那段文字抄下來：一格一個字，從右上角開始。
  3. When the timer rings, stop, even in the middle of a line.計時器響了就停，就算一行還沒寫完。
  4. Look at your page. Are the first character and the last character the same size? How did the five minutes feel?看看自己的那一頁。第一個字和最後一個字一樣大嗎？這五分鐘感覺怎麼樣？

Any respectful text works: a poem, a class promise, or a favorite sentence. Students should feel free to choose.
抄什麼都可以，只要是值得珍惜的文字：一首詩、班級公約、一句喜歡的話。讓學生自己選。

Classroom Activity 2 · 課堂活動二

Two Speeds, One Line 同一句話，兩種速度

You need · 準備材料

  • Two sheets of squared paper for each student每人兩張方格紙
  • A stopwatch碼表

Steps · 步驟

  1. Copy the eight characters as fast as you can. Write down your time.用最快的速度抄那八個字。記下時間。
  2. Copy them again, taking at least three times as long.再抄一次，至少花三倍的時間。
  3. Put the two pages side by side. Circle any character that left the middle of its square.把兩張並排。把沒有寫在格子正中間的字圈起來。
  4. Talk about it: when is fast writing useful, and when is slow writing better?討論：什麼時候寫快比較有用？什麼時候寫慢比較好？

Lesson 7 showed that fast scripts have their uses too. Slow is right for this job, not for every job.
第七課說過，寫得快的字體也有它的用處。「慢」適合這件事，不是每件事都要慢。
