Regular, Running, Cursive: How Fast Can You Write?楷書、行書、草書：同一個字，三種速度

One character, three ways to write it. In regular script every stroke stands alone. In running script the strokes join. In cursive script they melt into one flowing line. Watch three brushes race in 3D, name five scripts in a game, and time your own writing on the practice pad.
同一個字，三種寫法：楷書一筆一畫分開，行書把筆畫連起來，草書連成一條流動的線。看三枝毛筆在 3D 裡比賽，玩五種字體的認字遊戲，再用練字板的碼表量量自己寫多快。

Big idea · 一句話看懂

Regular, running, and cursive script are three ways to write the same character. Regular script writes each stroke separately, so it is the clearest and the slowest. Running script links the strokes, so it is quicker and still easy to read. Cursive script joins and simplifies the strokes, so it is the fastest and the hardest to read.
楷書、行書、草書，是同一個字的三種寫法。楷書一筆一畫分開寫，最清楚、也最慢；行書把筆畫連起來，比較快、還認得出來；草書把筆畫連在一起又簡化，最快、也最難認。

Reading · 英文閱讀

7

Unit 7: Regular, Running, Cursive: How Fast Can You Write?

Write your name slowly and neatly. Now write it again as fast as you can. The letters lean, join, and lose some of their parts. Chinese calligraphy has names for these different ways of writing. Three of them are regular script, running script, and cursive script.

慢慢地、整整齊齊地寫下你的名字；再用最快的速度寫一次。字母歪了、連在一起了，有些地方還不見了。中文書法替這些不同的寫法取了名字，其中三種是：楷書、行書、草書。

In regular script, every stroke is written separately. The brush starts each stroke with care, finishes it with care, and then leaves the paper before the next one. This makes regular script the clearest of the three, and the slowest. It is the form you see in textbooks and learn first.

楷書一筆一畫分開寫。每一筆都仔細地起筆、仔細地收筆，筆離開紙以後，才寫下一筆。所以楷書是三種裡面最清楚、也最慢的。課本上印的、你最先學的，就是這種字。

Running script sits between regular and cursive script. The Ministry of Education's dictionary says it is close to regular script but more flowing and lively. The brush does not always leave the paper, so some strokes are joined by a thin line called a thread. The characters are quicker to write and still easy to recognize.

行書介於楷書和草書之間。教育部辭典說，它接近楷書，但筆勢比較流暢活潑。毛筆不一定每次都離開紙，有些筆畫之間就留下一條細細的線，叫做「牽絲」。行書寫起來比較快，也還很容易認。

Cursive script was made for speed. The same dictionary says it appeared so that people could write conveniently and quickly. Strokes are joined, shortened, or left out, so a whole character can become one or two flowing lines. It is the fastest of the three, and the hardest to read.

草書是為了快而產生的。同一本辭典說，它是「為書寫方便、快速而產生的字體」。筆畫有的連起來、有的縮短、有的省略，一個字可能變成一兩條流動的線。它是三種裡面最快、也最難認的。

Here is a surprise: cursive script is not regular script written in a hurry. It began around the Qin and Han dynasties as a quick way to write clerical script. Regular script took shape later, in the Later Han dynasty, and was complete by the Wei. So the three scripts are three ways of writing, and all three are still used today.

有件事可能讓你意外：草書不是把楷書寫快變來的。草書大約在秦漢之際就出現了，一開始是隸書的快寫；楷書比較晚才成形，大約起源於後漢，到魏才完備。所以這三種字體是三種寫法，而且到今天都還有人在寫。

In this lesson, three brushes write the same character at the same time, and you can see which one finishes first. Then name all five scripts you have met in this unit, and use the stopwatch on the practice pad to find your own three speeds.

這一課，三枝毛筆同時寫同一個字，看看哪一枝最先寫完；再認一認這個單元學過的五種字體，然後用練字板的碼表，量量你自己的三種速度。

Questions · 閱讀理解

Which of the three scripts is the clearest, and which is the fastest?

Regular script is the clearest. Cursive script is the fastest.

What is a thread in running script?

It is a thin line that joins two strokes, left where the brush does not quite leave the paper.

Did cursive script come from writing regular script quickly? What does the lesson say?

No. Cursive script began around the Qin and Han dynasties as a quick way to write clerical script. Regular script took shape later.

Stories of Three Scripts · 三種字體的故事

Stand, walk, run; a monk's model book; the most famous running script; and cursive treasures in Taipei 站、走、跑；智永的範本；最有名的行書；台北故宮的草書國寶

Four short stories about writing slowly, writing quickly, and learning to do both.
關於慢慢寫、快快寫，還有兩種都要學的四個小故事。

An Old Saying · 老話

Stand, walk, run真如立，行如行，草如走

The Song dynasty writer Su Shi put it this way: regular script is like standing, running script is like walking, and cursive script is like running, and nobody can run before learning to stand and walk. (English says running script, but 行 means to walk.) In Taiwan, elementary school students practice regular script, and in junior high they learn to recognize the different scripts.
宋代的蘇軾說：「真如立，行如行，草如走，未有未能行立而能走者也。」楷書像站著，行書像走路，草書像跑步（古文的「走」是跑）；還不會站、不會走的人，不可能會跑。在台灣，國小學生練寫楷書，到國中「認識各種書體」。

A Model Book · 範本

One text, two scripts一篇文章，兩種字體

Zhiyong was a monk and a seventh-generation descendant of Wang Xizhi. He is said to have written 800 copies of the Thousand Character Classic, each in regular and cursive script, and given them to temples. Putting the two scripts side by side helps learners recognize the cursive forms. The National Palace Museum in Taipei has a rubbing of a version carved in stone in 1109; the pictures above come from it.
智永是一位出家人，也是王羲之的七世孫。據記載，他臨寫了八百本〈真草千字文〉，每一本都用楷書和草書並列，分送給各寺院。兩種字體並排，方便學的人認草書。台北國立故宮博物院藏有大觀三年（1109）刻石的拓本，上面的圖片就是從那裡來的。

Running Script · 行書

The best running script under heaven天下第一行書

In 353, Wang Xizhi and his friends met at the Orchid Pavilion to write poems, and he wrote the preface to their poems: the Lanting Xu. It has long been called the best running script under heaven. The original is lost, and we know it only from copies. Our running-script 永 was traced from the first character of one early tracing copy, known as the Shenlong copy. Lesson 8 tells the story.
東晉永和九年（353），王羲之和謝安等四十一人在蘭亭聚會作詩，他為詩集寫了序，就是〈蘭亭序〉，素有「天下第一行書」之稱。原蹟已經失傳，今天只能看到摹本。我們行書的「永」，就是照著一件古摹本（神龍本）的第一個字描的。第八課會說這個故事。

Taiwan · 台灣

Cursive treasures in Taipei台北故宮的草書國寶

Two famous cursive scrolls are in the National Palace Museum in Taipei, and both are National Treasures. Sun Guoting wrote his Treatise on Calligraphy (書譜) in 687. The monk Huaisu wrote his Autobiography (自敘帖) in 777 in wild cursive, the freest kind, with many simplified and joined strokes.
兩件有名的草書長卷都在台北國立故宮博物院，都是國寶。孫過庭的〈書譜〉寫於垂拱三年（687）。出家人懷素的〈自敘帖〉寫於大曆十二年（777），用的是狂草——最奔放的一種草書，簡筆、連筆很多。

Myth vs. Fact · 常見迷思

Four things people get wrong 四個常見的誤會

✗ Myth · 迷思Cursive script is just messy writing.草書就是亂寫。

✓ Fact · 事實Cursive script has its own forms to learn. Zhiyong wrote a whole model book with every line in regular script and again in cursive, and the Ministry of Education's dictionary calls it an excellent model for learning both.草書有自己的寫法要學。智永寫了一整本範本，每一行都有楷書和草書；教育部辭典說它是「學習楷書與草書的絕佳範本」。

✗ Myth · 迷思Cursive script came from writing regular script faster.草書是把楷書寫快變來的。

✓ Fact · 事實It is the other way around in time. Cursive script began around the Qin and Han dynasties as a quick form of clerical script. Regular script took shape in the Later Han and was complete by the Wei.時間上正好相反。草書大約起於秦漢之際，是隸書的快寫；楷書約起源於後漢，到魏才完備。

✗ Myth · 迷思In cursive script, the brush never leaves the paper.寫草書，毛筆從頭到尾都不離開紙。

✓ Fact · 事實Sometimes it does. In our tracing of Zhiyong's cursive 水, there are three separate strokes. Early cursive script even kept every character apart from the next one.有時候還是會離開。我們描的智永草書「水」就有三筆。早期的草書（章草）甚至每個字都各自獨立，不和下一個字相連。

✗ Myth · 迷思草書 means “grass writing.”「草書」的「草」是青草的草。

✓ Fact · 事實The character 草 can mean grass, but it also means rough or quick, as in 潦草 (careless writing) and 草稿 (a draft). The dictionary describes early cursive script as clerical script written quickly and roughly.「草」可以是青草，但也有「草率、粗略」和「草稿」的意思，像「潦草」「起草」。教育部辭典說，早期的草書是「將隸書草率寫成」。

Remember It · 記憶口訣

Three scripts in a sentence 一句話記住楷行草

Stand, walk, run真如立，行如行，草如走

楷書像站著，行書像走路，草書像跑步。

每一筆都自己起筆、收筆。

In the Classroom · 教室提醒

Before you pick up a brush 拿起毛筆之前

  • Ink stains clothes and is very hard to wash out. Wear old clothes or an apron, and roll up your sleeves.墨沾到衣服很難洗掉：穿舊衣服或圍裙，袖子捲起來。
  • Cover the desk with old newspaper, and keep the ink in a heavy, steady dish so it will not tip over.桌上先鋪舊報紙；墨要裝在重一點、放得穩的碟子裡，免得打翻。
  • Press gently. Crushing the brush against the paper squashes the hairs and can split or break them.輕輕地按就好：把筆用力壓在紙上，筆毛會擠扁，也可能分岔或折斷。
  • Do not put the brush, the ink, or the ink stick in your mouth, and do not lick the brush tip to shape it.毛筆、墨汁、墨條都不要放進嘴裡，也不要用嘴巴舔筆尖。
  • After writing, wash the brush in clean water until the water runs clear, gently shape it into a point, and hang it tip-down or lay it flat to dry. Wash your hands, too.寫完後用清水洗筆，洗到水變清為止；輕輕把筆毛理成尖形，筆尖朝下掛起來或平放晾乾。也要洗手。

Classroom Activity 1 · 課堂活動一

My Three Speeds 我的三種速度

You need · 準備材料

  • A pencil and paper for each student (or a brush and water)每人一枝鉛筆和紙（或毛筆和清水）
  • A stopwatch or a phone timer for each pair每兩人一個碼表或手機計時器

Steps · 步驟

  1. Write 永 slowly, stroke by stroke. Your partner times you and counts how many times your pencil leaves the paper.一筆一畫慢慢寫「永」。同伴幫你計時，並數一數鉛筆離開紙幾次。
  2. Write it again, lifting the pencil as few times as you can. Time and count again.再寫一次，盡量少提筆。再計時、再數一次。
  3. Write it a third time as fast as you can. Can your partner still read it?第三次用最快的速度寫。同伴還認得出來嗎？
  4. Make a small table: lifts and seconds for each try. What changed the most?畫一個小表格：每一次提筆幾次、花了幾秒。哪一項變得最多？

Ask: when would you choose the slow way? When would the fast way be good enough?
問問大家：什麼時候要選慢的寫法？什麼時候快的寫法就夠了？

Classroom Activity 2 · 課堂活動二

Cursive Detectives 草書小偵探

You need · 準備材料

  • This lesson's pictures of Zhiyong's rubbing, shown on a screen or printed這一課的智永拓本圖片（投影或印出來）
  • Paper and a pencil紙和鉛筆

Steps · 步驟

  1. Look at the regular and cursive forms of 永 and 水 side by side. Point to the part of the cursive form that matches each regular stroke.把「永」和「水」的楷書、草書放在一起看：指出草書的哪一部分，對應楷書的哪一筆。
  2. Which strokes were joined? Which became shorter? Was anything left out?哪些筆畫連起來了？哪些變短了？有沒有哪一筆省略了？
  3. Now invent a quick form for a simple character, such as 山. Show it to a classmate without saying what it is.現在替一個簡單的字（例如「山」）自己設計一個快寫的樣子，不要說是什麼字，拿給同學看。
  4. Could they read it? Talk about why cursive script needs forms that everyone learns.同學認得出來嗎？討論一下：為什麼草書需要大家都學同一套寫法？

This links back to Lesson 4: a shared standard lets people read one another's writing.
這和第四課有關：有共同的標準，大家才看得懂彼此寫的字。
