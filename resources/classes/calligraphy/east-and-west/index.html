Chinese Calligraphy · Unit 4 · Lesson 14 · 書法 單元四 第十四課

How Is Chinese Calligraphy Different from Western Calligraphy?中文書法和西洋書法有什麼不同？

Chinese and Western calligraphy are both beautiful writing, but the tools are different, and the tools shape the lines. Watch a soft brush and a stiff broad-edge pen write side by side in 3D, turn the nib and see the thick strokes move, guess which tool made each stroke, and try both on two practice pads.
中文書法和西洋書法都是把字寫得美，可是工具不一樣，而工具會決定線條的樣子。在 3D 裡看軟軟的毛筆和硬硬的平頭筆並排寫字，轉一轉筆嘴、看粗的筆畫跑到哪裡去，猜猜每一筆是哪一種筆寫的，再到兩個練字板上各試一次。

Big idea · 一句話看懂

The tool shapes the line. A Chinese brush is soft, so its line gets thicker when the hand presses harder. The broad-edge pen of Western calligraphy is stiff and flat, and it is held at one angle, so its line gets thicker or thinner only when the pen changes direction.
工具決定線條。中文書法用的毛筆是軟的，手按得越重，線就越粗。西洋書法的平頭筆又硬又扁，寫的時候筆嘴的角度固定，所以只有換方向的時候，線才會變粗或變細。

Unit 14: How Is Chinese Calligraphy Different from Western Calligraphy?

The English word calligraphy comes from Greek, and dictionaries trace it to words for beauty and writing. People in many parts of the world have turned writing into an art. In this lesson we compare two traditions: writing Chinese characters with a brush, and writing the Latin alphabet with a broad-edge pen.

英文的 calligraphy 這個字來自希臘文，字典把它追溯到「美」和「寫」這兩個字。世界上很多地方的人，都把寫字變成了一門藝術。這一課比較兩個傳統：用毛筆寫中文字，和用平頭筆寫拉丁字母。

Start with the brush. Its hairs are soft and springy. Press down, and the hairs spread out and the line grows thick. Lift up, and the line grows thin. A calligrapher can change the width anywhere in a stroke, in any direction. You practiced this in Lesson 2.

先看毛筆。筆毛又軟又有彈性。往下按，筆毛散開，線就變粗；提起來，線就變細。寫字的人可以在一筆的任何地方、往任何方向改變粗細。這是第二課練過的。

Now the broad-edge pen. For more than a thousand years in Europe, the main writing tool was a quill, cut from a feather. Scribes cut the tip flat, like a tiny chisel. The writer holds this flat edge at one angle and keeps it there. Pressing harder does almost nothing, because the tip is stiff.

再看平頭筆。在歐洲，有一千多年的時間，主要的書寫工具是用羽毛削成的羽毛筆。抄寫的人把筆尖削成平的，像一把很小的鑿子。寫字的時候，這條扁平的邊保持同一個角度不動。再用力按也幾乎沒有用，因為筆尖是硬的。

So where do the thick and thin lines come from? From direction. When the pen moves across its own edge, it leaves a wide line. When it slides along its edge, it leaves a line as thin as a hair. In a round letter like o, the line swells and thins by itself as the pen goes around.

那麼粗細是怎麼來的？從方向來。筆往「垂直筆嘴」的方向走，留下寬寬的線；順著筆嘴的方向滑過去，只留下頭髮一樣細的線。像 o 這樣圓圓的字母，筆繞一圈，線就自己由粗變細、再由細變粗。

The writing moves differently too. Traditional Chinese texts are written in columns from top to bottom, and the columns go from right to left. The Latin alphabet is written in rows from left to right. Chinese is built from thousands of characters, each with its own strokes; English uses twenty-six letters again and again.

寫的方向也不一樣。傳統的中文是直的寫：一行由上到下，行由右到左。拉丁字母是橫的寫：由左到右。中文由幾千個字組成，每個字有自己的筆畫；英文則是把二十六個字母一再重複使用。

Both traditions ask for the same things: steady hands, patient practice, and a good eye for spacing. In this lesson, watch the two tools write side by side, turn the nib, and then try both yourself.

兩個傳統要求的東西其實一樣：穩定的手、耐心的練習，還有對間距的好眼力。這一課，看兩種筆並排寫字，轉一轉筆嘴，再自己兩種都試試看。

Questions · 閱讀理解

What makes a brush line thicker?

Pressing down harder: the soft hairs spread out.

What makes a broad-edge pen line thicker or thinner?

The direction the pen moves. Across its flat edge the line is wide; along its edge the line is very thin.

In which directions are traditional Chinese texts and the Latin alphabet written?

Traditional Chinese texts go from top to bottom in columns, and the columns go from right to left. The Latin alphabet goes from left to right in rows.

History and Taiwan · 歷史與台灣

A feather for a pen, a man who brought it back, the art prized above all, and letters in Taiwan's schools 用羽毛當筆、把它找回來的人、最被看重的藝術，和台灣學校裡的字母

Three things from museums and encyclopedias, and one from Taiwan's own curriculum.
三件來自博物館和百科全書的事，和一件台灣課綱裡的事。

The Quill · 羽毛筆

A feather for a pen用羽毛當筆

An encyclopedia explains that the quill, the hollow barrel of a bird's feather, was the main writing tool in Europe from the 6th century until the middle of the 19th, and that goose feathers were the usual source. A museum that keeps medieval books adds that scribes also used swan feathers.
大英百科說：羽毛筆（鳥類羽毛中空的羽管）從六世紀到十九世紀中期，一直是歐洲主要的書寫工具，主要用鵝毛。收藏中世紀手抄本的蓋提博物館補充說，抄寫人也用天鵝的羽毛。

A Revival · 復興

The man who brought the broad pen back把平頭筆找回來的人

By 1900, few people in Britain knew how to write with an edged pen. Edward Johnston (1872–1944) studied old manuscripts, relearned how to cut reeds and quills, and published a famous handbook in 1906. He is credited with starting the modern revival of calligraphy. He also designed the lettering of the London Underground.
到了 1900 年左右，英國已經很少人會用有「邊」的筆寫字。愛德華・約翰斯頓（1872–1944）研究古老的手抄本，重新學會怎麼削蘆葦筆和羽毛筆，1906 年出版了一本很有名的手冊。大家認為現代書法的復興是從他開始的。倫敦地鐵的字體也是他設計的。

East Asia · 東亞

The art prized above all others最被看重的藝術

The Metropolitan Museum of Art describes calligraphy as the visual art prized above all others in traditional China. It points to the brush: unlike a rigid tool such as a ballpoint pen, a flexible hair brush lets the width of a stroke change.
大都會藝術博物館說，在傳統中國，書法是所有視覺藝術裡最被看重的。它特別提到毛筆：和原子筆這類硬的工具不同，有彈性的毛筆能讓一筆的粗細產生變化。

Taiwan · 台灣

Two directions, two kinds of letters兩種方向，兩種字母

Taiwan's Ministry of Education sets out both ways of writing Chinese: vertical text runs from top to bottom and right to left, and horizontal text runs from left to right. In English class, the curriculum asks elementary students to write the 26 letters in print form; in junior high they learn to recognize joined-up handwriting too.
教育部規定了中文的兩種寫法：「中文直式書寫及排印，自上而下，自右而左」「中文橫式書寫及排印，自左而右，自上而下」。英語課方面，課綱要求國小「能書寫 26 個印刷體大小寫字母」，國中再學會「辨識連續書寫體大小寫字母」。

Myth vs. Fact · 常見迷思

Four things people get wrong 四個常見的誤會

✗ Myth · 迷思A broad-edge pen makes thick lines when you press hard.平頭筆用力按，線就會變粗。

✓ Fact · 事實Its tip is stiff. The width depends on direction: wide across the flat edge, thin along it.它的筆尖是硬的。粗細看方向：垂直筆嘴走是寬的，順著筆嘴走是細的。

✗ Myth · 迷思All Western calligraphy is written with a stiff pen.西洋書法都是用硬筆尖寫的。

✓ Fact · 事實Not all. Later styles such as copperplate use a flexible pointed pen, and there the thick strokes do come from pressure. This lesson is about the older broad-edge pen.不是全部。後來的銅版體（copperplate）這類寫法用的是有彈性的尖頭筆，粗的筆畫就是靠壓力來的。這一課講的是更早的平頭筆。

✗ Myth · 迷思Calligraphy means Chinese writing with a brush.書法就是用毛筆寫中文字。

✓ Fact · 事實The Chinese word 書法 is defined that way in the dictionary. But the English word calligraphy is used for beautiful writing in any script: Latin, Arabic, and many more.教育部辭典確實把「書法」解釋成「毛筆字書寫的方法」。不過英文的 calligraphy 指的是任何文字的美術書寫：拉丁字母、阿拉伯文和其他很多文字都有。

✗ Myth · 迷思One tradition is harder or better than the other.其中一種比另一種難，或比較高級。

✓ Fact · 事實They solve different problems with different tools. Both take years of patient practice.它們是用不同的工具解決不同的問題。兩種都需要好幾年耐心的練習。

In the Classroom · 教室提醒

Before you pick up a brush 拿起毛筆之前

  • Ink stains clothes and is very hard to wash out. Wear old clothes or an apron, and roll up your sleeves.墨沾到衣服很難洗掉：穿舊衣服或圍裙，袖子捲起來。
  • Cover the desk with old newspaper, and keep the ink in a heavy, steady dish so it will not tip over.桌上先鋪舊報紙；墨要裝在重一點、放得穩的碟子裡，免得打翻。
  • Press gently. Crushing the brush against the paper squashes the hairs and can split or break them.輕輕地按就好：把筆用力壓在紙上，筆毛會擠扁，也可能分岔或折斷。
  • Do not put the brush, the ink, or the ink stick in your mouth, and do not lick the brush tip to shape it.毛筆、墨汁、墨條都不要放進嘴裡，也不要用嘴巴舔筆尖。
  • After writing, wash the brush in clean water until the water runs clear, gently shape it into a point, and hang it tip-down or lay it flat to dry. Wash your hands, too.寫完後用清水洗筆，洗到水變清為止；輕輕把筆毛理成尖形，筆尖朝下掛起來或平放晾乾。也要洗手。

Classroom Activity 1 · 課堂活動一

A Two-Pencil Pen 兩枝鉛筆做的平頭筆

You need · 準備材料

  • Two sharp pencils and two rubber bands for each student每人兩枝削尖的鉛筆和兩條橡皮筋
  • Plain paper白紙

Steps · 步驟

  1. Hold the two pencils side by side and tie them together with the rubber bands.把兩枝鉛筆並排，用橡皮筋綁在一起。
  2. Hold them so that both points touch the paper, with one point a little higher than the other. Keep that angle.讓兩個筆尖同時碰到紙，其中一個稍微高一點。保持這個角度不要變。
  3. Draw a line down, a line across, and a circle. The two pencil lines are the two edges of a wide pen stroke.畫一條直的、一條橫的，再畫一個圓。兩條鉛筆線就是一筆寬線的兩個邊。
  4. Write your name in big letters. Where are the two lines far apart? Where do they almost touch?用大大的字母寫自己的名字。兩條線在哪裡離得最遠？在哪裡幾乎碰在一起？

Do not turn your wrist as you go around a curve. The angle of the two points should stay the same.
畫彎的地方手腕不要跟著轉。兩個筆尖的角度要從頭到尾一樣。

Classroom Activity 2 · 課堂活動二

Same Word, Two Tools 同一個字，兩種工具

You need · 準備材料

  • A brush with clean water and newspaper, and the two-pencil pen毛筆、清水和舊報紙，還有兩枝鉛筆做的平頭筆
  • A chart with two columns: Brush and Pen一張兩欄的表格：毛筆、平頭筆

Steps · 步驟

  1. With the brush and water, write 大. Then write the letter A.用毛筆蘸清水寫「大」。再寫字母 A。
  2. With the two-pencil pen, write the letter A. Then try to write 大.用兩枝鉛筆的平頭筆寫字母 A。再試著寫「大」。
  3. In the chart, write what each tool does well and what it cannot do.在表格裡寫下每一種工具擅長什麼、做不到什麼。
  4. Share one sentence with the class that begins: “The tool shapes the line because…”和全班分享一句話，開頭是：「工具決定線條，因為……」

There is no wrong answer here. The point is to notice what your hand had to do differently.
這裡沒有錯的答案。重點是發現自己的手做了哪些不一樣的事。
