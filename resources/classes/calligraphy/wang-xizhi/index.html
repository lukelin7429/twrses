Big idea · 一句話看懂

Wang Xizhi lived in the Eastern Jin dynasty, and later generations called him the Sage of Calligraphy. In 353, at a spring gathering beside a winding stream, he wrote the Lanting Xu, the best-known work of running script. Its 324 characters include 之 twenty times, and no two are written the same way. The original is lost; we know it from later copies.
王羲之是東晉人，後人稱他「書聖」。永和九年（353）春天，他在一條彎彎的小溪邊和朋友聚會，寫下〈蘭亭序〉——最有名的行書。全文 324 個字裡有二十個「之」，沒有兩個寫得一樣。原蹟已經失傳，我們今天看到的是後來的摹本。

Unit 8: Why Is Wang Xizhi Called the Sage of Calligraphy?

On the third day of the third month in the year 353, Wang Xizhi met his friends at the Orchid Pavilion in Kuaiji, near today's Shaoxing in Zhejiang. It was the day of an old spring custom: people went to the water to wash away bad luck. There were high mountains, tall bamboo, and a clear, fast stream.

永和九年（353）三月三日，王羲之和朋友們在會稽山陰的蘭亭聚會，地點在今天的浙江紹興。那一天是古老的春天習俗「修禊」：大家到水邊洗濯，去除不祥。那裡有高高的山、長長的竹子，還有一條清澈湍急的溪水。

The friends sat along a winding stream and played a game. Cups of wine floated down from upstream, and when a cup stopped beside a guest, that guest picked it up and drank. They also wrote poems. An early record says that 26 of them wrote poems that day, and 15 could not and had to drink more wine as a penalty. Wang Xizhi described the day as “one cup, one poem.”

朋友們沿著彎彎的小溪坐下來，玩一個遊戲：酒杯從上游漂下來，停在誰面前，誰就拿起來喝。大家也作詩。早期的記載說，那天有 26 個人寫出了詩，另外 15 個人寫不出來，被罰喝酒。王羲之形容這一天是「一觴一詠」。

At the end of the day, the poems were gathered into a collection, and Wang Xizhi wrote a preface for it. We call it the Lanting Xu, the Preface to the Orchid Pavilion Collection. It begins with the happy gathering and ends with deeper thoughts about how quickly life passes.

聚會結束後，大家的詩編成一本詩集，王羲之為它寫了一篇序，就是〈蘭亭集序〉，簡稱〈蘭亭序〉。文章從愉快的聚會寫起，最後寫到更深的感想：人生過得好快。

The preface is written in running script. A Tang dynasty writer, He Yanzhi, counted 28 lines and 324 characters. He noticed that when a character appears more than once, each one has a different shape. The character 之 appears most often, about twenty times, and no two are alike. Count them in the gallery below: there are exactly twenty.

這篇序是用行書寫的。唐朝的何延之數過：一共 28 行、324 個字。他還注意到，同一個字出現不只一次的時候，每一個的寫法都不同；其中「之」字最多，「乃有二十許個，變轉悉異，遂無同者」。到下面的圖裡數數看：正好二十個。

Where is the original today? Nobody has it. Tang dynasty books say that Emperor Taizong loved Wang Xizhi's writing so much that the Lanting Xu was buried with him. What we have are copies made by hand in the Tang dynasty and later. That is why museums call the Lanting Xu “the best running script under heaven” and yet can show only copies.

原蹟現在在哪裡？沒有人有。唐朝的書上說，唐太宗非常喜愛王羲之的字，〈蘭亭序〉後來跟著他入葬了。我們今天看到的，是唐朝和後來的人照著摹寫的本子。所以博物館一方面說〈蘭亭序〉是「天下第一行書」，一方面能展出的都是摹本。

Later generations called Wang Xizhi the Sage of Calligraphy. In this lesson, float a cup down the stream, watch the brush write 之 four different ways, find the matching 之 in the game, and trace them yourself.

後人稱王羲之為「書聖」。這一課，在小溪上放一只酒杯，看毛筆把「之」寫成四種樣子，在遊戲裡找出一樣的「之」，再自己描寫看看。

No. 1: 暮春之初第 1 個：暮春之初

Character · 範字
Thin · 細Thick · 粗

Mouse or finger: slow = thick, fast = thin滑鼠或手指：寫得慢＝粗、寫得快＝細Stylus: press harder for a thicker line觸控筆：越用力線越粗

  1. Write the dot first, then the zigzag line without lifting.先寫上面那一點，再一筆寫完彎彎的長線，中間不要提筆。

Stories of Wang Xizhi · 王羲之的故事

A scroll for a flock of geese, fans that sold out, a famous son-in-law, and a letter in Taipei 一卷字換一群鵝、賣光的扇子、東床快婿，和台北的一封短信

Stories people have told about him for more than a thousand years, and one letter you can see in Taiwan. They are old stories; nobody can check every detail.
一千多年來人們一直說著他的故事，還有一封在台灣看得到的短信。這些是古書裡的故事，細節沒有人能一一查證。

Geese · 鵝

A scroll for a flock of geese寫經換鵝

The history of the Jin dynasty says a Daoist priest in Shanyin kept fine geese. Wang Xizhi loved them and asked to buy them. The priest said: write out the Daodejing for me, and the whole flock is yours. Wang Xizhi gladly wrote it and carried the geese home in a cage, very pleased.
《晉書》記載：山陰有一位道士養了好鵝，王羲之很喜歡，想跟他買。道士說：「為寫《道德經》，當舉群相贈耳。」王羲之欣然寫完，「籠鵝而歸，甚以為樂」。

Fans · 扇子

The fans that sold out老婆婆的竹扇

The same history tells of an old woman selling six-sided bamboo fans. Wang Xizhi wrote five characters on each one. She was upset at first. He told her: just say these were written by Wang Youjun, and ask a hundred coins. She did, and people rushed to buy them.
《晉書》又說：王羲之看見一位老婆婆在賣六角竹扇，就在每把扇子上寫了五個字。老婆婆起先不高興，他說：「但言是王右軍書，以求百錢邪。」老婆婆照著說，大家搶著買。

A Son-in-Law · 女婿

The young man on the east bed東床快婿

A Tales of the World story: a great official sent a messenger to the Wang family to choose a husband for his daughter. All the young men tried to look their best, except one, who lay on the east bed with his belly bare as if he had heard nothing. “That is the one!” said the official. It was Wang Xizhi. Today 東床 still means a son-in-law.
《世說新語》的故事：太尉郗鑒派人到王家挑女婿。王家的年輕人個個裝得很端正，只有一個人「在東床上坦腹臥，如不聞」。郗鑒說：「正此好！」那個人就是王羲之。直到今天，「東床」還是女婿的意思。

Taiwan · 台灣

A letter about snow, in Taipei台北故宮的〈快雪時晴帖〉

The National Palace Museum in Taipei keeps a short letter of Wang Xizhi's, Timely Clearing After Snowfall: four lines, 28 characters, beginning “Xizhi bows his head. After the quick snow, the sky has cleared.” The museum says it is a fine tracing copy made in the Tang dynasty. The Qianlong Emperor loved it and counted it among his Three Rarities. It is a National Treasure.
台北國立故宮博物院藏有王羲之的一封短信〈快雪時晴帖〉：行書四行、二十八個字，開頭是「羲之頓首。快雪時晴，佳想安善」。故宮說它是唐代的精摹本。乾隆皇帝非常珍愛，把它列為「三希」之一。它是國寶。

Myth vs. Fact · 常見迷思

Four things people get wrong 四個常見的誤會

✗ Myth · 迷思Museums show the Lanting Xu that Wang Xizhi wrote himself.博物館裡展出的〈蘭亭序〉是王羲之親筆寫的。

✓ Fact · 事實The original is lost. What museums have are copies made by hand in the Tang dynasty and later, and rubbings from stone carvings.原蹟已經失傳。博物館裡的是唐朝和後來的人摹寫的本子，以及從刻石拓下來的拓本。

✗ Myth · 迷思A great calligrapher writes the same character the same way every time.厲害的書法家，同一個字每次都寫得一模一樣。

✓ Fact · 事實In the Lanting Xu it is the opposite. He Yanzhi wrote that every repeated character has a different form, and that the twenty or so 之 are all different.〈蘭亭序〉正好相反。何延之說「有重者皆構別體」，二十來個「之」「變轉悉異，遂無同者」。

✗ Myth · 迷思The Lanting Xu is only about a party.〈蘭亭序〉只是在寫一場聚會。

✓ Fact · 事實It begins with the gathering, but then it turns to time, life, and death. You can read the whole essay in our Classical Chinese Prose series.文章從聚會寫起，後半卻轉到時間、生與死。全文可以到我們的「古文選讀」讀。

✗ Myth · 迷思Wang Xizhi invented running script.行書是王羲之發明的。

✓ Fact · 事實Running script was already in use before him. He is remembered because he wrote it so well that later generations took his writing as their model.在他以前就有行書了。大家記得他，是因為他寫得太好，後人都拿他的字當範本。

In the Classroom · 教室提醒

Before you pick up a brush 拿起毛筆之前

  • Ink stains clothes and is very hard to wash out. Wear old clothes or an apron, and roll up your sleeves.墨沾到衣服很難洗掉：穿舊衣服或圍裙，袖子捲起來。
  • Cover the desk with old newspaper, and keep the ink in a heavy, steady dish so it will not tip over.桌上先鋪舊報紙；墨要裝在重一點、放得穩的碟子裡，免得打翻。
  • Press gently. Crushing the brush against the paper squashes the hairs and can split or break them.輕輕地按就好：把筆用力壓在紙上，筆毛會擠扁，也可能分岔或折斷。
  • Do not put the brush, the ink, or the ink stick in your mouth, and do not lick the brush tip to shape it.毛筆、墨汁、墨條都不要放進嘴裡，也不要用嘴巴舔筆尖。
  • After writing, wash the brush in clean water until the water runs clear, gently shape it into a point, and hang it tip-down or lay it flat to dry. Wash your hands, too.寫完後用清水洗筆，洗到水變清為止；輕輕把筆毛理成尖形，筆尖朝下掛起來或平放晾乾。也要洗手。

Classroom Activity 1 · 課堂活動一

A Winding Stream in the Classroom 教室裡的曲水流觴

You need · 準備材料

  • Desks moved into a long winding line, with students sitting on both sides把桌子排成一條彎彎的長線，同學坐在兩邊
  • A paper cup with a word card inside (no water needed)一個紙杯，裡面放一張字卡（不用裝水）
  • Music, or a student who claps a beat音樂，或一位同學打拍子

Steps · 步驟

  1. Pass the cup along the “stream” while the music plays.音樂響的時候，把紙杯沿著「小溪」一個傳一個。
  2. When the music stops, the student holding the cup takes out the card and reads the word.音樂一停，拿到紙杯的同學抽出字卡、唸出上面的字。
  3. That student says one line of a poem, or one English sentence, using the word.這位同學用那個字說一句詩，或造一個英文句子。
  4. Put in a new card and start the music again.換一張新的字卡，音樂再開始。

Use this lesson's vocabulary for the cards: stream, float, poem, gathering.
字卡可以用這一課的生字：stream、float、poem、gathering。

Classroom Activity 2 · 課堂活動二

Five Different 之 五個不一樣的「之」

You need · 準備材料

  • A brush and water (or a pencil) and paper for each student每人一枝毛筆和清水（或鉛筆）、一張紙
  • This lesson's picture of the twenty 之這一課「二十個之」的圖

Steps · 步驟

  1. Choose three 之 from the picture and copy them as closely as you can.從圖裡選三個「之」，盡量照樣子臨寫。
  2. Now write two more of your own that are different from all three.再寫兩個你自己的「之」，要和前面三個都不一樣。
  3. Show your five to a partner. Which parts did you change: the dot, the turn, or the last line?把五個「之」給同伴看：你改了哪裡？那一點、轉彎，還是最後一筆？
  4. Pick the one you like best and say why.選出你最喜歡的一個，說說為什麼。

There is no wrong answer here. The point is to see how much one simple character can change.
這裡沒有標準答案，重點是看見一個簡單的字可以有多少變化。
