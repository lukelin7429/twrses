How Computers Work · Unit 3 · Lesson 9 · 電腦概論 單元三 第九課

What Is a Program?程式是什麼？

A small robot on a floor of squares does exactly what its program says, one instruction at a time. Watch it walk a staircase in fifteen instructions, then in five. Leave one instruction out and see where it ends up. Then write your own programs for three levels: in order, with a repeat, and with a question.
格子地板上的一台小機器人，完全照程式說的做，一次一條指令。看它用十五條指令走一段階梯，再看它只用五條；漏掉一條，看它最後停在哪裡。接著自己寫程式闖三關：照順序、用重複、會問問題。

Big idea · 一句話看懂

A program is a list of instructions, in order, with nothing left unclear. The computer does exactly what the program says. It does not guess what you meant. Three ideas are enough to build one: do things in order, repeat things, and choose what to do by asking a question.
程式是一串照順序排好、沒有任何含糊地方的指令。電腦完全照程式說的做，不會去猜你的意思。組出一個程式只需要三個觀念：照順序做、重複做、先問一個問題再決定做什麼。

    9

    Unit 9: What Is a Program?

    In Lesson 6 you watched a processor fetch an instruction, carry it out, and fetch the next one. But where do the instructions come from? People write them. A list of instructions written for a computer is called a program. An encyclopedia describes a program as an unambiguous, ordered sequence of instructions. Two words there matter most: ordered, and unambiguous.

    在第六課，你看過處理器拿一條指令、照著做、再拿下一條。可是這些指令是哪裡來的？是人寫的。寫給電腦的一串指令，就叫做程式。百科全書對程式的描述是：一串沒有含糊之處、照順序排好的指令。這裡面最重要的是兩個詞：照順序，以及沒有含糊之處。

    Think of a small robot on a floor of squares. It understands only three instructions: forward, turn left, and turn right. To send it to a flag, you write the instructions one under another, and the robot carries them out from top to bottom. The order matters. “Forward, then turn left” and “turn left, then forward” end on different squares.

    想像格子地板上有一台小機器人。它只聽得懂三種指令：前進、左轉、右轉。要讓它走到旗子，你把指令一條一條往下寫，機器人就從上到下照著做。順序很重要。「先前進再左轉」和「先左轉再前進」，最後停在不同的格子。

    The robot does exactly what is written. If you leave out one “turn right,” it does not notice. It carries on and finishes somewhere else. If you tell it to go forward when a wall is in the way, it walks into the wall. A mistake in a program is called a bug, and finding and fixing it is called debugging. When a program goes wrong, it is usually because the computer did just what it was told.

    機器人完全照寫的做。你漏寫一條「右轉」，它不會發現，繼續做下去，最後停在別的地方。前面明明有牆，你還叫它前進，它就撞上去。程式裡的錯誤叫做 bug（程式錯誤），把它找出來、改掉叫做除錯（debugging）。程式出問題的時候，通常正是因為電腦完全照著指示做了。

    Often a program does the same thing again and again. To climb four stairs, the robot does “forward, turn left, forward, turn right” over and over. Written out one by one, that is a long list. It is better to write those four once and say “repeat 4 times.” This is called a loop. The program becomes shorter, and it is easier to read and to change.

    程式常常要把同一件事做很多遍。要爬四級階梯，機器人得把「前進、左轉、前進、右轉」做了又做。一條一條寫出來，是很長的一串。比較好的寫法是：這四條只寫一次，然後說「重複 4 次」。這叫做迴圈（loop）。程式變短了，也比較好讀、好改。

    Sometimes the right move depends on what the robot finds. So a program can ask a question: “If there is a wall ahead, turn left.” The robot checks, and it does the inside part only when the answer is yes. A program with a question in it can work in rooms its writer has never seen. In the same way, a real program can wait to see which key you press.

    有時候該怎麼走，要看機器人遇到什麼。所以程式可以問一個問題：「如果前面是牆，就左轉。」機器人會去檢查，答案是「是」才做裡面的事。帶著問題的程式，連寫的人從來沒看過的房間都走得通。同樣的道理，真正的程式可以等著看你按的是哪一個鍵。

    These are the three building blocks: doing things in order, repeating, and choosing. Real programs are written in programming languages such as Python, and then they are translated, in several stages, into the machine language of Lesson 6. A game or a web browser may have millions of lines. But line by line, it is still someone’s instructions, carried out exactly.

    這就是三種積木：照順序做、重複、選擇。真的程式是用 Python 這類程式語言寫的，再分好幾個階段翻譯成第六課的機器語言。一個遊戲或一個瀏覽器可能有幾百萬行。可是一行一行看，它仍然是某個人寫下的指令，被一絲不差地執行。

    Questions · 閱讀理解

    What is a program, and which two words in the encyclopedia’s description matter most?

    A program is a list of instructions written for a computer. The two words are “ordered” (the instructions are carried out in order) and “unambiguous” (each one has only one meaning).

    The robot ends up in the wrong place. Whose mistake is it, and why?

    It is the mistake of the person who wrote the program. The robot did exactly what was written. It cannot guess what the writer meant.

    What are the three building blocks of a program? Give an example of each from the robot.

    Doing things in order: forward, then turn left. Repeating: “repeat 4 times” around four instructions. Choosing: “if there is a wall ahead, turn left.”

      Stories Behind Programs · 程式的故事

      A program before computers, a turtle, a moth, and a translator 電腦出現之前的程式、一隻海龜、一隻飛蛾、一位翻譯

      Four short stories about writing instructions for machines.
      關於「寫指令給機器」的四個小故事。

      1843

      A program for a machine that was never finished寫給一台沒有造完的機器的程式

      In 1843 the English mathematician Ada Lovelace translated an article about Charles Babbage’s Analytical Engine and added long notes of her own. According to Britannica, they included a description of how the machine could be programmed to compute a series of numbers called Bernoulli numbers. Babbage built only a small part of the engine, but Lovelace has been called the first computer programmer, and a programming language, Ada, was named for her.
      1843 年，英國數學家愛達・勒芙蕾絲（Ada Lovelace）翻譯了一篇介紹巴貝奇「分析機」的文章，還加上自己寫的長篇註解。根據大英百科，註解裡描述了怎麼替這台機器寫程式，算出一串叫做白努利數的數。巴貝奇只造出分析機的一小部分，但勒芙蕾絲被稱為第一位電腦程式設計師，後來還有一種程式語言以她的名字命名，叫 Ada。

      Logo

      Forward, left 90前進、左轉 90

      The robot in this lesson has a famous ancestor. Britannica explains that the language Logo began in the late 1960s, and that Seymour Papert and others used it at MIT to teach mathematical thinking to schoolchildren. It featured “turtle graphics”: an object was moved around the screen by commands such as “left 90” and “forward,” which describe moves from where the turtle is and the way it is facing. The name came from an early project to program a turtle-like robot.
      這一課的機器人有個有名的前輩。大英百科說，Logo 這種語言誕生於 1960 年代後期，派普特（Seymour Papert）等人在麻省理工學院用它教小學生數學思考。它的特色是「海龜繪圖」：用「left 90」「forward」這樣的指令讓一個東西在螢幕上移動，這些指令說的是從海龜現在的位置、面向的方向出發要怎麼動。這個名字來自早期一個替像海龜的機器人寫程式的計畫。

      September 9, 1947

      A real bug一隻真的蟲

      The Computer History Museum tells this story. On September 9, 1947, a team working on the Harvard Mark II computer found a moth caught between the contacts of a relay. They taped it into the logbook, and someone wrote beside it: “First actual case of bug being found.” The programmer Grace Hopper was there and later made the story famous in her lectures. The word was not new: the museum notes that “bug” had been used for a fault in a machine since the 1800s.
      這個故事出自電腦歷史博物館。1947 年 9 月 9 日，操作哈佛馬克二號電腦的團隊發現一隻飛蛾卡在繼電器的接點之間。他們把牠用膠帶貼進工作日誌，旁邊有人寫了一句：「第一次真的找到蟲（bug）。」程式設計師葛麗絲・霍普（Grace Hopper）當時在場，後來在演講裡把這個故事講得家喻戶曉。這個詞並不是那時才有的：博物館指出，用 bug 來稱呼機器的毛病，從 1800 年代就開始了。

      Translation · 翻譯

      From your words to the machine’s從你的話，到機器的話

      A processor understands only machine language, the kind of simple instructions you met in Lesson 6. Britannica explains that a program is first written in a language suited to the task, and is then translated, commonly in several stages, into a coded program the computer can carry out directly. The translating is done by other programs. There are even programs called debuggers, which run a program a little at a time so that the programmer can check whether it is working correctly.
      處理器只懂機器語言，也就是第六課那種簡單的指令。大英百科說，程式會先用適合那件工作的語言寫出來，再翻譯（通常分好幾個階段）成電腦可以直接執行的編碼程式。做翻譯的，是別的程式。甚至還有一種叫除錯器（debugger）的程式，可以把一個程式一小段一小段地執行，讓程式設計師檢查它運作得對不對。

      Myth vs. Fact · 常見迷思

      Four things people get wrong 四個常見的誤會

      ✗ Myth · 迷思A computer understands what you mean.電腦懂你的意思。

      ✓ Fact · 事實It carries out the instructions it is given, exactly. That is why a program has to be unambiguous: every instruction must have only one meaning.它只是一絲不差地執行拿到的指令。所以程式不能有含糊的地方：每一條指令都只能有一個意思。

      ✗ Myth · 迷思When a program gives the wrong result, the computer made a mistake.程式的結果不對，是電腦弄錯了。

      ✓ Fact · 事實Usually the computer did just what it was told, and the mistake is in the instructions. That is what a bug is.通常是電腦完全照指示做了，錯在指令本身。這就是 bug。

      ✗ Myth · 迷思A longer program is a better program.程式越長越厲害。

      ✓ Fact · 事實The robot’s staircase takes fifteen instructions written out one by one, and five with a repeat. The short one is easier to read, to check, and to change.機器人走階梯，一條一條寫要十五條指令，用「重複」只要五條。短的那個比較好讀、好檢查、也好改。

      ✗ Myth · 迷思Programs began with electronic computers.有了電子電腦，才有程式。

      ✓ Fact · 事實Ada Lovelace described how to program a machine in 1843, about a hundred years before electronic computers. A program is an idea: ordered, unambiguous instructions.愛達・勒芙蕾絲在 1843 年就描述了怎麼替一台機器寫程式，比電子電腦早了大約一百年。程式是一個觀念：照順序、不含糊的指令。

      Before You Start · 動手之前

      A few reminders 幾個提醒

      • For the human robot game, clear the floor first. Mark walls with tape or paper, not with chairs or desks.玩真人機器人之前，先把地板清空。牆用膠帶或紙來標示，不要用椅子或桌子。
      • The human robot keeps their eyes open and walks slowly. “Walking into a wall” means stopping at the tape and saying “Bump!”當機器人的同學眼睛要張開、慢慢走。「撞牆」的意思是：走到膠帶前停下來，喊一聲「碰！」
      • When a program goes wrong, laugh at the program, not at the person who wrote it or the person who walked it. Every programmer writes bugs.程式出錯的時候，笑的是程式，不是寫的人，也不是走的人。每個寫程式的人都會寫出 bug。
      • Use a school or family computer only with permission.用學校或家裡的電腦要先得到同意。

      Classroom Activity 1 · 課堂活動一

      The Human Robot 真人機器人

      You need · 準備材料

      • Tape to mark a grid of 5 by 5 squares on the floor, each big enough to stand in膠帶，在地板上貼出 5 × 5 的格子，每格要站得下一個人
      • Sheets of paper for walls, and one for the flag幾張紙當作牆，一張當作旗子
      • Cards that say FORWARD, TURN LEFT, TURN RIGHT, REPEAT __ TIMES, and IF WALL AHEAD指令卡：FORWARD（前進）、TURN LEFT（左轉）、TURN RIGHT（右轉）、REPEAT __ TIMES（重複 __ 次）、IF WALL AHEAD（如果前面是牆）

      Steps · 步驟

      1. One student is the robot and waits outside the room. The others lay out walls and a flag, and lay the cards in a row as a program.一位同學當機器人，先到教室外面等。其他人擺好牆和旗子，再把指令卡排成一列，當作程式。
      2. The robot comes in. A reader reads the cards aloud, one at a time, in English. The robot does exactly what each card says, and nothing else. No one may help or change a card.機器人進來。一位同學用英文把卡片一張一張唸出來，機器人完全照卡片做，別的都不做。誰都不可以幫忙，也不可以改卡片。
      3. If the robot bumps or misses the flag, find the bug together. Change one card and run the whole program again from the start.如果機器人撞牆或沒走到旗子，大家一起找 bug。改一張卡片，再從頭把整個程式跑一次。
      4. Now make the program shorter with a REPEAT card. Then move one wall and see whether an IF WALL AHEAD card can make one program work for both rooms.接著用 REPEAT 卡把程式變短。然後移動一面牆，看看加上 IF WALL AHEAD 卡，能不能讓同一個程式在兩種房間都走得到。

      The rule that makes this work is that the robot may not think. Students quickly find how much they leave unsaid when they give instructions. In Taiwan’s junior high Information Technology curriculum, this lesson matches “Program design”: 資 P-IV-1, basic concepts of programming languages, and 資 P-IV-2, structured programming, whose notes list sequence, selection, and repetition.
      這個活動成立的關鍵，是機器人「不可以動腦」。學生很快會發現，自己下指令的時候有多少話沒說出口。對照十二年國教國中「資訊科技」的學習內容，這一課對應「程式設計」主題：資 P-IV-1 程式語言基本概念、功能及應用，以及資 P-IV-2 結構化程式設計（說明列有循序、選擇結構與重複結構）。

      Classroom Activity 2 · 課堂活動二

      Swap Programs with a Friend 和朋友交換程式

      You need · 準備材料

      • Squared paper and a pencil for each student每人一張方格紙和一枝鉛筆
      • A coin or small counter to be the robot一枚硬幣或小棋子，當作機器人
      • The “Write the Program” game on this page, for checking這一頁的「換你寫程式」小遊戲，用來對照

      Steps · 步驟

      1. Draw a 5 by 5 room. Shade three or four squares as walls, mark a start with an arrow, and mark a flag.畫一個 5 × 5 的房間。把三、四格塗黑當牆，用箭頭標出起點和面向，再標一面旗子。
      2. On another sheet, write a program for your room: one instruction on each line.在另一張紙上替你的房間寫一個程式：一行一條指令。
      3. Swap rooms and programs with a friend. Move the coin exactly as your friend’s program says. Does it reach the flag?和朋友交換房間和程式。完全照朋友的程式移動硬幣。走得到旗子嗎？
      4. Count the instructions. Can either of you make your program shorter with a repeat?數一數有幾條指令。你們誰能用「重複」把程式變短？

      If the coin goes wrong, do not explain what you meant. Fix the program on the paper and let your friend run it again. That is debugging. Then play Level 2 on this page and compare: a staircase takes 5 instructions with a repeat.
      硬幣走錯的時候，不要用嘴巴解釋你的意思。把紙上的程式改好，請朋友再跑一次——這就是除錯。接著玩這一頁的第二關比一比：階梯用「重複」只要 5 條指令。
