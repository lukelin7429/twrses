Big idea · 一句話看懂

A computer is a small team of parts, and each part has one job. The processor carries out instructions, memory holds what is in use right now, storage keeps things when the power is off, the graphics card makes the picture, the power supply feeds them all, and the motherboard connects everything.
電腦是一小隊零件，每個零件只做一件事。處理器執行指令，記憶體放現在正在用的東西，儲存裝置在關機後把東西留住，顯示卡把畫面做出來，電源供應器供電給大家，主機板把所有零件連在一起。

Unit 5: What Is Inside a Computer?

In Unit 1 you built the ideas a computer runs on: switches, numbers, codes, logic gates, and an adder. Now it is time to open the box and see where those ideas live. You might expect something mysterious inside. What you find is a small team of parts. Each part has one job, and one big board holds them all together.

在單元一，你把電腦運作靠的幾個想法都做出來了：開關、數、編碼、邏輯閘，還有加法器。現在該打開箱子，看看這些想法住在哪裡。你也許以為裡面會是什麼神祕的東西，結果看到的是一小隊零件。每個零件只做一件事，一塊大板子把它們全部連在一起。

That board is the motherboard. It is the largest board inside the case, and every other part plugs into it. Thin copper lines run across it and connect the parts, so that they can pass data to one another. The motherboard does not do any calculating itself. Its job is to connect.

那塊板子就是主機板。它是機殼裡最大的一塊板子，其他每個零件都插在它上面。板子上有細細的銅線把零件連起來，讓它們能互相傳資料。主機板自己不做任何計算，它的工作是「連接」。

The most important part plugged into the motherboard is the processor, also called the CPU. It carries out the instructions of programs, one after another. All the adding and deciding you saw in Lessons 3 and 4 happens here, on one small chip. Its transistors turn electrical energy into heat as they work, so a block of metal fins and a fan sit on top of the processor to carry the heat away.

插在主機板上最重要的零件是處理器，也叫 CPU。它一條接一條地執行程式的指令。第三課和第四課看到的那些「做決定」和「做加法」，全都發生在這裡，一片小小的晶片上。它裡面的電晶體工作時會把電能變成熱，所以處理器上面壓著一塊金屬鰭片和一個風扇，把熱帶走。

A computer has two places to keep things, and they are easy to mix up. Memory, or RAM, holds the programs and data that are in use right now. It is fast, but it loses everything when the power is turned off. Storage keeps your programs and files even when the computer is off. In many computers today the storage is a solid-state drive, which keeps data in chips and has no moving parts. Others use a hard disk, with disks that spin.

電腦有兩個放東西的地方，很容易搞混。記憶體（RAM）放的是現在正在用的程式和資料。它很快，可是電一關，裡面的東西就全部不見了。儲存裝置則是在電腦關機的時候，也把你的程式和檔案留住。現在很多電腦的儲存裝置是固態硬碟，把資料存在晶片裡，沒有任何會動的零件；也有的用硬碟，裡面有會轉的碟片。

Two more parts finish the team. The graphics card draws the picture that you see, working out the color of every pixel. In many computers this job is done by graphics built into the processor, with no separate card. The power supply takes electricity from the wall socket and changes it into the low voltages that the other parts need.

還有兩個零件，這一隊才算到齊。顯示卡把你看到的畫面畫出來，每個像素的顏色都是它算的。很多電腦沒有獨立的顯示卡，這件事由內建在處理器裡的顯示功能來做。電源供應器從牆上的插座取電，把它變成其他零件需要的低電壓。

Now watch the team work. When you open an app, it is copied from storage into memory. The processor takes its instructions from memory and carries them out. The graphics card draws the result as a picture for the screen. The power supply keeps every part running, and the motherboard connects them all. A laptop has the same kinds of parts, only smaller and packed closer together. Some chips even combine the processor, the graphics, and more in a single piece, called a system on a chip.

現在看看這一隊怎麼合作。你打開一個 App，它就從儲存裝置複製到記憶體。處理器從記憶體拿出它的指令，一條一條照著做。顯示卡把結果畫成畫面，交給螢幕。電源供應器讓每個零件都有電，主機板把它們全部連在一起。筆記型電腦裡也有同樣這幾種零件，只是比較小、擠得比較近。有些晶片甚至把處理器、顯示功能和更多東西做在同一顆裡面，叫做「系統單晶片」。

Stories Behind the Parts · 零件的故事

A processor on one chip, a drive with nothing moving, two kinds of gigabyte, and a coffee shop in Taipei 一顆晶片上的處理器、沒有東西在動的硬碟、兩種 GB、台北的一家咖啡店

Four short stories about the parts inside the case.
機殼裡這些零件的四個小故事。

1971 · Intel 4004

The processor on a single chip做在一顆晶片上的處理器

Early computers needed many separate parts to do the work of a processor. In 1971 Intel introduced the 4004, which became the first general-purpose microprocessor: a processor on one chip. Today’s processors do the same basic job of interpreting and carrying out program instructions, with vastly more transistors. The chip lessons on this site tell that part of the story.
早期的電腦要用很多分開的零件，才做得了處理器的工作。1971 年，英特爾推出 4004，它成為第一顆通用的微處理器：做在一顆晶片上的處理器。今天的處理器做的還是同一件基本的事（看懂並執行程式的指令），只是電晶體多了非常非常多。這一段故事，本站的晶片系列有講。

SSD · 固態硬碟

A drive with nothing moving沒有東西在動的硬碟

A hard disk drive stores data on a spinning platter, with an arm that moves across it. A solid-state drive stores data in chips of flash memory instead, so it does not rely on any moving parts. Both do the same job for the computer: they are its long-term storage, remembering files even when the device is off.
硬碟把資料存在會轉的碟片上，還有一支讀寫臂在碟片上移動。固態硬碟改用快閃記憶體晶片來存資料，所以不靠任何會動的零件。對電腦來說，兩者做的是同一件事：當它長期存放東西的地方，就算關機也記得檔案。

GB · GiB

Why a 1 TB drive shows as 931 GB為什麼 1 TB 的硬碟顯示成 931 GB

Drive makers count in tens: one gigabyte is 1,000,000,000 bytes. But some software counts in twos and treats a “gigabyte” as 1,073,741,824 bytes. To end the confusion, a standard approved in 1998 gave the binary units their own names, such as gibibyte (GiB). Seagate notes that people are often confused when a 1 TB drive is reported as only about 931 gigabytes. Nothing is missing: 1,000,000,000,000 bytes is about 931 GiB.
硬碟廠商用十進位來數：1 GB 是 1,000,000,000 個位元組。可是有些軟體用二進位來數，把「1 GB」當成 1,073,741,824 個位元組。為了不再混淆，1998 年通過的一項標準替二進位的單位另外取了名字，例如 gibibyte（GiB）。希捷（Seagate）提到，1 TB 的硬碟被顯示成只有大約 931 GB 時，很多人會搞不清楚。其實什麼都沒少：1,000,000,000,000 個位元組，大約就是 931 GiB。

1989 · Taipei 台北

Motherboards from Taiwan從台灣出發的主機板

ASUS, one of the best-known makers of motherboards, was established in 1989. By the company’s own account, the idea began with a conversation among engineers in a coffee shop in Taipei, and within eight months they had built their first two motherboards. ASUS says that the success of those early boards helped make Taiwan known around the world as a place for high-quality computer products.
華碩（ASUS）是最有名的主機板廠商之一，成立於 1989 年。照華碩自己的說法，這家公司的構想來自幾位工程師在台北一家咖啡店裡的一次談話，八個月內他們就做出了最早的兩款主機板。華碩說，這些早期主機板的成功，讓世界開始知道台灣能做出高品質的電腦產品。

Myth vs. Fact · 常見迷思

Four things people get wrong 四個常見的誤會

✗ Myth · 迷思Memory and storage are the same thing.記憶體和儲存空間是同一個東西。

✓ Fact · 事實They are two different parts. Memory holds what is in use right now and is wiped when the power goes off. Storage keeps your files without power. “My phone is out of memory” usually means it is out of storage.它們是兩個不同的零件。記憶體放現在正在用的東西，電一關就清空；儲存裝置不用電也留得住檔案。大家說「手機記憶體滿了」，通常指的其實是儲存空間滿了。

✗ Myth · 迷思The screen is the computer.螢幕就是電腦。

✓ Fact · 事實A screen only shows the picture that is sent to it. The work is done by the parts on the motherboard. In a laptop or an all-in-one computer, those parts are simply behind or under the screen.螢幕只是把送來的畫面顯示出來，工作是主機板上那些零件做的。筆記型電腦或一體成型的電腦，只是把那些零件放在螢幕的後面或下面。

✗ Myth · 迷思The big metal block with a fan is the processor.那一大塊有風扇的金屬就是處理器。

✓ Fact · 事實That is the cooler. The processor is the small flat square underneath it.那是散熱器。處理器是它底下那一小片扁扁的方形晶片。

✗ Myth · 迷思A laptop works in a completely different way from a desktop computer.筆記型電腦和桌上型電腦的運作方式完全不同。

✓ Fact · 事實It has the same kinds of parts doing the same jobs. They are smaller and closer together, and some are combined: a system on a chip puts the processor, the graphics, and more on one chip.它有同樣那幾種零件，做同樣的事，只是比較小、擠得比較近，有的還合在一起：「系統單晶片」把處理器、顯示功能和更多東西做在同一顆晶片上。

Before You Start · 動手之前

Looking inside a real computer 要看真的電腦裡面之前

  • Do not open a computer on your own. Ask a teacher or a parent, and use only a machine you have permission to open.不要自己拆電腦。先問老師或家長，而且只能拆有得到同意的那一台。
  • Before anyone opens the case, the computer must be shut down completely and its power cable unplugged from the wall socket.任何人打開機殼之前，電腦一定要完全關機，並把電源線從牆上的插座拔掉。
  • Static electricity can permanently damage the parts, even from a spark too small to feel. Touch an unpainted metal surface first, or wear an anti-static wrist strap.靜電會讓零件永久損壞，就算是小到你感覺不到的火花也一樣。先摸一下沒有上漆的金屬表面，或戴上防靜電手環。
  • Leave the power supply closed. It is a sealed box that takes wall electricity directly, and it is not meant to be opened.不要拆電源供應器。它是一個直接接牆上的電、封起來的盒子，本來就不是要讓人打開的。

Classroom Activity 1 · 課堂活動一

Be the Computer 全班當一台電腦

You need · 準備材料

  • Six signs: Storage, Memory, Processor, Graphics, Screen, Power六張牌子：儲存裝置、記憶體、處理器、顯示卡、螢幕、電源
  • A stack of cards with one simple instruction on each, such as “draw a circle” or “add 3 and 4”一疊卡片，每張寫一個簡單的指令，例如「畫一個圓」「把 3 和 4 加起來」
  • A whiteboard for the Screen一塊白板，當作螢幕

Steps · 步驟

  1. Six students take the signs and stand in order: Storage, Memory, Processor, Graphics, Screen. Power stands to one side. The rest of the class is the motherboard and passes things along.六位同學拿牌子，照順序站好：儲存裝置、記憶體、處理器、顯示卡、螢幕；電源站在旁邊。其他同學當主機板，負責傳東西。
  2. Power says “On.” Storage holds the whole stack of cards. To open an app, Storage hands a copy of five cards to Memory.電源說「開」。儲存裝置拿著整疊卡片。要打開一個 App 時，儲存裝置把其中五張的副本交給記憶體。
  3. Processor takes one card at a time from Memory, does exactly what it says, and tells Graphics the result. Graphics draws it on the whiteboard, the Screen.處理器一次從記憶體拿一張卡片，一字不差地照著做，把結果告訴顯示卡。顯示卡把它畫在白板（螢幕）上。
  4. Power says “Off.” Memory drops all its cards. Storage still has the full stack. Start again and see what was lost.電源說「關」。記憶體把手上的卡片全部丟掉，儲存裝置手上那一疊還在。再開一次，看看什麼不見了。

The moment Memory drops its cards is the point of the game: anything that was not handed back to Storage is gone. That is why you save your work. In Taiwan’s junior high Information Technology curriculum, this lesson matches the topic “System platforms”: 資 S-IV-2, whose notes list the CPU, memory, and input and output.
記憶體把卡片丟掉的那一刻，就是這個遊戲的重點：沒有交回給儲存裝置的東西，就不見了。這就是為什麼作業要記得存檔。對照十二年國教國中「資訊科技」的學習內容，這一課對應「系統平台」主題：資 S-IV-2 系統平台之組成架構與基本運作原理（附錄的說明列有 CPU、記憶體、輸入輸出）。

Classroom Activity 2 · 課堂活動二

Check It on Your Own Computer 在自己的電腦上驗證

You need · 準備材料

  • A Windows or Mac computer (you do not open the case)一台 Windows 或 Mac 電腦（不用打開機殼）
  • Paper and a pencil紙和鉛筆

Steps · 步驟

  1. On a Mac, open the Apple menu and choose About This Mac. On Windows, right-click Start, choose Settings, select System, and pick About.Mac：打開蘋果選單，選「關於這台 Mac」（About This Mac）。Windows：在「開始」上按右鍵，選「設定」（Settings），再選「系統」（System）裡的 About 那一頁。
  2. Find the processor. A Mac shows the chip or processor. Windows lists the processor type under Device specifications. Write its name down.找出處理器。Mac 會顯示晶片或處理器；Windows 在「裝置規格」（Device specifications）下面列出處理器的型號。把它的名字寫下來。
  3. Find the memory. A Mac shows the amount of memory. Windows shows the amount of RAM installed. How many gigabytes?找出記憶體。Mac 會顯示記憶體有多少；Windows 會顯示安裝了多少 RAM。是幾 GB？
  4. Find the storage. On Windows, go back to System and pick Storage. On a Mac, open System Settings, click General, then About, and look for Storage. How many gigabytes, and how many are free?找出儲存空間。Windows：回到「系統」，選 Storage（儲存空間）。Mac：打開「系統設定」，按「一般」（General），再按 About，找到儲存空間那一行。一共幾 GB？還剩多少？

You should now have three facts about your own computer: its processor, its memory, and its storage. Notice that storage is many times larger than memory. Use “How Much Fits?” above to see what your storage could hold. The names of menus differ a little between versions of Windows and macOS.
現在你手上有自己電腦的三件事：處理器、記憶體、儲存空間。注意儲存空間比記憶體大了好多倍。用上面的「裝得下多少？」看看你的儲存空間能放多少東西。不同版本的 Windows 和 macOS，選單的名稱會略有不同。
