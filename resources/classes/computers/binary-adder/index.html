How Does a Computer Add?電腦怎麼做加法？

Set two numbers with switches and watch a four-bit adder work in 3D, one column at a time, as the carry hops from right to left. See the two gates inside a half adder, then be the adder yourself.
用開關設好兩個數，在 3D 裡看一台四位元加法器一位一位地算，進位從右邊跳到左邊。看看半加器裡面的兩個閘，再換你自己當加法器。

Big idea · 一句話看懂

A few logic gates wired together can add. For one column, an XOR gate gives the sum and an AND gate gives the carry. Put one adder in every column, pass each carry to the column on its left, and the circuit adds whole numbers.
幾個邏輯閘接起來就能做加法。只看一位的話，XOR 閘算出「和」，AND 閘算出「進位」。每一位放一個加法器，把進位傳給左邊那一位，整個電路就能把兩個數加起來。

Unit 4: How Does a Computer Add?

Lesson 3 ended with a promise: logic gates, which only answer yes or no, can add. That sounds odd. AND, OR, and NOT know nothing about numbers. But Lesson 1 showed that a number is only a row of switches, and adding is a job you can do one column at a time. If gates can handle one column, a row of them can handle the whole sum.

第三課最後留下一句話：只會回答「是或不是」的邏輯閘，可以做加法。聽起來很奇怪，AND、OR、NOT 根本不懂什麼是數。可是第一課說過，一個數只不過是一排開關；而加法是一件可以一位一位做的事。只要邏輯閘處理得了一位，一排邏輯閘就處理得了整個加法。

Start with the smallest problem: add two bits. There are only four cases. 0 + 0 is 0, 0 + 1 is 1, 1 + 0 is 1, and 1 + 1 is two, which in binary is written 10: write 0 and carry 1. Now look at what you write down in each case: 0, 1, 1, 0. That is the table for XOR. Look at the carry: 0, 0, 0, 1. That is the table for AND. So one XOR gate and one AND gate, side by side, add two bits. This small circuit is called a half adder.

先從最小的問題開始：把兩個位元加起來。一共只有四種情況：0＋0 是 0，0＋1 是 1，1＋0 是 1，1＋1 是二，二進位寫成 10，也就是寫 0、進 1。現在看看每一種情況你寫下來的那個數字：0、1、1、0，這正是 XOR 的真值表。再看進位：0、0、0、1，這正是 AND 的真值表。所以一個 XOR 閘和一個 AND 閘並排，就能把兩個位元加起來。這個小電路叫做「半加器」。

A half adder is enough for the column on the far right. Every other column has a third number to add: the carry coming in from its right. A circuit that adds three bits is called a full adder. It can be built from two half adders and an OR gate. Its hardest case is 1 + 1 + 1, which is three, written 11 in binary: write 1 and carry 1.

最右邊那一位用半加器就夠了。其他每一位都多了第三個數要加：從右邊進過來的進位。能把三個位元加起來的電路叫做「全加器」，它可以用兩個半加器和一個 OR 閘做出來。它最難的一種情況是 1＋1＋1，也就是三，二進位寫成 11：寫 1、進 1。

Now give every column its own full adder, and connect each carry out to the carry in of the column on its left. That is a complete adding machine. Try 0111 + 0001 in the model. The right column makes a carry, the next column passes it on, and so does the next, until it lands in the fourth column: 1000. This is the same falling-domino carry you saw in Lesson 1. Because the carry ripples along, engineers call this a ripple-carry adder.

現在給每一位一個自己的全加器，再把每一位的「進位出去」接到左邊那一位的「進位進來」，就是一台完整的加法機了。在模型裡試試 0111＋0001：最右邊那一位產生進位，下一位把它傳下去，再下一位也一樣，最後落在第四位，得到 1000。這就是第一課看過的、像骨牌一樣倒過去的進位。因為進位是像漣漪一樣一位一位傳過去的，工程師把這種電路叫做「漣波進位加法器」。

People built adding machines long before electronics. About 1642, the French mathematician Blaise Pascal designed one that added by turning wheels, to help his father, a tax collector. Nearly three hundred years later, George Stibitz of Bell Laboratories took two telephone relays, which are switches worked by electricity, some flashlight bulbs, and strips cut from a tobacco can, and made a circuit that added two binary digits. He built it at home on his kitchen table, so it became known as the Model K.

早在有電子零件之前，人們就做過加法機。大約 1642 年，法國數學家巴斯卡為了幫當稅務官的父親，設計了一台轉動輪子來做加法的機器。將近三百年後，貝爾實驗室的喬治・史提比茲（George Stibitz）拿了兩個電話用的繼電器（用電來控制的開關）、幾個手電筒燈泡，還有從菸草罐上剪下來的金屬片，做出一個能把兩個二進位數字加起來的電路。他是在自家廚房的桌子上做的，所以後來大家叫它「K 型」（K 就是 kitchen，廚房）。

The adder inside a processor today works on many more bits at once than our four: 16, 32, or 64, depending on the computer. Its designers also use clever circuits that work out the carries ahead of time, so the answer does not have to wait for a long ripple. The idea has not changed. This is also the end of Unit 1. You started with one switch, on or off. A row of switches became a number. Codes turned numbers into words, pictures, and sound. Gates answered questions, and now gates add. Everything else a computer does is built from these few ideas.

今天處理器裡的加法器，一次處理的位元比我們這四位多得多：16、32 或 64 位，看是哪一種電腦。設計的人還用了聰明的電路，事先把進位算出來，答案就不必等一長串的進位慢慢傳完。但想法沒有變。單元一也到這裡結束。你從一個開關開始，它不是開就是關；一排開關變成一個數；編碼把數變成文字、圖片和聲音；邏輯閘會回答問題，現在邏輯閘還會做加法。電腦做的其他每一件事，都是用這幾個想法搭起來的。
Read aloud · 課文朗讀

Questions · 閱讀理解

Which two gates make a half adder, and what does each one give?

An XOR gate and an AND gate. The XOR gives the sum digit to write down, and the AND gives the carry.

Why does every column except the rightmost need a full adder instead of a half adder?

Because those columns have three bits to add: one from each number and the carry coming in from the column on the right. A half adder can add only two.

What happens when you add 0111 and 0001, and why is it called a ripple?

The right column makes a carry, and each column passes a carry to the next until it reaches the fourth column, giving 1000. The carry moves along one column at a time, like a ripple or a row of falling dominoes.

Stories of Adding Machines · 加法機的故事

Gears for a tax collector, a kitchen table, one gate for everything, and the adder’s home 替稅務官做的齒輪、一張廚房的桌子、一種閘做出全部、加法器住的地方

Four short stories about machines that add.
關於會做加法的機器的四個小故事。

1642–1644 · Pascaline

An adding machine with wheels用輪子做的加法機

Blaise Pascal designed and built his machine between 1642 and 1644 for his father, a tax collector. Numbers were entered by turning its dials, and it could only add and subtract. Britannica calls it the first calculator or adding machine to be produced in any quantity and actually used: Pascal built 50 of them over the next ten years. It counted in tens, not twos, and it was made of moving parts, not switches.
巴斯卡在 1642 到 1644 年之間，為當稅務官的父親設計並製造了這台機器。用轉盤把數撥進去，它只會加和減。大英百科說，它是第一種有一定產量、而且真的有人使用的計算機：之後十年間巴斯卡造了 50 台。它用的是十進位，不是二進位；裡面是會動的機械零件，不是開關。

1937 · Model K

The adder on the kitchen table廚房桌上的加法器

One weekend at home, George Stibitz of Bell Laboratories fastened two relays from the laboratory’s scrap pile to a piece of plywood, added flashlight bulbs and strips cut from a tobacco can, and had a one-digit binary adder. It was named the Model K, for the kitchen table it was built on. Most accounts date it to 1937; some say 1936. The Computer History Museum says it proved that Boolean logic could be used to design computers.
某個週末，貝爾實驗室的喬治・史提比茲在家裡，把兩個從實驗室廢料堆撿來的繼電器釘在一塊夾板上，加上手電筒燈泡和從菸草罐剪下來的金屬片，做出一個一位的二進位加法器。它叫做 Model K，K 就是它誕生的那張廚房（kitchen）桌子。大多數資料說這是 1937 年的事，也有說 1936 年的。電腦歷史博物館說，它證明了布林邏輯可以用來設計電腦。

NAND

One kind of gate is enough只要一種閘就夠了

The adder in this lesson uses three kinds of gates: AND, OR, and XOR. Surprisingly, a chip does not need three kinds. A NAND gate is an AND followed by a NOT, and every other gate can be built out of NAND gates alone. So a whole adder, and in principle a whole computer, can be made by repeating one tiny part again and again.
這一課的加法器用了三種閘：AND、OR、XOR。讓人意外的是，晶片並不需要三種。NAND 閘就是 AND 後面接一個 NOT，而其他每一種閘都可以只用 NAND 閘做出來。所以一整個加法器，原則上連一整台電腦，都可以靠同一個小零件一再重複做出來。

ALU

Where the adder lives加法器住在哪裡

Inside a processor, the adder is part of a section called the arithmetic logic unit, or ALU. The same section also holds the circuits for the logic of Lesson 3. When a program adds two numbers, compares them, or combines them with AND or OR, the work is done here. You will meet the rest of the processor in Unit 2.
在處理器裡，加法器屬於一個叫做「算術邏輯單元」（ALU）的部分，第三課的邏輯運算電路也在這裡。程式要把兩個數加起來、比大小，或用 AND、OR 把它們合起來的時候，工作就是在這裡做的。處理器其他的部分，單元二會看到。

Myth vs. Fact · 常見迷思

Four things people get wrong 四個常見的誤會

✗ Myth · 迷思A computer looks up the answer to a sum, the way you remember that 7 + 8 is 15.電腦是用「查」的，就像你背得出 7＋8＝15。

✓ Fact · 事實There is nothing to remember. The answer falls out of the wiring: each column’s gates produce a sum bit and a carry, every time, for any two numbers.它沒有背任何東西。答案是從接線裡自己跑出來的：每一位的閘都會算出一個和、一個進位，不管是哪兩個數，每次都一樣。

✗ Myth · 迷思In binary, 1 + 1 = 10 means one plus one is ten.二進位的 1＋1＝10，意思是一加一等於十。

✓ Fact · 事實The amount is still two. “10” is how two is written with binary digits: one in the twos place and zero in the ones place.數量還是二。「10」只是二用二進位寫出來的樣子：「二」那一位是 1，「一」那一位是 0。

✗ Myth · 迷思The adder works on one column at a time, like a person with a pencil.加法器跟拿筆算的人一樣，一次只做一位。

✓ Fact · 事實All the columns are wired up at once, and every block starts working as soon as its inputs arrive. Only the carries have to wait, because each one depends on the column to its right.每一位的線路都是同時接好的，輸入一到，每個方塊就開始工作。要等的只有進位，因為每個進位都得看右邊那一位的結果。

✗ Myth · 迷思Adding bigger numbers needs a smarter kind of circuit.要加更大的數，就需要更聰明的電路。

✓ Fact · 事實It needs more of the same. Each extra column is one more full adder, and each extra column doubles the largest number the adder can take.需要的是更多同樣的東西。每多一位就多一個全加器，而每多一位，加法器能處理的最大的數就加倍。

Before You Start · 動手之前

A few reminders 幾個提醒

  • If your class builds an adder with real switches and bulbs, use only small batteries, and never the wall socket.如果班上要用真的開關和燈泡做加法器，只能用小電池，絕對不要用牆上的插座。
  • Never join the two ends of a battery directly with a wire. The wire and the battery can get hot very quickly.絕對不要用一條線直接把電池的兩頭接在一起，電線和電池會很快變燙。
  • In the developer console, type only what this lesson shows, and never paste in code that someone else tells you to paste.在開發人員主控台裡，只輸入這一課寫的那幾行；別人叫你貼的程式碼絕對不要貼。
  • Use a school or family computer only with permission, and do not change its settings.用學校或家裡的電腦要先得到同意，也不要更改設定。

  • Four small balls or beanbags, to be the carries四顆小球或沙包，當作進位
  • Space for four students to stand in a row facing the class讓四位同學面向全班站成一排的空間

Steps · 步驟

  1. Four students stand in a row. Each one is the adder for one column: 8, 4, 2, and 1, as the class sees them from left to right.四位同學站成一排，每人負責一位。從全班看過去，由左到右是 8、4、2、1。
  2. The teacher gives each student two cards, one bit of A and one bit of B. For example, 0111 and 0001.老師發給每位同學兩張卡片：A 的一個位元和 B 的一個位元。例如 0111 和 0001。
  3. Start with the student on the class’s right. Count your 1s, including a ball if someone hands you one. With two or three 1s, pass a ball to the student on your left.從全班看過去最右邊的同學開始。數數自己有幾個 1（有人交球給你的話，球也算一個）。有兩個或三個 1，就把一顆球交給自己左邊的同學。
  4. Then show your answer: raise a hand if your count was one or three, and keep it down if it was zero or two. The class reads the four hands as a binary number.接著比出自己的答案：數到一個或三個就舉手，零個或兩個就不舉。全班把四隻手當成一個二進位的數讀出來。

Nobody in the row knows what the whole sum is. Each student only counts to three and follows two rules, and the right answer still appears. That is exactly how the adder in a chip works. Try 1111 + 0001 and ask where the last ball should go. In Taiwan’s junior high Information Technology curriculum, adders are not listed by name; this lesson supports the topic “System platforms” (資 S-IV-2, how system platforms are built and basically work).
這一排人沒有一個知道整個加法是多少。每個人只數到三、照兩條規則做，正確答案還是出現了。晶片裡的加法器就是這樣工作的。試試 1111＋0001，問問大家最後那顆球該交給誰。十二年國教國中「資訊科技」的學習內容沒有直接列出加法器；這一課可以用來支援「系統平台」主題（資 S-IV-2 系統平台之組成架構與基本運作原理）。

Classroom Activity 2 · 課堂活動二

Check It on Your Own Computer 在自己的電腦上驗證

You need · 準備材料

  • A Windows or Mac computer with its Calculator app一台 Windows 或 Mac 電腦，以及內建的「計算機」
  • Paper and a pencil紙和鉛筆

Steps · 步驟

  1. On paper, add 0101 and 0011 in binary, one column at a time from the right. Write the carries above the columns.先在紙上用二進位算 0101＋0011，從右邊開始一位一位加，把進位寫在每一位的上面。
  2. Open Calculator in programmer mode, as you did in Lesson 1. On a Mac, choose View > Programmer and click Show Binary. On Windows, choose Programmer from the menu.照第一課的方法，把「計算機」切換到程式設計的模式。Mac：選「顯示方式」＞「程式設計」，再按「顯示二進位」。Windows：在選單裡選「程式設計人員」。
  3. Type 5 + 3 and press =. Read the binary line and compare it with your paper.輸入 5＋3 再按 =。看二進位那一行，和你紙上的答案比一比。
  4. Try 7 + 1, and then 15 + 1. Find the carry in each answer.再試 7＋1，然後 15＋1。在每個答案裡找出進位。

The answers are 1000, 1000, and 10000. For a second check, open a browser’s developer console and type (0b0101 + 0b0011).toString(2). In JavaScript, a number that starts with 0b is written in binary. The console answers "1000".
答案分別是 1000、1000、10000。還有第二種驗證法：打開瀏覽器的開發人員主控台，輸入 (0b0101 + 0b0011).toString(2)。在 JavaScript 裡，0b 開頭的數就是用二進位寫的。主控台會回答 "1000"。
