How Did Computers Get So Small?電腦怎麼越變越小？

Five stands in a row show the same thing, a switch, as it was built in 1937, 1946, 1947, 1971, and 2024: a relay, a vacuum tube, the first transistor, and two chips. Behind each stand is a column for how many switches one machine held, from 2 to 28 billion. Then compare any two, name the switch, and look back over all sixteen lessons.
一排五個展示台，放的是同一樣東西——開關——在 1937、1946、1947、1971、2024 年的樣子：繼電器、真空管、第一顆電晶體，還有兩片晶片。每個展示台後面有一根柱子，表示一台機器裡有幾個開關，從 2 個到 280 億個。接著隨意挑兩個來比、說出那是哪一種開關，再回頭看一遍十六課。

Big idea · 一句話看懂

A computer has always been the same idea: a great many switches. What changed is what the switches are made of and how small they are. Relays gave way to vacuum tubes, tubes to transistors, and separate transistors to chips that hold billions. Smaller switches mean more of them in the same space, and that is how a machine that filled a room came to fit in a pocket.
電腦從頭到尾都是同一個想法：很多很多個開關。變的是開關用什麼做、做得多小。繼電器讓位給真空管，真空管讓位給電晶體，一顆一顆的電晶體又讓位給裝得下幾十億顆的晶片。開關變小，同樣的空間就放得下更多，於是原本占滿一個房間的機器，後來放得進口袋。

Unit 16: How Did Computers Get So Small?

In Lesson 1 you worked eight switches by hand. Every lesson since then has been about switches too: switches wired into gates, gates into an adder, adders into a processor, and programs telling the processor what to do. One question is left. How did a machine made of switches shrink from the size of a room to something you carry in your pocket?

在第一課，你用手撥了八個開關。從那以後，每一課講的也都是開關：開關接成邏輯閘，邏輯閘接成加法器，加法器組成處理器，再由程式告訴處理器該做什麼。還剩一個問題。一台用開關做成的機器，是怎麼從一個房間那麼大，縮到可以放進口袋帶著走的？

The first switches were relays. A relay is a switch moved by an electromagnet, so one electric current can turn another on and off. In 1937 George Stibitz built an adder from just two of them. But a relay has parts that move, and moving parts are slow. The next switch had no moving parts at all: the vacuum tube.

最早的開關是繼電器（relay）。繼電器是用電磁鐵帶動的開關，所以一股電流可以把另一股電流打開、關掉。1937 年，史提比茲只用兩個繼電器就做出一個加法器。可是繼電器裡有會動的零件，而會動的零件就慢。下一種開關完全沒有會動的零件：真空管（vacuum tube）。

ENIAC, finished early in 1946, used more than 17,000 vacuum tubes. It was far faster than any relay machine: it could do up to 5,000 additions a second. It was also enormous. It filled a basement 50 feet long and 30 feet wide, and it gave off so much heat that it needed its own air conditioning.

ENIAC 在 1946 年初完成，用了超過 17,000 根真空管。它比任何繼電器做的機器都快得多：一秒最多可以做 5,000 次加法。它也非常巨大。它占滿一間長 50 英尺、寬 30 英尺的地下室，散發的熱多到得有自己的空調。

Then, in December 1947, two researchers at Bell Labs made a switch inside a small piece of solid material. It was called a transistor. Nothing in it moved and nothing had to glow hot. Eleven years later came the next step: the integrated circuit, in which many parts are made together from one piece of material. We call it a chip.

接著，1947 年 12 月，貝爾實驗室的兩位研究者在一小塊固體材料裡面做出了一個開關，叫做電晶體（transistor）。它裡面沒有東西在動，也沒有東西要燒得發亮。十一年後，下一步出現了：積體電路（integrated circuit），把很多零件一起做在同一塊材料上。我們叫它晶片。

From then on, the story is one of fitting more switches onto a chip. In 1971 the Intel 4004 put a whole processor on one chip, with 2,300 transistors. In 2024 Apple announced a chip with 28 billion. In 1965 Gordon Moore had noticed how quickly the number was growing; ten years later he described it as doubling every two years. That pattern became known as Moore’s law.

從那時候起，故事就是「在一片晶片上放進更多開關」。1971 年，Intel 4004 把一整個處理器做在一片晶片上，有 2,300 個電晶體。2024 年，Apple 發表了一片有 280 億個電晶體的晶片。1965 年，摩爾（Gordon Moore）注意到這個數目增加得有多快；十年後，他把它說成是每兩年增加一倍。這個規律後來被稱為摩爾定律（Moore’s law）。

So the answer is simple to say and astonishing to think about. The idea never changed: a switch is off or on, 0 or 1. The switch itself changed, from a clicking relay to something far too small to see. Everything in these sixteen lessons, from adding two numbers to opening a web page, rests on that one idea, repeated an enormous number of times.

所以答案說起來很簡單，想起來卻很驚人。想法從來沒有變：開關不是關就是開，不是 0 就是 1。變的是開關本身，從喀噠作響的繼電器，變成小到根本看不見的東西。這十六課裡的一切，從把兩個數加起來到打開一個網頁，都建立在這一個想法上，只是重複了多到數不清的次數。

Questions · 閱讀理解

Name the kinds of switch in this lesson, in order of time. What was better about each new one?

The relay, the vacuum tube, and the transistor. The vacuum tube had no moving parts, so it was faster than the relay. The transistor was made inside a solid piece of material: nothing moved, and nothing had to glow hot.

What is an integrated circuit, and why did it matter?

It is a circuit in which many parts are made together from one piece of material; we call it a chip. It mattered because many transistors could now be made at once, and more and more of them fitted on one chip.

What stayed the same from the relay to today’s chip, and what changed?

The idea stayed the same: a switch is either off or on, 0 or 1. What changed is what the switch is made of and how small it is, so that far more switches fit in the same space.

Stories Behind the Shrinking · 越變越小的故事

A room that needed air conditioning, a slab of germanium, a circuit in one piece, and a prediction 一間需要空調的房間、一小塊鍺、做成一整塊的電路、一個預測

Four short stories about the switch.
關於開關的四個小故事。

ENIAC

Forty panels along three walls沿著三面牆的四十片面板

Britannica describes ENIAC as the first programmable general-purpose electronic digital computer. Its 40 panels stood in a U along three walls of a basement at the University of Pennsylvania, and each panel was about 2 feet wide, 2 feet deep, and 8 feet high. Besides its vacuum tubes it had 1,500 relays, so it stood between two ages. Changing its program meant rewiring it, which could take days.
大英百科把 ENIAC 稱為第一台可程式化的通用電子數位電腦。它的 40 片面板在賓州大學的一間地下室裡，沿著三面牆排成 U 字形，每一片大約寬 2 英尺、深 2 英尺、高 8 英尺。除了真空管，它還有 1,500 個繼電器，所以它正好站在兩個時代之間。要換程式就得重新接線，可能要花好幾天。

December 16, 1947

A wedge, two gold contacts, and germanium一塊楔子、兩個金接點、一塊鍺

The Computer History Museum describes the first transistor. John Bardeen and Walter Brattain pressed two closely spaced gold contacts, held by a plastic wedge, onto a small slab of very pure germanium. The voltage on one contact controlled the current through the other. A week later, on December 23, they showed it to others at the laboratory.
電腦歷史博物館描述了第一顆電晶體。巴丁和布拉頓用一塊塑膠楔子固定兩個靠得很近的金接點，壓在一小塊純度很高的鍺上。一個接點上的電壓，控制著流過另一個接點的電流。一個星期後的 12 月 23 日，他們把它展示給實驗室裡的其他人看。

September 12, 1958

A circuit made in one piece做成一整塊的電路

By the 1950s engineers were wiring together thousands of separate transistors, and the wiring itself had become the problem. According to the Computer History Museum, on September 12, 1958, Jack Kilby of Texas Instruments built a circuit whose transistor, capacitor, and resistor were all formed from pieces of germanium. That was the first integrated circuit. The chips series on this site tells how such circuits are made today.
到了 1950 年代，工程師要把幾千顆一顆一顆的電晶體用線接起來，接線本身成了難題。根據電腦歷史博物館，1958 年 9 月 12 日，德州儀器的基爾比做出一個電路，裡面的電晶體、電容和電阻都是用鍺做成的。這就是第一個積體電路。今天這種電路是怎麼做出來的，本站的「晶片與半導體」系列有說明。

1965, 1975

Moore’s prediction摩爾的預測

In 1965 Gordon Moore tried to predict the next ten years of integrated circuits. The Computer History Museum reports that he projected a doubling every 12 months, reaching 65,000 parts on a chip by 1975. In 1975 he looked again and slowed the rate to “a doubling every two years, rather than every year.” The museum calls it a prediction that became a self-fulfilling prophecy, one of the driving principles of the industry.
1965 年，摩爾試著預測積體電路往後十年的發展。電腦歷史博物館記載，他推估每 12 個月增加一倍，到 1975 年一片晶片上會有 65,000 個元件。1975 年他重新檢視，把速度放慢成「每兩年增加一倍，而不是每年」。博物館說，這個預測成了一個自我實現的預言，也是推動整個產業的原則之一。

Myth vs. Fact · 常見迷思

Four things people get wrong 四個常見的誤會

✗ Myth · 迷思Computers got smaller because each new one was a different kind of machine.電腦變小，是因為每一代都是不一樣的機器。

✓ Fact · 事實The idea stayed the same: switches that are off or on. What changed was how the switch is made and how small it is.想法一直沒變：不是關就是開的開關。變的是開關的做法和大小。

✗ Myth · 迷思Newer always means more switches.比較新，開關就一定比較多。

✓ Fact · 事實The 4004 of 1971 had 2,300 transistors, far fewer than ENIAC’s 17,000 tubes of 1946. Its great step was to put a whole processor on one chip.1971 年的 4004 只有 2,300 個電晶體，比 1946 年 ENIAC 的 17,000 根真空管少得多。它了不起的地方，是把一整個處理器做在一片晶片上。

✗ Myth · 迷思The transistor replaced the vacuum tube because it was invented later.電晶體取代真空管，只因為它發明得比較晚。

✓ Fact · 事實The Bell Labs group was looking for a replacement for unreliable vacuum tubes and mechanical switches. A switch with nothing moving and nothing glowing was the point.貝爾實驗室那個小組，本來就是在找東西取代不可靠的真空管和機械式開關。重點是「沒有東西在動、沒有東西在發亮」的開關。

✗ Myth · 迷思Moore’s law is a law of nature.摩爾定律是一條自然定律。

✓ Fact · 事實It began as one person’s prediction in 1965, which he changed in 1975. The Computer History Museum calls it a self-fulfilling prophecy.它一開始是一個人在 1965 年的預測，1975 年他自己還修改過。電腦歷史博物館把它稱為自我實現的預言。

Before You Start · 動手之前

A few reminders 幾個提醒

  • Do not open a phone, a computer, or a power supply to look for the chips. Lesson 5 explained why.不要為了找晶片而把手機、電腦或電源供應器拆開。原因第五課說過。
  • Old electronic parts such as vacuum tubes can break easily. If your school has any, look, and let the teacher handle them.真空管這類舊的電子零件很容易弄破。學校如果有，用看的就好，讓老師來拿。
  • When you look up your own device, use the maker’s official page, and do not type personal information into sites that ask for it.查自己裝置的資料時，請看製造商的官方網頁；有網站要你填個人資料的話，不要填。
  • Use a school or family computer only with permission.用學校或家裡的電腦要先得到同意。

Classroom Activity 1 · 課堂活動一

A Timeline You Can Walk 可以走的時間軸

You need · 準備材料

  • A long corridor or playground, and chalk or tape一條長走廊或操場，和粉筆或膠帶
  • A tape measure一條捲尺
  • Five cards: 1937 relay, 1946 vacuum tubes, 1947 transistor, 1971 one chip, 2024 one chip五張卡片：1937 繼電器、1946 真空管、1947 電晶體、1971 一片晶片、2024 一片晶片

Steps · 步驟

  1. Mark a line on the ground. Let 10 centimeters stand for one year, so the whole line from 1937 to 2024 is 8.7 meters long.在地上畫一條線。用 10 公分代表一年，所以從 1937 到 2024 的整條線長 8.7 公尺。
  2. Five students each take a card and stand at the right place. Measure from 1937: the 1946 card stands at 0.9 meters, 1947 at 1 meter, 1971 at 3.4 meters, and 2024 at 8.7 meters.五位同學各拿一張卡，站到正確的位置。從 1937 量起：1946 那張站在 0.9 公尺，1947 在 1 公尺，1971 在 3.4 公尺，2024 在 8.7 公尺。
  3. Each student says in English what the switch was and how many there were: 2, more than 17,000, 1, 2,300, and 28 billion.每位同學用英文說出那是什麼開關、有幾個：2、超過 17,000、1、2,300、280 億。
  4. Now the class adds its own cards: the year each student was born, and the year the school was built. Where do they stand?接著全班加上自己的卡片：每位同學出生的那一年，還有學校蓋好的那一年。它們站在哪裡？

Notice how close together 1946 and 1947 stand, and how long the walk is from 1971 to 2024. In Taiwan’s junior high Information Technology curriculum, this lesson matches the topic “System platforms”: 資 S-IV-1, important developments and the evolution of system platforms.
注意 1946 和 1947 站得有多近，而從 1971 走到 2024 又有多遠。對照十二年國教國中「資訊科技」的學習內容，這一課對應「系統平台」主題：資 S-IV-1 系統平台重要發展與演進。

Classroom Activity 2 · 課堂活動二

Check It on Your Own Device 在自己的裝置上驗證

You need · 準備材料

  • A computer, tablet, or phone一台電腦、平板或手機
  • The steps from Lesson 5 for finding the name of the processor第五課教的、找出處理器名稱的步驟
  • Paper and a pencil紙和鉛筆

Steps · 步驟

  1. Find the name of the processor or chip in your device, as you did in Lesson 5. Write it down exactly.照第五課的做法，找出你裝置裡處理器或晶片的名稱。一字不差地抄下來。
  2. Look for that name on its maker’s official website. Does the maker say how many transistors it has? Not every maker does.到製造商的官方網站上找這個名稱。製造商有沒有說它有幾個電晶體？不是每一家都會說。
  3. If you find a number, write it down with the web address and the date. If you do not, write “not published.”如果找到數字，連同網址和日期一起記下來。如果找不到，就寫「not published」（沒有公布）。
  4. Compare your number with 2,300 in the “Compare Any Two” way: about how many times as many?用「挑兩個來比」的方式，拿你的數字和 2,300 比：大約是幾倍？

This activity is about sources as much as chips. A number is only worth writing down if you can say where it came from, which is how every number on these pages was chosen. “Not published” is a perfectly good finding.
這個活動談的是晶片，也是「來源」。一個數字要說得出它是哪裡來的，才值得寫下來——這幾頁上的每一個數字都是這樣挑的。「沒有公布」也是一個很好的發現。
