How Computers Work · Unit 3 · Lesson 10 · 電腦概論 單元三 第十課

What Is an Algorithm?演算法是什麼？

Two rows of closed boxes, one number to find, and two ways to look: open the boxes one by one, or open the middle one and throw away half. Watch both at once and count. Then double the boxes, mix them up, play a number-guessing game, and see what happens with a million boxes.
兩排蓋著的箱子、一個要找的數、兩種找法：一個一個打開，或是打開中間那個、把一半丟掉。兩種同時進行，數數看各開了幾個。接著把箱子加倍、把箱子弄亂、玩猜數字，再看看一百萬個箱子會怎樣。

Big idea · 一句話看懂

An algorithm is a clear, step-by-step method for solving a problem. The same problem can have more than one algorithm, and some take far fewer steps than others. The more things there are to deal with, the bigger the difference becomes.
演算法是解決問題的一套清楚的、一步一步的方法。同一個問題可以有不只一種演算法，有的要做的步數比別的少得多。要處理的東西越多，差別就越大。

Unit 10: What Is an Algorithm?

In Lesson 9 you saw that a program is a list of exact instructions. But before anyone can write the instructions, they need a method. A clear, step-by-step method that reaches the answer in a limited number of steps is called an algorithm. You already know some. The long division you learned at school is an algorithm.

在第九課你看到，程式是一串分毫不差的指令。可是在動手寫指令之前，得先有一個方法。一套清楚的、一步一步的、在有限步數內得到答案的方法，就叫做演算法（algorithm）。你其實已經會幾個了。學校教的直式除法，就是一個演算法。

Here is a problem. A row of closed boxes each holds a number, and you want to find the box that holds 48. One algorithm is very simple: open the first box, then the second, then the third, and go on until you find it. This always works. But with 16 boxes you may have to open all 16.

來看一個問題。一排蓋著的箱子，每個裡面有一個數，你要找出裝著 48 的那一個。有一個演算法非常簡單：打開第一個，再打開第二個、第三個，一直開到找到為止。這個方法一定找得到。可是如果有 16 個箱子，你可能 16 個都得打開。

Now suppose the numbers are in order, from small to large. Then there is another algorithm. Open the box in the middle. If its number is too small, the one you want must be to the right, so you can forget the whole left half without opening it. Do the same again with the boxes that are left. With 16 boxes, you never need to open more than 5.

現在假設這些數是照順序排好的，由小到大。那就有另一個演算法。打開中間那個箱子。如果裡面的數太小，你要的那個一定在右邊，所以左邊那一半連開都不用開，可以整個不管。剩下的箱子再照同樣的做法來一次。16 個箱子，最多只要開 5 個。

The real surprise comes when the boxes increase. Double them, and the first method may need twice as many tries. The second method needs only one more. With a million boxes, opening them one by one could take a million tries. Halving never takes more than 20.

真正讓人驚訝的，是箱子變多的時候。箱子加倍，第一種方法最多要開的數目也加倍；第二種方法只多開一個。一百萬個箱子，一個一個開可能要開一百萬個；每次砍一半，最多不超過 20 個。

There is a catch. Halving works only when the boxes are in order. Mix them up, and it throws away the half where your number is hiding. That is why a dictionary is kept in alphabetical order. Putting things in order is called sorting, and it takes work too. Also, the simple method sometimes wins by luck, when the number happens to be in the first box. So we compare methods by the most steps they could ever need.

可是有一個條件。每次砍一半的方法，只有在箱子照順序排好的時候才管用。把箱子弄亂，它就會把藏著那個數的那一半丟掉。字典要照字母順序排，就是這個道理。把東西排好順序叫做排序（sorting），這也是要花工夫的。另外，簡單的方法有時候會靠運氣贏，例如那個數剛好在第一個箱子。所以我們比較方法時，比的是「最多可能要幾步」。

This is why programmers care so much about algorithms. A computer that is twice as fast can only cut the time in half. A better method can turn a million steps into twenty. The method comes first. Then a program is written to tell the computer how to carry it out.

這就是寫程式的人那麼在乎演算法的原因。電腦快一倍，時間只能省一半；換一個比較好的方法，卻可以把一百萬步變成二十步。先有方法，然後才寫程式，告訴電腦怎麼照這個方法做。

Questions · 閱讀理解

What is an algorithm? Give one example that has nothing to do with computers.

An algorithm is a clear, step-by-step method that reaches the answer in a limited number of steps. Long division is one example.

Describe the two ways of finding a number in a row of boxes. Which one needs the boxes to be in order?

One way is to open the boxes one by one from the start. The other is to open the middle box and rule out half of the boxes each time. The second way works only when the boxes are in order.

Why does the difference between the two methods grow when there are more boxes?

Opening one by one may need as many tries as there are boxes, so doubling the boxes doubles the tries. Halving needs only one more try each time the boxes are doubled.

    Stories Behind Algorithms · 演算法的故事

    A name from Baghdad, a method 2,300 years old, a machine made of an idea, and twenty questions 來自巴格達的名字、兩千三百年前的方法、用想法造出來的機器、二十個問題

    Four short stories about step-by-step methods.
    關於「一步一步的方法」的四個小故事。

    al-Khwārizmī · 花拉子米

    The word is a person’s name這個字是一個人的名字

    According to Britannica, the mathematician al-Khwārizmī was born about 780 and died about 850. He worked in Baghdad at the House of Wisdom. One of his books introduced Hindu-Arabic numerals and their arithmetic to the West. It survives only in a Latin translation, Algoritmi de numero Indorum, and from the Latin form of his name, Algoritmi, came the word algorithm. The title of another of his books gave us the word algebra.
    根據大英百科，數學家花拉子米（al-Khwārizmī）大約生於 780 年、卒於 850 年，在巴格達的「智慧宮」工作。他有一本書把印度－阿拉伯數字和它的算術介紹到西方，現在只留下拉丁文譯本《Algoritmi de numero Indorum》。他的名字在拉丁文裡寫成 Algoritmi，algorithm（演算法）這個字就是這樣來的。他另一本書的書名，則給了我們 algebra（代數）這個字。

    About 300 BCE · 約西元前 300 年

    Algorithms are older than computers演算法比電腦老得多

    Britannica notes that Euclid’s Elements, published about 300 BCE, contained an algorithm for finding the greatest common divisor of two whole numbers. It also points out that every elementary-school student is drilled in long division, which is an algorithm too: a fixed set of steps that, followed carefully, always gives the quotient and the remainder.
    大英百科指出，歐幾里得的《幾何原本》大約在西元前 300 年問世，裡面就有一個演算法：求兩個自然數的最大公因數。它還提到，每個小學生都反覆練習的直式除法也是一個演算法：一套固定的步驟，只要仔細照做，一定得到商和餘數。

    Alan Turing · 圖靈

    What exactly is an algorithm?演算法到底是什麼？

    For centuries people used algorithms without saying precisely what one was. Britannica explains that the English mathematician Alan Turing rigorously defined the loosely understood concept of an algorithm, and that his description of a general-purpose algorithm machine, the Turing machine, became the foundation of computer science. Britannica calls a computer program “a special type of algorithm.”
    好幾百年來，大家都在用演算法，卻沒有人精確地說出它到底是什麼。大英百科說，英國數學家圖靈（Alan Turing）替這個原本只是大略理解的概念下了嚴格的定義；他所描述的通用演算法機器，也就是圖靈機，成了電腦科學的基礎。大英百科把電腦程式稱為「一種特別的演算法」。

    2 × 2 × 2 …

    Twenty questions, a million things二十個問題，一百萬樣東西

    This one is our own arithmetic. Each time you halve, you answer one yes-or-no question: is it in this half? One question separates 2 things, two questions 4, three questions 8. Twenty questions separate 1,048,576 things, which is why a million boxes need at most 20 tries. It is the doubling from Lesson 1 run backward: every extra bit doubles what you can count, and every question halves what is left.
    這一則是我們自己算的。每砍一半，就是回答一個「是或不是」的問題：它在這一半裡嗎？一個問題分得出 2 樣東西，兩個問題 4 樣，三個問題 8 樣。二十個問題分得出 1,048,576 樣東西，所以一百萬個箱子最多只要開 20 個。這是把第一課的「加倍」倒過來走：每多一個位元，能數的數目加倍；每多一個問題，剩下的少一半。

    Myth vs. Fact · 常見迷思

    Four things people get wrong 四個常見的誤會

    ✗ Myth · 迷思Algorithms are something only computers use.演算法是電腦才用的東西。

    ✓ Fact · 事實Long division is an algorithm, and Euclid wrote one down about 300 BCE. An algorithm is a method. A computer is one thing that can carry it out.直式除法就是演算法，歐幾里得在大約西元前 300 年就寫下了一個。演算法是方法；電腦只是能照著做的其中一樣東西。

    ✗ Myth · 迷思To make a program faster, you need a faster computer.要讓程式變快，就得換一台更快的電腦。

    ✓ Fact · 事實A computer twice as fast halves the time. Changing the method can turn a million steps into twenty.電腦快一倍，時間省一半。換一個方法，卻可以把一百萬步變成二十步。

    ✗ Myth · 迷思The halving method always works.每次砍一半的方法永遠管用。

    ✓ Fact · 事實It works only when things are in order. On boxes that are mixed up, it rules out the half where the answer is hiding.它只有在東西照順序排好時才管用。箱子弄亂之後，它會把藏著答案的那一半排除掉。

    ✗ Myth · 迷思The cleverer method is quicker every single time.比較聰明的方法，每一次都比較快。

    ✓ Fact · 事實If the number is in the first box, opening one by one takes 1 try and halving takes 4. Methods are compared by the most steps they could need, not by one lucky case.如果那個數剛好在第一個箱子，一個一個找只要 1 次，砍一半要 4 次。比較方法，比的是最多可能要幾步，不是某一次的運氣。

    Before You Start · 動手之前

    A few reminders 幾個提醒

    • In the card race, the two searchers take turns. It is a count of flips, not a speed race, so there is no need to grab.翻牌比賽時，兩位同學輪流翻。比的是翻了幾張，不是手快，所以不用搶。
    • Turn the pages of a dictionary gently. Halving means opening it in the middle, not bending it backward.翻字典的時候動作輕一點。砍一半是從中間翻開，不是把書往後折。
    • The simple method is not a wrong method. Do not laugh at a classmate who searches one by one; ask when that way is the right one.簡單的方法不是錯的方法。同學一個一個找，不要笑他；想一想什麼時候那樣找才是對的。
    • Use a school or family computer only with permission.用學校或家裡的電腦要先得到同意。

    Classroom Activity 1 · 課堂活動一

    The Face-Down Card Race 蓋牌比賽

    You need · 準備材料

    • Two sets of 16 cards, each with a different number, the same numbers in both sets兩組各 16 張的數字卡，每張數字都不同，兩組的數字一樣
    • A table long enough for two rows of 16 cards一張放得下兩排各 16 張卡片的長桌
    • Paper to keep score記分用的紙

    Steps · 步驟

    1. Lay both sets face down in two rows, in order from the smallest number to the largest.把兩組卡片面朝下排成兩排，都由最小的數排到最大的數。
    2. The teacher calls a number. Student A turns cards one by one from the left. Student B always turns the middle card of what is left. They take turns, one card each.老師喊一個數。A 同學從左邊一張一張翻；B 同學每次都翻剩下範圍中間的那一張。兩人輪流，一次各翻一張。
    3. Count how many cards each one turned. Do it five times with different numbers and write down the counts.數一數兩人各翻了幾張。換不同的數做五次，把次數記下來。
    4. Now shuffle both rows and play once more. What happens to student B?接著把兩排都洗亂，再玩一次。B 同學會怎麼樣？

    With 16 cards in order, student B never needs more than 5 turns, and student A may need 16. Ask the class when A wins. In Taiwan’s junior high Information Technology curriculum, this lesson matches “Algorithms”: 資 A-IV-1, basic concepts of algorithms, and 資 A-IV-3, an introduction to basic algorithms: searching and sorting. Sequential search and binary search are named in the senior high curriculum.
    16 張照順序排好的卡片，B 同學最多翻 5 張，A 同學可能要翻 16 張。問問全班：什麼時候 A 會贏？對照十二年國教國中「資訊科技」的學習內容，這一課對應「演算法」主題：資 A-IV-1 演算法基本概念，以及資 A-IV-3 基本演算法的介紹（搜尋、排序）。「循序搜尋與二元搜尋」這兩個名稱出現在高中階段的課綱。

    Classroom Activity 2 · 課堂活動二

    Check It with a Dictionary 用字典驗證

    You need · 準備材料

    • A paper dictionary (English or Chinese) for each pair每兩人一本紙本字典（英文或中文都可以）
    • A list of five words to look up要查的五個字
    • Paper and a pencil紙和鉛筆

    Steps · 步驟

    1. One student looks up a word by halving: open the dictionary in the middle of what is left, decide whether the word comes before or after, and repeat.一位同學用砍一半的方法查字：從剩下範圍的中間翻開，判斷要查的字在前面還是後面，再重複。
    2. The partner counts how many times the dictionary is opened before the right page is found.另一位同學數一數，翻到正確的那一頁之前，一共翻開了幾次。
    3. Do all five words and write down each count. What is the largest?五個字都查完，把每次的次數記下來。最多的是幾次？
    4. Look at the last page number. How many openings would it take to go through the dictionary one page at a time?看一下最後一頁的頁碼。如果一頁一頁翻，要翻幾次？

    For 1,000 pages, exact halving never needs more than 10 openings; going page by page could take 1,000. Your hands are not exact, so your counts may be a little higher. Then play “Guess My Number” on this page and see whether you can always finish within 7 guesses.
    1,000 頁的字典，精確地對半切最多只要翻 10 次；一頁一頁翻可能要 1,000 次。手沒辦法切得那麼準，所以你們的次數可能多一點。接著玩這一頁的「猜猜我想的數」，看看能不能每次都在 7 次以內猜到。
