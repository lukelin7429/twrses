How Can a File Get Smaller?檔案為什麼可以變小？

A wall of 256 colored squares is packed row by row in 3D: squares of the same color in a row join into one bar with a number on it. Watch one picture shrink to a fraction of its size and another grow to twice its size. Then draw your own picture to pack, throw detail away and try to get it back, and decide what must never be lost.
在 3D 裡把一面 256 格的彩色方塊牆一列一列壓起來：同一列裡相鄰的同色方塊合成一長條，上面寫著格數。看一張圖縮到原來的幾分之一，另一張卻變成兩倍大。接著自己畫一張圖來壓、把細節丟掉再試著還原，並分辨哪些東西一點都不能少。

Big idea · 一句話看懂

A file gets smaller when its repetition is written in a shorter way: not “red, red, red, red, red,” but “5 red.” That kind of compression loses nothing, and the file can be unpacked exactly as it was. Another kind goes further and throws away detail that people hardly notice. It makes files much smaller, but what is thrown away cannot come back.
把檔案裡重複的地方用比較短的方式寫下來，檔案就變小了：不寫「紅、紅、紅、紅、紅」，而寫「5 個紅」。這種壓縮什麼都不會丟，解開來和原來一模一樣。另一種壓縮更進一步，把人不太注意得到的細節丟掉。檔案會小得多，可是丟掉的拿不回來。

Unit 12: How Can a File Get Smaller?

In Lesson 2 you saw that a picture is a long list of numbers, and that a photo from a phone holds millions of them. Yet the photo files on a phone are often far smaller than that. Songs and videos are smaller than you would expect too. Something is making them shrink. It is called data compression: reducing the amount of data needed to store a piece of information or to send it.

第二課說過，一張圖是很長的一串數，手機拍的一張照片就有好幾百萬個數。可是手機裡的照片檔，常常比這小得多。歌曲和影片也比你以為的小。有什麼東西讓它們縮小了。這叫做資料壓縮（data compression）：減少儲存或傳送一份資訊所需要的資料量。

The first idea is repetition. Suppose a row of a picture has sixteen squares, all of them red. You could write “red” sixteen times. Or you could write “16 red.” That is two numbers instead of sixteen, and nothing has been lost. A picture with large areas of one color, such as a blue sky above green grass, shrinks a great deal this way.

第一個想法是「重複」。假設圖裡有一列是十六格，全部都是紅色。你可以把「紅」寫十六次，也可以寫「16 個紅」。這樣只要兩個數，不用十六個，而且什麼都沒有丟掉。有大片同色的圖，例如綠草地上方一片藍天，用這個方法可以縮小很多。

But it does not always work. Take a picture where no two squares next to each other are the same color. Now every square has to be written as “1 of this color,” which is two numbers instead of one. The file gets bigger, not smaller. Compression is not magic. It finds patterns, and where there is no pattern, there is nothing to save.

可是它不是每次都管用。拿一張圖，裡面沒有任何相鄰的兩格同色。這時候每一格都得寫成「1 個這種顏色」，原來一個數，現在變成兩個數。檔案不但沒變小，反而變大。壓縮不是魔法。它找的是規律；沒有規律，就沒有東西可以省。

Compression of this kind is called lossless. When the file is unpacked, every number comes back exactly as it was. That matters for text, where every character is important, and for programs. ZIP files work this way, with cleverer methods than counting. English text can generally be compressed to one-half or one-third of its original size.

這一種壓縮叫做無失真壓縮（lossless）。把檔案解開，每一個數都原封不動地回來。這對文字很重要，因為每一個字元都不能錯；對程式也一樣。ZIP 檔就是這一種，只是用的方法比數個數聰明得多。英文的文字，一般可以壓到原來的二分之一到三分之一。

For photos and music there is a second kind, called lossy compression. It throws away detail that people hardly notice. The JPEG format for photos discards what the human eye cannot see. The MP3 format for music discards sounds that the ear is least likely to miss. The files become very much smaller. But the detail that was thrown away is gone, and no program can bring it back.

照片和音樂還有第二種，叫做失真壓縮（lossy）。它把人不太注意得到的細節丟掉。照片用的 JPEG 格式，丟掉的是人眼看不出來的部分；音樂用的 MP3 格式，丟掉的是耳朵最不容易察覺少了的聲音。檔案會小非常多。可是丟掉的細節就是沒了，沒有任何程式能把它找回來。

So there is a choice to make. An essay must come back letter for letter, so it needs lossless compression. A photo to share with friends can lose a little. Either way, a smaller file takes up less storage and travels more quickly to another computer. And a file that has already been compressed, such as a JPEG photo, will hardly shrink at all if you compress it again.

所以要做一個選擇。作文必須一個字母都不差地回來，所以要用無失真壓縮。要分享給朋友的照片，少一點點沒關係。不管哪一種，檔案變小了，占的儲存空間就比較少，傳到另一台電腦也比較快。還有，已經壓縮過的檔案（例如 JPEG 照片），再壓一次幾乎不會變小。

Questions · 閱讀理解

How can “16 red” replace sixteen squares without losing anything?

All sixteen squares are the same, so it is enough to say how many there are and which color they are. From those two numbers, all sixteen can be written out again exactly.

Why does a picture with no repetition get bigger when it is packed this way?

Every square is different from its neighbor, so each one has to be written as “1 of this color.” That takes two numbers for each square instead of one.

What is the difference between lossless and lossy compression? Give one thing that needs each.

Lossless compression gives back exactly what went in; an essay or a program needs it. Lossy compression throws away detail that people hardly notice, and that detail cannot come back; it is acceptable for a photo or a song.

    Four short stories about writing things in less space.

    Morse Code · 摩斯電碼

    Older than computers比電腦還早

    Britannica points out that compression is older than digital technology. Morse Code assigned the shortest codes to the most common characters, so that a message took less time to send. And telephones cut off the high frequencies of the voice, which is an early example of lossy compression: some of the sound is thrown away, and the words are still understood.
    大英百科指出，壓縮比數位科技還早。摩斯電碼把最短的碼分給最常用的字元，這樣發一則訊息花的時間就比較少。電話則把人聲裡的高頻切掉，這是很早的失真壓縮：聲音的一部分被丟掉了，話還是聽得懂。

    36 → 32

    A rat ate a tart at a teaA rat ate a tart at a tea

    Britannica gives an example with only four letters: a, e, r, and t. With four letters, each one needs two bits, so the eighteen letters of “A rat ate a tart at a tea” take 36 bits. But a is the most common letter here and t the next. Give a the code 0, t the code 10, r the code 110, and e the code 111, and the same sentence takes only 32 bits. Short codes for common things: the Morse idea again, in bits.
    大英百科舉了一個只有四個字母的例子：a、e、r、t。四個字母，每個要用兩個位元，所以「A rat ate a tart at a tea」這十八個字母要 36 個位元。可是這裡 a 最常出現，t 第二。讓 a 的碼是 0、t 是 10、r 是 110、e 是 111，同一句話就只要 32 個位元。常用的給短碼——又是摩斯電碼的想法，只是換成位元。

    JPEG, MP3

    What the eye and the ear will not miss眼睛和耳朵不會發現少了的東西

    According to Britannica, the Joint Photographic Experts Group was formed in 1986 to create a standard that used data compression to keep picture files small. The JPEG standard works by averaging color variation and discarding what the human eye cannot see. The MP3 format, part of a standard released in 1993, did the same for music: it reduced the size of a CD audio file by discarding certain sounds, based on what the ear is least likely to miss. Britannica adds that the data lost in an MP3 is not reversible.
    根據大英百科，聯合圖像專家小組（Joint Photographic Experts Group）在 1986 年成立，目的是訂出一套用資料壓縮讓圖檔變小的標準。JPEG 標準的做法是把顏色的變化平均掉，丟掉人眼看不出來的部分。MP3 格式屬於 1993 年發布的一套標準，它對音樂做了同樣的事：根據耳朵最不容易察覺少了什麼，丟掉某些聲音，把 CD 音訊檔變小。大英百科還說，MP3 壓縮時丟掉的資料是無法復原的。

    Lempel, Ziv

    The text is its own dictionary文章自己就是字典

    The model on this page can only count squares that sit side by side. Real compression programs do better. Britannica describes the Lempel-Ziv algorithm, invented by the Israeli computer scientists Abraham Lempel and Jacob Ziv. It uses the text itself as a dictionary: when a string of characters appears again, it is replaced by numbers that say where it appeared before and how long it was. Britannica notes that Zip uses variations of this algorithm.
    這一頁的模型只會數並排的格子。真的壓縮程式厲害得多。大英百科介紹了 Lempel-Ziv 演算法，由以色列電腦科學家倫佩爾（Abraham Lempel）和立夫（Jacob Ziv）發明。它把文章自己當成字典：一串字元再次出現時，就換成幾個數，說明它之前出現在哪裡、有多長。大英百科提到，Zip 用的就是這個演算法的變化版本。

    Myth vs. Fact · 常見迷思

    Four things people get wrong 四個常見的誤會

    ✗ Myth · 迷思Compression always makes a file smaller.壓縮一定會讓檔案變小。

    ✓ Fact · 事實Not when there is nothing to shorten. In the model, the scrambled picture grows from 256 numbers to 512.沒有東西可以寫短的時候就不會。在模型裡，那張雜亂的圖從 256 個數變成 512 個。

    ✗ Myth · 迷思Compressing a file always loses some quality.壓縮一定會損失一點品質。

    ✓ Fact · 事實Lossless compression can be reversed to give back the original data exactly. ZIP is lossless.無失真壓縮可以完全還原成原來的資料。ZIP 就是無失真的。

    ✗ Myth · 迷思If a photo was saved with too much compression, you can get the detail back later.照片壓得太過頭，之後還能把細節救回來。

    ✓ Fact · 事實Lossy compression throws detail away, and what was not kept cannot be brought back.失真壓縮是把細節丟掉；沒有留下來的，就拿不回來。

    ✗ Myth · 迷思Putting photos in a ZIP file makes them much smaller.把照片放進 ZIP 檔，就會小很多。

    ✓ Fact · 事實Microsoft notes that JPEG images are already highly compressed, so a zipped folder of them is about the same size as the pictures were.Microsoft 的說明提到，JPEG 圖片本來就已經高度壓縮，所以把它們壓成一個資料夾，大小和原來那些照片差不多。

    Before You Start · 動手之前

    A few reminders 幾個提醒

    • Compressing makes a new file and leaves the original alone. Do not delete the original until you have opened the new one and checked it.壓縮會做出一個新檔案，原來的檔案還在。打開新檔案確認過之前，不要把原來的刪掉。
    • Practice on copies of your own files, never on someone else’s work.用自己檔案的副本來練習，不要拿別人的作業來試。
    • Do not open a compressed file from someone you do not know. Ask a teacher or a parent first.不認識的人傳來的壓縮檔不要打開。先問老師或家長。
    • Use a school or family computer only with permission.用學校或家裡的電腦要先得到同意。

    Classroom Activity 1 · 課堂活動一

    Read Me a Picture 把圖唸給我聽

    You need · 準備材料

    • Squared paper with grids of 8 by 8 squares畫好 8 × 8 格子的方格紙
    • Colored pencils in four colors四種顏色的色鉛筆
    • A folder or book to stand between two partners一個資料夾或一本書，立在兩位同學之間擋住視線

    Steps · 步驟

    1. Student A colors a picture on a grid without letting B see it. Large shapes work best to begin with.A 同學在格子上畫一張圖，不要讓 B 看到。一開始先畫大塊的形狀。
    2. A reads the picture to B one square at a time, in English: “red, red, red, blue …” B colors what B hears. Count the words A says for one row.A 用英文一格一格把圖唸給 B 聽：「red, red, red, blue …」B 照聽到的塗。數一數 A 唸一列說了幾個字。
    3. On a new grid, A reads the same picture with counts: “three red, one blue, four white.” Count the words again.換一張新的格子，A 用「幾個」來唸同一張圖：「three red, one blue, four white」。再數一次說了幾個字。
    4. Compare B’s two copies with A’s picture. Are they exactly the same? Then swap, and let B draw the hardest picture B can think of.拿 B 的兩張和 A 的原圖比一比。一模一樣嗎？接著交換，讓 B 畫一張他想得到最難唸的圖。

    Reading with counts is lossless compression: B’s copy should match square for square. Ask which pictures took fewer words and which took more. In Taiwan’s junior high Information Technology curriculum, this lesson builds on “Data representation, processing, and analysis”: 資 D-IV-1 and 資 D-IV-2, on how pictures and sound are turned into digital data. Compression itself is not named in the junior high content.
    用「幾個」來唸，就是無失真壓縮：B 的那一張應該一格不差。問問大家，哪些圖說的字比較少，哪些反而比較多。對照十二年國教國中「資訊科技」的學習內容，這一課延伸自「資料表示、處理及分析」主題的資 D-IV-1 資料數位化之原理與方法、資 D-IV-2 數位資料的表示方法；國中的學習內容並沒有直接列出「壓縮」。

    Classroom Activity 2 · 課堂活動二

    Check It on Your Own Computer 在自己的電腦上驗證

    You need · 準備材料

    • A Windows or Mac computer一台 Windows 或 Mac 電腦
    • A copy of a long text file you made, and a copy of a JPEG photo you took一份你自己做的長文字檔的副本，和一張你自己拍的 JPEG 照片的副本
    • Paper and a pencil紙和鉛筆

    Steps · 步驟

    1. Write down the size of each file before you start.開始之前，先把兩個檔案的大小記下來。
    2. On a Mac, Control-click the file and choose Compress. On Windows, right-click the file and select Show more options, then Send to, then Compressed (zipped) folder.Mac：按住 Control 鍵點一下檔案，選 Compress。Windows：在檔案上按右鍵，依序選 Show more options、Send to、Compressed (zipped) folder。（中文介面的選單名稱會不一樣，找意思相同的那一項。）
    3. Write down the size of each new .zip file. Which file shrank more?把兩個新的 .zip 檔的大小記下來。哪一個縮得比較多？
    4. Unzip the text file and open it. Is every word still there?把文字檔解壓縮，打開來看。每一個字都還在嗎？

    The text file should shrink a good deal. The photo should stay about the same size, because a JPEG has already been compressed. Menu names differ a little between versions of Windows and macOS. Measure for yourself: the exact numbers depend on your files.
    文字檔應該會縮小不少。照片的大小應該差不多，因為 JPEG 本來就已經壓縮過了。不同版本的 Windows 和 macOS，選單的名稱會略有不同。請自己量量看：實際的數字要看你的檔案。

    Sources · 資料出處

    Facts on this page were checked against these sources (October 2026); the picture wall is our own illustration, and every number is tested · 本頁的事實依下列資料查證（2026 年 10 月）；像素牆是我們自己的示意，每一個數字都有測試

    1. Data compression is “the process of reducing the amount of data needed for the storage or transmission of a given piece of information”; Morse Code assigned the shortest codes to the most common characters, and telephony cuts off high frequencies; “A rat ate a tart at a tea” takes 36 bits at two bits a letter and 32 bits with the codes a: 0, t: 10, r: 110, e: 111; lossless compression can be reversed to yield the original data, while lossy compression loses detail; lossless compression is necessary for text; Zip uses lossless compression; run-length encoding replaces repetitive data by a count and one copy of the repeated item; the Lempel-Ziv algorithm, and Zip’s use of variations of it; JPEG and MPEG use both lossless and lossy techniques; English text can generally be compressed to one-half or one-third of its original size資料壓縮是「減少儲存或傳送一份資訊所需資料量的過程」；摩斯電碼把最短的碼分給最常用的字元，電話切掉高頻；「A rat ate a tart at a tea」每個字母兩個位元要 36 個位元，用 a: 0、t: 10、r: 110、e: 111 只要 32 個；無失真壓縮可以還原出原來的資料，失真壓縮會失去細節；文字必須用無失真壓縮；Zip 用無失真壓縮；運行長度編碼把重複的資料換成「次數＋一份」；Lempel-Ziv 演算法，以及 Zip 使用它的變化版本；JPEG 與 MPEG 同時使用無失真與失真的技術；英文文字一般可以壓到原來的二分之一到三分之一David Hemmendinger, “data compression,” Encyclopaedia Britannica ↗
    2. The Joint Photographic Experts Group was formed in 1986; the JPEG standard “works by averaging colour variation and discarding what the human eye cannot see, a process known as ‘lossy’ compression”聯合圖像專家小組 1986 年成立；JPEG 標準把顏色變化平均掉，丟掉人眼看不出來的部分，這個過程稱為「失真」壓縮The Editors of Encyclopaedia Britannica, “JPEG” (revised by Erik Gregersen) ↗
    3. The MPEG-1 standard was released in 1993; MP3 encoding reduced the size of a CD audio file “by discarding certain sounds based on assumptions of what the ear is least likely to miss”; “the data loss from compression was not reversible”MPEG-1 標準 1993 年發布；MP3 編碼根據「耳朵最不容易察覺少了什麼」丟掉某些聲音，把 CD 音訊檔變小；壓縮造成的資料損失無法復原The Editors of Encyclopaedia Britannica, “MP3” ↗
    4. “Zipped (compressed) files take up less storage space and can be transferred to other computers more quickly than uncompressed files”; right-click the file, select Show more options > Send to > Compressed (zipped) folder; “Some types of files, like JPEG images, are already highly compressed,” so a zipped folder of them is about the same size壓縮（zip）過的檔案占的儲存空間比較少，傳到其他電腦也比較快；在檔案上按右鍵，選 Show more options > Send to > Compressed (zipped) folder；JPEG 圖片這類檔案本來就已經高度壓縮，壓成資料夾後大小差不多Microsoft Support, “Zip and unzip files” ↗
    5. “Compressed files take up less disk space than uncompressed files”; to compress a file or folder, Control-click it, then choose Compress壓縮過的檔案占的磁碟空間比較少；要壓縮檔案或資料夾，按住 Control 鍵點一下，選 CompressApple Support, “Zip and unzip files and folders on Mac” ↗

For lossless and lossy compression the term bank gives 無損壓縮／不失真壓縮 and 有損壓縮／失真壓縮; this page uses 無失真壓縮 and 失真壓縮, the wording common in Taiwan’s textbooks台灣用語：run length encoding 運行長度編碼。lossless／lossy compression 樂詞網作「無損壓縮／不失真壓縮」「有損壓縮／失真壓縮」；本頁用台灣教科書常見的「無失真壓縮」「失真壓縮」國家教育研究院《樂詞網》 ↗
