Why Do Flowers Bloom?花為什麼要開？

Flowers are not there to look pretty for us. They are how a plant makes seeds, and a plant cannot walk over to another plant. In a 3D model, cut a flower open and follow one grain of pollen: carried in by a bee, caught by the stigma, and then growing a tube down to a waiting seed-to-be. Switch to the wind and see why some plants must throw away so much pollen. Last, look at a flower and work out who delivers for it.
花不是為了讓我們覺得好看才開的。花是植物製造種子的方法，而植物沒辦法走到另一株植物那裡去。在 3D 模型裡把一朵花切開，跟著一粒花粉走：被蜜蜂帶進來、被柱頭接住，然後長出一條管子，通到等在那裡的「未來的種子」。換成風來送，看看為什麼有些植物非得浪費那麼多花粉不可。最後看著一朵花，推理出是誰在替它送貨。

Big idea · 一句話看懂

A flower is the part of a plant that makes seeds. To make a seed, pollen from one flower has to reach the stigma of another. A plant cannot move, so it uses a carrier. Many plants hire animals, and they pay with nectar and advertise with color and scent. Others let the wind do it and make huge amounts of pollen. When pollen arrives, it grows a tube down to an ovule. The ovule becomes a seed, and the part around it becomes a fruit.
花是植物用來製造種子的部分。要做出種子，一朵花的花粉必須送到另一朵花的柱頭上。植物不能移動，所以要找人送貨。很多植物雇用動物，用花蜜付工錢，用顏色和香味打廣告；另外一些讓風來送，於是得製造非常大量的花粉。花粉送到以後，會長出一條管子通到胚珠。胚珠變成種子，包著它的部分變成果實。

What the plant pays · 植物付的報酬

A bee is coming for the nectar, the sweet drink at the bottom of the flower. The bright petals showed it where to land. It has just been to another flower of the same kind, and that flower's pollen is stuck to its body.一隻蜜蜂為了花蜜而來——花朵底部那種甜甜的飲料。鮮豔的花瓣告訴牠該降落在哪裡。牠剛剛去過另一朵同一種的花，那朵花的花粉還黏在牠身上。

As the bee pushes in, it brushes against the stigma, the sticky tip in the middle, and leaves pollen there. It also picks up fresh pollen from the anthers to carry to the next flower. The bee gets a meal, and the plant gets a delivery.蜜蜂往裡面鑽的時候，擦過了正中央那個黏黏的頂端——柱頭，把花粉留在上面。同時牠又從花藥沾了新的花粉，帶去下一朵花。蜜蜂吃到一餐，植物的貨也送到了。

This flower has no bright petals, no scent, and almost no nectar. It does not need them, because the wind does not have to be paid. But the wind does not aim either. The plant lets go of a great cloud of light, dry pollen, and nearly all of it is lost.這朵花沒有鮮豔的花瓣、沒有香味，也幾乎沒有花蜜。它用不著這些，因為風不必付工錢。可是風也不會瞄準。植物放出一大片又輕又乾的花粉，其中幾乎全部都白白飄走了。

One grain has been caught. Look at the stigma of this flower. It is large and feathery, like a net held up in the air. In the model, one grain in forty arrives. For real plants the chance is far smaller.有一粒被接住了。看看這朵花的柱頭：又大、又像羽毛，就像舉在空中的一張網。在模型裡，四十粒當中到了一粒；真的植物，機會還要小得多。

The pollen grain is on the stigma, but the ovules are far below. So the grain grows a thin tube down through the middle of the flower. The tube carries what the ovule is waiting for.花粉粒到了柱頭，可是胚珠還在下面很遠的地方。於是花粉粒長出一條細細的管子，穿過花的正中央往下走。管子帶著胚珠在等的東西。

The tube has reached an ovule, and the ovule is turning into a seed. Now the flower's work is done. The petals fall, and the ovary around the seed swells into a fruit. The other ovules got no pollen, so they will not become seeds.管子通到了一顆胚珠，那顆胚珠正在變成種子。花的工作到這裡就完成了：花瓣掉落，包著種子的子房膨大成為果實。另外幾顆胚珠沒有等到花粉，就不會變成種子。

Unit 6: Why Do Flowers Bloom?

A plant has a problem that an animal does not have. To make seeds, it usually needs something from another plant of its own kind. But it is rooted to the ground and cannot go and get it. A flower is the answer to that problem.

植物有一個動物沒有的難題。要做出種子，它通常需要另一株同類植物身上的東西；可是它的根扎在地上，沒辦法自己過去拿。花，就是用來解決這個難題的。

Look into the middle of a flower. Around the center there is a ring of thin stalks, each with a small bag on top. The bags are called anthers, and they make pollen, a fine yellow dust. Right in the center stands the stigma. It is often sticky, and below it, at the bottom of the flower, are the ovules. Each ovule can become a seed.

往一朵花的中間看。圍著中心有一圈細細的柄，每一根的頂端有一個小袋子。這些小袋子叫做花藥，會製造花粉——一種細細的黃色粉末。正中央立著的是柱頭，通常黏黏的；在它的下方、花的最底部，是胚珠。每一顆胚珠都可能變成一粒種子。

A seed begins when pollen from one flower lands on the stigma of another. Something has to carry it there. About four out of five kinds of flowering plants use animals: bees, butterflies, moths, flies, beetles, birds, and bats. The animal comes for nectar, a sugary drink, and carries pollen away on its body without meaning to.

一朵花的花粉落到另一朵花的柱頭上，種子才會開始形成。花粉得有人送過去。每五種開花植物裡，大約有四種靠動物：蜜蜂、蝴蝶、蛾、蠅、甲蟲、鳥，還有蝙蝠。動物是為了花蜜（一種含糖的飲料）而來，花粉沾在身上，就這樣在不知不覺中被帶走了。

This is why so many flowers are bright and sweet-smelling. The color and the scent are advertising, and the nectar is the pay. Some flowers even have lines on their petals that point the way to the nectar.

所以才有這麼多花又鮮豔又香。顏色和香味是廣告，花蜜是工錢。有些花的花瓣上甚至有線條，指出花蜜在哪裡。

Other plants use the wind. Grasses and oak trees are like this. Their flowers are small and dull, with no scent, because the wind does not need to be invited. But the wind does not aim, so these plants make enormous amounts of light pollen, and they catch it with stigmas shaped like feathers.

另外一些植物靠風。禾草和橡樹就是這樣。它們的花又小又不起眼，也沒有香味，因為風用不著邀請。可是風不會瞄準，所以這些植物會製造非常大量、非常輕的花粉，再用羽毛形狀的柱頭去接。

When a grain of pollen lands on the right stigma, it grows a thin tube down to an ovule. Then the ovule becomes a seed, and the part of the flower around it, the ovary, grows into a fruit. A tomato, a peach, and a bean pod were all once the middle of a flower.

一粒花粉落在對的柱頭上以後，會長出一條細細的管子，通到一顆胚珠。接著胚珠變成種子，而花朵裡包著它的那個部分——子房——長成果實。番茄、桃子和豆莢，原本都是一朵花的中心。

Questions · 閱讀理解

What problem does a flower solve for a plant?

A plant cannot move, so it cannot go to another plant to get pollen. A flower lets something else carry the pollen.

Why are many flowers bright and sweet-smelling?

The color and the scent are advertising for animals, and the nectar is their pay for carrying pollen.

What does the ovary of a flower become?

It becomes a fruit, with the seeds inside it.

Flowers by the Numbers · 花的數字

Six numbers about flowers 六個和花有關的數字

Who carries the pollen, and how far it has to go.
誰在送花粉，花粉又要走多遠。

80%of flowering plants · 的開花植物

About this share rely on living carriers, mostly insects, to move their pollen.大約這麼高的比例，是靠生物（大多是昆蟲）來搬運花粉的。

4rings · 圈

A typical flower is built in four rings: sepals, petals, stamens, and the pistil in the middle.典型的花由四圈構成：萼片、花瓣、雄蕊，還有正中央的雌蕊。

30 cmand more · 以上

In corn, a single pollen tube can grow longer than this to reach its ovule.玉米的一條花粉管可以長得比這還長，才到得了胚珠。

1793the year · 年

Christian Konrad Sprengel published the idea that flowers exist to attract insects.施普倫格爾發表了他的看法：花的存在是為了吸引昆蟲。

0paid · 報酬

Wind-pollinated flowers make little or no nectar and no scent. The wind works for free.靠風傳粉的花幾乎不製造花蜜，也沒有香味。風是免費的。

1793

The secret of nature大自然的祕密

Christian Konrad Sprengel was a German teacher who spent years watching insects visit flowers. In 1793 he published a book explaining that the purpose of a flower is to attract insects, and that nature favors pollen brought from another plant. Few people took notice. His work was not widely recognized until Charles Darwin tested and confirmed his observations many years later.
施普倫格爾是一位德國教師，花了好多年觀察昆蟲造訪花朵。1793 年他出版了一本書，說明花的功用是吸引昆蟲，而且大自然偏好從別株帶來的花粉。當時沒有什麼人注意。直到很多年以後，達爾文檢驗並證實了他的觀察，他的研究才被廣泛認可。

Ultraviolet · 紫外光

Lines we cannot see我們看不見的線條

Some flowers have marks on their petals that guide a visitor to the nectar, like lines painted on a runway. In some plants, such as the meadow buttercup, the marks show up only in ultraviolet light, which human eyes cannot see. To us the flower is plain yellow. Seen in ultraviolet, it has a dark center where the nectar is.
有些花的花瓣上有記號，把訪客引到花蜜那裡，就像畫在跑道上的線。有些植物（例如草地毛茛）的記號只有在紫外光下才看得出來，而人的眼睛看不見紫外光。在我們眼裡，那朵花是一片單純的黃色；在紫外光下，它的中心是深色的，花蜜就在那裡。

Kitchen · 廚房

Fruits in disguise偽裝的果實

To a cook, a fruit is something sweet. To a botanist, a fruit is whatever grows from the ovary of a flower and holds the seeds. By that meaning, bean pods and nuts are fruits as well. Next time you cut one open, look for the seeds, and remember that each one began with a grain of pollen.
對廚師來說，水果是甜的東西。對植物學家來說，凡是由花的子房長成、裡面裝著種子的，都是果實。照這個意思，豆莢和堅果也是果實。下次切開它們的時候，找找裡面的種子，並且記得：每一粒種子，最初都是從一粒花粉開始的。

Taiwan · 台灣

Why the fields of Tianwei are lit at night田尾的田為什麼晚上點燈

Chrysanthemums bloom in autumn, when the nights grow long. The plant measures the length of the night, and a long night is its signal to make flower buds. Growers in Tianwei, Changhua, hang lamps over their fields and switch them on after dark. The night is made short, so the plants keep growing and do not bloom. When the growers want flowers, they turn off the lamps. That is how flowers can be ready for the right day, on long stems.
菊花在秋天開花，那時候夜晚變長了。菊花會測量夜晚的長度，夜一長，就是它長出花苞的信號。彰化田尾的花農在田裡吊起燈泡，天黑之後點亮，把長夜變成短夜，菊花就繼續長高而不開花。等到想要花的時候，再把燈關掉。這樣花就能在需要的日子開，而且花莖夠長。

Myth vs. Fact · 常見迷思

Four things people get wrong 四個常見的誤會

✗ Myth · 迷思Flowers are there to look pretty.花是為了好看才開的。

✓ Fact · 事實Flowers are there to make seeds. The color is advertising for the animals that carry pollen.花是為了製造種子。顏色是給搬運花粉的動物看的廣告。

✗ Myth · 迷思Bees visit flowers to help the plant.蜜蜂去採花是為了幫植物的忙。

✓ Fact · 事實Bees come for nectar and pollen to eat. Carrying pollen between flowers is a side effect.蜜蜂是為了吃花蜜和花粉而來。在花與花之間搬運花粉，只是順帶發生的事。

✗ Myth · 迷思Grass and trees like oaks have no flowers.草和橡樹這類的樹不會開花。

✓ Fact · 事實They do. Their flowers are small and plain because the wind carries their pollen.它們會開花，只是花又小又樸素，因為它們的花粉是風送的。

✗ Myth · 迷思Fruits and flowers are separate things.果實和花是兩回事。

✓ Fact · 事實A fruit grows from the ovary of a flower. Every fruit was once part of a flower.果實是由花的子房長成的。每一顆果實，都曾經是花的一部分。

在花粉管抵達之後。

An Observer's Code · 觀察守則

Watching flowers without harming them 看花，但不傷害它們

  • Pick only flowers that you have permission to pick, and never pick wild flowers in a park or a nature reserve.只摘得到允許的花；公園和保護區裡的野花不要摘。
  • Watch bees and wasps from an arm's length away, and do not wave at them or try to touch them.觀察蜜蜂和胡蜂時，保持一隻手臂的距離，不要揮趕，也不要去碰。
  • Leave every insect where you found it.每一隻昆蟲都留在原來的地方。

Classroom Activity 1 · 課堂活動一

Take a Flower Apart 把一朵花拆開

You need · 準備材料

  • One large flower that you are allowed to pick, such as a lily or a hibiscus一朵得到允許可以摘的大花，例如百合或朱槿
  • A sheet of white paper, tape, and a magnifying glass一張白紙、膠帶和一支放大鏡

Steps · 步驟

  1. Starting from the outside, gently pull off each ring of parts and tape it to the paper in a row.從最外面開始，輕輕把每一圈的構造摘下來，在紙上貼成一排。
  2. Label each row: sepals, petals, stamens, and the pistil in the middle.替每一排寫上名稱：萼片、花瓣、雄蕊，還有正中央的雌蕊。
  3. Touch an anther to the paper. Look at the dust it leaves through the magnifying glass.拿一個花藥在紙上輕輕碰一下，用放大鏡看它留下的粉末。
  4. Ask an adult to cut the bottom of the pistil across. Can you see the ovules inside?請大人把雌蕊的底部橫切開來。你看得到裡面的胚珠嗎？

Lily pollen stains cloth, so cover the table. Count the parts in each ring. In many flowers the numbers are related, such as three and six, or five and ten.
百合的花粉會把布染色，桌面先鋪一層紙。數一數每一圈有幾個；很多花各圈的數目是有關係的，例如三和六，或是五和十。

Classroom Activity 2 · 課堂活動二

Ten Minutes at a Flower 在一朵花旁邊待十分鐘

You need · 準備材料

  • A notebook and a pencil一本筆記本和一支鉛筆
  • A patch of flowers outdoors on a warm, dry day晴朗溫暖的日子裡，戶外的一小片花

Steps · 步驟

  1. Choose one flower, or one small group of flowers, and sit where you can see it clearly.選一朵花，或一小叢花，坐在看得清楚的地方。
  2. For ten minutes, make a mark for every visitor: bee, butterfly, fly, beetle, or something else.在十分鐘裡，每來一位訪客就畫一個記號：蜜蜂、蝴蝶、蠅、甲蟲，或是別的。
  3. For each visitor, notice where it lands and which parts of the flower it touches.留意每一位訪客降落在哪裡、碰到了花的哪些部分。
  4. Do the same at a different kind of flower. Are the visitors the same?換一種花再做一次。來的訪客一樣嗎？

Try it at different times of day. Some flowers open only in the morning, and some are visited mostly at dusk.
在一天裡不同的時間試試看。有些花只在早上開，有些花的訪客大多在黃昏才來。
