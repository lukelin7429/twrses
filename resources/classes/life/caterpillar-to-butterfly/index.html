How Does a Caterpillar Become a Butterfly?毛毛蟲怎麼變成蝴蝶？

A caterpillar and a butterfly do not look like relatives, and yet they are the same animal at two different ages. In a 3D model, follow one insect from an egg on a leaf to a caterpillar that outgrows its skin again and again, and then into a chrysalis. Look inside the chrysalis while the body is taken apart and built again. Watch the new butterfly open its wings. Last, find out which insects go through this great change and which do not.
毛毛蟲和蝴蝶看起來一點也不像親戚，但牠們其實是同一隻動物，只是年紀不同。在 3D 模型裡，跟著一隻昆蟲，從葉子上的卵，到一次又一次把皮撐破的毛毛蟲，再到蛹。看看蛹的裡面：身體被拆開，再重新蓋起來。看剛出來的蝴蝶把翅膀撐開。最後查一查，哪些昆蟲會經歷這樣的大改造，哪些不會。

Big idea · 一句話看懂

A butterfly lives its life in four stages: egg, caterpillar, chrysalis, and adult. The caterpillar has one job, which is to eat and grow. The adult has a different job, which is to fly, find a mate, and lay eggs. Between them comes the chrysalis. Inside it, most of the caterpillar's body is broken down, and the adult's body grows from small groups of cells that the caterpillar carried all along. Both bodies are built from the same set of instructions.
蝴蝶的一生分成四個階段：卵、毛毛蟲、蛹、成蟲。毛毛蟲只有一個工作：吃，然後長大。成蟲的工作不一樣：飛行、找伴侶、產卵。夾在中間的是蛹。在蛹裡面，毛毛蟲大部分的身體被分解掉，而成蟲的身體，是從毛毛蟲一直帶在身上的幾小團細胞長出來的。兩種身體，用的是同一份說明書。

A butterfly has laid one egg on a leaf. She chose this plant with care, because it is the food her young will need. Inside the egg, a tiny caterpillar is forming.一隻蝴蝶在葉子上產下了一顆卵。這株植物是牠仔細挑過的，因為這就是牠的孩子要吃的食物。卵裡面，一隻小小的毛毛蟲正在成形。

The caterpillar is an eating machine. Its skin cannot stretch very far, so when it gets too tight the caterpillar sheds it and comes out in a larger one. Each of these steps is a jump in size.毛毛蟲是一部專門吃東西的機器。牠的皮撐不了多大，等皮太緊了，牠就把舊皮蛻掉，換上一層比較大的。每蛻一次皮，體型就跳大一號。

The caterpillar has shed its skin one last time, and what is underneath is not another caterpillar. It is a chrysalis. From the outside it hangs still, as if nothing were happening. Switch on See inside the chrysalis.毛毛蟲最後一次蛻皮，底下出現的不再是毛毛蟲，而是一個蛹。從外面看，它一動也不動地掛著，好像什麼事都沒有發生。打開「看蛹裡面」看看。

Inside, the caterpillar's body is being taken apart. Its materials are reused. At the same time, wings, legs, and the rest of the adult body are growing from small groups of cells that were tucked inside the caterpillar from the start.在裡面，毛毛蟲的身體正在被拆解，材料拿來重新利用。同時，翅膀、腳和成蟲身體的其他部分，正從幾小團細胞長出來——這些細胞從一開始就藏在毛毛蟲的身體裡。

The skin of the chrysalis has split, and the butterfly has climbed out. Its wings are small, soft, and crumpled. It hangs still while they open and dry. It cannot fly yet.蛹的皮裂開了，蝴蝶爬了出來。牠的翅膀又小、又軟、皺成一團。牠掛著不動，等翅膀撐開、變乾。現在牠還不能飛。

The wings are open and firm, and the butterfly can fly. It will never grow any bigger. It no longer chews leaves, and it drinks nectar through a long tube. Its job now is to find a mate and lay eggs, and the story begins again.翅膀張開、變硬了，蝴蝶可以飛了。牠再也不會長大。牠不再啃葉子，而是用一根長長的管子吸花蜜。現在牠的工作是找伴侶、產卵，然後故事重新開始。

Unit 11: How Does a Caterpillar Become a Butterfly?

If you had never been told, would you guess that a caterpillar and a butterfly are the same animal? One crawls and chews leaves. The other flies and sips from flowers. They have different mouths, different legs, and different eyes. And yet every butterfly was once a caterpillar.

要是從來沒有人告訴你，你猜得到毛毛蟲和蝴蝶是同一種動物嗎？一個用爬的、啃葉子；另一個用飛的、吸花蜜。牠們的嘴不一樣、腳不一樣、眼睛也不一樣。可是每一隻蝴蝶，都曾經是一隻毛毛蟲。

The story starts with an egg. A female butterfly lays it on a plant that her young can eat. Out of the egg comes a tiny caterpillar, and it starts eating at once.

故事從一顆卵開始。雌蝶把卵產在孩子可以吃的植物上。從卵裡出來一隻很小的毛毛蟲，一出來就開始吃。

A caterpillar's whole life is eating and growing. Its skin does not grow with it, so from time to time the old skin splits and the caterpillar crawls out in a new, larger one. A monarch caterpillar does this in five steps.

毛毛蟲的一生就是吃和長大。牠的皮不會跟著長，所以每隔一陣子，舊皮就裂開，毛毛蟲穿著一層比較大的新皮爬出來。帝王斑蝶的毛毛蟲分五個階段這樣長大。

When it is fully grown, the caterpillar stops eating and sheds its skin one last time. What appears is a chrysalis, a hard case that hangs still. Inside, most of the caterpillar's body is broken down. The adult's body is built from small groups of cells that the caterpillar has carried since it hatched. These cells become the wings, the legs, and the eyes of the butterfly.

長到最大以後，毛毛蟲不再吃東西，最後一次蛻皮。這次出現的是蛹——一個掛著不動的硬殼。在裡面，毛毛蟲大部分的身體被分解掉；成蟲的身體，是用毛毛蟲從孵化起就帶在身上的幾小團細胞蓋起來的。這些細胞變成蝴蝶的翅膀、腳和眼睛。

At last the case splits, and the butterfly climbs out. Its wings are soft and folded. It hangs and waits while they open and dry, and then it flies away.

最後殼裂開，蝴蝶爬出來。牠的翅膀又軟又皺。牠掛著等翅膀撐開、變乾，然後才飛走。

Why go to so much trouble? One reason is that the young and the adults do not get in each other's way. The caterpillar eats leaves, and the butterfly drinks nectar, so they are not competing for food. Each body is built for one job.

為什麼要這麼大費周章？原因之一是：小的和大的不會互相妨礙。毛毛蟲吃葉子，蝴蝶喝花蜜，所以牠們不必搶食物。每一種身體，都只為了一種工作而打造。

Questions · 閱讀理解

What are the four stages in the life of a butterfly?

Egg, caterpillar, chrysalis, and adult.

Why does a caterpillar shed its skin?

Its skin does not grow with it, so it has to change to a larger one.

What is one advantage of having two very different bodies?

The young and the adults eat different food, so they do not compete with each other.

    Change by the Numbers · 變態的數字

    Six numbers about growing up as an insect 六個和昆蟲長大有關的數字

    How many stages, how many skins, and how many butterflies in one valley.
    幾個階段、幾層皮，還有一座山谷裡有多少隻蝴蝶。

    4stages · 個階段

    Egg, larva, pupa, and adult. Butterflies, beetles, flies, and bees all grow up this way.卵、幼蟲、蛹、成蟲。蝴蝶、甲蟲、蒼蠅和蜜蜂都是這樣長大的。

    Egg, nymph, and adult. Grasshoppers, dragonflies, and cicadas skip the pupa.卵、若蟲、成蟲。蝗蟲、蜻蜓和蟬沒有蛹的階段。

    5growth steps · 齡

    A monarch caterpillar grows in five steps, shedding its skin at the end of each.帝王斑蝶的毛毛蟲分五個階段長大，每個階段結束時蛻一次皮。

    2weeks · 個星期

    A monarch comes out of its chrysalis after about this long.帝王斑蝶大約過這麼久，就從蛹裡出來。

    14 cmlong · 長

    The largest caterpillars reach this length. The smallest are about 1 millimeter.最大的毛毛蟲可以長到這麼長；最小的大約只有 1 毫米。

    Butterfly Stories · 蝴蝶的故事

    A painter who watched, a memory that survives, a worm that spins, and a purple valley 一位仔細觀察的畫家、留下來的記憶、會吐絲的蟲，還有一座紫色的山谷

    What people have learned by keeping caterpillars and waiting.
    人們養著毛毛蟲、耐心等待之後學到的事。

    1647–1717

    The painter who watched仔細觀察的畫家

    In the 1600s, many people in Europe believed that insects simply appeared by themselves, without parents. Maria Sibylla Merian, a German artist, did not take anyone's word for it. She kept caterpillars, fed them, and painted every stage as it changed into the next. Her careful pictures helped to show that insects have a life cycle, and that each butterfly comes from an egg laid by another butterfly.
    十七世紀的歐洲，很多人相信昆蟲是自己冒出來的，沒有爸媽。德國畫家梅里安不肯人云亦云。她自己養毛毛蟲、餵牠們，把每一個階段怎麼變成下一個階段都畫下來。她仔細的畫作，幫助人們明白昆蟲有自己的生命週期：每一隻蝴蝶，都來自另一隻蝴蝶產下的卵。

    2008

    Does a moth remember being a caterpillar?蛾記得自己當過毛毛蟲嗎？

    If the body is taken apart inside the pupa, is everything lost? In 2008, scientists reported an experiment with a moth called the tobacco hornworm. They trained the caterpillars to behave in a certain way. After the change, the adult moths still behaved that way. So something is carried through, and the pupa is not a completely fresh start.
    身體在蛹裡被拆開，是不是什麼都沒留下？2008 年，科學家發表了一項用菸草天蛾做的實驗：他們訓練毛毛蟲學會一種行為，結果變成成蟲以後，這些蛾仍然保有這種行為。所以有些東西被帶過去了——蛹並不是徹底的從頭來過。

    Silk · 絲

    A case made of thread用線做的殼

    A butterfly's chrysalis is bare. Many moths add something more. A silkworm, which is the caterpillar of a moth, eats mulberry leaves and sheds its skin four times. Then it spins a cocoon of silk around itself and becomes a pupa inside. People learned long ago to unwind that thread and weave it into cloth.
    蝴蝶的蛹是光溜溜的；很多蛾則多加了一層。蠶是一種蛾的幼蟲，牠吃桑葉、蛻四次皮，然後吐絲把自己包成一個繭，在裡面變成蛹。人類很早以前就學會把這條絲線抽出來，織成布。

    Taiwan · 台灣

    The purple butterfly valleys紫蝶幽谷

    The purple crow butterflies of Taiwan do something unusual. Before winter, great numbers of adults fly south and gather in sheltered valleys, where they wait out the cold together. In spring they fly north again, along a route that passes over Bagua Mountain in Changhua. The Maolin National Scenic Area explains that a purple butterfly valley is not one place but a way of living. Taiwan has more than thirty such valleys, and Maolin in Kaohsiung has at least ten.
    台灣的紫斑蝶有不尋常的做法：冬天來臨之前，大批成蟲往南飛，聚在背風的山谷裡，一起度過寒冷的日子；春天再往北飛回去，北返的路線會經過彰化的八卦山。茂林國家風景區管理處說明：「紫蝶幽谷」不是一個地名，而是一種生態現象；台灣有超過三十處這樣的山谷，高雄的茂林就有至少十處。

    Myth vs. Fact · 常見迷思

    Four things people get wrong 四個常見的誤會

    ✗ Myth · 迷思A caterpillar and a butterfly are two different animals.毛毛蟲和蝴蝶是兩種不同的動物。

    ✓ Fact · 事實They are one animal at two stages of its life.牠們是同一隻動物的兩個生命階段。

    ✗ Myth · 迷思The caterpillar rests inside the chrysalis and grows wings.毛毛蟲在蛹裡休息，然後長出翅膀。

    ✓ Fact · 事實Most of its body is broken down, and a new body is built.牠大部分的身體被分解掉，再蓋出一個新的身體。

    ✗ Myth · 迷思A small butterfly will grow into a big one.小蝴蝶會長成大蝴蝶。

    ✓ Fact · 事實Adult butterflies do not grow. All the growing is done by the caterpillar.蝴蝶成蟲不會再長大；所有的成長都是毛毛蟲完成的。

    ✗ Myth · 迷思All insects have a caterpillar stage and a pupa.所有的昆蟲都有毛毛蟲和蛹的階段。

    ✓ Fact · 事實Grasshoppers, dragonflies, and cicadas have no pupa. Their young are called nymphs.蝗蟲、蜻蜓和蟬沒有蛹；牠們的幼體叫做若蟲。

    An Observer's Code · 觀察守則

    Watching without harming 觀察，但不傷害

    • Do not touch hairy caterpillars. Some have hairs that can irritate the skin.不要摸有毛的毛毛蟲，有些種類的毛會讓皮膚發癢、紅腫。
    • Never pull open a chrysalis or help a butterfly out of it. It needs to do this by itself.不要把蛹剝開，也不要「幫」蝴蝶出來；牠必須靠自己完成。
    • If you raise a caterpillar, feed it leaves from the plant you found it on, and let the adult go where you found it.如果要養毛毛蟲，請餵牠原本所在的那種植物的葉子；羽化以後，在發現牠的地方把成蟲放走。

    Classroom Activity 1 · 課堂活動一

    A Life Cycle Wheel 生命週期轉盤

    You need · 準備材料

    • Two paper plates, a paper fastener, scissors, and colored pencils兩個紙盤、一個雙腳釘、剪刀和色鉛筆
    • A ruler一把尺

    Steps · 步驟

    1. Divide one plate into four equal parts. Draw one stage in each part, in order around the plate: egg, caterpillar, chrysalis, butterfly.把一個紙盤分成四等份，照順序繞著盤子，每一格畫一個階段：卵、毛毛蟲、蛹、蝴蝶。
    2. Cut one quarter out of the second plate to make a window.把第二個紙盤剪掉四分之一，做成一個窗口。
    3. Fix the second plate on top of the first with the fastener through the middle.用雙腳釘穿過中心，把第二個盤子固定在第一個上面。
    4. Turn the top plate, and tell the story of each stage as it appears. What is the animal's job at this stage?轉動上面的盤子，每出現一個階段就講一講它的故事：這個階段，牠的工作是什麼？

    Make a second wheel for a grasshopper, with three parts. Put the two side by side and explain the difference to someone at home.
    再替蝗蟲做一個三等份的轉盤。把兩個擺在一起，把它們的差別講給家人聽。

    Classroom Activity 2 · 課堂活動二

    The Leaf Detective 葉子偵探

    You need · 準備材料

    • A notebook, a pencil, and a magnifying glass一本筆記本、一支鉛筆和一支放大鏡
    • A garden, a park, or a row of plants at school一座花園、公園，或學校裡的一排植物

    Steps · 步驟

    1. Look for leaves with holes or chewed edges. Something has been eating here.找找有洞或邊緣被啃過的葉子。有東西在這裡吃過飯。
    2. Check the underside of the leaf and the stems nearby, without picking anything.看看葉子的背面和附近的莖，什麼都不要摘。
    3. Draw what you find: eggs, a caterpillar, droppings, an empty skin, or a chrysalis.把找到的東西畫下來：卵、毛毛蟲、糞便、蛻下來的空皮，或是蛹。
    4. Go back to the same plant a week later. What has changed?一個星期後回到同一株植物。有什麼不一樣了？

    Many caterpillars eat only one kind of plant, so the plant is a clue to which butterfly it will be.
    很多毛毛蟲只吃一種植物，所以從植物就能推測牠會變成哪一種蝴蝶。
