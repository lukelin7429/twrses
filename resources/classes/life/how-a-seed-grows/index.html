Living Things · Unit 2 · Lesson 5 · 生命與生態 單元二 第五課

How Does a Seed Know When to Grow?種子怎麼知道什麼時候該發芽？

A seed can lie in a paper packet for years and do nothing. Put it in damp soil, and within days it is a plant. In a 3D model, give a seed water and warmth and watch it wake: first a root going down, then a shoot going up. Turn the seed upside down and see whether it gets confused. Grow it in the dark and see what goes wrong. Last, decide which seeds in four different places will wake up.
種子放在紙袋裡，可以好幾年一點動靜也沒有；把它放進溼溼的土裡，幾天之內就變成一株植物。在 3D 模型裡給種子水和溫度，看它醒過來：先是一條根往下，再是一根芽往上。把種子倒過來放，看它會不會搞錯方向。把它放在暗處，看看會出什麼問題。最後判斷看看：放在四個不同地方的種子，哪幾顆會醒？

Big idea · 一句話看懂

A seed is a baby plant, asleep, packed together with its lunch inside a tough coat. It stays asleep until it has water, the right warmth, and air. Then it swells and splits its coat. The root comes out first and grows down, and the shoot follows and grows up. The young plant lives on its packed lunch until its leaves reach the light and it can make food for itself.
種子是一株睡著的小植物，和它的便當一起包在一層堅韌的種皮裡。它會一直睡，直到有了水、合適的溫度和空氣。這時它吸水膨脹，把種皮撐破。根最先出來，往下長；芽跟在後面，往上長。小植物靠著便當過日子，直到葉子碰到陽光、能夠自己做食物為止。

What the seed is waiting for · 種子在等什麼

Packed lunch left in the seed · 種子裡的便當還剩多少

The seed is · 種子現在
It has grown · 已經長出

Too dry. Without water a seed stays asleep, and it can stay that way for a very long time. A dry seed is not dead. It is waiting. If it has already started to grow, it stops.太乾了。沒有水，種子就繼續睡，而且可以這樣睡上很久很久。乾的種子並沒有死，它在等。要是已經開始長了，這時候也會停下來。

Too much water. The soil is flooded, and there is no air left between its grains. A waking seed needs oxygen to turn its stored food into energy, so it cannot grow here.水太多了。土壤被淹沒，土粒之間沒有空氣了。醒來的種子需要氧氣，才能把存好的養分變成能量，所以在這裡它長不了。

Too cold. There is water, but the seed still waits. Each kind of seed has a range of temperatures it will wake in. For many seeds, cold means winter, and winter is a bad time to be a small plant.太冷了。雖然有水，種子還是繼續等。每一種種子都有自己願意醒來的溫度範圍。對很多種子來說，冷代表冬天，而冬天不是當一株小苗的好時候。

Too hot. There is water, but the seed does not start. Each kind of seed has an upper limit as well as a lower one.太熱了。雖然有水，種子還是不動。每一種種子願意醒來的溫度，有下限，也有上限。

Water, warmth, and air are all right. Inside the coat are a tiny plant and two thick halves full of stored food. Press Play, or move the Growth slider, and watch it wake.水、溫度和空氣都對了。種皮裡面有一株很小的植物，還有兩片厚厚的、裝滿養分的便當。按「播放」或拉動「生長」滑桿，看它醒來。

The seed is drinking. It soaks up water and swells until the coat splits. Inside, the tiny plant starts using its stored food.種子正在喝水。它吸飽了水，脹到把種皮撐破。裡面那株小植物開始動用存好的養分。

The root comes first. It grows down, where the water is, and holds the plant in place. Look at the lunch meter. The plant has no leaves yet, so everything it builds comes out of the seed.最先出來的是根。它往下長，那裡有水，也能把植物固定住。看看便當的量表：植物還沒有葉子，所以它長出來的每一樣東西，用的都是種子裡的養分。

Now the shoot grows up toward the surface. It has never seen the light, and yet it goes the right way. A plant can feel which way is down, and its shoot grows away from it.現在芽往上長，朝地面去。它從來沒有見過光，卻走對了方向。植物感覺得出哪邊是下面，而芽會背著那個方向長。

The leaves open in the light and turn green. From now on the plant makes its own food from sunlight, air, and water. It got here just as the lunch ran out.葉子在陽光下展開，變成綠色。從現在起，植物用陽光、空氣和水自己做食物。它趕在便當吃完的時候走到了這一步。

In the dark, the shoot grows long, thin, and pale, and the leaves stay small and yellow. The plant is spending all its lunch on reaching for light. If it finds none before the lunch runs out, it cannot go on.在暗處，芽長得又長、又細、又白，葉子又小又黃。植物把便當全花在往上找光。要是便當吃完之前還找不到光，它就撐不下去了。

  • Brown: the seed coat
    咖啡色：種皮
  • Cream: the stored food, which shrinks as it is used
    米色：存好的養分（便當），用掉就變小
  • White: the root
    白色：根
  • Green: the shoot and leaves in the light
    綠色：照到光的芽和葉子
  • Pale yellow: a shoot and leaves grown in the dark
    淡黃色：在暗處長出來的芽和葉子

A teaching model. The numbers for water and warmth are examples chosen for the model. Real seeds differ a great deal: some wake just above freezing, and some need light or darkness as well. The lunch meter is also an example. In many beans the two food halves are lifted above the ground with the shoot. Here they stay where they are, to keep the picture simple. The growth shown would take days, not seconds.
這是教學用的模型。水和溫度的數字是為了模型選的例子；真的種子差別很大：有些剛過冰點就會醒，有些還需要光，或是需要黑暗。便當的量表也是示意。很多豆子的兩片便當會跟著芽一起被抬到地面上；這裡為了畫面簡單，讓它們留在原地。畫面裡的生長，實際上要花好幾天，不是幾秒鐘。

Unit 5: How Does a Seed Know When to Grow?

A dry bean looks like a small stone. You can keep it in a jar for a year, and nothing will happen. Then you put it on wet cotton, and in a few days it has a root. How did it know that the time had come?

一顆乾的豆子看起來像一粒小石頭。你把它放在罐子裡一整年，什麼事也不會發生。然後你把它放在溼棉花上，幾天之後它就長出了根。它是怎麼知道時候到了的？

A seed has three parts. On the outside is a tough coat. Inside is a very small plant, already formed, with the start of a root and the start of a shoot. The rest is stored food. In a bean, the food fills the two thick halves that you can pull apart with your fingers.

種子有三個部分。最外面是一層堅韌的種皮。裡面有一株非常小的植物，已經成形了，有根的開頭，也有芽的開頭。其餘的部分是存好的養分。以豆子來說，養分就裝在你用手指掰得開的那兩片厚厚的豆瓣裡。

The little plant is alive, but it is asleep. It will stay asleep until three things are right: water, warmth, and air. Some kinds of seed need light or darkness too. Waiting keeps a seed from starting to grow in a dry month or in the middle of winter.

這株小植物是活的，只是睡著了。它會一直睡，直到三件事都對了：水、溫度和空氣。有些種類的種子還需要光，或是需要黑暗。這樣等待，種子才不會在乾旱的月份或嚴冬裡開始生長。

When the time is right, the seed soaks up water and swells until its coat splits. The root comes out first and grows down. Then the shoot grows up. It does not matter which way up the seed is lying. A plant can feel the pull of gravity, so its root grows toward the pull and its shoot grows away from it.

時候到了，種子吸水膨脹，把種皮撐破。根最先出來，往下長；接著芽往上長。種子是正著放還是倒著放都沒有關係。植物感覺得到重力，所以根順著重力的方向長，芽背著重力的方向長。

Until its leaves open, the young plant cannot make food. It lives on what is packed in the seed, and it needs oxygen from the air to use that food. This is a race. The shoot has to reach the light before the packed lunch is gone.

在葉子展開之前，小植物沒辦法做食物。它靠種子裡裝好的養分過活，而且需要空氣裡的氧氣才能使用這些養分。這是一場賽跑：芽必須在便當吃完之前碰到陽光。

Bean sprouts show what happens without light. They are grown in the shade, so the plant keeps stretching upward, long and pale, and it does not turn green. In the light, the same plant would stay short, open green leaves, and begin to feed itself.

豆芽讓我們看到沒有光會怎麼樣。豆芽是在遮光的地方種的，所以它一直往上伸，又長又白，不會變綠。要是在有光的地方，同一株植物會長得矮矮的，展開綠色的葉子，開始自己養活自己。

Seeds by the Numbers · 種子的數字

Six numbers about seeds 六個和種子有關的數字

How many things they need, how small they can be, and how long they can wait.
它們需要幾樣東西、可以多小、可以等多久。

3parts · 個部分

A coat, a tiny plant, and stored food.種皮、一株小植物，還有存好的養分。

3needs · 樣東西

Water, the right warmth, and air. Some seeds also need light or darkness.水、合適的溫度和空氣。有些種子還需要光，或需要黑暗。

1stthe root · 先長根

The root is the first part to come out of a seed.從種子裡最先出來的是根。

1,000,000per gram · 顆一公克

Orchid seeds are the smallest of all. They are like dust, and about a million of them weigh one gram.蘭花的種子是所有種子裡最小的，像灰塵一樣，大約一百萬顆才一公克重。

1,300years · 年

A sacred lotus seed this old, found in a dry lake bed in northeastern China, was still able to grow.在中國東北一處乾涸的湖底找到的一顆古蓮子，這麼老了還能發芽生長。

2,000years · 年

A date palm seed about this old was sprouted by scientists in 2005.2005 年，科學家讓一顆大約這麼老的椰棗種子發了芽。

Seed Stories · 種子的故事

An old jar, a tilted pot, a white vegetable, and a cold room in Taichung 一個古老的罐子、一盆放倒的植物、一種白色的蔬菜，還有台中的一間冷房

What seeds can wait for, and what people have learned from them.
種子等得了什麼，人們又從它們身上學到了什麼。

2005

Two thousand years asleep睡了兩千年

In the 1960s, people digging at Masada, an ancient palace in a very dry part of Israel, found date palm seeds in an old jar. Tests showed that the seeds were about two thousand years old. In 2005, a team of scientists planted one, and it sprouted. The palm that grew from it was named Methuselah.
1960 年代，考古人員在以色列非常乾燥的地區挖掘古代宮殿馬薩達，在一個古老的罐子裡發現了椰棗的種子。檢驗的結果顯示，這些種子大約有兩千年那麼老。2005 年，一組科學家種下其中一顆，它發芽了。長出來的那棵椰棗樹被取名為「瑪土撒拉」。

Darwin · 達爾文

Which way is down?哪邊是下面？

Charles Darwin was one of the first people to study this carefully, and he wrote that roots grow toward the pull of gravity while stems grow away from it. You can see it with a potted plant. Lay the pot on its side, and in time the growing tip of the stem turns and points up again. Inside the tip of a root, tiny heavy grains of starch settle to the low side and tell the root which way is down.
達爾文是最早仔細研究這件事的人之一。他記錄下：根順著重力的方向長，莖背著重力的方向長。用一盆植物就看得到：把花盆放倒，過一陣子，莖正在生長的頂端會轉個彎，重新朝上。在根的尖端裡面，有一些又小又重的澱粉粒會沉到低的那一邊，告訴根哪邊是下面。

Kitchen · 廚房

Why bean sprouts are white豆芽為什麼是白的

Bean sprouts are mung beans that have been sprouted in the shade and watered until the stem grows long. A seedling kept from the light does not make the green substance in its leaves. It puts everything into a long, pale stem. In nature this helps a buried seedling to reach the surface. In the kitchen, it gives us a crisp white vegetable.
豆芽是在遮光的地方發芽的綠豆，一直澆水，直到莖長得長長的。見不到光的小苗不會製造葉子裡那種綠色的物質，而是把力氣全用在一根又長又白的莖上。在野外，這能幫助埋在土裡的小苗鑽到地面；在廚房裡，它給了我們一種又脆又白的蔬菜。

Taiwan · 台灣

A library of sleeping seeds一座沉睡種子的圖書館

In Wufeng, Taichung, the Taiwan Agricultural Research Institute keeps the National Plant Genetic Resources Center, which began work in October 1993. Seeds are dried first and then sealed and kept cold and dry. The long-term storeroom is held at 12 degrees below zero Celsius. By the end of 2005 the center was keeping 68,211 samples of crop plants, ready to be woken when someone needs them.
在台中霧峰，農業試驗所設有「國家作物種原中心」，1993 年 10 月開始運作。種子先經過乾燥，再密封起來，存放在又冷又乾的地方；長期庫的溫度保持在攝氏零下 12 度。到 2005 年底，中心保存的作物種原有 68,211 份，等到有人需要的時候，再把它們叫醒。

Myth vs. Fact · 常見迷思

Four things people get wrong 四個常見的誤會

✗ Myth · 迷思A dry seed is dead.乾的種子是死的。

✓ Fact · 事實It is alive and asleep. Given water, warmth, and air, it can grow.它是活的，只是睡著了。有了水、溫度和空氣，它就能生長。

✗ Myth · 迷思A seed needs sunlight to start growing.種子要曬到太陽才會開始長。

✓ Fact · 事實Many seeds start in the dark, under the soil. The light is needed later, by the leaves.很多種子在土裡、在黑暗中就能開始長。光是後來葉子才需要的。

✗ Myth · 迷思You must plant a seed the right way up.種子一定要擺正了種。

✓ Fact · 事實The root finds its way down and the shoot finds its way up, however the seed lies.不管種子怎麼躺，根都會自己往下，芽都會自己往上。

✗ Myth · 迷思The more water, the better.水愈多愈好。

✓ Fact · 事實A seed under water gets too little oxygen. It needs damp soil with air in it.泡在水裡的種子得不到足夠的氧氣。它需要的是潮溼、而且含有空氣的土。

Classroom Activity 1 · 課堂活動一

Beans in a Jar 罐子裡的豆子

You need · 準備材料

  • A clear jar, paper towels, and water一個透明的罐子、幾張紙巾和水
  • Four dried beans, such as mung beans or red beans四顆乾豆子，例如綠豆或紅豆

Steps · 步驟

  1. Roll the paper towels and line the inside of the jar. Wet them well, and pour away any water that collects at the bottom.把紙巾捲起來，貼在罐子的內壁上。把紙巾澆溼，再把積在底部的水倒掉。
  2. Push the beans down between the paper and the glass, each one lying a different way: up, down, and sideways.把豆子塞進紙巾和玻璃之間，每一顆擺的方向都不一樣：朝上、朝下、橫著放。
  3. Keep the paper damp. Draw each bean every day for a week.讓紙巾一直保持潮溼。連續一個星期，每天把每一顆豆子畫下來。
  4. Which part came out first? Which way did the roots go? Did it matter how the bean was lying?最先出來的是哪個部分？根往哪個方向長？豆子怎麼擺有沒有差別？

When the roots are a few centimeters long, turn the whole jar on its side and leave it for two more days. Watch what the tips of the roots do. Do not eat the beans from this activity.
等根長到幾公分長，把整個罐子放倒，再放兩天，看根的尖端會怎麼做。這個活動用過的豆子不要吃。

Classroom Activity 2 · 課堂活動二

Light and Dark 有光和沒光

You need · 準備材料

  • Two small cups with damp cotton or soil, and six mung beans兩個小杯子，裝上溼棉花或土，還有六顆綠豆
  • A cardboard box that covers one cup completely一個可以把其中一杯完全罩住的紙箱

Steps · 步驟

  1. Put three beans in each cup and keep both cups damp.每個杯子放三顆豆子，兩杯都保持潮溼。
  2. Leave one cup by a bright window. Cover the other with the box.一杯放在明亮的窗邊，另一杯用紙箱罩起來。
  3. After a week, measure the tallest plant in each cup, and write down the color of its stem and leaves.一個星期之後，量一量每一杯裡最高的那一株，並記下莖和葉子的顏色。
  4. Which plants are taller? Which look healthier? Are those the same plants?哪一杯的植物比較高？哪一杯看起來比較健康？是同一杯嗎？

The plants in the dark will probably be taller, and that does not mean they are doing better. Now put them by the window too, and see how many days it takes for them to turn green.
暗處的植物很可能比較高，但這不代表它們長得比較好。現在把它們也移到窗邊，看看要幾天才會變綠。
