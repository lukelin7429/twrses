Living Things · Unit 2 · Lesson 8 · 生命與生態 單元二 第八課

Why Do Leaves Change Color?葉子為什麼會變色？

A leaf does not turn yellow in autumn. It was yellow all summer, and you could not see it. In a 3D model, look inside a leaf at the three pigments that color it. Move the season forward and watch the green fade until the yellow underneath shows through. Then choose a tree that turns red, and find out what kind of weather makes the brightest reds. Last, mix the three pigments yourself.
葉子不是到了秋天才變黃的。它整個夏天都是黃的，只是你看不見。在 3D 模型裡，看看讓葉子有顏色的三種色素。把季節往前推，看綠色慢慢褪去，底下的黃色露出來。接著選一棵會變紅的樹，找出什麼樣的天氣能讓紅色最鮮豔。最後自己動手調一調這三種色素。

Big idea · 一句話看懂

A green leaf holds more than one color. The green pigment, chlorophyll, is so strong that it hides the yellow and orange pigments that are there with it. In autumn the days grow short and cool. The tree stops renewing its chlorophyll and takes the useful parts back into its branches. As the green fades, the yellow and orange show. Some trees also make a red pigment at this time. Then the tree seals off each leaf, and the leaf falls.
一片綠色的葉子裡，其實不只一種顏色。綠色的色素——葉綠素——太強了，把同時存在的黃色和橘色色素都蓋住了。到了秋天，白天變短、天氣變涼，樹不再補充葉綠素，並且把裡面有用的東西收回枝條裡。綠色一褪，黃色和橘色就露出來。有些樹在這個時候還會另外製造一種紅色的色素。接著樹把每一片葉子的出入口封住，葉子就掉落了。

The three pigments in the leaf · 葉子裡的三種色素

Green · chlorophyll綠色 · 葉綠素
Yellow and orange · carotenoids黃和橘 · 類胡蘿蔔素
Red · anthocyanins紅色 · 花青素

What we see · 我們看到的顏色

Summer. The leaf is full of chlorophyll, the green pigment that catches sunlight. Look closely at the circle. The yellow dots are already there, but the green ones cover them. Chlorophyll wears out in the light, so all summer long the tree keeps making more.夏天。葉子裡滿滿的都是葉綠素——負責捕捉陽光的綠色色素。仔細看那個圓圈：黃點其實已經在那裡了，只是被綠點蓋住。葉綠素在光線下會耗損，所以整個夏天，樹都在不停地補充。

The days are getting shorter and cooler. The tree has stopped replacing its chlorophyll, and it is taking the valuable parts back into its branches to use again next year. The green dots are thinning out, and nothing new has been added yet.白天愈來愈短，也愈來愈涼。樹不再補充葉綠素，而且正把裡面珍貴的東西收回枝條，留到明年再用。綠點愈來愈少了；到目前為止，並沒有加進任何新的東西。

The green is gone, and the leaf is yellow. No yellow was made this autumn. These pigments, called carotenoids, were in the leaf all summer. They are the same family of pigments that color carrots and corn.綠色不見了，葉子變成黃色。這些黃色並不是今年秋天才做出來的：這類色素叫做類胡蘿蔔素，整個夏天都在葉子裡。紅蘿蔔和玉米的顏色，也是來自同一類色素。

This tree is doing something extra. It is making a new pigment, the red one, out of sugar in the leaf. Bright days make plenty of sugar. Leaves turn reddest in a run of bright days with cool nights.這棵樹多做了一件事：它用葉子裡的糖，製造一種新的色素——紅色的那一種。晴朗的白天能做出很多糖；連續幾天白天晴朗、夜裡涼爽，葉子就最紅。

The same tree, in a gray, mild autumn. With little sun there is little sugar, so the leaf makes only a little red. It turns orange and brownish this year. The tree is the same, and only the weather was different.同一棵樹，但是這個秋天陰陰的、不太冷。陽光少，糖就少，葉子只做得出一點點紅色。今年它變成橘色帶點褐色。樹還是同一棵，不一樣的只有天氣。

A thin layer of cork has grown across the base of the stalk and sealed it. No more water comes in, and the leaf lets go. The tree has kept what it needed, and it will grow new leaves in spring.葉柄的基部長出了一層薄薄的木栓，把通道封住。水再也進不來，葉子就鬆手掉落了。樹已經把需要的東西留了下來，春天會再長出新的葉子。

Reading · 英文閱讀

8

Unit 8: Why Do Leaves Change Color?

In some parts of the world, whole hillsides turn yellow, orange, and red for a few weeks every autumn. It looks as if someone has painted the trees. But most of that color was not added in autumn. It was in the leaves all summer.

世界上有些地方，每年秋天整片山坡會有幾個星期變成黃色、橘色和紅色，看起來就像有人替樹上了顏料。可是這些顏色大部分並不是秋天才加上去的，它們整個夏天都在葉子裡。

A leaf is green because it is full of chlorophyll. This is the pigment that catches sunlight so that the leaf can make food. There are other pigments in the leaf as well, yellow and orange ones called carotenoids. In summer you cannot see them, because there is so much green on top.

葉子是綠的，因為裡面充滿了葉綠素。這種色素負責捕捉陽光，讓葉子能夠做食物。葉子裡還有別的色素：黃色和橘色的，叫做類胡蘿蔔素。夏天你看不到它們，因為蓋在上面的綠色實在太多了。

Chlorophyll does not last. Sunlight wears it out, and the tree has to keep making more. In autumn the days grow shorter and cooler, and the tree stops. It breaks the chlorophyll down and moves the useful parts back into its branches, to keep through the winter.

葉綠素並不耐用。陽光會讓它耗損，樹得不停地補充。到了秋天，白天變短、天氣變涼，樹就不再補了。它把葉綠素拆開，把有用的部分搬回枝條裡，留著過冬。

As the green fades, the yellow and orange are still there, and now you can see them. That is all it takes to turn a tree gold.

綠色褪去以後，黃色和橘色還在，這時候你就看得到它們了。一棵樹變成金黃色，就只是這樣而已。

Red is different. Red comes from a third kind of pigment, and it is not in the leaf during the summer. Some trees make it in autumn from the sugar in their leaves. They make the most when the days are bright and the nights are cool but not freezing. That is why the same tree can be brilliant one year and dull the next.

紅色就不一樣了。紅色來自第三種色素，夏天的葉子裡並沒有它。有些樹到了秋天，才用葉子裡的糖把它做出來。白天晴朗、夜裡涼而不結霜的時候，做得最多。所以同一棵樹，可能今年紅得發亮，明年卻很黯淡。

At last the tree grows a thin layer of cork across the base of each leaf stalk. This shuts off the water. The leaf dries, turns brown, and falls. The tree has not lost much. It has taken back what it could, and it will grow new leaves in spring.

最後，樹在每一根葉柄的基部長出一層薄薄的木栓，把水路關上。葉子乾掉、變成褐色，然後掉落。樹並沒有損失多少：能收回的都收回了，春天會再長出新的葉子。

Why can we not see the yellow in a leaf during the summer?

There is so much green chlorophyll on top that it hides the yellow.

What does a tree do with its chlorophyll in autumn?

What kind of weather makes the reddest leaves?

Bright days, and nights that are cool but not freezing.

Color by the Numbers · 顏色的數字

Six numbers about autumn leaves 六個和秋葉有關的數字

How many pigments, how many trees, and for how long.
幾種色素、多少樹、持續多久。

3pigments · 種色素

Green chlorophyll, yellow and orange carotenoids, and red anthocyanins.綠色的葉綠素、黃色和橘色的類胡蘿蔔素、紅色的花青素。

2changes · 個變化

Autumn color comes from two things: losing the green, and in some trees gaining red.秋天的顏色來自兩件事：失去綠色，以及某些樹多了紅色。

10%make red · 會做紅色

In temperate regions, only about one tree species in ten makes the red pigment.在溫帶地區，大約每十種樹裡只有一種會製造紅色的色素。

70%in New England · 在新英格蘭

In a few places, most famously the northeastern United States, as many as this share of tree species make it.在少數地方（最有名的是美國東北部），會製造紅色色素的樹種可以高到這個比例。

Leaf Stories · 葉子的故事

A man named Color, a puzzle about red, trees that drop their leaves in the dry season, and a valley in Nantou 名字就叫「顏色」的人、紅色之謎、乾季落葉的樹，還有南投的一座山谷

What people have found out by looking closely at a leaf.
人們仔細看一片葉子之後發現的事。

1900

A man named Color名字就叫「顏色」的人

Mikhail Tsvet was a botanist who wanted to know what pigments a green leaf holds. In 1900 he found a way to pull them apart, so that the green, the yellow, and the orange each ended up in a different place. His method is called chromatography, and scientists everywhere still use it. His family name, Tsvet, is the Russian word for color.
茨維特是一位植物學家，他想知道綠色的葉子裡到底有哪些色素。1900 年，他找到一個把它們拆開的方法，讓綠色、黃色和橘色各自跑到不同的位置。這個方法叫做「層析法」，全世界的科學家到今天都還在用。他的姓氏 Tsvet，在俄文裡就是「顏色」的意思。

Open question · 還沒有定論

Why bother to make red?何必費力做紅色？

Yellow costs a tree nothing, because it was already there. Red is different. The tree has to spend sugar to make it, in leaves it is about to throw away. Why? Scientists have several ideas and have not settled the matter. One idea is that red works like a sunshade. It protects the leaf from strong light on cold days, so that the tree has more time to take its nutrients back.
黃色不花樹什麼力氣，因為它本來就在。紅色不一樣：樹得花掉糖才做得出來，而且是做在馬上就要丟掉的葉子上。為什麼？科學家有好幾種想法，還沒有定論。其中一種想法是，紅色像一把遮陽傘：在寒冷的日子裡替葉子擋住強光，讓樹有更多時間把養分收回去。

Tropics · 熱帶

Not only in autumn不只在秋天

Trees that drop all their leaves for part of the year are called deciduous. In cool countries they do it before winter. But in many warm parts of the world there is no cold winter, and trees there drop their leaves in the dry season, when water is short. Other trees, called evergreens, keep green leaves all year and replace them a few at a time.
一年裡有一段時間把葉子全部落光的樹，叫做落葉樹。在涼爽的國家，它們在冬天之前落葉。可是世界上很多溫暖的地方沒有寒冷的冬天，那裡的樹是在缺水的乾季落葉。另外一些樹叫做常綠樹，整年都有綠葉，葉子是一次換幾片、慢慢輪替的。

Taiwan · 台灣

The red valley of Aowanda奧萬大的紅色山谷

Aowanda National Forest Recreation Area lies in the mountains of Nantou, between 1,100 and 2,600 meters above the sea. It has a large natural forest of Formosan sweetgum, and every autumn these trees, with the maples near the visitor center, color the valley. Taiwan's Ministry of Agriculture explains that leaves of the same kind of tree change color sooner high in the mountains than they do lower down. That is one reason people in Taiwan go up into the mountains to see red leaves.
奧萬大國家森林遊樂區位在南投的山區，海拔 1,100 到 2,600 公尺之間。這裡有大片原生的楓香林，每年秋天，這些楓香和遊客中心附近的青楓一起把山谷染上顏色。農業部的農業知識入口網說明：同一種樹的葉子，在高海拔會比在低海拔先變色。這也是在台灣大家往山上去看紅葉的原因之一。

Myth vs. Fact · 常見迷思

Four things people get wrong 四個常見的誤會

✗ Myth · 迷思Leaves turn yellow because the tree makes yellow in autumn.葉子變黃，是因為樹在秋天製造了黃色。

✓ Fact · 事實The yellow was there all summer. It shows when the green fades.黃色整個夏天都在，綠色褪去之後才看得見。

✗ Myth · 迷思Frost is what turns leaves red.葉子是被霜凍紅的。

✓ Fact · 事實The best reds come with bright days and cool nights that do not freeze.最漂亮的紅色出現在白天晴朗、夜裡涼而不結霜的時候。

✗ Myth · 迷思Autumn leaves are dying, and the tree is losing them.秋葉是快死了，樹正在失去它們。

✓ Fact · 事實The tree drops them on purpose, after taking back the useful parts.是樹主動讓它們掉落的，而且先把有用的東西收了回去。

✗ Myth · 迷思Every tree drops its leaves when it gets cold.天一冷，每一種樹都會落葉。

✓ Fact · 事實Evergreens keep their leaves, and in warm places many trees drop theirs in the dry season.常綠樹不會；在溫暖的地方，很多樹是在乾季落葉。

Classroom Activity 1 · 課堂活動一

Pull the Colors Apart 把顏色拆開

  • A small glass, a pencil, and a little rubbing alcohol, poured by an adult一個小玻璃杯、一支鉛筆，還有請大人倒的一點點藥用酒精

Steps · 步驟

  1. Lay a leaf across a paper strip, about two centimeters from one end. Roll the edge of the coin over it to press a dark green line onto the paper.把葉子橫放在紙條上，離一端大約兩公分。用硬幣的邊緣在上面滾壓，在紙上壓出一條深綠色的線。
  2. Ask an adult to pour alcohol into the glass, just enough to cover the bottom.請大人把酒精倒進杯子，蓋住杯底就夠了。
  3. Hang the strip from the pencil so that only its tip touches the alcohol. The green line must stay above the liquid.把紙條掛在鉛筆上，只讓最下面的尖端碰到酒精；那條綠線一定要在液面上方。
  4. Wait twenty minutes. What colors have traveled up the paper, and which one went farthest?等二十分鐘。有哪些顏色沿著紙條爬上去了？哪一種跑得最遠？

You should find a yellow band above the green one. That is the hidden pigment. Work near an open window, keep the alcohol away from flames and from your eyes, and let the adult put it away.
你應該會在綠色帶的上方看到一條黃色的帶子，那就是藏起來的色素。請在打開的窗戶旁邊做，酒精要遠離火源和眼睛，用完請大人收好。

Classroom Activity 2 · 課堂活動二

A Leaf Calendar 葉子的日曆

You need · 準備材料

  • A tree near your home or school that drops its leaves住家或學校附近一棵會落葉的樹
  • A notebook, colored pencils, and tape一本筆記本、色鉛筆和膠帶

Steps · 步驟

  1. Once a week, pick up one fallen leaf from under the tree and tape it into your notebook.每個星期一次，在樹下撿一片落葉，貼在筆記本裡。
  2. Write the date and the weather that week: sunny or cloudy, warm or cool.寫下日期，還有那個星期的天氣：晴天還是陰天，溫暖還是涼爽。
  3. Color a small square beside it to match the leaf as closely as you can.在旁邊畫一個小方格，盡量塗成和葉子一樣的顏色。
  4. After two months, look at the row of squares. When did the color change fastest?兩個月後，看看那一排小方格。顏色是在什麼時候變得最快？

Pick up fallen leaves only, and leave the ones on the tree. If your tree stays green, that is a result too. Some trees keep their leaves all year.
只撿掉在地上的葉子，樹上的不要摘。要是你的樹一直是綠的，那也是一個結果：有些樹整年都留著葉子。
