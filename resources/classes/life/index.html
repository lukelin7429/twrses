Living Things · 生命與生態

Living Things 生命與生態

Every living thing, from a blade of grass to a hawk in the sky, has to find energy, grow, and leave young behind. See how a leaf makes food from sunlight, how a caterpillar becomes a butterfly, and why a mudflat is one of the busiest places on the coast.
每一種生物，從一根小草到天上的老鷹，都得取得能量、長大、留下後代。看一片葉子怎麼用陽光做出食物、毛毛蟲怎麼變成蝴蝶，還有為什麼一片泥灘是海岸上最熱鬧的地方。

Every lesson starts with something you can find near your school: a leaf, a sparrow, a line of ants. A 3D model shows what is going on inside, one idea at a time, and an English reading explains it, with Chinese a tap away.
每一課都從學校附近找得到的東西開始：一片葉子、一隻麻雀、一排螞蟻。先用 3D 模型看清楚裡面發生了什麼事，一課只講一個道理；再讀一篇英文短文把它說明白，中文翻譯隨點隨看。

The lessons are grouped into four units: cells and life, plants, animals, and living together. When a lesson touches something already explained in The Human Body, How Things Work, or Earth and Weather, it links there instead of explaining it twice.
課程分成四個單元：細胞與生命、植物、動物、生態。遇到「人體探索」「萬物原理」或「地球與天氣」已經講過的東西，就直接連過去，不重講一次。

Every number is checked against a source listed at the bottom of each lesson. Each lesson also tells you clearly which parts of its model are real and which are only a picture.
每一個數字都查證過，出處列在每課最下方。每一課也會清楚告訴你，模型裡哪些是真的、哪些只是示意。

6 of 16 lessons are ready so far; more are on the way.
目前完成 6 課（共規劃 16 課），持續增加中。

Unit 1 · 單元一

Cells and Life 細胞與生命

What every living thing is built of, where food begins, and the instructions inside every cell.
所有生物是用什麼蓋成的、食物最初從哪裡來，還有每個細胞裡的那份說明書。

Lesson 1 · 第一課入門 · Beginner

What Is a Cell?

細胞是什麼？A whale, a mushroom, a blade of grass, and you are all built from the same kind of tiny living block. It is called a cell. In a 3D model, cut one open and tap each part to see what it does. Then switch to a plant cell and find the three things it has that yours do not. Last, learn to tell what kind of cell you are looking at.鯨魚、香菇、一根小草，還有你，都是用同一種很小很小的、活的積木蓋成的。它叫做細胞。在 3D 模型裡把一個細胞切開，點一點每個部分，看它在做什麼。接著換成植物細胞，找出它有、你的細胞卻沒有的三樣東西。最後，學會分辨眼前看到的是哪一種細胞。Start the lesson · 開始上課 →Lesson 2 · 第二課入門 · Beginner

How Does a Leaf Make Food from Sunlight?

葉子怎麼用陽光做出食物？A plant never eats, yet it grows from a tiny seed into a tall tree. Where does all that stuff come from? In a 3D model of a leaf cut open, give it light, air, and water, and watch it make sugar and give off oxygen. Take one of the three away and see what happens. Then find out why almost everything you eat began as sunlight on a leaf.植物從來不吃東西，卻能從一顆小小的種子長成一棵大樹。那麼多的東西是從哪裡來的？在一片切開的葉子的 3D 模型裡，給它光、空氣和水，看它怎麼做出糖、放出氧氣。再拿掉其中一樣，看看會怎麼樣。最後找出答案：為什麼你吃的東西，幾乎每一樣最初都是照在葉子上的陽光？Start the lesson · 開始上課 →Lesson 3 · 第三課入門 · Beginner

What Is DNA?

DNA 是什麼？Inside almost every one of your cells there is a set of instructions for building you. It is written with only four letters. In a 3D model, unzip a piece of DNA and watch each half build itself a new partner, until one molecule has become two that match. Then change a single letter and see where the change goes. Last, write a code of your own and count how many different codes four letters can make.你身上幾乎每一個細胞裡，都有一套把你做出來的說明書。它只用四個字母寫成。在 3D 模型裡把一段 DNA 解開，看兩邊各自補上新的搭檔，一個分子變成一模一樣的兩個。接著換掉一個字母，看看這個改變會跑到哪裡去。最後自己寫一段密碼，算一算四個字母能排出多少種不同的密碼。Start the lesson · 開始上課 →Lesson 4 · 第四課入門 · Beginner

How Are Bacteria and Viruses Different?

細菌和病毒哪裡不一樣？People call both of them germs, and both are far too small to see. But they are not the same kind of thing at all. In a 3D model, watch one bacterium split into two, then four, then thirty-two, all by itself. Then watch a virus, which can do nothing alone, get a living cell to build new viruses for it. Last, find out how many of each would fit across one millimeter.大家把它們都叫做「病菌」，兩種也都小到看不見。可是它們根本不是同一類東西。在 3D 模型裡，看一個細菌靠自己分裂成兩個、四個，一直到三十二個。再看一個自己什麼都做不了的病毒，怎麼讓一個活的細胞替它做出新的病毒。最後算算看，一毫米的寬度可以排下幾個細菌、幾個病毒。Start the lesson · 開始上課 →

Unit 2 · 單元二

Plants 植物

How a seed wakes up, why flowers bloom, and how water climbs to the top of a tree.
種子怎麼醒過來、花為什麼要開，還有水怎麼爬到樹頂。

Lesson 5 · 第五課入門 · Beginner

How Does a Seed Know When to Grow?

種子怎麼知道什麼時候該發芽？A seed can lie in a paper packet for years and do nothing. Put it in damp soil, and within days it is a plant. In a 3D model, give a seed water and warmth and watch it wake: first a root going down, then a shoot going up. Turn the seed upside down and see whether it gets confused. Grow it in the dark and see what goes wrong. Last, decide which seeds in four different places will wake up.種子放在紙袋裡，可以好幾年一點動靜也沒有；把它放進溼溼的土裡，幾天之內就變成一株植物。在 3D 模型裡給種子水和溫度，看它醒過來：先是一條根往下，再是一根芽往上。把種子倒過來放，看它會不會搞錯方向。把它放在暗處，看看會出什麼問題。最後判斷看看：放在四個不同地方的種子，哪幾顆會醒？Start the lesson · 開始上課 →Lesson 6 · 第六課入門 · Beginner

Why Do Flowers Bloom?

花為什麼要開？Flowers are not there to look pretty for us. They are how a plant makes seeds, and a plant cannot walk over to another plant. In a 3D model, cut a flower open and follow one grain of pollen: carried in by a bee, caught by the stigma, and then growing a tube down to a waiting seed-to-be. Switch to the wind and see why some plants must throw away so much pollen. Last, look at a flower and work out who delivers for it.花不是為了讓我們覺得好看才開的。花是植物製造種子的方法，而植物沒辦法走到另一株植物那裡去。在 3D 模型裡把一朵花切開，跟著一粒花粉走：被蜜蜂帶進來、被柱頭接住，然後長出一條管子，通到等在那裡的「未來的種子」。換成風來送，看看為什麼有些植物非得浪費那麼多花粉不可。最後看著一朵花，推理出是誰在替它送貨。Start the lesson · 開始上課 →
