Living Things · 生命與生態

Living Things 生命與生態

Every living thing, from a blade of grass to a hawk in the sky, has to find energy, grow, and leave young behind. See how a leaf makes food from sunlight, how a caterpillar becomes a butterfly, and why a mudflat is one of the busiest places on the coast.
每一種生物，從一根小草到天上的老鷹，都得取得能量、長大、留下後代。看一片葉子怎麼用陽光做出食物、毛毛蟲怎麼變成蝴蝶，還有為什麼一片泥灘是海岸上最熱鬧的地方。

Every lesson starts with something you can find near your school: a leaf, a sparrow, a line of ants. A 3D model shows what is going on inside, one idea at a time, and an English reading explains it, with Chinese a tap away.
每一課都從學校附近找得到的東西開始：一片葉子、一隻麻雀、一排螞蟻。先用 3D 模型看清楚裡面發生了什麼事，一課只講一個道理；再讀一篇英文短文把它說明白，中文翻譯隨點隨看。

The lessons are grouped into four units: cells and life, plants, animals, and living together. When a lesson touches something already explained in The Human Body, How Things Work, or Earth and Weather, it links there instead of explaining it twice.
課程分成四個單元：細胞與生命、植物、動物、生態。遇到「人體探索」「萬物原理」或「地球與天氣」已經講過的東西，就直接連過去，不重講一次。

Every number is checked against a source listed at the bottom of each lesson. Each lesson also tells you clearly which parts of its model are real and which are only a picture.
每一個數字都查證過，出處列在每課最下方。每一課也會清楚告訴你，模型裡哪些是真的、哪些只是示意。

1 of 16 lessons is ready so far; more are on the way.
目前完成 1 課（共規劃 16 課），持續增加中。

Lesson 2 · 第二課入門 · Beginner

How Does a Leaf Make Food from Sunlight?

葉子怎麼用陽光做出食物？A plant never eats, yet it grows from a tiny seed into a tall tree. Where does all that stuff come from? In a 3D model of a leaf cut open, give it light, air, and water, and watch it make sugar and give off oxygen. Take one of the three away and see what happens. Then find out why almost everything you eat began as sunlight on a leaf.植物從來不吃東西，卻能從一顆小小的種子長成一棵大樹。那麼多的東西是從哪裡來的？在一片切開的葉子的 3D 模型裡，給它光、空氣和水，看它怎麼做出糖、放出氧氣。再拿掉其中一樣，看看會怎麼樣。最後找出答案：為什麼你吃的東西，幾乎每一樣最初都是照在葉子上的陽光？Start the lesson · 開始上課 →
