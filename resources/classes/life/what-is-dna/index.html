Living Things · Unit 1 · Lesson 3 · 生命與生態 單元一 第三課

What Is DNA?DNA 是什麼？

Inside almost every one of your cells there is a set of instructions for building you. It is written with only four letters. In a 3D model, unzip a piece of DNA and watch each half build itself a new partner, until one molecule has become two that match. Then change a single letter and see where the change goes. Last, write a code of your own and count how many different codes four letters can make.
你身上幾乎每一個細胞裡，都有一套把你做出來的說明書。它只用四個字母寫成。在 3D 模型裡把一段 DNA 解開，看兩邊各自補上新的搭檔，一個分子變成一模一樣的兩個。接著換掉一個字母，看看這個改變會跑到哪裡去。最後自己寫一段密碼，算一算四個字母能排出多少種不同的密碼。

Big idea · 一句話看懂

DNA is a very long molecule shaped like a twisted ladder. Each rung of the ladder is a pair of chemical letters, and there are only four letters: A, T, C, and G. A always pairs with T, and C always pairs with G. The order of the letters is the message. Because of the pairing rule, a cell can pull the ladder apart and rebuild both halves, so every new cell gets its own full copy.
DNA 是一種非常長的分子，形狀像一條扭起來的梯子。梯子的每一根橫槓是一對化學字母，而字母總共只有四個：A、T、C、G。A 一定配 T，C 一定配 G。字母排列的順序，就是訊息的內容。因為有這條配對規則，細胞可以把梯子從中間拉開，再把兩半各自補齊，所以每一個新的細胞都拿得到完整的一份。

Now there are two ladders, and they read exactly the same. Each one has one old strand and one new strand. This is how a cell copies its instructions before it divides.現在有兩條梯子了，而且讀起來一模一樣。每一條都是一股舊的加一股新的。細胞在分裂之前，就是這樣把說明書複製一份。

You changed a letter, and its partner changed with it, because the pairing rule still holds. A change in the letters of DNA is called a mutation. Now unzip the ladder and see what happens to your change.你換了一個字母，它的搭檔也跟著換了，因為配對規則還是一樣。DNA 的字母發生改變，叫做突變。現在把梯子解開，看看這個改變會怎麼樣。

Unit 3: What Is DNA?

Identical twins can look so much alike that even their friends mix them up. Why? Both of them grew from the same single cell, so both carry almost exactly the same set of instructions. Those instructions are written in a molecule called DNA.

同卵雙胞胎可以長得非常像，連朋友都會認錯。為什麼？因為他們兩個是從同一個細胞長出來的，所以身上帶著幾乎一模一樣的一套說明書。這套說明書寫在一種叫做 DNA 的分子裡。

DNA is very long and very thin, and it is shaped like a ladder that has been twisted. Scientists call this shape a double helix. The two sides of the ladder are long strands. The rungs between them are made of four small chemical parts, which we call by their first letters: A, T, C, and G.

DNA 非常長，也非常細，形狀像一條被扭轉過的梯子。科學家把這種形狀叫做雙螺旋。梯子的兩邊是兩股長長的鏈子，中間的橫槓由四種小小的化學零件組成，我們用它們英文名字的第一個字母來稱呼：A、T、C、G。

The letters follow one simple rule. A always pairs with T, and C always pairs with G. So each rung is a pair, and if you know the letters along one side of the ladder, you can work out the letters along the other side.

這些字母遵守一條簡單的規則：A 一定和 T 配對，C 一定和 G 配對。所以每一根橫槓都是一對；只要知道梯子其中一邊的字母，就能推出另一邊的字母。

This rule is what lets a cell copy its DNA. Before a cell divides, the ladder unzips down the middle. Each loose letter picks up a new partner, A with T and C with G, until there are two ladders where there was one. They read the same, and each has one old strand and one new strand.

細胞能複製 DNA，靠的就是這條規則。細胞分裂之前，梯子會從中間像拉鍊一樣解開。每一個落單的字母都接上一個新的搭檔，A 配 T、C 配 G，直到原來的一條梯子變成兩條。這兩條讀起來一樣，而且每一條都是一股舊的加一股新的。

The order of the letters is the message. A stretch of DNA that carries the instructions for one job is called a gene. People have about 19,000 to 20,000 genes that tell cells how to build proteins. One full set of human DNA has about 3.1 billion pairs of letters, and it is packed into 23 pairs of bundles called chromosomes.

字母的順序就是訊息。DNA 上面負責一件工作的那一段說明，叫做基因。人類大約有一萬九千到兩萬個基因，告訴細胞怎麼製造蛋白質。人的一整套 DNA 大約有三十一億對字母，收在二十三對叫做染色體的小捆裡。

Now and then a letter is changed. This is called a mutation. Many mutations make no difference at all, and some change how a living thing is built. Every living thing we know, from a mushroom to an orchid to you, writes its instructions with the same four letters. Only the order is different.

偶爾會有一個字母被換掉，這叫做突變。很多突變一點影響也沒有，有些則會改變生物長出來的樣子。我們知道的每一種生物，從香菇、蘭花到你，都用同樣的四個字母寫說明書，不一樣的只有順序。

DNA by the Numbers · DNA 的數字

Six numbers about DNA 六個和 DNA 有關的數字

From four letters to three billion.
從四個字母到三十億。

4letters · 個字母

A, T, C, and G. Every living thing we know uses the same four.A、T、C、G。我們知道的每一種生物，用的都是這四個。

1869the year · 年

1953the year · 年

James Watson and Francis Crick published the double helix shape of DNA.華生和克里克發表了 DNA 的雙螺旋結構。

3.1billion pairs · 十億對

About this many pairs of letters make up one set of human DNA.人的一套 DNA，大約由這麼多對字母組成。

23pairs · 對

DNA Stories · DNA 的故事

Bandages, a photograph, a very long book, and an orchid 繃帶、一張照片、一本很長的書，還有一種蘭花

How people found DNA, saw its shape, and learned to read it.
人們怎麼找到 DNA、看出它的形狀，又怎麼學會讀它。

1869

Found on old bandages在用過的繃帶上發現的

A young Swiss doctor named Friedrich Miescher wanted to know what cells are made of. He collected used bandages from a hospital, because the pus on them was full of cells. In the nuclei of those cells he found a substance that nobody had described before. He called it nuclein. Today we call it DNA.
年輕的瑞士醫生米歇爾想知道細胞是什麼東西做的。他從醫院收集用過的繃帶，因為上面的膿含有大量的細胞。在這些細胞的細胞核裡，他找到一種從來沒有人描述過的物質，把它叫做「核素」。今天我們叫它 DNA。

1952–1953

Photo 51第 51 號照片

On May 2, 1952, at King's College London, Raymond Gosling took an X-ray picture of DNA while working under Rosalind Franklin. It is known as Photo 51, and it became an important piece of evidence for the shape of DNA. On April 25, 1953, James Watson and Francis Crick published the double helix in the journal Nature. Watson, Crick, and Maurice Wilkins shared a Nobel Prize in 1962. Franklin had died in 1958.
1952 年 5 月 2 日，在倫敦國王學院，葛斯林在富蘭克林的指導下拍下一張 DNA 的 X 光照片，後來被稱為「第 51 號照片」，成為判斷 DNA 形狀的重要證據。1953 年 4 月 25 日，華生和克里克在《自然》期刊發表了雙螺旋結構。1962 年，華生、克里克和威爾金斯共同獲得諾貝爾獎；富蘭克林已經在 1958 年過世。

1990–2022

Reading the whole book把整本書讀完

In 1990, scientists in many countries began the Human Genome Project. Their aim was to read every letter of human DNA. The project was declared complete on April 14, 2003, with about 92 percent of the letters read. The parts that were hardest to read took much longer. A complete sequence with no gaps was reported in 2022.
1990 年，許多國家的科學家展開「人類基因體計畫」，目標是把人類 DNA 的每一個字母都讀出來。這個計畫在 2003 年 4 月 14 日宣布完成，當時讀出了大約 92% 的字母。最難讀的那些部分又花了很久，直到 2022 年，才有沒有缺口的完整序列發表。

Taiwan · 台灣

An orchid's instructions一種蘭花的說明書

Phalaenopsis equestris is a small moth orchid that is native to Taiwan and the Philippines, and growers often use it as a parent when they breed new orchids. In 2014, a team of scientists that included researchers at National Cheng Kung University in Tainan published its genome in the journal Nature Genetics. They counted 29,431 predicted genes for building proteins.
桃紅蝴蝶蘭（又叫姬蝴蝶蘭）是一種小型的蝴蝶蘭，原生於台灣和菲律賓，也是育種者培育新品種時常用的親本。2014 年，一個包含台南國立成功大學研究人員在內的科學團隊，在《自然—遺傳學》期刊發表了它的基因體。他們預測出 29,431 個製造蛋白質的基因。

Myth vs. Fact · 常見迷思

Four things people get wrong 四個常見的誤會

✗ Myth · 迷思DNA is found only in blood.只有血液裡才有 DNA。

✓ Fact · 事實It is in the cells of every part of you, such as your skin, your hair roots, and the inside of your cheek.你身體每個部位的細胞裡都有，例如皮膚、髮根，還有臉頰內側。

✗ Myth · 迷思Only people and animals have DNA.只有人和動物才有 DNA。

✓ Fact · 事實Plants, mushrooms, and bacteria have it too, written with the same four letters.植物、香菇和細菌也都有，而且用的是同樣的四個字母。

✗ Myth · 迷思A change in DNA is always harmful.DNA 只要改變就一定有害。

✓ Fact · 事實Many changes make no difference. Some do harm, and a few turn out to be useful.很多改變完全沒有影響；有些有害，也有少數後來反而有用。

✗ Myth · 迷思Identical twins are the same in every way.同卵雙胞胎每一個地方都一樣。

✓ Fact · 事實Their DNA is nearly identical, but they are two different people. Even their fingerprints are not the same.他們的 DNA 幾乎一樣，卻是兩個不同的人，連指紋都不相同。

Classroom Activity 1 · 課堂活動一

A Paper Zipper 紙做的拉鍊

You need · 準備材料

  • Paper in four colors, scissors, and a pen四種顏色的紙、剪刀和一支筆
  • A long table or a clear space on the floor一張長桌，或地上的一塊空位

Steps · 步驟

  1. Cut about twenty small cards of each color. Write A on one color, T on the next, and then C and G.每種顏色各剪大約二十張小卡。在一種顏色上寫 A，另一種寫 T，其餘兩種寫 C 和 G。
  2. Lay ten cards in a column, in any order you like. Beside each one, place its partner. You have made a ladder.隨意排十張卡，排成一直行。在每一張旁邊放上它的搭檔。你做出了一條梯子。
  3. Slide the two columns apart. Now give every card a new partner from the cards that are left.把兩行往兩邊拉開。接著用剩下的卡片，替每一張卡找一個新的搭檔。
  4. Read the two ladders from top to bottom. Are they the same? Which cards in each ladder are the old ones?把兩條梯子從上往下讀一遍。它們一樣嗎？每一條梯子裡，哪些卡是原來的？

Ask a friend to swap one card while you look away, before you slide the columns apart. After copying, can you find the change in both ladders?
在把兩行拉開之前，請朋友趁你沒看的時候偷偷換掉一張卡。複製完以後，你能在兩條梯子裡都找到那個改變嗎？

Classroom Activity 2 · 課堂活動二

A Three-Letter Code 三個字母的密碼

You need · 準備材料

  • Paper and a pencil紙和鉛筆
  • A partner to send a message to一位可以互傳訊息的夥伴

Steps · 步驟

  1. Make a code table together. Give each letter of the alphabet its own three-letter word made only of A, T, C, and G, such as AAA for a and AAT for b.兩個人一起做一張密碼表。替每一個英文字母指定一組只用 A、T、C、G 排成的三字組合，例如 AAA 代表 a、AAT 代表 b。
  2. Write a short word in your code, as one long row of letters with no spaces.用你們的密碼寫一個短短的英文字，寫成一長串、中間不留空格。
  3. Trade messages, and read your partner's message three letters at a time.互相交換訊息，然後一次讀三個字母，把對方的訊息解出來。
  4. Now change one letter in the row, and read it again. What happens to the word?現在把那一串裡的一個字母換掉，再讀一次。那個字變成什麼了？

There are 64 three-letter words and only 26 letters in the alphabet, so you have plenty to spare. Cells have spare ones too. Their 64 words stand for only about 20 building blocks, so several words can mean the same thing.
三字組合一共有 64 種，英文字母只有 26 個，所以綽綽有餘。細胞也一樣有多的：它的 64 種組合只代表大約 20 種積木，所以好幾種組合可以是同一個意思。
