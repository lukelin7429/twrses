Living Things · Unit 1 · Lesson 2 · 生命與生態 單元一 第二課

How Does a Leaf Make Food from Sunlight?葉子怎麼用陽光做出食物？

A plant never eats, yet it grows from a tiny seed into a tall tree. Where does all that stuff come from? In a 3D model of a leaf cut open, give it light, air, and water, and watch it make sugar and give off oxygen. Take one of the three away and see what happens. Then find out why almost everything you eat began as sunlight on a leaf.
植物從來不吃東西，卻能從一顆小小的種子長成一棵大樹。那麼多的東西是從哪裡來的？在一片切開的葉子的 3D 模型裡，給它光、空氣和水，看它怎麼做出糖、放出氧氣。再拿掉其中一樣，看看會怎麼樣。最後找出答案：為什麼你吃的東西，幾乎每一樣最初都是照在葉子上的陽光？

Big idea · 一句話看懂

A leaf is a food factory that runs on sunlight. It takes in carbon dioxide from the air and water from the roots. With the energy of light, it joins them into sugar, and it gives off oxygen. The plant uses the sugar to live and to grow. The leaf needs all three things at once: light, carbon dioxide, and water. Whichever one is shortest sets the speed.
葉子是一座用陽光發動的食物工廠。它從空氣裡吸進二氧化碳，從根部得到水，再用光的能量把它們結合成糖，同時放出氧氣。植物靠這些糖活著、長大。葉子同時需要三樣東西：光、二氧化碳和水；最缺的那一樣，決定它做得多快。

The leaf has plenty of light, carbon dioxide, and water, so the factory is running fast. Sugar flows out along the vein to the rest of the plant, and oxygen drifts out through the small holes underneath.光、二氧化碳和水都很充足，工廠全速運轉。糖沿著葉脈送到植物的其他部位，氧氣從底下的小孔飄出去。

There is plenty of carbon dioxide and water, but not enough light. Light is the energy that joins them together, so the leaf can only work as fast as the light allows. Adding more water or air will not help.二氧化碳和水都夠，可是光不夠。光是把它們結合起來的能量，所以光有多少，葉子就只能做多快。這時候再多給水或空氣也沒有用。

There is plenty of light and water, but the leaf is short of carbon dioxide. Carbon dioxide is where the carbon in sugar comes from. Without enough of it, the light has little to work on.光和水都夠，可是二氧化碳不夠。糖裡面的碳就是從二氧化碳來的；二氧化碳不夠，光再強也沒有東西可以加工。

The leaf is short of water. Look at the small holes underneath: they are closing. That saves water, but it also shuts out the carbon dioxide. A thirsty plant in bright sun cannot make much food.葉子缺水了。看看底下的小孔：它們正在關起來。這樣可以省水，可是二氧化碳也被擋在外面了。大太陽下口渴的植物，做不出多少食物。

It is dark, and the factory has stopped. No sugar is being made, and no oxygen is given off. The small holes have closed for the night. The plant now lives on the sugar it made during the day.天黑了，工廠停工了。沒有糖做出來，也沒有氧氣放出來，底下的小孔也關起來過夜。這時候植物靠白天做好的糖過日子。

Unit 2: How Does a Leaf Make Food from Sunlight?

You have to eat to stay alive. So does a dog, a bird, and a fish. But a tree stands in one place for a hundred years and never eats a thing. All the same, it grows. A seed that weighs less than a coin becomes a trunk too heavy to lift. Where does all that wood come from?

你必須吃東西才能活下去，狗、鳥和魚也一樣。可是一棵樹在同一個地方站了一百年，從來沒有吃過任何東西，卻照樣長大。一顆比硬幣還輕的種子，可以長成重到搬不動的樹幹。那麼多的木頭是從哪裡來的？

For a long time people thought the answer was the soil. About four hundred years ago, a scientist named Jan van Helmont tested the idea. He planted a small willow tree in a pot of soil and gave it nothing but water for five years. The tree gained about 74 kilograms. The soil had lost only 57 grams. So the tree was not made of soil.

很長一段時間，人們以為答案是土壤。大約四百年前，一位叫做范．海爾蒙特的科學家做了實驗來檢驗這個想法。他把一棵小柳樹種在一盆土裡，五年當中只給它澆水。這棵樹增加了大約 74 公斤，土卻只少了 57 公克。所以樹並不是土做的。

The real answer is in the leaves. A leaf takes in a gas called carbon dioxide from the air, through tiny holes on its underside. Water comes up from the roots. Inside the leaf there are small green parts called chloroplasts. They catch the energy of sunlight and use it to join the carbon dioxide and the water into sugar.

真正的答案在葉子裡。葉子透過背面細小的孔，從空氣中吸進一種叫做二氧化碳的氣體；水則從根部送上來。葉子裡面有一些綠色的小東西，叫做葉綠體。它們抓住陽光的能量，用它把二氧化碳和水結合成糖。

This is called photosynthesis, which means putting together with light. Sugar is the plant's food. It burns some for energy, and it builds the rest into leaves, roots, and wood. So most of a tree is made from air and water, joined together by sunlight.

這件事叫做「光合作用」，意思是「用光來合成」。糖就是植物的食物：一部分燒掉當作能量，其餘的拿來長出葉子、根和木頭。所以一棵樹的大部分，是空氣和水做的，是陽光把它們接在一起的。

Something is left over when sugar is made: oxygen. The leaf lets it out through the same tiny holes, and it mixes into the air. That oxygen comes from the water, not from the carbon dioxide. It is the gas that you, and nearly every other animal, must breathe to stay alive.

做糖的時候會多出一樣東西：氧氣。葉子從同樣那些小孔把它放出去，混進空氣裡。這些氧氣是從水來的，不是從二氧化碳來的。它就是你和幾乎所有動物都必須呼吸才能活下去的那種氣體。

Now think about your last meal. Rice, bread, and vegetables are parts of plants. Meat and eggs come from animals that ate plants. Follow any food back far enough, and you arrive at a green leaf in the sun. In a way, you run on sunlight too.

現在想一想你上一餐吃了什麼。米飯、麵包和青菜是植物的一部分；肉和蛋來自吃植物的動物。任何一種食物，只要往回追得夠遠，都會追到一片曬著太陽的綠葉。從某個角度來說，你也是靠陽光在運轉的。

Questions · 閱讀理解

What did van Helmont's willow tree show?

The tree gained about 74 kilograms while the soil lost only 57 grams, so a tree is not made of soil.

What three things does a leaf need to make sugar?

It needs light, carbon dioxide from the air, and water from the roots.

Where does the oxygen that a leaf gives off come from?

It comes from the water. It is left over when the leaf joins water and carbon dioxide into sugar.

The recipe for sugar 做糖的配方

Goes in · 放進去

Comes out · 做出來

The sugar here is glucose. Each dot stands for one molecule, the smallest bit of a substance. A real leaf makes an enormous number of them every second, and it needs light for every one.這裡的糖是葡萄糖。每一個點代表一個分子，也就是一種物質最小的一份。真的葉子每一秒都做出數量龐大的分子，而且每一個都需要光。

The leaf needs · 葉子需要

6 carbon dioxide · 二氧化碳

6 water · 水

And it makes · 做出

1 sugar · 糖（葡萄糖）

6 oxygen · 氧氣

The recipe never changes: 6 carbon dioxide and 6 water make 1 sugar and 6 oxygen. Nothing is lost. Every atom that goes in comes out again in the sugar or in the oxygen.這份配方永遠不變：6 個二氧化碳加 6 個水，做出 1 顆糖和 6 個氧氣。什麼都沒有少：放進去的每一個原子，都在糖或氧氣裡面。

Leaf Stories · 葉子的故事

The willow, the bubbles, the color green, and the rice in your bowl 一棵柳樹、一串氣泡、綠色，還有你碗裡的飯

How people worked it out, and where you can see it today.
人們是怎麼弄懂的，還有今天在哪裡看得到。

1600s · 十七世紀

The willow in the pot盆子裡的柳樹

Jan van Helmont weighed a pot of soil, planted a small willow in it, and watered it for five years. Then he weighed everything again. The tree had gained about 74 kilograms, and the soil had lost only 57 grams. He decided that the tree was made of water. He was half right. He did not know that the air was giving it something too.
范．海爾蒙特先秤了一盆土的重量，在裡面種下一棵小柳樹，澆了五年的水，然後把所有東西重新秤一次。樹大約增加了 74 公斤，土只少了 57 公克。他的結論是：樹是水做的。他對了一半。他不知道，空氣也給了這棵樹東西。

1779

Bubbles in the sun陽光下的氣泡

Jan Ingenhousz put green plants under water and watched. In sunlight, small bubbles rose from the green parts. In the shade, the bubbles stopped. The plants were making a gas, and they needed light to do it. That was the missing piece. In the 1900s, another scientist, Cornelis van Niel, showed that the oxygen in those bubbles comes from water that the plant has split apart.
英根豪斯把綠色的植物放在水裡觀察。在陽光下，綠色的部分冒出一顆顆小氣泡；到了陰影裡，氣泡就停了。植物正在製造一種氣體，而且需要光才做得到。這就是缺少的那一塊拼圖。到了二十世紀，另一位科學家范尼爾證明，氣泡裡的氧氣來自被植物拆開的水。

Color · 顏色

Why leaves are green葉子為什麼是綠的

Sunlight is a mix of many colors. The green stuff in a leaf, called chlorophyll, takes in mostly the blue light and the red light and uses their energy. It takes in much less of the green, so the green light bounces off and reaches your eyes. The color you see is the part of the sunlight that the leaf uses least.
陽光是很多種顏色混在一起的。葉子裡綠色的東西叫做葉綠素，它主要吸收藍光和紅光，利用它們的能量；綠光吸收得少得多，所以被反射出來、進到你的眼睛。你看到的顏色，其實是陽光裡葉子用得最少的那一部分。

Changhua · 彰化

The rice in your bowl你碗裡的飯

Changhua County grows so much rice that it has been called the rice barn of Taiwan. Stand beside a paddy field on a sunny day, and you are looking at this lesson at work. Every leaf is taking in air and water and making sugar. The plant packs that sugar into its seeds as starch, and those seeds are the grains of rice in your bowl.
彰化縣盛產稻米，有「臺灣米倉」的稱號。大晴天站在稻田旁邊，你看到的就是這一課正在發生的事：每一片葉子都在吸進空氣和水、做出糖。稻子再把這些糖變成澱粉，存進種子裡；那些種子，就是你碗裡的一粒粒米飯。

Myth vs. Fact · 常見迷思

Four things people get wrong 四個常見的誤會

✗ Myth · 迷思Plants get their food from the soil.植物的食物是從土裡來的。

✓ Fact · 事實Plants make their own food from air and water, using light. From the soil they take water and small amounts of minerals.植物用光，把空氣和水做成自己的食物。它們從土裡吸收的是水和少量的礦物質。

✗ Myth · 迷思A tree is made mostly of soil.一棵樹大部分是土做的。

✓ Fact · 事實Most of a tree is made from carbon dioxide and water. Van Helmont's soil lost only 57 grams while his tree gained 74 kilograms.樹的大部分是二氧化碳和水做的。范．海爾蒙特的土只少了 57 公克，樹卻增加了 74 公斤。

✗ Myth · 迷思The oxygen that leaves give off comes from carbon dioxide.葉子放出來的氧氣是從二氧化碳來的。

✓ Fact · 事實It comes from water. The plant splits water apart, keeps what it needs, and lets the oxygen go.它是從水來的。植物把水拆開，留下需要的部分，把氧氣放掉。

✗ Myth · 迷思Leaves are green because they use green light.葉子是綠的，因為它們用的是綠光。

✓ Fact · 事實It is the opposite. Leaves use mostly blue and red light. Green is the color they use least, so it is the color that bounces back.正好相反。葉子主要用的是藍光和紅光；綠光用得最少，所以被反射回來。

Classroom Activity 1 · 課堂活動一

Bubbles from a Leaf 葉子冒泡泡

You need · 準備材料

  • A clear glass or bowl of water and a fresh green leaf (a water plant works best)一個裝了水的透明杯子或碗，還有一片新鮮的綠葉（水草效果最好）
  • A sunny windowsill, a dark cupboard, and a clock一個曬得到太陽的窗台、一個黑暗的櫃子，還有時鐘

Steps · 步驟

  1. Put the leaf under the water. Use a small stone to hold it down if it floats.把葉子放進水裡。如果它浮起來，用一顆小石頭壓住。
  2. Stand the glass in bright sunlight for one hour. Look closely at the leaf. What do you see on it?把杯子放在明亮的陽光下一個小時。仔細看葉子：上面出現了什麼？
  3. Tap the glass gently and wipe the bubbles off. Now put it in the dark cupboard for one hour.輕輕敲一敲杯子，把氣泡抹掉。然後把它放進黑暗的櫃子裡一個小時。
  4. Look again. Are there as many bubbles as before? What made the difference?再看一次。氣泡和剛才一樣多嗎？差別是什麼造成的？

This is the experiment Ingenhousz did in 1779. The bubbles in sunlight are mostly oxygen. Some bubbles may also form just because the water warms up, so compare the sunny glass with the dark one before you decide.
這就是英根豪斯在 1779 年做的實驗。陽光下的氣泡大部分是氧氣。不過水變暖本身也會冒出一些氣泡，所以要拿曬太陽的那一杯和放在暗處的那一杯比過，再下結論。

Classroom Activity 2 · 課堂活動二

A Leaf in the Dark 見不到光的葉子

You need · 準備材料

  • A healthy potted plant with large leaves一盆葉子比較大的健康盆栽
  • Black paper or aluminum foil, and paper clips黑紙或鋁箔紙，還有迴紋針

Steps · 步驟

  1. Choose one leaf and leave it on the plant. Cover part of it, on both sides, with a strip of black paper, and hold the paper in place with paper clips.選一片葉子，不要摘下來。用一條黑紙把它的一部分上下兩面都遮住，用迴紋針固定。
  2. Leave the plant in a sunny place for one week. Water it as usual.把盆栽放在曬得到太陽的地方一個星期，照平常那樣澆水。
  3. Take the paper off. Compare the part that was covered with the rest of the leaf.把黑紙拿掉。比一比被遮住的部分和葉子的其他部分。
  4. What color is the covered part? What do you think happened to it?被遮住的地方是什麼顏色？你覺得它發生了什麼事？

The covered part could not make any food, and the plant stopped keeping it green. Take the clips off gently, and the leaf will usually recover in the light. Use a plant that belongs to you or to your class.
被遮住的部分沒辦法做出食物，植物也不再讓它保持綠色。輕輕把迴紋針拿掉，葉子曬到光之後通常會慢慢恢復。請用自己或班上的盆栽來做。
