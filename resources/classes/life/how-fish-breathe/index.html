How Does a Fish Breathe Under Water?魚怎麼在水裡呼吸？

A fish needs oxygen just as you do, and there is oxygen in water, but only a little. In a 3D model, look inside a gill, where water and blood slide past each other on two sides of a very thin wall. Watch the oxygen cross over. Then turn the blood around so that it flows the same way as the water, and see how much the fish would lose. Last, meet four fish that breathe in four different ways.
魚和你一樣需要氧氣。水裡有氧氣，只是很少。在 3D 模型裡看看鰓的內部：水和血隔著一層很薄的膜，從兩邊擦身而過。看氧氣怎麼跑過去。接著把血掉頭，讓它和水往同一個方向流，看看魚會少拿到多少。最後認識四種魚，牠們各有各的呼吸辦法。

Big idea · 一句話看懂

Water holds oxygen, but far less than air does. A fish keeps water flowing over its gills, which are packed with thin plates full of blood. Oxygen moves from the water into the blood through a very thin wall. The clever part is the direction. The blood flows the opposite way to the water, so all along the gill the water still has a little more oxygen than the blood beside it. That lets a fish take most of the oxygen out of the water.
水裡有氧氣，但是比空氣裡少得多。魚讓水不停地流過鰓；鰓裡排滿了充滿血液的薄片，氧氣穿過一層很薄的膜，從水裡跑進血裡。巧妙的地方在於方向：血的流向和水相反，所以沿著整個鰓，水裡的氧氣永遠都比旁邊的血多一點點。這樣魚就能把水裡大部分的氧氣都拿走。

Because the two flow in opposite directions, the water is always a little ahead.這裡的水就要流出去了，氧氣大部分都沒了。可是旁邊的血才剛到，氧氣更少，所以一直到最後，氧氣都還在跑過去。因為兩邊的方向相反，水永遠領先一點點。

Now the blood flows the same way as the water. At the start the difference is huge, and oxygen rushes across. But look at the two bars as you slide the ring along. They are closing in on each other fast.現在血和水往同一個方向流。一開始兩邊差很多，氧氣大量湧過去。不過你把圓環往前滑的時候，注意那兩條量表：它們很快就要靠在一起了。

The water and the blood now hold the same amount, so nothing more crosses. The rest of the gill is wasted, and half of the oxygen leaves with the water. Unit 10: How Does a Fish Breathe Under Water?

Watch a fish in a tank. Its mouth opens and closes all day long, as if it were drinking. What it is really doing is breathing. Each gulp sends water across its gills and out through a slit behind its head.

看看魚缸裡的魚。牠的嘴整天一開一合，好像在喝水。其實牠是在呼吸。每一口都把水送過鰓，再從頭後面的一道縫流出去。

Fish need oxygen for the same reason that you do. Their cells use it to get energy from food. There is oxygen dissolved in water, but not much. A liter of fresh water holds only about 8 cubic centimeters of oxygen, and a liter of air holds about 210. Water is also much heavier and thicker than air, so it is hard work to move.

魚需要氧氣的原因和你一樣：細胞要用氧氣從食物裡取得能量。水裡溶有氧氣，但是不多。一公升的淡水只含大約 8 立方公分的氧氣，一公升的空氣卻有大約 210 立方公分。水又比空氣重得多、稠得多，要推動它很費力。

Gills are built to make the most of that little bit. Under the gill cover there are rows of soft red threads. Each thread carries many thin plates, and each plate is full of tiny blood vessels. Together they give the water a very large surface to touch, and the wall between the water and the blood is extremely thin.

鰓的構造，就是為了把這一點點氧氣用到極致。鰓蓋底下有一排排柔軟的紅色細絲；每一條細絲上有許多薄片，每一片薄片裡佈滿了微小的血管。它們加起來，讓水有非常大的表面可以接觸，而水和血之間的那層膜又極薄。

Oxygen always moves from where there is more of it to where there is less. So when water rich in oxygen passes blood that is poor in oxygen, the oxygen crosses the wall into the blood.

氧氣永遠是從多的地方往少的地方移動。所以含氧多的水流過含氧少的血的時候，氧氣就穿過那層膜，進到血裡。

Here is the clever part. In a gill, the blood flows in the opposite direction to the water. Fresh water first meets blood that is nearly full, and gives it a little more. As the water moves on, it meets blood that is emptier and emptier. All the way along, the water has slightly more oxygen than the blood beside it, so the oxygen never stops crossing.

接下來是巧妙的地方。在鰓裡，血的流向和水相反。新鮮的水最先遇到的是快要裝滿的血，再給它補上一點；水繼續往前，遇到的血愈來愈空。一路上，水裡的氧氣都比旁邊的血多一點點，所以氧氣一直在過去，沒有停過。

This is why a fish dies in air, even though air has much more oxygen. Out of the water, the soft plates of the gills collapse and stick together, and almost no surface is left for the oxygen to cross.

這也是魚離開水會死的原因——雖然空氣裡的氧氣多得多。離開水以後，鰓那些柔軟的薄片會塌下來、黏在一起，幾乎沒有表面可以讓氧氣通過了。

Questions · 閱讀理解

Why does a fish keep opening and closing its mouth?

It is breathing. Each gulp sends water across its gills.

Which way does the blood in a gill flow, compared with the water?

In the opposite direction.

Why does a fish die in air?

Out of the water, the thin plates of its gills collapse and stick together, so there is almost no surface for oxygen to cross.

Gills by the Numbers · 鰓的數字

Six numbers about breathing water 六個和在水裡呼吸有關的數字

Why it is hard, and how well gills do it anyway.
為什麼這件事很難，而鰓又做得多好。

8cm³ per liter · 立方公分／公升

The oxygen in a liter of fresh water. The same amount of air holds about 210.一公升淡水裡的氧氣。同樣體積的空氣大約有 210。

777×denser · 倍的密度

Water is this many times denser than air, and about 100 times thicker to push through.水的密度是空氣的這麼多倍，而且大約黏稠一百倍，推動起來費力得多。

80%and more · 以上

Gills can take more than this share of the oxygen out of the water that passes over them.流過鰓的水，裡面的氧氣可以被拿走超過這個比例。

50%at most · 最多

If blood and water flowed the same way, no more than half could ever be taken.要是血和水往同一個方向流，最多永遠只拿得到一半。

10,000×slower · 分之一的速度

Oxygen spreads this many times more slowly through water than through air.氧氣在水裡擴散的速度，只有在空氣裡的這麼多分之一。

25kinds · 種

There are this many kinds of mudskipper, fish that spend much of their time out of the water.彈塗魚有這麼多種——牠們有很多時間都待在水面以上。

Engineering · 工程

A trick used twice同一招用兩次

Two streams flowing in opposite directions can pass almost everything from one to the other. Gills use this for oxygen. Birds that stand in cold water use it for heat. Warm blood going down the leg runs beside cold blood coming back up and warms it, so the heat stays in the body and is not lost through the feet.
兩股往相反方向流的東西，可以把幾乎全部的內容物從一邊交給另一邊。鰓用這一招來傳氧氣；站在冷水裡的鳥用這一招來留住熱：往腳流下去的溫血，緊貼著往回流的冷血，把它重新加熱，所以熱留在身體裡，不會從腳散掉。

Sharks · 鯊魚

Swim, or stop breathing不游，就沒辦法呼吸

A goldfish can rest on the bottom and still breathe, because it pumps water over its gills with its mouth and gill covers. Most sharks do it another way. They swim forward with the mouth a little open and let the water rush through. Some kinds have no other method, and would probably suffocate if they could not keep moving.
金魚可以停在水底休息，照樣呼吸，因為牠用嘴和鰓蓋把水打過鰓。大部分的鯊魚用的是另一招：嘴微微張開往前游，讓水自己沖進來。有些種類沒有別的辦法，要是不能一直游動，很可能就會窒息。

Africa · 非洲

A fish that waits in the mud在泥裡等待的魚

Lungfish have lungs as well as gills, and African lungfish must come up for air or they suffocate. That sounds like a weakness in a fish. But when the dry season comes and the pond turns to cracked mud, the lungfish burrows in and waits, breathing air, until the rain returns.
肺魚除了鰓還有肺，而非洲肺魚一定得浮上來呼吸空氣，不然會窒息。對一條魚來說，這聽起來像是弱點。可是乾季來臨、池塘變成龜裂的泥地時，肺魚鑽進泥裡，呼吸著空氣等待，直到雨季回來。

Taiwan · 台灣

The flower jumpers of the mudflats泥灘上的花跳

On the mudflats of Taiwan's west coast, small fish with eyes on top of their heads skip across the mud at low tide. People call them flower jumpers. They are mudskippers. Taiwan's Ministry of Agriculture explains that their skin and gill chambers let them breathe in air as well as in water. A mudskipper first fills its gill chambers with water to keep its throat wet, and then it breathes air through the lining of its mouth and throat.
在台灣西海岸的泥灘上，退潮的時候，有一種眼睛長在頭頂的小魚在泥地上跳來跳去。大家叫牠「花跳」，牠就是彈塗魚。農業部的農業知識入口網說明：彈塗魚的皮膚和鰓腔，讓牠在水裡和空氣裡都能呼吸。牠會先讓鰓腔裝滿水，保持咽喉溼潤，再用口腔內壁和咽喉呼吸空氣。

Myth vs. Fact · 常見迷思

Four things people get wrong 四個常見的誤會

✗ Myth · 迷思Fish breathe water the way we breathe air, in and out.魚呼吸水，就像我們呼吸空氣，一進一出。

✓ Fact · 事實Water goes one way only: in through the mouth, over the gills, and out behind the gill covers.水只往一個方向走：從嘴進來，流過鰓，從鰓蓋後面出去。

✗ Myth · 迷思Fish do not need oxygen.魚不需要氧氣。

✓ Fact · 事實They need it as much as we do. They take it from the water.牠們和我們一樣需要氧氣，只是從水裡取得。

✗ Myth · 迷思A fish dies in air because air has no oxygen it can use.魚離開水會死，是因為空氣裡沒有牠能用的氧氣。

✓ Fact · 事實Air has plenty. The gills collapse without water to hold them open.空氣裡的氧氣很多；是鰓沒有水撐著，塌掉了。

✗ Myth · 迷思All fish breathe only with gills.所有的魚都只用鰓呼吸。

✓ Fact · 事實Some also use their skin or the lining of the mouth, and lungfish have lungs.有些魚還會用皮膚或口腔內壁；肺魚甚至有肺。

Classroom Activity 1 · 課堂活動一

The Bead Trains 珠子列車

You need · 準備材料

  • Ten paper cups and fifty beads, beans, or paper clips十個紙杯，還有五十顆珠子、豆子或迴紋針
  • A long table, and a notebook一張長桌和一本筆記本

Steps · 步驟

  1. Make a water train: five cups in a row with ten beads in each. Beside it make a blood train: five empty cups.排一列「水列車」：五個杯子，每杯十顆珠子。旁邊排一列「血列車」：五個空杯子。
  2. The rule: whenever two cups are side by side, the fuller one gives beads to the other until they hold the same number, or differ by one.規則：只要兩個杯子並排，比較滿的那一杯就把珠子分給另一杯，直到兩杯一樣多（或只差一顆）。
  3. Same way: the trains travel together, so each cup stays beside the same partner. Apply the rule, and count the beads in the blood train.同方向：兩列車一起走，每個杯子的鄰居都不變。照規則做完，數一數血列車裡有幾顆珠子。
  4. Opposite ways: start again. This time slide the trains past each other, one cup at a time, from end to end. Apply the rule at every step, and count again.反方向：重新開始。這次讓兩列車從頭到尾交錯而過，一次移動一個杯子的距離，每一步都照規則做。最後再數一次。

Traveling together, the blood train ends with 25 of the 50 beads and can never get more. Passing in opposite directions, it ends with more than half. That is the whole secret of a gill.
一起走的時候，血列車最後有五十顆裡的二十五顆，再多也不可能了。交錯而過的時候，它拿到的會超過一半。鰓的祕密就是這個。

Classroom Activity 2 · 課堂活動二

Count the Breaths 數呼吸

You need · 準備材料

  • A fish in a tank or a pond that you can watch without disturbing it魚缸或池塘裡一條可以觀察、又不會被你打擾的魚
  • A watch or a timer, and a notebook一支手錶或計時器，還有筆記本

Steps · 步驟

  1. Find the gill cover, the flap behind the eye. Watch it open and close.找到鰓蓋——眼睛後面那片會動的蓋子。看它一開一合。
  2. Count how many times it opens in one minute while the fish is resting.魚在休息的時候，數一數鰓蓋一分鐘開幾次。
  3. Count again just after the fish has been swimming fast, for example at feeding time.在魚剛快速游動過之後（例如餵食的時候）再數一次。
  4. Is the number different? Why might a busy fish need to move more water?數字不一樣嗎？忙碌的魚為什麼需要讓更多的水流過？

Do not tap on the glass, and do not take the fish out of the water. Watching quietly gives the best numbers.
不要敲玻璃，也不要把魚撈出水面。安安靜靜地看，數字最準。
