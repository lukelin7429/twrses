Living Things · Unit 1 · Lesson 4 · 生命與生態 單元一 第四課

How Are Bacteria and Viruses Different?細菌和病毒哪裡不一樣？

People call both of them germs, and both are far too small to see. But they are not the same kind of thing at all. In a 3D model, watch one bacterium split into two, then four, then thirty-two, all by itself. Then watch a virus, which can do nothing alone, get a living cell to build new viruses for it. Last, find out how many of each would fit across one millimeter.
大家把它們都叫做「病菌」，兩種也都小到看不見。可是它們根本不是同一類東西。在 3D 模型裡，看一個細菌靠自己分裂成兩個、四個，一直到三十二個。再看一個自己什麼都做不了的病毒，怎麼讓一個活的細胞替它做出新的病毒。最後算算看，一毫米的寬度可以排下幾個細菌、幾個病毒。

Big idea · 一句話看懂

A bacterium is a whole living cell. It takes in food, grows, and splits in two by itself. A virus is not a cell. It is a small packet of instructions in a coat, and it is about a hundred times smaller. A virus cannot grow or copy itself. It has to get inside a living cell, and then the cell builds the new viruses. Most bacteria do us no harm, and many are useful.
細菌是一個完整的、活的細胞。它會吸收養分、長大，然後靠自己一分為二。病毒不是細胞，它只是一小包說明書，外面包著一層殼，而且大約比細菌小一百倍。病毒不會長大，也不能複製自己；它必須進到一個活的細胞裡，由那個細胞替它做出新的病毒。大部分的細菌對我們無害，還有很多是有用的。

This is one bacterium. It is a complete living cell, with a wall, a membrane, and its own DNA. Look inside: the DNA is a loop, and there is no nucleus around it. Move the slider to let it grow.這是一個細菌。它是一個完整的、活的細胞，有細胞壁、細胞膜，還有自己的 DNA。往裡面看：DNA 是一個圈，外面沒有細胞核包著。拉動滑桿，讓它長大。

The cell grows longer, copies its DNA, and pinches apart in the middle. Nothing else is needed. With food, water, and warmth, a bacterium makes more bacteria all by itself.細胞愈長愈長，把 DNA 複製一份，然後從中間斷開。除此之外什麼都不需要：只要有養分、水和溫暖，細菌自己就能生出更多的細菌。

Each split doubles the number: 1, 2, 4, 8, 16, 32. In the best conditions, some bacteria can split about every 20 minutes. In real life they soon slow down, because food and room run out.每分裂一次，數量就加倍：1、2、4、8、16、32。在最理想的條件下，有些細菌大約每 20 分鐘就能分裂一次。不過在真實的環境裡，養分和空間很快就不夠，速度會慢下來。

Five splits have turned one cell into thirty-two. Every one is a complete living cell, and every one has its own copy of the DNA.分裂五次，一個細胞變成了三十二個。每一個都是完整的、活的細胞，也都有自己的一份 DNA。

This is a virus next to a cell. The virus is drawn much too large here. A real one is about a hundred times smaller than a bacterium. It has no way to eat, grow, or move by itself. It drifts until it bumps into the right kind of cell, and then it sticks.這是一個病毒，旁邊是一個細胞。這裡把病毒畫得太大了：真的病毒大約比細菌小一百倍。它不會吃東西、不會長大，也不會自己移動，只是漂來漂去，直到碰上對的那一種細胞，然後黏上去。

The virus sends its instructions into the cell. This kind of virus leaves its empty coat outside. Other kinds get in by other ways, but the important part is always the same: the instructions go in.病毒把它的說明書送進細胞裡。這一種病毒會把空殼留在外面；別種病毒有別的進法，但重點都一樣：說明書進去了。

Now the cell reads the virus's instructions as if they were its own. It uses its own materials and its own energy to build new viruses. The virus itself builds nothing.現在細胞把病毒的說明書當成自己的來讀，用自己的材料、自己的能量，做出一個又一個新的病毒。病毒自己什麼都沒有做。

The new viruses leave the cell, and each one can go on to another cell. Here the cell breaks open, which is what happens with this kind of virus. The model shows eight new viruses as an example.新的病毒離開細胞，每一個都可以再去找另一個細胞。在這裡細胞破開了，這一種病毒就是這樣。模型裡畫了八個新的病毒，只是舉例。

Unit 4: How Are Bacteria and Viruses Different?

People often use one word, germs, for both bacteria and viruses. Both are far too small to see, and some of each can make us sick. But they are not two kinds of the same thing. One is a living cell. The other is not a cell at all.

大家常常用「病菌」一個詞，同時指細菌和病毒。兩種都小到看不見，也各有一些會讓我們生病。可是它們並不是同一類東西裡的兩種：一個是活的細胞，另一個根本不是細胞。

A bacterium is one whole cell. It has a wall, a membrane, and its own DNA, though it has no nucleus to keep the DNA in. It takes in food and grows. When it is big enough, it copies its DNA and splits in two. In the best conditions, some bacteria can do this about every 20 minutes.

細菌是一個完整的細胞。它有細胞壁、細胞膜，還有自己的 DNA，只是沒有細胞核來裝 DNA。它吸收養分、長大；長得夠大了，就把 DNA 複製一份，然後一分為二。在最理想的條件下，有些細菌大約每 20 分鐘就能分裂一次。

Bacteria live almost everywhere: in soil, in water, in hot springs, and deep in rock. They live on your skin and inside you too. Your body carries about as many bacterial cells as it has cells of its own. Most of them do no harm, and many are useful. Other bacteria turn milk into yogurt. Only some kinds cause disease.

細菌幾乎到處都有：土壤裡、水裡、溫泉裡，還有很深的岩石裡。你的皮膚上和身體裡面也有。你身上帶著的細菌，數量和你自己的細胞差不多。它們大部分無害，還有很多是有用的。另外有些細菌能把牛奶變成優格。只有一部分種類的細菌會造成疾病。

A virus is much simpler and much smaller. It is a short set of instructions wrapped in a coat of protein. Most viruses are about a hundred times smaller than a bacterium. A virus cannot take in food, and it cannot grow. Left alone, it does nothing at all.

病毒簡單得多，也小得多。它是一小段說明書，外面包著一層蛋白質做的殼。大部分的病毒大約比細菌小一百倍。病毒不會吸收養分，也不會長大。只有它自己的時候，它什麼事也不會做。

To make more viruses, a virus has to get inside a living cell. Once its instructions are in, the cell follows them and builds new viruses out of its own materials. The new viruses then leave and find other cells. There are viruses for every kind of living thing, even for bacteria.

要有更多的病毒，病毒就得進到一個活的細胞裡。它的說明書一進去，細胞就照著做，用自己的材料做出新的病毒。新的病毒再離開，去找別的細胞。每一類生物都有會感染它的病毒，連細菌也有。

So is a virus alive? Scientists do not all agree. It carries instructions and it changes over time, as living things do, but it cannot do anything by itself. The difference matters in medicine. Antibiotics are medicines that stop bacteria from growing or kill them. They do not work on viruses, such as the ones that cause a cold.

那麼病毒算不算活的？科學家的看法並不一致。它帶著說明書，也會像生物一樣隨著時間改變；可是它自己什麼都做不了。這個差別在醫療上很重要。抗生素是抑制細菌生長或殺死細菌的藥，對病毒沒有用；引起感冒的就是病毒。

Germs by the Numbers · 病菌的數字

Six numbers about bacteria and viruses 六個和細菌、病毒有關的數字

How small, how fast, and how many.
多小、多快、多少。

0.5–5micrometers · 微米

The usual length of a bacterium. A micrometer is a thousandth of a millimeter.細菌一般的長度。一微米是千分之一毫米。

20–300nanometers · 奈米

The width of many round viruses. A nanometer is a thousandth of a micrometer.許多球形病毒的直徑。一奈米是千分之一微米。

1/100the size · 的大小

Most viruses are about one-hundredth the size of most bacteria.大部分的病毒，大約只有大部分細菌的百分之一大。

20minutes · 分鐘

In the best conditions, the bacterium E. coli can split in as little as this.在最理想的條件下，大腸桿菌最快這麼久就能分裂一次。

38trillion · 兆

The estimated number of bacterial cells in and on one human body, about as many as its own cells.一個人身上和體內的細菌細胞，估計有這麼多，和人自己的細胞數量差不多。

1892the year · 年

Dmitri Ivanovsky found the first sign of something smaller than any bacterium that could still spread a disease.伊凡諾夫斯基第一次發現：有一種比任何細菌都小的東西，還是能傳播疾病。

Germ Stories · 病菌的故事

A filter, a mold, a hunter of bacteria, and a common cold 一個濾器、一種黴菌、獵捕細菌的病毒，還有感冒

How people learned that bacteria and viruses are different things.
人們是怎麼知道細菌和病毒是兩回事的。

1892–1898

It went through the filter它穿過了濾器

A disease was spoiling tobacco plants. In 1892, Dmitri Ivanovsky pressed the sap of sick plants through a filter fine enough to hold back bacteria. The filtered sap still made healthy plants sick. In 1898, Martinus Beijerinck repeated the test and showed that the unseen thing multiplied only inside the living cells of the plant. He called it a virus.
有一種病讓菸草一株一株壞掉。1892 年，伊凡諾夫斯基把病株的汁液壓過一種細到能擋住細菌的濾器，結果濾過的汁液還是能讓健康的植物生病。1898 年，貝傑林克重做了這個實驗，並且證明這種看不見的東西只在植物活的細胞裡才會增加。他把它叫做 virus（病毒）。

1928

A mold that stops bacteria一種擋住細菌的黴菌

In 1928, the Scottish doctor Alexander Fleming discovered penicillin, a substance made by a mold. Penicillin kills bacteria by blocking a step in the building of their cell walls. It was the start of the medicines we call antibiotics. A virus has no cell wall and builds nothing by itself, so there is nothing there for such a medicine to block.
1928 年，蘇格蘭醫生弗萊明發現了盤尼西林（青黴素），那是一種黴菌製造的物質。盤尼西林擋住細菌建造細胞壁的其中一個步驟，細菌就活不成。這是我們今天叫做「抗生素」的這類藥的起點。病毒沒有細胞壁，自己也不建造任何東西，所以這種藥在它身上沒有可以擋的地方。

1915

Viruses that hunt bacteria獵捕細菌的病毒

Bacteria get viruses too. In 1915, the British scientist Frederick Twort found a tiny agent that infected and killed bacteria. Such a virus is called a bacteriophage, which means bacteria eater. It lands on a bacterium and injects its instructions, and the bacterium builds new viruses. Bacteriophages are among the most common things in the living world.
細菌也會被病毒感染。1915 年，英國科學家特沃特發現一種會感染並殺死細菌的微小東西。這樣的病毒叫做「噬菌體」，意思是「吃細菌的」。它落在細菌上，把說明書注射進去，細菌就替它做出新的病毒。噬菌體是生物世界裡數量最多的東西之一。

Taiwan · 台灣

A cold is a virus感冒是病毒

Taiwan's Centers for Disease Control explains that the common cold is an infection by viruses, and that the medicines a doctor gives for it treat the fever, the runny nose, and the cough while the body does the rest. It describes antibiotics as substances that stop bacteria from growing or kill them. Which medicine fits an illness is for a doctor to decide.
衛生福利部疾病管制署說明：感冒泛指病毒性感染，醫師開的藥是針對發燒、流鼻水、咳嗽這些症狀，其餘的靠身體自己痊癒。疾管署也說明，抗生素是用來抑制細菌生長或殺死細菌的物質。生什麼病該用什麼藥，要由醫師判斷。

Myth vs. Fact · 常見迷思

Four things people get wrong 四個常見的誤會

✗ Myth · 迷思Bacteria and viruses are the same thing.細菌和病毒是同一種東西。

✓ Fact · 事實A bacterium is a living cell. A virus is a packet of instructions that needs a cell.細菌是活的細胞；病毒是一包說明書，要靠細胞才行。

✗ Myth · 迷思All bacteria are bad for you.細菌都對人有害。

✓ Fact · 事實Most of the bacteria on you and in you do no harm, and many are useful.你身上和體內的細菌大部分無害，還有很多是有用的。

✗ Myth · 迷思Antibiotics work on colds.抗生素可以治感冒。

✓ Fact · 事實Colds are caused by viruses, and antibiotics act on bacteria.感冒是病毒引起的，而抗生素是對付細菌的。

✗ Myth · 迷思Viruses grow and multiply wherever they land.病毒落在哪裡，就在哪裡長大變多。

✓ Fact · 事實A virus cannot grow. It multiplies only inside a living cell.病毒不會長大，而且只能在活的細胞裡增加。

病毒和細菌比起來就是這樣。

Classroom Activity 1 · 課堂活動一

The Doubling Game 加倍遊戲

You need · 準備材料

  • A bag of dried beans or small paper squares一袋乾豆子，或一些小紙片
  • A sheet of paper, a pencil, and a tray一張紙、一支鉛筆和一個托盤

Steps · 步驟

  1. Put one bean on the tray. This is one bacterium. Write down 0 minutes and 1.在托盤上放一顆豆子，代表一個細菌。寫下「0 分鐘、1 個」。
  2. Every bean splits in two. Add a bean for each bean on the tray, and write down 20 minutes and 2.每一顆豆子都一分為二：托盤上有幾顆，就再加幾顆。寫下「20 分鐘、2 個」。
  3. Keep going, adding 20 minutes each time. How many rounds can you do before you run out of beans?繼續下去，每一次加 20 分鐘。在豆子用完之前，你能做幾輪？
  4. Work out the number after ten rounds without using beans. How much time has passed?不用豆子，算出第十輪之後的數目。這時候過了多久？

After ten rounds there are 1,024, in 200 minutes. Running out of beans is part of the lesson. Real bacteria run out of food and room in the same way, which is why they cannot keep doubling for long.
十輪之後是 1,024 個，花了 200 分鐘。豆子用完也是這個活動要教的事：真的細菌一樣會把養分和空間用完，所以沒辦法一直加倍下去。

Classroom Activity 2 · 課堂活動二

Cell or Packet? 細胞，還是一包說明書？

You need · 準備材料

  • Ten small cards and a pen十張小卡片和一支筆
  • Two sheets of paper, one labeled Bacterium and one labeled Virus兩張紙，一張寫「細菌」、一張寫「病毒」

Steps · 步驟

  1. On each card, write one fact from this lesson, such as “splits in two by itself” or “needs a living cell.”在每張卡片上寫一件這一課學到的事，例如「自己一分為二」或「需要活的細胞」。
  2. Mix the cards. Take turns drawing one and placing it on the sheet where it belongs.把卡片洗一洗，輪流抽一張，放到它該去的那張紙上。
  3. Some cards may be true for both, such as “too small to see.” Decide together where to put those.有些卡片可能兩邊都對，例如「小到看不見」。大家一起決定該放在哪裡。
  4. Then try to explain the difference to someone at home, using only three of the cards.最後只用其中三張卡片，試著把兩者的差別講給家人聽。

A good set of cards covers four questions. Is it a cell? How big is it? How does it make more of itself? Do antibiotics act on it?
一副好的卡片會回答四個問題：它是不是細胞？它有多大？它怎麼變多？抗生素對它有沒有作用？
