How Does Water Reach the Top of a Tree?水怎麼爬到樹頂？

A tall tree lifts water higher than a ten-story building, every day, without a pump and without a heart. In a 3D model, watch a thread of water run from the roots to the leaves, and find out what is doing the lifting. Turn off the sun, dry out the soil, or fill the air with mist, and see the flow slow down or stop. Last, compare some real trees with the highest that sucking alone can ever raise water.
一棵大樹每天都把水送到比十層樓還高的地方，沒有幫浦，也沒有心臟。在 3D 模型裡，看一條水柱從根一路通到葉子，找出到底是誰在出力。把太陽關掉、讓土乾掉，或是讓空氣充滿水氣，看水流怎麼變慢、甚至停下來。最後拿幾棵真的樹，和「只靠吸」最多能把水抬到的高度比一比。

Big idea · 一句話看懂

A tree has no heart to push water up. The water is pulled up from the top. Water evaporates out of the leaves through tiny pores, and each bit that leaves pulls the next bit up behind it, because water clings to itself. The pull reaches all the way down the trunk to the roots, through very narrow tubes. The sunnier and drier the day, the harder the leaves pull.
樹沒有心臟可以把水往上推。水是從上面被拉上去的。水從葉子上很小的孔蒸發出去，每離開一點，就把後面的那一點往上拉，因為水會緊緊黏住自己。這股拉力沿著樹幹一路傳到根，走的是非常細的管子。天氣愈晴朗、愈乾燥，葉子拉得就愈用力。

What sets the pull · 什麼決定拉力

How fast the water is rising · 水上升的速度

Pores in the leaves · 葉子上的氣孔
Held back by · 被什麼卡住

A bright, dry day. Water is evaporating quickly from the leaves, and the whole thread of water is sliding up to replace it. Notice that the beads never bunch up or spread out. The water moves as one unbroken chain.晴朗又乾燥的一天。水從葉子上很快地蒸發，整條水柱跟著往上滑，把缺口補上。注意珠子不會擠成一堆，也不會散開：水是連成一條不斷的鏈子在移動的。

Water is rising, but gently. Less is evaporating from the leaves, so the pull is weaker. The tree is not choosing a speed. The weather is setting it.水在上升，不過很慢。從葉子蒸發出去的水比較少，所以拉力比較弱。這不是樹在決定速度，是天氣在決定。

It is dark. A leaf opens its pores to take in the gas it needs for making food, and that only happens in the light. So at night the pores shut, almost no water escapes, and the thread of water waits in place.天黑了。葉子打開氣孔，是為了吸進做食物要用的氣體，而那只有在有光的時候才進行。所以到了夜裡氣孔關上，幾乎沒有水散出去，水柱就停在原地等著。

The air is already full of water vapor, so very little more can evaporate into it. The pores are open, but the pull is weak. This is also why wet clothes dry slowly on a damp day.空氣裡已經充滿水氣，所以能再蒸發進去的水很少。氣孔是開著的，可是拉力很弱。溼衣服在潮溼的日子乾得慢，也是同樣的道理。

The soil has dried out. If the leaves kept pulling now, the thread of water could snap and let air into the tubes. So the tree shuts its pores to save water. The leaves droop, and the tree stops making food until rain comes.土乾掉了。要是葉子這時候還繼續拉，水柱可能會斷掉，讓空氣跑進管子裡。所以樹把氣孔關上，省下水分。葉子垂了下來，在下雨之前，樹也沒辦法做食物。

Unit 7: How Does Water Reach the Top of a Tree?

Stand under a tall tree and look up. Every leaf at the top needs water, and all of that water came in through the roots, far below your feet. Your heart pumps blood around your body. A tree has no heart. So how does the water get up there?

站在一棵大樹下往上看。樹頂的每一片葉子都需要水，而這些水全都是從你腳底下很深的根吸進來的。你的心臟把血液打到全身；樹沒有心臟。那麼水是怎麼上去的？

You might guess that the tree sucks it up, the way you drink through a straw. But sucking has a limit. Hundreds of years ago, people who built pumps found that suction could not raise water more than about ten meters, however hard they tried. Many trees are much taller than that.

你可能會猜：樹是把水吸上去的，就像你用吸管喝飲料一樣。可是「吸」有極限。幾百年前，做抽水機的人就發現，不管怎麼努力，靠吸的最多只能把水抬高大約十公尺。很多樹比這高得多。

The answer starts in the leaves. A leaf is covered with tiny pores. They open in the daytime to let in the gas the leaf needs for making food. While they are open, water inside the leaf evaporates and drifts out into the air.

答案要從葉子說起。葉子上佈滿了很小的孔。這些孔在白天打開，讓葉子做食物所需要的氣體進來。孔開著的時候，葉子裡的水就蒸發，飄到空氣裡去。

Here is the important part. Water clings to itself very strongly. Inside a tree the water fills very narrow tubes, and it forms one unbroken column from the leaves all the way down to the roots. When a little water leaves at the top, it pulls the whole column up behind it, like a chain being drawn up through a pipe.

接下來是關鍵。水會非常緊地黏住自己。在樹裡面，水裝在非常細的管子裡，從葉子到根連成一條不斷的水柱。上面走掉一點水，整條水柱就被往上拉，像一條鏈子從管子裡被拉上去一樣。

So the tree does not spend energy to lift the water. The sun and the air do the work by drying the leaves. On a bright, dry day the pull is strong. On a damp day it is weak, and at night it nearly stops.

所以樹並沒有花力氣把水抬上去。出力的是太陽和空氣：它們把葉子弄乾。晴朗乾燥的日子，拉力很強；潮溼的日子拉力弱；到了晚上，幾乎就停了。

A tree uses only a tiny part of this water to grow. Nearly all of it, from 97 to more than 99 parts in every hundred, passes straight through and out into the air. That may sound like waste, but the stream carries minerals up from the soil, and the evaporation cools the leaves.

這些水，樹只用掉極小的一部分來生長。幾乎全部——每一百份裡有 97 份到 99 份以上——都只是穿過樹身，散到空氣裡去。聽起來很浪費，不過這股水流把礦物質從土裡帶了上來，而蒸發也讓葉子降溫。

Questions · 閱讀理解

Why can a tree not simply suck water up, like a straw?

Suction cannot raise water more than about ten meters, and many trees are much taller than that.

What happens to water when it reaches the leaves?

It evaporates and drifts out into the air through tiny pores.

Why does the water in a tree move as one column?

Water clings to itself very strongly, so when a little leaves at the top, it pulls the rest up behind it.

Tree Stories · 樹的故事

A stubborn pump, a careful vicar, drops at dawn, and a sword that touches the sky 不聽話的抽水機、細心的牧師、清晨的水珠，還有一把倚天劍

How people worked out what trees were doing all along.
人們是怎麼弄懂樹一直在做的這件事的。

1600s · 十七世紀

The pump that would not go higher再也抽不上去的抽水機

People who built pumps in Italy had a problem. A suction pump could not raise water more than about ten meters, no matter how it was built. Galileo wrote about the puzzle, and his student Evangelista Torricelli solved it. The pump does not pull the water. The air outside pushes it, and the air has only so much weight. Trees, it turned out, must be doing something else.
義大利造抽水機的人遇到一個難題：用吸的抽水機，不管怎麼造，都沒辦法把水抬高超過大約十公尺。伽利略寫下了這個謎題，他的學生托里切利解開了它：抽水機並沒有在「拉」水，是外面的空氣把水「推」上來的，而空氣的重量就只有那麼多。這麼說來，樹一定是用了別的辦法。

1727

The vicar who weighed plants替植物秤重的牧師

Stephen Hales was an English clergyman who loved to measure things. In a book published in 1727 he described his experiments on plants. He compared how much water a plant took in with how much it gave off through its leaves, and his numbers showed water passing through the plant and out into the air.
海爾斯是一位熱愛測量的英國牧師。在 1727 年出版的書裡，他記錄了自己對植物做的實驗：比較一株植物吸進多少水、又從葉子散出去多少水，他的數字顯示：水穿過植物，散到了空氣裡。

Dawn · 清晨

Drops on the tips of leaves葉尖上的水珠

Roots can also push a little. At night, when the leaves are not pulling, the roots of some plants go on taking in water, and the pressure builds from below. By morning it forces drops of sap out at the tips or edges of the leaves. They look like dew, but they came from inside the plant. This push is much too weak to supply a tall tree.
根也會推一點點。夜裡葉子不拉水的時候，有些植物的根還是繼續吸水，壓力從下面慢慢累積；到了早上，就把一滴一滴的汁液從葉尖或葉緣擠出來。它們看起來像露水，其實是從植物裡面出來的。這股推力太弱，供應不了一棵大樹。

Taiwan · 台灣

The tree that touches the sky倚天而立的樹

In January 2023, a team from the Taiwan Forestry Research Institute and National Cheng Kung University walked for days into the mountains of the upper Da'an River. They had picked out one treetop from laser maps made from the air. They climbed the tree and measured it at 84.1 meters, the tallest tree known in Taiwan and in East Asia. It is a Taiwania. Because it stands out above the giants around it, the team named it the Da'an River Yitian Sword. Every drop of water at its top has been pulled up the whole way.
2023 年 1 月，林業試驗所和成功大學組成的團隊，在大安溪上游的山區走了好幾天。他們事先從空中雷射測繪的資料裡，挑出了一個特別高的樹頂。隊員爬上那棵樹實地測量，結果是 84.1 公尺——台灣、也是東亞已知最高的樹。它是一棵台灣杉；因為它在周圍眾多巨木當中特別挺拔，團隊把它取名為「大安溪倚天劍」。它樹頂的每一滴水，都是這樣一路被拉上去的。

Myth vs. Fact · 常見迷思

Four things people get wrong 四個常見的誤會

✗ Myth · 迷思Trees suck water up like a straw.樹像吸管一樣把水吸上去。

✓ Fact · 事實Suction stops at about ten meters. The water is pulled up as a column, by evaporation from the leaves.用吸的只到大約十公尺。水是連成一條水柱，被葉子上的蒸發拉上去的。

✗ Myth · 迷思The roots pump the water to the top.是根把水打到樹頂的。

✓ Fact · 事實Roots can push only a little. Almost all of the lifting comes from the pull at the leaves.根只能推一點點。幾乎所有的力氣，都來自葉子那一端的拉力。

✗ Myth · 迷思A tree uses up all the water it drinks.樹把喝進去的水都用掉了。

✓ Fact · 事實It keeps only a tiny part. Nearly all of the water passes through and into the air.它只留下極小的一部分；幾乎所有的水都只是穿過樹身，散到空氣裡。

✗ Myth · 迷思Water rises in a tree at the same speed all day.樹裡的水整天都以同樣的速度上升。

✓ Fact · 事實It rises fastest on bright, dry days and nearly stops at night, when the pores are shut.晴朗乾燥的時候最快；到了晚上氣孔關上，就幾乎停了。

Classroom Activity 1 · 課堂活動一

A Bag on a Branch 套在樹枝上的袋子

You need · 準備材料

  • A clear plastic bag and a piece of string一個透明的塑膠袋和一段繩子
  • A leafy branch on a living tree or bush, in the sun一根長在活的樹或灌木上、曬得到太陽、葉子多的枝條

Steps · 步驟

  1. Slip the bag over the end of the branch so that several leaves are inside. Do not break the branch.把袋子套在枝條的末端，讓幾片葉子在袋子裡。不要把樹枝折斷。
  2. Tie the mouth of the bag gently around the stem.把袋口輕輕綁在枝條上。
  3. Look at the bag after one hour, and again after three hours. What do you see on the inside?一小時後看看袋子，三小時後再看一次。袋子裡面出現了什麼？
  4. Try a second bag on a branch in the shade. Which bag collects more?在陰影裡的枝條上再套一個袋子。哪一個袋子收集到的比較多？

The drops are water that came up from the roots and out through the leaves. Take the bag off on the same day, so that the leaves are not harmed.
那些水珠是從根上來、再從葉子散出去的水。當天就把袋子拿下來，免得傷到葉子。

Classroom Activity 2 · 課堂活動二

Celery and Colored Water 芹菜和有顏色的水

You need · 準備材料

  • A stalk of celery with its leaves, or a white flower一根帶葉子的芹菜，或一朵白色的花
  • A glass of water with food coloring in it一杯加了食用色素的水

Steps · 步驟

  1. Ask an adult to cut the bottom off the stalk, and stand it in the colored water.請大人把芹菜的底部切掉一小段，然後把芹菜插在有顏色的水裡。
  2. Leave it in a bright place. Look at the leaves after a few hours, and again the next day.放在明亮的地方。幾小時後看看葉子，隔天再看一次。
  3. Ask the adult to cut across the stalk. Where exactly is the color?請大人把芹菜橫切開。顏色到底在哪裡？
  4. Set up a second stalk in a dark cupboard. Does the color travel as fast?再準備一根，放在暗的櫃子裡。顏色走得一樣快嗎？

The colored dots in the cut stalk are the tubes that carry water. Do not eat the celery afterward.
切面上那些有顏色的小點，就是運水的管子。做完之後這根芹菜不要吃。
