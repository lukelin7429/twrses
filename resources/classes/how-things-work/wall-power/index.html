Unit 2: Where Does the Electricity in the Wall Come From?

Plug in a phone charger, and electricity is there instantly, day and night. A battery runs out, but the outlet in the wall never seems to. Where does that electricity come from? Follow the wires behind the wall, and they lead to a power line, then to a substation, and finally to a power plant that may be far away. And inside almost every power plant, you will find the same simple thing: a magnet that spins.

插上手機充電器，電馬上就來了，白天晚上都一樣。電池會用完，牆上的插座卻好像永遠用不完。這些電是從哪裡來的？順著牆裡的電線往外找，會接到電線桿上的電線，再接到變電所，最後到達可能很遠很遠的發電廠。而幾乎每一座發電廠裡，都有同一樣簡單的東西：一塊會轉的磁鐵。

In 1831, the English scientist Michael Faraday discovered something amazing. When he moved a magnet near a coil of wire, electricity flowed in the wire. When the magnet stopped, the electricity stopped too. A magnet sitting still does nothing; it is the change, the movement, that pushes the electrons. This is called electromagnetic induction, and almost every power plant in the world depends on it.

1831 年，英國科學家法拉第發現了一件驚人的事：他把磁鐵在一圈線圈旁邊移動，線圈裡就有電流；磁鐵一停，電也跟著停。磁鐵靜靜放著什麼都不會發生，是「變化」、是「動」在推電子。這叫做電磁感應，世界上幾乎每一座發電廠都靠它。

A generator is a faster, bigger version of Faraday's experiment. A magnet spins next to coils of wire. As the north pole sweeps past a coil, it pushes the electrons one way. Half a turn later, the south pole sweeps past and pushes them the other way. So the current keeps changing direction. This is called alternating current, or AC. In Taiwan, the current makes 60 complete back-and-forth cycles every second, which is why we say the power is 60 hertz.

發電機就是法拉第實驗的放大、加速版：一塊磁鐵在線圈旁邊轉。N 極掃過線圈時，把電子往一個方向推；轉了半圈，換 S 極掃過，又把電子往反方向推。所以電流的方向一直在改變，這叫做交流電（AC）。在台灣，電流每秒完整來回 60 次，所以我們說台灣的電是 60 赫茲。

So what turns the magnet? In most power plants, it is a turbine, a wheel with blades, pushed by something moving fast. Coal and nuclear plants boil water into high-pressure steam that blasts through the turbine. Gas plants burn natural gas in a turbine like a jet engine, and many also use the leftover heat to make steam. At a dam, falling water turns the turbine, and on a wind farm, the wind does. Only one common source works differently: solar panels turn sunlight into electricity with no moving parts at all.

那是什麼在轉磁鐵？大多數電廠裡，是一具渦輪機：一個有葉片的輪子，被高速流動的東西推著轉。燃煤和核能電廠把水燒成高壓蒸汽，衝過渦輪；燃氣電廠在像噴射引擎的渦輪裡燒天然氣，很多還會利用剩下的熱再燒出蒸汽。在水壩，是落下的水推動渦輪；在風場，則是風。常見的發電方式裡，只有一種不一樣：太陽能板直接把陽光變成電，完全沒有會動的零件。

From the generator, electricity travels across the island on tall towers. To travel far with little waste, it is raised to a very high voltage; Taipower's biggest lines carry 345,000 volts. Substations step it down again and again, until a transformer near your street lowers it to the 110 volts in your outlet. The electrons in your wall do not travel all the way from the power plant. They just wiggle back and forth in place while the energy rushes along the wires.

電從發電機出來後，沿著高高的電塔橫越全島。為了送得遠、少浪費，電壓會被升得非常高；台電最高的輸電線是 34 萬 5 千伏特。變電所再一層一層把它降下來，最後由你家附近的變壓器降到插座的 110 伏特。你家牆裡的電子並不是從發電廠一路跑過來的，它們只是在原地來回晃動，真正沿著電線飛快傳過來的是能量。

So where does Taiwan's electricity come from? In 2025, about 48% came from burning natural gas, 35% from coal, 13% from renewable sources such as solar and wind, and about 1% from nuclear power. Taiwan's last nuclear reactor shut down in May 2025. Every choice has a cost: coal and gas release carbon dioxide, and the sun and wind come and go with the weather. But apart from solar panels, all of them come back to the idea Faraday found almost two hundred years ago: spin a magnet, and the electrons move.

那台灣的電從哪裡來？2025 年，大約 48% 來自燃燒天然氣，35% 來自燃煤，13% 來自太陽能、風力等再生能源，大約 1% 來自核能。台灣最後一座核能機組在 2025 年 5 月停機。每一種選擇都有代價：燃煤和燃氣會排放二氧化碳，太陽和風則隨天氣來來去去。但除了太陽能板，它們都回到法拉第將近兩百年前發現的道理：讓磁鐵轉起來，電子就會動。

Questions · 閱讀理解

What did Faraday discover in 1831?

He discovered that moving a magnet near a coil of wire makes electricity flow in the wire, and that a magnet that is not moving makes no electricity.

Why is the electricity in the wall called alternating current?

Because as the magnet spins, its north and south poles take turns sweeping past the coils, so the current keeps changing direction. In Taiwan, it goes back and forth 60 times a second.

Which common way of making electricity does not spin a generator?

Solar panels. They turn sunlight directly into electricity with no moving parts.

Six Ways to Make Power · 六種發電方式

What turns the generator? 是什麼在轉發電機？

Five of these six spin a generator. They differ only in what pushes the turbine, and in what that costs the air, the land, and the future.
這六種裡有五種都在轉發電機，差別只在「是什麼推動渦輪」，以及它對空氣、土地和未來的代價。

🔥

Natural gas天然氣

47.7% in 2025 · 2025 年 47.7%⟳ Gas turbine, often plus a steam turbine · 燃氣渦輪，常再加蒸汽渦輪

Burns gas in a turbine like a jet engine. In a combined-cycle plant, the hot exhaust also boils water for a second, steam-driven turbine.
在像噴射引擎的渦輪裡燒天然氣。複循環電廠還會用排出的熱氣燒水，推動第二具蒸汽渦輪。

🏭

Coal燃煤

35.3% in 2025 · 2025 年 35.3%⟳ Steam turbine · 蒸汽渦輪

Burns coal to boil water, and the steam spins the turbine. For the same amount of electricity, coal releases more than twice as much carbon dioxide as natural gas.
燒煤把水煮成蒸汽，蒸汽轉動渦輪。發同樣多的電，燃煤排放的二氧化碳是天然氣的兩倍以上。

⚛️

Nuclear核能

1.1% in 2025 · 2025 年 1.1%⟳ Steam turbine · 蒸汽渦輪

Splitting uranium atoms heats water into steam. Taiwan's last reactor, Maanshan Unit 2, shut down in May 2025. An August 2025 vote on restarting it did not pass because too few people voted.
鈾原子分裂放出的熱把水變成蒸汽。台灣最後一座機組核三廠二號機在 2025 年 5 月停機；同年 8 月重啟核三的公投，因投票人數不足門檻而沒有通過。

💧

Water水力

Part of renewables and pumped storage · 屬於再生能源與抽蓄水力⟳ Water turbine · 水輪機

Falling water spins the turbine. At Sun Moon Lake, Taipower pumps water uphill in the daytime with solar power and lets it fall to make electricity at night, like a giant battery.
落下的水轉動水輪機。在日月潭，台電白天用太陽光電把水抽上去，晚上再放水發電，像一顆巨大的電池。

🌬️

Wind風力

Part of renewables · 屬於再生能源⟳ The wind turns the blades · 風推動葉片

The wind pushes long blades that turn a generator at the top of the tower. Some of Asia's largest offshore wind farms stand in the sea off Changhua.
風推動長長的葉片，帶動塔頂的發電機。亞太地區最大的離岸風場之一，就在彰化外海。

☀️

Solar太陽能

Part of renewables · 屬於再生能源⟳ Nothing spins! · 完全不用轉！

The one exception: solar panels turn light straight into electricity with no moving parts. How they do it is Lesson 3.
唯一的例外：太陽能板直接把光變成電，沒有任何會動的零件。它怎麼做到，是下一課的主題。

No generator to spin · 沒有發電機要轉

Myth vs. Fact · 常見迷思

Four things people get wrong 四個常見的誤會

✗ Myth · 迷思The electricity in the wall is stored somewhere, like water in a tank.插座裡的電是儲存在某個地方，像水塔裡的水。

✓ Fact · 事實Most of it is made at the very moment you use it. Power plants speed up and slow down all day to match what everyone is using right now.大部分的電是在你使用的同一時刻才發出來的。發電廠一整天都在加速、減速，配合所有人此刻用了多少電。

✗ Myth · 迷思A strong magnet next to a wire makes electricity.強力磁鐵放在電線旁邊就會發電。

✓ Fact · 事實Only a changing magnetic field makes electricity. The magnet has to move, or the coil has to move. A magnet sitting still makes none at all.只有「變化」的磁場才會發電：磁鐵要動，或線圈要動。靜止的磁鐵一點電也不會產生。

✗ Myth · 迷思The electrons in your outlet traveled all the way from the power plant.插座裡的電子是從發電廠一路跑過來的。

✓ Fact · 事實In alternating current, electrons just wiggle back and forth in place. What travels from the power plant is energy, carried along the wires very fast.交流電的電子只在原地來回晃。從發電廠傳過來的是能量，沿著電線飛快地送到。

✗ Myth · 迷思Taiwan's electricity is the same everywhere in the world.全世界的插座都和台灣一樣。

✓ Fact · 事實Taiwan uses 110 volts at 60 hertz, like the United States. Most of Europe and mainland China use about 220 to 230 volts at 50 hertz, so travelers need the right adapter.台灣用 110 伏特、60 赫茲，和美國相同；歐洲大部分國家和中國大陸用 220–230 伏特、50 赫茲，出國要帶合適的轉接頭與變壓器。

Remember It · 記憶口訣

Power plants in a sentence 一句話記住發電

No motion, no electricity不動，就沒有電

A magnet only makes electricity while it is moving. Spin it, and the electrons move; stop it, and they stop.
磁鐵只有在動的時候才發電。轉它，電子就動；停下來，電子也停。

North pushes, south pulls backN 極推過去，S 極推回來

The poles take turns passing the coil, so the current keeps switching direction. That is AC.
兩個磁極輪流經過線圈，電流就一直換方向，這就是交流電。

Everything turns the same shaft大家都在轉同一根軸

Steam, gas, water, and wind are just different hands turning the crank. Only solar has no crank at all.
蒸汽、燃氣、水和風，只是換了不同的手在搖同一根曲柄；只有太陽能完全沒有曲柄。

High on the road, low at home路上用高壓，到家變低壓

Electricity travels at hundreds of thousands of volts to waste less, then steps down to 110 volts for your home.
電在路上用幾十萬伏特傳送以減少浪費，到家前再降成 110 伏特。

Safety First · 安全提醒

Before you try anything 動手之前先讀

  • Never put anything except a plug into an outlet, and never touch a plug with wet hands. The electricity in the wall can seriously hurt or kill you.插座只插插頭，絕不放進其他東西；手濕的時候絕不碰插頭。插座裡的電會造成重傷甚至致命。
  • Stay away from fallen power lines, especially after a typhoon. Tell an adult and call Taipower; do not go near them.遠離掉落的電線，颱風過後尤其要小心。告訴大人並通報台電，不要靠近。
  • Never fly kites, drones, or balloons near power lines, and never climb electric towers or poles.絕不在電線附近放風箏、無人機或氣球，也絕不攀爬電塔或電線桿。
  • A gasoline generator must never be run indoors or in a garage. Its exhaust contains carbon monoxide, a gas you cannot see or smell that can kill.汽油發電機絕不能在室內或車庫裡運轉。它的廢氣含有一氧化碳，看不見也聞不到，會致命。
  • Strong magnets can pinch skin and are very dangerous if swallowed. Keep them away from young children.強力磁鐵會夾傷皮膚，誤吞非常危險，要放在幼兒拿不到的地方。

Classroom Activity 1 · 課堂活動一

Make Electricity with a Magnet 用磁鐵發電

You need · 準備材料

  • About 10 meters of thin enameled copper wire (magnet wire)約 10 公尺的細漆包銅線
  • A cardboard tube, such as a toilet-paper roll一個紙筒（例如衛生紙捲筒）
  • A strong bar magnet that fits inside the tube一根放得進紙筒的強力磁棒
  • A multimeter, or a compass三用電表，或一個指南針
  • Sandpaper and tape砂紙、膠帶

Steps · 步驟

  1. Wind the wire around the tube at least 100 times, keeping the turns close together. Tape the coil so it does not unwind, and leave about 30 centimeters free at each end.把銅線在紙筒上繞至少 100 圈，一圈緊挨一圈，用膠帶固定，兩端各留約 30 公分。
  2. Sand the last 2 centimeters of each end until the copper shines. The colored coating does not carry electricity.用砂紙把兩端最後 2 公分磨到露出亮亮的銅色；外面那層漆不導電。
  3. Connect the two ends to a multimeter set to its smallest voltage range. (No meter? Wrap the ends 20 times around a compass instead and watch the needle.)兩端接到三用電表，轉到最小的電壓檔。（沒有電表？把兩端在指南針上繞 20 圈，看指針。）
  4. Push the magnet quickly into the tube. Watch the reading jump. Hold the magnet still inside: the reading drops back to zero.把磁鐵快速推進紙筒，讀數會跳一下；把磁鐵停在裡面不動，讀數又回到零。
  5. Pull the magnet out. The reading jumps the other way. Push it in and out over and over: you have made alternating current.把磁鐵拉出來，讀數往反方向跳。一進一出來回做：你做出的就是交流電。
  6. Try it faster and slower. Which gives the bigger reading?快一點、慢一點各試試看，哪一種讀數比較大？

Strong magnets can pinch fingers, so an adult should handle them. Keep magnets away from phones, bank cards, and anyone with a pacemaker, and never let small children put magnets in their mouths. The reading is tiny, only thousandths of a volt, which is why power plants spin magnets so fast in huge coils.
強力磁鐵會夾傷手指，請由大人拿取。磁鐵要遠離手機、金融卡和裝心律調節器的人，也絕不能讓小孩放進嘴裡。讀數非常小，只有千分之幾伏特，這也是電廠要在巨大的線圈裡把磁鐵轉得那麼快的原因。

Classroom Activity 2 · 課堂活動二

Follow the Wires 順著電線找電從哪裡來

You need · 準備材料

  • A walk around the school or neighborhood (with an adult)在校園或社區走一圈（大人陪同）
  • A notebook for a simple map一本可以畫簡單地圖的筆記本

Steps · 步驟

  1. Start at a classroom outlet. Where do the wires go? Look for the electrical panel with the switches (breakers), but do not open it.從教室的插座開始：電線往哪裡去？找找看有開關（斷路器）的配電箱，但不要打開。
  2. Go outside and find where the power comes into the building, and the transformer nearby: a gray barrel on a pole or a green box on the ground.到戶外找電從哪裡進入大樓，以及附近的變壓器：電線桿上的灰色圓桶，或地上的綠色箱子。
  3. Follow the lines along the street with your eyes. Can you see tall towers in the distance? Those carry the high-voltage lines.用眼睛順著街上的電線看過去。遠方看得到高高的電塔嗎？那就是高壓輸電線。
  4. Draw the path: outlet → panel → transformer → power lines → substation → power plant. Write the voltage at each step that you learned in the reading.畫出路線：插座 → 配電箱 → 變壓器 → 電線 → 變電所 → 發電廠，並寫上課文裡學到的各段電壓。
  5. Back in class, look up Taipower's live data, which shows how much each power plant is making right now, updated every 10 minutes.回到教室，查台電的即時資料：每 10 分鐘更新一次，顯示每座電廠此刻發多少電。

Look, but never touch. Stay far away from substations, transformer boxes, and fallen power lines, and never fly a kite or climb a tree near power lines.
只看不碰。遠離變電所、變壓器箱和掉落的電線；絕不在電線附近放風箏或爬樹。
