How Things Work · 萬物原理

How Things Work 萬物原理

Everyday questions with surprising answers: how batteries store power, how a message crosses the ocean, why the sky is blue. Read the answer in English, then see it work in 3D.
生活裡的問題，背後都有驚人的答案：電池怎麼存電、訊息怎麼橫越大海、天空為什麼是藍的。先用英文讀懂，再用 3D 看它怎麼運作。

Every lesson starts with an everyday question. A 3D model shows the answer at work, one idea at a time, and an English reading explains it, with Chinese a tap away.
每一課都從一個生活裡的問題開始：先用 3D 模型看答案怎麼運作，一課只講清楚一個道理；再讀一篇英文短文把它說明白，中文翻譯隨點隨看。

The lessons are grouped into five units. Each lesson has words to learn, a short quiz, common myths, easy ways to remember the idea, and an activity to try at home or in class. Facts and numbers are checked, and every lesson lists its sources.
課程分成五個單元。每一課都有生字、小測驗、常見迷思、記憶口訣，以及在家或在教室就能做的活動。事實與數字都經過查證，每課最後列出資料出處。

11 of 20 lessons are ready so far; more are on the way.
目前完成 11 課（共規劃 20 課），持續增加中。

Unit 1 · 單元一

Electricity and Energy 電與能源

Where the power in your pocket, your wall, and on your roof comes from.
口袋裡、插座裡、屋頂上的電，都是從哪裡來的。

Lesson 1 · 第一課入門 · Beginner

How Does a Battery Store Electricity?

電池怎麼儲存電？A battery is not a tank of electricity. Look inside a lithium-ion battery in 3D and watch ions cross inside while electrons light the bulb outside.電池不是裝著電的罐子。用 3D 看進鋰離子電池裡：離子在裡面跨過去，電子在外面點亮燈泡。Start the lesson · 開始上課 →Lesson 2 · 第二課入門 · Beginner

Where Does the Electricity in the Wall Come From?

插座裡的電從哪裡來？Every outlet is connected to a spinning magnet. Turn the generator in 3D, watch the current swing back and forth, and see where Taiwan's power comes from.每一個插座，另一頭都連著一塊轉動的磁鐵。用 3D 轉轉看發電機，看電流來回擺動，再看看台灣的電從哪裡來。Start the lesson · 開始上課 →Lesson 3 · 第三課入門 · Beginner

How Does a Solar Panel Turn Light into Electricity?

太陽能板怎麼把光變成電？A solar panel never moves, yet electricity flows out of it all day. Watch photons knock electrons loose in a slice of silicon, then try red, blue, infrared, clouds, and night.太陽能板一動也不動，卻整天流出電來。用 3D 看光子在矽片裡敲出電子，再換紅光、藍光、紅外線、陰天和夜晚試試看。Start the lesson · 開始上課 →Lesson 4 · 第四課入門 · Beginner

How Does a Wind Turbine Make Power?

風力發電機怎麼發電？Giant wings at sea turn the monsoon into electricity. Turn up the wind on a 3D offshore turbine, look inside at the generator, and see why twice the wind means eight times the power.海上的巨大翅膀，把季風變成電。用 3D 離岸風機把風調大調小、看進機艙裡的發電機，看看為什麼風速加倍、電就變八倍。Start the lesson · 開始上課 →

Unit 2 · 單元二

Information and Communication 資訊與通訊

How a message, a phone call, and your location travel through wires, air, and space.
訊息、電話和你的位置，怎麼穿過電線、空氣與太空。

Lesson 5 · 第五課入門 · Beginner

How Does the Internet Work?

網際網路怎麼運作？A message to Boston is cut into numbered packets that find their own way under the ocean. Send a text or a photo in 3D, lose a packet, cut a cable, and watch the internet fix it.傳到波士頓的訊息，會被切成有編號的封包，各自從海底找路過去。用 3D 傳文字或照片、弄丟一個封包、剪斷一條海纜，看網路怎麼自己補救。Start the lesson · 開始上課 →Lesson 6 · 第六課入門 · Beginner

How Does a Phone Call Travel Without Wires?

手機訊號怎麼在空中傳遞？Your phone talks to a tower using invisible radio waves. Walk a hiker over a 3D mountain, watch the signal bars drop behind the ridge, then add a tower and switch to 5G.手機用看不見的無線電波和基地台說話。在 3D 山區帶著登山客走過山脊，看訊號格數在山後掉下來，再加一座基地台、換成 5G 試試看。Start the lesson · 開始上課 →Lesson 7 · 第七課入門 · Beginner

How Does GPS Know Where You Are?

GPS 怎麼知道你在哪裡？Satellites 20,200 kilometers up keep telling the time. Turn a 3D Earth, watch circles from one, two, three, and four satellites meet at Changhua, then put the phone's clock off and see the blue dot go wrong.兩萬公里高的衛星不停報時。轉動 3D 地球，看一、二、三、四顆衛星畫出的圓交在彰化，再讓手機的時鐘差一點點，看藍點跑掉多遠。Start the lesson · 開始上課 →Lesson 8 · 第八課入門 · Beginner

How Does a Computer Remember Things?

電腦怎麼記住東西？Everything a computer remembers is stored as 0s and 1s. Type a word into a 3D memory chip, watch each letter become eight tiny cups of charge, then pull the plug and see which kind of memory forgets.電腦記住的每一樣東西，都存成 0 和 1。在 3D 記憶體晶片裡打一個字，看每個字母變成 8 個裝電荷的小杯子；再拔掉電源，看哪一種記憶體會忘記。Start the lesson · 開始上課 →

Unit 3 · 單元三

Light and Sound 光與聲音

Why the sky is blue, where rainbows come from, and how sounds and pictures are captured.
天空為什麼是藍的、彩虹從哪裡來，聲音和影像怎麼被捕捉下來。

Lesson 9 · 第九課入門 · Beginner

Why Is the Sky Blue?

天空為什麼是藍色的？Sunlight looks white, so why is the sky blue and the sunset red? Watch light cross the air in 3D, slide the Sun down to the horizon, and see the sky change color over Changhua.陽光看起來是白的，天空為什麼是藍的、夕陽又為什麼是紅的？在 3D 裡看光穿過大氣，把太陽拉到地平線，看彰化的天空怎麼變色。Start the lesson · 開始上課 →Lesson 10 · 第十課入門 · Beginner

How Does a Rainbow Form?

彩虹是怎麼形成的？A rainbow is sunlight bent and bounced inside millions of raindrops. Follow one ray through a 3D raindrop, then stand with the Sun behind you and watch falling rain light up in a 42-degree circle.彩虹是陽光在幾百萬顆雨滴裡轉彎、反射出來的光。在 3D 雨滴裡跟著一道光走，再背對太陽站好，看落下的雨在 42 度的圓上亮起來。Start the lesson · 開始上課 →Lesson 11 · 第十一課入門 · Beginner

How Does Sound Reach Your Ears?

聲音怎麼傳到耳朵？Every sound is a push passed from molecule to molecule. Watch a drum send waves of squeezed air to an ear in 3D, change the pitch and the loudness, take the air away, and work out how far away the lightning was.每一個聲音，都是空氣分子一個推一個傳過來的。在 3D 裡看鼓把一波波擠壓的空氣送到耳朵，調整音調和音量、把空氣拿走，再算算閃電有多遠。Start the lesson · 開始上課 →
