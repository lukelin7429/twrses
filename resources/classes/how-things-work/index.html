How Things Work · 萬物原理

How Things Work 萬物原理

Everyday questions with surprising answers: how batteries store power, how a message crosses the ocean, why the sky is blue. Read the answer in English, then see it work in 3D.
生活裡的問題，背後都有驚人的答案：電池怎麼存電、訊息怎麼橫越大海、天空為什麼是藍的。先用英文讀懂，再用 3D 看它怎麼運作。

Every lesson starts with an everyday question. A 3D model shows the answer at work, one idea at a time, and an English reading explains it, with Chinese a tap away.
每一課都從一個生活裡的問題開始：先用 3D 模型看答案怎麼運作，一課只講清楚一個道理；再讀一篇英文短文把它說明白，中文翻譯隨點隨看。

The lessons are grouped into five units. Each lesson has words to learn, a short quiz, common myths, easy ways to remember the idea, and an activity to try at home or in class. Facts and numbers are checked, and every lesson lists its sources.
課程分成五個單元。每一課都有生字、小測驗、常見迷思、記憶口訣，以及在家或在教室就能做的活動。事實與數字都經過查證，每課最後列出資料出處。

3 of 20 lessons are ready so far; more are on the way.
目前完成 3 課（共規劃 20 課），持續增加中。

How Does a Battery Store Electricity?

Where Does the Electricity in the Wall Come From?

插座裡的電從哪裡來？Every outlet is connected to a spinning magnet. Turn the generator in 3D, watch the current swing back and forth, and see where Taiwan's power comes from.每一個插座，另一頭都連著一塊轉動的磁鐵。用 3D 轉轉看發電機，看電流來回擺動，再看看台灣的電從哪裡來。Start the lesson · 開始上課 →Lesson 3 · 第三課入門 · Beginner

How Does a Solar Panel Turn Light into Electricity?

太陽能板怎麼把光變成電？A solar panel never moves, yet electricity flows out of it all day. Watch photons knock electrons loose in a slice of silicon, then try red, blue, infrared, clouds, and night.太陽能板一動也不動，卻整天流出電來。用 3D 看光子在矽片裡敲出電子，再換紅光、藍光、紅外線、陰天和夜晚試試看。Start the lesson · 開始上課 →
