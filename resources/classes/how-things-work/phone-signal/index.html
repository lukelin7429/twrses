How Does a Phone Call Travel Without Wires?手機訊號怎麼在空中傳遞？

Your phone talks to a tower using invisible radio waves. Walk a hiker over a 3D mountain, watch the signal bars drop behind the ridge, then add a tower and switch to 5G.
手機用看不見的無線電波和基地台說話。在 3D 山區帶著登山客走過山脊，看訊號格數在山後掉下來，再加一座基地台、換成 5G 試試看。

Big idea · 一句話看懂

A phone turns your voice and data into invisible radio waves and sends them only as far as the nearest base station. The base station passes them into the cable network, so the wireless part is just the last short hop, and a mountain in the way can block it.
手機把你的聲音和資料變成看不見的無線電波，只送到最近的基地台；基地台再把它接進電纜網路。所以無線只是最後短短的一段，而中間只要有山擋住，訊號就會被擋掉。

Unit 6: How Does a Phone Call Travel Without Wires?

You are hiking in Taiwan's mountains with your family. In the parking lot, your phone shows full bars. An hour up the trail, behind a ridge, the bars drop to one, and then to none. The phone has not changed, and neither has the network. What changed is the mountain between you and a tower you cannot see. How does a phone talk through thin air, and why can a mountain stop it?

你和家人在台灣的山裡健行。在停車場時，手機訊號滿格；沿著步道走了一個小時、繞到山脊後面，格數掉到一格，然後完全沒有。手機沒變，電信網路也沒變，變的是你和一座看不見的基地台之間多了一座山。手機到底怎麼穿過空氣說話？一座山又為什麼能把它擋住？

In 1901, the Italian inventor Guglielmo Marconi sent a radio signal across the Atlantic Ocean, about 3,400 kilometers from England to Canada. The whole message was the letter S in Morse code. In 1973, Martin Cooper of Motorola made the first call from a handheld mobile phone, standing on a street in New York. His phone weighed more than a kilogram. Today's phones are far lighter, but they still use the same invisible waves.

1901 年，義大利發明家馬可尼把無線電訊號送過大西洋，從英國傳到約 3,400 公里外的加拿大，整則訊息只有摩斯電碼的一個字母 S。1973 年，摩托羅拉的庫柏站在紐約街頭，打出了第一通手持行動電話，他的電話重超過一公斤。今天的手機輕多了，但用的仍是同一種看不見的波。

A phone works by sending and receiving radio waves. Radio waves are a kind of light that our eyes cannot see, and they travel at the speed of light, about 300,000 kilometers every second. Your phone turns your voice into a pattern of tiny changes in a radio wave, and its antenna sends the wave out in all directions. A tower three kilometers away receives it in about ten millionths of a second.

手機靠著發送和接收無線電波運作。無線電波是一種眼睛看不見的光，以光速前進，每秒大約 30 萬公里。手機把你的聲音變成無線電波上一連串細微的變化，天線再把波往四面八方送出去。三公里外的基地台，大約只要十萬分之一秒就收到了。

The waves do not travel all the way to your friend. They only go as far as the nearest base station, a tower with antennas on a hill, a rooftop, or a pole. The base station connects to the cable network from Lesson 5, which carries the call the rest of the way. Each base station covers an area called a cell, which is why Americans say cell phone. As you move, your phone switches from one base station to the next without dropping the call. This switch is called a handoff.

這些波並不會一路傳到朋友那裡，它們只送到最近的基地台：裝著天線的鐵塔，可能在山頭、屋頂或電線桿上。基地台接到第五課講的電纜網路，由電纜把通話送完剩下的路。每個基地台負責一塊叫做「細胞」（cell）的區域，所以美國人把手機叫做 cell phone。你移動的時候，手機會從一個基地台換到下一個，通話不會中斷，這叫做「換手」。

Radio waves grow weaker as they spread out, and they lose even more strength when something solid is in the way. They can bend a little around a hilltop, but a big mountain blocks most of the signal. That is why hikers often have signal on a summit but none in the valley behind it. Lower-frequency waves travel farther and get around obstacles better. Higher-frequency waves, like the 3.5 gigahertz used for 5G, carry more data but do not reach as far, so 5G needs more base stations.

無線電波越擴散越弱，碰到堅固的東西擋在中間，又會損失更多。它可以繞過山頂一點點，但一座大山會擋掉大部分的訊號。所以登山客常在山頂有訊號，到了山後的谷地卻完全沒有。頻率比較低的波傳得比較遠、也比較能繞過障礙；頻率比較高的波，像 5G 用的 3.5 GHz，能帶更多資料，但傳不了那麼遠，所以 5G 需要更多基地台。

Taiwan's first 5G service began on June 30, 2020. By the end of 2025, Taiwan had more than 120,000 licensed base stations, and for the first time more of them were for 5G than for 4G. Still, nobody can put a tower behind every mountain. In an emergency, dial 112. If any phone company's base station can hear your phone, the call goes through, even without a SIM card. On long hikes, carry another way to call for help too, such as a satellite messenger.

台灣的第一個 5G 服務在 2020 年 6 月 30 日開台。到 2025 年底，台灣有超過 12 萬張基地台執照，而且 5G 的數量第一次超過了 4G。不過，沒有人能在每一座山後面都蓋基地台。遇到緊急狀況，請撥 112：只要任何一家電信業者的基地台收得到你的手機，就算沒有 SIM 卡也打得通。走長程的登山路線時，也要帶另一種求救工具，例如衛星通訊器。

Questions · 閱讀理解

Where do the radio waves from your phone go first, and what happens to the call after that?

They go to the nearest base station. The base station passes the call into the cable network, which carries it the rest of the way.

Why can you sometimes get a signal on a mountaintop but not in the valley behind it?

On the mountaintop nothing blocks the path to the base station, but in the valley behind it the mountain blocks most of the radio waves.

Why does 5G need more base stations than older networks?

5G uses higher-frequency waves, which carry more data but do not travel as far or get around obstacles as well, so the towers have to be closer together.

Myth vs. Fact · 常見迷思

Four things people get wrong 四個常見的誤會

✗ Myth · 迷思More bars means the tower is closer.格數越多，代表基地台越近。

✓ Fact · 事實Bars show how strong the signal is. A far tower with a clear view can beat a near tower behind a hill.格數表示訊號有多強。視線沒被擋的遠方基地台，可能比躲在山後的近基地台還強。

✗ Myth · 迷思Phones talk directly to each other.手機是直接和對方的手機通話。

✓ Fact · 事實Each phone talks only to its nearest base station. The network of cables and towers carries the call in between.每支手機只和最近的基地台說話，中間由電纜和基地台組成的網路把通話送過去。

✗ Myth · 迷思5G waves travel faster than 4G waves.5G 的電波跑得比 4G 快。

✓ Fact · 事實All radio waves travel at the speed of light. 5G is faster because it carries more data at once, not because its waves move faster.所有無線電波都以光速前進。5G 比較快，是因為它一次能帶更多資料，不是電波跑得比較快。

✗ Myth · 迷思Phone signals have been shown to cause brain cancer.手機訊號已被證實會造成腦癌。

✓ Fact · 事實A 2024 review for the World Health Organization looked at 63 studies and found no link between mobile phone use and brain cancer. Scientists keep studying the question.2024 年一份為世界衛生組織做的回顧研究，分析了 63 項研究，找不到使用手機和腦癌之間的關聯；科學家仍持續研究。

Safety First · 安全提醒

Before you try anything 動手之前先讀

  • Never use a phone while crossing the street or riding a bike or scooter. Look up, stop, and then use your phone.過馬路、騎腳踏車或機車時絕不使用手機。先停下來，再看手機。
  • Never climb a base station tower, a pole, or a rooftop to get a better signal.絕不為了收訊爬上基地台鐵塔、電線桿或屋頂。
  • Before hiking, tell someone your route, charge your phone, and keep it warm and dry. In an emergency, dial 112.登山前告訴家人路線、把手機充飽電、保持乾燥保暖。遇到緊急狀況撥 112。
  • On long mountain trips, carry a satellite messenger or personal locator beacon too, because some valleys have no signal at all.長程登山要另外帶衛星通訊器或個人求救信標（PLB），因為有些山谷完全沒有訊號。
  • Never put phones or metal in a microwave oven.絕不把手機或金屬放進微波爐。

Classroom Activity 1 · 課堂活動一

Map the Dead Spots 畫出收不到訊號的地方

You need · 準備材料

  • A teacher's or parent's phone一支老師或家長的手機
  • A simple map of the school drawn on paper一張畫在紙上的簡單校園地圖
  • Colored pencils: green, yellow, red綠、黃、紅三色色鉛筆

Steps · 步驟

  1. Walk to at least eight places: classrooms, a stairwell, an elevator lobby, a basement or underground parking lot, the playground, and near the school gate.走到至少八個地方：教室、樓梯間、電梯口、地下室或地下停車場、操場、校門口附近。
  2. At each place, read the signal bars and color that spot on the map: green for 3–4 bars, yellow for 1–2, red for none.在每個地方看訊號格數，在地圖上塗色：3–4 格綠色、1–2 格黃色、沒有訊號紅色。
  3. Look at your finished map. Where are the red spots? What is between those places and the outside: thick concrete, metal, or earth?看看完成的地圖：紅色在哪裡？那些地方和外面之間隔著什麼：厚混凝土、金屬，還是泥土？
  4. Compare it with the coverage map in the 3D model. How is a building like a mountain?和 3D 模型的覆蓋圖比一比：一棟建築物哪裡像一座山？

An adult should hold the phone. Stay out of places that are off limits, and do not stand in the way of traffic in the parking lot.
手機由大人拿著。不要進入禁止進入的地方，在停車場不要站在車道上。

Classroom Activity 2 · 課堂活動二

Block the Waves 擋住電波

You need · 準備材料

  • Two phones (an adult's), or a phone and a landline兩支手機（大人的），或一支手機加一支市話
  • Aluminum foil, plastic wrap, a paper bag, and a metal cookie tin with a lid鋁箔紙、保鮮膜、紙袋、一個有蓋的金屬餅乾盒

Steps · 步驟

  1. Wrap one phone in plastic wrap and call it. Does it ring?用保鮮膜包住一支手機，打給它。會響嗎？
  2. Try the paper bag, then the closed cookie tin, then several layers of aluminum foil with no gaps.依序試紙袋、蓋上蓋子的餅乾盒、包好幾層沒有縫隙的鋁箔紙。
  3. Record which ones let the call through. Metal around a phone can block radio waves; this is called a Faraday cage.記錄哪些打得通。金屬把手機包起來，會擋住無線電波，這叫做「法拉第籠」。
  4. Talk about it: why might your phone have no signal inside an elevator?討論：為什麼在電梯裡手機常常沒訊號？

Unwrap the phone right after each test. Never put a phone, foil, or any metal in a microwave oven: it can spark and start a fire.
每次測完馬上把手機拿出來。絕對不要把手機、鋁箔或任何金屬放進微波爐，會冒火花甚至起火。
