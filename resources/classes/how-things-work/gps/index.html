How Does GPS Know Where You Are?GPS 怎麼知道你在哪裡？

Satellites 20,200 kilometers up keep telling the time. Turn a 3D Earth, watch circles from one, two, three, and four satellites meet at Changhua, then put the phone's clock off and see the blue dot go wrong.
兩萬公里高的衛星不停報時。轉動 3D 地球，看一、二、三、四顆衛星畫出的圓交在彰化，再讓手機的時鐘差一點點，看藍點跑掉多遠。

Big idea · 一句話看懂

GPS satellites keep broadcasting the exact time and where they are. Your phone measures how long each signal took to arrive, which tells it how far away each satellite is, and the place where those distances all meet is where you are.
GPS 衛星不停廣播「現在幾點、我在哪裡」。手機量出每個訊號花了多少時間才到，就知道離每顆衛星多遠；幾顆衛星的距離交會的那一點，就是你的位置。

    7

    Unit 7: How Does GPS Know Where You Are?

    Open a map app, and a blue dot shows where you are, even on a quiet farm road in Changhua. Sometimes a light blue circle surrounds the dot, and the smaller the circle, the surer the app is. Walk between tall buildings, and the dot may jump to the next street. Nobody typed in your address. So how does your phone know where you are? It listens to satellites more than 20,000 kilometers above your head.

    打開地圖 App，一個藍點就標出你在哪裡，就算是在彰化安靜的田間小路上也一樣。有時候藍點外面還有一圈淺藍色的圓，圓越小，代表 App 越確定。走到高樓之間，藍點可能跳到隔壁那條街。沒有人輸入你的地址，手機到底怎麼知道你在哪裡？答案是：它在聽頭頂兩萬多公里高的衛星。

    GPS stands for Global Positioning System. The United States military started the project in 1973 and launched the first satellite in 1978. At first, ordinary users got a signal that was blurred on purpose, so it was less accurate. On May 1, 2000, the United States turned the blurring off, and since then everyone gets the accurate signal for free. The system needs at least 24 satellites, and for more than ten years about 31 have been working. They fly about 20,200 kilometers up, and each one circles Earth twice a day.

    GPS 是「全球定位系統」的英文縮寫。美國軍方在 1973 年開始這個計畫，1978 年發射第一顆衛星。一開始，一般民眾收到的訊號被故意弄模糊，比較不準。2000 年 5 月 1 日，美國把這個模糊關掉，從此每個人都能免費收到準確的訊號。這個系統至少需要 24 顆衛星，十多年來大約一直有 31 顆在運作。它們飛在約 20,200 公里的高空，每顆一天繞地球兩圈。

    Each GPS satellite carries several atomic clocks, which are extremely precise, and keeps broadcasting a simple message: which satellite it is, exactly where it is, and the exact time it sent the message. Your phone never sends anything back. It only listens. The signal is a radio wave, so it travels at the speed of light. From a satellite straight overhead, it takes about 0.07 seconds to reach you. Your phone compares the time in the message with the time the signal arrived, and travel time multiplied by the speed of light gives the distance to that satellite.

    每顆 GPS 衛星都帶著好幾個原子鐘，準得不得了，而且不停廣播一則簡單的訊息：我是哪一顆衛星、我現在的確切位置、這則訊息送出的確切時間。你的手機從來不回傳任何東西，它只負責聽。這個訊號是無線電波，以光速前進；從正上方的衛星傳下來，大約 0.07 秒到達。手機比對訊息裡的時間和訊號到達的時間，用「走了多久」乘上光速，就得到離那顆衛星多遠。

    One distance is not enough. If you are 21,000 kilometers from one satellite, you could be anywhere on a huge circle on Earth's surface. A second satellite draws a second circle, and the two circles cross at only two points. A third satellite's circle passes through just one of them, and that point is you. Finding a place by measuring distances like this is called trilateration. It uses distances, not angles.

    只有一段距離還不夠。如果你離一顆衛星 21,000 公里，你可能在地表上一個巨大的圓上的任何一點。第二顆衛星畫出第二個圓，兩個圓只交在兩個點。第三顆衛星的圓只通過其中一點，那一點就是你。像這樣量距離來找出位置，叫做「三邊定位」：用的是距離，不是角度。

    There is a catch. Light travels about 300 meters in one millionth of a second, so a tiny clock error turns into a big mistake. Your phone has a cheap quartz clock, not an atomic one. The fix is a fourth satellite. With four distances, the phone can work out its position and its own clock error at the same time. That is why GPS needs at least four satellites. Even Einstein matters here: the satellites are high up, where gravity is weaker, and they move fast, so their clocks would gain about 38 microseconds a day. Light travels about 11 kilometers in that time, so engineers set the satellite clocks to tick a tiny bit slower before launch.

    但有個難題：光在百萬分之一秒裡就走了約 300 公尺，時鐘只要差一點點，位置就錯很多。手機裡的是便宜的石英鐘，不是原子鐘。解決辦法是第四顆衛星：有了四段距離，手機就能同時算出自己的位置和自己的時鐘誤差。這就是 GPS 至少需要四顆衛星的原因。連愛因斯坦都派上用場：衛星在高空，重力比較弱，又飛得很快，所以它們的時鐘每天會快約 38 微秒。光在這段時間能走約 11 公里，所以工程師在發射前，先把衛星的時鐘調得慢一點點。

    Today your phone listens to more than GPS. Many phones also use Europe's Galileo, China's BeiDou, Russia's GLONASS, and Japan's QZSS, which serves the Asia-Oceania region. More satellites in view mean a better fix between tall buildings. Indoors, where satellite signals are weak, phones also use Wi-Fi and the cell towers from Lesson 6. Under open sky, a phone's GPS is usually accurate to about 5 meters. Near tall buildings, signals bounce off walls and travel farther, so the distances come out wrong and the blue dot drifts.

    今天的手機不只聽 GPS。很多手機也用歐洲的伽利略（Galileo）、中國的北斗、俄羅斯的格洛納斯（GLONASS），以及服務亞洲與大洋洲地區的日本準天頂衛星（QZSS）。看得到的衛星越多，在高樓之間就定得越準。在室內，衛星訊號很弱，手機還會用 Wi-Fi 和第六課講的基地台幫忙。在空曠的地方，手機的 GPS 通常準到約 5 公尺以內；在高樓旁邊，訊號會在牆上反彈、多走一段路，距離就算錯了，藍點也就跟著飄。

    Questions · 閱讀理解

    What message does each GPS satellite keep broadcasting?

    Which satellite it is, exactly where it is, and the exact time it sent the message.

    How does your phone turn a signal's travel time into a distance?

    It multiplies the travel time by the speed of light.

    Why does GPS need at least four satellites?

    Three distances could find your position if the phone's clock were perfect, but phones have cheap clocks. A fourth distance lets the phone work out its own clock error too.

    GPS by the Numbers · GPS 的數字

    Numbers behind the blue dot 藍點背後的數字

    Every one of these numbers is part of putting your blue dot in the right place.
    每一個數字，都是把藍點放對位置的一部分。

    31satellites working · 顆衛星在運作

    At least 24 are needed; for more than ten years, about 31 have been kept working.至少需要 24 顆；十多年來一直維持約 31 顆在運作。

    20,200km above Earth · 公里高

    Each GPS satellite circles Earth twice a day.每顆 GPS 衛星一天繞地球兩圈。

    0.07seconds · 秒

    Time for a signal from a satellite straight overhead to reach you at the speed of light.正上方衛星的訊號，以光速傳到你這裡所花的時間。

    300meters per microsecond · 公尺／微秒

    A clock off by one millionth of a second makes a distance about 300 meters wrong.時鐘差百萬分之一秒，距離就差約 300 公尺。

    38microseconds a day · 微秒／天

    How much faster the satellite clocks would run because of relativity if engineers did not adjust them.因為相對論，衛星時鐘若不調整，每天會快這麼多。

    4.9meters · 公尺

    A smartphone's GPS is typically accurate to within this distance under open sky.在空曠處，手機 GPS 通常準到這個距離以內。

    Myth vs. Fact · 常見迷思

    Four things people get wrong 四個常見的誤會

    ✗ Myth · 迷思Your phone sends your location to the GPS satellites.手機會把你的位置傳給 GPS 衛星。

    ✓ Fact · 事實GPS signals go one way. The satellites only broadcast, and your phone only listens, so the satellites never know where you are.GPS 訊號是單向的：衛星只負責廣播、手機只負責聽，所以衛星根本不知道你在哪裡。

    ✗ Myth · 迷思GPS needs mobile data or the internet.GPS 要有行動數據或網路才能用。

    ✓ Fact · 事實Your phone can find its position from satellite signals alone, for free. The map pictures may need the internet, unless you download them ahead of time.手機只靠衛星訊號就能定位，而且免費；需要網路的通常是地圖的圖資，事先下載離線地圖就不用。

    ✗ Myth · 迷思GPS finds you by measuring angles to three satellites.GPS 是量三顆衛星的角度來找到你。

    ✓ Fact · 事實GPS measures distances, not angles, from the signals' travel time. And it needs at least four satellites, because the phone's clock is not perfect.GPS 量的是距離，不是角度，靠的是訊號走了多久；而且至少要四顆衛星，因為手機的時鐘並不完美。

    ✗ Myth · 迷思The blue dot is always exactly right.藍點永遠準確無誤。

    ✓ Fact · 事實Under open sky a phone is usually within about 5 meters, but buildings, bridges, and trees can block or bounce the signals. The light blue circle shows how unsure the app is.在空曠處手機通常準到約 5 公尺以內，但大樓、橋和樹會擋住或反彈訊號。藍點外的淺藍色圓，就是 App 不確定的範圍。

    Remember It · 記憶口訣

    GPS in a sentence 一句話記住 GPS

    Time is distance時間就是距離

    Travel time × speed of light = how far away the satellite is.
    訊號走的時間 × 光速 ＝ 衛星離你多遠。

    One circle, two points, one place一個圓、兩個點、一個位置

    Each satellite adds a circle, and three circles meet at just one point.
    每顆衛星加一個圓，三個圓只交在一點。

    Three for where, one for when三顆定位置，一顆對時間

    The fourth satellite lets your phone fix its own clock.
    第四顆衛星讓手機校正自己的時鐘。

    One microsecond, 300 meters差一微秒，差 300 公尺

    Light is so fast that a tiny clock error becomes a big distance error.
    光太快了，時鐘差一點點，距離就差很多。

    Safety First · 安全提醒

    Before you try anything 動手之前先讀

    • On a hike, never rely on your phone's GPS alone. Batteries run out, and the dot can drift in deep valleys. Download offline maps, carry a power bank and a paper map, and tell your family your route.登山不能只靠手機 GPS：手機會沒電，在深谷裡藍點也會飄。事先下載離線地圖、帶行動電源和紙本地圖，並把路線告訴家人。
    • Do not stare at a map while you walk. Stop in a safe place, check the map, and then keep going. Never use a phone while crossing a street or riding a bike or scooter.走路時不要盯著地圖。先停在安全的地方看地圖，再繼續走。過馬路、騎腳踏車或機車時絕不用手機。
    • Share your live location only with people you trust, such as your family, and turn sharing off when you no longer need it.即時位置只分享給信任的人，例如家人；不需要時就關掉分享。
    • Ask a parent before you let an app use your location, and check in your phone's settings which apps can see it.讓 App 使用你的位置之前先問家長，也到手機設定裡看看有哪些 App 看得到你的位置。
    • If you get lost, stop, stay calm, and call 112. Describe what you can see around you.迷路時先停下來、保持冷靜，撥 112，說出你身邊看得到的東西。

    Classroom Activity 1 · 課堂活動一

    Find the Hidden Town with Three Circles 用三個圓找出藏起來的城鎮

    You need · 準備材料

    • A printed map of Taiwan with a scale bar一張有比例尺的台灣地圖
    • A drawing compass and a ruler圓規和尺
    • Colored pencils色鉛筆

    Steps · 步驟

    1. The teacher secretly picks a town on the map and tells the class only its distance from three cities, such as Taichung, Tainan, and Hualien.老師偷偷在地圖上選一個城鎮，只告訴全班它離三個城市（例如台中、台南、花蓮）各多遠。
    2. Use the scale bar to open the compass to each distance, and draw a circle around each city in a different color.用比例尺把圓規張開到每一段距離，以每個城市為圓心，用不同顏色畫圓。
    3. Where do all three circles cross? That is the hidden town. With only two circles, how many possible places are there?三個圓交在哪裡？那就是藏起來的城鎮。如果只有兩個圓，有幾個可能的地方？
    4. Now the teacher adds the same extra distance to every number, like a clock that runs fast. Draw the circles again. Do they still meet? Shrink all three by the same amount until they do, just as the fourth satellite helps a phone do.接著老師把每段距離都多加同樣的長度，就像時鐘走快了。再畫一次：三個圓還交在一點嗎？把三個圓縮小同樣的長度，直到交在一起，這就是第四顆衛星幫手機做的事。

    Compass points are sharp. Keep them on the paper, and never point them at anyone.
    圓規的針很尖，只能放在紙上，絕對不要對著別人。

    Classroom Activity 2 · 課堂活動二

    Watch Your Blue Dot 觀察你的藍點

    You need · 準備材料

    • An adult's phone with a map app一支裝有地圖 App 的大人手機
    • A notebook and a pencil筆記本和鉛筆

    Steps · 步驟

    1. With an adult, open the map app in the middle of the playground and wait 30 seconds. Look at the size of the light blue circle around the dot.和大人一起，在操場正中央打開地圖 App，等 30 秒，看看藍點外淺藍色圓的大小。
    2. Then stand next to a tall building, under a big tree, and just inside a doorway. Each time, notice whether the light blue circle grows or the dot moves.再分別站到高樓旁、大樹下、門口內側，每次都看看淺藍色的圓有沒有變大、藍點有沒有移動。
    3. Make a table: the place, the circle size (small, medium, or large), and whether the dot jumped.做一張表：地點、圓的大小（小、中、大）、藍點有沒有跳。
    4. Talk about it: where was the dot best, and why? Think about walls and roofs that block or reflect the signals.討論：哪裡的藍點最準？為什麼？想想牆壁和屋頂怎麼擋住或反射訊號。

    Stay on school grounds with an adult, and stop walking whenever you look at the screen.
    和大人一起待在校園裡，看螢幕的時候就停下腳步。
