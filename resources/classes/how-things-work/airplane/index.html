How Does an Airplane Stay in the Air?飛機為什麼飛得起來？

A wing flies by pushing air down. Step into a 3D wind tunnel, tilt the wing, change the speed until lift beats weight, release a line of smoke, and push the angle too far to see a stall.
機翼靠著把空氣往下推飛起來。走進 3D 風洞，調整機翼角度、加快速度直到升力大過重量，放一排煙，再把角度拉太大，看看什麼是失速。

Big idea · 一句話看懂

A wing meets the air at a small angle and turns the air flowing past it downward. Pushing air down pushes the wing up, and that upward push is called lift. The faster the plane goes, the more lift its wings make, so a plane races down the runway until the lift is bigger than its weight.
機翼帶著一個小角度迎向空氣，把流過的空氣往下轉。把空氣往下推，機翼就被往上推，這股向上的力叫升力。飛機跑得越快，機翼的升力越大，所以飛機要在跑道上衝刺，直到升力大過自己的重量。

Unit 13: How Does an Airplane Stay in the Air?

At Taoyuan International Airport, a huge jet rolls onto the runway. Its engines roar, it races faster and faster, and then its nose lifts and the whole plane climbs into the sky. A passenger jet is very heavy, much heavier than the air around it. So how can something so heavy stay up in the air?

在桃園國際機場，一架巨大的噴射客機滑上跑道。引擎轟鳴，它越衝越快，接著機頭抬起，整架飛機爬上天空。客機非常重，比周圍的空氣重得多。這麼重的東西，怎麼留得在空中？

People dreamed of flying for thousands of years. On December 17, 1903, at Kitty Hawk, North Carolina, Orville Wright made the first powered airplane flight in the Wright brothers' Flyer. It lasted just 12 seconds and covered about 37 meters. Today flying is ordinary: in 2025, Taoyuan International Airport alone handled about 47.8 million passengers.

人類夢想飛行已經好幾千年了。1903 年 12 月 17 日，在美國北卡羅來納州的小鷹鎮，奧維爾．萊特駕駛萊特兄弟的「飛行者號」，完成了第一次有動力的飛機飛行：只飛了 12 秒、大約 37 公尺。今天，飛行已經稀鬆平常：2025 年，光是桃園國際機場就有約 4,780 萬人次旅客。

The secret is in the wing. A wing is shaped and tilted so that it meets the air at a small angle. As the plane races forward, the wing turns the air flowing past it, pushing a huge amount of air downward every second. Pushing air down pushes the wing up, just as pushing down on the ground with your legs lifts you into the air when you jump. This upward push is called lift.

祕密就在機翼。機翼的形狀和角度，讓它以一個小角度迎向空氣。飛機往前衝時，機翼把流過的空氣轉向，每秒把大量的空氣往下推。把空氣往下推，機翼就被往上推，就像跳起來時，雙腳往下蹬地，人就往上飛。這股向上的推力叫做升力。

Look closely at the air flowing around a wing, and you will see that the air over the top moves faster than the air underneath, and its pressure is lower. Many books say the air on top must hurry to meet the air underneath at the back of the wing. NASA explains that this is wrong: the air over the top actually gets to the back first. The lower pressure on top and the air pushed downward are two parts of the same story.

仔細看機翼周圍的氣流，會發現機翼上方的空氣比下方流得快，壓力也比較低。很多書說：上方的空氣必須趕路，才能在機翼後面和下方的空氣會合。NASA 說明這是錯的：上方的空氣其實比較早到達機翼後面。上方壓力較低，和空氣被往下推，是同一件事的兩個面向。

Lift grows quickly with speed. If a plane goes twice as fast, its wings make four times as much lift. That is why a plane races down the runway: it must go fast enough for the lift to be bigger than its weight. Tilting the wing up a little more gives more lift too, but only up to a point. If the angle gets too steep, the air can no longer follow the top of the wing, and the lift suddenly drops. This is called a stall.

升力會隨速度快速變大：飛機速度加倍，機翼的升力就變成四倍。這就是飛機要在跑道上衝刺的原因：它得跑得夠快，升力才會大過重量。把機翼再抬高一點，升力也會變大，但有個限度。角度太陡時，空氣沒辦法再沿著機翼上表面流，升力會突然掉下來，這叫做「失速」。

A flying plane is a balance of four forces. Lift pushes it up, and weight pulls it down. Thrust from the engines pushes it forward, and drag from the air pulls it back. When lift is bigger than weight, the plane climbs, and when thrust is bigger than drag, it speeds up. Birds, paper airplanes, and giant jets all fly by the same rules.

飛行中的飛機，是四個力的平衡：升力往上推、重力往下拉，引擎的推力往前推、空氣的阻力往後拉。升力大過重力，飛機就爬升；推力大過阻力，飛機就加速。鳥、紙飛機和巨大的噴射客機，都遵守同樣的規則飛行。

Questions · 閱讀理解

How does a wing push a plane up?

The wing turns the air flowing past it downward. Pushing the air down pushes the wing up.

What happens to the lift if a plane flies twice as fast?

The wings make four times as much lift.

What is a stall?

When the wing is tilted too steeply, the air can no longer follow the top of the wing, and the lift suddenly drops.

Myth vs. Fact · 常見迷思

Four things people get wrong 四個常見的誤會

✗ Myth · 迷思The air on top of the wing must meet the air underneath at the back.機翼上方的空氣，必須和下方的空氣在後面會合。

✓ Fact · 事實NASA explains that this “equal transit time” idea is wrong. The air over the top actually reaches the back of the wing first.NASA 說明這種「上下同時到達」的說法是錯的。上方的空氣其實比較早到達機翼後面。

✗ Myth · 迷思Planes float because they are lighter than air.飛機會飄，是因為它比空氣輕。

✓ Fact · 事實Planes are much heavier than air. Their wings make lift by pushing air downward as they move fast.飛機比空氣重得多，是靠機翼在快速前進時把空氣往下推，產生升力。

✗ Myth · 迷思The engines push the plane up into the sky.是引擎把飛機推上天的。

✓ Fact · 事實The engines push the plane forward. The wings turn that forward speed into lift.引擎把飛機往前推，機翼再把往前的速度變成升力。

✗ Myth · 迷思A stall means the engine has stopped.失速就是引擎熄火了。

✓ Fact · 事實A stall is about the wing: it is tilted so steeply that the air can't follow it, and lift drops. The engines can still be running.失速說的是機翼：角度太陡，空氣跟不上，升力掉下來。引擎可能還在運轉。

Safety First · 安全提醒

Before you try anything 動手之前先讀

  • Never throw paper airplanes at anyone's face.絕不把紙飛機往別人臉上丟。
  • Fly kites far from power lines and airports.放風箏要遠離電線和機場。
  • Drones have rules in Taiwan, such as no flying near airports. Fly one only with an adult who knows the rules.在台灣飛無人機有規定，例如不能在機場附近飛。只能和懂規定的大人一起飛。
  • Never shine a laser pointer at an airplane. It can dazzle the pilots.絕不拿雷射筆照飛機，會讓飛行員看不清楚。
  • On a plane, keep your seat belt fastened whenever you are seated.搭飛機時，只要坐著就要繫好安全帶。

Classroom Activity 1 · 課堂活動一

Feel the Lift with Your Hand 用手感受升力

You need · 準備材料

  • A strong electric fan or a hair dryer on the cool setting一台電風扇，或開冷風的吹風機
  • Your flat hand你攤平的手

Steps · 步驟

  1. Hold your hand flat in front of the fan, like a wing, with your fingers pointing at the fan.把手攤平放在電風扇前面，當作機翼，手指對著電風扇。
  2. Tilt the front of your hand up a little. What do you feel?把手的前端稍微抬高，感覺到什麼？
  3. Tilt it more and more. When does the push stop growing and start to feel shaky?越抬越高：什麼時候推力不再變大、開始抖動？
  4. Turn the fan to a lower speed. How does the push change?把風速調低，推力怎麼變？

Never put your hand or anything else near the fan blades. Only hold your hand in front of the fan's cover.
手和任何東西都不能靠近扇葉，只在電扇的護網前面感受。

Classroom Activity 2 · 課堂活動二

Paper Airplane Test Flights 紙飛機試飛

You need · 準備材料

  • Sheets of A4 paper幾張 A4 紙
  • A measuring tape and paper clips捲尺和迴紋針

Steps · 步驟

  1. Fold a simple paper airplane and throw it gently, level with the floor. Measure how far it flies.摺一架簡單的紙飛機，水平地輕輕丟出去，量它飛多遠。
  2. Bend the back edges of both wings up a tiny bit, and throw again. What changes?把兩邊機翼的後緣往上折一點點，再丟一次：有什麼變化？
  3. Add a paper clip to the nose, then to the tail. Which way flies farther?在機頭夾一根迴紋針，再改夾在機尾：哪一種飛得遠？
  4. Throw it with the nose pointed steeply up. Does it stall and drop?把機頭朝上很陡地丟：會不會失速、掉下來？

Throw in an open space, and never at anyone's face.
在空曠的地方丟，絕不對著別人的臉。
