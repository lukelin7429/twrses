How Does a Rainbow Form?彩虹是怎麼形成的？

A rainbow is sunlight bent and bounced inside millions of raindrops. Follow one ray through a 3D raindrop, then stand with the Sun behind you and watch falling rain light up in a 42-degree circle.
彩虹是陽光在幾百萬顆雨滴裡轉彎、反射出來的光。在 3D 雨滴裡跟著一道光走，再背對太陽站好，看落下的雨在 42 度的圓上亮起來。

Big idea · 一句話看懂

Sunlight bends as it enters a raindrop, reflects off the back, and bends again as it leaves, and water bends each color a slightly different amount. So the colors come back out at slightly different angles, all close to 42 degrees from the point straight opposite the Sun. That is why a rainbow is part of a circle and is always on the side of the sky away from the Sun.
陽光射進雨滴時轉彎，在水滴背面反射，出來時再轉彎；水讓每種顏色轉彎的程度差一點點。所以各色從稍微不同的角度回頭射出，全都在「太陽正對面那一點」外圍約 42 度的地方。這就是為什麼彩虹是圓的一部分，而且永遠在背對太陽的那一邊。

    Reading · 英文閱讀

    10

    Unit 10: How Does a Rainbow Form?

    A summer afternoon shower ends, the Sun comes out, and suddenly a rainbow arches across the sky. Look closely and you will notice something every time: the Sun is behind you, and the rainbow is in front of you. Try to walk to the end of a rainbow, and it moves away as you move. A rainbow is not an object sitting in one place. So what is it?

    夏天午後一場陣雨剛停，太陽露臉，天空突然掛起一道彩虹。仔細看，你每次都會發現同一件事：太陽在你背後，彩虹在你前面。想走到彩虹的盡頭，你一走它就跟著退。彩虹不是待在某個地方的東西，那它到底是什麼？

    People have wondered about rainbows for a very long time. More than 900 years ago in Song dynasty China, the scholar Shen Kuo wrote that rainbows form when sunlight meets raindrops in the air. Around 1300, Kamal al-Din al-Farisi in Persia explained that light bends twice and reflects inside each drop, and he tested the idea with a glass sphere filled with water. In 1637, the French thinker René Descartes measured the angles with a large water-filled glass sphere of his own.

    人們對彩虹好奇了很久很久。九百多年前，中國宋朝的沈括就寫道：彩虹是陽光碰到空氣中的雨滴形成的。大約 1300 年，波斯的法里西解釋光在每顆雨滴裡轉彎兩次、反射一次，還用一個裝滿水的玻璃球做實驗。1637 年，法國的笛卡兒也用一個裝水的大玻璃球，量出了光出來的角度。

    Here is what happens inside one raindrop. Sunlight enters the drop and bends, which is called refraction. It crosses the drop, reflects off the back, and bends again as it leaves, heading back toward the side the Sun is on. Light that enters at different places comes back at different angles, but a lot of it piles up close to 42 degrees, so the light is brightest there. This angle does not depend on the size of the drop.

    一顆雨滴裡發生的事是這樣的：陽光射進雨滴時轉彎，這叫做「折射」；穿過水滴、在背面反射，出來時再轉彎一次，往太陽那一側射回去。從不同位置射進來的光，回頭的角度不一樣，但有很多都擠在 42 度附近，所以那裡最亮。這個角度跟雨滴大小無關。

    Water bends each color by a slightly different amount. Red light bends the least and comes back at about 42 degrees, while violet bends the most and comes back at about 41 degrees. When you look at the sky, the drops that send red light into your eyes form an outer ring, and the drops that send violet form a ring just inside it. That is why red is on the outside of a rainbow and violet is on the inside.

    水讓每種顏色轉彎的程度差一點點：紅光轉得最少，回頭約 42 度；紫光轉得最多，回頭約 41 度。你看天空時，把紅光送進你眼睛的雨滴排成外圈，把紫光送進來的雨滴排成緊貼在內側的一圈。所以彩虹外側是紅色、內側是紫色。

    All the drops that send color to your eyes lie on a circle around the point straight opposite the Sun, where the shadow of your head points. That is why a rainbow is part of a circle and is always opposite the Sun. When the Sun is higher than about 42 degrees, the whole circle is below the horizon, so rainbows are usually seen in the morning or late afternoon. From an airplane, you can sometimes see the whole circle.

    所有能把顏色送進你眼睛的雨滴，都排在一個圓上，圓心就是太陽的正對面，也就是你頭的影子所指的地方。所以彩虹是圓的一部分，而且永遠在太陽的對面。太陽高過約 42 度時，整個圓都沉到地平線以下，所以彩虹通常出現在早上或傍晚。從飛機上，有時能看到整個圓。

    Sometimes a second, fainter rainbow appears outside the first, at about 50 to 52 degrees. Its light reflects twice inside each drop, so its colors are in the opposite order, with red on the inside. The darker sky between the two bows is called Alexander's band, after the Greek thinker Alexander of Aphrodisias, who described it about 1,800 years ago. And because each person's eyes catch light from a different set of drops, no two people ever see exactly the same rainbow.

    有時候，第一道彩虹外面還會出現一道比較淡的第二道彩虹，大約在 50 到 52 度。它的光在每顆雨滴裡反射兩次，所以顏色順序相反，紅色在內側。兩道彩虹之間比較暗的天空叫做「亞歷山大暗帶」，名字來自大約 1,800 年前描述它的希臘學者亞歷山大。還有，每個人的眼睛接收的是不同一批雨滴的光，所以沒有兩個人看到的彩虹完全一樣。

    Questions · 閱讀理解

    What three things happen to sunlight inside a raindrop to make a rainbow?

    It bends as it enters the drop, reflects off the back of the drop, and bends again as it leaves.

    Why is red on the outside of a rainbow and violet on the inside?

    Red light comes back at a larger angle, about 42 degrees, than violet light, about 41 degrees, so the drops that send red to your eyes form the outer ring.

    Why do you rarely see a rainbow at midday?

    At midday the Sun is often higher than about 42 degrees, so the whole rainbow circle is below the horizon.

    Rainbows by the Numbers · 彩虹的數字

    Angles that make a rainbow 形成彩虹的角度

    Every rainbow follows the same few angles, no matter how big the raindrops are.
    不管雨滴多大，每一道彩虹都遵守同樣幾個角度。

    42°red, outside · 紅色，在外側

    Red light comes back about 42 degrees from the point opposite the Sun.紅光從太陽正對面那一點往外約 42 度。

    41°violet, inside · 紫色，在內側

    Violet bends more, so it comes back at a slightly smaller angle.紫光轉彎轉得多，回頭的角度小一點。

    51°second rainbow · 第二道彩虹

    Light that reflects twice makes a fainter bow at about 50 to 52 degrees, colors reversed.反射兩次的光，在約 50 到 52 度形成較淡、顏色相反的彩虹。

    1.33how much water bends light · 水的折射率

    From about 1.330 for red to 1.343 for violet: that small difference spreads out the colors.紅光約 1.330、紫光約 1.343：差這麼一點點，就把顏色分開了。

    1637Descartes's glass sphere · 笛卡兒的玻璃球

    Descartes measured the angles with a large glass sphere filled with water.笛卡兒用一個裝水的大玻璃球量出了角度。

    Myth vs. Fact · 常見迷思

    Four things people get wrong 四個常見的誤會

    ✗ Myth · 迷思You can walk to the end of a rainbow.可以走到彩虹的盡頭。

    ✓ Fact · 事實A rainbow is light coming at a certain angle from many raindrops. When you move, a different set of drops makes your rainbow, so it moves with you.彩虹是從很多雨滴、以特定角度射來的光。你一移動，換成另一批雨滴替你做彩虹，所以它會跟著你走。

    ✗ Myth · 迷思A rainbow is shaped like an arch.彩虹本來就是拱橋的形狀。

    ✓ Fact · 事實A rainbow is really a circle. The ground usually hides the lower part, but from an airplane you can sometimes see the whole ring.彩虹其實是一個圓，只是地面通常擋住了下半部；從飛機上，有時能看到一整圈。

    ✗ Myth · 迷思Two friends standing side by side see the same rainbow.並肩站的兩個朋友看到的是同一道彩虹。

    ✓ Fact · 事實Each person's eyes catch light from a different set of raindrops, so no two people see exactly the same rainbow.每個人的眼睛接收的是不同一批雨滴的光，所以沒有兩個人看到的彩虹完全一樣。

    ✗ Myth · 迷思Bigger raindrops make a bigger rainbow.雨滴越大，彩虹越大。

    ✓ Fact · 事實The 42-degree angle depends on how much water bends light, not on the size of the drops.42 度這個角度取決於水讓光轉彎的程度，跟雨滴大小無關。

    Safety First · 安全提醒

    Before you try anything 動手之前先讀

    • Never look straight at the Sun while you look for a rainbow. Keep your back to it.找彩虹時絕不直視太陽，要背對著它。
    • When you make a rainbow with a garden hose, stand with your back to the Sun, and never spray people, pets, or anything electrical.用水管噴霧做彩虹時，背對太陽站，絕不朝人、寵物或任何電器噴。
    • Wet ground is slippery. Wear shoes and walk, don't run.地面濕了會滑，要穿鞋、用走的，不要跑。
    • If you hear thunder, go indoors right away. Look for rainbows only after the storm has passed.聽到打雷就馬上進到室內，等暴風雨過了再找彩虹。
    • Glass can break. Ask an adult to help with the glass of water in the activity.玻璃會破，活動裡的水杯請大人幫忙拿。

    Classroom Activity 1 · 課堂活動一

    Make Your Own Rainbow 自己做一道彩虹

    You need · 準備材料

    • A garden hose with a spray nozzle, or a spray bottle一條有噴頭的水管，或一個噴霧瓶
    • A sunny morning or late afternoon一個晴朗的早上或傍晚

    Steps · 步驟

    1. Stand with your back to the Sun, so your shadow points in front of you.背對太陽站好，讓你的影子落在前方。
    2. Spray a fine mist in front of you, above the shadow of your head.在前方、你頭的影子上方噴出細細的水霧。
    3. Look for the rainbow. Is red on the outside? Point to the center of its circle: it should be the shadow of your head.找找彩虹：紅色在外側嗎？指出這個圓的圓心，應該就是你頭的影子。
    4. Try it at noon. Can you still see a rainbow on the ground in front of you? Why or why not?中午再試一次：還看得到嗎？為什麼？

    A spray bottle works on a balcony too. Keep the mist away from phones and power outlets.
    在陽台用噴霧瓶也可以，水霧要遠離手機和插座。

    Classroom Activity 2 · 課堂活動二

    A Rainbow from a Glass of Water 一杯水做出的彩虹

    You need · 準備材料

    • A clear glass of water (an adult holds it)一杯透明的水（由大人拿）
    • A sheet of white paper一張白紙
    • A sunny windowsill or a bright flashlight有陽光的窗台，或一支亮的手電筒

    Steps · 步驟

    1. Set the glass at the edge of a sunny windowsill, with the paper on the floor below.把水杯放在有陽光的窗台邊，下方地上鋪白紙。
    2. Move the paper until a small band of colors appears on it.移動白紙，直到上面出現一小條彩色光帶。
    3. Which color is bent the most? Which is bent the least? Compare with the model.哪個顏色轉彎最多？哪個最少？和模型比一比。
    4. Talk about it: how is the glass of water like a raindrop, and how is it different?討論：這杯水哪裡像一顆雨滴？哪裡不一樣？

    The colors appear faintly; a darker room and stronger sunlight help.
    顏色會很淡，房間暗一點、陽光強一點比較容易看到。
