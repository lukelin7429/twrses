How Does the Internet Work?網際網路怎麼運作？

A message to Boston is cut into numbered packets that find their own way under the ocean. Send a text or a photo in 3D, lose a packet, cut a cable, and watch the internet fix it.
傳到波士頓的訊息，會被切成有編號的封包，各自從海底找路過去。用 3D 傳文字或照片、弄丟一個封包、剪斷一條海纜，看網路怎麼自己補救。

Big idea · 一句話看懂

The internet cuts every message into small numbered packets. Routers pass each packet along, one hop at a time, by whatever path is open, and the receiving end puts the numbers back in order and asks for any piece that is missing.
網際網路把每一則訊息切成一個個有編號的小封包。路由器一站一站把封包往前傳，哪條路通就走哪條；收件端再照編號排回原樣，缺了哪一片就請對方重送。

Unit 5: How Does the Internet Work?

Your grandfather lives in Boston, and you send him a photo on LINE from Changhua. Less than a second later, his phone buzzes on the other side of the world. The photo did not fly through the air the whole way, and it did not travel in one piece. It was chopped into thousands of small pieces that raced under the Pacific Ocean and were put back together on his phone.

你的爺爺住在波士頓，你在彰化用 LINE 傳一張照片給他。不到一秒，他在地球另一邊的手機就響了。這張照片並不是一路在空中飛過去的，也不是整張一起送過去的：它被切成好幾千個小片，從太平洋底下飛奔過去，再在他的手機上拼回來。

The internet began with a crash. On October 29, 1969, a student at UCLA tried to send the word login to a computer at a research institute about 560 kilometers away. The letters L and O arrived, and then the system crashed. So the first message on the network that grew into the internet was simply LO. In 1974, Vint Cerf and Bob Kahn described a set of rules called TCP/IP, and the network switched to those rules on January 1, 1983. The internet still runs on them today.

網際網路是從一次當機開始的。1969 年 10 月 29 日，加州大學洛杉磯分校的一位學生，想把 login 這個字傳到約 560 公里外一間研究機構的電腦。L 和 O 到了，系統就當機了。所以這個後來長成網際網路的網路，第一則訊息只有「LO」。1974 年，瑟夫和卡恩寫下一套叫做 TCP/IP 的規則，這個網路在 1983 年 1 月 1 日正式改用它，網際網路到今天都還在用這套規則。

The big idea is packets. Your phone cuts your photo into small pieces called packets, each up to about 1,500 bytes, so a three-megabyte photo becomes about 2,000 of them. Every packet carries three things: a piece of the photo, the address where it is going, and its number in the line. Like postcards, the packets travel separately, and each one finds its own way.

最重要的觀念是「封包」。手機把你的照片切成一小片一小片，叫做封包，每個最多約 1,500 位元組，所以一張 3 MB 的照片大約會變成 2,000 個封包。每個封包都帶著三樣東西：照片的一小片、要送去的地址，以及它排在第幾號。封包就像一張張明信片，各自上路，各自找路。

Along the way, packets pass through routers, special computers that read the address and send each packet one step closer, like mail sorters at the post office. Messaging apps first send your photo to the company's computers, called servers, which pass it on to your grandfather's phone. Because each router chooses a path on its own, two packets from the same photo may take different roads and arrive out of order. If one is lost, his phone notices the missing number and asks for it again.

一路上，封包會經過「路由器」：一種特別的電腦，會讀封包上的地址，再把它往目的地送近一步，就像郵局的分信員。通訊軟體會先把你的照片送到公司的電腦，也就是伺服器，再由伺服器轉給爺爺的手機。因為每個路由器自己決定路線，同一張照片的兩個封包可能走不同的路，到達的順序也可能亂掉。要是掉了一個，爺爺的手機會發現少了哪一號，請對方再送一次。

To cross the ocean, the packets travel through undersea cables. Almost all data that goes between continents uses these cables, not satellites. In deep water, a cable is about as thick as a garden hose, and inside are glass fibers thinner than a hair that carry the data as flashes of light. Light in glass travels about 200,000 kilometers every second, so even the 12,500 kilometers from Changhua to Boston take only a fraction of a second.

要越過海洋，封包走的是海底電纜。洲與洲之間的資料，幾乎全部都走海底電纜，而不是衛星。在深海裡，一條電纜大約只有水管那麼粗，裡面是比頭髮還細的玻璃光纖，用一閃一閃的光傳送資料。光在玻璃裡每秒約跑 20 萬公里，所以就算彰化到波士頓有 12,500 公里，也只要一眨眼的一小部分時間。

Taiwan is connected to the world by 14 international undersea cables, which come ashore at landing stations in places like Tamsui and Toucheng. Because there are many cables and many routes, the internet can keep working when one breaks. In 2023, when both cables to the Matsu Islands were cut by passing ships, the islands had very slow internet until they were repaired. That is why engineers keep building more paths: the internet works because no single road is the only road.

台灣靠 14 條國際海底電纜和世界相連，它們在淡水、頭城等地的登陸站上岸。因為電纜很多、路線很多，就算斷了一條，網路還能繼續運作。2023 年，通往馬祖的兩條海纜都被經過的船隻弄斷，馬祖的網路變得非常慢，直到修好為止。所以工程師一直在多蓋幾條路：網際網路能運作，是因為沒有哪一條路是唯一的路。

Questions · 閱讀理解

What was the first message sent on the network that grew into the internet, and why was it so short?

It was LO. The student was trying to type login, but the system crashed after the first two letters.

What three things does every packet carry?

A small piece of the message, the address where it is going, and its number, so the pieces can be put back in order.

Why can the internet keep working when one undersea cable breaks?

Because there are many cables and many routes. Routers send the packets another way when one path is broken.

Myth vs. Fact · 常見迷思

Four things people get wrong 四個常見的誤會

✗ Myth · 迷思The internet travels mostly through satellites in space.網路大多是靠太空的衛星傳的。

✓ Fact · 事實Almost all data between continents travels through undersea cables. Satellites help where cables cannot reach, but they carry a small share.洲與洲之間的資料幾乎全部走海底電纜。衛星用在電纜到不了的地方，但只占一小部分。

✗ Myth · 迷思Your message travels in one piece, straight to your friend.訊息是整個一起、直接送到朋友手上。

✓ Fact · 事實It is cut into packets that may take different routes, usually passes through the app's servers, and is put back together at the end.它被切成封包、可能各走不同的路，通常還會先經過通訊軟體的伺服器，最後才拼回原樣。

✗ Myth · 迷思Wi-Fi and the internet are the same thing.Wi-Fi 就是網際網路。

✓ Fact · 事實Wi-Fi only connects your device to the router in your home or school. From there, the data travels on cables to the rest of the internet.Wi-Fi 只負責把你的裝置連到家裡或學校的路由器；從那裡開始，資料走電纜到網際網路的其他地方。

✗ Myth · 迷思If one cable breaks, the whole country goes offline.一條海纜斷了，整個國家就斷網。

✓ Fact · 事實Routers send the traffic another way. A place with only one or two cables, like Matsu in 2023, suffers most, which is why more cables are built.路由器會把資料改走別的路。只有一兩條電纜的地方受害最大，像 2023 年的馬祖，所以才要多蓋幾條。

Remember It · 記憶口訣

The internet in a sentence 一句話記住網際網路

Cut, number, send, rebuild切開、編號、送出、拼回

Four steps for every message: cut it into packets, number them, send them, and rebuild them in order.
每則訊息四步驟：切成封包、標上編號、送出去、照順序拼回來。

Routers are mail sorters路由器是分信員

They read the address and pass the packet one step closer. Nobody plans the whole trip.
它們讀地址，把封包往前送一步；沒有人事先規劃整趟路。

Under the sea, not in space走海底，不走太空

Between continents, the internet is mostly glass fibers lying on the ocean floor.
洲與洲之間的網路，大多是躺在海底的玻璃光纖。

No single road沒有唯一的路

Many cables and many routes mean one break does not stop the message.
電纜多、路線多，斷一條也擋不住訊息。

Safety First · 安全提醒

Before you try anything 動手之前先讀

  • Before you send a photo, ask: would I be happy if anyone saw this? Once it leaves your phone, copies can stay on servers and other people's phones.傳照片之前先問自己：如果任何人都看得到，我會介意嗎？照片一離開手機，伺服器和別人的手機上就可能留著副本。
  • Never send your address, passwords, or school name to someone you have not met in person.絕不把住址、密碼或學校名稱傳給沒有見過面的人。
  • If a message or link makes you feel uncomfortable or asks for secrets, stop and show a trusted adult.收到讓你不舒服、或要你說秘密的訊息或連結，停下來，拿給信任的大人看。
  • Unplug a router only with an adult's permission, and never open the case of any electronic device.要拔路由器的插頭，先問過大人；絕不打開任何電子設備的外殼。

Classroom Activity 1 · 課堂活動一

The Packet Relay 封包接力

You need · 準備材料

  • A short sentence or a simple drawing cut into 8 to 12 pieces一句話或一張簡單的圖，剪成 8 到 12 片
  • Envelopes or sticky notes for each piece每一片一個信封或便利貼
  • Students spread around the room as routers分散站在教室各處當路由器的同學

Steps · 步驟

  1. The sender writes on each piece: the receiver's name (the address) and a number (1 of 10, 2 of 10, and so on).寄件人在每一片寫上：收件人的名字（地址）和編號（1/10、2/10……）。
  2. Routers may pass a piece only to a neighbor next to them, choosing whichever neighbor seems closer to the receiver.路由器只能把紙片交給旁邊的同學，選看起來離收件人比較近的那一位。
  3. The teacher secretly takes one piece away (a lost packet) and later blocks one row of desks (a broken cable).老師偷偷拿走一片（掉了的封包），稍後再擋住一排桌子（斷掉的電纜）。
  4. The receiver lays the pieces out by number, finds the gap, and sends a request back for the missing one.收件人照編號排好，找出缺的那一號，送一張「請重送」回去。
  5. Talk about it: Did the pieces arrive in order? What happened when the row was blocked?討論：紙片到的順序對嗎？擋住一排之後發生了什麼事？

Time the whole relay, then remember that the real internet does the same thing for a photo of 2,000 packets in well under a second.
幫整場接力計時，再想想：真正的網路處理一張 2,000 個封包的照片，全程不到一秒。

Classroom Activity 2 · 課堂活動二

Explore the Cable Map 看海底電纜地圖

You need · 準備材料

  • A computer or tablet with a web browser一台有瀏覽器的電腦或平板
  • The free Submarine Cable Map by TeleGeography (submarinecablemap.com)TeleGeography 的免費海底電纜地圖（submarinecablemap.com）

Steps · 步驟

  1. Zoom in on Taiwan. How many cables can you count coming ashore?放大台灣。數一數有幾條電纜在台灣上岸？
  2. Click a cable that crosses the Pacific. Where does it land in the United States, and how long is it?點一條橫越太平洋的電纜：它在美國哪裡上岸？有多長？
  3. Find a cable that reaches Japan or the Philippines. Why might a cable stop at islands on the way?找一條到日本或菲律賓的電纜。為什麼電纜會在途中的島嶼停靠？
  4. Look at Matsu, Kinmen, and Penghu. Compare how many cables they have with Taiwan's main island.看看馬祖、金門、澎湖，比較它們和台灣本島各有幾條電纜。

Teachers can project the map for the whole class. It is a good starting point for a talk about why islands care so much about their cables.
老師可以把地圖投影給全班看，正好用來討論：為什麼島嶼這麼在乎自己的海纜。
