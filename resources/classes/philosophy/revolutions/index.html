Does Science Ever Change Its Mind? 科學會改變想法嗎？

For fourteen hundred years the best astronomers put the earth at the center, and their tables worked. Kuhn on normal science and revolutions, Copernicus’s nervous preface, the loop that Mars makes in the sky, and the calendar quarrel at the Chinese court.
一千四百年間，最好的天文學家把地球放在中心，而他們的星表是管用的。孔恩談常態科學與科學革命、哥白尼那篇惴惴不安的序、火星在天上打的那個圈，以及中國宮廷裡的那場曆法之爭。

The big idea · 大觀念

The last lesson described how a theory is tested. This one asks what happens when a whole way of doing science is replaced by another. If the experts were wrong for a thousand years, we should like to know how they came to change, and what that tells us about the experts of today.

上一課描述了一個理論是怎麼被檢驗的。這一課問的是：當一整套「做科學的方式」被另一套取代的時候，發生了什麼事？如果專家錯了一千年，我們會想知道他們是怎麼改過來的，以及這告訴了我們關於今天的專家的什麼事。

The essay · 課文

Does Science Ever Change Its Mind? 科學會改變想法嗎？

01A loop in the sky 天上的一個圈

Watch Mars for a few months and you will see something odd. Night after night it drifts eastward against the stars. Then it slows, stops, moves backward for several weeks, stops again, and goes on as before. Every planet does this. Around 150 CE the astronomer Ptolemy of Alexandria gave an explanation that satisfied the learned world for fourteen centuries. The earth stands still at the center. Each planet rides on a small circle, the epicycle, whose center moves around a large one. When the planet is on the inner part of its small circle it appears from the earth to move backward. By choosing the sizes and speeds of the circles Ptolemy could predict where each planet would be on any night, well enough for calendars, navigation, and horoscopes.

觀察火星幾個月，你會看到一件怪事。一夜又一夜，它在星空的背景上向東漂移。然後它慢下來、停住，倒退幾個星期，又停住，再照原來的方向走下去。每一顆行星都這樣。大約公元 150 年，亞歷山卓的天文學家托勒密給出了一個解釋，讓有學問的世界滿意了十四個世紀。地球靜止在中心。每一顆行星騎在一個小圓上，叫做「本輪」；本輪的圓心沿著一個大圓運行。當行星在小圓靠內側的那一段時，從地球看去，它就像在倒退。藉著選擇這些圓的大小與速度，托勒密能預測每一顆行星在任何一個夜晚的位置；準確到足夠用來編曆、航海與占星。

This was good science. It was mathematical, it was tested against observation, and it was improved by generations of astronomers in Greek, Arabic, and Latin. In 1543 Nicolaus Copernicus published a book that put the sun at the center and set the earth moving. He knew what to expect. “I can easily conceive, most Holy Father,” he wrote to the pope, “that as soon as some people learn that in this book … I ascribe certain motions to the Earth, they will cry out at once that I and my theory should be rejected.” He had kept the manuscript back for decades.

這是好的科學。它是數學的；它拿觀察來檢驗；一代又一代用希臘文、阿拉伯文與拉丁文寫作的天文學家改進了它。1543 年，尼古拉・哥白尼出版了一本書，把太陽放在中心，讓地球動了起來。他知道會遇到什麼。他寫給教宗：「至聖的聖父，我很容易想像得到：一旦有些人得知，我在這本書裡……把某些運動歸給了地球，他們會立刻大喊，說我和我的理論都應該被摒棄。」這份手稿，他壓了幾十年。

02Normal science 常態科學

In 1962 the historian Thomas Kuhn asked how such changes come about, and gave an answer that surprised philosophers. Most science, he said, is not a search for new theories. It is work done inside a paradigm, by which he meant a shared achievement that tells a community what the world contains, which questions are worth asking, and what a good answer looks like. Ptolemy’s astronomy was a paradigm, and Newton’s mechanics was another. Within one, scientists do what Kuhn called normal science. They solve puzzles, measuring a constant more exactly or extending the theory to a new case. They do not test the paradigm. They assume it, and when an experiment disagrees with it they suspect the experiment.

1962 年，歷史學家湯瑪斯・孔恩問：這樣的改變是怎麼發生的？而他給出的答案讓哲學家很意外。他說，大部分的科學並不是在尋找新的理論。它是在一個「典範」之內所做的工作。他說的典範，是一項共同的成就；它告訴一個社群：世界裡有些什麼、哪些問題值得問，以及一個好的答案長什麼樣子。托勒密的天文學是一個典範；牛頓的力學是另一個。在典範之內，科學家做的是孔恩所謂的「常態科學」。他們解謎：把一個常數量得更精確，或者把理論推廣到一個新的情況。他們並不檢驗典範。他們預設它；而當一個實驗與它不一致的時候，他們懷疑的是實驗。

Kuhn thought this conservatism necessary. Only people who trust a framework will push it far enough to find where it breaks. Results that will not fit, which he called anomalies, pile up slowly. For a long time they are set aside. When there are too many of them, or one of them touches something central, the field enters a crisis. Rival ideas appear, and the young begin to take them seriously.

孔恩認為這種保守是必要的。只有信任一個框架的人，才會把它推得夠遠，遠到發現它在哪裡斷裂。怎麼也兜不攏的結果，他稱為「異常」；它們慢慢地堆積起來。有很長一段時間，它們被擱在一邊。等到異常太多，或者其中有一個碰到了核心的東西，這個領域就進入「危機」。對立的想法出現了；而年輕人開始認真看待它們。

03Revolution 革命

A revolution, for Kuhn, is the replacement of one paradigm by another, and it does not happen the way a theorem is proved. The two sides do not share enough to settle the matter by a single experiment. Copernicus’s system was at first no more accurate than Ptolemy’s. It still used epicycles, since Copernicus kept the old belief that heavenly motion must be circular. It also contradicted the best physics of the day, which said that a moving earth would leave falling stones and clouds behind. What it offered was a kind of order. In Ptolemy’s system every planet’s epicycle was mysteriously tied to the sun’s yearly motion. Copernicus explained this at a stroke: the yearly circle is ours. The backward loop of Mars is what we see when the faster earth overtakes it.

對孔恩來說，一場革命就是一個典範被另一個取代；而它發生的方式，並不像證明一條定理。雙方共有的東西不夠多，沒辦法用單一的實驗把事情定下來。哥白尼的體系一開始並不比托勒密的更準。它仍然用到本輪；因為哥白尼保留了那個舊信念：天體的運動必定是圓的。它還牴觸了當時最好的物理學；那套物理學說，如果地球在動，落下的石頭與天上的雲就會被拋在後面。它提供的是一種秩序。在托勒密的體系裡，每一顆行星的本輪，都莫名其妙地跟太陽每年的運動綁在一起。哥白尼一舉解釋了這件事：那個一年一圈的圓，是我們自己的。火星倒退著打的那個圈，是比較快的地球追過它的時候，我們所看到的景象。

It took more than a century, and the work of Kepler, Galileo, and Newton, before the new system was plainly better by every measure. During that time, Kuhn argued, choosing it was a judgment that the evidence did not force. He compared the change to the drawing that can be seen as a duck or as a rabbit. The lines are the same, and what one sees in them has shifted. Scientists on either side of a revolution, he wrote, work in different worlds. The physicist Max Planck had said something harsher from his own experience: “A new scientific truth does not triumph by convincing its opponents and making them see the light, but rather because its opponents eventually die, and a new generation grows up that is familiar with it.”

要等到一個多世紀之後，經過克卜勒、伽利略與牛頓的工作，新的體系才在每一個標準上都明顯比較好。孔恩主張，在那段期間，選擇它是一個判斷，而證據並沒有強迫人做出這個判斷。他把這個改變比作那幅既可以看成鴨子、也可以看成兔子的圖。線條是一樣的；人在線條裡看到的東西變了。他寫道，革命兩邊的科學家，是在不同的世界裡工作。物理學家馬克斯・普朗克根據自己的經驗，說過更嚴厲的話：「一個新的科學真理得勝，並不是靠著說服它的反對者、使他們看見光明；而是因為它的反對者終於死了，而熟悉它的新一代長大了。」

04The calendar case 曆法之爭

China had its own astronomy, with careful records of eclipses, comets, and “guest stars” going back two thousand years, and its own rival pictures of the sky. In the 1600s Jesuit missionaries brought European methods to the court in Beijing and showed that they predicted eclipses more accurately. In 1644 the German Jesuit Adam Schall was put in charge of the imperial calendar. Twenty years later an official named Yang Guangxian brought charges against him and the Chinese astronomers who worked with him. Yang’s objection was not mainly about accuracy. He is remembered for the sentence: “It is better for China to have no good calendar than to have Westerners in China.” Schall was condemned, and five Chinese astronomers were executed. Yang took over the bureau, his predictions failed, and in 1669 a public contest of predictions restored the Jesuits’ methods.

中國有自己的天文學，有兩千年來對日月食、彗星與「客星」的仔細紀錄，也有自己幾種互相對立的天體圖像。十七世紀，耶穌會的傳教士把歐洲的方法帶到北京的宮廷，並且顯示它們預測日月食更準確。1644 年，德國耶穌會士湯若望受命掌管欽天監。二十年後，一位名叫楊光先的官員，控告他以及與他共事的中國天文官。楊光先的反對，主要並不在準確與否。後人記得他的這句話：「寧可使中夏無好曆法，不可使中夏有西洋人。」湯若望被定罪；五位中國的天文官被處決。楊光先接掌了欽天監；他的預測失敗了；1669 年，一場公開的預測比試，恢復了耶穌會士的方法。

The episode shows both of Kuhn’s points and one that tells against him. A way of doing astronomy was bound up with institutions, loyalties, and a view of the world, and it was defended on those grounds. Yet the two sides could agree on a test, the length of a shadow at noon on a named day, and the result decided the matter for the emperor. Paradigms may be different worlds. They can still be made to meet at particular points.

這段插曲同時顯示了孔恩的兩個論點，以及一個不利於他的論點。一種做天文學的方式，與制度、效忠的對象，以及一種世界觀綁在一起；而人們是基於這些理由來捍衛它的。然而雙方還是能對一項檢驗達成共識：在指定的那一天，正午的日影有多長；而結果為皇帝把事情定了下來。典範也許是不同的世界。它們仍然可以被安排在某些特定的點上相遇。

05Is it progress? 這算進步嗎？

Kuhn’s book made many readers conclude that science is a succession of fashions and that no paradigm is truer than another. He denied this for the rest of his life. Later theories, he said, are better at solving puzzles than earlier ones: more accurate, wider in scope, simpler, and more fruitful of new results. These values are shared across revolutions, and they are why science does not go round in circles. What he would not say is that later theories are closer to the truth about what the world is really like, since he saw no way to compare a theory with the world except through another theory.

孔恩的書使許多讀者得出結論：科學只是一連串的流行；沒有哪個典範比另一個更真。他在餘生裡一直否認這一點。他說，後來的理論比先前的理論更善於解謎：更準確、範圍更廣、更簡單，也更能結出新的成果。這些價值是跨越革命而共有的；而它們就是科學不會原地打轉的原因。他不肯說的是：後來的理論更接近「世界真正是什麼樣子」的真相；因為他看不出有什麼辦法，能不經由另一個理論，就拿一個理論去跟世界比較。

That last step is still argued over. Realists point out that theories which survive a revolution usually keep the successes of the old ones as special cases, as Einstein’s equations give Newton’s at low speeds, and that this looks like getting nearer to something. Their opponents reply with history: most past theories that were once successful are now thought false, so we should expect the same of ours. Both sides accept that science changes its mind. It does so rarely, slowly, and under pressure, and when it has changed it can do more than it could before.

最後這一步，至今仍在爭論。實在論者指出：在革命中存活下來的理論，通常把舊理論的成功保留為特例，就像愛因斯坦的方程式在低速下給出牛頓的方程式；而這看起來像是在接近某個東西。他們的對手用歷史來回應：過去曾經成功的理論，大多數現在被認為是假的；所以我們應該預期，我們的理論也會一樣。雙方都接受：科學會改變想法。它很少這樣做，做得很慢，而且是在壓力之下；而改變之後，它能做的事比以前多。

Try it: the loop of Mars 親手做一次：火星的那個圈

Did he really say that? · 這句話真的是他說的嗎？

“Science advances one funeral at a time.” (Max Planck)「科學是一場葬禮一場葬禮地前進的。」（普朗克）

A summary of what he wrote · 是他寫的話的濃縮版

The neat version is a later paraphrase and is not found in Planck’s works. What he wrote, in the scientific autobiography published after his death in 1947, is this: “A new scientific truth does not triumph by convincing its opponents and making them see the light, but rather because its opponents eventually die, and a new generation grows up that is familiar with it.” He was remembering how long it had taken older physicists to accept ideas about heat and energy that he had helped to establish. Kuhn quoted the passage with approval. It should be read as one physicist’s bitter experience, since the general claim is doubtful. Studies of how fast scientists of different ages accepted Darwin’s theory, or plate tectonics, have found the effect of age to be small. Many of the old changed their minds, Planck among them. He accepted the reality of atoms and of light quanta only after resisting both for years.

那個俐落的版本是後來的人濃縮的；在普朗克的著作裡找不到。他寫的話，出自 1947 年他去世之後出版的科學自傳，是這樣的：「一個新的科學真理得勝，並不是靠著說服它的反對者、使他們看見光明；而是因為它的反對者終於死了，而熟悉它的新一代長大了。」他回想的是：年長的物理學家，花了多久才接受他參與建立的那些關於熱與能量的觀念。孔恩贊許地引用了這段話。它應該被讀成一位物理學家的苦澀經驗；因為那個普遍的主張是可疑的。有人研究過不同年齡的科學家接受達爾文理論、或板塊構造理論的速度，發現年齡的影響很小。許多年長的人改變了想法；普朗克自己也是。對於原子的實在性與光量子，他都是抗拒了多年之後才接受的。

Both sides · 正反兩面

Objections and replies 反駁與回應

Objection 1 · 反駁

If science has been wrong so often, there is no reason to trust it now on vaccines or the climate.

如果科學錯了這麼多次，那麼在疫苗或氣候的問題上，現在也沒有理由信任它。

Reply · 回應

The history supports the opposite conclusion. Science was found to be wrong by scientists, using better evidence, and the corrections were kept. No other institution has that record. Revolutions are also rare and seldom overturn what was well measured. Ptolemy’s eclipse predictions still work, and Newton’s laws still send spacecraft to Mars. What is likely to be revised is the deep picture. The practical findings on which medicine and engineering rest are much less likely to change. To distrust a result one needs a reason that bears on that result. That science has changed before is not such a reason.

歷史支持的是相反的結論。科學被發現是錯的，是科學家用更好的證據發現的；而那些更正被保留了下來。沒有別的制度有這樣的紀錄。革命也很少見；而且很少推翻那些被好好測量過的東西。托勒密對日月食的預測至今仍然管用；牛頓的定律至今仍然把太空船送上火星。有可能被修正的，是深層的圖像。醫學與工程所倚賴的那些實用的發現，改變的可能性小得多。要不信任某一個結果，需要一個與那個結果相關的理由。「科學以前改過」並不是這樣的理由。

Objection 2 · 反駁

Kuhn makes science a matter of crowd psychology. On his account the winners are whoever persuades the next generation.

孔恩把科學變成了群眾心理學。照他的說法，贏家就是說服了下一代的那些人。

Reply · 回應

This was the charge of Popper and Lakatos, who called Kuhn’s picture “mob psychology.” Kuhn replied that he was describing how a community of experts weighs reasons, and that the reasons are good ones: accuracy, consistency, scope, simplicity, fruitfulness. Experts can weigh these differently without being irrational, as judges can disagree about a hard case. The reply saves rationality and gives up the idea that a rule could ever replace judgment.

這是波普與拉卡托斯的指控；他們把孔恩的圖像叫做「暴民心理學」。孔恩回應說：他描述的是一個由專家組成的社群怎麼權衡理由；而那些理由是好的理由：準確、一致、範圍、簡單、能結出成果。專家可以對這些有不同的權衡，而並不因此就不理性；就像法官對一件疑難案件可以意見不一。這個回應保住了理性，而放棄了這個想法：有一天可以用一條規則來取代判斷。

Objection 3 · 反駁

The word “paradigm” is so vague that the theory cannot be tested. One critic counted twenty-one senses in Kuhn’s book.

「典範」這個詞太模糊了，以致這個理論無法被檢驗。有一位批評者在孔恩的書裡數出了二十一種意思。

Reply · 回應

Kuhn accepted the criticism and in a postscript of 1969 separated two main senses: the whole set of beliefs and methods a community shares, and the concrete model solutions that students learn to imitate. The second is the more original idea. Scientists learn their trade from worked examples, as apprentices do, and they extend it by seeing new problems as like old ones. That part of his account has held up well, and it is why the word has passed into ordinary speech.

孔恩接受了這個批評，並且在 1969 年的後記裡，分出了兩個主要的意思：一個社群所共有的整套信念與方法；以及學生學著去模仿的那些具體的示範解答。第二個是比較有原創性的觀念。科學家是從做過的例題裡學會這一行的，就像學徒那樣；而他們推展這一行的方式，是把新問題看成與舊問題相似。他的說法裡，這一部分站得很穩；而這也是這個詞進入了日常語言的原因。

Terms of art · 哲學術語

The vocabulary of the subject 這門學問的用語

paradigm典範

Gk. paradeigma ‘pattern, example’

A shared achievement that defines the problems and methods of a scientific community.

一項共同的成就；它界定了一個科學社群的問題與方法。

normal science常態科學

The puzzle-solving work done within an accepted paradigm.

在一個被接受的典範之內所做的解謎工作。

anomaly異常

Gk. anōmalos ‘uneven’

A result that the current paradigm cannot account for.

現行的典範無法說明的結果。

scientific revolution科學革命

The replacement of one paradigm by another.

一個典範被另一個取代。

incommensurable不可共量的

Having no common measure. Kuhn’s term for paradigms that cannot be compared point by point.

沒有共同的尺度。孔恩用這個詞來形容無法逐點比較的典範。

epicycle本輪

Gk. epi ‘upon’ + kyklos ‘circle’

In Ptolemy’s astronomy, the small circle on which a planet moves while the circle’s center goes around the earth.

在托勒密的天文學裡，行星所在的小圓；這個小圓的圓心繞著地球運行。

retrograde motion逆行

L. retro ‘backward’

The apparent backward movement of a planet against the stars.

行星在星空背景上看起來的倒退運動。

scientific realism科學實在論

The view that successful theories are at least approximately true descriptions of the world.

一種看法：成功的理論對世界的描述，至少是近似為真的。

Dharma and the West · 佛法與西方哲學

A view from the Dharma 佛法怎麼看

☸

Buddhism has had to face a change of this kind. The traditional texts describe a flat world with a great mountain, Meru, at its center, four continents around it, and the sun and moon circling its peak. For two thousand years this was the cosmos of Buddhist Asia, taught in monasteries and painted on walls. It is not the cosmos of modern astronomy. Buddhists have met the difficulty in several ways, and the plainest was stated by the Dalai Lama: if scientific analysis were conclusively to show that certain claims in Buddhism are false, then Buddhists must accept the findings of science and abandon those claims. He has applied this to Mount Meru.

佛教不得不面對過這一類的改變。傳統的經論描述了一個平坦的世界：中央有一座大山，須彌山；四周是四大洲；日月繞著山腰運行。兩千年間，這就是佛教亞洲的宇宙：在寺院裡被教授，被畫在牆上。它不是現代天文學的宇宙。佛教徒用過好幾種方式來面對這個困難；而說得最直白的，是達賴喇嘛：如果科學的分析確鑿地顯示，佛教的某些說法是錯的，那麼佛教徒就必須接受科學的發現，放棄那些說法。他把這一點用在了須彌山上。

The early discourses give a reason why this can be done without loss. A monk once told the Buddha that he would leave the order unless he was told whether the cosmos is eternal or not, finite or infinite. The Buddha replied with the simile of a man struck by a poisoned arrow who refuses to have it removed until he knows who shot it, of what clan, with what kind of bow. He would die first. “I have not declared” these things, the Buddha said, and what he had declared was suffering, its origin, its ending, and the path. The cosmology in the texts was the common knowledge of ancient India, used as a setting. It was never the teaching. In Kuhn’s terms the Buddha asked his followers to keep the puzzle and hold the paradigm loosely, which is what a tradition must do if it is to outlive the science of its time (Dharma and Western Philosophy D18).

早期的經典給了一個理由，說明為什麼這樣做並不會有什麼損失。有一位比丘曾經告訴佛陀：除非佛陀告訴他宇宙是永恆的還是不永恆的、有邊的還是無邊的，否則他就要還俗。佛陀用一個譬喻來回答：有個人被毒箭射中，卻不肯讓人把箭拔出來，除非他先知道是誰射的、屬於哪個種姓、用的是哪一種弓。他會先死掉。佛陀說，這些事「我沒有說明」；而他說明了的，是苦、苦的集起、苦的滅，以及道。經論裡的宇宙論，是古代印度的共同知識，被拿來當作背景。它從來不是教法本身。用孔恩的話說，佛陀請他的追隨者守住那個謎題，而鬆鬆地持有典範；一個傳統如果要活得比它那個時代的科學更久，就必須這樣做（佛法與西方哲學 D18）。
