A16 · Mind and Self 心靈與自我

Is There a Self at All? 真的有一個「我」嗎？

Hume looks inward and finds no one there. A Greek king asks a Buddhist monk who he is, and is asked in return what a chariot is. Kant, Zhuangzi, and the brain sciences on why it still feels as though someone is home.
休謨往內看，發現那裡沒有人。一位希臘國王問一位佛教比丘他是誰，卻被反問車是什麼。康德、莊子與腦科學，談為什麼感覺上還是「有人在家」。

A16 Big Questions · 大哉問Mind and Self · 心靈與自我Advanced · 高級1,315 words · about 25 min
The big idea · 大觀念

The lesson on personal identity asked what makes you the same person over time. This one asks an earlier question: whether there is, at any one moment, a subject who has your experiences, or only the experiences.

「人格同一」那一課問的是：什麼使你歷經時間仍是同一個人？這一課問一個更前面的問題：在任何一個時刻，是否有一個「擁有」你的經驗的主體？還是只有那些經驗？

The essay · 課文

Is There a Self at All? 真的有一個「我」嗎？

01The search 尋找

In 1739 a Scotsman of twenty-seven published the result of an experiment that anyone can repeat. Philosophers, David Hume observed, say that we are at every moment conscious of what we call our self, and certain of its perfect identity and simplicity. He decided to look. “For my part, when I enter most intimately into what I call myself, I always stumble on some particular perception or other, of heat or cold, light or shade, love or hatred, pain or pleasure. I never can catch myself at any time without a perception, and never can observe any thing but the perception.” If someone else thinks he has a different notion of himself, Hume adds, “I must confess I can reason no longer with him.” Setting aside such metaphysicians, he ventures to say of the rest of mankind that they are nothing but a bundle or collection of different perceptions, which succeed one another with inconceivable rapidity.

1739 年，一位二十七歲的蘇格蘭人發表了一個人人都可以重做的實驗的結果。休謨觀察到，哲學家們說：我們每一刻都意識到我們稱為「自我」的東西，並且確知它完全的同一與單純。他決定看一看。「就我而言，當我最貼近地進入我稱為『自己』的東西時，我總是碰到某個個別的知覺：冷或熱、明或暗、愛或恨、苦或樂。我任何時候都抓不到一個沒有知覺的自己，而且除了知覺之外，什麼也觀察不到。」他補充說，假如別人認為自己對「自己」有不同的概念，「我必須承認，我沒辦法再跟他講理了」。把這類形上學家擱在一邊，他大膽地說：其餘的人類，不過是一束或一堆不同的知覺，以無法想像的速度前後相續。

02A theater with no stage 沒有舞台的劇場

Hume offered an image and at once withdrew half of it. “The mind is a kind of theatre, where several perceptions successively make their appearance; pass, re-pass, glide away, and mingle in an infinite variety of postures and situations.” Then: “The comparison of the theatre must not mislead us. They are the successive perceptions only, that constitute the mind; nor have we the most distant notion of the place, where these scenes are represented.” There is a performance. There is no stage on which it happens and no one in the stalls. This is the difference between Hume and Descartes, who had made the same inward turn a century earlier and reported finding a thing that thinks. Hume’s reply is that Descartes found thinking and added the thing.

休謨提出了一個意象，又立刻收回了一半。「心靈是一種劇場，種種知覺相繼登場：走過、再走過、滑走，並且以無窮多樣的姿態與情境交融在一起。」接著他說：「劇場這個比喻不可以誤導我們。構成心靈的，只是那些前後相續的知覺；至於這些場景在什麼地方上演，我們連最模糊的概念也沒有。」有一場演出。沒有它在上面進行的舞台，觀眾席裡也沒有人。這就是休謨與笛卡兒的差別；笛卡兒早一個世紀做了同樣的向內轉向，回報說他找到了一個思考的東西。休謨的回答是：笛卡兒找到的是思考，那個「東西」是他加上去的。

03The king and the monk 國王與比丘

The same conclusion had been defended in debate nineteen centuries before, and the record survives in Pali and in Chinese. Around 150 BCE a Greek king named Menander ruled a realm in what is now Afghanistan and Pakistan, one of the states left behind by Alexander’s conquests. Indian tradition calls him Milinda. He liked to argue with holy men, and one day he called on a Buddhist monk and asked his name. “I am known as Nāgasena,” said the monk, but that is “only a generally understood term, a designation in common use,” and no permanent individual is involved in the matter. The king saw the difficulty immediately. If there is no Nāgasena, who receives the robes and the food? Who keeps the precepts, and who breaks them? If a man killed you, there would be no murder.

同樣的結論，早在十九個世紀之前就有人在辯論中為它辯護過；紀錄以巴利文與中文保存了下來。大約西元前 150 年，一位名叫米南德的希臘國王，統治著今天阿富汗與巴基斯坦一帶的一個國度，那是亞歷山大的征服所留下的諸國之一。印度的傳統稱他為彌蘭陀。他喜歡跟修行人辯論；有一天，他去拜訪一位佛教比丘，問他的名字。比丘說：「人們稱我為那先」，但那「只是一個通用的稱呼、一個日常使用的名稱」，這裡面並不涉及任何恆常的個體。國王立刻看出了困難。如果沒有那先，是誰接受衣服與飲食？是誰持戒？又是誰破戒？如果有人殺了你，那就沒有殺人這回事了。

He then conducts Hume’s search aloud. Is the hair Nāgasena? The nails, the teeth, the skin, the heart, the brain? Is it the body, or the feelings, or the perceptions, or the impulses, or the consciousness? All of them together? Something apart from them? To each question the monk answers no. “Then thus, ask as I may, I can discover no Nāgasena. Nāgasena is a mere empty sound.” The monk asks how the king traveled there. In a chariot. Then what is the chariot? Is it the pole, the axle, the wheels, the frame, the yoke? All these together? Something apart from them? Now it is the king who must say no each time, and the monk who announces to the applauding crowd that there is no chariot and the king has told a lie. The king’s answer, and the point of the dialogue, are in the exercise below.

接著，他把休謨的那場尋找，出聲進行了一遍。頭髮是那先嗎？指甲、牙齒、皮膚、心臟、腦呢？是身體嗎？是感受、認知、意志的衝動，還是識？是它們全部合起來嗎？是它們之外的某個東西嗎？每一問，比丘都答「不是」。「那麼，我不管怎麼問，都找不到那先。那先只是一個空洞的聲音。」比丘問國王是怎麼來的。坐車來的。那麼車是什麼？是轅嗎？軸嗎？輪子、車廂、軛嗎？是這些全部合起來嗎？是這些之外的某個東西嗎？現在輪到國王每一次都得說「不是」；而比丘向鼓掌的群眾宣布：根本沒有車，國王說了謊。國王怎麼回答，以及這場對話的要點，就在下面的練習裡。

04A hundred joints and no ruler 百骸而無主

Zhuangzi carries out the same inventory in three sentences. “The hundred joints, the nine openings, the six organs are all present and complete. Which of them should I feel closest to? Are you pleased with them all, or is there one you favor? Are they all servants? Then can servants not keep one another in order? Or do they take turns as ruler and subject?” He draws a conclusion that is more guarded than Hume’s. “It seems as though there were a true ruler, and yet we find no trace of him.” Something makes joy and anger arise, day and night, and “no one knows from where they sprout.” He does not deny that anything governs. He reports that the governor cannot be found, and he lets the matter rest there.

莊子用三句話做完了同一份清點。「百骸、九竅、六藏，賅而存焉，吾誰與為親？汝皆說之乎？其有私焉？如是皆有為臣妾乎？其臣妾不足以相治乎？其遞相為君臣乎？」他引出的結論，比休謨的更有保留：「若有真宰，而特不得其朕。」有某個東西使喜怒日夜生起，而「莫知其所萌」。他並沒有否認有什麼在主宰。他回報的是：那個主宰找不到；然後他就讓事情停在那裡。

05Who is looking? 是誰在看？

The most serious reply to Hume came from Kant, who had been, he said, awakened by him. Hume describes himself as searching, stumbling on perceptions, failing to catch anything further. Who is this “I” that searches and fails? A heap of perceptions cannot look through itself and notice that something is missing. For there to be experience at all, Kant argued, the perceptions must be capable of being brought together as mine: “The ‘I think’ must accompany all my representations,” or they would be nothing to me. So the unity of the self is not one more item to be found on the stage. It is the condition of there being a stage. But Kant refused to give the old view its victory. From this unity, he said, nothing follows about what I am. It is a form that every experience must have, and it tells us no more about an underlying soul than the frame tells us about the painter.

對休謨最嚴肅的回應來自康德；他說自己是被休謨喚醒的。休謨描述自己在尋找，碰到一個個知覺，卻抓不到更多的東西。這個尋找而失敗的「我」是誰？一堆知覺不可能把自己翻過一遍，然後察覺少了什麼。康德論證：要有經驗，這些知覺就必須能夠被匯集起來，成為「我的」：「『我思』必須能夠伴隨我的一切表象」，否則它們對我來說就什麼也不是。所以，自我的統一，並不是舞台上另一件有待尋找的東西。它是「有舞台」這件事的條件。但康德拒絕把勝利交給舊的看法。他說，從這個統一，推不出關於「我是什麼」的任何事。它是每一個經驗都必須具有的形式；它告訴我們的關於底下那個靈魂的事，並不比畫框告訴我們的關於畫家的事更多。

06Why it feels as though someone is home 為什麼感覺上有人在家

If Hume is right, a stubborn appearance needs explaining. The brain sciences have added three pieces. No place has been found in the brain where everything comes together for a single observer, and processing is spread over many regions working at once. Patients whose hemispheres have been surgically separated will act on an instruction shown only to the mute right half and then, when asked why, give a fluent explanation that the speaking half has invented on the spot. The talking part of us appears to be a commentator who takes itself for the author. And the sense of owning a body can be moved: in a well-known experiment, people who watch a rubber hand being stroked while their own hidden hand is stroked in time come to feel the touch in the rubber one.

如果休謨是對的，就有一個頑固的表象需要解釋。腦科學補上了三塊拼圖。在腦裡，找不到一個「一切都匯集起來、呈給單一觀察者」的地方；處理的工作分散在許多同時運作的區域。兩個腦半球被手術分開的病人，會依照只給不能說話的右半邊看的指令行動；被問到為什麼的時候，卻能給出一個流利的解釋，那是會說話的那一半當場編出來的。我們身上那個會說話的部分，看來是一個把自己當成作者的評論員。而「擁有一個身體」的感覺是可以被搬動的：在一個著名的實驗裡，受試者看著一隻橡膠手被刷，自己那隻藏起來的手同時被同步地刷，後來就感覺那個觸感是在橡膠手上。

Daniel Dennett proposed that the self is like a center of gravity. Every object has one, and engineers cannot do without it, yet nobody expects to find it by cutting the object open. It is an abstraction that lets us summarize how a complicated thing will behave. A self, on this view, is the center of the story a brain tells in order to hold a life together. The German philosopher Thomas Metzinger says that the brain builds a model of the organism and cannot recognize the model as a model, so that we look through it as through a window and take ourselves to be what it shows.

丹尼特提議：自我就像重心。每一個物體都有重心，工程師少了它不行；可是沒有人指望把物體切開就能找到它。它是一個抽象，讓我們得以概括一個複雜的東西會怎麼表現。照這個看法，自我是一個腦為了把一段人生維繫在一起而講的那個故事的中心。德國哲學家梅辛格說：腦為這個有機體建造了一個模型，卻認不出這個模型是模型；於是我們像透過一扇窗那樣透過它往外看，並且把自己當成它所呈現的東西。

07Hume’s labyrinth 休謨的迷宮

Hume was the first to see that his account was incomplete. A year after the first volumes of the Treatise he added an appendix in which he reviews his section on the self and writes: “I find myself involved in such a labyrinth, that, I must confess, I neither know how to correct my former opinions, nor how to render them consistent.” If perceptions are distinct existences, something must explain why these ones belong together as one mind and are not scattered among many, and he could find nothing that does. He never returned to the subject. The puzzle he left has three parts, and every later theory is an attempt at one of them. Something makes experience unified at a moment. Something makes it seem owned. And something makes the thought “there is no self” so hard to feel, even for someone who is sure it is true.

休謨是第一個看出自己的說明並不完整的人。《人性論》前兩卷出版一年後，他加了一篇附錄，回頭檢視談自我的那一節，寫道：「我發現自己陷在這樣一座迷宮裡；我必須承認，我既不知道怎麼修正先前的意見，也不知道怎麼使它們前後一致。」如果知覺是各自獨立的存在，就必須有某個東西說明：為什麼這些知覺同屬於一個心，而不是散落在許多個心當中？而他找不到任何這樣的東西。他此後再也沒有回到這個題目。他留下的難題有三個部分，而後來的每一種理論，都是在嘗試解決其中之一。有某個東西，使經驗在一個時刻是統一的。有某個東西，使它看起來是「被擁有的」。還有某個東西，使「沒有自我」這個想法這麼難以感受到，即使是對確信它為真的人也一樣。

None of this abolishes the person who signs your letters. Nāgasena went on answering to his name. The claim is that the name refers to a great many things working together, and that our habit of picturing one small owner inside them is a picture and nothing more. What follows from the claim is taken up on another shelf.

這一切並沒有取消那個在你的信上簽名的人。那先仍然繼續回應別人叫他的名字。這裡的主張是：那個名字指的是許許多多一起運作的東西；而我們習慣在它們裡面想像一個小小的擁有者，那只是一幅圖像，僅此而已。從這個主張會推出什麼，是另一個書架的題目。

Thought experiment · 思想實驗

Try it: take the chariot apart, then look for yourself 親手做一次：把車拆開，然後找找你自己

Two searches. In the first you are King Milinda and Nāgasena is questioning you. In the second you repeat Hume’s experiment for thirty seconds and report what you found.

兩場尋找。第一場，你是彌蘭王，那先在盤問你。第二場，你花三十秒重做休謨的實驗，然後回報你找到了什麼。

Part 1 · The chariot 第一部分：車

“You came in a chariot, Sire. Explain to me what that is. Point to it.”

「大王，您是坐車來的。請向我說明車是什麼。指給我看。」

Click any part of the chariot. 點車上的任何一個部分。

“Then is it all these parts lying here that are the chariot?” — “No, Sir.” “Is there anything outside them that is the chariot?” — “No.” Nāgasena turns to the crowd: “Then thus, ask as I may, I can discover no chariot. Chariot is a mere empty sound. It is a falsehood that your Majesty has spoken!”

「那麼，擺在這裡的這些零件全部，是車嗎？」——「不是，尊者。」「在它們之外，有什麼東西是車嗎？」——「沒有。」那先轉向群眾：「那麼，我不管怎麼問，都找不到車。車只是一個空洞的聲音。大王說的是假話！」

You are the king. How do you answer?你是國王。你怎麼回答？

Then you walked here across the hot sand, and your feet say otherwise. This answer is called nihilism, and Nāgasena does not want it. Something carried you. The argument has shown that the chariot is not a part and not an extra thing. It has not shown that the word refers to nothing.

那麼你是踩著滾燙的沙子走來的，而你的腳說不是這樣。這個回答叫作虛無論，那先並不想要它。確實有東西載你來。這個論證顯示的是：車不是某一個零件，也不是額外的一樣東西。它並沒有顯示這個詞什麼也不指。

Then it should be possible to remove every part and still have it, or to point to it beside them. You have just agreed that you cannot. This answer, applied to a person, is the soul. Its attraction is that it gives the word something single to stand for. Its cost is that the single thing can never be produced.

那麼，應該可以把每一個零件都拿掉而它還在，或者可以在零件旁邊把它指出來。你剛剛才同意你做不到。這個回答用在人身上，就是靈魂。它吸引人的地方，是給了這個詞一個單一的東西來代表。它的代價是：那個單一的東西永遠拿不出來。

This is Milinda’s answer: “It is on account of its having all these things… that it comes under the generally understood term, the designation in common use, of ‘chariot.’” Nāgasena replies: “Very good! Your Majesty has rightly grasped the meaning of ‘chariot.’ And just even so it is on account of all those things you questioned me about… that I come under the generally understood term, the designation in common use, of ‘Nāgasena.’” The word is used correctly, and it does not name a further thing.

這就是彌蘭王的回答：「正是由於它具有這一切……它才歸在『車』這個通用的稱呼、日常使用的名稱之下。」那先回答：「很好！大王正確地掌握了『車』的意思。同樣地，正是由於您盤問我的那一切……我才歸在『那先』這個通用的稱呼、日常使用的名稱之下。」這個詞用得正確，而它並不指稱一樣額外的東西。

Part 2 · Hume’s experiment 第二部分：休謨的實驗

Now Hume’s experiment. For thirty seconds, stop reading and attend to whatever is present in your experience. Look for the one who is having it. Then tick everything you found.

現在是休謨的實驗。三十秒之內，停止閱讀，注意你的經驗裡出現的任何東西。找一找那個「正在擁有」它的人。然後把你找到的每一樣都勾起來。

The text was composed in northwest India around the beginning of the common era and exists in a Pali version and two Chinese ones. The Chinese under each passage is a modern rendering of the English.

這部經大約在西元紀年之初成於印度西北，現存一個巴利文本與兩個漢譯本。每一段下面的中文，是依英譯所作的白話翻譯。

The Questions of King Milinda (Milindapañha), Book II, chapter 1 《彌蘭王問經》；漢譯《那先比丘經》 · tr. T. W. Rhys Davids, 1890 (public domain)

Did he really say that? · 這句話真的是他說的嗎？

“You do not have a soul. You are a soul. You have a body.” (C. S. Lewis)「你並不是『有』一個靈魂。你『是』一個靈魂。你有的是一個身體。」（C. S. 路易斯）

Not in Lewis · 路易斯的著作裡沒有

The sentence is quoted everywhere under Lewis’s name and has not been found in anything he wrote. Versions of it were in circulation in Britain in the 1890s, before he was born, and it has been credited to the Scottish writer George MacDonald among others. Whoever said it, it is a clear statement of the view this lesson has been testing: that behind the body and the passing states of mind there is one thing that you are. It is Descartes’ view and Plato’s, and it remains what most people believe.

這句話到處被掛在路易斯名下引用，卻不曾在他寫的任何東西裡被找到。它的各種版本，在 1890 年代的英國就已經流傳了，那時他還沒出生；它曾被歸給蘇格蘭作家喬治・麥克唐納等人。不管是誰說的，它清楚地陳述了這一課一直在檢驗的那個看法：在身體與來來去去的心理狀態背後，有一樣東西，那就是你。這是笛卡兒的看法，也是柏拉圖的看法；而它仍然是多數人相信的。

Both sides · 正反兩面

Objections and replies 反駁與回應

Objection 1 · 反駁

The search is rigged. A self is the subject of experience and so cannot turn up among its objects, any more than an eye can appear in its own field of view.

這場尋找是被動過手腳的。自我是經驗的主體，所以不可能出現在經驗的對象當中，就像眼睛不可能出現在自己的視野裡。

Reply · 回應

This is the reply of Kant and, long before him, of the Upanishads. It concedes Hume’s finding and disputes what follows. The difficulty is to say anything more about this subject. If it has no observable features, we cannot know that it is one and the same from moment to moment, or that it is mine and not shared, or that it is a thing and not a feature of how experience is organized. Kant accepted all of this, which is why his “I think” is a form and no longer a soul.

這是康德的回應；而早在他之前，這也是奧義書的回應。它承認休謨的發現，爭的是由此能推出什麼。困難在於：對這個主體還能再說些什麼？如果它沒有任何可觀察的特徵，我們就無法知道它每一刻都是同一個，無法知道它是我的、而不是共有的，也無法知道它是一樣東西、而不是經驗組織方式的一個特徵。這一切康德都接受了；這就是為什麼他的「我思」是一個形式，不再是一個靈魂。

Objection 2 · 反駁

The denial defeats itself. To say “I find no self” is to use the word “I.” Someone must be doing the denying.

這個否定是自己打敗自己的。說「我找不到自我」，就用了「我」這個字。總得有人在否定。

Reply · 回應

The objection shows that English requires a subject for its verbs. Georg Lichtenberg, a contemporary of Kant, suggested that Descartes should have said “it thinks,” as we say “it rains,” where nobody supposes that an “it” is doing the raining. Nāgasena makes the same move with his name. He goes on saying “I” and answering to “Nāgasena,” and holds that the usage is correct and convenient and commits him to nothing beyond the parts working together.

這個反駁顯示的是：英文的動詞需要一個主詞。跟康德同時代的利希滕貝格提議，笛卡兒本來應該說「（它）在思考」，就像我們說「（天）在下雨」；沒有人認為有一個「它」在做下雨這件事。那先對自己的名字用的是同一招。他繼續說「我」，繼續回應「那先」這個名字；他主張這個用法是正確而方便的，並且沒有讓他承諾零件一起運作之外的任何東西。

Objection 3 · 反駁

If no one is there, no one deserves praise or blame, and no one is harmed by cruelty.

如果那裡沒有人，就沒有人值得讚美或責備，也沒有人會被殘忍所傷。

Reply · 回應

This is Milinda’s objection, and it mistakes the claim. Pain occurs whether or not it has an owner, and it is as bad as it ever was. What the view removes is the idea of an inner proprietor in addition to the pain. Responsibility likewise attaches to a continuous stream of intention and action, as it does in a court of law, where no one inquires about souls. Later in the same book Nāgasena says that the one who is reborn is neither the same as nor different from the one who died, as milk is neither the same as nor different from the curds it becomes.

這是彌蘭王的反駁，而它誤解了這個主張。不管痛有沒有主人，痛都在發生，而且跟向來一樣糟。這個看法拿掉的，是在痛之外另有一個內在業主的那個想法。責任也一樣，是附著在一道連續的意圖與行動之流上的；法庭上就是這樣，沒有人在那裡查問靈魂。在同一部經稍後的地方，那先說：再生的那一位，與死去的那一位，既不相同也不相異，就像牛奶與它變成的凝乳，既不相同也不相異。

Terms of art · 哲學術語

The vocabulary of the subject 這門學問的用語

bundle theory束論

The view that a mind is a collection of perceptions with no owner in addition to them.

主張心靈是一堆知覺的集合，在它們之外沒有另一個擁有者。

impression印象

L. impressio ‘a pressing in’

Hume’s term for a vivid perception had in sensing or feeling, as against an idea, which is its fainter copy in thought.

休謨的用語：在感覺或感受時所具有的鮮明知覺；相對於「觀念」，後者是它在思想中較淡的副本。

introspection內省

L. introspicere ‘to look within’

Attending to one’s own mental states.

注意自己的心理狀態。

unity of apperception統覺的統一

L. ad + percipere ‘to perceive in addition’

Kant’s term for the fact that all my experiences can be thought of together as mine.

康德的用語：我的一切經驗都能夠被一起想成「我的」這個事實。

substance實體

L. substantia ‘that which stands under’

A thing that has properties and persists through changes in them. The soul was held to be one.

擁有種種性質、並且歷經這些性質的變化而持續存在的東西。靈魂曾被認為是一個實體。

designation假名、施設

Pali paññatti

A name used for convenience of many things together, with no single thing answering to it.

為了方便而用來稱呼許多東西合在一起的名稱；並沒有單一的東西與它對應。

confabulation虛談

L. confabulari ‘to talk together’

The sincere production of a false explanation for one’s own behavior.

真誠地為自己的行為編出一個不實的解釋。

narrative self敘事自我

The self understood as the main character of the story a person tells about his or her life.

把自我理解為一個人講述自己人生的那個故事裡的主角。

    把休謨的實驗重做兩分鐘，然後用大約 120 個英文字，確切描述你找到了什麼、沒找到什麼。

Dharma and the West · 佛法與西方哲學

A view from the Dharma 佛法怎麼看

☸

This lesson has been half Buddhist already, since the chariot is the tradition’s own illustration. Two things may be added. Nāgasena’s list is not random. When the king has finished with hair and bones he asks about five things in turn: form, feeling, perception, formations, and consciousness. These are the five aggregates, the scheme by which the Buddha sorted everything a person might take to be a self. Hume searched without a map and reported “some particular perception or other.” The Buddhist searcher is handed a map with five regions and told to examine each.

這一課本來就有一半是佛教的了，因為車喻是這個傳統自己的例證。可以再補充兩點。那先的清單並不是隨意的。國王問完了頭髮與骨頭之後，依序問了五樣東西：色、受、想、行、識。這就是五蘊，佛陀用來把一個人可能當成「我」的一切加以歸類的架構。休謨是沒有地圖地在找，回報說找到「某個個別的知覺」。佛教的尋找者手上拿到的是一張分成五區的地圖，並且被告知要逐區檢視。

The second point is the purpose. Hume found his result disturbing, shut the study door, and went to play backgammon. In the Buddhist texts the same result is the beginning of freedom, because the search is undertaken in order to stop clinging to what is found. A companion page takes up the map itself: what the five aggregates are, and which Western account of the mind comes closest to them.

第二點是目的。休謨覺得自己的結果令人不安，關上書房的門，跑去下雙陸棋了。在佛教經典裡，同樣的結果卻是自由的開端，因為這場尋找本來就是為了不再執取所找到的東西而進行的。姊妹篇處理的是那張地圖本身：五蘊是什麼？西方對心靈的哪一種說明最接近它？
