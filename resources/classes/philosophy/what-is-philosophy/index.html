The big idea · 大觀念

Philosophy is the disciplined examination of the beliefs we rely on most and question least, carried out by giving reasons and testing them.

哲學是對我們最依賴、卻最少質疑的信念所做的嚴格檢驗；做法是提出理由，再檢驗理由。

The essay · 課文

What Is Philosophy? 哲學是什麼？

01The question behind the questions 問題背後的問題

A four-year-old asks why the sky is dark at night, and you answer. She asks why the sun goes away, and you answer again. By the fifth why you have usually reached one of two walls. The first wall is ignorance: there is a fact of the matter, and you do not happen to know it. The second is stranger. You have run out of facts altogether, and what is left is a question such as why there is anything at all, or how you know you are not mistaken about all of it. No further observation would settle a question like that. Philosophy is what people do at the second wall.

四歲的孩子問，為什麼晚上天是黑的，你回答了。她再問太陽為什麼會走掉，你又回答了。問到第五個「為什麼」，你通常會撞上兩種牆。第一種是無知：這件事有答案，只是你剛好不知道。第二種比較奇怪：事實整個用完了，剩下的是這樣的問題——為什麼會有東西存在，而不是什麼都沒有？你怎麼知道自己不是全盤都弄錯了？再多觀察也解決不了這種問題。人在第二道牆前面做的事，就是哲學。

02A name and a legend 一個名字，一個傳說

The word is Greek: philosophia, from philein, to love, and sophia, wisdom. An ancient story, told by Cicero some five centuries after the supposed event, credits it to Pythagoras, who is said to have declined the title sophos, wise man, and called himself only a lover of wisdom. The story is probably legend, but it has survived because it captures something true. A philosopher is defined by a relation to wisdom, and the relation is desire rather than possession.

這個字是希臘文：philosophia，由 philein（愛）與 sophia（智慧）組成。有一個古老的故事說它出自畢達哥拉斯：他不肯接受 sophos（智者）的稱號，只說自己是「愛智慧的人」。這個故事是西塞羅在事隔約五百年後記下的，多半是傳說；它流傳下來，是因為說中了一件事。哲學家是由他跟智慧的關係來界定的，而那個關係是渴望，不是擁有。

03Beginning in wonder 始於驚奇

Plato has Socrates say that “philosophy begins in wonder” (Theaetetus 155d), and Aristotle agreed: “it is owing to their wonder that men both now begin and at first began to philosophize” (Metaphysics 982b). The wonder they had in mind is not the pleasant awe of a sunset. It is closer to puzzlement, the discomfort of noticing that something you took to be obvious does not, on inspection, make sense. Everyone knows what time is until asked to say. Everyone knows what a fair share is until two people claim the same thing.

柏拉圖筆下的蘇格拉底說「哲學始於驚奇」（《泰阿泰德篇》155d），亞里斯多德也同意：「人們現在開始、以及最初開始做哲學思考，都是由於驚奇」（《形上學》982b）。他們說的驚奇，不是看夕陽時那種愉快的讚嘆，比較接近困惑：你發現一件向來以為理所當然的事，仔細一看竟然說不通，心裡因此不安。人人都知道時間是什麼，直到有人要他說出來。人人都知道什麼叫公平的一份，直到兩個人要同一樣東西。

04The Socratic turn 蘇格拉底的轉向

Socrates (c. 470–399 BCE) made a method of that discomfort. He wrote nothing. What we have are portraits by people who knew him, above all Plato, and in those portraits he does one thing again and again: he finds someone who claims to know what courage or justice or piety is, asks for a definition, and tests it against cases the speaker himself accepts. A general says that courage is standing firm in the line; Socrates points out that soldiers sometimes fight bravely while retreating. The definition is revised, tested again, and fails again.

蘇格拉底（約西元前 470–399 年）把這種不安變成一套方法。他一個字也沒寫。我們有的是認識他的人為他畫的像，其中最重要的是柏拉圖；在這些畫像裡，他反覆做同一件事：找到一個自稱知道什麼是勇敢、正義或虔敬的人，請對方下定義，再拿對方自己也承認的例子來檢驗這個定義。一位將軍說，勇敢就是在陣線上堅守不退；蘇格拉底指出，士兵有時是一邊撤退一邊英勇作戰的。定義修改了，再受檢驗，又垮了。

The technical name for this cross-examination is the elenchus, and its usual result is aporia, a state of being at a loss. Socrates considered that result a gain. A person who has discovered that he does not know is at least in a position to start looking.

這種詰問的術語叫 elenchus（詰問法），通常的結果是 aporia，也就是不知所措、無路可走的狀態。蘇格拉底認為這個結果是收穫。一個人發現自己並不知道，至少就站到了可以開始尋找的位置。

05What philosophy is not 哲學不是什麼

Three neighbors help to mark the borders. Philosophy is not science, because its questions are not settled by measurement, though it reasons about what measurement can show. It is not religion, because it accepts no conclusion on authority, though it asks many of the same questions. And it is not mere opinion, because a philosophical claim has to be argued for, and an argument can be checked by anyone.

有三個鄰居可以幫我們標出邊界。哲學不是科學，因為它的問題不是靠測量解決的，雖然它會推敲測量到底能證明什麼。哲學不是宗教，因為它不憑權威接受任何結論，雖然它問的許多問題跟宗教相同。哲學也不只是意見，因為哲學主張必須有論證支持，而論證是任何人都能檢查的。

The discipline lives on that last point. You are free to believe whatever you like, but once you give reasons, your reasons become public property, and other people may show you that they do not support what you thought they did.

這門學問就靠最後這一點活著。你愛相信什麼都可以；可是一旦你給出理由，你的理由就成了公共財，別人可以指給你看：這些理由並不支持你以為它們支持的結論。

06The map 地圖

Over twenty-five centuries the questions have sorted themselves into branches. Logic studies what follows from what. Epistemology asks what knowledge is and whether we have any. Metaphysics asks what there is and what it is like: time, causation, the self, free will. Ethics asks how one should live, political philosophy how we should live together, and aesthetics what beauty and art are. Around these have grown the “philosophies of”: of mind, of language, of science, of religion, of education. The borders are porous. Ask whether a machine can think, and within a few minutes you will be doing metaphysics, epistemology, and ethics at once.

兩千五百年下來，這些問題自己分成了幾個分支。邏輯學研究什麼可以從什麼推出來。知識論問知識是什麼、我們到底有沒有知識。形上學問有什麼東西存在、它們是什麼樣子：時間、因果、自我、自由意志。倫理學問人應該怎麼活，政治哲學問我們應該怎麼一起活，美學問美與藝術是什麼。在這些周圍又長出一批「某某哲學」：心靈哲學、語言哲學、科學哲學、宗教哲學、教育哲學。分支之間的邊界是透水的。問一句「機器會不會思考」，幾分鐘之內你就同時在做形上學、知識論和倫理學了。

07Not only Greece 不只是希臘

The Greek word should not mislead us about ownership. Confucius died about a decade before Socrates was born, and by then he had spent a lifetime asking what makes a person humane. The Buddha, whose dates are less certain, taught in the same era that what we call a self is a process and not a thing. Neither had a word for “philosophy.” The Chinese term 哲學 was coined only in 1874, by the Japanese scholar Nishi Amane, to translate the European word.

這個字是希臘文，但我們不該因此弄錯這門學問歸誰所有。孔子過世大約十年後蘇格拉底才出生，而孔子已經用了一輩子追問：什麼使一個人成為仁者。佛陀的年代比較不確定，但他在同一個時代教導：我們稱為「我」的，是一個過程，不是一個東西。他們兩位都沒有「哲學」這個詞。中文的「哲學」要到 1874 年才出現，是日本學者西周為了翻譯這個歐洲字而造的。

Yet a tradition does not need the label to do the work. Wherever people have given reasons for claims about knowledge, reality, and the good life, and have answered objections to them, philosophy has been going on. This series therefore follows three lines, Greek, Chinese, and Indian, and pays attention to the places where they meet.

不過，一個傳統不需要有這個標籤，也能做這件工作。只要有人為自己關於知識、真實與美好人生的主張提出理由，並且回應別人的反駁，哲學就在進行。所以這個系列沿著三條線走：希臘、中國、印度，並且特別留意它們相遇的地方。

08Why bother 何必呢

Bertrand Russell faced the obvious complaint, that philosophy never seems to settle anything, and gave two replies. First, the complaint is partly an illusion: when a philosophical question becomes answerable, it gets a new name. Physics was once “natural philosophy”; psychology left home in the nineteenth century. What remains under the old name is, by definition, the unfinished business.

最常見的抱怨是：哲學好像從來沒有解決過任何問題。羅素正面回應了這個抱怨，給了兩個答覆。第一，這個抱怨有一部分是錯覺：一個哲學問題一旦變得可以回答，就會換一個名字。物理學從前叫「自然哲學」；心理學是十九世紀才離家的。還留在舊名字底下的，按定義就是沒做完的事。

Second, the unfinished business is worth doing for its own sake. Philosophy, he wrote, is to be studied “not for the sake of any definite answers to its questions,” but “for the sake of the questions themselves; because these questions enlarge our conception of what is possible.” A mind that has never doubted its own assumptions is confined by them. Socrates put the point more severely at his trial. The unexamined life, he told the jury, is not worth living.

第二，沒做完的事本身就值得做。他寫道，研究哲學「不是為了它的問題有什麼確定的答案」，而是「為了這些問題本身；因為這些問題擴大了我們對於什麼是可能的想像」。一個從來沒有懷疑過自己預設的心靈，就被這些預設關住了。蘇格拉底在受審時把話說得更重。他對陪審團說：沒有經過省察的人生，不值得活。

Five tests for any definition 檢驗任何定義的五個問題

  1. 1

    Is it a definition, or only an example? A list of brave acts does not say what makes them brave.

    它是定義，還是只是例子？列出幾件勇敢的事，並沒有說出是什麼使它們勇敢。

  2. 2

    Is it too narrow? Look for a clear case of the thing that the definition leaves out.

    它是不是太窄？找一個明明屬於這個概念、卻被定義排除在外的例子。

  3. 3

    Is it too broad? Look for something the definition lets in that plainly does not belong.

    它是不是太寬？找一個明明不屬於這個概念、卻被定義放進來的例子。

  4. 4

    Is it circular? Check whether the word being defined, or a near synonym, has slipped into the definition.

    它是不是循環？檢查被定義的那個詞，或它的近義詞，有沒有溜進定義裡。

  5. 5

    Does it conflict with something else you believe? If it does, one of the two has to go.

    它跟你相信的別的事衝不衝突？如果衝突，兩者之中總得放棄一個。

Now write your own definition of friendship, fairness, or respect, and run the five tests on it. Nothing you type leaves this page.

現在換你：替「友誼」「公平」或「尊重」寫一個自己的定義，再用上面五個問題檢驗它。你輸入的內容不會離開這個頁面。

Primary text · 原典

In his own words 原典怎麼說

The Apology is Plato’s version of the speech Socrates gave at his trial in 399 BCE. (Apologia means a defense in court; he apologizes for nothing.) Two short passages carry the lesson.

《申辯篇》是柏拉圖筆下，蘇格拉底在西元前 399 年受審時的發言。（apologia 是法庭上的「辯護」，他什麼歉也沒道。）兩小段話就帶出了這一課的重點。

Plato, Apology 柏拉圖《申辯篇》 · translated by Benjamin Jowett (public domain)

Did he really say that? · 這句話真的是他說的嗎？

“I know that I know nothing.”「我只知道我一無所知。」

Not in Plato · 柏拉圖沒有這樣寫

This sentence appears nowhere in Plato. It is a later compression of Apology 21d, where Socrates says only that he does not think he knows what he does not know. The compressed version is also self-defeating, since it claims to know something. The Latin tag often quoted for it, scio me nihil scire, is likewise a later formulation.

柏拉圖的著作裡找不到這句話。它是後人把《申辯篇》21d 濃縮而成的；蘇格拉底在那裡只說，他不知道的事，他不以為自己知道。濃縮版還會自打嘴巴，因為它宣稱「知道」了一件事。常被引用的拉丁文版本 scio me nihil scire，同樣是後來才有的說法。

Both sides · 正反兩面

Objections and replies 反駁與回應

Objection 1 · 反駁

Philosophy makes no progress. After twenty-five centuries philosophers still disagree about everything, while a first-year physics student knows more than Newton did.

哲學沒有進步。兩千五百年了，哲學家還是什麼都吵不出結論；而一個大一物理系學生知道的，已經比牛頓多。

Reply · 回應

Russell’s reply is that solved problems are renamed and leave the field, so what remains is always the unsolved part. A second reply is that progress in philosophy is often negative: we now know why a great many tempting answers fail, and that is knowledge. A critic may fairly press the point, however. If no positive answer has ever been secured, on what grounds do we call the activity a form of inquiry at all?

羅素的回答是：解決了的問題會改名、離開這個領域，所以留下來的永遠是沒解決的那一部分。第二個回答是，哲學的進步常常是否定式的：我們現在知道許多誘人的答案為什麼不成立，而這就是知識。不過批評者可以合理地追問下去：如果從來沒有一個正面的答案站穩過，我們憑什麼說這是一種「探究」？

Objection 2 · 反駁

The elenchus only destroys. Socrates refutes every answer and offers none, and his victims leave humiliated, having learned nothing.

詰問法只會破壞。蘇格拉底駁倒每一個答案，自己卻不給答案；被他問倒的人顏面盡失地離開，什麼也沒學到。

Reply · 回應

In the Meno, Socrates leads a boy to see that his confident answer to a geometry problem is wrong, and then asks whether the boy is worse off for it. Before, he thought he knew and would not have looked for the answer; now he knows that he does not know, and wants to find out (Meno 84a–c). Clearing away false confidence is the first step of learning. Whether Socrates took that step kindly is another matter, and Athens evidently thought he did not.

在《美諾篇》裡，蘇格拉底帶一個少年看出他對一道幾何題的自信答案是錯的，然後問：這個少年因此變糟了嗎？先前他以為自己知道，所以不會去找答案；現在他知道自己不知道，而且想弄清楚（《美諾篇》84a–c）。清掉虛假的自信，是學習的第一步。至於蘇格拉底做這一步的時候夠不夠厚道，那是另一回事；雅典人顯然認為他不夠。

Objection 3 · 反駁

“The unexamined life is not worth living” is elitist. It condemns most of the human beings who have ever lived.

「未經省察的人生不值得活」是菁英主義，它等於否定了有史以來絕大多數人的人生。

Reply · 回應

One defense reads the line narrowly: Socrates is speaking of himself, explaining why he could not live silenced. Another reads “examined” modestly, as the ordinary practice of asking whether you are living as you think you should, which requires no schooling. On either reading the claim is easier to defend than the slogan suggests. Read at full strength, as a verdict on other people’s lives, it is very hard to defend.

一種辯護把這句話讀得很窄：蘇格拉底是在說他自己，在解釋為什麼他沒辦法閉著嘴活下去。另一種辯護把「省察」讀得很平實：就是平常問問自己，是不是照著自己認為應該的方式在活；這不需要讀過書。照這兩種讀法，這個主張都比那句口號容易辯護。但如果照字面的全部力道，把它讀成對別人人生的判決，就很難辯護了。

Terms of art · 哲學術語

The vocabulary of the subject 這門學問的用語

philosophy哲學

Gk. philosophia: philein ‘to love’ + sophia ‘wisdom’

The examination, by argument, of basic questions about knowledge, reality, and value.

以論證的方式，檢驗關於知識、真實與價值的基本問題。

elenchus詰問法

Gk. elenchos ‘cross-examination, refutation’

Socrates’ method: draw out a definition, then show that it conflicts with other things the speaker believes.

蘇格拉底的方法：引出一個定義，再指出它跟說話者相信的其他事互相衝突。

aporia困惑、無路可走

Gk. a- ‘without’ + poros ‘passage’

The state of being at a loss when every proposed answer has failed.

所有提出的答案都失敗之後，那種不知所措的狀態。

epistemology知識論

Gk. epistēmē ‘knowledge’ + logos ‘account’

The branch that studies knowledge, belief, and justification.

研究知識、信念與證成的分支。

metaphysics形上學

Gk. ta meta ta physika, ‘the books after the Physics’

The branch that studies what exists and its most general features. The name began as a librarian’s label for where Aristotle’s treatise was shelved.

研究什麼存在、以及存在物最普遍特徵的分支。這個名字原本只是圖書編目的標籤，指亞里斯多德那部書排在《物理學》之後。

ethics倫理學

Gk. ēthos ‘character’

The branch that studies how one ought to live and act.

研究人應該怎麼生活、怎麼行動的分支。

argument論證

L. arguere ‘to make clear’

A set of premises offered in support of a conclusion. In philosophy the word never means a quarrel.

用來支持一個結論的一組前提。在哲學裡，這個字從來不是「吵架」的意思。

counterexample反例

A single case that shows a general claim to be false.

足以證明一個普遍主張為假的單一個案。

Advanced English · 高級英文

Words and phrases worth keeping 值得帶走的字與片語

settle(v.)解決、定論

No further observation would settle a question like that.

再多觀察也解決不了這種問題。

credit (something) to(v. phr.)把……歸功於

An ancient story credits the word to Pythagoras.

Dharma and the West · 佛法與西方哲學

A view from the Dharma 佛法怎麼看

☸

The Buddha was also a master of the question. An early discourse (Aṅguttara Nikāya 4.42) sorts questions into four kinds: those to be answered directly, those to be answered after drawing distinctions, those to be answered with a counter-question, and those to be set aside. The third kind is what Socrates did for a living, and the second, vibhajja, “analysis,” gave one early school its name.

佛陀也是提問的大師。早期的一部經（《增支部》4.42）把問題分成四種：應當直接回答的（一向記）、應當分別之後再回答的（分別記）、應當反問之後再回答的（反詰記）、應當擱置不答的（捨置記）。第三種正是蘇格拉底終身在做的事；第二種「分別」（vibhajja），後來成了早期一個部派的名字。

The fourth kind marks the real difference. Socrates held that examination is itself the good life, and he pursued a question wherever it led. The Buddha set certain questions aside, among them whether the world is eternal and whether it is finite, on the ground that pursuing them does not lead to the end of suffering. For him, inquiry is governed by a practical aim. Whether that restriction is a limit on philosophy or a philosophical position in its own right is a question this series will return to (Dharma and the West, D21).

第四種才是真正的差別所在。蘇格拉底認為省察本身就是美好的人生，問題走到哪裡他就追到哪裡。佛陀則把某些問題擱在一邊，例如世界是不是永恆、是不是有邊，理由是追究這些問題並不導向苦的止息。對佛陀來說，探究受一個實踐目的節制。這個限制到底是對哲學的限縮，還是本身就是一種哲學立場？這個系列之後會回來談（佛法與西方哲學 D21）。
