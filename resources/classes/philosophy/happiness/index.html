A21 · The Good Life 美好人生

What Is Happiness? 幸福是什麼？

A machine that could give you any experience you chose, for life. Whether you would plug in decides between three theories of what makes a life go well, with Epicurus, Aristotle, Zhuangzi, and Yan Hui in his alley.
一台機器，可以給你任何你選的經驗，給一輩子。你願不願意接上去，就在三種「什麼使人生過得好」的理論之間作了裁決；還有伊比鳩魯、亞里斯多德、莊子，以及陋巷裡的顏回。

A21 Big Questions · 大哉問The Good Life · 美好人生Advanced · 高級1,167 words · about 25 min
The big idea · 大觀念

Everyone wants to be happy and few can say what they are wanting. The word covers a feeling, a judgment about one’s life, and a way of living, and the three do not always go together.

人人都想要幸福，卻很少有人說得出自己想要的是什麼。這個詞涵蓋了一種感受、一個對自己人生的判斷，以及一種生活的方式；而這三者並不總是走在一起。

The essay · 課文

What Is Happiness? 幸福是什麼？

01The machine 那台機器

In 1974 the Harvard philosopher Robert Nozick described a device that neuroscientists might one day build. You float in a tank with electrodes attached to your brain, and the machine gives you any experience you like. You could seem to write a great novel, win a friend, or climb a mountain, and it would feel from the inside exactly as the real thing feels. You may choose your experiences in advance from a large library, and once you are in you will not know that you are in. Your body is cared for. Others can plug in too, so there is no one you need stay outside to help. Would you plug in for the rest of your life?

1974 年，哈佛的哲學家諾齊克描述了一種神經科學家有朝一日也許造得出來的裝置。你漂浮在一個水槽裡，腦上接著電極；這台機器可以給你任何你喜歡的經驗。你可以「好像」寫出了一部偉大的小說、交到了一位朋友、登上了一座山；而從裡面感覺起來，會跟真的一模一樣。你可以事先從一座龐大的資料庫裡挑選你要的經驗；一旦進去，你就不會知道自己在裡面。你的身體有人照料。別人也可以接上去，所以你不需要為了幫助誰而留在外面。你願意接上去，度過餘生嗎？

Most people who are asked say no, and Nozick thought their refusal showed something. If all that mattered to us were how our lives feel from the inside, we would have no reason to hesitate. Since we do hesitate, something else must matter. He suggested three things. We want to do certain things, and not merely to have the experience of doing them. We want to be a certain kind of person, and someone floating in a tank is not brave or kind or anything at all. And we want to be in contact with reality, not confined to a world that human beings have made.

被問到的人，多數說不；而諾齊克認為，他們的拒絕顯示了某件事。如果對我們來說要緊的，只是人生「從裡面感覺起來」如何，我們就沒有理由猶豫。既然我們確實猶豫，就必定還有別的東西要緊。他提出了三樣。我們想要「做」某些事，而不只是擁有「做了那些事」的經驗。我們想要「是」某一種人；而一個漂在水槽裡的人，既不勇敢也不仁慈，什麼也不是。還有，我們想要與「真實」接觸，而不是被關在一個由人造出來的世界裡。

02Three theories 三種理論

The machine sorts the answers that philosophers have given to the question of what makes a life go well for the person who lives it. The first is hedonism: a good life is one with the most pleasure and the least pain, and nothing else counts in itself. It is the simplest view and the one the machine was designed to embarrass, since a hedonist ought to plug in at once. The second is the desire theory: a life goes well to the extent that the person gets what he wants. It explains the refusal neatly. I want to write a novel, and in the tank I would only believe that I had. Its weakness is that people want things that do them no good, and can be pleased by the fulfillment of desires that were planted in them. The third view holds that some things are good for a person whether or not he wants them or enjoys them: knowledge, friendship, achievement, the exercise of his abilities. Philosophers call it the objective list theory, and its weakness is the question of who writes the list.

關於「什麼使一個人生對過這個人生的人來說是好的」，哲學家給過種種答案；這台機器把它們分了類。第一種是享樂論：好的人生就是快樂最多、痛苦最少的人生，此外沒有任何東西本身算數。這是最簡單的看法，也是這台機器被設計來為難的看法，因為一位享樂論者應該立刻接上去。第二種是欲望論：一個人得到他想要的東西到什麼程度，他的人生就好到什麼程度。它俐落地解釋了那份拒絕。我想要的是寫出一部小說；在水槽裡，我只會「相信」自己寫了。它的弱點是：人會想要對自己毫無益處的東西，也可能因為被植入的欲望得到滿足而高興。第三種看法主張：有些東西對一個人是好的，不管他想不想要、享不享受：知識、友誼、成就、能力的發揮。哲學家稱之為「客觀清單論」；而它的弱點是這個問題：清單由誰來寫？

03The garden 花園

Hedonism has had one great defender who was no friend of luxury. Epicurus taught in a garden outside Athens around 300 BCE, and his name has become a word for fine dining through a misunderstanding he tried to prevent. “When we say, then, that pleasure is the end and aim, we do not mean the pleasures of the prodigal or the pleasures of sensuality, as we are understood to do by some through ignorance, prejudice, or willful misrepresentation. By pleasure we mean the absence of pain in the body and of trouble in the soul.” The pleasant life is produced by “sober reasoning,” which examines every desire and drives out the beliefs that disturb us. He sorted desires into three kinds. Some are natural and necessary, like the desire for food and shelter, and these are easily met. Some are natural and unnecessary, like the desire for fine food. Some are neither, like the desires for fame and power, and these can never be satisfied, since they have no limit. Happiness comes from wanting what is enough.

享樂論有過一位偉大的辯護者，而他並不是奢華的朋友。伊比鳩魯在西元前 300 年前後，在雅典城外的一座花園裡教學；由於一個他曾經設法防止的誤解，他的名字後來成了「講究美食」的代名詞。「所以，當我們說快樂是目的與目標的時候，我們指的並不是揮霍者的快樂或聲色的快樂；有些人出於無知、偏見或故意的曲解，以為我們是這個意思。我們所說的快樂，是身體沒有痛苦、靈魂沒有擾動。」愉快的生活是由「清醒的推理」產生的；它檢視每一個欲望，並且趕走那些擾亂我們的信念。他把欲望分成三種。有些是自然而必要的，像對食物與遮蔽的欲望；這些很容易滿足。有些是自然卻不必要的，像對美食的欲望。有些兩者都不是，像對名聲與權力的欲望；這些永遠無法滿足，因為它們沒有限度。幸福來自「想要的是足夠的東西」。

04Activity, and luck 活動，與運氣

Aristotle would have refused the machine without hesitation, and his reason is the root of the third theory. Happiness, for him, is something we do. It is “activity of soul in accordance with virtue,” over a complete life, and a person asleep or inactive is no more happy than a harp that is never played is making music. The man in the tank does nothing. Aristotle also saw a consequence that gentler philosophers have tried to avoid. If happiness is a life of excellent activity, it needs equipment: health, some money, friends, a city at peace. A good person who suffers the fate of King Priam, who saw his sons killed and his city burned, cannot be called happy. So happiness is partly a matter of luck, and he discusses the old saying of Solon that we must “see the end” before calling anyone happy.

亞里斯多德會毫不猶豫地拒絕那台機器；而他的理由，就是第三種理論的根。對他來說，幸福是我們「做」的事。它是「靈魂合乎德行的活動」，而且要在完整的一生當中；一個睡著的人或無所作為的人並不幸福，就像一把從來沒有人彈的豎琴並沒有在演奏音樂。水槽裡的那個人什麼也沒做。亞里斯多德還看到了一個比較溫和的哲學家們設法迴避的後果。如果幸福是卓越活動的一生，它就需要配備：健康、一些錢、朋友、一座太平的城邦。一個好人，如果遭遇了普里阿摩斯王那樣的命運（眼看著兒子們被殺、城被焚毀），就不能被稱為幸福。所以，幸福有一部分是運氣的事；而他討論了梭倫那句古老的話：在說任何人幸福之前，我們必須「看到結局」。

05The alley and the highest joy 陋巷與至樂

The Chinese tradition has a famous case that tests Aristotle’s claim about equipment. Confucius said of his favorite student: “How admirable Hui is! A bamboo dish of rice, a gourd of water, a mean alley. Others could not bear the misery, and Hui does not let it change his joy.” Later Confucians made a standing question of it: what was it that Yan Hui delighted in? The answer could not be poverty, which no one enjoys. It was something that poverty could not reach, and they took it to be the delight of a person whose desires and whose sense of what is right had become one. This is a happiness that needs little luck.

中國的傳統有一個著名的案例，檢驗了亞里斯多德關於「配備」的主張。孔子這樣說他最鍾愛的學生：「賢哉回也！一簞食，一瓢飲，在陋巷，人不堪其憂，回也不改其樂。」後來的儒者把它變成了一個常設的問題：顏回樂的是什麼？答案不可能是貧窮；沒有人享受貧窮。那是貧窮搆不到的某樣東西；他們認為，那是一個人的欲望與他對「什麼是對的」的感知已經合而為一的時候，所擁有的那份悅樂。這是一種不太需要運氣的幸福。

Zhuangzi goes further and doubts the pursuit itself. He looks at what the world calls happiness, wealth, rank, long life, good food, and at the people rushing after these things “as though they could not stop,” and says that he cannot tell whether their happiness is happiness or not. His own conclusion is a paradox: “The highest happiness is without happiness.” The joy that is sought and held is already anxious. What remains when the seeking stops has no name that the seekers would recognize. Mill discovered the same thing in his breakdown: “Ask yourself whether you are happy, and you cease to be so.”

莊子走得更遠，懷疑起這份追求本身。他看著世人所謂的幸福（富、貴、壽、美食），看著那些追逐這些東西的人「誙誙然如將不得已」，說他分不出他們的樂到底是樂還是不樂。他自己的結論是一個弔詭：「至樂無樂。」被追求、被握住的那種樂，已經是焦慮的了。追求停下來之後剩下的東西，沒有一個追求者認得出來的名字。彌爾在他的崩潰裡發現了同一件事：「問問自己是否幸福，你就不再幸福了。」

06What the measurements say 測量說了什麼

Psychologists now measure happiness by asking large numbers of people how they feel and how satisfied they are with their lives, and several findings bear on the old theories. People adapt. Those who win lotteries and those who suffer serious injuries both return, within a few years, much of the way to where they were, a pattern known as the hedonic treadmill. Epicurus would have expected it. Income raises reported happiness steeply among the poor and less and less above a moderate level. The two measures come apart: a parent of small children often reports less pleasure hour by hour and more satisfaction with life as a whole. And the longest study of adult lives, which has followed a group of Harvard graduates and Boston men since 1938, found that the best predictor of a healthy and contented old age was the quality of a person’s close relationships. No one in the study was in a tank.

心理學家現在測量幸福的方式，是去問為數眾多的人：你感覺如何？你對自己的人生有多滿意？有幾項發現與古老的理論相關。人會適應。中了彩券的人與受了重傷的人，都會在幾年之內，回到離原來不遠的地方；這個樣式被稱為「享樂跑步機」。伊比鳩魯會預料到它。收入在窮人當中使自陳的幸福急遽上升；超過一個中等的水準之後，就越來越少。那兩種量度會脫鉤：幼兒的父母，往往每一個小時回報的快樂較少，對整個人生的滿意卻較多。而關於成人生活歷時最久的那項研究（從 1938 年起追蹤一群哈佛畢業生與波士頓的男性），發現健康而滿足的晚年最好的預測指標，是一個人親密關係的品質。研究裡沒有任何人待在水槽裡。

The machine remains a good test because it separates what we feel from what is so. A reader who would refuse it has learned that he wants more from life than good feelings. A reader who would accept it holds a view with a long and honorable history. Either way it is worth doing the experiment slowly.

那台機器仍然是一個好的檢驗，因為它把「我們感覺到的」與「實際如此的」分開了。一位會拒絕它的讀者，已經得知自己想從人生得到的不只是好的感覺。一位會接受它的讀者，持有的是一個歷史悠久而可敬的看法。不管是哪一種，這個實驗都值得慢慢地做。

Thought experiment · 思想實驗

Try it: the experience machine 親手做一次：體驗機

First choose the life you would have inside. Then answer four questions. At the end you will see what, besides feeling good, your answers show that you care about.

先選出你在裡面要過的人生。然後回答四個問題。最後你會看到：除了感覺好之外，你的答案顯示你還在乎什麼。

Primary text · 原典

In his own words 原典怎麼說

One of three letters by Epicurus that survive, preserved by a biographer five centuries later. It is three pages long and contains his whole ethics.

伊比鳩魯流傳下來的三封信之一，由五個世紀之後的一位傳記作者保存下來。它只有三頁長，卻包含了他整套的倫理學。

Did he really say that? · 這句話真的是他說的嗎？

“Eat, drink, and be merry, for tomorrow we die.” (Epicurus)「吃吧，喝吧，快活吧，因為明天我們就要死了。」（伊比鳩魯）

The Bible, quoting his opponents’ caricature · 出自聖經，引的是他的對手對他的醜化

Epicurus said nothing of the kind, and the letter quoted above rejects “drinking-bouts and merrymaking” by name. The sentence is stitched together from two verses of the Bible. Ecclesiastes commends eating, drinking, and being merry, and Isaiah, repeated by Paul, has “Let us eat and drink, for tomorrow we die,” as the motto of people without hope. It was attached to the Epicureans by ancient critics who found it easier to attack a party of gluttons than a community that lived on bread and water and counted a pot of cheese a feast.

伊比鳩魯沒有說過這種話；上面引的那封信，還指名拒絕了「飲宴與作樂」。這句話是由聖經的兩節經文拼接而成的。《傳道書》稱許吃、喝與快樂；《以賽亞書》（保羅引用過）則有「我們吃喝吧，因為明天要死了」，那是沒有盼望的人的座右銘。把它安到伊比鳩魯學派頭上的，是古代的批評者；他們發現，攻擊一群饕客，比攻擊一個靠麵包與水過活、把一罐乳酪當成盛宴的團體容易得多。

Both sides · 正反兩面

Objections and replies 反駁與回應

Objection 1 · 反駁

Happiness is subjective. If a person feels happy, he is happy, and no philosopher can tell him otherwise.

幸福是主觀的。一個人覺得幸福，他就是幸福的；沒有哪位哲學家可以說他不是。

Reply · 回應

This is true of the feeling and less clearly true of the thing we wish for our children. Consider a man whose wife and friends secretly despise him and who never finds out. He feels as happy as a man whose wife and friends love him. Most people judge that the second man’s life is going better, and would choose it for themselves. If so, how a life is going can differ from how it seems to the one living it.

就那個「感覺」而言，這是真的；就我們希望自己孩子擁有的那個東西而言，就沒有那麼清楚了。設想一個人，他的妻子與朋友暗地裡瞧不起他，而他始終沒有發現。他覺得自己跟一個被妻子與朋友所愛的人一樣幸福。多數人判斷後者的人生過得比較好，並且會為自己選擇那一個。若是如此，一個人生過得怎樣，就可能不同於它對過這個人生的人來說看起來怎樣。

Objection 2 · 反駁

The objective list is arrogance. Who is anyone to say that knowledge or friendship is good for a person who does not want it?

客觀清單是傲慢。誰有資格說，知識或友誼對一個不想要它的人是好的？

Reply · 回應

The worry is fair, and lists have been used to push people around. But the theory does not say that anything should be forced on anyone. It says that a person can be mistaken about what would be good for him, which everyone accepts about his own past. We all remember wanting things that would have harmed us. The list is a summary of what people who have tried many ways of living tend to report, and it can be revised as the reports come in.

這個憂慮是合理的；清單確實曾被用來擺布人。但這個理論並沒有說任何東西應該被強加在任何人身上。它說的是：一個人可能弄錯什麼對自己是好的；而對於自己的過去，每個人都接受這一點。我們都記得自己曾經想要過會傷害自己的東西。這份清單，是那些試過許多種生活方式的人往往會回報的東西的摘要；隨著回報陸續進來，它可以修訂。

Objection 3 · 反駁

Seeking happiness is selfish. A good person thinks about duty and about others, and lets happiness look after itself.

追求幸福是自私的。好人想的是義務、是別人，讓幸福自己照顧自己。

Reply · 回應

Kant held something like this, and Mill’s crisis taught him that happiness pursued directly escapes. Both points can be granted without giving up the subject. To ask what makes a life go well is necessary for anyone who cares about others, since we cannot benefit them without some idea of what a benefit is. And on the best ancient accounts, Aristotle’s and the Confucian, the happy life is the life of a person who is good to others. The two questions were asked together for most of history.

康德主張過類似的看法；而彌爾的危機教會了他：直接去追求的幸福會溜走。這兩點都可以承認，而不必放棄這個題目。對任何一個在乎別人的人來說，問「什麼使人生過得好」是必要的；因為如果對「什麼算是益處」毫無概念，我們就無法使別人受益。而照古代最好的說法（亞里斯多德的與儒家的），幸福的人生，就是一個善待他人的人的人生。在歷史上大部分的時間裡，這兩個問題是一起問的。

Terms of art · 哲學術語

The vocabulary of the subject 這門學問的用語

well-being福祉

How well a life is going for the person whose life it is.

一個人生對過這個人生的人來說，過得有多好。

hedonism享樂論

Gk. hēdonē ‘pleasure’

The view that well-being consists in pleasure and the absence of pain.

主張福祉就在於快樂與沒有痛苦。

desire theory欲望論

The view that well-being consists in getting what one wants.

主張福祉就在於得到自己想要的。

objective list theory客觀清單論

The view that certain things are good for a person whether or not he wants or enjoys them.

主張有些東西對一個人是好的，不管他想不想要、享不享受。

ataraxia不動心、寧靜

Gk. a- ‘not’ + tarachē ‘disturbance’

Freedom from mental disturbance, the Epicurean goal.

心靈不受擾動；伊比鳩魯學派的目標。

eudaimonia幸福、興旺

Gk.

Aristotle’s term for a life of excellent activity. A way of living, not a feeling.

Dharma and the West · 佛法與西方哲學

A view from the Dharma 佛法怎麼看

☸

The Buddha ranks kinds of happiness in an ascending order, and the order is the reverse of what the machine offers. Lowest is the pleasure of the senses, which is real, brief, and bound up with the fear of losing it. Above it is the happiness of a mind that has given up such pleasures and become collected in meditation, which the texts describe as a joy that drenches the body as water soaks a ball of bath powder. Above that is the peace of a mind that no longer clings. The machine supplies the first kind without limit and cannot supply the others, since they are states of the person and not contents of experience. Epicurus, who placed tranquility above excitement, is the Western philosopher who saw this most clearly.

佛陀把各種樂排成一個上升的次第；而這個次第，與那台機器所提供的正好相反。最低的是感官之樂；它是真實的、短暫的，並且與「怕失去它」的恐懼綁在一起。在它之上，是捨離了這類快樂、在禪定中收攝起來的心的樂；經典形容那種喜悅浸潤全身，就像水浸透一團澡粉。再往上，是一顆不再執取的心的安穩。那台機器可以無限量地供應第一種，卻供應不了其餘的；因為它們是「人」的狀態，而不是經驗的內容。伊比鳩魯把寧靜放在刺激之上；他是把這一點看得最清楚的西方哲學家。

The tradition then makes a claim that goes beyond him. Sāriputta once declared, “Extinguishment is bliss!” A monk objected: “What’s blissful about it, since nothing is felt?” He answered: “The fact that nothing is felt is precisely what’s blissful about it.” Every feeling, however pleasant, is something that has arisen and will pass, and to depend on it is to be exposed. The Dhammapada puts the ranking in a line: contentment is the greatest wealth, and nibbāna “the ultimate happiness.” Whether a happiness that is not a feeling can be called happiness at all is the point at which Zhuangzi’s paradox, “the highest happiness is without happiness,” and the Buddha’s meet (Dharma and the West, D6 and D22).

接著，這個傳統提出了一個超出他的主張。舍利弗有一次宣告：「涅槃是樂！」一位比丘提出異議：「既然那裡沒有任何感受，有什麼樂可言？」他回答：「正是沒有任何感受，才是其中的樂。」每一個感受，不管多麼愉悅，都是已生起、將消逝的東西；依賴它，就是暴露在危險之中。《法句經》用一句話說出了這個排序：知足是最大的財富，涅槃是「最上的樂」。一種「不是感受」的樂，到底還能不能叫作樂？莊子的弔詭「至樂無樂」與佛陀的說法，就在這一點上相會（佛法與西方哲學 D6、D22）。
