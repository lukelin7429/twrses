Philosophers · 哲學家 · Greece and Rome

Life · 生平

A life in three scenes 三幕人生

A citizen of Athens 雅典的公民

Socrates was born around 470 BCE, when Athens was becoming the richest and most confident city in Greece. His father, Sophroniscus, is said to have worked in stone; his mother, Phaenarete, was a midwife, a fact Plato later turned into a metaphor. He was not rich and not handsome. His friends compared him to a satyr: snub nose, bulging eyes, the same old cloak in every season, and no shoes.

蘇格拉底大約生於西元前 470 年，當時雅典正要成為希臘最富有、最有自信的城邦。據說他的父親索弗羅尼斯科斯是石匠；母親費納瑞特是接生婆，這件事後來被柏拉圖變成一個比喻。他不富有，也不好看。朋友拿他比作森林裡的薩提爾：塌鼻、凸眼，一年四季同一件舊斗篷，而且不穿鞋。

He served as a heavy infantryman in three campaigns of the long war with Sparta, at Potidaea, Delium, and Amphipolis. The young aristocrat Alcibiades, whose life he saved at Potidaea, reported that Socrates endured cold and hunger better than anyone and walked off the lost field at Delium so calmly that no enemy cared to approach him.

在對斯巴達的長年戰爭中，他以重裝步兵的身分參加過三場戰役：波提狄亞、德里昂、安菲波利斯。年輕貴族阿爾基比亞德在波提狄亞被他救過一命；據他說，蘇格拉底比誰都耐寒耐餓，而德里昂兵敗撤退時，他走得那樣從容，敵人沒有一個想靠近。

The oracle and the marketplace 神諭與市集

At his trial Socrates explained how his odd career began. His friend Chaerephon had asked the oracle at Delphi whether anyone was wiser than Socrates, and the priestess had answered that no one was. Socrates, aware of having “no wisdom, small or great,” set out to refute the god by finding a wiser man. He questioned politicians, poets, and craftsmen, and found that each believed he knew things he did not. He concluded that the oracle meant something modest: he was wiser only in not mistaking his ignorance for knowledge.

蘇格拉底在法庭上解釋過，他那古怪的生涯是怎麼開始的。他的朋友凱勒豐去德爾菲問神諭：有沒有人比蘇格拉底更有智慧？女祭司回答說沒有。蘇格拉底自知「大小智慧都沒有」，就動身去找一個更有智慧的人，想藉此反駁神。他問過政治家、詩人、工匠，發現每一個都以為自己知道一些其實不知道的事。他的結論是，神諭的意思很平實：他比較有智慧，只在於他沒有把自己的無知誤認為知識。

From then on he spent his days in the agora and the gymnasia, questioning anyone who would stop. He charged no fee, which set him apart from the professional teachers called sophists, and he denied that he taught anything at all. He compared himself to a gadfly sent to sting a large and sluggish horse, and to a midwife who helps others deliver ideas while having none of her own.

從那以後，他整天待在市集與體育場，逢人就問，只要對方肯停下來。他不收學費，這一點使他跟那些稱為「智者」的職業教師不同；他甚至否認自己教過任何東西。他把自己比作一隻牛虻，被派來叮一匹龐大而遲鈍的馬；又比作接生婆，幫別人把想法生出來，自己卻沒有孩子。

Trial and death 審判與死亡

Athens lost the war in 404 BCE and endured a brutal oligarchy, the Thirty, several of whose leaders had once kept company with Socrates. Socrates himself had defied them: ordered to arrest an innocent man, Leon of Salamis, he simply went home. Democracy was restored the next year, and in 399 three citizens, Meletus, Anytus, and Lycon, brought him to court. The charge, in Plato’s wording, was that he “corrupts the youth” and “does not believe in the gods of the state, but has other new divinities of his own.”

西元前 404 年雅典戰敗，接著經歷了殘暴的寡頭統治「三十人僭主」，其中幾位領袖從前跟蘇格拉底來往過。蘇格拉底自己倒是違抗過他們：他們命令他去逮捕一個無辜的人，薩拉米斯的勒翁，他直接回家了。第二年民主政體恢復；西元前 399 年，美勒托斯、安尼托斯、呂孔三位公民把他告上法庭。照柏拉圖的記載，罪名是他「敗壞青年」，並且「不信城邦所信的神，另立自己的新神」。

A jury of several hundred citizens found him guilty by a narrow margin; had thirty votes gone the other way, he says, he would have been acquitted. Asked to propose his own penalty, he suggested free meals at public expense, then a small fine. The jury chose death. He refused a chance to escape, arguing that a citizen who has accepted the laws all his life may not break them when they turn against him, and a month later he drank the hemlock among his friends. In Plato’s account his last words were a reminder to pay a debt: “Crito, we owe a cock to Asclepius.”

由數百位公民組成的陪審團以些微差距判他有罪；他說，只要有三十票投向另一邊，他就無罪了。輪到他自己提議刑罰時，他先提議由公家供他免費用餐，後來才改口說可以繳一筆小額罰金。陪審團選了死刑。他拒絕了逃亡的機會，理由是：一個公民一輩子都接受法律，就不能在法律轉而對他不利的時候違反它。一個月後，他在朋友圍繞之下喝了毒芹汁。照柏拉圖的記述，他最後一句話是提醒朋友還一筆債：「克里托，我們還欠阿斯克勒庇俄斯一隻公雞。」

The central idea · 核心觀念

Knowing what you do not know 知道自己不知道

Before Socrates, Greek thinkers had mostly asked what the world is made of. Socrates asked how to live, and he asked it in a new way. He assumed that anyone who is good at something can say what he is doing. A doctor can explain health; a brave man ought to be able to explain courage. When the explanation collapses under questioning, the person’s confidence has been shown to rest on nothing, and the search can start.

在蘇格拉底之前，希臘思想家問的多半是世界由什麼構成。蘇格拉底問的是人該怎麼活，而且用一種新的方式問。他假定：一個人真的擅長一件事，就說得出自己在做什麼。醫生能解釋健康；勇敢的人應該能解釋勇敢。當這個解釋在追問之下垮掉，就表示這個人的自信沒有根據，而尋找可以開始了。

Behind the method lies a bold thesis, usually called Socratic intellectualism: virtue is a kind of knowledge. If you really knew what was good for you, you would do it; so no one does wrong willingly, and all wrongdoing is a form of ignorance. That is why examining beliefs was, for him, a moral task and not an academic one.

這套方法背後有一個大膽的主張，通常稱為蘇格拉底的「主知主義」：德行是一種知識。如果你真的知道什麼對自己是好的，你就會去做；所以沒有人是自願作惡的，一切過錯都是某種無知。這就是為什麼對他來說，省察信念是道德的工作，不是學院的工作。

The strongest objection · 最強的反駁

Weakness of will 意志薄弱

If virtue is knowledge, it should be impossible to know what is best and do otherwise. Yet this seems to happen every day: the smoker knows, the procrastinator knows. The Greeks called it akrasia, lack of self-mastery. Aristotle, who admired Socrates, said bluntly that the Socratic view is at odds with what we plainly observe (Nicomachean Ethics VII.2).

如果德行就是知識，那麼「明知什麼最好卻不去做」應該是不可能的。可是這種事似乎天天發生：抽菸的人知道，拖延的人也知道。希臘人稱之為 akrasia，缺乏自制。亞里斯多德很敬佩蘇格拉底，卻直截了當地說，蘇格拉底的看法跟我們明明看到的事實不合（《尼各馬可倫理學》卷七第二章）。

A Socratic can reply that the smoker does not fully know: at the moment of choice the nearer pleasure looks larger than it is, the way a nearby object looks bigger than a distant one, and what he lacks is an art of measuring (Protagoras 356c–357b). Whether that reply rescues the thesis or merely redefines “knowledge” is still disputed.

蘇格拉底這一邊可以回答：抽菸的人並不是「完全知道」。在選擇的當下，近處的快樂看起來比實際大，就像近的東西看起來比遠的大；他缺的是一門「衡量的技藝」（《普羅塔哥拉篇》356c–357b）。這個回答究竟救回了原來的主張，還是只是把「知識」重新定義了一次，至今仍有爭論。

How we know · 史料

The Socratic problem 蘇格拉底問題

Because Socrates wrote nothing, everything we know comes through others, and they do not agree. Aristophanes, writing while Socrates was alive, shows a ridiculous charlatan in a basket. Xenophon shows a sensible moral adviser. Plato shows a philosophical genius, and he keeps using “Socrates” as a character for decades, long after the views expressed have become Plato’s own. Scholars usually treat the early, short dialogues that end in aporia (Apology, Euthyphro, Laches) as closest to the historical man, and admit that this is a judgment and not a certainty.

因為蘇格拉底什麼都沒寫，我們知道的一切都是經由別人，而這些人的說法並不一致。阿里斯托芬在蘇格拉底還活著的時候寫劇本，劇中的他是個吊在籃子裡的可笑騙子。色諾芬筆下的他，是個通情達理的道德顧問。柏拉圖筆下的他是哲學天才；而且柏拉圖把「蘇格拉底」這個角色用了幾十年，用到後來，角色口中的見解早已是柏拉圖自己的。學者通常把早期那些以困惑收場的短篇對話錄（《申辯篇》《歐緒弗洛篇》《拉凱斯篇》）視為最接近歷史上的那個人，同時也承認這是一種判斷，不是定論。

Did he really say that? · 這句話真的是他說的嗎？

“The only true wisdom is in knowing you know nothing.”「唯一真正的智慧，是知道自己一無所知。」

A paraphrase · 後人的改寫

No ancient source has Socrates say this. It paraphrases Apology 21d and 23a–b, where he interprets the oracle to mean that human wisdom is worth little, and that the wisest person is the one who, like Socrates, recognizes this.

沒有任何古代文獻記載蘇格拉底說過這句話。它是《申辯篇》21d 與 23a–b 的改寫；在那裡，他把神諭解釋為：人的智慧沒什麼價值，而最有智慧的，是像蘇格拉底這樣看出這一點的人。

Dharma and the West · 佛法與西方哲學

Socrates and the Buddha 蘇格拉底與佛陀

☸

The two were near contemporaries and never heard of each other. Both wrote nothing, taught by conversation, took no fee, and were remembered through dialogues composed by followers. Both held that the root of going wrong is a kind of ignorance: for Socrates, not knowing what is good; for the Buddha, avijjā, not seeing things as they are.

這兩個人的年代相去不遠，彼此從未聽過對方。兩人都沒有著作，都用談話教人，都不收費，也都靠弟子編寫的對話被後人記得。兩人都認為，人走錯路的根源是某種無知：在蘇格拉底是不知道什麼是善；在佛陀是無明（avijjā），沒有如實看見事物。

The difference shows in the remedy. For Socrates the cure is argument, and he seems to think that seeing the truth is enough to act on it. The Buddhist path adds training of attention and conduct, on the view that craving does not dissolve merely because one has reached a correct conclusion. That is, in effect, a Buddhist answer to the problem of akrasia.

差別在藥方。蘇格拉底的藥是論證，而且他似乎認為，看見了真理就足以照著去做。佛教的道路多了對注意力與行為的訓練（戒與定），因為它認為，貪愛不會只因為人得出了正確的結論就消散。這其實就是佛教對「意志薄弱」問題的回答。

Terms of art · 哲學術語

Words that come with him 跟著他一起來的詞

sophist智者

Gk. sophistēs ‘expert’

A paid traveling teacher of rhetoric and public skill in fifth-century Greece.

西元前五世紀希臘收費授課、教授修辭與公共事務能力的巡迴教師。

Socratic irony蘇格拉底式反諷

Gk. eirōneia ‘feigned ignorance’

His habit of professing ignorance and praising the other speaker’s expertise before testing it.

他在檢驗對方之前，先自稱無知、稱讚對方內行的習慣。

maieutic產婆術的

Gk. maieutikos ‘of midwifery’

Describing teaching that draws ideas out of the learner by questions (Theaetetus 149a–151d).

形容用提問把想法從學習者身上引出來的教法（《泰阿泰德篇》149a–151d）。

akrasia意志薄弱、不能自制

Gk. a- ‘without’ + kratos ‘power’

Acting against one’s own better judgment.

違背自己較好的判斷去行動。

daimonion靈機、神靈的聲音

Gk. ‘divine something’

The inner sign Socrates said warned him against certain actions; it only ever said no.

蘇格拉底說會警告他別做某些事的內在徵兆；它只會說「不」。

Socratic problem蘇格拉底問題

The difficulty of recovering the historical Socrates from conflicting sources.

從彼此衝突的史料中還原歷史上的蘇格拉底，這件事的困難。
