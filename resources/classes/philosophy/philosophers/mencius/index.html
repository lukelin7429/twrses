Philosophers · 哲學家 · China

Mencius 孟子 · 孟軻 Meng Ke

The most eloquent of Confucius’s heirs, who told kings to their faces that the people matter more than the ruler, and who rested the whole of morality on a feeling anyone can find in himself.
孔子的繼承者當中最雄辯的一位；他當著國君的面說，人民比君主重要，並且把整個道德，建立在任何人都能在自己身上找到的一種感受上。

Life · 生平

A life in three scenes 三幕人生

A century after the Master 夫子身後一百年

Mencius was born about a hundred years after Confucius died, in Zou, a day’s walk from the Master’s home. Sima Qian gives him one paragraph. He studied with the followers of Zisi, the grandson of Confucius, and when he had mastered the Way he went to serve King Xuan of Qi, who could not use him, and then King Hui of Liang, who thought his advice far-fetched and remote from actual affairs. The times explain why. Qin was growing strong under the Legalist reforms of Lord Shang. The other states were hiring generals. Everyone was busy with alliances and conquest, the historian says, and Meng Ke went about expounding the virtue of the ancient sage kings. Wherever he went he did not fit.

孟子大約在孔子去世一百年後出生，生在鄒，離夫子的家鄉只有一天的路程。司馬遷只給了他一段。他受業於子思（孔子的孫子）的門人；「道既通」，去事奉齊宣王，宣王不能用他；又到了梁國，梁惠王認為他的建議「迂遠而闊於事情」。時代說明了為什麼。秦國靠著商鞅的法家改革日漸強大。其他各國都在延攬將領。這位史家說，天下正忙於合縱連橫、以攻伐為能事，而孟軻卻到處陳述唐、虞、三代之德，「是以所如者不合」。

Later tradition filled the silence about his youth with stories of his mother. She is said to have moved house three times, away from a cemetery and then from a market, until she found a home beside a school, because the boy imitated whatever he saw. When he came home early from his lessons she cut through the cloth on her loom, to show him what it is to abandon work half done. The stories were written down some three centuries after his death and cannot be checked. They made her the model of a parent in East Asia, and they fit a philosopher who taught that character is a matter of what one is given to grow in.

後來的傳統，用他母親的故事填補了關於他少年時代的空白。據說她搬了三次家：先是離開墓地旁，再離開市場旁，直到在學校旁邊找到一個家，因為這個男孩看到什麼就學什麼。他有一次提早從學堂回來，她就把織布機上的布割斷，讓他看看半途而廢是怎麼回事。這些故事是在他死後大約三個世紀才被寫下來的，無從查證。它們使她成為東亞父母的典範；而它們也很適合這樣一位哲學家：他教導，品格取決於一個人被放在什麼樣的環境裡生長。

Speaking to kings 對國君說話

His book opens with an audience. King Hui of Liang greets him: “Venerable sir, you have not counted a thousand miles too far to come. You must have something that will profit my state?” Mencius replies: “Why must Your Majesty speak of profit? There is humaneness and rightness, and that is all.” If the king asks how to profit his state, his ministers will ask how to profit their houses and the people how to profit themselves, and the state will be torn apart. The exchange sets the tone for everything that follows. He lectured rulers as a tutor lectures a slow pupil. He told one that he did not love his people, another that he was leading beasts to devour men.

他的書以一場晉見開頭。梁惠王迎接他：「叟！不遠千里而來，亦將有以利吾國乎？」孟子回答：「王！何必曰利？亦有仁義而已矣。」如果國君問怎麼利我的國，大夫就會問怎麼利我的家，百姓就會問怎麼利我自己，國家就會被撕裂。這段交談為後面的一切定了調。他訓誡國君，就像家庭教師訓誡一個遲鈍的學生。他告訴一位國君，他並不愛他的人民；告訴另一位，他是在率領野獸吃人。

His political claims were the boldest of the age. “The people are the most important. The altars of soil and grain come next. The ruler is the least.” A king asked him whether it was true that the founder of the Zhou had killed his sovereign, the tyrant Zhou of Shang, and whether a subject may do such a thing. Mencius answered that one who outrages humaneness and rightness is a mere fellow. “I have heard of the punishment of the fellow Zhou. I have not heard of the killing of a ruler.” Eighteen centuries later the first Ming emperor, reading such passages, had Mencius’s tablet removed from the Confucian temple and ordered an edition with eighty-five sections cut out.

他的政治主張是那個時代最大膽的。「民為貴，社稷次之，君為輕。」有位國君問他：周的開國者殺了自己的君主（商的暴君紂），這是真的嗎？臣子可以做這種事嗎？孟子回答：賊仁賊義的人，只是個「一夫」。「聞誅一夫紂矣，未聞弒君也。」十八個世紀之後，明朝的開國皇帝讀到這類段落，下令把孟子的牌位從孔廟撤掉，並且命人編了一個刪去八十五章的本子。

Retirement and the book 退隱與著書

He spent some twenty years at the courts, honored and unheeded, and resigned his post in Qi when the king ignored his advice about a war. A disciple told him that outsiders all called him fond of arguing. “How should I be fond of arguing?” he said. “I have no choice.” The teachings of Yang Zhu, who would not pluck out one hair to benefit the world, and of Mozi, who would love all equally, filled the land, and if no one refuted them the way of Confucius would not be seen. He went home to Zou, and with his student Wan Zhang and others set in order the Odes and Documents and composed the seven books that bear his name.

他在各國的朝廷上度過了大約二十年，受尊敬而不被採納；齊王不理會他對一場戰爭的建議，他就辭去了在齊國的職位。有弟子告訴他，外面的人都說他好辯。他說：「予豈好辯哉？予不得已也。」楊朱（不肯拔一根毛來利天下）與墨翟（主張兼愛）的學說充斥天下；如果沒有人駁斥它們，孔子之道就無法彰顯。他回到了鄒，與學生萬章等人整理《詩》《書》，並且寫成了以他為名的七篇。

For a thousand years the book was one Confucian text among many, and Xunzi was at least as much read. Its rise came in the Song dynasty, when Zhu Xi placed it among the Four Books that every candidate for office had to master. From then until 1905 it was memorized by every educated person in China, and Mencius was known as the Second Sage.

有一千年之久，這本書只是眾多儒家文獻當中的一種；荀子被閱讀的程度至少不亞於他。它的地位上升是在宋代；朱熹把它列入《四書》，成為每一位求取功名的人必須精通的書。從那時起直到 1905 年，中國每一個受過教育的人都背過它，而孟子被稱為「亞聖」。

The central idea · 核心觀念

The heart that cannot bear 不忍人之心

Confucius had said what a good person does and left open why anyone should be able to do it. Mencius supplied the foundation. Every human being, he held, has a heart that cannot bear the suffering of others. His proof is the child at the well, and from it he draws a list of four beginnings: the feeling of compassion is the sprout of humaneness, the feeling of shame the sprout of rightness, the feeling of deference the sprout of propriety, and the sense of right and wrong the sprout of wisdom. People have these four as they have four limbs. Moral cultivation is the growth of what is already there, and it must be neither neglected nor forced. He tells of a farmer in Song who thought his grain was growing too slowly and went out and pulled up each stalk a little. He came home exhausted and pleased, and his son ran to the field and found the plants withered.

孔子說了好人做什麼，卻沒有說明為什麼任何人都做得到。孟子補上了地基。他主張，每一個人都有「不忍人之心」。他的證明是井邊的那個孩子；由此他列出了四個開端：惻隱之心是仁之端，羞惡之心是義之端，辭讓之心是禮之端，是非之心是智之端。人有這四端，就像有四肢一樣。道德的修養，是讓本來就在那裡的東西生長；既不可以疏於照料，也不可以強求。他講過宋國一個農夫的故事：那人嫌自己的禾苗長得太慢，就到田裡去把每一株往上拔一點。他疲憊而得意地回到家；他的兒子跑到田裡一看，苗都枯了。

From this psychology he derives a politics. A ruler has the same heart as anyone else, and good government is that heart extended: take what you feel for your own old people and children and extend it to the old people and children of others. A government that leaves the people without a steady livelihood and then punishes them for crime is setting a trap for them. And a state so governed would be irresistible, because people everywhere would wish to live in it. He also describes what the cultivated person gains. By long accumulation of right action one develops a “flood-like energy,” vast and unyielding, that fills the space between heaven and earth. Such a person cannot be corrupted by wealth, moved by poverty, or bent by force. And he will know what to choose when the choice is hard: “Fish is what I want, and bear’s paw is also what I want. If I cannot have both, I give up the fish and take the bear’s paw. Life is what I want, and rightness is also what I want. If I cannot have both, I give up life and take rightness.”

從這套心理學，他引出了一套政治學。君主的心跟任何人的心一樣；而仁政就是把這顆心推出去：「老吾老，以及人之老；幼吾幼，以及人之幼。」一個讓人民沒有穩定生計、然後再因犯罪而懲罰他們的政府，是在給他們設陷阱。而一個這樣治理的國家會無敵於天下，因為各地的人都會想住到那裡去。他也描述了修養有成的人得到什麼。靠著長久積累合於義的行為，人會養成「浩然之氣」，至大至剛，充塞於天地之間。這樣的人，富貴不能使他墮落，貧賤不能使他動搖，威武不能使他屈服。而當抉擇很難的時候，他知道該選什麼：「魚，我所欲也；熊掌，亦我所欲也。二者不可得兼，舍魚而取熊掌者也。生，亦我所欲也；義，亦我所欲也。二者不可得兼，舍生而取義者也。」

The strongest objection · 最強的反駁

Xunzi’s reply 荀子的回答

The sharpest critic was a fellow Confucian. Xunzi, who was born about the time Mencius died, wrote that Mencius did not understand the difference between nature and deliberate effort. What is inborn needs no learning: the eye sees and the ear hears without being taught. Courtesy and rightness have to be learned by everyone, with labor, from teachers. So they are not inborn. If people were good by nature, he adds, there would have been no need for sage kings or ritual, and the fact that every society has laws and punishments shows what its members would do without them. The passing pang at the well proves little. A feeling that leads nowhere unless it is trained is hardly the root of morality. The training is.

最犀利的批評者是一位儒家同道。荀子大約在孟子去世前後出生；他寫道，孟子不懂「性」與「偽」的分別。天生的東西不必學：眼睛會看、耳朵會聽，都不用教。禮義卻是每個人都得費力向老師學的。所以它們不是天生的。他又說：如果人本性是善的，就不需要聖王與禮義了；而每一個社會都有法與刑，這個事實就顯示了，沒有它們的時候成員們會做什麼。井邊那一閃而過的不忍，證明不了多少。一個若不經訓練就哪裡也到不了的感受，很難算是道德的根。訓練才是。

A second objection concerns his politics. He promised the kings that humane government would make them invincible, and the state that actually united China a few decades after his death was Qin, the harshest of them. His defenders say that the Qin empire lasted fifteen years and the dynasties built on Confucian principles lasted centuries. A third objection is about method. He argues by analogy, with water, sprouts, and mountains, and an analogy can always be met with another. Gaozi’s images were as good as his. He won the debates in his own book, which he wrote.

第二個反駁針對他的政治學。他向國君們保證，仁政會使他們無敵；而在他死後幾十年真正統一中國的，是其中最嚴酷的秦。為他辯護的人說：秦帝國只維持了十五年；建立在儒家原則上的各個王朝，則延續了好幾個世紀。第三個反駁關乎方法。他靠類比來論證：水、嫩芽、山；而一個類比，永遠可以用另一個類比來回應。告子的意象並不比他的差。他在自己的書裡贏了那些辯論，而那本書是他寫的。

How we know · 史料

A book with one voice 一本只有一個聲音的書

Unlike the Analects, the Mencius reads as the work of one mind. Its passages are long, its arguments are developed, and the same images recur. Sima Qian says that Mencius composed it himself with his students after retiring, and most scholars accept that it was at least put together under his eye or soon after his death. That makes it a better witness to its author’s thought than we have for almost any other early Chinese thinker. It also means that we hear his opponents only as he reports them. Gaozi, Yang Zhu, and the followers of Mozi are given a few sentences each and are then refuted, and of Gaozi nothing survives anywhere else. For his life there is little beyond the book: the paragraph in the Records and the stories of his mother, which first appear in the Han dynasty.

跟《論語》不同，《孟子》讀起來像是出自一個人的心思。它的段落長，論證有所展開，同樣的意象一再出現。司馬遷說，孟子退隱之後與學生一起親自撰作了它；多數學者接受它至少是在他眼前、或他死後不久編成的。這使它比我們為幾乎任何其他早期中國思想家所擁有的文獻，都更能見證作者的思想。這也意味著：他的對手，我們只聽得到他轉述的版本。告子、楊朱與墨者，各自只得到幾句話，然後就被駁倒了；而關於告子，別處什麼也沒有留下。至於他的生平，除了這本書之外所知甚少：《史記》裡的那一段，以及他母親的故事，而後者最早見於漢代。

Did he really say that? · 這句話真的是他說的嗎？

“The great man is one who does not lose the heart of a newborn child.” (Mencius)「大人者，不失其赤子之心者也。」（孟子）

Authentic, and often misread · 是真的，卻常被誤讀

The sentence is Mencius 4B12, and it is quoted today in praise of people who stay playful and innocent. Mencius did not admire childishness. The “heart of a newborn” is the unspoiled moral responsiveness he believed everyone starts with, the heart that cannot bear to see suffering. The great man is the one who has kept that sprout alive through a lifetime of dealings with the world and has grown it into something a child could never have. The line just before it in his book says that the great man’s words need not be kept to the letter, since he goes where rightness is.

這句話是《孟子・離婁下》第十二章；今天它被引來稱讚那些保持童心與天真的人。孟子並不欣賞孩子氣。「赤子之心」是他相信每個人起初都有的、未受污損的道德感應力，也就是那顆不忍看見苦難的心。「大人」，是在一輩子與世界打交道的過程中讓那株嫩芽活了下來、並且把它養成了孩子絕不可能擁有的東西的那個人。在他的書裡，緊接在這句話前面的那一句說：「大人者，言不必信，行不必果，惟義所在。」

Dharma and the West · 佛法與西方哲學

Mencius and the Buddha 孟子與佛陀

☸

When the teaching that all beings have Buddha-nature reached China in the fifth century, Chinese readers had been prepared for it by Mencius for seven hundred years. They already believed that the capacity for sagehood is present in everyone, that it is covered over and not destroyed by bad conditions, and that cultivation uncovers what was there. The Chan saying that one should see one’s nature and become a Buddha is in a Mencian key, and so is the Sixth Patriarch’s exclamation that our own nature is “originally pure.” Some historians think that Buddha-nature thought flourished in China, and remained marginal in India, largely for this reason. The debt was repaid. The Neo-Confucians who made Mencius canonical in the Song had learned from Buddhists to speak of the mind’s original nature.

「一切眾生皆有佛性」的教導在五世紀傳到中國的時候，中國的讀者已經被孟子預備了七百年。他們早已相信：成聖的能力人人都有；它是被惡劣的條件遮蓋，而不是被毀掉；修養所做的，是讓本來就在的東西顯露出來。禪宗「見性成佛」的說法，是孟子的調子；六祖驚嘆「何期自性，本自清淨」，也是。有些史家認為，佛性思想之所以在中國興盛、在印度卻始終處於邊緣，主要就是這個原因。這筆債後來還了回去。宋代把孟子奉為經典的理學家，是從佛教徒那裡學會談「心之本體」的。

The two differ in what the inborn endowment is. For Mencius it is a set of moral feelings, and their natural growth is into the virtues of family and state. For the Buddhist it is the mind’s capacity to awaken, which is found in animals as well as people, and its fulfillment lies beyond every social role. Mencius’s extension of love moves outward by degrees, from one’s own parents to other people’s, and he attacked Mozi for loving all alike. The bodhisattva’s compassion makes no such distinction. And Mencius never doubts that there is a self to cultivate. He would have been puzzled to hear that the heart which cannot bear suffering has no owner (Dharma and the West, D25).

兩者的差別，在於那份天生的稟賦是什麼。對孟子來說，它是一組道德情感；它們自然的生長方向，是家與國的種種德行。對佛教徒來說，它是心的覺悟能力，在動物身上跟在人身上一樣找得到；而它的圓成，在一切社會角色之外。孟子的「推恩」是一層一層往外推的，從自己的父母到別人的父母；他並且攻擊墨子的兼愛。菩薩的悲心不作這樣的分別。還有，孟子從來不懷疑有一個「我」可以修養。如果聽到有人說那顆不忍人之心並沒有主人，他會感到困惑（佛法與西方哲學 D25）。

Reading check · 理解測驗

Did it land? 讀懂了嗎

1. What did Mencius add to the teaching of Confucius?
