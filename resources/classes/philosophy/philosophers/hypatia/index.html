Philosophers · 哲學家 · Greece and Rome

Hypatia 希帕提亞 · Ὑπατία

The leading mathematician and philosopher of Alexandria in her day, teacher of pagans and Christians alike, killed by a mob in a quarrel between a bishop and a governor, and remade by every later age to suit itself.
她那個時代亞歷山卓首屈一指的數學家與哲學家；異教徒與基督徒都是她的學生。她在一位主教與一位總督的爭鬥當中被暴民殺害；而後來的每一個時代，都照著自己的需要把她重新塑造了一遍。

Life · 生平

A life in three scenes 三幕人生

Theon’s daughter 席昂的女兒

Hypatia was born in Alexandria in the second half of the fourth century. The exact year is not known. Her father Theon was the last recorded member of the Museum, the city’s old institute of learning, and is remembered for the edition of Euclid’s Elements from which nearly all later copies descend. He taught his daughter mathematics and astronomy, and ancient writers agree that she surpassed him. She never married.

希帕提亞在四世紀的後半葉生於亞歷山卓。確切的年份不詳。她的父親席昂，是有紀錄可查的最後一位「繆斯館」成員；繆斯館是這座城市古老的學術機構。後人記得他，是因為他校訂了歐幾里得的《幾何原本》；後來幾乎所有的抄本，都源自他的那個版本。他教女兒數學與天文學；而古代的作者一致認為，她超過了他。她終身未婚。

The mathematics of her time was the work of preserving and explaining. The great discoveries of Euclid, Apollonius, and Ptolemy were five to seven hundred years old, and they survived because teachers like Theon and Hypatia corrected the texts, wrote commentaries, and taught them to students. She wrote on Diophantus’s Arithmetic and on Apollonius’s study of conic sections. Neither commentary survives under her name. The heading of her father’s commentary on Book III of Ptolemy’s Almagest says that the text was revised “by my daughter Hypatia, the philosopher,” and many scholars think that the version of that book we read is hers.

她那個時代的數學，是保存與講解的工作。歐幾里得、阿波羅尼奧斯與托勒密的偉大發現，已經是五到七百年前的事了；它們之所以流傳下來，是因為有席昂與希帕提亞這樣的老師校正文本、撰寫註釋，並且把它們教給學生。她為丟番圖的《算術》與阿波羅尼奧斯的圓錐曲線研究寫過註釋。這兩部註釋都沒有以她的名字流傳下來。她父親為托勒密《天文學大成》卷三所寫的註釋，標題說這個文本是「由我的女兒、哲學家希帕提亞」校訂的；許多學者認為，我們今天讀到的那一卷就是她的版本。

The teacher 老師

By about 390 she was the head of a school of her own. She taught the philosophy of Plato as interpreted by Plotinus, in which the study of mathematics and the heavens is a training of the soul for the knowledge of higher things. The church historian Socrates, a Christian writing about twenty-five years after her death, says that she “made such attainments in literature and science, as to far surpass all the philosophers of her own time,” and that students came to her from a distance. She spoke in public before the magistrates, and “all men on account of her extraordinary dignity and virtue admired her the more.”

到了大約 390 年，她已經主持著自己的學校。她教的是經由普羅提諾詮釋的柏拉圖哲學；在這套哲學裡，研習數學與天體，是訓練靈魂去認識更高的事物。教會史家蘇格拉底是一位基督徒，在她死後大約二十五年寫作；他說，她「在文學與科學上造詣之高，遠遠超過了她那個時代所有的哲學家」，學生從遠方來向她求教。她在官員面前公開發言；而「所有的人都因為她非凡的莊重與德行，而更加敬佩她」。

We know her teaching best from the letters of one student, Synesius of Cyrene, who later became a bishop and went on writing to her as “mother, sister, teacher.” He asks her advice on his books and on a hydrometer he wants made, and tells her that he has designed an astrolabe with her help. Her students included Christians and pagans, and several rose to high office. In a city where the two faiths fought in the streets, her lecture room was one of the few places where they sat together.

我們對她的教學了解得最清楚的來源，是一位學生的書信：昔蘭尼的敘內修斯。他後來成了主教，卻繼續寫信給她，稱她「母親、姊妹、老師」。他向她請教自己的著作，也請教一件他想請人製作的比重計；他還告訴她，他在她的幫助之下設計了一件星盤。她的學生有基督徒，也有異教徒；其中好幾位後來當上了高官。在一座兩種信仰在街頭鬥毆的城市裡，她的講堂是少數幾個他們坐在一起的地方之一。

March 415 415 年 3 月

In 412 Cyril became bishop of Alexandria and set about enlarging the power of his office. He was opposed by Orestes, the imperial governor, who was himself a Christian. Their quarrel turned violent. Monks loyal to the bishop attacked the governor in the street, and one of them was tortured to death on the governor’s order. Hypatia was known to be among the governor’s advisers. A rumor spread among Cyril’s supporters, Socrates says, “that it was she who prevented Orestes from being reconciled to the bishop.”

412 年，區利羅成為亞歷山卓的主教，並且著手擴張這個職位的權力。反對他的是皇帝派來的總督奧瑞斯特斯；總督自己也是基督徒。兩人的爭執演變成了暴力。效忠主教的修士在街上攻擊了總督；其中一人在總督的命令下被拷打至死。人們知道希帕提亞是總督的顧問之一。蘇格拉底說，區利羅的支持者當中流傳著一個謠言：「阻撓奧瑞斯特斯與主教和解的，就是她。」

In March 415, during Lent, a crowd led by a church reader named Peter stopped her carriage as she was returning home. They dragged her to a church, stripped her, and killed her with roof tiles or shards. They tore her body apart and burned the pieces. Socrates, who had no love of pagans, wrote that the act “brought not the least opprobrium” on Cyril and on the whole church of Alexandria, and that “nothing can be farther from the spirit of Christianity.” No one was punished. Orestes disappears from the record soon afterward, and Cyril went on to become one of the most powerful churchmen of the century and, later, a saint.

415 年 3 月，正值大齋期；一群人在一位名叫彼得的教會讀經員帶領之下，攔住了她回家的馬車。他們把她拖到一座教堂，剝光她的衣服，用屋瓦或碎陶片把她殺死。他們撕裂她的屍體，把碎塊燒掉。蘇格拉底對異教徒並無好感；而他寫道，這件事給區利羅與整個亞歷山卓教會「帶來了不小的恥辱」，並且「沒有什麼比這離基督教的精神更遠了」。沒有人受到懲罰。不久之後，奧瑞斯特斯從紀錄裡消失；區利羅後來成為那個世紀最有權勢的教會人物之一，並且在日後被封為聖人。

The central idea · 核心觀念

Mathematics as a way upward 數學是向上的路

No philosophical work by Hypatia survives, and what she taught has to be gathered from her school and her students. She was a Platonist of the school of Plotinus. In that tradition the world we see is the lowest level of a reality that descends from a single source, the One, through intellect and soul. The aim of philosophy is to turn the soul back toward its source, and the turn begins with mathematics. Numbers and figures are grasped by the mind and not by the senses. They do not change, and they are the same for everyone. A student who learns to love them has taken a first step away from appetite and opinion.

希帕提亞沒有任何哲學著作流傳下來；她教了什麼，必須從她的學派與她的學生那裡去拼湊。她是普羅提諾一系的柏拉圖主義者。在那個傳統裡，我們所見的世界，是實在的最低一層；實在從單一的源頭「太一」，經由理智與靈魂，一層一層降下來。哲學的目標，是使靈魂轉回它的源頭；而這個轉向是從數學開始的。數與形，是由心智、而不是由感官掌握的。它們不變，而且對每個人都一樣。一個學會了愛它們的學生，就踏出了離開欲望與意見的第一步。

Astronomy came next, as the place where mathematics meets the visible world. To calculate an eclipse is to find order behind appearances, and for a Platonist that was a religious exercise as well as a scientific one. This explains what can look like a puzzle in her career, that a philosopher spent her days correcting tables and building instruments. It also explains why Christians could study with her. Her philosophy asked for discipline of mind and conduct and for a reverence toward the source of things. It did not ask them to sacrifice to the old gods. Synesius kept a good deal of what she taught him when he became a bishop.

接下來是天文學；它是數學與可見世界相遇的地方。計算一次日食，就是在現象的背後找到秩序；而對一位柏拉圖主義者來說，那既是科學的練習，也是宗教的練習。這解釋了她的生涯裡一件看起來像謎的事：一位哲學家，把日子花在校正星表與製作儀器上。它也解釋了為什麼基督徒可以向她求學。她的哲學要求心智與行為上的紀律，以及對萬物之源的敬畏。它並不要求他們向舊神獻祭。敘內修斯當上主教之後，保留了她教給他的東西當中的不少。

The strongest objection · 最強的反駁

A commentator, and no more? 只是一位註釋者？

Hypatia is often described as a great scientist whose death ended ancient learning. The evidence does not support so large a claim. No discovery is credited to her by any ancient source. She did not invent the astrolabe or the hydrometer, both of which were older. Her works were commentaries and editions, the ordinary scholarly labor of her age. Alexandrian mathematics and philosophy went on after her for two more centuries. If the measure of a thinker is new results, she has none that we can point to.

人們常把希帕提亞描述成一位偉大的科學家，說她的死終結了古代的學術。證據並不支持這麼大的主張。沒有任何古代的資料，把任何一項發現歸功於她。星盤與比重計都不是她發明的；兩者都更早。她的著作是註釋與校訂本，是她那個時代尋常的學術勞動。在她之後，亞歷山卓的數學與哲學又延續了兩個世紀。如果衡量一位思想家的標準是新的成果，那麼我們指不出她有任何一項。

The objection applies the wrong measure. In a period when books were copied by hand and schools were closing, a science survived only if someone kept the texts correct and trained people to read them. The Almagest that reached the astronomers of Baghdad, and through them Copernicus, had passed through the hands of Theon and probably of Hypatia. In Kuhn’s terms she was a master of normal science, and normal science is what carries a tradition across the centuries until someone is in a position to overturn it. Her contemporaries, who knew what she had and had not done, called her the best philosopher of the age.

這個反駁用錯了尺度。在一個書籍靠手抄、學校一間間關閉的時期，一門科學只有在「有人保持文本正確、並且訓練人去讀它們」的情況下，才能存活。傳到巴格達的天文學家手上、再經由他們傳到哥白尼手上的《天文學大成》，是經過席昂之手的，很可能也經過了希帕提亞之手。用孔恩的話說，她是常態科學的大師；而常態科學，就是把一個傳統帶過好幾個世紀的東西，直到有人有條件去推翻它。她同時代的人知道她做了什麼、沒做什麼；而他們稱她為那個時代最好的哲學家。

How we know · 史料

Every age its own Hypatia 每個時代都有自己的希帕提亞

We have three early sources. Socrates the church historian respects her and blames the mob. The pagan philosopher Damascius, writing a century later, uses her death against the Christians and makes Cyril directly responsible. The Coptic bishop John of Nikiu, writing in the seventh century, calls her a witch who beguiled the city with astrolabes and instruments of music, and reports her killing with approval. Each is telling the story for a purpose.

我們有三份早期的資料。教會史家蘇格拉底尊敬她，把責任歸給暴民。異教哲學家達馬斯基烏斯在一個世紀之後寫作；他用她的死來攻擊基督徒，並且讓區利羅直接負責。科普特主教尼基烏的約翰在七世紀寫作；他說她是一個女巫，用星盤與樂器迷惑了全城，並且贊許地記下了她的被殺。每一位講這個故事，都有他的目的。

Later ages did the same. In the eighteenth century Voltaire and others made her a martyr of reason murdered by priests. A Victorian novel made her a beautiful young woman and the last of the Greeks, though she was probably about sixty. The twentieth century made her a symbol of women in science. A recent film connects her death with the burning of the Library of Alexandria, which had ceased to exist long before she was born, and shows her discovering that the planets move in ellipses, for which there is no evidence at all. What the sources support is plainer. A learned woman who advised the governor was killed by partisans of the bishop in a struggle over who should rule the city. Her science and her religion were not the cause. Her death is best understood as a political killing.

後來的時代也做了同樣的事。十八世紀，伏爾泰等人把她變成了一位被教士謀殺的理性殉道者。一部維多利亞時代的小說，把她寫成一位美麗的年輕女子、最後的希臘人；而她當時大概已經六十歲上下了。二十世紀把她變成了女性從事科學的象徵。近年有一部電影，把她的死與亞歷山卓圖書館的焚毀連在一起，而那座圖書館在她出生之前很久就已經不存在了；電影還演她發現了行星沿橢圓運行，而這件事完全沒有證據。資料所支持的，要樸素得多。一位為總督提供意見的博學女性，在一場「誰來統治這座城市」的鬥爭裡，被主教的黨羽殺害了。她的科學與她的宗教都不是原因。她的死，最好理解為一樁政治謀殺。

Did he really say that? · 這句話真的是他說的嗎？

“Reserve your right to think, for even to think wrongly is better than not to think at all.” (Hypatia)「保留你思考的權利；因為就算想錯了，也比完全不想好。」（希帕提亞）

Invented in 1908 · 是 1908 年編出來的

This and several other sayings, among them “To teach superstitions as truths is a most terrible thing,” appear on posters and in books of quotations under Hypatia’s name. Not one word of her own writing survives, and no ancient author quotes her on these subjects. The sayings come from an essay published in 1908 by the American writer Elbert Hubbard, who composed a popular series of imaginary visits to the homes of great teachers and supplied his subjects with suitable remarks. The one authentic anecdote that gives her words is in Damascius, and it is of a different kind. She rebuked a student who had fallen in love with her by telling him that what he loved was a body and not beauty itself. The misattributed sayings express the outlook of 1908. Her own was that of a late Platonist, for whom thinking was a discipline directed at a truth that does not depend on us.

這一句，以及另外幾句（其中有「把迷信當作真理來教，是最可怕的事」），頂著希帕提亞的名字，出現在海報與名言集裡。她自己寫的東西，連一個字也沒有流傳下來；而沒有任何古代作者，在這些題目上引述過她的話。這些句子出自美國作家埃爾伯特・哈伯德 1908 年發表的一篇文章；他寫了一個很受歡迎的系列，想像自己造訪偉大教師們的家，並且為筆下的人物配上合適的言論。唯一一則記有她的話、而且可信的軼事，在達馬斯基烏斯的書裡；而它是另一種類型。有個學生愛上了她；她斥責他，告訴他：他愛的是一具身體，而不是美本身。那些被誤植的句子，表達的是 1908 年的觀點。她自己的觀點，是一位晚期柏拉圖主義者的：對她來說，思考是一種紀律，指向一個不取決於我們的真理。

Dharma and the West · 佛法與西方哲學

Hypatia and the Buddha 希帕提亞與佛陀

☸

The Platonism that Hypatia taught has more in common with Buddhist practice than its vocabulary suggests. Both treat philosophy as a training and not only as a set of doctrines. Both begin with conduct and restraint of the senses, go on to the steadying of attention, and aim at an insight that changes the person. The one story that preserves her words shows her using a method the Buddhist texts know well. To a student infatuated with her body she pointed out what a body is, in order to turn his mind toward something that does not decay. The contemplation of the body’s unattractive aspects serves the same purpose in the monastic training.

希帕提亞所教的柏拉圖主義，與佛教修行的共通之處，比它的用語所顯示的要多。兩者都把哲學當作一種訓練，而不只是一套教義。兩者都從行為與守護感官開始，接著使注意力穩定下來，而以一種改變人的洞見為目標。唯一保存了她的話的那個故事，顯示她用了一個佛教經典很熟悉的方法。對一個迷戀她身體的學生，她指出身體是什麼，為的是使他的心轉向某種不會朽壞的東西。在僧團的訓練裡，觀察身體不可愛的一面，起的是同樣的作用。

The destination differs. For the Platonist the soul rises toward an eternal and unchanging reality, and mathematics is prized because its objects never change. The Buddha taught that nothing whatever is permanent, the knower included, and that release comes from seeing this. There is also a difference in how each tradition met the kind of violence that killed her. Her school had no answer to a mob. The Buddha’s community met hostile kings and rival sects many times, and its rule was to give no pretext: to stay out of the quarrels of the powerful, to accept support from every side, and to leave a place where the teaching could not be heard. It is a hard rule, and Hypatia, who chose to advise a governor, lived by a different one. One may admire her choice and still see what it cost (Dharma and Western Philosophy D18).

終點則不同。對柏拉圖主義者來說，靈魂升向一個永恆不變的實在；而數學之所以被珍視，是因為它的對象永不改變。佛陀教導：沒有任何東西是常住的，連能知者也不例外；而解脫來自看清這一點。此外，面對殺死她的那種暴力，兩個傳統的回應方式也不同。她的學派對暴民沒有對策。佛陀的僧團，多次遇到懷有敵意的國王與對立的教派；而它的規矩是不給人藉口：不捲入有權勢者的爭執，接受各方的供養，並且離開教法無法被聽見的地方。這是一條艱難的規矩；而選擇為總督出謀劃策的希帕提亞，是照著另一條規矩生活的。我們可以敬佩她的選擇，同時也看見它的代價（佛法與西方哲學 D18）。

Terms of art · 哲學術語

Words that come with her 跟著她一起來的詞

Neoplatonism新柏拉圖主義

The form of Plato’s philosophy developed by Plotinus in the third century, in which all reality flows from the One.

普羅提諾在三世紀發展出來的柏拉圖哲學形式；其中一切實在都從「太一」流出。

commentary註釋

L. commentarius

A work that explains an earlier text passage by passage. The main form of scholarship in late antiquity.

逐段解釋一部較早文本的著作。古代晚期學術的主要形式。

Almagest《天文學大成》

Ar. al-majisṭī ‘the greatest’

Ptolemy’s treatise on astronomy, the standard work for fourteen centuries.

托勒密的天文學專著；十四個世紀裡的標準著作。

astrolabe星盤

Gk. astrolabos ‘star-taker’

An instrument for measuring the height of stars and solving problems of time and position.

一種儀器，用來測量星體的高度，並解決時間與位置的問題。

conic sections圓錐曲線

The curves made by slicing a cone: circle, ellipse, parabola, hyperbola.

切割圓錐所得的曲線：圓、橢圓、拋物線、雙曲線。

prefect總督

L. praefectus

The Roman governor of a province, here of Egypt.

羅馬一個行省的長官；這裡指埃及的。
