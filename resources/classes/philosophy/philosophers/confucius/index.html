Philosophers · 哲學家 · China

Confucius 孔子 · 孔丘 Kong Qiu

A failed official and wandering teacher who said he invented nothing, and whose conversations became the education of East Asia for two thousand years.
一個失意的官員與周遊的老師；他說自己什麼也沒有創作，而他的談話成了東亞兩千年的教育。

Life · 生平

A life in three scenes 三幕人生

A poor boy who loved the old ways 愛好古道的貧寒少年

Confucius was born in 551 BCE in Lu, a small state in what is now Shandong. The Zhou kings, who had ruled for five centuries, still reigned in name, and real power had passed to a dozen states that fought one another and were themselves torn by their great families. His father, an old soldier, died when he was a small child, and he grew up poor. He later summed up his life in six steps: “At fifteen I set my heart on learning. At thirty I stood firm. At forty I had no doubts. At fifty I knew the decree of Heaven. At sixty my ear was attuned. At seventy I could follow what my heart desired without overstepping the line.”

孔子西元前 551 年生於魯國，一個位於今天山東的小國。已經統治了五個世紀的周王，名義上仍然在位；實權則已落入十幾個諸侯國手中，它們彼此攻伐，各自又被國內的大家族撕裂。他的父親是一位老武士，在他年幼時就去世了；他在貧寒中長大。他後來用六步總結了自己的一生：「吾十有五而志于學，三十而立，四十而不惑，五十而知天命，六十而耳順，七十而從心所欲，不踰矩。」

What he set his heart on was the culture of the early Zhou: its rites, its music, its odes and records. He believed that the founders of the dynasty had known how human beings should live together, and that the violence of his own day came from forgetting it. He described himself as “a transmitter and not a maker, one who trusts and loves the ancients.” In his thirties he began to take students, and he seems to have been the first person in China to make a living by teaching. He took anyone who came. “From the man who brought a bundle of dried meat upward,” he said, “I have never refused instruction to anyone.”

他立志去學的，是周初的文化：它的禮、它的樂、它的詩與典冊。他相信，這個王朝的開創者懂得人應該怎樣共同生活；而他自己那個時代的暴力，來自把這些忘掉了。他形容自己是「述而不作，信而好古」。三十多歲時，他開始收學生；他似乎是中國第一個以教書為生的人。來者不拒。他說：「自行束脩以上，吾未嘗無誨焉。」

Fourteen years on the road 路上的十四年

He wanted office, since he held that a good man’s place is in government. Around the age of fifty he was given a post in Lu and rose, tradition says, to be minister of justice. It did not last. Finding that the duke would not listen, he left the state in 497 with a band of students and spent fourteen years traveling from court to court in search of a ruler who would put his ideas into practice. None would. He was threatened, surrounded, and once nearly starved. At the gate of one city he became separated from his students, and a local man told them he had seen a stranger standing there who looked “like a dog from a house in mourning,” with no one to feed him. When this was repeated to Confucius he laughed and said, “That is it exactly.”

他想做官，因為他主張好人的位置在政府裡。大約五十歲時，他在魯國得到一個職位；傳說他升到了大司寇。好景不長。他發現國君不肯聽，就在西元前 497 年帶著一群學生離開了魯國，花了十四年從一個朝廷走到另一個朝廷，尋找一位肯把他的主張付諸實行的君主。沒有一位肯。他被威脅過、被圍困過，有一次差點餓死。在一座城的城門口，他與學生走散了；有個當地人告訴他的學生，看見一個陌生人站在那裡，「纍纍若喪家之狗」。這話轉述給孔子聽，他欣然笑了，說：「然哉！然哉！」

The hermits he met on the road thought him a fool. Two of them, plowing a field, told his student that the whole world was a flood and that it would be wiser to follow those who flee the age than one who merely flees one bad ruler after another. Confucius sighed when he heard it. “One cannot flock with birds and beasts. If I do not associate with these people, with whom should I associate? If the Way prevailed in the world, I would not be trying to change it.” A gatekeeper put the general opinion in one line: is that the man who knows it cannot be done and keeps on doing it?

他在路上遇到的隱士，都認為他是個傻子。其中兩位正在耕田，他們告訴他的學生：天下滔滔，到處都是洪水；與其跟著一個只是躲開一個又一個壞君主的人，不如跟著躲開這整個時代的人。孔子聽了，悵然嘆息：「鳥獸不可與同群！吾非斯人之徒與而誰與？天下有道，丘不與易也。」一位守城門的人，用一句話說出了一般人的看法：「是知其不可而為之者與？」

The return 歸來

He came home to Lu in 484, aged sixty-seven, and gave his last years to teaching and to the old texts. Tradition credits him with editing the Odes and the Documents and with composing the chronicle of Lu, and says that he read the Book of Changes so often that the leather thongs binding its bamboo strips wore through three times. His son died before him, and so did Yan Hui, the student he loved best. He died in 479, believing that he had failed. Some of his students mourned at the grave for three years, and one stayed for six.

西元前 484 年，他回到魯國，六十七歲；他把最後的歲月給了教學與古籍。傳統把刪定《詩》《書》、作《春秋》歸功於他，並且說他讀《易》讀到「韋編三絕」。他的兒子比他先死；他最鍾愛的學生顏回也是。西元前 479 年，他去世了，相信自己失敗了。有些學生在墓旁守喪三年，有一位守了六年。

Four centuries later the historian Sima Qian visited the temple at Qufu, saw the carriage, the robes, and the ritual vessels, and watched the students practicing the rites. He wrote that he lingered and could not bring himself to leave. Kings and worthies beyond number were honored in their day and forgotten at their death. Confucius was a commoner, and after more than ten generations scholars still took him as their master.

四個世紀之後，史家司馬遷造訪了曲阜的孔廟，看了那輛車、那些衣服與禮器，看著儒生們在那裡按時習禮。他寫道，自己「祗回留之不能去」。天下的君王與賢人多得數不清，「當時則榮，沒則已焉」。孔子是一介布衣，「傳十餘世，學者宗之」。

The central idea · 核心觀念

Becoming human through ritual 經由禮而成為人

The word at the center of his teaching is rén, written with the signs for “person” and “two.” It has been translated as benevolence, humaneness, and goodness, and none fits exactly, because it names the whole quality of a person who is as a human being ought to be in relation to others. Asked what it is, he gives a different answer to each student. To one he says, “Love others.” To another he gives the rule he calls reciprocity: “What you do not want for yourself, do not do to others.” To his best student he gives the hardest answer: “To master oneself and return to ritual is rén.” Asked for the details, he adds: do not look at, listen to, say, or do what is contrary to ritual.

他的教導的核心字是「仁」，由「人」與「二」組成。它被譯成 benevolence、humaneness、goodness，沒有一個完全貼切；因為它指稱的，是一個在與他人的關係裡「成為人所應當是的樣子」的人的整體品質。有人問它是什麼，他對每一個學生的回答都不同。對一位他說：「愛人。」對另一位，他給了他稱為「恕」的那條規則：「己所不欲，勿施於人。」對他最好的學生，他給了最難的答案：「克己復禮為仁。」學生請問細目，他補充：「非禮勿視，非禮勿聽，非禮勿言，非禮勿動。」

The pairing of inner goodness with outward ritual is his distinctive idea. By ritual, lǐ, he means the whole body of inherited forms through which people show respect: mourning, greeting, serving a meal, addressing a parent. A modern reader takes these for etiquette. Confucius saw in them the means by which raw feeling is shaped into something humane, as a language shapes thought. A child who is taught to bow to his elders is being taught to notice them. Done without feeling, the forms are empty, and he says so often. Done well, they make a community in which force is rarely needed. His political theory follows directly. Government is a matter of setting people right by example: “Let the ruler be a ruler, the minister a minister, the father a father, the son a son.” The person formed in this way he calls the jūnzǐ, the gentleman, a word that had meant a nobleman by birth and that he redefined as a nobleman by character.

把內在的善與外在的禮配成一對，是他獨特的想法。他所說的「禮」，指的是人們藉以表達敬意的那一整套傳承下來的形式：守喪、問候、奉食、稱呼父母。現代的讀者把這些當成禮節。孔子在其中看到的，是把原始的情感塑造成合乎人道的東西的手段，就像語言塑造思想一樣。一個被教導要向長輩鞠躬的孩子，是在被教導去「注意到」他們。沒有情感而行，這些形式是空的；這一點他常說。行得好，它們造就一個很少需要動用強力的共同體。他的政治理論由此直接推出。為政就是以身作則地使人歸正：「君君，臣臣，父父，子子。」這樣養成的人，他稱為「君子」；這個詞原本指出身上的貴族，而他把它重新界定為品格上的貴族。

The strongest objection · 最強的反駁

Whose order? 誰的秩序？

His first critics were his contemporaries. Mozi, a generation later, attacked the Confucians on two counts. Their elaborate funerals and three years of mourning waste the wealth of the living, and their ethic of graded love, in which one cares most for one’s own family, is the very partiality that causes war. The Daoists said that he was treating the symptom. Preaching benevolence and ritual is what people do when natural harmony has already been lost. The Legalists, who actually built the first empire, said that he was naive. A state cannot be run on the hope that rulers will be sages. It needs clear laws and reliable punishments.

他最早的批評者是他同時代的人。晚他一代的墨子，從兩方面攻擊儒者。他們繁複的葬禮與三年之喪，浪費了生者的財富；而他們那套「愛有差等」的倫理（最關心的是自己的家人），正是造成戰爭的那種偏私。道家說，他治的是標。宣講仁與禮，是人們在自然的和諧已經失去之後才做的事。真正建立了第一個帝國的法家說，他太天真。治國不能寄望於君主會是聖人。它需要明確的法與可靠的刑。

The modern objection is different. For two thousand years his teaching was the official creed of the Chinese state, and the relations he named, ruler and subject, father and son, husband and wife, elder and younger, were used to demand obedience from the lower party in each. The students who marched in Beijing in 1919 blamed the weakness of China on “Confucius and Sons” and called for the shop to be torn down. His defenders answer that he taught duties in both directions and said that a ruler who is not a ruler has lost his claim. That is true of the Analects. It remains a fair question whether an ethic built on roles and deference can protect the person whom the roles oppress, and whether it has room for the kind of right that can be claimed against one’s own family.

現代的反駁不同。有兩千年之久，他的教導是中國這個國家的官方信條；而他所舉出的那些關係（君臣、父子、夫婦、長幼），被用來要求每一對當中居下位的一方服從。1919 年在北京遊行的學生，把中國的積弱歸咎於「孔家店」，喊著要把它打倒。為他辯護的人回答：他教的是雙向的責任，並且說過，一位不像君主的君主，已經失去了他的資格。就《論語》而言，這是真的。然而這仍然是一個合理的問題：一套建立在角色與敬順之上的倫理，保護得了被那些角色壓迫的人嗎？它容得下那種可以向自己的家人主張的「權利」嗎？

How we know · 史料

A book he did not write 一本不是他寫的書

Confucius wrote nothing that we can be sure of. What we have is the Analects, some five hundred short passages in twenty books, most of them beginning “The Master said.” It was put together by his students and their students over a period that may have lasted two centuries, and scholars agree that the books differ in date. The earliest layers are thought to lie in books 3 to 9, and some of the later books show the concerns of schools that formed after his death. The passages have no context and are arranged in no clear order, so that every reader must decide what question a given saying was answering. The biography in Sima Qian’s Records was written about four hundred years after the events, from materials of very uneven value, and includes stories told by his enemies.

我們能夠確定是孔子寫的東西，一樣也沒有。我們有的是《論語》：二十篇裡大約五百則簡短的文字，大多數以「子曰」開頭。它是由他的學生、以及學生的學生編成的，歷時也許長達兩個世紀；學者們一致認為各篇的年代不同。最早的層次被認為在第三到第九篇；較晚的幾篇，有些顯示的是他身後才形成的學派所關切的事。這些文字沒有脈絡，也沒有清楚的排列次序；所以每一位讀者都得自己判定，某一句話當初回答的是什麼問題。司馬遷《史記》裡的傳，寫於事發約四百年之後，所根據的材料價值極不均等，其中還包括他的敵人所講的故事。

There is also the problem of the name. “Confucianism” has meant at different times the teaching of the Analects, the cosmology of the Han court, the metaphysics of the Song philosophers, and a state examination system. He would have recognized perhaps the first. Reading him well means setting aside what was later built in his name and listening to a man in conversation with his students, who is often tired, sometimes funny, and seldom sure that he has succeeded.

還有名稱的問題。「儒家」在不同的時代，指的分別是《論語》的教導、漢代朝廷的宇宙論、宋代哲學家的形上學，以及一套國家考試制度。他大概只認得出第一個。要把他讀好，就得把後來以他的名義建造起來的東西擱在一邊，去聽一個正在跟學生談話的人說話；他常常疲倦，有時很風趣，而很少確定自己成功了。

Did he really say that? · 這句話真的是他說的嗎？

“Choose a job you love, and you will never have to work a day in your life.” (Confucius)「選一份你愛的工作，你這輩子就一天也不必工作。」（孔子）

Not in any Confucian text · 任何儒家文獻裡都沒有

This is among the most widely shared “Confucius” quotations in English, and it has no Chinese original. It cannot be found in the Analects or in any other early text, and it began to be attached to his name in American publications in the late twentieth century. The thought is foreign to him. He did not think of work as something to be escaped, and the word he would have used for what one loves is learning. A genuine saying on the subject runs: “To know it is not as good as to love it, and to love it is not as good as to delight in it.”

這是英文世界裡流傳最廣的「孔子語錄」之一，而它並沒有中文原文。在《論語》或任何其他早期文獻裡都找不到它；它是在二十世紀後期的美國出版品裡，才開始被掛到他名下的。這個想法對他來說是陌生的。他並不把工作看成要逃離的東西；而他會用來稱呼「人所愛的事」的那個詞，是「學」。關於這個題目，一句真正出自他的話是：「知之者不如好之者，好之者不如樂之者。」

Dharma and the West · 佛法與西方哲學

Confucius and the Buddha 孔子與佛陀

☸

The two were close contemporaries, three thousand kilometers apart, and neither knew of the other. They share a practical temper. When a student asked Confucius about serving the spirits and about death, he replied: “You cannot yet serve the living. How can you serve the spirits? You do not yet understand life. How can you understand death?” The Buddha set aside questions about the origin of the world on similar grounds. Both taught that a person is made by training, both distrusted speculation, and both said in almost the same words that one should not do to others what one would not want for oneself. “To master oneself” is an instruction either could have given.

兩人是年代很接近的同時代人，相隔三千公里，誰也不知道對方。他們有同樣的務實性情。有學生問孔子怎麼事奉鬼神、問死是怎麼回事，他回答：「未能事人，焉能事鬼？……未知生，焉知死？」佛陀也基於類似的理由，把關於世界起源的問題擱在一邊。兩人都教導人是由訓練造就的，兩人都不信任玄想，兩人也都用幾乎相同的話說過：自己不想要的，不要加在別人身上。「克己」是兩人都可能下的指示。

They part over the family. For Confucius the bonds of parent and child are the root of all virtue, and to cut them is to cut the root. The Buddha left his wife and infant son to seek awakening, and he founded an order of men and women who had done the same. When Buddhism entered China this was the first and deepest objection to it. A monk shaves his head, produces no heir, and does not bow to his parents or his king, and by Confucian standards each of these is a failure of filial duty. Chinese Buddhists replied for centuries that the monk repays his parents in a greater way by leading them toward liberation, and they gave new prominence to texts on gratitude to parents. The long negotiation produced the characteristically Chinese settlement in which the two teachings, with Daoism, were said to be three ways to one end. Each had its sphere. Confucius governed the family and the state, and the Buddha was consulted on suffering and death, about which the Master had declined to speak (Dharma and the West, D18).

他們在「家」這件事上分道揚鑣。對孔子來說，親子之間的連結是一切德行的根；割斷它，就是割斷那條根。佛陀離開了妻子與襁褓中的兒子去尋求覺悟，並且創立了一個由做了同樣事情的男女組成的僧團。佛教傳入中國時，這是對它最先、也最深的反對。出家人剃髮、不留後嗣、不拜父母與君王；照儒家的標準，每一樣都是不孝。中國的佛教徒有好幾個世紀都這樣回答：出家人是以更大的方式報答父母，引導他們走向解脫；他們並且讓談報父母恩的經典有了新的顯著地位。這場漫長的協商，產生了中國特有的那個安排：這兩種教導，加上道教，被說成是通往同一個目的的三條路。各有各的領域。孔子管家與國；而關於苦與死（夫子不肯談的事），人們去請教佛陀（佛法與西方哲學 D18）。

Reading check · 理解測驗

Did it land? 讀懂了嗎
