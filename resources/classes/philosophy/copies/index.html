A32 · Beauty and Art 美與藝術

Can a Copy Be as Good as the Original? 摹本能跟真跡一樣好嗎？

The most admired piece of Chinese calligraphy has not been seen for thirteen hundred years, and a forger once fooled every expert in Holland. What a copy lacks, if it lacks anything, with Goodman, Benjamin, the Ise shrine, and eight cases for you to judge.
中國最受推崇的一件書法，已經有一千三百年沒有人見過；而曾經有一位偽造者，騙過了荷蘭的每一位專家。摹本如果少了什麼，那到底是什麼？古德曼、班雅明、伊勢神宮，以及八個讓你來判斷的案例。

A32 Big Questions · 大哉問Beauty and Art · 美與藝術Advanced · 高級1,057 words · about 25 min
The big idea · 大觀念

This lesson closes the unit on beauty and art. If the value of a work lay only in how it looks or sounds, a perfect copy would be worth exactly as much as the original. Almost no one believes that it is, and almost no one finds it easy to say why.

這一課是「美與藝術」單元的最後一課。如果一件作品的價值只在於它看起來、聽起來怎麼樣，那麼一件完美的複製品就跟原作恰好一樣有價值。幾乎沒有人相信是這樣；而幾乎沒有人覺得「為什麼不是」很容易說清楚。

The essay · 課文

Can a Copy Be as Good as the Original? 摹本能跟真跡一樣好嗎？

01A masterpiece no one has seen 一件沒有人見過的傑作

In the spring of 353 CE the calligrapher Wang Xizhi met forty-one friends at the Orchid Pavilion near Shaoxing. They floated cups of wine down a stream and wrote poems, and Wang wrote a preface for the collection on the spot, 324 characters in running script. Tradition says that he tried later to write it out again and could not equal the first version. It became the most famous piece of writing in China. Three centuries afterward the Tang emperor Taizong acquired the scroll, had copies made for his ministers, and, according to an account written in the following century, had it sealed in his tomb when he died in 649.

公元 353 年的春天，書法家王羲之與四十一位朋友在紹興附近的蘭亭聚會。他們讓酒杯順著溪水漂流，各自賦詩；王羲之當場為這本詩集寫了一篇序，三百二十四個字，行書。傳說他後來想重寫一遍，卻比不上第一次寫的。它成了中國最出名的一件書蹟。三百年後，唐太宗得到了這個卷子，命人摹了幾本賜給大臣；而據下一個世紀寫成的一則記載，他在 649 年去世的時候，把它封進了自己的陵墓。

No one has seen it since. What every student of calligraphy has studied for thirteen hundred years is a set of copies: tracings made at the Tang court, free copies by the great calligraphers of the day, and rubbings from stone engravings of those. The one thought closest to the original is a tracing attributed to the court copyist Feng Chengsu. The work that sets the standard for the whole art is known only through reproductions, and the tradition has not felt this as a scandal.

從那以後，沒有人再見過它。一千三百年來，每一個學書法的人所學的，是一批摹本：唐代宮廷裡做的雙鉤摹本、當時的大書法家的臨本，以及根據這些刻石再拓下來的拓本。被認為最接近原蹟的一本，是相傳出自宮廷拓書人馮承素之手的摹本。為整門藝術立下標準的那件作品，只能經由複製品被認識；而這個傳統並不覺得這是一樁醜聞。

02The forger who fooled Holland 騙過荷蘭的偽造者

European painting had the opposite experience. In 1937 a newly discovered Vermeer, The Supper at Emmaus, was bought for a Rotterdam museum. Abraham Bredius, the leading authority on Dutch painting, had called it the masterpiece of Vermeer. It had been painted the year before by Han van Meegeren, an artist whom the critics had dismissed. He used old canvas and pigments of the period and hardened his paint with a synthetic resin so that it would pass the tests for age. More “Vermeers” followed, and one was sold during the war to Hermann Göring.

歐洲的繪畫有過相反的經驗。1937 年，一幅新發現的維梅爾〈以馬忤斯的晚餐〉，被一家鹿特丹的博物館買下。荷蘭繪畫的首席權威亞伯拉罕・布雷迪烏斯，稱它是維梅爾的傑作。它是前一年由漢・凡・米格倫畫的；這位畫家曾被評論家看不起。他用了舊畫布與那個時代的顏料，並且用一種合成樹脂使顏料變硬，好通過年代的檢驗。更多的「維梅爾」接著出現；其中一幅在戰爭期間賣給了赫爾曼・戈林。

After the war van Meegeren was arrested for selling a national treasure to the enemy, a crime that could carry the death penalty. He saved himself by confessing to a lesser one. The Vermeer was his own work, he said, and he painted another in custody to prove it. He was sentenced to a year for forgery and died before serving it. The paintings had not changed by a single brushstroke. Their price fell to almost nothing, and the experts who had wept in front of them now found them clumsy. The case raises the question of what, exactly, had changed.

戰後，凡・米格倫因為把國寶賣給敵人而被捕；這項罪名可以判死刑。他招認了一項較輕的罪，救了自己。他說，那幅維梅爾是他自己畫的；為了證明，他在拘留期間又畫了一幅。他因偽造被判一年徒刑，還沒服刑就死了。那些畫一筆也沒有改變。它們的價格跌到幾乎一文不值；而曾經在畫前落淚的專家，現在覺得它們很笨拙。這個案子提出的問題是：到底是什麼變了？

03If you cannot see the difference 如果你看不出差別

One answer is that nothing of aesthetic importance changed. If beauty lies in what can be seen, and two pictures cannot be told apart, they are equally beautiful. The collapse in price shows only that buyers pay for names, rarity, and the right to boast. On this view the experts were right the first time and are snobs now.

一個答案是：在審美上要緊的東西，一點也沒變。如果美在於看得見的東西，而兩幅畫分辨不出來，它們就同樣美。價格的崩跌只顯示了：買家付錢買的是名字、稀有，以及炫耀的權利。照這個看法，專家第一次是對的；現在他們是勢利眼。

Nelson Goodman replied in 1968 that “cannot be told apart” hides a question. Apart by whom, and when? That I cannot see a difference today does not show that I never will. Knowing that one of two pictures is a forgery gives me a reason to look for differences and trains my eye, and once Vermeer’s real paintings and van Meegeren’s were hung side by side the forgeries came to look obviously of the 1930s. Goodman added a distinction that explains why the question arises for some arts and not for others. A novel or a symphony is defined by a text or a score, and any correct copy is the work itself. He called these arts allographic. A painting has no such notation. Its identity depends on the history of its making, on its having come from a particular hand. He called these autographic, and only they can be forged.

尼爾森・古德曼在 1968 年回應說：「分辨不出來」這句話藏著一個問題。誰分辨不出來？什麼時候？我今天看不出差別，並不表示我永遠看不出。知道兩幅畫當中有一幅是偽作，就給了我一個去找差別的理由，也訓練了我的眼睛；而維梅爾的真蹟與凡・米格倫的畫一旦並排掛出來，那些偽作看起來就明顯是 1930 年代的東西了。古德曼還補了一個區分；它解釋了為什麼這個問題在某些藝術裡出現，在另一些藝術裡不出現。一部小說或一首交響曲，是由一份文本或樂譜界定的；任何一份正確的複本，都是那件作品本身。他把這些藝術稱為「他書的」。一幅畫沒有這樣的記譜。它的身分取決於它被製作出來的歷史，取決於它出自某一隻特定的手。他把這些稱為「親筆的」；而只有它們能被偽造。

04Here and now 此時此地

Walter Benjamin, writing in exile in 1935, gave the missing quality a name. Even the most perfect reproduction, he said, lacks one thing: the work’s presence in time and space, its unique existence at the place where it is. He called this its aura. An original has been through things. It was touched by its maker, has aged, changed hands, and hung in particular rooms, and all this history is present to a person who stands before it. Photography and film, he saw, were producing art that has no original at all, since it makes no sense to ask for the authentic print of a negative. Benjamin thought the result a loss and also a liberation. Art that can be copied without limit belongs to everyone and is no longer an object of worship.

華特・班雅明 1935 年在流亡中寫作，為那個缺少的性質取了一個名字。他說，就算是最完美的複製品，也少了一樣東西：作品在時間與空間裡的在場，它在它所在之處獨一無二的存在。他把這叫做作品的「靈光」。一件原作經歷過事情。它被它的創作者碰過，它老了，它換過主人，它掛在過某些特定的房間裡；而這整段歷史，對一個站在它面前的人來說是在場的。他看到，攝影與電影正在產生一種根本沒有原作的藝術；因為問一張底片的「真正的」那一張相片是哪一張，是沒有意義的。班雅明認為這個結果是一種失落，也是一種解放。可以無限複製的藝術，屬於每一個人，不再是膜拜的對象。

A later writer, Denis Dutton, put the point in terms of achievement. A work of art is the trace of something a person did. When we admire the Lanting Preface we admire what Wang Xizhi brought off in one morning, with wine in him, and no chance to correct. A forgery lies about what was achieved. Van Meegeren did not solve the problems Vermeer solved in 1660. He solved the easier problem of imitating the solutions three centuries later, and it is this false claim, more than any visible fault, that makes the forgery worth less.

後來的一位作者丹尼斯・達頓，用「成就」來表達這一點。一件藝術作品，是一個人做過的某件事留下的痕跡。當我們讚賞〈蘭亭序〉的時候，我們讚賞的是王羲之在一個早晨、帶著酒意、沒有機會修改的情況下完成的那件事。偽作在「成就了什麼」上說了謊。凡・米格倫並沒有解決維梅爾在 1660 年解決的那些問題。他解決的是一個比較容易的問題：在三個世紀之後模仿那些解答；而使偽作比較不值錢的，與其說是任何看得見的毛病，不如說是這個不實的宣稱。

05Copying as a way of keeping 以複製來保存

Dutton’s account also explains why the Tang tracings of the Lanting are treasures. They make no false claim. Feng Chengsu’s achievement was fidelity, and everyone knows it. Chinese connoisseurs kept four words apart: to trace, to copy freehand, to work in someone’s manner, and to fabricate. Only the last deceives. Xie He had listed “transmission by copying” as the sixth principle of painting, and every calligrapher still learns by copying models stroke by stroke. In this tradition a good copy is how a work survives the loss of its paper.

達頓的說法也解釋了，為什麼〈蘭亭序〉的唐摹本是珍寶。它們沒有做出任何不實的宣稱。馮承素的成就是「忠實」；而人人都知道這一點。中國的鑑賞家把四個字分得很清楚：摹、臨、仿、造。只有最後一個是欺騙。謝赫把「傳移模寫」列為繪畫的第六法；而每一位書法家至今仍然靠著一筆一筆地臨摹範本來學習。在這個傳統裡，一件好的摹本，是作品在它的紙張失去之後繼續存在的方式。

Japan’s Ise shrine carries the idea further. Its wooden halls have been taken down and rebuilt on the adjoining plot every twenty years for some thirteen centuries. No timber in them is old, and the shrine is counted among the oldest in the country. What is preserved is the form, the craft, and the unbroken act of rebuilding. Whether the same holds for a painting depends on what one thinks a work of art is: an object, a design, or something done. Wang Xizhi ended his preface by imagining its future readers: “Those who come after will look on us as we now look on the past.” He did not expect the paper to last, and it has not. The words and the gesture have reached us through people who cared enough to copy them.

日本的伊勢神宮把這個想法推得更遠。大約一千三百年來，它的木造殿舍每二十年就被拆掉，在相鄰的那塊地上重建一次。殿舍裡沒有一根木料是舊的；而這座神宮被算作全國最古老的神社之一。被保存下來的，是形制、是工藝，以及那個不曾中斷的「重建」的行動。同樣的道理對一幅畫成不成立，取決於一個人認為藝術作品是什麼：一件物品、一個設計，還是一件被做出來的事。王羲之在序的結尾，想像著它未來的讀者：「後之視今，亦由今之視昔。」他並不指望那張紙能留存；而它確實沒有。那些字與那個姿態能傳到我們手上，是經由一些「在乎到願意去摹寫它們」的人。

Thought experiment · 思想實驗

Try it: original or copy? 親手做一次：原作還是複本？

Eight cases. In each, something stands beside its copy. Say whether the original is worth more or whether the two are equal. After each you will see what the philosophers say, and at the end, what your answers assume a work is.

八個案例。每一個裡，都有一樣東西與它的複本並列。請回答：原作比較有價值，還是兩者相等？每答完一題，你會看到哲學家怎麼說；最後會看到，你的答案假定了「作品」是什麼。

Primary text · 原典

In their own words 原典怎麼說

The end of the preface, where Wang thinks about being read by people not yet born, and the earliest account of what became of the scroll.

序的結尾：王羲之想到自己會被還沒出生的人讀到；以及關於那個卷子下落的最早記載。

Wang Xizhi, Preface to the Orchid Pavilion Collection (353 CE), closing lines; Liu Su, “Record of the Orchid Pavilion,” in Fine Tales of Sui and Tang (8th century) 王羲之〈蘭亭集序〉結尾；劉餗〈蘭亭記〉（見《隋唐嘉話》） · Both translated for this series

Did he really say that? · 這句話真的是他說的嗎？

「故宮裡看到的〈蘭亭序〉是王羲之寫的。」「故宮裡看到的〈蘭亭序〉是王羲之寫的。」

No. Every surviving Lanting is a copy, and some have doubted more than that · 不是。傳世的〈蘭亭〉每一件都是摹本；而有人懷疑的還不只這個

No scroll of the Orchid Pavilion Preface in any museum is from Wang Xizhi’s hand. The most admired, known as the Shenlong version from a seal it bears, is a Tang tracing attributed to Feng Chengsu and is kept in the Palace Museum in Beijing. Others are free copies attributed to Yu Shinan and Chu Suiliang, and rubbings from a stone engraving after Ouyang Xun. In fact no certain original of any work by Wang Xizhi survives anywhere. The pieces in Taipei and Tokyo are also early tracings. In 1965 the scholar Guo Moruo went further and argued that the Lanting as we have it could not have been written in the fourth century at all, since inscriptions of that time are still close to clerical script, and that both text and handwriting were produced later. Most specialists rejected his conclusion, and the debate could not be settled, because the evidence that would settle it is in a tomb.

任何一家博物館裡的〈蘭亭序〉卷子，都不是出自王羲之之手。最受推崇的一本，因為上面的一方印而被稱為「神龍本」；它是相傳出自馮承素之手的唐代摹本，藏在北京故宮博物院。其他的，有相傳出自虞世南與褚遂良之手的臨本，以及根據歐陽詢臨本刻石的拓本。事實上，王羲之的任何一件作品，都沒有確定的真蹟存世。台北與東京的那幾件，同樣是早期的摹本。1965 年，學者郭沫若更進一步，主張我們所見的〈蘭亭〉根本不可能寫於四世紀；因為那個時代的碑刻還很接近隸書。他認為文章與書蹟都是後來才產生的。多數專家拒絕了他的結論；而這場爭論無法定案，因為能夠定案的證據在一座陵墓裡。

Both sides · 正反兩面

Objections and replies 反駁與回應

Objection 1 · 反駁

“Aura” is a polite word for superstition. To care which lump of matter Leonardo touched is to treat paintings as relics of saints.

「靈光」是「迷信」的客氣說法。在乎達文西碰過的是哪一塊物質，就是把畫當成聖人的遺物。

Reply · 回應

Benjamin half agreed. He traced aura to the religious use of images and thought its decline a gain for democracy. But the feeling is too widespread to be dismissed. People keep a parent’s handwriting and would not exchange it for a scan. Psychologists find that young children already prefer their own toy to an exact duplicate. Caring about the particular thing with its particular past seems to be part of how humans value, and a theory of art should explain it before correcting it.

班雅明同意了一半。他把靈光追溯到圖像的宗教用途，並且認為它的衰落對民主是一項收穫。但這種感受太普遍了，不能就這樣打發。人們保存父母的筆跡，不肯拿它去換一份掃描檔。心理學家發現，幼兒就已經偏好自己的那個玩具，勝過一個一模一樣的複製品。在乎「那一個帶著它那段過去的特定的東西」，似乎是人類賦予價值的方式的一部分；而一個藝術理論應該先解釋它，再去糾正它。

Objection 2 · 反駁

The Chinese acceptance of copies shows that the whole problem is a product of the European art market.

中國對摹本的接受，顯示了這整個問題是歐洲藝術市場的產物。

Reply · 回應

The market sharpened the problem. It did not create it. Chinese collectors paid vastly more for a genuine hand than for a copy, wrote treatises on telling them apart, and were cheated by forgers in every dynasty. What differs is the place given to the honest copy. In China it was a recognized form with its own masters. In Europe after the Renaissance it was a student exercise. The philosophical question of what a copy lacks is the same in both, and the Chinese answer, that it lacks nothing so long as it does not lie, is one of the better ones.

市場使這個問題變得尖銳。它並沒有創造這個問題。中國的收藏家為真蹟付出的價錢，遠遠高於摹本；他們寫下了辨別真偽的專著；而每一個朝代，他們都被偽造者騙過。不同的地方，是「誠實的摹本」被賦予的位置。在中國，它是一種被承認的形式，有它自己的名家。在文藝復興之後的歐洲，它是學生的習作。「摹本少了什麼」這個哲學問題，在兩邊是同一個；而中國的答案——只要它不說謊，它什麼也不少——是比較好的答案之一。

Objection 3 · 反駁

With digital files the question disappears. Every copy of a photograph or a song is identical, and there is no original.

有了數位檔案，這個問題就消失了。一張照片或一首歌的每一份複本都一模一樣；沒有原作。

Reply · 回應

Benjamin predicted this of film. What has happened since is instructive. People have gone to great lengths to bring originals back: numbered prints, signed copies, and digital certificates of ownership for images that anyone can download. This suggests that the wish for a unique object with a history is strong enough to be manufactured where the medium does not supply it, which counts for Benjamin’s view of what we value and against his hope that we would stop.

班雅明對電影預言過這一點。從那以後發生的事很有啟發性。人們費了很大的力氣要把「原作」找回來：限量編號的版畫、簽名本，以及為任何人都能下載的圖像所發的數位所有權憑證。這顯示：對「一件獨一無二、帶著歷史的物品」的渴望，強烈到在媒材不提供它的地方，會被人製造出來。這支持了班雅明對「我們珍視什麼」的看法；而不利於他對「我們會停止這樣做」的盼望。

Terms of art · 哲學術語

The vocabulary of the subject 這門學問的用語

forgery偽作、贗品

A work presented as being by someone other than its real maker, with intent to deceive.

一件被當成出自他人之手來呈現的作品；意在欺騙。

autographic親筆的（藝術）

Goodman, 1968

Arts in which even the most exact duplicate does not count as genuine, such as painting.

在這類藝術裡，就算是最精確的複製品也不算真品；例如繪畫。

allographic他書的（藝術）

Goodman, 1968

Arts in which any correct copy of the text or score is the work, such as literature and music.

在這類藝術裡，文本或樂譜的任何一份正確複本都是作品本身；例如文學與音樂。

aura靈光

L. ‘breeze, breath’

Benjamin’s term for the unique presence of an original in its own time and place.

班雅明的用語：一件原作在它自己的時間與地點裡獨一無二的在場。

provenance流傳經過、來歷

Fr. provenir ‘to come from’

The record of who has owned a work since it was made.

一件作品從製作完成以來曾經歸誰所有的紀錄。

tracing copy摹本

Ch. 摹 mó

A copy made by tracing the outlines of each stroke and filling them in.

把每一筆的輪廓鉤出來、再填墨而成的複本。

freehand copy臨本

Ch. 臨 lín

A copy made by looking at the model and writing it out with one’s own brush.

看著範本、用自己的筆寫出來的複本。

rubbing拓本

Ch. 拓 tà

An ink impression taken on paper from an inscription cut in stone.

從刻在石頭上的字，用紙與墨拓下來的印本。

  1. Think of an object you own that could be replaced by an identical one, and that you would not want replaced. Explain what you would lose. Then say whether the same reasoning applies to a painting. About 150 words.

    想一樣你擁有的東西：它可以被一件一模一樣的取代，而你不會願意讓它被取代。解釋你會失去什麼。然後說說看，同樣的推理對一幅畫適不適用。約 150 個字。

  2. A student copies a famous work of calligraphy stroke by stroke for a year. Is the result her work, the master’s, or neither? Use the four Chinese terms 摹, 臨, 仿, 造 in your answer.

  3. Benjamin thought that art without aura would belong to everyone. Looking at how images are shared today, argue in 200 words whether he was right.

    班雅明認為，沒有靈光的藝術會屬於每一個人。看看今天圖像被分享的方式，用 200 個字論證他說得對不對。

Dharma and the West · 佛法與西方哲學

A view from the Dharma 佛法怎麼看

☸

The Buddhist tradition began with a decision about originals. The Buddha wrote nothing. After his death the monks met and recited what they remembered, and for some four hundred years the teaching existed only in memory and voice, passed from teacher to student. There was never an autograph, and no one felt the lack of one. When the texts were finally written down, copying them became a practice in its own right. A person who copies a sutra by hand is thought to gain by the attention the copying takes, and the tradition has not valued an old manuscript over a new one for its doctrine. In Goodman’s terms the Dharma is as allographic as anything could be. What counts is that the content has been passed on correctly. The teaching itself warns against attachment to the vehicle. It is a raft for crossing over, and a finger pointing at the moon.

佛教的傳統，是從一個關於「原本」的決定開始的。佛陀什麼也沒寫。他去世之後，比丘們集會，誦出他們所記得的；而大約四百年間，教法只存在於記憶與聲音裡，由老師傳給學生。從來就沒有一份親筆本；而沒有人覺得少了它。等到經典終於被寫下來，抄寫它們本身就成了一種修行。一個人親手抄經，人們認為他從抄寫所需要的專注裡得益；而就教義而言，這個傳統並不把一份舊抄本看得比新抄本更有價值。用古德曼的話來說，佛法是再「他書」不過的了。要緊的是內容被正確地傳了下去。這個教導本身就警告人不要執著於載具。它是渡河的筏，是指著月亮的手指。

The same tradition also knows the pull that Benjamin called aura, and has made careful use of it. Relics, the tree at Bodh Gaya grown from cuttings of cuttings, a robe or a bowl handed down through a line of teachers: all are valued for their history. The Chan image for transmission is one lamp lighting another. The second flame is not the first, and it is not a copy of the first either. The question whether it is the same flame is treated as badly put. This suggests a way of thinking about the Lanting. What Wang Xizhi did in 353 is gone, as every conditioned thing goes. What has come down is a line of people each of whom took up the brush and made it present again, and in each of them it was real (Dharma and Western Philosophy D3).

同一個傳統，也懂得班雅明稱為靈光的那股吸引力，並且小心地運用了它。舍利；菩提伽耶那棵由一代又一代插枝長成的樹；在一系師承當中傳下來的一件袈裟、一只缽：這些都是因為它們的歷史而被珍視的。禪宗用來比喻傳承的意象，是一盞燈點燃另一盞。第二朵火焰不是第一朵；它也不是第一朵的複製品。「它是不是同一朵火焰」這個問題，被看成問得不好。這提示了一種思考〈蘭亭〉的方式。王羲之在 353 年做的那件事已經過去了，就像每一樣有為法都會過去。傳下來的，是一系列的人；他們每一個都提起筆，使它再一次在場；而在他們每一個人手上，它都是真的（佛法與西方哲學 D3）。
