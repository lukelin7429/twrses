Are We Born Knowing Anything? 我們生下來就知道什麼嗎？

Plato’s slave boy, Locke’s white paper, Mencius’s child, and the long quarrel between reason and experience over where knowledge comes from.
柏拉圖筆下的少年、洛克的白紙、孟子的孩提之童，以及理性與經驗之間那場漫長的爭論：知識從哪裡來？

A7 Big Questions · 大哉問Knowledge · 知識Advanced · 高級1,174 words · about 25 min
The big idea · 大觀念

Experience supplies the content of what we know, and yet a mind with nothing in it could not learn from experience at all. The live question is how much structure a mind must bring.

我們所知的內容由經驗供應；可是一個裡面什麼都沒有的心靈，根本無法從經驗學到任何東西。真正的問題是：心靈必須自備多少結構？

The essay · 課文

Are We Born Knowing Anything? 我們生下來就知道什麼嗎？

01Where does it come from? 它從哪裡來？

A newborn can do very little, and within five years the same child speaks a language, recognizes faces, counts, and has firm opinions about what is fair. Where did all of that come from? There seem to be only two possible sources. It was put in from outside, by the senses and by teaching, or it was in some form there already. The first answer is called empiricism and the second rationalism or, in its modern form, nativism. The dispute between them is the oldest in the theory of knowledge, and it is unusual among philosophical disputes because experiments now bear on it.

新生兒能做的事非常少；不出五年，同一個孩子會說一種語言、認得出人臉、會數數，而且對什麼叫公平有堅定的意見。這一切是從哪裡來的？可能的來源似乎只有兩個：由感官與教導從外面放進去的；或者它以某種形式本來就在那裡。第一個答案叫作經驗主義，第二個叫理性主義，它的現代版本稱為先天論。這兩者的爭論是知識論裡最古老的一場；而它在哲學爭論中很特別，因為現在有實驗可以介入。

02The boy and the square 少年與正方形

The first great argument for inborn knowledge is a demonstration. In Plato’s Meno, Socrates calls over a household slave, a boy who has never been taught geometry, draws a square in the sand, and asks him how long the side of a square twice as large would be. The boy answers confidently and wrongly, twice. Socrates then draws a few more lines and goes on asking questions, and the boy arrives at the correct answer, which is a hard one. Socrates insists that he told the boy nothing. “Without any one teaching him he will recover his knowledge for himself, if he is only asked questions.”

支持「天生知識」的第一個重要論證，是一場示範。在柏拉圖的《美諾篇》裡，蘇格拉底叫來一個家奴，一個從沒學過幾何的少年；他在沙地上畫了一個正方形，問少年：面積兩倍大的正方形，邊長是多少？少年很有把握地答錯了，而且錯了兩次。蘇格拉底接著又畫了幾條線，繼續提問；少年得出了正確答案，而那是個不容易的答案。蘇格拉底堅持，他什麼也沒有告訴少年。「沒有任何人教他，只要有人問他問題，他就會自己把知識找回來。」

Plato’s explanation is that the soul existed before birth and learned everything then, so that “all enquiry and all learning is but recollection.” Few people have accepted the explanation. The demonstration is harder to dismiss. The boy did not learn the theorem by measuring squares, and once he sees it he sees that it must be so. Truths of that kind, known independently of experience, are called a priori, and they are the rationalist’s best evidence.

柏拉圖的解釋是：靈魂在出生之前就存在，並且在那時學會了一切，所以「一切探究、一切學習，都不過是回憶」。很少有人接受這個解釋。但那場示範比較難打發。少年並不是靠測量正方形學到這條定理的；而且他一旦看出來，就看出它「非如此不可」。這一類不依賴經驗而被知道的真理，稱為先驗的（a priori），是理性主義者最好的證據。

03White paper 白紙

The classic statement of the other side is John Locke’s, in An Essay Concerning Human Understanding (1690). “Let us then suppose the mind to be, as we say, white paper, void of all characters, without any ideas:—How comes it to be furnished?” His answer is one word: “from EXPERIENCE.” Experience has two channels. Sensation gives us ideas of yellow, hot, hard, and sweet. Reflection, the mind’s notice of its own operations, gives us ideas of thinking, doubting, and willing. Every other idea, however abstract, is built by combining and comparing these.

另一邊的經典陳述出自洛克的《人類理解論》（1690）。「那麼讓我們假設，心靈就像我們所說的那樣，是一張白紙，上面沒有任何記號，沒有任何觀念：它是怎麼被填滿的？」他的回答只有一個詞：「來自經驗。」經驗有兩個管道。感覺給我們黃、熱、硬、甜的觀念。反省，也就是心靈對自己運作的察覺，給我們思考、懷疑、意願的觀念。其他一切觀念，不管多抽象，都是把這些拿來組合、比較而造出來的。

Locke had an argument as well as a picture. If any principles were stamped on the mind at birth, he said, everyone would be aware of them, and children plainly are not aware of the laws of logic. He also had a motive. Claims of innate truth had been used to put favored doctrines beyond question, and a mind that begins blank has to be given reasons.

洛克除了有一幅圖像，也有論證。他說，如果有任何原則在出生時就印在心靈上，那麼每個人都會察覺到它們；而小孩顯然並沒有察覺到邏輯定律。他也有動機。「天生真理」的說法，曾經被用來讓受偏愛的教條不容質疑；而一個從空白開始的心靈，別人必須給它理由。

04Veins in the marble 大理石裡的紋路

Leibniz read Locke’s Essay and wrote a book-length reply. He granted that nothing is in the intellect which was not first in the senses, and added three words: except the intellect itself. Locke had taken “innate” to mean “consciously present from birth,” which is easy to refute. The serious claim is that the mind has built-in tendencies. It is less like blank paper, Leibniz said, than like a block of marble with veins in it. The sculptor’s blows are needed to bring the figure out, and the veins make it easier to carve one figure than another. Experience is the occasion of our knowledge of necessary truths. It cannot be the source of their necessity, since no number of observed cases shows that a thing must be so.

萊布尼茲讀了洛克的《人類理解論》，寫了一整本書來回應。他承認「凡在理智中的，無不先在感官中」，然後加了幾個字：理智本身除外。洛克把「天生」理解成「從出生起就有意識地在場」，那很容易駁倒。嚴肅的主張是：心靈有內建的傾向。萊布尼茲說，與其說它像白紙，不如說它像一塊帶有紋路的大理石。要把雕像顯現出來，需要雕刻家的敲擊；而紋路使得雕出某個形象比雕出另一個容易。經驗是我們認識必然真理的機緣；它不可能是那些真理之必然性的來源，因為觀察到再多個案，也無法顯示一件事「非如此不可」。

05The child who loves his parents 愛父母的孩子

In China the same question was asked about morality. Mencius, in the fourth century BCE, held that the beginnings of goodness are part of our original equipment. “What people are able to do without having learned it is their innate ability; what they know without having to reflect is their innate knowledge (良知). There are no young children who do not know to love their parents.” Humaneness and rightness, he says elsewhere, “are not welded onto us from outside; we have them from the start.” He did not think that this made anyone good automatically. The beginnings are sprouts, and a sprout that is neglected dies. His opponent Xunzi argued that goodness is the product of deliberate effort and ritual training imposed on a nature that would otherwise run to conflict. Leibniz’s marble and Locke’s paper had been set against each other, on moral ground, two thousand years before either man was born.

在中國，同一個問題是針對道德問的。西元前四世紀的孟子主張，善的端倪是我們原初配備的一部分。「人之所不學而能者，其良能也；所不慮而知者，其良知也。孩提之童，無不知愛其親者。」他在別處說，仁義禮智「非由外鑠我也，我固有之也」。他並不認為這樣就讓任何人自動成為好人。那些端倪是芽，而芽被忽略就會死。他的對手荀子則論證說，善是刻意努力與禮的訓練的產物，是加在一個本來會走向爭奪的本性之上的。萊布尼茲的大理石與洛克的白紙，早在這兩個人出生的兩千年前，就已經在道德的領域裡對峙過了。

06Kant’s settlement 康德的調停

A century after Locke, Immanuel Kant proposed terms of peace. “Though all our knowledge begins with experience,” he wrote, “it by no means follows that all arises out of experience.” The senses supply the material. The mind supplies the form in which any material must be arranged in order to be experienced at all: it places things in space and time and connects them as cause and effect. We are not born knowing any facts, on this view. We are born with the framework that makes facts available to us. Whether Kant identified the right framework is disputed. That some framework is needed is now close to common ground.

洛克之後一個世紀，康德提出了議和的條件。他寫道：「雖然我們的一切知識都始於經驗，卻絕不能因此說它全都出於經驗。」感官供應材料。心靈供應的是形式：任何材料要成為經驗，都必須被安排進這個形式裡；心靈把事物放在空間與時間之中，並且把它們連結成因與果。照這個看法，我們並不是生來就知道任何事實；我們生來就帶著讓事實得以向我們呈現的那個框架。康德認定的框架對不對，還有爭議。但「需要某種框架」，現在已經接近共識。

07What the laboratory says 實驗室怎麼說

Three lines of research have changed the debate. Infants only a few months old look longer at events that break the rules of solid objects, which suggests that they expect objects to persist and not to pass through one another. Children acquire grammar from a small and messy sample of speech, a fact Noam Chomsky called the poverty of the stimulus and took as proof of an inborn language faculty. And a question that the Irish scientist William Molyneux put to Locke in 1688 has been tested. Would a man born blind, who knew a cube and a sphere by touch, recognize them by sight alone if he were suddenly able to see? Locke said no. In 2011 researchers in India examined children just after surgery for congenital blindness. They could not match what they saw to what they felt, and they learned to do it within days.

有三條研究路線改變了這場辯論。才幾個月大的嬰兒，對違反固體規則的事件注視得比較久；這顯示他們預期物體會持續存在，而且不會彼此穿透。孩子從少量而雜亂的語言樣本就習得了文法；喬姆斯基把這個事實稱為「刺激的貧乏」，並且當作天生語言機能的證明。還有，愛爾蘭科學家莫利紐茲在 1688 年向洛克提出的一個問題，已經被檢驗了。一個生來失明、靠觸覺認得立方體與球體的人，如果突然看得見，單憑視覺認得出它們嗎？洛克說不能。2011 年，研究者在印度檢查了剛做完先天性失明手術的孩子。他們沒辦法把看到的跟摸到的配對起來；而他們在幾天之內就學會了。

That result is a fair picture of where things stand. The newly sighted children had no ready-made knowledge linking sight to touch, as Locke predicted. They acquired it far faster than blank paper could, as Leibniz would have predicted. Neither side has been proved simply right. What has faded is the old either-or.

這個結果，相當公允地呈現了目前的局面。剛復明的孩子並沒有現成的、把視覺連到觸覺的知識，如洛克所預言。他們習得這種知識的速度，遠比白紙做得到的快，如萊布尼茲會預言的。沒有哪一邊被證明是單純地對。淡出的是舊日那個「非此即彼」。

08Why it matters to a teacher 這對老師為什麼重要

The question was never only academic. If the mind is white paper, education is everything, and differences among people are differences in what has been written on them. Locke drew that conclusion, and it has been a great engine of reform. If the mind has veins, a teacher’s task is to find them and work with them, which is what Socrates did in the sand. Each view has its danger. The first tempts us to think that children can be made into anything. The second tempts us to read our expectations into their nature. Mencius’s image may be the wisest of the three, since a sprout has a nature of its own and still has to be tended. He warns against the farmer who, impatient with his slow crop, went out and pulled on the shoots to help them grow.

這個問題從來不只是學術的。如果心靈是白紙，教育就是一切，人與人的差別就是寫在他們身上的東西的差別。洛克得出了這個結論，而它一直是改革的強大引擎。如果心靈有紋路，老師的任務就是找出紋路、順著它們工作；蘇格拉底在沙地上做的正是這件事。兩種看法各有危險。第一種誘使我們以為孩子可以被塑造成任何樣子。第二種誘使我們把自己的期待讀進他們的本性裡。孟子的意象也許是三者當中最有智慧的，因為芽有它自己的本性，卻仍然需要照料。他警告我們別學那個農夫：嫌莊稼長得慢，就跑去把苗一棵棵往上拔。

A second experiment 第二個實驗

Molyneux’s question (1688): a man born blind knows a cube and a sphere by touch. He is made to see. Before touching them, can he tell by sight which is which?

莫利紐茲的問題（1688）：一個生來失明的人，靠觸覺認得立方體與球體。現在他被治好、看得見了。在碰到它們之前，他能單憑視覺分辨哪個是哪個嗎？

Yes · 能

Leibniz said yes, given time to reason: a sphere has no corners and a cube has eight, and that structure is the same for sight and touch.

萊布尼茲說能，只要給他時間推理：球沒有角，立方體有八個角，而這個結構對視覺與觸覺是相同的。

No · 不能

Locke and Molyneux said no: he has not yet learned which look goes with which feel.

洛克與莫利紐茲說不能：他還沒學到哪一種樣子對應哪一種觸感。

What happened · 結果

The test was finally run in 2011, on children in India who had just had surgery for congenital blindness. They could not match the seen object to the felt one, performing near chance. Within about a week they could. The connection is learned, as Locke said, and learned remarkably fast, which suggests that the mind was prepared for it.

這個測驗終於在 2011 年做了，對象是印度一群剛做完先天性失明手術的孩子。他們沒辦法把看到的物體跟摸到的配起來，答對率接近亂猜。大約一週之內，他們就做得到了。這個連結是學來的，如洛克所說；而且學得驚人地快，這顯示心靈早就為此做好了準備。

Did he really say that? · 這句話真的是他說的嗎？

Locke said the mind is a tabula rasa.洛克說心靈是一塊 tabula rasa（白板）。

Not his phrase · 不是他的用語

The Latin phrase, meaning a scraped writing tablet, does not appear in the Essay. Locke’s images there are “white paper” and an “empty cabinet.” The tablet goes back to Aristotle, who compared the intellect before it thinks to a tablet on which nothing has yet been written, and tabula rasa became the standard term among medieval philosophers. Later writers attached it to Locke because he gave the idea its most influential defense.

這個拉丁詞組的意思是「刮乾淨的書寫板」，在《人類理解論》裡並沒有出現。洛克在那裡用的意象是「白紙」與「空櫃子」。書寫板可以追溯到亞里斯多德，他把尚未思考的理智比作一塊還沒寫上任何東西的板子；tabula rasa 則成了中世紀哲學家的標準用語。後來的作者把它掛在洛克名下，因為這個想法最有影響力的辯護是他提出的。

Both sides · 正反兩面

Objections and replies 反駁與回應

Objection 1 · 反駁

The slave boy proves nothing. Socrates asks leading questions and draws the crucial figure himself. The boy was taught, by a very good teacher.

那個少年什麼也沒證明。蘇格拉底問的是誘導性的問題，而且關鍵的那個圖是他自己畫的。少年是被教會的，而且老師很高明。

Reply · 回應

The objection is fair as far as the discovery goes: the boy would not have found the diagonal alone. But Plato’s point concerns what happens next. When the figure is drawn, the boy does not take the answer on trust. He sees for himself that it is right and could not be otherwise. That act of seeing was not supplied by the teacher, and it is what needs explaining.

就「發現」這件事而言，這個反駁是公允的：少年靠自己找不到那條對角線。但柏拉圖的重點在於接下來發生的事。圖畫出來之後，少年並不是憑信任接受答案。他自己看出那是對的，而且不可能是別的樣子。這個「看出來」的動作不是老師供應的，而需要解釋的正是它。

Objection 2 · 反駁

Talk of innate ideas is dangerous. It has always been used to declare some people naturally superior and some beliefs beyond criticism.

「天生觀念」的說法是危險的。它一向被用來宣稱某些人天生優越、某些信念不容批評。

Reply · 回應

This was part of Locke’s motive, and the history bears him out. The reply is that the blank slate has been abused as well, by regimes that believed human beings could be remade to order. A doctrine’s political uses do not settle whether it is true. They are a reason to be careful about which claims of innateness we accept, and to demand evidence for each.

這是洛克的動機之一，而歷史證實了他的擔憂。回應是：白板說同樣被濫用過，濫用它的是那些相信人可以照訂單重新製造的政權。一個學說在政治上被怎麼用，並不能決定它是不是真的。那是一個理由，要我們小心自己接受哪些「天生」的主張，並且對每一個都要求證據。

Objection 3 · 反駁

The whole debate rests on a false choice. Asking whether knowledge comes from nature or from experience is like asking whether the area of a rectangle comes from its length or its width.

整場辯論建立在一個假的二選一上。問知識來自天性還是來自經驗，就像問長方形的面積來自長還是來自寬。

Reply · 回應

Most researchers would now agree, and Kant said something similar. But the particular questions remain open and are worth asking one at a time. How much of grammar is built in? Is there an inborn sense of number, of fairness, of cause? “Both” is the right general answer, and it tells us nothing about any particular case.

現在多數研究者會同意，而康德說過類似的話。但個別的問題仍然沒有定論，而且值得一個一個地問。文法有多少是內建的？有沒有天生的數感、公平感、因果感？「兩者都有」是正確的總答案，而它對任何個別的情況什麼也沒說。

The vocabulary of the subject 這門學問的用語

empiricism經驗主義

Gk. empeiria ‘experience’

The view that all knowledge of the world derives from experience.

主張關於世界的一切知識都來自經驗。

rationalism理性主義

L. ratio ‘reason’

The view that reason gives us some knowledge independently of experience.

主張理性能給我們某些不依賴經驗的知識。

innate天生的

L. innatus ‘inborn’

Present in the mind by nature, whether as knowledge or as a disposition.

出於本性而在心靈之中的，不論是作為知識還是作為傾向。

a priori / a posteriori先驗的／後驗的

L. ‘from what comes before’ / ‘from what comes after’

Knowable independently of experience; knowable only through experience.

不依賴經驗而可知的；只有透過經驗才可知的。

recollection回憶說

Gk. anamnēsis

Plato’s doctrine that learning is the recovery of knowledge the soul already has.

柏拉圖的學說：學習是找回靈魂已有的知識。

sensation and reflection感覺與反省

Locke’s two sources of ideas: the senses, and the mind’s awareness of its own operations.

洛克的兩個觀念來源：感官，以及心靈對自身運作的察覺。

disposition傾向

L. disponere ‘to arrange’

A standing tendency to act or think in a certain way when the occasion arises.

機緣出現時，以某種方式行動或思考的持續趨向。

nativism先天論

L. nativus ‘inborn’

The modern view that the mind has substantial inborn structure.

現代的看法：心靈有相當多的天生結構。

poverty of the stimulus刺激的貧乏

Chomsky’s argument that children know more about language than their experience could have taught them.

喬姆斯基的論證：孩子對語言的了解，多過他們的經驗所能教給他們的。

Dharma and the West · 佛法與西方哲學

A view from the Dharma 佛法怎麼看

☸

Buddhist thought contains both positions, and adds a third that neither Locke nor Leibniz considered. The empiricist strand is early and strong. Asked to define “the all,” the Buddha lists the eye and forms, the ear and sounds, the nose and odors, the tongue and tastes, the body and tangibles, the mind and mental objects, and says that anyone who claims to describe another “all” beyond these is speaking of what lies outside his range (Saṃyutta Nikāya 35.23). Knowledge begins from contact at the six sense bases, and mind is counted as a sixth sense, which is not far from Locke’s sensation and reflection.

佛教思想裡兩種立場都有，還多了第三種，是洛克與萊布尼茲都沒考慮過的。經驗主義的這一脈出現得早，而且很強。有人請佛陀界定「一切」，佛陀列出：眼與色、耳與聲、鼻與香、舌與味、身與觸、意與法；並且說，誰要是宣稱在這些之外另有一個「一切」，那就是在談超出他範圍的東西（《相應部》35.23）。知識始於六處的「觸」；而意被算作第六個感官，這跟洛克的感覺與反省相去不遠。

The other strand resembles Mencius. An early text says that the mind is luminous and is defiled by visiting defilements (Aṅguttara Nikāya 1.49). The Mahāyāna doctrine of buddha-nature develops the thought: awakening is not manufactured, it is uncovered. When Huineng awakens in the Platform Sūtra he exclaims, “Who would have thought that my own nature is complete in itself from the beginning!” (何期自性，本自具足). And the third position comes from rebirth. On the Buddhist account a newborn is not a blank, because it arrives carrying dispositions (vāsanā) laid down by earlier action. This is Plato’s answer, experience before birth, with one difference. What is carried over consists of habits and tendencies, good and bad, and includes no knowledge of eternal truths (Dharma and the West, D12).

另一脈近似孟子。一部早期的經說：這個心是明淨的，而它被客塵煩惱所染污（《增支部》1.49）。大乘的佛性（如來藏）學說發展了這個想法：覺悟不是製造出來的，是被揭開的。在《六祖壇經》裡，惠能開悟時驚嘆：「何期自性，本自具足！」第三種立場則來自輪迴。照佛教的說法，新生兒不是空白的，因為它來的時候帶著由過去的行為留下的習氣（vāsanā）。這是柏拉圖的答案——出生之前的經驗——只有一點不同：被帶過來的是習慣與傾向，有好有壞，其中並沒有對永恆真理的知識（佛法與西方哲學 D12）。
