Do Animals Have Rights? 動物有權利嗎？

Descartes heard a clock where others heard a cry. Bentham asked a question in a footnote that took two centuries to be heard. Singer, Regan, and their critics on who counts and why, with Mencius’s king, the Buddha’s first precept, and a test of where you draw your own line.
別人聽見的是哀號，笛卡兒聽見的是一座鐘。邊沁在一個註腳裡問了一個問題，過了兩個世紀才被聽見。辛格、雷根與他們的批評者，談誰算數、為什麼；還有孟子筆下的齊宣王、佛陀的第一條戒，以及一項測驗：你自己的那條線畫在哪裡。

The big idea · 大觀念

This lesson opens the last unit, on questions that philosophy is asked to settle now. Earlier lessons supplied the tools: the theories of right action, the problem of other minds, and the question of how far our duties reach. Here they are put to work on a question most of us answer three times a day without noticing that we have been asked.

這一課是最後一個單元的開始；這個單元談的，是人們現在就要哲學來回答的問題。前面的課提供了工具：關於正確行為的幾種理論、他心問題，以及「我們的義務延伸到多遠」這個問題。在這裡，要把它們用在一個問題上；我們大多數人每天回答它三次，卻沒有注意到有人問過。

The essay · 課文

Do Animals Have Rights? 動物有權利嗎？

01The clock and the cry 鐘與哀號

For most of the history of Western philosophy the question had a short answer. Animals lack reason, and beings without reason are outside morality. Descartes gave the view its sharpest form in 1637. Animals do many things better than we do, he observed, and this shows only that “it is nature which acts in them according to the disposition of their organs,” as “a clock composed only of wheels and weights can number the hours and measure time more exactly than we.” An animal is a machine made by the hands of God. Some of his followers drew the conclusion that its cries under the knife were the noise of a mechanism.

在西方哲學史的大部分時間裡，這個問題有一個簡短的答案。動物沒有理性；而沒有理性的東西，在道德之外。笛卡兒在 1637 年給了這個看法最尖銳的形式。他觀察到：動物有很多事情做得比我們好；而這只顯示了「是自然依照牠們器官的配置，在牠們裡面運作」，就像「一座只由齒輪與重錘組成的鐘，能比我們更準確地報時、量度時間」。動物是出自上帝之手的一部機器。他的一些追隨者由此推出：牠在刀下的哀號，是機件的聲響。

Kant did not think animals were machines, and he condemned cruelty to them. His reason was indirect. A man who is cruel to animals becomes hard in his dealings with people, so we should treat animals kindly for the sake of humanity. On this view we have duties concerning animals and none to them. The dog has no claim of its own. It has the standing of a neighbor’s window, which we must not break because of what breaking it would do to the neighbor and to us.

康德並不認為動物是機器；而且他譴責對牠們殘忍。他的理由是間接的。一個對動物殘忍的人，待人也會變得冷酷；所以我們應該為了人性的緣故而善待動物。照這個看法，我們有「關於」動物的義務，卻沒有「對」動物的義務。那條狗自己並沒有任何可以主張的東西。牠的地位，像是鄰居家的窗戶：我們不可以打破它，是因為打破它會對鄰居、對我們自己造成什麼。

02Bentham’s footnote 邊沁的註腳

In 1789 Jeremy Bentham, in a footnote to a book on the principles of law, compared the treatment of animals to slavery. The French had already seen, he wrote, that the blackness of the skin is no reason for abandoning a human being to a tormentor. One day it may be recognized that the number of legs or the hairiness of the skin is no better reason. What else could mark the line? Reason, or speech? “But a full-grown horse or dog is beyond comparison a more rational, as well as a more conversable animal, than an infant of a day or a week or even a month, old. But suppose they were otherwise, what would it avail? The question is not, Can they reason? nor Can they talk? but, Can they suffer?”

1789 年，傑瑞米・邊沁在一本談法律原則的書的一個註腳裡，把對待動物的方式比作奴隸制。他寫道：法國人已經看出，皮膚黑並不是把一個人丟給折磨者的理由。也許有一天人們會承認：腿的數目，或者皮膚上有沒有毛，也不是更好的理由。還有什麼能劃出那條線？理性，還是語言？「但是，一匹成年的馬或一條成年的狗，比起一個出生一天、一週，甚至一個月的嬰兒，理性得多、也更能交流，簡直不能相比。可是，就算情況不是如此，那又怎樣？問題不在於牠們能不能推理，也不在於牠們能不能說話；而在於牠們會不會受苦。」

The argument has two steps, and both have lasted. The first is that any property we might use to exclude animals, such as reason or language, is also missing in some human beings whom no one proposes to exclude. The second is that the capacity to suffer is what gives a being interests at all. A stone has no interests because nothing we do to it matters to it. A being that can suffer has at least an interest in not suffering, and Bentham saw no reason why the pain should count for less because of the shape of the body that feels it.

這個論證有兩步；而兩步都流傳了下來。第一步：任何一種我們可能拿來排除動物的性質，例如理性或語言，在某些人類身上也是沒有的；而沒有人提議要排除那些人。第二步：受苦的能力，正是使一個存在者有「利益」可言的東西。一塊石頭沒有利益，因為我們對它做什麼，對它都無所謂。一個會受苦的存在者，至少有「不受苦」這項利益；而邊沁看不出有什麼理由，可以因為感受痛苦的那個身體長成什麼形狀，就讓那份痛苦算得比較少。

03Equal consideration 平等的考量

The footnote was little noticed for a long time. In 1975 Peter Singer made it the foundation of a book, Animal Liberation. His principle is the equal consideration of interests: like interests are to be given equal weight, whoever’s interests they are. To give less weight to an animal’s pain merely because it is not a member of our species is, he argued, a prejudice of the same form as racism, and he took up a new word for it, speciesism. The principle does not say that a pig and a child should be treated alike, since their interests differ. A pig has no interest in learning to read. It does have an interest in not being confined for life in a crate too narrow to turn around in. Most of the book is a description of what is done to animals in laboratories and on industrial farms, where today more than eighty billion land animals are killed each year.

這個註腳很久都沒有什麼人注意。1975 年，彼得・辛格把它當作一本書的地基：《動物解放》。他的原則是「利益的平等考量」：相似的利益，不管是誰的利益，都要給予同等的份量。他主張：只因為一隻動物不是我們這個物種的成員，就把牠的痛苦算得比較輕，是一種與種族主義同樣形式的偏見；他採用了一個新字來稱呼它：物種主義。這個原則並不是說，豬與孩子應該受到一樣的對待；因為他們的利益不同。豬沒有學識字的利益。牠卻有這樣的利益：不要一輩子被關在窄得轉不了身的欄架裡。那本書大部分的篇幅，是在描述實驗室與工業化農場裡對動物做的事；今天，每年有超過八百億隻陸生動物在那裡被宰殺。

Singer is a utilitarian, and strictly he does not speak of rights. For him animals count in the sum of well-being, and an experiment that caused some suffering and prevented far more could be justified, on an animal or on a human being. Tom Regan thought this conceded too much. In The Case for Animal Rights, in 1983, he argued in the manner of Kant that some beings are not to be used as means at all. The beings he had in mind are those he called subjects of a life: creatures with perception, memory, desires, and a welfare that matters to them. They have a value that is not a quantity to be added up, and it gives them a right not to be treated as resources. On Regan’s view the cages should be emptied. Making them larger is not enough.

辛格是效益主義者；嚴格說來，他並不談「權利」。對他來說，動物是算在福祉的總和裡的；一項造成若干痛苦、卻防止了多得多的痛苦的實驗，可以是正當的，不管是用在動物還是用在人身上。湯姆・雷根認為這讓步太多了。他在 1983 年的《為動物權利辯護》裡，以康德的方式主張：有些存在者，根本就不可以被當作工具來用。他所想的，是他稱為「生命主體」的存在者：有知覺、記憶、欲望，並且有一份對牠自己要緊的福祉的生物。牠們有一種價值，不是可以加總的數量；而這給了牠們一項權利：不被當成資源來對待。照雷根的看法，籠子應該清空。把籠子做大一點是不夠的。

04The case against 反方的理由

The strongest reply comes from the idea of a contract. Rights, on this view, belong to members of a moral community, beings who can make claims on one another, recognize duties, and be held to account. A wolf cannot respect the rights of a deer, and we do not blame it. To have rights is to stand in relations that animals cannot enter. The philosopher Carl Cohen put the point by saying that animals are not the kind of being that can have rights, though we may still have duties of kindness toward them. Human infants are covered because they belong to a kind whose normal members are moral agents.

最強的回應，來自「契約」這個觀念。照這個看法，權利屬於一個道德社群的成員：能夠互相提出主張、認得義務、並且可以被追究責任的存在者。狼沒辦法尊重鹿的權利；而我們並不責怪牠。擁有權利，就是處在一些動物進不去的關係之中。哲學家卡爾・柯亨這樣表達這一點：動物不是那種能夠擁有權利的存在者；不過我們對牠們仍然可以有仁慈的義務。人類的嬰兒之所以包括在內，是因為他們屬於這樣的一個類：這個類的正常成員是道德主體。

A second reply defends partiality. The lesson on strangers found that we owe more to those close to us, and that this is not obviously a prejudice. Mary Midgley argued that a preference for one’s own species is like the preference for one’s own family. It is a natural bond that gives a reason, and it leaves room for real duties to outsiders. The defenders of animals answer that a bond explains why we care more about some beings, and it cannot show that the suffering of the others counts for nothing.

第二個回應，是為偏私辯護。講陌生人的那一課發現：我們對親近的人虧欠得比較多；而這並不明顯是一種偏見。瑪麗・米雷主張：偏愛自己的物種，就像偏愛自己的家人。它是一種自然的連結，能給出理由；而它也為「對外人的真正義務」留了餘地。為動物辯護的人回答：連結能解釋我們為什麼更在乎某些存在者；它卻不能顯示，其他存在者的痛苦一點都不算數。

05The kitchen at a distance 遠庖廚

The Chinese tradition met the question early and gave an honest and uncomfortable answer. King Xuan of Qi, as an earlier lesson told, could not bear the fear of an ox being led to slaughter and ordered a sheep to be used in its place. Mencius approved of the feeling and drew a conclusion about the gentleman: having seen animals alive, he cannot bear to see them die, and having heard their cries, he cannot bear to eat their flesh. “That is why the gentleman keeps his distance from the kitchen.” The feeling is taken as the root of humaneness, and the practice goes on out of sight. Mencius held that love has degrees: affection for family, humaneness toward people, and a sparing care for creatures.

中國的傳統很早就碰到了這個問題，並且給了一個誠實而令人不安的答案。齊宣王，正如前面有一課講過的，不忍心看一頭牛被牽去宰殺時的恐懼，下令用一隻羊來代替。孟子肯定了那份感受，並且對君子下了一個結論：「君子之於禽獸也，見其生，不忍見其死；聞其聲，不忍食其肉。是以君子遠庖廚也。」那份感受被當作仁的根苗；而那件事，在看不見的地方照樣進行。孟子主張愛有等差：「親親而仁民，仁民而愛物。」

Buddhism went further and made not killing the first of its precepts, on a ground close to Bentham’s. “All tremble at the rod, all fear death. Treating others like oneself, neither kill nor incite to kill.” Chinese Buddhists, from the sixth century, drew the conclusion that monastics should not eat meat at all. Between Mencius’s kitchen and the monastery’s table lie most of the positions that people hold today. The philosophical question is whether the place where each of us has stopped rests on a reason, or only on what we have managed not to see.

佛教走得更遠，把不殺生定為第一條戒；根據很接近邊沁的那一個。「一切懼刀杖，一切皆畏死，以自度他情，莫殺教他殺。」中國的佛教徒從六世紀起得出了這樣的結論：出家人根本不該吃肉。在孟子的庖廚與寺院的齋桌之間，就是今天人們所持的大多數立場。哲學上的問題是：我們每個人停下來的那個地方，是立足於一個理由，還是只立足於我們設法不去看見的東西。

Thought experiment · 思想實驗

Try it: where is your line? 親手做一次：你的線畫在哪裡？

Ten beings. For each, decide whether its suffering matters morally for its own sake, so that hurting it for a small convenience would wrong it. Then choose the reason that best explains your line, and see whether the reason and the line agree.

十個存在者。對每一個，決定牠（他、它）的痛苦在道德上是不是「為了牠自己的緣故」而要緊；也就是說，為了一點小小的方便而傷害牠，是不是虧待了牠。然後選出最能解釋你那條線的理由，看看理由與線是不是一致。

Did he really say that? · 這句話真的是他說的嗎？

“The greatness of a nation and its moral progress can be judged by the way its animals are treated.” — Mahatma Gandhi「一個國家的偉大與它的道德進步，可以從它對待動物的方式來判斷。」——甘地

No source in Gandhi’s writings · 在甘地的著作裡找不到出處

The sentence appears on posters, in speeches, and in the publications of animal charities around the world, always under Gandhi’s name. Researchers have looked for it in his collected works and have not found it. It is sometimes referred to an essay of his on the moral basis of vegetarianism, and it is not there either. A similar thought occurs in a book of 1872 by the German theologian David Friedrich Strauss, which may be where the wording began. Gandhi was a vegetarian by conviction and wrote a good deal about it, so the sentiment is not foreign to him. The case shows how a saying attaches itself to the most suitable famous name. It also shows why the argument does not need him. Bentham’s footnote is authentic, is older, and gives a reason.

這句話出現在世界各地的海報上、演講裡，以及動物保護團體的出版品裡；總是掛著甘地的名字。研究者在他的全集裡找過，並沒有找到。有時候它被說成出自他談素食的道德基礎的一篇文章；那裡面也沒有。德國神學家大衛・弗里德里希・史特勞斯 1872 年的一本書裡，有一個類似的想法；這個措辭可能就是從那裡開始的。甘地是出於信念而吃素的，也寫過不少談這件事的文字；所以這份情懷對他並不陌生。這個案例顯示了一句話是怎麼自己貼到最合適的那個名人身上的。它也顯示了為什麼這個論證並不需要他。邊沁的註腳是真的，更早，而且給了一個理由。

Both sides · 正反兩面

Objections and replies 反駁與回應

Objection 1 · 反駁

Animals eat each other. If killing animals for food is wrong, nature is one vast crime.

動物會互相吃。如果為了食物而殺動物是錯的，那麼大自然就是一場巨大的罪行。

Reply · 回應

The lion has no choice and no concept of choice, so it cannot do wrong, for the reason that the contract theorists gave. We are in a different position, since most people in rich countries can live well without meat. What other animals do is no guide to what we may do. No one cites the habits of lions to settle other moral questions.

獅子沒有選擇，也沒有「選擇」這個概念；所以牠不可能做錯事，理由正是契約論者給的那一個。我們的處境不同：富裕國家的大多數人，不吃肉也能活得很好。別的動物做什麼，並不能指引我們可以做什麼。沒有人會引用獅子的習性，來解決其他的道德問題。

Objection 2 · 反駁

If animals have rights, then a mouse counts as much as a child, and that is absurd.

如果動物有權利，那麼一隻老鼠就跟一個孩子一樣重要；而那是荒謬的。

Reply · 回應

Neither Singer nor Regan holds this. Equal consideration of interests is compatible with thinking that a child’s death is a greater loss, because a child has more to lose: plans, relationships, a future understood as her own. Regan says the same in the case of a lifeboat that cannot hold everyone. The claim is that the mouse’s suffering counts for something. It is not that all lives are worth the same.

辛格與雷根都不這樣主張。利益的平等考量，跟「一個孩子的死是更大的損失」這個想法是相容的；因為孩子可失去的更多：計畫、關係，以及一個她理解為「自己的」未來。在「救生艇載不下所有人」的情況裡，雷根也是這樣說的。這裡主張的是：老鼠的痛苦算點什麼；並不是說，所有的生命價值相同。

Objection 3 · 反駁

Where does it stop? Plants respond to injury. If we cannot eat pigs, we cannot eat rice.

到哪裡為止？植物對傷害也有反應。如果我們不能吃豬，那我們也不能吃稻米。

Reply · 回應

Responding to damage is not the same as feeling it. Plants have no nervous system, and nothing in their behavior requires us to suppose that there is something it is like to be one. The line between feeling and not feeling is hard to locate near insects and shellfish, and that is a reason for caution there. It is no reason to doubt the cases far from the line. A person who truly worried about plants would also eat fewer animals, since farm animals are fed many times their own weight in crops.

對損傷有反應，跟感受到它，並不是同一回事。植物沒有神經系統；而它們的行為裡，沒有任何東西需要我們去假定「當一株植物是有某種感覺的」。有感受與沒感受之間的那條線，在昆蟲與貝類附近很難定位；而那是在那一帶要謹慎的理由。它並不是懷疑那些離線很遠的情況的理由。一個真正擔心植物的人，也會少吃動物；因為養殖的動物所吃掉的作物，是牠們自身重量的許多倍。

The vocabulary of the subject 這門學問的用語

L. sentire ‘to feel’

The capacity to have experiences, especially of pleasure and pain.

擁有經驗的能力；尤其是苦與樂的經驗。

speciesism物種主義

coined by Richard Ryder, 1970

Giving less weight to the interests of a being merely because of its species.

只因為一個存在者屬於哪個物種，就把牠的利益看得比較輕。

Singer’s principle that like interests count equally, whoever has them.

辛格的原則：相似的利益同樣算數，不管是誰的。

subject of a life生命主體

Regan’s term for a being with perception, memory, desires, and a welfare of its own.

雷根的用語：有知覺、記憶、欲望，並且有自己的福祉的存在者。

indirect duty間接義務

A duty concerning a being that is owed to someone else, as in Kant’s view of animals.

「關於」某個存在者、卻是「對」別人負的義務；康德對動物的看法就是這樣。

moral agent / moral patient道德主體／道德受體

One who can act rightly or wrongly; one who can be treated rightly or wrongly.

能夠做對或做錯事的；能夠被對待得對或不對的。

argument from marginal cases邊緣案例論證

The argument that any property excluding animals also excludes some humans.

一種論證：任何一項把動物排除在外的性質，也會把某些人排除在外。

not killing不殺生

P. pāṇātipātā veramaṇī; Skt. ahiṃsā

The first Buddhist precept: to refrain from taking the life of any living being.

佛教的第一條戒：不奪取任何生命。

Dharma and the West · 佛法與西方哲學

A view from the Dharma 佛法怎麼看

☸

The Buddha’s position is stated in a verse of the Dhammapada: “All tremble at the rod, all fear death. Treating others like oneself, neither kill nor incite to kill.” The ground is the one Bentham would give, with a different emphasis. Bentham counts pains. The verse asks the hearer to put himself in the other’s place. The class of beings covered is “all sentient beings,” and no line is drawn at the human. The teaching of rebirth adds a thought that has no Western parallel. In the long course of wandering, the Buddha says, “it’s not easy to find a sentient being who in all this long time has not previously been your mother.” An animal is a fellow traveler in another form. It is not a different kind of thing.

佛陀的立場，陳述在《法句經》的一首偈裡：「一切懼刀杖，一切皆畏死，以自度他情，莫殺教他殺。」根據就是邊沁會給的那一個，重點卻不同。邊沁計算的是痛苦。這首偈要求聽的人把自己放在對方的位置上。它涵蓋的範圍是「一切有情」；並沒有在「人」那裡劃一條線。輪迴的教法又添了一個在西方沒有對應的想法。佛陀說：在漫長的流轉當中，「要找到一個在這麼長的時間裡不曾做過你母親的眾生，並不容易」。動物是以另一種形態同行的旅伴。牠並不是另一類東西。

On what follows for the table, the tradition divides, and it is honest to say so. In the early discourses the Buddha did not require vegetarianism. He told the physician Jīvaka that a monk may not eat meat when he has seen, heard, or suspected that the animal was killed for him, and may eat it otherwise. The rule fixes on the eater’s part in the killing. The Mahāyāna scriptures that shaped Chinese Buddhism went further and held that eating meat cuts off the seed of great compassion, and Emperor Wu of Liang made abstention the rule for monasteries in the sixth century. Singer and Regan would recognize both positions. The first asks what I cause. The second asks what kind of person the practice makes of me, which is the question that virtue ethics asks (A18).

至於這對餐桌意味著什麼，這個傳統是分歧的；而誠實的做法就是把這一點說出來。在早期的經典裡，佛陀並沒有要求吃素。他告訴醫師耆婆：當一位比丘看見、聽見，或者懷疑那隻動物是為了他而殺的，他就不可以吃那個肉；除此之外可以吃（三淨肉）。這條規則盯著的，是吃的人在那場殺生裡的份。塑造了漢傳佛教的大乘經典走得更遠，主張食肉「斷大慈種」；而梁武帝在六世紀使戒除肉食成為寺院的規矩。這兩種立場，辛格與雷根都會認得。第一種問的是：我造成了什麼？第二種問的是：這個習慣把我塑造成什麼樣的人？那就是德性倫理學問的問題（A18）。
