Are People Born Good? 人性本善嗎？

Mencius says that human nature tends toward good as water runs downhill. Xunzi says that it is crooked timber and must be steamed straight. Hobbes, Rousseau, and babies in a laboratory on the oldest quarrel in ethics.
孟子說，人性之向善，就像水往下流。荀子說，它是彎曲的木頭，必須蒸過才扳得直。霍布斯、盧梭，還有實驗室裡的嬰兒，談倫理學裡最古老的一場爭論。

A20 Big Questions · 大哉問Right and Wrong · 對與錯Advanced · 高級1,253 words · about 25 min
The big idea · 大觀念

How we raise children, punish criminals, and design governments depends on what we think people are like before anything has been done to them. The question is partly one of fact, and for the first time some of the facts can be tested.

我們怎麼養育孩子、怎麼懲罰罪犯、怎麼設計政府，都取決於我們認為人在「還沒有被動過任何手腳之前」是什麼樣子。這個問題有一部分是事實問題；而這些事實當中，有一些如今第一次可以被檢驗。

The essay · 課文

Are People Born Good? 人性本善嗎？

01Water and willow 水與杞柳

Around 320 BCE two philosophers argued about human nature in images, and the record of it is the oldest sustained debate on the subject that we have. Gaozi said that human nature is like willow wood, and morality is like the cups and bowls a craftsman carves from it. Goodness is something made out of us. Mencius asked whether the craftsman follows the nature of the willow or does violence to it. If morality is a violence done to human beings, people will have every reason to hate it. Gaozi tried again. Human nature is like water whirling in a pool. Open a channel to the east and it flows east, open one to the west and it flows west. It has no preference between good and bad.

大約西元前 320 年，兩位哲學家用意象爭論人性；這份紀錄，是我們手上關於這個題目最古老的一場持續的辯論。告子說：人性就像杞柳，仁義就像工匠從它雕出來的杯盤。善是從我們身上「做」出來的東西。孟子問：工匠是順著杞柳的本性呢，還是戕害它？如果仁義是對人的一種戕害，人們就大有理由憎恨它。告子換了一個說法。人性就像在水潭裡打轉的水。往東開一個口，它就往東流；往西開一個口，它就往西流。它在善與不善之間沒有偏好。

Mencius accepted the image and turned it. “Water indeed has no preference between east and west. But has it no preference between up and down? Human nature is good as water flows downward. There is no person who is not good, and there is no water that does not flow down.” You can splash water over your head by striking it, and you can force it up a hill by damming it, but that is what circumstance does to it. When people are made to do wrong, something similar has been done to their nature.

孟子接受了這個意象，然後把它轉了過來。「水信無分於東西，無分於上下乎？人性之善也，猶水之就下也。人無有不善，水無有不下。」你拍打水，可以讓它濺過額頭；你築壩擋水，可以逼它上山；但那是形勢對它造成的。人被弄到去做不善的事，他們的本性也是遭到了類似的對待。

02The four sprouts 四端

Mencius did not mean that people are born virtuous. His claim was about tendencies. Anyone who suddenly sees a child about to fall into a well, he says, feels alarm and compassion, and feels it before any thought of gain or reputation. That feeling is a sprout. There are four of them, compassion, shame, deference, and the sense of right and wrong, and from them grow humaneness, rightness, propriety, and wisdom. “These are not welded onto me from outside. I have them from the start.” A sprout can be tended or trampled. His most famous image is Ox Mountain, which was once covered in trees. It stood near a great city, and axes cut it down. The stumps put out new shoots with the night air and the rain, and cattle and sheep grazed them off. People who see it bare suppose that it never had timber. “But is that the nature of the mountain?”

孟子的意思並不是人生下來就有德行。他的主張是關於「傾向」的。他說，任何人突然看見一個小孩快要掉進井裡，都會感到驚懼與不忍，而且是在想到任何利益或名聲之前就感到了。那個感受是一個「端」，一株嫩芽。這樣的嫩芽有四個：惻隱、羞惡、恭敬、是非；從它們長出仁、義、禮、智。「仁義禮智，非由外鑠我也，我固有之也。」嫩芽可以被照料，也可以被踐踏。他最著名的意象是牛山；它曾經長滿了樹。它位在一座大城的郊外，斧頭把樹砍光了。殘留的樹樁靠著夜氣與雨露冒出新芽，牛羊又接著把它們啃掉。人們看它光禿禿的，就以為它從來沒有過木材。「此豈山之性也哉？」

03Crooked timber 枸木

Two generations later Xunzi, the most systematic of the early Confucians, wrote a chapter that opens: “Human nature is bad. Its goodness is the product of deliberate effort.” People are born fond of profit, he observes, and if they follow that fondness there will be strife and no yielding. They are born with envy and hatred, and with desires of the ear and eye. To follow nature is to end in violence. “Crooked wood must be steamed and bent before it is straight, and dull metal must be ground before it is sharp.” Goodness comes from teachers, models, and ritual, which the sages invented because they saw what people are like without them. Xunzi was no pessimist about the result. He agreed with Mencius that the man in the street can become a sage. He located the capacity in intelligence and effort, where Mencius had located it in feeling. The practical difference is large. For Mencius education is gardening, and for Xunzi it is carpentry.

兩代之後，早期儒者當中最有系統的荀子，寫了一篇這樣開頭的文章：「人之性惡，其善者偽也。」（「偽」是「人為」的意思。）他觀察到：人生而好利，順著這份喜好，就會有爭奪而沒有辭讓。人生而有嫉妒與憎惡，生而有耳目之欲。順著本性走，結局是暴亂。「枸木必將待檃栝、烝、矯然後直，鈍金必將待礱、厲然後利。」善來自師、法與禮義；聖人之所以創制它們，是因為看到了人沒有它們的時候是什麼樣子。荀子對結果並不悲觀。他同意孟子：路上的普通人也可以成為聖人，「塗之人可以為禹」。只是孟子把這份能力放在情感裡，他把它放在知性與努力裡。實際上的差別很大。對孟子來說，教育是園藝；對荀子來說，教育是木工。

04The same quarrel in Europe 歐洲的同一場爭論

Europe had the argument nineteen centuries later, and its two great voices line up with the Chinese ones closely. Thomas Hobbes, writing during a civil war, asked what life would be like with no government to keep people in awe. His answer is that every man would be the enemy of every other, and there would be “no Arts; no Letters; no Society; and which is worst of all, continuall feare, and danger of violent death; And the life of man, solitary, poore, nasty, brutish, and short.” People are not wicked on his account. They are roughly equal, they want the same scarce things, and none can trust the rest. Order has to be imposed.

歐洲在十九個世紀之後才有了這場爭論；而它的兩個最響亮的聲音，跟中國的那兩個對得很整齊。霍布斯在一場內戰期間寫作；他問：如果沒有政府使人們有所畏懼，生活會是什麼樣子？他的回答是：每個人都會是每個人的敵人；那裡將「沒有技藝，沒有文字，沒有社會；而最糟的是，持續不斷的恐懼，以及暴死的危險；人的生活孤獨、貧困、污穢、野蠻而短暫」。照他的說法，人並不邪惡。他們大致平等，想要同樣稀少的東西，而誰也信不過其餘的人。秩序必須被強加上去。

Jean-Jacques Rousseau replied in 1755 that Hobbes had described people as society has made them and mistaken the portrait for nature. Natural man, he argued, is solitary, healthy, and peaceable, with two instincts, a care for his own survival and a pity that makes him unwilling to see another creature suffer. Corruption begins with property. “The first man, who, after enclosing a piece of ground, took it into his head to say, ‘This is mine,’ and found people simple enough to believe him, was the true founder of civil society,” and of all the crimes and wars that followed. His book on education opens with the sentence: “God makes all things good; man meddles with them and they become evil.” Ox Mountain could not be put more briefly.

盧梭在 1755 年回應：霍布斯描述的，是被社會造就之後的人，卻把這幅畫像誤當成了本性。他論證：自然人是孤獨、健康而平和的，有兩種本能，一是對自己存活的關切，一是憐憫，使他不願看見另一個生命受苦。腐化始於財產。「第一個圈起一塊地、想到要說『這是我的』，並且找到一些頭腦簡單得肯相信他的人，就是文明社會真正的奠基者」，也是隨之而來的一切罪行與戰爭的奠基者。他談教育的那本書，第一句就是：「出自造物主之手的一切都是好的；一到了人的手裡，就全變壞了。」牛山的道理，沒辦法說得更簡短了。

05Babies in the laboratory 實驗室裡的嬰兒

For most of history the question could be argued only from anecdote. In the last few decades psychologists have found ways to put it to infants, who have had little time to be taught. In experiments at Yale published in 2007, babies of six and ten months watched a puppet show in which one figure helped another up a hill and a third pushed it down. Offered the two afterward, most reached for the helper. In studies at Leipzig, toddlers of eighteen months who saw a stranger drop something and reach for it went and picked it up for him, unasked and unrewarded. These findings are Mencius’s child at the well, observed under controlled conditions.

在歷史上大部分的時間裡，這個問題只能靠軼事來爭論。過去幾十年，心理學家找到了向嬰兒提出這個問題的方法；嬰兒還沒有多少時間被教導。在耶魯大學 2007 年發表的實驗裡，六個月與十個月大的嬰兒看了一場玩偶戲：一個角色幫另一個角色爬上山坡，第三個角色把它推下去。事後把那兩個角色拿給嬰兒選，多數嬰兒伸手去拿幫忙的那一個。在萊比錫的研究裡，十八個月大的幼兒看見一個陌生人掉了東西、伸手去搆，就走過去替他撿起來，沒有人要求，也沒有獎賞。這些發現，就是在受控條件下觀察到的「孺子將入於井」。

The same researchers have found the other side. Infants prefer those who resemble them, and they like to see a puppet who chose a different food from theirs treated badly. Small children help freely and share grudgingly. And adults are easily led. In Stanley Milgram’s experiments of the 1960s about two-thirds of ordinary volunteers, told to do so by a man in a gray coat, went on pressing switches that they believed were giving severe electric shocks to a stranger. The prison experiment run at Stanford in 1971 is cited for the same lesson, though later study of the records has shown that the guards were coached, and it is now treated with caution.

同一批研究者也發現了另一面。嬰兒偏好跟自己相像的對象；而且他們喜歡看到一個「選了跟他們不同食物」的玩偶被惡劣對待。幼兒幫忙很大方，分享卻很勉強。而成年人很容易被帶著走。在米爾格倫 1960 年代的實驗裡，大約三分之二的普通志願者，在一位穿灰色外套的人的指示下，持續按下他們相信正在對一個陌生人施加強烈電擊的開關。1971 年在史丹佛進行的監獄實驗也常被引來說明同一個教訓；不過後來對紀錄的研究顯示，那些「獄卒」是被指點過的，現在人們引用它時比較謹慎。

06What the evidence settles 證據解決了什麼

The picture that emerges gives each ancient party something. Mencius was right that the beginnings of sympathy and of a sense of fairness are present before instruction, and Gaozi’s blank water is the view that has fared worst. Xunzi was right that we are also born with partiality, appetite, and readiness to follow, and that the first moral impulses are narrow: they extend to the helper, the kin, and the familiar face, and it takes training to carry them further. Both were right that everything depends on what is done next. They disagreed about which part of our equipment to call “nature,” and that is partly a dispute about a word. Mencius meant by nature what is distinctive of human beings and grows under good conditions. Xunzi meant what is present at birth without effort.

浮現出來的圖像，給了古代的每一方一些東西。孟子說對了：同情與公平感的開端，在教導之前就已經存在；而告子那潭沒有方向的水，是處境最糟的一種看法。荀子也說對了：我們同樣生而具有偏私、欲望與隨從的傾向；而最初的道德衝動是狹窄的，它們只及於幫忙的人、親屬與熟悉的臉，需要訓練才能推得更遠。兩人都說對的是：一切取決於接下來怎麼做。他們的分歧在於：把我們天賦裡的哪一部分叫作「性」？而這有一部分是關於一個字的爭論。孟子所說的「性」，是人之所以為人的特點、在良好的條件下會生長的東西。荀子所說的「性」，是生下來就有、不必費力的東西。

Something real is left when the word is set aside, and it is the choice between the gardener and the carpenter. A parent or a state that believes people are shoots to be tended will rely on example, trust, and room to grow. One that believes people are crooked timber will rely on rules, incentives, and supervision. Each policy tends to produce the evidence that supports it, since people who are trusted often become trustworthy and people who are watched often give cause for watching. That may be the most important fact about human nature: it responds to what is believed about it.

把那個字擱在一邊之後，還剩下某個真實的東西，那就是「園丁」與「木匠」之間的選擇。一位相信人是有待照料的嫩芽的父母，或一個這樣相信的國家，會倚重榜樣、信任與成長的空間。相信人是枸木的，會倚重規則、誘因與監督。每一種方針，都傾向於製造出支持它的證據；因為被信任的人往往變得值得信任，被監視的人往往給出了該被監視的理由。這也許是關於人性最重要的一個事實：它會回應人們對它的看法。

The two passages every Chinese student once knew by heart. The Chinese is given under each.

Mencius 6A2; Xunzi, chapter 23, “Human Nature Is Bad” 《孟子・告子上》第二章；《荀子・性惡》 · translated for this series from the Chinese text

Did he really say that? · 這句話真的是他說的嗎？

“Man is born free, and everywhere he is in chains.” (Rousseau)「人生而自由，卻無處不在枷鎖之中。」（盧梭）

Authentic, and not about innocence · 是真的，但談的不是純真

It is the first sentence of the first chapter of The Social Contract (1762), and it is usually quoted as a lament for lost natural freedom. The next sentences show a different purpose. Rousseau says that he does not know how the change came about and that his question is what can make it legitimate. The book defends a kind of chain: the laws that free people give themselves. The phrase people have in mind when they misuse this one, the “noble savage,” appears nowhere in Rousseau. It comes from a play by the English poet John Dryden, written forty years before Rousseau was born.

這是《社會契約論》（1762）第一章的第一句話；它通常被引來哀嘆失去的自然自由。接下來的幾句話顯示的是另一個目的。盧梭說，他不知道這個轉變是怎麼發生的；他要問的是：什麼能使它成為正當的？這本書辯護的是某一種枷鎖：自由的人為自己訂立的法律。人們誤用這句話時心裡想的那個詞，「高貴的野蠻人」，在盧梭的著作裡哪裡也沒有出現。它出自英國詩人德萊頓的一齣戲，寫於盧梭出生前四十年。

Both sides · 正反兩面

Objections and replies 反駁與回應

Objection 1 · 反駁

The whole debate is confused. “Good” and “bad” are standards that societies apply. Nature is neither, as a river is neither just nor unjust.

整場辯論是混淆的。「善」與「惡」是社會所施加的標準。本性兩者都不是，就像一條河既不公正也不不公正。

Reply · 回應

This is Gaozi’s position, and it has a point: the words are evaluations. But the question can be restated without them. Do human beings, before instruction, tend toward the conduct that makes shared life possible, or away from it? That is a question about dispositions, and it has an answer whether or not we attach the words. Infants who prefer helpers are evidence about it.

這是告子的立場，而它有它的道理：這些詞是評價。但這個問題可以不用這些詞重新表述。人在受教導之前，是傾向於使共同生活成為可能的那種行為，還是背離它？這是一個關於傾向的問題；不管我們貼不貼上那些詞，它都有答案。偏好幫助者的嬰兒，就是關於它的證據。

Objection 2 · 反駁

If people are born good, there is no explaining how evil came into the world. Someone must have been the first to corrupt the rest.

如果人生而善，就無法解釋惡是怎麼來到世界上的。總得有人是第一個去腐化其他人的。

Reply · 回應

The objection hits Rousseau harder than Mencius. Rousseau must explain how good creatures invented bad institutions, and his story of the first fence is not much of an explanation. Mencius never said that we have good impulses only. He said that we also have a body with appetites, which he called the lesser part, and that a person goes wrong by letting the lesser part lead. His view needs no first corrupter. It needs only neglect.

這個反駁對盧梭的打擊，比對孟子的大。盧梭必須解釋：善良的生物，怎麼會發明出惡劣的制度？而他那個「第一道圍籬」的故事，算不上什麼解釋。孟子從來沒有說過我們「只有」善的衝動。他說我們還有一個帶著種種欲望的身體，他稱之為「小體」；一個人之所以走偏，是因為讓小體來帶頭。他的看法不需要一個「最初的腐化者」。它只需要疏於照料。

Objection 3 · 反駁

If people are born bad, the sages who invented morality could not have existed. Where did they get the goodness to see that it was needed?

如果人生而惡，那些創制道德的聖人就不可能存在。他們從哪裡得到那份善，看出道德是需要的？

Reply · 回應

This was pressed against Xunzi in his own day, and he answers it in the same chapter. The sages did not differ from other people in nature. They differed in the accumulation of thought and effort. A potter is not made of clay, and one need not be good by nature in order to work out, by intelligence, that a life without rules is miserable for everyone. The reply shows what Xunzi really believed in. He trusted human intelligence more than human feeling.

在荀子自己的時代，就有人拿這個來質問他；而他在同一篇裡回答了。聖人在本性上與別人並無不同。不同的是思慮與努力的積累。陶匠並不是陶土做的；一個人不必本性善良，也能憑著知性推想出：沒有規矩的生活對每個人都是悲慘的。這個回答顯示了荀子真正相信的是什麼。比起人的情感，他更信任人的知性。

the four sprouts四端

Ch. 四端 sìduān

Mencius’s four inborn beginnings of virtue: compassion, shame, deference, and the sense of right and wrong.

孟子所說的四種天生的德行開端：惻隱、羞惡、恭敬（辭讓）、是非。

deliberate effort偽（人為）

Ch. 偽 wěi

Xunzi’s term for what is achieved by conscious activity, as opposed to what is inborn.

荀子的用語：由有意識的活動所成就的東西，相對於天生的東西。

state of nature自然狀態

The condition of human beings without government, real or imagined.

人在沒有政府的情況下的狀態；不論是真實的還是想像的。

innate天生的

L. innatus ‘inborn’

Present from birth and not acquired by experience.

生來就有，而不是由經驗取得的。

disposition傾向

A standing tendency to act or feel in a certain way under certain conditions.

在某些條件下以某種方式行動或感受的常態趨勢。

in-group bias內團體偏私

The tendency to favor members of one’s own group.

偏袒自己所屬群體成員的傾向。

pity憐憫

Fr. pitié

In Rousseau, a natural reluctance to see any feeling creature suffer.

在盧梭，指不願看見任何有感覺的生命受苦的天然情感。

Dharma and the West · 佛法與西方哲學

A view from the Dharma 佛法怎麼看

☸

The Buddha makes two statements that seem to belong to opposite camps. “This mind, mendicants, is radiant. But it’s corrupted by visiting corruptions.” The defilements are called visitors. They arrive and can be made to leave, and what they cover is bright. That is close to Ox Mountain. But when a monk suggested that an infant must be free of the fetters, since it has no concepts with which to form a wrong view, the Buddha corrected him. A baby has no notion of sensual pleasure or of other beings, “yet the underlying tendency to sensual desire still lies within them,” and the tendency to ill will too. That is close to Xunzi. The two statements are consistent. Latent tendencies toward greed, hatred, and delusion are present from the start, and none of them belongs to the mind’s nature, since all can be removed.

佛陀說過兩句話，看起來分屬相反的陣營。「比丘們，這個心是光淨的，卻被客塵煩惱所染污。」煩惱被稱為「客」。它們是來訪的，也可以被請走；而它們所遮蓋的，是明亮的。這很接近牛山。然而，當一位比丘提出「嬰兒必定沒有結使，因為他沒有藉以形成邪見的概念」時，佛陀糾正了他。嬰兒沒有欲樂的概念，也沒有其他眾生的概念，「然而欲貪的隨眠仍然潛伏在他裡面」，瞋恚的隨眠也是。這很接近荀子。這兩句話是一致的。貪、瞋、癡的潛在傾向從一開始就在；而它們沒有一樣屬於心的本性，因為全都可以被去除。

The Mahāyāna developed the first statement into the teaching of Buddha-nature: every sentient being has within it, covered by defilements, the nature of an awakened one. A companion page asks which Western and Chinese view of human nature this comes closest to, and where the comparison fails.

大乘把第一句話發展成了佛性的教導：每一個有情眾生裡面，都有被煩惱覆蓋著的覺悟者的本性。姊妹篇要問的是：這最接近西方與中國的哪一種人性觀？這個比較又在哪裡行不通？
