A8 · Knowledge 知識

Will the Sun Rise Tomorrow? 明天太陽會升起嗎？

Hume’s problem of induction: why no amount of past experience can prove anything about the future, and why we go on trusting it all the same.
休謨的歸納問題：為什麼再多的過往經驗，也無法對未來證明任何事；以及為什麼我們照樣繼續信任它。

A8 Big Questions · 大哉問Knowledge · 知識Advanced · 高級1,243 words · about 25 min
The big idea · 大觀念

Every belief about what we have not observed rests on the assumption that nature will go on as before. That assumption cannot be proved by logic and cannot be proved by experience without circularity.

我們對未曾觀察之事的每一個信念，都建立在「自然會照舊運行」這個假定上。這個假定無法用邏輯證明；用經驗來證明，又免不了循環。

The essay · 課文

Will the Sun Rise Tomorrow? 明天太陽會升起嗎？

01The safest bet in the world 世界上最穩的賭注

Nothing seems more certain than that the sun will rise tomorrow. Ask how you know it, and the first answer is that it always has. Ask why that is a reason, and the answer is that things which have always happened go on happening. Ask how you know that, and the honest reply is that it has always been so in the past. At this point the same reason has been given twice. David Hume noticed the repetition in the 1730s, when he was in his twenties, and nobody has found a way out of it that everyone accepts.

沒有什麼事看起來比「明天太陽會升起」更確定。問你怎麼知道，第一個答案是：它一向如此。問這為什麼算是理由，答案是：一向發生的事會繼續發生。再問你怎麼知道這一點，誠實的回答是：過去一向是這樣。到這裡，同一個理由已經給了兩次。休謨在 1730 年代注意到這個重複，當時他才二十幾歲；至今沒有人找到一條人人接受的出路。

02Two kinds of truth 兩種真理

Hume begins by sorting everything we can know into two kinds. Relations of ideas are truths such as “three times five is equal to the half of thirty.” They can be established by thinking alone, and to deny one is to contradict oneself. Matters of fact are truths such as “the sun is shining.” They can be learned only from experience, and the denial of any of them is perfectly conceivable. His example has become famous: “That the sun will not rise to-morrow is no less intelligible a proposition, and implies no more contradiction than the affirmation, that it will rise.” So no matter of fact can be proved by pure reasoning. If we know the sun will rise, we know it from experience.

休謨一開始，把我們能知道的一切分成兩類。「觀念的關係」是像「三乘五等於三十的一半」這樣的真理；它們光靠思考就能確立，否定其中任何一個就會自相矛盾。「實際的事情」是像「太陽正照耀著」這樣的真理；它們只能從經驗得知，而它們當中任何一個的否定，都完全可以設想。他舉的例子後來很有名：「『明天太陽不會升起』這個命題，並不比『它會升起』這個斷言更難理解，也沒有蘊含更多的矛盾。」所以，沒有任何實際的事情可以靠純粹的推理來證明。如果我們知道太陽會升起，那是從經驗知道的。

03What we see when one ball hits another 一顆球撞上另一顆時，我們看見了什麼

Experience tells us directly only about what we are now perceiving or remember. To get beyond that, to the fire in the next room or the eclipse next year, we reason from cause to effect. Hume therefore asks what we observe when we observe one thing causing another. One billiard ball rolls toward a second; they touch; the second moves. We see the first motion, the contact, and the second motion. We do not see any power in the first ball that forces the second to move. A person who had never seen bodies collide could not tell, from looking at the first ball, what would happen. What experience adds is repetition: events of the first kind have always been followed by events of the second. He calls this constant conjunction. The idea that the effect must follow is something we supply ourselves.

經驗直接告訴我們的，只有我們此刻知覺到的、或記得的事。要越過這個範圍，推到隔壁房間的火、明年的日食，我們靠的是從原因到結果的推理。所以休謨問：當我們觀察到一件事「造成」另一件事時，我們到底觀察到了什麼？一顆撞球滾向第二顆；兩者接觸；第二顆動了。我們看見第一個運動、接觸、第二個運動。我們並沒有看見第一顆球裡有什麼「力量」，逼得第二顆非動不可。一個從沒見過物體碰撞的人，光看第一顆球，是說不出會發生什麼的。經驗添上的是重複：第一類事件之後，總是跟著第二類事件。他把這稱為「恆常連結」。至於「結果必定跟著發生」這個觀念，是我們自己添上去的。

04The circle 那個圓圈

Now the problem can be stated exactly. All our conclusions from experience take the form: such things have always gone together, so they will go together again. That step is valid only with an extra premise, that the future will resemble the past. How might the premise be established? It is not a relation of ideas, since a change in the course of nature is conceivable. It must then be a matter of fact, known from experience. But “all inferences from experience suppose, as their foundation, that the future will resemble the past.” To argue that nature will be uniform because it has been uniform so far is to use the principle in order to prove the principle. In Hume’s words, it “is begging the question.”

現在可以把問題精確地說出來了。我們從經驗得出的一切結論，都是這個形式：這些事一向相伴出現，所以它們會再度相伴出現。這一步要有效，需要一個額外的前提：未來會與過去相似。這個前提要怎麼確立？它不是觀念的關係，因為自然進程的改變是可以設想的。那它就必定是實際的事情，要從經驗得知。可是「一切從經驗出發的推論，都預設未來會與過去相似，以此作為基礎」。主張「自然會保持齊一，因為它到目前為止都是齊一的」，就是用這個原則來證明這個原則。用休謨的話說，這「是乞題」。

Notice what the argument does and does not claim. Hume does not say that the sun will fail to rise, or that expecting it is foolish. He says that the expectation is not produced by any argument, because no good argument for it exists.

注意這個論證主張了什麼、沒有主張什麼。休謨沒有說太陽將不會升起，也沒有說期待它升起是愚蠢的。他說的是：這個期待不是由任何論證產生的，因為根本沒有支持它的好論證。

05Custom 習慣

If reasoning does not produce our expectations, what does? Hume’s answer is habit. After seeing two kinds of event joined many times, the mind passes from one to the other automatically, and feels that passage as necessity. “Custom, then, is the great guide of human life. It is that principle alone which renders our experience useful to us.” Infants and animals learn from experience in just this way, and nobody supposes that they do it by constructing arguments. Hume calls this a skeptical solution, because it explains why we believe and leaves the question of justification where it was. He was content with that. Nature, he observed, has not left so important a matter to our fallible reasoning.

如果產生我們期待的不是推理，那是什麼？休謨的答案是習慣。看見兩類事件多次相連之後，心靈就自動地從一個過渡到另一個，並且把這個過渡感受為必然。「所以，習慣是人類生活的偉大嚮導。唯獨這個原則，使我們的經驗對我們有用。」嬰兒與動物正是這樣從經驗學習的，而沒有人認為牠們是靠建構論證辦到的。休謨把這稱為「懷疑論的解決」，因為它解釋了我們為什麼相信，卻讓證成的問題留在原地。他對此感到滿意。他說，這麼重要的事，自然並沒有交給我們那容易出錯的推理。

06The chicken and the hare 雞與兔

Bertrand Russell gave the problem its most memorable form. A chicken is fed by the farmer every morning of its life and comes to expect its food when he appears. “The man who has fed the chicken every day throughout its life at last wrings its neck instead, showing that more refined views as to the uniformity of nature would have been useful to the chicken.” The chicken’s reasoning was the same as ours about the sunrise. Europeans were in a similar position with “all swans are white,” on the evidence of every swan anyone had seen, until black swans were found in Australia in 1697.

羅素給了這個問題最令人難忘的形式。一隻雞，一輩子每天早上都由農夫餵食，於是一見他出現就期待食物。「那個在雞的一生中每天餵牠的人，最後卻扭斷了牠的脖子；這表明，對自然的齊一性若有更精細的看法，對這隻雞會很有用。」這隻雞的推理，跟我們對日出的推理是一樣的。歐洲人對「所有天鵝都是白的」也曾處在類似的位置上，憑的是任何人見過的每一隻天鵝；直到 1697 年在澳洲發現了黑天鵝。

Chinese tradition supplies a pair of stories that mark the two ways of going wrong. In the Han Feizi a farmer sees a hare run into a tree stump and break its neck, and he puts down his plow and waits by the stump for another (守株待兔). He has generalized from a single case. In the Liezi a man of Qi cannot eat or sleep for fear that the sky will fall (杞人憂天). He refuses to rely on any regularity at all. Everyone agrees that good sense lies somewhere between the two. Hume’s point is that we cannot prove where.

中國的傳統提供了一對故事，正好標出兩種走錯的方式。在《韓非子》裡，一個農夫看見一隻兔子撞上樹樁、折斷了脖子，於是放下犁，守在樹樁旁等下一隻（守株待兔）。他是從單一個案做了概括。在《列子》裡，一個杞國人因為害怕天會塌下來而寢食難安（杞人憂天）。他拒絕倚賴任何規律。人人都同意，明智的做法在兩者之間的某處。休謨的重點是：我們無法證明是在哪裡。

07Four replies 四種回應

Hume’s problem has been called the scandal of philosophy, and the attempts to answer it fall into four groups. Kant held that causality is not learned from experience at all. It is a form the mind imposes, a condition of having any ordered experience, and so it is guaranteed to hold of whatever we can experience. Karl Popper accepted Hume’s argument and concluded that science does not use induction. Scientists make bold guesses and try to refute them, and a theory that survives is never thereby shown to be true or even probable. Hans Reichenbach offered a practical defense. We cannot know that nature is uniform, but if it is, induction will find the pattern, and if it is not, no method will work, so we lose nothing by using it. And P. F. Strawson argued that the demand is confused. To ask whether it is reasonable to rely on induction is like asking whether the law is legal: relying on past experience is part of what “reasonable” means.

休謨的問題被稱為「哲學的醜聞」；回答它的嘗試可以分成四組。康德主張，因果性根本不是從經驗學來的。它是心靈加上去的形式，是擁有任何有秩序的經驗的條件；所以對我們所能經驗的一切，它保證成立。波普接受休謨的論證，並且推出：科學並不使用歸納。科學家提出大膽的猜測，再設法反駁它們；一個存活下來的理論，並不因此就被證明為真，甚至也沒有被證明為很可能為真。萊辛巴哈提出一種實用的辯護：我們無法知道自然是不是齊一的；但如果它是，歸納法會找到那個規律；如果它不是，任何方法都不管用；所以用歸納法，我們什麼也不會損失。史陶生則論證，這個要求本身就搞混了。問「倚賴歸納是否合理」，就像問「法律是否合法」：倚賴過往經驗，本來就是「合理」這個詞的一部分意思。

Each reply has its critics, and in 1955 Nelson Goodman made the problem harder. Call an object “grue” if it is first examined before the year 2100 and is green, or is not examined before then and is blue. Every emerald seen so far has been green, and every one has also been grue. Yet we expect future emeralds to be green and not grue. Even if induction is granted, then, something must tell us which patterns to project, and that further rule cannot come from the evidence, which fits both.

每一種回應都有批評者；而在 1955 年，古德曼把問題弄得更難了。把一個東西稱為「綠藍」（grue），如果它在 2100 年之前首次被檢視而且是綠的，或者在那之前沒被檢視過而且是藍的。迄今見過的每一顆祖母綠都是綠的，也都是「綠藍」的。然而我們預期未來的祖母綠是綠的，不是「綠藍」的。所以，就算承認了歸納法，還是得有什麼東西告訴我們該投射哪些規律；而這條進一步的規則，不可能來自證據，因為證據跟兩者都相符。

08Living with it 與它共處

What should one conclude? Not that anything goes. Hume himself drew a sober moral: “A wise man … proportions his belief to the evidence.” The lesson of the problem is that evidence comes in degrees and never amounts to proof. Scientific laws are the best-supported expectations we have, and they remain expectations. This is the second unit’s conclusion, and it matches the first. Logic could tell us that our premises conflict and could not tell us which to give up. Experience can tell us what has happened so far, and what will happen next is something we infer at our own risk. The sun will rise tomorrow. You are right to be sure of it, and you cannot say why without assuming it.

該得出什麼結論？不是「怎樣都行」。休謨自己引出了一個清醒的教訓：「有智慧的人……使他的信念與證據成比例。」這個問題的教訓是：證據有程度之分，而且永遠不等於證明。科學定律是我們擁有的、根據最充分的期待，而它們仍然是期待。這是第二單元的結論，而它跟第一單元的結論相呼應。邏輯能告訴我們前提互相衝突，卻不能告訴我們該放棄哪一個。經驗能告訴我們到目前為止發生了什麼；至於接下來會發生什麼，那是我們自負風險去推論的。明天太陽會升起。你對此有把握是對的；而你說不出為什麼，除非先假定它。

Thought experiment · 思想實驗

Try it: the chicken’s reasoning 親手做一次：那隻雞的推理

Be the chicken. Each morning the farmer comes. Watch your confidence grow as the evidence piles up, and notice that the method you are using is the one you use for the sunrise. Then try to justify it.

你來當那隻雞。每天早上農夫都會來。看著你的信心隨著證據累積而上升，並且留意：你用的方法，就是你對日出用的方法。然後試著替它找理由。

Day 0 · 第 0 天
50%

Confidence here follows a rule of Laplace’s: after n mornings with grain and none without, expect grain tomorrow with probability (n + 1) / (n + 2). The rule itself assumes that tomorrow is drawn from the same process as the days before.

這裡的信心照拉普拉斯的一條規則計算：連續 n 個早上有穀子、沒有例外，則明天有穀子的機率是 (n + 1) / (n + 2)。這條規則本身就假定：明天跟之前的日子，出自同一個過程。

Now justify the method 現在替這個方法找理由

Now defend the method. Why is it reasonable to expect the future to resemble the past?

現在替這個方法辯護。為什麼期待未來會與過去相似，是合理的？

Primary text · 原典

In his own words 原典怎麼說

Hume first set out the argument in his Treatise of Human Nature, which almost nobody read. The Enquiry is his second attempt, shorter and written to please.

休謨最早是在《人性論》裡提出這個論證的，而那本書幾乎沒有人讀。《人類理解研究》是他的第二次嘗試，比較短，而且是寫來讓人讀得愉快的。

David Hume, An Enquiry Concerning Human Understanding (1748), sections IV and V 休謨《人類理解研究》第四、五章 · in the original English (public domain)

Did he really say that? · 這句話真的是他說的嗎？

“Extraordinary claims require extraordinary evidence.” (David Hume)「非凡的主張需要非凡的證據。」（休謨）

Carl Sagan, 1980 · 卡爾・薩根，1980 年

The slogan in this form comes from the astronomer Carl Sagan’s television series Cosmos. It is often described as “Hume’s maxim,” and the thought is certainly Hume’s. What he wrote, in his essay on miracles, is the plainer sentence quoted above: “A wise man, therefore, proportions his belief to the evidence.”

這個說法的這種形式，出自天文學家卡爾・薩根的電視影集《宇宙》。它常被稱為「休謨的箴言」，而這個想法確實是休謨的。他在論神蹟的那一章裡寫下的，是上面引過的那個比較平實的句子：「所以，有智慧的人使他的信念與證據成比例。」

Both sides · 正反兩面

Objections and replies 反駁與回應

Objection 1 · 反駁

Induction obviously works. Science built on it has given us vaccines and spacecraft. A method with that record needs no further defense.

歸納法顯然行得通。建立在它上面的科學，給了我們疫苗與太空船。有這種紀錄的方法，不需要別的辯護。

Reply · 回應

This is the most natural reply, and it is the circle again. The record shows that induction has worked. To conclude that it will go on working is an inductive inference. Hume never denied the record. What he wanted to know was what entitles us to extend it.

這是最自然的回應，而它又是那個圓圈。這份紀錄顯示歸納法「一直」行得通。要推出它「將會繼續」行得通，是一個歸納推論。休謨從來沒有否認這份紀錄。他想知道的是：我們憑什麼有資格把它延伸下去。

Objection 2 · 反駁

The problem only shows that induction is not deduction. Of course past evidence does not entail future events. Probable reasoning was never supposed to give certainty.

這個問題只是顯示歸納不是演繹。過去的證據當然不蘊涵未來的事件。或然的推理，本來就不是要給人確定性的。

Reply · 回應

Hume would accept the first sentence and deny that it disposes of the difficulty. His argument applies to probability too. To say that past observations make a future event likely, we must assume that past frequencies are a guide to future ones, and that is the same principle in a weaker form.

休謨會接受第一句話，但否認這樣就解決了困難。他的論證對或然性同樣適用。要說過去的觀察使某個未來事件「很可能」發生，我們必須假定過去的頻率是未來頻率的指引；而這是同一個原則，只是比較弱的形式。

Objection 3 · 反駁

If the argument were sound, we could never choose between a physicist and a fortune-teller. Since we plainly can, the argument must contain a mistake.

如果這個論證是健全的，我們就永遠無法在物理學家與算命師之間做選擇。既然我們顯然可以，這個論證裡一定有錯。

Reply · 回應

This is Moore’s move from the lesson on dreaming, applied to induction, and many philosophers think it is right. But knowing that a mistake exists is different from finding it. After nearly three centuries there is no agreement about which premise fails. That is a reason to regard the problem as deep. It is no reason to stop consulting physicists.

這是「夢」那一課裡摩爾的那一招，用在歸納上；許多哲學家認為它是對的。但知道有一個錯誤存在，跟找到它是兩回事。將近三百年過去了，對於是哪一個前提出錯，仍然沒有共識。這是把這個問題看得很深的理由；這並不是不再請教物理學家的理由。

Terms of art · 哲學術語

The vocabulary of the subject 這門學問的用語

induction歸納

L. inducere ‘to lead in’

Reasoning from observed cases to unobserved ones, or to a general law.

從觀察過的個案，推到未觀察的個案，或推到普遍的定律。

relations of ideas / matters of fact觀念的關係／實際的事情

Hume’s two kinds of truth: those whose denial is contradictory, and those known only from experience. The division is called Hume’s fork.

休謨的兩類真理：否定它就會矛盾的，以及只能從經驗得知的。這個劃分稱為「休謨之叉」。

uniformity of nature自然的齊一性

The principle that unobserved cases will resemble observed ones.

未觀察的個案會與已觀察的個案相似，這個原則。

constant conjunction恆常連結

The regular following of one kind of event by another, which is all we observe of causation.

一類事件之後規律地跟著另一類事件；這就是我們對因果所觀察到的全部。

necessary connection必然連結

The supposed tie by which a cause forces its effect, of which Hume says we have no impression.

原因「逼出」結果的那個假想的紐帶；休謨說，我們對它沒有任何印象。

custom習慣

L. consuetudo ‘habit’

In Hume, the habit of expectation produced by repeated experience.

在休謨，指由重複的經驗產生的期待習慣。

falsification否證

L. falsus ‘false’ + facere ‘to make’

Popper’s account of scientific method: theories cannot be proved, only refuted.

波普對科學方法的說明：理論無法被證明，只能被反駁。

grue綠藍

‘green’ + ‘blue’

Goodman’s invented predicate, used to show that evidence alone does not tell us which regularities to project.

古德曼發明的謂詞，用來說明：單憑證據，不足以告訴我們該投射哪些規律。

    描述一個你完全有把握的、關於明天的信念。列出你的證據，然後說出是什麼原則把你從證據帶到這個信念。

Dharma and the West · 佛法與西方哲學

A view from the Dharma 佛法怎麼看

☸

The Buddha’s summary of causation is strikingly spare. “When this exists, that comes to be; with the arising of this, that arises. When this does not exist, that does not come to be; with the cessation of this, that ceases” (Saṃyutta Nikāya 12.61). The formula states a regular dependence between conditions and what they condition. It says nothing about a power or force that passes from one to the other. Hume, analyzing the billiard balls, found constant conjunction and no necessary connection, and a reader of the early texts may feel that the two accounts are describing the same thing. Nāgārjuna later pressed the point as hard as Hume did, arguing that the notion of a cause producing its effect by an inner power cannot be made coherent.

佛陀對因果的概括，簡省得驚人。「此有故彼有，此生故彼生；此無故彼無，此滅故彼滅」（《相應部》12.61）。這個公式陳述的是條件與被條件者之間一種有規律的依待。它完全沒有提到有什麼力量從一方傳到另一方。休謨分析撞球，找到了恆常連結，沒有找到必然連結；讀早期經典的人也許會覺得，這兩種說法描述的是同一件事。龍樹後來把這一點追問得跟休謨一樣緊，論證說「原因憑內在的力量產生結果」這個概念，沒辦法說得前後一致。

The traditions differ over what follows. Hume concludes that our causal beliefs rest on habit and leaves it there. Buddhist philosophers treat the regularity as discoverable and as the basis of the path: because suffering arises from conditions, it ceases when the conditions cease. They were aware of the difficulty. The Indian materialists, the Cārvākas, rejected inference altogether on the ground that no number of observed cases can establish a universal rule, which is Hume’s objection many centuries early. Dharmakīrti’s reply was that some connections are grounded in the natures of things and are not mere regularities, as smoke depends on fire by being produced from it. Whether that reply succeeds is the question of whether Hume can be answered (Dharma and the West, D4 and D13).

兩個傳統的差別，在於接下來怎麼走。休謨的結論是我們的因果信念建立在習慣上，然後就停在那裡。佛教哲學家則認為這種規律是可以被發現的，而且是道的基礎：因為苦從條件生起，所以條件止息，苦就止息。他們知道這裡有困難。印度的唯物論者順世派（Cārvāka）整個否定了比量（推論），理由是觀察到再多個案，也無法確立一條普遍規則；這是早了許多個世紀的休謨式反駁。法稱的回應是：有些連結是根植於事物的本性的，不只是規律而已，就像煙依待火，是因為煙由火所生。這個回應成不成功，就是「休謨能不能被回答」這個問題（佛法與西方哲學 D4、D13）。
