What Is a Friend? 朋友是什麼？

Aristotle’s three kinds of friendship, Montaigne’s one friend, a zither player who broke his instrument, and the man who said that his parents gave him life but Bao understood him.
亞里斯多德的三種友誼、蒙田唯一的那位朋友、一位摔碎了琴的琴師，以及那個說「生我者父母，知我者鮑子也」的人。

A23 Big Questions · 大哉問The Good Life · 美好人生Advanced · 高級1,318 words · about 25 min
The big idea · 大觀念

We use one word for the person we do business with, the person we laugh with, and the person who knows us. Philosophers have asked whether these are the same relation in different strengths or different relations altogether, and why a life without the last kind seems not worth living.

跟我們有生意往來的人、跟我們一起笑的人、了解我們的人，我們都用同一個詞稱呼。哲學家問過：這些是同一種關係的不同強度，還是根本不同的關係？還有，為什麼少了最後那一種，人生似乎就不值得過？

The essay · 課文

What Is a Friend? 朋友是什麼？

01The thing no one would live without 誰也不肯沒有的東西

Aristotle gave two of the ten books of his Ethics to friendship, more than to any other single subject, and he opens with a sentence that states its importance flatly: “without friends no one would choose to live, though he had all other goods.” The rich need friends in order to have someone to benefit, the poor need them as a refuge, the young to keep them from error, and the old to care for them. Modern moral philosophy has had much less to say. Its main theories are about what we owe to everyone, and a friend is someone to whom we give what we do not owe and from whom we expect what we could not demand. The ancient writers, Greek and Chinese, treated the subject as central, and the best way into it is still Aristotle’s question: what is it that we like when we like a person?

亞里斯多德把他的《倫理學》十卷當中的兩卷給了友誼，比給任何別的單一主題的都多；而他用來開頭的那句話，直截了當地說出了它的重要：「沒有朋友，誰也不會選擇活下去，即使他擁有其他一切的善。」富人需要朋友，才有人可以施惠；窮人需要朋友作為避難所；年輕人需要朋友使他們不犯錯；老人需要朋友照顧他們。現代的道德哲學對此說得少得多。它的主要理論談的是我們「欠每一個人」什麼；而朋友，是我們給他並不欠他的東西、並且從他那裡期待我們無法要求的東西的那個人。古代的作者，不論希臘的還是中國的，都把這個題目當作核心；而進入它最好的路，仍然是亞里斯多德的那個問題：當我們喜歡一個人的時候，我們喜歡的是什麼？

02Three kinds 三種

There are three things, he says, for which we can love anything: because it is useful, because it is pleasant, or because it is good. “There are therefore three kinds of friendship.” In the first, each party values the other for some advantage. Business partners are friends in this way, and so are many neighbors and colleagues. In the second, each enjoys the other’s company: the people we drink, travel, or play with. Aristotle does not despise these. He observes two things about them. In both, the other person is loved for something he supplies, and so they last only as long as the supply. “Those who love each other for their utility do not love each other for themselves but in virtue of some good which they get from each other.” When the usefulness ends or tastes change, the friendship dissolves, and no one is to blame.

他說，我們之所以能愛任何東西，原因有三：因為它有用，因為它令人愉快，或因為它是善的。「所以友誼有三種。」在第一種裡，雙方各自因為某種好處而看重對方。生意上的夥伴就是這樣的朋友；許多鄰居與同事也是。在第二種裡，雙方各自享受對方的陪伴：跟我們一起喝酒、旅行或玩樂的人。亞里斯多德並不輕視這些。他對它們觀察到兩點。在這兩種裡，對方之所以被愛，都是因為他所提供的某樣東西；所以它們只能維持到那個提供停止為止。「因為有用而彼此相愛的人，並不是因為對方本身而愛對方，而是因為從對方得到的某種好處。」用處沒了，或者口味變了，友誼就消散了，而這不能怪誰。

The third kind is different. “Perfect friendship is the friendship of men who are good, and alike in virtue,” and such friends “wish well to their friends for their sake.” Here the person himself is what is loved, his character and not his services. This friendship includes the other two, since good people are both useful and pleasant to each other, and it lasts, because character is the most durable thing about a person. It is also rare. It needs time and shared life, “for men cannot know each other till they have eaten salt together,” and nobody can have it with many people, any more than one can be in love with many at once. Aristotle adds a phrase that has been repeated ever since: a friend of this kind is “another self.”

第三種不一樣。「完美的友誼，是善良而在德行上相似的人之間的友誼」；這樣的朋友「為了朋友本身的緣故而願他好」。在這裡，被愛的是這個人本身，是他的品格，而不是他的效勞。這種友誼包含了另外兩種，因為好人彼此既有用又令人愉快；而且它持久，因為品格是一個人身上最經久的東西。它也很罕見。它需要時間，需要共同的生活；「人若沒有一起吃過鹽，就無法彼此了解」。而且沒有人能跟很多人擁有它，就像一個人不可能同時愛上很多人。亞里斯多德還加了一個從此被一再重複的說法：這一種朋友，是「另一個自己」。

03Because it was he 因為是他

Aristotle’s account makes friendship reasonable: we love the good because it is good. The French essayist Michel de Montaigne, who had one such friend and lost him, thought that this leaves out the center of the experience. He met Étienne de La Boétie at a crowded feast in Bordeaux when both were young magistrates, and from that first meeting, he says, nothing was so near to either as the other. La Boétie died a few years later, at thirty-two. Montaigne wrote the essay on friendship some ten years afterward. In such a friendship, he says, two souls “mix and work themselves into one piece, with so universal a mixture, that there is no more sign of the seam by which they were first conjoined.” Asked for the reason, he can give none. “If a man should importune me to give a reason why I loved him, I find it could no otherwise be expressed, than by making answer: because it was he, because it was I.”

亞里斯多德的說明，使友誼成為合理的：我們愛善，因為它是善的。法國的隨筆作家蒙田有過一位這樣的朋友，又失去了他；他認為，這個說明漏掉了這種經驗的核心。他是在波爾多一場擁擠的宴會上遇見拉波埃西的，當時兩人都是年輕的法官；他說，從那第一次見面起，對兩人來說就沒有什麼比對方更親近的了。幾年後，拉波埃西去世，三十二歲。大約十年之後，蒙田寫下了那篇論友誼的隨筆。他說，在這樣的友誼裡，兩個靈魂「混合、交融成一體，融合得那麼徹底，以致於當初把它們縫合起來的那道接縫，再也看不出痕跡」。問他理由，他給不出來。「如果有人逼我說出我為什麼愛他，我覺得除了這樣回答之外，沒有別的說法：因為是他，因為是我。」

The disagreement is real. For Aristotle a friend is loved for qualities, and anyone with the same qualities would in principle do as well. For Montaigne the friend cannot be replaced, and a list of his virtues would explain nothing. Both seem to be describing something true. We do admire our closest friends, and we would be dismayed to learn that they were cruel. Yet we would not exchange them for someone more admirable.

這個分歧是真實的。對亞里斯多德來說，朋友是因為種種特質而被愛的；而任何具有同樣特質的人，原則上都一樣可以。對蒙田來說，那位朋友無可取代；把他的德行列成清單，什麼也解釋不了。兩人描述的似乎都是真的。我們確實欽佩自己最親近的朋友；如果得知他們是殘忍的人，我們會大為沮喪。然而，我們也不會拿他們去換一個更值得欽佩的人。

04The one who knows the tune 知音

The Chinese tradition has its own word for Montaigne’s kind of friend, and it comes from a story. Bo Ya was a master of the zither, and Zhong Ziqi was good at listening. When Bo Ya played with his mind on high mountains, Zhong Ziqi said, “Splendid! Lofty as Mount Tai!” When his mind was on flowing water, Zhong Ziqi said, “Splendid! Broad as the great rivers!” Whatever Bo Ya had in mind, Zhong Ziqi never failed to grasp it. When Zhong Ziqi died, another version adds, Bo Ya broke the zither and cut its strings and never played again, judging that there was no one left in the world worth playing for. The phrase zhīyīn, “one who knows the tone,” has meant a true friend ever since. What the story picks out is being understood. Zhong Ziqi did nothing for Bo Ya and gave him no pleasure that a paying audience could not have given. He heard what was meant.

中國的傳統，對蒙田那一種朋友有它自己的詞；而這個詞來自一個故事。伯牙善於彈琴，鍾子期善於聽。伯牙彈琴，心裡想著高山，鍾子期說：「善哉！峩峩兮若泰山！」心裡想著流水，鍾子期說：「善哉！洋洋兮若江河！」伯牙心裡想的是什麼，鍾子期必定聽得出來。另一個版本接著說：鍾子期死了，伯牙「破琴絕弦，終身不復鼓琴，以為世無足復為鼓琴者」。從此以後，「知音」（懂得那個音的人）就是真朋友的意思。這個故事挑出來的，是「被了解」。鍾子期沒有為伯牙做任何事，他給伯牙的快樂，也沒有哪一樣是付錢的聽眾給不了的。他聽出了伯牙的意思。

The historian Sima Qian preserves a second example in the words of the statesman Guan Zhong. When he was poor and took the larger share of their profits, his friend Bao Shuya did not think him greedy, knowing that he was poor. When he fled three times in battle, Bao did not think him a coward, knowing that he had an old mother. “It was my parents who gave me life,” Guan Zhong said, “and it is Bao who knows me.” To be known in this way is to be seen at one’s worst and read correctly. It asks more of a friend than Aristotle’s likeness in virtue.

史家司馬遷用政治家管仲自己的話，保存了第二個例子。他窮的時候，跟鮑叔牙合夥做生意，分錢時自己多拿，鮑叔不認為他貪，知道他窮。他打仗時三次臨陣脫逃，鮑叔不認為他膽小，知道他家有老母。管仲說：「生我者父母，知我者鮑子也。」以這種方式被了解，就是在自己最糟的時候被看見，並且被正確地解讀。這對朋友的要求，比亞里斯多德的「德行相似」更多。

05Three that help and three that harm 益者三友，損者三友

Confucius approaches the matter as a teacher would, by asking what a friend does to you. “Three kinds of friends are a gain and three are a loss. To have friends who are upright, friends who are true to their word, and friends who have heard much is a gain. To have friends who are smooth, friends who are soft and pleasing, and friends who are glib is a loss.” The helpful friend tells you the truth, can be relied on, and knows things you do not. The harmful ones are, in Aristotle’s terms, friends of pleasure. They agree with you. His student Zengzi states the purpose in a line: the gentleman “uses his friends to support his humaneness.” A friend is a partner in becoming a better person, and friendship is the one relationship among the five Confucian bonds that is chosen and that holds between equals.

孔子是像一位老師那樣切入這件事的：他問的是朋友對你「做了什麼」。「益者三友，損者三友：友直，友諒，友多聞，益矣；友便辟，友善柔，友便佞，損矣。」有益的朋友對你說實話、靠得住，並且知道你不知道的事。有害的那幾種，用亞里斯多德的話說，就是快樂的朋友。他們順著你。他的學生曾子用一句話說出了目的：君子「以友輔仁」。朋友是「成為更好的人」這件事上的夥伴；而在儒家的五倫當中，朋友是唯一出於選擇、而且存在於平等者之間的一倫。

Zhuangzi offers a caution about warmth. “The friendship of the gentleman is plain as water. The friendship of the small man is sweet as rich wine. The gentleman’s plainness brings closeness. The small man’s sweetness ends in a break.” A relation that needs constant sweetness is being held together by something other than the two people.

莊子對「熱絡」提出了一個警告。「君子之交淡若水，小人之交甘若醴；君子淡以親，小人甘以絕。」一段需要不斷加糖的關係，把它維繫在一起的，是這兩個人之外的別的東西。

06Friends by the hundred 數以百計的朋友

The word has recently been stretched. A person may now have a thousand “friends” on a screen, most of whom he has not eaten salt with. The anthropologist Robin Dunbar has argued, from the size of primate brains and from counts of human groups, that a person can keep up stable relationships with about a hundred and fifty people, of whom about fifteen are close and about five are intimate. The numbers have held up fairly well in studies of telephone records and of online networks. They are Aristotle’s point in figures: the highest kind of friendship needs shared time, and time is limited.

這個詞近來被拉長了。一個人現在可以在螢幕上有一千個「朋友」，其中大多數他沒有跟對方一起吃過鹽。人類學家鄧巴根據靈長類腦的大小與對人類群體的計數，論證一個人能夠維持穩定關係的對象大約是一百五十人，其中大約十五人算親近，大約五人算親密。在對電話紀錄與線上網絡的研究裡，這些數字相當站得住。它們就是用數字說出來的亞里斯多德的論點：最高的那一種友誼需要共度的時間，而時間是有限的。

That leaves a question the exercise below is meant to raise. Most of the people we call friends are friends of use or of pleasure, and there is nothing wrong with that. The difficulty comes from not knowing which is which. We are hurt when a friendship of convenience ends with the convenience, and we neglect, because it is plain as water, the one that would have lasted.

這就留下了一個問題，下面的練習就是要把它提出來。我們稱為朋友的人，大多數是有用的朋友或快樂的朋友，而這並沒有什麼不對。困難來自分不清哪一個是哪一種。一段因方便而起的友誼，隨著方便的結束而結束時，我們感到受傷；而那段本來可以長久的友誼，因為它淡若水，我們卻疏忽了。

Primary text · 原典

In their own words 原典怎麼說

The philosopher’s definition and the essayist’s refusal to give one, nineteen centuries apart.

哲學家的定義，與隨筆作家對下定義的拒絕；兩者相隔十九個世紀。

Did he really say that? · 這句話真的是他說的嗎？

“A friend is one soul dwelling in two bodies.” (Aristotle)「朋友是住在兩個身體裡的一個靈魂。」（亞里斯多德）

Reported six centuries later · 六個世紀之後的轉述

The saying is not in any surviving work of Aristotle. It comes from Diogenes Laertius, who wrote about 230 CE and records it among anecdotes of things Aristotle was said to have answered when asked. Montaigne, in the essay quoted above, uses it and calls it “that very proper definition of Aristotle.” What Aristotle wrote in the Ethics is cooler: a friend is “another self.” The two phrases point in different directions. One soul in two bodies is Montaigne’s fusion, with the seam gone. Another self keeps two people, each of whom regards the other as he regards himself.

這句話不見於亞里斯多德現存的任何著作。它出自第歐根尼・拉爾修；他大約在西元 230 年寫作，把它記在一批「據說亞里斯多德被問到時這樣回答」的軼事當中。蒙田在上面引的那篇隨筆裡用了它，並且稱之為「亞里斯多德那個非常恰當的定義」。亞里斯多德在《倫理學》裡寫的比較冷靜：朋友是「另一個自己」。這兩個說法指向不同的方向。「兩個身體裡的一個靈魂」是蒙田式的融合，接縫消失了。「另一個自己」保留了兩個人，各自像看待自己那樣看待對方。

Both sides · 正反兩面

Objections and replies 反駁與回應

Objection 1 · 反駁

Friendship is unfair. Morality tells us to treat everyone equally, and a friend is someone we favor.

友誼是不公平的。道德要我們平等對待每一個人；而朋友，是我們偏袒的人。

Reply · 回應

The tension is real, and Mozi and the utilitarians have pressed it. Two replies are available. One is that a world without special attachments would contain far less care in total, since care for everyone in general is thin, so that even an impartial judge would approve of friendship. The other is that this defense gets the matter backward. A man who visits his friend in the hospital because friendship maximizes welfare has, as one philosopher put it, one thought too many. On this second view friendship is among the things that make a human life worth having, and a morality with no room for it has left something out.

這個張力是真實的；墨子與效益主義者都曾緊追不放。有兩種回應可用。一種是：一個沒有特殊情誼的世界，總體上的關懷會少得多，因為對「一般的每一個人」的關懷是稀薄的；所以，即使是一位不偏私的裁判，也會贊成友誼。另一種是：這個辯護把事情弄顛倒了。一個人到醫院探望朋友，是因為「友誼能使福祉極大化」；用一位哲學家的話說，他「多想了一層」。照第二種看法，友誼屬於使人生值得擁有的那些東西；一套容不下它的道德，是漏掉了什麼。

Objection 2 · 反駁

Aristotle says we should not befriend those worse than ourselves, and Confucius says the same. If everyone followed that advice, the better person in each pair would refuse, and no one would have a friend.

亞里斯多德說，我們不該跟不如自己的人交朋友；孔子也這麼說。如果每個人都聽從這個建議，每一對當中比較好的那一個就會拒絕，而誰也不會有朋友了。

Reply · 回應

The puzzle about the Analects line “have no friend who is not your equal” is old, and commentators have long read it otherwise: do not take as your model of friendship someone who is unlike you in aims, or more simply, look for what in each friend is better than you. Confucius also says that among any three people walking he will find a teacher. Friends are seldom better or worse across the board. One is braver and the other more patient, and each can be the other’s superior in something.

《論語》那句「無友不如己者」的難題由來已久；而歷來的註家有別的讀法：不要拿志向與你不同的人作為交友的榜樣；或者更簡單地說，去找每一位朋友身上比你好的地方。孔子也說過：「三人行，必有我師焉。」朋友很少是全面地比你好或比你差。一個比較勇敢，另一個比較有耐心；每一方都可以在某件事上勝過對方。

Objection 3 · 反駁

“Another self” is the wrong ideal. If my friend is another me, I have learned nothing and loved no one but myself.

「另一個自己」是錯誤的理想。如果我的朋友是另一個我，那我什麼也沒學到，而且除了自己之外誰也沒愛。

Reply · 回應

The objection takes the phrase too literally. Aristotle means that I stand to my friend as I stand to myself: I want his good as directly as I want my own, and his joys and griefs are mine. He does not mean that the friend resembles me in every respect, and elsewhere he says that we know ourselves best by looking at a friend, as we see our own face only in a mirror. That needs someone who is other. The Chinese stories make the same point by contrast. Zhong Ziqi could not play. What he gave Bo Ya was an ear.

這個反駁把那個說法看得太字面了。亞里斯多德的意思是：我對朋友的關係，就像我對自己的關係；我直接地想要他好，就像想要自己好一樣；他的喜與悲就是我的。他的意思並不是朋友在每一方面都像我。他在別處還說過：我們最能認識自己的方式，是看著一位朋友；就像我們只有在鏡子裡才看得見自己的臉。那需要一個「他者」。中國的故事用對比說出了同一點。鍾子期不會彈琴。他給伯牙的，是一隻耳朵。

Terms of art · 哲學術語

The vocabulary of the subject 這門學問的用語

friendship友誼、友愛

Gk. philia

In Aristotle, any relation of mutual and recognized goodwill, wider than the English word.

在亞里斯多德，指任何彼此而且相互知曉的善意關係；範圍比英文的 friendship 廣。

utility效用

L. utilis ‘useful’

Usefulness. The basis of the first kind of friendship.

有用。第一種友誼的基礎。

goodwill善意

Gk. eunoia

Wishing another person well. It becomes friendship when it is mutual and known.

願另一個人好。當它是相互的、而且彼此知道的時候，就成為友誼。

another self另一個自己

Gk. allos autos; L. alter ego

Aristotle’s phrase for a friend, toward whom one stands as toward oneself.

亞里斯多德對朋友的稱呼：一個人對他的關係，就像對自己的關係。

one who knows the tone知音

Ch. 知音 zhīyīn

A friend who understands one fully, from the story of Bo Ya and Zhong Ziqi.

完全了解自己的朋友；出自伯牙與鍾子期的故事。

partiality偏私

Giving special weight to particular people, as against impartiality.

給特定的人特別的分量；相對於不偏私。

reciprocity相互性

L. reciprocus ‘moving back and forth’

Mutual exchange. A friendship of use rests on it, and a friendship of character does not keep accounts.

相互的交換。有用的友誼建立在它上面；品格的友誼則不記帳。

Dunbar’s number鄧巴數

The proposed limit, about 150, to the number of stable relationships a person can maintain.

一個人能夠維持的穩定關係數目的上限；據提議大約是 150。

Dharma and the West · 佛法與西方哲學

A view from the Dharma 佛法怎麼看

☸

The Buddha’s attendant Ānanda once remarked that good friendship is half of the spiritual life. “Not so, Ānanda! Not so, Ānanda! Good friends, companions, and associates are the whole of the spiritual life.” A person with good friends can be expected to develop the path, and the Buddha describes himself as the good friend by relying on whom beings are freed. The Pali term is kalyāṇamitta, the beautiful or admirable friend. It is close to Aristotle’s friendship of character, with the goal moved: the friend is the one who helps you toward liberation, where Aristotle’s helps you toward a flourishing life in the city.

佛陀的侍者阿難有一次說：善友是梵行的一半。「不是這樣，阿難！不是這樣，阿難！善友、善伴、善交遊，是梵行的全部。」有善友的人，可以期待他修習八正道；而佛陀把自己描述為那位善友：眾生依靠他而得解脫。巴利文的用語是 kalyāṇamitta，美善的、可敬的朋友。這很接近亞里斯多德的品格之友，只是目標移動了：這位朋友，是幫助你走向解脫的人；亞里斯多德的那一位，是幫助你在城邦裡活出興旺人生的人。

For lay life the Buddha gave advice that reads like a longer version of the Analects passage. He tells the young householder Sigālaka to recognize four enemies disguised as friends, among them the one who only takes and the flatterer, and four good-hearted friends: “the helper, the friend in good times and bad, the counselor, and the one who’s sympathetic.” The counselor is described in words Confucius could have used: “They keep you from doing bad. They support you in doing good. They teach you what you do not know.” The tradition adds one thing the philosophers lack. Loving-kindness, mettā, comes from the same root as mitta, friend. It is friendliness trained until it no longer depends on who the other person is, and it is to be extended in the end to strangers and enemies. Aristotle says that we can be friends in the full sense with very few. The Buddha agrees about friendship and holds that the goodwill at its center can be made boundless.

對在家的生活，佛陀給過一段建議，讀起來像是《論語》那一章的加長版。他告訴年輕的居士善生：要認出四種偽裝成朋友的敵人，其中有只知道拿的人與諂媚的人；也要認出四種善心的朋友：「能幫助的、苦樂與共的、能勸導的、有同情心的。」對「能勸導的」那一種的描述，用的是孔子也可能用的話：「他們使你遠離惡，支持你行善，教你所不知道的事。」這個傳統還多了一樣哲學家們所沒有的東西。慈（mettā）與朋友（mitta）出自同一個字根。它是被訓練到「不再取決於對方是誰」的友善，而最終要推及陌生人與敵人。亞里斯多德說，完整意義上的朋友，我們只能有非常少的幾位。佛陀對「友誼」同意這一點，卻主張友誼核心的那份善意，可以被修到沒有邊界。
