Philosophy 哲學

Big questions, the people who asked them, and the story of how the answers changed. Read in English, then work through the thought experiment yourself.
人類問過的大問題、問這些問題的人，以及答案怎麼一路改變。先用英文讀懂，再親自做一次思想實驗。

This series is written at an advanced level, for adult readers and teachers. It uses the real vocabulary of the subject and explains each term the first time it appears.

這個系列以高級程度寫成，對象是成人讀者與老師。內文直接使用這門學問真正的術語，並在第一次出現時解釋。

There are five shelves. Start anywhere: every lesson links to the philosophers, the history, and the sources behind it. Greek, Chinese, and Indian philosophy are treated side by side, and one shelf is given to a single question: for each idea in the Dharma, which Western position comes closest?

一共五個書架，從哪裡開始都可以：每一課都連到它背後的哲學家、歷史與原典。希臘、中國、印度哲學並列處理；其中一個書架專門回答一個問題：佛法的每一個觀念，西方哲學誰最接近？
