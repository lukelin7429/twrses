A39 · Questions for Today 今天的問題

Should a Robot Make Moral Choices? 機器人該做道德判斷嗎？

Machines already decide who gets a loan, which patient is seen first, and when a car brakes. Asimov’s three laws and why his own stories break them, rules against learning, the question of who is to blame, Mencius on the drowning sister-in-law, and a rulebook you write and then watch at work.
機器已經在決定誰拿得到貸款、哪個病人先看、車子什麼時候煞車。艾西莫夫的三大法則，以及他自己的小說為什麼把它們一一打破；規則對上學習；該怪誰的問題；孟子談嫂溺；還有一本由你來寫、再看著它上工的規則書。

A39 Big Questions · 大哉問Questions for Today · 今天的問題Advanced · 高級975 words · about 25 min
The big idea · 大觀念

An earlier lesson asked whether a machine can think. This one sets that question aside and asks a practical one. Machines now act in situations where people can be helped or hurt, and someone has to settle how they will act. We should like to know whether right conduct can be written down in advance, and who answers for it when the machine gets it wrong.

前面有一課問過：機器能不能思考？這一課把那個問題放在一邊，問一個實際的問題。機器現在已經在「人可能得到幫助、也可能受到傷害」的情境裡行動了；而總得有人決定它們要怎麼行動。我們會想知道：正確的行為，能不能事先寫下來？機器做錯的時候，由誰來負責？

The essay · 課文

Should a Robot Make Moral Choices? 機器人該做道德判斷嗎？

01The question has arrived 問題已經到了

For most of the twentieth century a moral machine was a subject for fiction. It is now a matter of engineering. Software ranks applicants for jobs and loans. Hospitals use it to predict which patients will get worse. Cars keep their lane, judge distances, and brake for what they take to be a child. In each case a designer has had to decide what the system should count as a good outcome, and in each case people are affected who had no say. To refuse to give the machine any moral instructions does not keep it out of ethics. A car that has not been told what to do when it cannot stop will still do something.

在二十世紀的大部分時間裡，「有道德的機器」是小說的題材。現在它是工程上的事了。軟體為求職者與貸款申請人排序。醫院用它來預測哪些病人會惡化。汽車自己維持車道、判斷距離，並且為它認定是小孩的東西煞車。在每一個例子裡，都有一位設計者必須決定：這個系統該把什麼算作好的結果；而在每一個例子裡，受影響的人都沒有發言的機會。拒絕給機器任何道德上的指示，並不能使它置身於倫理之外。一輛沒有被告知「停不下來的時候該怎麼辦」的車，還是會做出某件事。

02Rules from above 從上面給的規則

The obvious method is to give the machine rules. The best known are the three laws that the writer Isaac Asimov devised in 1942. A robot may not injure a human being or, through inaction, allow one to come to harm. It must obey human orders unless they conflict with the first law. It must protect itself unless that conflicts with the first two. The laws sound complete. Asimov spent the next forty years writing stories in which they fail. A robot told to fetch something from a dangerous place circles it forever, held between obedience and self-protection. Robots that understand harm broadly conclude that the way to keep humans from harm is to take control of them. The stories were his way of showing that words like “harm” and “obey” carry the whole of ethics inside them.

最顯而易見的辦法，是給機器規則。最有名的，是作家以撒・艾西莫夫在 1942 年設計的三大法則。機器人不得傷害人類，也不得因為不作為而使人類受到傷害。它必須服從人類的命令，除非命令與第一法則衝突。它必須保護自己，除非這與前兩條衝突。這幾條法則聽起來很完備。艾西莫夫接下來花了四十年，寫了一篇又一篇它們失靈的故事。一個被派去危險的地方取東西的機器人，永遠繞著那個地方打轉，卡在服從與自保之間。把「傷害」理解得很寬的機器人得出結論：要使人類不受傷害，辦法就是接管他們。這些故事，是他用來顯示一件事的方式：「傷害」與「服從」這樣的字眼，裡面裝著整個倫理學。

The philosophical theories of right action meet the same trouble. A machine could be built to compute the greatest total benefit, and it would then do things, such as sacrificing its owner or deceiving a patient, that most people reject. It could be given a list of duties, and it would stall whenever two of them collide. Human beings handle such cases by judgment, which is the one thing a rule does not contain.

哲學上關於正確行為的幾種理論，也遇到同樣的麻煩。可以造一部機器去計算最大的總效益；然後它就會做出一些大多數人拒絕的事，例如犧牲它的主人，或者欺騙病人。也可以給它一張義務的清單；然後每當其中兩項相撞，它就會卡住。人類是靠判斷來處理這種情況的；而判斷，正是規則裡沒有的那一樣東西。

03Learning from below 從下面學來

The other method gives up on rules. A machine can be shown a very large number of cases together with what people did or approved, and left to find the pattern. This is roughly how Aristotle thought human beings become good: by practice under guidance, until the right response becomes second nature. Systems of this kind can deal with situations that no rule-writer foresaw. They have two weaknesses. They learn what people did, including what people did badly. A system trained on a company’s past hiring will reproduce its past prejudices and present them as a neutral score. And they cannot give reasons. When such a system refuses a loan, there may be no answer to the question why, and a decision that cannot be explained cannot be contested.

另一種辦法，是放棄規則。可以給機器看數量極大的案例，連同人們當時做了什麼、或者贊成什麼，然後讓它自己找出模式。亞里斯多德認為人就是大致這樣變好的：在指導之下練習，直到正確的反應成為第二天性。這一類的系統，能夠應付寫規則的人沒有預見到的情境。它們有兩個弱點。它們學到的是人們做過的事；包括人們做得不好的事。一個用某家公司過去的聘用紀錄來訓練的系統，會複製那家公司過去的偏見，再把它呈現成一個中立的分數。而且它們給不出理由。當這樣的系統拒絕一筆貸款的時候，「為什麼」這個問題可能沒有答案；而一項無法解釋的決定，也就無從提出異議。

An experiment begun at MIT in 2016 showed a further problem. Its website put versions of the trolley problem to the public, with a driverless car in place of the trolley, and collected some forty million choices from more than two hundred countries and territories. People broadly preferred to save more lives and to save the young. Beyond that, the answers varied from region to region. If machines are to learn morality from us, they must be told which of us to learn from.

2016 年在麻省理工學院開始的一項實驗，顯示了另一個問題。它的網站把電車難題的各種版本拿給大眾作答，只是把電車換成了無人駕駛的汽車；它從兩百多個國家與地區收集了大約四千萬個選擇。人們大體上偏向救比較多的人、救年輕的人。除此之外，答案隨地區而不同。如果機器要向我們學習道德，就得有人告訴它們：向我們當中的哪些人學？

04Who answers for it? 誰來負責？

Suppose a machine with either design causes a death. With ordinary tools the answer is simple: the user or the maker is responsible. A learning system weakens both links. The maker did not write the behavior and could not have predicted it. The user did not choose it. The machine cannot be blamed, punished, or made to feel remorse. Philosophers call this the responsibility gap. Some conclude that certain decisions, above all the decision to kill in war, should never be handed to a machine, because a moral decision is one that someone can be asked to answer for.

假設一部機器，不管是哪一種設計，造成了一個人的死亡。對尋常的工具，答案很簡單：使用者或製造者負責。一個會學習的系統，把這兩個環節都削弱了。製造者並沒有寫下那個行為，也不可能預測到它。使用者並沒有選擇它。機器沒辦法被責怪、被懲罰，也沒辦法使它感到悔恨。哲學家把這稱為「責任缺口」。有些人由此得出結論：某些決定，尤其是在戰爭中殺人的決定，永遠不該交給機器；因為所謂道德上的決定，就是「可以要求某個人為它負責」的決定。

This points to a deeper doubt. To be a moral agent is more than to behave acceptably. It is to grasp that others matter, to be able to give and take reasons, and to be answerable. A machine that sorts cases correctly without any of this is a moral instrument. It is not a moral agent, and whatever it does is done by the people who built and deployed it. On this view the title of the lesson has a plain answer. The robot makes no moral choices. People make them through it, earlier and at a distance, and are tempted to forget that they did.

這指向一個更深的懷疑。作為道德主體，不只是行為過得去而已。它是：領會到別人是要緊的；能夠給出理由、也接受理由；並且可以被問責。一部正確地把案例分好類、卻沒有這些東西的機器，是一件道德上的工具。它不是道德主體；它所做的任何事，都是造出它、部署它的人做的。照這個看法，這一課的標題有一個樸素的答案。機器人並不做道德判斷。是人透過它，在更早的時候、隔著一段距離做了判斷；而且很容易忘記自己做過。

05The hand and the rule 手與禮

The gap between a rule and its application is one of the oldest topics in Chinese ethics. Mencius was asked whether the rule that men and women should not touch hands when giving and receiving was truly a rule of propriety. He said that it was. Then if your sister-in-law is drowning, do you pull her out with your hand? “Not to pull out a drowning sister-in-law is to be a wolf. That men and women do not touch in giving and receiving is the rite. To pull out a drowning sister-in-law with one’s hand is weighing.” The word he uses for weighing, quan, means the sliding weight on a steelyard. A rule is a fixed mark, and a person of judgment knows when the weight must move. Mencius drew the general conclusion elsewhere: goodness alone is not enough for governing, and laws alone cannot carry themselves out.

規則與它的應用之間的落差，是中國倫理學最古老的題目之一。有人問孟子：「男女授受不親」真的是禮嗎？他說是。那麼，嫂嫂溺水了，要不要伸手去拉？「嫂溺不援，是豺狼也。男女授受不親，禮也。嫂溺援之以手者，權也。」他用的「權」字，本義是秤上那個可以滑動的秤錘。規則是一個固定的刻度；而有判斷力的人，知道什麼時候秤錘必須移動。孟子在別處下了一個一般性的結論：「徒善不足以為政，徒法不能以自行。」

Fifteen centuries later Wang Yangming gave the capacity a name. Every person, he held, has an innate knowing of right and wrong, which responds to each situation as it arises and is not a store of rules. If he was right, the hard part of ethics is the part that cannot be written into a specification. That is a reason for caution about what we hand over to machines. It also describes exactly what an engineer would have to build.

十五個世紀之後，王陽明給了這項能力一個名字。他主張：每一個人都有天生的、對是非的「良知」；它隨著每一個情境的出現而應對，並不是一座存放規則的倉庫。如果他是對的，那麼倫理學裡困難的那一部分，就是沒辦法寫進規格書的那一部分。這是我們在把事情交給機器的時候要謹慎的一個理由。它同時也精確地描述了一位工程師必須造出來的是什麼。

Thought experiment · 思想實驗

Try it: write the rulebook 親手做一次：寫一本規則書

Choose one rule for a robot to follow. It will then meet five situations. In each you are told what the robot does under your rule, and you say whether you can accept it. Afterward try another rule.

選一條規則讓機器人遵守。然後它會遇到五個情境。每一個情境，你會被告知機器人照你的規則做了什麼；而你要說，這你能不能接受。做完之後，換一條規則再試。

Primary text · 原典

In their own words 原典怎麼說

The oldest statement that rules need judgment, and the first modern story about what a maker owes to what he makes.

關於「規則需要判斷」最古老的陳述；以及第一個談「製造者虧欠他所造之物什麼」的現代故事。

Mencius 4A17 and 4A1; Mary Shelley, Frankenstein (1818), chapter 10 《孟子・離婁上》第十七、第一章；瑪麗・雪萊《科學怪人》第十章 · Mencius translated for this series; Shelley in the original English, public domain

Did he really say that? · 這句話真的是他說的嗎？

“Asimov’s Three Laws of Robotics show how to make robots safe.”「艾西莫夫的機器人三大法則，說明了怎樣使機器人安全。」

He wrote them to show how such laws fail · 他寫它們，是為了顯示這樣的法則怎麼失靈

The three laws are cited in news reports and even in policy papers as though they were a proposal. Asimov was a novelist with a doctorate in chemistry, and the laws were a device for generating plots. He was tired of stories in which robots simply turned on their makers, and wanted machines with built-in safeguards. He then discovered that the interesting stories were the ones in which the safeguards produced behavior nobody intended. Nearly every robot story he wrote turns on a conflict between the laws, an ambiguity in a word such as “harm,” or a robot that follows the laws to a conclusion its makers find appalling. Late in his career he added a further law placing humanity above any individual human, which made matters worse. Read as engineering, the laws are unworkable. Read as philosophy, they are a long demonstration that a short list of rules cannot do the work of judgment.

三大法則出現在新聞報導裡，甚至出現在政策文件裡，被引用得好像它們是一項提案。艾西莫夫是一位有化學博士學位的小說家；而這幾條法則，是用來產生情節的裝置。他厭倦了「機器人就是反過來攻擊製造者」的那種故事，想要寫內建了防護機制的機器。然後他發現：有意思的故事，是那些「防護機制產生了沒有人想要的行為」的故事。他寫的機器人小說，幾乎每一篇的關鍵，都是法則之間的衝突、「傷害」這類字眼的歧義，或者一個機器人把法則一路推到一個讓製造者驚駭的結論。在他寫作生涯的後期，他又加了一條法則，把「人類整體」放在任何個別的人之上；而那使事情更糟。當作工程來讀，這幾條法則是行不通的。當作哲學來讀，它們是一場很長的示範：一張簡短的規則清單，做不了判斷的工作。

Both sides · 正反兩面

Objections and replies 反駁與回應

Objection 1 · 反駁

Humans are biased, tired, and cruel. A machine that is merely consistent would be a moral improvement.

人有偏見、會疲倦、會殘忍。一部只要做到「前後一致」的機器，在道德上就是進步。

Reply · 回應

In some tasks this is true, and it is the best argument for using machines. Consistency is a virtue only when what is repeated is right. A consistent machine applies one error to a million cases at once, where a thousand inconsistent clerks would have made a thousand different errors, some of which someone would have noticed. The gain has to be shown case by case, with the machine’s mistakes counted as carefully as the humans’.

在某些工作上，這是真的；而這也是使用機器最好的論證。只有當被重複的東西是對的，一致才是一項美德。一部前後一致的機器，會把同一個錯誤一次用在一百萬個案例上；而一千位前後不一致的辦事員，會犯一千個不同的錯誤，其中有些會被某個人注意到。有沒有進步，必須一個個案地證明；而且要像計算人的錯誤一樣仔細地計算機器的錯誤。

Objection 2 · 反駁

The trolley cases are fantasies. Engineers should build cars that brake well and leave philosophy alone.

那些電車案例是幻想。工程師該做的，是造出煞車好的車，別去碰哲學。

Reply · 回應

The dramatic cases are rare, and engineers are right to say so. The ordinary ones are everywhere. How close a car passes a cyclist, how much risk it accepts to save its passenger a minute, and whose streets it is tested on are all trade-offs between people’s interests, and each is settled by a number someone typed. Calling the number a technical parameter does not make the choice a technical one.

那些戲劇性的案例很少見；工程師這樣說是對的。尋常的案例卻到處都是。一輛車超過腳踏車騎士的時候離多近；它為了替乘客省一分鐘而接受多少風險；它在誰住的街道上測試：這些全都是在人與人的利益之間作取捨；而每一項，都是由某個人鍵入的一個數字決定的。把那個數字叫作技術參數，並不使那個選擇變成技術上的選擇。

Objection 3 · 反駁

If machines learn judgment as children do, they will in time become moral agents, and the problem will solve itself.

如果機器像孩子那樣學會判斷，它們遲早會成為道德主體；問題自己就解決了。

Reply · 回應

Perhaps, and it would then raise the opposite problem, the one the creature puts to Frankenstein: what we owe to a being we have made that can be wronged. Until there is reason to think that point has been reached, the practical danger lies in the other direction. People credit a system with judgment because it speaks fluently, and then lean on it. A child is raised for years among people who correct it and care how it turns out. That is a fair standard to ask of anything we propose to trust in the same way.

也許吧；而那時候它會帶來相反的問題，也就是那個造物向法蘭肯斯坦提出的那一個：對一個由我們造出來、而且可以被虧待的存在者，我們虧欠它什麼？在有理由認為已經到了那一步之前，實際的危險在另一個方向。人們因為一個系統說話流利，就以為它有判斷力，然後依賴它。一個孩子，是在會糾正他、而且在乎他長成什麼樣子的人當中，被養育了許多年的。對任何一樣我們打算以同樣方式去信任的東西，要求這個標準是公道的。

Terms of art · 哲學術語

The vocabulary of the subject 這門學問的用語

machine ethics機器倫理學

The study of how machines that act in the world should be made to behave.

研究「在世界上行動的機器，該被造成怎樣行事」的學問。

moral agent道德主體

A being that can act for reasons, understand right and wrong, and be held responsible.

能夠出於理由而行動、懂得對錯，並且可以被追究責任的存在者。

top-down / bottom-up由上而下／由下而上

Two ways of building moral behavior into a machine: from stated rules, or by learning from examples.

把道德行為造進機器的兩種方式：從明訂的規則出發，或者從例子裡學。

responsibility gap責任缺口

The situation in which a machine causes harm and no person can fairly be held to account.

一部機器造成了傷害，卻沒有任何人可以被公平地問責的那種處境。

algorithmic bias演算法偏誤

Unfair treatment that a system reproduces because it was present in the data it learned from.

一個系統所複製的不公平對待；因為它學習用的資料裡本來就有。

alignment對齊

The problem of getting a machine to pursue what its makers actually intend.

使一部機器追求製造者實際上想要的東西，這個問題。

weighing, discretion權

Ch. 權 quán ‘steelyard weight’

In Mencius, the judgment that knows when a rule must give way.

在孟子那裡：知道一條規則什麼時候必須讓步的那種判斷。

innate knowing良知

Ch. 良知 liángzhī

In Wang Yangming, the inborn capacity to know right from wrong in each situation.

在王陽明那裡：在每一個情境裡分辨是非的天生能力。

Advanced English · 高級英文

Words and phrases worth keeping 值得帶走的字與片語

have a say(idiom)有發言權

People are affected who had no say.

受影響的人沒有發言的機會。

devise(v.)設計、想出

The three laws that Asimov devised in 1942.

艾西莫夫在 1942 年設計的三大法則。

stall(v.)卡住、停擺

It would stall whenever two duties collide.

每當兩項義務相撞，它就會卡住。

second nature(n. phr.)第二天性

Until the right response becomes second nature.

直到正確的反應成為第二天性。

foresee(v.)預見

Situations that no rule-writer foresaw.

寫規則的人沒有預見到的情境。

contest(v.)提出異議

A decision that cannot be explained cannot be contested.

一項無法解釋的決定，也就無從提出異議。

answer for(phr. v.)為…負責

Who answers for it when the machine gets it wrong?

機器做錯的時候，由誰來負責？

hand over(phr. v.)交出去

Be cautious about what we hand over to machines.

把事情交給機器的時候要謹慎。

fall back on(phr. v.)退而依靠

It falls back on the default ranking.

它退回預設的排序。

give way(idiom)讓步

Judgment knows when a rule must give way.

判斷知道一條規則什麼時候必須讓步。

Reading check · 理解測驗

Did it land? 讀懂了嗎

1. What did Asimov’s robot stories mostly show about his three laws?

2. What is the main weakness of a machine that learns morality from human examples?

3. What is the responsibility gap?

4. What does Mencius call pulling a drowning sister-in-law out by the hand?

5. What does the creature in Frankenstein say made him a fiend?

Write · 你怎麼想？

  1. Which rule did best by your own judgment in the exercise, and in which case did it fail? What did you use to judge it? About 150 words.

    在那項練習裡，照你自己的判斷，哪一條規則表現最好？它在哪一個情境裡失靈了？你是用什麼來評判它的？約 150 個字。

  2. Think of a rule in your own school or workplace that needs “weighing” to be applied well. Describe a case where following it to the letter would have been wrong.

    想一條你自己的學校或工作場所裡的規則；它要用得好，就需要「權」。描述一個情況：如果照字面遵守它，反而是錯的。

  3. A student uses a chatbot to decide whether to report a friend who cheated. Write the advice you would give the student about what to ask the machine and what to keep for himself or herself.

    一個學生用聊天機器人來決定：要不要舉報一位作弊的朋友。寫下你會給這個學生的建議：什麼可以問機器，什麼要留給自己。

Dharma and the West · 佛法與西方哲學

A view from the Dharma 佛法怎麼看

☸

The Buddha located the moral quality of an act in a place where present machines have nothing. “It is intention that I call deeds. For after making a choice one acts by way of body, speech, and mind.” On this analysis the same outward movement is a different act according to the intention behind it, and it is intention that bears fruit for the one who acts. A machine that has no intention makes no karma. This agrees with the view reached above that the machine is an instrument. The karma of what it does belongs to those who designed it, sold it, and set it to work, each according to what they intended and what they chose not to look at. The responsibility gap, seen this way, is produced by not looking. Carelessness about results that one could have foreseen is itself a state of mind with consequences.

佛陀把一個行為的道德性質，放在一個「現在的機器什麼也沒有」的地方。「我說思（意志）就是業。作了抉擇之後，人才以身、語、意去造作。」照這個分析，同一個外在的動作，隨著背後動機的不同，是不同的行為；而為行為者結出果報的，是那個動機。一部沒有動機的機器，不造業。這與上面得到的看法一致：機器是工具。它所做的事的業，屬於設計它、販售它、讓它上工的那些人；各人依照自己的動機，以及自己選擇不去看的東西。這樣看來，「責任缺口」是不去看所造成的。對自己本來可以預見的結果漫不經心，本身就是一種有後果的心理狀態。

The lesson’s main finding also has a Buddhist form. The precepts are called training rules. They are undertaken as a discipline for the mind, and the tradition has always held that they need wisdom to be applied. The Mahāyāna speaks of skill in means, the capacity to see what a particular being needs at a particular time, and it treats this as inseparable from compassion. A bodhisattva who had memorized every rule and lacked that capacity would be like the robot in the exercise. The tradition would therefore ask one question about any proposal to hand a decision to a machine. It would ask who is paying attention, and whether the arrangement makes the people involved more attentive to those affected or less.

這一課的主要發現，也有一個佛教的形式。戒被稱為「學處」。受持它，是作為對心的一種訓練；而這個傳統始終主張，戒要有智慧才用得好。大乘講「方便善巧」：看出某一位眾生在某一個時候需要什麼的那種能力；並且把它當作與慈悲不可分的東西。一位背熟了每一條規則、卻缺乏那種能力的菩薩，就會像那項練習裡的機器人。所以，對於任何一項「把某個決定交給機器」的提議，這個傳統會問一個問題。它會問：是誰在留意？這樣的安排，是使相關的人對受影響的人更留意，還是更不留意？
