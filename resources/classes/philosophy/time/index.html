Augustine’s puzzle about the vanishing present, the quarrel over whether time flows, and what physics has done to the word “now.”
奧古斯丁關於「消失的現在」的難題、時間到底流不流動的爭論，以及物理學對「現在」這個詞做了什麼。

The big idea · 大觀念

We live as though only the present exists and as though it moves. Each belief is very hard to defend. The alternative, that all times are equally real, fits the physics and leaves the experience of passage unexplained.

我們活得好像只有現在存在，而且好像現在會移動。這兩個信念都很難辯護。另一種看法是一切時刻同樣真實；它符合物理學，卻讓「流逝」的經驗沒有得到解釋。

The essay · 課文

What Is Time? 時間是什麼？

01I know, until you ask 你不問，我是知道的

Around the year 400 Augustine of Hippo, writing the story of his life as a long prayer, stopped to ask a question that seemed to have nothing to do with it. “What then is time? If no one asks me, I know: if I wish to explain it to one that asketh, I know not.” The remark is often quoted as a joke about philosophy. He meant it as a statement of the problem. We handle time without difficulty. We say that a speech was long, that a friend is late, that an hour has passed. Yet when we try to say what it is that passes, or where the long time is that we measured, the confidence disappears.

大約在西元 400 年，希波的奧古斯丁正把自己一生的故事寫成一篇長長的禱告；他停下來，問了一個看似與此無關的問題。「那麼時間是什麼？沒有人問我，我是知道的；如果我想向發問的人解釋，我就不知道了。」這句話常被當成一個關於哲學的笑話來引用。他說這句話，是當作問題的陳述。我們處理時間毫無困難。我們說一場演講很長，說朋友遲到了，說一個小時過去了。可是，當我們試著說出「過去的那個東西」是什麼，或者我們量到的那段「很長的時間」在哪裡，那份把握就消失了。

02The vanishing present 消失的現在

His argument is short. The past no longer exists, and the future does not yet exist. Only the present is. But how long is the present? This century is not all present, since most of its years are gone or still to come. The same is true of this year, this day, this hour. Take a single minute: part has already fled and part has not arrived. “If an instant of time be conceived, which cannot be divided into the smallest particles of moments, that alone is it, which may be called present. Which yet flies with such speed from future to past, as not to be lengthened out with the least stay.” He concludes: “The present hath no space.” So the past and future are nothing, and the present is a boundary with no extent. What, then, are we measuring when we say that a time was long?

他的論證很短。過去已經不存在，未來還沒有存在。存在的只有現在。可是現在有多長？這個世紀並不是整個都是現在，因為它大部分的年頭不是已經過去，就是還沒到來。今年、今天、這個小時，也都一樣。拿單單一分鐘來說：一部分已經逃走，一部分還沒抵達。「如果設想有一個時間的瞬間，它不能再被分割成最小的片刻，那麼只有它才可以被稱為現在。然而它以如此的速度從未來飛向過去，以致於沒有絲毫的停留可以把它拉長。」他的結論是：「現在沒有長度。」所以過去與未來是無，而現在是一條沒有寬度的界線。那麼，當我們說一段時間很長的時候，我們量的是什麼？

03Time in the mind 心靈裡的時間

Augustine’s answer is that we measure something in ourselves. It is not strictly correct, he says, to speak of past, present, and future. One should speak of “a present of things past, a present of things present, and a present of things future,” and these exist in the soul: “present of things past, memory; present of things present, sight; present of things future, expectation.” When I recite a psalm that I know by heart, my expectation reaches forward over the whole of it before I begin. As I go on, what was expected passes through my attention and becomes memory, until expectation is used up and all of it is memory. The long time I measure is a long memory or a long expectation. “It is in thee, my mind, that I measure times.” He called time a stretching of the mind.

奧古斯丁的回答是：我們量的是自己裡面的東西。他說，嚴格來講，說「過去、現在、未來」並不恰當。應該說的是「過去事物的現在、現在事物的現在、未來事物的現在」，而這三者存在於靈魂之中：「過去事物的現在是記憶；現在事物的現在是直觀；未來事物的現在是期待。」當我背誦一首熟記的詩篇，在開口之前，我的期待就向前伸展，涵蓋了整首。隨著我往下背，被期待的東西通過我的注意力，變成記憶；直到期待用盡，全部成了記憶。我量到的那段「長時間」，是一段長的記憶，或一段長的期待。「我的心靈啊，我是在你裡面度量時間的。」他把時間稱為心靈的伸展。

04A thing, or a relation? 是一個東西，還是一種關係？

Most philosophers have been unwilling to put time inside the mind, since the world surely had a history before there were minds. But they have disagreed about what sort of thing it is outside. Aristotle tied time to change. “Time is just this—number of motion in respect of ‘before’ and ‘after.’” Where nothing whatever changes, no time passes. Newton needed something firmer for his physics, and declared that “absolute, true, and mathematical time, of itself, and from its own nature flows equably without regard to anything external.” Time, for him, is like an empty container that would go on even if the universe were frozen. His rival Leibniz replied that this is a fiction. Time is the order in which events succeed one another, and to speak of time with no events in it is like speaking of a family tree with no people.

多數哲學家不願意把時間放進心靈裡，因為在有心靈之前，世界肯定已經有了歷史。可是，時間在心靈之外是哪一種東西，他們意見不一。亞里斯多德把時間繫在變化上。「時間就是這個：就『先』與『後』而言的運動之數。」什麼都不變的地方，就沒有時間流逝。牛頓的物理學需要更堅實的東西；他宣稱：「絕對的、真實的、數學的時間，自身依其本性均勻地流逝，與任何外在的事物無關。」對他來說，時間像一個空的容器，就算宇宙凍結了，它也會繼續走。他的對手萊布尼茲回應說，這是虛構。時間是事件彼此相續的次序；談論「裡面沒有任何事件的時間」，就像談論「沒有任何人的族譜」。

05Does time flow? 時間在流動嗎？

In 1908 the Cambridge philosopher J. M. E. McTaggart drew a distinction that has organized the debate ever since. Events can be ordered in two ways. In the first, each event is past, present, or future, and its position is always changing: the death of Socrates was once in the far future, became present, and has been sinking deeper into the past ever since. In the second, events are simply earlier or later than one another, and these relations never change. The death of Socrates is earlier than the birth of Augustine, and it always was and always will be.

1908 年，劍橋的哲學家麥塔加畫出了一個區分，此後這場辯論一直照著它來組織。事件可以用兩種方式排序。在第一種裡，每個事件是過去的、現在的或未來的，而且它的位置一直在變：蘇格拉底之死曾經在遙遠的未來，後來成了現在，從那以後就愈來愈深地沉入過去。在第二種裡，事件之間只有「早於」或「晚於」的關係，而這些關係永遠不變。蘇格拉底之死早於奧古斯丁的出生；它過去一向如此，將來也永遠如此。

The question is which ordering describes reality. On one view, often called presentism, only what is present exists, and the moving “now” is the most basic fact about the world. This agrees with experience and with Augustine’s premise. On the other view, called eternalism, all events are equally real, laid out along the dimension of time as places are laid out in space. “Now” is like “here,” a word that picks out wherever the speaker happens to be. Nothing moves. The first view has to explain what the present moves through and how fast, and the only available answer seems to be one second per second, which says nothing. The second has to explain why it feels so strongly as though time passes.

問題是：哪一種排序描述的是實在？有一種看法，常稱為「現在論」，主張只有現在的東西存在，而那個移動的「現在」是關於世界最根本的事實。這符合經驗，也符合奧古斯丁的前提。另一種看法，稱為「永恆論」，主張所有事件同樣真實，沿著時間這個維度鋪開，就像各個地點在空間裡鋪開一樣。「現在」就像「這裡」，是一個指出說話者碰巧所在之處的詞。沒有任何東西在動。第一種看法必須解釋：「現在」穿過的是什麼？移動得多快？而唯一說得出來的答案似乎是「每秒一秒」，這等於什麼都沒說。第二種看法必須解釋：為什麼我們如此強烈地覺得時間在流逝？

06What physics did to “now” 物理學對「現在」做了什麼

Einstein’s theory of relativity, published in 1905, gave the eternalists their strongest argument. It showed that two observers moving relative to each other will disagree about which distant events are happening at the same moment, and that neither is wrong. There is no single “now” that spans the universe. If so, there can be no fact about which events, everywhere, are the present ones, and presentism appears to lose its subject. Late in life Einstein wrote to the family of a friend who had just died that for physicists “the distinction between past, present and future is only a stubbornly persistent illusion.”

愛因斯坦在 1905 年發表的相對論，給了永恆論者最強的論證。它顯示：兩個彼此相對運動的觀察者，對於遠處的哪些事件「同時」發生，會有不同的判斷，而兩人都沒有錯。並沒有一個橫跨全宇宙的、單一的「現在」。若是如此，「全宇宙各處，哪些事件是現在的」這件事就沒有事實可言，而現在論似乎失去了它談論的對象。愛因斯坦晚年寫信給一位剛過世的朋友的家人，說對物理學家而言，「過去、現在與未來的區分，只是一個頑固持續的幻覺。」

Physics also deepens a second puzzle. Its basic laws work equally well in either direction of time, and yet eggs break and do not unbreak, and we remember yesterday and not tomorrow. The standard explanation appeals to entropy: the universe began in an extremely orderly state and has been growing more disordered ever since, and the direction of increasing disorder is what we call the future. Why the universe began that way is not known.

物理學還加深了第二個謎。它的基本定律在時間的兩個方向上同樣適用；然而蛋會破，不會復原，我們記得昨天，不記得明天。標準的解釋訴諸熵：宇宙從一個極度有序的狀態開始，此後一直變得更無序；而無序增加的方向，就是我們所說的未來。至於宇宙為什麼那樣開始，沒有人知道。

07The timely sage 聖之時者

Classical Chinese thought was little troubled by Augustine’s question and deeply interested in another. The word for the cosmos, 宇宙, is itself a theory of space and time. An early definition runs: “What has gone and what is to come, from of old until now, is called zhou (宙); the four directions with above and below are called yu (宇).” Time and space are named together as the two aspects of everything there is. Within that frame the pressing question was a practical one, what the moment calls for. The word 時 means “time,” “season,” and “timeliness,” and Mencius’s highest praise of Confucius was that he was “the sage who was timely” (聖之時者): he took office when it was right to take office and withdrew when it was right to withdraw. The Greek tradition treated time mainly as something to be measured or explained. Here it is something to be answered, as a farmer answers the seasons.

中國古典思想很少為奧古斯丁的問題煩惱，卻對另一個問題深感興趣。「宇宙」這個詞本身，就是一套關於空間與時間的理論。有一個早期的定義是這樣的：「往古來今謂之宙，四方上下謂之宇。」時間與空間被並列命名，當作一切存在之物的兩個面向。在這個框架之內，迫切的問題是實踐上的：這個時刻要求什麼？「時」這個字的意思是「時間」「時節」，也是「合乎時宜」；孟子對孔子最高的讚語，就是「聖之時者也」：該出仕的時候出仕，該退的時候退。希臘傳統主要把時間當作要被度量或被解釋的東西。在這裡，它是要被回應的東西，就像農夫回應四季。

08Why it matters 這為什麼重要

The metaphysics of time seems remote from life, and it bears on how we live more directly than most of philosophy does. If only the present exists, then the dead are simply nothing, and so is everything you have done. If all times exist, then every moment of your life is permanently part of what there is, and its being over means only that it lies in an earlier part of the whole. People have found comfort in the second picture and some have been horrified by it. Augustine found his own answer by looking inward. He concluded that we are stretched across time, pulled between what we remember and what we expect, and that this stretching is what a human life is. With that conclusion the third unit ends. We have asked what a thing is, whether change is real, whether we are free, and what time is. In each case the plain facts, that ships are repaired, rivers flow, people choose, and the clock moves, turned out to be much less clear than they seemed.

時間的形上學似乎離生活很遠；其實它對我們怎麼活的影響，比哲學的大部分內容都更直接。如果只有現在存在，那麼死者就是單純的無，你做過的一切也是。如果所有的時刻都存在，那麼你生命中的每一刻都永久地是「存在之物」的一部分；它「過去了」，只表示它位於整體當中較早的那一段。有人在第二幅圖像裡找到安慰，也有人被它嚇壞了。奧古斯丁靠著往內看，找到了他自己的答案。他的結論是：我們被拉開、橫跨在時間之上，在記得的與期待的之間被拉扯；而這個「被拉開」，就是人的一生。第三單元就以這個結論結束。我們問過：一樣東西是什麼？變化是不是真的？我們自由嗎？時間是什麼？在每一個問題上，那些明擺著的事實——船會被修補、河在流、人做選擇、時鐘在走——結果都遠不如看起來那麼清楚。

Thought experiment · 思想實驗

Try it: how long is now? 親手做一次：「現在」有多長？

Augustine’s argument, as something you can do. Start with the present year and keep looking closer. At every level the present turns out to contain a past and a future. Then measure a length of time, and ask yourself what it was that you measured.

把奧古斯丁的論證變成你可以動手做的事。從「今年」開始，一直往近處看。在每一層，「現在」結果都包含了一段過去與一段未來。然後量一段時間，再問問自己：你量的到底是什麼？

past · 過去called “present” · 被稱為「現在」future · 未來

Measure a time 量一段時間

Now measure a time. Press start, watch the light, and when it goes out, say how long it was on.

現在來量一段時間。按開始，看著那盞燈；等它熄了，說出它亮了多久。

Two orderings of the same events 同一串事件的兩種排法

Two ways of ordering the same events. Switch between them and watch which facts change while you read.

同一串事件的兩種排法。在兩者之間切換，看看在你閱讀的時候，哪些事實在變。

    Which picture? 哪一幅圖像？

    Which picture of time do you accept?

    你接受哪一幅時間的圖像？

    Presentism · 現在論

    This is the common-sense view and Augustine’s starting point. You then face his question, how long the present is, and Einstein’s, whose present it is. You must also say what makes it true that Socrates drank hemlock, if neither Socrates nor the hemlock exists in any sense.

    這是常識的看法，也是奧古斯丁的出發點。於是你要面對他的問題：現在有多長？還有愛因斯坦的問題：那是誰的現在？你還必須說明：如果蘇格拉底與那杯毒芹汁在任何意義上都不存在，是什麼使「蘇格拉底喝了毒芹汁」為真？

    The growing block · 成長塊體論

    Reality grows as new moments are added, which keeps the past fixed and the future open. The awkward question is how you know that you are at the growing edge. People in the past, who on this view are real, believe that their moment is the latest one, and they are wrong.

    隨著新的時刻加進來，實在不斷成長；這樣過去是固定的，未來是開放的。尷尬的問題是：你怎麼知道自己位於成長的那個邊緣？照這個看法，過去的人是真實的；他們也相信自己的那一刻是最新的，而他們錯了。

    Eternalism · 永恆論

    This is the view most physicists find natural. Past and future are like distant places. You owe an account of why we experience a flow, why we fear future pain and feel only relief about past pain, and in what sense the future is open to choice if it is already there.

    這是多數物理學家覺得自然的看法。過去與未來就像遙遠的地方。你欠一個說明：我們為什麼經驗到「流動」？我們為什麼害怕未來的痛苦，對過去的痛苦卻只感到鬆一口氣？還有，如果未來已經在那裡了，它在什麼意義上對選擇是開放的？

    McTaggart; Nāgārjuna · 麥塔加；龍樹

    McTaggart argued that the idea of time is contradictory, and Nāgārjuna that past, present, and future cannot be established either together or apart. You must then explain what the appearance of time is an appearance of. Nāgārjuna has an answer that McTaggart lacks: conventional reality is real enough to live in, and its being conventional is what he calls its emptiness.

    麥塔加論證「時間」這個觀念是自相矛盾的；龍樹論證過去、現在、未來不管合起來還是分開來，都無法成立。那麼你必須解釋：時間的表象，是什麼東西的表象？龍樹有一個麥塔加沒有的答案：世俗的實在，真實到足以讓人在其中生活；而它之為世俗，就是他所說的「空」。

    The argument, laid out 論證攤開來

    McTaggart’s argument of 1908 concludes that time does not exist. Almost nobody accepts the conclusion, and every theory of time since has been built by rejecting one of his premises. Click a step to see what can be said against it.

    麥塔加 1908 年的論證，結論是時間不存在。幾乎沒有人接受這個結論；而此後的每一個時間理論，都是靠否定他的某一個前提建立起來的。點任何一步，看看別人可以怎麼反對它。

    Primary text · 原典

    In his own words 原典怎麼說

    Book XI is a commentary on the first words of Genesis, “In the beginning.” Augustine wants to know what it means for God to have made time, and the inquiry turns into the first sustained philosophy of time in the West. Pusey’s Victorian English keeps “thee” and “hath.”

    卷十一是對〈創世記〉開頭「起初」二字的註解。奧古斯丁想知道「上帝造了時間」是什麼意思，而這場探究成了西方第一部一氣呵成的時間哲學。Pusey 維多利亞時代的英譯保留了 thee、hath 這類古體。

    Augustine, Confessions, Book XI 奧古斯丁《懺悔錄》卷十一 · translated by E. B. Pusey (public domain)

    Did he really say that? · 這句話真的是他說的嗎？

    Asked what God was doing before he made the world, Augustine replied: “Preparing hell for people who ask such questions.”有人問上帝在創造世界之前做什麼，奧古斯丁回答：「在為問這種問題的人準備地獄。」

    He quotes it to reject it · 他引這句話，是為了拒絕它

    The joke is in the Confessions, and Augustine says plainly that he will not use it. “I answer not as one is said to have done merrily … ‘He was preparing hell (saith he) for pryers into mysteries.’ It is one thing to answer enquiries, another to make sport of enquirers.” His own answer is philosophical: there was no “before,” because time was created with the world. “There was no ‘then,’ when there was no time.”

    這個笑話確實在《懺悔錄》裡，而奧古斯丁明白地說他不會用它。「我不像據說有人戲謔地回答的那樣……『他在為窺探奧祕的人準備地獄。』回答提問是一回事，取笑提問的人是另一回事。」他自己的回答是哲學的：根本沒有「之前」，因為時間是跟世界一起被造的。「沒有時間的時候，就沒有『那時』。」

    Both sides · 正反兩面

    Objections and replies 反駁與回應

    Objection 1 · 反駁

    Augustine makes time depend on the mind. But the dinosaurs lived and died over a hundred million years with no one to remember or expect anything.

    奧古斯丁讓時間依賴於心靈。可是恐龍活了又死了一億多年，當時並沒有任何人在記憶或期待什麼。

    Reply · 回應

    The objection is sound against the claim that there was no time before minds, and he does not make that claim. He holds that time began with the created world. What he places in the mind is our measuring of time and the three tenses. A modern eternalist can agree with him exactly: the order of earlier and later is in the world, and pastness, presentness, and futurity come from the standpoint of an experiencer.

    如果針對的是「在有心靈之前沒有時間」這個主張，這個反駁是成立的；但他並沒有這樣主張。他認為時間是跟受造的世界一起開始的。他放進心靈裡的，是我們對時間的度量，以及三種時態。現代的永恆論者可以跟他完全一致：「早於」與「晚於」的次序在世界裡；而「過去」「現在」「未來」這些性質，來自經驗者的立足點。

    Objection 2 · 反駁

    Physics has settled the matter. Relativity shows that there is no universal present, so the flow of time is an illusion and philosophers have nothing to add.

    物理學已經解決了這件事。相對論顯示沒有普遍的現在，所以時間的流動是幻覺，哲學家沒有什麼可補充的。

    Reply · 回應

    Relativity rules out a universal present defined by physics. It does not rule out a privileged present that physics cannot detect, though few find that attractive. More to the point, calling the flow an illusion does not explain it. An illusion is an experience, and an experience of passage seems itself to be something that passes. Explaining how a world without passage produces minds for which there is passage is unfinished work in physics and philosophy alike.

    相對論排除的是「由物理學界定的普遍現在」。它並沒有排除一個物理學偵測不到的、享有特殊地位的現在，雖然很少有人覺得那有吸引力。更要緊的是：把流動稱為幻覺，並沒有解釋它。幻覺是一種經驗；而一個「流逝」的經驗，本身似乎就是某種在流逝的東西。解釋一個沒有流逝的世界，怎麼產生出「對它們而言有流逝」的心靈，這在物理學與哲學裡同樣是未完成的工作。

    Objection 3 · 反駁

    The question is empty. Time is what clocks measure, and nothing more needs to be said.

    這個問題是空的。時間就是時鐘量出來的東西，沒有別的需要說。

    Reply · 回應

    This is a good definition for doing physics, and it leaves the philosophical question where it was. A clock is a process that repeats, and to call it a good clock is to say that its repetitions take equal times, which uses the idea we were trying to define. The definition also says nothing about the difference between past and future, since a clock runs equally well in a film played backward.

    對於做物理學，這是個好定義；而它讓哲學問題留在原地。時鐘是一個會重複的過程；說它是一個好時鐘，就是說它每一次重複所花的時間相等，而這用到了我們正要定義的那個觀念。這個定義對過去與未來的差別也什麼都沒說，因為在倒著播放的影片裡，時鐘走得同樣順暢。

    Dharma and the West · 佛法與西方哲學

    A view from the Dharma 佛法怎麼看

    ☸

    The debate between presentism and eternalism was conducted in India, in detail, some two thousand years before McTaggart. One early school took its very name from its answer. The Sarvāstivādins, “those who say that all exists,” held that past, present, and future things are all real (三世實有), and that a thing’s being present consists only in its exercising its activity. Their reason was a familiar one: if the past did not exist, past deeds could not bear fruit, and a memory would have nothing to be a memory of. Their opponents, the Sautrāntikas, replied that only the present moment exists, that the past is what has ceased and the future is what has not arisen, and that causal influence is passed along from moment to moment like a flame from lamp to lamp. The arguments on each side match the modern ones closely.

    現在論與永恆論之間的辯論，在印度被詳細地進行過，比麥塔加早了大約兩千年。有一個早期的部派，連名字都取自它的答案。說一切有部（Sarvāstivāda，「說一切存在者」）主張過去、現在、未來的事物全都是真實的（三世實有）；一樣東西之所以「現在」，只在於它正在發揮作用。他們的理由聽來很熟悉：如果過去不存在，過去的業就無法結果，而記憶也就沒有對象可記。他們的對手經量部回應說：只有現在這一剎那存在；過去是已經滅去的，未來是還沒生起的；因果的影響是一剎那一剎那地傳下去的，就像火焰從一盞燈傳到另一盞。雙方的論證，跟現代的論證非常吻合。

    Nāgārjuna rejected both. If present and future exist in dependence on the past, he argued, they would have to be in the past already; and if they do not depend on it, they cannot be established at all (若因過去時，有未來現在；未來及現在，應在過去時). The three times are defined by one another and none stands by itself, which is what he means by calling time empty. The practical teaching goes with this. A well-known verse says: “Let one not trace back the past, nor yearn for the future. What is past is left behind, and the future has not been reached” (Majjhima Nikāya 131). Augustine found that the mind is stretched painfully between memory and expectation, and he looked to eternity for rest. The Buddha, starting from the same observation, taught his followers to see each present state clearly as it arises, without being carried away by the other two (Dharma and the West, D16).

    龍樹兩邊都不接受。他論證：如果現在與未來是依待過去而有的，它們就得已經在過去之中；如果它們不依待過去，那它們根本無法成立（「若因過去時，有未來現在；未來及現在，應在過去時」）。三時是彼此界定的，沒有一個獨自站得住；這就是他說時間是「空」的意思。實踐上的教導與此相應。有一首著名的偈說：「不追憶過去，不期盼未來。過去已經捨離，未來還沒到來」（《中部》131）。奧古斯丁發現，心靈在記憶與期待之間被痛苦地拉扯，於是他望向永恆尋求安息。佛陀從同一個觀察出發，教導弟子在每一個當下的狀態生起時清楚地看見它，而不被另外兩者帶走（佛法與西方哲學 D16）。
