A38 · Questions for Today 今天的問題

What Do We Owe the Future? 我們欠未來的人什麼？

They cannot vote, bargain, or thank us, and nearly everything we do decides what they inherit. Jefferson’s claim that the earth belongs to the living, Burke’s partnership with the unborn, the economist’s discount rate, Parfit’s puzzle about who is harmed, Mencius on nets and axes, and the old man who set out to move two mountains.
他們不能投票、不能談判、也不能向我們道謝；而我們做的幾乎每一件事，都決定了他們繼承什麼。傑佛遜說大地屬於活著的人；柏克說社會是與未出生者的合夥；經濟學家的折現率；帕菲特關於「到底誰受了害」的難題；孟子談漁網與斧頭；以及那位動手要移走兩座山的老人。

The big idea · 大觀念

The last lesson asked how far the moral circle extends across species. This one asks how far it extends across time. The people in question do not yet exist, which makes every familiar tool of ethics behave strangely: contracts need two parties, rights need a holder, and harm needs a victim.

上一課問的是，道德的圈子跨過物種可以延伸多遠。這一課問的是，它跨過時間可以延伸多遠。這裡談的人還不存在；這使得倫理學裡每一件熟悉的工具都變得古怪起來：契約需要兩方，權利需要一個持有者，而傷害需要一個受害者。

The essay · 課文

What Do We Owe the Future? 我們欠未來的人什麼？

01Two letters from 1789 1789 年的兩種說法

In September 1789 Thomas Jefferson, then in Paris, wrote to James Madison about a question that the French Revolution had put in his mind: whether one generation has the right to bind another. He thought it plain that it does not. “I set out on this ground, which I suppose to be self-evident, ‘that the earth belongs in usufruct to the living’: that the dead have neither powers nor rights over it.” A generation that left debts for the next to pay would be ruling from the grave. He proposed that every law and constitution should expire after nineteen years.

1789 年 9 月，人在巴黎的湯瑪斯・傑佛遜寫信給詹姆斯・麥迪遜，談一個法國大革命使他想到的問題：一個世代有沒有權利約束另一個世代？他認為答案很明白：沒有。「我從這個我認為不證自明的立足點出發：『大地是以用益權的方式屬於活著的人的』；死者對它既沒有權力，也沒有權利。」一個把債務留給下一代去還的世代，是在墳墓裡統治。他提議，每一條法律、每一部憲法，都應該在十九年之後失效。

A year later Edmund Burke, writing against the same revolution, drew the opposite picture. Society is a contract, he agreed, and it is no ordinary one. It is a partnership in all science, all art, and every virtue, and since its ends cannot be reached in many generations, “it becomes a partnership not only between those who are living, but between those who are living, those who are dead, and those who are to be born.” The living are temporary possessors. They have no right to commit waste on the inheritance and leave to those who come after them “a ruin instead of an habitation.”

一年之後，艾德蒙・柏克在一本反對同一場革命的書裡，畫出了相反的圖像。他同意社會是一份契約；而它並不是一份尋常的契約。它是在一切學問、一切藝術與每一種德行上的合夥；而既然它的目的在許多世代之內都無法達成，「它就成了這樣的一種合夥：不只是活著的人之間的，而是活著的人、已死的人，與將要出生的人之間的。」活著的人是暫時的持有者。他們沒有權利糟蹋這份遺產，留給後來的人「一片廢墟，而不是一個住處」。

The two are usually set against each other, and they share more than they seem to. “Usufruct” is a term of law for the right to use a thing and enjoy its fruits without damaging the thing itself. Jefferson’s living generation may use the earth freely, and may not use it up. Both men hold that we are tenants. They differ on whether the dead may give the tenants instructions.

人們通常把這兩人對立起來；而他們共有的東西，比看起來的多。「用益權」是一個法律用語：使用一樣東西、享用它的收益，卻不損壞那樣東西本身的權利。傑佛遜筆下活著的那一代，可以自由地使用大地；卻不可以把它用光。兩人都主張我們是房客。他們不同的地方在於：死者可不可以對房客下指示。

02Why the usual tools slip 尋常的工具為什麼打滑

Three features make the future a hard case. The first is that power runs one way. We can help or hurt people who will live in two hundred years, and they can do nothing for us or to us. Any theory that grounds duties in mutual advantage has nothing to hold on to. Rawls saw the difficulty and placed the parties behind his veil of ignorance without knowledge of which generation they belong to. They would then agree to save for their successors at a rate they would have wanted their predecessors to follow.

有三個特點，使未來成為一個難題。第一，力量是單向的。我們可以幫助或傷害兩百年後活著的人；而他們既不能為我們做什麼，也不能對我們做什麼。任何一個把義務建立在互利之上的理論，在這裡都沒有著力點。羅爾斯看到了這個困難；他讓無知之幕後面的各方，不知道自己屬於哪一個世代。這樣一來，他們會同意為後繼者儲蓄，而儲蓄的比率，就是他們會希望前人遵守的那一個。

The second is that we do not know who they will be or what they will want. This is a reason for modesty about the gifts we choose for them. It does not weaken the case against leaving them poisons, since we can be confident that they will need air, water, soil, and a stable climate. The third feature is the strangest, and it was noticed only in the 1970s.

第二，我們不知道他們會是誰，也不知道他們會想要什麼。這是一個理由，要我們在替他們挑選禮物的時候謙虛一點。它並沒有削弱「不要留毒物給他們」的理由；因為我們可以確信，他們會需要空氣、水、土壤，以及穩定的氣候。第三個特點最奇怪；而且到了 1970 年代才有人注意到。

03Parfit’s puzzle 帕菲特的難題

Suppose a society must choose between two energy policies. One conserves resources, and the other depletes them and gives the people of the next two centuries a slightly easier life, followed by a much worse one. Under depletion, people would hold different jobs, live in different places, and meet different partners. Within a few generations no one would be born who would have been born under the other policy, because each of us comes from a particular pair of cells joined at a particular time. Now consider the people who live badly in the third century of depletion. They cannot say that the policy made them worse off. Had the other policy been chosen, they would never have existed. If their lives are worth living, depletion harmed no one.

假設一個社會必須在兩種能源政策之間選擇。一種保育資源；另一種把資源耗盡，給接下來兩個世紀的人稍微輕鬆一點的生活，接著是糟得多的生活。在耗盡的政策之下，人們會做不同的工作、住在不同的地方、遇到不同的伴侶。幾個世代之內，就不會有任何一個「在另一種政策之下也會出生」的人出生了；因為我們每一個人，都來自在某個特定時刻結合的某一對特定的細胞。現在考慮那些在耗盡政策的第三個世紀裡過得很糟的人。他們不能說，這項政策使他們的處境變差了。如果當初選了另一項政策，他們根本不會存在。只要他們的生命還值得活，耗盡政策就沒有傷害任何人。

Derek Parfit, who set out this non-identity problem in 1984, did not conclude that depletion is acceptable. He concluded that our ordinary idea of wrongdoing, on which an act is wrong only if it is worse for someone, cannot be the whole truth. Some acts are wrong because of the kind of world they bring about, whoever lives in it. He spent years looking for the principle that would say this without leading to conclusions he found repugnant, and said that he had not found it.

德瑞克・帕菲特在 1984 年提出了這個「非同一性問題」；他並沒有結論說耗盡是可以接受的。他的結論是：我們尋常的「做錯事」的觀念，也就是「一個行為只有在對某個人比較糟的時候才是錯的」，不可能是全部的真相。有些行為之所以錯，是因為它們帶來的那種世界；不管住在裡面的是誰。他花了許多年尋找那條原則：能把這一點說出來，又不導向他認為令人反感的結論。他說，他沒有找到。

04The discount rate 折現率

Governments answer the question every time they weigh a cost today against a benefit later, and they answer it with a number. Economists discount future costs and benefits at a yearly rate, for three reasons that are worth keeping apart. People in the future will probably be richer, so a dollar will mean less to them. The future is uncertain, and the benefit may never arrive. And people simply prefer goods sooner. The third reason, applied to the welfare of other people, has been attacked by economists themselves. Frank Ramsey, who founded the theory in 1928, called it ethically indefensible and said that it arose merely from the weakness of the imagination.

各國政府每一次拿今天的成本去衡量日後的效益，都在回答這個問題；而它們是用一個數字來回答的。經濟學家以一個年率把未來的成本與效益「折現」；理由有三個，值得分開來看。未來的人大概會比較富有，所以一塊錢對他們的意義比較小。未來是不確定的；那份效益也許永遠不會到來。還有，人們就是偏好早一點得到好東西。第三個理由用在別人的福祉上，受到了經濟學家自己的抨擊。法蘭克・拉姆齊在 1928 年奠定了這套理論；他說這種做法在倫理上站不住腳，只是出於想像力的薄弱。

The number matters more than any argument in this lesson. At a rate of three percent, a harm that falls in two hundred years counts for less than one three-hundredth of the same harm today. The dispute over climate policy between the Stern Review of 2006 and its critics was in large part a dispute about this number. At the other extreme stands the view called longtermism, which gives future people the same weight as present ones. Since they may outnumber us by thousands to one, it concludes that shaping the far future is the most important thing we do. Its critics reply that a theory which lets enormous and speculative future benefits outweigh the plain needs of the living poor has gone wrong somewhere.

這個數字，比這一課裡的任何一個論證都更要緊。以百分之三的比率，一項落在兩百年後的傷害，算起來還不到今天同樣傷害的三百分之一。2006 年的《史登報告》與它的批評者之間關於氣候政策的爭論，很大一部分就是關於這個數字的爭論。另一個極端，是稱為「長期主義」的看法：它給未來的人與現在的人同樣的份量。既然他們的人數可能是我們的幾千倍，它的結論是：塑造遙遠的未來，是我們所做的最重要的事。它的批評者回答：一個理論，如果讓龐大而出於推測的未來效益，壓過了活著的窮人明明白白的需要，那它一定在某個地方出了錯。

05Nets, axes, and two mountains 漁網、斧頭，與兩座山

The Chinese tradition approached the matter through the family and the seasons, and came to it earlier. Mencius told a king that good government begins with restraint in taking: “If close-meshed nets are not allowed in the pools and ponds, there will be more fish and turtles than can be eaten. If axes enter the hills and forests only at the proper time, there will be more timber than can be used.” The rule concerns what a community may take if those who come after are to take as well. The motive given is care for one’s own people. Rights do not come into it.

中國的傳統是透過家族與時令來處理這件事的；而且來得更早。孟子告訴一位國君，好的治理從取用的節制開始：「數罟不入洿池，魚鼈不可勝食也；斧斤以時入山林，材木不可勝用也。」這條規矩講的是：如果後來的人也要能取用，一個群體現在可以取多少。它給出的動機，是對自己人民的照顧。這裡沒有提到權利。

The other side of the bargain is told in the story of the Foolish Old Man. At nearly ninety he begins to dig away the two mountains that block his door. A clever neighbor laughs at him, and he answers: “Though I die, my sons will remain. My sons will have grandsons, and the grandsons will have sons. Sons and grandsons without end, and the mountains grow no higher. Why should they not be leveled?” A person who thinks of himself as one link in a line can begin work that he will not finish and plant trees whose shade he will not sit in. On this view what we owe the future is the reverse side of what we owe the past, and both are paid in the same coin.

這筆交易的另一面，是在愚公的故事裡講的。他年近九十，動手去挖那兩座擋住他家門的山。一個聰明的鄰人嘲笑他；他回答：「雖我之死，有子存焉；子又生孫，孫又生子；子又有子，子又有孫，子子孫孫無窮匱也，而山不加增，何苦而不平？」一個把自己看成一條線上的一環的人，可以開始做他完成不了的工作，可以種下他坐不到樹蔭的樹。照這個看法，我們欠未來的，就是我們欠過去的東西的另一面；而兩者是用同一種錢來還的。

Primary text · 原典

In their own words 原典怎麼說

Two men of the same generation, on opposite sides of the same revolution.

同一個世代的兩個人，站在同一場革命的兩邊。

Thomas Jefferson, letter to James Madison, September 6, 1789; Edmund Burke, Reflections on the Revolution in France (1790) 傑佛遜致麥迪遜的信；柏克《法國大革命反思》 · Both in the original English, public domain

Did he really say that? · 這句話真的是他說的嗎？

“In every deliberation, we must consider the impact on the seventh generation.” — The Great Law of the Iroquois「在每一次商議當中，我們都必須考慮對第七代的影響。」——易洛魁大法

The idea is theirs; the sentence is not in the Great Law · 想法是他們的；這句話卻不在大法裡

The sentence is printed on household products and quoted in environmental reports as a clause of the Great Law of Peace, the constitution of the Haudenosaunee, or Iroquois, Confederacy. The written versions of the Great Law do not contain it. What they contain is an instruction to newly installed chiefs: “Look and listen for the welfare of the whole people and have always in view not only the present but also the coming generations, even those whose faces are yet beneath the surface of the ground—the unborn of the future Nation.” The number seven occurs in the same passage in another connection. The chief’s skin is to be seven spans thick, which is to say proof against anger and criticism. The seventh generation as a rule of decision comes from modern Haudenosaunee leaders, among them Oren Lyons, who state it as a living teaching of their people. So the principle is authentically theirs, and the popular wording is a twentieth-century summary of it. The original image, of faces waiting beneath the ground, is better than the slogan.

這句話印在家用品上，也被環境報告引用，說它是「和平大法」的一個條款；和平大法是豪德諾索尼（易洛魁）聯盟的憲法。大法的書面版本裡並沒有這句話。裡面有的，是對新就任的酋長的一段訓示：「要為全體人民的福祉而看、而聽；眼中始終不只有現在，還有將要到來的世代，甚至是那些面孔還在地表之下的人：未來民族裡尚未出生的人。」「七」這個數字，在同一段裡是為了別的事出現的。酋長的皮要有七拃厚；也就是說，要經得起憤怒與批評。把「第七代」當作決策的規則，是出自現代的豪德諾索尼領袖，奧倫・萊昂斯是其中一位；他們把它當作自己民族活著的教導來陳述。所以，這條原則確確實實是他們的；而流行的那個措辭，是二十世紀對它的概括。原本的那個意象，那些在地底下等待著的面孔，比口號更好。

Both sides · 正反兩面

Objections and replies 反駁與回應

Objection 1 · 反駁

Future people will have technology we cannot imagine. They will solve their own problems, as every generation has.

未來的人會有我們想像不到的科技。他們會解決自己的問題，就像每一代人都做到的那樣。

Reply · 回應

They may. This is the “they will be richer” argument in another form, and it has the same limit. Technology replaces some things and not others, and it cannot bring back what is extinct. The argument also asks them to bear a risk that we chose for our own benefit, which we would not think fair between two people living now. Mencius’s rule about nets does not rest on any forecast. It asks only that we leave the pond able to renew itself.

也許會。這是「他們會比較富有」那個論證的另一種形式；而它有同樣的限度。科技能取代某些東西，取代不了另一些；而它沒辦法把已經滅絕的東西帶回來。這個論證還要他們去承擔一項風險，而那項風險是我們為了自己的好處而選的；在兩個現在活著的人之間，我們不會認為這樣公平。孟子關於漁網的那條規矩，並不立足於任何預測。它只要求：我們留下的池塘，還能自己恢復。

Objection 2 · 反駁

There are people suffering now. To spend on the unborn while the living starve is a moral luxury.

現在就有人在受苦。在活著的人挨餓的時候把錢花在還沒出生的人身上，是一種道德上的奢侈。

Reply · 回應

This objection has force against the strongest forms of longtermism, and it is one reason not to set the rate at zero without thought. It has less force than it seems against ordinary duties to the future. Most of what protects the next century, such as clean air, sound soil, and schools, also helps the living poor, who suffer first from every kind of depletion. The two causes compete far less often than the objection supposes.

這個反駁，對最強形式的長期主義是有力的；而它也是「不要不加思索地把比率定為零」的一個理由。可是，用來反對對未來的尋常義務，它沒有看起來那麼有力。保護下一個世紀的東西，例如乾淨的空氣、健全的土壤與學校，大部分也幫助了活著的窮人；每一種耗竭，最先受害的就是他們。這兩件事彼此競爭的時候，遠比這個反駁所設想的少。

Objection 3 · 反駁

Nobody represents the future. A duty that no one can claim or enforce is not a real duty.

沒有人代表未來。一項沒有人能主張、也沒有人能執行的義務，不是真正的義務。

Reply · 回應

Many real duties are owed to those who cannot claim them: to infants, to animals, to the dead whose wills we honor. The absence of a claimant is a practical problem, and societies have begun to answer it. Wales has had a commissioner for future generations since 2016, and several constitutions now name them. The older answer is the one in Burke and in the Foolish Old Man: people who see themselves as part of a line do the representing themselves.

許多真正的義務，都是對那些無法主張的對象負的：對嬰兒，對動物，對我們尊重其遺囑的死者。沒有人來主張，是一個實務上的問題；而各個社會已經開始回應它。威爾斯從 2016 年起設有一位「未來世代專員」；現在也有好幾部憲法寫到了未來世代。比較古老的回答，就是柏克與愚公那裡的那一個：把自己看成一條線上的一部分的人，自己就在做那個代表。

Terms of art · 哲學術語

The vocabulary of the subject 這門學問的用語

intergenerational justice世代正義

The part of ethics that concerns what generations owe to one another.

倫理學裡談「各個世代彼此虧欠什麼」的那一部分。

usufruct用益權

L. usus fructus ‘use and fruit’

The right to use something and enjoy its produce without damaging the thing itself.

使用一樣東西並享用它的產出、卻不損壞那樣東西本身的權利。

non-identity problem非同一性問題

Parfit’s puzzle that choices affecting who is born seem to harm no one.

帕菲特的難題：那些影響「誰會出生」的選擇，似乎沒有傷害任何人。

discount rate折現率

The yearly rate at which future costs and benefits are reduced when compared with present ones.

把未來的成本與效益拿來跟現在的相比時，每年打折的比率。

pure time preference純粹時間偏好

Valuing a benefit less merely because it comes later.

只因為一項好處來得比較晚，就把它看得比較輕。

just savings principle正義的儲蓄原則

Rawls’s rule for how much each generation should set aside for the next.

羅爾斯的規則：每一個世代該為下一個世代留下多少。

longtermism長期主義

The view that positively influencing the long-term future is a key moral priority of our time.

一種看法：對長遠的未來產生正面的影響，是我們這個時代道德上的一項首要之務。

sustainability永續

Using a resource at a rate that leaves it able to renew itself.

以一種讓資源還能自己恢復的速率來使用它。

Dharma and the West · 佛法與西方哲學

A view from the Dharma 佛法怎麼看

☸

Buddhism removes one of the premises that make the Western problem hard. The debate assumes that future people are strangers to us, with whom we have no dealings. On the teaching of rebirth, the beings who will inhabit the world in three hundred years include those now alive, and the state of that world is in part the ripening of what is done now. The discourse on the wheel-turning monarch tells the history of a society in this way. A king fails to provide for the poor, theft appears, then weapons, then killing and lying, and with each generation life grows shorter and coarser, until people recover by deciding to stop killing. The decline and the recovery are both the work of conduct handed down. The text presents no fate or decree behind them.

佛教拿掉了使西方這個問題變難的前提之一。那場爭論假定：未來的人對我們來說是陌生人，我們與他們沒有任何往來。照輪迴的教法，三百年後住在這個世界上的眾生，包括了現在活著的；而那個世界的狀況，有一部分是現在所做的事的成熟。《轉輪聖王獅子吼經》就是這樣講述一個社會的歷史的。一位國王沒有照顧窮人；於是有了偷盜，接著有了武器，接著有了殺生與說謊；每過一代，壽命就更短、生活就更粗劣；直到人們決定不再殺生，才恢復過來。衰敗與恢復，都是一代傳一代的行為造成的。經文並沒有說背後有什麼命運或天命。

A second point of contact is Parfit himself, who thought his view of the self close to the Buddha’s and said so. If there is no deep fact that makes my future self me, and only a chain of connected states, then my concern for my own old age and my concern for people yet unborn differ in degree. They do not differ in kind. The boundary that made the future someone else’s problem grows thin. The Mahāyāna gives the practical form of this in the bodhisattva’s vow, which is taken for the sake of all beings through all future time, and in the dedication of merit at the end of every practice. Both train a person to act as the Foolish Old Man acted, for a result that he will not be present to collect (Dharma and Western Philosophy D2).

第二個接觸點是帕菲特本人；他認為自己對「自我」的看法很接近佛陀的，而且這樣說過。如果並沒有一項深層的事實，使未來的那個我成為「我」，有的只是一串相連的狀態；那麼，我對自己晚年的關心，與我對尚未出生者的關心，只有程度上的不同。兩者並沒有種類上的不同。那條使未來成為「別人的問題」的界線，就變薄了。大乘給了這一點實踐上的形式：菩薩的誓願，是為了盡未來際的一切眾生而發的；每一次修行結束時的迴向也是。兩者都訓練一個人像愚公那樣行動：為了一個他不會在場領取的結果（佛法與西方哲學 D2）。
