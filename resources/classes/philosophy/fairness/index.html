A26 · Society and Justice 社會與正義

What Is Fair? 什麼是公平？

Everyone wants a fair share and no one agrees on what it is. Aristotle’s proportion, Rawls’s veil of ignorance, Nozick’s basketball player, and a society you must design before you know which place in it will be yours.
人人都要公平的一份，而沒有人對「那是多少」有共識。亞里斯多德的比例、羅爾斯的無知之幕、諾齊克的籃球員；以及一個你必須在知道自己會落在哪個位置之前，就先設計好的社會。

A26 Big Questions · 大哉問Society and Justice · 社會與正義Advanced · 高級1,220 words · about 25 min
The big idea · 大觀念

The last lesson asked why we need rules at all. This one asks how the rules should divide what a society produces. People who agree that shares should be fair turn out to mean at least four different things, and the choice among them decides who pays for schools and hospitals, and who is owed what.

上一課問的是：我們到底為什麼需要規則。這一課問的是：規則應該怎麼分配一個社會所生產的東西。大家都同意每個人的那一份應該公平；結果他們指的至少是四件不同的事。而在這四者之間怎麼選，決定了誰為學校與醫院付錢，以及誰被欠了什麼。

The essay · 課文

What Is Fair? 什麼是公平？

01One cuts, the other chooses 一個切，另一個選

The sense of unfairness is older than philosophy and probably older than our species. In a well-known experiment, two capuchin monkeys in neighboring cages were paid for the same small task. Both were content to receive a slice of cucumber until one of them was given a grape. The other then threw her cucumber back at the experimenter. Children of three behave in much the same way. What takes longer to develop is the ability to state a rule that both sides can accept.

不公平的感覺比哲學古老，大概也比我們這個物種古老。在一個著名的實驗裡，兩隻住在相鄰籠子裡的捲尾猴，做同一件小事而得到報酬。兩隻本來都滿足於拿到一片小黃瓜，直到其中一隻拿到了一顆葡萄。另一隻於是把她的小黃瓜朝實驗者丟了回去。三歲的孩子，行為也差不多是這樣。需要比較久才發展出來的，是說出一條雙方都能接受的規則的能力。

Parents know one such rule. Two children are to share a cake, so one cuts and the other chooses. The child with the knife does not know which piece will be hers, and so she cuts as evenly as she can. No one has to trust anyone’s good will. The fairness comes from a procedure in which a person decides before she knows where she will end up. The most influential theory of justice of the last hundred years is that kitchen rule applied to a whole society.

做父母的知道一條這樣的規則。兩個孩子要分一塊蛋糕；那就一個切，另一個選。拿刀的孩子不知道哪一塊會是她的，所以她盡可能切得一樣大。誰也不必信任誰的善意。公平來自一個程序：一個人在知道自己最後會落在哪裡之前，就先做了決定。過去一百年裡最有影響力的正義理論，就是把這條廚房裡的規則，用在一整個社會上。

02Equal to what? 跟什麼相等？

Aristotle gave the first careful account. Justice in distribution, he said, is a kind of proportion. Quarrels arise “when either equals have and are awarded unequal shares, or unequals equal shares.” If two people worked on a job and one worked twice as long, equal pay would be unfair. He then identified the real difficulty. Everyone agrees that shares should follow merit, and they do not agree about what merit is. Democrats say it is being a free citizen, oligarchs say wealth, and aristocrats say excellence.

亞里斯多德給出了第一個仔細的說明。他說，分配上的正義是一種比例。爭吵的起因是：「相等的人擁有或得到了不相等的份，或者不相等的人得到了相等的份。」如果兩個人做同一件工作，而其中一人做了兩倍的時間，那麼同酬就是不公平的。接著他指出了真正的困難。人人都同意，份額應該依照「配得」；而他們對「配得」是什麼，意見並不一致。民主派說是自由公民的身分；寡頭派說是財富；貴族派說是卓越。

The argument has not changed much since. One side holds that shares should follow contribution, another that they should follow need, and a third that they should simply be equal. Chinese thinkers divided along the same lines. Mencius, arguing against a reformer who wanted goods of the same size sold at the same price whatever their quality, replied that “it is the nature of things to be unequal,” and that to treat them alike would throw the world into confusion. The Daodejing took the other side: the way of Heaven takes from what has too much and gives to what has too little, while the way of men takes from those who have too little to offer to those who have too much.

從那以後，這場爭論並沒有改變多少。一方主張份額應該跟著貢獻走；另一方說跟著需要走；第三方說就該相等。中國的思想家也沿著同樣的線分開。有一位改革者主張，大小相同的貨物不論品質都賣同一個價錢；孟子反駁他說：「夫物之不齊，物之情也」，把它們一律看待，是把天下搞亂。《道德經》站在另一邊：「天之道，損有餘而補不足。人之道則不然，損不足以奉有餘。」

03Behind the veil 在幕的後面

In 1971 the American philosopher John Rawls proposed a way to settle the matter without first agreeing on merit. Imagine that the members of a society meet to choose the principles that will govern it. They know the general facts about economics and human psychology. They do not know anything about themselves: whether they will be rich or poor, clever or slow, healthy or sick, male or female, of which race or which religion. Rawls called this condition the veil of ignorance. Since no one can design rules to favor his own case, whatever is chosen there is fair, for the same reason the cake is fairly cut.

1971 年，美國哲學家約翰・羅爾斯提出了一個辦法：不必先對「配得」有共識，就能把這件事定下來。想像一個社會的成員聚在一起，要選出將來治理這個社會的原則。他們知道關於經濟與人類心理的一般事實。他們對自己一無所知：自己會是富是窮、聰明還是遲鈍、健康還是多病、是男是女、屬於哪個種族或哪個宗教。羅爾斯把這種狀況稱為「無知之幕」。既然沒有人能設計出偏袒自己那種情況的規則，那麼在那裡選出來的不管是什麼，都是公平的；理由跟蛋糕切得公平是同一個。

Rawls argued that people in that position would choose two principles. The first gives everyone the same basic liberties, such as freedom of speech and conscience and the vote. The second allows inequalities of wealth and position on two conditions: the positions must be open to all under fair equality of opportunity, and the inequalities must work to the greatest benefit of the least advantaged. He called this last condition the difference principle. A surgeon may earn more than a porter if paying surgeons more makes the porter better off than he would be under any other arrangement. The reasoning is that of someone who may turn out to be the porter.

羅爾斯主張，處在那個位置的人會選出兩條原則。第一條給每個人同樣的基本自由，例如言論與良心的自由、投票權。第二條容許財富與地位的不平等，但有兩個條件：那些職位必須在公平的機會平等之下對所有人開放；而那些不平等必須對處境最不利的人最有好處。他把最後這個條件稱為「差異原則」。外科醫師可以賺得比搬運工多，如果付給外科醫師更多錢，會讓搬運工過得比在任何別的安排之下都好。這是一個「最後可能發現自己就是搬運工」的人的推理。

Behind the veil lies a claim about luck. Nobody chooses his parents, his native talents, or the country and century of his birth. “No one deserves his greater natural capacity,” Rawls wrote, “nor merits a more favorable starting place in society.” If so, the fortunate may keep the fruits of their gifts only on terms that the unlucky could accept.

幕的後面，有一個關於運氣的主張。沒有人選擇自己的父母、天生的才能，或是出生的國家與世紀。羅爾斯寫道：「沒有人應得他那較高的天生能力，也沒有人配得一個在社會裡較有利的起點。」如果是這樣，那麼幸運的人要保有他們天賦的果實，就只能依照不幸的人也能接受的條件。

04Liberty upsets patterns 自由會打亂模式

Three years later Robert Nozick, a younger colleague of Rawls in the Harvard philosophy department, published the best-known reply. Begin, he said, with whatever distribution you think is just. Call it D1. Now suppose that the basketball star Wilt Chamberlain signs a contract under which twenty-five cents from every ticket goes to him. In the course of a season a million people gladly drop their quarters in a box, and Chamberlain ends up with $250,000, far more than anyone else. The new distribution, D2, no longer fits your pattern. Yet everyone began with a just share and moved to D2 by free choices, so it is hard to see what could make D2 unjust.

三年後，羅伯特・諾齊克發表了最有名的回應；他是羅爾斯在哈佛哲學系裡較年輕的同事。他說：從任何一個你認為正義的分配開始。叫它 D1。現在假設籃球明星張伯倫簽了一份合約：每張門票有二十五分錢歸他。一個球季下來，有一百萬人高高興興地把他們的二十五分錢丟進一個箱子裡；張伯倫最後有了二十五萬美元，遠比任何人都多。新的分配 D2，不再符合你的模式了。然而每個人都是從正義的一份開始，並且經由自由的選擇走到 D2；那就很難看出，是什麼能讓 D2 不正義。

Nozick concluded that justice depends on how holdings came about and that no particular shape of distribution is required. What a person acquired fairly and received by free exchange or gift is his. To preserve any pattern, a government would have to interfere with people’s choices without end. Hume had made the same point in 1751: “Render possessions ever so equal, men’s different degrees of art, care, and industry will immediately break that equality.” Nozick drew a sharper conclusion than Hume did. Taxing earnings, he wrote, is “on a par with forced labor.”

諾齊克的結論是：正義取決於持有物是怎麼來的；它並不要求任何特定形狀的分配。一個人公平地取得的、以及經由自由交換或餽贈得到的東西，就是他的。要維持任何一種模式，政府就得沒完沒了地干涉人們的選擇。休謨在 1751 年已經說過同樣的話：「就算把財產分得再怎麼平等，人們在技藝、用心與勤勉上的不同程度，也會立刻打破那個平等。」諾齊克得出的結論比休謨更尖銳。他寫道：對勞動所得課稅，「與強迫勞動無異」。

Rawls’s reply was that his principles govern the basic structure of society, meaning its tax system, its schools, and its law of property and inheritance. They do not govern each transaction. Within fair background rules, people may trade as they like. Without such rules, Chamberlain’s children start the next game with an advantage they did nothing to earn, and their children with a greater one.

羅爾斯的回應是：他的原則管的是社會的「基本結構」，也就是它的稅制、學校、財產法與繼承法。它們不管每一筆交易。在公平的背景規則之內，人們愛怎麼交易就怎麼交易。沒有這樣的規則，張伯倫的孩子在下一場比賽開始時，就帶著一份他們什麼都沒做而得來的優勢；而他們的孩子，帶著更大的一份。

05Would you really choose that? 你真的會那樣選嗎？

A second objection accepts the veil and disputes what would be chosen behind it. The economist John Harsanyi had used a similar device some years before Rawls, and he drew the opposite conclusion. A rational person who has an equal chance of being anyone would choose the society with the highest average, and that is the utilitarian answer. To insist on the best possible worst case is to reason like someone who never crosses a street. Rawls answered that the stake here is one’s whole life, with no second draw, and that no one could honestly ask the losers to accept their lot for the sake of a higher average.

第二個反駁接受了那道幕，而對「幕後會選出什麼」提出異議。經濟學家約翰・海薩尼比羅爾斯早幾年就用過一個類似的設計，而他得出了相反的結論。一個理性的人，如果有同等的機會成為任何一個人，就會選擇平均最高的那個社會；而那是功利主義的答案。堅持「最壞的情況要盡可能地好」，就像一個從來不過馬路的人的推理。羅爾斯回答說：這裡的賭注是一個人的整個人生，沒有第二次抽籤；而沒有人能誠實地要求輸的人，為了一個較高的平均而接受他們的命運。

In the 1980s two political scientists, Norman Frohlich and Joe Oppenheimer, tried the experiment on real people. Groups of students were asked to agree on a rule for dividing income before they knew what their own income would be. Almost none chose Rawls’s difference principle, and few chose the highest average. Most groups, in several countries, settled on a mixed rule: guarantee a floor below which no one may fall, and above it let the total grow as large as it can.

1980 年代，兩位政治學家諾曼・弗羅利希與喬・歐本海默，在真人身上做了這個實驗。他們請一組一組的學生，在知道自己的收入會是多少之前，先對一條分配收入的規則達成共識。幾乎沒有一組選擇羅爾斯的差異原則；選擇最高平均的也很少。在好幾個國家，多數的小組都落在一條混合的規則上：保證一個誰都不會跌破的底線；在那之上，讓總量能長多大就長多大。

06What the veil is for 這道幕是做什麼用的

None of this has ended the argument. What Rawls gave to both sides is a test for our own opinions. Most views about fairness fit the interests of those who hold them. The well-paid tend to believe in desert, and the badly paid in need. The veil asks one question: would you still favor this rule if you did not know which side of it you would be on? It is the question the child with the knife has to answer. A rule that passes may still be mistaken, but it is not merely self-serving.

這一切都沒有終結這場爭論。羅爾斯給了雙方的，是一個檢驗自己意見的方法。大多數關於公平的看法，都很合乎持有這些看法的人的利益。高薪的人傾向於相信「應得」；低薪的人傾向於相信「需要」。這道幕只問一個問題：如果你不知道自己會落在這條規則的哪一邊，你還會贊成它嗎？這就是拿刀的那個孩子必須回答的問題。通過了的規則仍然可能是錯的；但它不只是為自己打算。

Primary text · 原典

In their own words 原典怎麼說

The oldest statement of what fairness in distribution is, and the classic warning about trying to enforce equal shares. Rawls’s and Nozick’s books are still in copyright and are quoted only briefly in the essay.

關於「分配上的公平是什麼」最古老的陳述；以及關於「試圖強制執行均分」的經典警告。羅爾斯與諾齊克的書仍在版權期內，只在課文裡簡短引用。

Aristotle, Nicomachean Ethics, Book V, chapter 3 (1131a); David Hume, An Enquiry Concerning the Principles of Morals (1751), Section III, Part II 亞里斯多德《尼各馬可倫理學》卷五第三章；休謨《道德原則研究》第三節第二部分 · Aristotle translated by W. D. Ross (public domain); Hume in the original English and spelling

Did he really say that? · 這句話真的是他說的嗎？

「不患寡而患不均」 (Confucius: do not worry that there is little, worry that it is unequally shared)「不患寡而患不均」（孔子：不怕少，只怕分得不平均）

His words; the usual reading is doubtful · 是他的話；通行的讀法有疑問

The line is in the Analects (16.1) and is often quoted as a call for equal shares. The context is a planned attack by the powerful Ji family on a small dependent state, and Confucius is rebuking two disciples who serve the family. The full sentence reads: “不患寡而患不均，不患貧而患不安.” Zhu Xi’s standard commentary glosses 寡 as “few people” and 均 as “each receiving his proper portion,” which is proportion in Aristotle’s sense. It does not mean equal shares. The Han scholar Dong Zhongshu quotes the saying in a different form, “不患貧而患不均,” and draws a conclusion that sounds like Rawls: let the rich have enough to show their rank without becoming arrogant, and the poor enough to sustain life without falling into distress.

這句話在《論語》裡（〈季氏〉第一章），常被引用來主張均分。它的脈絡是：有權勢的季氏打算攻打一個附庸小國顓臾；孔子是在責備兩個為季氏做事的弟子。完整的句子是：「不患寡而患不均，不患貧而患不安。」朱熹的《集注》把「寡」解作「民少」，把「均」解作「各得其分」；那是亞里斯多德意義下的比例。它的意思不是均分。漢代的董仲舒引這句話時，是另一個樣子：「不患貧而患不均」；而他由此得出的結論，聽起來很像羅爾斯：「使富者足以示貴而不至於驕，貧者足以養生而不至於憂。」

Both sides · 正反兩面

Objections and replies 反駁與回應

Objection 1 · 反駁

People deserve what they earn by hard work. The veil hides exactly the facts that justify unequal rewards.

人應得他們靠努力工作賺來的東西。那道幕遮住的，恰恰是讓不平等的報酬有正當理由的事實。

Reply · 回應

Rawls replied that the capacity for effort is itself shaped by a fortunate family and upbringing, so that desert cannot be the foundation. Many readers find this his least convincing step, since it seems to leave nothing that a person can take credit for. A moderate position is open: effort deserves reward, and the size of the reward that a given effort commands in the market is largely luck.

羅爾斯回答說：努力的能力本身，就是由幸運的家庭與教養塑造的；所以「應得」不能當作基礎。許多讀者覺得這是他最沒有說服力的一步，因為它似乎沒有留下任何一個人可以居功的東西。有一個溫和的立場可以採取：努力值得回報；而某一份努力在市場上換得到多大的回報，大體上是運氣。

Objection 2 · 反駁

If taxing earnings is forced labor, as Nozick says, then every modern state is a slave owner. That cannot be right.

如果像諾齊克說的，對所得課稅就是強迫勞動，那麼每一個現代國家都是奴隸主。這不可能對。

Reply · 回應

Critics answer that a worker who is taxed may choose his occupation, his hours, and whether to work at all, which a forced laborer may not. They add that Nozick’s theory requires holdings to have been justly acquired in the first place, and that almost no actual holding has a clean history. Nozick admitted that past injustice might call for large corrections, and he later wrote that the libertarian position he had defended was seriously inadequate.

批評者回答：一個被課稅的工作者，可以選擇自己的職業、工時，以及到底要不要工作；被強迫勞動的人不能。他們還補充：諾齊克的理論要求持有物一開始就是正義地取得的；而現實中幾乎沒有哪一份持有物有乾淨的歷史。諾齊克承認，過去的不正義也許需要大幅的矯正；他後來還寫道，他辯護過的那個自由至上主義立場有嚴重的不足。

Objection 3 · 反駁

Fairness is about how we treat one another. A society could satisfy Rawls’s principles and still be full of contempt for those at the bottom.

公平關乎我們怎麼對待彼此。一個社會可以滿足羅爾斯的原則，而仍然對底層的人充滿輕蔑。

Reply · 回應

Elizabeth Anderson, who studied under Rawls, pressed this point. The aim of equality, she argued, is that people stand as equals, with no one made to bow. Compensating the unlucky for their bad luck is a different thing. A check that comes with pity may worsen the insult. Rawls would largely agree. He listed the social bases of self-respect among the goods to be distributed, and called it perhaps the most important of them.

曾是羅爾斯學生的伊莉莎白・安德森，力主這一點。她主張：平等的目標，是人們以平等者的身分站在一起，沒有人被迫低頭。為不幸的人的壞運氣提供補償，是另一回事。一張帶著憐憫而來的支票，可能使羞辱更深。羅爾斯大體上會同意。他把「自尊的社會基礎」列在要分配的那些善當中，並且說它也許是其中最重要的一項。

Terms of art · 哲學術語

The vocabulary of the subject 這門學問的用語

distributive justice分配正義

Justice in the division of benefits and burdens among the members of a society.

在一個社會的成員之間，分配利益與負擔時的正義。

original position原初立場

Rawls’s imagined situation in which people choose principles of justice without knowing their own place in society.

羅爾斯所設想的情境：人們在不知道自己在社會裡的位置的情況下，選擇正義的原則。

veil of ignorance無知之幕

The condition of not knowing one’s own class, talents, sex, race, or beliefs when choosing principles.

在選擇原則時，不知道自己的階級、才能、性別、種族或信念的那種狀況。

difference principle差異原則

Inequalities are just only if they work to the greatest benefit of the least advantaged.

不平等只有在對處境最不利者最有好處的時候，才是正義的。

maximin最大化最小值

L. maximum minimorum

The rule of choosing the option whose worst outcome is better than the worst outcome of any other.

一條選擇的規則：選那個「最壞結果」比其他任何選項的最壞結果都好的選項。

desert應得、配得

OFr. deservir ‘to deserve’

What a person deserves on account of what he has done or what he is like. Pronounced like dessert.

一個人因為他做過的事或他的為人而應當得到的東西。發音跟 dessert（甜點）一樣。

entitlement theory資格理論

Nozick’s view that a holding is just if it was justly acquired and justly transferred.

諾齊克的看法：一份持有物如果是正義地取得、正義地轉讓的，它就是正義的。

each receiving his portion均（各得其分）

Ch. 均 jūn

In the Analects, a fitting distribution. The word later came to mean equal.

在《論語》裡，指合宜的分配。這個字後來才有了「相等」的意思。

安德森對自己的老師力主這一點。

    想一條你支持的規則：在工作上、在你的學校裡，或是在國家的政治裡。用那道幕檢驗它：如果你不知道自己會落在它的哪一邊，你還會支持它嗎？寫 150 個字。

Dharma and the West · 佛法與西方哲學

A view from the Dharma 佛法怎麼看

☸

The Buddha gave no theory of distribution, but one discourse reads like a parable about the floor. A king is told the duties of a just ruler, one of which is: “Provide money to the penniless in the realm.” He keeps order and neglects this one duty. Poverty spreads, and a man steals. Brought before the king, he says, “Your Majesty, I can’t survive.” The king gives him money. Others hear of it and steal as well, so the king turns to executions, and people arm themselves. The discourse sums up: “From not providing money to the penniless, poverty became widespread. When poverty was widespread, theft became widespread. When theft was widespread, swords became widespread.” The text does not say that everyone must have the same. It says that a society which lets some fall below what is needed to live will pay for it in violence, and that relief after the theft comes too late.

佛陀沒有給出分配的理論；但有一部經，讀起來像一則關於「底線」的寓言。有人告訴一位國王，正直的統治者有哪些職責；其中一條是：「國內有貧窮的人，要給他們資財。」他維持了秩序，卻忽略了這一條。貧窮蔓延開來，有一個人偷了東西。他被帶到國王面前時說：「大王，我活不下去。」國王給了他錢。其他人聽說了，也去偷；於是國王改用處決，而人們開始武裝自己。這部經總結說：「因為不把資財給貧窮的人，貧窮就蔓延了。貧窮蔓延，偷盜就蔓延。偷盜蔓延，刀劍就蔓延。」經文並沒有說每個人擁有的必須一樣多。它說的是：一個社會如果任由一些人跌到維持生活所需的水準之下，就會以暴力來償付；而等到偷了才救濟，就太遲了。

Within the monastic community the rule was closer to equality. Among the six qualities that hold a community together, the Buddha lists this: a monk “shares without reservation any material things they have gained by legitimate means, even the food placed in the alms-bowl.” Chinese Buddhism calls this 利和同均, harmony in the equal sharing of gains. The Buddha also rejected the oldest criterion of desert in India, which was birth: “You’re not a lowlife by birth, nor by birth are you a brahmin.” On one point, though, the tradition differs sharply from Rawls. For Rawls the circumstances of birth are a lottery and no one deserves his place. On the Buddhist account they are connected with past action. That teaching was never meant to excuse the fortunate. The king’s duty in the story does not depend on how the poor came to be poor (Dharma and Western Philosophy D18, D19).

在僧團內部，規則更接近平等。在使一個團體和合的六種特質當中，佛陀列了這一條：比丘對於如法得來的任何物品，「乃至缽中所得的食物」，都毫無保留地與同修共用。漢傳佛教稱之為「利和同均」。佛陀也拒絕了印度最古老的那個「配得」的標準，也就是出身：「不因出身而成為賤民，不因出身而成為婆羅門。」不過在一點上，這個傳統與羅爾斯有鮮明的不同。對羅爾斯來說，出生的境遇是一場抽籤，沒有人應得他的位置。照佛教的說法，它們與過去的行為有關。這個教導從來就不是要為幸運的人開脫。在那個故事裡，國王的職責並不取決於窮人是怎麼變窮的（佛法與西方哲學 D18、D19）。
