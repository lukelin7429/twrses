A11 · Reality 真實

Do We Have Free Will? 我們有自由意志嗎？

If every event follows from earlier events by fixed laws, your choices were settled before you were born. Three ways of living with that thought, and a fourth that says it does not matter.
如果每個事件都依固定的定律從先前的事件而來，你的選擇在你出生之前就已經定了。有三種方式與這個想法共處，還有第四種說：這根本無關緊要。

A11 Big Questions · 大哉問Reality · 真實Advanced · 高級1,270 words · about 25 min
The big idea · 大觀念

The dispute about free will is largely a dispute about what freedom is. If it means being uncaused, we probably lack it. If it means acting from our own reasons without constraint, we often have it, whatever physics says.

關於自由意志的爭論，大體上是在爭「自由是什麼」。如果它的意思是「不被任何原因造成」，我們大概沒有。如果它的意思是「不受強制、出於自己的理由而行動」，那麼不管物理學怎麼說，我們常常是有的。

The essay · 課文

Do We Have Free Will? 我們有自由意志嗎？

01Breakfast 早餐

This morning you chose what to eat. It felt as though the choice was yours, and as though you could have taken the other thing. Almost everything we care about in human life rests on that feeling. We praise people and blame them, make promises and hold grudges, deliberate and regret, all on the assumption that what a person does is up to that person. The difficulty is that we also believe every event has a cause. Your choice was an event in your brain. It had causes, which had causes of their own, and the chain runs back to a time before you existed. If so, in what sense was the choice up to you?

今天早上，你選了要吃什麼。感覺上，這個選擇是你的，而且你本來可以拿另一樣。人類生活裡我們在乎的幾乎一切，都建立在這個感覺上。我們稱讚人、責備人，許下承諾、懷著怨恨，斟酌與後悔，全都預設了：一個人做什麼，取決於這個人自己。困難在於，我們同時也相信每個事件都有原因。你的選擇是你腦中的一個事件。它有原因，那些原因又有自己的原因；這條鏈子一路回溯到你存在之前。如果是這樣，這個選擇在什麼意義上「取決於你」？

02The demon 惡魔

The clearest statement of the worry was written in 1814 by the French mathematician Pierre-Simon Laplace. “We ought then to regard the present state of the universe as the effect of its anterior state and as the cause of the one which is to follow.” Imagine an intelligence that knew, at one instant, all the forces of nature and the position of every particle, and that was vast enough to calculate with them. “For it, nothing would be uncertain and the future, as the past, would be present to its eyes.” This is determinism: given the state of the world at one time and the laws of nature, only one future is possible. Laplace’s intelligence, which later writers called his demon, would know what you will have for breakfast on a morning ten years from now.

把這個憂慮說得最清楚的，是法國數學家拉普拉斯在 1814 年寫下的一段話。「因此，我們應當把宇宙的現在狀態，看作它先前狀態的結果，以及隨後狀態的原因。」設想有一個智能，在某一瞬間知道自然界所有的力，以及每一個粒子的位置，並且龐大到足以拿這些資料來計算。「對它來說，沒有什麼是不確定的；未來就像過去一樣，會呈現在它眼前。」這就是決定論：給定世界在某一時刻的狀態，加上自然定律，就只有一個未來是可能的。拉普拉斯的這個智能，後人稱之為他的「惡魔」，會知道十年後的某個早上你要吃什麼早餐。

03Spinoza: the stone that thinks it flies 史賓諾莎：以為自己在飛的石頭

A century and a half earlier, Baruch Spinoza had drawn the conclusion without flinching. “Nothing in the universe is contingent,” he wrote. Everything that happens follows from the nature of things with the same necessity by which the angles of a triangle add up to two right angles. People believe otherwise for a reason he states exactly: “men are mistaken in thinking themselves free; their opinion is made up of consciousness of their own actions, and ignorance of the causes by which they are conditioned.” We notice our desires and do not notice where they come from. “Thus an infant believes that of its own free will it desires milk, an angry child believes that it freely desires vengeance, a timid child believes that it freely desires to run away.” In a letter he imagined a stone that became conscious while flying through the air. It would suppose, he said, that it was moving because it wished to.

比這早一個半世紀，史賓諾莎就毫不退縮地得出了這個結論。他寫道：「宇宙中沒有任何東西是偶然的。」發生的一切，都從事物的本性必然地推出，就像三角形的內角和等於兩個直角那樣必然。人們之所以不這麼相信，他把理由說得很精確：「人們自以為自由，這是錯的；他們這個意見的由來，是意識到自己的行動，卻不知道決定這些行動的原因。」我們察覺到自己的欲望，卻沒有察覺它們從哪裡來。「於是嬰兒相信，他是憑自己的自由意志想喝奶；生氣的孩子相信，他是自由地想要報復；膽小的孩子相信，他是自由地想要逃跑。」在一封信裡，他設想有一顆石頭在空中飛的時候有了意識。他說，那顆石頭會以為，自己是因為想動才動的。

04The consequence 後果

Suppose determinism is true. Then, the argument runs, nobody could ever have done anything other than what he did. The thief could not have refrained and the hero could not have run away. It seems unfair to blame a person for what he could not help, and pointless to praise him. Philosophers who accept this reasoning are called hard determinists. They conclude that moral responsibility, in the sense of deserving reward or punishment, is an illusion, and that we should treat wrongdoers as we treat the sick or the dangerous, with treatment and restraint and without resentment. Spinoza recommended exactly that attitude: not to laugh at human actions, or weep at them, or hate them, but to understand them.

假設決定論是真的。那麼，這個論證說，從來沒有人能做出他實際所做之外的任何事。小偷不可能忍住不偷，英雄不可能逃跑。為了一個人不由自主的事而責備他，似乎不公平；稱讚他，似乎也沒有意義。接受這個推理的哲學家，稱為硬決定論者。他們的結論是：道德責任，就「理應受賞或受罰」這個意思而言，是一個幻覺；我們對待做錯事的人，應該像對待病人或危險的人那樣，給予治療與約束，而不帶怨恨。史賓諾莎建議的正是這種態度：對人的行為，不嘲笑，不哀嘆，不憎恨，而是去理解。

05Two ways to keep freedom 保住自由的兩條路

One reply accepts that determinism and freedom cannot both be true, and concludes that determinism is false. Those who take this line are called libertarians, in a sense that has nothing to do with politics. They hold that when I choose, I start a new chain of causes that was not fixed by anything earlier. The ancient atomist Epicurus thought that atoms occasionally swerve for no reason, and some modern writers appeal to the indeterminacy of quantum physics. The standing objection is that chance is no better than necessity. If my arm moves because of a random event in a neuron, the movement is a twitch, and a twitch is not a choice.

一種回應承認決定論與自由不可能同時為真，然後推斷：決定論是假的。採取這條路線的人稱為自由意志論者（libertarian，這個詞在這裡跟政治無關）。他們主張，當我做選擇的時候，我開啟了一條新的因果鏈，它不是由先前的任何東西決定的。古代的原子論者伊比鳩魯認為，原子偶爾會毫無理由地偏斜；有些現代作者則訴諸量子物理的不確定性。對這條路線的一貫反駁是：機遇並不比必然好。如果我的手臂是因為神經元裡的一個隨機事件而動，那個動作只是抽搐，而抽搐不是選擇。

The other reply is that the conflict was never real. This is compatibilism, and its classic statement is Hume’s: “By liberty, then, we can only mean a power of acting or not acting, according to the determinations of the will; that is, if we choose to remain at rest, we may; if we choose to move, we also may.” Freedom, on this view, is the absence of constraint. A prisoner in chains is unfree. So is a person at gunpoint, or in the grip of a compulsion he would give anything to be rid of. A person who does what he wants, for his own reasons, is free, and it makes no difference that his wants have causes. Indeed they had better have causes, since an action that sprang from nothing in my character would hardly be mine. The contemporary philosopher Harry Frankfurt refined the idea. What matters is whether the desire I act on is one that I want to have, as the addict’s craving is not.

另一種回應是：衝突從來就不是真的。這是相容論，它的經典陳述出自休謨：「那麼，所謂自由，我們只能是指一種依照意志的決定而行動或不行動的能力；也就是說，如果我們選擇保持靜止，我們可以；如果我們選擇移動，我們也可以。」照這個看法，自由就是沒有強制。戴著鎖鍊的囚犯是不自由的。被槍指著的人也是，被某種他願意付出一切代價擺脫的強迫性衝動攫住的人也是。一個人做他想做的事、出於他自己的理由，就是自由的；至於他的欲望有原因，這毫無差別。事實上，它們最好有原因：一個不是從我的性格中任何東西產生的行動，很難說是我的行動。當代哲學家法蘭克福把這個想法磨得更細：要緊的是，我據以行動的那個欲望，是不是一個我「想要擁有」的欲望；而成癮者的渴求並不是。

06What is decreed and what is up to me 命，與求在我者

Chinese philosophers argued about ming (命), which means both “fate” and “what is decreed.” The Mohists attacked fatalism as a doctrine that ruins people. If the rich are fated to be rich and the poor to be poor, then, as the fatalists say, effort is useless, and a ruler who believes it will stop governing and a farmer will stop plowing. Mozi called this teaching a great harm to the world. Mencius drew a finer line. Some things, he said, are such that “if you seek them you get them, and if you neglect them you lose them,” because the seeking is within yourself. Others depend on what is outside, and there, seeking is no help to getting. Virtue is of the first kind and wealth and long life are of the second. This is not a theory of causation. It is closer to the compatibilist’s point, that the question of what is in my power can be answered by looking at what responds to my effort, without first settling the metaphysics.

中國哲學家爭論的是「命」，這個字既指「命運」，也指「被決定的」。墨家攻擊宿命論，說它是一種毀人的學說。如果富人命中注定富、窮人命中注定窮，那麼正如主張有命的人所說，努力是沒有用的；相信這一套的君主會不再治理，農夫會不再耕田。墨子稱這種說法是「天下之大害」。孟子畫了一條更細的線。他說，有些東西是「求則得之，舍則失之」的，因為「求在我者也」。另一些取決於外在，在那裡，「求無益於得也」。德行屬於第一類，富貴與長壽屬於第二類。這不是一套因果理論。它比較接近相容論者的論點：要回答「什麼在我的能力之內」，可以看什麼東西會回應我的努力，而不必先把形上學解決。

07The brain gets there first 大腦先到一步

In 1983 the physiologist Benjamin Libet asked volunteers to flex a wrist whenever they felt like it and to note the moment at which they became aware of the urge. Electrodes on the scalp showed a build-up of activity in the brain about half a second before the movement, and about a third of a second before the reported decision. Many people took this as proof that the conscious will arrives too late to cause anything. The interpretation is disputed. The actions were trivial and unreasoned, the timing of an inner event is hard to report, and later work suggests that the early signal may reflect background fluctuation and not a decision already made. A compatibilist adds that the result would not matter in any case. A decision made by my brain is a decision made by me.

1983 年，生理學家利貝特請受試者想動的時候就彎一下手腕，並且記下自己察覺到那股衝動的時刻。頭皮上的電極顯示，在動作之前大約半秒，腦中就出現了活動的累積；這比受試者回報的「決定」時刻，早了大約三分之一秒。許多人把這當作證據，證明有意識的意志來得太晚，不可能是任何事的原因。這種解讀有爭議。那些動作是瑣碎而不涉及理由的；內在事件的時間點很難回報；後來的研究也顯示，早期的訊號反映的可能是背景波動，而不是一個已經做出的決定。相容論者還會補一句：無論如何，這個結果都無關緊要。由我的大腦做出的決定，就是由我做出的決定。

08What would change 什麼會改變

Suppose you became convinced that there is no free will. What would you do differently? You would still have to decide what to eat. You could not sit back and wait to see what you were determined to do, since sitting back would be one of the options. P. F. Strawson argued in 1962 that our practices of gratitude, resentment, and forgiveness do not rest on a theory that might be refuted. They are part of what it is to live with other people, and no argument could lead us to give them up wholesale. What can change is their tone. A person who sees how much of anyone’s character was shaped by things he did not choose may judge others more gently and take less credit for himself. On that modest conclusion Spinoza, Hume, Mencius, and the Buddha are closer together than their theories would lead one to expect.

假設你被說服了，相信沒有自由意志。你會有什麼不同的做法？你還是得決定吃什麼。你沒辦法往後一靠，等著看自己「被決定」去做什麼，因為往後一靠也是選項之一。史陶生在 1962 年論證：我們感激、怨恨與寬恕的種種做法，並不是建立在一個可能被駁倒的理論上。它們是「跟別人一起生活」這件事的一部分，沒有哪個論證能讓我們把它們全盤放棄。能改變的是它們的調子。一個人如果看清，任何人的性格有多少是由他沒有選擇的東西塑造的，他對別人的判斷也許會溫和一些，對自己的功勞也會少居一些。在這個平實的結論上，史賓諾莎、休謨、孟子與佛陀彼此的距離，比他們的理論讓人預期的要近。

Where do you stand? 你站在哪一邊？

Where do you stand?

你站在哪一邊？

Hard determinism · 硬決定論

You are with Spinoza. The cost is desert: no one ever truly deserves praise or blame, including the people you love and the people who have wronged you. Ask whether you can actually take up that attitude toward someone who has betrayed you, and whether you would want to.

你跟史賓諾莎站在一起。代價是「應得」：沒有人真正應得稱讚或責備，包括你愛的人，也包括對不起你的人。問問自己：面對一個背叛過你的人，你真的採取得了這種態度嗎？你會想要這樣嗎？

Libertarianism · 自由意志論

You hold that your choices are not fixed by the past. Then something in you must act without being caused to, and you owe an account of what that is. If it is chance, the choice is not yours. If it is your character and reasons, those have a history. The demand is for a cause that is yours and has no causes itself.

你主張你的選擇不是由過去決定的。那麼你裡面就必須有某個東西，不被任何原因造成而行動；而你欠一個說明：那是什麼？如果是機遇，這個選擇就不是你的。如果是你的性格與理由，它們是有來歷的。這裡要求的是一個「屬於你、而本身沒有原因」的原因。

Compatibilism · 相容論

This is the majority view among philosophers today. The difficulty is the manipulation case. Suppose a neuroscientist had secretly installed all your desires and reasons. You would still act from them without constraint, and few would call you free. You must say how being shaped by genes and upbringing differs from that.

這是今天哲學家當中的多數看法。困難在於「操控」的案例。假設有一位神經科學家偷偷植入了你全部的欲望與理由。你仍然是出於它們、不受強制地行動；可是很少有人會說你是自由的。你必須說明：被基因與教養塑造，跟這個案例差在哪裡？

Hard incompatibilism · 硬不相容論

This is the position of Galen Strawson. To be truly responsible for what you do, you would have to be responsible for the way you are, and for that you would have had to make yourself, which nothing can do. The view is hard to refute. Its defenders tend to live exactly like everyone else, and they regard that as a fact about human psychology that leaves the argument untouched.

這是蓋倫・史陶生的立場。要為你所做的事真正負責，你就得為「你是什麼樣的人」負責；而要做到這一點，你得先造出你自己，這是任何東西都辦不到的。這個看法很難駁倒。為它辯護的人，日子過得往往跟其他人一模一樣；而他們認為，那只是關於人類心理的一個事實，論證本身並沒有因此受到影響。

Did he really say that? · 這句話真的是他說的嗎？

“A man can do what he wants, but not want what he wants.” (Einstein)「人能做他想做的，卻不能想要他所想要的。」（愛因斯坦）

Schopenhauer · 叔本華

Einstein did say this, and said whose it was. In a statement of his beliefs written around 1932 he quoted it as Schopenhauer’s saying and added that it had consoled him since his youth and taught him patience with other people. The thought comes from Schopenhauer’s prize essay On the Freedom of the Will (1839). It is a fair summary of the compatibilist’s premise and the hard determinist’s conclusion at once.

愛因斯坦確實說過這句話，而且說明了它是誰的。在大約 1932 年寫下的一篇信念自述裡，他把它當作叔本華的話來引用，並且補充說，這句話從他年輕時就安慰著他，教他對別人有耐心。這個想法出自叔本華的得獎論文《論意志的自由》（1839）。它同時相當貼切地概括了相容論者的前提，以及硬決定論者的結論。

Both sides · 正反兩面

Objections and replies 反駁與回應

Objection 1 · 反駁

I know that I am free, because I experience it every time I choose. No argument can be more certain than that experience.

我知道我是自由的，因為每次做選擇的時候我都經驗到它。沒有任何論證能比這個經驗更確定。

Reply · 回應

Samuel Johnson put it in one line: “Sir, we know our will is free, and there’s an end on’t.” Spinoza’s answer is the drunkard. What we experience is the wanting and the doing. We do not experience the absence of causes, since an absence is not the kind of thing that can be felt. The experience is the same whether determinism is true or false, and so it cannot decide between them.

山繆・約翰遜用一句話說完了：「先生，我們知道自己的意志是自由的，這事就到此為止。」史賓諾莎的回答是那個醉漢。我們經驗到的是「想要」與「去做」。我們並沒有經驗到「沒有原因」，因為「沒有」不是一種可以被感覺到的東西。不管決定論是真是假，這個經驗都一樣；所以它無法在兩者之間做出裁決。

Objection 2 · 反駁

Quantum physics has shown that the world is not deterministic. The problem has been dissolved by science.

量子物理已經證明世界不是決定論的。這個問題已經被科學化解了。

Reply · 回應

Whether quantum theory is indeterministic depends on how it is interpreted, and physicists disagree. Suppose it is. The uncertainty of a single atom is mostly swamped at the scale of neurons, and where it is not, it gives us randomness. A decision that came from the unpredictable decay of a particle would be no more mine than one that came from the laws of Newton. Indeterminism removes one threat and does nothing to supply control.

量子理論是不是非決定論的，取決於怎麼詮釋它，而物理學家對此意見不一。就假設它是吧。單一原子的不確定性，到了神經元的尺度多半已經被淹沒；沒被淹沒的地方，它給我們的是隨機。一個出自粒子不可預測的衰變的決定，並不比一個出自牛頓定律的決定更屬於我。非決定論移除了一個威脅，卻絲毫沒有提供控制力。

Objection 3 · 反駁

If people come to believe there is no free will, they will behave worse. So the doctrine is dangerous, whatever its truth.

如果人們開始相信沒有自由意志，他們的行為會變糟。所以不管這個學說是真是假，它都是危險的。

Reply · 回應

The Mohists said this of fatalism, and some experiments have suggested that people cheat more after reading arguments against free will, though the findings have not held up well. Two replies are available. Determinism is not fatalism: it says that your efforts are among the causes of what happens, so effort makes all the difference. And the consequences of believing a thing are not evidence for or against it. If the truth is uncomfortable, the remedy is to understand it properly.

墨家對宿命論說過這樣的話；也有一些實驗顯示，人讀了反對自由意志的論證之後比較會作弊，雖然這些發現後來不太站得住。有兩個回應。決定論不是宿命論：它說你的努力是事情發生的原因之一，所以努力造成一切差別。還有，相信一件事會有什麼後果，並不是支持或反對這件事的證據。如果真相令人不舒服，解方是把它理解對。

Terms of art · 哲學術語

The vocabulary of the subject 這門學問的用語

determinism決定論

L. determinare ‘to set limits to’

The thesis that the state of the world at one time, with the laws of nature, fixes its state at every later time.

主張世界在某一時刻的狀態加上自然定律，就決定了它在此後每一時刻的狀態。

fatalism宿命論

L. fatum ‘what has been spoken’

The view that what will happen will happen whatever we do. Distinct from determinism, on which what we do is among the causes.

主張不管我們做什麼，該發生的都會發生。與決定論不同：依決定論，我們所做的事正是原因之一。

compatibilism相容論

The view that free will and determinism can both be true.

主張自由意志與決定論可以同時為真。

incompatibilism不相容論

The view that they cannot. It includes hard determinism and libertarianism.

主張兩者不能同時為真。包括硬決定論與自由意志論。

libertarianism自由意志論

L. libertas ‘freedom’

In metaphysics, the view that we are free and that determinism is therefore false.

在形上學裡，指主張我們是自由的、因此決定論為假的看法。

hard determinism硬決定論

The view that determinism is true and that free will is therefore an illusion.

主張決定論為真、因此自由意志是幻覺的看法。

moral responsibility道德責任

The condition of deserving praise or blame for what one does.

對自己所做的事理應受到稱讚或責備的狀態。

contingent偶然的、適然的

L. contingere ‘to happen’

Such that it could have been otherwise; the opposite of necessary.

本來可以是別的樣子；「必然」的反面。

reactive attitudes反應性態度

P. F. Strawson’s term for resentment, gratitude, indignation, and the like, which we feel toward persons as responsible agents.

史陶生的術語，指怨恨、感激、義憤之類，我們把人當作負責的行為者時對他們產生的感受。

Dharma and the West · 佛法與西方哲學

A view from the Dharma 佛法怎麼看

☸

The Buddha lived among teachers who held nearly every position in this debate, and he rejected the extremes on practical grounds. One discourse lists three views that, when pressed, lead to inaction: that everything a person experiences is caused by past deeds, that everything is caused by the creative act of a supreme god, and that everything happens without cause or condition (Aṅguttara Nikāya 3.61). If any of them were true, he says, there would be no reason to make an effort to do this and avoid that. The first of the three is notable, because it is a view about karma. The Buddha denies that the present is wholly fixed by past action.

佛陀生活的時代，幾乎每一種立場都有老師在主張；而他基於實踐的理由，拒絕了那些極端。有一部經列出三種見解，說它們一經追問，就會歸結為無所作為：一個人所經驗的一切都由過去的行為造成；一切都由至高之神的創造造成；一切都無因無緣地發生（《增支部》3.61）。他說，如果其中任何一種是真的，人就沒有理由努力去做這個、避免那個。三者當中的第一種值得注意，因為那是一種關於業的見解。佛陀否認現在完全由過去的業所決定。

What he affirms looks like compatibilism reached by another road. Actions arise from conditions, including intention, and intention is itself conditioned. Nothing stands outside the causal order, and there is no self behind the will to do the choosing. Yet when a brahmin tells him that there is no such thing as acting on one’s own initiative, the Buddha asks how a man who has just walked up to him of his own accord can say so. “Is there an element of initiative?” There is. And since there is, beings who initiate actions are found (Aṅguttara Nikāya 6.38). Karma on this account is a condition that can be worked with. The whole path assumes that understanding the causes of one’s states changes what those causes produce, and Spinoza built the last part of his Ethics on the same assumption. On the Buddhist view, freedom has to be gained by training. One comes to be less driven by craving and aversion as one sees more clearly how they arise (Dharma and the West, D17).

他肯定的東西，看起來像是從另一條路走到的相容論。行為從條件生起，條件包括「思」（意圖），而意圖本身也是有條件的。沒有任何東西站在因果秩序之外，意志的背後也沒有一個「我」在做選擇。然而，當一位婆羅門告訴他「沒有所謂出於自己的主動而行動這回事」時，佛陀反問：一個剛剛自己走過來的人，怎麼能這樣說？「有沒有『發起』這個要素？」有。既然有，就找得到發起行動的眾生（《增支部》6.38）。照這個說法，業是一種可以拿來下工夫的條件。整條道路都預設：理解自己種種狀態的原因，會改變這些原因所產生的結果；而史賓諾莎《倫理學》的最後一部分，就建立在同一個預設上。依佛教的看法，自由必須靠修習才能獲得。人把貪與瞋怎麼生起看得愈清楚，受它們驅使的程度就愈低（佛法與西方哲學 D17）。
