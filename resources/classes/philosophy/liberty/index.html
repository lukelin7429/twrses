How Free Should We Be? 我們應該有多自由？

Seat belts, drugs, hateful pamphlets, and a contract to sell yourself. Mill’s one very simple principle, the cases that strain it, Berlin’s two kinds of freedom, and Laozi’s ruler who governs by leaving people alone.
安全帶、毒品、充滿仇恨的小冊子，還有一份把自己賣掉的契約。彌爾那條「非常簡單的原則」、讓它吃力的那些案例、柏林的兩種自由，以及老子那位「不去管人民」的統治者。

The big idea · 大觀念

The last two lessons gave reasons for having rules and for sharing things fairly. Both give other people power over us. This lesson asks where that power must stop: which parts of a life are nobody’s business but one’s own, and why.

前兩課給出了要有規則、要公平分配的理由。兩者都讓別人有了支配我們的權力。這一課問的是：那個權力必須在哪裡停下來？一個人的生活裡，哪些部分除了他自己，誰也管不著？為什麼？

The essay · 課文

How Free Should We Be? 我們應該有多自由？

01Where to draw the line 線要劃在哪裡

A government that can stop a thief can also stop a reader, a drinker, or a preacher. Every society therefore has to decide which of the things people do it will leave alone. The decisions are familiar ones. May the law require a motorcyclist to wear a helmet? May it forbid an adult to take a drug that harms only himself, or a newspaper to print what most people find disgusting? For most of history the answer was that rulers may forbid whatever is bad for the people or offensive to the gods. In 1859 John Stuart Mill argued, in a short book called On Liberty, that this answer is wrong.

一個能攔住小偷的政府，也能攔住一個讀書的人、喝酒的人或傳道的人。所以每一個社會都得決定：人們做的事情當中，哪些它不去管。這些決定都是我們熟悉的。法律可以規定騎機車的人戴安全帽嗎？可以禁止一個成年人服用只傷害他自己的藥物嗎？可以禁止報紙刊登多數人覺得噁心的東西嗎？在歷史上大部分的時間裡，答案是：凡是對人民不好的、或冒犯神明的，統治者都可以禁止。1859 年，約翰・史都華・彌爾在一本叫做《論自由》的小書裡主張：這個答案是錯的。

02One very simple principle 一條非常簡單的原則

Mill proposed what he called “one very simple principle.” It reads: “the only purpose for which power can be rightfully exercised over any member of a civilised community, against his will, is to prevent harm to others. His own good, either physical or moral, is not a sufficient warrant.” We may argue with a man who is ruining his health, plead with him, or avoid his company. We may not force him. “Over himself, over his own body and mind, the individual is sovereign.”

彌爾提出了他所謂的「一條非常簡單的原則」。它是這樣寫的：「對一個文明社群的任何成員，可以違反他的意願而正當地行使權力的唯一目的，是防止對他人的傷害。他自己的好處，不論是身體上的還是道德上的，都不是充分的理由。」對一個正在毀掉自己健康的人，我們可以跟他爭論、懇求他，或是避開他。我們不可以強迫他。「對於他自己，對於他自己的身體與心靈，個人是最高的主權者。」

Two features of the principle are easy to miss. It applies to social pressure as well as to law. Mill feared what he called the tyranny of the majority, by which he meant the power of neighbors and employers to make an unusual person’s life impossible, and he thought it reached further than any police. And the principle gives a necessary condition for interference. Harm to others makes interference permissible, but whether to interfere remains a further question. Competition harms the losing shopkeeper, and no one proposes to forbid it.

這條原則有兩個特點很容易漏看。它既適用於法律，也適用於社會壓力。彌爾害怕他所謂的「多數的暴政」；他指的是鄰居與雇主讓一個不尋常的人活不下去的那種力量，而他認為那比任何警察都管得更深。此外，這條原則給的是干涉的必要條件。對他人的傷害，使干涉成為可以容許的；至於要不要干涉，仍然是進一步的問題。競爭傷害了輸掉的那位店主，而沒有人提議禁止競爭。

03Why leave people alone 為什麼不去管人

Mill was a utilitarian, and he does not appeal to natural rights. He argues that liberty makes human life go better. The case is clearest for speech. If we silence an opinion, it may be true, and we lose the chance of “exchanging error for truth.” No one who silences is infallible. If the opinion is false, we still lose, because a truth that is never challenged comes to be held “as a dead dogma, not a living truth.” People repeat it without knowing why it is so. “He who knows only his own side of the case, knows little of that.” And most opinions in dispute are partly true, so that each side needs the other to correct it. Hence his most quoted sentence: “If all mankind minus one, were of one opinion, and only one person were of the contrary opinion, mankind would be no more justified in silencing that one person, than he, if he had the power, would be justified in silencing mankind.”

彌爾是功利主義者，他並不訴諸自然權利。他論證的是：自由使人的生活過得更好。這個論證在言論上最清楚。如果我們壓制一個意見，它可能是真的；那我們就失去了「以真理換掉錯誤」的機會。沒有哪一個壓制別人的人是不會錯的。如果那個意見是假的，我們還是有損失；因為一個從來不受挑戰的真理，會被人當作「死的教條，而不是活的真理」來持有。人們複述它，而不知道它為什麼是這樣。「只知道自己這一邊的理由的人，對這一邊也所知甚少。」而大多數有爭議的意見，都有一部分是真的；所以每一邊都需要另一邊來糾正它。於是有了他最常被引用的那個句子：「假如全人類除了一個人之外，都持同一個意見，而只有那一個人持相反的意見；那麼人類要那一個人沉默，並不比那一個人（假如他有權力的話）要全人類沉默更為正當。」

The same reasoning covers ways of living. No one knows in advance what kind of life is best for human beings, so there should be “different experiments of living.” A person who lets custom choose for him needs no faculty, Mill says, but “the ape-like one of imitation.” Choosing for oneself is how judgment and character are exercised, and a people that is not allowed to choose will stop improving.

同樣的推理也涵蓋生活的方式。沒有人事先知道哪一種生活對人最好；所以應該有「不同的生活實驗」。彌爾說，一個讓習俗替他選擇的人，除了「像猿猴那樣的模仿能力」之外，不需要任何別的能力。自己做選擇，判斷力與品格才得到鍛鍊；而一個不被允許選擇的民族，會停止進步。

04Hard cases 難題

The principle is simple to state and hard to apply, and Mill knew it. He distinguished an opinion from an incitement. The view that corn dealers starve the poor may be printed freely, but it may be punished “when delivered orally to an excited mob assembled before the house of a corn-dealer.” He allowed one case of protecting a person from himself. A man who sees another about to step onto an unsafe bridge may seize him, since he “does not desire to fall into the river.” And he held that the law should refuse to enforce a contract by which a person sells himself into slavery: “The principle of freedom cannot require that he should be free not to be free.”

這條原則說起來簡單，用起來困難；彌爾自己知道。他把「意見」與「煽動」分開。「糧商害窮人挨餓」這個看法可以自由刊印；但是「當它被口頭傳達給一群聚集在某位糧商門前的激動群眾時」，就可以懲罰。他容許一種「保護一個人免於傷害自己」的情況。看見別人正要踏上一座不安全的橋，可以把他一把抓住；因為他「並不想要掉進河裡」。他還主張，法律應該拒絕執行一份把自己賣為奴隸的契約：「自由的原則，不能要求他有『不自由』的自由。」

Critics have pressed two points. The first is that almost nothing harms only oneself. The drinker has a family and the injured rider needs a hospital bed. Mill answered that we should punish the neglect of a definite duty, such as failing to support one’s children, and leave the drinking alone. The second is that the word harm is asked to do too much work. Should deep offense count as harm? Mill said no for opinions, but he accepted laws against public indecency. In the 1950s the English judge Patrick Devlin argued that a society may defend its shared morality as it defends itself against treason. The legal philosopher H. L. A. Hart replied, in Mill’s spirit, that disgust is no proof of harm.

批評者緊追兩點。第一：幾乎沒有什麼事是只傷害自己的。喝酒的人有家人；受傷的騎士需要一張病床。彌爾回答說：我們應該懲罰的是對某個明確義務的疏忽，例如不扶養自己的孩子；至於喝酒，就別去管它。第二：「傷害」這個詞被要求做太多的事了。深深的冒犯算不算傷害？就意見而言，彌爾說不算；但他接受禁止公然猥褻的法律。1950 年代，英國法官派屈克・德富林主張：一個社會可以捍衛它共有的道德，就像它防衛叛國一樣。法哲學家哈特以彌爾的精神回應說：厭惡並不是傷害的證明。

05Two kinds of freedom 兩種自由

In 1958 Isaiah Berlin pointed out that Mill’s question is only one of two that go by the name of freedom. Negative liberty is the absence of interference: how many doors are open to me. Positive liberty is self-mastery: who is in control. An addict whom no one stops has the first and lacks the second. Berlin thought the positive idea the more dangerous in politics. Once a ruler claims to know what my true self wants, he can coerce me and call it liberation, and Rousseau had spoken of forcing a man to be free. Others replied that negative liberty alone is a thin thing to someone too poor or too ignorant to walk through any of the open doors.

1958 年，以撒・柏林指出：頂著「自由」這個名字的問題有兩個，彌爾的問題只是其中之一。消極自由是沒有干涉：有多少扇門對我開著。積極自由是自我主宰：誰在掌控。一個沒有人攔阻的成癮者，有第一種而沒有第二種。柏林認為，在政治上，積極的那個觀念比較危險。一旦統治者宣稱他知道我「真正的自我」要什麼，他就可以強制我，而把這叫做解放；盧梭就說過要「強迫一個人自由」。其他人則回應：對一個太窮或太無知、以致走不進任何一扇敞開的門的人來說，只有消極自由，是很單薄的東西。

06The ruler who does nothing 什麼也不做的統治者

China has an older tradition of governing by leaving alone. The Daodejing says that the more prohibitions there are, the poorer the people become, and that the more laws are published, the more thieves there are. Its sage says: “I do nothing, and the people transform themselves.” Yet Laozi is no Mill. The same book advises the ruler to empty the people’s minds and fill their bellies, and praises the ancients who kept the people simple and did not enlighten them. Laozi wanted a government that did not meddle and a people that did not argue. Mill wanted the argument above all. The Legalist Han Fei drew the opposite conclusion from both: in the state of an enlightened ruler there are no books, and “the law is the teaching.” The disagreement among the three is one that every government still has to settle.

中國有一個更古老的「以不管來治理」的傳統。《道德經》說：「天下多忌諱，而民彌貧」；「法令滋彰，盜賊多有」。它的聖人說：「我無為而民自化。」然而老子並不是彌爾。同一本書勸統治者「虛其心，實其腹」，並且稱讚古人「非以明民，將以愚之」。老子要的，是一個不多事的政府，與一群不爭辯的人民。彌爾最想要的，偏偏是爭辯。法家的韓非則從兩人那裡得出相反的結論：「明主之國，無書簡之文，以法為教。」這三個人之間的分歧，是每一個政府到今天都還得做出決定的。

Thought experiment · 思想實驗

Try it: you are the lawmaker 親手做一次：你來立法

Nine cases. For each, decide whether the law should forbid it or leave it alone. After each answer you will see how Mill decided and why. At the end you will see which principles you were using.

九個案例。每一個，請決定法律應該禁止它，還是不去管它。每次作答之後，你會看到彌爾怎麼判、為什麼。最後你會看到你自己實際上在用哪些原則。

Did he really say that? · 這句話真的是他說的嗎？

“You can’t shout fire in a crowded theater.” (the limit of free speech in law)「你不能在擁擠的戲院裡大喊失火。」（法律上言論自由的界限）

Misquoted, and never the law · 引錯了，而且從來不是法律

The line comes from Justice Oliver Wendell Holmes in Schenck v. United States (1919). What he wrote was: “The most stringent protection of free speech would not protect a man in falsely shouting fire in a theatre and causing a panic.” The word “crowded” is not there, and the two conditions that are there, “falsely” and “causing a panic,” are usually dropped. The sentence was an illustration. It was not the ruling. The ruling upheld the imprisonment of a man who had distributed leaflets against military conscription, which is exactly the kind of political opinion Mill wanted protected. The United States Supreme Court replaced the Schenck standard in 1969 with a narrower one much closer to Mill’s corn dealer: speech may be punished only where it is directed to inciting imminent lawless action and is likely to produce it.

這句話出自大法官奧利佛・溫德爾・霍姆斯，在「申克訴合眾國案」（1919）裡。他寫的是：「對言論自由最嚴格的保護，也不會保護一個在戲院裡謊稱失火、並造成恐慌的人。」原文沒有「擁擠」這個詞；而原文有的兩個條件，「謊稱」與「造成恐慌」，通常被拿掉了。這個句子是一個比方。它並不是判決。那個判決維持了對一個人的監禁；他散發的是反對徵兵的傳單，而這恰恰是彌爾想要保護的那一種政治意見。美國最高法院在 1969 年以一個較窄的標準取代了申克案的標準，那個標準更接近彌爾的糧商：只有在言論旨在煽動「立即的不法行為」、而且很可能產生它的時候，才可以懲罰。

Both sides · 正反兩面

Objections and replies 反駁與回應

Objection 1 · 反駁

People do not choose as Mill imagines. They are hasty, ill-informed, and weak-willed, and they regret their choices. A state that knows this and does nothing is negligent.

人並不像彌爾想像的那樣做選擇。他們倉促、資訊不足、意志薄弱，而且為自己的選擇後悔。一個明知如此卻什麼也不做的國家，是怠忽職守。

Reply · 回應

This is the modern case for paternalism, and psychology supports its premise. Mill’s bridge example already allows interference where a person lacks information and there is no time to warn him. What he resists is the step from “people make mistakes” to “officials should choose for them,” since officials are people too, with less knowledge of the life in question and less interest in it. A middle course keeps the choice and changes the setting: warnings, waiting periods, and defaults that can be refused.

這是現代為家長主義提出的理由；心理學支持它的前提。彌爾的那個橋的例子，已經容許在「一個人缺乏資訊、又來不及警告他」的時候加以干涉。他所抗拒的，是從「人會犯錯」跨到「官員應該替他們選」的那一步；因為官員也是人，而且對那個人的生活知道得更少、在乎得也更少。有一條中間路線：保留選擇，改變情境——警語、等待期，以及可以拒絕的預設選項。

Objection 2 · 反駁

The harm principle protects the strong. A worker “freely” accepts a dangerous job because the alternative is hunger. Leaving people alone means leaving them to whoever has power over them.

傷害原則保護的是強者。一個工人「自由地」接受一份危險的工作，因為另一個選項是挨餓。不去管人，就是把他們留給任何一個對他們握有權力的人。

Reply · 回應

This is the objection from positive liberty, and Mill half accepted it. He supported laws limiting working hours and requiring education, and in later life called himself a socialist of a kind. The honest reply is that On Liberty answers one question, when the majority may coerce an individual. It does not address the question of what a person needs in order to be able to use his freedom. A complete view has to answer both.

這是從積極自由而來的反駁；彌爾接受了一半。他支持限制工時與強制教育的法律，晚年還自稱是某一種社會主義者。誠實的回應是：《論自由》回答的是一個問題，也就是多數人什麼時候可以強制一個個人。它沒有處理另一個問題：一個人需要什麼，才有能力使用他的自由？一個完整的看法，兩個都得回答。

Objection 3 · 反駁

Mill’s ideal of the independent individual who questions everything is one way of life among others. A society that prizes harmony and respect for elders is not thereby less civilized.

彌爾的理想——凡事都質疑的獨立個人——只是許多種生活方式當中的一種。一個重視和諧與敬老的社會，並不因此就比較不文明。

Reply · 回應

The objection has force, and it has been made from Confucian and from religious standpoints. Mill can concede much of it. The harm principle does not require anyone to be an individualist. It forbids making people conform by force. A person may live by tradition, and in a free society his doing so is a choice he could have refused. Whether a tradition remains the same thing once it is one option among many is a real question, and Mill, who wanted every custom tested, would not have minded the answer.

這個反駁有力量；儒家的與宗教的立場都提出過它。彌爾可以承認其中的大部分。傷害原則並不要求任何人做個人主義者。它禁止的是用強制力使人順從。一個人可以依循傳統而活；在一個自由的社會裡，他這樣做，是一個他本來可以拒絕的選擇。一個傳統一旦成為眾多選項之一，是否還是原來那個東西，是個真問題；而希望每一種習俗都受到檢驗的彌爾，不會介意那個答案。

Terms of art · 哲學術語

The vocabulary of the subject 這門學問的用語

harm principle傷害原則

Power may be used over a person against his will only to prevent harm to others.

只有為了防止對他人的傷害，才可以違反一個人的意願而對他行使權力。

paternalism家長主義

L. pater ‘father’

Interfering with a person’s choices for his own good.

為了一個人自己的好處，而干涉他的選擇。

legal moralism法律道德主義

The view that the law may forbid conduct because it is immoral, whether or not it harms anyone.

一種看法：法律可以因為某種行為不道德而禁止它，不管它有沒有傷害任何人。

tyranny of the majority多數的暴政

The oppression of individuals by prevailing opinion, through law or through social pressure.

主流意見經由法律或社會壓力，對個人的壓迫。

negative liberty消極自由

Freedom as the absence of interference by others.

把自由理解為：沒有他人的干涉。

positive liberty積極自由

Freedom as self-mastery: being in control of one’s own life.

把自由理解為自我主宰：掌控自己的人生。

incitement煽動

L. incitare ‘to urge on’

Urging others to an unlawful act in circumstances where they are likely to do it.

在別人很可能照做的情境下，慫恿他們去做不法的事。

non-action無為

Ch. 無為 wúwéi

Laozi’s term for acting without forcing, and for governing without meddling.

老子的用語：不強求地行動，不多事地治理。

Advanced English · 高級英文

Words and phrases worth keeping 值得帶走的字與片語

leave (someone) alone(v. phr.)不去管、不打擾

Every society has to decide what it will leave alone.

Dharma and the West · 佛法與西方哲學

A view from the Dharma 佛法怎麼看

☸

On Mill’s question the Buddha is close to Mill. He founded no state and gave laypeople no laws. The five precepts are undertaken by choice and are not enforced by anyone. He told the Kālāmas not to go by tradition or scripture or by the thought “The ascetic is our respected teacher,” and to accept a teaching only “when you know for yourselves” what it leads to. He held that no one can be made good from outside: “You yourselves must do the work, the Realized Ones just show the way.” And again: “Purity and impurity are personal matters, no one can purify another.” A Buddhist argument for the harm principle would rest on this last point. Virtue that is forced on a person has no value as virtue.

在彌爾的那個問題上，佛陀很接近彌爾。他沒有建立國家，也沒有給在家人立法。五戒是自願受持的，沒有任何人來強制執行。他告訴卡拉瑪人：不要因為傳統、經典，或因為想到「這位沙門是我們尊敬的老師」就接受；只有「當你們自己知道」一個教導會導向什麼的時候，才接受它。他主張沒有人能從外面被弄成善的：「你們必須自己努力；如來只是指路的人。」又說：「清淨與不清淨都在於自己；沒有人能使另一個人清淨。」佛教為傷害原則提出的論證，會以最後這一點為根據。被強加在一個人身上的德行，作為德行沒有任何價值。

On Berlin’s second question the Buddha goes much further than Mill. Mill calls a person free when no one stops him from doing what he desires. The Buddha asks whether the desire is free. One discourse puts it this way: “The world is wanting, insatiable, the slave of craving.” A person driven by craving, anger, and confusion has no outside master and is still unfree. The word for the goal of the path, vimutti, means release. This is positive liberty in its strongest form, and it avoids the danger Berlin saw in the idea. No one can be forced into it, since the release is something a person has to see for himself. A verse of the Dhammapada says, “One is indeed the lord of oneself, for who else would be one’s lord?” (Dharma and Western Philosophy D18).

在柏林的第二個問題上，佛陀走得比彌爾遠得多。彌爾說，沒有人攔著一個人去做他想要的事，這個人就是自由的。佛陀問的是：那個「想要」是自由的嗎？有一部經這樣說：「世間不足，不知滿足，是渴愛的奴隸。」一個被貪、瞋、癡驅使的人，外面沒有主人，卻仍然不自由。這條道路的目標叫做 vimutti，意思是解脫。這是最強形式的積極自由；而它避開了柏林在這個觀念裡看到的危險。沒有人能被強迫進入它，因為解脫是一個人必須自己看見的東西。《法句經》有一首偈說：「自己才是自己的依怙，還有誰能做自己的依怙？」（佛法與西方哲學 D18）。
