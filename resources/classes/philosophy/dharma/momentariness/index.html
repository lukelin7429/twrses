D16 · Dharma and the West 佛法與西方哲學

Momentariness, Dharmas, and Atoms 剎那滅、法的分析與原子論

Break a pot and the idea of the pot is gone. Analyze water into color, smell, and touch, and the idea of water is gone. The Abhidharma took experience apart into its smallest elements and argued that each one perishes the moment it arises. This lesson sets that analysis beside Democritus’ atoms, Descartes’ divided time, and the modern view that chairs do not exist, and asks what the analysis was for.
把瓶打破，「瓶」的觀念就沒有了。用智慧把水分析成色、香、觸，「水」的觀念就沒有了。阿毘達磨把經驗拆成最小的元素，並且論證：每一個元素一生起就滅去。這一課把這種分析，跟德謨克利特的原子、笛卡兒被切開的時間，以及「椅子並不存在」的現代主張並列，並且問：這種分析是為了什麼？

The verdict · 判定

Closest: Democritus, who also divided the world into what exists “by convention” and what exists “in truth,” and found the truth at the end of an analysis. On time, the closest is Descartes, who held that a life divides into moments none of which guarantees the next. The likeness ends at two points. The Buddhist elements include feelings and thoughts as well as matter, and they are events that perish. Democritus’ atoms are eternal bits of matter. And the Abhidharma analysis was a practice for loosening attachment, and later Buddhists turned it on the elements themselves.

最接近的是德謨克利特：他也把世界分成「依約定」而存在的，與「在真實中」存在的；而真實，是在一場分析的盡頭找到的。在時間上最接近的是笛卡兒：他主張，一生可以分成許多瞬間，其中沒有一刻能保證下一刻。相似之處停在兩個地方。佛教的元素不只包括物質，也包括感受與念頭；而且它們是會滅去的事件。德謨克利特的原子，是永恆的物質碎片。另外，阿毘達磨的分析是一種鬆開執著的修行；而後來的佛教徒，把這種分析轉過來用在元素本身上。

1 · What the Dharma says · 佛法怎麼說

Taking experience apart 把經驗拆開

The analysis starts in the suttas. When Māra asks the nun Vajirā who created the “sentient being,” she answers, in Bhikkhu Sujato’s translation, “This is just a pile of conditions, you won’t find a sentient being here. When the parts are assembled we use the word ‘chariot’. So too, when the aggregates are present ‘sentient being’ is the convention we use.” The Abhidharma, the third basket of the canon, made this a system. It listed the elements, the dharmas, that remain when everything made of parts has been taken apart. Vasubandhu’s Treasury of Abhidharma, translated by Xuanzang, gives the test: “If the idea of a thing disappears when it is broken, or when the mind analyzes it into other things, it is conventional, like a pot or water. What is not like this is ultimate.” Break a pot and nothing in the pieces is a pot. Analyze water into color, smell, taste, and touch, and nothing is left that is water. Color itself, or a feeling, survives any analysis, and so it counts as ultimately real.

這種分析始於經典。當魔羅問金剛比丘尼：是誰造了「眾生」？她回答（依 Sujato 比丘的英譯）：「這只是一堆條件，你在這裡找不到眾生。當零件組合起來，我們就用『車』這個字。同樣地，當諸蘊存在，『眾生』就是我們使用的約定。」阿毘達磨（三藏中的論藏）把這一點做成一套體系。它列出了元素，也就是「法」：在一切由部分構成的東西都被拆開之後，所剩下的東西。世親的《俱舍論》（玄奘譯）給了一個檢驗：「彼覺破便無，慧析餘亦爾，如瓶水世俗，異此名勝義。」把瓶打破，碎片裡沒有任何一片是瓶。把水分析成色、香、味、觸，就沒有剩下任何東西是水。顏色本身，或者一個感受，經得起任何分析；所以它算是勝義上真實的。

The elements are not lasting things. The Sautrāntika and Sarvāstivāda schools disagreed about much, and both held that every conditioned element is momentary. The Treasury defines the term: a moment is “ceasing immediately upon obtaining its existence.” “Conditioned things, as soon as they come into being, immediately perish.” The masters of Abhidharma even measured it: “In the time it takes a strong man to snap his fingers once, there are sixty-five moments.” Vasubandhu drew a striking conclusion about motion. If a thing perishes where it arises, nothing ever travels from one place to another. What we call movement is a series of new elements arising in neighboring places, the way a row of lamps seems to carry a light along.

這些元素不是持久的東西。經量部與說一切有部在許多事情上意見不同，而兩者都主張：每一個有為的元素都是剎那的。《俱舍論》界定了這個詞：「剎那何謂？得體無間滅。」「諸有為法纔得自體，從此無間必滅歸無。」阿毘達磨的論師們甚至量過它：「如壯士一疾彈指頃六十五剎那。」世親從這裡得出一個驚人的結論，關於運動。如果一個東西在哪裡生起就在哪裡滅去，那麼就沒有任何東西真正從一個地方走到另一個地方：「若此處生即此處滅，無容從此轉至餘方。」我們所謂的移動，是一連串新的元素在相鄰的地方生起；就像一排燈，看起來好像把一道光傳了過去。

Vasubandhu argued for momentariness, and did not simply assert it. His argument, examined in the next section, rests on the claim that destruction needs no cause. The same Vasubandhu later wrote the Twenty Verses on consciousness-only, and there he turned the analysis against the atoms that the Abhidharma had treated as the smallest units of matter. “If an atom joins with six others, it must have six parts. If the six share its place, the whole heap would be the size of one atom.” Either the atom has sides, and so is not the smallest thing, or it has none, and then nothing can be built from it. The man who wrote the great textbook of analysis also showed where analysis runs out.

世親是論證剎那滅，而不只是宣稱它。他的論證（下一節會檢查）建立在一個主張上：滅不需要原因。同一位世親，後來寫了《唯識二十論》；在那裡，他把分析轉過來對付阿毘達磨當作物質最小單位的極微：「極微與六合，一應成六分；若與六同處，聚應如極微。」要嘛極微有面，那它就不是最小的東西；要嘛它沒有面，那就沒有任何東西能用它堆出來。寫出那部偉大的分析教科書的人，也顯示了分析在哪裡走到盡頭。

3 · The nearest Western views · 西方最接近的說法

Atoms, moments, and simples 原子、瞬間，與單純物

Convention and truth · 約定與真實

Democritus 德謨克利特c. 420 BCE

Democritus is the closest Greek parallel to the Abhidharma. His best-known fragment, in one common translation, reads: “By convention sweet is sweet, bitter is bitter, hot is hot, cold is cold, color is color; but in truth there are only atoms and the void.” The structure is the same as Vasubandhu’s two truths. Everyday things exist by agreement, and the analysis reveals what really exists. Both thinkers reach the real by taking things apart. The differences are large, though. Democritus’ atoms are eternal, unchanging, and purely physical, and the soul itself is made of fine atoms. The Abhidharma’s ultimate elements include feelings, perceptions, and intentions, and they are events that last a moment. For Democritus, color is the thing that exists only by convention, and the atom is real. For the Abhidharma, the moment of seeing color is itself one of the real elements.

德謨克利特是阿毘達磨在希臘最接近的對應。他最有名的殘篇，在一個常見的英譯裡說：「依約定，甜是甜，苦是苦，熱是熱，冷是冷，顏色是顏色；但在真實中，只有原子與虛空。」結構跟世親的二諦一樣：日常的東西是依共識而存在的，分析揭示什麼真正存在。兩位思想家都是靠把東西拆開，才到達真實。然而差別很大。德謨克利特的原子是永恆的、不變的、純粹物理的；連靈魂本身都是由精細的原子構成。阿毘達磨的究竟元素包括感受、想與思（意志）；而且它們是只持續一剎那的事件。對德謨克利特來說，顏色正是那種只依約定而存在的東西，原子才是真實的。對阿毘達磨來說，看見顏色的那一剎那，本身就是真實的元素之一。

Time in separate moments · 分成獨立瞬間的時間

René Descartes 笛卡兒1641

In the third Meditation Descartes made an argument about time that sounds like the Treasury. “The whole time of my life may be divided into an infinity of parts, each of which is in no way dependent on any other; and, accordingly, because I was in existence a short time ago, it does not follow that I must now exist, unless in this moment some cause create me anew as it were, that is, conserve me.” He added that conserving a thing “requires the same power and act that would be necessary to create it.” Like Vasubandhu, he denied that existing at one moment carries a thing into the next. The two drew opposite conclusions from it. Descartes concluded that God must re-create the world at every instant, and he used the argument to prove God’s existence. Vasubandhu concluded that each moment arises from the conditions of the moment before, with no creator and no lasting substance, and that this is all continuity has ever been.

笛卡兒在第三沉思裡，提出了一個關於時間的論證，聽起來很像《俱舍論》。「我一生的全部時間，可以分成無限多個部分，其中每一部分都完全不依賴其他部分；因此，從我不久之前存在，並不能推出我現在必然存在，除非在這一刻有某個原因彷彿重新創造了我，也就是保存了我。」他又說：保存一個東西，「需要的力量與行為，跟創造它所需要的相同」。跟世親一樣，他否認在某一刻存在，就能把一個東西帶到下一刻。兩人從這裡得出相反的結論。笛卡兒的結論是：上帝必須在每一瞬間重新創造世界；他用這個論證來證明上帝存在。世親的結論是：每一剎那都從前一剎那的條件中生起，沒有造物者，也沒有持久的實體；而連續性從來就只是這樣。

Chairs do not exist · 椅子並不存在

Peter van Inwagen 范・因瓦根1990

Some contemporary philosophers defend a view called mereological nihilism, which says that objects made of parts do not exist at all. There are no chairs, only simple particles “arranged chairwise.” The American philosopher Peter van Inwagen, in Material Beings, defended a version very close to this, with one exception. He argued that living organisms are real wholes, because the activity of their parts makes up a single life. The nihilist’s pot and the Treasury’s pot are the same: nothing in the world is a pot over and above the pieces, and the word is a convenience. The exception is where the comparison becomes sharp. Van Inwagen kept the living being, and in particular the human being, as a real whole. For the Abhidharma the person is the clearest example of a conventional thing, Vajirā’s chariot. The Buddhist analysis was aimed at the self first and at pots only for practice.

一些當代哲學家為一種叫做「部分整體虛無論」的觀點辯護：由部分構成的物體根本不存在。沒有椅子，只有「以椅子的方式排列」的單純粒子。美國哲學家彼得・范・因瓦根在《物質存有者》裡，為一個非常接近的版本辯護，只有一個例外：他論證說，活的有機體是真實的整體，因為它的部分的活動構成了一個單一的生命。虛無論者的瓶與《俱舍論》的瓶是同一個：世界上沒有任何東西在碎片之外還是一個瓶，這個字只是一種方便。那個例外，正是比較變得尖銳的地方。范・因瓦根保留了活的存有者，特別是人，作為真實的整體。對阿毘達磨來說，人正是約定之物最清楚的例子，就是金剛比丘尼的那輛車。佛教的分析，首先瞄準的是自我；瓶只是用來練習的。

4 · Verdict · 判定

Who is closest, and where the likeness ends 誰最接近，相似到哪裡為止

On structure the closest is Democritus. Both divide what exists by convention from what exists in truth, and both find the truth by analysis. On time the closest is Descartes, who saw as clearly as Vasubandhu that existing now does not guarantee existing next. On composite objects the closest is the mereological nihilist, for whom a pot is only its pieces. Each of these Western thinkers shares one part of the Abhidharma picture, and none of them shares all of it.

在結構上，最接近的是德謨克利特。兩人都把依約定而存在的，與在真實中存在的分開；也都靠分析找到真實。在時間上，最接近的是笛卡兒：他跟世親一樣清楚地看到，現在存在並不保證下一刻存在。在複合物體上，最接近的是部分整體虛無論者：對他們來說，一個瓶只是它的碎片。這幾位西方思想家，每一位都分享了阿毘達磨圖像的一部分；沒有一位分享了全部。

The likeness ends at the purpose. Democritus wanted to explain nature, Descartes wanted to prove God, and the nihilists want the simplest true account of what there is. The Abhidharma analysis was meant to be seen in meditation as well as believed. The practitioner watches feelings and thoughts arise and pass, and sees that nothing in them stays long enough to be a self or to be worth clinging to. Vajirā’s verse ends this way: “it’s only suffering that comes to be, lasts a while, then expires.” The list of elements was a map for that seeing. Later Buddhists, beginning with Vasubandhu’s own critique of atoms, said that the elements too lack any fixed essence. That claim would make no sense in Democritus’ system, and it is the next step on the Buddhist path.

相似之處，停在目的上。德謨克利特想要解釋自然，笛卡兒想要證明上帝，虛無論者想要對「有什麼」給出最簡單的真實說明。阿毘達磨的分析，不只是要被相信，也是要在禪修中被看見。修行人看著感受與念頭生起又消逝，看見其中沒有任何東西停留得夠久，可以成為一個自我，或值得執取。金剛比丘尼的偈子這樣結尾：「唯有苦生，暫住而滅。」那份元素的清單，是為這種看見而畫的地圖。後來的佛教徒，從世親自己對極微的批判開始，說元素本身也沒有固定的自性。這個主張在德謨克利特的體系裡毫無意義；而它是佛教道路上的下一步。

5 · The step the Dharma takes further · 佛法多走的一步

Analysis you can watch, and analysis that dissolves itself 可以觀察的分析，以及消解自己的分析

The first further step is that the analysis is meant to be watched. Democritus could not see his atoms, and nobody can observe Descartes’ moments of re-creation. The Abhidharma elements are things like a feeling, a sound, an intention, and a trained attention can catch them as they arise and pass. The insight traditions of both Theravāda and Tibet teach exactly this: watch the rise and fall closely enough, and the sense of a solid, lasting observer begins to loosen. The claim that a moment is one sixty-fifth of a finger snap should not be read as physics. It says that the arising and passing is much faster than ordinary attention, and that training can bring attention closer to it. Modern readers sometimes compare momentariness to quantum physics or to film frames. The comparisons can help a beginner, and the teaching does not rest on them.

多走的第一步是：這種分析是要被觀察的。德謨克利特看不見他的原子；也沒有人能觀察到笛卡兒那些重新創造的瞬間。阿毘達磨的元素，是像一個感受、一個聲音、一個意圖這樣的東西；受過訓練的注意力，可以在它們生起與消逝時捕捉到它們。南傳與藏傳的內觀傳統教的正是這個：把生滅看得夠仔細，一個堅固、持久的觀察者的感覺就開始鬆動。「一剎那是彈指的六十五分之一」這個說法，不應被當作物理學來讀。它說的是：生滅比平常的注意力快得多；而訓練可以使注意力更接近它。現代讀者有時把剎那滅比作量子物理或電影膠片的格子。這些比較可以幫助初學者；而教法並不依靠它們。

The second further step is that Buddhist analysis did not stop at the elements. The Abhidharma treated dharmas as the ultimate truth, the place where analysis ends. Vasubandhu’s own argument against atoms showed that even the smallest unit of matter cannot be coherently conceived, and the Madhyamaka, in the lesson on emptiness, went further: every element, examined, depends on other things and has no fixed nature of its own. The tradition kept the Abhidharma anyway. It still trains monks and nuns in the lists, because the analysis does its work even if it is not the last word. It breaks the hold of the pot, the chariot, and the self, and then the student is ready to let go of the elements too. This is the raft again, from the first lesson of this shelf.

多走的第二步是：佛教的分析並沒有停在元素上。阿毘達磨把法當作勝義諦，是分析的終點。世親自己反駁極微的論證顯示：即使是物質最小的單位，也無法被一致地設想；而中觀（見講「空」的那一課）走得更遠：每一個元素經過檢查，都依賴其他東西，沒有它自己固定的自性。儘管如此，傳統還是保留了阿毘達磨。它至今仍用那些法數訓練出家人，因為即使分析不是最後的話，它仍然起作用。它打破瓶、車與自我的掌握；然後學生就準備好，連元素也放下。這又是那艘筏，出自這個書架的第一課。

6 · How to say it to a Western audience · 對西方人怎麼講

The pot, the water, and the finger snap 瓶、水，與彈指

Westerners usually know atomism from school, so begin there. Democritus said that sweet and bitter exist by convention and atoms exist in truth. Then give Vasubandhu’s test, which is easy to picture: break the pot and the pot is gone, analyze the water and the water is gone. Point out the two differences. The Buddhist elements include the mind, and they last only a moment. The finger snap with sixty-five moments makes a memorable image, and you should say plainly that it is a measure of attention and not a laboratory figure. Then make the practical point. The analysis was meant to be seen, in meditation, and its target was the sense of a lasting self. End with Vajirā’s chariot, because it shows that the whole system grew out of a short verse spoken by a nun to Māra.

西方人通常在學校就知道原子論，所以從那裡開始。德謨克利特說：甜與苦是依約定而存在的，原子是在真實中存在的。然後給出世親的檢驗，它很容易想像：把瓶打破，瓶就沒了；把水分析，水就沒了。指出兩個差別：佛教的元素包括心；而且它們只持續一剎那。「彈指有六十五剎那」是一個令人難忘的意象；你應該直接說明，它是注意力的尺度，不是實驗室的數字。然後講實際的重點：這種分析是要在禪修中被看見的；它瞄準的是那種持久自我的感覺。最後用金剛比丘尼的車作結，因為它顯示：整套體系，是從一位比丘尼對魔羅說的一首短偈長出來的。

Sentences you can use 可以直接用的句子

  1. The Abhidharma took experience apart into its smallest elements, called dharmas.

    阿毘達磨把經驗拆成最小的元素，稱為「法」。

  2. Its test: if the idea of a thing disappears when you break it or analyze it, the thing is conventional, like a pot or water.

    它的檢驗是：如果一個東西被打破或被分析之後，它的觀念就消失了，那它就是世俗的，像瓶或水。

  3. The elements include feelings and thoughts, not only matter, and each lasts only a moment.

    這些元素包括感受與念頭，不只是物質；而且每一個都只持續一剎那。

  4. Vasubandhu argued that destruction needs no cause, so things perish as soon as they arise.

    世親論證說：滅不需要原因，所以東西一生起就滅去。

  5. Descartes also said that existing now does not guarantee existing next. He concluded that God keeps re-creating us.

    笛卡兒也說：現在存在不保證下一刻存在。他的結論是：上帝不斷地重新創造我們。

  6. The analysis was meant to be watched in meditation, and its main target was the self.

    這種分析是要在禪修中被觀察的；它主要的目標是自我。

Phrases to avoid 要避開的說法

“Buddhism says nothing exists.” 「佛教說什麼都不存在。」

The Abhidharma says composites are conventional and the elements are real.

阿毘達磨說：複合物是世俗的，元素是真實的。

“Physics has proved momentariness.” 「物理學已經證明了剎那滅。」

The comparison may help a beginner. The argument and the practice stand without it.

這個比較或許能幫助初學者；論證與修行不靠它也站得住。

“The Abhidharma is just Greek atomism.” 「阿毘達磨不過就是希臘的原子論。」

Its elements are mental as well as physical, and they are momentary events.

它的元素有心也有物；而且是剎那的事件。

“If everything is momentary, no one is responsible for anything.” 「如果一切都是剎那的，就沒有人要為任何事負責。」

The series carries the results of actions. The next lesson takes this up.

相續承載著行為的結果。下一課會處理這一點。

Reading check · 理解測驗

Did it land? 讀懂了嗎

1. What is the Treasury’s test for whether something is conventional?

2. What is Vasubandhu’s main premise for momentariness?

3. How does Descartes’ conclusion differ from Vasubandhu’s?

4. What does Vasubandhu’s argument in the Twenty Verses target?

5. According to the lesson, what is the main target of the Abhidharma analysis?

Write · 你怎麼講？

  1. Pick an everyday object near you and apply the Treasury’s test. Break it in imagination, then analyze it. What, if anything, survives? About 150 words.

    挑一個你身邊的日常物品，套用《俱舍論》的檢驗。在想像中把它打破，然後把它分析開來。有什麼東西留下來嗎？約 150 個字。

  2. Sit for five minutes and watch sounds arise and pass. Write down what you noticed about how long any one sound seemed to last.

    坐五分鐘，看著聲音生起又消逝。寫下你注意到的：任何一個聲音，看起來持續了多久。

  3. Descartes and Vasubandhu agree that existing now does not guarantee existing next, and then disagree completely. In 200 words, explain which conclusion you find more reasonable and why.

    笛卡兒與世親同意「現在存在不保證下一刻存在」，然後就完全分道揚鑣。用 200 個字說明：你覺得哪一個結論比較合理，為什麼。
