D15 · Dharma and the West 佛法與西方哲學

Exclusion and the Problem of Universals 遮詮與共相問題

No two trees are the same, yet one word covers them all. Is there a real “treeness” they share? Dignāga and Dharmakīrti said no: the word “tree” works by excluding everything that is not a tree. This lesson compares their theory with John Locke’s nominalism, Ferdinand de Saussure’s “differences,” and Maimonides’ negative theology, and asks why Buddhists wanted to see through concepts at all.
沒有兩棵樹是一樣的，但一個字就涵蓋了它們全部。它們之間，有沒有一個真實的「樹性」是共有的？陳那與法稱說：沒有；「樹」這個字的作用，是把一切「非樹」排除掉。這一課把他們的理論，跟洛克的唯名論、索緒爾的「差異」、邁蒙尼德的否定神學對照，並且問：佛教徒為什麼要看穿概念？

The verdict · 判定

Closest on what exists: John Locke, for whom general and universal things are “the inventions and creatures of the understanding,” with a foundation in the likeness of things. Closest on how a word means: Saussure, for whom a sign is defined by what it is not. The likeness ends at the purpose. Locke and Saussure wanted to describe how understanding and language work. Dharmakīrti wanted to show that every concept is in one respect a mistake, a useful one, and that the path runs through concepts and then beyond them.

在「什麼存在」上最接近的是洛克：對他來說，一般的、普遍的東西是「知性的發明與產物」，而它的根據在事物的相似。在「一個字怎麼有意義」上最接近的是索緒爾：對他來說，一個符號是由它「不是什麼」來界定的。相似之處，停在目的上。洛克與索緒爾想要描述知性與語言如何運作。法稱想要顯示：每一個概念在某個意義上都是一個錯誤，一個有用的錯誤；而道路是穿過概念、然後越過概念的。

1 · What the Dharma says · 佛法怎麼說

A word that excludes 一個排除的字

Indian philosophers argued for centuries about universals. The Nyāya and Mīmāṃsā schools held that “cowness” is a real, eternal thing present in every cow, and that this is what the word “cow” names. The Buddhist logicians could not accept this. For them what is real is the particular, each thing in its own unique character at its own moment, and nothing real is shared by many things. That left them a problem. If there is no cowness, what does the word “cow” mean, and how can one word apply to many different animals? Dignāga answered in a chapter of his Compendium of the Means of Knowledge whose title the Chinese renders as “Examination of Exclusion.” The verse that opens it, in Lü Cheng’s Chinese translation from the Tibetan, reads: “Knowledge arising from words is no different from inference. Like ‘produced,’ it shows its own meaning by exclusion.”

印度的哲學家為共相爭論了好幾個世紀。正理派與彌曼差派主張：「牛性」是一個真實的、永恆的東西，存在於每一頭牛之中；而「牛」這個字指稱的就是它。佛教的邏輯學家無法接受這一點。對他們來說，真實的是個別之物（自相）：每一樣東西，在它自己的那一刻，有它獨一無二的性質；而沒有任何真實的東西是被許多東西共有的。這就留給他們一個問題：如果沒有牛性，「牛」這個字是什麼意思？一個字又怎麼能用在許多不同的動物身上？陳那在《集量論》的一品裡回答了這個問題；這一品的中文名稱是「觀遮詮品」。開頭的那首頌，呂澂從藏文譯成中文：「音聲所起者，此無異比量，亦如說所作，遮詮顯自義。」

Dharmakīrti developed the theory with the example of a tree. Every tree is different from every other. What they have in common is only that each is not a non-tree. The word “tree” draws a line around them by excluding everything else, and it is set up for those things that differ from non-trees. The mind that thinks “tree” sees many different things as one kind, and Dharmakīrti calls this cognition mistaken. A Tibetan commentary on his work, translated into Chinese by Fazun together with the verses, puts it plainly: the thought that takes separate trees as one thing is an inverted cognition. Yet it is not groundless. Dharmakīrti gives an analogy: “Medicines differ, yet taken together or one at a time they are seen to cure fever and other illnesses, and other things do not.” Different particulars can do the same work. That shared ability is the real basis of the concept, and there is no shared essence behind it.

法稱用樹的例子發展了這個理論。每一棵樹都跟其他每一棵樹不同。它們的共同之處，只是每一棵都「不是非樹」。「樹」這個字，是靠著排除其他一切，在它們周圍畫出一條線；它是為那些「異於非樹」的東西而安立的。想著「樹」的那個心，把許多不同的東西看成同一類；法稱稱這個認識為錯亂。法尊連同本頌一起譯成中文的那部藏文註釋，說得很直白：「其於各別樹義，執為一體之分別，是顛倒覺。」但它並不是沒有根據的。法稱給了一個比喻：「如見藥雖異，或共或各別，能治疫病等，餘物則不爾。」不同的個別之物，可以做同樣的工作。這個共同的能力，才是概念的真實基礎；在它背後，沒有一個共同的本質。

The Chinese term for the theory, zhequan, “expression by negation,” took on a second life in China. The ninth-century master Zongmi used it for a style of teaching. “Negation removes what it is not. Affirmation shows what it is.” When the sūtras say “not arising, not ceasing, not defiled, not pure,” they speak by negation, and he gave a homely example: “To say salt is not bland is negation. To say it is salty is affirmation.” He also gave a warning. Students of his day thought negation was profound and affirmation shallow, and so they valued only phrases like “not mind, not Buddha.” Zongmi said this came from loving words of negation and being unwilling to recognize the thing itself. The Indian theory and the Chinese teaching style are not the same idea, and this lesson treats both because they share a name and a lesson. Concepts are drawn by exclusion, and exclusion is not yet seeing.

這個理論的中文名稱「遮詮」，在中國有了第二個生命。九世紀的宗密，用它來指一種說法的方式：「遮謂遣其所非，表謂顯其所是。」當經典說「不生不滅、不垢不淨」的時候，就是用遮詮在說；而他舉了一個家常的例子：「如說鹽云不淡是遮，云鹹是表。」他也給了一個警告：當時的學人都認為遮言為深、表言為淺，所以只看重「非心非佛」這一類的話。宗密說，這是因為「但以遮非之詞為妙，不欲親自證認法體」。印度的理論與中國的這種說法並不是同一個觀念；這一課兩者都談，因為它們共用一個名字，也共有一個教訓：概念是靠排除畫出來的，而排除還不是看見。

3 · The nearest Western views · 西方最接近的說法

Nominalism, difference, negation 唯名論、差異、否定

The same ontology · 同樣的存有論

John Locke 洛克1690

Locke asked the Buddhist question in almost the same words. “Since all things that exist are only particulars, how come we by general terms; or where find we those general natures they are supposed to stand for?” His answer was that “general and universal belong not to the real existence of things; but are the inventions and creatures of the understanding, made by it for its own use, and concern only signs.” Like Dharmakīrti he did not make the sorting arbitrary. The kinds are “the workmanship of the understanding,” he wrote, “but have their foundation in the similitude of things.” The difference lies in how the general idea is formed. Locke thought the mind builds it positively, by leaving out the particular circumstances of time and place until a common core remains. Dharmakīrti thought there is no common core to keep and that the mind gets its general idea by excluding what the thing is not. Berkeley later attacked Locke’s abstract ideas as impossible, and the Buddhist theory avoids that problem because it never posits them.

洛克幾乎用同樣的話問了佛教的那個問題：「既然一切存在的東西都只是個別之物，我們是怎麼得到一般名詞的？或者說，它們被認為代表的那些一般的性質，要到哪裡去找？」他的回答是：「一般的與普遍的，並不屬於事物的真實存在；而是知性的發明與產物，由知性為了自己的用途而造，只關乎符號。」跟法稱一樣，他沒有讓分類變成任意的。他寫道：種類是「知性的作品，但以事物的相似為根據」。差別在於一般觀念是怎麼形成的。洛克認為，心是正面地把它建構出來的：把時間、地點等個別的情況去掉，直到剩下一個共同的核心。法稱認為，根本沒有共同的核心可以保留；心是靠排除「這東西不是什麼」，得到它的一般觀念。後來柏克萊攻擊洛克的抽象觀念，說它不可能存在；佛教的理論避開了這個問題，因為它從來沒有設定那種觀念。

Meaning by difference · 由差異而有意義

Ferdinand de Saussure 索緒爾1916

The Swiss linguist Ferdinand de Saussure founded modern linguistics on the idea that a sign has no meaning on its own. Its value comes from its place in a system, from its differences from all the other signs. In the English translation of his lectures, “A linguistic system is a series of differences of sound combined with a series of differences of ideas.” The word “sheep” means what it means partly because English also has “mutton,” and a language with one word for both carves the world differently. Modern scholars have often compared Saussure with the apoha theory, and the likeness is real. Both say that a word is defined by what it excludes. The difference is in what each is explaining. Saussure was describing a social system of signs and set aside the question of how words connect to things. Dharmakīrti was explaining how concepts connect to real particulars, and his answer depends on the work those particulars do. Saussure stays inside language. Dharmakīrti is always asking whether a concept will get you to the jewel.

瑞士語言學家索緒爾，把現代語言學建立在一個觀念上：一個符號本身沒有意義。它的價值來自它在一個系統裡的位置，來自它與其他所有符號的差異。他的講稿的英譯說：「一個語言系統，是一系列聲音的差異，與一系列觀念的差異結合起來。」英文的 sheep 之所以是那個意思，部分是因為英文另外還有 mutton；一個用一個字涵蓋兩者的語言，就把世界切得不一樣。現代學者常拿索緒爾跟遮詮理論比較；而這種相似是真的。兩者都說：一個字是由它排除的東西來界定的。差別在於各自要解釋什麼。索緒爾描述的是一個社會的符號系統，並把「字怎麼連到物」這個問題擱在一邊。法稱解釋的是概念怎麼連到真實的個別之物；而他的答案取決於那些個別之物所做的工作。索緒爾停留在語言之內。法稱一直在問：一個概念能不能帶你拿到那顆寶珠。

Speaking by negation · 以否定來說

Maimonides 邁蒙尼德c. 1190

Zongmi’s sense of the word has its own Western parallel in negative theology, and its sharpest statement is by the Jewish philosopher Maimonides. In the Guide for the Perplexed he wrote, “Know that the negative attributes of God are the true attributes.” His illustration is a ship. One person knows only the word. Others learn, one step at a time, that it is not an accident, not a mineral, not a plant growing in the earth, not a flat object like boards or doors, not a sphere, and so on, until the tenth “has almost arrived at the correct notion.” In the same way one comes nearer to knowing God by learning what God is not. Then comes a warning very like Zongmi’s: “You must be careful, in what you negative, to negative by proof, not by mere words.” Negation, for both, is a discipline and should not be treated as a mood or a style. The difference is the goal. Maimonides held that God’s essence stays beyond human understanding however far the negations go. Zongmi held that after the negations one must recognize the thing itself, the knowing that is the nature of mind.

宗密意義上的遮詮，在西方有它自己的對應：否定神學；而它最尖銳的陳述出自猶太哲學家邁蒙尼德。他在《迷途指津》裡寫道：「要知道，上帝的否定屬性，才是真正的屬性。」他的例子是一艘船。一個人只知道這個字。其他人一步一步得知：它不是偶性、不是礦物、不是長在地上的植物、不是像木板或門那樣的扁平物、不是球體⋯⋯直到第十個人「幾乎到達了正確的觀念」。同樣地，一個人藉著得知上帝不是什麼，而更接近對上帝的認識。接著是一個非常像宗密的警告：「你在否定的時候必須小心，要以證明來否定，而不是只憑言詞。」對兩人來說，否定都是一種紀律，不應被當成一種情調或一種文風。差別在於目標。邁蒙尼德認為：無論否定走得多遠，上帝的本質始終超出人的理解。宗密認為：否定之後，必須認得那個東西本身，也就是那個作為心之本性的「知」。

4 · Verdict · 判定

Who is closest, and where the likeness ends 誰最接近，相似到哪裡為止

On what exists, the closest is Locke. Both deny real universals, both make kinds a work of the mind, and both say the work is not arbitrary because it follows real likeness or real capacity in things. On how a word means, the closest is Saussure, since both define a word by what it excludes. On the Chinese sense of zhequan, the closest is Maimonides, who like Zongmi treats negation as the right way to approach what cannot be grasped, and also warns against negation for its own sake.

在「什麼存在」上，最接近的是洛克。兩人都否認真實的共相；都把種類當作心的作品；也都說這個作品不是任意的，因為它順著事物中真實的相似或真實的能力。在「一個字怎麼有意義」上，最接近的是索緒爾，因為兩人都用一個字所排除的東西來界定它。在中文意義上的遮詮，最接近的是邁蒙尼德：他跟宗密一樣，把否定當作接近那無法被把握之物的正確方式，也一樣警告不要為了否定而否定。

The likeness ends at the purpose. Locke wanted to map the limits of human understanding, and Saussure wanted a science of language. Both describe concepts and leave them in place. Dharmakīrti calls every concept mistaken, because it presents many things as one, and still useful, because it can lead to the jewel. He says the whole framework of conventional thought is set up by the wise “in order to enter the ultimate.” Concepts are a means. The practitioner uses them to reason, to teach, and to act, and comes in the end to a perception that does not need them. Neither Locke nor Saussure thought there was any such perception to come to.

相似之處，停在目的上。洛克想要畫出人類知性的界限，索緒爾想要一門語言的科學。兩人都描述概念，然後讓它們留在原地。法稱說每一個概念都是錯亂的，因為它把許多東西呈現為一個；而它仍然有用，因為它能帶人拿到寶珠。他說，整個世俗思維的架構，是智者「為入勝義故」而建立的。概念是一種手段。修行人用概念來推理、教學與行動，最後來到一種不需要概念的覺知。洛克與索緒爾都不認為有這樣一種覺知可以到達。

5 · The step the Dharma takes further · 佛法多走的一步

A useful mistake, and the warning against negation 有用的錯誤，以及對否定的警告

The first further step is the idea of a useful mistake. Western philosophy has usually divided concepts into true and false, or into those that match reality and those that do not. Dharmakīrti put them in a third place. Every concept misrepresents, because it shows many things as one. Some concepts still lead reliably to what we want, because they track real differences in what things can do, and others lead nowhere. This is the two truths again, applied to logic. Conventional thought is not true in the final sense, and it is not to be thrown away, because it is how anyone reasons toward the final sense at all. A teacher can say this plainly to a Western student: every word you use is a simplification, and some simplifications are maps that get you home.

多走的第一步，是「有用的錯誤」這個觀念。西方哲學通常把概念分成真的與假的，或者分成符合實在的與不符合的。法稱把它們放在第三個位置。每一個概念都有所誤現，因為它把許多東西呈現為一個。有些概念仍然可靠地帶我們到想去的地方，因為它們追蹤事物「能做什麼」的真實差異；另一些則哪裡也去不了。這又是二諦，用在邏輯上。世俗的思維在究竟的意義上不是真的；而它也不該被丟掉，因為任何人都是靠它，才能朝向究竟的意義推理。一位老師可以把這一點直接告訴西方學生：你用的每一個字都是一種簡化；而有些簡化是能帶你回家的地圖。

The second step is Zongmi’s warning, which applies to every teacher who works in English. Western audiences drawn to Buddhism often love the language of negation: no self, emptiness, not this, not that. It can become a style, a way of sounding deep, and Zongmi saw the same thing among the Chan students of his day. They valued only “not mind, not Buddha” and would not recognize the thing itself. Maimonides gave the same warning in another tradition: negate by proof, not by mere words. The lesson for a teacher is to use negation the way Dharmakīrti and Maimonides used it, as a disciplined way to remove a specific wrong idea, one at a time. It should never be a mood. After the negations there is still something to see, and the point of all the clearing is to see it.

第二步是宗密的警告，它適用於每一位用英文教學的老師。被佛教吸引的西方聽眾，常常喜愛否定的語言：無我、空、不是這個、不是那個。它可能變成一種文風，一種聽起來很深的方式；而宗密在他那個時代的禪學人之間，看到了同樣的事。他們只看重「非心非佛」，而不肯認得那個東西本身。邁蒙尼德在另一個傳統裡給了同樣的警告：要以證明來否定，而不是只憑言詞。對一位老師來說，教訓是：像法稱與邁蒙尼德那樣使用否定，把它當作一種有紀律的方式，一次去除一個具體的錯誤觀念。它永遠不應該是一種情調。否定之後，仍然有東西要看；而一切清除的重點，就是看見它。

6 · How to say it to a Western audience · 對西方人怎麼講

Tree, non-tree, and the jewel 樹、非樹，與寶珠

Start with the old puzzle, which Westerners know from Plato and Locke. Point to two very different trees and ask what makes both of them trees. Most people will say they share something. Then give the Buddhist answer: they share nothing, except that neither is a non-tree, and both do what trees do. Mention Saussure for anyone who has studied linguistics, since the comparison will help them. Then tell the story of the two lights, because it carries the main point better than any argument. Every concept is a little mistaken, and the question is whether it gets you to the jewel. End with Zongmi’s salt, which is easy to remember. “Not bland” clears away an error, and “salty” is what you taste.

從那個古老的難題開始；西方人從柏拉圖與洛克那裡就知道它。指著兩棵非常不同的樹，問：是什麼使它們兩者都是樹？大多數人會說：它們共有某樣東西。然後給出佛教的答案：它們什麼也不共有，只是兩者都不是非樹，而且兩者都做樹做的事。對學過語言學的人提一下索緒爾，這個比較會幫助他們。然後講兩團光的故事，因為它比任何論證都更能承載重點：每一個概念都有一點錯，問題在於它能不能帶你拿到寶珠。最後用宗密的鹽作結，很容易記住：「不淡」清除一個錯誤；「鹹」是你嚐到的東西。

Sentences you can use 可以直接用的句子

  1. Buddhist logicians held that only particular things are real. There is no treeness shared by all trees.

    佛教的邏輯學家主張：只有個別的東西是真實的。沒有一個被所有樹共有的「樹性」。

  2. The word “tree” works by excluding everything that is not a tree.

    「樹」這個字的作用，是把一切不是樹的東西排除掉。

  3. What grounds the word is that different trees do the same work, as different medicines all cure a fever.

    使這個字有根據的，是不同的樹做同樣的工作；就像不同的藥都能治發燒。

  4. Every concept is a little mistaken, because it treats many things as one. Some of them still lead you to the jewel.

    每一個概念都有一點錯，因為它把許多東西當成一個。其中有些仍然能帶你拿到寶珠。

  5. Saying salt is “not bland” removes an error. Saying it is “salty” tells you what it is.

    說鹽「不淡」，是去除一個錯誤。說它「鹹」，是告訴你它是什麼。

  6. Negation is a tool for clearing away wrong ideas. It should not become a way of sounding deep.

    否定是清除錯誤觀念的工具。它不應該變成一種聽起來很深的方式。

Phrases to avoid 要避開的說法

“Buddhists think words are meaningless.” 「佛教徒認為字是沒有意義的。」

They had a detailed theory of how words mean and why they work.

他們有一套詳細的理論，說明字怎麼有意義、為什麼行得通。

“Concepts are just illusions, so ignore them.” 「概念只是幻覺，所以別理它們。」

Dharmakīrti called them mistaken and reliable. The jewel story shows both.

法稱說它們是錯亂的，而且可靠。寶珠的故事兩面都顯示了。

“This is just Saussure.” 「這不過就是索緒爾。」

The likeness is real, but Dharmakīrti ties words to what things do, and Saussure did not.

相似是真的；但法稱把字連到事物所做的事，索緒爾沒有。

“The deepest teaching is pure negation.” 「最深的教法就是純粹的否定。」

Zongmi called that a mistake: negation clears the way to recognizing something.

宗密說那是個錯誤：否定是為認得某樣東西清出道路。

Reading check · 理解測驗

Did it land? 讀懂了嗎

1. Why did the Buddhist logicians need a theory like exclusion?

2. According to Dharmakīrti, what grounds the concept “tree”?

3. What is the point of the two lights, one from a jewel and one from a lamp?

4. How does Zongmi illustrate negation and affirmation?

5. Where does the lesson say the likeness with Locke and Saussure ends?

Write · 你怎麼講？

  1. Choose a word for a kind of thing, such as “chair” or “game.” Try to define it by what it excludes. Then say what work the things it covers have in common. About 150 words.

    選一個指稱某類東西的字，例如「椅子」或「遊戲」。試著用它所排除的東西來界定它。然後說出它所涵蓋的那些東西，在「做的工作」上有什麼共同點。約 150 個字。

  2. Name a concept you use every day that is a useful simplification. When does it lead you to the jewel, and when does it lead you to the lamp?

    說出一個你每天使用、而且是有用的簡化的概念。什麼時候它帶你拿到寶珠？什麼時候它帶你跑向燈光？

  3. Zongmi and Maimonides both warn against negation for its own sake. In 200 words, describe a time when saying what something is not was more honest than saying what it is, and a time when it was an evasion.

    宗密與邁蒙尼德都警告不要為否定而否定。用 200 個字描述：有一次，說某樣東西「不是什麼」比說它「是什麼」更誠實；另有一次，那只是一種迴避。
