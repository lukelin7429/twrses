D20 · Dharma and the West 佛法與西方哲學

Mindfulness and Attention 正念與注意力

Carry a bowl of oil filled to the brim through a crowd watching a dancer, with a man behind you holding a drawn sword. That, the Buddha said, is mindfulness of the body. The Stoic Epictetus taught a discipline of constant attention, and William James called the power to bring back a wandering mind “the very root of judgment, character, and will.” This lesson asks what each tradition watched, and what it watched for.
端著一缽滿到邊緣的油，穿過一群正在看舞者表演的人；你身後有一個人拔著劍跟著。佛陀說：這就是身念。斯多噶的愛比克泰德教一種持續注意的紀律；威廉・詹姆斯說，把游走的心拉回來的能力，是「判斷、品格與意志的根」。這一課問：每個傳統觀察的是什麼？又是為了什麼而觀察？

The verdict · 判定

Closest: Epictetus, whose prosochē, constant attention to one’s own impressions and responses, is a daily discipline in the same sense as sati, and like sati it is practiced while walking, eating, and speaking. William James is closest on the psychology. He saw that training attention would be “the education par excellence” and admitted he could not say how to give it, and the Buddhist manuals are exactly such instructions. The likeness ends at what attention is for. The Stoic watches to keep the ruling reason in charge of its judgments. The Buddhist watches body, feeling, mind, and phenomena arise and pass until it is clear that no one is in charge.

最接近的是愛比克泰德：他的 prosochē（持續注意自己的印象與反應），跟「念」一樣是一種日常的紀律；而且跟「念」一樣，是在走路、吃飯、說話時修的。在心理學上，最接近的是威廉・詹姆斯：他看出訓練注意力會是「最上乘的教育」，也承認自己說不出怎麼給這種教育；而佛教的禪修手冊，正是這樣的指導。相似之處，停在注意力是為了什麼。斯多噶的人觀察，是為了讓那個主宰的理性掌管它的判斷。佛教的人觀察身、受、心、法的生起與消逝，直到清楚看見：沒有一個人在掌管。

1 · What the Dharma says · 佛法怎麼說

The four foundations, the bowl of oil, the acrobats 四念處、一缽油、雜技師

The Pali word sati first meant memory, and the suttas keep that sense. Defining the faculty of mindfulness, the Buddha says that a noble disciple “can remember and recall what was said and done long ago” (Saṁyutta Nikāya 48.10, in Bhikkhu Sujato’s translation). Mindfulness is a kind of remembering that keeps the present object in mind and does not let it slip. The Discourse on Mindfulness Meditation opens with large claims. The Middle-Length Discourses in Chinese render it: “There is one path that purifies beings, carries them past grief and fear, ends pain and distress, stops weeping, and attains the true Dharma: the four foundations of mindfulness.” The four are body, feelings, mind, and phenomena. In each the practitioner is, in Sujato’s rendering of the Pali, “keen, aware, and mindful, rid of covetousness and displeasure for the world.”

巴利文的 sati，原本的意思是記憶；經典保留了這個意思。佛陀在界定念根時說，聖弟子「能憶念、能回想很久以前所說所做的事」（《相應部》48.10，依 Sujato 比丘的英譯）。正念是一種記得：把當下的對象保持在心裡，不讓它滑走。〈念處經〉的開頭說得很大。《中阿含經》的譯文是：「有一道淨眾生，度憂畏，滅苦惱，斷啼哭，得正法，謂四念處。」四念處是身、受（感受）、心、法。在每一處，修行人都是（依 Sujato 對巴利文的英譯）「精勤、正知、具念，除去對世間的貪與憂」。

The practice is ordinary. “When walking they know: ‘I am walking.’ When standing they know: ‘I am standing.’” The Chinese text has the same: “When walking, know walking; when standing, know standing; when sitting, know sitting; when lying down, know lying down.” The practitioner brings the same plain knowing to feelings as they arise, pleasant, painful, or neutral, to states of mind such as anger or calm, and to the patterns of experience the Buddha taught, such as the hindrances and the factors of awakening. Nothing here requires a special place. The instructions include eating, drinking, dressing, and going to the toilet.

這種修行是平常的。「行則知行，住則知住，坐則知坐，臥則知臥。」巴利文版本也一樣。修行人用同樣平實的「知」，對待生起的感受（樂、苦、不苦不樂），對待像瞋或平靜這樣的心的狀態，也對待佛陀所教的經驗模式，例如五蓋與七覺支。這裡沒有任何東西需要一個特別的地方。指導裡包括吃、喝、穿衣，以及上廁所。

Two short suttas give the spirit of it. In one, a man is told to carry “a bowl full to the brim with oil” between a large crowd and “the finest lady in the land,” who is dancing and singing, while “a man with a drawn sword will follow right behind you. Wherever you spill even a drop, he’ll chop off your head right there.” Would he lose focus on that bowl? No. “‘A bowl of oil filled to the brim’ is a term for mindfulness of the body” (Saṁyutta Nikāya 47.20). In the other, two acrobats on a bamboo pole discuss safety. The teacher says, “You look after me, and I shall look after you.” The apprentice disagrees: “You look after yourself, and I shall look after myself.” The Buddha sides with the apprentice and then adds, “Looking after yourself, you look after others; and looking after others, you look after yourself,” the latter “by acceptance, harmlessness, love, and sympathy” (Saṁyutta Nikāya 47.19). Mindfulness looks inward, and the suttas never treat it as private.

兩部短經傳達了它的精神。一部說：有一個人被要求端著「一缽滿到邊緣的油」，穿過一大群人與「國中最美的女子」之間；她正在唱歌跳舞；「有一個拔劍的人緊跟在你後面。你在哪裡灑出一滴，他就在那裡砍下你的頭。」他會不會對那缽油失去專注？不會。「『滿到邊緣的一缽油』，指的是身念。」（《相應部》47.20）另一部說：兩位雜技師在竹竿上討論安全。師父說：「你照顧我，我照顧你。」徒弟不同意：「你照顧你自己，我照顧我自己。」佛陀站在徒弟這一邊，然後補充：「照顧自己，就是照顧他人；照顧他人，就是照顧自己。」後者是「以忍耐、不害、慈心與悲憫」（《相應部》47.19）。正念是向內看的；而經典從來不把它當作私事。

3 · The nearest Western views · 西方最接近的說法

Attention as a discipline 作為紀律的注意力

Constant attention · 持續的注意

Epictetus 愛比克泰德c. 108 CE

The Stoics had a word for attention, prosochē, and Epictetus devoted a discourse to it. In George Long’s translation: “When you have remitted your attention for a short time, do not imagine this, that you will recover it when you choose.” A lapse forms “a habit of not attending” and then “a habit of deferring your attention.” He asks, “Is there any part of life excepted, to which attention does not extend?” and answers with the carpenter and the ship’s captain, who do not work better by not attending. Like sati, prosochē is practiced in ordinary activity: “Today I choose to play. Well then, ought you not to play with attention?” What is attended to is a little different. Epictetus wants us to watch our impressions and ask, “How shall I use the appearances presented to me? according to nature, or contrary to nature?” The aim is to keep the will on what is in its power and to let go of the rest.

斯多噶學派有一個指注意力的字：prosochē；愛比克泰德為它寫了一篇論說。George Long 的英譯是：「當你讓注意力鬆懈了一小段時間，不要以為你想收回它的時候就能收回。」一次鬆懈會形成「一種不注意的習慣」，然後是「一種延後注意的習慣」。他問：「生活中有沒有哪一部分，是注意力不及的？」並用木匠與船長回答：他們不會因為不注意而做得更好。跟「念」一樣，prosochē 是在平常的活動中修的：「今天我選擇去玩。那麼，你不應該帶著注意去玩嗎？」所注意的東西有一點不同。愛比克泰德要我們觀察自己的印象，並問：「我該怎麼使用呈現給我的這些表象？順著自然，還是違反自然？」目的是讓意志守在它力所能及的事上，放下其餘的。

The education par excellence · 最上乘的教育

William James 威廉・詹姆斯1890

In the chapter on attention in The Principles of Psychology, William James wrote two sentences that read like a description of mindfulness. “My experience is what I agree to attend to. Only those items which I notice shape my mind.” And later: “The faculty of voluntarily bringing back a wandering attention, over and over again, is the very root of judgment, character, and will. No one is compos sui if he have it not.” Compos sui means master of oneself. Then he made an admission. “An education which should improve this faculty would be the education par excellence. But it is easier to define this ideal than to give practical directions for bringing it about.” The Buddhist tradition had spent two thousand years on exactly those practical directions. The basic instruction in breathing meditation is what James describes: notice that the mind has wandered, and bring it back, over and over again.

威廉・詹姆斯在《心理學原理》談注意力的那一章裡，寫了兩段讀起來像是在描述正念的話。「我的經驗，是我同意去注意的東西。只有我注意到的那些東西，才塑造我的心。」稍後又說：「自願地把游走的注意力一次又一次拉回來的能力，是判斷、品格與意志的根。沒有它，沒有人能自主。」接著他承認了一件事：「一種能增進這種能力的教育，會是最上乘的教育。但界定這個理想，比給出實現它的實際指導容易。」佛教傳統花了兩千年，正是在做這些實際的指導。數息觀的基本指示，就是詹姆斯所描述的：注意到心游走了，然後把它拉回來，一次又一次。

Philosophy as a way of life · 哲學作為生活方式

Pierre Hadot 皮耶・阿多1981

The French historian Pierre Hadot argued that ancient philosophy was not mainly a set of theories. It was a way of life, sustained by what he called spiritual exercises: attention to the present moment, the examination of conscience, the view from above, the rehearsal of misfortune, the memorizing of key maxims to have them “at hand.” In his reading, attention to oneself was the fundamental Stoic attitude, and the doctrines served the practice and were not its goal. Hadot noticed the parallel with Buddhist meditation and wrote about it with interest and caution. His work matters here for a different reason. It shows that the Western tradition once had a practice of attention as serious as the Buddhist one, and that modern academic philosophy forgot it. A Westerner who takes up mindfulness is in one sense recovering something from their own past, and Hadot is the best guide to what that was.

法國的哲學史家皮耶・阿多主張：古代哲學主要不是一套理論。它是一種生活方式，靠他所謂的「精神修練」來維持：注意當下、省察良心、從高處俯看、預演不幸、背誦關鍵的格言，好讓它們「隨手可得」。依他的解讀，對自己的注意，是斯多噶的基本態度；學說是為修行服務的，不是修行的目標。阿多注意到了它與佛教禪修的相似，並且帶著興趣與謹慎寫到它。他的研究在這裡重要，是因為另一個理由：它顯示西方傳統曾經有過跟佛教一樣認真的注意力修行，而現代學院哲學把它忘了。一個開始修正念的西方人，在某個意義上是在找回自己過去的一樣東西；而阿多是了解那是什麼的最好嚮導。

4 · Verdict · 判定

Who is closest, and where the likeness ends 誰最接近，相似到哪裡為止

The closest is Epictetus. Prosochē and sati are both disciplines of constant attention, both are practiced in ordinary activity, and both aim at the gap between an event and our response to it. Hadot showed that this attention was the center of Stoic practice, as sati is the center of Buddhist practice. William James is closest on the psychology. He saw that the power to return a wandering mind is the root of character and that training it would be the highest education, and he could not say how to train it.

最接近的是愛比克泰德。prosochē 與 sati 都是持續注意的紀律；都在平常的活動中修；都瞄準事件與我們對它的反應之間的那個空隙。阿多顯示：這種注意是斯多噶修行的中心，就像念是佛教修行的中心。在心理學上，最接近的是威廉・詹姆斯。他看出把游走的心拉回來的能力，是品格的根；訓練它，會是最高的教育；而他說不出怎麼訓練它。

The likeness ends at what attention is for. The Stoic watches in order to judge rightly. The ruling reason inspects each impression and assents only to what is true and in its power, and the self that does this is strengthened by the practice. The Buddhist watches body, feeling, mind, and phenomena until they are seen to arise and pass, and the watcher is included. The practice does not strengthen a ruler. It shows that there was never one. The Stoic ideal is a mind in command of itself, and the Buddhist ideal is a mind no longer driven by craving, which is a different thing. The practices can look the same for years, and they lead in different directions.

相似之處，停在注意力是為了什麼。斯多噶的人觀察，是為了正確地判斷：那個主宰的理性檢查每一個印象，只認可真的、力所能及的東西；而做這件事的那個自我，因修行而被加強。佛教的人觀察身、受、心、法，直到它們被看見是生起又消逝的；觀察者也包括在內。這種修行不加強一個主宰者，而是顯示從來就沒有一個主宰者。斯多噶的理想，是一顆能掌控自己的心；佛教的理想，是一顆不再被貪愛驅動的心；這是兩回事。這兩種修行可能好幾年看起來都一樣，而它們通往不同的方向。

5 · The step the Dharma takes further · 佛法多走的一步

The watcher watched, and the acrobats 被觀察的觀察者，以及雜技師

The first further step is to watch the watcher. Epictetus, James, and Hadot all assume someone who attends, a reason, a will, or a self in formation, and the practice strengthens that someone. The fourth foundation of mindfulness turns attention on the elements of experience themselves, including the mind that is attending. The meditator who notices that “the mind has wandered” can also notice that the noticing arises and passes. This is the point at which mindfulness becomes insight. Nothing in the Western practices of attention requires it, and most Western philosophers would not know what to do with it. It is also where a teacher should be most careful, because the claim can only be checked in practice, the way the means of knowledge lesson described.

多走的第一步，是觀察那個觀察者。愛比克泰德、詹姆斯與阿多，都預設了一個在注意的人：一個理性、一個意志，或一個正在形成的自我；而修行加強了那個人。四念處的第四處，把注意轉向經驗的元素本身，包括那顆正在注意的心。注意到「心游走了」的禪修者，也可以注意到這個注意本身生起又消逝。正是在這一點上，正念變成了觀（內觀、智慧）。西方的注意力修行沒有任何東西要求這一步；而大多數西方哲學家也不知道該拿它怎麼辦。這裡也是老師最應該小心的地方，因為這個主張只能在修行中查核，就像講「量論」的那一課所描述的。

The second step is the acrobats. Modern secular mindfulness programs have brought sati to millions of people, and much good has come of it. They usually present it as a technique for reducing stress, with the ethics removed, and critics inside and outside Buddhism have asked whether a soldier or a trader can use it to do harm more calmly. The suttas answer in advance. Mindfulness is right mindfulness only within the path, alongside right intention and right action. And the acrobat sutta says that looking after yourself is looking after others, and looking after others means “acceptance, harmlessness, love, and sympathy.” A teacher presenting mindfulness to Westerners can honor the secular programs and still say what they leave out. In the original teaching, attention is for liberation, and it includes care for others.

第二步是雜技師。現代世俗的正念課程，把 sati 帶給了數以百萬計的人；而且帶來了許多好處。它們通常把它當作一種減壓的技術來介紹，拿掉了倫理；佛教內外的批評者都問過：一個士兵或一個交易員，能不能用它更冷靜地去造成傷害？經典事先就回答了。正念只有在道路之中，與正思惟、正業並行，才是「正」念。而雜技師經說：照顧自己就是照顧他人；照顧他人的意思是「忍耐、不害、慈心與悲憫」。一位向西方人介紹正念的老師，可以尊重那些世俗課程，同時說出它們遺漏了什麼。在原本的教法裡，注意力是為了解脫，而且它包括對他人的關照。

6 · How to say it to a Western audience · 對西方人怎麼講

A bowl of oil and two acrobats 一缽油與兩位雜技師

Most Westerners have met mindfulness already, through an app, a workplace course, or a therapist. Do not start by correcting them. Start with the bowl of oil, which shows how alert and how calm real attention is. Then read William James’ sentence about bringing back a wandering attention, and point out that he wanted exactly the training that meditation provides. Mention Epictetus to show that the West once had its own practice of attention. Then make the two further points. Watching closely enough shows that the watcher is also a process. And the acrobats show that attention is meant to include care for others. Close with the basic instruction, because the audience can try it before they leave: notice the breath, notice when the mind wanders, and bring it back.

大多數西方人已經接觸過正念了：透過一個應用程式、一門職場課程，或一位治療師。不要從糾正他們開始。從那缽油開始，它顯示真正的注意力有多警覺、又有多平靜。然後讀威廉・詹姆斯那句把游走的注意力拉回來的話，並指出：他想要的，正是禪修所提供的訓練。提一下愛比克泰德，顯示西方也曾有過自己的注意力修行。然後講兩個多走的重點：觀察得夠仔細，就會看見觀察者也是一個過程；而雜技師顯示，注意力本來就包括對他人的關照。最後用基本的指示作結，因為聽眾在離開之前就能試：注意呼吸；注意心什麼時候游走；把它拉回來。

Sentences you can use 可以直接用的句子

  1. The Pali word for mindfulness, sati, originally meant memory: keeping something in mind.

    正念的巴利文 sati，原本的意思是記憶：把某樣東西記在心裡。

  2. The Buddha compared it to carrying a full bowl of oil through a crowd without spilling a drop.

    佛陀把它比作端著一缽滿滿的油穿過人群，一滴都不灑出來。

  3. William James said that bringing back a wandering attention is “the very root of judgment, character, and will.”

    威廉・詹姆斯說，把游走的注意力拉回來，是「判斷、品格與意志的根」。

  4. The Stoics practiced attention too, to keep reason in charge. Buddhists practice it until they see that no one is in charge.

    斯多噶學派也修注意力，為的是讓理性掌管。佛教徒修它，直到看見沒有任何人在掌管。

  5. Looking after yourself, you look after others; looking after others, you look after yourself.

    照顧自己，就是照顧他人；照顧他人，就是照顧自己。

  6. Try it now: notice the breath, notice when the mind wanders, and bring it back.

    現在就試試：注意呼吸；注意心什麼時候游走；把它拉回來。

Phrases to avoid 要避開的說法

“Mindfulness means emptying your mind.” 「正念就是把心清空。」

It means knowing clearly what is present, including thoughts.

它的意思是清楚地知道當下有什麼，包括念頭。

“Mindfulness is just a stress-reduction technique.” 「正念只是一種減壓的技術。」

In the suttas it is part of a path with ethics and wisdom.

在經典裡，它是一條包括戒與慧的道路的一部分。

“The Stoics and the Buddha taught the same thing.” 「斯多噶學派與佛陀教的是同一件事。」

The practices look alike. The aims differ: a ruling reason, or no ruler at all.

修行看起來相像，目的卻不同：一個主宰的理性，或根本沒有主宰。

“Mindfulness is a private, inward thing.” 「正念是一件私人的、向內的事。」

The acrobat sutta says that looking after yourself is looking after others.

雜技師經說：照顧自己，就是照顧他人。

Write · 你怎麼講？

  1. For one day, choose an ordinary activity such as walking to class or washing dishes, and do it the way the sutta says: walking, know walking. Write about what you noticed. About 150 words.

    選一天，挑一件平常的活動，例如走路去上課或洗碗；照經典說的方式做：行則知行。寫下你注意到的。約 150 個字。

  2. Epictetus asks, “Is there any part of life excepted, to which attention does not extend?” Name one part of your life where your attention usually lapses, and say what it costs.

    愛比克泰德問：「生活中有沒有哪一部分，是注意力不及的？」說出你生活中注意力通常會鬆懈的一個部分，並說說它讓你付出了什麼代價。

  3. Should mindfulness be taught without ethics? In 200 words, answer using the acrobat sutta and the criticisms of secular programs.

    正念應該脫離倫理來教嗎？用 200 個字回答，用上雜技師經以及對世俗課程的批評。
