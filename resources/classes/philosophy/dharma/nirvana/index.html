D22 · Dharma and the West 佛法與西方哲學

Nirvana and the Highest Good 涅槃與至善

“The ending of greed, hate, and delusion is called extinguishment.” Is nirvana a state of bliss, a kind of death, or a place? Sāriputta said it is bliss “precisely” because nothing is felt, and he rebuked a monk who thought the awakened are annihilated. This lesson sets nirvana beside Epicurus’ freedom from disturbance, Aristotle’s life of contemplation, and Spinoza’s blessedness, and asks why the Buddhist goal fits none of the Western categories.
「貪欲永盡，瞋恚永盡，愚癡永盡，一切諸煩惱永盡，是名涅槃。」涅槃是一種極樂的狀態、一種死亡，還是一個地方？舍利弗說，涅槃之所以是樂，「正因為」沒有任何感受；他也糾正了一位以為覺者死後斷滅的比丘。這一課把涅槃，跟伊比鳩魯的「心無紛擾」、亞里斯多德的沉思生活，以及史賓諾莎的「至福」並列，並且問：為什麼佛教的目標，放不進任何一個西方的類別？

The verdict · 判定

Closest: Epicurus, for whom the goal is “the absence of pain in the body and of trouble in the soul,” reached by removing false beliefs, and who taught that a right understanding takes away “the yearning after immortality.” Nirvana is also defined by what has ended, and it also cures the craving for more existence. The likeness ends at what remains. For Epicurus death is simply the end of sentience, and a realized one, the suttas say, cannot be described as annihilated. For Aristotle and Spinoza the highest good is the best activity of a lasting mind. Nirvana is not the activity of a self at all. It is the ending of the fires that made a self seem necessary.

最接近的是伊比鳩魯：對他來說，目標是「身體沒有痛苦，靈魂沒有紛擾」，靠除去錯誤的信念而達到；他也教導，正確的理解會拿走「對不朽的渴望」。涅槃同樣是由「已經結束了什麼」來界定的；它同樣治好了對更多存在的渴求。相似之處，停在剩下什麼。對伊比鳩魯來說，死亡就是感覺的終止；而經典說，一位如來不能被描述為斷滅。對亞里斯多德與史賓諾莎來說，至善是一顆持久的心最好的活動。涅槃根本不是一個自我的活動。它是那些使自我看起來不可或缺的火的熄滅。

1 · What the Dharma says · 佛法怎麼說

The fires go out 火熄滅了

The word nibbāna means the going out of a fire, and the suttas define it by what ends. A wanderer asks Sāriputta, “What is extinguishment?” In Bhikkhu Sujato’s translation the answer is: “Reverend, the ending of greed, hate, and delusion is called extinguishment” (Saṁyutta Nikāya 38.1). The Chinese Connected Discourses give the same exchange: “Nirvana is the permanent ending of greed, the permanent ending of hatred, the permanent ending of delusion, the permanent ending of all defilements.” And is there a path to it? “There is: the noble eightfold path.” The goal is not a reward handed out at the end of a road. It is the condition of a mind in which three fires have gone out.

nibbāna 這個字的意思，是火的熄滅；經典用「什麼結束了」來界定它。一位遊方者問舍利弗：「什麼是涅槃？」《雜阿含經》的回答是：「涅槃者，貪欲永盡，瞋恚永盡，愚癡永盡，一切諸煩惱永盡，是名涅槃。」巴利文的對應經（《相應部》38.1，Sujato 英譯）也說：貪、瞋、癡的結束，就叫做涅槃。那有沒有通往它的道路？「有。謂八正道。」這個目標不是走到路的盡頭時被頒發的獎賞。它是一顆心的狀態：在其中，三把火已經熄滅了。

Is it happiness? “Extinguishment is the ultimate happiness,” says the Dhammapada. But when Sāriputta exclaims, “Reverends, extinguishment is bliss!” a monk objects: “What’s blissful about it, since nothing is felt?” Sāriputta answers, “The fact that nothing is felt is precisely what’s blissful about it” (Aṅguttara Nikāya 9.34). The happiness of nirvana is the end of being driven, and it is not a pleasant feeling of a higher kind. The Itivuttaka distinguishes two aspects. In a living arahant, “their five sense faculties still remain” and they still feel pleasure and pain, but greed, hate, and delusion have ended. That is “extinguishment with residue.” At death, “everything that’s felt, being no longer relished, will become cool right here,” which is “extinguishment with no residue” (Itivuttaka 44).

它是快樂嗎？《法句經》說，涅槃是「最上樂」。但當舍利弗讚歎「諸賢，涅槃是樂！」時，一位比丘反駁：「既然什麼都感受不到，它有什麼樂？」舍利弗回答：「正因為什麼都感受不到，所以它是樂。」（《增支部》9.34）涅槃的快樂，是不再被驅使的結束；它並不是一種更高級的愉快感受。《如是語》區分了兩個面向。在一位活著的阿羅漢身上，「五根仍在」，仍然感受樂與苦，但貪、瞋、癡已經結束了；這叫「有餘依涅槃」。死時，「一切所感受的，因為不再被喜愛，就在這裡變得清涼」，這叫「無餘依涅槃」（《如是語》44）。

Two misreadings appear in the suttas themselves. The first is that nirvana is annihilation. A monk named Yamaka held that a mendicant who has ended the defilements “is annihilated and destroyed when their body breaks up.” The other monks call this a “harmful misconception,” and Sāriputta shows him that even in this life a realized one cannot be found in or apart from the five aggregates (Saṁyutta Nikāya 22.85). If he cannot be located now, it makes no sense to say that something is destroyed later. The second misreading is that nirvana is nothing at all. The Udāna answers: “There is, mendicants, freedom from rebirth, freedom from what has been produced, made, and conditioned. If there were no freedom … you would find no escape here” (Udāna 8.3). Nirvana is real, and it is not a thing among things, a self that survives, or a heaven.

經典本身就出現了兩種誤讀。第一種是：涅槃就是斷滅。一位叫閻摩迦的比丘主張：漏盡的比丘「身壞命終之後就斷滅、消失」。其他比丘稱之為「惡見」；舍利弗則讓他看見：即使在今生，也無法在五蘊之中或五蘊之外找到一位如來（《相應部》22.85）。如果現在都無法定位他，說以後有某樣東西被毀滅了，就沒有道理。第二種誤讀是：涅槃什麼也不是。《自說經》回答（依 Sujato 英譯）：「比丘們，有一個從再生、從被產生、被造作、被條件決定的東西之中的解脫。如果沒有這個解脫……你在這裡就找不到出離。」（《自說》8.3）涅槃是真實的；而它不是萬物之中的一物，不是一個存續的自我，也不是一個天堂。

3 · The nearest Western views · 西方最接近的說法

Tranquility, contemplation, blessedness 寧靜、沉思、至福

The goal defined by absence · 以「沒有」來界定的目標

Epicurus 伊比鳩魯c. 300 BCE

Epicurus defined the highest good negatively, as nirvana is defined. In R. D. Hicks’ translation of the Letter to Menoeceus: “By pleasure we mean the absence of pain in the body and of trouble in the soul.” It is reached not by indulgence but by “sober reasoning, searching out the grounds of every choice and avoidance, and banishing those beliefs through which the greatest tumults take possession of the soul.” Most striking is his teaching on death. “Accustom thyself to believe that death is nothing to us,” because death is the end of sentience, and so “a right understanding that death is nothing to us makes the mortality of life enjoyable, not by adding to life an illimitable time, but by taking away the yearning after immortality.” The Buddha also cured the craving for continued existence. The difference is the picture of death. For Epicurus death is simple nonexistence, and he offered this as comfort. The Buddha refused that description of a realized one, and the Yamaka sutta calls it a harmful misconception.

伊比鳩魯用否定的方式界定至善，就像涅槃被界定的方式一樣。R. D. Hicks 所譯的〈致梅諾寇斯的信〉說：「我們所說的快樂，是身體沒有痛苦，靈魂沒有紛擾。」達到它的方法不是放縱，而是「清醒的推理：探究每一個取捨的根據，並驅除那些使靈魂陷入最大騷亂的信念」。最驚人的是他關於死亡的教導：「要讓自己習慣相信：死亡對我們來說什麼都不是」，因為死亡是感覺的終止；所以「正確地理解死亡對我們什麼都不是，會使生命的有限變得可以享受；不是藉著給生命加上無限的時間，而是藉著拿走對不朽的渴望」。佛陀也治好了對持續存在的渴求。差別在於死亡的圖像。對伊比鳩魯來說，死亡就是單純的不存在，而他把這當作安慰。佛陀拒絕用這種方式描述一位如來；閻摩迦經稱之為惡見。

The highest activity · 最高的活動

Aristotle 亞里斯多德c. 330 BCE

For Aristotle the highest good is eudaimonia, and in the last book of the Nicomachean Ethics he identifies its best form with contemplation. In W. D. Ross’s translation: “If happiness is activity in accordance with virtue, it is reasonable that it should be in accordance with the highest virtue; and this will be that of the best thing in us.” Contemplation is the most self-sufficient activity, since “the philosopher, even when by himself, can contemplate truth.” Then comes a famous exhortation: “we must, so far as we can, make ourselves immortal, and strain every nerve to live in accordance with the best thing in us.” The Buddhist and the Aristotelian ideals share the value of self-sufficiency, a life that does not depend on fortune, and both place it in the mind. They point in opposite directions, though. Aristotle’s happiness is the fullest activity of the best part of a person, a self raised to its highest power. The Buddha’s goal is the end of the fires, and it is not the perfection of a faculty. Aristotle asks us to make ourselves immortal, while Epicurus and the Buddha both ask us to drop the yearning.

對亞里斯多德來說，至善是 eudaimonia（幸福、人的興盛）；在《尼各馬可倫理學》的最後一卷，他把它最好的形式等同於沉思。W. D. Ross 的英譯是：「如果幸福是合乎德行的活動，那麼它合乎最高的德行，是合理的；而那將是我們之中最好的東西的德行。」沉思是最自足的活動，因為「哲學家即使獨自一人，也能沉思真理」。接著是一段有名的勸勉：「我們必須盡我們所能，使自己不朽，並竭盡全力，按照我們之中最好的東西去生活。」佛教與亞里斯多德的理想，共享「自足」這個價值，一種不依賴運氣的生活；兩者也都把它放在心裡。然而它們指向相反的方向。亞里斯多德的幸福，是一個人最好的部分最充分的活動，一個被提升到最高能力的自我。佛陀的目標是火的熄滅，不是一種能力的完成。亞里斯多德要我們使自己不朽；伊比鳩魯與佛陀都要我們放下那份渴望。

Blessedness is virtue itself · 至福就是德行本身

Baruch Spinoza 史賓諾莎1677

Spinoza’s Ethics ends with an account of the highest good that resembles nirvana in several ways. In R. H. M. Elwes’ translation: “Blessedness is not the reward of virtue, but virtue itself; neither do we rejoice therein, because we control our lusts, but, contrariwise, because we rejoice therein, we are able to control our lusts.” The wise man “is scarcely at all disturbed in spirit” and “always possesses true acquiescence of his spirit,” and the way there is understanding. The ignorant man, by contrast, “is distracted in various ways by external causes.” Then the famous last line: “all things excellent are as difficult as they are rare.” Like nirvana, Spinoza’s blessedness is not a prize for good behavior, and it comes through seeing things as they are. The difference lies in what is seen. Spinoza’s wise man understands himself and all things as part of God, and his blessedness is “love towards God.” The Buddhist sees that nothing, including the self, has a fixed nature to be loved or held.

史賓諾莎的《倫理學》以一段關於至善的說明作結；它在好幾個方面像涅槃。R. H. M. Elwes 的英譯是：「至福不是德行的報酬，而是德行本身；我們不是因為控制了情慾才享有它，相反地，正因為我們享有它，我們才能控制情慾。」智者「心靈幾乎不受擾動」，「總是擁有心靈真正的安寧」；而到那裡的道路是理解。相對地，無知的人「被外在的原因以各種方式牽引分散」。然後是那句有名的結語：「一切美好的事物，都既困難又稀少。」跟涅槃一樣，史賓諾莎的至福不是善行的獎品，而是透過如實地看見事物而來。差別在於看見了什麼。史賓諾莎的智者把自己與萬物理解為上帝的一部分；他的至福是「對上帝的愛」。佛教徒看見的是：沒有任何東西，包括自我，有一個固定的本性可以去愛或執持。

4 · Verdict · 判定

Who is closest, and where the likeness ends 誰最接近，相似到哪裡為止

The closest is Epicurus. His goal is defined by absence, the absence of pain and trouble, as nirvana is defined by the ending of three fires. He reaches it by removing false beliefs, and he treats the craving for immortality as a disease to be cured. Spinoza is closest on the relation between the goal and the path, since blessedness is “not the reward of virtue, but virtue itself,” and it comes through understanding. Aristotle shares the ideal of a self-sufficient life of the mind and points it the other way, toward an activity that makes us as immortal as we can be.

最接近的是伊比鳩魯。他的目標由「沒有」來界定，沒有痛苦、沒有紛擾，就像涅槃由三把火的熄滅來界定。他靠除去錯誤的信念到達那裡；他也把對不朽的渴求，當作一種要被治好的病。在目標與道路的關係上，最接近的是史賓諾莎：至福「不是德行的報酬，而是德行本身」，而且是透過理解而來。亞里斯多德分享了「自足的心靈生活」這個理想，卻把它指向另一個方向：朝向一種使我們盡可能不朽的活動。

The likeness ends at what remains. Each Western view needs a subject to enjoy the good: Epicurus’ untroubled soul, Aristotle’s contemplating intellect, Spinoza’s mind that loves God. The Buddhist goal has no such subject. Nirvana is not something the self gets, and the suttas will not describe it as the self’s survival or its destruction. This is why Western readers have alternately called nirvana nihilism and mysticism, and why both labels miss. Nāgārjuna pressed the point furthest: “Between nirvana and the world there is not the slightest difference.” Nirvana is not another place. It is this same world, seen and lived without the fires.

相似之處停在剩下什麼。每一個西方的觀點，都需要一個主體來享受這個善：伊比鳩魯的那個不受紛擾的靈魂、亞里斯多德的那個沉思的理智、史賓諾莎的那顆愛上帝的心。佛教的目標沒有這樣的主體。涅槃不是自我得到的東西；經典也不肯把它描述為自我的存續或毀滅。這就是為什麼西方讀者時而稱涅槃為虛無主義，時而稱之為神祕主義；而兩個標籤都沒有說中。龍樹把這一點推得最遠：「涅槃與世間，無有少分別；世間與涅槃，亦無少分別。」涅槃不是另一個地方。它就是這同一個世界，在沒有那些火的情況下被看見、被生活。

5 · The step the Dharma takes further · 佛法多走的一步

Not a self’s reward, and not elsewhere 不是自我的報酬，也不在別處

The first further step is to take the self out of the highest good. Western ethics, from Aristotle to Spinoza, asks what the best life is for a person, and assumes there is a person to whom the good belongs. The Buddha asked what remains when the fires that build the sense of a person go out, and he declined to name it in terms of a self. Sāriputta’s answer to Yamaka is the key. Even now, a realized one cannot be found in or apart from the five aggregates. So “annihilated after death” and “survives after death” both mistake what was there to begin with. This is hard for Western listeners, and it should not be made easier by calling nirvana a state of the soul or a void. The honest statement is that the tradition describes it by what ends, by its peace, and by its reality, and declines to describe it as a possession.

多走的第一步，是把自我從至善中拿出來。西方倫理學，從亞里斯多德到史賓諾莎，都問：對一個人來說，最好的生活是什麼？並預設有一個人，善屬於他。佛陀問的是：當那些建構「人」之感的火熄滅時，剩下什麼？而他拒絕用自我的語言為它命名。舍利弗對閻摩迦的回答是關鍵：即使是現在，也無法在五蘊之中或之外找到一位如來。所以「死後斷滅」與「死後存續」，都誤解了一開始在那裡的是什麼。這對西方聽眾來說很難；而不應該靠著把涅槃稱為靈魂的一種狀態或一片虛空，來讓它變簡單。誠實的說法是：傳統用「什麼結束了」、用它的寂靜、用它的真實來描述它，而拒絕把它描述為一樣擁有之物。

The second step is that nirvana is not elsewhere. Popular Western images, and some popular Buddhist ones, picture nirvana as a distant heaven or a final exit. The suttas already say that the arahant with residue lives in the world, still seeing and hearing, with the fires out. Nāgārjuna went further in the Middle Treatise. “Between nirvana and the world there is not the slightest difference,” and “the limit of nirvana and the limit of the world: between these two there is not the slightest hair’s breadth of difference.” The commentary explains that because all things are without arising and ceasing, the world and nirvana cannot be told apart. This is where Mahāyāna teaching on the bodhisattva, who stays in the world out of compassion, finds its ground. For a teacher, the practical point is that nirvana is described as a change in how one lives here, and not as a journey to somewhere else.

第二步是：涅槃不在別處。西方流行的意象，以及一些流行的佛教意象，把涅槃想像成一個遙遠的天堂，或一個最後的出口。經典已經說：有餘依的阿羅漢活在世間，仍然看、仍然聽，而火已經熄了。龍樹在《中論》裡走得更遠：「涅槃與世間，無有少分別」；「涅槃之實際，及與世間際，如是二際者，無毫釐差別」。注釋解釋說：「以一切法不生不滅故，世間與涅槃無有分別。」大乘關於菩薩的教法（菩薩因為慈悲而留在世間），就在這裡找到它的根據。對一位老師來說，實際的重點是：涅槃被描述為一個人在這裡怎麼生活的轉變，而不是一趟去別處的旅程。

6 · How to say it to a Western audience · 對西方人怎麼講

A fire going out 一把火熄滅

Westerners usually meet “nirvana” as a word for bliss, or as a rock band, or as a polite name for oblivion. Begin with the word itself: it means a fire going out, and the fires are greed, hatred, and delusion. Then give Sāriputta’s two answers. Asked what nirvana is, he said the ending of those three. Asked how it can be bliss when nothing is felt, he said that is exactly why. For people who know some philosophy, Epicurus is the best bridge, because he too defined the goal as an absence and wanted to cure the yearning for immortality. Then name the difference plainly. The Buddha would not call the awakened annihilated, and he would not call them a surviving soul. End with Nāgārjuna’s line, because it keeps nirvana from sounding like an escape.

西方人通常是把「nirvana」當作「極樂」的同義詞、一個搖滾樂團的名字，或者「湮滅」的委婉說法來認識的。從這個字本身開始：它的意思是一把火熄滅；而那些火是貪、瞋、癡。然後給出舍利弗的兩個回答。被問到涅槃是什麼，他說是那三者的結束。被問到既然沒有感受、它怎麼會是樂，他說正因為如此。對懂一點哲學的人來說，伊比鳩魯是最好的橋，因為他也把目標界定為一種「沒有」，也想治好對不朽的渴望。然後直接說出差別：佛陀不肯說覺者斷滅了，也不肯說他們是一個存續的靈魂。最後用龍樹的那句話作結，因為它使涅槃聽起來不像一種逃離。

Sentences you can use 可以直接用的句子

  1. Nirvana means a fire going out. The fires are greed, hatred, and delusion.

    涅槃的意思是一把火熄滅。那些火是貪、瞋、癡。

  2. Sāriputta said, “The ending of greed, hate, and delusion is called extinguishment.”

    舍利弗說：「貪、瞋、癡的結束，就叫做涅槃。」

  3. Asked how it could be bliss when nothing is felt, he said that was exactly why.

    被問到既然沒有感受，它怎麼會是樂，他說正因為如此。

  4. Epicurus also defined the goal by absence: no pain in the body, no trouble in the soul.

    伊比鳩魯也用「沒有」來界定目標：身體沒有痛苦，靈魂沒有紛擾。

  5. The Buddha would not say that the awakened are annihilated, or that they survive as a soul.

    佛陀不肯說覺者斷滅了，也不肯說他們以一個靈魂的形式存續。

  6. Nāgārjuna said there is not the slightest difference between nirvana and the world.

    龍樹說：涅槃與世間，無有少分別。

Phrases to avoid 要避開的說法

“Nirvana is a kind of heaven.” 「涅槃是一種天堂。」

It is the ending of the three fires, and it is not a place.

它是三把火的熄滅，不是一個地方。

“Nirvana means you cease to exist.” 「涅槃的意思是你不再存在。」

The suttas call that a harmful misconception (Saṁyutta Nikāya 22.85).

經典稱之為惡見（《相應部》22.85）。

“Nirvana is the ultimate pleasant feeling.” 「涅槃是最極致的愉快感受。」

Sāriputta said it is bliss because nothing is felt.

舍利弗說它是樂，正因為沒有感受。

“Nirvana is just Epicurean tranquility.” 「涅槃不過是伊比鳩魯式的寧靜。」

Epicurus needs a soul to be untroubled. Nirvana has no such subject.

伊比鳩魯需要一個不受紛擾的靈魂；涅槃沒有這樣的主體。

Reading check · 理解測驗

Did it land? 讀懂了嗎

1. How does Sāriputta define nirvana in Saṁyutta Nikāya 38.1?

2. Why does Sāriputta say nirvana is bliss?

3. What view does Yamaka hold, and how is it judged?

4. Which Western thinker is closest, according to the lesson?

5. What does Nāgārjuna say about nirvana and the world?

Write · 你怎麼講？

  1. Recall a moment when a strong craving or anger simply ended. What was it like afterward? Describe it in terms of what was absent. About 150 words.

    回想一個強烈的貪求或怒氣就這樣結束了的時刻。之後是什麼樣子？用「什麼不在了」來描述它。約 150 個字。

  2. Compare Epicurus’ “death is nothing to us” with the Yamaka sutta. Why does the Buddha refuse Epicurus’ picture of death?

    比較伊比鳩魯的「死亡對我們什麼都不是」與閻摩迦經。佛陀為什麼拒絕伊比鳩魯對死亡的圖像？

  3. Aristotle says we should make ourselves immortal; Epicurus says we should drop the yearning for immortality. In 200 words, say where the Buddha stands and why.

    亞里斯多德說我們應該使自己不朽；伊比鳩魯說我們應該放下對不朽的渴望。用 200 個字說明：佛陀站在哪裡，為什麼。
