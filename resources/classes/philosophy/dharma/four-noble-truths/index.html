D7 · Dharma and the West 佛法與西方哲學

The Four Noble Truths as an Argument 四聖諦作為一個論證

Illness, cause, recovery, treatment. The Buddha called himself a surgeon and set out his teaching as a physician sets out a case. The Greeks and Romans said the same of philosophy. Whether the four truths are a creed, a prescription, or an argument, who in the West practiced philosophy as medicine, and what kind of truth a truth is that comes with a task.
病、病因、康復、療法。佛陀自稱外科醫師，並且像醫生陳述一個病例那樣陳述他的教法。希臘人與羅馬人對哲學也說過同樣的話。四聖諦是信條、處方，還是論證？西方有誰把哲學當作醫療來做？而一個附帶著一項任務的「諦」，又是哪一種真理？

四聖諦
cattāri ariyasaccāni Pāli　catvāri āryasatyāni Sanskritthe four noble truths
The verdict · 判定

Closest: the Hellenistic schools as a group, and Epicurus most of all, who said that a philosopher’s argument is empty if it heals no suffering and who summed up his teaching as a fourfold remedy. The likeness ends at what the medicine is. For the Greeks the cure is a set of correct beliefs, held firmly. For the Buddha each truth carries a task, and the fourth is a training of conduct and attention that does its work whether or not it is believed in advance.

最接近的：希臘化時期的各個學派整體；其中又以伊比鳩魯為最。他說過，一位哲學家的論證如果治不了任何苦，就是空的；並且把自己的教導總結成「四重藥方」。相似到「那帖藥是什麼」為止。對希臘人來說，療法是一組牢牢持有的正確信念。對佛陀來說，每一個諦都帶著一項任務；而第四個諦是對行為與注意力的訓練：不管事先信不信，它都起作用。

1 · What the Dharma says · 佛法怎麼說

A physician states a case 一位醫生陳述一個病例

The four truths were the content of the Buddha’s first discourse: there is suffering; it has an origin, which is craving; it has a cessation, which is the ending of that craving; and there is a practice that leads to the cessation, the eightfold path. The order is that of a medical case. A discourse preserved in the Chinese canon makes the comparison explicit. A great physician, the Buddha says there, has four skills. He knows the illness well, knows its source well, knows its remedy well, and knows how to treat it so that it does not return. “The Tathāgata, the great king of physicians, has four qualities and heals the illness of living beings in the same way,” and the four qualities are his knowledge of the four truths as they are.

四聖諦是佛陀第一次說法的內容：有苦；它有起因，就是渴愛；它有止息，就是那個渴愛的滅盡；而且有一種通向止息的修行，就是八正道。這個順序，是一份病例的順序。漢譯經藏裡保存的一部經，把這個比擬明白地說了出來。佛陀在那裡說：大醫王成就四法：「一者善知病，二者善知病源，三者善知病對治，四者善知治病已，當來更不動發。」「如來、應、等正覺為大醫王，成就四德，療眾生病，亦復如是」；而那四德，就是他對四聖諦的如實知。

The Pali discourses use the same language. “I, mendicants, am a brahmin, committed to charity, always open-handed, bearing my final body, a healer, a surgeon.” In another place the Buddha describes a surgeon who cuts open a wound, probes for the arrow, draws it out, and then tells the patient how to care for the wound so that it heals. He remarks elsewhere that there are three kinds of patients: those who will recover whatever is done, those who will not recover whatever is done, and those who will recover only if they are given suitable food, medicine, and care. It is for the sake of the third kind, he says, that medicine is prescribed. The teaching is addressed to people for whom it can make the difference.

巴利經典用的是同樣的語言。「比丘們，我是婆羅門：樂於布施、常開著手、持最後身；是醫者，是外科醫師。」在另一處，佛陀描述了一位外科醫師：他切開傷口，探到箭，把它拔出來；然後告訴病人該怎麼照顧傷口，使它癒合。他在別處提到：病人有三種。有的不管怎麼做都會好；有的不管怎麼做都不會好；有的只有在得到合適的飲食、藥物與照顧時才會好。他說：藥，是為了第三種人而開的。這個教法，是對那些「它能夠造成差別」的人說的。

The first discourse adds something that the medical comparison could not supply by itself. Each truth comes with a task. The truth of suffering “should be completely understood.” The origin “should be given up.” The cessation “should be realized.” The practice “should be developed.” The Buddha says that he did not claim to be awakened until he had seen each truth, seen its task, and seen that the task was done, twelve aspects in all. A truth here is something to be acted on in a specific way. It would make no sense to give up the first truth or to develop the second. This is the feature that most distinguishes the four truths from a creed, and Western summaries most often leave it out.

第一次說法還補上了一樣東西，是醫療的比擬本身提供不了的。每一個諦都附帶著一項任務。苦聖諦「應當被完全了知」。集「應當被斷除」。滅「應當被作證」。道「應當被修習」。佛陀說：他要到看見了每一個諦、看見了它的任務、並且看見那項任務已經完成（總共十二個面向，三轉十二行相），才宣稱自己覺悟。在這裡，一個「諦」是要以特定的方式去處理的東西。去「斷除」第一個諦，或者去「修習」第二個諦，都是說不通的。這是四聖諦最不同於信條的特點；而西方的摘要最常把它漏掉。

3 · The nearest Western views · 西方最接近的說法

Philosophy as medicine in the West 西方把哲學當作醫療的傳統

The fourfold remedy · 四重藥方

Epicurus 伊比鳩魯c. 300 BCE

A saying attributed to Epicurus puts the medical view of philosophy in its strongest form: the argument of a philosopher is empty if it heals no human suffering, as medicine is useless if it does not drive illness from the body. His followers summed up his teaching in four lines that they called the fourfold remedy: God is not to be feared, death is not to be felt, what is good is easy to get, and what is terrible is easy to endure. They memorized it, as one keeps a medicine at hand. The parallel with the four truths is close in spirit and in number. Both reject speculation that does not bear on the cure. The difference is in the ingredients. Each line of the Epicurean remedy is a belief that removes a fear, and the patient is cured when he holds all four securely. The four truths include one belief of that kind at most. The rest is diagnosis and regimen.

一句歸在伊比鳩魯名下的話，把「哲學是醫療」這個看法說到了最強的形式：一位哲學家的論證，如果治不了任何人類的苦，就是空的；正如醫術若不能把病從身體裡趕出去，就沒有用處。他的追隨者把他的教導總結成四行，稱為「四重藥方」：神不足懼；死不足憂；善易得；惡易忍。他們把它背起來，就像人把藥放在手邊。它與四聖諦的對應，在精神上與數目上都很接近。兩者都拒絕與治療無關的玄想。差別在於成分。伊比鳩魯藥方的每一行，都是一個能除去一種恐懼的信念；當病人把四個信念都牢牢持有的時候，他就痊癒了。四聖諦裡，那一類的信念頂多只有一個。其餘的是診斷與療程。

Medicine of the soul · 靈魂的醫藥

Cicero and the Stoics 西塞羅與斯多噶派45 BCE

The Stoics developed the comparison in most detail, and Cicero, who was not a Stoic, gave it its best-known wording while mourning his daughter. “Philosophy is certainly the medicine of the soul, whose assistance we do not seek from abroad, as in bodily disorders, but we ourselves are bound to exert our utmost energy and power in order to effect our cure.” The passions are diseases, on the Stoic account, and each consists of a false judgment about what is good or bad. Treatment is the correction of the judgment by argument and by daily exercises, such as rehearsing in the morning the troubles the day may bring. Cicero’s sentence could stand at the head of a Buddhist text: the patient must do the work. The Stoics located the illness in reason and trusted reason to cure itself. The Buddha traced it to thirst and ignorance, which argue well in their own defense, and his treatment begins with conduct and attention before it reaches views.

斯多噶派把這個比擬發展得最詳細；而西塞羅（他並不是斯多噶派）在哀悼女兒的時候，給了它最有名的措辭。「哲學確實是靈魂的醫藥；它的幫助，我們不像對身體的疾病那樣向外去求；而是我們自己必須竭盡全部的精力與力量，才能使自己痊癒。」照斯多噶派的說明，激情是疾病；而每一種激情，都是一個關於「什麼是好、什麼是壞」的錯誤判斷。治療，就是用論證、以及每天的練習來糾正那個判斷；例如早晨預先演練這一天可能帶來的麻煩。西塞羅的那句話，可以放在一部佛教典籍的開頭：病人必須自己下工夫。斯多噶派把病定位在理性裡，並且信任理性能治好自己。佛陀把病追溯到渴與無明；而這兩者很會為自己辯護。所以他的治療，是從行為與注意力開始的；然後才到見解。

Therapy for philosophy itself · 對哲學本身的治療

Ludwig Wittgenstein 維根斯坦1953

Wittgenstein revived the medical picture in the twentieth century and narrowed it. A philosophical problem, he held, is a confusion produced by language, and the philosopher treats a question as one treats an illness. The aim is to show the fly the way out of the bottle, after which the problem does not get solved. It disappears. He shares with the Buddha a suspicion of theories and a view of his own remarks as tools to be set down once they have done their work, which the Buddha said of his teaching in the simile of the raft. The patient is different. Wittgenstein’s therapy is for people who have been made ill by philosophy, and when it succeeds they return to ordinary life as it was. The Buddha thought that ordinary life was where the illness lay.

維根斯坦在二十世紀復興了這個醫療的圖像，並且把它收窄了。他主張：一個哲學問題，是語言所造成的一種混淆；而哲學家處理一個問題，就像治療一種疾病。目標是給蒼蠅指出飛出瓶子的路；在那之後，問題並沒有被解決。它消失了。他與佛陀共有的，是對理論的懷疑；以及把自己的話看成工具：一旦完成了工作，就該放下。佛陀在筏喻裡，對自己的教法說過同樣的話。病人卻不同。維根斯坦的治療，是給那些被哲學弄病了的人的；而當它成功的時候，他們回到原來那樣的日常生活。佛陀認為：病就在日常生活裡。

4 · Verdict · 判定

Who is closest, and where the likeness ends 誰最接近，相似到哪裡為止

On the purpose, which the first lesson on this shelf made the deciding level, the Hellenistic schools stand closer to the Buddha than any later Western philosophy does. Epicureans, Stoics, and Skeptics all held that philosophy exists to remove suffering, judged doctrines by whether they did so, and trained their students with exercises. For several centuries in the West, as in India, a philosophy was a way of life that one entered. Epicurus is closest among them, for his test of an argument and for the form of his remedy. This tradition lapsed in the West when philosophy became a university subject, and the French scholar Pierre Hadot spent his career recovering it.

在「目的」上（這個書架的第一課把它定為作決定的那一層），希臘化時期的各個學派，比後來任何的西方哲學都更接近佛陀。伊比鳩魯派、斯多噶派與懷疑派，都主張哲學的存在是為了除去苦；都以「做不做得到這一點」來評判學說；也都用各種練習來訓練學生。有好幾個世紀，在西方就像在印度一樣，一種哲學是一個人「進入」的生活方式。他們當中最接近的是伊比鳩魯：因為他檢驗論證的標準，也因為他那帖藥方的形式。當哲學成了大學裡的一門學科，這個傳統在西方就中斷了；法國學者皮耶・阿多用了一生的學術工作，把它找回來。

On the conclusion and the argument the likeness is partial, and it ends at the nature of the medicine. The Greek schools treated by changing what the patient believes. Their exercises served to fix correct opinions so firmly that they would be present in a crisis. The four truths are built differently. Only the second and third are propositions to be accepted, and even they are stated as things to be given up and realized. The fourth is eight kinds of training, in speech, action, livelihood, effort, attention, and concentration as well as view. A person can begin it with very little belief, and the tradition expects understanding to come from the practice. The Greek doctor says: come to see that death is nothing to you. The Buddhist doctor says: keep these precepts, watch the breath, and tell me what you find.

在結論與論證上，相似是部分的；而它停在「那帖藥的性質」上。希臘各學派的治法，是改變病人所相信的東西。他們的練習，是用來把正確的看法固定得那麼牢，使它們在危機的時候還在。四聖諦的構造不同。只有第二與第三個諦是要接受的命題；而就連它們，也是被陳述成「要斷除」與「要作證」的東西。第四個諦是八種訓練：除了見解之外，還有言語、行為、生計、努力、注意力與定。一個人可以帶著很少的信念就開始修它；而這個傳統期待理解是從修行裡來的。希臘的醫生說：你要看出，死亡與你無關。佛教的醫生說：守住這幾條戒，看著呼吸；然後告訴我你發現了什麼。

5 · The step the Dharma takes further · 佛法多走的一步

Truths with tasks 附帶任務的諦

The first further step is the one already described: each truth is paired with something to do. This changes what it means to accept the teaching. To accept a creed is to affirm its articles. To accept the four truths, on the Buddha’s own account, is to be engaged in understanding suffering, giving up its origin, realizing its end, and developing the path, and he did not count the truths as known until the tasks were complete. The word translated “noble truth” has been read in more than one way, and one old reading is “truths of the noble ones,” the things that are evident to those who have done the work. On that reading the four truths resemble a report from people who have recovered more than they resemble a set of doctrines for the sick to believe.

多走的第一步，就是前面已經描述過的那一步：每一個諦都配著一件要做的事。這改變了「接受這個教法」的意思。接受一份信條，是肯定它的各個條款。照佛陀自己的說明，接受四聖諦，是投入於了知苦、斷除它的起因、作證它的止息、修習那條道路；而他要到這些任務完成了，才算是「知道」了這些諦。譯作「聖諦」的那個詞，有不只一種讀法；其中一種古老的讀法是「聖者們的諦」：對那些做完了工夫的人來說顯而易見的事。照這種讀法，四聖諦比較像是康復者帶回來的報告，而比較不像一套要病人去相信的教義。

The second step concerns the scope of the cure. The Hellenistic schools offered a better life: calmer, freer, less afraid. The patient remained the person he was, with his judgment repaired. The third truth claims something more radical, a complete end of craving, and the lessons on not-self and impermanence have shown what that involves. When the treatment is finished, the one who came for it is no longer found in the form in which he arrived. This is the reason the Buddha was careful about whom he taught and in what order. He usually began, the texts say, with generosity and conduct, and gave the four truths when the listener’s mind was ready. A surgeon does not operate first and explain afterward. The simile of the three patients shows the same care from another side. The medicine is prescribed for those it can help, and it is offered to the others too, in case they turn out to be among them.

第二步關係到治療的範圍。希臘化時期的各個學派，提供的是一個更好的人生：更平靜、更自由、更少恐懼。病人還是原來的那個人；只是他的判斷被修好了。第三個諦主張的東西更徹底：渴愛的完全止息；而講無我與講無常的那兩課，已經顯示了那牽涉到什麼。治療結束的時候，前來求治的那個人，已經不再以他到來時的那個樣子被找到。這就是為什麼佛陀對「教誰、照什麼順序教」很謹慎。經文說，他通常從布施與持戒講起；等到聽者的心準備好了，才說四聖諦。外科醫師不會先動刀、事後再解釋。三種病人的譬喻，從另一面顯示了同樣的謹慎。藥，是為了它幫得上忙的人而開的；而它也提供給其他的人，以防他們其實也在其中。

6 · How to say it to a Western audience · 對西方人怎麼講

A doctor’s four questions 醫生的四個問題

Westerners usually meet the four truths as a list to be memorized, which makes them sound like commandments or a statement of faith. Present them as the four questions any doctor asks. What is wrong? What is causing it? Can it be cured? What is the treatment? Everyone has sat in a consulting room, and the frame carries three points without argument: the tone is practical and not gloomy, the third truth is the good news, and nothing happens unless the patient follows the treatment. Then add what the frame leaves out. Each truth has its own task, and one is asked to understand suffering before trying to get rid of it. Listeners who have tried to skip straight to the cure will recognize themselves.

西方人遇到四聖諦的時候，它通常是一張要背的清單；這使它聽起來像誡命，或者像一份信仰宣言。把它呈現成任何一位醫生都會問的四個問題。哪裡不對勁？是什麼造成的？治得好嗎？怎麼治？每個人都在診間裡坐過；而這個框架不必費唇舌，就帶出了三點：語氣是實際的，而不是陰鬱的；第三個諦是好消息；而且，除非病人照著療程去做，否則什麼也不會發生。然後補上這個框架漏掉的東西。每一個諦有它自己的任務；而人是被要求先「了知」苦，然後才試著去除它。那些試過直接跳到「治好」的聽眾，會認出自己來。

Sentences you can use 可以直接用的句子

  1. The four truths are a doctor’s four questions: what is wrong, what causes it, can it be cured, and how.

    四聖諦就是醫生的四個問題：哪裡不對勁？什麼造成的？治得好嗎？怎麼治？

  2. The third truth is the good news. The Buddha said that the illness has a cure.

    第三個諦是好消息。佛陀說：這個病有藥可醫。

  3. Each truth comes with a job. Suffering is to be understood, its cause given up, its end realized, the path practiced.

    每一個諦都附帶一件工作。苦要了知；它的起因要斷除；它的止息要作證；道要修習。

  4. The ancient Greeks also called philosophy medicine for the soul. The Buddha’s medicine is a training more than a belief.

    古希臘人也把哲學稱為靈魂的醫藥。佛陀的藥，比較是一種訓練，而不是一種信念。

  5. You are not asked to believe the fourth truth. You are asked to try it and see.

    並沒有人要求你相信第四個諦。是請你去試，然後自己看。

  6. We usually want to get rid of suffering at once. The first instruction is to understand it.

    我們通常想立刻擺脫苦。第一項指示卻是：了知它。

Phrases to avoid 要避開的說法

“The Four Noble Truths are what Buddhists believe.” 「四聖諦就是佛教徒所相信的東西。」

Makes them a creed. Say they are a diagnosis and a treatment, each with a task.

把它們講成了信條。要說：它們是一份診斷與一套療法；每一項各有任務。

“The first truth is that life is suffering.” 「第一個諦是：人生是苦。」

See the previous lesson. Say “there is suffering, and it is to be understood.”

見上一課。要說「有苦；而它是要被了知的」。

“Buddhism is really just a form of therapy.” 「佛教其實只是一種心理治療。」

Shrinks the aim. Therapy restores ordinary functioning. The third truth claims the end of craving.

把目標縮小了。心理治療恢復的是日常的功能。第三個諦主張的，是渴愛的止息。

“The Buddha was a psychologist, not a religious teacher.” 「佛陀是心理學家，不是宗教導師。」

A modern either-or. He called himself a healer. Use his word.

這是現代人的二分法。他稱自己為醫者。用他自己的詞。

Reading check · 理解測驗

Did it land? 讀懂了嗎

1. What four skills does the discourse on the physician name?

2. What task belongs to the first noble truth?

3. Why is the argument of the four truths called practical?

4. What did Epicurus’s followers call the summary of his teaching?

5. Where does the likeness between the Greek schools and the Buddha end?

Write · 你怎麼講？

  1. State a difficulty of your own as a doctor’s four questions: what is wrong, what causes it, can it end, what would the treatment be. About 150 words.

    把你自己的一個難處，陳述成醫生的四個問題：哪裡不對勁？什麼造成的？它能結束嗎？療法會是什麼？約 150 個字。

  2. The first truth is “to be understood,” not removed. Describe a time when understanding a trouble had to come before fixing it.

    第一個諦是「要被了知」，而不是被除去。描述一次經驗：必須先了解一個困擾，才能去處理它。

  3. Epicurus offers four beliefs to memorize and the Buddha eight kinds of training. In 200 words, say which kind of medicine you think works on people, and why.

    伊比鳩魯提供四個要背熟的信念；佛陀提供八種訓練。用 200 個字說說：你認為哪一種藥對人有效？為什麼？
