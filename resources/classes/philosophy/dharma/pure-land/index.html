D23 · Dharma and the West 佛法與西方哲學

Pure Land, Faith, and Other-Power 淨土、信與他力

“Walking by land is hard; traveling by water in a boat is pleasant.” Nāgārjuna described an easy path to the stage of no return, and Chinese and Japanese masters built a whole tradition on it: entrust yourself to Amitābha’s vow and call his name. Is this the same as salvation by faith in the West? This lesson sets the Pure Land path beside Luther’s faith alone, Pascal’s wager, and Kierkegaard’s passion of faith, and asks where a tradition without a creator God puts its trust.
「陸道步行則苦，水道乘船則樂。」龍樹描述了一條通往不退轉地的易行道；中國與日本的祖師在它上面建立了一整個傳統：信靠阿彌陀佛的本願，稱念他的名號。這跟西方的「因信得救」是一樣的嗎？這一課把淨土法門，跟路德的「唯獨信心」、帕斯卡的賭注、齊克果的信仰熱情並列，並且問：一個沒有造物主上帝的傳統，把它的信靠放在哪裡？

淨土・他力
saddhā Pāli　sukhāvatī, śraddhā Sanskritthe Pure Land; faith; other-power
The verdict · 判定

Closest: Martin Luther, for whom faith alone, and not works, makes a person righteous, and good works follow from a person already made good. Tanluan’s other-power and Luther’s grace both answer the same despair, that ordinary people cannot save themselves by effort. The likeness ends at what is trusted. Amitābha is not a creator or a judge. He was a bodhisattva whose vow became effective through his own practice, and his Pure Land is a place where beings complete the path to awakening. The trust points finally to the same goal as every other Buddhist path, and later teachers said that self-power and other-power are not two.

最接近的是馬丁・路德：對他來說，使人稱義的是唯獨信心，不是行為；而善行是從一個已經被變好的人身上流出來的。曇鸞的他力與路德的恩典，回應的是同一種絕望：凡夫無法靠努力救自己。相似之處，停在所信靠的對象上。阿彌陀佛不是造物主，也不是審判者。他原是一位菩薩，他的願因為他自己的修行而生效；他的淨土是眾生完成覺悟之道的地方。這份信靠最終指向的，跟每一條佛教道路的目標相同；而後來的祖師說：自力與他力不二。

1 · What the Dharma says · 佛法怎麼說

The vow, the name, and the boat 本願、名號，與船

Faith was part of Buddhism from the start. It heads the list of the five faculties, faith, energy, mindfulness, immersion, and wisdom, and at the end of one discourse the Buddha says, in Bhikkhu Sujato’s translation, that “those who have a degree of faith and love for me are all bound for heaven” (Majjhima Nikāya 22). The Mahāyāna Pure Land sūtras made faith central. In the Sūtra of Immeasurable Life, the monk Dharmākara makes forty-eight vows before becoming the buddha Amitābha. The eighteenth, in Saṃghavarman’s Chinese translation, reads: “If, when I attain buddhahood, the beings of the ten directions, with sincere faith and joy, wish to be born in my land and call on me even ten times, and are not born there, may I not attain perfect awakening, excepting only those who commit the five grave offenses and slander the true Dharma.” Since he did become a buddha, the tradition reasons, the vow is fulfilled. The shorter Amitābha Sūtra adds the practice: hold to his name “for one day, two days … seven days, with a single mind undisturbed.”

信從一開始就是佛教的一部分。它是五根（信、精進、念、定、慧）之首；在一部經的結尾，佛陀說（依 Sujato 比丘的英譯）：「對我有幾分信與愛的人，都將生天」（《中部》22）。大乘的淨土經典把信放在中心。在《無量壽經》裡，法藏比丘在成為阿彌陀佛之前發了四十八願。第十八願，康僧鎧的譯文是：「設我得佛，十方眾生至心信樂欲生我國，乃至十念，若不生者，不取正覺，唯除五逆、誹謗正法。」傳統的推理是：既然他確實成佛了，這個願就已經實現。較短的《阿彌陀經》補上了修行的方法：「執持名號，若一日、若二日……若七日，一心不亂。」

The idea of an easier path goes back to Nāgārjuna, or at least to the Ten Stages Treatise traditionally attributed to him. A questioner asks whether there is an easy way to reach the stage of no return. The treatise first scolds him: this is “the talk of the timid and inferior, not the resolve of a great person.” Then it gives the easy way anyway. “The Buddha’s teaching has countless gates. As with the roads of the world, some are hard and some are easy. Walking by land is hard; traveling by water in a boat is pleasant. The bodhisattva path is the same. Some practice with diligent effort, and some, by the skillful means of faith, practice the easy way and quickly reach the stage of no return.” The easy way is to call respectfully on the names of the buddhas.

「較容易的道路」這個觀念，可以追溯到龍樹，或至少追溯到傳統上歸於他的《十住毘婆沙論》。一位發問者問：有沒有一條容易的路，可以到達不退轉地？論先責備他：這是「怯弱下劣之言，非是大人志幹之說」。然後還是給了那條容易的路：「佛法有無量門，如世間道，有難、有易；陸道步行則苦，水道乘船則樂。菩薩道亦如是：或有勤行精進，或有以信方便易行疾至阿惟越致者。」這條易行道，就是以恭敬心稱念諸佛的名號。

The Chinese master Tanluan, in the sixth century, turned this into a teaching about two kinds of power. On the hard path, one difficulty is that it is “self-power alone, without the support of other-power.” The easy path is “simply, through faith in the Buddha, to wish to be born in the Pure Land, and riding on the Buddha’s vow-power, to be born there.” His illustration is memorable. A person who keeps the precepts, then develops concentration, then supernormal powers, and so travels the four continents, does it by self-power. “A weak man who cannot even climb onto a donkey, if he follows the procession of a wheel-turning king, rides through the sky and travels the four continents without obstruction. This is called other-power.” Japanese teachers, above all Shinran in the thirteenth century, pushed the idea further, until even the faith was understood as Amitābha’s gift. In Chinese Buddhism the emphasis falls on faith, vow, and practice together, and the chapter called “The True Cause of Rebirth,” in Xia Lianju’s compiled edition of the Sūtra of Immeasurable Life, promises that even busy people with families can be born there.

六世紀的中國祖師曇鸞，把這一點變成一個關於兩種力量的教法。難行道的困難之一，是「唯是自力，無他力持」。易行道是「但以信佛因緣願生淨土，乘佛願力便得往生彼清淨土」。他的比喻令人難忘：一個人受持禁戒，然後修禪定，然後修神通，因而能遊四天下，「如是等名為自力」。「又如劣夫跨驢不上，從轉輪王行，便乘虛空遊四天下無所障礙，如是等名為他力。」日本的祖師，尤其是十三世紀的親鸞，把這個觀念推得更遠，直到連信心本身都被理解為阿彌陀佛的賜予。在漢傳佛教裡，重點落在信、願、行三者並重；《無量壽經》會集本的〈往生正因品〉也應許，即使是有家室、事務繁忙的人，也能往生。

3 · The nearest Western views · 西方最接近的說法

Faith alone, the wager, and passion 唯獨信心、賭注，與熱情

Faith alone · 唯獨信心

Martin Luther 馬丁・路德1520

Luther’s Concerning Christian Liberty sets out a view of faith and works that Pure Land readers find startlingly familiar. In the translation by Wace and Buchheim: “Faith alone and the efficacious use of the word of God, bring salvation.” Works do not make a person good. “True, then, are these two sayings: ‘Good works do not make a good man, but a good man does good works.’” And the treatise opens with a paradox: “A Christian man is the most free lord of all, and subject to none; a Christian man is the most dutiful servant of all, and subject to every one.” The structure matches Tanluan and Shinran closely. Human effort cannot reach the goal, the goal is given through trust in another’s power, and the good life flows from that trust and does not earn it. Christian theologians have noticed the parallel with Shinran, and some have been unsettled by it. The difference is the one who gives. Luther’s God is the creator and judge of all, who justifies the sinner. Amitābha is neither creator nor judge.

路德的〈論基督徒的自由〉，提出了一種關於信與行的觀點，淨土的讀者會覺得熟悉得驚人。Wace 與 Buchheim 的英譯說：「唯獨信心，以及上帝之道的有效運用，帶來救恩。」行為不會使人變好：「所以這兩句話是真的：『善行不會造就一個好人，而是一個好人做出善行。』」這篇論文以一個弔詭開頭：「基督徒是最自由的主人，不受任何人管轄；基督徒是最盡責的僕人，受每一個人管轄。」這個結構與曇鸞、親鸞非常吻合：人的努力無法到達目標；目標透過信靠另一者的力量而被賜予；美好的生活從這份信靠流出，而不是掙得它。基督教神學家注意到了與親鸞的相似，其中有些人為此感到不安。差別在於賜予者。路德的上帝是萬物的創造者與審判者，祂使罪人稱義。阿彌陀佛既不是創造者，也不是審判者。

The wager and the heart · 賭注與心

Blaise Pascal 帕斯卡c. 1660

Pascal argued that one cannot stand outside the question of faith. In W. F. Trotter’s translation of the Pensées: “Yes; but you must wager. It is not optional. You are embarked.” Then the calculation: “Let us weigh the gain and the loss in wagering that God is. … If you gain, you gain all; if you lose, you lose nothing. Wager, then, without hesitation that He is.” He also knew that argument alone does not produce faith. “The heart has its reasons, which reason does not know,” and “It is the heart which experiences God, and not the reason.” Pure Land teachers made similar moves. The image of being already embarked fits Nāgārjuna’s boat, and the tradition also says that calling the name with a sincere heart matters more than understanding the doctrine. The difference is in what is staked. Pascal’s wager concerns whether God exists at all. The Pure Land practitioner is not asked to bet on a creator. They are asked to trust a buddha’s vow and to act on it, and the tradition claims that the trust bears fruit in a softer, more grateful life now.

帕斯卡主張：一個人無法站在信仰的問題之外。W. F. Trotter 翻譯的《思想錄》說：「是的；但你必須下注。這不是可以選擇的。你已經上船了。」然後是計算：「讓我們衡量押注上帝存在的得與失……如果你贏了，你贏得一切；如果你輸了，你什麼也沒失去。那麼，毫不猶豫地押注祂存在吧。」他也知道光靠論證不會產生信仰：「心有它的理由，是理性所不知道的」；「感受到上帝的是心，而不是理性」。淨土的祖師也有類似的做法。「已經上船」的意象，正好合乎龍樹的船；而傳統也說，以至誠的心稱名，比理解教義更重要。差別在於下注的東西。帕斯卡的賭注，關乎上帝究竟存不存在。淨土行者不是被要求押注一位造物主；他們被要求信靠一位佛的願，並依之而行；而傳統主張，這份信靠在今生就結出果實：一種更柔軟、更懂得感恩的生活。

Faith as passion · 信仰作為熱情

Søren Kierkegaard 齊克果1846

The Danish philosopher Søren Kierkegaard insisted that faith is not the conclusion of an argument and cannot be reached by piling up evidence. It is a decision made with the whole of one’s existence, in the face of uncertainty, and for that reason it is a passion rather than a piece of knowledge. In the Concluding Unscientific Postscript he described truth, for an existing person, as a matter of how one relates to what one believes, and he mocked the scholar who postpones commitment until the research is complete. The popular phrase “leap of faith” summarizes his view, though he did not use exactly those words. The parallel to the Pure Land is in the role of the whole person. Shinran’s faith is not assent to propositions, and it is meant to transform how one lives. The difference is in the tone. Kierkegaard’s faith is lonely and full of dread, a single self before God. The Pure Land’s faith is meant to bring relief from exactly that burden, the weak man carried along in the king’s procession.

丹麥哲學家齊克果堅持：信仰不是一個論證的結論，也無法靠堆積證據達到。它是一個以整個存在、在不確定之中作出的決定；正因為如此，它是一種熱情，而不是一項知識。在《最後的非科學附筆》裡，他把對一個存在的人而言的真理，描述為一個人如何與他所相信的東西發生關係；他也嘲笑那位要等研究完成才肯委身的學者。流行的說法「信仰的跳躍」概括了他的觀點，雖然他並沒有用一模一樣的字。與淨土的對應在於「整個人」的角色：親鸞的信心不是對命題的同意，它是要轉化一個人怎麼生活的。差別在於基調。齊克果的信仰孤獨而充滿怖慄，是一個單獨的自我面對上帝。淨土的信心，恰恰是要使人從那份重擔中解脫：那個被轉輪王的行列帶著走的劣夫。

4 · Verdict · 判定

Who is closest, and where the likeness ends 誰最接近，相似到哪裡為止

The closest is Luther. Both he and the Pure Land masters begin from the conviction that ordinary people cannot save themselves by effort, both place the turning point in trust in another’s power, and both say that good conduct follows from that trust and does not purchase it. Pascal is closest on the predicament of being already embarked, and on the place of the heart. Kierkegaard is closest on faith as an act of the whole person, which no argument can replace.

最接近的是路德。他與淨土祖師都從一個信念出發：凡夫無法靠努力救自己；都把轉折點放在對另一者力量的信靠上；也都說良善的行為從那份信靠流出，而不是用來購買它。在「已經上船」這個處境，以及心的位置上，最接近的是帕斯卡。在信仰作為「整個人」的行為、任何論證都無法取代這一點上，最接近的是齊克果。

The likeness ends at what is trusted. Amitābha is not the creator of the world, he does not judge sins, and he does not forgive them as a judge forgives. He was a bodhisattva named Dharmākara whose vows took effect because he fulfilled them through practice, and his power works, in Tanluan’s word, as a “dominant condition,” one cause among the causes of a being’s awakening. Tanluan says, “If it were not for the Buddha’s power, the forty-eight vows would have been made in vain,” and the power is still part of the web of conditions the Buddha taught. And the goal is the same as on every Buddhist path. The Pure Land is a place where awakening becomes easy, and it is not the final salvation of a soul. Later Chinese teachers drew the conclusion, following the Vimalakīrti Sūtra: “If a bodhisattva wishes to attain a pure land, he should purify his mind. As the mind is purified, the buddha land is purified.” In the end self-power and other-power are not two.

相似之處，停在所信靠的對象上。阿彌陀佛不是世界的創造者，他不審判罪，也不像法官那樣赦免罪。他原是一位名叫法藏的菩薩；他的願之所以生效，是因為他透過修行實現了它們；而他的力量，用曇鸞的話說，是作為一個「增上緣」起作用，是一個眾生覺悟的諸多原因之一。曇鸞說：「若非佛力，四十八願便是徒設」；而這份力量，仍然在佛陀所教的因緣之網之中。目標也與每一條佛教道路相同。淨土是一個覺悟變得容易的地方，不是一個靈魂最後的得救。後來的中國祖師，順著《維摩詰經》得出結論：「若菩薩欲得淨土，當淨其心；隨其心淨，則佛土淨。」到最後，自力與他力不二。

5 · The step the Dharma takes further · 佛法多走的一步

A boat that goes somewhere, and a mind that is the land 要去某處的船，以及即是淨土的心

The first further step is that the Pure Land is a means. In Christian thought, salvation is the end of the story, eternal life with God. In the Pure Land sūtras, birth in Amitābha’s land is a beginning. The vows promise that those born there will not fall back and will reach awakening, and some vows promise that they will return to help others. The Pure Land is a better school, and in Tanluan’s image the weak man is carried along in the king’s procession so that he can travel without obstruction. This changes the meaning of faith. It is not trust that someone will save you from judgment. It is trust that conditions for awakening can be given to those who cannot make them for themselves. A Western teacher can present the Pure Land honestly as compassion built into the structure of the path, and need not call it a Buddhist heaven.

多走的第一步是：淨土是一種手段。在基督教的思想裡，救恩是故事的終點，是與上帝同在的永生。在淨土經典裡，往生阿彌陀佛的國土是一個開始。本願應許：往生者不會退轉，必定到達覺悟；有些願還應許他們會回來幫助別人。淨土是一所更好的學校；在曇鸞的意象裡，劣夫被轉輪王的行列帶著，好讓他能無礙地遊歷。這改變了信的意思。它不是信靠有人會把你從審判中救出來，而是信靠：覺悟的條件，可以被賜給那些無法為自己創造條件的人。一位西方的老師可以誠實地把淨土介紹為「被建構進道路結構裡的慈悲」，而不需要稱它為佛教的天堂。

The second further step is that self-power and other-power finally meet. Shinran stressed other-power so strongly that even faith became Amitābha’s gift, and some critics thought this made practice pointless. The Chinese tradition kept faith, vow, and practice together. In “The True Cause of Rebirth” the sūtra asks busy householders to keep the ten good deeds, to think of Amitābha day and night, and to “be filial and obedient, sincere and loyal.” And Mahāyāna philosophy dissolves the opposition from the other side. If all things are empty and interdependent, there is no fixed self to save itself and no fixed other to do the saving. The Vimalakīrti Sūtra’s line, “as the mind is purified, the buddha land is purified,” puts the Pure Land in the purified mind. This is why Chinese masters could say that Chan and Pure Land are one practice, which the next lesson takes up.

多走的第二步是：自力與他力最終相會。親鸞把他力強調到連信心都成為阿彌陀佛的賜予；有些批評者認為這使修行變得沒有意義。漢傳傳統則讓信、願、行並重。〈往生正因品〉要求忙碌的在家人持守十善、晝夜思惟阿彌陀佛，並且「要當孝順，至誠忠信」。而大乘哲學從另一邊消解了這個對立：如果一切法都是空、都是相互依存的，就沒有一個固定的自我能救自己，也沒有一個固定的他者來施救。《維摩詰經》那句「隨其心淨，則佛土淨」，把淨土放在被淨化的心裡。這就是為什麼中國的祖師能說禪與淨土是同一種修行；下一課會處理這一點。

6 · How to say it to a Western audience · 對西方人怎麼講

The boat and the donkey 船與驢

Westerners who know Buddhism mainly as meditation are often surprised by the Pure Land, and those raised Protestant may say at once, “That’s salvation by grace.” Both reactions are useful. Start with Nāgārjuna’s two roads, walking by land and sailing by water, because it shows that the easy path was presented from the beginning as one gate among many and was never offered as a shortcut for the lazy. Then tell Tanluan’s story of the weak man and the king’s procession. Agree with the Protestant listener that the structure is like Luther’s, and then name the difference: Amitābha is not a creator and does not judge, and the Pure Land is a place to finish the path. End with the Vimalakīrti Sūtra’s line, which brings the whole teaching back to the mind.

主要把佛教認識為禪修的西方人，常常對淨土感到驚訝；而在新教環境中長大的人，可能會立刻說：「那就是因恩典得救嘛。」兩種反應都有用。從龍樹的兩條路（陸路步行與水路乘船）開始，因為它顯示：易行道從一開始就是被當作眾多法門中的一門來介紹的，從來不是給懶人的捷徑。然後講曇鸞那個劣夫與轉輪王行列的故事。同意新教的聽眾：這個結構像路德的；然後說出差別：阿彌陀佛不是造物主，也不審判；淨土是一個完成道路的地方。最後用《維摩詰經》那句話作結，它把整個教法帶回到心上。

Sentences you can use 可以直接用的句子

  1. Nāgārjuna said some paths are like walking over land, and some are like sailing in a boat.

    龍樹說：有些道路像陸地步行，有些像乘船航行。

  2. Amitābha vowed that all who sincerely wish to be born in his land and call his name will be born there.

    阿彌陀佛發願：凡是至心願生他的國土、稱念他名號的人，都將往生。

  3. Tanluan compared other-power to a weak man who cannot climb onto a donkey but rides through the sky in a king’s procession.

    曇鸞把他力比作一個爬不上驢的劣夫，卻能隨著國王的行列乘空而行。

  4. The structure resembles Luther’s faith alone, but Amitābha is not a creator or a judge.

    這個結構像路德的「唯獨信心」，但阿彌陀佛不是造物主，也不是審判者。

  5. The Pure Land is a place to finish the path to awakening. It is not the end of the story.

    淨土是一個完成覺悟之道的地方。它不是故事的終點。

  6. “As the mind is purified, the buddha land is purified.” Finally, self-power and other-power are not two.

    「隨其心淨，則佛土淨。」到最後，自力與他力不二。

Phrases to avoid 要避開的說法

“Pure Land Buddhism is just Christianity with Buddhist names.” 「淨土宗不過是換了佛教名字的基督教。」

There is no creator, no judgment, and the goal is awakening.

沒有造物主，沒有審判，目標是覺悟。

“Amitābha is the Buddhist God.” 「阿彌陀佛就是佛教的上帝。」

He was a bodhisattva who became a buddha through practice.

他是一位透過修行而成佛的菩薩。

“You just say the name and nothing else matters.” 「只要念佛號，其他都不重要。」

The sūtras also ask for sincere faith, vows, and good conduct.

經典也要求至誠的信、願，以及善行。

“Pure Land is for people who can’t do real practice.” 「淨土是給做不了真正修行的人的。」

Great scholars practiced it, and Nāgārjuna called it one gate among many.

許多大學問家都修淨土；龍樹稱它為眾多法門之一。

Reading check · 理解測驗

Did it land? 讀懂了嗎

1. What image does the Ten Stages Treatise use for the easy path?

2. What does Amitābha’s eighteenth vow promise?

3. How does Tanluan illustrate other-power?

4. Where does the likeness with Luther end, according to the lesson?

5. What does the Vimalakīrti Sūtra say about the pure land?

Write · 你怎麼講？

  1. Describe a time when you could not solve a problem by your own effort and had to rely on someone else. What changed in you? About 150 words.

    描述一次你無法靠自己努力解決問題、必須依靠別人的經驗。你身上有什麼改變了？約 150 個字。

  2. A Protestant friend says, “Pure Land is salvation by grace.” Write a friendly reply that agrees with what is right and names one difference.

    一位新教的朋友說：「淨土就是因恩典得救。」寫一個友善的回應：同意說對的部分，並指出一個差別。

  3. Self-power or other-power? In 200 words, explain why later Buddhist teachers said the two are not finally separate.

    自力還是他力？用 200 個字說明：為什麼後來的佛教祖師說兩者最終並不分開。
