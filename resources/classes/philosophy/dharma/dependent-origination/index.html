D4 · Dharma and the West 佛法與西方哲學

Dependent Origination and the West 緣起與西方哲學

“When this exists, this comes to be.” The Buddha called it deep and told Ānanda not to say it was plain. What the teaching claims about causes, which Western philosopher saw the same thing in a game of billiards, and how to explain it without saying “everything is connected.”
「此有故彼有。」佛陀說它甚深，並且告訴阿難不要說它淺顯。這個教法對「因」主張了什麼？哪一位西方哲學家在一場撞球裡看見了同一件事？又該怎麼解釋它，而不說「萬物都相連」？

緣起
paṭiccasamuppāda Pāli　pratītyasamutpāda Sanskritdependent origination
The verdict · 判定

Closest: David Hume on what a cause is, and A. N. Whitehead on what a thing is. Hume found no hidden power in causes, only regular dependence, and even stated it in the Buddha’s form: had the first not been, the second had never existed. The likeness ends at the use. Hume’s result was a doubt that he set aside at dinner. The Buddha’s was a map of how suffering is produced, drawn so that it could be undone.

最接近的：在「因是什麼」上是大衛・休謨；在「事物是什麼」上是懷海德。休謨在因裡找不到任何隱藏的力量，只找到有規律的依賴；他甚至用佛陀的那個形式把它說了出來：若無前者，後者絕不會存在。相似到「用途」為止。休謨得到的是一個懷疑；吃晚飯的時候他就把它擱下了。佛陀得到的，是一張「苦是怎麼被造出來的」地圖；畫它，是為了能把苦拆掉。

1 · What the Dharma says · 佛法怎麼說

A law, a chain, and a middle 一條法則、一條鏈子，與一個中道

The teaching has a general form and a particular application. The general form is one sentence: “When this exists, this comes to be; due to the arising of this, this arises.” Its reverse follows: when this does not exist, that does not come to be, and with the ceasing of this, that ceases. Nothing arises by itself, from nothing, or from a single cause. Whatever arises does so in dependence on conditions, and lasts as long as they do. The Buddha presented this as a fact about the world that he had found and had not made. “Whether Realized Ones arise or not, this law of nature persists, this regularity of natural principles, this surety of natural principles, specific conditionality.”

這個教法有一個一般的形式，以及一個特定的應用。一般的形式是一句話：「此有故彼有，此生故彼生。」它的反面隨之而來：此無故彼無，此滅故彼滅。沒有任何東西是自己生起的、從無生起的，或者從單一的因生起的。凡是生起的，都是依靠條件而生起；條件在多久，它就在多久。佛陀把這當作一項關於世界的事實來陳述：是他發現的，不是他造的。「不管如來出世或不出世，這個法性都安住著：法的定則、法的確定性，也就是此緣性。」

The particular application is a chain of twelve links that traces how suffering is produced. “Ignorance is a requirement for choices. Choices are a requirement for consciousness. Consciousness is a requirement for name and form,” and so on through the six senses, contact, feeling, craving, grasping, continued existence, and rebirth to old age and death. Each link is a condition for the next. It is not said to be its sole cause. The monk Sāriputta explained the relation with a simile: “Suppose there were two sheaves of reeds leaning up against each other.” Each stands because of the other, and “if the first of those sheaves of reeds were to be pulled away, the other would collapse.” The chain is given in order to be run backward. “When ignorance fades away and ceases with no residue left behind, choices cease,” and so on to the ceasing of “this entire mass of suffering.”

特定的應用，是一條十二個環節的鏈子；它追溯苦是怎麼被造出來的。「無明緣行，行緣識，識緣名色」；然後依序是六入、觸、受、愛、取、有、生，一直到老死。每一環都是下一環的條件。經文並沒有說它是唯一的原因。舍利弗用一個譬喻來解釋這個關係：「譬如兩束蘆葦，互相倚靠著立起來。」每一束都是因為另一束才立得住；而「如果把其中一束抽走，另一束就會倒下」。給出這條鏈子，是為了讓人倒著走。「無明無餘地褪去、滅盡，行就滅」；依此類推，一直到「這一整堆的苦」滅盡。

The teaching is also called the middle. A monk named Kaccāna asked what right view is, and the Buddha answered: “‘All exists’: this is one extreme. ‘All does not exist’: this is the second extreme. Avoiding these two extremes, the Realized One teaches by the middle way,” and he recited the twelve links. What arises dependently is not nothing, since it arises and has effects. It does not exist in its own right either, since it is there only while its conditions are. When Ānanda remarked that dependent origination, though deep, seemed to him “as plain as can be,” the Buddha replied: “Don’t say that, Ānanda, don’t say that!” Elsewhere the tradition reports him as saying that one who sees dependent origination sees the teaching.

這個教法也被稱為「中」。一位名叫迦旃延的比丘問：什麼是正見？佛陀回答：「『一切有』，這是一邊。『一切無』，這是第二邊。離開這兩邊，如來依中道說法」；接著他誦出了十二支。依緣而起的東西並不是「無」；因為它生起了，而且有作用。它也不是憑自己而存在的；因為只有在它的條件還在的時候，它才在。當阿難說，緣起雖然甚深，在他看來卻「再明白不過」的時候，佛陀回答：「阿難，不要這樣說！不要這樣說！」在別處，傳統記述他說過：見緣起者即見法。

3 · The nearest Western views · 西方最接近的說法

Three accounts of what a cause is 關於「因是什麼」的三種說明

No hidden tie · 沒有隱藏的繫帶

David Hume 休謨1748

Hume asked what we observe when one billiard ball strikes another. We see the first move, the contact, and the second move. We do not see a power passing between them. “All events seem entirely loose and separate. One event follows another; but we never can observe any tie between them. They seem conjoined, but never connected.” A cause, on his account, is an object regularly followed by another, and he added a second definition in other words: “where, if the first object had not been, the second never had existed.” Set that beside “when this does not exist, that does not come to be.” Both describe causation as dependence that can be relied on, with no inner force required. They differ in confidence. Hume concluded that our belief in causes rests on custom and cannot be justified by reason. The Buddha treated the regularity as a discovered law and built a practice on it.

休謨問：一顆撞球撞上另一顆的時候，我們觀察到的是什麼？我們看到第一顆在動、兩顆相碰、第二顆在動。我們並沒有看到有一股力量在它們之間傳遞。「一切事件似乎都是完全鬆散而分離的。一件事跟著另一件事；而我們永遠觀察不到它們之間有任何繫帶。它們看起來是相連的，卻從來不是相繫的。」照他的說明，因就是一個「有規律地被另一個對象跟隨」的對象；而他換了一種說法，補上第二個定義：「若無第一個對象，第二個就絕不會存在。」把這句話放在「此無故彼無」的旁邊。兩者都把因果描述成一種可以倚賴的依存關係，不需要任何內在的力。兩者的差別在於信心。休謨的結論是：我們對因的信念立足於習慣，無法用理性來辯護。佛陀把這種規律當作一條被發現的法則，並且在它上面建立了一套修行。

Conditions, and no single cause · 是眾多條件，沒有單一的因

J. L. Mackie 麥基1965

When investigators say that a short circuit caused a fire, they do not mean that it was enough by itself. There had to be oxygen, something flammable nearby, and no sprinkler. They do not mean that it was necessary either, since other things can start fires. Mackie proposed that what we call a cause is an insufficient but necessary part of a set of conditions that is together sufficient, though other sets would also have done. We pick one member of the set and call it the cause because it interests us. This is the analysis the reed simile requires. Each link of the twelve is a condition among others, and the tradition says in so many words that many conditions give rise to each thing. Mackie was analyzing ordinary talk about causes. He drew no conclusion about suffering.

當調查人員說「是短路造成了火災」的時候，他們的意思並不是：單靠短路就夠了。還得有氧氣、附近有可燃物，而且沒有灑水裝置。他們的意思也不是：非有短路不可；因為別的東西也能引起火災。麥基提出：我們所謂的「因」，是一組合起來才充分的條件當中，一個本身不充分、卻不可少的部分；而別組條件本來也能造成同樣的結果。我們從那一組裡挑出一個成員，稱它為「原因」；是因為我們對它感興趣。蘆葦的譬喻需要的，正是這個分析。十二支的每一支，都是眾多條件裡的一個；而傳統明白地說：「眾緣具足和合而物生。」麥基分析的是日常談論原因的方式。關於苦，他沒有得出任何結論。

Things as events in relation · 事物是處在關係中的事件

Alfred North Whitehead 懷海德1929

Whitehead, who had written Principia Mathematica with Russell, turned in later life to metaphysics and rejected the picture of a world made of enduring substances that have properties. The basic units of reality, he proposed, are momentary events, which he called actual occasions. Each one arises by taking account of the occasions before it, comes to completion, and becomes in its turn a condition for those that follow. What we call a thing, a stone or a person, is a society of such events with a common pattern. Relation comes first on this view, and the thing is what the relations compose. Of Western systems this is the nearest to the Buddhist analysis of persons and things into dependently arising events, and Whitehead himself remarked on the kinship. He kept a God in his system, as the source of order and novelty, where the Buddhist account has none.

懷海德曾與羅素合著《數學原理》；晚年他轉向形上學，並且拒絕了這樣的圖像：世界是由「擁有性質的、持久的實體」構成的。他提出：實在的基本單位，是剎那的事件；他稱之為「現實事態」（actual occasions）。每一個事件，都是藉著攝受在它之前的事件而生起；達到完成；然後輪到它成為後來者的條件。我們所謂的一樣東西，一塊石頭或者一個人，是一群有共同模式的這類事件所組成的「社會」。照這個看法，關係在先；而事物是由關係所組成的。在西方的各種體系裡，這是最接近佛教「把人與事物分析成依緣而起的事件」的一個；而懷海德自己也提到過這份親緣。他在自己的體系裡保留了一位上帝，作為秩序與新穎性的來源；在這一點上，佛教的說明裡沒有。

4 · Verdict · 判定

Who is closest, and where the likeness ends 誰最接近，相似到哪裡為止

Apply the three questions of the first lesson on this shelf. On the conclusion, Hume is closest about causes and Whitehead about things. Hume and the Buddha agree that causation is regular dependence and that no further power is found behind it. Whitehead and the Buddha agree that what looks like an enduring thing is a pattern of dependent events. Mackie supplies what both leave unsaid, that the dependence is always on many conditions together.

把這個書架第一課的那三個問題用上。在結論上，關於「因」最接近的是休謨；關於「事物」最接近的是懷海德。休謨與佛陀同意：因果是有規律的依賴；而在它背後，找不到更進一步的力。懷海德與佛陀同意：看起來像一樣持久的東西的，是一個由依存的事件構成的模式。麥基補上了兩人都沒有明說的一點：這種依賴，總是對許多條件一起的依賴。

On the argument, the three reach these views by different roads. Hume looked for the source of an idea and failed to find it. Whitehead built a system to fit modern physics. The Buddha examined how grief and craving arise in his own experience. On the purpose, the likeness ends. Hume said that his doubts, strong in the study, vanished when he dined and played backgammon with his friends, and he thought this a good thing. For the Buddha the analysis was made in order to be used. If suffering has conditions, and the conditions can be found, the effect can be removed by removing them. Western philosophy studied causation chiefly to understand how we know nature. The Buddha studied it to find out where a chain can be cut.

在論證上，三人是走不同的路到達這些看法的。休謨去找一個觀念的來源，而沒有找到。懷海德建造了一個體系，使它合乎現代物理學。佛陀檢視的，是哀傷與渴愛怎麼在他自己的經驗裡生起。在目的上，相似就到此為止了。休謨說：他的懷疑在書房裡很強烈；等他跟朋友吃飯、下雙陸棋的時候就消散了；而他認為這是件好事。對佛陀來說，作這番分析，是為了要用它。如果苦有條件，而條件找得出來，那麼把條件拿掉，就能把那個結果拿掉。西方哲學研究因果，主要是為了理解我們怎麼認識自然。佛陀研究它，是為了查出一條鏈子可以從哪裡切斷。

5 · The step the Dharma takes further · 佛法多走的一步

Where the chain can be cut 鏈子可以從哪裡切斷

The first further step is practical. The twelve links are not equally within reach. No one can decide to stop feeling pleasure and pain when the senses meet their objects. Between feeling and craving there is a gap. A pleasant feeling arises, and wanting more of it usually follows so quickly that the two seem one. With training in attention the feeling can be known as a feeling before the wanting takes hold, and at that point the next link does not form. The teachers of the tradition point to this joint as the place where practice does its work. A theory of causation, in Buddhist hands, becomes an instruction about where to look.

多走的第一步是實踐上的。十二支並不是每一支都同樣搆得著。當感官遇上它們的對象的時候，沒有人能夠決定不再感受苦與樂。而在「受」與「愛」之間，有一道縫隙。一個樂受生起了；想要更多的那個念頭，通常跟得那麼快，以致兩者看起來像是一個。經過注意力的訓練，可以在「想要」抓住人之前，就把那個受認作一個受；而在那一點上，下一環就不形成了。這個傳統的老師們指出：修行就是在這個關節上起作用的。一個關於因果的理論，到了佛教手裡，成了一項「該往哪裡看」的指示。

The second step is philosophical, and it was taken by Nāgārjuna about six centuries after the Buddha. If a thing arises only in dependence on conditions, it has no nature that is its own apart from them. That absence of own-nature is what he meant by emptiness. His best-known verse says that whatever arises from conditions is what he calls empty, that this is a provisional designation, and that this is the middle way. He drew the consequence: no thing whatever arises without conditions, and so there is nothing that is not empty. Emptiness in this sense is dependent origination seen from the other side. It is the opposite of nothingness, since only what lacks a fixed nature can arise, change, and cease. Chinese Buddhists later pictured the result as a net of jewels in which each jewel reflects all the others. Hume stopped at the loose and separate events. Nāgārjuna went on to say that the events too have no being apart from their relations, which is a step Whitehead would recognize.

第二步是哲學上的；是龍樹在佛陀之後大約六個世紀邁出的。如果一樣東西只有依靠條件才生起，那麼離開了那些條件，它就沒有屬於它自己的本性。這種「沒有自性」，就是他所說的空。他最有名的那首偈說：「眾因緣生法，我說即是無，亦為是假名，亦是中道義。」他接著推出了結論：「未曾有一法，不從因緣生，是故一切法，無不是空者。」這個意義上的空，就是從另一面看到的緣起。它是「虛無」的反面；因為只有沒有固定本性的東西，才能生起、變化、滅去。後來中國的佛教徒把這個結果描繪成一張寶珠結成的網：每一顆寶珠都映現出其他所有的寶珠。休謨停在那些鬆散而分離的事件上。龍樹則繼續說：那些事件離開了它們的關係，同樣沒有存在；而這一步，懷海德會認得。

6 · How to say it to a Western audience · 對西方人怎麼講

Conditions you can work on 你可以下工夫的條件

Western listeners often meet this teaching as “everything is connected,” a phrase that is pleasant, vague, and impossible to act on. Others hear the word “cause” and think of fate. Both miss what the Buddha was doing. Begin with something small and checkable, such as a moment of irritation, and ask what had to be in place for it: a tired body, a noise, an expectation, a habit of reacting. Then ask which of these could have been otherwise. The listener has now seen the two halves of the teaching, that things arise from many conditions and that some of the conditions are within reach. The twelve links and emptiness can come afterward.

西方的聽眾遇到這個教法的時候，聽到的常常是「萬物都相連」；這句話聽起來舒服、含糊，而且沒辦法照著去做。另一些人聽到「因」這個字，想到的是命運。兩者都錯過了佛陀當時在做的事。從一件小而可以檢查的事開始，例如一陣煩躁；然後問：要有哪些東西到位，它才會出現？一個疲倦的身體、一陣噪音、一個期待、一種反應的習慣。然後問：這些當中，哪一個本來可以是別的樣子？聽者這時已經看到了這個教法的兩半：事物是從許多條件生起的；而其中有些條件是搆得著的。十二支與空，可以之後再講。

Sentences you can use 可以直接用的句子

  1. The Buddha’s formula is very simple: when this is present, that comes to be; when this is absent, that does not.

    佛陀的公式非常簡單：這個在，那個就出現；這個不在，那個就不出現。

  2. Nothing happens from one cause. A fire needs a spark, and it also needs fuel and air.

    沒有任何事是出於單一的因。一場火需要火花；它也需要燃料與空氣。

  3. Hume noticed that we never see a force passing from cause to effect. The Buddha agreed, and then asked which conditions we can change.

    休謨注意到：我們從來看不到有一股力從因傳到果。佛陀同意；然後問：哪些條件是我們能改變的？

  4. Dependent origination is not fate. A thing that depends on conditions can be changed by changing them.

    緣起不是命運。一樣依靠條件的東西，可以藉著改變條件而被改變。

  5. Between a feeling and the craving that follows it there is a small gap. Practice is learning to notice it.

    在一個感受，與跟在它後面的渴愛之間，有一道小小的縫隙。修行就是學著注意到它。

  6. “Empty” means empty of independent existence. A flower is full of sun, rain, soil, and time.

    「空」的意思是：沒有獨立的存在。一朵花裡，滿滿的是陽光、雨水、土壤與時間。

Phrases to avoid 要避開的說法

“Everything is connected.” 「萬物都相連。」

True and useless. Say what depends on what, and which conditions can be changed.

是真的，卻沒有用處。要說出什麼依靠什麼，以及哪些條件可以改變。

“Everything happens for a reason.” 「凡事發生都有它的道理。」

Heard as purpose or destiny. Say “everything arises from conditions.” Conditions are not intentions.

會被聽成目的或天命。要說「凡事都從條件生起」。條件不是意圖。

“It all starts with ignorance.” 「一切都從無明開始。」

Makes ignorance a first cause. The chain is a circle, and ignorance has conditions of its own.

把無明講成了第一因。那條鏈子是一個圈；而無明也有它自己的條件。

“Quantum physics has proved dependent origination.” 「量子物理已經證明了緣起。」

Borrowed authority that a physicist in the room will reject. The teaching is about experience and stands without it.

借來的權威；在場的物理學家會不接受。這個教法講的是經驗；不靠它也站得住。

Reading check · 理解測驗

Did it land? 讀懂了嗎

1. What is the general formula of dependent origination?

2. What does the simile of the two sheaves of reeds show?

3. Which two extremes does the teaching to Kaccāna avoid?

4. On what do Hume and the Buddha agree?

5. Where does the likeness between Hume and the Buddha end?

Write · 你怎麼講？

  1. Take one recent moment of irritation or worry. List at least five conditions that had to be present for it, and mark the ones you could have changed. About 150 words.

    拿最近的一次煩躁或擔憂。列出至少五個「必須到位，它才會出現」的條件；並且標出哪些是你本來可以改變的。約 150 個字。

  2. Explain dependent origination to a friend in five English sentences without using the words “connected,” “karma,” or “universe.”

    用五個英文句子向一位朋友解釋緣起；不要用 connected、karma、universe 這三個字。

  3. Hume said we never observe a necessary connection. Write 200 words on whether this helps or hurts the Buddhist argument that suffering can end.

    休謨說，我們從來觀察不到必然的連結。用 200 個字寫出：這對「苦可以止息」的佛教論證是幫助，還是損害？
