What Makes a Good Argument? 什麼是好的論證？

Validity, soundness, and the discovery that the shape of an argument can be judged apart from what it is about.
有效、健全，以及一個發現：論證的形狀，可以跟它在談什麼分開來評判。

A2 Big Questions · 大哉問Thinking Tools · 思考的工具Advanced · 高級964 words · about 25 min
The big idea · 大觀念

A good deductive argument does two separate jobs: its conclusion follows from its premises, and its premises are true. Logic can check the first without knowing anything about the second.

一個好的演繹論證要做到兩件各自獨立的事：結論從前提推得出來，而且前提是真的。邏輯可以檢查第一件，而完全不必知道第二件。

The essay · 課文

What Makes a Good Argument? 什麼是好的論證？

01Reasons on the table 把理由攤在桌上

In ordinary speech an argument is a quarrel. In philosophy it is something quieter and more useful: a set of statements, the premises, offered as reasons for accepting another statement, the conclusion. To give an argument is to put your reasons on the table where other people can inspect them. There are then exactly two ways it can fail. One or more of the premises may be false, or the conclusion may not follow from the premises even if they are all true. The whole of logic grows out of keeping these two failures apart.

在日常用語裡，argument 是吵架。在哲學裡，它安靜得多，也有用得多：一組陳述（前提）被提出來，當作接受另一個陳述（結論）的理由。提出論證，就是把你的理由攤在桌上，讓別人檢查。這樣一來，它失敗的方式恰好只有兩種：前提當中有一個或幾個是假的；或者，就算前提全是真的，結論也推不出來。整門邏輯學，都是從把這兩種失敗分開而長出來的。

02Validity: a matter of form 有效：形式的問題

Take the most famous example in the subject. All humans are mortal; Socrates is human; therefore Socrates is mortal. If you grant the two premises you cannot consistently deny the conclusion. An argument with this property is called valid: it is impossible for all of its premises to be true while its conclusion is false. The premises are then said to entail the conclusion.

拿這門學問裡最有名的例子來看。所有人都會死；蘇格拉底是人；所以蘇格拉底會死。如果你承認這兩個前提，就不可能不自相矛盾地否認結論。有這種性質的論證叫作「有效」：它的前提不可能全部為真而結論為假。這時我們說，前提「蘊涵」結論。

Aristotle (384–322 BCE) saw that this necessity has nothing to do with Socrates or with death. It holds by virtue of the pattern alone. Replace the terms with letters, as he was the first to do, and the pattern still compels: if A belongs to every B, and B belongs to every C, then A belongs to every C. Whatever you put in for the letters, true premises will never lead to a false conclusion. With that step, the study of reasoning became a science of forms.

亞里斯多德（西元前 384–322 年）看出，這種必然性跟蘇格拉底無關，也跟死亡無關；它單單憑著那個樣式成立。像他首創的那樣，把詞項換成字母，這個樣式照樣有約束力：如果 A 屬於每一個 B，而 B 屬於每一個 C，那麼 A 屬於每一個 C。不管你把什麼填進字母裡，真的前提永遠不會導出假的結論。跨出這一步，對推理的研究就成了一門關於形式的科學。

03Validity is not truth 有效不等於真

The definition has a consequence that surprises almost everyone at first. Validity says nothing about whether the premises are in fact true. “All fish can fly; all whales are fish; therefore all whales can fly” is a perfectly valid argument. Every statement in it is false, and yet the conclusion follows: if the premises were true, the conclusion would have to be.

這個定義有一個後果，幾乎每個人初次聽到都會吃驚：有效性完全不管前提事實上是不是真的。「所有的魚都會飛；所有的鯨都是魚；所以所有的鯨都會飛」是一個完全有效的論證。裡面每一句都是假的，可是結論推得出來：假如前提為真，結論就非真不可。

The reverse also happens. “All poets are human; Li Bai is human; therefore Li Bai is a poet” has two true premises and a true conclusion, and it is invalid. The quickest way to see this is to keep the form and change the content: all poets are human; Socrates is human; therefore Socrates is a poet. The premises are still true and the conclusion is now false, so the form cannot be trusted, and it never could. This is the method of counterexample, and it is the logician’s basic instrument.

反過來的情況也有。「所有詩人都是人；李白是人；所以李白是詩人」有兩個真前提、一個真結論，卻是無效的。要看出這一點，最快的辦法是保留形式、更換內容：所有詩人都是人；蘇格拉底是人；所以蘇格拉底是詩人。前提還是真的，結論卻變成假的了，可見這個形式靠不住，而且從來就靠不住。這就是反例法，邏輯學家最基本的工具。

04Soundness: validity plus truth 健全：有效加上真

An argument that is valid and also has true premises is called sound. Soundness is what we actually want, because only a sound argument guarantees its conclusion. The division of labor is worth noticing. Whether an argument is valid can be settled from an armchair. Whether its premises are true usually cannot: that depends on biology, or history, or whatever else the premises bear on. Logic transmits truth from premises to conclusion. It does not supply the truth in the first place.

既有效、前提又都為真的論證，叫作「健全」。健全才是我們真正要的，因為只有健全的論證保證它的結論。這裡的分工值得留意。一個論證有沒有效，坐在扶手椅上就能判定。它的前提是不是真的，通常不行：那要看生物學、歷史，或前提所涉及的任何別的領域。邏輯把真從前提傳送到結論；它並不一開始就供應真。

05Four patterns worth knowing by name 四個值得記住名字的樣式

Much everyday reasoning runs on “if.” Two patterns are valid. Modus ponens: if P then Q; P; therefore Q. Modus tollens: if P then Q; not Q; therefore not P. Each has an invalid twin that looks almost the same. Affirming the consequent: if it rained, the street is wet; the street is wet; therefore it rained. (A street cleaner may have passed.) Denying the antecedent: if it rained, the street is wet; it did not rain; therefore the street is not wet. A wet street does not rule out other causes, and that is all it takes for both twins to fail.

日常推理有一大半是靠「如果」在運轉的。有兩個樣式有效。肯定前件（modus ponens）：如果 P 則 Q；P；所以 Q。否定後件（modus tollens）：如果 P 則 Q；非 Q；所以非 P。它們各有一個長得幾乎一樣的無效雙胞胎。肯定後件：如果下過雨，街道是溼的；街道是溼的；所以下過雨。（也許是洗街車剛經過。）否定前件：如果下過雨，街道是溼的；沒下過雨；所以街道不是溼的。街道溼，並不排除別的原因；單憑這一點，兩個雙胞胎就都不成立。

06When the conclusion goes beyond the premises 當結論超出前提

Everything so far concerns deduction, where the premises are meant to guarantee the conclusion. Most reasoning in science and in life is of another kind. From “every swan observed so far has been white” to “all swans are white” is an inductive step: the conclusion says more than the premises do, and so it can be false even when they are true. Inductive arguments are therefore never valid, and it would be a mistake to blame them for that. They are judged by strength, and a strong one makes its conclusion probable. Whether that standard can itself be justified is a famous problem, taken up in lesson A8.

到目前為止談的都是演繹：前提要保證結論。科學與生活裡的推理，大半屬於另一種。從「目前觀察到的每一隻天鵝都是白的」到「所有天鵝都是白的」是歸納的一步：結論說的比前提多，所以即使前提為真，結論也可能為假。因此歸納論證永遠不是有效的，而為此責怪它是弄錯了對象。評判它的標準是「強度」：強的歸納論證使結論很可能為真。這個標準本身能不能被證成，是一個著名的難題，留到 A8 那一課。

07Three inventions 三次發明

Logic was invented at least three times. In China the later Mohists, in the third century BCE, analyzed disputation and held that an explanation must bring out the reason for a claim: 以說出故. In India the Nyāya school set out a five-step inference that became the common property of Hindu and Buddhist philosophers: the hill has fire; because it has smoke; wherever there is smoke there is fire, as in a kitchen; this hill has smoke; therefore it has fire.

邏輯至少被發明過三次。在中國，西元前三世紀的後期墨家分析了辯論，主張解說必須把一個主張的理由提出來：「以說出故」。在印度，正理派定出了五支的推論，後來成為印度教與佛教哲學家的共同資產：此山有火；因為有煙；凡有煙處必有火，如廚房；此山有煙；所以此山有火。

The Indian pattern differs from Aristotle’s in one instructive way. It insists on an example, the kitchen, so that the general rule is tied to a case both parties have seen. A Greek syllogism asks only whether the conclusion follows. An Indian inference asks in addition how the rule is known. That second question, as we shall see, is exactly where Aristotle’s critics attacked.

印度的樣式跟亞里斯多德的有一個很有啟發性的差別：它堅持要舉一個例子（廚房），把普遍規則繫在雙方都見過的個案上。希臘的三段論只問結論推不推得出來；印度的推論還多問一句：這條規則是怎麼知道的？我們接下來會看到，批評亞里斯多德的人，攻擊的正是這第二個問題。

08Why form matters 形式為什麼重要

It may seem that validity is trivially easy to get, and in a sense it is: for any conclusion at all, one can invent premises that entail it. That is why a valid argument settles nothing by itself, and why serious disagreement is nearly always about premises. Yet the discipline of form is what makes such disagreement productive. Set an opponent’s reasoning out in valid form, supplying in its strongest version whatever was left unsaid, and you will see which premise carries the weight. You then know precisely what the two of you disagree about, and in turn what evidence would bear on it. Philosophers call this the principle of charity. It is good manners, and it is also the fastest way to find out who is right.

有效性似乎輕而易舉就能到手；在某個意義上確實如此：不管什麼結論，都可以編出蘊涵它的前提。正因為這樣，一個有效的論證本身什麼也沒解決，而認真的分歧幾乎總是關於前提的。可是，讓這種分歧有成果的，正是對形式的講究。把對手的推理整理成有效的形式，沒說出口的部分用最強的版本替他補上，你就會看出是哪一個前提在承重。這時你確切知道你們兩人到底在爭什麼，進而知道什麼證據跟它有關。哲學家稱之為「善意理解原則」。這是好的風度，同時也是找出誰對的最快方法。

Thought experiment · 思想實驗

Try it: valid, sound, or neither? 親手做一次：有效、健全，還是都不是？

Six short arguments. For each, decide first whether the conclusion follows. Only if it does is there a second question: are the premises all true? Do not let a true conclusion, or a false one, make the decision for you.

六個短論證。每一個先判斷結論推不推得出來；推得出來，才有第二個問題：前提全是真的嗎？別讓結論的真假替你做決定。

Valid, premises all true有效，前提全真Sound: the conclusion must be true健全：結論必真
Valid, a premise false有效，有假前提Unsound: the conclusion may be true or false不健全：結論可真可假
Invalid, premises all true無效，前提全真Unsound: the conclusion may be true or false不健全：結論可真可假

Primary text · 原典

In his own words 原典怎麼說

The Prior Analytics is the first treatise on formal logic in any language. It opens by saying what a deduction is; three chapters later, letters replace words.

《前分析篇》是任何語言裡第一部形式邏輯的專論。它開頭先說明什麼是演繹；三章之後，字母取代了詞語。

Aristotle, Prior Analytics I.1–4 亞里斯多德《前分析篇》卷一第一至四章 · translated by A. J. Jenkinson (public domain)

Did he really say that? · 這句話真的是他說的嗎？

“All men are mortal; Socrates is a man; therefore Socrates is mortal.”「所有人都會死；蘇格拉底是人；所以蘇格拉底會死。」

Not in Aristotle · 亞里斯多德沒有這樣寫

The world’s best-known syllogism appears nowhere in Aristotle’s logical works. He stated his patterns with letters and seldom used a named individual as a term. The Socrates example is a schoolroom tradition of much later date; by 1843 Mill could treat it as the standard specimen. It is still a fair illustration of the pattern Aristotle discovered.

全世界最有名的三段論，在亞里斯多德的邏輯著作裡找不到。他用字母陳述他的樣式，很少拿一個有名有姓的個人當詞項。蘇格拉底這個例子是很久以後才有的課堂傳統；到了 1843 年，彌爾已經把它當成標準範例。用它來說明亞里斯多德發現的那個樣式，仍然是恰當的。

Both sides · 正反兩面

Objections and replies 反駁與回應

Objection 1 · 反駁

Deduction can never teach us anything. If the conclusion is already contained in the premises, a valid argument only tells us what we knew.

演繹永遠不能教我們任何東西。如果結論已經包含在前提裡，有效論證只是把我們已經知道的事再說一遍。

Reply · 回應

Logical containment is not psychological possession. The axioms of geometry contain the Pythagorean theorem, and no one who learns the axioms thereby knows the theorem. Deduction is informative for limited minds, and ours are limited. How an inference can be both necessary and informative is nevertheless a real puzzle, sometimes called the scandal of deduction.

邏輯上的包含，不等於心理上的擁有。幾何學的公理包含了畢氏定理，但沒有人學了公理就因此知道那條定理。對有限的心靈來說，演繹帶來新知，而我們的心靈是有限的。不過，一個推論怎麼能既是必然的、又帶來新知，確實是個難題，有人稱之為「演繹的醜聞」。

Objection 2 · 反駁

The rules of logic cannot be justified without using them. Any proof that modus ponens is reliable will rely on modus ponens.

邏輯規則沒辦法不靠它們自己來證成。任何證明「肯定前件」可靠的證明，都會用到肯定前件。

Reply · 回應

Lewis Carroll dramatized this in 1895. His Tortoise accepts the premises of a valid argument and refuses the conclusion until the rule of inference is written down as a further premise, and then asks for another, without end. The usual moral is that a rule of inference is not one more premise. It is something we do with premises, and at some point one must simply reason. Whether that amounts to a justification or to an admission that there is none is still argued.

路易斯・卡羅在 1895 年把這個問題寫成了一場戲。他筆下的烏龜接受一個有效論證的前提，卻不肯接受結論，除非先把推論規則寫下來當作又一個前提；寫下來之後，牠又要求再一個，沒完沒了。通常引出的教訓是：推論規則不是多一個前提，它是我們拿前提來「做」的事，到了某一點人就是得動手推理。這究竟算是一種證成，還是承認沒有證成，至今仍有爭論。

Objection 3 · 反駁

Validity is beside the point. People are not moved by syllogisms, and the arguments that matter in life are never deductive.

有效性根本不是重點。人不會被三段論打動，而人生中重要的論證從來不是演繹的。

Reply · 回應

The second half is nearly right: most reasoning that matters is inductive or practical. But the deductive standard still does work there. It lets us ask what would have to be added to make an argument valid, and the added premise is usually the one worth fighting about. As for persuasion, logic was never offered as a description of how people are moved. It describes what they would be right to be moved by.

後半句幾乎是對的：重要的推理大半是歸納的或實踐的。但演繹的標準在那裡仍然派得上用場。它讓我們問：要補上什麼，這個論證才會有效？而被補上的那個前提，通常就是最值得爭的那一個。至於說服力，邏輯從來不是要描述人實際上被什麼打動，它描述的是人被什麼打動才是對的。

Terms of art · 哲學術語

The vocabulary of the subject 這門學問的用語

premise前提

L. praemissa ‘(proposition) set in front’

A statement offered as a reason for the conclusion.

被提出來當作結論之理由的陳述。

valid有效的

L. validus ‘strong’

Such that the premises cannot all be true while the conclusion is false. Said of arguments, never of statements.

前提不可能全真而結論為假。只用來形容論證，不用來形容陳述。

sound健全的

Valid, and with premises that are all true.

有效，而且前提全部為真。

entail蘊涵

Premises entail a conclusion when it follows from them necessarily.

結論必然從前提推出時，我們說前提蘊涵結論。

syllogism三段論

Gk. syllogismos ‘a reckoning together’

In Aristotle, any deduction; later, an argument with two premises and three terms.

在亞里斯多德是任何演繹；後來專指兩個前提、三個詞項的論證。

deduction / induction演繹／歸納

L. deducere ‘to lead down’; inducere ‘to lead in’

Reasoning whose premises are meant to guarantee the conclusion; reasoning whose premises are meant to make it probable.

前提要保證結論的推理；前提要使結論很可能為真的推理。

modus ponens肯定前件

L. ‘the mode that affirms’

If P then Q; P; therefore Q.

如果 P 則 Q；P；所以 Q。

modus tollens否定後件

L. ‘the mode that denies’

If P then Q; not Q; therefore not P.

如果 P 則 Q；非 Q；所以非 P。

petitio principii乞題、循環論證

L. ‘assuming the starting point’

Assuming in the premises the very thing to be proved.

在前提裡預設了正要證明的那件事。

Advanced English · 高級英文

Words and phrases worth keeping 值得帶走的字與片語

grant(v.)（姑且）承認

If you grant the two premises you cannot consistently deny the conclusion.

如果你承認這兩個前提，就不可能不自相矛盾地否認結論。

by virtue of(prep. phr.)憑藉、由於

It holds by virtue of the pattern alone.

它單單憑著那個樣式成立。

hold(v.)成立、為真

The necessity holds whatever the argument is about.

不管論證在談什麼，這個必然性都成立。

compel(v.)迫使（人接受）

Replace the terms with letters and the pattern still compels.

把詞項換成字母，這個樣式照樣有約束力。

from an armchair(adv. phr.)不靠實地調查、光憑思考

Whether an argument is valid can be settled from an armchair.

一個論證有沒有效，坐在扶手椅上就能判定。

bear on(phr. v.)與……有關、涉及

That depends on whatever the premises bear on.

那要看前提所涉及的是什麼。

rule out(phr. v.)排除

A wet street does not rule out other causes.

街道溼，並不排除別的原因。

trivially(adv.)輕而易舉地；不足道地

Validity may seem trivially easy to get.

有效性似乎輕而易舉就能到手。

in turn(adv. phr.)進而、接著

You know what you disagree about, and in turn what evidence would bear on it.

你知道你們在爭什麼，進而知道什麼證據跟它有關。

beg the question(v. phr.)乞題（預設了待證的結論）

An argument whose premises assume its conclusion begs the question.

前提預設了結論的論證，犯了乞題。

  1. Find a short argument in today’s news or in an advertisement. Set it out as numbered premises and a conclusion, supplying any premise that was left unsaid. Is it valid? Which premise would you challenge?

    從今天的新聞或一則廣告裡找一個短論證。把它整理成編號的前提與結論，沒說出口的前提替它補上。它有效嗎？你會質疑哪一個前提？

Dharma and the West · 佛法與西方哲學

A view from the Dharma 佛法怎麼看

☸

Buddhist philosophers had a logic of their own, hetuvidyā, the “science of reasons,” known in Chinese as 因明. Around 500 CE the logician Dignāga reduced the Nyāya’s five steps to three. Thesis: sound is impermanent. Reason: because it is produced. Example: whatever is produced is impermanent, like a pot; and nothing permanent is produced, like space.

佛教哲學家有自己的邏輯：hetuvidyā，「關於理由的學問」，漢譯「因明」。大約西元 500 年，邏輯學家陳那把正理派的五支簡化成三支。宗：聲是無常。因：所作性故（因為它是造作出來的）。喻：凡所作者皆是無常，如瓶（同喻）；凡是常者皆非所作，如虛空（異喻）。

Set beside Aristotle’s syllogism, the form is close: the example states the general rule, the reason applies it, and the thesis follows. The difference is the pot. A Buddhist inference is incomplete until the rule has been tied to a case that both parties accept, and tested against a contrasting case. Mill complained that the syllogism never tells us how its general premise is known. The Indian logicians had built an answer to that complaint into the form itself, some thirteen centuries earlier. Whether an example can really support a universal rule is, of course, the problem of induction again (Dharma and the West, D14).

跟亞里斯多德的三段論擺在一起，形式很接近：喻陳述普遍規則，因把它應用上去，宗就隨之成立。差別在那只瓶子。佛教的推論要等到規則繫在雙方都接受的個案上，並且用一個相反的個案檢驗過，才算完整。彌爾抱怨三段論從不告訴我們，它的普遍前提是怎麼知道的。印度的邏輯學家早了大約一千三百年，就把對這個抱怨的回答做進了形式本身。當然，一個例子到底能不能支持一條普遍規則，那又是歸納問題了（佛法與西方哲學 D14）。
