A18 · Right and Wrong 對與錯

What Makes an Action Right? 是什麼讓一件事是對的？

An honest shopkeeper and three explanations of why he does right: the results, the rule, and the kind of person he is. Mill, Kant, and Aristotle at their strongest, their hardest cases, and their Chinese counterparts.
一位誠實的店主，以及「他為什麼做得對」的三種解釋：結果、規則，以及他是哪一種人。彌爾、康德與亞里斯多德各自最強的版本、最難的案例，還有他們在中國的對應者。

A18 Big Questions · 大哉問Right and Wrong · 對與錯Advanced · 高級1,421 words · about 25 min
The big idea · 大觀念

The three great theories of ethics agree about most of what we should do and disagree about why. The disagreement matters in the cases where the reasons point in different directions, and those are the cases that test a life.

倫理學的三大理論，對於我們該做的事大多意見一致，卻對「為什麼」意見分歧。這個分歧，在種種理由指向不同方向的那些情況裡才要緊；而考驗一個人一生的，正是那些情況。

The essay · 課文

What Makes an Action Right? 是什麼讓一件事是對的？

01The honest shopkeeper 誠實的店主

A child is sent to buy bread and hands the shopkeeper a large note. The shopkeeper could keep some of the change, and the child would never know. He counts it out correctly. Everyone agrees that he has done the right thing. The question of this lesson is what makes it right, and three answers have dominated Western philosophy. The act is right because of what it brings about: the child is not cheated, the family eats, people can trust the shops. Or it is right because of the rule it follows: one must not deceive, whatever one might gain. Or it is right because it is what an honest man does, and honesty is part of a good human life. Each answer picks out a different feature of the same act. A theory of ethics takes one of them to be the foundation.

一個孩子被派去買麵包，遞給店主一張大鈔。店主可以扣下一些零錢，而那個孩子永遠不會知道。他把零錢一分不少地數給了孩子。每個人都同意他做了對的事。這一課的問題是：是什麼使它成為對的？有三個答案主導了西方哲學。這個行為是對的，因為它帶來的結果：孩子沒有被騙，一家人有飯吃，人們可以信任店家。或者，它是對的，因為它所遵循的規則：不管能得到什麼，都不可以欺騙。或者，它是對的，因為這是一個誠實的人會做的事，而誠實是美好人生的一部分。每一個答案，都挑出了同一個行為的一項不同的特徵。一套倫理學理論，就是把其中之一當作地基。

02Results 結果

The first theory was met in the last lesson. For the utilitarian, the right act is the one that produces the most well-being for everyone it affects, each person counting for one and nobody for more than one. Its strength is its impartiality. It has no place for the claim that my happiness matters more because it is mine, or that the suffering of a distant stranger matters less, and most of the moral reforms of the last two centuries, from the abolition of slavery to the protection of animals, were argued for in its terms. The Chinese philosopher Mozi had made the same move in the fifth century BCE. The disorders of the world, he taught, come from partiality, and the remedy is to “regard other people’s states as one regards one’s own, other people’s families as one regards one’s own, and other people’s persons as one regards one’s own.” He judged every policy by whether it benefited the people.

第一種理論，上一課已經遇過了。對效益主義者來說，對的行為就是為所有受影響的人產生最多福祉的那一個；每個人算一個，沒有人算超過一個。它的力量在於不偏私。它容不下這樣的主張：我的幸福比較重要，因為那是我的；或者，一個遠方陌生人的苦比較不重要。過去兩個世紀大多數的道德改革，從廢除奴隸制到保護動物，都是用它的語言來論證的。中國哲學家墨子在西元前五世紀就走了同一步。他教導，天下的禍亂來自「別」（偏私），對治的辦法是「視人之國若視其國，視人之家若視其家，視人之身若視其身」。他評判每一項政策，看的是它是否利於人民。

03Duty 義務

Kant begins from a different observation. “Nothing can possibly be conceived in the world, or even out of it, which can be called good, without qualification, except a good will.” Intelligence, courage, and happiness itself can all be put to bad use. Only the will to do right is good in every circumstance, and its goodness does not depend on what it achieves. He uses the shopkeeper as his example. A prudent tradesman “does not overcharge, but keeps a fixed price for everyone, so that a child buys of him as well as any other.” People are honestly served. But if he does it because cheating would be bad for business, his act agrees with duty and has no moral worth. It has moral worth only if he does it because it is right.

康德是從另一個觀察出發的。「在世界之內，甚至在世界之外，除了一個善的意志，不可能設想任何東西可以無條件地稱為善。」聰明、勇氣，乃至幸福本身，都可能被拿去做壞事。只有「要做對的事」的那個意志，在每一種情境下都是善的；而它的善，並不取決於它成就了什麼。他用的例子就是那位店主。一位精明的商人「不抬價，對每個人都維持固定的價格，所以一個小孩向他買東西，跟任何別人一樣划算」。人們得到了誠實的服務。但是，如果他這樣做是因為欺騙對生意不好，他的行為只是「合乎」義務，並沒有道德價值。只有當他是因為這樣做是對的才這樣做，它才有道德價值。

How do we know what duty requires? Kant’s test is that I must be able to will the principle I act on as a law for everyone: “Act only on that maxim whereby thou canst at the same time will that it should become a universal law.” Suppose I need money and consider borrowing it with a promise to repay that I know I cannot keep. If everyone in need did this, no one would believe such promises, and the practice I am trying to exploit would not exist. My plan works only if others do not act as I do. I am making an exception of myself, and that, for Kant, is the mark of wrongdoing. The strength of the theory is that it explains why a person may not be sacrificed for the sum. Mencius draws the same line when he says of the sages that they would not commit one unrighteous act or kill one innocent person, though they could gain the whole world by it.

我們怎麼知道義務要求什麼？康德的檢驗是：我必須能夠意願我據以行動的那條原則，成為每一個人的法則：「你要只依照這樣的準則行動：你能夠同時意願它成為一條普遍法則。」假設我需要錢，考慮去借，並且許下一個我明知無法兌現的還錢承諾。如果每一個有需要的人都這樣做，就不會有人相信這種承諾，而我想要利用的這個慣例也就不存在了。我的計畫，只有在別人不像我這樣做的時候才行得通。我是在把自己當成例外；而對康德來說，這就是作惡的標記。這個理論的力量，在於它解釋了為什麼不可以為了總和而犧牲一個人。孟子談到聖人時劃的是同一條線：「行一不義、殺一不辜而得天下，皆不為也。」

04Character 品格

The oldest of the three theories asks a different question. Aristotle wants to know what kind of life is good for a human being, and his answer is that we flourish when we do well the things that are distinctive of our kind: “human good turns out to be activity of soul in accordance with virtue,” and this “in a complete life. For one swallow does not make a summer.” A virtue is a settled trait of character, and each lies between two ways of going wrong. Courage is between cowardice and rashness, generosity between meanness and waste. The mean is not a matter of arithmetic. It is to feel and act “at the right times, with reference to the right objects, towards the right people, with the right motive, and in the right way.”

三種理論當中最古老的一個，問的是另一個問題。亞里斯多德想知道的是：對人來說，哪一種人生是好的？他的答案是：當我們把自己這個物種特有的事做好的時候，我們就活得興旺：「人的善，原來就是靈魂合乎德行的活動」，而且是「在完整的一生當中。因為一隻燕子造不成夏天」。一項德行是一種穩定的品格特質；每一項都位於兩種出錯的方式之間。勇敢在怯懦與魯莽之間，慷慨在吝嗇與揮霍之間。這個「中道」並不是算術問題。它是「在對的時候、對於對的事、向著對的人、出於對的動機、以對的方式」去感受與行動。

No rule can say in advance what that is. It is seen by a person of practical wisdom, who has become wise through experience, and virtue itself is acquired by practice: “we become just by doing just acts, temperate by doing temperate acts, brave by doing brave acts.” On this view the honest shopkeeper is no longer tempted. He does not consult a rule or calculate an outcome. Cheating a child is simply not something he would do. Confucius describes the same ideal. The gentleman, he says, “has nothing he is set on and nothing he is set against. He sides with what is right.” The strength of the theory is that it is about a whole life, and that it finds a place for the feelings, the relationships, and the judgment that the other two leave out.

沒有任何規則能預先說出那是什麼。那要由一個有實踐智慧的人來「看出」，而他是透過經驗才變得有智慧的；德行本身，則是靠練習得來的：「我們做公正的事而成為公正的人，做節制的事而成為節制的人，做勇敢的事而成為勇敢的人。」照這個看法，那位誠實的店主已經不再受到誘惑了。他不查閱規則，也不計算結果。欺騙一個孩子，根本不是他會做的事。孔子描述的是同一個理想：「君子之於天下也，無適也，無莫也，義之與比。」這個理論的力量，在於它談的是整個人生，並且為另外兩種理論所遺漏的情感、關係與判斷找到了位置。

05Where each one strains 每一個吃緊的地方

Each theory has a case that embarrasses it. The utilitarian’s is the surgeon of the last lesson, or the sheriff who hangs an innocent man to stop a riot. Kant’s was put to him in his lifetime by the French writer Benjamin Constant. A murderer comes to your door and asks whether your friend, whom he means to kill, is hiding in your house. Kant replied that even here you may not lie. Truthfulness is a duty owed to everyone, and a lie, however kindly meant, damages the basis of all agreements. Few readers have followed him, and his defenders have worked ever since to show that his principles do not require the answer he gave. The difficulty for Aristotle is of another kind. Told to do what the virtuous person would do, I may fairly ask how to tell who the virtuous person is, and it seems that I must already know what is right in order to pick him out.

每一種理論，都有一個讓它難堪的案例。效益主義者的，是上一課的那位外科醫師，或是為了平息暴動而吊死一個無辜者的警長。康德的，是他在世時法國作家貢斯當對他提出的。一個兇手來到你家門口，問你的朋友（他打算殺掉的那個人）是不是藏在你家裡。康德回答：即使在這裡，你也不可以說謊。誠實是對每一個人都負有的義務；而一個謊言，不管出於多大的善意，都損害了一切約定的基礎。很少有讀者跟著他走；而為他辯護的人，從那時起就一直在努力證明，他的原則並不要求他所給的那個答案。亞里斯多德的困難是另一種。有人告訴我「去做有德之人會做的事」，我大可以問：怎麼分辨誰是有德之人？而看來我必須已經知道什麼是對的，才挑得出他來。

06The father who stole a sheep 攘羊的父親

One case from the Analects separates the three more cleanly than any trolley. The governor of She said to Confucius: “In my district there is a man called Upright Gong. His father stole a sheep, and the son gave evidence against him.” Confucius replied: “The upright in my district are different. A father covers for his son, and a son covers for his father. Uprightness lies in that.” The governor speaks for impartial law. A theft is a theft, and justice does not ask who the thief is. Confucius holds that the bond between parent and child is the root of all moral feeling, and that a society in which children inform on their parents has cut that root, whatever it gains in recovered sheep. Mozi would have sided with the governor, and so, on most readings, would Kant, though he would add that the son need not volunteer what he is not asked. Aristotle, who gave two books of his Ethics to friendship and family, is closer to Confucius. The quarrel is alive today wherever a law requires relatives to testify.

《論語》裡有一個案例，把這三者分得比任何電車都乾淨。葉公對孔子說：「吾黨有直躬者，其父攘羊，而子證之。」孔子回答：「吾黨之直者異於是：父為子隱，子為父隱，直在其中矣。」葉公代表的是不偏私的法。偷就是偷，正義不問偷的人是誰。孔子主張：親子之間的連結是一切道德情感的根；一個子女告發父母的社會，已經把那條根砍斷了，不管它找回了多少隻羊。墨子會站在葉公那一邊；照多數的讀法，康德也會，不過他會補充：沒有人問，兒子就不必主動說。亞里斯多德把《倫理學》的兩卷給了友愛與家庭，他比較接近孔子。凡是法律要求親屬作證的地方，這場爭論今天都還活著。

07Three lenses 三面鏡片

It would be convenient if one theory were simply correct. Two centuries of argument have not produced a winner, and many philosophers now doubt that there will be one. Derek Parfit spent his last years arguing that the best versions of the three are “climbing the same mountain on different sides.” The Oxford philosopher W. D. Ross held that we have several distinct duties, to keep faith, to do good, to avoid harm, to be just, and that no formula ranks them. When they conflict we can only weigh them in the case before us, which brings back Mencius’s word quán.

如果其中一種理論就是正確的，那會很方便。兩個世紀的爭論並沒有產生贏家，而許多哲學家現在懷疑將來也不會有。帕菲特晚年一直在論證：這三者最好的版本，是在「從不同的側面攀登同一座山」。牛津的哲學家羅斯主張：我們有好幾種各不相同的義務，守信、行善、避免傷害、公正，而沒有任何公式為它們排出高下。當它們衝突的時候，我們只能在眼前的個案裡權衡它們；這就把孟子的那個「權」字帶回來了。

In the meantime the theories can be used as lenses. Faced with a hard choice, one can ask three questions in turn. What will happen to everyone affected if I do this? Could I accept everyone’s acting on the principle I am about to act on, and am I using anyone? What would this choice make of me, and what would a person I admire do? When the three answers agree, as they do for the shopkeeper, one can act with confidence. When they disagree, one has at least found out where the difficulty lies.

在這之前，這些理論可以當作鏡片來用。面對一個困難的抉擇，可以依序問三個問題。如果我這樣做，所有受影響的人會怎麼樣？我能不能接受每個人都依照我即將依照的這條原則去行動？我有沒有在利用任何人？這個抉擇會把我造就成什麼樣的人？一個我敬佩的人會怎麼做？當三個答案一致的時候（就像那位店主的情況），人可以有把握地行動。當它們不一致的時候，至少已經找出困難在哪裡了。

Thought experiment · 思想實驗

Try it: three dilemmas, four advisers 親手做一次：三個兩難，四位顧問

Decide each case yourself. Then hear what Mill, Kant, Aristotle, and Confucius would advise and why. At the end you will see whose advice you followed most often.

每一個案例都先自己決定。然後聽聽彌爾、康德、亞里斯多德與孔子各會怎麼建議、為什麼。最後你會看到，你最常聽從的是誰的建議。

Primary text · 原典

In their own words 原典怎麼說

The first sentence of Kant’s book and the central definition of Aristotle’s. Each states a whole theory in a few lines.

康德那本書的第一句話，以及亞里斯多德那本書的核心定義。兩者都用幾行字陳述了一整套理論。

Kant, Fundamental Principles of the Metaphysic of Morals (1785); Aristotle, Nicomachean Ethics II.6 康德《道德形上學基礎》；亞里斯多德《尼各馬可倫理學》卷二第六章 · tr. T. K. Abbott; tr. W. D. Ross (both public domain; original wording kept)

Did he really say that? · 這句話真的是他說的嗎？

“Kant’s categorical imperative is the Golden Rule in philosophical dress.”「康德的定言令式，就是穿上哲學外衣的金律。」

Kant denied it · 康德否認

The comparison is made in countless textbooks, and Kant rejected it in a footnote to the Groundwork: “Let it not be thought that the common quod tibi non vis fieri, etc.” (what you do not wish done to yourself) “could serve here as the rule or principle.” His reasons are sharp. The Golden Rule appeals to what I happen to want, so a man who is content to receive no help could use it to excuse himself from helping. And a criminal could invoke it against the judge who sentences him. Kant’s test asks what a rational being could will as a law, whatever anyone’s wishes. Confucius had given the negative form more than two thousand years earlier, when asked for one word to live by: “Is it not reciprocity? What you do not want for yourself, do not do to others.”

無數的教科書都作過這個類比；而康德在《道德形上學基礎》的一個註腳裡否定了它：「不要以為那句通俗的 quod tibi non vis fieri（己所不欲）之類的話，可以在這裡充當準繩或原則。」他的理由很銳利。金律訴諸的是我碰巧想要什麼；所以一個甘願得不到任何幫助的人，可以拿它來為自己不幫助別人開脫。而一個罪犯，可以拿它來對付判他刑的法官。康德的檢驗問的是：一個理性的存在者能夠意願什麼成為法則，不管任何人的願望是什麼。孔子早在兩千多年前就給了它否定式的表述；有人請他說出一個可以終身奉行的字，他說：「其恕乎！己所不欲，勿施於人。」

Both sides · 正反兩面

Objections and replies 反駁與回應

Objection 1 · 反駁

If the experts have disagreed for two thousand years, there is no fact of the matter. Ethics is opinion.

如果專家們爭論了兩千年還沒有共識，那就表示根本沒有事實可言。倫理學只是意見。

Reply · 回應

The disagreement is narrower than it looks. All three theories condemn cruelty, cheating, and cowardice and commend honesty and generosity. They differ over the explanation and over a small number of hard cases. Physics is in a similar state: its practitioners agree about what will happen when a ball is dropped and argue about what quantum theory means. Lasting dispute over foundations is compatible with a great deal of knowledge.

這個分歧比看起來的窄。三種理論都譴責殘忍、欺騙與怯懦，都讚許誠實與慷慨。它們的分歧在於「解釋」，以及為數不多的困難案例。物理學也處於類似的狀態：物理學家對於一顆球放手後會怎樣意見一致，卻對量子理論是什麼意思爭論不休。對基礎的長久爭論，與擁有大量的知識是可以並存的。

Objection 2 · 反駁

Kant’s ethics is cold. It says that a man who helps others because he loves them deserves less credit than one who helps from duty while feeling nothing.

康德的倫理學是冷的。它說：一個因為愛人而幫助別人的人，比一個毫無感覺、只是出於義務而幫忙的人，更不值得稱許。

Reply · 回應

The poet Schiller made the joke in Kant’s lifetime: I gladly serve my friends, but alas, I do it with pleasure. Kant’s point was narrower. He wanted to isolate what gives an act its moral worth, and chose cases where inclination is absent so that duty could be seen working alone. He did not say that warm feelings are bad. He held that they are unreliable, since they come and go and do not extend to people we dislike, and that morality must not depend on them. Aristotle’s reply is that a person who has to force himself is not yet good.

詩人席勒在康德在世時就開過這個玩笑：我樂於為朋友效勞，可惜啊，我是帶著愉快去做的。康德的重點比較窄。他想要把「賦予一個行為道德價值的東西」分離出來，所以選了沒有愛好參與的情況，好讓義務單獨運作時可以被看見。他並沒有說溫暖的情感是壞的。他主張的是：情感靠不住，因為它們來來去去，而且延伸不到我們不喜歡的人身上；道德不可以依賴它們。亞里斯多德的回應是：一個必須勉強自己的人，還不算是好人。

Objection 3 · 反駁

Virtue ethics gives no guidance. “Be brave, be just” tells me nothing when I do not know what bravery or justice requires here.

德行倫理學不給指引。當我不知道勇敢或公正在這裡要求什麼的時候，「要勇敢，要公正」什麼也沒告訴我。

Reply · 回應

Its defenders answer that the other theories do no better in hard cases, where the utilitarian cannot make the calculation and the Kantian cannot find the right maxim. What virtue ethics offers is different in kind. It gives a vocabulary of several hundred words, such as callous, petty, loyal, and fair, each of which carries guidance, and it directs attention to education, since a person who has been brought up well will see what to do where a formula would fail. Confucian ethics rests on the same wager.

為它辯護的人回答：在困難的案例裡，另外兩種理論也沒有做得更好；效益主義者算不出那筆帳，康德主義者找不到正確的準則。德行倫理學提供的是另一種東西。它給了一套好幾百個詞的語彙，例如冷酷、小氣、忠誠、公平，每一個詞都帶著指引；它並且把注意力導向教育，因為一個教養得好的人，在公式失靈的地方看得出該怎麼做。儒家倫理押的是同一注。

Dharma and the West · 佛法與西方哲學

A view from the Dharma 佛法怎麼看

☸

All three lenses are found in the Buddha’s teaching, and none is made the foundation. He tells his young son Rāhula to examine every act before, during, and after doing it, as one looks in a mirror, and to ask: does this “lead to hurting myself, hurting others, or hurting both?” That is a test by consequences. He gives lay followers five precepts and monastics several hundred rules, which look like duties. And he says that what makes an act unwholesome is its root in greed, hatred, or delusion, and sums up the whole teaching as “not to do any evil; to embrace the good; to purify one’s mind,” which is the language of character.

這三面鏡片在佛陀的教導裡都找得到，而沒有哪一面被當作地基。他告訴年幼的兒子羅睺羅：每一個行為，在做之前、做的時候、做了之後，都要像照鏡子那樣檢視它，並且問：這會不會「導致傷害自己、傷害別人，或兩者都傷害」？那是一個依後果的檢驗。他給在家弟子五戒，給出家眾好幾百條戒律，那些看起來像義務。而他又說，使一個行為成為不善的，是它根植於貪、瞋或癡；並且把整個教導總結為「諸惡莫作，眾善奉行，自淨其意」，那是品格的語言。

Modern scholars have argued over which Western theory this most resembles. One camp reads it as a virtue ethics close to Aristotle’s, and another reads the Mahāyāna as a form of consequentialism aimed at the welfare of all beings. A companion page examines the question and gives a verdict.

現代的學者一直在爭論：這最像西方的哪一種理論？一派把它讀成接近亞里斯多德的德行倫理學；另一派把大乘讀成一種以一切眾生福祉為目標的後果論。姊妹篇會檢視這個問題，並且給出判定。
