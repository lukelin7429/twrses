A30 · Beauty and Art 美與藝術

Is Beauty in the Eye of the Beholder? 美是主觀的嗎？

Two people look at one painting and disagree. Hume’s wine tasters and the key in the barrel, Kant’s universal voice, Zhuangzi’s fish, Mencius’s cook, Pythagoras’s strings, and a three-part test of your own eye.
兩個人看同一幅畫，意見不同。休謨的品酒人與酒桶裡的鑰匙、康德的「普遍的聲音」、莊子的魚、孟子的廚子、畢達哥拉斯的琴弦；還有一個分三部分的測驗，測的是你自己的眼睛。

A30 Big Questions · 大哉問Beauty and Art · 美與藝術Advanced · 高級1,080 words · about 25 min
The big idea · 大觀念

The last lesson asked what art is. This one asks about its oldest companion. When we call something beautiful, are we describing the thing or only reporting how it makes us feel? Most people give one answer in theory and the other in practice.

上一課問的是藝術是什麼。這一課問的是它最古老的同伴。當我們說某樣東西美的時候，我們是在描述那樣東西，還是只在報告它讓我們有什麼感覺？大多數人在理論上給一個答案，在實踐上給另一個。

The essay · 課文

Is Beauty in the Eye of the Beholder? 美是主觀的嗎？

01No disputing about taste 品味沒什麼好爭的

Two friends stand in front of a painting. One says it is beautiful and the other says it is ugly. If they were arguing about its width, they would fetch a ruler. For beauty there is no ruler, and an old proverb says that it is pointless to dispute about taste. David Hume, in an essay of 1757, gave the argument behind the proverb in its strongest form: “Beauty is no quality in things themselves: It exists merely in the mind which contemplates them; and each mind perceives a different beauty.” To look for the real beauty of a thing is as fruitless as to look for the real sweet or the real bitter.

兩個朋友站在一幅畫前面。一個說它美，另一個說它醜。如果他們爭的是畫有多寬，他們會去拿一把尺。對於美，沒有尺；而一句古老的諺語說，品味是沒什麼好爭的。大衛・休謨在 1757 年的一篇文章裡，把這句諺語背後的論證用最強的形式說了出來：「美不是事物本身的性質：它只存在於觀照事物的心裡；而每一顆心看到的美都不一樣。」去尋找一樣東西「真正的美」，就像去尋找「真正的甜」或「真正的苦」一樣徒勞。

Hume then pointed out that nobody believes this. Anyone who claimed that a forgotten versifier was the equal of Milton would be thought to defend “no less an extravagance, than if he had maintained a mole-hill to be as high as Teneriffe.” We say that taste is personal, and we also say that some people have bad taste. A theory of beauty has to explain how both can be said.

接著休謨指出：沒有人真的相信這一點。誰要是宣稱一位被遺忘的打油詩人與彌爾頓平起平坐，人們會認為他所辯護的荒謬，「不亞於主張一個鼴鼠丘跟特內里費峰一樣高」。我們說品味是個人的事；我們也說有些人品味很差。一個關於美的理論，必須解釋這兩句話怎麼能同時說得出口。

02The key in the barrel 酒桶裡的鑰匙

Hume’s answer begins with a story from Don Quixote. Two of Sancho Panza’s kinsmen were asked to judge a barrel of wine. One tasted it and pronounced it good, except for a slight taste of leather. The other agreed that it was good, except for a taste of iron. Everyone laughed at them. When the barrel was emptied, there was found at the bottom “an old key with a leathern thong tied to it.”

休謨的回答，從《唐吉訶德》裡的一個故事開始。桑丘・潘薩的兩個親戚被請去品評一桶酒。一個嘗了，宣布酒是好的，只是有一點點皮革味。另一個也同意酒是好的，只是有一點鐵味。大家都嘲笑他們。等到酒桶倒空了，人們在桶底發現了「一把舊鑰匙，上面繫著一條皮繩」。

The pleasure of the wine was in the drinkers, but the leather and the iron were in the barrel, and some palates could detect them where others could not. Hume calls this delicacy of taste and holds that it can be trained. A true judge needs five things: “Strong sense, united to delicate sentiment, improved by practice, perfected by comparison, and cleared of all prejudice.” The joint verdict of such judges, he says, “is the true standard of taste and beauty.” We find them, in practice, by the test of time: “The same Homer, who pleased at Athens and Rome two thousand years ago, is still admired at Paris and at London.”

酒帶來的愉快是在喝的人身上；但皮革與鐵是在桶子裡的，而有些人的味覺嘗得出來，有些人嘗不出來。休謨稱之為品味的「細膩」，並且主張它是可以訓練的。一位真正的評判者需要五樣東西：「健全的理智，結合細膩的感受，因練習而增進，因比較而完善，並且清除了一切偏見。」他說，這樣的評判者共同的裁決，「就是品味與美的真正標準」。在實際上，我們是用時間的考驗來找到他們的：「兩千年前在雅典與羅馬討人喜歡的同一位荷馬，今天在巴黎與倫敦仍然受人讚賞。」

03A universal voice 一個普遍的聲音

Kant thought Hume’s judges could not settle the matter, since an expert’s say-so does not make me feel anything. He looked more closely at what we mean. If I say “Canary wine is pleasant,” I am quite content to be corrected: “It is pleasant to me.” No one argues about that. If I say that a rose or a poem is beautiful, I mean more. I am not reporting a private sensation. In Kant’s words, “we believe that we speak with a universal voice, and we claim the assent of every one.” I expect you to see it too, and I think something is missing in you if you do not.

康德認為，休謨的評判者解決不了這件事；因為專家說了算，並不會讓我感覺到什麼。他更仔細地看我們到底是什麼意思。如果我說「加那利的葡萄酒很可口」，別人糾正我「是『對我來說』可口」，我完全沒有意見。沒有人為這個爭論。如果我說一朵玫瑰或一首詩是美的，我的意思就多了一些。我並不是在報告一個私人的感覺。用康德的話說：「我們相信自己是用一個普遍的聲音在說話，並且要求每一個人的同意。」我期待你也看得到；如果你看不到，我會覺得你身上少了點什麼。

What entitles me to this, Kant argued, is that the pleasure is disinterested. Taste is “the faculty of judging of an object … by an entirely disinterested satisfaction.” I do not want to eat the rose, own it, or use it. Since my liking rests on nothing peculiar to my appetites or my situation, I suppose that it rests on what I share with every other mind. A judgment of beauty is therefore subjective, being a feeling and not a measurement, and it still makes a claim on everyone. It cannot be proved. I can only say “look again” and point.

康德論證說，使我有資格這樣要求的，是這份愉快是「無利害的」。品味是「藉著一種完全無利害的滿足……來評判一個對象的能力」。我並不想吃那朵玫瑰、擁有它，或使用它。既然我的喜歡並不建立在我的胃口或我的處境所特有的任何東西上，我就假定它建立在我與其他每一顆心共有的東西上。所以，一個關於美的判斷是主觀的，因為它是一種感受而不是一次測量；而它仍然對每個人提出了要求。它無法被證明。我只能說「再看一次」，然後用手指給你看。

04The fish and the cook 魚與廚子

The two positions had been stated in China two thousand years earlier. Zhuangzi took the skeptic’s side and made it wider than Hume’s. Mao Qiang and Lady Li were the women that people thought most beautiful, he says. “Fish, seeing them, dived deep. Birds, seeing them, flew high. Deer, seeing them, ran away. Which of the four knows the true beauty of the world?” Human taste is the taste of one species.

這兩個立場，兩千年前在中國就已經被說出來了。莊子站在懷疑者那一邊，而且把它推得比休謨更寬。他說：「毛嬙、麗姬，人之所美也；魚見之深入，鳥見之高飛，麋鹿見之決驟。四者孰知天下之正色哉？」人類的品味，只是一個物種的品味。

Mencius accepted the point about species and turned it around. Within one kind, he argued, natures are alike. “Mouths have the same preferences in flavors.” That is why the whole world follows the cook Yi Ya, who found out first what all our mouths enjoy. Ears are alike, and so the world follows the music master Kuang. Eyes are alike too: anyone who cannot see that Zidu is handsome “has no eyes.” Mencius’s interest was in morals, and he drew the conclusion that hearts must be alike as well. But the argument is Hume’s. Agreement in taste is evidence of a common human nature, and those who notice first what the rest of us will come to like are its experts.

孟子接受了關於物種的那一點，然後把它倒過來用。他論證說：在同一類之內，本性是相似的。「口之於味，有同嗜也。」這就是為什麼天下人都跟著廚子易牙走；他先發現了我們所有人的嘴喜歡什麼。耳朵是相似的，所以天下人跟著樂師師曠。眼睛也相似：誰要是看不出子都的俊美，就是「無目者也」。孟子關心的是道德，他得出的結論是：人心必定也是相似的。但這個論證就是休謨的論證。品味上的一致，是共同人性的證據；而那些先注意到「我們其餘的人終將喜歡什麼」的人，就是其中的行家。

05Beauty in the numbers 數字裡的美

The oldest claim that beauty is in the object comes from the followers of Pythagoras. They found that two strings sound well together when their lengths stand in simple ratios: two to one gives the octave, three to two the fifth, four to three the fourth. Harmony, it seemed, was arithmetic made audible. The idea that beauty is proportion ruled Greek architecture and sculpture and returned in the Renaissance. Its best-known modern form, the claim that people prefer rectangles in the golden ratio of about 1.62 to 1, has not held up well. Gustav Fechner reported such a preference in 1876, and later experiments have found it weak or absent.

「美在對象身上」這個主張，最古老的版本來自畢達哥拉斯的追隨者。他們發現：兩根弦的長度成簡單的比例時，合在一起就好聽：二比一是八度，三比二是五度，四比三是四度。看起來，和諧就是聽得見的算術。「美就是比例」這個觀念，支配了希臘的建築與雕塑，並在文藝復興時回來。它最出名的現代形式，是「人們偏好長寬比約 1.62 比 1 的黃金比例長方形」這個主張；而它並沒有站得很穩。古斯塔夫・費希納在 1876 年報告了這樣的偏好；後來的實驗發現它很弱，或者根本不存在。

Psychology has found other regularities. Infants a few months old look longer at the faces that adults rate as attractive. Faces close to the average of a population, and symmetrical ones, are preferred in many cultures. People also come to like shapes, tunes, and even nonsense words more simply through having met them before, which means that some agreement in taste is habit. The evidence supports neither extreme. Part of what we find beautiful comes with being human, part comes from what we have grown used to, and part depends on a trained eye.

心理學找到了別的規律。幾個月大的嬰兒，注視成年人評為有吸引力的臉，時間比較久。接近一個族群平均值的臉，以及對稱的臉，在許多文化裡都比較受喜愛。人們也會只因為以前見過，就比較喜歡某些形狀、曲調，甚至無意義的字；這表示品味上的一致，有一部分是習慣。證據兩個極端都不支持。我們覺得美的東西，一部分是生而為人就有的，一部分來自我們習慣了什麼，一部分取決於一雙訓練過的眼睛。

06What the proverb leaves out 那句諺語漏掉的

“Beauty is in the eye of the beholder” is true in the sense that Hume and Kant both granted. Without a mind that feels, nothing is beautiful. The proverb leaves out that the eyes of beholders are built much alike, that an eye can be more or less acute, and that it can be educated. A teacher who shows a class how a poem works is trying to bring the students to hear something they did not hear before, and their preferences are left to them. If beauty were only private feeling, there would be nothing for the teacher to show.

「美在觀看者的眼裡」這句話，在休謨與康德都承認的那個意義上是真的。沒有一顆會感受的心，就沒有任何東西是美的。這句諺語漏掉的是：觀看者的眼睛，構造大致相同；眼睛可以比較敏銳或比較不敏銳；而且眼睛可以被教育。一位老師向全班說明一首詩是怎麼運作的，是想讓學生聽見他們以前沒聽見的東西；至於偏好，仍然由他們自己決定。如果美只是私人的感受，老師就沒有什麼可以指給人看的了。

Thought experiment · 思想實驗

Try it: three tests of your eye 親手做一次：測測你的眼睛

First, a test with right answers: find the square that differs. Second, a test with none: choose the rectangle you like best. Third, three statements to accept or reject. Together they place you between Zhuangzi and Pythagoras.

第一關有正確答案：找出不一樣的那個方塊。第二關沒有：選你最喜歡的長方形。第三關：三句話，接受或不接受。合起來，它們會把你放在莊子與畢達哥拉斯之間的某個位置。

Did he really say that? · 這句話真的是他說的嗎？

“Beauty is no quality in things themselves: It exists merely in the mind which contemplates them.” (Hume’s view of beauty)「美不是事物本身的性質：它只存在於觀照事物的心裡。」（休謨對美的看法）

His sentence, stating a view he goes on to correct · 是他的句子；陳述的是一個他接下來要修正的看法

The sentence is in “Of the Standard of Taste,” and it is quoted everywhere as Hume’s own position. In the essay it belongs to a paragraph that sets out “a species of philosophy” which holds that all sentiment is right and that seeking real beauty is fruitless. Hume calls its conclusion a proverb that has “attained the sanction of common sense,” and in the next paragraph he says that “there is certainly a species of common sense which opposes it.” The remaining three-quarters of the essay argue that there is a standard after all. He never takes back the claim that beauty is a sentiment. What he denies is that every sentiment is as good as every other. The familiar English saying that beauty is in the eye of the beholder is later, and is usually traced in that wording to a novel of 1878.

這個句子在〈論品味的標準〉裡；而它到處被引用，當作休謨自己的立場。在那篇文章裡，它屬於這樣的一段：那一段陳述「某一種哲學」，主張一切感受都是對的，尋找真正的美是徒勞的。休謨說，它的結論是一句已經「取得了常識的認可」的諺語；而在下一段他就說：「確實有另一種常識反對它。」文章其餘的四分之三，論證的是終究有一個標準。他從來沒有收回「美是一種感受」這個主張。他否認的是：每一種感受都跟其他任何一種一樣好。英文裡那句耳熟能詳的「美在觀看者的眼裡」出現得比較晚；就這個措辭而言，通常追溯到 1878 年的一部小說。

Both sides · 正反兩面

Objections and replies 反駁與回應

Objection 1 · 反駁

The “test of time” only shows which works the powerful chose to preserve and teach. Homer survived because schoolmasters made boys read him.

「時間的考驗」只顯示了有權勢的人選擇保存、選擇教授哪些作品。荷馬之所以流傳下來，是因為教書先生逼著男孩子讀他。

Reply · 回應

There is truth in this, and Hume’s list of approved authors shows the limits of his century. Two things can be said in reply. Much that was imposed has been forgotten as soon as the pressure stopped, so preservation by authority is not enough to explain survival. And works have crossed into cultures that had no reason to defer to their origin. Tang poems are read in translation by people who owe nothing to the Tang court.

這話有道理；而休謨那張受認可作者的名單，顯示了他那個世紀的侷限。有兩點可以回應。許多被強加的東西，壓力一停就被遺忘了；所以單靠權威的保存，不足以解釋流傳。此外，有些作品跨進了毫無理由要對其源頭表示敬意的文化。讀著唐詩譯本的人，並不欠唐朝的朝廷什麼。

Objection 2 · 反駁

If beauty were a real feature of things, science would have found it by now. Physics has no term for it.

如果美是事物真實的特徵，科學到現在早該找到它了。物理學裡沒有它這一項。

Reply · 回應

Physics has no term for red either, only for wavelengths. Redness is how certain surfaces look to creatures with eyes like ours, and it would be odd to conclude that nothing is really red. Beauty may be a property of that kind: real, and dependent on the kind of observer. This is roughly where Hume ends up, and it is what the passages from Zhuangzi and Mencius say when they are read together.

物理學裡也沒有「紅」這一項，只有波長。紅，是某些表面在擁有像我們這樣的眼睛的生物看來的樣子；而如果由此斷定沒有任何東西真的是紅的，那會很奇怪。美也許是那一類的性質：真實，而且取決於觀察者是哪一種。這大致就是休謨最後抵達的地方；而莊子與孟子的那兩段話放在一起讀，說的也是這個。

Objection 3 · 反駁

Kant’s disinterested pleasure is a fiction. No one looks at anything without wants, memories, and a position in society.

康德的「無利害的愉快」是虛構的。沒有人看任何東西的時候，不帶著欲望、記憶與自己在社會裡的位置。

Reply · 回應

The sociologist Pierre Bourdieu argued that taste marks social class and that the “pure” gaze is the luxury of those who do not have to worry about use. Kant need not deny that taste is used in this way. His claim concerns what we are entitled to say. To call a thing beautiful and add that I like it because it flatters me or will sell well is to withdraw the claim. The ideal may seldom be met and still be what the word means.

社會學家皮耶・布赫迪厄主張：品味標示著社會階級；而那種「純粹的」凝視，是不必操心用途的人才有的奢侈。康德不必否認品味被這樣使用。他的主張關乎我們有資格說什麼。說一樣東西美，然後補一句「我喜歡它，是因為它討好我」或「它賣得好」，就等於收回了那個說法。這個理想也許很少被達到，卻仍然是這個詞的意思。

Terms of art · 哲學術語

The vocabulary of the subject 這門學問的用語

taste品味、鑑賞力

The capacity to perceive and judge beauty. In the eighteenth century, the central term of aesthetics.

感知與評判美的能力。在十八世紀，是美學的核心術語。

sentiment感受、情感

Hume’s word for a feeling of approval or disapproval, as opposed to a judgment of fact.

休謨的用語：一種贊許或不贊許的感受，相對於對事實的判斷。

delicacy of taste品味的細膩

The ability to detect fine qualities and small differences in what one perceives.

在所感知的東西裡，察覺細微的性質與微小差別的能力。

disinterested無利害的

Not depending on any desire to own, use, or consume the object. Not the same as uninterested.

不取決於任何想要擁有、使用或消耗那個對象的欲望。不等於 uninterested（不感興趣）。

subjective universality主觀的普遍性

Kant’s term for a judgment that rests on feeling and still claims everyone’s agreement.

Dharma and the West · 佛法與西方哲學

A view from the Dharma 佛法怎麼看

☸

The Buddhist analysis sides with Hume on where beauty is found and goes on to ask what it does. Pleasant feeling arises when a sense organ, an object, and attention come together. It belongs to that meeting and is not a property of the object. What we call the beauty of a thing is a perception laid over the contact, shaped by habit and desire. The texts treat this perception as trainable, as Hume does, and they train it in the opposite direction. A monk troubled by desire for bodies is taught to contemplate the body as a collection of parts, hair, nails, teeth, skin, until the perception of beauty stops arising of itself. The aim is freedom. The practice does not hold that bodies are in truth ugly. It holds that a mind which cannot help finding things attractive is not in command of itself.

佛教的分析，在「美在哪裡」這個問題上站在休謨這邊，然後進一步問：它起了什麼作用？樂受，是在感官、對象與作意相遇的時候生起的。它屬於那個相遇，並不是對象的性質。我們所謂一樣東西的「美」，是覆蓋在觸之上的一種想，由習慣與欲望所塑造。經典跟休謨一樣，把這種想看成可以訓練的；而它們是往相反的方向訓練它。一位被對身體的欲望所困擾的比丘，被教導去觀察身體是一堆部分的集合：髮、爪、齒、皮……直到「美」的想不再自動生起。目的是自由。這個修習並不是主張身體其實是醜的。它主張的是：一顆不由自主地覺得東西有吸引力的心，並不能自己作主。

The same tradition contains some of the earliest nature poetry in the world, composed by monks and nuns who had finished that training. Mahākassapa, the strictest ascetic among the Buddha’s disciples, describes the hills where he lived: “The rain comes down on the lovely flats, in the mountains frequented by seers. Echoing with the cries of peacocks, these rocky crags delight me!” The line is repeated like a refrain. It shows that the end of craving does not end the sense of beauty. What is left is delight without the wish to own or keep, which is very close to what Kant meant by disinterested pleasure. On this account disinterested pleasure is the ordinary condition of a mind that has stopped grasping (Dharma and Western Philosophy D25).

同一個傳統裡，有世界上最早的一批自然詩；作者是完成了那個訓練的比丘與比丘尼。摩訶迦葉是佛陀的弟子當中最嚴格的頭陀行者；他這樣描寫他所住的山：「雨落在可愛的平地上，在仙人常到的群山之間。孔雀的叫聲迴盪著：這些岩石的山崖使我歡喜！」這一句像副歌一樣反覆出現。它顯示：渴愛的止息，並沒有終結美感。留下來的，是不帶著「想要擁有、想要留住」的歡喜；這跟康德所說的「無利害的愉快」非常接近。照這個說法，無利害的愉快，是一顆不再抓取的心的平常狀態（佛法與西方哲學 D25）。
