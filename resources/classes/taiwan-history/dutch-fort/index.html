Big idea · 一句話看懂

The Dutch East India Company wanted a base for trading with China. At Ming officials’ request it left Penghu for Tayouan, where it traded Chinese silk to Japan and built a fort. But the island was not empty: Siraya villages lived there, and the Company soon fought, made treaties, collected taxes, and relied on Chinese farmers. A trading post became a colony.
荷蘭東印度公司想要一個和中國貿易的據點。它在明朝官員的要求下撤出澎湖、移到大員，在那裡把中國的生絲轉賣到日本，也蓋了一座城。但這座島不是空的：西拉雅族的村社就住在這裡；公司很快就開始打仗、訂條約、收稅，也依靠漢人農民。一個貿易據點，變成了殖民地。

    Unit 5: Why Did the Dutch Build a Fort in Tainan?

    In the early 1600s, the Dutch East India Company, a trading company from the Netherlands, wanted to trade with China. Ming officials would not let it set up a base on China’s coast, or on the Penghu Islands. In September 1624, at their request, the Company left Penghu and moved to Tayouan, a sandbar near today’s Anping in Tainan. The understanding was that Chinese merchants would be allowed to come and trade there.

    1600 年代初，來自荷蘭的貿易公司「荷蘭東印度公司」想要和中國做生意。明朝官員不准它在中國沿岸設立據點，也不准它留在澎湖。1624 年 9 月，公司在明朝官員的要求下撤出澎湖，進駐大員——今天台南安平附近的一片沙洲。雙方的默契是：明朝會允許中國商人到這裡來貿易。

    At Tayouan the Company built a fort. Work began in 1624, the fort was named Zeelandia in 1627, and it was finished in 1634. A print showing Tayouan in 1632 has it on a hill by the water, with pointed corners, wooden fences on the slope, and ships in the bay. It became the Company’s military, trading, and government center in Taiwan.

    公司在大員蓋了一座城。1624 年開始興建，1627 年定名為熱蘭遮城，1634 年完工。一張畫出 1632 年大員景象的版畫上，城蓋在海邊的高地上，有尖尖的城角、山坡上有木柵欄，海灣裡停著船。它成為荷蘭人在台灣的軍事、貿易與行政中心。

    Tayouan was not empty land. Siraya villages lived nearby, among them Sinkan, Soulang, Bakloan, and Mattau. In 1627 Sinkan built a house for Company soldiers, but in 1629, 63 Company soldiers were killed at Mattau. In 1635 the Company attacked Mattau, with Sinkan warriors at its side, and burned the village. Mattau then signed a treaty, written by the Company, giving up its land. A trading post was turning into a colony.

    大員並不是沒有人的土地。西拉雅族的村社就住在附近，其中有新港、蕭壠、目加溜灣和麻豆。1627 年，新港社為公司的士兵蓋了一間房子；但 1629 年，有 63 名公司士兵死在麻豆社。1635 年，公司帶著新港社的戰士出兵麻豆社，燒了村莊；接著麻豆社簽下一份由公司起草的條約，交出了土地。一個貿易據點，正在變成殖民地。

    The Company’s main business was buying Chinese silk at Tayouan and selling it in Japan. From 1635 to 1639, raw silk and silk cloth made up 87 percent of everything the Company’s trading posts sent to Japan. Taiwan’s own best-known product was deerskins: Japanese traders said they had come to buy them six years before the Dutch, and in 1636 Taiwan exported 60,440 skins. Indigenous villagers hunted deer; from 1636 to 1645, the Company also licensed Chinese hunters.

    公司最主要的生意，是在大員買中國的生絲，運到日本賣。1635 到 1639 年間，公司各地商館運到日本的商品，有 87% 是生絲和絲織品。台灣本地最有名的產品是鹿皮：日本商人說，他們在荷蘭人來之前六年就來台灣買鹿皮了；1636 年，台灣出口了 60,440 張鹿皮。原住民族的村社獵鹿；1636 到 1645 年間，公司也發獵鹿執照給漢人獵戶。

    From the 1630s, Chinese farmers came to grow sugarcane and rice. The Company bought their sugar at a guaranteed price and sold it as far away as Japan and Persia, and every Chinese farmer paid a head tax. From 1644, Chinese traders bid for the right to be the only trader in a village. In 1652 Chinese settlers rose up against the Company, in what is called the Guo Huaiyi incident. Afterward, the Company built another fort, Provintia, at Chikan.

    從 1630 年代起，漢人農民來到台灣種甘蔗和稻米。公司用保證價格收購他們的糖，賣到日本，甚至遠到波斯；每一位漢人農民都要繳人頭稅。從 1644 年起，漢人商人用競標取得和一個社獨家交易的權利，這叫做贌社。1652 年，漢人移民起事反抗公司，史稱郭懷一事件。事件之後，公司在赤崁又蓋了另一座城：普羅民遮城（Provintia）。

    Did Taiwan make money for the Company? Historians disagree. Some read the account books as showing large profits; the economist Wu Tsong-min recalculated them and found a profit in only a few years. In 1662 the Company surrendered to Zheng Chenggong and left. That December, its council in Batavia had doubts about sending a force to retake Taiwan, and suggested looking for a trading harbor farther north instead.

    台灣到底有沒有替公司賺錢？歷史學者的看法不一樣。有人認為帳簿顯示利潤很大；經濟學者吳聰敏重新計算後發現，只有少數幾年有賺。1662 年，公司向鄭成功投降、離開台灣。同年 12 月，巴達維亞的評議會對出兵收復台灣有所保留，建議另外在北方找一個貿易港口。

    Questions · 閱讀理解

    Why did the Dutch East India Company move to Tayouan in 1624?

    Because Ming officials would not let it stay on the Penghu Islands or set up a base on China’s coast; at their request it moved to Tayouan, where Chinese merchants would be allowed to come and trade.

    The Company came to trade. Name two things it did that a simple trading post would not do.

    For example: it attacked Mattau, and a treaty written by the Company has Mattau giving up its land; it collected a head tax from Chinese farmers; it built another fort after the 1652 incident.

    In the deerskin trade, what did Indigenous villagers, Chinese hunters and traders, and the Company each do?

    Indigenous villagers hunted deer, and from 1636 to 1645 licensed Chinese hunters did too; from 1644, one Chinese trader per village, chosen by bidding, handled the village trade; the Company bought the deerskins and shipped them to Japan.

      Did Taiwan make money for the Company?台灣有沒有替公司賺錢？

      Nakamura Takashi (1997), as summarized by Wu中村孝志（1997），依吳聰敏的整理

      From the account books, most years after 1646 show a surplus, so the Company’s rule in Taiwan was a success; losing Taiwan in 1662 was a heavy blow, like losing a pearl from the palm of one’s hand (our translation of Wu’s Chinese wording).從帳簿看，1646 年以後多數年度都有盈餘，所以荷蘭人在台灣的經營是成功的；1662 年失去台灣，對公司來說正如「喪失掌中明珠般的大打擊」。

      吳聰敏〈從貿易與產業發展看荷治時期台灣殖民地經營之績效〉，《經濟論文叢刊》44:3（2016） ↗

      Van Veen (1996), as summarized by WuVan Veen（1996），依吳聰敏的整理

      From the early 1650s, the Company’s rule in Taiwan created large profits.從 1650 年代初期開始，公司在台灣的經營創造了很大的利潤。

      吳聰敏〈從貿易與產業發展看荷治時期台灣殖民地經營之績效〉，《經濟論文叢刊》44:3（2016） ↗

      Wu Tsong-min (2016)吳聰敏（2016）

      The account books do not show the real result: profits from Taiwan’s deerskins and silk were booked in Japan, and costs were left out. Recalculated, the colony made a profit in only a few years from the mid-1630s on. Wu argues that this explains why the Batavia council, in 1662, did not push to retake Taiwan.帳面上的盈虧不能反映真正的成效：台灣鹿皮與生絲的利潤記在日本商館的帳上，成本也沒有算進去。重新估算的結果，從 1630 年代中期到荷治末期，只有少數幾個年度有利潤。吳聰敏認為，這說明了巴城評議會 1662 年為什麼沒有積極收復台灣。

      吳聰敏〈從貿易與產業發展看荷治時期台灣殖民地經營之績效〉，《經濟論文叢刊》44:3（2016） ↗

      All three answers are reported here from Wu’s article, which sets out the two earlier views before giving its own; we read Wu’s summary, not the earlier books. They disagree mainly about how to read the same account books.三個答案都是依吳聰敏的論文轉述；他的論文先列出前兩位學者的看法，再提出自己的估算（我們讀的是吳文的摘述，不是前兩位的原書）。分歧主要在於：同一批帳簿應該怎麼讀。

      The rule is the same in every lesson: views are set side by side, each with its source, and the lesson does not choose.每一課的規則都一樣：說法並列、各附出處，課文不替誰選邊。

      History English · 歷史英語

      Eight sentences for talking about trade and power 談貿易與權力的八句英文

      Some of these describe trade; others report what a source says. Tap 🔊 to hear each one, then try swapping the words in bold.
      有幾句描述貿易；有幾句轉述資料怎麼說。點 🔊 聽每一句，再試著把粗體的部分換掉。

      1. The Company moved its base to Tayouan in 1624.公司在 1624 年把據點移到大員。moved its base to: changed where it worked from.moved its base to：把據點移到某地。
      2. The fort took ten years to build.這座城花了十年才蓋好。took … to build: how long a building took.took … to build：花了多久蓋好。
      3. Villagers traded deerskins in exchange for cloth and salt.村社的人用鹿皮換布和鹽。in exchange for: what you get back in a trade.in exchange for：交換得到的東西。
      4. Silk made up most of what the Company sent to Japan.公司運到日本的東西，大部分是生絲和絲織品。made up: was the largest part of a whole.made up：構成（整體的大部分）。
      5. According to a Company letter, 63 soldiers were sent to Mattau.依照公司的一封信，有 63 名士兵被派到麻豆社。According to: tells the listener whose account this is.According to：說明這是誰的說法。
      6. The treaty says that Mattau gave up its land.條約上寫著，麻豆社交出了土地。says that: reports what a document states, without claiming it is the whole story.says that：轉述文件上寫的內容，不代表那就是全部的事實。
      7. Historians disagree about whether Taiwan made money for the Company.歷史學者對台灣有沒有替公司賺錢看法不一。disagree about whether: introduces an open question.disagree about whether：提出一個還有爭論的問題。
      8. The sea in front of the fort has since become land.城前面的海，後來已經變成了陸地。has since become: a change from then until now.has since become：從那時到現在的改變。

      In Taiwan · 在台灣

      A wall with roots, a price list, a missionary, and a fixed price for sugar 爬滿樹根的牆、一張價目表、一位傳教士，和糖的保證價格

      Four short notes on what the Company left behind.
      四則關於公司留下了什麼的短短說明。

      A wall · 一段牆

      Banyan roots on Dutch bricks荷蘭磚牆上的榕樹根

      At Anping Old Fort, the only part of Fort Zeelandia left from the Dutch period is a brick wall outside the front gate, wrapped in the roots of old banyan trees. Most of what you see today is much later: the fort was rebuilt after the Japanese period, and in 1975 the city added a pointed roof to the observation tower and painted the wall white.
      安平古堡裡，熱蘭遮城在荷蘭時期留下來的，只有正門外的一段磚牆，被老榕樹的根緊緊纏住。今天看到的大多晚得多：日治時期之後重建，1975 年市政府在瞭望台上加了尖頂，並把牆漆成白色。

      A list · 一張表

      Deer legs for iron pots用鹿腿換鐵鍋

      In 1648 the Company posted fixed prices for trade in the villages: how many deer legs, deerskins, or bundles of rice a villager would give the village trader for salt, a length of cloth, or an iron pot. The historian Chiu Hsin-hui reads the list as a step to hold back the traders’ monopoly, after complaints that trade was unfair, and as a sign of how much the villages now wanted outside goods.
      1648 年，公司公告了村社交易的規定物價：一擔鹽、一段布或一個鐵鍋，村社的人要付給贌商幾隻鹿腿、幾張鹿皮或幾束稻穀。歷史學者邱馨慧認為，這張表是在交易被抱怨不公平之後，壓抑贌商壟斷的措施，也顯示出村社對外來物品的需求有多大。

      A missionary · 一位傳教士

      A preacher in Sinkan新港社的傳教士

      In 1627 the Dutch minister Georgius Candidius arrived in Taiwan, and by 1628 he was preaching in Sinkan. The Company’s ties with Sinkan were close, but they were never simple: in 1629, after a quarrel with Japanese traders, the Company raided Sinkan and demanded pigs and rice as a punishment.
      1627 年，荷蘭牧師甘迪留斯來到台灣，1628 年已經在新港社傳教。公司和新港社的關係很密切，但從來不單純：1629 年初，在和日本商人的糾紛之後，公司突襲新港社，要求交出豬和稻米作為懲罰。

      A price · 一個價格

      Sugar at a guaranteed price保證價格的糖

      The Company did not grow sugar itself. Chinese farmers grew the cane and Chinese workers made the sugar; the Company promised to buy it at a guaranteed price and shipped it to Japan and Persia. Sugar from Taiwan was first sent to Japan in 1636.
      公司自己不種甘蔗。甘蔗由漢人農民種、糖由漢人工人做；公司承諾用保證價格收購，再運到日本和波斯。台灣本地的糖，1636 年第一次運到日本。

      Myth vs. Fact · 常見迷思

      Four things people get wrong 四個常見的誤會

      ✗ Myth · 迷思Taiwan was empty when the Dutch came.荷蘭人來的時候，台灣沒有人住。

      ✓ Fact · 事實Siraya villages such as Sinkan and Mattau lived near Tayouan, and Chinese and Japanese traders were already coming to trade.大員附近就有新港、麻豆等西拉雅族村社，中國和日本的商人也早就來這裡交易。

      ✗ Myth · 迷思The Dutch came to Taiwan to settle and farm.荷蘭人來台灣是為了定居、種田。

      ✓ Fact · 事實The Company wanted a base for trade with China; it moved to Tayouan after leaving Penghu at Ming officials’ request. The sugar it sold was grown by Chinese farmers.公司要的是和中國貿易的據點；它在明朝官員的要求下撤出澎湖後，才移到大員。公司賣的糖，是漢人農民種的。

      ✗ Myth · 迷思Anping Old Fort today is the Dutch fort.今天的安平古堡就是荷蘭人蓋的城。

      ✓ Fact · 事實Only a brick wall on the south side is left of Fort Zeelandia from the Dutch period; the rest was rebuilt later.熱蘭遮城在荷蘭時期只留下南邊的一段磚牆，其餘都是後來重建的。

      ✗ Myth · 迷思Everyone agrees Taiwan made the Company rich.大家都同意台灣讓公司大賺一筆。

      ✓ Fact · 事實Historians disagree. Some read the account books as showing large profits; Wu Tsong-min found a profit in only a few years.歷史學者看法不一。有人認為帳簿顯示利潤很大；吳聰敏重新計算後，只有少數幾年有利潤。

      Remember It · 記憶口訣

      Four things to keep in your pocket 四句帶著走的話

      Trade first, then rule先貿易，後統治

      A trading post can turn into a colony.
      一個貿易據點，可以變成殖民地。

      Three groups, one trade三群人，一門生意

      Villagers, Chinese traders, and the Company all had a part.
      村社、漢人商人、公司都有一份。

      Whose letter?誰寫的信？

      Much of what we know was written by the Company.
      我們知道的很多事，是公司自己寫的。

      A print is a choice版畫是一種選擇

      It shows what its maker chose to show.
      它畫的是作者選擇要畫的。

      For Teachers · 給老師的提醒

      Curriculum links, violence, and whose voice 課綱對應、暴力事件，以及誰的聲音

      • Curriculum links (12-Year Basic Education, social studies, junior high history): 歷 Bb-Ⅳ-1 (the powers in East Asian waters in the 16th and 17th centuries) and 歷 Bb-Ⅳ-2 (contact between Indigenous peoples and outsiders).課綱對應（十二年國教社會領域，國中歷史）：歷 Bb-Ⅳ-1「十六、十七世紀東亞海域的各方勢力。」、歷 Bb-Ⅳ-2「原住民族與外來者的接觸。」
      • Hard history. The events at Mattau in 1629 and 1635, and the incident of 1652, involved killing. The lesson states what the sources say, without detail, and does not take sides; most of these sources are Company letters. The National Academy for Educational Research’s booklet asks students whether 1629 should be called an “incident” or a “war”; that is a good question to open with.困難的歷史。1629 年、1635 年的麻豆社事件和 1652 年的郭懷一事件都有殺戮。課文只寫出處說了什麼，不描寫細節，也不替任何一方說話；這些出處大多是公司的書信。國家教育研究院的補充教材問學生：這應該叫「事件」還是「戰爭」？可以用這個問題開場。
      • Whose voice. Almost everything about these years comes from Company documents. When you read a quotation, ask who wrote it and why, and what the Siraya villages or the Chinese settlers might have said.誰的聲音。這段時間的資料幾乎都來自公司的文件。讀到一段引文時，問一問是誰寫的、為什麼寫，西拉雅族的村社或漢人移民會怎麼說。
      • The model is a reconstruction from one print. Please tell students so, and let them compare the model and the print number by number.模型是依一張版畫做的示意。請跟學生說明這一點，也讓他們一個編號一個編號比對模型和版畫。

      Classroom Activity 1 · 課堂活動一

      Follow the Wind 跟著風走

      You need · 準備材料

      • The trade map on this page本頁的季風航路圖
      • Cards with the names of goods: silk, deerskins, sugar, silver, pepper, deer meat寫著貨物名稱的卡片：生絲、鹿皮、糖、白銀、胡椒、鹿肉

      Steps · 步驟

      1. In groups, place each goods card on a map: where does it start, and where does it go?分組把每一張貨物卡放到地圖上：它從哪裡出發？要去哪裡？
      2. Mark which season each trip happens in.標出每一段路是在哪一個季節走的。
      3. Check your answers on the trade map.用季風航路圖核對答案。
      4. Discuss: why was Tayouan a good place for the Company to stop?討論：為什麼大員是公司停靠的好地方？

      Ask students to explain one route in English, using “in exchange for” or “made up.”
      請學生用英文說明一條航路，用上 in exchange for 或 made up。

      Classroom Activity 2 · 課堂活動二

      Read a Company Letter 讀一封公司的信

      You need · 準備材料

      • Governor Putmans’s letter about the attack on Mattau (in the timeline, 1635)時間軸上（1635 年）普特曼斯長官描述出兵麻豆社的那封信

      Steps · 步驟

      1. Read the quotation together.一起讀那段引文。
      2. Answer: Who wrote it? To whom? What did he want them to think?回答：誰寫的？寫給誰？他希望對方怎麼想？
      3. In pairs, write two sentences that the people of Mattau might have written about the same days.兩人一組，寫兩句麻豆社的人可能會怎麼寫同樣這幾天。
      4. Share, and discuss: what can a letter tell us, and what can it not?分享並討論：一封信能告訴我們什麼？不能告訴我們什麼？

      Keep the focus on whose voice we hear; do not ask students to act out violence.
      重點放在「我們聽到的是誰的聲音」；不要讓學生演出暴力的場面。
