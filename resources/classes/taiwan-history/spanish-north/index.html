What Did the Spanish Leave in the North?西班牙人在北部留下了什麼？

Slide through the years from 1624 to 1668 on a map of the forts in the north and the south, and see who held each one. Then look at what is left: a church and its cemetery under the ground on Heping Island, a name on a park in Tamsui, and books about the northern languages that no one has found so far.
在一張標出南北據點的地圖上，從 1624 年拖到 1668 年，看每一個據點由誰駐守。再看看留下了什麼：和平島地底下的教堂和墓地、淡水一座園區的名字，還有記錄北部原住民語言、到現在還沒有被找到的書。

Big idea · 一句話看懂

Spanish soldiers and missionaries from Manila held a fort at Keelung for sixteen years, from 1626 to 1642, and one at Tamsui for about ten, among the Basay and other peoples of the north. They never controlled much land, and the books their missionaries wrote about the local languages have not been found. What they left is mostly under the ground: archaeology tells the part of the story the papers do not.
來自馬尼拉的西班牙士兵和傳教士，從 1626 到 1642 年在雞籠駐守了十六年，在淡水駐守了大約十年，身邊是巴賽人和北部其他族群。他們從來沒有控制多少土地，傳教士記錄當地語言的書也到現在都沒有找到。他們留下的，大多在地底下：文字沒有說的那一部分，要靠考古來講。

Unit 6: What Did the Spanish Leave in the North?

In 1626, two years after the Dutch settled at Tayouan in the south, Spanish forces sailed from Manila, in the Philippines, to the north of Taiwan. They wanted to counter the Dutch, to have a base for missions to China, and to draw Chinese and Japanese merchants to trade with them. In May they sailed into the harbor at Keelung, held a ceremony of possession on today’s Heping Island, and began building a fort called San Salvador.

1626 年，荷蘭人在南部的大員落腳兩年後，西班牙的軍隊從菲律賓的馬尼拉出發，來到台灣北部。他們想對抗荷蘭人、建立向中國傳教的據點，也想吸引中國和日本的商人來和他們交易。5 月，他們駛進雞籠港，在今天的和平島舉行佔領儀式，開始興建一座叫做聖薩爾瓦多的城。

In 1627, the Spanish demanded rice from a chief at Tamsui, and an ambush followed. The next year they sent soldiers and built a second fort, Santo Domingo, on a hill above the mouth of the Tamsui River. Supplies, and silver from the Americas for trade, came from Manila on ships twice a year, but the ships were often late.

1627 年，西班牙人向淡水的原住民首領要米糧，結果招致了一場誘殺事件。第二年，他們派兵在淡水河口的山頂上蓋了第二座城：聖多明哥城。物資，還有做生意用的美洲白銀，都靠每年兩次從馬尼拉來的補給船，但船常常誤期。

The Spanish never controlled much land. They kept only two or three hundred soldiers in the north, and most of the time they stayed inside their forts. Around them lived the Basay, whom Spanish records describe as skilled traders, and, around Tamsui, the Ketagalan. Some villages became allies; others fought back. In 1636, villagers at Tamsui, angry at a tax of chickens and rice, attacked the fort at night.

西班牙人從來沒有控制多少土地。他們在北部只派了兩、三百人駐守，多數時候都待在堡壘裡。在他們周圍住著巴賽人（西班牙文獻描述他們很擅長交易），淡水一帶則是凱達格蘭族的活動範圍。有些部落和他們結盟，有些起來反抗。1636 年，淡水的原住民不滿雞和米的課稅，夜襲了城砦。

Spanish missionaries learned the local languages. Jacinto Esquivel, in Taiwan from 1631 to 1633, wrote a vocabulary of the language of the people of Tamsui, and Teodoro Quirós wrote a grammar of “the language of Formosa.” They may have been about varieties of Basay. But no copy of these books has been found so far; we know of them from old catalogs. The oldest word lists of Taiwan’s languages that do survive are Dutch-era lists of languages farther south.

西班牙的傳教士學了當地的語言。1631 到 1633 年在台灣的 Jacinto Esquivel，寫過一本淡水原住民語言的詞彙；Teodoro Quirós 寫過一本「福爾摩沙語」的文法。它們記錄的可能是巴賽語的幾種方言。但是這些書到現在一本都還沒有被找到；我們是從舊的書目知道它們的。今天留得下來、最早的台灣語言詞彙，是荷蘭時期記錄更南邊語言的詞彙。

Trade with Japan stopped after 1633, many of the Spanish in Taiwan fell ill and died, and fewer Chinese ships came than hoped. In 1637 the Spanish decided to give up Tamsui. On August 21, 1642, the Dutch attacked Keelung, and four days later the Spanish surrendered. Their time in the north had lasted sixteen years. The Dutch stayed in the north for about nineteen years; in 1661, pressed by local villages, they burned their fort at Tamsui and left.

1633 年以後和日本的貿易中斷，在台灣的西班牙人很多生病死去，來北台灣的中國商船也比預期少。1637 年，西班牙人決定放棄淡水。1642 年 8 月 21 日，荷蘭人進攻雞籠，四天後西班牙人投降。他們在北部一共待了十六年。荷蘭人在北部待了大約十九年：1661 年，在當地原住民的壓力下，他們放火燒了淡水的堡壘，撤離了淡水。

So what did the Spanish leave? Excavations on Heping Island began in 2011, and a Taiwan–Spain team dug again from 2016 and widened the dig in 2019. They have uncovered the foundations of a church, Todos los Santos, and its cemetery. One person was buried with hands folded in prayer. Early findings suggest some of the people came from Europe, others from Asia, and possibly some from Africa. The site, which also holds much older layers, is now a national archaeological site.

那麼，西班牙人留下了什麼？和平島的發掘從 2011 年開始，台灣與西班牙的團隊 2016 年起再度發掘，2019 年擴大範圍。他們挖出一座教堂（諸聖教堂）和它的墓地的基礎。有一個人下葬時雙手合十。初步的發現認為，這些人有的來自歐洲、有的來自亞洲，可能也有人來自非洲。這個遺址還有年代早得多的文化層，現在是國定考古遺址。

Questions · 閱讀理解

Why did the Spanish come to northern Taiwan in 1626?

To counter the Dutch, who had settled at Tayouan in 1624; to have a base for missions to China; and to draw Chinese and Japanese merchants to trade with them.

The Spanish missionaries wrote books about the northern languages. Why can’t we read them?

Because no copy of them has been found so far. We know they existed only because their titles were listed in old catalogs.

Name two ways the Indigenous peoples of the north shaped what happened to the Spanish and the Dutch.

For example: some villages became allies while others fought back; Tamsui villagers attacked the Spanish fort in 1636 over a tax; villages on the south bank of the Tamsui River rose against the Dutch in 1655; and in 1661 local pressure helped push the Dutch out of Tamsui.

Did the Spanish colony in the north matter?西班牙在北部的據點重要嗎？

An older view, as described by Cruz Berrocal較早的看法（依 Cruz Berrocal 的描述）

In Taiwan · 在台灣

A market street, silver from the Americas, beads, and a red-haired fort 一條市街、美洲來的白銀、珠子，和一座紅毛城

Four short notes on the north in the 1600s.
四則關於 1600 年代北台灣的短短說明。

A street · 一條街

The Parián of Keelung雞籠的「澗內」

Near Dasha Bay at Keelung, the Spanish set up a Parián, a Chinese market street.
西班牙人在雞籠的大沙灣附近，設了一條華人的街市，叫做「澗內」（Parián）。

A coin · 一枚銀幣

Silver from the Americas美洲來的白銀

The Spanish forts depended on supply ships from Manila, twice a year. They brought soldiers, food, and silver from the Americas to pay for trade. When a ship was late, the forts ran short.
西班牙的城堡靠每年兩次從馬尼拉來的補給船。船上載著士兵、物資，還有拿來做生意的美洲白銀。船一誤期，城裡就缺東西。

Beads · 珠子

The Basay, good at trade擅長交易的巴賽人

A researcher on the Heping Island dig says Spanish records describe the Basay as people good at trade; among the goods they traded were agate and glass beads. Objects left by the Basay have been found at the site.
和平島發掘計畫的研究員說，西班牙文獻描述巴賽人很擅長交易，交易的商品裡有瑪瑙珠和玻璃珠。遺址裡也挖到巴賽人留下的各種生活物品。

A name · 一個名字

Why “Red-Haired Fort”?為什麼叫「紅毛城」？

At Tamsui, the Dutch built Fort Antonio near the old Spanish site. According to the New Taipei City guide, local people called the Dutch red-haired people, so the fort became Hongmaocheng, the Red-Haired Fort. Yet today the whole historic site carries the Spanish name: Fort San Domingo, after Santo Domingo.
荷蘭人在淡水的西班牙舊城附近蓋了安東尼堡。依新北市政府的導覽資料，當時的當地居民稱荷蘭人為紅髮的人，這座堡壘就被叫做「紅毛城」。可是今天整個古蹟園區，用的卻是西班牙人那座城（聖多明哥城）的名字：Fort San Domingo。

Myth vs. Fact · 常見迷思

Four things people get wrong 四個常見的誤會

✗ Myth · 迷思The Spanish ruled all of northern Taiwan.西班牙人統治了整個北台灣。

✓ Fact · 事實They kept only two or three hundred soldiers in the north, and most of the time they stayed inside their forts.他們在北部只派了兩、三百人駐守，多數時候都待在堡壘裡。

✗ Myth · 迷思The Spanish left nothing behind.西班牙人什麼都沒有留下。

✓ Fact · 事實Archaeologists have found their church and cemetery on Heping Island, and a historic site in Tamsui still carries their fort’s name.考古學者在和平島找到他們的教堂和墓地；淡水的古蹟園區也還用著他們那座城的名字。

✗ Myth · 迷思We can read the Spanish books about the northern languages.西班牙人寫的北部語言的書，我們還讀得到。

✓ Fact · 事實No copy has been found so far. We know of them only from old catalogs.到現在一本都還沒有被找到。我們只從舊的書目知道它們。

✗ Myth · 迷思The Indigenous peoples just watched as Europeans came and went.原住民只是在旁邊看著歐洲人來來去去。

✓ Fact · 事實They traded, made alliances, and fought back: in 1636 against the Spanish at Tamsui, and in 1655 and 1661 against the Dutch, who left Tamsui under local pressure.他們交易、結盟，也反抗：1636 年在淡水反抗西班牙人，1655 年和 1661 年反抗荷蘭人；荷蘭人是在當地的壓力下撤出淡水的。

Remember It · 記憶口訣

Four things to keep in your pocket 四句帶著走的話

Lost is not never失傳不等於沒有

A lost book was still written.
失傳的書，還是有人寫過。

Dig where the papers stop文字停下來的地方，往下挖

Archaeology fills in what records leave out.
考古補上紀錄沒寫的部分。

A fort is not the land守住城，不等於守住土地

Holding a fort is not ruling a region.
駐守一座城，不代表統治一個地區。

They were there all along他們一直都在

Forts changed hands; the peoples stayed.
城換了主人，族群一直都在。

For Teachers · 給老師的提醒

Curriculum links, a change to the plan, and care with human remains 課綱對應、規劃的一處改動，以及對遺骸的尊重

  • Curriculum links (12-Year Basic Education, social studies, junior high history): 歷 Bb-Ⅳ-1 (the powers in East Asian waters in the 16th and 17th centuries) and 歷 Bb-Ⅳ-2 (contact between Indigenous peoples and outsiders).課綱對應（十二年國教社會領域，國中歷史）：歷 Bb-Ⅳ-1「十六、十七世紀東亞海域的各方勢力。」、歷 Bb-Ⅳ-2「原住民族與外來者的接觸。」
  • A change to the plan. This lesson was planned around a romanized document in a northern Indigenous language. The Spanish did write such books, but none is known to survive, so the lesson teaches them as lost books. The romanized texts that do survive from the 1600s are Dutch-era texts in languages of the south and central west, such as Siraya and Favorlang.規劃的一處改動。這一課原本規劃要讀一份用羅馬字寫的北部原住民族語言文書。西班牙人確實寫過這樣的書，但目前沒有任何一本留存，所以這一課把它們當成「失傳的書」來教。留存下來的 1600 年代羅馬字文本，是荷蘭時期記錄南部與中西部語言（例如西拉雅語、虎尾壠語）的。
  • Human remains. The Heping Island cemetery holds real people. The lesson shows no images of the burials; please keep the same respect in class.遺骸。和平島的墓地裡是真實的人。這一課不放任何墓葬的影像；也請在課堂上保持同樣的尊重。
  • Names. The lesson uses Basay for the people of the Keelung coast and Ketagalan for the people of the Tamsui area, following the sources; some scholars count the Basay within the Ketagalan. The Ketagalan are among the Pingpu peoples who have applied to the Council of Indigenous Peoples for recognition (Lesson 3).名稱。依出處，這一課用「巴賽人」稱雞籠海岸的族群，用「凱達格蘭族」稱淡水一帶的族群；有些學者把巴賽人歸在凱達格蘭族之內。凱達格蘭族是向原民會提出申請核定的平埔族群之一（見第三課）。

Classroom Activity 1 · 課堂活動一

Lost and Found 失物招領

You need · 準備材料

  • A large sheet of paper divided into three columns: Dug up, Still there, Lost一張分成三欄的大紙：挖出來了、還在、失傳了

Steps · 步驟

  1. In groups, list everything this lesson says the Spanish left or made.分組列出課文說西班牙人留下或做過的所有東西。
  2. Put each one in a column.把每一樣放進一欄。
  3. For the Lost column, write how we know the thing existed at all.在「失傳了」那一欄，寫下我們怎麼知道它曾經存在。
  4. Share: which column is the longest, and what does that tell us about this period?分享：哪一欄最長？這告訴我們這段時期的什麼？

Point out that “lost” items can still be evidence, if someone recorded them.
提醒學生：失傳的東西，只要有人記下來，仍然可以是證據。

Classroom Activity 2 · 課堂活動二

Write a Museum Label 寫一張博物館說明牌

You need · 準備材料

  • Index cards小卡片
  • The map cards and the reading地圖上的卡片和課文

Steps · 步驟

  1. Choose one place: Heping Island, Fort San Domingo, or Tayouan.選一個地方：和平島、淡水紅毛城園區，或大員。
  2. Write a label of no more than 40 English words: what it is, when, and how we know.寫一張不超過 40 個英文字的說明牌：它是什麼、什麼時候、我們怎麼知道。
  3. Add one sentence about the people who lived there before the Europeans came.加一句話，寫歐洲人來之前就住在那裡的人。
  4. Read the labels aloud and check each fact against the lesson.把說明牌念出來，逐一對照課文核對事實。

Ask students to use one History English sentence, such as “Early studies suggest that…”
請學生用上一句歷史英語，例如 Early studies suggest that…
