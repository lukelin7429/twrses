Who Are Taiwan’s Indigenous Peoples?台灣的原住民族是誰？

Meet the peoples on a map of names, not borders, and slide through the years to watch the government’s official list grow from nine to sixteen, and then take in the Siraya under a new law in 2026. Read each people’s own account of where it came from, and follow the Austronesian languages from Taiwan across two oceans.
在一張「標名字、不標界線」的地圖上認識各族，拖著年份看政府的官方名單從九族變成十六族，再在 2026 年依新的法律加入西拉雅族。讀各族對自己來歷的說法，再跟著南島語言從台灣走過兩個大洋。

Big idea · 一句話看懂

Taiwan’s Indigenous peoples are many peoples, with many languages and their own accounts of where they came from. The government’s official list has grown over time as peoples asked to be recognized by their own names; the peoples were here long before the list. Their languages belong to the Austronesian family, and many scholars place its homeland in Taiwan, though not all of the evidence agrees.
台灣的原住民族是很多個民族，有很多種語言，也各有自己對來歷的說法。政府的官方名單隨著各族要求以自己的名字被承認而一再增加；這些民族早在名單出現之前就在這裡了。他們的語言屬於南島語系，許多學者認為南島語言的原鄉在台灣，但證據並不全都一致。

Unit 3: Who Are Taiwan’s Indigenous Peoples?

How many Indigenous peoples are there in Taiwan? The answer depends on when you ask. In 2000, the government’s official list had nine peoples. By 2014 it had sixteen. In 2026 the Siraya were approved under a new law for the Pingpu peoples. The peoples themselves did not suddenly appear. They had been here all along, and many of them had to work for years to be recognized by the names they call themselves. So a list made by a government is one kind of record, and like every record, it has a date.

台灣有幾個原住民族？答案要看你什麼時候問。2000 年，政府的官方名單上有九族；到了 2014 年有十六族；2026 年，西拉雅族又依平埔原住民族群的新法律被核定。這些民族不是突然出現的。他們一直都在這裡，而且很多族花了好多年，才讓政府用他們自己的名字承認他們。所以，政府訂的名單是一種記錄；就像每一份記錄一樣，它也有日期。

In July 2026 the Ministry of the Interior counted 638,466 Indigenous people in Taiwan, about 2.75 percent of the population. Of these, 7,727 had not yet registered with a particular people. The Amis are the largest people, with more than 238,000 members; the Kanakanavu are among the smallest, with about 500. The Council of Indigenous Peoples counts 42 varieties of Indigenous languages spoken by the sixteen peoples recognized before 2026. Since 2017, the law has called Indigenous languages national languages of Taiwan.

2026 年 7 月，內政部統計台灣的原住民人口是 638,466 人，大約占總人口的 2.75%；其中 7,727 人尚未申報族別。阿美族是人口最多的一族，超過二十三萬八千人；卡那卡那富族是人口最少的幾族之一，大約五百人。原住民族委員會說，2026 年以前承認的十六族，族語共有 42 種語言別。從 2017 年起，法律明定原住民族語言是台灣的國家語言。

Each people has its own account of where it came from. One Bunun account tells of a gourd that fell from the sky and split open, and a man and a woman walked out. The Atayal tell of a great rock that burst apart. The Cou say the god Hamo made them from maple leaves, and that they later moved from Yushan to where they live now. The Kavalan say their ancestors came from islands to the south. Lesson 1 called these words passed down by mouth. They are evidence of another kind: they tell us how a people understands itself, and they deserve the same respect as any other source.

每一族都有自己對來歷的說法。布農族的說法之一是：有一天從天上掉下一顆葫蘆，裂開以後走出一對男女。泰雅族說，一塊大石頭分裂爆開。鄒族說，大神哈默用楓葉創造了他們，後來他們從玉山遷到現在住的地方。噶瑪蘭族說，祖先來自南方的島嶼。第一課把這些叫做「用嘴巴傳下來的話」。它們是另一種證據：告訴我們一個民族怎麼理解自己，也值得和其他史料一樣被尊重。

Names matter, too. In the 1992 constitutional amendments, the government still used the word shanbao, meaning “mountain compatriots.” In 1994 the amendments changed it to yuanzhumin, “original inhabitants,” and in 1997 to yuanzhuminzu, “Indigenous peoples,” a word that recognizes them as peoples. Some names were given by outsiders. The name Yami was first used by a Japanese anthropologist in the late 19th century; some people on Orchid Island also use Tao, which means “person” in their language.

名稱也很重要。1992 年的憲法增修條文，還用「山胞」這個詞。1994 年改成「原住民」，1997 年再改成「原住民族」——承認他們是一個一個的民族。有些名稱是外人取的。「雅美」這個名稱，是十九世紀末一位日本人類學家開始使用的；蘭嶼也有族人使用「達悟」，在他們的語言裡是「人」的意思。

The list keeps changing. In 2001 the Thao were recognized as the tenth people. In 2022 Taiwan’s Constitutional Court ruled that the Indigenous peoples protected by the Constitution include all the Austronesian peoples that already existed in Taiwan, and so the Pingpu peoples as well. A law on the status of the Pingpu peoples followed in 2025, and in July 2026 the Siraya became the first people approved under it. In October 2025 the Council said that nine Pingpu peoples had applied, including the Siraya. Those cases were at different stages.

名單一直在改變。2001 年，邵族成為第十個被承認的民族。2022 年，憲法法庭判決：憲法保障的原住民族，應包括既存於臺灣之所有臺灣南島語系民族——因此也包括平埔族群。2025 年通過了平埔原住民族群身分法；2026 年 7 月，西拉雅族成為依這部法律核定的第一個民族。原民會在 2025 年 10 月說，包括西拉雅族在內，已有九個平埔族群提出申請，各案進度不一。

Taiwan’s Indigenous languages belong to a large family called Austronesian. Encyclopaedia Britannica counts about 1,200 Austronesian languages, spoken from Madagascar, off Africa, to Easter Island in the eastern Pacific. Many linguists think the family began in Taiwan. A 2009 study of language trees placed its origin here about 5,230 years ago. Others have argued that the ancestors of the Polynesians came from farther south, and a 2014 study notes that the genetic evidence is “more controversial” than the evidence from languages. This series sets these views side by side.

台灣原住民族的語言屬於一個很大的語系，叫做南島語系。《大英百科全書》說，南島語言大約有 1,200 種，從非洲外海的馬達加斯加，一直分布到東太平洋的復活節島。許多語言學家認為，這個語系起源於台灣。2009 年一份比較語言樹的研究，把它的起源放在大約 5,230 年前的台灣。也有學者主張，波里尼西亞人的祖先來自更南方；2014 年的一份研究則指出，遺傳學的證據比語言的證據「更有爭議」。這個系列把這些看法並列。

Questions · 閱讀理解

Why did the number of peoples on the official list change between 2000 and 2026?

Because peoples asked, often for years, to be recognized by their own names, and because a 2022 court ruling and a 2025 law opened the way for Pingpu peoples. The peoples did not change; the government’s list did.

What is the difference between the names Yami and Tao?

Yami, meaning “we,” was first used by a Japanese anthropologist in the late 19th century and is still the official name. Tao means “person” in their own language and is a name some people on Orchid Island use.

Why does this lesson treat each people’s origin account as evidence?

Because words passed down by mouth are one of the three kinds of evidence from Lesson 1. They show how a people understands itself, so they deserve respect alongside archaeology and written records.

⚖️A ruling and a law判決與法律

Constitutional Court Judgment 111-Hsien-Pan-17 (2022)憲法法庭 111 年憲判字第 17 號判決（2022）

The court ruled that the Indigenous peoples protected by the Constitution include all the Austronesian peoples that already existed in Taiwan, and that other Austronesian peoples whose languages, customs, and traditions still survive, whose members keep their identity, and whose history is on record may apply to be recognized. It gave the government three years to change the law. In the hearing, the Council had argued that the Pingpu peoples were not included.判決說，憲法保障的原住民族，應包括既存於臺灣之所有臺灣南島語系民族，；語言、習俗、傳統等文化特徵至今仍然存續、成員仍維持族群認同、且有客觀歷史紀錄可稽的其他臺灣南島語系民族，均得依其民族意願申請核定為原住民族；並要求在三年內修法或另訂特別法。言詞辯論時，原民會的主張是平埔族群不在其內。

Where did the Austronesian languages begin?南島語言從哪裡開始？

A study of language trees (2009)語言樹的研究（2009）

“[T]he language trees place the Austronesian origin in Taiwan approximately 5230 years ago”這份研究的語言樹，把南島語言的起源放在大約 5,230 年前的台灣。

Gray, Drummond & Greenhill, Science (2009), PubMed abstract ↗

An argument against (2001)持不同看法的文章（2001）

Of the “out of Taiwan” model, “popularly known as the ‘express train to Polynesia’,” they wrote that “it is becoming clear that there is, in fact, little supporting evidence,” and that the immediate genetic substrate of the Polynesian expansion came “not from Taiwan, but from east of the Wallace line.”他們說，俗稱「到波里尼西亞的快車」的「出台灣」模型，「越來越清楚的是，其實支持的證據很少」；波里尼西亞人擴張的直接遺傳基礎「不是來自台灣，而是華萊士線以東」。

Oppenheimer & Richards, Science Progress (2001), PubMed abstract ↗

A study of ancient DNA that supports a Taiwan origin (2014)支持起源於台灣的古代 DNA 研究（2014）

“A Taiwan origin … is well supported by linguistic and archaeological evidence. However, human genetic evidence is more controversial.”起源於台灣的看法，有語言學與考古學證據的充分支持；但人類遺傳學的證據比較有爭議。

Ko et al., American Journal of Human Genetics (2014) ↗

Official pages do not all speak the same way, either. A 2010 page of the Council of Indigenous Peoples says plainly that Taiwan is the common origin of the Austronesian languages 5,000 years ago; a 2003 newsletter of the National Museum of Prehistory lays out several competing models and ends by saying a final answer is still to come. Notice also that each people has its own account of where it came from (see the cards on the map). Those accounts answer a different question, and this lesson does not test them against the scientific studies.官方的頁面說法也不完全一樣。原民會 2010 年的一頁直接寫「臺灣……係5,000年前南島語言之共同起源」；史前館 2003 年的電子報則列出好幾種互相競爭的模型，最後說還要更多研究，「才可對於南島語族起源提出最終的答案」。也請注意：每一族對自己的來歷都有自己的說法（見地圖上的卡片）。那些說法回答的是另一個問題，這一課不拿它們去和科學研究比對真假。

How many Indigenous languages are there?台灣有幾種原住民族語言？

Council of Indigenous Peoples原住民族委員會

Taiwan’s Indigenous languages have 42 varieties among 16 peoples.「臺灣原住民族語言具 16 族 42 種語言別」。

Council of Indigenous Peoples, 2024 language program (PDF) · 原民會 113 年度族語推動計畫 ↗

Encyclopaedia Britannica大英百科全書

“Fourteen of the 21 or 22 Austronesian languages spoken by the pre-Chinese aboriginal population of Taiwan (also called Formosa) survive.”台灣原住民族說過的 21 或 22 種南島語言當中，有 14 種留存至今。

Encyclopaedia Britannica, “Austronesian languages” ↗

These numbers do not contradict each other so much as count different things. The Council counts language varieties within each recognized people (its 2024 plan gives 42); Britannica counts languages, including ones no longer spoken. Britannica lists Siraya among the languages no longer spoken, while the Tainan City Government describes classes and books to revive it. Telling a language from a dialect is itself a choice that linguists make in different ways.這兩組數字與其說互相矛盾，不如說算的東西不一樣。原民會算的是每一個法定民族裡的「語言別」（它 2024 年的計畫寫的是 42 種）；《大英百科全書》算的是語言，也包括已經沒有人說的。《大英百科全書》把西拉雅語列為已經沒有人說的語言，臺南市政府則說明了族語教師研習、出版繪本與教材等復育的工作。什麼算一種語言、什麼算方言，本身就是語言學家用不同方法做的選擇。

What should a people be called?一個民族應該怎麼稱呼？

Yami or Tao雅美或達悟

The name Yami was first used by the anthropologist Torii Ryūzō in the late 19th century; some people use Tao. Studies and reports use Yami (official) and Tao (everyday).「雅美」一詞「由十九世紀末期人類學家鳥居龍藏開始使用」；「部分族人在族群名稱上也另外使用『達悟』」；研究與報導「分別出現官方採用雅美、民間採用達悟這兩種不同的族稱」。

Council of Indigenous Peoples, Yami page · 原民會 雅美族 ↗

Puyuma or PinuyumayanPuyuma 或 Pinuyumayan

The people’s self-name Puyuma is also the name of one village, so in recent years some have proposed Pinuyumayan for the whole people. The Council’s English pages use Pinuyumayan.族人的自稱 Puyuma 也是南王部落的名稱；為了避免混用，「近年有族人提倡以『比努優瑪樣』（Pinuyumayan）來作為卑南族新的族稱」。原民會的英文頁使用 Pinuyumayan。

Council of Indigenous Peoples, Pinuyumayan page · 原民會 卑南族 ↗

Both examples come from the same official source, which itself records both names. The safest habit is the one the Council follows: give the official name and the name people use, and say who uses which.兩個例子都來自同一個官方出處，而它自己就把兩個名稱都記下來了。最穩當的習慣就是原民會的做法：寫出官方的名稱，也寫出族人用的名稱，並說明誰用哪一個。

The rule is the same in every lesson: views are set side by side, each with its source, and the lesson does not choose.每一課的規則都一樣：說法並列、各附出處，課文不替誰選邊。

History English · 歷史英語

Eight sentences for talking about peoples and names 談民族與名稱的八句英文

Some of these report what a people says about itself; others report what a law or a study says. Tap a speaker to hear each one, then try changing the part in bold.
有幾句轉述的是一個民族對自己的說法；有幾句轉述的是法律或研究怎麼說。點 🔊 聽每一句，再試著把粗體的部分換掉。

  1. The Bunun tell of a gourd that fell from the sky.布農族的說法裡，有一顆從天上掉下來的葫蘆。tell of: to report a people’s own account without judging it.tell of：轉述一個民族自己的說法，不加評斷。
  2. According to Atayal tradition, the first ancestors came out of a rock.依照泰雅族的傳說，最早的祖先是從石頭裡走出來的。According to … tradition: says whose account it is.According to … tradition：說明這是誰的說法。
  3. Some Yami people call themselves Tao, which means person.有些雅美族人自稱達悟，意思是「人」。call themselves: the name a people uses for itself.call themselves：一個民族對自己的稱呼。
  4. The Thao were recognized as the tenth Indigenous people in 2001.邵族在 2001 年被承認為第十個原住民族。were recognized as: a decision made by a government.were recognized as：政府所做的決定。
  5. Indigenous people make up about 2.75 percent of Taiwan’s population.原住民大約占台灣人口的 2.75%。make up: to be a part of a whole.make up：構成整體的一部分。
  6. Since 2017, Indigenous languages have been national languages of Taiwan.從 2017 年起，原住民族語言就是台灣的國家語言。since + year, with have been: true from then until now.since＋年份，配 have been：從那時到現在都是。
  7. Austronesian languages are spoken from Madagascar to Easter Island.從馬達加斯加到復活節島，都有人說南島語言。are spoken from … to …: the range where a language is used.are spoken from … to …：說明一種語言使用的範圍。
  8. Many linguists think the family began in Taiwan, but the genetic evidence is debated.許多語言學家認為這個語系起源於台灣，但遺傳學的證據還有爭論。is debated: careful people do not yet agree.is debated：認真的人們還沒有共識。

In Taiwan · 在台灣

A changed word, a lake, a national language, and the Pingpu peoples 一個改掉的詞、一座湖、一種國家語言，以及平埔族群

Four short notes on names and recognition.
四則關於名稱與承認的短短說明。

A word · 一個詞

From “mountain compatriots” to “Indigenous peoples”從「山胞」到「原住民族」

The constitutional amendments of 1992 spoke of protecting the status of shanbao, “mountain compatriots.” The amendments of 1994 changed the word to yuanzhumin, “original inhabitants.” Those of 1997 changed it again, to yuanzhuminzu, “Indigenous peoples,” and added that the state would act “according to the will of the peoples.” Three amendments in five years, and each word says something different about who is being named.
1992 年的憲法增修條文寫的是保障「山胞」的地位。1994 年的增修條文把這個詞改成「原住民」。1997 年再改成「原住民族」，並加上國家應「依民族意願」保障其地位。五年之內改了三次，每一個詞，對被稱呼的人說的都不一樣。

A lake · 一座湖

The Thao and Sun Moon Lake邵族與日月潭

Sun Moon Lake is one of Taiwan’s best-known sights. In Thao tradition, their ancestors came there following a white deer. The Thao were once classified as part of the Cou, though the two differ greatly in language, religion, and customs; they campaigned to be called by their own name, and in 2001 they became the tenth people on the list. Fewer than 1,000 Thao were counted in July 2026.
日月潭是台灣最有名的景點之一。在邵族的傳說裡，祖先是追著一隻白鹿來到這裡的。邵族在民族分類上曾經被歸類為鄒族，但兩族在語言、宗教、生命禮俗上有很大的差異；他們爭取以自己的名字被稱呼，2001 年成為名單上的第十族。2026 年 7 月統計的邵族人口不到 1,000 人。

Language · 語言

National languages國家語言

Article 1 of the Indigenous Languages Development Act of 2017 begins: “Indigenous languages are national languages.” The Council of Indigenous Peoples counts 42 language varieties among the 16 peoples recognized before 2026. The National Languages Development Act of 2019 defines national languages as the natural languages used by each of Taiwan’s original groups, together with Taiwan Sign Language.
2017 年《原住民族語言發展法》第一條開頭就說：「原住民族語言為國家語言」。原民會說，2026 年以前承認的十六族共有 42 種語言別。《國家語言發展法》則把國家語言定義為「臺灣各固有族群使用之自然語言及臺灣手語」。

Pingpu · 平埔

The newest people on the list名單上最新的一族

Pingpu, “of the plains,” is a name for a group of peoples, each with its own name, such as the Siraya. In 2022 the Constitutional Court ruled that they, too, are among the Indigenous peoples the Constitution protects. A law followed in 2025, and in July 2026 the Siraya of Tainan became the first people approved under it; the Council’s pages give both July 30 and July 31 for the approval. In October 2025 the Council said eight other Pingpu peoples had also applied.
「平埔」是平原的意思，指的是一群民族；每一族都有自己的名字，例如西拉雅族。2022 年，憲法法庭判決他們也屬於憲法保障的原住民族。2025 年通過了相關的法律；2026 年 7 月，臺南的西拉雅族成為依這部法律核定的第一個民族——原民會的頁面上，核定的日期一處寫 7 月 30 日、一處寫 7 月 31 日。原民會在 2025 年 10 月說，另外還有八個平埔族群也已經提出申請。

Myth vs. Fact · 常見迷思

Four things people get wrong 四個常見的誤會

✗ Myth · 迷思Taiwan’s Indigenous people are one group with one language.台灣的原住民是同一群人，說同一種語言。

✓ Fact · 事實They are many peoples: sixteen recognized peoples, plus the Siraya approved in 2026 under the Pingpu law. The Council counts 42 language varieties among the sixteen.他們是很多個民族：十六個法定原住民族，加上 2026 年依平埔族群的法律核定的西拉雅族。原民會說，那十六族共有 42 種語言別。

✗ Myth · 迷思The number of peoples is fixed.原住民族的數目是固定的。

✓ Fact · 事實The government’s list changed seven times between 2001 and 2026, and more Pingpu peoples have applied. The list is a record with a date.政府的名單在 2001 到 2026 年之間改變了七次，還有更多平埔族群提出申請。名單是一份有日期的記錄。

✗ Myth · 迷思Indigenous peoples all live in the mountains.原住民族都住在山上。

✓ Fact · 事實The Amis live mainly on the eastern plains, the Kavalan named themselves “people of the plain,” the Yami live on an island, and the Siraya live in Tainan.阿美族主要住在東部的平原，噶瑪蘭族的族名就是「平原的人」，雅美族住在島上，西拉雅族住在臺南。

✗ Myth · 迷思Origin stories are just fairy tales.起源故事只是童話。

✓ Fact · 事實They are a people’s own account, passed down by mouth for generations: evidence of how a people understands itself. They answer a different question from archaeology, not a lesser one.它們是一個民族自己的說法，口耳相傳了好幾代——是這個民族怎麼理解自己的證據。它們回答的問題和考古學不一樣，但並不比較不重要。

Remember It · 記憶口訣

Classroom Activity 1 · 課堂活動一

A Name Card 一張名字卡

You need · 準備材料

  • An index card for each student每人一張小卡片
  • Colored pens彩色筆

Steps · 步驟

  1. On one side, write the name your family calls you, and what it means or why it was chosen, if you know.在一面寫下家人叫你的名字，以及它的意思或為什麼取這個名字（如果你知道的話）。
  2. On the other side, write a name someone outside your family once gave you, such as a nickname or an English name.在另一面寫下家人以外的人給你取過的名字，例如綽號或英文名字。
  3. In pairs, explain which name you prefer, and why.兩人一組，說說你比較喜歡哪一個名字，為什麼。
  4. As a class, connect this to the lesson: why might a people want to be called by its own name?全班一起把它連到這一課：為什麼一個民族會希望用自己的名字被稱呼？

Keep the focus on the students’ own names; do not ask students to guess or use other people’s ethnic names. The point is to feel why a self-name matters before reading the Yami and Tao card again.
焦點放在學生自己的名字；不要請學生去猜或使用別人的族名。重點是先體會「自己的名字」為什麼重要，再回頭讀雅美與達悟那張卡。

Classroom Activity 2 · 課堂活動二

Languages in Our Class 我們班上的語言

You need · 準備材料

  • A large sheet of paper or the board一大張紙或白板
  • Sticky notes便利貼

Steps · 步驟

  1. Each student writes on a sticky note one language spoken in their home, even a few words of it.每位同學在便利貼上寫下家裡說的一種語言，就算只會幾個詞也可以。
  2. Group the notes on the board. Count how many languages the class has together.把便利貼在白板上分組，數一數全班一共有幾種語言。
  3. Read Article 3 of the National Languages Development Act together: national languages are the natural languages used by each of Taiwan’s original groups, and Taiwan Sign Language.一起讀《國家語言發展法》第三條：國家語言是臺灣各固有族群使用之自然語言及臺灣手語。
  4. Discuss: what helps a language stay alive in a family?討論：什麼能讓一種語言在家裡繼續活下去？

No student should feel pressed to name a language they would rather not. Students who speak an Indigenous language may be invited, never required, to teach the class a greeting.
不要讓任何學生覺得必須說出自己不想說的語言。會說族語的學生可以邀請、但絕不要求，教全班一句問候。
