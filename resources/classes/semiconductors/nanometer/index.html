How Small Is a Nanometer? An Angstrom?奈米、埃米有多小？

News about chips is full of nanometers and angstroms. Zoom in 3D from your fingernail to a hair, a blood cell, a virus, the lines on a chip, and at last single silicon atoms. Then find out how many nanometers your nails have grown since you opened this page.
晶片的新聞裡到處是奈米和埃米。在 3D 裡從指甲一路放大：頭髮、紅血球、病毒、晶片上的線，最後是一顆顆矽原子。再算算看，從你打開這一頁到現在，指甲長了幾奈米。

Big idea · 一句話看懂

A nanometer is one billionth of a meter, and an angstrom is one tenth of a nanometer, about the size of a single atom. The lines on today's chips are tens of nanometers apart. Names like “3 nanometer” and “2 nanometer” are names for generations of chips, not lengths that anyone measured.
1 奈米是十億分之一公尺；1 埃米是十分之一奈米，差不多就是一顆原子的大小。現在晶片上的線，彼此相隔幾十奈米。「3 奈米」「2 奈米」是每一代晶片的名字，不是量出來的長度。

Unit 5: How Small Is a Nanometer? An Angstrom?

Turn on the news in Taiwan and you will hear about 3-nanometer chips, 2-nanometer chips, and now chips measured in angstroms. These words sound very small, and they are. But how small? Smaller than a hair? Smaller than a germ? Let us take a trip down to the bottom, ten times smaller at each step.

在台灣打開新聞，常會聽到 3 奈米晶片、2 奈米晶片，現在還有用「埃米」來算的晶片。這些詞聽起來很小，也真的很小。可是到底多小？比頭髮細嗎？比細菌小嗎？我們一路往下走，每一步都縮小十倍。

Start with your fingernail, about one centimeter wide. Zoom in about a hundred times and you reach a single hair, about 75 micrometers thick. A micrometer is one thousandth of a millimeter. Zoom in ten times more and you meet a red blood cell, about 7 micrometers across. A bacterium is smaller still, about 2 micrometers long.

從你的指甲開始，寬度大約 1 公分。放大大約一百倍，會看到一根頭髮，粗細大約 75 微米。1 微米是千分之一公釐。再放大十倍，會遇到紅血球，直徑大約 7 微米。細菌又更小，大約 2 微米長。

Keep going, and micrometers become too big. We need a smaller unit: the nanometer, one thousandth of a micrometer, or one billionth of a meter. A flu virus is about 100 nanometers across. The finest lines on a modern chip are a few tens of nanometers apart. A strand of DNA is only about 2 nanometers wide.

繼續往下，微米就太大了，我們需要更小的單位：奈米。1 奈米是千分之一微米，也就是十億分之一公尺。流感病毒的直徑大約 100 奈米。現代晶片上最細的線，彼此相隔幾十奈米。一條 DNA 只有大約 2 奈米寬。

At the very bottom are atoms. Silicon atoms in a chip sit about 0.24 nanometers from their neighbors. For sizes like this, scientists use an even smaller unit, the angstrom, which is one tenth of a nanometer. It is named after Anders Ångström, a Swedish scientist. So one silicon atom is a little more than 2 angstroms wide.

最底下是原子。晶片裡的矽原子，和旁邊的原子相隔大約 0.24 奈米。這麼小的尺寸，科學家用一個更小的單位，叫做埃米（也寫成「埃」），是十分之一奈米。它是用瑞典科學家埃格斯特朗（Anders Ångström）的名字命名的。所以一顆矽原子的寬度是 2 埃米多一點。

Now for a surprise. A “3-nanometer chip” does not have parts that are 3 nanometers wide. Long ago, the number in the name was a real length on the chip. Today it is only a name for a generation of chips: a smaller number means a newer design that packs in more transistors. In a “3-nanometer” chip, the closest metal lines are expected to be about 24 nanometers apart.

接下來的事可能讓你很意外：「3 奈米晶片」裡面，並沒有哪個零件是 3 奈米寬。很久以前，名字裡的數字確實是晶片上量得到的長度。現在它只是一代晶片的名字：數字越小，代表設計越新、塞進去的電晶體越多。在一顆「3 奈米」的晶片裡，最密的金屬線預計相隔大約 24 奈米。

Names with angstroms work the same way. One chip maker uses names like 18A, and another uses A16, where the A stands for angstrom. They tell you which generation a chip belongs to, not what a ruler would read. Still, the real sizes are amazing. Chip makers now build parts only a few dozen atoms wide, billions of times on every chip.

用埃米取的名字也是一樣。有一家晶片公司用 18A 這樣的名字，另一家用 A16，其中的 A 就是埃米（angstrom）。這些名字告訴你晶片屬於哪一代，不是用尺量出來的讀數。不過真正的尺寸還是很驚人：晶片公司現在做出來的零件只有幾十顆原子寬，而且每一顆晶片上要做幾十億次。

Questions · 閱讀理解

How big is a nanometer, and how big is an angstrom?

A nanometer is one billionth of a meter. An angstrom is one tenth of a nanometer, about the size of a single atom.

Which is smaller: a flu virus or the spacing between the finest lines on a modern chip?

The spacing between the lines on the chip. It is a few tens of nanometers, and a flu virus is about 100 nanometers across.

What does the “3” in “3-nanometer chip” tell you?

It is the name of a generation of chips. A smaller number means a newer design with more transistors. It is not a length that anyone measured on the chip.

Small by the Numbers · 小的數字

From a billionth of a meter to an atom 從十億分之一公尺到一顆原子

Six numbers for thinking small.

1 nmone billionth of a meter · 十億分之一公尺

Stories of Small Units · 小單位的故事

A dwarf, a Swedish scientist, a name, and a letter A 一個小矮人、一位瑞典科學家、一個名字、一個字母 A

Four stories behind the words in the news.
新聞裡那些詞背後的四個小故事。

nano · 奈

Nano means dwarfnano 的意思是小矮人

The prefix nano comes from the Greek word nanos, which means dwarf. In 1960 it was officially approved as the prefix for one billionth. In Taiwan it is written 奈米; you may also see 納米 in other places.
字首 nano 來自希臘文的 nanos，意思是「矮人」。1960 年，它正式成為代表「十億分之一」的字首。台灣寫成「奈米」，在其他地方也會看到「納米」。

1868 · Ångström

The man who measured sunlight測量陽光的人

In 1868 the Swedish physicist Anders Ångström made a chart of the colors in sunlight. He wrote the wavelengths in units of one ten-millionth of a millimeter. That unit was later named the angstrom after him. It is not an official SI unit, but scientists still use it for atoms.
1868 年，瑞典物理學家埃格斯特朗把陽光裡的各種顏色畫成一張圖表，波長用「千萬分之一公釐」當單位。這個單位後來就用他的名字命名為埃米。它不是國際單位制（SI）的正式單位，但科學家講原子時還是常用。

IRDS 2021

A name, not a ruler是名字，不是尺

An international roadmap for the chip industry says that a “3-nanometer” chip is expected to have gates 48 nanometers apart and metal lines 24 nanometers apart. Nothing in it is 3 nanometers. The number is a label that tells you the generation.
半導體產業的一份國際技術藍圖指出，「3 奈米」晶片的閘極預計相隔 48 奈米、金屬線相隔 24 奈米，裡面沒有任何東西是 3 奈米。這個數字是一個標籤，告訴你它是哪一代。

18A · A16

The letter A字母 A

In 2021 Intel began naming its newest generations with the letter A for angstrom, such as 20A and 18A. In 2024 TSMC announced a generation called A16, planned for production in 2026. Like the nanometer names, these are names of generations.
2021 年，英特爾開始用代表埃米的字母 A 替最新幾代製程命名，例如 20A、18A。2024 年，台積電發表了名為 A16 的製程，預計 2026 年生產。和奈米的名字一樣，這些也是世代的名字。

Myth vs. Fact · 常見迷思

Four things people get wrong 四個常見的誤會

✗ Myth · 迷思A 3-nanometer chip has transistors that are 3 nanometers wide.3 奈米晶片裡的電晶體是 3 奈米寬。

✓ Fact · 事實The 3 is the name of a generation. The closest lines in such a chip are expected to be about 24 nanometers apart.3 是這一代的名字。這種晶片裡最密的線，預計相隔大約 24 奈米。

✗ Myth · 迷思So the numbers in chip names mean nothing at all.所以晶片名字裡的數字完全沒有意義。

✓ Fact · 事實They still tell you the order. A smaller number means a newer generation that fits more transistors into the same space.它們還是告訴你先後順序：數字越小，代表越新的一代，同樣的面積放得下更多電晶體。

✗ Myth · 迷思A nanometer is the smallest length there is.奈米是最小的長度。

✓ Fact · 事實Atoms are smaller. A silicon atom is about 0.24 nanometers, or 2.4 angstroms, and the parts inside an atom are far smaller still.原子更小。矽原子大約 0.24 奈米，也就是 2.4 埃米；原子裡面的東西還要小得多。

✗ Myth · 迷思With a good school microscope you can see viruses and atoms.學校的顯微鏡夠好的話，就看得到病毒和原子。

✓ Fact · 事實A light microscope can only show details down to about 200 nanometers. Viruses, chip lines, and atoms need other kinds of microscopes.光學顯微鏡大約只能看清楚 200 奈米以上的細節。病毒、晶片上的線和原子，要用別種顯微鏡才看得到。

Each step is a thousand times smaller: millimeter, micrometer, nanometer.
每一步都小一千倍：公釐（毫米）、微米、奈米。

Ten angstroms in a nanometer1 奈米＝10 埃米

An angstrom is about one atom wide.
1 埃米大約是一顆原子寬。

A name, not a ruler是名字，不是尺

“3 nanometer” names a generation of chips. Nobody measured 3 nanometers.
「3 奈米」是一代晶片的名字，沒有人量到 3 奈米。

One nanometer a second一秒一奈米

That is about how fast your fingernails grow.
你的指甲差不多就是長這麼快。

Safety First · 安全提醒

Before you try anything 動手之前先讀

  • Use safety scissors, and cut away from your fingers.使用安全剪刀，剪的時候刀口不要對著手指。
  • Do not pull out anyone's hair for the activity. Use a hair that has already fallen out.不要為了活動去拔別人的頭髮，用已經掉下來的頭髮就好。
  • Pick up all the tiny pieces of paper when you finish, and keep them away from small children.做完之後把小紙屑全部撿起來，不要讓小小孩拿到。
  • If you use a microscope or a magnifying glass, never point it at the sun.使用顯微鏡或放大鏡時，絕對不要對著太陽。

Classroom Activity 1 · 課堂活動一

Cut Your Way to a Nanometer 一路剪到奈米

You need · 準備材料

  • A strip of A4 paper cut along the long side (297 millimeters long)沿著長邊剪下來的一條 A4 紙（長 297 公釐）
  • Safety scissors, a ruler, and a pencil安全剪刀、尺和鉛筆

Steps · 步驟

  1. Cut the strip in half. Throw one half away, and cut the other half in half again. Count each cut.把紙條對半剪開。丟掉一半，剩下的一半再對半剪。每剪一次就數一次。
  2. After each cut, measure the piece and write the length down. How many cuts can you make before the piece is too small to hold?每剪一次就量一量、記下長度。剪到第幾次，紙片就小到拿不住了？
  3. Keep going with a calculator instead of scissors. After 12 cuts the piece would be as wide as a hair. What is it after 22 cuts?接下來不用剪刀，改用計算機繼續算。剪 12 次之後，紙片差不多是一根頭髮寬。剪 22 次之後呢？
  4. How many cuts until the piece is shorter than 1 nanometer? Check your answer with the slider on this page.要剪幾次，紙片才會短於 1 奈米？用這一頁的滑桿對一對答案。

Most people can make only six or seven cuts. The other twenty-two cuts are the distance between what our hands can do and what a chip factory does.
大部分的人只剪得了六、七次。剩下的二十二次，就是我們的手和晶圓廠之間的距離。

Classroom Activity 2 · 課堂活動二

The Powers-of-Ten Line 十的次方排排站

You need · 準備材料

  • Eight cards with these names: fingernail, hair, red blood cell, bacterium, flu virus, chip lines, DNA, silicon atom八張卡片，寫上：指甲、頭髮、紅血球、細菌、流感病毒、晶片上的線、DNA、矽原子
  • A long strip of tape on the floor with eight marks, one big step apart在地上貼一條長膠帶，上面做八個記號，每個記號相隔一大步

Steps · 步驟

  1. Label the marks from 1 centimeter down to 1 angstrom. Each mark is ten times smaller than the one before.把記號從 1 公分標到 1 埃米，每個記號都比前一個小十倍。
  2. Without looking at the page, put each card where you think it belongs.先不要看網頁，把每張卡片放到你覺得對的位置。
  3. Check with the 3D model and move the cards that were wrong. Which one surprised you most?用 3D 模型對答案，把放錯的卡片移到對的位置。哪一張最讓你意外？
  4. Where would you put “3 nanometers”? Is anything on your cards really that size?「3 奈米」該放在哪裡？你的卡片裡有哪一樣東西真的是那個大小嗎？

On this line, one step is ten times. Three steps are a thousand times, which is one change of unit.
在這條線上，走一步是十倍；走三步是一千倍，剛好換一個單位。
