Chips and Semiconductors · Unit 3 · Lesson 8 · 晶片與半導體 單元三 第八課

How Did Taiwan Become a Chip Island?台灣怎麼成為晶片島？

A chip is designed, made, and packaged by different companies, and Taiwan has important ones for every step. On a 3D map, follow one chip from Hsinchu to Tainan to Kaohsiung and out to the world, then drag a timeline from 1973 to see how the industry grew. Finish by sorting companies by the job they do.
一顆晶片由不同的公司負責設計、製造和封裝，而每一段台灣都有重要的公司。在 3D 地圖上跟著一顆晶片從新竹到台南、再到高雄，最後送往全世界；再從 1973 年開始拉時間軸，看這個產業怎麼長大。最後玩個小遊戲，分分看哪家公司做哪一段。

Big idea · 一句話看懂

Making a chip takes three big jobs: designing it, manufacturing it, and packaging and testing it. In 1987 a company in Taiwan chose to do only the manufacturing, for anyone who had a design. That idea, the foundry, changed how the world makes chips. Today Taiwan has important companies for all three jobs, gathered in a few science parks.
做出一顆晶片有三件大事：設計、製造、封裝測試。1987 年，台灣有一家公司決定只做「製造」這一段，誰有設計就幫誰做。這個點子叫做晶圓代工，改變了全世界做晶片的方式。今天，這三件事台灣都有重要的公司，而且集中在幾個科學園區裡。

Three jobs, then the world · 三段工作，再到全世界

Step 1 · 第 1 步 · Hsinchu 新竹

Design the chip設計晶片

Engineers at a design company draw the circuit on computers: billions of transistors and the wires between them. A company that only designs chips is called fabless, because it has no factory. MediaTek, in Hsinchu, is one.設計公司的工程師在電腦上畫出電路：幾十億個電晶體，和它們之間的連線。只做設計、沒有工廠的公司叫做無廠半導體公司（fabless）。總部在新竹的聯發科就是其中一家。

Step 2 · 第 2 步 · Tainan 台南

Make it on a wafer在晶圓上做出來

The design goes to a foundry, a company that manufactures chips for others. In a wafer fab in a science park, the pattern is printed onto silicon wafers layer by layer, as you saw in Lessons 3 and 4.設計圖送到晶圓代工廠，也就是替別人製造晶片的公司。在科學園區的晶圓廠裡，圖案一層一層印到矽晶圓上，就像第三、四課看到的那樣。

Step 3 · 第 3 步 · Kaohsiung 高雄

Package and test封裝與測試

The wafers travel to a packaging and testing company. There the chips are cut apart, put into packages, sometimes stacked together as in Lesson 6, and tested one by one.晶圓接著送到封裝測試公司。晶片在那裡被切開、放進封裝，有時像第六課那樣疊在一起，再一顆一顆測試。

Step 4 · 第 4 步 · To the world 全世界

Out to the world送往全世界

The finished chips leave Taiwan for factories that build phones, computers, cars, and servers. This route is only an example: every chip has its own path, and many paths cross other countries too.做好的晶片離開台灣，送到組裝手機、電腦、汽車和伺服器的工廠。這條路線只是一個例子：每顆晶片都有自己的路，很多還會經過別的國家。

Unit 8: How Did Taiwan Become a Chip Island?

Taiwan is a small island, yet people all over the world call it a chip island. Phones, computers, cars, and the servers that run AI depend on chips that passed through Taiwan. How did this happen? It did not happen by luck, and it did not happen quickly. It took about fifty years, and it began with a plan.

台灣是一座小島，全世界卻有很多人叫它「晶片島」。手機、電腦、汽車，還有跑 AI 的伺服器，都靠著經過台灣的晶片。這是怎麼來的？不是靠運氣，也不是一下子就發生的。它花了大約五十年，而且是從一個計畫開始的。

In 1973 the government founded a research institute called ITRI in Hsinchu. A year later, a small group of officials and engineers met to discuss how Taiwan could start making integrated circuits. In 1976 teams of young engineers were sent to RCA, a company in the United States, to learn how. They came home and built a demonstration factory, and it worked well.

1973 年，政府在新竹成立了一個研究機構，叫做工業技術研究院（工研院）。隔年，一小群官員和工程師開會，討論台灣要怎麼開始做積體電路。1976 年，一批批年輕的工程師被派到美國的 RCA 公司去學。他們回來之後蓋了一座示範工廠，而且做得很成功。

The next step was to turn research into companies. In 1980 UMC was spun off from ITRI and became Taiwan's first chip company. In the same year Hsinchu Science Park opened beside two universities, so that companies, engineers, and students could be close together. Today hundreds of companies have their homes in that park.

下一步是把研究變成公司。1980 年，聯華電子（聯電）從工研院獨立出來，成為台灣第一家積體電路公司。同一年，新竹科學園區在兩所大學旁邊成立，讓公司、工程師和學生靠得很近。今天有好幾百家公司把家安在這個園區裡。

Then came the big idea. In those days, a chip company did everything itself: it designed its chips and also built the expensive factories to make them. In 1987 Morris Chang founded TSMC to do only one part. It would manufacture chips for any company that had a design, and it would not design chips of its own. This kind of company is called a foundry.

接著是那個重要的點子。那個年代，一家晶片公司什麼都自己來：自己設計晶片，也自己蓋昂貴的工廠來製造。1987 年，張忠謀創辦台積電，只做其中一段：誰有設計，它就幫誰製造，自己不設計晶片。這種公司叫做晶圓代工廠。

The foundry changed the industry. Now a small team with a good idea could design a chip without building a factory. Design companies, called fabless companies, grew up around the world. And Taiwan filled in the other steps as well: design companies such as MediaTek in Hsinchu, and packaging and testing companies such as ASE in Kaohsiung.

晶圓代工改變了整個產業。從此以後，一個有好點子的小團隊不用蓋工廠也能設計晶片。只做設計的「無廠半導體公司」在世界各地冒出來。而其他幾段，台灣也補上了：做設計的公司，例如新竹的聯發科；做封裝測試的公司，例如高雄的日月光。

So a chip's trip can look like this: designed in one place, manufactured in a science park, packaged and tested in another city, and then shipped to the world. Hsinchu, Taichung, Tainan, and Kaohsiung all lie along the west coast of one small island, and that closeness helps. The chip island was built by many people over many years, one step at a time.

所以一顆晶片的旅程可能是這樣：在一個地方設計，在科學園區製造，到另一個城市封裝測試，然後送往全世界。新竹、台中、台南、高雄都在同一座小島的西岸，這種「近」很有幫助。晶片島是很多人花了很多年，一步一步蓋起來的。

Questions · 閱讀理解

What are the three big jobs in making a chip?

Designing the chip, manufacturing it, and packaging and testing it.

What was new about TSMC in 1987?

It only manufactured chips for other companies and did not design chips of its own. It was the world's first company of this kind, called a foundry.

Why does it help that the science parks and companies are close together?

Because a chip has to pass from one company to the next. When they are close, people, wafers, and ideas can move between them quickly.

Chip Island Stories · 晶片島的故事

A meeting, a classroom abroad, a park beside two universities, and one bold idea 一場會議、一間海外的教室、兩所大學旁的園區、一個大膽的點子

Four moments that shaped the island.
塑造這座島的四個時刻。

1974

The meeting那一場會議

In 1974 a small group met to talk about Taiwan's future: the minister of economic affairs, Sun Yun-suan, several other officials, the president of ITRI, and Pan Wen-Yuan, a research director from RCA. They talked about how Taiwan could build a semiconductor industry. Taiwan's chip industry counts its start from that year.
1974 年，一小群人坐下來談台灣的未來：經濟部長孫運璿、幾位官員、工研院院長，還有 RCA 研究室主任潘文淵。他們討論的是台灣要怎麼發展半導體產業。台灣的半導體產業，就從這一年算起。

1976 · RCA

Students again再當一次學生

In 1976 RCA agreed to transfer its chipmaking technology. ITRI sent four teams of engineers to train at RCA in the United States. Back home, they built a demonstration factory, and it reached higher yields than RCA's own factories.
1976 年，RCA 同意把製造晶片的技術轉移過來。工研院派了四組工程師到美國 RCA 受訓。回來之後，他們蓋了一座示範工廠，良率比 RCA 自己的工廠還高。

1980 · 竹科

A park beside two universities兩所大學旁邊的園區

The idea for Hsinchu Science Park came in 1976: build a place like Silicon Valley, next to National Tsing Hua University and National Chiao Tung University, so that companies and classrooms would be neighbors. The park was established on December 15, 1980.
新竹科學園區的構想出現在 1976 年：仿照矽谷，把園區蓋在清華大學和交通大學旁邊，讓公司和教室當鄰居。園區在 1980 年 12 月 15 日成立。

1987 · 台積電

Make chips, design none只製造，不設計

Morris Chang had worked at Texas Instruments for 25 years before he was invited to Taiwan to lead ITRI. In 1987 he founded TSMC as a joint venture of the government, ITRI, and private investors. It would manufacture chips for others and design none of its own. That way of working became a model for the whole industry.
張忠謀在德州儀器工作了 25 年，之後受邀到台灣擔任工研院院長。1987 年，他創辦台積電，由政府、工研院和民間一起出資。這家公司只替別人製造晶片，自己不設計。這種做法後來成了整個產業的模式。

Myth vs. Fact · 常見迷思

Four things people get wrong 四個常見的誤會

✗ Myth · 迷思One company makes a chip from start to finish.一顆晶片從頭到尾都是同一家公司做的。

✓ Fact · 事實Usually not. One company designs it, another manufactures it, and often a third packages and tests it.通常不是。一家設計，另一家製造，常常還有第三家負責封裝和測試。

✗ Myth · 迷思Taiwan's chip industry appeared suddenly a few years ago.台灣的晶片產業是這幾年突然冒出來的。

✓ Fact · 事實It grew step by step over about fifty years, starting with a research institute in 1973.它是花了大約五十年一步一步長大的，從 1973 年的一個研究機構開始。

✗ Myth · 迷思A foundry designs the chips it makes.晶圓代工廠自己設計它做的晶片。

✓ Fact · 事實A foundry manufactures chips that other companies designed. That is exactly what makes it a foundry.晶圓代工廠製造的是別的公司設計的晶片，這正是「代工」的意思。

✗ Myth · 迷思Chips made in Taiwan are made only from things found in Taiwan.台灣做的晶片，用的都是台灣自己的東西。

✓ Fact · 事實Chipmaking is a worldwide effort. The machines, materials, designs, and customers come from many countries, as you saw in Lessons 3 and 4.做晶片要靠全世界一起合作。機器、材料、設計和客戶來自很多國家，就像第三、四課看到的那樣。

Safety First · 安全提醒

Before you try anything 動手之前先讀

  • A science park is a workplace, not a playground. Visit only on an arranged tour, and stay with your teacher.科學園區是上班的地方，不是遊樂場。只在安排好的參訪時進去，並且跟緊老師。
  • When you interview a family member about work, ask first, and do not share anything that their company keeps secret.訪問家人工作上的事情之前要先問過，公司要保密的事情不要寫出來。
  • Use scissors and pins carefully when you make your map.做地圖的時候，小心使用剪刀和圖釘。

Classroom Activity 1 · 課堂活動一

The Chip Relay 晶片接力賽

You need · 準備材料

  • A sheet of paper and a pencil for each team, and three tables每隊一張紙和一支鉛筆，還有三張桌子
  • Signs for the tables: Design, Manufacture, Package and Test三張桌子的牌子：設計、製造、封裝測試

Steps · 步驟

  1. At the Design table, one student draws a simple pattern of five shapes and passes the paper on.在「設計」桌，一位同學畫出由五個形狀組成的簡單圖案，再把紙傳下去。
  2. At the Manufacture table, the next student copies the pattern exactly, four times, on a new sheet (four chips on one wafer).在「製造」桌，下一位同學把圖案一模一樣地畫四次在新的紙上（一片晶圓上四顆晶片）。
  3. At the Package and Test table, the last student cuts the four apart, checks each against the design, and throws away any that are wrong.在「封裝測試」桌，最後一位同學把四顆剪開，一顆一顆對照設計圖檢查，把錯的丟掉。
  4. Count the good chips. Then talk about it: what happens to the whole team if one table is slow, or far away?數一數有幾顆好晶片。再討論：如果有一張桌子很慢、或離得很遠，整隊會怎麼樣？

Nobody at the Manufacture table changes the design. That is the foundry's promise.
「製造」桌的人不會去改設計。這就是晶圓代工的承諾。

Classroom Activity 2 · 課堂活動二

My Chip Map 我的晶片地圖

You need · 準備材料

  • An outline map of Taiwan, colored pencils, and string一張台灣輪廓圖、色鉛筆和線
  • This lesson's timeline這一課的時間軸

Steps · 步驟

  1. Mark Hsinchu, Taichung, Tainan, and Kaohsiung on your map. Then mark your own town.在地圖上標出新竹、台中、台南和高雄，再標出你住的地方。
  2. Use string to show one chip's trip: design, manufacture, package and test.用線拉出一顆晶片的旅程：設計、製造、封裝測試。
  3. Beside each city, write one date from the timeline and what happened there.在每個城市旁邊寫上時間軸裡的一個年份，和那裡發生了什麼事。
  4. Ask at home: does anyone in your family, or a neighbor, work with chips or electronics? Which of the three jobs is theirs?回家問問看：家人或鄰居裡，有沒有人做和晶片或電子產品有關的工作？他們做的是三段裡的哪一段？

From Changhua, Taichung is right next door, and Hsinchu is up the coast to the north. You live in the middle of the chip island.
從彰化出發，台中就在隔壁，新竹在北邊的海岸線上。你就住在晶片島的中間。
