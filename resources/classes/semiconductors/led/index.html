What Do LEDs Have to Do with Semiconductors?LED 和半導體有什麼關係？

The lights in your classroom, the screen at the train station, and the little light on a charger are all LEDs, and an LED is a semiconductor. In a 3D model, connect a battery and watch electrons and holes meet and turn into light. Change the color, turn the battery around, and then mix three LEDs into one pixel.
教室的燈、火車站的大螢幕、充電器上的小燈，都是 LED，而 LED 就是一種半導體。在 3D 模型裡接上電池，看電子和電洞相遇、變成光。換個顏色、把電池反過來接，最後用三顆 LED 混出一個像素。

Big idea · 一句話看懂

An LED is a semiconductor that turns electricity straight into light. It has two layers: n-type, with spare electrons, and p-type, with holes. When a battery pushes them together, an electron drops into a hole and gives off its extra energy as light. The size of that energy step sets the color, and different semiconductor materials have different steps.
LED 是一種把電直接變成光的半導體。它有兩層：有多餘電子的 n 型，和有電洞的 p 型。電池把它們推在一起的時候，電子掉進電洞，多出來的能量就變成光放出來。這一步能量落差有多大，決定了光的顏色；不同的半導體材料，落差也不一樣。

The battery pushes electrons up from the n-type layer and holes down from the p-type layer. Where they meet, each electron drops into a hole and gives off one tiny packet of light, called a photon.電池把 n 型那層的電子往上推，把 p 型那層的電洞往下推。它們相遇的地方，每個電子掉進一個電洞，放出一小包光，叫做光子。

Connected backward, the battery pulls the electrons and the holes away from the junction. They never meet, so there is no light. A diode lets current flow in one direction only.反過來接的時候，電池把電子和電洞都從接面拉開。它們碰不到面，所以沒有光。二極體只讓電流往一個方向走。

With no battery, the electrons stay in their layer and the holes stay in theirs. Nothing pushes them together, and the LED is dark.沒有接電池的時候，電子待在自己那一層，電洞也待在自己那一層。沒有東西把它們推在一起，LED 是暗的。

There is no white LED chip. This is a blue LED under a layer of yellow phosphor. The phosphor turns part of the blue light into yellow, and blue and yellow together look white to our eyes.其實沒有「白光」的 LED 晶粒。這是一顆藍光 LED，上面蓋了一層黃色的螢光粉。螢光粉把一部分藍光變成黃光，藍光和黃光混在一起，我們的眼睛看起來就是白色。

  • Pink dots: holes in the p-type layer
    粉紅色的點：p 型層裡的電洞
  • Colored dots flying out: light

Unit 12: What Do LEDs Have to Do with Semiconductors?

Look around you. The lights on the ceiling, the traffic light at the corner, the big screen at the station, and the tiny light that tells you a charger is working are probably all LEDs. LED stands for light-emitting diode. And here is the surprise: an LED is a semiconductor, a close cousin of the chips in this series.

看看四周。天花板上的燈、路口的紅綠燈、車站的大螢幕，還有告訴你充電器正在工作的那顆小燈，很可能全都是 LED。LED 的意思是「發光二極體」。讓人意外的是：LED 就是一種半導體，和這個系列講的晶片是近親。

In Lesson 1 you saw that adding a few other atoms to a semiconductor changes it. One kind of atom gives spare electrons and makes n-type material. Another kind leaves empty places called holes and makes p-type material. An LED is simply a layer of n-type and a layer of p-type joined together. The place where they touch is called the junction.

在第一課你看過，在半導體裡加進一點點別的原子，它就會不一樣。有一種原子會多出電子，做成 n 型材料；另一種原子會留下叫做「電洞」的空位，做成 p 型材料。LED 其實就是一層 n 型和一層 p 型接在一起。它們碰在一起的地方，叫做接面。

Connect a battery the right way, and it pushes the electrons and the holes toward the junction. When an electron meets a hole, it drops in. As it drops, it gives up some energy, and that energy leaves as a tiny packet of light called a photon. A huge number of these, one after another, make the glow you see.

電池接對方向的時候，會把電子和電洞都往接面推。電子遇到電洞，就掉進去。往下掉的時候，它會放出一些能量，這些能量變成一小包光離開，叫做光子。數也數不清的光子一個接一個出來，就成了你看到的亮光。

What about the color? It depends on how big the energy step is. A bigger step makes bluer light, and a smaller step makes redder light. The size of the step is fixed by the material, so engineers choose different semiconductors for different colors. The plastic cover does not decide the color. The crystal inside does.

那顏色呢？要看這一步能量落差有多大。落差大，光就偏藍；落差小，光就偏紅。落差的大小是材料決定的，所以工程師要用不同的半導體來做不同的顏色。決定顏色的不是外面的塑膠殼，而是裡面的晶體。

Red LEDs came first, in the 1960s. Blue was much harder, and it took until the early 1990s. That mattered a great deal. With red, green, and blue together, a screen can show every color. And a blue LED covered with a yellow material called phosphor gives white light, the kind that now lights our homes.

紅光 LED 最早出現，是在 1960 年代。藍光難得多，一直到 1990 年代初期才做出來。這件事非常重要：有了紅、綠、藍三種光，螢幕就能顯示所有的顏色；而藍光 LED 蓋上一層叫做螢光粉的黃色材料，就會發出白光，也就是現在照亮我們家裡的那種光。

An old light bulb works by making a thin wire so hot that it glows, and most of its energy is lost as heat. An LED turns electricity into light directly, so it uses less power, makes less waste heat, and lasts longer. The next time you switch on a lamp, remember: you are using a semiconductor.

舊式的燈泡是把一條細細的金屬絲加熱到發亮，大部分的能量都變成熱浪費掉了。LED 直接把電變成光，所以比較省電、廢熱比較少，也用得比較久。下次開燈的時候，別忘了：你正在用一個半導體。

Questions · 閱讀理解

What two layers is an LED made of?

A layer of n-type semiconductor, which has spare electrons, and a layer of p-type semiconductor, which has holes.

How does an LED make light?

A battery pushes electrons and holes to the junction. When an electron drops into a hole, it gives off its extra energy as a photon of light.

What decides the color of an LED?

The size of the energy step in the semiconductor material. A bigger step gives bluer light, and a smaller step gives redder light.

Three LEDs, One Pixel · 三顆 LED，一個像素

Mix red, green, and blue light 把紅光、綠光、藍光混在一起

A full-color LED screen has no yellow LEDs, no white ones, and no pink ones. Each pixel has just three: red, green, and blue. From far away their light blends, and by changing how bright each one is, the screen can make a broad range of colors. Try it.
全彩的 LED 螢幕裡沒有黃色的 LED，也沒有白色或粉紅色的。每個像素只有三顆：紅、綠、藍。從遠處看，它們的光會混在一起；只要改變每一顆有多亮，螢幕就能做出各式各樣的顏色。試試看。

Or try one of these · 或試試這幾種

Mixing light is different from mixing paint. Light adds up, so more colors make the result brighter, and all three together make white. This is called the RGB color model. Your screen shows the mix only roughly.混光和混顏料不一樣。光是加起來的，所以顏色越多越亮，三種一起就是白色。這叫做 RGB 色彩模型。你的螢幕只能大致顯示混出來的顏色。

Stories About LEDs · LED 的故事

A glowing crystal, a red light, the blue that took thirty years, and how to make white 發光的晶體、一顆紅燈、等了三十年的藍，還有白光是怎麼來的

Four steps from a strange glow to the lamp on your desk.
從一道奇怪的微光，到你書桌上的檯燈，一共四步。

1927

A glowing crystal發光的晶體

Long before anyone used the word LED, the Soviet inventor Oleg Losev created one from silicon carbide in 1927. Light that comes from a material when electricity passes through it is called electroluminescence, and it is how every LED works.
早在有人使用「LED」這個詞之前，蘇聯發明家奧列格．洛謝夫就在 1927 年用碳化矽做出了一顆。電流通過材料時發出的光，叫做「電致發光」，所有的 LED 都是靠這個原理。

1962

A red light一顆紅燈

In 1962 Nick Holonyak was working at a General Electric laboratory when he demonstrated a semiconductor device that gave off visible light. It was the forerunner of the first commercial LEDs. Those early LEDs were dim and came only in red, and they were used as small indicator lights.
1962 年，尼克．何倫亞克在奇異公司的實驗室裡，展示了一個會發出可見光的半導體元件，它是第一批商用 LED 的前身。早期的 LED 很暗，而且只有紅色，被拿來當作小小的指示燈。

1990s · 藍光

The blue that took thirty years等了三十年的藍

Blue needed a different material, and it was very hard to make well. In the early 1990s three scientists from Japan, Isamu Akasaki, Hiroshi Amano, and Shuji Nakamura, finally developed blue LEDs. Their work brought white lighting and full-color LED displays into practical use, and in 2014 they won the Nobel Prize in Physics.
藍光需要另一種材料，而且很難做得好。1990 年代初期，三位來自日本的科學家赤崎勇、天野浩和中村修二，終於開發出藍光 LED。他們的成果讓白光照明和全彩 LED 顯示器真正派上用場；2014 年，三人獲得諾貝爾物理學獎。

White · 白光

How to make white白光是怎麼來的

No single LED chip makes white light. One way is to mix red, green, and blue LEDs. The more common way is to put a phosphor over a blue LED. The phosphor turns part of the blue light into yellow, and the mix looks white.
沒有哪一顆 LED 晶粒能自己發出白光。一種做法是把紅、綠、藍三種 LED 混在一起；更常見的做法是在藍光 LED 上面蓋一層螢光粉。螢光粉把一部分藍光變成黃光，混起來看就是白色。

Myth vs. Fact · 常見迷思

Four things people get wrong 四個常見的誤會

✗ Myth · 迷思The colored plastic decides the color of an LED.LED 的顏色是外面那層有顏色的塑膠決定的。

✓ Fact · 事實The semiconductor material inside decides it. A clear LED can still shine red, green, or blue.是裡面的半導體材料決定的。外殼透明的 LED，一樣可以發出紅光、綠光或藍光。

✗ Myth · 迷思An LED is a tiny light bulb with a wire that gets hot.LED 就是一顆很小的燈泡，裡面有一條會發熱的金屬絲。

✓ Fact · 事實There is no glowing wire. Light comes from electrons dropping into holes in a semiconductor.裡面沒有發亮的金屬絲。光是半導體裡的電子掉進電洞時放出來的。

✗ Myth · 迷思A white LED has a white chip inside.白光 LED 裡面有一顆白色的晶粒。

✓ Fact · 事實It usually has a blue chip covered with phosphor.裡面通常是一顆藍光晶粒，上面蓋著螢光粉。

✗ Myth · 迷思It does not matter which way you connect an LED.LED 怎麼接都可以。

✓ Fact · 事實It does. An LED is a diode, so it lights only when it is connected the right way.有差別。LED 是二極體，接對方向才會亮。

Safety First · 安全提醒

Before you try anything 動手之前先讀

  • Use only small batteries, such as a 3-volt coin cell, for LED experiments. Never connect an LED or any homemade circuit to a wall socket.做 LED 實驗只能用小電池，例如 3 伏特的鈕扣電池。絕對不要把 LED 或任何自己做的電路接到牆上的插座。
  • Coin cells are dangerous if swallowed. Keep them away from small children, and tell an adult right away if one is missing.鈕扣電池吞下去非常危險。不要讓小小孩拿到；如果少了一顆，馬上告訴大人。
  • Do not stare into a bright LED or a flashlight, and never shine one into anyone's eyes.不要盯著很亮的 LED 或手電筒看，也不要拿來照別人的眼睛。

Classroom Activity 1 · 課堂活動一

Which Way Does It Light? 哪一邊接才會亮？

You need · 準備材料

  • A small LED with two legs and a 3-volt coin cell battery一顆有兩隻腳的小 LED，和一顆 3 伏特的鈕扣電池
  • A piece of tape, and an adult to help一小段膠帶，還要請一位大人幫忙

Steps · 步驟

  1. Look closely at the LED. One leg is longer than the other.仔細看 LED，有一隻腳比另一隻長。
  2. Slide the battery between the legs so that the long leg touches the side marked +. Does it light?把電池夾在兩隻腳中間，讓長腳碰到標著「+」的那一面。它亮了嗎？
  3. Turn the battery around so that the long leg touches the other side. What happens now?把電池轉過來，讓長腳碰到另一面。現在呢？
  4. Draw what you saw. Add arrows to show which way the battery connection lets the LED light.把你看到的畫下來，用箭頭標出電池怎麼接 LED 才會亮。

You have just seen what the word diode means: one way works, and the other way does not.
你剛剛親眼看到了「二極體」這個詞的意思：一個方向可以，另一個方向不行。

Classroom Activity 2 · 課堂活動二

Pixel Hunt 尋找像素

You need · 準備材料

  • A phone, tablet, or computer screen showing a white page一個顯示白色畫面的手機、平板或電腦螢幕
  • A magnifying glass, or a small drop of clean water on a clear plastic sheet laid over the screen放大鏡；或是在螢幕上鋪一張透明塑膠片，上面滴一小滴乾淨的水

Steps · 步驟

  1. Look at a white part of the screen through the magnifying glass or the water drop. What colors do you see?透過放大鏡或水滴看螢幕上白色的地方。你看到哪些顏色？
  2. Now look at a yellow picture. Which of the three colors is missing or dim?再看一張黃色的圖。三種顏色裡，哪一種不見了或變暗了？
  3. Try red, green, blue, and black, and write down what you see each time.試試紅、綠、藍和黑色，每一次都記下你看到的。
  4. Compare your notes with the mixer on this page.把你的紀錄和這一頁的混色工具比一比。

Put the water on the plastic sheet, not on the screen itself, and wipe everything dry afterward. Not every screen uses LEDs for its pixels, but nearly all of them mix red, green, and blue.
水要滴在塑膠片上，不要直接滴在螢幕上，做完全部擦乾。不是每一種螢幕的像素都是 LED，不過幾乎都是用紅、綠、藍三色來混。
