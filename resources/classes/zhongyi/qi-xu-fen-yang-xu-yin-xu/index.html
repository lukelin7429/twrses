Unit 7: How Qi Deficiency Splits Into Yang and Yin

The first unit of this course ended with qi deficiency — a state where the body's basic functions are not being carried out with full force. Once yin and yang enter the picture, qi deficiency can be described more precisely, because it can lean in one of two directions. When the "warming" and "moving" side of qi runs low, the result is called yang deficiency: a person feels cold, especially in the hands and feet, moves slowly, and often prefers warm drinks and warm weather. When the "cooling" and "moistening" side runs low instead, the result is called yin deficiency: a person may feel warm in the afternoon or evening, have a dry mouth, and sleep restlessly despite feeling tired.

這門課的第一個單元最後停在氣虛——身體的基本功能沒有被全力執行的狀態。陰陽這個概念加進來之後，氣虛可以講得更精確，因為它可以偏向兩個方向的其中一個。當氣裡「溫煦」和「推動」的那一側不足，結果叫做陽虛：人會怕冷，尤其是手腳，動作變慢，通常偏好溫熱的飲料和天氣。當「滋潤」和「安定」的那一側不足，結果叫做陰虛：人可能在下午或傍晚覺得燥熱，口乾，雖然疲倦卻睡不安穩。

It helps to picture qi as a single team that yin and yang split between them. Yang qi handles warming and moving; yin qi handles moistening and calming. Plain qi deficiency is a general shortage of staff. Yang deficiency and yin deficiency describe which side of the team is short-staffed. A person can, in theory, have signs of both at once, which is simply qi deficiency that has not yet settled clearly into one direction.

把氣想像成一支被陰陽兩邊分掉的隊伍會比較好理解。陽氣負責溫煦和推動；陰氣負責滋潤和安定。單純的氣虛是整體人手不足；陽虛跟陰虛講的是隊伍的哪一邊人手不足。理論上一個人可以同時有兩邊的跡象，那只是還沒明確偏向某一個方向的氣虛。

None of this is something to self-diagnose from a reading passage. What matters here is the logic: Chinese medicine rarely stops at a single, flat label like "weak qi." It keeps asking a more specific question — weak in which direction — because the answer changes what actually helps.

這些都不是要你從一篇閱讀文章裡自我診斷。這裡重要的是背後的邏輯：中醫很少停在一個籠統的標籤，像是「氣虛」，就結束。它會繼續問一個更具體的問題——虛在哪個方向——因為答案會改變真正有幫助的做法。

Questions · 閱讀理解

According to the passage, what is the difference between yang deficiency and yin deficiency?

Yang deficiency is a shortage of the warming/moving side of qi (cold, slow, prefers warmth), while yin deficiency is a shortage of the cooling/moistening side (afternoon warmth, dry mouth, restless sleep despite tiredness).

Why does the passage compare qi to "a single team that yin and yang split between them"?

Because it helps explain that yang qi and yin qi handle different jobs within the same overall resource — general qi deficiency is a shortage of the whole team, while yang or yin deficiency describes which side is short-staffed.

According to the last paragraph, why does Chinese medicine avoid stopping at a flat label like "weak qi"?

Because asking "weak in which direction" gives a more specific answer, and that more specific answer is what actually changes what kind of help is appropriate.

Quick Check · 小測驗

1. According to the passage, what happens when the "warming and moving" side of qi runs low?

2. What does yin qi handle, according to the "team" comparison in the passage?

3. Why does the passage say Chinese medicine keeps asking "weak in which direction" instead of stopping at "weak qi"?

This lesson explains a distinction, not a self-test. Whether a set of symptoms points to yang deficiency, yin deficiency, or something else entirely is a judgment for a licensed doctor of Chinese medicine to make.
這是分類邏輯的說明，不是自我檢測；一組症狀究竟是陽虛、陰虛，還是別的情況，判斷交給合格中醫師。
