Unit 105: The Five Emotions, Mapped to the Five Elements

A later unit in this course covers the seven emotions as one of the causes of disease. The five-element system has its own, older version of the same idea, called the "five emotions": anger assigned to wood and the liver, joy to fire and the heart, worry (or pensiveness) to earth and the spleen, grief to metal and the lung, and fear to water and the kidney — five emotions mapped onto five elements and their associated organs, rather than seven.

這門課後面一個單元，把七情當成疾病的成因之一來討論。五行系統有自己一個更古老的版本，叫做「五志」：怒被分配給木與肝、喜被分配給火與心、思被分配給土與脾、悲被分配給金與肺、恐被分配給水與腎——五種情緒對應五行與它們各自關聯的臟腑，而不是七種。

The two systems don't map onto each other perfectly, and that mismatch is worth noticing rather than smoothing over: the seven-emotions framework adds fright and worry-as-a-separate-category to the five-emotions list, splitting some categories the five-element system treats as one. This isn't a contradiction to be resolved so much as two overlapping tools built for slightly different purposes — the five-emotions version ties each feeling tightly to the five-element cycles this unit has spent several lessons explaining, making it easier to reason about how one emotion's excess might affect an entirely different organ through the generating or controlling cycle.

這兩套系統彼此並不完全對應，這個不吻合值得被注意，而不是被含糊帶過：七情框架在五志清單上，加了驚跟另立一類的思，把五行系統當成同一類的一些情緒又拆開了。這不是一個需要被解決的矛盾，而更像是兩個為了稍微不同的目的而建構出來的、彼此重疊的工具——五志的版本，把每一種感覺都緊緊綁在這個單元花了好幾課解釋過的五行循環上，讓人更容易推理一種情緒的過度，可能透過相生或相剋的循環，如何影響到一個完全不同的臟腑。

This is what gives the five-emotions framework its distinct practical use: because anger is tied to wood, and wood generates fire, sustained anger is traditionally read as eventually placing a burden on the heart as well as the liver, following the same generating-cycle logic covered in an earlier lesson on treating the mother to rescue the child. Reading emotion through the five-element map, rather than as seven separate items on a list, is what makes that kind of cross-organ prediction possible in the first place.

這正是五志框架具備的獨特實用之處：因為怒綁定木，而木生火，持續的怒，傳統上被讀成最終不只加重肝的負擔，也會加重心的負擔，遵循的是稍早講「虛則補其母」那一課提過的同一套相生循環邏輯。透過五行地圖來讀情緒，而不是把它們當成清單上七個各自獨立的項目，正是讓這種跨臟腑的預測，一開始就能夠成立的關鍵。

Questions · 閱讀理解

According to the passage, what are the five emotions, and which element and organ is each mapped to?

Anger (wood/liver), joy (fire/heart), worry (earth/spleen), grief (metal/lung), and fear (water/kidney).

According to the passage, how does the seven-emotions framework differ from the five-emotions framework?

The seven-emotions framework adds fright and a separate category for worry, splitting some categories the five-emotions system treats as one.

According to the last paragraph, why might sustained anger eventually place a burden on the heart, not just the liver?

Because anger is tied to wood, and wood generates fire, so sustained anger is read as eventually burdening the heart too, following generating-cycle logic.
