Unit 114: Dryness: The Excess That Targets the Lung Specifically

This unit's lessons on the six excesses have so far covered wind, damp-heat, summer-heat, and cold, most recently discussed through the modern lens of air conditioning sickness. Dryness, most closely associated with autumn's climate, completes the list, and is traditionally described as an excess with an unusually specific target: because the lung, as an earlier lesson in another unit explained, connects directly to the outside air and is easily affected by whatever that air is carrying, dryness is traditionally understood to injure the lung specifically, more directly than any of the other five excesses target any other single organ.

這個單元講六淫的課，到目前為止講過風、濕熱、暑，還有寒，最近一次是透過現代的空調病這個角度來討論。燥，跟秋天的氣候關係最密切，補上了這份清單的最後一個，而且傳統上被描述成一種帶著異常具體目標的外邪：因為肺，就像另一個單元稍早一課解釋過的，直接連通外面的空氣，容易受到那股空氣所帶著的任何東西影響，燥傳統上被理解成特別傷肺，比其他五種外邪對任何單一臟腑的鎖定都更直接。

The traditional reasoning follows the lung's own described function: the lung depends on adequate moisture to keep its tissue supple and its passages clear, the way the earlier lesson on dispersing-and-descending described qi and fluids moving outward and downward through it. Dryness disrupts that moisture directly, traditionally producing a dry cough with little or no phlegm, a dry throat and nose, and dry skin — a cluster of symptoms all pointing toward moisture loss at the lung's own domain rather than a broader, whole-body complaint.

傳統的推理，跟著肺自身被描述的功能走：肺依賴充足的濕潤，來維持組織的柔軟與通道的暢通，就像稍早講宣發肅降的那一課描述過的，氣與津液透過它向外、向下運行。燥直接擾亂這份濕潤，傳統上會產生一種痰很少或沒有痰的乾咳、乾燥的喉嚨與鼻子、還有乾燥的皮膚——一組症狀，全都指向肺自己的領域裡濕潤的流失，而不是一個更廣泛的、全身性的抱怨。

This gives dryness a narrower, more predictable reputation than an excess like wind, which this unit's earlier lesson described as capable of combining with almost any other excess and reaching almost any part of the body. Dryness is traditionally read as comparatively single-minded, which is also why traditional seasonal advice for autumn, covered in a later unit, leans so heavily on keeping the body and its immediate environment adequately moist — a direct, specific countermeasure aimed at a direct, specific threat.

這讓燥擁有一個比風更狹窄、更可預測的名聲——這個單元稍早一課描述過，風有能力跟幾乎任何其他外邪結合、觸及身體幾乎任何部位。燥傳統上被讀成相對單純專一，這也是為什麼之後一個單元講秋天的傳統養生建議，這麼大量地依賴讓身體與周遭環境保持充足的濕潤——一個針對一個直接、具體威脅的直接、具體對策。

Questions · 閱讀理解

According to the passage, which of the six excesses does dryness complete the list of, and what organ does it target specifically?

It completes the list of six excesses, most closely associated with autumn, and is traditionally understood to injure the lung specifically.

According to the passage, what symptoms does dryness traditionally produce, and why?

A dry cough with little or no phlegm, a dry throat and nose, and dry skin — because dryness disrupts the moisture the lung depends on to keep its tissue supple and passages clear.

According to the last paragraph, how does dryness's reputation differ from wind's?

Wind is capable of combining with almost any other excess and reaching almost any part of the body, while dryness is comparatively single-minded, targeting the lung specifically.
