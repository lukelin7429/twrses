Unit 57: Qi Sinking: When the Lift Fails

An earlier unit lesson described qi's five functions, one of which was containment — holding blood in its vessels, holding organs in their place, holding fluids from leaking out for no reason. Qi sinking describes what happens when this specific function fails severely enough that things that should stay up or stay in start, quite literally, to sink or slip: a persistent dragging or heavy sensation in the lower abdomen, a tendency toward frequent, urgent urination, or in pronounced cases, an organ prolapsing lower than its normal position.

這門課稍早的一課描述了氣的五個功能，其中一個是固攝——把血固定在血管裡、把臟腑固定在它們該在的位置、把津液固定住不讓它無故流失。氣陷描述的是當這個特定的功能失效到夠嚴重的程度，該留在上面或留在裡面的東西，開始真的往下沉或滑脫：下腹部持續的下墜感或沉重感、頻繁且急迫的排尿傾向，嚴重的情況下，一個臟器下垂到低於它正常的位置。

This is best understood as a directional worsening of ordinary qi deficiency, rather than a completely separate problem — the same underlying shortage, but concentrated enough in the "holding up" function that gravity starts to win. It's traditionally linked most closely to the spleen, since the spleen's qi is specifically credited with keeping organs and tissues lifted in their proper place, on top of its more commonly cited role in digestion covered in an earlier unit.

最好把這理解成一般氣虛方向性的惡化，而不是一個完全獨立的問題——同樣底層的短缺，只是集中在「往上撐」這個功能上，集中到重力開始佔上風。傳統上它最常被連結到脾，因為脾氣被特別記上一筆，負責把臟腑與組織撐在該在的位置，這是在它更常被提到的消化角色（這門課稍早提過）之外的功能。

The classic response follows directly from the mechanism: rather than treating this as a local, mechanical problem to be pushed back into place, the approach is to restore the spleen qi that was supposed to be doing the lifting in the first place — the same rest-and-replenish logic used for ordinary qi deficiency, just aimed specifically at the function that gave out. Recognizing "sinking" as its own named pattern, rather than lumping it in with general fatigue, is what lets a doctor connect a seemingly unrelated symptom like frequent urination back to the same qi framework this whole course has been built on.

經典的應對方式直接延續自這個機制：不是把這當成一個要用機械方式推回原位的局部問題，而是要恢復一開始就該負責往上撐的脾氣——跟一般氣虛用的同一套休息與回補的邏輯，只是這次瞄準的是那個放棄了的特定功能。把「下陷」認出成它自己命名的一個模式，而不是把它跟一般的疲倦混在一起，讓醫師能把一個看似不相關的症狀，像頻尿，重新連回這整門課建立起來的同一套氣的框架。

Questions · 閱讀理解

According to the passage, what does qi sinking describe, and what symptoms might it produce?

What happens when qi's containment function fails severely enough that things that should stay up or stay in start to sink or slip — producing a dragging sensation in the lower abdomen, frequent urgent urination, or in severe cases, organ prolapse.

According to the passage, which organ is qi sinking most closely linked to, and why?

The spleen, since spleen qi is specifically credited with keeping organs and tissues lifted in their proper place, in addition to its role in digestion.

According to the last paragraph, why does recognizing "sinking" as its own named pattern matter for a doctor?

Because it lets a doctor connect a seemingly unrelated symptom like frequent urination back to the same qi framework, rather than lumping it in with general fatigue.
