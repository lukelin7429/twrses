Unit 99: The Four Directions Qi Moves In

Earlier lessons in this unit described qi sinking, where qi fails to hold things in their proper place, and rebellious qi, where qi moves in the wrong direction entirely. Both are specific breakdowns of a more general concept Chinese medicine calls "qi mechanism": the idea that healthy qi is always doing one of four things — ascending, descending, exiting, or entering — and that health depends on these four movements happening in the right balance and at the right time, not on qi simply being present in sufficient quantity.

稍早的課程描述過氣陷——氣沒能把東西固攝在該在的位置——跟氣逆——氣整個往錯的方向走。這兩者，都是中醫稱為「氣機」的一個更一般概念底下，具體的故障案例：健康的氣，永遠在做四件事的其中一件——升、降、出、入——而健康，取決於這四種運動以正確的比例、在正確的時機發生，而不是取決於氣單純足量地存在。

Each of the four movements has an organ most associated with directing it: the spleen is credited with the ascending movement, lifting nutrients upward; the stomach and lung with the descending movement, sending waste and turbid substances downward; the lung, again, with the exiting movement, distributing qi outward to the body's surface with each breath; and the kidney with the entering movement, drawing qi inward and downward to be anchored in reserve. A qi mechanism working correctly keeps all four moving in coordination, each one making room for the others.

四種運動，各自有一個最被認為負責主導它的臟腑：脾被歸功於升的運動，把養分往上提；胃跟肺被歸功於降的運動，把廢物與濁物往下送；肺，再一次，被歸功於出的運動，透過每一次呼吸，把氣分布到身體表面；腎則被歸功於入的運動，把氣往內、往下拉，固藏在儲備裡。一個正確運作的氣機，讓這四種運動協調地進行，每一個都為其他三個讓出空間。

Read through this framework, qi sinking and rebellious qi aren't two unrelated problems — they're both cases of one of the four movements failing at its job: qi sinking is a failure of the ascending movement, and rebellious qi, most often, is a failure of the descending movement being overridden by an upward surge. This is also why this unit's advice has so often mentioned specific directions — lifting, descending, venting, storing — rather than simply telling a reader to "have more qi": in this framework, direction is at least as important as quantity.

透過這個框架來讀，氣陷跟氣逆不是兩個不相關的問題——它們都是四種運動裡的其中一個，沒能盡到自己職責的案例：氣陷，是升的運動失職；氣逆，最常見的情況，是降的運動被一股向上的衝擊蓋過去。這也是為什麼這個單元的建議，這麼常提到具體的方向——提、降、宣發、收藏——而不是單純告訴讀者「多一點氣就好」：在這個框架下，方向至少跟數量一樣重要。

Questions · 閱讀理解

According to the passage, what are the four movements that make up "qi mechanism"?

Ascending, descending, exiting, and entering — health depends on these four movements happening in the right balance and at the right time.

According to the passage, which organ is credited with the entering movement of qi?

The kidney, which draws qi inward and downward to be anchored in reserve.

According to the last paragraph, how do qi sinking and rebellious qi relate to the qi mechanism framework?

They are specific cases of one of the four movements failing — qi sinking is a failure of the ascending movement, and rebellious qi is most often a failure of the descending movement being overridden.
