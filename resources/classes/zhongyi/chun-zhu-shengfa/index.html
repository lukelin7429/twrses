Unit 33: Spring: Encouraging Growth Without Forcing It

Spring is matched to wood and the liver in the five-element framework introduced earlier in this course, and Chinese medicine's classic advice for the season follows directly from that match: the season's job is encouraging growth, not forcing it. The Yellow Emperor's Inner Classic recommends going to bed a little later and rising earlier in spring, then taking a walk in loose, comfortable clothing — letting the body's energy stretch outward and upward the way new shoots do, rather than staying wrapped up and contracted as in winter.

春天在這門課稍早介紹的五行框架裡對應到木與肝，中醫對這個季節的經典建議，正是直接從這個對應而來：這個季節的任務是鼓勵生長，而不是強迫它。《黃帝內經》建議春天晚一點睡、早一點起，然後穿著寬鬆舒適的衣服去散步——讓身體的能量像新生的嫩芽一樣往外、往上伸展，而不是像冬天那樣裹得緊緊的、收縮著。

The emotional advice for spring follows the same logic. Because anger is matched to the liver, and the liver governs smooth, unobstructed qi flow, spring is considered a season when suppressed frustration does particular damage — qi that wants to move outward, like a spring shoot, but is blocked, builds pressure with nowhere to go. The classic recommendation isn't to avoid strong feelings entirely, but to avoid bottling them, and to give the mood room to move the way the season itself is moving.

春天的情緒建議遵循著同一套邏輯。因為怒對應到肝，而肝主導氣順暢、不受阻礙地流動，春天被認為是一個壓抑的挫折感會造成特別傷害的季節——想要像春天的嫩芽一樣往外移動的氣，卻被堵住，會累積壓力卻無處可去。經典的建議不是要完全避免強烈的感受，而是不要把它們悶住，並且讓心情有空間，跟著這個季節本身移動的方向移動。

None of this is about performing enthusiasm on command. It's about not working against a direction the season and the body are already leaning toward. A person forcing themselves to stay indoors, wrapped tightly, holding in frustration during spring is, in this framework, fighting the season on every front at once — physical posture, activity level, and emotional expression, all pointed the wrong way at the same time.

這一切都不是在講表演出來的、按指令展現的熱情。它講的是不要跟季節與身體本來就傾向的方向對著幹。一個在春天強迫自己待在室內、把自己裹得緊緊的、把挫折感悶在心裡的人，在這個框架下，是在同一時間、從每一個方向對抗這個季節——身體姿態、活動量、情緒表達，全部同時指向錯誤的方向。

Questions · 閱讀理解

According to the passage, what classic advice does the Yellow Emperor's Inner Classic give for spring, and why?

To go to bed a little later, rise earlier, and take a walk in loose clothing, letting the body's energy stretch outward and upward the way new shoots do — matching spring's association with wood and growth.

According to the passage, why is spring considered a season when suppressed frustration does particular damage?

Because anger is matched to the liver, which governs smooth qi flow; qi that wants to move outward but is blocked builds pressure with nowhere to go.

According to the last paragraph, what does the passage say a person is doing if they force themselves to stay indoors and hold in frustration during spring?

They are fighting the season on every front at once — physical posture, activity level, and emotional expression — all pointed the wrong way at the same time.
