Unit 10: Explaining Yin-Yang to a Friend

Suppose a friend asks, "Isn't yin-yang just a symbol from martial arts movies?" A good answer starts by separating the symbol from the idea. The black-and-white circle is a visual summary, not the concept itself, and the concept is worth explaining on its own terms: "Yin and yang describe two complementary functions in the body — substance and activity, cooling and warming, rest and movement. Neither one is 'good' or 'bad.' They're more like inhaling and exhaling: both necessary, neither one superior."

假設一位朋友問：「陰陽不就是武術電影裡的一個符號嗎？」一個好的回答要先把符號跟概念分開。黑白相間的圓形圖案是一種視覺上的濃縮，不是概念本身，概念值得用它自己的方式來解釋：「陰陽描述的是身體裡兩種互補的功能——物質與活動、寒涼與溫熱、休息與活動。沒有哪一個是『好』或『壞』的。它們比較像吸氣跟呼氣：兩者都是必要的，沒有誰比較優越。」

Next, it helps to explain why the idea matters practically, not just philosophically: "When a doctor of Chinese medicine says someone's yang is weak, they mean the warming and moving side of the body's functions isn't strong enough — the person feels cold, moves slowly, prefers warmth. It's a description of function, the same way saying someone has 'low energy' describes a pattern without claiming to have found a single broken part." This keeps the explanation grounded in something concrete rather than mystical.

接著，解釋這個概念為什麼在實際上有意義，而不只是哲學上有意義會有幫助：「當中醫師說一個人的陽虛，意思是身體功能裡溫煦與推動的那一側不夠強——這個人會怕冷、動作變慢、偏好溫暖。這是一種功能上的描述，就像說一個人『沒什麼精神』是在描述一種模式，而不是聲稱找到了某個壞掉的零件。」這讓解釋保持在具體的層面，而不是神祕的層面。

Finally, it's worth naming what yin-yang is not: "It's not a fixed personality type, and it's not a horoscope. It's not something a person 'is' once and for all — it shifts throughout the day, the seasons, and a person's life, and a doctor's job is to notice which direction it's currently leaning, not to hand out a permanent label." Ending on this note keeps a friend from walking away thinking yin-yang works like a zodiac sign.

最後，值得講清楚陰陽不是什麼：「它不是一種固定的人格類型，也不是星座。它不是一個人一次性、永久地『就是』什麼——它會隨著一天、季節、一個人的人生階段而改變，醫師的工作是注意它目前偏向哪個方向，而不是發一個永久的標籤。」用這句話收尾，能避免朋友離開時覺得陰陽的運作方式就像星座一樣。

Questions · 閱讀理解

According to the passage, why does a good explanation of yin-yang start by separating the symbol from the idea?

Because the black-and-white circle is only a visual summary, not the concept itself, so starting with it risks the listener mistaking a symbol for an explanation.

What everyday comparison does the passage use to explain why neither yin nor yang is "better" than the other?

It compares yin and yang to inhaling and exhaling — both are necessary, and neither one is superior to the other.

According to the last paragraph, what is yin-yang NOT, and why does the passage say ending on that note matters?

Yin-yang is not a fixed personality type or a horoscope — it shifts over time. Ending on this note matters because it prevents a friend from mistakenly thinking yin-yang assigns a permanent label, like a zodiac sign.
