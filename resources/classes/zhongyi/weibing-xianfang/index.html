Unit 119: Treating What Hasn't Happened Yet

An earlier lesson in this unit distinguished yangsheng, which operates before any problem appears, from treating disease, which responds to a problem that has already appeared. "Treat what hasn't happened yet" is the classical principle that names exactly this priority explicitly, ranking prevention above cure and describing a physician who waits for illness to fully develop before acting as already working at a disadvantage, no matter how skilled the eventual treatment turns out to be.

這個單元稍早的一課，把在任何問題出現之前就運作的養生，跟回應一個已經出現的問題的治病，區分開來。「未病先防」，是把這個優先順序明確命名出來的經典原則，把預防排在治療之上，並且把一個等到疾病完全發展出來才開始行動的醫師，描述成不論最終的治療多麼高明，都已經處在一個不利的位置。

The principle is traditionally illustrated with a comparison to two other kinds of waiting too long: digging a well only after becoming thirsty, and forging weapons only after a war has already begun. In both comparisons, the action itself isn't wrong, but its timing has already given away most of its usefulness — the well takes time to dig that a thirsty person doesn't have, and weapons take time to forge that a war already underway doesn't allow for. Treating an illness that has already fully developed is placed in the same category: not a wrong action, but one whose timing has already surrendered much of its potential effectiveness.

這個原則，傳統上用另外兩種等太久的比喻來說明：口渴了才去挖井，戰爭已經開打了才去打造武器。在這兩個比喻裡，這個行動本身並沒有錯，但它的時機，已經放棄了它大部分的用處——挖井需要時間，一個口渴的人沒有那個時間；打造武器需要時間，一場已經開打的戰爭不允許那個時間。治療一個已經完全發展出來的疾病，被歸進同一個類別：不是一個錯誤的行動，而是一個時機已經交出了它大部分潛在效果的行動。

This principle also gives a name to what this unit's hidden-pathogen lesson and this course's yangsheng lesson were both circling without stating directly: that Chinese medicine treats the gap between a problem's origin and its visible symptom as the most valuable window for action, precisely because it's the one window every later stage of treatment has already lost. Everything this course has categorized as yangsheng — regular sleep, seasonal eating, moderated emotion, appropriate movement — is, under this principle, an attempt to keep using that window for as long as a life allows, rather than an alternative to medicine for whenever that window finally closes.

這個原則，也為這個單元講伏邪的那一課、跟這門課講養生的那一課，兩者都在繞著、卻沒有直接講出來的東西，提供了一個名字：中醫把一個問題起源、跟它可見症狀之間的那段間隔，當成行動最有價值的窗口，正是因為這是每一個較晚階段的治療都已經失去的那個窗口。這門課歸類成養生的每一件事——規律睡眠、順應季節的飲食、節制的情緒、適當的活動——在這個原則下，都是一種嘗試，盡量長久地繼續使用那個窗口，而不是等那個窗口終於關閉之後，用來取代醫療的替代方案。
