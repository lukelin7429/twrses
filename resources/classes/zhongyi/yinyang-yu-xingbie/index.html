Unit 82: Yin-Yang and Gender: A Common Misreading

A frequent misreading among newcomers to Chinese medicine, especially readers encountering yin and yang through pop culture rather than the original texts, is treating the pair as a direct stand-in for gender: yin equals female, yang equals male, end of story. The association isn't invented from nothing — yin and yang do map loosely onto traits historically associated with femininity and masculinity, such as yin's stillness and yang's assertiveness — but treating the mapping as an equation rather than a loose analogy misrepresents how the framework actually works.

剛接觸中醫的人常見的一個誤解，尤其是透過流行文化而不是原始經典認識陰陽的讀者，是把這一對概念直接當成性別的替身：陰等於女性，陽等於男性，就這樣結束。這個聯想不是憑空捏造的——陰陽確實粗略對應到歷史上跟陰柔與陽剛連結在一起的特質，例如陰的靜止與陽的主動——但把這個對應當成一個等式，而不是一個鬆散的類比來看待，就誤解了這個框架實際運作的方式。

The clearest evidence against a strict equation is that every single person, regardless of gender, is described as containing both yin and yang, in a mix that shifts by organ, by time of day, and by season, not as one fixed label. A single body considered entirely yang, or entirely yin, doesn't appear anywhere in this framework — which alone rules out mapping it onto a two-category system like gender that a person is (in this framework's own terms) supposed to belong to entirely.

反對嚴格等式最清楚的證據是：每一個人，不論性別，都被描述成同時含有陰與陽，比例會隨著臟腑、隨著一天中的時辰、隨著季節而變動，而不是一個固定的標籤。一個被認為完全是陽、或完全是陰的身體，在這個框架裡根本不存在——單單這一點，就排除了把它對應到像性別這樣一個人（照這個框架自己的說法）應該完全屬於其中一類的二分系統上。

This matters for anyone trying to explain the concept to an English-speaking audience: reaching for "yin is basically the feminine principle" as a shortcut is exactly the kind of collapse that leads to garbled explanations. A more accurate description treats yin and yang as a pair of directional tendencies — toward stillness, interiority, and cooling on one side; toward movement, exteriority, and warming on the other — that apply to every body, every organ, and every process, independent of the gender of the person being described.

這一點，對任何想跟英語聽眾解釋這個概念的人來說都很重要：用「陰基本上就是陰柔原則」當捷徑，正是導致解釋走樣的那種塌陷。比較準確的描述，是把陰陽當成一對方向性的傾向來看——一邊傾向靜止、內收、偏涼；另一邊傾向活動、外顯、偏溫——適用於每一個身體、每一個臟腑、每一個過程，跟被描述的人的性別無關。

Questions · 閱讀理解

According to the passage, what is the common misreading of yin and yang among newcomers?

Treating yin as simply "female" and yang as simply "male," as a direct one-to-one equation rather than a loose analogy.

According to the passage, what is the clearest evidence against treating yin-yang as a strict equation with gender?

Every person, regardless of gender, is described as containing both yin and yang in a shifting mix — no body is entirely one or the other, which rules out mapping it onto a two-category system like gender.

According to the last paragraph, how does the passage suggest describing yin and yang more accurately?

As a pair of directional tendencies — toward stillness, interiority, and cooling on one side, toward movement, exteriority, and warming on the other — applying to every body regardless of gender.
