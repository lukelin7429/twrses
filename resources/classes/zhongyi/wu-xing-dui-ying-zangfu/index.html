Unit 14: Matching Organs to Elements

Chinese medicine assigns each of the five elements to a pair of organs, and this mapping is what makes the five-element system practically useful rather than purely decorative. Wood is matched to the liver and gallbladder. Fire is matched to the heart and small intestine. Earth is matched to the spleen and stomach. Metal is matched to the lung and large intestine. Water is matched to the kidney and bladder. Each pairing also comes with an associated emotion, season, and sense organ — anger with the liver, joy with the heart, worry with the spleen, grief with the lung, and fear with the kidney.

中醫把五行的每一個都對應到一對臟腑，而這個對應正是讓五行系統真正實用、而不只是裝飾性的關鍵。木對應肝與膽。火對應心與小腸。土對應脾與胃。金對應肺與大腸。水對應腎與膀胱。每一對搭配也都伴隨著一種情緒、一個季節、一個感覺器官——肝配憤怒，心配喜悅，脾配憂慮，肺配悲傷，腎配恐懼。

This matching is not anatomical in the Western sense — the "liver" in this system is a wider functional category than the organ a surgeon would remove, and it includes functions modern anatomy assigns elsewhere. What the mapping is genuinely useful for is noticing clusters of symptoms that, on the surface, look unrelated. A person under prolonged frustration who also develops a bitter taste in the mouth, tension headaches, and irregular digestion is, in this framework, showing a cluster that traces back to one element — wood — rather than four separate, coincidental problems.

這種對應不是西方意義上的解剖學對應——這套系統裡的「肝」是一個比外科醫師會摘除的那個器官更廣的功能類別，它包含了現代解剖學分配到別處的一些功能。這個對應真正有用的地方，是能注意到表面上看似不相關的一群症狀。一個長期處在挫折中、同時嘴巴發苦、有緊張性頭痛、消化又不規律的人，在這個框架下，展現的是一群可以追溯回同一個元素——木——的症狀群，而不是四個各自獨立、剛好同時發生的問題。

This is the real payoff of learning the five elements: not memorizing five categories for their own sake, but gaining a way to notice when several seemingly unrelated complaints are actually one pattern wearing different disguises. Chinese medicine calls this "treating the root rather than the branch" — and the five-element map is one of the main tools for finding the root in the first place.

這才是學五行真正的收穫：不是為了背誦五個類別本身，而是獲得一種能力，能注意到好幾個看似不相關的抱怨，其實是同一個模式換了不同的偽裝出現。中醫把這叫做「治本不治標」——而五行對照表正是找到「本」的主要工具之一。

Questions · 閱讀理解

According to the passage, which organs are matched to the "Wood" element, and which emotion is associated with it?

The liver and gallbladder are matched to Wood, and the associated emotion is anger.

Why does the passage say the "liver" in this system is not the same as the organ a surgeon would remove?

Because the "liver" in this system is a wider functional category that includes functions modern anatomy assigns elsewhere, not a strict anatomical match.

According to the last paragraph, what is the "real payoff" of learning the five elements?

Not memorizing five categories for their own sake, but gaining the ability to notice when several seemingly unrelated complaints are actually one pattern showing up in different forms.
