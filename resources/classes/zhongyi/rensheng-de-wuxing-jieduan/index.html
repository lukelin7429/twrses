The Five Elements of a Lifetime

The same five-stage pattern that describes a single year — growth, expansion, transformation, gathering, storage — also describes a human life.
描述一整年的同一套五階段模式——生長、擴張、轉化、收斂、儲藏——也能拿來描述一個人的一生。

Unit 65: The Five Elements of a Lifetime

This course's seasonal unit already applied the five elements to a single year and a single day, and Chinese medicine extends the same five-stage pattern one scale further, to an entire human life. Childhood and youth are matched to wood, the stage of rapid growth this course has already associated with spring — the body building itself outward with more raw material than judgment. Young adulthood is matched to fire, the peak of outward activity and expansion, the stage of highest physical vigor and social engagement.

這門課的節氣單元已經把五行套用到一整年跟一整天，中醫把同一套五階段模式再延伸一個尺度，套用到整個人生上。童年與青年對應到木，這門課已經跟春天連結過的快速生長階段——身體向外建構自己，原料比判斷力多。青壯年對應到火，向外活動與擴張的高峰，身體活力與社會參與最旺盛的階段。

Middle age is matched to earth, the stage of transformation and centering — no longer purely building outward, but converting accumulated experience into stability, the same role earth plays at the center of the five-element cycle rather than at either extreme. Later adulthood is matched to metal, a stage of gathering in and refining down to what matters most, mirroring autumn's turn from expansion toward selectivity. Old age is matched to water, the stage of storage and stillness, drawing on a lifetime's accumulated reserves rather than generating new ones.

中年對應到土，轉化與居中的階段——不再單純向外建構，而是把累積的經驗轉化成穩定，跟土在五行循環中央扮演的角色一樣，而不是在任何一個極端上。晚年對應到金，一個內收、把重要的東西提煉出來的階段，呼應著秋天從擴張轉向精選。老年對應到水，儲藏與靜止的階段，動用一輩子累積下來的儲備，而不是再產生新的。

None of this is a rigid timetable — people move through these stages at different paces, and the point isn't to assign an exact age to each one. What the mapping offers is a way to expect a person's needs to shift in a specific, recognizable direction as they age, the same way this course has already used the five elements to expect a season's or a day's needs to shift — treating a stage of life not as a fixed category to diagnose, but as a direction a person is currently moving through.

這一切都不是一份僵化的時間表——每個人以不同的步調經歷這些階段，重點不是給每個階段指定一個確切的年齡。這個對應提供的，是一種方式，去預期一個人的需求會隨著年紀增長，往一個特定、可辨識的方向轉變，就跟這門課已經用五行來預期一個季節或一天的需求會怎麼轉變一樣——把人生的一個階段，當成一個人目前正在經歷的方向，而不是一個要拿來診斷的固定類別。

Questions · 閱讀理解

According to the passage, which element is childhood and youth matched to, and which season does the passage connect it to?

Wood, connected to spring — the stage of rapid growth, building outward with more raw material than judgment.

According to the passage, what role does earth play at middle age, and how does it mirror earth's role in the five-element cycle generally?

Earth represents transformation and centering — converting accumulated experience into stability — mirroring earth's role at the center of the five-element cycle rather than at either extreme.

According to the last paragraph, what does the passage say this mapping is NOT meant to be?

A rigid timetable assigning an exact age to each stage — people move through the stages at different paces.
