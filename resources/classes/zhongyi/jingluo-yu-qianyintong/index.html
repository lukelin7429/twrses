Unit 23: How Meridians Explain Referred Pain

One of the clearest ways to make meridians feel less abstract is to connect them to something Western medicine already accepts: referred pain. A heart attack, for example, is well known in Western emergency medicine to sometimes cause pain that radiates down the left arm rather than staying in the chest — a connection modern medicine explains through shared nerve pathways. Chinese medicine had already mapped a very similar connection centuries earlier: the Heart meridian runs from the chest down the inner arm, and pain along that exact path has long been read as a sign of a heart-related pattern.

讓經絡感覺不那麼抽象的一個最清楚的方法，是把它們連結到西醫已經接受的一件事：牽涉痛。舉例來說，心臟病發作在西方急診醫學裡是眾所周知會有時候造成疼痛沿著左手臂放射，而不是只停留在胸口——現代醫學透過共享的神經路徑來解釋這個連結。中醫在好幾個世紀以前，就已經標出了一個非常類似的連結：心經從胸口一路走到手臂內側，沿著這條確切路徑出現的疼痛，長久以來被讀成心相關病證的一個徵象。

This kind of pattern shows up elsewhere too. Chinese medicine has long used the location of a headache as a diagnostic clue, because different meridians cross different parts of the head. Pain across the forehead is traditionally linked to the Stomach meridian; pain along the temples is linked to the Gallbladder meridian; pain at the very top of the head is linked to the Liver meridian. A patient describing exactly where a headache sits is, without knowing it, giving a meridian-based clue that a practitioner trained in this system knows how to read.

這種模式在別的地方也會出現。中醫長久以來把頭痛的位置當成一個診斷線索，因為不同的經絡穿過頭部的不同區域。橫跨前額的疼痛，傳統上跟胃經有關；太陽穴附近的疼痛，跟膽經有關；頭頂正中央的疼痛，跟肝經有關。一位病人描述頭痛確切位在哪裡，在自己不知情的狀況下，其實提供了一個以經絡為基礎的線索，讓受過這套系統訓練的醫師知道怎麼解讀。

None of this claims that a headache location has only one possible cause, or that this reading replaces an actual examination. What it shows is that "referred pain" and "location-specific symptoms" are not exotic ideas unique to Chinese medicine — they are a shared observation that two very different medical traditions arrived at through different methods, and meridians are simply the map Chinese medicine built to organize that observation systematically.

這一切並不是在主張某個頭痛位置只可能有一個原因，也不是說這種解讀能取代真正的檢查。它顯示的是「牽涉痛」跟「位置特定的症狀」，並不是中醫獨有的異國情調概念——這是兩套非常不同的醫學傳統，透過不同的方法，共同抵達的一個觀察，而經絡只是中醫用來有系統地整理這個觀察所建構的地圖。

Questions · 閱讀理解

According to the passage, what modern medical phenomenon does the passage compare to the Heart meridian's path down the arm?

Referred pain from a heart attack, which is well known in Western emergency medicine to sometimes radiate down the left arm.

According to the passage, which meridian is traditionally linked to pain across the forehead?

The Stomach meridian.

According to the last paragraph, what is the passage's main point about "referred pain" and "location-specific symptoms"?

That these are not exotic ideas unique to Chinese medicine, but a shared observation that two different medical traditions arrived at through different methods — meridians are simply the map Chinese medicine built to organize it.
