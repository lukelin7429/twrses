Unit 97: What "Yangsheng" Actually Means

Every unit in this course so far has used the word "yangsheng," usually translated loosely as "wellness" or "nourishing life," without ever pausing to define it precisely against its more familiar counterpart: treating disease. The two aren't the same activity aimed at different severities of the same problem; they're traditionally described as separate goals entirely. Treating disease responds to a problem that has already appeared, aiming to correct it. Yangsheng operates before any problem appears, aiming to keep the conditions that produce disease from arising in the first place.

這門課到目前為止的每個單元，都用過「養生」這個詞，通常鬆散地翻譯成「wellness」或「nourishing life」，卻從來沒有停下來，把它跟一個更熟悉的對照組——治病——精確地定義清楚。這兩者不是針對同一個問題、不同嚴重程度所做的同一種活動；它們傳統上被描述成完全分開的兩個目標。治病回應的是一個已經出現的問題，目標是把它矯正過來。養生則在任何問題出現之前就運作，目標是讓製造疾病的條件，從一開始就不會產生。

This distinction explains why this course's advice has consistently sounded so unremarkable — sleep on a regular schedule, eat with the season, avoid extremes of emotion, move the body regularly — rather than offering anything resembling a treatment protocol. Under the yangsheng framework, these ordinary habits aren't preliminary steps on the way to real medicine; they are the actual practice, evaluated by a completely different standard than a treatment would be. A treatment is judged by whether a specific problem resolves. Yangsheng is judged by whether problems keep failing to arise at all.

這個區分，解釋了為什麼這門課的建議，一直聽起來這麼平凡無奇——按規律作息睡覺、順應季節飲食、避免情緒的極端、規律地活動身體——而不是提供任何看起來像治療方案的東西。在養生的框架下，這些平凡的習慣不是通往真正醫學路上的初步準備；它們就是實際的practice，用一套跟評判治療完全不同的標準來評判。治療是用一個特定問題有沒有解決來評判的。養生是用問題有沒有一直不出現來評判的。

This is also the reason this entire series has repeated its closing reminder to see a licensed doctor at the end of nearly every lesson: yangsheng was never meant to substitute for treating disease once disease has actually appeared, and conflating the two is a traditionally recognized error in the framework itself, not just a caution added for a modern audience. Knowing which of the two goals a given piece of advice belongs to is what keeps "eat with the season" from ever being mistaken for a substitute for seeing a doctor about an actual symptom.

這也是為什麼這整個系列，幾乎每一課結尾都重複提醒去看合格醫師：養生從來就不是被設計來取代已經出現疾病之後的治療，把這兩者混為一談，是這個框架自己傳統上就認可的一個錯誤，不只是為了現代讀者才加上去的謹慎提醒。知道眼前這條建議屬於這兩個目標裡的哪一個，正是讓「順應季節飲食」永遠不會被誤認為，可以取代因為一個實際症狀去看醫師這件事的關鍵。

Questions · 閱讀理解

According to the passage, how does yangsheng differ from treating disease, in terms of its goal?

Treating disease responds to a problem that has already appeared and aims to correct it; yangsheng operates before any problem appears, aiming to keep the conditions that produce disease from arising at all.

According to the passage, why has this course's advice sounded consistently unremarkable rather than like a treatment protocol?

Because under the yangsheng framework, ordinary habits like regular sleep and seasonal eating aren't preliminary steps toward real medicine — they are the actual practice, judged by whether problems keep failing to arise.

According to the last paragraph, why does this series repeat its reminder to see a licensed doctor at the end of nearly every lesson?

Because yangsheng was never meant to substitute for treating disease once disease has appeared, and conflating the two is a traditionally recognized error, not just a modern caution.

Quick Check · 小測驗

1. According to the passage, what is the goal of yangsheng, as distinct from treating disease?

2. According to the passage, why do ordinary habits count as "the actual practice" of yangsheng?

3. According to the last paragraph, why does the series keep repeating its reminder to see a licensed doctor?

This lesson explains a conceptual distinction, not medical advice. Yangsheng practices are not a substitute for treating an actual illness — any symptom should be evaluated by a licensed doctor.
這是概念區分的說明，不是醫療建議；養生practice不能取代對實際疾病的治療——任何症狀，應該由合格醫師評估。
