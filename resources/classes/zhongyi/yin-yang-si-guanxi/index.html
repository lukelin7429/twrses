Unit 8: The Four Relationships Between Yin and Yang

Chinese medicine describes yin and yang as connected through four different kinds of relationship, and each one answers a different question about how change happens in the body. The first is opposition: yin and yang restrain each other, the way cold restrains heat and heat restrains cold. If one grows unchecked, it naturally holds the other back. The second is interdependence: neither one can exist without the other, the way a shadow cannot exist without light. Yin provides the substance that yang acts on; yang provides the activity that keeps yin from becoming stagnant.

中醫講陰陽之間有四種不同的關係，每一種都回答身體裡「變化怎麼發生」的一個不同問題。第一種是對立制約：陰陽互相牽制，就像冷會抑制熱、熱也會抑制冷。如果其中一個不受限制地增長，另一個自然會把它拉回來。第二種是互根互用：兩者都無法脫離對方獨立存在，就像影子不能沒有光而存在。陰提供物質讓陽去作用；陽提供活動讓陰不至於停滯。

The third relationship is called waxing and waning: yin and yang do not stay fixed — they shift back and forth constantly, the way day gradually gives way to night and back again. A small increase in one is normally matched by a small decrease in the other, keeping the overall balance steady. The fourth relationship is transformation: under the right conditions, yin can convert into yang and yang can convert into yin, the way water can become steam and steam can become water again. This is the relationship that explains sudden change — a long illness marked by cold, quiet symptoms (yin) can, at a certain point, suddenly flip into a hot, agitated state (yang), and Chinese medicine treats this as a known pattern, not a mystery.

第三種關係叫做消長平衡：陰陽不是固定不變的——它們持續來回移動，就像白天逐漸讓位給黑夜，再逐漸回到白天。一方小幅增加，通常會對應另一方小幅減少，讓整體維持穩定。第四種關係是相互轉化：在適當的條件下，陰可以轉變成陽，陽也可以轉變成陰，就像水可以變成蒸氣，蒸氣也可以再變回水。這個關係解釋了突然的變化——一場以寒冷、安靜症狀（陰）為主的長期疾病，可能在某個時間點突然翻轉成燥熱、躁動的狀態（陽），中醫把這當成一個已知的模式，不是一個謎。

These four relationships are not four separate rules to memorize in isolation. They describe one continuously moving system, seen from four different angles: how the two hold each other in check, how they depend on each other, how they shift gradually, and how they can suddenly flip. Together, they explain why "balance" in Chinese medicine is never a fixed point — it is a moving target that a healthy body keeps hitting, over and over, without ever settling into one extreme.

這四種關係不是四條要分開背誦的規則。它們描述的是同一個持續在動的系統，從四個不同的角度看：兩者怎麼互相牽制、怎麼互相依存、怎麼漸進地移動、又怎麼會突然翻轉。合起來，它們解釋了為什麼中醫講的「平衡」從來不是一個固定的點——它是一個健康的身體不斷重新命中的移動目標，不會停在某一個極端上。

Questions · 閱讀理解

According to the passage, what is the difference between the "opposition" relationship and the "interdependence" relationship between yin and yang?

Opposition means yin and yang restrain each other so neither grows unchecked, like cold and heat holding each other back, while interdependence means neither can exist without the other, like light and shadow.

What everyday comparison does the passage use to explain the "waxing and waning" relationship?

The passage compares it to day gradually giving way to night and back again, with small shifts in one side matched by small shifts in the other.

According to the passage, how does the "transformation" relationship explain a sudden change in illness, such as a cold state suddenly becoming a hot one?

It explains that under the right conditions, yin can convert into yang (and the reverse), the way water can become steam — so a long, cold, quiet illness can suddenly flip into a hot, agitated state, following a known pattern rather than being a mystery.
