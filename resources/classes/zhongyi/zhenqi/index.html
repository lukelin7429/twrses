Unit 79: True Qi: When All the Named Kinds Combine

An earlier lesson in this unit named four distinct kinds of qi — source, gathering, nutritive, and defensive — each with its own origin and job. Taken separately, this can make qi sound like four different substances working side by side rather than one coherent whole. Chinese medicine has a term for the combined, functioning result: "true qi," the actual qi circulating through a living body once source qi has been activated by gathering qi and distributed as nutritive and defensive qi together.

這個單元稍早的一課，分別命名了四種不同的氣——元氣、宗氣、營氣、衛氣——各自有自己的起源與工作。分開來看，這可能讓氣聽起來像是四種各自並行運作的不同物質，而不是一個一致的整體。中醫有一個詞，用來描述合併之後、實際運作的結果：「真氣」，一旦元氣被宗氣啟動、並以營氣與衛氣的形式合起來分布出去之後，真正在一個活生生的身體裡循環的那個氣。

The distinction matters because the four named varieties are, in a strict sense, components and precursors rather than the finished product doing the day-to-day work. Source qi sits in reserve; gathering qi is freshly produced but not yet distributed; nutritive and defensive qi are already circulating, but describing them separately obscures that a living body experiences them as a single, integrated force rather than four separate streams running in parallel.

這個區分很重要，因為嚴格來說，這四種被命名的種類，是組成成分與前身，而不是真正每天在做工作的成品。元氣坐在儲備裡；宗氣剛剛被生產出來，還沒被分布出去；營氣與衛氣已經在循環了，但把它們分開來描述，掩蓋了一個活生生的身體，體驗到的是一股單一、整合起來的力量，而不是四條各自平行運行的流。

True qi is, in effect, this course's answer to a question a careful reader might have been holding since its first lesson: if qi is one concept with four sources and functions, what do you call it once it's actually working in a real, living person? The answer is that "true qi" was never a fifth, separate category — it's simply the name for the other four once they've stopped being distinguished from each other and are doing what qi actually does.

真氣其實是這門課對一個細心的讀者可能從第一課就一直放在心裡的問題的回答：如果氣是一個有四個來源與功能的單一概念，那當它真正在一個活生生的人身上運作時，該叫它什麼？答案是「真氣」從來不是第五個、獨立的類別——它只是另外四種一旦不再彼此區分、真正在做氣本來就該做的事之後，得到的那個名字。

Questions · 閱讀理解

According to the passage, what is "true qi"?

The combined, functioning result once source qi has been activated by gathering qi and distributed as nutritive and defensive qi together — the actual qi circulating through a living body.

According to the passage, why can the four named kinds of qi, taken separately, be misleading?

Because they can make qi sound like four different substances working side by side, when in reality a living body experiences them as a single, integrated force rather than four separate streams.

According to the last paragraph, is "true qi" a fifth, separate category of qi?

No — it's simply the name for the other four once they've stopped being distinguished from each other and are actually doing what qi does.
