Unit 91: The Governing and Conception Vessels: The Body's Front-and-Back Axis

An earlier lesson in this unit introduced the eight extraordinary vessels as the meridian system's reservoirs, distinct from the twelve main meridians tied to specific organs. Two of those eight have become far more widely known outside Chinese medicine than the rest, largely through martial-arts fiction: the governing vessel and the conception vessel, running respectively along the midline of the back and the midline of the front of the body.

這個單元稍早的一課，介紹過奇經八脈是經絡系統的儲備庫，跟綁定特定臟腑的十二正經不同。這八條裡，有兩條在中醫之外變得遠比其他幾條有名，主要是透過武俠小說：督脈跟任脈，分別沿著背部正中線與身體前面正中線走。

In popular fiction, "breaking through" these two vessels is often portrayed as a dramatic, almost magical breakthrough unlocking hidden power. The traditional medical description is considerably less dramatic: the governing vessel is said to govern all yang meridians, acting as a kind of collecting point for yang qi running along the back, while the conception vessel plays the equivalent role for yin, collecting yin qi running along the front — together forming a front-back axis that the other meridians are traditionally described as connecting into.

在通俗小說裡，「打通」這兩條脈，常常被描寫成一種戲劇化、近乎神奇的突破，解鎖隱藏的力量。傳統醫學的描述遠沒有那麼戲劇化：督脈被說成統攝所有陽經，扮演一種沿著背部運行的陽氣的匯集點；任脈則為陰扮演對等的角色，匯集沿著身體前面運行的陰氣——兩者一起，構成一條前後軸線，其他經絡傳統上被描述成會連接進這條軸線裡。

Because they aren't tied to a single organ the way the twelve main meridians are, the governing and conception vessels are traditionally treated less as an emergency reserve for a single crisis and more as a structural spine the rest of the system leans on — one more reason overall posture and spinal alignment are traditionally discussed alongside meridian health, rather than in a separate category of "just movement." A fictional shortcut to power turns out, in the original framework, to be a description of structural support running quietly along two straight lines.

因為它們不像十二正經那樣綁定單一臟腑，督脈跟任脈傳統上被當成的，比較不是應付單一危機的緊急儲備，而更像是整個系統倚靠的一條結構性的脊柱——這也是為什麼整體姿勢與脊椎排列，傳統上會跟經絡的健康放在一起討論，而不是被歸進「純粹只是動作」這樣一個分開的類別。一個小說裡通往力量的捷徑，回到原本的框架裡，其實是一種沿著兩條直線安靜運行的結構性支撐的描述。

Questions · 閱讀理解

The governing vessel and the conception vessel — widely known largely through martial-arts fiction, which portrays "breaking through" them as unlocking hidden power.

The governing vessel governs all yang meridians, collecting yang qi along the back; the conception vessel plays the equivalent role for yin, collecting yin qi along the front — together forming a front-back axis.
