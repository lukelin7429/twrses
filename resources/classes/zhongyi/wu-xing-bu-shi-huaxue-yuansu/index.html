Unit 11: The Five Elements Are Categories, Not Chemicals

The phrase "Five Elements" — wood, fire, earth, metal, and water — sounds, to an English speaker, like an ancient and mistaken attempt at chemistry, the way early Greek thinkers once proposed that everything was made of four elements. Chinese medicine is not making a claim about chemistry at all. The five elements are a classification system, a way of sorting phenomena — organs, seasons, emotions, colors, tastes, even sounds — into five families based on shared patterns of behavior, not shared physical substance.

「五行」——木、火、土、金、水——這個說法對英語使用者來說，聽起來像是一種古老又錯誤的化學嘗試，就像古希臘思想家曾經主張萬物由四種元素組成一樣。中醫在這裡完全不是在講化學。五行是一套分類系統，一種把現象——臟腑、季節、情緒、顏色、味道，甚至聲音——依照共同的行為模式（不是共同的物質）分成五個家族的方法。

Wood describes things associated with growth, flexibility, and upward movement — spring, the color green, the emotion of anger, a shoot pushing through soil. Fire describes things associated with heat, activity, and rising — summer, the color red, joy, a flame reaching upward. Earth describes things associated with stability, nourishment, and centering — late summer, the color yellow, worry, soil that holds and feeds a plant's roots. Metal describes things associated with contraction, clarity, and letting go — autumn, the color white, grief, a blade that cuts cleanly. Water describes things associated with stillness, storage, and downward movement — winter, the color black, fear, water pooling at the lowest point.

木描述的是跟生長、柔韌、向上移動有關的事物——春天、綠色、憤怒這個情緒、一根從土裡冒出來的嫩芽。火描述的是跟熱、活動、向上升有關的事物——夏天、紅色、喜悅、一團向上竄的火焰。土描述的是跟穩定、滋養、居中有關的事物——長夏、黃色、憂慮、能承接並滋養植物根部的土壤。金描述的是跟收斂、清晰、放下有關的事物——秋天、白色、悲傷、一把切割乾淨俐落的刀刃。水描述的是跟靜止、儲藏、向下移動有關的事物——冬天、黑色、恐懼、匯聚到最低點的水。

None of these five categories describes a physical ingredient inside the body. They describe a pattern — the same way saying someone has a "fiery temper" doesn't mean flames are involved. Once the five elements are read as five patterns rather than five materials, the rest of the system stops sounding mystical and starts sounding like what it actually is: an organizing chart for how different parts of a person, and different parts of nature, tend to behave.

這五個類別沒有一個是在描述身體裡的某種物質成分。它們描述的是一種模式——就像說一個人「脾氣火爆」不代表真的有火焰參與其中。一旦把五行讀成五種模式而不是五種物質，這套系統的其餘部分就不再聽起來神祕，而會開始聽起來像它實際的樣子：一張整理「一個人的不同部分、以及自然界的不同部分，傾向怎麼表現」的組織圖。

Questions · 閱讀理解

According to the passage, what mistake does an English speaker often make when first hearing "Five Elements"?

They often assume it's a mistaken ancient attempt at chemistry, similar to the four elements proposed by early Greek thinkers, when it's actually a classification system based on shared patterns of behavior.

According to the passage, what does the category "Wood" describe, and what is one example given?

Wood describes things associated with growth, flexibility, and upward movement, such as spring, the color green, the emotion of anger, or a shoot pushing through soil.

According to the last paragraph, what does the comparison to a "fiery temper" help explain about the five elements?

It helps explain that the five elements describe patterns of behavior, not physical ingredients — just as a "fiery temper" doesn't literally involve flames.
