Unit 107: How Anything Gets Sorted Into One of the Five Elements

This unit has, across many lessons, assigned organs, colors, sounds, odors, flavors, and emotions to one of the five elements, without pausing to explain how any given item ends up in one category rather than another. The underlying method is called "comparison and categorization": each element is defined by a small set of characteristic qualities — wood by growth and flexible expansion, fire by warmth and upward movement, earth by stability and nourishing transformation, metal by contraction and clearing, water by coolness and downward flow — and anything observed in the world is sorted by comparing its own qualities against those five reference profiles.

這個單元在許多課裡，把臟腑、顏色、聲音、氣味、味道、情緒，分配到五行的其中一個裡，卻從來沒有停下來解釋，任何一個項目為什麼會落在這一類、而不是另一類。底層的方法叫做「取象比類」：每個元素，都由一小組特徵性的特質來定義——木是生長與柔順的擴張、火是溫暖與向上的運動、土是穩定與滋養轉化、金是收斂與肅清、水是清涼與向下的流動——而世界上被觀察到的任何東西，都是透過把它自己的特質，拿去跟這五個參考輪廓比對，來分類的。

This explains why the assignments this course has covered aren't arbitrary lookup-table entries to memorize by rote: the liver is assigned to wood because it's traditionally credited with a smooth, unobstructed, expansive function similar to a growing plant; anger is assigned to wood because a surge of anger produces an upward, expansive, hard-to-contain quality similar to rapid growth. Once the underlying qualities of an element are understood, many of this unit's individual correspondences become predictable rather than needing to be memorized as isolated facts.

這解釋了為什麼這門課講過的這些分配，不是要死背的隨意查表項目：肝被分配給木，是因為它傳統上被歸功於一種順暢、不受阻礙、擴張性的功能，類似一株正在生長的植物；怒被分配給木，是因為一陣怒氣，會產生一種向上、擴張、難以收束的特質，類似快速的生長。一旦理解了一個元素底層的特質，這個單元裡許多各自獨立的對應關係，就變得可以預測，而不需要當成孤立的事實來死背。

This is also why the same method can, in principle, keep extending indefinitely to items this course hasn't covered: a food, a weather pattern, a personality trait, a stage of an illness — anything with an identifiable set of qualities can, in theory, be compared against the same five reference profiles and sorted accordingly. The five-element system was never meant to be a closed list of five-item categories; it's a method for sorting, applicable to anything new that comes along, which is also why practitioners across centuries kept finding new items to add to each category without ever needing to invent a sixth element.

這也是為什麼同一套方法，原則上可以一直往下延伸到這門課還沒講過的項目：一種食物、一種天氣模式、一種性格特質、一個疾病的階段——任何有一組可辨識特質的東西，理論上都可以拿去跟同樣這五個參考輪廓比對，然後被分類進去。五行系統從來就不是一份封閉的、五個項目的清單；它是一套分類的方法，適用於任何新出現的東西，這也是為什麼歷代的醫家不斷找到新的項目加進每一類，卻從來不需要發明第六個元素。

Questions · 閱讀理解

According to the passage, what is "comparison and categorization," and what five reference qualities define the elements?

The method of sorting anything by comparing its own qualities against five reference profiles: wood (growth, expansion), fire (warmth, upward movement), earth (stability, nourishing transformation), metal (contraction, clearing), water (coolness, downward flow).

According to the passage, why is the liver assigned to wood, and why is anger assigned to wood?

The liver is assigned to wood because of its smooth, unobstructed, expansive function like a growing plant; anger is assigned to wood because it produces an upward, expansive, hard-to-contain quality similar to rapid growth.

According to the last paragraph, why was the five-element system never meant to be a closed list?

Because it's a method for sorting applicable to anything new, not a closed list — practitioners kept finding new items to add to each category without needing a sixth element.
