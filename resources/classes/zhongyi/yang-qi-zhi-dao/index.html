Restoring Qi Without Medicine

The basic principle for restoring qi has nothing to do with herbs — the hard part is following through.
補氣的大原則其實很簡單，而且不需要吃藥——難的是做到。

Unit 4: Restoring Qi Without Medicine

If overexertion drains qi, then restoring it should, in theory, be simple: spend less, and replenish more. Chinese medicine turns this into four practical habits. Reduce the mental load of constant worry and responsibility. Go to sleep earlier, ideally before eleven at night, when the body's yang qi is naturally being nourished. Eat and drink with some restraint, rather than relying on cold or raw food. And avoid sweating to excess, since heavy sweating draws directly on qi.

如果勞則氣耗，理論上要補氣就該很簡單：少支出，多回補。中醫把它變成四個具體的生活習慣：減少思慮與責任帶來的心理負荷；早點睡，理想上在晚上十一點以前，這時身體的陽氣正在被滋養；飲食有所節制，不要靠冰的或生冷的食物；避免過度流汗，因為大量流汗會直接耗損氣。

These four habits are not four unrelated tips picked at random. Each one addresses a different one of the five functions from earlier in this course. Reducing mental load cuts down on what qi has to move. Sleeping earlier increases how much qi gets replenished overnight. Eating with restraint keeps the transforming function from being overworked. Avoiding excessive sweating protects the holding function from being forced to work overtime.

這四個習慣不是四個隨機挑出來、彼此無關的小撇步。每一個都對應著這門課前面提過的五功能裡的不同一項。減少心理負荷，減少了氣需要去推動的量；早睡，增加了夜間氣被回補的量；飲食節制，避免了氣化功能被過度使用；避免過度流汗，保護了固攝功能不被迫超時工作。

In other words, the four habits are the same five-function framework, simply read in reverse: instead of asking what happens when qi runs low, they ask what protects it from running low in the first place. None of them require a prescription. All of them require follow-through, which is precisely why they are harder than they sound.

換句話說，這四個習慣就是同一套五功能框架，只是反過來讀：不是問氣不足時會發生什麼，而是問要怎麼保護氣，不讓它一開始就不足。這些都不需要藥方；需要的是持之以恆——這正是為什麼它們聽起來簡單，做起來卻困難。

Questions · 閱讀理解

List the four habits for restoring qi described in the passage.

Reducing mental load, sleeping earlier, eating and drinking with restraint, and avoiding excessive sweating.

How does the passage connect these four habits to the five functions of qi taught earlier in the course?

Each habit protects a specific function — reducing mental load lightens what qi must move, earlier sleep increases replenishment, restrained eating protects the transforming function, and avoiding excess sweat protects the holding function.

According to the last paragraph, why are these habits harder than they sound?

Because none of them require a prescription — they only require consistent follow-through, which is harder to sustain than following a one-time instruction.
