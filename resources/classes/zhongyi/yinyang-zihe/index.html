Unit 102: The Body's Own Drive Back Toward Balance

This unit's lessons on yin-yang imbalance — excess heat versus deficient heat, yang deficiency versus yin excess, the doubling point where an extreme flips into its opposite — have mostly described situations where something has gone wrong. A separate principle, "yin and yang harmonize themselves," describes something the framework credits the body with doing on its own: after a disturbance, yin and yang are traditionally said to have their own tendency to drift back toward balance, without every single imbalance requiring active correction from outside.

這個單元講陰陽失衡的那些課——實熱對虛熱、陽虛對陰盛、重陰必陽這個極端翻轉成對立面的臨界點——大多描述的是出了問題的狀況。一個獨立的原則，「陰陽自和」，描述的是這個框架歸功於身體自己會做的事：一次擾動之後，陰陽傳統上被說成有自己漂移回平衡的傾向，不是每一次失衡都需要外力主動來矯正。

This isn't a claim that no imbalance ever needs help — the many earlier lessons on specific imbalances make clear that plenty do. Rather, it describes a baseline capacity traditionally credited to a body that isn't severely depleted: a mild, temporary disturbance, such as a single night of poor sleep or a single day of overexertion, is expected to resolve largely on its own once the triggering circumstance passes, as yin and yang settle back into their usual relationship.

這不是在說沒有任何一次失衡需要幫助——稍早講各種具體失衡的那些課，已經清楚說明很多失衡確實需要。而是描述一種傳統上歸功於一個沒有嚴重耗損的身體的基本能力：一次輕微、暫時的擾動，例如一夜沒睡好，或一天過度勞累，一旦觸發它的情況過去了，就被預期大致上會自己解決，因為陰陽會自己安頓回它們平常的關係。

The principle matters because it sets an expectation for what actually needs intervention. Under this framework, a good question to ask before reaching for any active correction is whether the imbalance is one the body's own self-harmonizing capacity should reasonably be able to handle given a bit of rest and time, or whether it has gone on long enough, or deep enough, that the capacity itself is likely to be part of what's compromised. Yangsheng, covered in a later unit, is largely aimed at protecting this self-harmonizing capacity rather than replacing it.

這個原則很重要，因為它為到底什麼真的需要介入，設下了一個期待。在這個框架下，在伸手去做任何主動的矯正之前，一個好問題是：這次失衡，是不是身體自己的自我調和能力，只要給一點休息與時間就應該合理處理得來的那種，還是它已經持續得夠久、夠深，深到這個能力本身很可能也是被損害的一部分。之後一個單元會講到的養生，很大程度上，目標就是保護這個自我調和的能力，而不是取代它。

Questions · 閱讀理解

According to the passage, what does "yin and yang harmonize themselves" describe?

The body's own tendency, after a disturbance, to drift back toward yin-yang balance without every imbalance requiring active correction from outside.

According to the passage, does this principle claim that no imbalance ever needs active correction?

No — it describes a baseline capacity in a body that isn't severely depleted, not a claim that every imbalance resolves on its own.

According to the last paragraph, what question does this principle suggest asking before reaching for active correction?

Whether the imbalance is one the body's self-harmonizing capacity can reasonably handle with rest and time, or whether it has gone on long or deep enough that the capacity itself is compromised.
