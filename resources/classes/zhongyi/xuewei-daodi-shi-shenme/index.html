Unit 24: What Acupuncture Points Actually Are

An acupuncture point is best understood as a specific, mapped location along a meridian where the flow of qi is considered especially accessible — a place where stimulation reliably produces an effect somewhere else along that same meridian's path. There are roughly 360 commonly used points across the twelve main meridians, each with its own name, location, and traditional set of effects, built up through the same centuries of clinical observation that produced the meridian map itself.

一個穴位最好被理解成經絡上一個特定、已被標定出來的位置，在這個位置上，氣的流動被認為特別容易被觸及——一個刺激它就能可靠地在同一條經絡路徑上別的地方產生效果的地方。十二條主要經絡上，大約有 360 個常用穴位，每一個都有自己的名稱、位置，以及傳統上記錄的一套效果，是透過跟建構出經絡地圖同一個過程——好幾個世紀的臨床觀察——累積出來的。

A useful comparison is a light switch versus the wiring behind a wall. The meridian is the wiring — the path along which influence travels, mostly hidden. An acupuncture point is like a switch: a specific, accessible spot where a person can interact with that hidden system to produce an effect, without needing to see or touch the wiring itself. Just as flipping a switch in one room can turn on a light in a completely different room, stimulating a point on the foot can be intended to affect an organ deep in the torso.

一個有用的比喻是電燈開關跟牆壁後面的電線。經絡就是電線——影響力傳遞的路徑，大部分是隱藏起來的。一個穴位就像一個開關：一個特定、容易觸及的點，讓人可以跟那個隱藏的系統互動，產生一個效果，不需要看到或摸到電線本身。就像在一個房間按下開關，可以打開完全不同房間裡的燈一樣，刺激腳上的一個穴位，目的可能是要影響軀幹裡深處的一個臟腑。

This course will not name specific points or teach anyone to use them — locating and stimulating a point safely and appropriately is a skill that takes years of supervised training, and doing it incorrectly is not harmless. What matters here is understanding the concept clearly enough to make sense of what a practitioner is actually doing during treatment, and to know that acupuncture is a precise, trained skill — not a matter of poking anywhere that seems to hurt.

這門課不會指名特定的穴位，也不會教任何人怎麼使用它們——安全且恰當地定位並刺激一個穴位，是需要好幾年受監督訓練的技能，做錯了並不是無害的。這裡重要的是把這個概念理解得夠清楚，好讓你看懂醫師在治療過程中實際在做什麼，也知道針灸是一項精確、受過訓練的技能——不是隨便哪裡覺得痛就往哪裡扎。

Questions · 閱讀理解

According to the passage, what is an acupuncture point, and roughly how many commonly used ones are there?

A specific, mapped location along a meridian where the flow of qi is considered especially accessible; there are roughly 360 commonly used points.

According to the passage, what comparison is used to explain the relationship between a meridian and an acupuncture point?

A light switch versus the wiring behind a wall — the meridian is the hidden wiring, and the point is the accessible switch used to interact with that system.

According to the last paragraph, why does this course not name specific points or teach how to use them?

Because locating and stimulating a point safely and appropriately takes years of supervised training, and doing it incorrectly is not harmless.
