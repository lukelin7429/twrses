Unit 112: Which End of a Meridian Is the "Root"?

An earlier lesson in this unit explained why a point far from the pain is sometimes chosen over a point right at the site of the problem. That lesson didn't specify how a practitioner decides which distant point to pick among many candidates along the same meridian. "Root and branch, origin and knot" is the theory that answers that more specific question, describing each of the twelve main meridians as having a "root" near the limbs, where the meridian's qi is considered most concentrated and easiest to influence, and a "branch" or "knot" near the head or torso, where that same qi is considered most spread out and expressed.

這個單元稍早的一課，解釋過為什麼有時候會選擇一個離疼痛很遠的穴位，而不是問題發生位置正上方的穴位。那一課沒有具體說明，執業者如何在同一條經絡上眾多候選穴位中，決定要挑哪一個遠端的穴位。「標本根結」是回答這個更具體問題的理論，把十二正經每一條都描述成，有一個靠近四肢的「根」，這條經絡的氣被認為在那裡最集中、最容易被影響；還有一個靠近頭部或軀幹的「標」或「結」，同樣這股氣，在那裡被認為最分散、最被表達出來。

The root, typically located at or near the fingers and toes, is traditionally treated as the more powerful and more responsive location for adjusting a meridian's qi precisely because it's concentrated there rather than dispersed. The branch, located closer to the meridian's associated organ or the head, is where problems most often show up as visible symptoms, but is traditionally considered a harder location to influence directly, since qi arriving there is already spread thin across a wider area.

根，通常位在手指或腳趾附近，傳統上被當成調整一條經絡的氣時，比較有力、比較有反應的位置，正是因為氣在那裡是集中的，而不是分散的。標，位在比較靠近經絡對應臟腑或頭部的地方，是問題最常以可見症狀表現出來的地方，但傳統上被認為比較難直接影響，因為到達那裡的氣，已經分散到比較廣的範圍了。

This is the specific mechanism behind the general principle covered in the earlier lesson on distal point selection: a point is chosen far from the pain not simply because tradition says so, but because the root end of the relevant meridian is traditionally considered the more effective place to influence qi that will, following the meridian's own pathway, eventually reach and affect the branch end where the symptom is actually showing up.

這正是稍早講遠端取穴的那一課，所提到的一般原則背後的具體機制：選擇一個離疼痛很遠的穴位，不只是因為傳統這麼說，而是因為相關經絡的根端，傳統上被認為是影響氣比較有效的地方，而這股氣，沿著這條經絡自己的路徑，最終會到達並影響到症狀實際表現出來的那個標端。

Questions · 閱讀理解

According to the passage, what does "root and branch, origin and knot" describe?

Each of the twelve main meridians having a "root" near the limbs, where qi is most concentrated, and a "branch" or "knot" near the head or torso, where that qi is most spread out and expressed.

According to the passage, why is the root end traditionally considered a more powerful location for adjusting a meridian's qi?

Because qi is concentrated there rather than dispersed, making it easier to influence directly, unlike the branch end where qi has already spread thin.

According to the last paragraph, how does this theory explain the earlier lesson on distal point selection?

It gives the specific mechanism: a distal point is chosen because the root end is a more effective place to influence qi that will travel along the meridian and eventually reach the branch end where the symptom shows up.
