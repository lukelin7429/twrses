Unit 98: Rising With Natural Light Across the Year

This unit's lesson on the organ clock described a fixed daily cycle, and its lessons on the four seasons each described a different relationship to activity and rest. A much older and simpler line ties both ideas together: "rise with the sun, rest with the sun," originally describing farming life directly, but adopted by Chinese medicine as a basic template for how daily rhythm should shift alongside the changing seasons rather than staying fixed to a single clock time year-round.

這個單元講臟腑時鐘的那一課，描述了一個固定的每日循環，講四季的那些課，各自描述了一種跟活動與休息不同的關係。一句更古老、更簡單的話，把這兩個概念綁在一起：「日出而作，日入而息」，原本直接描述的是農耕生活，但被中醫拿來當成一個基本範本：每日的節律應該隨著變化的季節而調整，而不是全年固定在同一個時鐘時間上。

Applied through this unit's seasonal lessons, the line doesn't mean waking at the same hour every day of the year; it means letting the clock itself drift with sunrise and sunset, which change substantially between seasons. Spring and summer, described earlier in this unit as seasons of outward growth and venting, call for rising earlier alongside the earlier sunrise; autumn and winter, described as seasons of gathering in and storing, call for rising later and resting earlier alongside the later sunrise and earlier sunset — treating the sun's own schedule as the actual reference point, not the reverse.

透過這個單元的節氣課程來應用，這句話的意思，不是每年每天都在同一個時間起床；而是讓時鐘本身跟著日出日落漂移，而日出日落在不同季節之間變化很大。這個單元稍早描述過，春天與夏天是向外生長與宣洩的季節，因此要求隨著較早的日出而更早起床；秋天與冬天，被描述成收斂與儲存的季節，因此要求隨著較晚的日出與較早的日落，晚一點起床、早一點休息——把太陽本身的時程當成真正的參考點，而不是反過來。

The principle is a plain, low-tech example of something this whole course keeps returning to: Chinese medicine treats the body as one part of a larger natural system, not a machine that should run on a fixed schedule regardless of what's happening outside it. A modern, artificially lit environment can make it easy to ignore sunrise and sunset entirely, but the traditional advice was never really about clocks — it was about staying aligned with a rhythm the body was traditionally assumed to share with everything else moving through the same seasonal cycle.

這個原則，是這整門課一直回頭提到的一件事的一個樸素、低科技的例子：中醫把身體當成一個更大的自然系統的一部分，而不是一台不管外面發生什麼事、都應該按固定時程運轉的機器。一個現代的、人工照明的環境，可能很容易讓人完全忽略日出日落，但傳統的建議，從來就不是真的在講時鐘——它講的是，跟一個身體傳統上被假定會共享的節律保持一致，跟其他一切在同一個季節循環裡移動的東西一樣。

Questions · 閱讀理解

According to the passage, what does "rise with the sun, rest with the sun" mean when applied through this unit's seasonal lessons?

Not waking at the same fixed hour every day, but letting the daily schedule drift with sunrise and sunset, which change substantially between seasons.

According to the passage, how does the recommended rising and resting time differ between spring/summer and autumn/winter?

Spring and summer call for rising earlier alongside the earlier sunrise; autumn and winter call for rising later and resting earlier alongside the later sunrise and earlier sunset.

According to the last paragraph, what broader idea does this principle exemplify?

That Chinese medicine treats the body as one part of a larger natural system, staying aligned with a shared seasonal rhythm, rather than a machine running on a fixed schedule.
