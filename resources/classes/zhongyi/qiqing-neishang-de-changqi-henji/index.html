Unit 50: The Body Remembers: How Old Emotional Patterns Show Up Physically

An earlier lesson in this unit described how a single emotion, in excess, can disturb qi in a specific, describable way — anger raising it, fear sinking it, worry knotting it. What that lesson didn't fully address is what happens when the emotional pattern isn't a single event but a habit repeated for years, and the answer this course's framework gives is that the disturbance can outlast the feeling that caused it, settling into the body as a lasting pattern rather than a passing one.

這個單元稍早的一課，描述了單一一種情緒過度時，能以一種具體、可描述的方式擾亂氣——怒讓氣上升，恐讓氣下沉，思讓氣打結。那一課沒有完全談到的是，當這種情緒模式不是單一一次事件，而是重複好幾年的習慣時，會發生什麼事，而這門課的框架給的答案是：這種擾亂可能會比造成它的那個感受活得更久，在身體裡安頓成一個持續存在的模式，而不是一個過去式的模式。

A person who spent a decade in a high-conflict job, for instance, may no longer feel angry on a given day, yet still carry the physical pattern that years of frequent anger left behind — a liver qi that stays slightly too eager to move, showing up as a short temper triggered by small annoyances, or a tendency toward tension headaches that has nothing to do with that day's mood at all. The emotion that built the pattern is gone; the pattern it built remains.

舉例來說，一個在高衝突的工作裡待了十年的人，或許在某一天已經不再感到憤怒，卻仍然帶著多年頻繁憤怒留下的身體模式——一種稍微太急著往外衝的肝氣，表現成被小小的煩擾就觸發的短暫脾氣，或是一種跟那天的心情完全無關的緊張性頭痛傾向。造成這個模式的那個情緒已經消失了；它建立起來的那個模式卻留了下來。

This is part of why this course has repeatedly separated "an emotion" from "a physical pattern with that emotion's signature," rather than treating them as the same thing. A person can present with a liver-qi pattern while reporting they don't feel angry at all — the felt emotion and the bodily disturbance it once caused have become two different things, on two different timelines, and addressing only the mood a person reports in the moment misses the pattern that outlasted it.

這是為什麼這門課一再把「一種情緒」跟「帶著那種情緒特徵的一種身體模式」分開來看待，而不是把它們當成同一件事的一部分原因。一個人可能呈現出肝氣的模式，卻回報自己一點也不覺得憤怒——那個被感受到的情緒，跟它曾經造成的身體擾亂，已經變成兩件不同的事，在兩條不同的時間軸上，只處理一個人當下回報的心情，會漏掉那個比心情活得更久的模式。
