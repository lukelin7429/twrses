Unit 71: The Twelve Meridians Outside the Twelve Meridians: Sinew Channels

Every meridian covered so far in this unit has one thing in common: it connects, sooner or later, to an internal organ. Chinese medicine also describes a parallel system of twelve "sinew channels," following broadly similar paths along the limbs but connecting only to muscles, tendons, and joints — never reaching an internal organ at all. Where the main meridians are the system this unit has used to explain referred pain and organ-related symptoms, the sinew channels are the system behind purely physical strain: a pulled muscle, a stiff joint, a movement-based injury with no organ involvement whatsoever.

這個單元目前為止提過的每一條經絡都有一個共同點：它遲早都會連結到一個內在的臟腑。中醫也描述了一套平行的系統，十二「經筋」，大致沿著肢體上類似的路徑走，但只連結到肌肉、肌腱、關節——從來不會連到任何一個內在臟腑。這個單元用來解釋牽涉痛跟臟腑相關症狀的系統是主要經絡，經筋則是純粹身體勞損背後的系統：拉傷的肌肉、僵硬的關節，一個完全不牽涉任何臟腑的、動作造成的傷害。

This distinction matters diagnostically because two complaints that sound similar — pain along the same general path on the leg, for instance — can belong to entirely different systems. Pain following the sinew channel tends to be sharply localized to the affected muscle or joint, worsens with a specific movement, and has an obvious mechanical trigger, like an injury or overuse. Pain following the main meridian is more likely to fluctuate with internal factors — mood, digestion, time of day — the very things this unit's earlier lessons connected to organ function.

這個區分在診斷上很重要，因為兩個聽起來相似的抱怨——舉例來說，沿著腿部大致相同路徑的疼痛——可能屬於完全不同的系統。沿著經筋走的疼痛，往往鮮明地侷限在受影響的肌肉或關節上，會隨著特定的動作而加重，而且有一個明顯的機械性觸發點，像是受傷或過度使用。沿著主要經絡走的疼痛，比較可能隨著內在因素波動——心情、消化、一天中的時間——正是這個單元稍早幾課連結到臟腑功能的那些東西。

Recognizing sinew channels as their own system prevents a common mistake: treating an ordinary muscle strain as though it were an organ-related pattern, or the reverse, missing an organ-related pattern by assuming a symptom is purely mechanical. The two systems run alongside each other on the body's surface, but they answer to different causes — which is precisely the kind of distinction this unit has treated as worth making explicit rather than left to guesswork.

把經筋認出成它自己的一套系統，能預防一個常見的錯誤：把一個普通的肌肉拉傷當成臟腑相關的模式來處理，或反過來，因為假設一個症狀純粹是機械性的，而漏掉一個臟腑相關的模式。這兩套系統在身體表面彼此並行，卻回應著不同的成因——這正是這個單元一直當成值得講清楚、而不是留給猜測的那種區分。

Questions · 閱讀理解

According to the passage, what do the twelve sinew channels connect to, in contrast to the main meridians?

Sinew channels connect only to muscles, tendons, and joints, never reaching an internal organ, while the main meridians connect, sooner or later, to an internal organ.

According to the passage, what characteristics distinguish pain following a sinew channel from pain following a main meridian?

Pain following a sinew channel tends to be sharply localized, worsens with a specific movement, and has an obvious mechanical trigger; pain following a main meridian is more likely to fluctuate with internal factors like mood, digestion, or time of day.

According to the last paragraph, what common mistake does recognizing sinew channels as their own system help prevent?

Treating an ordinary muscle strain as though it were an organ-related pattern, or the reverse — missing an organ-related pattern by assuming a symptom is purely mechanical.
