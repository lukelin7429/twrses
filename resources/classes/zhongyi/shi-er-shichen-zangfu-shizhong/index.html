Unit 32: Living with the Body's Organ Clock

Chinese medicine describes a twenty-four-hour cycle in which each of twelve two-hour periods is associated with peak activity in a particular organ, following the same twelve meridians introduced earlier in this course. The pattern is often called the organ clock, and it gives a time-based answer to a question this course has asked from several angles: not just what an organ does, but when it's doing its most important work.

中醫描述了一個二十四小時的循環，其中十二個各兩小時的時段，分別對應到某個臟腑活動的高峰期，遵循的正是這門課稍早介紹過的十二經絡。這個模式常被稱為臟腑時鐘，它給了這門課從好幾個角度問過的一個問題一個以時間為基礎的答案：不只是一個臟腑做什麼，還有它什麼時候做最重要的工作。

A few points on this clock carry the most practical weight. The period from eleven at night to one in the morning is associated with the gallbladder, and from one to three in the morning with the liver — together considered the window in which blood is renewed and detoxified most efficiently, which is the basis for this course's earlier advice to sleep before eleven. The period from five to seven in the morning is associated with the large intestine, offering a time-based explanation for why a morning bowel movement is considered a healthy sign rather than a coincidence.

這個時鐘上有幾個點特別有實際的份量。晚上十一點到凌晨一點對應到膽，凌晨一點到三點對應到肝——這兩者合起來被認為是血液被最有效率地更新與解毒的窗口，這也是這門課稍早建議十一點前睡覺的依據。早上五點到七點對應到大腸，用時間的角度解釋了為什麼早晨排便被認為是一個健康的徵象，而不是巧合。

The organ clock isn't a rule that a person must be awake or asleep at exact minutes to stay healthy — bodies and schedules vary, and rigid anxiety about the clock defeats its purpose. Its real use is as a rough map: symptoms that reliably show up at the same time of day (a headache every afternoon, a cough that worsens every night) are treated as a meaningful clue pointing toward a specific organ's current state, not treated as a random coincidence of timing.

臟腑時鐘不是一條「必須在精確的分鐘睡著或醒來才能保持健康」的規則——每個人的身體與作息各不相同，對時鐘過度焦慮反而違背了它的用意。它真正的用處，是當成一張粗略的地圖：可靠地在一天中同一個時段出現的症狀（每天下午的頭痛、每晚加重的咳嗽），會被當成指向某個臟腑目前狀態的一個有意義的線索，而不是當成時間上隨機的巧合。

Questions · 閱讀理解

According to the passage, what is the "organ clock" and how does it relate to the twelve meridians?

The organ clock is a twenty-four-hour cycle in which each of twelve two-hour periods is associated with peak activity in a particular organ, following the same twelve meridians introduced earlier in the course.

According to the passage, which two organs are associated with the eleven p.m. to three a.m. window, and why does that window matter?

The gallbladder (11 p.m.–1 a.m.) and the liver (1–3 a.m.); this window is considered when blood is renewed and detoxified most efficiently, which is why the course earlier recommended sleeping before eleven.

According to the last paragraph, what does the passage say the organ clock is NOT meant to be used for?

A strict rule requiring exact wake and sleep times to stay healthy — rigid anxiety about the clock defeats its purpose.
