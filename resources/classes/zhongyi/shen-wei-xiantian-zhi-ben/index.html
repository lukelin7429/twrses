Unit 66: The Kidney as the Root of Both Yin and Yang

This course's organs unit described the kidney as storing a person's inherited constitutional strength, and its yin-yang unit described every part of the body as having its own local balance of yin and yang. Chinese medicine connects these two ideas directly: the kidney is said to house kidney-yin and kidney-yang specifically, and these two are treated as the root supply that every other organ's own yin and yang ultimately draws from, the way a single water table beneath the ground feeds every well drawing from it.

這門課的臟腑單元把腎描述成儲藏一個人先天體質根本的地方，陰陽單元則把身體的每一個部分都描述成有自己局部的陰陽平衡。中醫把這兩個概念直接連結起來：腎特別被認為藏著腎陰與腎陽，這兩者被當成一個根本的供應，其他每個臟腑自己的陰陽，最終都從這裡取用，就像地底下單一一層地下水，餵養著每一口從它取水的井。

This is why kidney-yin and kidney-yang deficiencies show up with an unusually wide reach compared to a weakness in most other organs. Kidney-yin deficiency, since it's the deepest reservoir of yin in the body, can show up as the deficient heat pattern covered earlier in this course, but originating from the root rather than staying local. Kidney-yang deficiency, as the deepest reservoir of yang, can show up as the yang deficiency pattern covered in the same lesson, again reaching wider than a single organ's cold.

這就是為什麼腎陰虛跟腎陽虛，相較於大部分其他臟腑的虛弱，會展現出異常廣泛的波及範圍。腎陰虛，因為它是身體裡陰最深的儲備，可能表現成這門課稍早提過的虛熱模式，但源頭來自根本，而不是停留在局部。腎陽虛，作為陽最深的儲備，可能表現成同一課提過的陽虛模式，同樣波及得比單一臟腑的寒還要廣。

This is also why prenatal qi, discussed in this course's first unit, and kidney-yin and kidney-yang are treated as different angles on the same underlying resource rather than three separate topics: the fixed inheritance from birth is stored specifically as this root balance of yin and yang, which is part of why depleting it is taken more seriously than depleting a more replaceable, locally generated resource elsewhere in the body.

這也是為什麼這門課第一個單元討論過的先天之氣，跟腎陰、腎陽，被當成同一個底層資源的不同角度，而不是三個各自獨立的主題：出生時那份固定的遺產，特別就是以這個陰陽的根本平衡儲藏起來的，這也是為什麼耗損它，比耗損身體其他地方比較容易再生、局部產生的資源，被看得更嚴重的部分原因。

Questions · 閱讀理解

According to the passage, what does the water-table comparison illustrate about kidney-yin and kidney-yang?

That kidney-yin and kidney-yang act as a root supply that every other organ's own yin and yang ultimately draws from, the way a single underground water table feeds every well drawing from it.

According to the passage, why can kidney-yin or kidney-yang deficiency show up with an unusually wide reach compared to weakness in other organs?

Because the kidney is the deepest reservoir of both yin and yang in the body, so a deficiency there can show up as the same deficient-heat or yang-deficiency patterns covered earlier, but originating from the root rather than staying local to one organ.

According to the last paragraph, why is depleting kidney-yin/yang taken more seriously than depleting a resource elsewhere in the body?

Because the fixed inheritance from birth (prenatal qi) is stored specifically as this root balance of yin and yang, making it less replaceable than a resource generated locally elsewhere in the body.
