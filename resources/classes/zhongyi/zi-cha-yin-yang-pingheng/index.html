Unit 9: Reading Your Own Yin-Yang Balance

Chinese medicine offers a simple, everyday way to notice which direction a person's yin and yang currently lean, long before anything becomes a medical problem. The signs are ordinary: temperature preference, energy through the day, thirst, and sleep. A person leaning toward yang excess or yin deficiency tends to run warm, prefer cold drinks, feel most energetic in the afternoon or evening, and sleep lightly or restlessly. A person leaning toward yang deficiency tends to run cold, especially in the hands and feet, prefer warm drinks, feel most sluggish in the morning, and sleep heavily but wake up unrefreshed.

中醫提供了一個簡單、日常就能觀察的方法，讓人能注意到自己的陰陽目前偏向哪個方向——遠在任何事情變成醫療問題之前。這些跡象都很平常：體溫偏好、一天中的精神狀態、口渴程度、睡眠品質。偏向陽亢或陰虛的人，通常容易覺得燥熱、偏好冰涼的飲料、在下午或傍晚精神最好、睡得淺或不安穩。偏向陽虛的人，通常容易怕冷，尤其是手腳，偏好溫熱的飲料，早上精神最差，睡得沉但醒來不解乏。

None of these signs mean anything on their own. A single cold morning does not make someone "yang deficient," any more than one sleepless night makes someone "yin deficient." What matters is a pattern that holds steady over weeks, not an isolated day. This is also why Chinese medicine asks so many small, seemingly unrelated questions in a consultation — thirst, sleep, sweating, appetite, mood — because no single answer is diagnostic by itself. The pattern across all of them is.

這些跡象單獨看都沒有意義。一個寒冷的早晨不能讓一個人變成「陽虛」，就像一個失眠的夜晚不能讓一個人變成「陰虛」。重要的是一個持續好幾週、穩定出現的模式，而不是單一一天的狀態。這也是為什麼中醫問診時會問許多看似不相關的小問題——口渴、睡眠、流汗、食慾、情緒——因為沒有任何單一答案本身能拿來診斷。所有答案合起來的模式才是關鍵。

The purpose of noticing these patterns in daily life is not to self-treat. It is to know your own baseline well enough to notice when something changes, and to describe that change clearly to a doctor. "I've been sleeping restlessly and craving cold drinks for the last month" is a far more useful sentence to bring into a clinic than "I don't feel like myself."

在日常生活裡注意這些模式，目的不是自己治療。目的是清楚了解自己的基準狀態，好在有變化時能注意到，並且能把那個變化清楚地描述給醫師聽。「我這一個月睡得不安穩，很想喝冰的」比「我覺得不太對勁」對看診有用得多。

Questions · 閱讀理解

According to the passage, what four everyday signs does Chinese medicine use to notice a person's yin-yang tendency?

Temperature preference, energy through the day, thirst, and sleep quality.

Why does the passage say a single cold morning does not make someone "yang deficient"?

Because what matters is a pattern that holds steady over weeks, not a single, isolated day — one instance doesn't establish a lasting tendency.

According to the last paragraph, what is the real purpose of noticing these patterns in daily life?

Not to self-treat, but to know your own baseline well enough to notice a real change and describe it clearly to a doctor.
