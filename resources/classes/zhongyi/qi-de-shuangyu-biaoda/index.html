Unit 5: Explaining Qi to a Friend

Suppose a friend who has never studied Chinese medicine asks, "What is qi?" A good answer follows a specific order, because getting the order right is what keeps the conversation from going in the wrong direction. Start here: "Qi isn't a substance you store — it's closer to the body doing its work. When we say someone's qi is weak, we're not saying a battery ran low; we're saying the body's functions aren't being carried out with full force."

假設一位從沒學過中醫的朋友問：「氣是什麼？」一個好的回答要按照特定的順序來講，因為順序對了，對話才不會走偏。可以這樣開始：「氣不是一種你儲存起來的物質，它更接近身體正在運作這件事本身。當我們說一個人氣虛，不是在說電池沒電了，而是在說身體的功能沒有被全力執行。」

Next, explain what those functions are: "In Chinese medicine, qi does five things: it moves things along, keeps you warm, defends you, holds things in place, and turns what you eat into something your body can use." Only after this should the conversation turn to measurement, because that question will come up regardless: "It's not a measurable quantity in the physics sense — it's a way of describing patterns of function. Asking how much qi there is is a bit like asking how much fitness someone has: real, observable in its effects, but not a substance sitting somewhere."

接著，解釋這些功能是什麼：「在中醫裡，氣做五件事：它推動事物、讓你保持溫暖、保護你、把東西固定住、把你吃的東西變成身體能用的東西。」只有講完這些之後，才輪到談測量，因為這個問題遲早會被問到：「從物理學的意義上，它不是一個可以測量的量——它是一種描述功能模式的方式。問氣有多少，有點像問一個人有多少 fitness：真實存在、能從它的效果看出來，但不是放在某處等著被秤重的一種物質。」

Finally, close with the boundary that matters most: "None of this replaces seeing a doctor. Qi is a framework for understanding how the body works as a whole — not a diagnosis, and not a substitute for one." Explaining qi well is not about finding a clever English word to replace it. It's about walking someone through the idea in an order that keeps them from getting stuck on the wrong question too early.

最後，用最重要的那條邊界收尾：「這一切都不能取代看醫生。氣是理解身體如何整體運作的一個框架——不是診斷，也不能替代診斷。」把氣解釋好，重點不是找到一個聰明的英文字來取代它，而是用一個順序帶著對方走過這個概念，不讓他們太早卡在錯的問題上。

Questions · 閱讀理解

According to the passage, why does the order of an explanation matter as much as its content?

Because if the wrong question — such as one about measurement — comes up too early, the listener gets stuck thinking of qi as a measurable substance, which makes the rest of the explanation harder to follow.

What is the purpose of comparing qi to "fitness" in this kind of conversation?

It shows that something can be real and observable in its effects without being a physical substance that can be measured or stored, which helps the listener let go of the "energy" framework.

Why does the passage suggest ending the explanation with a boundary about seeing a doctor?

Because without that boundary, a listener might mistakenly think the explanation is offering medical advice, rather than a cultural and theoretical framework.

Quick Check · 小測驗

1. According to the passage, why should the question about measurement come after explaining the five functions, not before?

2. What is the purpose of ending the explanation with a note about seeing a doctor?

3. According to the passage, what is explaining qi well really about?

Explaining qi to a friend is a cultural and language exercise, not medical advice. If your friend has a real health concern, the answer is always to see a doctor — Chinese or Western.
