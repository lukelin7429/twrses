Unit 77: Eating With the Solar Terms: One Concrete Example

This course's very first seasonal lesson argued that a fifteen-day solar term makes advice specific enough to actually follow, unlike a three-month season. This lesson makes good on that claim with one concrete example: "white dew," the solar term marking the point in early autumn when nighttime temperatures first drop enough for dew to visibly form on plants by morning — a specific, observable marker rather than a vague sense that "autumn is coming."

這門課最一開始的節氣課主張，一個十五天的節氣，能讓建議具體到真正可以照著做，不像一個三個月的季節。這一課用一個具體的例子，兌現了那個主張：「白露」，這個節氣標記著初秋，夜間溫度第一次降到足以讓露水在早晨明顯凝結在植物上的那個時間點——一個具體、可觀察的標記，而不是一種模糊的「秋天快到了」的感覺。

Because white dew sits early in the autumn stretch covered by an earlier lesson on the season's general advice, it sharpens that advice into something more actionable: the nights are cool enough to require covering the abdomen and feet while sleeping, since dampness and cold entering through the extremities is considered more likely once nighttime temperatures have specifically dropped this much, but the days are usually still warm enough that autumn's full dryness and its effect on the lung, discussed in the same earlier lesson, hasn't fully arrived yet.

因為白露位在稍早一課提過、秋天一般建議所涵蓋的那段時期的早期，它把那個建議磨得更具體、更能執行：夜晚已經涼到需要在睡覺時蓋住腹部和腳，因為一旦夜間溫度已經特別下降到這個程度，濕與寒從四肢末端入侵被認為更有可能發生，但白天通常還暖到，同一課討論過的秋天完全的燥、以及它對肺的影響，還沒有完全到來。

This is the payoff this course promised back in its first seasonal lesson: general advice like "eat moistening foods in autumn" is true but directionless, while a solar-term-specific version — introduce moistening foods gradually starting around white dew, before autumn's dryness peaks, rather than waiting until symptoms of dryness already appear — gives an actual window of time to act in. One solar term, examined closely, is what turns this entire unit's abstract seasonal framework into something a person could genuinely use on a specific day of the year.

這正是這門課早在它第一堂節氣課就承諾過的收穫：像「秋天吃滋潤的食物」這種一般建議是對的，卻沒有方向，而一個節氣特定的版本——從白露前後開始逐漸引入滋潤的食物，在秋燥達到高峰之前，而不是等到乾燥的症狀已經出現才開始——給了一個真正可以行動的時間窗口。一個節氣，仔細檢視之後，正是把這整個單元抽象的節氣框架，變成一個人在一年當中某個特定的一天真正能拿來用的東西。

Questions · 閱讀理解

According to the passage, what does the solar term "white dew" specifically mark?

The point in early autumn when nighttime temperatures first drop enough for dew to visibly form on plants by morning.

According to the passage, how does white dew's timing sharpen autumn's general advice into something more actionable?

It suggests covering the abdomen and feet while sleeping, since nighttime cold and dampness are more likely once temperatures have dropped this much, while days are usually still too warm for autumn's full dryness to have arrived.

According to the last paragraph, what is the difference between "eat moistening foods in autumn" and the solar-term-specific version of that advice?

The general advice is true but directionless, while the solar-term-specific version gives an actual window of time — introducing moistening foods gradually starting around white dew, before dryness peaks, rather than waiting until dryness symptoms already appear.
