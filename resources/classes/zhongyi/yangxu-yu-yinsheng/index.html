Unit 60: Yang Deficiency and Yin Excess: Same Cold, Different Cause

An earlier lesson in this unit distinguished excess heat from deficient heat — one caused by too much yang, the other by too little yin. Cold works the same way, in reverse, and deserves the same careful separation. Yang deficiency is a shortage: yang itself has grown too weak to warm the body properly, the way a fire that's been allowed to die down produces less heat than before. This kind of cold tends to be constant, accompanied by visibly pale skin, a preference for warm drinks and warm weather, and a general low energy that matches the qi deficiency covered earlier in this course.

這個單元稍早的一課，把實熱跟虛熱區分開來——一個是陽太多造成的，另一個是陰太少造成的。寒的運作方式一樣，只是反過來，也值得同樣仔細的區分。陽虛是一種短缺：陽本身變得太虛弱，沒辦法好好溫暖身體，就像一團被放任熄滅下去的火，產生的熱比之前少。這種寒通常是持續性的，伴隨著明顯蒼白的皮膚、偏好溫熱的飲料與天氣，還有一種跟這門課稍早提過的氣虛相符的整體低能量狀態。

Yin excess works by addition rather than subtraction: yin itself — the cooling, condensing substance — has become too abundant, overwhelming yang's warming function even though yang itself may be perfectly adequate. This kind of cold tends to feel heavier and more localized, often described as an oppressive coldness in a specific area, sometimes with visible fluid retention or swelling, since excess yin also tends to bring an excess of the fluid it's associated with.

陰盛的運作方式是「加法」而不是「減法」：陰本身——那個寒涼、凝結的物質——變得太過剩，壓過了陽的溫煦功能，即使陽本身可能完全足夠。這種寒通常感覺更沉重、更侷限在一個地方，常被描述成一種在特定區域感到的壓迫性寒冷，有時伴隨著明顯的水液滯留或水腫，因為過剩的陰也往往帶來跟它有關的津液的過剩。

The response, once again, runs in opposite directions. Yang deficiency calls for warming and strengthening yang directly, the way a fire needs more fuel. Yin excess calls for draining or transforming the excess substance itself, the way a flooded room needs drainage rather than a stronger heater. Confusing the two — warming a room that's actually flooded, or trying to drain a room that's simply lost its heat source — repeats the same error this unit already warned against for heat, just with the temperature running the other way.

應對方式再一次朝著相反的方向走。陽虛需要直接溫煦、強化陽，就像一團火需要更多燃料。陰盛需要把過剩的物質本身排掉或轉化掉，就像一個淹水的房間需要排水，而不是更強的暖氣機。把兩者搞混——去溫暖一個其實淹水的房間，或試圖排乾一個只是失去熱源的房間——重演了這個單元稍早關於熱已經警告過的同一個錯誤，只是溫度朝反方向運行。

Questions · 閱讀理解

According to the passage, what causes yang deficiency, and what symptoms are associated with it?

Yang deficiency is caused by yang itself growing too weak to warm the body properly; it's associated with constant cold, pale skin, a preference for warmth, and general low energy.

According to the passage, what causes yin excess, and what symptoms are associated with it?

Yin excess is caused by yin becoming too abundant, overwhelming yang's warming function; it's associated with heavier, localized coldness and sometimes visible fluid retention or swelling.

According to the last paragraph, why does the passage compare yin excess to "a flooded room" rather than "a room that's lost its heat source"?

Because yin excess involves an excess substance (like fluid) that needs to be drained or transformed, similar to how a flooded room needs drainage rather than a stronger heater — while a room that's lost its heat source (yang deficiency) needs warming instead.
