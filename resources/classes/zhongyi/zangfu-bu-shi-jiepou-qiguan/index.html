Unit 16: The Organs Are Functions, Not Anatomy

The biggest translation trap in this entire course arrives with a single word: "spleen." In English, the spleen is a fist-sized organ mostly known for filtering blood and supporting the immune system — most people could not name a single symptom of a damaged one. In Chinese medicine, the spleen (脾) is one of the busiest organs in the entire body: it governs digestion, transforms food into usable qi and blood, and keeps blood contained within the vessels. When a Chinese medicine textbook talks about the "spleen," it is describing a functional system that overlaps only partly with what a Western doctor calls by the same English word.

這整門課裡最大的翻譯陷阱，來自一個字：「脾」。在英文裡，spleen 指的是一個拳頭大小的器官，主要功能是過濾血液、支持免疫系統——多數人連受損的脾臟會有什麼症狀都說不出來。在中醫裡，脾是全身最忙碌的臟腑之一：它主導消化、把食物轉化成能用的氣與血，還負責把血液固攝在血管裡。當中醫教科書講到「脾」，它描述的是一個功能系統，跟西醫用同一個英文字（spleen）指稱的器官，只有部分重疊。

This is not an isolated mistake limited to one organ. Every organ name in Chinese medicine — liver, heart, lung, kidney — refers to a broader functional category, not a strict anatomical structure. The "kidney" in this system, for example, includes reproductive function and the storage of a person's inherited constitutional strength, roles that Western anatomy assigns to entirely different systems. None of this means Chinese medicine is wrong about anatomy; it means the two systems are answering different questions. Western anatomy asks, "What does this physical structure do?" Chinese medicine asks, "What set of functions travels together, and what should we call that set?"

這不是侷限在單一臟腑的個別錯誤。中醫裡的每一個臟腑名稱——肝、心、肺、腎——指的都是一個較廣的功能類別，而不是嚴格的解剖結構。舉例來說，這套系統裡的「腎」，包含了生殖功能，也包含儲存一個人先天體質根本的功能，這些角色在西方解剖學裡被分配到完全不同的系統。這不代表中醫在解剖學上是錯的；意思是這兩套系統在回答不同的問題。西方解剖學問的是：「這個物理結構做什麼？」中醫問的是：「哪一組功能總是一起出現，我們該把這組功能叫做什麼？」

Once this is understood, a strange-sounding sentence like "the spleen governs digestion" stops sounding like bad biology and starts sounding like what it actually is: a claim within its own coherent system, using a borrowed English word that happens to overlap only partially with its meaning. The organs in this unit should be read as functional categories first, anatomical structures a distant second.

理解這一點之後，「脾主導消化」這種聽起來很怪的句子，就不再像是錯誤的生物學，而會開始聽起來像它實際的樣子：在它自己這套自成一體的系統裡的一個主張，只是借用了一個剛好只有部分重疊的英文字。這個單元裡的臟腑，應該先被讀成功能類別，其次才是解剖結構。

Questions · 閱讀理解

According to the passage, what is the main difference between the "spleen" in Western anatomy and the "spleen" (脾) in Chinese medicine?

In Western anatomy, the spleen mainly filters blood and supports the immune system, while in Chinese medicine, the spleen governs digestion, transforms food into qi and blood, and keeps blood contained in the vessels — a much broader functional role.

According to the passage, what does the "kidney" include in Chinese medicine that Western anatomy assigns to a different system?

Reproductive function and the storage of a person's inherited constitutional strength.

According to the last paragraph, how should the organs in this unit be read — as anatomical structures first, or as functional categories first?

As functional categories first, with anatomical structure a distant second.
