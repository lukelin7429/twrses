Unit 106: Supporting One Element to Rescue Another

An earlier lesson in this unit described a treatment principle built on the generating cycle: strengthening an element's "mother" to rescue a weak "child." The controlling cycle, covered in one of this unit's first lessons, supports a parallel set of treatment principles, built on restraint rather than nourishment — restraining an element that has grown too strong in order to relieve the element it's been suppressing.

這個單元稍早的一課，描述過一個建立在相生循環上的治療原則：強化一個元素的「母」，來拯救虛弱的「子」。這個單元一開始的課程講過的相剋循環，支撐著一組平行的治療原則，建立在抑制而不是滋養上——抑制一個變得太強的元素，來緩解它一直在壓制的那個元素。

"Restrain wood to support earth" is the clearest example: when wood — associated with the liver — grows excessively strong, it's traditionally described as suppressing earth, associated with the spleen, past the controlling cycle's normal healthy limit, producing digestive symptoms that trace back to an overactive liver rather than a weak spleen on its own. The traditional response isn't only to strengthen the struggling spleen directly, but to restrain the overactive liver at the same time, relieving the pressure at its actual source.

「抑木扶土」是最清楚的例子：當木——對應肝——變得過度強盛，傳統上被描述成會把土——對應脾——壓制得超過相剋循環正常健康的限度，產生消化方面的症狀，這些症狀追溯回去，是一個過度活躍的肝，而不是脾自己單獨的虛弱。傳統的處理方式，不只是直接強化那個掙扎中的脾，同時也要抑制那個過度活躍的肝，在真正的源頭緩解壓力。

"Nourish water to moisten wood" runs the same logic through a different pair: water, associated with the kidney, is traditionally understood to generate wood, associated with the liver, along the generating cycle — so a liver showing signs of dryness or excess heat can be treated by nourishing the kidney that feeds it, rather than only cooling the liver directly. Between this lesson and the earlier one on mother-child treatment, the two cycles this unit opened with turn out to double as two separate treatment toolkits, not just two diagrams describing how the five elements relate to each other in theory.

「滋水涵木」把同樣的邏輯，套用在一組不同的配對上：水，對應腎，傳統上被理解成沿著相生循環滋養木，對應肝——所以一個表現出乾燥或過度燥熱徵象的肝，可以透過滋養餵養它的腎來處理，而不是只直接去清肝的熱。把這一課跟稍早講母子治療的那一課放在一起看，這個單元一開始的兩個循環，原來不只是描述五行理論上如何互相關聯的兩張圖表，而是雙雙兼任著兩套獨立的治療工具箱。

Questions · 閱讀理解

According to the passage, what does "restrain wood to support earth" mean, and which organs does it involve?

That when wood (the liver) grows excessively strong and suppresses earth (the spleen) past a healthy limit, treatment restrains the overactive liver as well as supporting the spleen directly.

According to the passage, why might digestive symptoms trace back to the liver rather than the spleen alone?

Because an excessively strong liver (wood) can suppress the spleen (earth) past the controlling cycle's normal healthy limit, producing digestive symptoms traced back to the liver.

According to the last paragraph, what does "nourish water to moisten wood" apply the generating cycle to?

It applies the generating cycle to the kidney (water) and liver (wood): nourishing the kidney that feeds the liver, to treat a liver showing dryness or excess heat, rather than only cooling the liver directly.
