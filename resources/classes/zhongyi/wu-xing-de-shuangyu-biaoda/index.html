Unit 15: Explaining the Five Elements to a Friend

Suppose a friend asks, "Why does Chinese medicine talk about wood and fire like they're organs?" A good answer starts by naming what the five elements are not: "They're not a claim that your liver is made of wood, and they're not an ancient chemistry theory. They're a classification system — a way of sorting things like organs, emotions, and seasons into five families based on how they behave, not what they're physically made of."

假設一位朋友問：「為什麼中醫講木跟火，講得好像它們是臟器一樣？」一個好的回答，要先說清楚五行不是什麼：「這不是在說你的肝是木頭做的，也不是一套古老的化學理論。它是一套分類系統——一種把臟腑、情緒、季節這類東西，依照它們怎麼表現（而不是它們物理上是什麼材質）分成五個家族的方法。」

From there, it helps to give one concrete example rather than listing all five: "Take 'wood.' It's associated with growth and upward movement, so it's matched with the liver, the emotion of anger, and springtime — not because they share a material, but because they share a pattern of pushing forward and expanding. When a doctor says stress is 'affecting your wood element,' they mean it's showing up in that whole family of related functions, not that there's actual wood somewhere in your body." This keeps the abstraction tied to something the friend can picture.

接下來，給一個具體的例子會比列出全部五個更有幫助：「拿『木』來說。它跟生長、向上移動有關，所以被對應到肝、憤怒這個情緒，還有春天——不是因為它們共享同一種材質，而是因為它們共享一種向前推進、向外擴張的模式。當醫師說壓力『在影響你的木』，意思是它正表現在那整個相關功能的家族裡，不是說你身體裡真的有木頭。」這讓抽象的概念保持在朋友能想像得出來的層面。

Finally, it's worth explaining why doctors bother with the system at all: "The real use is noticing patterns across seemingly unrelated symptoms — irritability, headaches, and digestive trouble showing up together, for example, all pointing back to one element rather than being three separate coincidences. It's a diagnostic tool, not a belief system, and it still ends the same way every lesson in this course does: for anything real, see an actual doctor."

最後，值得解釋為什麼醫師要費心用這套系統：「真正的用處在於能注意到看似不相關的症狀之間的模式——比方說，易怒、頭痛、消化不良同時出現，全都指回同一個元素，而不是三個各自獨立的巧合。這是一種診斷工具，不是一套信仰系統，而且它的結尾跟這門課每一課一樣：任何真實的健康問題，還是要去看真正的醫師。」

Questions · 閱讀理解

According to the passage, what are the five elements NOT, according to the explanation given to the friend?

They are not a claim that an organ is literally made of a material, and not an ancient chemistry theory.

According to the passage, why is "wood" matched with the liver, anger, and springtime?

Because they share a pattern of growth and upward movement — pushing forward and expanding — not because they share a physical material.

According to the last paragraph, what is the "real use" of the five-element system, and how does the passage frame it — as a belief system or a diagnostic tool?

The real use is noticing patterns across seemingly unrelated symptoms that trace back to one element; the passage frames it as a diagnostic tool, not a belief system.
