Winter: Storing Rather Than Spending

Unit 34: Winter: Storing Rather Than Spending

Winter is matched to water and the kidney in the five-element framework, and its classic advice runs in the opposite direction from spring's: the season's job is storing, not spending. The Yellow Emperor's Inner Classic recommends going to bed early and rising late in winter, "waiting for the sunlight" rather than rising in the dark — the reverse of spring's instruction to rise early, and a direct match to water's pattern of stillness, depth, and conservation.

冬天在五行框架裡對應到水與腎，它的經典建議跟春天走的方向剛好相反：這個季節的任務是儲藏，而不是支出。《黃帝內經》建議冬天早睡晚起，「必待日光」——等太陽出來再起床，而不是摸黑就起——這跟春天早起的指示正好相反，也直接對應著水靜止、深沉、保存的模式。

The same logic extends to physical activity and sweat. Because the kidney is said to store a person's inherited constitutional strength, and because excessive sweating was described earlier in this course as something that drains qi directly, winter is considered a poor season for intense exercise that produces heavy sweating. Moderate movement is still encouraged, but the classic advice draws a clear line between staying active and deliberately draining the body's reserves during the season meant for building them back up.

同樣的邏輯延伸到身體活動與流汗上。因為腎被認為儲藏著一個人先天的體質根本，而過度流汗在這門課稍早被描述成一種會直接耗損氣的事，冬天被認為不適合做會大量出汗的劇烈運動。適度的活動仍然被鼓勵，但經典的建議清楚劃出一條界線：在一個本該用來把身體的儲備補回來的季節裡，「維持活動」跟「刻意耗盡身體的儲備」是兩件不同的事。

The emotional dimension follows the pattern too: winter is associated with fear, matched to the kidney, and the classic advice is neither to chase excitement nor to dwell in anxiety, but to keep the mind as calm and settled as the season itself — closer to quiet contentment than to either fear or forced cheerfulness. Winter's entire set of instructions, read together, describes one coherent posture: conserve now, so there is something to spend later.

情緒的面向也遵循同樣的模式：冬天跟恐懼有關，對應到腎，而經典的建議既不是去追求刺激，也不是沉溺於焦慮，而是讓心保持像這個季節本身一樣的平靜與安定——比較接近安靜的滿足，而不是恐懼或勉強擠出來的開朗。冬天的整套指示，合在一起讀，描述的是同一種一致的姿態：現在保存起來，以後才有東西可以支出。

Questions · 閱讀理解

According to the passage, what does the Yellow Emperor's Inner Classic recommend for sleep in winter, and how does it compare to spring's advice?

Going to bed early and rising late, waiting for the sunlight — the reverse of spring's instruction to rise early, matching water's pattern of stillness and conservation.

According to the passage, why is winter considered a poor season for intense exercise that produces heavy sweating?

Because the kidney stores a person's inherited constitutional strength, and excessive sweating drains qi directly — winter is meant for building reserves back up, not draining them.

According to the last paragraph, what "one coherent posture" does winter's entire set of instructions describe?

Conserve now, so there is something to spend later.

Words & Phrases · 生字及片語

conserve(v.)保存；節省It's important to conserve energy during a long hike.在一段長途健行中，保存體力很重要。reserve(n.)儲備The body draws on its reserves when food intake is low.食物攝取不足時，身體會動用它的儲備。deliberately(adv.)刻意地He deliberately avoided the topic during the meeting.他在會議中刻意迴避了這個話題。contentment(n.)滿足There was a quiet contentment in her voice as she described the trip.她描述這趟旅行時，聲音裡有一種安靜的滿足。cheerfulness(n.)開朗；愉快Forced cheerfulness can feel just as tiring as sadness.勉強擠出來的開朗，感覺起來可能跟悲傷一樣累人。dwell(v.)沉溺；久留Try not to dwell on a mistake once you've learned from it.從一個錯誤中學到教訓之後，盡量不要一直沉溺其中。
