Unit 73: The Epidemic Factor: When an Excess Spreads Between People

This course's earlier lesson on the six excesses made a specific point: the same weather can leave one person unaffected and another person unwell, because the excess only becomes a problem at the meeting point between the environment and a particular body's resilience. Chinese medicine describes a separate category that doesn't follow this rule: the "epidemic factor," a pattern recognized, centuries before germ theory existed, as spreading from person to person regardless of individual resilience, and often striking an entire community within a similar window of time.

這門課稍早關於六淫的一課提出了一個具體的重點：同樣的天氣，可以讓一個人完全沒事，卻讓另一個人明顯不舒服，因為病邪只有在環境跟一個人自己的抵抗力相遇的那個點上才會變成問題。中醫描述了一個不遵循這條規則的獨立類別：「疫癘之氣」，一個在細菌致病論存在之前好幾個世紀就被認出來的模式，不論個人的抵抗力如何都會從一個人傳播到另一個人，而且常常在相近的一段時間裡打擊整個社群。

This category was set apart from the ordinary six excesses specifically because it didn't behave like them. A cold that only affects people already run down by exhaustion is one kind of pattern; an illness that moves rapidly through an entire household, a school, or a town, affecting the strong and the weak alike, is described as a different kind of pattern entirely, with its own name reflecting that difference — closer to a modern epidemiologist's category than to an individual patient's constitution.

這個類別之所以被從一般的六淫裡分開來，正是因為它的表現方式跟六淫不一樣。一種只影響那些已經因為過勞而虛弱的人的感冒，是一種模式；一種快速地在整個家庭、一間學校，或一座城鎮裡傳開，強壯的人跟虛弱的人都一樣受影響的疾病，被描述成完全不同的另一種模式，有自己的名字反映這個差別——比較接近現代流行病學家的類別，而不是一個個病人的體質。

The reason this deserves its own lesson, rather than being folded into the six excesses, is what it shows about the limits and honesty of the observational method this course has described throughout: Chinese medicine, working centuries before microorganisms could be seen or named, still noticed and separately categorized a pattern of illness that clearly spread between people rather than arising independently in each one — a genuine observation about transmission, arrived at without the tools modern medicine would later use to explain the mechanism behind it.

這件事值得擁有自己的一課，而不是折疊進六淫裡，理由在於它揭示了這門課從頭到尾描述的觀察方法的極限與誠實：中醫在能看見或命名微生物的好幾個世紀之前，就仍然注意到並且另外分類出一種明顯在人與人之間傳播、而不是各自獨立產生的疾病模式——一個關於傳染的真實觀察，在沒有現代醫學後來會用來解釋這個機制背後的工具的情況下，就得出來了。

Questions · 閱讀理解

According to the passage, what earlier point about the six excesses does the epidemic factor NOT follow?

That the same weather can leave one person unaffected and another unwell, depending on individual resilience — the epidemic factor spreads regardless of individual resilience.

According to the passage, what distinguishes an illness caused by the epidemic factor from an ordinary cold?

An ordinary cold tends to affect people already weakened by exhaustion, while an epidemic-factor illness moves rapidly through an entire community, affecting the strong and weak alike.

According to the last paragraph, what does the epidemic factor category reveal about Chinese medicine's observational method?

That Chinese medicine, working centuries before microorganisms could be seen, still noticed and separately categorized a pattern of illness that clearly spread between people — a genuine observation about transmission made without the tools to explain its mechanism.
