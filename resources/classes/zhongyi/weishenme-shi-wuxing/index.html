Why Five, Not Four

Ancient Greece settled on four elements. Chinese medicine settled on five. The extra one is what makes the whole system close into a loop.
古希臘定下了四元素。中醫定下了五行。多出來的那一個，正是讓整套系統閉合成一個循環的關鍵。

Unit 63: Why Five, Not Four

A fair question, once earth, water, fire, and air sound familiar from other traditions, is why Chinese medicine insists on a fifth element rather than stopping at four. The answer isn't aesthetic — it's structural, and it comes down to what this course's earlier lessons on the generating and controlling cycles actually require to function. Both cycles depend on each element having exactly one "parent," one "child," one controller, and one element it controls, forming a complete, closed loop with no loose ends.

一旦土、水、火、風聽起來跟其他傳統的說法很像，一個公道的問題是，為什麼中醫堅持要有第五個元素，而不是停在四個。答案不是美學上的——是結構上的，而且要回到這門課稍早關於相生跟相剋循環的那兩課，實際上需要什麼才能運作。這兩個循環都依賴每個元素恰好有一個「母」、一個「子」、一個克它的、一個它克的，形成一個完整、閉合、沒有鬆散端點的循環。

Four elements arranged in a cycle leave an awkward asymmetry: with an even number, opposite elements end up directly across from each other with no natural connecting step between them, and the neat one-directional flow the generating and controlling cycles depend on breaks down. Five elements, by contrast, form the smallest possible loop where every single element has a clean, single relationship to every other position in the cycle — one step to generate forward, one step to be controlled, and so on, with nothing left over and nothing doubled up.

四個元素排成一個循環，會留下一個彆扭的不對稱：偶數的情況下，相對的元素最終會直接面對面，中間沒有自然的連接步驟，而相生相剋循環依賴的那種整齊、單一方向的流動就會崩解。相反地，五個元素形成了最小可能的循環，每一個元素對循環裡的每一個其他位置，都有一個乾淨、單一的關係——往前一步是生，被剋一步，以此類推，沒有剩下的，也沒有重複的。

The fifth element, earth, does more than complete the count — it's assigned to the center, matched to the transformation function of the spleen covered in an earlier unit, sitting at the pivot point the other four's movement passes through. Rather than four elements pointing outward in four directions with nothing holding the middle, earth gives the system a stable center that the other four rotate around — which is arguably the more important reason for "five" than simply needing one more item to complete a list.

第五個元素，土，做的不只是湊足數量——它被分配到中央，對應著這個單元稍早提過的、脾的轉化功能，坐在其他四個的運行都會經過的樞紐點上。中央不是四個元素向外指向四個方向、中間卻沒有東西撐著，土給了這套系統一個穩定的中心，讓其他四個繞著它轉——這可以說是比「單純需要多一項來湊滿清單」更重要的、「五」的理由。
