Unit 56: Qi Deficiency and Qi Stagnation Are Not the Same Thing

This course's first unit described qi deficiency in detail: a general shortage, where the body's functions aren't being carried out with full force. Chinese medicine also describes a second, easily confused problem with the opposite mechanism: qi stagnation, where the overall amount of qi may be perfectly adequate, but its movement has become blocked in a specific place, the way a traffic jam isn't a shortage of cars but a failure of cars to keep moving.

這門課的第一個單元詳細描述過氣虛：一種整體的短缺，身體的功能沒有被全力執行。中醫也描述了第二個容易被搞混、機制卻正好相反的問題：氣滯，整體的氣的份量可能完全足夠，只是它的運行在一個特定的地方被堵住了，就像塞車不是車子不夠，而是車子沒辦法繼續往前開。

The two can produce a surprisingly similar complaint — a person might describe both as "tired" or "not right" — but the details point in different directions. Qi deficiency tends to produce a dull, constant fatigue that worsens with exertion and improves with rest; the person often speaks softly and prefers to sit still. Qi stagnation tends to produce a distending, moving discomfort — often in the chest or abdomen — that comes and goes with mood, worsens with sitting still, and can actually improve somewhat with movement or a long sigh, since sighing is traditionally read as the body's own attempt to force stuck qi to move.

這兩者可能產生出乎意料地相似的抱怨——一個人可能同時把兩者都描述成「累」或「怪怪的」——但細節指向不同的方向。氣虛往往產生一種鈍重、持續的疲倦，隨著用力而加重，隨著休息而改善；這個人通常講話輕聲細語，偏好靜靜坐著。氣滯往往產生一種脹悶、會移動的不適——常在胸口或腹部——隨著心情起伏，靜坐不動時會加重，動一動或長嘆一口氣反而能稍微改善，因為嘆氣傳統上被讀成身體自己嘗試逼迫卡住的氣動起來的一種方式。

The distinction changes the entire response. Qi deficiency calls for rest and gentle replenishment — pushing a depleted system harder only depletes it further. Qi stagnation calls for movement and release — resting a stuck system only lets it stay stuck longer. Mistaking one for the other, by resting when movement was needed or pushing through activity when depletion was the real issue, is a common and unhelpful error, which is exactly why telling them apart is treated as a first, essential step rather than an afterthought.

這個區分改變了整個應對方式。氣虛需要的是休息與溫和的回補——逼一個已經耗竭的系統更用力，只會讓它更耗竭。氣滯需要的是活動與釋放——讓一個卡住的系統休息，只會讓它卡得更久。把一個誤認成另一個——在需要動的時候休息，或在真正的問題是耗竭時硬撐著活動——是一個常見又沒有幫助的錯誤，這正是為什麼分辨這兩者被當成第一個、必要的步驟，而不是事後才想到的補充。

Questions · 閱讀理解

According to the passage, what is the difference between qi deficiency and qi stagnation, using the traffic comparison?

Qi deficiency is a shortage of qi overall, like a shortage of cars; qi stagnation is qi that is adequate in amount but blocked in movement at a specific place, like a traffic jam where cars aren't short but fail to keep moving.

According to the passage, how does qi deficiency's fatigue typically respond to exertion and rest, compared to qi stagnation's discomfort?

Qi deficiency's fatigue worsens with exertion and improves with rest, while qi stagnation's discomfort worsens with sitting still and can improve with movement or a long sigh.

According to the last paragraph, why is mistaking one for the other described as "a common and unhelpful error"?

Because the correct responses are opposite — deficiency needs rest and replenishment, while stagnation needs movement and release — so resting when movement was needed, or pushing through activity when depletion was the real issue, makes the problem worse rather than better.
