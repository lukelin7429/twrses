Unit 22: The Twelve Main Meridians

Chinese medicine describes twelve main meridians, one connected to each of the six zang and six fu organs introduced in the last unit. Each meridian runs along either an arm or a leg, linking a limb to its associated organ deep inside the torso — the Lung meridian, for example, runs along the inner arm, and the Stomach meridian runs down the front of the leg. This is why an acupuncturist might work on a point far from where a symptom is felt: the meridian connects that distant point to the organ in question.

中醫描述了十二條主要經絡，分別連結到上個單元介紹過的六臟六腑。每條經絡沿著一隻手臂或一條腿走，把一個肢體連結到它對應、深藏在軀幹裡的臟腑——舉例來說，肺經沿著手臂內側走，胃經沿著腿的前側往下走。這就是為什麼針灸師有時候會在離症狀感覺到的地方很遠的一個點上下針：經絡把那個遙遠的點，連結到相關的臟腑。

The twelve meridians are organized in a system of three yin and three yang meridians on each limb, six on the arms and six on the legs. Yin meridians generally run along the inner, more protected surface of a limb and connect to zang organs; yang meridians generally run along the outer surface and connect to fu organs. This is not an arbitrary naming choice — it follows the same yin-yang logic from earlier in this course: interior and storing (yin) versus exterior and processing (yang).

十二條經絡按照每個肢體三陰三陽的系統來組織，手臂上六條、腿上六條。陰經通常沿著肢體內側、比較受保護的表面走，連結到五臟；陽經通常沿著外側表面走，連結到六腑。這不是任意取的命名方式——它遵循的是這門課前面講過的同一套陰陽邏輯：內部、儲存性質（陰）對上外部、處理性質（陽）。

None of this needs to be memorized point by point to be useful. What matters is the underlying idea: the twelve meridians form one continuous, connected network rather than twelve separate, isolated lines, and qi is said to flow through all twelve in a fixed sequence over the course of a day, passing the "baton" from one meridian to the next. A blockage or weakness doesn't stay isolated in one line — it can affect the flow reaching every meridian downstream.

這一切不需要一個點一個點地背下來才有用。重要的是背後的概念：十二條經絡形成一個連續、彼此連結的網絡，而不是十二條各自獨立的線，而氣被認為在一天當中依照固定的順序流過全部十二條，把「接力棒」從一條經絡傳給下一條。一個地方的阻塞或虛弱，不會停留在孤立的一條線上——它可以影響到下游每一條經絡的氣血流動。

Questions · 閱讀理解

According to the passage, what does each of the twelve main meridians connect?

Each meridian connects a limb (arm or leg) to its associated zang or fu organ deep inside the torso.

According to the passage, what is the difference between yin meridians and yang meridians in terms of location and the organs they connect to?

Yin meridians generally run along the inner, more protected surface of a limb and connect to zang organs, while yang meridians run along the outer surface and connect to fu organs.

According to the last paragraph, what is the "underlying idea" that matters most about the twelve meridians?

That the twelve meridians form one continuous, connected network, with qi flowing through all twelve in a fixed sequence, rather than twelve separate, isolated lines.
