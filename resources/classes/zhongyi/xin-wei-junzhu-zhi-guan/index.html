Unit 42: The Heart as Emperor: Organs as a Government

The Yellow Emperor's Inner Classic organizes the entire set of organs covered in this unit using a single extended metaphor: a royal court, with each organ holding a specific government post. The heart is named "the ruler," responsible for the overall direction of the whole body, the way an emperor sets the direction of a kingdom without personally performing every task. The lung is named "the prime minister," managing the flow of qi throughout the body the way a minister manages the flow of resources and orders through a government.

《黃帝內經》用一個延伸的比喻，把這個單元涵蓋的整組臟腑組織起來：一個朝廷，每個臟腑擔任一個特定的官職。心被稱為「君主之官」，負責整個身體的整體方向，就像皇帝設定一個國家的方向，而不用親自執行每一件事。肺被稱為「相傅之官」，管理全身氣的流動，就像一位宰相管理著政府裡資源與命令的流動。

The liver is named "the general," in charge of planning and strategy — a fitting role for the organ this course has already connected to smooth, forward-moving qi and to anger when that movement is blocked. The spleen and stomach are named officials in charge of granaries and storage, responsible for the intake and processing that feeds everyone else in the "government." Each title isn't decoration; it's a compressed description of the organ's role relative to all the others.

肝被稱為「將軍之官」，負責規劃與謀略——這個角色，剛好對應著這門課稍早已經連結過的、順暢向前運行的氣，以及那個運行受阻時出現的憤怒。脾與胃被稱為負責倉廩、儲藏的官員，負責攝入與處理，餵養這個「朝廷」裡的每一個人。每一個官銜都不是裝飾；它是這個臟腑相對於所有其他臟腑角色的一種濃縮描述。

The real value of the metaphor is what it says about failure. A kingdom doesn't collapse because one official had a single bad day — it collapses when officials stop coordinating, when the ruler's direction isn't being carried out, or when one office starts overreaching into another's duties. Reading illness this way shifts the question from "which single part is broken" to "where has the coordination between offices broken down" — the same systems-level thinking this entire unit has been building toward.

這個比喻真正的價值，在於它對「失敗」的講法。一個朝廷不會因為某個官員某一天表現不好就垮台——它會垮台，是因為官員們停止協調、君主的方向沒有被執行下去，或是某個官署開始越權侵犯到另一個官署的職責。用這個角度來讀疾病，把問題從「哪個單一部分壞掉了」，轉移到「哪裡的官署間協調出了問題」——正是這整個單元一直在建構的那種系統層級的思考方式。

Questions · 閱讀理解

According to the passage, what government role is the heart given in the Yellow Emperor's Inner Classic, and what role is the lung given?

The heart is named "the ruler," responsible for the overall direction of the whole body; the lung is named "the prime minister," managing the flow of qi throughout the body.

According to the passage, why is "the general" a fitting title for the liver?

Because the liver has already been connected in this course to smooth, forward-moving qi and to anger when that movement is blocked — matching a general's role in planning and strategy.

According to the last paragraph, how does the metaphor change the question asked about illness?

It shifts the question from "which single part is broken" to "where has the coordination between offices broken down," reflecting systems-level thinking rather than isolating one broken part.
