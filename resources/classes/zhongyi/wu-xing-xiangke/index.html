Unit 13: The Controlling Cycle

Alongside the generating cycle, Chinese medicine describes a second relationship among the five elements: the controlling cycle, which keeps any one element from growing unchecked. Wood controls earth, the way tree roots break through and hold soil in place. Earth controls water, the way a riverbank contains a flood. Water controls fire, the way water extinguishes flame. Fire controls metal, the way heat melts and shapes metal. And metal controls wood, the way an axe cuts down a tree, closing this cycle as well.

除了相生循環之外，中醫還描述了五行之間的第二種關係：相剋循環，用來阻止任何一個元素不受限制地增長。木克土，就像樹根穿透並固定住土壤一樣。土克水，就像河岸圍住洪水一樣。水克火，就像水澆熄火焰一樣。火克金，就像高溫熔化並塑形金屬一樣。金克木，就像斧頭砍倒一棵樹一樣，也讓這個循環閉合起來。

Mapped onto the organs, this cycle explains why a problem in one place can create a problem somewhere else that looks, at first, unrelated. The liver (wood) is said to control the spleen (earth). When a person experiences prolonged frustration or anger — associated with the liver — that strain can, over time, show up as digestive trouble, the domain of the spleen. A patient describing "stress that upsets my stomach" is, in this framework, describing exactly this relationship, even without using its vocabulary.

把這個循環對應到臟腑上，可以解釋為什麼一個地方的問題，一開始看起來會在另一個看似不相關的地方造成問題。肝（木）被認為會克脾（土）。當一個人長期經歷挫折或憤怒——跟肝有關——那股壓力久而久之，可能會以消化方面的問題表現出來，那是脾的領域。一位病人描述「壓力大到胃都不舒服」，在這個框架下，即使沒用到這套詞彙，講的正是這種關係。

The controlling cycle is a safety mechanism, not a punishment. Under normal conditions, each element checks the next one just enough to keep it from overreaching — the way a good editor checks a writer's excesses without silencing them. Illness, in this view, often isn't a single part malfunctioning in isolation. It's a checking relationship that has become too strong, too weak, or badly timed.

相剋循環是一種安全機制，不是一種懲罰。在正常情況下，每個元素只會恰到好處地牽制住下一個，讓它不至於過度擴張——就像一位好編輯會節制作者的過度之處，而不是讓作者噤聲。在這個觀點下，生病往往不是單一部分孤立地故障，而是一種牽制關係變得太強、太弱，或時機不對。

Questions · 閱讀理解

According to the passage, what is the relationship between wood and earth in the controlling cycle, and what everyday comparison is given?

Wood controls earth, the way tree roots break through and hold soil in place.

According to the passage, how does the liver-spleen controlling relationship explain "stress that upsets my stomach"?

The liver (wood) controls the spleen (earth); prolonged frustration or anger (associated with the liver) can, over time, show up as digestive trouble (the spleen's domain) — which is what a patient describes when stress upsets their stomach.

According to the last paragraph, why does the passage compare the controlling cycle to "a good editor"?

Because a good editor checks a writer's excesses without silencing them — similarly, the controlling cycle keeps each element in check just enough to prevent overreaching, not to shut it down entirely.
