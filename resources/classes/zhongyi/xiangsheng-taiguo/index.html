Unit 64: Excess Generating: When Support Becomes a Burden

An earlier lesson in this unit described the generating cycle failing through shortage — a weak "parent" organ failing to adequately nourish its "child," so the weakness travels downstream. Chinese medicine also describes the opposite failure: a "parent" organ that is not weak but excessively strong, overwhelming the child it's supposed to be gently supporting rather than nourishing it in a measured way — support tipping over into a burden.

這個單元稍早的一課，描述了相生循環透過短缺而失效——一個虛弱的「母」臟腑，沒能好好滋養它的「子」，於是虛弱往下游傳遞。中醫也描述了相反的失效方式：一個「母」臟腑不是虛弱，而是過度強盛，壓垮了它本該溫和支持的那個「子」，而不是有節制地滋養它——支持翻轉成了負擔。

A concrete version shows up in the relationship between the liver (wood) and the heart (fire), where wood normally generates fire in a measured, supportive way. If the liver becomes excessively active — the same kind of prolonged, unresolved frustration discussed in an earlier unit — it can generate too much rather than too little, pushing an excess into the heart that shows up as symptoms of heart-fire: agitation, insomnia, a flushed face, and a racing, restless quality to the mind that looks like too much support rather than too little.

一個具體的版本，出現在肝（木）與心（火）的關係裡，木通常以一種有節制、支持性的方式生火。如果肝變得過度活躍——這個單元稍早討論過的那種長期、未化解的挫折感——它可能生得太多而不是太少，把過剩的東西推進心裡，表現成心火的症狀：躁動、失眠、滿臉通紅，還有一種心思奔騰、不得安寧的性質，看起來像是支持太多，而不是太少。

This matters because it means "the generating relationship is working" isn't automatically good news — the question is always whether it's working in a measured amount, not simply whether it's working at all. A doctor evaluating a heart-fire pattern needs to ask not just "is the heart getting support from the liver," but "is it getting the right amount," since in this pattern, the organ supposedly nourishing another is precisely the source of the problem, not the solution to it.

這件事很重要，因為它意味著「相生關係正在運作」不自動代表是好消息——問題永遠是它是不是以有節制的份量在運作，而不只是它有沒有在運作。評估心火模式的醫師，需要問的不只是「心有沒有從肝那裡得到支持」，而是「它得到的份量對不對」，因為在這個模式裡，那個本該滋養另一方的臟腑，恰恰就是問題的來源，而不是解決問題的方法。

Questions · 閱讀理解

According to the passage, what is the "opposite failure" of the generating cycle, compared to a weak parent organ failing to nourish its child?

A "parent" organ that is not weak but excessively strong, overwhelming the child it's supposed to gently support rather than nourishing it in a measured way.

According to the passage, what concrete example illustrates excess generating between the liver and heart?

An excessively active liver, from prolonged, unresolved frustration, generates too much rather than too little, pushing excess into the heart and producing heart-fire symptoms like agitation, insomnia, and a flushed face.

According to the last paragraph, why isn't "the generating relationship is working" automatically good news?

Because the question is always whether the relationship is working in a measured amount, not simply whether it's working at all — in this pattern, the "supporting" organ is actually the source of the problem.
