When One Organ's Problem Hides Behind Another's Symptom

Unit 68: When One Organ's Problem Hides Behind Another's Symptom

This course's five-elements unit described the generating cycle as a mother-child relationship between elements, and its organs unit assigned each element a specific pair of organs. Put together, they describe something Chinese medicine calls "mother and child conditions affecting each other" — disease traveling along the generating cycle from one organ to its neighbor, in either direction, rather than staying confined to wherever it started.

這門課的五行單元把相生循環描述成元素之間的母子關係，臟腑單元則把每個元素分配到一對特定的臟腑。合在一起，它們描述了中醫稱為「母子相及」的東西——疾病沿著相生循環，從一個臟腑走向它的鄰居，可能朝任一個方向，而不是侷限在它一開始出現的地方。

A disease can move "from mother to child," the pattern already covered in this course's five-elements unit: a weak kidney (water) failing to nourish the liver (wood), so liver-type symptoms appear even though the kidney is the organ actually running low. It can also move the other way, "from child to mother": a liver under sustained strain drawing so heavily on the kidney's support that kidney-type symptoms — lower back weakness, for instance — begin to appear as a secondary effect of a problem that started in the liver.

疾病可以「母病及子」地移動，這是這門課五行單元已經提過的模式：一個虛弱的腎（水）沒能滋養肝（木），於是肝型的症狀出現了，即使真正不足的臟腑是腎。它也可以反方向移動，「子病及母」：一個長期承受壓力的肝，過度支取腎的支援，於是腎型的症狀——比方說腰部無力——開始以肝一開始出問題的次發效應出現。

What makes this worth its own lesson, rather than being folded into the earlier generating-cycle lesson, is the diagnostic habit it trains: a symptom's location is not automatically where the underlying problem is located. A doctor who sees kidney-type symptoms doesn't stop at the kidney — the five-element map gives a short, specific list of neighboring organs worth checking first, turning what could be a wide-open guessing game into a small number of well-reasoned possibilities.

讓這件事值得擁有自己的一課，而不是折疊進稍早那堂相生循環的課裡的原因，是它訓練出的一種診斷習慣：一個症狀出現的位置，不自動代表根本問題所在的位置。一位看到腎型症狀的醫師，不會停在腎——五行地圖給了一份簡短、具體的鄰近臟腑清單，值得先去檢查，把一場原本可能漫無邊際的猜測遊戲，變成少數幾個有理有據的可能性。

Words & Phrases · 生字及片語

confined(adj.)侷限的The damage was confined to a small area of the roof.損害侷限在屋頂的一小塊區域。secondary(adj.)次發的；次要的The headache was a secondary effect of poor sleep.這個頭痛是睡眠不佳的次發效應。sustained(adj.)持續的Sustained effort over months matters more than one hard day.持續好幾個月的努力，比一天拚命更重要。neighboring(adj.)鄰近的The neighboring town shared the same water supply.鄰近的城鎮共用同一個水源供應。well-reasoned(adj.)有理有據的She gave a well-reasoned argument for her decision.她為自己的決定提出了一個有理有據的論證。guessing game(ph.)猜測遊戲Without more data, the diagnosis became a guessing game.沒有更多資料，診斷就變成了一場猜測遊戲。
