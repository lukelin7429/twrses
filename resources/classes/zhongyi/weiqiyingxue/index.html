The Four-Layer Model for How a Fever Progresses

This course has discussed qi, blood, and body fluids as substances. This lesson uses those same categories to map how a warm disease moves deeper into the body.
這門課討論過氣、血、津液是什麼樣的物質。這一課要用同樣的類別，來畫出一個溫病如何逐漸深入身體的地圖。

Unit 101: The Four-Layer Model for How a Fever Progresses

This course has, across several lessons, treated qi and blood as substances the body produces and relies on. A framework called "defense, qi, nutritive, blood" puts those same categories, plus a fourth layer, to a different use: mapping how a warm-type illness is traditionally understood to progress through the body in four increasingly deep stages, rather than describing what qi and blood do when the body is healthy.

這門課在好幾課裡，都把氣跟血當成身體產生、並且依賴的物質。一個叫做「衛氣營血」的框架，把同樣這些類別，加上第四層，用在一個不同的地方：畫出一個溫病傳統上被理解成如何以四個逐漸加深的階段，在身體裡推進，而不是描述身體健康時氣跟血在做什麼。

The four stages run from surface to core. The defense-level stage is the shallowest, corresponding to early symptoms like fever and chills as a pathogen first meets the body's surface defenses. If unresolved, illness is said to progress to the qi-level stage, marked by higher fever without chills, as the pathogen moves past the surface. Beyond that lies the nutritive-level stage, where disturbed sleep and a reddened tongue are said to appear as the pathogen reaches deeper, more vital tissue. The blood-level stage is the deepest and most serious, traditionally associated with bleeding or severe agitation, as the pathogen affects blood itself.

這四個階段，從表層一路到核心。衛分是最淺的階段，對應到發燒與惡寒這類早期症狀，這是病邪剛接觸到身體表層防禦的時候。如果沒有被解決，病情被說成會推進到氣分階段，特徵是發燒加重、但不再惡寒，因為病邪已經越過了表層。再往裡是營分階段，睡眠被擾亂、舌頭發紅，被說成是病邪到達更深、更重要的組織時會出現的徵象。血分階段是最深、也最嚴重的，傳統上跟出血或嚴重躁動連結在一起，因為病邪這時已經影響到血本身。

The practical value of the four-layer model is that it turns an otherwise vague question — "is this getting worse?" — into a specific one: which of the four layers is a given set of symptoms consistent with, and is the pattern of symptoms moving toward the surface or toward the core. This is one more example of a theme running through the whole course: Chinese medicine consistently prefers naming a specific stage or direction over describing severity in only vague terms like "mild" or "serious."

衛氣營血這個模型實際的用處，是把一個原本模糊的問題——「這個是不是變嚴重了？」——變成一個具體的問題：眼前這組症狀，符合四層裡的哪一層，而症狀的模式，是正往表層移動、還是正往核心移動。這是貫穿整門課的一個主題的又一個例子：中醫一貫偏好指出一個具體的階段或方向，而不是只用「輕微」或「嚴重」這種模糊的字眼來描述嚴重度。

Questions · 閱讀理解

According to the passage, what does the "defense, qi, nutritive, blood" framework map, as distinct from describing healthy body function?

How a warm-type illness is traditionally understood to progress through the body in four increasingly deep stages.

According to the passage, what symptoms mark the qi-level stage, and how do they differ from the defense-level stage?

Higher fever without chills, as the pathogen moves past the surface — differing from the defense-level stage's fever with chills.

According to the last paragraph, what practical question does the four-layer model turn a vague question into?

Which of the four layers a given set of symptoms is consistent with, and whether the pattern is moving toward the surface or the core.

Words & Phrases · 生字及片語

progress(v.)推進；發展The illness began to progress more quickly than expected.這場病開始比預期更快地推進。chills(n., pl.)惡寒；發冷She had a fever accompanied by chills.她發燒還伴隨著惡寒。unresolved(adj.)未解決的The issue remained unresolved for several weeks.這個問題好幾個星期都沒有解決。reddened(adj.)發紅的Her eyes were reddened from lack of sleep.她的眼睛因為睡眠不足而發紅。agitation(n.)躁動The patient showed signs of agitation before the procedure.這位病人在手術前顯露出躁動的跡象。vague(adj.)模糊的His answer was too vague to be useful.他的回答太模糊了，沒有用處。consistent with(ph.)符合；一致Her symptoms were consistent with a mild cold.她的症狀跟輕微感冒的情況一致。
