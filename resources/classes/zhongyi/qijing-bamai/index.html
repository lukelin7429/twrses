Unit 45: The Eight Extraordinary Vessels: The Meridian System's Reservoirs

The twelve main meridians introduced earlier in this unit each belong to a specific organ, run through a specific limb, and follow a fixed daily sequence. Chinese medicine describes eight additional channels that don't follow any of those rules, called the "eight extraordinary vessels." They aren't matched to individual organs, and they don't carry qi in the same continuous, hand-to-organ relay described earlier — instead, they're traditionally compared to reservoirs, holding and releasing qi and blood to support the twelve regular meridians when needed, rather than being part of the everyday flow themselves.

這個單元稍早介紹的十二正經，每一條都屬於一個特定的臟腑、走過一個特定的肢體、遵循一個固定的每日順序。中醫描述了八條不遵循這些規則的額外通道，叫做「奇經八脈」。它們沒有對應到個別的臟腑，也不像稍早描述的那種手到臟腑接力式的連續氣血運行——傳統上，它們更常被比喻成水庫，在需要的時候儲存並釋放氣血來支援十二正經，而不是本身就參與日常的循環。

Two of the eight carry particular weight because, unlike the other six, they have their own dedicated acupuncture points rather than only borrowing points from the twelve regular meridians. The Governing Vessel runs up the center of the back and is associated with all of the body's yang meridians, sometimes described as the "sea of yang." The Conception Vessel runs up the center of the front of the body and is associated with all of the yin meridians, sometimes described as the "sea of yin" — together forming a kind of central spine for the entire meridian system.

八條裡有兩條特別有份量，因為跟其他六條不同，它們有自己專屬的穴位，不只是借用十二正經的穴位。督脈沿著背部正中線往上走，跟身體所有的陽經有關，有時被稱為「陽脈之海」。任脈沿著身體前方正中線往上走，跟所有的陰經有關，有時被稱為「陰脈之海」——兩者合起來，形成整個經絡系統的一種中軸脊柱。

The reservoir comparison matters because it explains why these eight vessels come up specifically in discussions of chronic, long-standing conditions rather than sudden ones. A sudden problem is usually read through the twelve regular meridians, the way a delayed bus is read through the regular schedule rather than the emergency backup system. The eight extraordinary vessels are the backup system — rarely the first place a doctor looks, but the reserve that becomes relevant when the everyday network has been under strain for a long time.

水庫的比喻很重要，因為它解釋了為什麼這八條經脈特別會在討論慢性、長期的狀況時被提起，而不是突發的狀況。突發的問題通常透過十二正經來解讀，就像誤點的公車會透過正常班表來解讀，而不是透過緊急備援系統。奇經八脈是那套備援系統——很少是醫師第一個查看的地方，但當日常的網絡承受長期壓力時，它就是變得相關的那份儲備。

Questions · 閱讀理解

According to the passage, how are the eight extraordinary vessels different from the twelve main meridians?

They aren't matched to individual organs, don't run through a specific limb in the same continuous relay, and are traditionally compared to reservoirs rather than being part of the everyday flow.

According to the passage, which two of the eight extraordinary vessels have their own dedicated acupuncture points, and what are they associated with?

The Governing Vessel (associated with all yang meridians, the "sea of yang") and the Conception Vessel (associated with all yin meridians, the "sea of yin").

According to the last paragraph, why do the eight extraordinary vessels come up specifically in discussions of chronic conditions rather than sudden ones?

Because they act as a reserve, becoming relevant when the everyday twelve-meridian network has been under strain for a long time — a sudden problem is usually read through the regular meridians instead, like a delayed bus read through the regular schedule rather than the emergency backup system.
