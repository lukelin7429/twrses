Unit 109: Why the Spleen Lifts and the Stomach Sinks

An earlier lesson in this unit paired zang and fu organs into biao-li partnerships, including the spleen and stomach, without detailing what makes that particular partnership work in practice. Chinese medicine describes the spleen and stomach as dividing digestion into two opposite directional jobs: the spleen "raises the clear," lifting refined, usable nutrients upward toward the lung and heart to be distributed as qi and blood; the stomach "descends the turbid," pushing waste and unusable residue downward to be expelled.

這個單元稍早的一課，把臟腑配成表裡的搭檔，包括脾與胃，但沒有詳細說明，是什麼讓這個特定的搭檔在實務上真正運作起來。中醫把脾胃描述成，把消化拆成兩個方向相反的工作：脾「主升清」，把精純、可用的養分往上提，送到肺與心，分布成氣與血；胃「主降濁」，把廢物與不能用的殘渣往下推，送去排出體外。

This division of direction is treated as just as essential as the division of labor itself. A spleen that fails to lift is traditionally associated with a cluster of "sinking" symptoms — this unit's earlier lesson on qi sinking specifically named the spleen as the organ most often behind it — including fatigue, a feeling of heaviness, and in more pronounced cases, a sense of organs literally dropping. A stomach that fails to descend is traditionally associated with the opposite cluster: nausea, bloating, and belching, as waste that should be moving downward and out instead backs up.

這種方向上的分工，被當成跟工作本身的分工一樣重要。一個沒能好好上提的脾，傳統上跟一組「下陷」的症狀連結在一起——這個單元稍早講氣陷的那一課，特別點名脾是背後最常見的原因——包括疲勞、一種沉重感，在比較明顯的案例裡，甚至是臟器實際下垂的感覺。一個沒能好好下降的胃，傳統上跟相反的一組症狀連結在一起：噁心、脹氣、打嗝，因為原本應該往下、往外走的廢物，反而往回堵。

Reading spleen and stomach as a paired-but-opposite system, rather than as two organs each independently doing "digestion," is what allows this framework to explain why a single meal can produce two very different kinds of complaint depending on which half of the pair is malfunctioning — heaviness and fatigue pointing toward a lifting problem, nausea and bloating pointing toward a descending problem — even though both symptoms trace back to the same digestive event.

把脾胃讀成一個配對、卻方向相反的系統，而不是兩個各自獨立在做「消化」的臟腑，正是讓這個框架能夠解釋，為什麼同一餐飯，可能根據配對裡哪一半出了故障，而產生兩種非常不同的抱怨——沉重與疲勞指向一個上提的問題，噁心與脹氣指向一個下降的問題——即使兩種症狀追溯回去，都是同一次消化事件。

Questions · 閱讀理解

According to the passage, what does "the spleen raises the clear" mean, and what does "the stomach descends the turbid" mean?

The spleen lifts refined, usable nutrients upward to be distributed as qi and blood; the stomach pushes waste and unusable residue downward to be expelled.

According to the passage, what symptoms are traditionally associated with a spleen that fails to lift?

Fatigue, a feeling of heaviness, and in more pronounced cases, a sense of organs dropping — a cluster of "sinking" symptoms.

According to the last paragraph, why can a single meal produce two very different kinds of complaint?

Because spleen and stomach are a paired-but-opposite system — depending on which half malfunctions, symptoms point toward either a lifting problem or a descending problem, even from the same digestive event.
