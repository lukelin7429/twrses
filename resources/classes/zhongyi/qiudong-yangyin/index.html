Unit 118: Nourish Yin in Autumn and Winter, Nourish Yang in Spring and Summer

This unit's lessons on the four seasons have each described a natural process already underway — spring's growth, summer's venting, autumn's gathering, winter's storing — largely from the outside, as something happening in the wider world. A classical line from the Huangdi Neijing turns the same seasonal structure into direct advice: "nourish yin in autumn and winter, nourish yang in spring and summer," instructing a person to actively support whichever of the two, yin or yang, the current season is already naturally building toward.

這個單元講四季的課，各自描述了一個已經在進行中的自然過程——春天的生長、夏天的宣洩、秋天的收斂、冬天的儲藏——大多是從外部的角度，當成正在更大的世界裡發生的事情來描述。《黃帝內經》裡的一句經典的話，把同樣的節氣結構，轉成直接的行動建議：「秋冬養陰，春夏養陽」，指示一個人主動去支持陰陽當中，目前這個季節本來就已經在自然建立的那一方。

The logic follows the mutual-rooting relationship covered in this course's yin-yang unit: supporting a season's own natural direction, rather than working against it, is considered the easier and more effective path, since the effort lines up with a process the season is already doing on its own instead of fighting it. Autumn and winter are yin's own seasons — cooler, more interior, more storing — so nourishing yin during this stretch works with the grain; spring and summer are yang's own seasons — warmer, more outward, more active — so nourishing yang during this stretch does the same in the opposite direction.

這個邏輯，跟著這門課陰陽單元講過的互根關係走：支持一個季節本身自然的方向，而不是逆著它做，被認為是比較輕鬆、也比較有效的路，因為努力的方向，跟這個季節本來就已經在自己做的過程一致，而不是在跟它對抗。秋冬是陰自己的季節——比較涼、比較內收、比較儲藏——所以在這段期間滋養陰，是順著紋理做；春夏是陽自己的季節——比較溫暖、比較外放、比較活躍——所以在這段期間滋養陽，是往相反方向做同樣的事。

This principle ties together nearly everything this unit has covered about the four seasons into one usable instruction: rather than memorizing a separate, unrelated piece of advice for each of the four seasons, a reader can start from this single line and derive most of the specifics — favor cooling, moistening habits in autumn and winter; favor warming, activating habits in spring and summer — arriving at broadly the same seasonal guidance this unit's earlier lessons described one season at a time.

這個原則，把這個單元幾乎所有講四季的內容，綁進一條可以實際使用的指示裡：與其為四季分別記住四條各自獨立、不相關的建議，讀者可以從這一句話出發，推導出大部分的細節——秋冬偏好偏涼、滋潤的習慣；春夏偏好偏溫、活絡的習慣——得出的，大致上就是這個單元稍早那些課，一個季節一個季節分別描述過的同一套節氣指引。

Questions · 閱讀理解

According to the passage, what does "nourish yin in autumn and winter, nourish yang in spring and summer" instruct a person to do?

To actively support whichever of the two, yin or yang, the current season is already naturally building toward.

According to the passage, why is supporting a season's own natural direction considered easier and more effective than working against it?

Because the effort lines up with a process the season is already doing on its own, working with the grain rather than fighting it.

According to the last paragraph, what does this single principle allow a reader to do?

Derive most of the specific seasonal guidance from a single line — favor cooling, moistening habits in autumn and winter, and warming, activating habits in spring and summer — rather than memorizing separate advice for each season.
