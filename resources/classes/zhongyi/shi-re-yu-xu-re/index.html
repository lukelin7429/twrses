Excess Heat and Deficient Heat Are Not the Same Thing

Two patients both feel hot — but one has too much yang, and the other has too little yin. Same symptom, opposite cause.
兩個人都覺得燥熱——一個是陽太多，一個是陰太少。同一個症狀，相反的成因。

Unit 36: Excess Heat and Deficient Heat Are Not the Same Thing

A patient who feels hot doesn't automatically have "too much heat" in the way English speakers imagine a fever. Chinese medicine distinguishes two very different mechanisms behind a feeling of heat, and confusing them leads to exactly the wrong approach. The first is excess heat: yang itself has grown too strong, the way a fire burns hotter than it should. This kind of heat tends to be intense, constant, and accompanied by a strong thirst for cold drinks, a red face, and a loud, forceful voice.

一個覺得燥熱的病人，不代表身體裡「熱太多」，就像英文使用者想像發燒那樣。中醫把「熱」這種感覺背後區分成兩種非常不同的機制，把它們搞混會導向完全錯誤的方向。第一種是實熱：陽本身變得太強，就像一團燒得比該有的還旺的火。這種熱通常強烈、持續，伴隨著想喝冰水的強烈口渴、滿臉通紅，還有洪亮、有力的聲音。

The second is deficient heat, and it works by subtraction rather than addition: yin — the cooling, moistening, restraining substance — has become too thin to hold yang in check, so yang appears to run hot simply because nothing is containing it anymore. This kind of heat tends to be milder, shows up specifically in the afternoon or evening, and comes with a dry mouth that doesn't demand much water, flushed cheekbones, and night sweats rather than daytime sweating.

第二種是虛熱，它的運作方式是「減法」而不是「加法」：陰——那個負責寒涼、滋潤、牽制的物質——變得太薄弱，沒辦法再把陽扣住，於是陽看起來很燥熱，只是因為已經沒有東西在約束它了。這種熱通常比較輕微，特別會在下午或傍晚出現，伴隨著不太想喝很多水的口乾、顴骨發紅，還有夜間盜汗，而不是白天流汗。

The distinction matters because the response runs in opposite directions. Excess heat calls for clearing and draining — cooling the fire down directly. Deficient heat calls for nourishing yin — refilling what's missing so yang has something to be held by again. Treating deficient heat as if it were excess heat, by cooling aggressively without replenishing yin, is a common and unhelpful mismatch — which is exactly why this distinction is left to a trained diagnosis, not a symptom checklist.

這個區分很重要，因為對應的做法方向完全相反。實熱需要清熱瀉火——直接把那團火降下來。虛熱需要滋陰——把缺少的東西補回來，讓陽重新有東西可以被扣住。把虛熱當成實熱來處理，用力清熱卻不滋陰，是一個常見又沒有幫助的誤配——這正是為什麼這個區分要交給受過訓練的診斷，而不是一份症狀清單。

Questions · 閱讀理解

According to the passage, what mechanism causes "excess heat," and what symptoms are associated with it?

Excess heat is caused by yang itself growing too strong, like a fire burning hotter than it should; it's associated with intense, constant heat, strong thirst for cold drinks, a red face, and a loud voice.

According to the passage, what mechanism causes "deficient heat," and what symptoms are associated with it?

Deficient heat is caused by yin becoming too thin to hold yang in check; it's associated with milder heat specifically in the afternoon or evening, a dry mouth without much thirst, flushed cheekbones, and night sweats.

According to the last paragraph, why does the passage say treating deficient heat as if it were excess heat is "a common and unhelpful mismatch"?

Because the correct responses run in opposite directions — excess heat calls for cooling and draining, while deficient heat calls for nourishing yin — so cooling aggressively without replenishing yin fails to address the actual mechanism.
