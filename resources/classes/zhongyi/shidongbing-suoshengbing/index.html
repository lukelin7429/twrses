Unit 113: Two Kinds of Meridian-Related Illness, According to the Classics

This unit's lessons on meridian pathology — referred pain, sinew channels, connecting channels, cutaneous regions — have generally described problems tracing back to a meridian without distinguishing between different kinds of meridian-related illness. An early classical text on meridians, the Ling Shu, draws exactly that distinction for each of the twelve main meridians, sorting the illnesses associated with each one into two categories: "movement-caused disease" and "generated disease."

這個單元講經絡病理的課——牽涉痛、經筋、絡脈、皮部——通常把問題描述成追溯回一條經絡，卻沒有區分不同種類的經絡相關疾病。一部早期講經絡的經典《靈樞》，為十二正經每一條，都畫出了正是這樣的區分，把跟每一條經絡有關的疾病，分成兩類：「是動病」與「所生病」。

Movement-caused disease describes disorders traditionally understood as arising from a disturbance in the meridian's own qi flow — its movement — producing symptoms that tend to appear suddenly along the meridian's actual pathway, such as pain that follows the meridian's course closely. Generated disease describes a broader category: disorders the meridian is associated with through the organ or tissue it connects to, which can include symptoms with no obvious direct connection to the meridian's physical pathway at all, tracing back to the meridian by association with its organ rather than by direct route.

是動病，描述的是傳統上被理解成源自這條經絡本身氣的流動——它的「動」——出了擾動的疾患，產生的症狀往往沿著這條經絡實際的路徑突然出現，例如緊緊跟著經絡走向的疼痛。所生病，描述的是一個更廣的類別：這條經絡透過它連結到的臟腑或組織而跟它相關的疾患，可能包括跟這條經絡的物理路徑完全沒有明顯直接連結的症狀，是透過跟它的臟腑有關聯、而不是透過直接路線，才追溯回這條經絡的。

This two-way split matters for the same reason the root-and-branch theory covered in a previous lesson matters: it turns a single meridian into a source of two functionally different kinds of information, one about disturbances following its physical course directly, the other about disorders in whatever organ or tissue it's paired with. Reading a symptom through this classical lens means asking not just which meridian is involved, but which of the two categories the symptom actually belongs to — a distinction the classics themselves considered worth making explicit, unit by unit, for every one of the twelve meridians.

這種兩分法很重要，理由跟前一課講的標本根結理論很重要的理由一樣：它把單一一條經絡，變成兩種功能上不同的資訊來源——一種是關於直接沿著它物理路徑而來的擾動，另一種是關於它所配對的臟腑或組織裡的疾患。透過這個古典的鏡頭來讀一個症狀，意味著不只要問哪一條經絡涉入其中，還要問這個症狀實際上屬於這兩類裡的哪一類——這是經典本身認為值得明確講清楚的一個區分，逐條經絡、逐一講清楚，涵蓋十二正經的每一條。

Questions · 閱讀理解

According to the passage, what are "movement-caused disease" and "generated disease"?

Movement-caused disease arises from a disturbance in the meridian's own qi flow; generated disease arises from disorders in the organ or tissue the meridian is associated with.

According to the passage, what kind of symptoms does movement-caused disease tend to produce?

Symptoms that tend to appear suddenly along the meridian's actual physical pathway, such as pain that follows the meridian's course closely.

According to the last paragraph, why does this two-way split matter?

It turns a single meridian into two functionally different kinds of information — disturbances along its physical course versus disorders in its paired organ or tissue — which the classics considered worth distinguishing explicitly.
