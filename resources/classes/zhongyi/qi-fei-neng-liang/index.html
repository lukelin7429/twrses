Qi Is Not "Energy"

The single biggest translation mistake in Chinese medicine — and why it matters.
中醫裡最常見的一個翻譯錯誤，也是最該先弄懂的一件事。

1

Unit 1: Qi Is Not "Energy"

When people first encounter Chinese medicine, they almost always meet the word qi in translation as "energy." This translation feels natural, but it causes more confusion than it solves. Energy, in the scientific sense, is something you can measure, store, and convert from one form into another. Qi is not that kind of thing. It is not a substance sitting somewhere, waiting to be used — it is closer to the body doing its work. When someone's qi is described as weak, the point is not that a battery has run low. The point is that the body's basic functions are not being carried out with full force.

第一次接觸中醫的人，幾乎都會先遇到「氣」被翻譯成 energy。這個翻譯聽起來很自然，卻製造了比它解決的還多的誤解。科學意義上的 energy 是可以被測量、儲存、轉換形式的東西；氣不是那種東西。氣不是放在某處等著被使用的物質，而更接近「身體正在運作」這件事本身。說某人氣虛，意思不是電池沒電了，而是身體的基本功能沒有被全力執行。

This is one reason many teachers of Chinese medicine now leave the word qi untranslated, the same way English speakers keep words like karma and nirvana rather than force them into an English equivalent. Once qi becomes "energy," the next question is almost always the same: can this be measured with a machine? That question has already gone wrong, because it assumes qi is a quantity in the first place. Asking how much qi someone has is a bit like asking how much fitness a person has. Fitness is real — you can see its effects in how someone moves, breathes, and recovers from illness — but it is not a substance sitting inside the body, waiting to be weighed.

這是為什麼許多中醫老師現在傾向保留 qi 這個字不翻譯——就像英文使用者保留 karma、nirvana 這些字，而不硬找一個英文對應詞。一旦氣被譯成 energy，下一個問題幾乎必然出現：這可以用儀器測量嗎？這個問題從一開始就走錯了方向，因為它預設了氣是一種數量。問一個人有多少氣，有點像問一個人有多少 fitness。fitness 是真實的——你可以從一個人的動作、呼吸、病後恢復力看出它的效果——但它不是身體裡等著被秤重的一種物質。

So the very first lesson in Chinese medicine has nothing to do with herbs or acupuncture. It is simply this: qi describes a pattern of function, not a form of energy. Get this distinction right, and every idea that follows — from the five functions of qi to the causes of fatigue — will rest on solid ground.

所以中醫的第一課，跟藥草或針灸都沒有關係。它只是這件事：氣描述的是一種功能的模式，不是一種能量的形式。把這個分野弄對，後面所有的道理——從氣的五個功能，到疲勞的成因——才站得住腳。

Questions · 閱讀理解

According to the passage, what is the difference between "energy" in the scientific sense and qi?

Energy can be measured, stored, and converted between forms, while qi describes the body's functions being carried out — it isn't a substance that exists on its own.

Why do many teachers of Chinese medicine prefer to leave the word qi untranslated?

Because translating qi as "energy" invites the wrong kind of question, such as whether it can be measured with a machine, when qi was never meant to describe a measurable quantity.

What does the comparison between qi and "fitness" help explain?

It shows that something can be real and observable in its effects without being a physical substance that can be stored or weighed.
