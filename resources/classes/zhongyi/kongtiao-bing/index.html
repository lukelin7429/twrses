Air Conditioning Sickness

A modern complaint that fits an old category perfectly: what happens when cold air is chased with more cold air, on purpose.
一個完全符合古老分類的現代抱怨：當冷空氣被人刻意地拿更多冷空氣去對付時，會發生什麼事。

Unit 95: Air Conditioning Sickness

This unit's early lessons described cold as one of the six excesses, an external factor traditionally understood to enter the body and disrupt its normal warmth. Air conditioning sickness is a modern label for an old pattern applied to a distinctly modern setting: prolonged exposure to artificially cooled air, especially after the body has been overheated, is described using the same cold-excess framework used for any other cold exposure, rather than treated as a separate, unrelated modern ailment.

這個單元前面幾課，把寒描述成六淫之一，一種傳統上被理解成會進入身體、擾亂身體正常溫暖的外在因素。空調病是一個現代的標籤，套用在一個古老模式上、放進一個明顯現代的場景裡：長時間暴露在人工冷卻的空氣中，尤其是身體才剛過熱之後，用的是跟任何其他寒邪外襲一樣的框架來描述，而不是被當成一種分開的、不相關的現代病。

The traditional reasoning follows a specific sequence: a body that has just been sweating in the heat has its pores open, in a state traditionally described as more vulnerable to invasion precisely because the usual protective barrier is relaxed. Stepping directly into strongly air-conditioned air at that moment is traditionally read as inviting cold to enter through those open pores far more easily than it would through a body that had time to cool down gradually and close them again first.

傳統的推理，遵循一個特定的順序：一個剛才在暑熱裡流過汗的身體，毛孔是開著的，傳統上被描述成一種因為平常的保護屏障放鬆下來，因而更容易被入侵的狀態。在這個當下，直接走進強力冷氣的空氣裡，傳統上被讀成是在邀請寒氣，透過那些張開的毛孔，比透過一個有時間慢慢降溫、把毛孔重新閉合起來的身體，更輕易地進來。

This is one more instance of a broader principle repeated throughout this unit: the six excesses were never meant to describe only naturally occurring weather. A modern room cooled well past outdoor temperature is, from this framework's perspective, simply another source of the same cold excess a sudden winter chill would produce — which is why the traditional advice attached to this pattern is unusually simple and unusually modern-sounding at the same time: let the body cool down gradually and let the pores close before stepping into strong air conditioning, rather than moving from one extreme straight into the other.

這是這個單元反覆講過的一個更廣泛原則的又一個例子：六淫從來就不是只用來描述自然發生的天氣。一個被冷卻到遠低於室外溫度的現代房間，從這個框架的角度來看，不過是另一個來源，製造出跟突如其來的冬天寒流一樣的寒邪——這也是為什麼，附在這個模式上的傳統建議，聽起來異常地簡單、同時也異常地符合現代：在走進強力冷氣之前，先讓身體慢慢降溫、讓毛孔閉合起來，而不是從一個極端直接跳進另一個極端。

Questions · 閱讀理解

According to the passage, how is air conditioning sickness understood in relation to the six excesses?

It's a modern label applied to the same cold-excess framework used for any other cold exposure, not a separate, unrelated modern ailment.

According to the passage, why is a sweating body traditionally considered more vulnerable to cold right after being in the heat?

Because sweating opens the pores, relaxing the usual protective barrier, making it easier for cold to enter directly into the body.

According to the last paragraph, what traditional advice follows from this pattern?

To let the body cool down gradually and let the pores close before stepping into strong air conditioning, rather than moving from one extreme straight into the other.
