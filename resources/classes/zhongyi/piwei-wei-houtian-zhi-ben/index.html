Unit 67: Why the Spleen Is Called "Later Heaven's Root"

This course's first unit distinguished prenatal qi, the fixed inheritance stored in the kidney, from postnatal qi, generated continuously from food and air. Chinese medicine gives the spleen and stomach a title that makes their role in this second category explicit: "the root of what comes after heaven" — heaven, in this phrase, meaning birth itself, and "after" meaning everything a person's body has to keep manufacturing on its own from that point onward.

這門課的第一個單元，把儲藏在腎裡、固定的先天遺產「先天之氣」，跟持續由食物與空氣生成的「後天之氣」區分開來。中醫給脾胃一個把它們在這第二個類別裡的角色講得很明白的稱號：「後天之本」——這裡的「天」，指的是出生本身，「後」指的是從那之後，一個人的身體必須自己持續製造的一切。

The title isn't just honorific. Every other organ's qi, blood, and fluids, all covered in this course's organs unit, ultimately depend on raw material the spleen and stomach convert from food — meaning a struggling spleen doesn't just cause a locally digestive problem, but can eventually show up as a shortage anywhere else in the body, the same wide reach this unit's lesson on the kidney described for prenatal qi, but running through a completely different, renewable channel.

這個稱號不只是尊稱。這門課臟腑單元提過的每一個其他臟腑的氣、血、津液，最終都依賴脾胃從食物轉化出來的原料——意思是一個出問題的脾，不只是造成局部的消化問題，最終可能表現成身體任何地方的短缺，跟這個單元關於腎那一課，對先天之氣描述的同樣廣泛的波及範圍一樣，只是走的是一條完全不同、可再生的管道。

This is why a doctor faced with a wide-ranging pattern of weakness — affecting several organs at once rather than one — is trained to check two roots rather than one: whether the problem traces back to a limited, fixed prenatal inheritance, or to an ongoing, correctable postnatal shortfall in what the spleen and stomach are managing to produce. The two roots explain most of what the rest of this organs unit has already covered, just from their source rather than their symptoms.

這就是為什麼一位面對廣泛虛弱模式——同時影響好幾個臟腑，而不是只有一個——的醫師，會被訓練去檢查兩個根本，而不是一個：問題追溯回去的，是一份有限、固定的先天遺產，還是一個持續、可修正的後天短缺，在脾胃設法生產的東西上。這兩個根本，解釋了這個臟腑單元剩下的大部分內容，只是從它們的來源，而不是它們的症狀來看。

Questions · 閱讀理解

According to the passage, what does "the root of what comes after heaven" mean, and which organs is it applied to?

"Heaven" refers to birth itself, and "after" refers to everything the body must keep manufacturing on its own afterward; it's applied to the spleen and stomach.

According to the passage, why can a struggling spleen eventually cause shortages elsewhere in the body?

Because every other organ's qi, blood, and fluids ultimately depend on raw material the spleen and stomach convert from food, so a struggling spleen affects the whole supply chain, not just digestion.

According to the last paragraph, what "two roots" is a doctor trained to check when facing a wide-ranging pattern of weakness?

Whether the problem traces back to a limited, fixed prenatal inheritance (kidney), or to an ongoing, correctable postnatal shortfall in what the spleen and stomach produce.
