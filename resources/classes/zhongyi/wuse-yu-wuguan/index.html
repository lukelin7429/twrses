Unit 40: Reading a Face by Its Element

A doctor of Chinese medicine traditionally begins an examination with observation, before a single question is asked, and the five-element framework gives that observation a specific vocabulary. Each element is matched not only to an organ, but to a facial color and a sense organ, treating the face as a kind of visible display panel for what's happening deeper inside the body.

中醫師傳統上會在問一句話之前，先從觀察開始問診，而五行框架給了這個觀察一套具體的詞彙。每個元素不只對應到一個臟腑，還對應到一種臉部顏色與一個感覺器官，把臉當成身體深處狀況的一種可見顯示面板。

Wood is matched to a greenish or bluish tinge and to the eyes; a person under prolonged liver strain is traditionally said to show it around the eyes first. Fire is matched to redness and the tongue, tying the heart to speech and to a flushed complexion. Earth is matched to a yellowish tinge and the mouth, connecting digestive strength to the color around the lips. Metal is matched to paleness and the nose, and water is matched to a darker, sometimes bluish-black tinge and the ears.

木對應到偏青或偏藍的色調，還有眼睛；一個長期處在肝的壓力下的人，傳統上被認為會先在眼睛周圍顯現出來。火對應到發紅與舌頭，把心跟言語、還有紅潤的氣色連在一起。土對應到偏黃的色調與嘴巴，把消化力的強弱跟嘴唇周圍的顏色連結起來。金對應到蒼白與鼻子，水對應到偏暗、有時偏藍黑的色調與耳朵。

None of this works as a single glance diagnosis — a person's natural skin tone, lighting, and momentary flush from heat or exercise all complicate a reading taken in isolation. The framework's real use is the same as everywhere else in this course: not a single clue treated as conclusive, but one thread in a larger pattern, cross-checked against everything else a trained practitioner asks and observes.

這一切都不能靠看一眼就下診斷——一個人天生的膚色、光線，還有因為熱或運動而暫時發紅，都會讓單獨拿出來看的一個線索變得複雜。這套框架真正的用途，跟這門課別處一樣：不是把單一線索當成定論，而是更大一個模式裡的一條線索，要跟受過訓練的醫師問到、觀察到的其他一切互相對照。

Questions · 閱讀理解

According to the passage, what does a doctor of Chinese medicine traditionally do before asking a single question?

They begin the examination with observation, using the five-element framework's vocabulary of facial color and sense organs.

According to the passage, which facial color and sense organ is matched to the "Fire" element?

Redness is matched to the Fire element, along with the tongue.

According to the last paragraph, why doesn't this framework work as "a single glance diagnosis"?

Because a person's natural skin tone, lighting, and momentary flush from heat or exercise all complicate a reading taken in isolation — a single clue needs to be cross-checked against everything else observed, not treated as conclusive on its own.
