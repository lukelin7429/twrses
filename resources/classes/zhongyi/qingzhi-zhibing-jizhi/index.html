Unit 29: When Emotion Becomes the Cause, Not Just the Feeling

The last lesson matched each of the seven emotions to an organ it can injure, but Chinese medicine goes one step further, describing exactly how an emotion causes damage: through its effect on the movement of qi. Anger is said to make qi rise — which is why prolonged anger is associated with headaches, a flushed face, and a pounding sensation, all symptoms of qi surging upward. Fear is said to make qi descend and scatter — which is why sudden, intense fear is associated with a sinking feeling in the stomach or a loss of bladder control.

上一課把七情裡的每一種，對應到它可能傷害的一個臟腑，但中醫更進一步，描述了情緒到底是怎麼造成傷害的：透過它對氣運行的影響。怒被認為會讓氣上升——這就是為什麼長期的憤怒會跟頭痛、臉紅、跳動感連在一起，這些都是氣往上衝的症狀。恐被認為會讓氣下降並渙散——這就是為什麼突然、強烈的恐懼會跟胃裡一沉的感覺，或膀胱失控連在一起。

Worry and overthinking are said to "knot" qi, causing it to stagnate in one place rather than flow — which shows up as a tight sensation in the chest or stomach, and a struggle to make decisions or move forward. Grief is said to drain qi directly, matching the exhaustion and shortness of breath that so often follow real loss. Joy, in its ordinary form, is said to relax and smooth qi's movement, which is part of why Chinese medicine considers joy protective rather than harmful in typical amounts — it is only sudden, overstimulating joy that is treated as potentially disruptive.

思與過度思考被認為會讓氣「結」，讓它停滯在一個地方，而不是流動——這會表現成胸口或胃部的緊繃感，還有難以下決定或往前邁進。悲被認為會直接耗損氣，這對應著真正失去所愛之後常常伴隨而來的疲憊與呼吸急促。喜，在一般的形式下，被認為會讓氣的運行放鬆、順暢，這也是為什麼中醫認為一般份量的喜是保護性的、而不是有害的——只有突然、過度刺激的喜，才會被當成可能造成紊亂的因素。

This mechanism explains why the emotional causes of illness in Chinese medicine were never meant to be treated as separate from the physical ones. An emotion isn't just a feeling that happens to accompany a symptom — in this framework, it is a specific, describable disturbance in how qi is moving, with its own physical signature. This is also why simply telling someone to "calm down" does very little: the goal is restoring qi's normal movement, not suppressing the emotion itself.

這個機制解釋了為什麼中醫從來不打算把情緒的致病因素跟身體的致病因素分開來看待。一種情緒不只是恰好伴隨著一個症狀出現的感受——在這個框架裡，它是氣運行上一種具體、可描述的紊亂，有它自己的身體特徵。這也是為什麼只是叫一個人「冷靜下來」幾乎沒什麼用：目標是恢復氣正常的運行，而不是壓抑情緒本身。

Questions · 閱讀理解

According to the passage, what does anger do to qi, and what symptoms match this effect?

Anger is said to make qi rise, matching symptoms like headaches, a flushed face, and a pounding sensation.

According to the passage, what does worry and overthinking do to qi, and what symptoms match this effect?

Worry and overthinking are said to "knot" qi, causing it to stagnate, matching a tight sensation in the chest or stomach and difficulty making decisions or moving forward.

According to the last paragraph, why does the passage say simply telling someone to "calm down" does very little?

Because the goal is restoring qi's normal movement, not suppressing the emotion itself — an emotion is a specific, describable disturbance in qi movement, not just a feeling to be silenced.
