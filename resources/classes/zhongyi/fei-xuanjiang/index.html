Unit 108: The Lung's Two Opposite Jobs: Dispersing Out, Bringing Down

An earlier lesson in this unit described the lung as the "delicate organ," easily disturbed because of its direct exposure to outside air and its position at the body's highest point. That lesson didn't detail what the lung is actually doing when it's working correctly. Chinese medicine describes the lung's function as two opposite movements held in constant balance: "dispersing" outward, and "descending" downward, together called the lung's dispersing-and-descending function.

這個單元稍早的一課，把肺描述成「嬌臟」，因為直接暴露在外面的空氣中、又位在身體最高的位置，所以容易受到擾動。那一課沒有詳細說明，肺正常運作的時候，到底在做什麼。中醫把肺的功能，描述成兩個相反的運動，維持在持續的平衡狀態：向外的「宣發」，跟向下的「肅降」，合稱肺的宣發肅降功能。

Dispersing describes the lung sending qi and fluids outward to the body's surface, warming the skin, opening the pores to release sweat, and distributing defensive qi to guard against external invasion — an outward, expanding motion. Descending describes the opposite: the lung sending qi and fluids downward toward the rest of the body and toward the kidney for storage, keeping the airway clear by moving used air and fluid down and out rather than letting them accumulate and stagnate at the top.

宣發，描述的是肺把氣與津液往外送到身體表面，溫暖皮膚、打開毛孔釋放汗水、把衛氣分布出去以防禦外來的入侵——一種向外、擴張的運動。肅降，描述的則是相反的方向：肺把氣與津液往下、往身體其他部位、往腎的儲藏送，透過把用過的空氣與津液往下、往外送，而不是讓它們在頂端堆積、停滯，來保持呼吸道的暢通。

The two movements are traditionally described as needing to happen in balance, not simply both happening at maximum strength: too much dispersing relative to descending is traditionally associated with an unproductive, dry cough and excessive sweating, while too much descending relative to dispersing is traditionally associated with a sense of tightness in the chest and unreleased phlegm. This dual, opposite-direction design is also offered as a deeper explanation for why the lung was called delicate in the earlier lesson — an organ balancing two opposite jobs at once has more ways for something to go wrong than an organ with only one job to do.

這兩個運動，傳統上被描述成需要維持平衡，而不是兩個都同時用最大強度運作：宣發相對肅降太多，傳統上被連結到一種不太有痰、乾燥的咳嗽跟過度流汗；肅降相對宣發太多，傳統上被連結到一種胸悶的感覺跟排不出去的痰。這種雙重、方向相反的設計，也被拿來當成一個更深一層的解釋，說明為什麼稍早一課把肺稱為嬌嫩——一個同時平衡著兩個相反工作的臟腑，比一個只有一個工作要做的臟腑，有更多可能出錯的方式。

Questions · 閱讀理解

According to the passage, what are the lung's two opposite movements, and what does each one do?

Dispersing sends qi and fluids outward to the surface (warming skin, opening pores, distributing defensive qi); descending sends qi and fluids downward, keeping the airway clear.

According to the passage, what happens when dispersing is excessive relative to descending?

It's traditionally associated with an unproductive, dry cough and excessive sweating.

According to the last paragraph, how does this dual-movement design explain the lung's "delicate" reputation from an earlier lesson?

An organ balancing two opposite jobs at once has more ways for something to go wrong than an organ with only one job — deepening why the lung is easily disturbed.
