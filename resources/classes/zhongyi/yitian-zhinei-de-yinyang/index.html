Unit 62: Yin and Yang in a Single Day

Chinese medicine treats a single day as a miniature version of the full year, with the same yin-yang logic simply compressed into twenty-four hours instead of twelve months. Dawn is compared to spring: yang begins rising after its lowest point, matching the pattern of new growth. Noon is compared to summer: yang reaches its peak, the same full, outward expansion covered in an earlier unit's lesson on the season. Dusk is compared to autumn: yang begins withdrawing, gathering in the way autumn's cooler air draws things inward. Midnight is compared to winter: yin reaches its own peak, and the body is meant to be at its most still and storing.

中醫把一天當成一整年的縮小版，用同一套陰陽邏輯，只是把時間從十二個月壓縮成二十四小時。清晨被比擬成春天：陽氣在它最低點之後開始上升，對應著新生生長的模式。中午被比擬成夏天：陽氣達到高峰，就是這個單元稍早那一課提過的、夏天那種完全、向外的擴張。傍晚被比擬成秋天：陽氣開始內收，像秋天較涼的空氣把東西往內拉一樣聚攏起來。半夜被比擬成冬天：陰氣達到自己的高峰，身體本該處在最靜止、最儲藏的狀態。

This isn't a poetic flourish layered on top of the science — it's the same logic this course has used throughout, applied at a smaller scale. The advice to sleep before eleven at night, covered in an earlier unit's lesson on the organ clock, lines up with this pattern precisely: eleven p.m. sits right at the entrance to the day's "winter," the point where yin should be gathering strength undisturbed, exactly the way this course's seasonal unit recommended not spending yang during the year's true winter.

這不是疊加在科學上面的詩意修飾——這是這門課從頭到尾都在用的同一套邏輯，套用在更小的尺度上。這個單元稍早關於臟腑時鐘那一課提過的「十一點前睡覺」的建議，跟這個模式精確地對上了：晚上十一點正好坐在一天的「冬天」的入口，那個陰氣本該不受打擾地聚集力量的時間點，正好就是這門課節氣單元建議在一年真正的冬天裡不要耗損陽氣的同一個道理。

Reading a single day this way changes what counts as "working against the day." A late-morning burst of activity aligns with the day's own rising yang; the same burst of activity at midnight works against the day's yin the same way exercising heavily in the dead of winter works against the year's. The seasons, the organ clock, and this one-day yin-yang cycle are three different sizes of the exact same clock, and this course has now covered all three.

用這個角度來讀一天，改變了什麼算是「跟這一天對著幹」。上午晚一點爆發出的活動，順著這一天本身上升的陽氣；同樣一陣爆發的活動如果發生在半夜，就跟這一天的陰對著幹，跟在隆冬時節劇烈運動跟這一年對著幹是同一個道理。節氣、臟腑時鐘，還有這個一天的陰陽循環，是同一個時鐘的三種不同尺寸，而這門課現在把三種都涵蓋到了。

Questions · 閱讀理解

According to the passage, what season is dawn compared to, and what season is midnight compared to?

Dawn is compared to spring (yang rising from its lowest point); midnight is compared to winter (yin at its peak).

According to the passage, how does the "sleep before eleven" advice from an earlier lesson line up with the one-day yin-yang cycle?

Eleven p.m. sits at the entrance to the day's "winter," the point where yin should be gathering strength undisturbed — matching the seasonal unit's advice not to spend yang during the year's true winter.

According to the last paragraph, what three "sizes" of the same clock does this course cover?

The seasons (yearly cycle), the organ clock (twenty-four-hour cycle by organ), and the one-day yin-yang cycle (dawn/noon/dusk/midnight compared to spring/summer/autumn/winter).
