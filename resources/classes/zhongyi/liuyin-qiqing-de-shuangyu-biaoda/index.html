Unit 30: Explaining Six Excesses and Seven Emotions to a Friend

Suppose a friend, hearing about the six excesses and seven emotions, asks, "So Chinese medicine thinks weather and feelings literally cause disease?" A precise answer avoids overclaiming: "Not exactly 'cause' in the germ-theory sense. Think of them as recognized risk patterns — the six excesses describe how environmental stress (cold, damp, wind) can overwhelm a body that isn't equipped to handle it, and the seven emotions describe how a sustained, extreme emotional state can disturb the body's functions from the inside, the same way chronic stress is now understood in Western medicine to affect the immune system and digestion."

假設一位朋友聽到六淫七情，問：「所以中醫真的認為天氣跟情緒會導致疾病？」一個精確的回答，要避免誇大：「不完全是細菌致病論那種意義上的『導致』。可以把它們想成已被認可的風險模式——六淫描述的是環境壓力（寒、濕、風）怎麼壓垮一個沒有能力應付的身體；七情描述的是一種持續、極端的情緒狀態，怎麼從內部擾亂身體的功能，就像現在西醫也理解慢性壓力會影響免疫系統和消化功能一樣。」

A concrete comparison helps here too: "It's a bit like saying, 'stress causes ulcers.' That's not one single germ causing a single disease — it's a real, documented pattern where a sustained internal state changes how the body functions, making certain problems more likely. Chinese medicine just built its own detailed map of which sustained states connect to which physical patterns, using its own vocabulary — wind, qi rising, qi knotting — instead of the vocabulary Western medicine developed later."

這裡有一個具體的比喻會有幫助：「這有點像是說『壓力導致潰瘍』。那不是單一一種細菌造成單一一種疾病——那是一個真實、有紀錄的模式，一種持續的內在狀態改變了身體運作的方式，讓某些問題更容易發生。中醫只是用它自己的詞彙——風、氣上升、氣結——建立了自己一套詳細的地圖，說明哪些持續的狀態連結到哪些身體模式，而不是用西醫後來發展出來的詞彙。」

It's worth ending, as always, on the practical boundary: "None of this means you can diagnose yourself by matching a mood to an organ. It means Chinese medicine takes seriously something a lot of modern medicine is only recently taking seriously too — that sustained emotional states have real physical effects. For an actual health concern, the answer is still the same: see a licensed doctor."

值得像往常一樣，用實際的界線來收尾：「這一切不代表你可以靠把一種心情對應到一個臟腑，來自我診斷。這代表中醫認真看待一件事——而現代醫學最近也才開始認真看待這件事——就是持續的情緒狀態，真的會有身體上的影響。真的有健康疑慮的話，答案還是一樣：去看合格醫師。」

Questions · 閱讀理解

According to the passage, why does the answer avoid saying weather and feelings "cause" disease "in the germ-theory sense"?

Because the six excesses and seven emotions describe recognized risk patterns — how environmental or emotional states can disturb the body's functions — rather than a single, direct cause-and-effect like a germ causing a disease.

According to the passage, what everyday comparison is used to make the six excesses and seven emotions less exotic?

The comparison to "stress causes ulcers" — a real, documented pattern where a sustained internal state changes how the body functions, making certain problems more likely.

According to the last paragraph, what does the passage say this framework does NOT mean?

That someone can diagnose themselves by matching a mood to an organ — a real health concern still requires seeing a licensed doctor.
