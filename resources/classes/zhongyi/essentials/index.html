Module 1 of 12第 1 單元・共 12 單元

What TCM Is — and How to Read It中醫是什麼——以及怎麼讀它

A two-thousand-year-old medical tradition, read with two lenses.

兩千多年的醫學傳統，要用兩種眼光來讀。

Traditional Chinese Medicine (TCM) is a medical tradition with more than two thousand years of written history. Its foundational text, the Huangdi Neijing (黃帝內經, The Yellow Emperor's Inner Classic), was not written by one person. It was put together by many hands, roughly between the third and first centuries BCE, and it has been commented on, argued over, and revised ever since.

中醫是一個有兩千多年文字紀錄的醫學傳統。它的奠基經典《黃帝內經》不是一個人寫的，大約在西元前三世紀到前一世紀之間，由許多人陸續編成；之後兩千年，歷代醫家不斷為它作注、辯論、修訂。

Today it is practiced in clinics and hospitals across the Chinese-speaking world, and related traditions grew up in Korea (Korean medicine, 韓醫) and Japan (Kampo, 漢方). In Taiwan, licensed TCM physicians (中醫師) work alongside Western-trained doctors, many hospitals have a TCM department, and National Health Insurance covers TCM clinic visits.

今天，華人世界各地的診所和醫院都有中醫；韓國的韓醫、日本的漢方，也是從同一個源頭發展出來的。在台灣，有執照的中醫師和西醫並肩執業，許多醫院設有中醫部，健保也給付中醫門診。

Two lenses兩種眼光

To read TCM well, you need two lenses at once. The first is the tradition's own logic: what its concepts mean and how practitioners use them. The second is modern evidence: what clinical research has tested, what held up, and what has not been shown. Mixing the two up is the most common mistake, in both directions. Some people dismiss the whole tradition because its theory sounds strange. Others treat every traditional idea as if science had already confirmed it.

要讀懂中醫，需要同時戴兩副眼鏡。第一副看中醫自己的邏輯：這些概念是什麼意思、醫師怎麼用。第二副看現代證據：臨床研究測試過什麼、哪些站得住、哪些還沒有證明。兩者混在一起，是最常見的錯誤，而且兩個方向都有：有人因為理論聽起來陌生就全盤否定；也有人把每一個傳統說法，都當成科學已經證實。

This course keeps the two apart. The main text explains what TCM says, in its own terms. The green What research says boxes tell you, as plainly as we can, where the evidence stands.

這門課把兩者分開。正文用中醫自己的語言說明中醫怎麼講；綠色的「研究怎麼說」方塊，則盡量直白地告訴你證據目前到哪裡。

Three ideas that make TCM different中醫跟西醫最不一樣的三個想法

Pattern, not only disease看「證」，不只看「病」
A TCM diagnosis names a pattern (證, zheng) in the whole person, such as "Spleen qi deficiency with dampness." Two people with the same Western diagnosis, say chronic insomnia, may be given different TCM patterns and different treatments. This approach is called 辨證論治: identify the pattern, then treat.
中醫的診斷是替整個人辨出一個「證」，例如「脾氣虛兼濕」。兩個西醫診斷相同的人，例如都是慢性失眠，可能被辨成不同的證，用不同的治法。這就叫「辨證論治」：先辨證，再論治。
Function, not anatomy講功能，不講解剖
When TCM says "Spleen," it means a set of functions — mainly turning food into usable nourishment — not the organ you dissected in biology class. Module 4 explains this; it is the key that unlocks almost everything else.
中醫說的「脾」，指的是一組功能，主要是把吃進去的東西轉化成身體能用的養分，而不是生物課解剖的那個器官。第四單元會說明這一點，它幾乎是讀懂其他一切的鑰匙。
Prevention first預防為先
The classics praise the physician who keeps people well over the one who rescues them once they are sick. Diet, sleep, emotion, and living with the seasons are treated as medicine, not as extras.
經典推崇的是讓人不生病的醫者，而不是等人病了才搶救的醫者。飲食、睡眠、情緒、順應四季，在中醫裡本身就是醫療，不是附加的養生小撇步。

What TCM is not中醫不是什麼

  • Not a religion. It borrows words like yin, yang, and qi from Chinese philosophy, but you do not have to believe anything to study it.
    不是宗教。它借用了陰陽、氣這些中國哲學的語彙，但學它不需要信仰任何東西。
  • Not a replacement for emergency care. A stroke, a heart attack, a broken bone, or a high fever in a baby needs a hospital first.
    不能取代急診。中風、心肌梗塞、骨折、嬰兒高燒，第一步都是去醫院。
  • Not "natural, therefore safe." Herbs are active substances. Some are toxic, and some interact with prescription drugs (Module 11).
    不是「天然的就安全」。中藥有藥理作用，有些有毒，有些會和西藥交互作用（見第十一單元）。

🔬 What research says研究怎麼說

The evidence is very uneven, so it has to be judged one therapy and one condition at a time. Acupuncture for some chronic pain conditions has enough trial support that the UK's NICE guidelines say it can be considered. The malaria drug artemisinin came out of a fourth-century Chinese herbal text and won a Nobel Prize in 2015. But the theoretical frameworks — qi, meridians, the five phases — have not been found as physical structures or measurable quantities. They are best read as a traditional way of organizing clinical observation. In 2019 the World Health Organization added a traditional-medicine chapter to its disease classification, ICD-11, for collecting data; WHO states that this does not mean it endorses the treatments.
證據的強弱差別很大，只能一種療法、一種病症分開來看。針灸用於某些慢性疼痛，臨床試驗的支持足夠，英國 NICE 的指引說可以考慮使用；抗瘧疾藥青蒿素出自四世紀的中醫古籍，在 2015 年得到諾貝爾獎。但氣、經絡、五行這些理論框架，至今沒有被找到對應的實體結構或可測量的量，比較恰當的理解，是把它們看成整理臨床觀察的一套傳統方法。世界衛生組織在 2019 年把傳統醫學章節納入疾病分類 ICD-11，目的是蒐集資料；WHO 明白表示，這不代表它背書這些療法。

🇹🇼 In Taiwan在台灣

To practice as a 中醫師 in Taiwan you must pass a national licensing examination. Most TCM clinics accept your NHI card: herbal granules, acupuncture, and 傷科 (manual therapy for injuries) are covered, with a small copayment.
在台灣要當中醫師，必須通過國家考試。大部分中醫診所都收健保卡，科學中藥、針灸、傷科推拿都有給付，只需付少許部分負擔。

Take with you帶走這一句

TCM is a complete medical tradition with its own logic. Learn that logic on its own terms — and check each specific claim against the evidence, one therapy at a time.
中醫是一套有自己邏輯的完整醫學傳統。用它自己的語言理解它的邏輯，同時把每一個具體說法，一種療法一種療法地拿證據來檢驗。

Quick check小測驗 5

  1. 1. Who wrote the Huangdi Neijing?《黃帝內經》是誰寫的？

    The Yellow Emperor is a legendary figure the text is framed around. The book was compiled by many hands, roughly in the third to first centuries BCE.
    黃帝是書中假託的傳說人物。這本書是許多人大約在西元前三到前一世紀之間陸續編成的。

  2. 2. Two patients have the same Western diagnosis. Will a TCM physician treat them the same way?兩位病人的西醫診斷一樣，中醫師會用同樣的方法治療嗎？

    This is 辨證論治: treatment follows the pattern, so the same disease can get different treatments (同病異治).
    這就是辨證論治：治法跟著證走，所以同一種病可能用不同治法，叫做「同病異治」。

  3. 3. When TCM talks about the "Spleen," it mainly means…中醫說的「脾」，主要指的是……

    TCM organs are systems of function. The TCM Spleen governs "transformation and transportation" (運化) of food and fluids.
    中醫的臟腑是功能系統。中醫的脾「主運化」，負責轉化、運送飲食與水液。

  4. 4. Which statement about the evidence for TCM is most accurate?關於中醫的證據，哪一句最正確？

    Evidence has to be judged therapy by therapy and condition by condition — acupuncture for chronic pain is not the same question as an herb for diabetes.
    證據要一種療法、一種病症分開判斷：針灸治慢性疼痛，跟某種中藥治糖尿病，是完全不同的兩個問題。

  5. 5. Your friend has sudden chest pain spreading to the left arm. What should they do first?朋友突然胸痛，痛到左手臂，第一步該怎麼做？

    Possible heart attack. Emergencies go to the hospital first; TCM can have a role later, in recovery.
    可能是心肌梗塞。急症一定先送醫院；中醫可以在之後的復原期再參與。

Yin and Yang陰陽

Not mysticism, and not good versus bad — a language for describing balance.

不是玄學，也不是善惡二分，而是描述平衡的一套語言。

The characters tell the story. 陰 (yin) first meant the shady side of a hill; 陽 (yang) meant the sunny side. Same hill, two sides, and which side is which depends on where the sun is. Yin and yang are not two substances. They are relational labels: anything can be called more yin or more yang compared with something else.

看字就知道。「陰」原本指山的背陽面，「陽」指山的向陽面：同一座山的兩面，哪一面是陰、哪一面是陽，要看太陽在哪裡。陰陽不是兩種物質，而是相對的標籤：任何東西都可以說比另一樣東西更陰或更陽。

active, moving活動、運動still, resting安靜、休息
warm, hot溫、熱cool, cold涼、寒
bright, daytime明亮、白天dark, night幽暗、夜晚
rising, outward上升、向外sinking, inward下降、向內

surface, back of the body體表、背部interior, front of the body體內、腹部

Four relationships四種關係

Opposition對立
They pull in opposite directions and keep each other in check — heat checks cold, activity checks rest.
兩者方向相反、互相制約：熱制寒，動制靜。
Interdependence互根
Neither exists alone. Night only means something because there is day; activity needs the rest that makes it possible. A saying from many centuries later puts it: 孤陰不生，獨陽不長 — yin alone cannot give life, yang alone cannot grow.
誰也離不開誰。有白天，夜晚才有意義；活動需要休息來支撐。晚了許多世紀的說法是「孤陰不生，獨陽不長」。
Waxing and waning消長
When one grows, the other shrinks, like the length of day and night across the year.
此消彼長，就像一年之中白天和黑夜的長短變化。
Transformation轉化
At an extreme, each turns into the other. The longest day of the year is the start of days getting shorter. The small dots in the taiji symbol show the seed of each inside the other.
到了極點會互相轉化：一年白天最長的那天，正是白天開始變短的起點。太極圖裡的小圓點，就是對方藏在自己裡面的種子。

Yin and yang in the body身體裡的陰陽

In TCM, health is a working balance between yang — the body's warming, moving, transforming functions — and yin — the cooling, moistening, nourishing substances that anchor them. When the balance tips, the tradition describes four basic patterns.

中醫認為，健康是陽與陰之間的動態平衡：陽是身體溫煦、推動、轉化的功能；陰是涼潤、滋養、把陽穩住的物質。平衡一旦傾斜，傳統上分成四種基本型態。

Pattern型態What the tradition describes傳統上的表現
Yang excess陽盛feeling hot, red face, thirst, irritability, constipation怕熱、臉紅、口渴、煩躁、便秘
Yin excess陰盛feeling cold, heaviness, pain eased by warmth, watery stools怕冷、身重、得溫痛減、大便稀
Yang deficiency陽虛always cold, tired, pale, frequent clear urine長期怕冷、疲倦、臉色蒼白、小便清長
Yin deficiency陰虛hot palms and soles, night sweats, dry mouth at night手足心熱、盜汗、夜間口乾

Notice that yang excess and yin deficiency can both make you feel hot — but in TCM logic the cause is opposite. One is too much heat; the other is too little cooling. The first is "cleared," the second "nourished." This is why the tradition warns against self-treating from a symptom alone.

注意：陽盛和陰虛都會讓人覺得熱，但在中醫的邏輯裡，原因剛好相反：一個是熱太多，一個是涼潤不夠。前者要「清」，後者要「補」。所以傳統上提醒，不要只憑一個症狀就自己下藥。

🔬 What research says研究怎麼說

Yin and yang are a way of classifying, not something a lab can measure. It is tempting to say yin-yang "is" homeostasis or the two branches of the nervous system; those comparisons can help a beginner, but they are analogies, not findings. Researchers have tried to link yin and yang patterns to body temperature, hormones, or metabolism, with mixed and small results.
陰陽是一種分類方法，不是實驗室能測量的東西。很多人喜歡說陰陽「就是」體內恆定、或自律神經的兩個系統。這些比喻對初學者有幫助，但只是比喻，不是研究結果。曾有研究想把陰虛、陽虛和體溫、荷爾蒙、代謝連在一起，結果不一致，差異也不大。

Take with you帶走這一句

Yin and yang are relative labels for two sides of one balance. In the body, the question TCM asks is not just "hot or cold?" but "too much of one, or too little of the other?"
陰陽是同一個平衡兩面的相對標籤。在身體上，中醫問的不只是「熱還是寒」，而是「這邊太多，還是那邊不夠」。

  1. 1. The character originally meant…「陽」字最早的意思是……

    陽 was the sunny side and 陰 the shady side of a hill — a picture of relationship, not of two things.
    陽是山的向陽面，陰是背陽面。這是一幅「關係」的圖，不是兩樣東西。

  2. 2. Which is more yang?哪一個比較偏陽？

  4. 4. Why does TCM treat yang excess and yin deficiency differently, though both feel hot?陽盛和陰虛都覺得熱，為什麼中醫治法不同？

    In TCM logic the cause is opposite, so the treatment is opposite: clear the extra heat, or replenish the missing yin.
    在中醫的邏輯裡原因相反，治法也相反：一個清掉多出來的熱，一個補足不夠的陰。

  5. 5. "The longest day of the year is when the days start getting shorter." Which relationship is this?「一年白天最長的那天，就是白天開始變短的起點。」這是哪一種關係？

    At its peak, yang begins to turn into yin —.
    陽到了極點就開始轉為陰，也就是「重陽必陰」。

Qi氣

Why "energy" is the wrong translation — and what qi does instead.

為什麼「energy」是錯誤的翻譯，以及氣到底做了什麼。

Qi (氣) is usually translated as "energy," and that translation causes trouble. Energy, in physics, is a quantity you can measure and store. Qi is not a substance sitting in a tank. The Five Phases五行

Wood, Fire, Earth, Metal, Water: a map of how systems feed and check one another.

木火土金水：一張彼此滋養、彼此制約的關係圖。

The five xing (五行) are often called the "Five Elements," but 行 means to move or to act, so many scholars prefer "Five Phases." They are not chemical elements. They are five categories for sorting how things behave: Wood grows and spreads, Fire rises and flares, Earth holds and nourishes, Metal gathers and contracts, Water sinks and stores.

五行常被譯成「Five Elements」，但「行」的意思是運行、作用，所以許多學者比較喜歡譯成「Five Phases」。它們不是化學元素，而是五種歸類事物行為的類別：木生長舒展，火上炎，土承載化育，金收斂，水下行閉藏。

Two cycles兩種循環

Generating cycle相生
Each phase nourishes the next, like a mother feeding a child: Wood → Fire → Earth → Metal → Water → Wood. Wood feeds fire; fire leaves ash, which becomes earth; earth yields metal; metal gathers water (think of dew on cool metal); water nourishes trees.

🔬 What research says研究怎麼說

The five phases come from early Chinese cosmology, which sorted everything — colors, seasons, directions, musical notes — into fives. The medical correspondences (Liver with anger, Lung with grief, and so on) are part of that system, not results of experiments. It is true that research links chronic stress to digestive, sleep, and immune problems, so the general idea that emotions affect the body is well supported. But the specific pairings, such as "grief damages the Lung," have not been shown to work the way the theory says.
五行出自中國早期的宇宙觀，把顏色、季節、方位、音律等一切事物都分成五類。醫學上的對應，例如肝配怒、肺配悲，是這套系統的一部分，不是實驗得出的結果。研究確實發現長期壓力會影響消化、睡眠和免疫，所以「情緒影響身體」這個大方向有充分證據；但像「悲傷肺」這種特定配對，並沒有被證明真的如理論所說的那樣運作。

Food as Medicine食療

Every food has a "nature" and a "flavor" — and cooking changes both.

每一種食物都有「性」和「味」，而烹調會改變它們。

A classical phrase says 藥食同源: medicine and food come from the same source. TCM draws no sharp line between the kitchen and the pharmacy; ginger, scallions, Chinese yam, and red dates all appear in classical formulas. The aim of eating well, in TCM terms, is to choose foods whose properties balance your body, the season, and the climate you live in.

古話說「藥食同源」。中醫不在廚房和藥房之間畫一條硬線：薑、蔥、山藥、紅棗都出現在經典方劑裡。照中醫的說法，吃得好，就是選擇性質能平衡你的身體、季節和居住氣候的食物。

Foods are sorted on two scales. Nature (性) runs cold – cool – neutral – warm – hot and describes the food's effect on the body, not its temperature on the plate: watermelon is cold even at room temperature, and ginger is warm even in an iced drink. Flavor (味) has five values — sour, bitter, sweet, pungent, salty — each said to act in a particular way.

食物用兩把尺來分。性從寒、涼、平、溫到熱，描述食物吃進去對身體的作用，不是盤子上的溫度：西瓜放在室溫下仍是寒的，薑加進冰飲裡仍是溫的。味有五種：酸、苦、甘、辛、鹹，傳統上各有不同的作用。

Meridians, or channels (經絡, jingluo), are the pathways along which TCM says qi and blood circulate, linking the surface of the body to the organs inside. Acupuncture and Related Therapies針灸與其他療法

What a session is like, what the evidence shows, and what to tell your practitioner.

看診是什麼樣子、證據顯示什麼，以及該告訴醫師哪些事。

The Chinese word 針灸 (zhenjiu) joins two therapies: 針, needling, and 灸, moxibustion — warming a point with burning mugwort (艾草). Modern acupuncture needles are nothing like injection needles. They are solid, flexible, and very thin (roughly 0.16 to 0.30 mm), sterile, and used once. Most people feel a brief prick, then a dull, heavy, or spreading ache called de qi (得氣).

「針灸」包含兩種療法：「針」是扎針，「灸」是用點燃的艾草溫熱穴位。現代針灸針和打針的針完全不同：實心、有彈性、非常細（大約 0.16 到 0.30 公釐），無菌、用一次就丟。大部分人只感到短暫的刺一下，接著是痠、脹、重或擴散的感覺，叫做「得氣」。

  1. Assessment. Questions about your history, sleep, and digestion; looking at your tongue; feeling the pulse at both wrists.
    四診。問病史、睡眠、消化；看舌頭；把兩手的脈。
  2. Needling. Usually several to a dozen or more needles, chosen for your pattern, sometimes far from where it hurts.
    下針。依照你的證型選穴，通常幾針到十幾針，有時扎在離痛處很遠的地方。
  3. Retention. You rest for 15 to 30 minutes; many people find it relaxing.
    留針。躺著休息 15 到 30 分鐘，很多人覺得很放鬆。
  4. A course of treatment. A fair trial for a long-standing problem is usually several sessions over some weeks. If nothing changes, reassess.
    療程。長期的問題通常要在幾週內做幾次，才算公平的嘗試；如果都沒有變化，就要重新評估。

🔬 What research says研究怎麼說

Acupuncture is one of the most studied parts of TCM. The UK's NICE guidelines say acupuncture can be considered for chronic primary pain and for preventing chronic tension-type headache (and migraine, if standard preventive drugs have not worked), and the US National Center for Complementary and Integrative Health summarizes evidence that it may help low-back pain, neck pain, knee osteoarthritis, and headaches. Guidelines do not always agree: NICE's own low-back-pain guideline says not to offer it. Two more honest caveats: in many trials, "sham" acupuncture (fake or shallow needling) does nearly as well as the real thing, so how much of the benefit comes from the specific points is debated; and for many other conditions — including fertility, where a large trial found no improvement in IVF live births — the evidence is weak or negative.
針灸是中醫裡研究最多的部分。英國 NICE 的指引說，慢性原發性疼痛、預防慢性緊縮型頭痛（以及標準預防藥物無效時的偏頭痛），可以考慮針灸；美國國家輔助與整合健康中心（NCCIH）整理的證據顯示，它可能對下背痛、頸痛、膝關節炎、頭痛有幫助。各家指引並不一致：NICE 自己的下背痛指引就說不要提供針灸。另外還有兩點要誠實說明：很多試驗中，「假針灸」（假針或淺刺）的效果和真針灸差不多，所以效果有多少來自特定穴位，至今仍有爭論；其他許多病症的證據薄弱甚至是負面的，例如生育，一個大型試驗發現針灸並沒有提高試管嬰兒的活產率。

⚠️ Safety安全

In trained hands with sterile single-use needles, acupuncture is very safe. Two large UK surveys published in 2001, covering more than 60,000 treatments, found no serious adverse events; minor bruising, soreness, and brief dizziness were the most common problems. Always tell your practitioner if you take blood thinners, are pregnant or trying to conceive, have a pacemaker (no electro-acupuncture), or have a bleeding disorder.
由受過訓練的人、使用無菌拋棄式針具，針灸非常安全。英國 2001 年發表的兩個大型調查，合計六萬多次治療，沒有發現嚴重不良事件；最常見的是輕微瘀青、痠痛和短暫頭暈。如果你在吃抗凝血藥、懷孕或準備懷孕、裝有心律調節器（不能做電針）、或有凝血疾病，一定要告訴醫師。

Take with you帶走這一句

Acupuncture is safe in trained hands and reasonably supported for some chronic pain and headaches. Give it a fair trial, stop if nothing changes, and keep your other doctors informed.
針灸在專業人員手上很安全，對某些慢性疼痛和頭痛有一定的證據。給它一次公平的嘗試，沒有變化就停；也要讓你的其他醫師知道。

Chinese Herbal Medicine中藥入門

Formulas, not single herbs — and why "natural" is not the same as "safe."

用的是方劑，不是單味藥；以及為什麼「天然」不等於「安全」。

Chinese herbal medicine is TCM's deepest clinical tool, with a written tradition running from the Shennong Bencao Jing (神農本草經, Eastern Han dynasty, 365 substances) to Li Shizhen's great Bencao Gangmu (本草綱目), finished in 1578 and printed in the 1590s. Most substances are plants; some are minerals or animal products.

中藥是中醫最深的臨床工具。文字傳統從東漢的《神農本草經》（收藥 365 種），一直到李時珍的《本草綱目》（1578 年完成，1590 年代刊行）。藥材大多是植物，也有礦物和動物來源。

Unlike much Western herbalism, TCM rarely uses one herb alone. It uses formulas (方劑) of several herbs with assigned roles — the "sovereign, minister, assistant, and envoy" (君臣佐使). The sovereign targets the main problem; the minister supports it; the assistant handles side issues or tempers harshness; the envoy guides and harmonizes the whole.

和許多西方草藥學不同，中醫很少只用一味藥，而是用好幾味藥組成方劑，各有角色，叫做「君臣佐使」：君藥針對主要問題，臣藥輔助君藥，佐藥處理次要問題或緩和藥性，使藥引導並調和全方。

    原因不明的體重減輕，或持續長大、變化的腫塊

Where TCM is often used中醫常被使用的領域

People commonly seek TCM for chronic pain, headaches, digestive complaints without a structural cause, sleep and stress, menstrual pain and menopausal symptoms, allergic rhinitis, and support during recovery from illness or cancer treatment. The strength of evidence differs across this list (Module 10), so it is reasonable to agree on a trial period and a clear sign of improvement before you start.

大家常為了這些找中醫：慢性疼痛、頭痛、沒有器質性原因的腸胃不適、睡眠與壓力、經痛與更年期症狀、過敏性鼻炎，以及病後或癌症治療期間的調養。這些項目的證據強弱不一（見第十單元），所以開始之前，和醫師約定一段嘗試期、以及怎樣才算改善，是合理的做法。

Choosing a practitioner怎麼挑選中醫師

  • In Taiwan, a 中醫師 holds a national license; clinics display it. Outside Taiwan, look for your country's or state's licensure — in the United States, many states require NCCAOM certification.
    在台灣，中醫師持有國家證照，診所會公開展示。在台灣以外，請確認當地的執照制度；例如美國，許多州要求 NCCAOM 認證。
  • Ask what they think is going on, what the treatment plan is, how long before you should expect change, and what it costs.
    問醫師：你認為我是什麼狀況？治療計畫是什麼？多久應該看到變化？費用多少？
  • A good practitioner is comfortable working with your other doctors and asks what medicines you take.
    好的醫師願意和你的其他醫師合作，也會主動問你在吃哪些藥。

⚠️ Red flags in a practitioner要提高警覺的醫師

Walk away from anyone who tells you to stop a prescribed medicine, claims to cure cancer or diabetes with herbs alone, discourages you from seeing other doctors, sells expensive prepaid packages under pressure, or will not show credentials.
遇到以下情況就離開：叫你停掉醫師開的藥、宣稱只用中藥就能治好癌症或糖尿病、勸你不要看其他醫生、強迫推銷昂貴的預付療程，或不肯出示證照。

🔬 The principle that ties it together把這一切串起來的原則

Use Western medicine for diagnosis, emergencies, and structural problems. Consider TCM for chronic patterns, recovery, and daily habits — and judge it by results. Above all, tell each practitioner what the other is doing. Hidden parallel treatment is where most real harm comes from: herb–drug interactions and delayed diagnoses.
診斷、急症和結構性問題，交給西醫；慢性體質、復原期和日常生活習慣，可以考慮中醫，並且用結果來判斷它。最重要的是：讓每一位醫師都知道另一位在做什麼。多數真正的傷害都來自彼此不知情的並行治療：中西藥交互作用，以及延誤診斷。

Take with you帶走這一句

Acute and serious: hospital first. Chronic and constitutional: TCM may be worth a fair trial. Always: tell every clinician about every other one.
急性、嚴重的：先去醫院。慢性、體質性的：中醫或許值得公平地試一試。永遠記得：讓每一位醫師都知道其他醫師的治療。
