Unit 74: Overthinking and the Spleen: Why Worry Specifically Blocks Digestion

An earlier unit lesson matched each of the seven emotions to an organ it can injure, pairing worry and overthinking with the spleen, and a later lesson described how each emotion disturbs qi's movement specifically — worry described as making qi "knot" rather than flow. This lesson connects those two ideas directly to the spleen's actual job, described in the organs unit: transforming food and drink into usable qi and blood, a process that depends on qi moving steadily through the digestive system.

這個單元稍早的一課，把七情裡的每一種對應到一個它可能傷害的臟腑，把思與過度思考跟脾配在一起，之後的一課描述了每種情緒具體怎麼擾亂氣的運行——思被描述成讓氣「結」，而不是流動。這一課把這兩個概念，直接連結到脾腑單元描述過的實際工作：把飲食轉化成能用的氣與血，這個過程依賴氣穩定地流過消化系統。

Overthinking, in this framework, doesn't just coexist with poor digestion — it interferes with the specific mechanical step the spleen needs to complete its job. Qi that has knotted rather than flowed can't drive the transformation process the way it normally would, which is why sustained worry is so often accompanied by a genuinely reduced appetite, a bloated or heavy feeling after eating even small amounts, and loose, poorly formed digestion — not vague complaints, but a fairly direct physical consequence of a specific organ's qi being obstructed.

在這個框架裡，過度思考不只是恰好跟消化不良同時存在——它干擾了脾完成工作所需要的那個具體、機械性的步驟。已經打結、而不是流動的氣，沒辦法像平常那樣推動轉化的過程，這也是為什麼持續的憂慮，這麼常伴隨著真正下降的食慾、就算只吃一點點，飯後也脹悶沉重的感覺，還有鬆散、成形不良的消化——不是模糊的抱怨，而是一個特定臟腑的氣被阻塞，相當直接的身體後果。

This is also why advice to simply "stop worrying" tends to fail as a fix for digestive trouble caused this way — it treats the emotion as the whole problem rather than the trigger for a specific, physical mechanism. The more useful question this framework suggests isn't "how do I feel less worried," but "how do I get qi moving through the spleen again" — sometimes overlapping with reducing worry, but not the same task, and not solved by willpower alone.

這也是為什麼單純叫人「不要擔心了」，作為這種方式造成的消化問題的解法，往往沒有用——它把情緒當成整個問題，而不是一個特定、身體機制的觸發點。這個框架建議的更有用的問題，不是「我要怎麼感覺不那麼擔心」，而是「我要怎麼讓氣重新流過脾」——有時候跟減少憂慮重疊，但不是同一件事，也不是光靠意志力就能解決的。
