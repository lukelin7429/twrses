Unit 25: Explaining Meridians to a Friend

Suppose a friend asks, "If meridians are real, why can't doctors find them with an MRI?" This question deserves a direct, honest answer rather than a defensive one: "You're right that no meridian shows up as a distinct physical structure. Chinese medicine doesn't actually claim it will — a meridian describes a mapped pattern of connection and influence between body regions, built from centuries of clinical observation, not a physical tube waiting to be found."

假設一位朋友問：「如果經絡是真的，為什麼醫師沒辦法用核磁共振找到它？」這個問題值得一個直接、誠實的回答，而不是防衛性的回答：「你說得對，沒有任何一條經絡會以一個明確的物理結構出現。中醫其實也不主張它會被找到——經絡描述的是身體區域之間一種被繪製出來的連結與影響模式，是好幾個世紀臨床觀察累積出來的，不是一根等著被找到的實體管子。」

A concrete example helps more than an abstract defense: "Think about referred pain — the way a heart attack can cause pain down the left arm. Western medicine explains that through shared nerve pathways. Chinese medicine noticed the exact same connection centuries earlier and mapped it as the Heart meridian. Two very different systems, arriving at a similar observation through different methods — that's a better way to think about meridians than 'invisible pipes.'"

一個具體的例子，會比抽象的辯護更有幫助：「想想牽涉痛——心臟病發作為什麼會痛到左手臂。西醫用共享的神經路徑來解釋這件事。中醫在好幾個世紀以前，就注意到完全相同的連結，並把它畫成了心經。兩套非常不同的系統，透過不同的方法，抵達了類似的觀察——這比『看不見的管子』是理解經絡更好的方式。」

Finally, it's worth being clear about where the explanation stops: "Acupuncture points are specific spots along these mapped connections, and using them safely takes years of training — it's not something to try on your own after reading about it. If you're curious enough to actually try acupuncture, the answer is the same as every lesson in this course: find a licensed, trained practitioner."

最後，值得講清楚這個解釋在哪裡打住：「穴位是沿著這些被繪製出來的連結上的特定點，安全地使用它們需要好幾年的訓練——讀完這篇文章之後，不是可以自己嘗試的事。如果你真的好奇想試針灸，答案跟這門課每一課一樣：找一位合格且受過訓練的醫師。」

Questions · 閱讀理解

According to the passage, why does the friend's question about MRIs deserve "a direct, honest answer rather than a defensive one"?

Because it's true that no meridian shows up as a distinct physical structure, and Chinese medicine doesn't actually claim it will — being upfront about this is more honest than being defensive.

According to the passage, what concrete example is used to make meridians less abstract for the friend?

Referred pain from a heart attack, which causes pain down the left arm — a pattern Western medicine explains through nerve pathways and that Chinese medicine mapped earlier as the Heart meridian.

According to the last paragraph, what does the passage say about trying acupuncture after reading about it?

That it's not something to try on your own — using acupuncture points safely takes years of training, and anyone curious should find a licensed, trained practitioner.
