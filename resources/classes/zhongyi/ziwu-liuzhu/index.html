Unit 92: Why Some Meridians Are More Active at Certain Hours

This course's seasonal-wellness unit described a two-hour cycle in which qi and blood are said to flow more strongly through a different organ across the day. This lesson names the underlying theory more precisely: "ebb-flow of the child and midday hours," which maps that same cycle directly onto the twelve main meridians introduced earlier in this unit, assigning each meridian its own two-hour window of peak activity, in a fixed sequence that repeats every day.

這門課的節氣養生單元，描述過一個兩小時一個循環的模式，說一天之中，氣血在不同臟腑裡流動得比較旺盛。這一課更精確地為底層的理論命名：「子午流注」，把同一個循環，直接對應到這個單元稍早介紹過的十二正經上，給每條經絡分配一個屬於自己的兩小時高峰活動時段，依照一個固定的順序，每天重複一次。

The theory doesn't just describe when a meridian is busiest; it's traditionally applied as a timing principle for treatment, on the reasoning that working with a meridian during its own peak window makes a technique more effective than working against a meridian at a time when its qi is traditionally considered to be at its lowest. A point on the lung meridian, for instance, is traditionally considered most responsive in the early morning hours when lung qi is said to peak, and comparatively less responsive twelve hours later.

這個理論不只是描述一條經絡什麼時候最忙；它傳統上被應用成一個治療的時機原則，理由是：在一條經絡自己的高峰時段配合它來運作，會比在它的氣傳統上被認為處於最低點的時候逆著它運作，讓一個技法更有效。舉例來說，肺經上的一個穴位，傳統上被認為在清晨、肺氣被說成達到高峰的時候最有反應，而十二小時之後，相對來說反應就沒那麼明顯。

This is one more instance of a principle this course has returned to repeatedly since its section on seasonal wellness: Chinese medicine treats time itself as a variable worth tracking, not a neutral backdrop indifferent to what's happening in the body. Applying that same logic down to the level of individual meridians and specific two-hour windows is what this theory adds — reading not just which organ is active in a given season, but which meridian is most active in a given hour.

這是這門課從節氣養生那個部分開始就一直回頭提到的一個原則的又一個例子：中醫把時間本身當成一個值得追蹤的變數，而不是一個對身體裡發生什麼事無動於衷的中性背景。把同一個邏輯，一路套用到單一經絡、特定兩小時時段的層次上，正是這個理論新增的東西——不只讀出某個季節裡哪個臟腑活躍，還讀出某個時辰裡哪條經絡最活躍。

Questions · 閱讀理解

According to the passage, what does the "ebb-flow of the child and midday hours" theory map the organ-clock cycle onto?

The twelve main meridians, assigning each one its own two-hour window of peak activity in a fixed, daily-repeating sequence.

According to the passage, why is a point on a given meridian traditionally considered more responsive during that meridian's own peak window?

Because working with a meridian during its own peak window is considered more effective than working against it when its qi is at its traditionally lowest point.

According to the last paragraph, what principle does this theory apply down to the level of individual meridians?

That time itself is a variable worth tracking, not a neutral backdrop — applied down to which meridian is most active in a given two-hour window.
