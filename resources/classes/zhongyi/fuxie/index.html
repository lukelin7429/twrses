Unit 96: Spring Diseases That Started in Winter

This unit's lessons on winter and spring described winter as a season for storing energy and spring as a season for encouraging its release. A classical principle connects the two seasons in a less obvious way: "if cold injures a person in winter, warm disease must follow in spring," describing a pathogen that enters the body during winter but doesn't produce symptoms right away, lying dormant instead and surfacing only once spring's warmth arrives — a pattern traditionally called "hidden pathogen."

這個單元講冬天與春天的那些課，把冬天描述成一個儲存能量的季節，把春天描述成一個鼓勵能量釋放的季節。一個經典原則，用一種不那麼明顯的方式，把這兩個季節連結起來：「冬傷於寒，春必溫病」，描述一種在冬天進入身體、卻沒有馬上產生症狀的病邪，反而潛伏著，直到春天的溫暖到來才浮現出來——這個模式傳統上被稱為「伏邪」。

The explanation given for the delay rests on winter's own storing character, covered in an earlier lesson: a body correctly following winter's pattern is holding its resources inward rather than actively fighting off every minor intrusion the way it might in a more outwardly active season. A cold pathogen that slips in during this storing period can, under this framework, simply be stored along with everything else, undetected, until spring's warming climate and the body's own shift toward outward release give it the conditions it needs to become active.

對這種延遲的解釋，建立在冬天本身儲存的特性上，這是稍早一課講過的：一個正確遵循冬天模式的身體，把資源往內收攏，而不是像在一個比較向外活躍的季節那樣，主動去對抗每一個小小的入侵。一個在這個儲存期間偷偷溜進來的寒邪，在這個框架下，可能就這樣跟其他一切一起被儲存起來，沒有被偵測到，直到春天溫暖的氣候、加上身體本身朝向向外釋放的轉變，給了它需要的條件，讓它變得活躍起來。

The idea is a specific, concrete case of a broader habit this course has pointed out repeatedly: reading a symptom in isolation from when it appears, without asking what happened in the preceding season, misses part of the picture this framework considers necessary. A spring illness that seems to come from nowhere is, under the hidden-pathogen principle, invited to be read partly as a delayed consequence of what happened, or didn't happen, months earlier — not a mystery that started the day the symptom did.

這個概念，是這門課反覆指出過的一個更廣泛習慣的一個具體案例：把一個症狀跟它出現的時間點分開來讀，不去問前一個季節發生了什麼事，會漏掉這個框架認為必要的一部分畫面。一個看似憑空冒出來的春天疾病，在伏邪原則下，被邀請部分地讀成幾個月前發生、或沒有發生的事情，所延遲產生的結果——而不是一個從症狀出現那天才開始的謎團。

Questions · 閱讀理解

According to the passage, what does "if cold injures a person in winter, warm disease must follow in spring" describe?

A pathogen that enters the body in winter but doesn't produce symptoms right away, lying dormant and surfacing only once spring's warmth arrives.

According to the passage, why can a cold pathogen go undetected throughout winter?

Because a body correctly following winter's storing pattern holds resources inward rather than actively fighting off every minor intrusion, so the pathogen can be stored along with everything else, undetected.

According to the last paragraph, what broader habit does the hidden-pathogen principle exemplify?

Reading a symptom in relation to what happened in the preceding season, rather than in isolation from when it appears — a spring illness may be a delayed consequence of an earlier season.

Words & Phrases · 生字及片語

dormant(adj.)休眠的；潛伏的The volcano had been dormant for over a century.這座火山已經休眠了超過一個世紀。surface(v.)浮現Old memories began to surface as she read the letter.她讀信時，舊回憶開始浮現。intrusion(n.)入侵；侵擾The noise felt like an intrusion on their quiet evening.這個噪音感覺像是對他們寧靜夜晚的一種侵擾。undetected(adj.)未被偵測到的The leak went undetected for months.這個漏水問題好幾個月都沒被偵測到。in isolation from(ph.)與…隔離開來You can't judge the decision in isolation from the context it was made in.你不能把這個決定跟它做出時的脈絡隔離開來評斷。preceding(adj.)先前的The results depended heavily on the preceding month's data.這些結果高度依賴前一個月的資料。mystery(n.)謎團The cause of the delay remained a mystery for weeks.這次延誤的原因，好幾個星期都還是個謎團。
