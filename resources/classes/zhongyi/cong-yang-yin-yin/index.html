Unit 103: Treating One Side by Drawing on the Other

An earlier lesson in this unit explained that yin and yang are never completely pure — each contains a trace of the other, even at their most opposed. "Treat yang by drawing on yin, treat yin by drawing on yang" is the treatment principle built directly on top of that fact: because a deficiency in one side can be supported by way of the other, correcting a yang problem doesn't always mean adding yang directly, and correcting a yin problem doesn't always mean adding yin directly.

這個單元稍早的一課解釋過，陰陽從來就不是完全純粹的——即使在最對立的時候，彼此都帶著一點對方的痕跡。「從陰引陽，從陽引陰」，是直接建立在這個事實上的治療原則：因為一方的虛弱，可以透過另一方來支援，矯正一個陽的問題，不一定代表要直接補陽；矯正一個陰的問題，也不一定代表要直接補陰。

The reasoning follows from the same mutual-rooting relationship covered elsewhere in this unit: yang is traditionally described as needing a yin foundation to rest on, the way a flame needs fuel to keep burning, and yin needs yang's warmth to keep from turning stagnant, the way water needs some warmth to keep from freezing solid. Under this view, a yang deficiency isn't always best addressed by only supplying more yang directly; sometimes strengthening the underlying yin foundation gives yang something firmer to draw on, indirectly restoring it.

這個道理，跟這個單元其他地方講過的互根關係是同一套：陽傳統上被描述成需要一個陰的基礎來立足，就像火焰需要燃料才能持續燃燒；陰則需要陽的溫暖，才不會變得停滯，就像水需要一點溫度，才不會結成一整塊冰。在這個觀點下，一個陽虛，不一定最好的處理方式，就是只直接供應更多的陽；有時候強化底層的陰的基礎，給了陽一個更穩固的東西可以依靠，間接地把它恢復過來。

This is one more example of a pattern this course keeps returning to: a symptom's location and its actual source aren't always the same place. Just as an earlier lesson on the five elements described treating a weak element by strengthening the one that feeds it, this principle applies the same indirect logic to yin and yang themselves — treating a visible shortfall on one side by strengthening its less visible counterpart, rather than only adding more of whatever seems to be missing.

這是這門課一直回頭提到的一個模式的又一個例子：一個症狀出現的位置，跟它真正的來源，不一定是同一個地方。就像稍早講五行的那一課描述過，透過強化滋養一個虛弱元素的那個元素來治療它一樣，這個原則把同樣間接的邏輯，套用到陰陽本身上——透過強化比較不明顯的那一方，來處理另一方明顯的短缺，而不是只補足看起來缺少的那個東西。

Questions · 閱讀理解

According to the passage, what does "treat yang by drawing on yin, treat yin by drawing on yang" mean?

That because yin and yang each contain a trace of the other, correcting a deficiency in one side doesn't always mean adding that same side directly — it can be supported by way of the other.

According to the passage, why might strengthening yin help resolve a yang deficiency?

Because yang needs a yin foundation to rest on, the way a flame needs fuel; strengthening yin gives yang something firmer to draw on, indirectly restoring it.

According to the last paragraph, what earlier lesson does this principle apply the same logic as?

The five-elements lesson on treating a weak element by strengthening the element that feeds it — the same indirect logic applied to yin and yang themselves.

Words & Phrases · 生字及片語

foundation(n.)基礎A strong foundation is essential before building higher.在往上蓋之前，穩固的基礎是必要的。fuel(n.)燃料The fire needed more fuel to keep burning through the night.這堆火需要更多燃料才能燒過整晚。stagnant(adj.)停滯的The pond water had become stagnant and still.這座池塘的水已經變得停滯不動。indirectly(adv.)間接地The policy change indirectly affected many other departments.這項政策的改變間接地影響了許多其他部門。shortfall(n.)短缺The budget showed a significant shortfall this quarter.這一季的預算出現了明顯的短缺。counterpart(n.)對應的一方Each department has a counterpart in the other office.每個部門在另一個辦公室都有對應的一方。draw on(ph.)依靠；汲取She had to draw on years of experience to solve the problem.她必須依靠多年的經驗，才解決了這個問題。
