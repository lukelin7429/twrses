Unit 76: The Equinoxes and Solstices: The Four Pivot Points of the Year

An earlier unit lesson introduced the twenty-four solar terms as a finer calendar than the four Western seasons, each marking a specific fifteen-day shift. Within those twenty-four, four hold special weight: the spring and autumn equinoxes, when day and night are of equal length, and the summer and winter solstices, marking the year's longest and shortest days. These four aren't just four terms among twenty-four — they're the exact pivot points the entire yearly cycle turns on.

這個單元稍早的一課，把二十四節氣介紹成一套比西方四季更細緻的曆法，每一個都標記著一個特定的十五天轉變。在這二十四個裡面，有四個帶著特別的份量：春分與秋分，這時候白天跟黑夜等長，還有夏至與冬至，標記著一年當中最長跟最短的白天。這四個不只是二十四個節氣裡的四個——它們是整個一年循環真正轉動的那個樞紐點。

Each of the four marks a moment of maximum or balanced yin-yang, tying directly back to concepts already covered in this course. The winter solstice marks yin at its absolute peak, the single darkest point of the year, and is traditionally treated as the moment yang begins its slow return — the seed of the coming spring planted at winter's deepest point. The summer solstice marks the mirror image, yang at its peak just as it's about to begin receding. The two equinoxes mark the two moments yin and yang sit in exact, momentary balance, before tipping toward one direction or the other.

這四個裡的每一個，都標記著陰陽最大值或平衡的一個時刻，直接連回這門課已經提過的概念。冬至標記著陰達到絕對的高峰，一年當中最黑暗的單一時刻，傳統上被當成陽開始緩慢回歸的那個時刻——來年春天的種子，就種在冬天最深的那個點上。夏至標記著鏡像的一刻，陽達到高峰，正好就在它即將開始消退之前。二分——春分秋分——標記著陰陽恰好、片刻地取得平衡，接著才傾向某一個方向的兩個時刻。

Understanding these four pivot points explains why they carry outsized cultural and medical weight compared to an ordinary solar term: they aren't just fifteen-day markers, but the four moments when the yin-yang cycle this course has described throughout — in a single day, a single year, and now the wider structure of the calendar itself — actually changes direction. Everything this course has said about a season's advice applying to a stretch of weeks turns out to hinge on these four precise hinge-points holding the whole structure together.

理解這四個樞紐點，解釋了為什麼相較於一般的節氣，它們帶著超乎比例的文化與醫療份量：它們不只是十五天的標記，而是這門課從頭到尾描述過的陰陽循環——在一天之內、在一整年之內，現在還有曆法本身更寬廣的結構裡——真正改變方向的四個時刻。這門課說過的、關於一個季節的建議適用於好幾週的一切，最終都取決於這四個精確的樞紐點，撐住了整個結構。

Questions · 閱讀理解

According to the passage, which four solar terms hold special weight, and what do they mark?

The spring and autumn equinoxes (equal day and night) and the summer and winter solstices (longest and shortest days) — the exact pivot points the yearly cycle turns on.

According to the passage, what does the winter solstice traditionally mark, beyond being the darkest day of the year?

It's traditionally treated as the moment yang begins its slow return — the seed of the coming spring planted at winter's deepest point.

According to the last paragraph, why do these four pivot points carry "outsized cultural and medical weight"?

Because they are the four moments when the yin-yang cycle actually changes direction, holding together the whole structure that this course's seasonal advice depends on.
