Unit 52: Autumn: Gathering In

Autumn is matched to metal and the lung in the five-element framework, and it marks the turn from the outward-facing first half of the year to the inward-facing second half. Where spring encourages growth and summer encourages full expansion, autumn's classic advice is about gathering in — the Yellow Emperor's Inner Classic recommends going to bed earlier and rising with the sunrise, keeping the mind calm and the emotions settled rather than scattered, matching a season whose defining image is falling leaves drawing inward and downward rather than a spring shoot reaching out.

秋天在五行框架裡對應到金與肺，標記著從一年前半段向外的方向，轉向後半段向內的方向。春天鼓勵生長，夏天鼓勵完全的擴張，秋天的經典建議則是關於內收——《黃帝內經》建議早一點睡、跟著日出起床，讓心保持平靜、情緒保持安定，而不是渙散，對應著這個定義性的意象是落葉向內、向下收攏，而不是春天嫩芽向外伸展的季節。

Autumn's defining physical challenge, in this framework, is dryness — the season's dominant excess from an earlier unit — and it's matched specifically to the lung, an organ this course has already connected to skin health and the body's outer defenses. Dry air is traditionally thought to injure the lung's own moisture first, showing up as a dry cough, dry skin, and a dry nose or throat that arrives with the season rather than from any illness. The classic response is to eat moistening foods and avoid activities, like excessive sweating, that dry the body further at exactly the time of year it can least afford it.

在這個框架裡，秋天定義性的身體挑戰是燥——這個單元稍早提過、這個季節主導的病邪——它特別對應到肺，這門課已經把肺跟皮膚健康、身體的外部防禦連結起來過。乾燥的空氣傳統上被認為會先傷害肺自己的濕潤，表現成隨著這個季節而來、不是因為任何疾病造成的乾咳、皮膚乾燥，還有鼻子或喉嚨的乾燥。經典的應對方式，是吃滋潤的食物，並避免像過度流汗這種在一年之中身體最負擔不起的時候，讓身體更乾燥的活動。

The emotion matched to autumn is grief, matched to the lung by the same logic covered in the organs unit, and the seasonal advice treats this pairing as reason for gentleness rather than alarm — a normal autumn tendency toward quiet reflection, even mild melancholy, isn't necessarily a problem to fix, but a pattern this framework expects and makes room for, the same way it expects spring's outward energy and winter's quiet. Reading a season's mood as part of its physiology, rather than as separate from it, is the through-line this entire unit has been building toward.

對應到秋天的情緒是悲，透過臟腑單元講過的同一套邏輯對應到肺，而季節性的建議把這個配對當成溫柔以待、而不是驚慌的理由——秋天一般傾向安靜的沉思，甚至輕微的憂鬱，不必然是一個要修正的問題，而是這個框架預期、並且留出空間容納的一個模式，就像它預期春天向外的活力、冬天的安靜一樣。把一個季節的心情讀成它生理狀態的一部分，而不是跟它分開的東西，是這整個單元一直在建構的那條主線。

Questions · 閱讀理解

According to the passage, what is autumn's classic advice, and how does it differ from spring's?

Autumn's advice is about gathering in — going to bed earlier, rising with the sunrise, keeping the mind calm and emotions settled — in contrast to spring's advice to encourage outward growth and activity.

According to the passage, why is dryness matched specifically to the lung in autumn?

Because dry air is traditionally thought to injure the lung's own moisture first, and the lung is already connected to skin health and the body's outer defenses.

According to the last paragraph, how does the passage suggest treating a normal autumn tendency toward quiet reflection or mild melancholy?

As a normal, expected pattern this framework makes room for, not necessarily a problem to fix — the same way it expects spring's outward energy and winter's quiet.
