Unit 2: The Five Jobs of Qi

Chinese medicine describes qi as doing five jobs in the body. It moves things along — pushing blood through the vessels, fluids through their channels, and the organs through their daily work. It keeps the body warm, which is why healthy hands and feet stay comfortably warm rather than cold. It defends the body against outside threats, which is why some people catch a cold at the first change of season while others seem to shrug it off. It holds things in their place, keeping blood inside the vessels and sweat from leaking out for no reason. And it transforms what a person eats and drinks into something the body can actually use.

中醫講氣在身體裡做五件事：推動——把血液推過血管、把水液推過通道、把臟腑推動去做每天的工作；溫煦——維持體溫，這是為什麼健康的手腳是舒服地溫暖，而不是冰冷；防禦——抵擋外來的威脅，這是為什麼有人一換季就感冒，有人卻好像完全不受影響；固攝——把東西固定在該在的位置，讓血液留在血管裡、汗不會無緣無故流出來；還有氣化——把吃進去、喝進去的東西轉變成身體真正能用的東西。

These five jobs are not five separate topics to memorize. They are five expressions of the same underlying activity. Once you understand them as one thing seen from five angles, the symptoms of weak qi stop looking like a random list and start looking like a single pattern, read backward. If qi cannot move things along, a person feels tired and doesn't want to talk. If it cannot keep the body warm, the hands and feet turn cold. If it cannot defend the body, colds become frequent. If it cannot hold things in place, a person may sweat without any exertion at all. If it cannot transform food into something usable, appetite drops even though the person is eating normally.

這五件事不是五個要分別背下來的主題，而是同一個底層活動從五個角度看出來的樣子。一旦你把它們理解成一件事，氣虛的症狀就不再像是一份隨機的清單，而是同一個模式倒過來讀。推不動，人就會累、不想講話；溫不了，手腳就會冰冷；防不住，就常常感冒；固不住，可能無緣無故就流汗；化不了，就算正常吃飯，食慾也會下降。

This is the real difference between understanding a principle and memorizing a checklist. A checklist of five symptoms is easy to forget. A principle that explains why those five symptoms show up together — and predicts a sixth one you haven't seen yet — is much harder to lose.

這正是「理解一個原理」跟「背一份檢查清單」的真正差別。五個症狀的清單很容易忘記；一個能解釋這五個症狀為什麼會一起出現——還能預測你還沒看過的第六個症狀——的原理，卻很難丟掉。

Questions · 閱讀理解

List the five jobs of qi described in the passage.

Moving things along, keeping the body warm, defending against outside threats, holding things in place, and transforming food and drink into something usable.

Why does the passage say these five jobs are "not five separate topics to memorize"?

Because they are five expressions of one underlying activity — once qi is weak, all five show up as one connected pattern, not five random, unrelated problems.

According to the passage, what happens to a person's appetite when qi cannot transform food properly, and why?

Appetite drops, even though the person may be eating a normal amount, because the body isn't turning food into something it can actually use.
