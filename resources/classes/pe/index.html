Sports and Movement · 體育

Sports and Movement 體育

Why does a wide stance keep you on your feet? Why does a spinning ball curve? This series looks at how bodies and balls move, how games are played, and how to stay safe, with models you can try yourself.
為什麼腳張開就站得穩？旋轉的球為什麼會轉彎？這個系列帶你看身體和球怎麼動、各種運動怎麼玩，以及怎麼保護自己；每一課都有可以自己動手試的模型。

In Taiwan's curriculum, this learning area is called Health and Physical Education. This series covers the physical education half: movement, ball games, safety, and the stories of sport. The inside of the body, such as muscles, the heart, and the lungs, is covered in our series The Human Body, and each lesson here links to it instead of repeating it.
在台灣的課綱裡，這個領域的名稱是「健康與體育」。這個系列講的是其中「體育」的部分：動作、球類運動、安全，以及運動的故事。身體裡面的事（肌肉、心臟、肺）在「人體探索」系列已經講過，這裡每一課都會連過去，不再重講。

PE is one of the subjects most often taught in two languages, because the teacher gives short commands all the time. So every lesson has a card called Classroom English for PE: eight things a teacher really says, each with a speaker button.
體育是最常拿來做雙語教學的科目之一，因為老師上課時本來就一直在下簡短的指令。所以每一課都有一張「體育課的課室英語」卡：八句老師真的會說的話，每句都可以按喇叭聽。

Every body is different, and no lesson here ranks anyone. Each activity comes with the space it needs, the times when you should sit it out, and another way to do it, such as holding a wall or sitting down. Nothing in this series is medical advice.
每個人的身體都不一樣，這裡沒有任何一課在比誰強誰弱。每個活動都會寫清楚需要的場地、哪些情況先不要做，以及「換個做法也可以」的版本，例如扶著牆做、坐著做。本系列的內容都不是醫療建議。

1 of 24 lessons is ready so far; more are on the way.
目前完成 1 課（共規劃 24 課），持續增加中。
