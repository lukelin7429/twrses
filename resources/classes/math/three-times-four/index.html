Math You Can See · Unit 1 · Lesson 2 · 看得見的數學 單元一 第二課

Why Is 3 × 4 the Same as 4 × 3?乘法為什麼可以交換？

Set cubes on a tray in 3 rows of 4, count across, count down, and then turn the whole tray. Nothing is added and nothing is taken away, so 3 × 4 and 4 × 3 must be the same. Then fold a multiplication table in half and see how much less there is to learn.
在盤子上把小方塊排成 3 排、每排 4 個，橫著數、直著數，再把整盤轉九十度。沒有多、也沒有少，所以 3 × 4 和 4 × 3 一定一樣。接著把乘法表對摺，看看要記的少了多少。

Big idea · 一句話看懂

Things set out in equal rows can be counted row by row or column by column. Both ways count every piece exactly once, so both give the same total. That is why the order of the two numbers in a multiplication does not change the answer.
排成一排一排、每排一樣多的東西，可以一排一排數，也可以一行一行數。兩種數法都把每一個東西剛好數到一次，所以數出來一樣多。這就是為什麼乘法的兩個數換個順序，答案不會變。

Unit 2: Why Is 3 × 4 the Same as 4 × 3?

Put twelve small cubes on a tray in neat rows: three rows, with four cubes in each row. Now count them. You could count one row at a time: four, eight, twelve. Your friend is sitting at the side of the table, and from there the tray looks different. Your friend sees four rows, with three cubes in each, and counts three, six, nine, twelve. You counted three fours. Your friend counted four threes. You both got twelve. Is that just luck?

在盤子上把十二個小方塊排整齊：三排，每排四個。現在數數看。你可以一排一排數：四、八、十二。你的朋友坐在桌子的側邊，從那邊看，盤子的樣子不一樣：他看到的是四排、每排三個，所以他數的是三、六、九、十二。你數的是三個四，他數的是四個三，兩個人都數到十二。這只是碰巧嗎？

It is not luck, and you can see why without doing any arithmetic. Each of you counted every cube on the tray exactly once. Nobody added a cube, and nobody took one away. The cubes did not care which way you were facing. In fact, you can skip the second chair. Just turn the tray a quarter of the way around. Three rows of four become four rows of three, and the pile is still the same pile.

不是碰巧，而且不用算就看得出為什麼。你們兩個人都把盤子上的每一個方塊剛好數到一次。沒有人多放一個，也沒有人拿走一個。方塊才不管你面向哪一邊。其實連第二張椅子都可以省了：把盤子轉九十度就好。三排、每排四個，變成四排、每排三個，而那一堆還是原來那一堆。

Counting equal groups like this is what multiplication is for. We write 3 × 4 and read it “three times four.” The numbers we multiply are called factors, and the answer is called the product. Things set out in rows and columns, like the cubes on the tray, make an array. Because an array can be counted by its rows or by its columns, 3 × 4 and 4 × 3 have the same product. Changing the order of the factors does not change the product. Mathematicians call this the commutative property of multiplication.

像這樣數「每一組一樣多」的東西，就是乘法在做的事。我們寫成 3 × 4，英文唸 three times four。相乘的數英文叫 factor，中文是被乘數和乘數；乘出來的答案叫做積（product）。像盤子上的方塊這樣排成幾排幾行的東西，叫做陣列（array）。因為一個陣列可以一排一排數，也可以一行一行數，所以 3 × 4 和 4 × 3 的積一樣。兩個數相乘，順序換過來，積不會變。數學家把這件事叫做乘法交換律。

This one idea saves a great deal of work. A multiplication table from 1 × 1 to 10 × 10 has one hundred squares. But every square off the diagonal has a twin: 7 × 8 and 8 × 7 are both 56. Fold the table along the diagonal, and each square lands on its twin. That leaves only fifty-five different facts to learn instead of one hundred. Learn one, and you know two.

這一個想法可以省下很多力氣。從 1 × 1 到 10 × 10 的乘法表有一百格。可是對角線以外的每一格都有一個雙胞胎：7 × 8 和 8 × 7 都是 56。沿著對角線把表對摺，每一格都會疊在它的雙胞胎上。這樣真正不一樣的只剩五十五格，而不是一百格。記住一個，兩個都會了。

The same picture explains area. Push the cubes together until they touch, and they make a rectangle four units long and three units wide. Its area is twelve square units, because twelve unit squares cover it. Turn the rectangle, and it is three units long and four units wide. It is the same rectangle, so it has the same area. Finding the area of a rectangle and counting an array are the same job.

同一張圖也能說明面積。把方塊推在一起、互相貼緊，它們就排成一個長四個單位、寬三個單位的長方形。它的面積是十二個平方單位，因為剛好用十二個單位正方形鋪滿。把長方形轉個方向，它變成長三個單位、寬四個單位；但它還是同一個長方形，所以面積一樣。算長方形的面積和數一個陣列，是同一件事。

Be careful, though. Not every operation lets you swap. Addition does: 3 + 4 and 4 + 3 are both 7. Subtraction and division do not: 7 − 3 is 4, but 3 − 7 is not, and 12 ÷ 4 is 3, but 4 ÷ 12 is not. Order matters in daily life, too. Putting on your socks and then your shoes is not the same as shoes and then socks! One more thing: classrooms in different countries write the same tray in different orders. In the end both orders give twelve, and now you know why.

不過要小心，不是每一種運算都可以交換。加法可以：3 + 4 和 4 + 3 都是 7。減法和除法不行：7 − 3 是 4，3 − 7 卻不是；12 ÷ 4 是 3，4 ÷ 12 卻不是。日常生活裡順序也常常有差：先穿襪子再穿鞋子，和先穿鞋子再穿襪子可不一樣！還有一件事：不同國家的教室，會用不同的順序來寫同一盤方塊。最後兩種寫法算出來都是十二，而現在你知道為什麼了。

Questions · 閱讀理解

You see 3 rows of 4, and your friend sees 4 rows of 3. Why do you both count twelve?

Because it is the same tray. Each of us counts every cube exactly once, and no cube is added or taken away, so the totals must be the same.

What does the commutative property of multiplication say?

Changing the order of the factors does not change the product. For example, 3 × 4 and 4 × 3 are both 12.

Name one operation where the order does matter, and give an example.

Subtraction or division. For example, 7 − 3 is 4 but 3 − 7 is not, and 12 ÷ 4 is 3 but 4 ÷ 12 is not.

Euclid · 歐幾里得

It is in the Elements《幾何原本》裡就有

Euclid's Elements, a mathematics book from ancient Greece, states it as Proposition 16 of Book VII: “If two numbers multiplied by one another make certain numbers, then the numbers so produced equal one another.” In today's words: A times B equals B times A. Euclid did not leave it as something obvious. He gave a proof.
古希臘數學家歐幾里得的《幾何原本》，在第七卷命題十六就寫了這件事：「如果兩個數互相乘，得到某些數，那麼這樣得到的數彼此相等。」用今天的話說，就是 A 乘 B 等於 B 乘 A。歐幾里得沒有把它當成理所當然，他給了證明。

The Sign · 符號

Where did × come from?× 是哪裡來的？

The cross for multiplication was used by the English mathematician William Oughtred in his book Clavis Mathematicae, published in London in 1631. It had appeared a little earlier, in 1618, in an unsigned appendix that is believed to be his as well. Not everyone liked it. In 1698 Gottfried Wilhelm Leibniz wrote in a letter to John Bernoulli, “I do not like X as a symbol for multiplication, as it is easily confounded with x.” He preferred a dot. That is why, once letters arrive in algebra, you will see a · b or just ab.
用叉來表示乘法，見於英國數學家奧特雷德（William Oughtred）1631 年在倫敦出版的《數學之鑰》。更早一點，1618 年一篇沒有署名的附錄裡就出現過，一般認為那也是他寫的。不是人人都喜歡這個符號。1698 年，萊布尼茲在給約翰・白努利的信裡說：「我不喜歡用 X 當乘號，因為它很容易和 x 弄混。」他比較喜歡用一個點。所以等代數裡出現字母以後，你會看到 a · b，或乾脆寫成 ab。

Nine Nines · 九九

A times table on bamboo寫在竹簡上的乘法表

In 2014 the journal Nature reported on twenty-one bamboo strips kept at Tsinghua University in Beijing. Dated to around 305 BC, they form what the report calls the world's oldest decimal multiplication table. Other old Chinese tables, from the Qin dynasty, were written as short sentences such as “six eights beget forty-eight.” The table is still recited in Chinese in the same short way: just the two numbers and the answer. The table from one to nine is called the jiu-jiu table, the “nine-nine” table.
2014 年，《自然》期刊報導了北京清華大學收藏的二十一支竹簡。它們的年代大約是西元前 305 年，報導說這是世界上最古老的十進位乘法表。另外一些秦代的乘法表，則是寫成一句一句的短句，例如「六八四十八」。到現在，中文背乘法表還是這種短短的句子：只唸兩個數和答案。從一到九的乘法表，就叫做九九乘法表。

A Long Word · 一個很長的字

You do not have to say “commutative”不一定要會說「交換律」

The earliest known use of the word commutative for a rule like this is from 1814, in French, by François Joseph Servois. It is a grown-up word, and both countries' guidelines say so. The U.S. Common Core standards add a note to Grade 3: “Students need not use formal terms for these properties.” Taiwan's guidelines for Grade 2 say that lessons should not use the term at all. The idea matters. The name can wait.
commutative（可交換的）這個字拿來稱呼這一類規則，目前查得到最早的是 1814 年塞爾瓦（François Joseph Servois）用法文寫的一篇文章。這是大人用的字，兩邊的課程文件也都這麼說。美國共同核心課程標準在三年級加了一條註：「學生不需要使用這些性質的正式名稱。」台灣的課綱在二年級寫的是：「教學不出現『乘法交換律』一詞。」重要的是想法，名字可以晚一點再學。

Myth vs. Fact · 常見迷思

Four things people get wrong 四個常見的誤會

✗ Myth · 迷思3 × 4 and 4 × 3 look different, so they must be different amounts.3 × 4 和 4 × 3 寫起來不一樣，所以一定不一樣多。

✓ Fact · 事實They are two ways of counting the same tray. Three rows of four and four rows of three both hold 12.它們是同一盤東西的兩種數法。三排每排四個、四排每排三個，都是 12 個。

✗ Myth · 迷思If the totals are equal, the two stories are the same.總數一樣，兩件事情就完全一樣。

✓ Fact · 事實Three bags of four apples and four bags of three apples both hold 12 apples, but they are different pictures. The total is the same; the number of bags is not.三袋、每袋四顆蘋果，和四袋、每袋三顆蘋果，都是 12 顆，但畫出來的圖不一樣。總數一樣，袋子的數目卻不一樣。

✗ Myth · 迷思You can swap the numbers in any operation.不管哪一種運算，兩個數都可以交換。

✓ Fact · 事實Addition and multiplication are commutative. Subtraction and division are not: 12 ÷ 4 is 3, but 4 ÷ 12 is not.加法和乘法可以交換，減法和除法不行：12 ÷ 4 是 3，4 ÷ 12 卻不是。

✗ Myth · 迷思To know the times table, you must memorize 100 separate facts.要學會乘法表，得把 100 格一格一格背起來。

✓ Fact · 事實Because of the twins, a 10 × 10 table holds only 55 different facts. Understanding the table first leaves much less to remember.因為每一格都有雙胞胎，10 × 10 的表其實只有 55 個不一樣的乘法。先弄懂這張表，要記的就少得多。

Remember It · 記憶口訣

The commutative property in a sentence 一句話記住交換律

Turn it and count again轉一下，再數一次

Not sure about 4 × 3? Picture 3 rows of 4 and turn the tray.
不確定 4 × 3？想像 3 排、每排 4 個，把盤子轉過來。

Swap the factors, keep the product兩個數對調，積不變

a × b = b × a, every time.
a × b = b × a，每一次都成立。

Learn one, know two記一個，會兩個

7 × 8 = 56 gives you 8 × 7 = 56 for free.
會了 7 × 8 = 56，8 × 7 = 56 就是送的。

Minus and divide do not swap減法、除法不能換

7 − 3 and 3 − 7 are not the same. Neither are 12 ÷ 4 and 4 ÷ 12.
7 − 3 和 3 − 7 不一樣；12 ÷ 4 和 4 ÷ 12 也不一樣。

For Teachers · 給老師的提醒

Where Taiwan and the United States write it differently 台灣和美國寫法不一樣的地方

  • Which number comes first? In U.S. materials the first factor usually counts the groups. The Common Core reads 5 × 7 as “5 groups of 7 objects each,” and Encyclopaedia Britannica writes 5 + 5 + 5 as 3 × 5, calling 5 the multiplicand and 3 the multiplier. In Taiwan the first number is the multiplicand: the Ministry of Education's dictionary says that when 3 is multiplied by 2, 3 is the beichengshu. So three rabbits with four legs each are 3 × 4 in an American classroom and 4 × 3 in a Taiwanese one, as a 2024 thesis at National Chengchi University describes. Tell students which convention your class is using before you mark an order wrong.哪一個數寫在前面？美國的教材通常把「有幾組」寫在前面：共同核心課程標準把 5 × 7 讀成「5 組、每組 7 個」；《大英百科》把 5＋5＋5 寫成 3 × 5，並說 5 是被乘數、3 是乘數。台灣則是被乘數在前：教育部《重編國語辭典》說「3 乘以 2 等於 6，則 3 為被乘數」。所以「三隻兔子，每隻四隻腳」在美國教室寫成 3 × 4，在台灣教室寫成 4 × 3——政治大學 2024 年的一篇碩士論文就是這樣描述的。要把順序算錯之前，請先告訴學生你的班上用的是哪一種約定。
  • Taiwan's curriculum guidelines themselves do not fix an order. What they do say, in N-2-6, is that students should gradually build the language of bei (“times as many”) and, in the end, use a rows-and-columns model to understand that the order can be swapped. Illustrative Mathematics, a widely used U.S. curriculum, shows a 3 by 4 array with both equations, 3 × 4 = 12 and 4 × 3 = 12.台灣的課綱本身並沒有規定順序。它在 N-2-6 寫的是：逐步發展「倍」的概念，做為統整乘法應用情境的語言，最後能以行列模型理解乘法交換律。美國通行的教材 Illustrative Mathematics 則是在一個 3 by 4 的陣列旁邊，把 3 × 4 = 12 和 4 × 3 = 12 兩條算式一起寫出來。
  • Rows and columns are a trap between Taiwan and mainland China. In Taiwan's official terminology a row (across) is lie and a column (down) is hang; the same database lists hang as the mainland term for row. To stay clear of the mix-up, this page says hengpai (across) and zhipai (down).「列」和「行」在兩岸剛好相反。國教院樂詞網的數學名詞：row 是「列」（橫的），column 是「行」（直的）；同一個資料庫也註明 row 的中國大陸譯名是「行」。為了不混淆，這一頁對學生一律說「橫排」和「直排」。
  • Where this lesson sits in Taiwan's curriculum: N-2-6 (the meaning of multiplication), R-2-3 (“the order of two numbers being multiplied does not affect the product,” with the advice to teach it late in Grade 2, with a rows-and-columns model, and without using the term), and N-2-7 (the ten-by-ten table, with the note that students must not be made to memorize it by rote). The rectangle area formula comes later, in S-4-3 (Grade 4).這一課在課綱的位置：N-2-6（乘法的意義與應用）、R-2-3（「兩數相乘的順序不影響其積：乘法交換律」；備註寫明不宜太早教學，建議在 2 年級後期，以行列模型教學，教學不出現「乘法交換律」一詞），以及 N-2-7（十十乘法；備註：不可要求學生死背乘法表）。長方形的面積公式要到 S-4-3（四年級）。

Classroom Activity 1 · 課堂活動一

Turn the Paper 把紙轉過來

You need · 準備材料

  • Counters, beans, or buttons: about 30 for each pair花片、豆子或鈕扣：每兩人約 30 個
  • A sheet of paper for each pair, to use as a tray每兩人一張紙，當作盤子
  • Pencil and a small notebook鉛筆和小本子

Steps · 步驟

  1. Partners sit at two sides of a desk, so that one sees the paper from the front and the other from the side.兩個人坐在桌子相鄰的兩邊：一個從正面看這張紙，一個從側面看。
  2. Lay out 3 rows of 4 counters on the paper. Each partner counts the rows as he or she sees them and writes an addition sentence: 4 + 4 + 4 or 3 + 3 + 3 + 3.在紙上排出 3 排、每排 4 個花片。兩個人各自照自己看到的「一排一排」數，寫下連加的算式：4＋4＋4，或 3＋3＋3＋3。
  3. Compare the totals. Then turn the paper a quarter turn without touching the counters, and count again.比一比兩個人的總數。然後不碰花片，把紙轉九十度，再數一次。
  4. Try other trays: 2 rows of 5, 4 rows of 6, 5 rows of 5. Ask: “Did turning the paper ever change the total? Could it?”換別的排法：2 排每排 5 個、4 排每排 6 個、5 排每排 5 個。問：「把紙轉過來，總數有沒有變過？有可能變嗎？」

Let students write addition sentences first; Taiwan's guidelines say that repeated addition may be used naturally while solving multiplication problems (N-2-6). This activity is the “rows-and-columns model” that R-2-3 recommends for late Grade 2. In the Common Core it matches 2.OA.C.4 (arrays with up to 5 rows and 5 columns) and 3.OA.B.5.
先讓學生寫連加的算式就好；課綱 N-2-6 的備註說，可在乘法解題脈絡中自然使用連加算式。這個活動就是 R-2-3 建議在二年級後期使用的「行列模型」。對照美國共同核心課程標準，是 2.OA.C.4（最多 5 列 5 行的陣列）與 3.OA.B.5。

Classroom Activity 2 · 課堂活動二

Fold the Times Table 對摺乘法表

You need · 準備材料

  • A printed 10 × 10 multiplication table on square paper for each student每人一張印在正方形紙上的 10 × 10 乘法表
  • Two colored pencils兩枝不同顏色的色鉛筆

Steps · 步驟

  1. Color the diagonal squares (1 × 1, 2 × 2, up to 10 × 10) with one color.用一種顏色把對角線上的格子（1 × 1、2 × 2……到 10 × 10）塗起來。
  2. Pick any other square, such as 3 × 7. Find the square with the same number on the other side of the diagonal and color both with the second color.任選一格不在對角線上的，例如 3 × 7。在對角線另一邊找到數字一樣的那一格，兩格都用第二種顏色塗起來。
  3. Fold the paper along the diagonal and hold it up to the light. Check that every square sits on a square with the same number.沿著對角線把紙對摺，拿起來對著光看：是不是每一格都疊在數字一樣的格子上？
  4. Count the squares on one side of the fold, including the diagonal. How many facts are there really?數一數摺線一邊的格子（包括對角線）。真正不一樣的乘法有幾個？

The count is 55: 10 on the diagonal plus 45 pairs. A teacher note in TERC's Investigations curriculum goes one step further: once the ×1, ×2, ×5, and ×10 facts are known, there are 21 facts left to learn, assuming students use the commutative property. The point is not speed. It is that understanding the table shrinks it.
答案是 55：對角線上 10 個，加上 45 對。美國 TERC 的 Investigations 教材有一則教師說明算得更細：學會乘以 1、2、5、10 之後，只剩 21 個要學（前提是學生會用交換律）。重點不是背得快，而是「弄懂這張表，它就變小了」。
