What Does Division Really Mean?除法到底是什麼意思？

Share candies equally among friends, or pack them into bags of the same size. Two different questions, one division sign. Watch what is left over, and see why it is always smaller than the number you divide by.
把糖平分給朋友，或是每幾顆裝成一袋。兩種不一樣的問題，用的是同一個除號。看看最後剩下了什麼，以及為什麼剩下的一定比除數小。

Big idea · 一句話看懂

Division answers two different questions. Sharing equally asks how many each group gets when you know how many groups there are. Making groups asks how many groups you can make when you know how big each group is. Both are written the same way, and anything left over, the remainder, is always less than the divisor.
除法回答兩種不一樣的問題。平分：知道有幾組，問每組分到幾個。分裝：知道每組幾個，問可以分成幾組。兩種寫成同一個算式；分不完剩下的叫做餘數，而且一定比除數小。

Unit 3: What Does Division Really Mean?

Here are two questions about the same twelve candies. First: you share the twelve candies equally among three friends. How many does each friend get? Second: you pack the twelve candies into bags, with three candies in each bag. How many bags do you fill? Both questions use the numbers 12 and 3, and both answers are 4. But they are not the same question, and if you act them out, your hands do very different things.

這裡有兩個關於同樣十二顆糖的問題。第一個：把十二顆糖平分給三個朋友，每個朋友分到幾顆？第二個：把十二顆糖裝袋，每袋裝三顆，可以裝滿幾袋？兩個問題用的都是 12 和 3，答案也都是 4。可是它們不是同一個問題；如果真的動手做做看，你的手做的事完全不一樣。

To answer the first question, you deal the candies out, one to each friend, around and around, like cards. You already know how many groups there are: three friends. You are finding out how big each group gets. American teachers often call this fair sharing. Taiwan's curriculum calls it pingfen, sharing equally. Dealing one at a time is a simple way to make sure that nobody gets more than anyone else.

要回答第一個問題，你會像發牌一樣，一人一顆、一輪一輪地把糖發出去。你已經知道有幾組了：三個朋友。你要找的是每一組會有多少。美國老師常把這叫做 fair sharing（公平地分）；台灣的課綱叫它「平分」。一次發一顆，是確保沒有人比別人多的簡單方法。

To answer the second question, you fill one bag with three candies, then a second bag, then a third, until you run out. This time you know how big each group is: three candies. You are finding out how many groups you can make. Even so, both questions are written the same way: 12 ÷ 3 = 4, read “twelve divided by three equals four.” The number being divided, 12, is the dividend. The number you divide by, 3, is the divisor. The answer, 4, is the quotient.

要回答第二個問題，你會先把一個袋子裝三顆，再裝第二袋、第三袋，一直裝到糖用完為止。這一次你知道每一組有多大：三顆。你要找的是可以分成幾組。即使如此，兩個問題的寫法完全一樣：12 ÷ 3 = 4，英文唸 twelve divided by three equals four。被拿來除的 12 叫做被除數，拿去除的 3 叫做除數，答案 4 叫做商。

Now try thirteen candies. Deal them to three friends, and each gets four, but one candy is left on the table. There is not enough for another round. The leftover is called the remainder. An American classroom writes 13 ÷ 3 = 4 R 1, where R stands for remainder. In Chinese you say the quotient is 4 and the remainder is 1. The remainder is always smaller than the divisor. If three candies were left, there would be enough for one more round.

現在換成十三顆糖。發給三個朋友，每人四顆，可是桌上還剩一顆，不夠再發一輪了。剩下的叫做餘數。美國的教室寫成 13 ÷ 3 = 4 R 1，R 代表 remainder（餘數）。中文則說：商是 4，餘數是 1。餘數永遠比除數小——如果剩下三顆，就夠再發一輪了。

Division and multiplication are partners. When the dealing is done, line up each plate's candies in a row. Three plates of four make three rows of four, which is the tray from the last lesson: 3 × 4 = 12. So 12 ÷ 3 = 4, because 3 × 4 = 12. This is also how to check an answer. Multiply the divisor by the quotient, add the remainder, and you should get back the number you started with: 3 × 4 + 1 = 13.

除法和乘法是一對搭檔。分完以後，把每個盤子裡的糖排成一排。三盤、每盤四顆，就成了三排、每排四顆——正是上一課的那一盤：3 × 4 = 12。所以 12 ÷ 3 = 4，因為 3 × 4 = 12。這也是驗算的方法：除數乘以商，再加上餘數，應該要回到原來的數：3 × 4 + 1 = 13。

One number needs special care: zero. If you have no candies, everyone gets zero, so 0 ÷ 3 = 0. But you cannot divide by zero. Try sharing twelve candies among zero people, or packing them into bags that hold zero candies each. You could keep filling empty bags forever and never use up a single candy. Mathematicians say that dividing by zero is undefined: it has no answer at all.

有一個數要特別小心：零。如果一顆糖也沒有，每個人都分到零顆，所以 0 ÷ 3 = 0。可是你不能除以零。試試看把十二顆糖分給零個人，或是裝進每袋裝零顆的袋子裡：你可以一直裝空袋子裝到永遠，一顆糖都用不掉。數學家說，除以零是「沒有定義」的：它根本沒有答案。
Read aloud · 課文朗讀

Questions · 閱讀理解

What is the difference between sharing equally and making groups?

When you share equally, you know how many groups there are and find how many go in each. When you make groups, you know how many go in each group and find how many groups there are. Both are division.

Why must the remainder always be smaller than the divisor?

If the leftover were as big as the divisor, there would be enough for one more round or one more group, so you would keep dividing.

How can you check that 13 ÷ 3 = 4 R 1 is right?

Multiply the divisor by the quotient and add the remainder: 3 × 4 + 1 = 13, which is the number you started with.

Stories Behind the Division Sign · 除號的故事

A sign that used to mean minus, a colon, a bracket with no name, and two long words 原本是減號的符號、一個冒號、一個沒有名字的括號、兩個很長的字

Four short stories about how people have written and talked about division.
關於人們怎麼寫除法、怎麼說除法的四個小故事。

The Sign · 除號

÷ used to mean minus÷ 原本是減號

The sign ÷ was first used for division by the mathematician Johann Rahn, in his book Teutsche Algebra in 1659. The mark itself was older. According to Jeff Miller's history of mathematical symbols, it “was used by many writers before Rahn as a minus sign.” Rahn's book was translated into English in 1668, and the new division sign came along with it.
÷ 第一次被拿來當除號，是數學家拉恩（Johann Rahn）在 1659 年出版的《德文代數》裡。這個記號本身更古老：根據傑夫・米勒整理的數學符號史，在拉恩之前，「有很多作者把它當成減號」。拉恩的書在 1668 年譯成英文，新的除號也跟著傳了過去。

The Colon · 冒號

Leibniz's two dots萊布尼茲的兩個點

Not everyone adopted ÷. In 1684 the mathematician Gottfried Wilhelm Leibniz used a colon, “:”, for both ratio and division. You may remember from Lesson 2 that Leibniz also disliked the × sign and used a dot instead. He liked his symbols small.
不是每個人都改用 ÷。1684 年，數學家萊布尼茲用冒號「:」同時表示比和除法。你可能還記得第二課：萊布尼茲也不喜歡 ×，改用一個點。他喜歡小小的符號。

Long Division · 直式除法

A bracket with no name一個沒有名字的括號

In an American classroom, long division is written with the divisor outside a bracket and the quotient on top. OpenStax notes that “in the past you may have used the notation 4)12.” Nineteenth-century U.S. textbooks wrote the divisor, dividend, and quotient on one line, as 36)116(3. And the bracket itself? Jeff Miller's history says simply: “The symbol does not have a name.”
在美國的教室，直式除法把除數寫在一個括號外面，商寫在上面。OpenStax 教科書提到：「你以前可能用過 4)12 這種寫法。」十九世紀美國的課本則把除數、被除數和商寫在同一行，像 36)116(3。至於那個括號本身呢？傑夫・米勒的符號史只說了一句：「這個符號沒有名字。」

Two Long Words · 兩個長字

Partitive and quotative等分除與包含除

Teachers have names for the two kinds of division. A teaching guide from the Utah State Board of Education calls them partitive (fair sharing) and quotative (measurement), and adds that students “are not expected to know or produce the terms.” Taiwan's curriculum calls them dengfen-chu and baohan-chu, but in Grade 2 it talks only about sharing and packing, without writing a division sign at all.
老師們替這兩種除法取了名字。美國猶他州教育委員會的教學指引叫它們 partitive（公平地分）和 quotative（測量），並且補一句：學生「不需要知道或說出這些名詞」。台灣的課綱叫它們「等分除」和「包含除」；不過在二年級，課綱只談「分裝與平分」，連除式都不寫。

Myth vs. Fact · 常見迷思

Four things people get wrong 四個常見的誤會

✗ Myth · 迷思Sharing and making groups are two different kinds of math.平分和分裝是兩種不同的數學。

✓ Fact · 事實They ask different questions, but both are division and both are written the same way: 12 ÷ 3 = 4.它們問的問題不一樣，但都是除法，寫法也一樣：12 ÷ 3 = 4。

✗ Myth · 迷思Dividing always makes a number smaller.除完以後，數一定會變小。

✓ Fact · 事實Not always. 12 ÷ 1 = 12: sharing twelve candies with one person leaves that person with all twelve.不一定。12 ÷ 1 = 12：十二顆糖分給一個人，他就拿到全部十二顆。

✗ Myth · 迷思A remainder can be any size.餘數可以是任何大小。

✓ Fact · 事實A remainder is always less than the divisor. If you had that many left, you could share one more round.餘數一定比除數小。如果剩下那麼多，就還能再分一輪。

✗ Myth · 迷思Dividing by zero gives zero.除以零的答案是零。

✓ Fact · 事實Zero divided by a number is zero, but a number divided by zero is undefined. No answer works.零除以一個數是零；但一個數除以零是「沒有定義」的，找不到任何答案。

For Teachers · 給老師的提醒

Names, order, and notation in Taiwan and the United States 台灣和美國的名稱、順序與寫法

  • The two meanings go by different names. The Common Core's table of problem types calls them “Group Size Unknown (‘How many in each group?’ Division)” and “Number of Groups Unknown (‘How many groups?’ Division),” with the plum examples used in the game above. Taiwan's curriculum says pingfen and fenzhuang (sharing and packing) in Grade 2 (N-2-9) and names dengfen-chu and baohan-chu in a Grade 4 note (N-4-3).兩種意思在兩邊有不同的名字。美國共同核心課程標準的題型表叫它們「每組幾個未知（How many in each group?）」和「組數未知（How many groups?）」，上面小遊戲裡的李子題就是它的例子。台灣課綱在二年級（N-2-9）說「分裝與平分」，到四年級（N-4-3）的備註才寫出「等分除和包含除」。
  • Taiwan's order of teaching: in Grade 2, N-2-9 is “not division teaching”; it does not write division sentences or use the word chu, and it only uses problems that come out even. Grade 3 brings “the meaning and uses of division” (N-3-4), with the advice to handle problems that come out even first and remainders after. The same grade adds long division by a one-digit number (N-3-5) and multiplication and division as inverses (R-3-1).台灣的教學順序：二年級 N-2-9 的備註寫明「本條目非除法教學，不列除式，不用『除』的名稱」，而且限相當於整除的問題。三年級 N-3-4 才是「除法的意義與應用」，備註建議「先處理整除情境，再處理有餘數的情境」；同年級還有 N-3-5（除以一位數的直式計算）和 R-3-1（乘除互逆）。
  • Writing remainders. U.S. textbooks write 13 ÷ 3 = 4 R 1 (OpenStax: “The R stands for remainder”) and say “four with a remainder of one.” The Ministry of Education's dictionary describes the same thing in words: 45 divided by 6 gives a quotient of 7, and the remainder is 3. We did not find an official rule for how Taiwanese textbooks write a remainder inside an equation, so this page uses words in Chinese. Please follow the notation in your own textbook.餘數怎麼寫。美國課本寫成 13 ÷ 3 = 4 R 1（OpenStax：「R 代表 remainder」），唸 four with a remainder of one。教育部《重編國語辭典》用文字描述：「如45除以6得商數7，則以45減去7乘以6所得的結果3即為餘數。」我們沒有找到官方規定台灣課本在算式裡怎麼寫餘數，所以這一頁的中文一律用「商幾，餘幾」的說法。請照您所用課本的寫法教。
  • Long division sounds different in English. Students will hear “3 goes into 18 six times” (OpenStax) and see the divisor outside a bracket, a layout whose symbol “does not have a name” (Jeff Miller). Taiwan's long division by a one-digit number is a Grade 3 topic (N-3-5); its note asks teachers to include dividends that contain a 0.直式除法的英文說法不一樣。學生會聽到「3 goes into 18 six times」（OpenStax），看到除數寫在括號外面——傑夫・米勒說這個符號「沒有名字」。台灣的除以一位數直式計算在三年級（N-3-5），備註提醒「須處理被除數有 0 的題型」。

Classroom Activity 1 · 課堂活動一

Deal or Pack? 發牌還是裝袋？

You need · 準備材料

  • About 20 counters, beans, or small candies for each pair每兩人約 20 個花片、豆子或小糖果
  • 4 paper cups or small plates4 個紙杯或小盤子
  • Story cards, each with one division story故事卡，每張寫一個除法小故事

Steps · 步驟

  1. One partner reads a story card aloud. The other partner acts it out with the counters before anyone writes a number.一個人把故事卡唸出來，另一個人先用花片演出來，演完之前不寫任何數字。
  2. Decide together: did your hands deal the counters around (sharing equally), or fill one cup at a time (making groups)?一起決定：剛才你的手是一個一個輪流發（平分），還是一杯裝滿再裝下一杯（分裝）？
  3. Write the division sentence and the answer. If some counters are left, say how many and why they cannot be shared.寫下除法算式和答案。如果有剩下的花片，說說剩下幾個，以及為什麼分不下去。
  4. Swap roles. After four stories, put your cards in two piles, sharing and grouping, and compare piles with another pair.交換角色。做完四個故事以後，把卡片分成「平分」和「分裝」兩疊，再和別組比一比。

Acting out first is what Taiwan's Grade 2 item N-2-9 asks for: sharing and packing with hands-on activities, before any division sentence is written. The Utah guide adds that students should meet both kinds of problems without having to know their names. Include a story where the divisor is larger than the number of counters (3 counters among 4 people) and talk about why each person gets zero.
先演再寫，正是台灣課綱 N-2-9 的做法：以操作活動理解分裝與平分，先不列除式。猶他州的教學指引也說，學生應該兩種題型都碰到，但不必知道它們的名稱。可以加一個除數比花片還多的故事（3 個花片分給 4 個人），討論為什麼每人分到零個。

Classroom Activity 2 · 課堂活動二

Remainder Hunt 找餘數

You need · 準備材料

  • 20 counters for each group每組 20 個花片
  • A recording table with the numbers 1 to 20 down the side and “÷ 3,” “÷ 4,” and “÷ 5” across the top一張記錄表：左邊直寫 1 到 20，上面橫寫「÷ 3」「÷ 4」「÷ 5」

Steps · 步驟

  1. For each number from 1 to 20, make groups of 3 with that many counters and record what is left over.1 到 20 的每一個數，都拿那麼多個花片三個三個分組，記下剩下幾個。
  2. Do the same with groups of 4 and groups of 5.再用四個一組、五個一組各做一次。
  3. Color each remainder a different color and look down each column. What pattern do you see?把不同的餘數塗不同顏色，沿著每一直行往下看。你看到什麼規律？
  4. Ask: what is the biggest remainder you found when dividing by 4? Could it ever be 4 or more?問：除以 4 的時候，你找到最大的餘數是多少？它有可能是 4 或更大嗎？

Students should find that the remainders repeat 0, 1, 2 for groups of 3, and never reach the divisor. Taiwan's curriculum suggests handling even division first and remainders after (N-3-4), so this activity fits after students are comfortable sharing without leftovers. The “What Will the Remainder Be?” strip above shows the same pattern up to 30.
學生應該會發現：三個一組時，餘數 0、1、2 一直重複，永遠到不了除數。台灣課綱建議先處理整除、再處理有餘數的情境（N-3-4），所以這個活動適合放在學生熟悉「剛好分完」之後。上面「餘數會是多少？」的那一條，把同樣的規律畫到 30。
