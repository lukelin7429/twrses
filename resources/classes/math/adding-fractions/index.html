Why Can't You Just Add the Bottoms?分數相加，為什麼不能分母加分母？

One half plus one third is not two fifths, and you can see why: two fifths is smaller than one half. Cut two strips into pieces of the same size, and the answer counts itself.
二分之一加三分之一不是五分之二，而且一看就知道為什麼：五分之二比二分之一還小。把兩條切成一樣大的塊，答案自己就數出來了。

Big idea · 一句話看懂

The bottom number of a fraction names the size of the pieces, and the top number counts them. You can only count pieces together when they are the same size, so before adding, cut both fractions into equal pieces.
分母說的是每一塊有多大，分子是在數有幾塊。只有一樣大的塊才能數在一起，所以相加以前，要先把兩個分數切成一樣大的塊。

Unit 4: Why Can't You Just Add the Bottoms?

You eat one half of a pizza, and your friend eats one third of another pizza of the same size. How much pizza did you eat together? A quick answer is to add the tops and the bottoms: 1 + 1 = 2 and 2 + 3 = 5, so 1/2 + 1/3 = 2/5. It looks neat, but it cannot be right. Two fifths is less than one half. You added food and somehow ended up with less than you started with. Something has gone wrong, and this lesson finds out what.

你吃了一個披薩的二分之一，你的朋友吃了另一個一樣大的披薩的三分之一。你們一共吃了多少？很快的答法是分子加分子、分母加分母：1＋1＝2，2＋3＝5，所以 1/2＋1/3＝2/5。看起來很整齊，可是不可能是對的：五分之二比二分之一還少。你加了食物，結果反而比原來還少？一定哪裡出錯了，這一課就來找出是哪裡。

Start with what a fraction means. The bottom number, the denominator, tells how many equal parts the whole has been divided into. The top number, the numerator, tells how many of those parts you have. So 1/2 means one of two equal pieces, and 1/3 means one of three. In English you say the top number first, “one half,” “one third,” “three fourths.” In Chinese you say the bottom number first: er fen zhi yi, two parts, take one.

先從分數的意思說起。下面的數叫分母，告訴你整體被分成了幾個一樣大的部分；上面的數叫分子，告訴你拿了其中幾個。所以 1/2 是兩個一樣大的塊裡的一塊，1/3 是三塊裡的一塊。英文先唸上面的數：one half、one third、three fourths。中文先唸下面的數：二分之一，就是「分成兩份，取其中一份」。

Here is the problem. The denominator is not a count of things; it is the name of the piece. A half and a third are different sizes, the way a quarter and a dime are different coins. One quarter and one dime are two coins, but they are not two quarters. In the same way, one half and one third are two pieces, but they are not two fifths. You can only count pieces together when they are the same size.

問題就在這裡：分母不是在數有幾個東西，它是塊的名字。二分之一和三分之一的一塊大小不一樣，就像美國的 25 分錢硬幣（quarter）和 10 分錢硬幣（dime）是不一樣的錢。一枚 quarter 加一枚 dime 是兩枚硬幣，但不是兩枚 quarter。同樣地，一個二分之一加一個三分之一是兩塊，但不是五分之二。只有一樣大的塊，才能數在一起。

So make them the same size. Cut each half into three smaller pieces and each third into two. Now both strips are cut into sixths. One half is the same amount as three sixths, and one third is the same as two sixths. Fractions that have the same value are called equivalent fractions. Now the pieces match, and counting is easy: three sixths plus two sixths is five sixths. The smallest number of pieces that works for both, here six, is called the least common denominator.

那就把它們切成一樣大吧。把二分之一的那一塊切成三小塊，三分之一的那一塊切成兩小塊，兩條就都變成六等分了。二分之一和六分之三一樣多，三分之一和六分之二一樣多。大小一樣的分數叫做等值分數。現在塊的大小對得上了，數起來就簡單：六分之三加六分之二，是六分之五。兩條都能用的最少的切法（這裡是 6）叫做最小公分母。

There is a quick way to catch the mistake, even without cutting anything. When you add two amounts, the answer has to be bigger than each of them. One U.S. standard asks students to recognize that 2/5 + 1/2 = 3/7 must be wrong, because 3/7 is less than 1/2. Adding the tops and the bottoms always gives an answer that lands between the two fractions, never above them. If your sum is smaller than one of its parts, go back and cut again.

就算不切，也有一個很快的方法抓出錯誤：兩個量相加，答案一定比其中任何一個都大。美國有一條課程標準就要學生看出 2/5＋1/2＝3/7 一定是錯的，因為 3/7 比 1/2 還小。分子加分子、分母加分母的答案，永遠落在兩個分數中間，不會比較大的那個還大。如果你算出的和比其中一個加數還小，就回頭重新切。

When the pieces are already the same size, adding is simple. One fourth plus two fourths is three fourths: you just add the number of pieces, and the denominator stays the same, because the size of each piece does not change. Americans often call fourths “quarters,” so this also reads as one quarter plus two quarters equals three quarters. Sometimes the answer is more than a whole: three fourths plus one half is five fourths, which is one and one fourth. That is a mixed number, a whole number together with a fraction.

如果兩邊的塊本來就一樣大，加起來很簡單。四分之一加四分之二是四分之三：只要把塊數加起來，分母不變，因為每一塊的大小沒有變。美國人常把 fourths 叫做 quarters，所以也可以說 one quarter plus two quarters equals three quarters。有時候答案會比一整個還多：四分之三加二分之一是四分之五，也就是一又四分之一。這種寫成整數加分數的數，叫做帶分數。

Questions · 閱讀理解

Why is 2/5 not the answer to 1/2 + 1/3?

Because 2/5 is less than 1/2. When you add something, the answer must be bigger than each part, so 2/5 cannot be right.

What do the denominator and the numerator of a fraction tell you?

The denominator tells how many equal parts the whole is divided into. The numerator tells how many of those parts you have.

How do you add 1/2 + 1/3 correctly?

Cut both into sixths: 1/2 = 3/6 and 1/3 = 2/6. Then add the pieces: 3/6 + 2/6 = 5/6.

Stories Behind Fractions · 分數的故事

Egyptian pieces, a bar from the Arab world, rules from ancient China, and a coin 埃及的單位分數、阿拉伯世界的分數線、中國古代的規則、一枚硬幣

Four short stories about how people have written and added fractions.

Egypt · 埃及

Only one piece at a time一次只用一塊

The Rhind papyrus was written around 1650 BC by a scribe named Ahmes, who says he is copying a document 200 years older. According to the MacTutor history archive, Egyptian fractions were limited to unit fractions, fractions with 1 on top, with the exception of 2/3 and, less often, 3/4. To write two fifths, a scribe used a sum of unit fractions; the papyrus's table of doubles lists 1/3 + 1/15 for twice 1/5.
萊因德紙草書大約寫於西元前 1650 年，抄寫員阿默斯說他是在抄一份兩百年前的文件。根據 MacTutor 數學史資料庫，古埃及的分數只用單位分數（分子是 1 的分數），例外是 2/3，以及比較少用的 3/4。要寫五分之二，抄寫員會用幾個單位分數加起來；紙草書的「加倍表」裡，五分之一的兩倍寫成 1/3＋1/15。

The Bar · 分數線

Where the line in the middle came from中間那條線從哪裡來

Jeff Miller's history of mathematical symbols says that the horizontal fraction bar “was introduced by the Arabs,” and that several sources credit al-Hassar around 1200. Fibonacci was the first European mathematician to use the bar the way we do today. The slanted slash came much later, partly because a horizontal bar was hard to print.
傑夫・米勒的數學符號史說，橫的分數線是阿拉伯人引進的，有好幾個來源認為是十二、十三世紀之交的哈薩爾（al-Hassar）。斐波那契是第一個像我們今天這樣使用分數線的歐洲數學家。斜線寫法出現得晚很多，原因之一是橫線在印刷上很難排。

China · 中國

The Nine Chapters《九章算術》

The first chapter of the Chinese classic The Nine Chapters on the Mathematical Art gives, in the words of the MacTutor archive, “rules for the addition, subtraction, multiplication and division of fractions,” along with a method for finding the greatest common divisor, which is what you need to reduce a fraction. Most historians think the text began around 200 BC, although others place it between 100 BC and AD 50.
中國古書《九章算術》的第一章，用 MacTutor 資料庫的話說，給出了「分數加、減、乘、除的規則」，還有求最大公因數的方法——約分就要用到它。多數歷史學者認為這本書大約形成於西元前 200 年左右，也有人認為是在西元前 100 年到西元 50 年之間。

A Coin · 一枚硬幣

Why Americans say “quarter”美國人為什麼說 quarter

A U.S. quarter is a coin worth one fourth of a dollar. The OpenStax textbook uses it to explain adding fractions with the same denominator: one quarter plus two quarters equals three quarters, and “Quarters are another way to say fourths.”
美國的 quarter 是一枚值一美元四分之一的硬幣。OpenStax 教科書就用它來說明同分母分數的加法：一個 quarter 加兩個 quarter 是三個 quarter，而且「quarter 就是 fourth 的另一種說法」。

Myth vs. Fact · 常見迷思

Four things people get wrong 四個常見的誤會

✗ Myth · 迷思To add fractions, add the tops and add the bottoms.分數相加，就是分子加分子、分母加分母。

✓ Fact · 事實That gives 1/2 + 1/3 = 2/5, which is less than 1/2. First cut both into pieces of the same size, then add the pieces.這樣會得到 1/2 + 1/3 = 2/5，比 1/2 還小。要先切成一樣大的塊，再把塊數加起來。

✗ Myth · 迷思A bigger denominator means a bigger fraction.分母越大，分數越大。

✓ Fact · 事實A bigger denominator means the whole is cut into more pieces, so each piece is smaller. One eighth is less than one half.分母越大，表示切得越多塊，每一塊就越小。八分之一比二分之一小。

✗ Myth · 迷思2/4 is more than 1/2, because its numbers are bigger.2/4 比 1/2 大，因為它的數字比較大。

✓ Fact · 事實They are the same amount. Cutting each half into two smaller pieces doubles both numbers but does not change the size.它們一樣多。把每一半再切成兩塊，兩個數都變成兩倍，大小卻沒有變。

✗ Myth · 迷思To find a common denominator, you always have to multiply the two denominators.找公分母，一定要把兩個分母乘起來。

✓ Fact · 事實Any common multiple works. For fourths and sixths, twelfths are enough; 24ths also work, with smaller pieces. Taiwan's curriculum even discourages simply multiplying the denominators.任何公倍數都可以。四分之幾和六分之幾，切成十二等分就夠了；二十四等分也行，只是塊比較小。台灣的課綱還特別寫明「通分不鼓勵以分母直接相乘」。

For Teachers · 給老師的提醒

Reading, order of teaching, and methods in Taiwan and the United States 台灣和美國的唸法、教學順序與做法

  • Reading order is reversed. English says the numerator first (“three fourths”); Chinese says the denominator first (the Ministry of Education's dictionary gives san fen zhi wu for 5/3). Mixed numbers: OpenStax reads 1 3/5 as “one and three-fifths,” and NAER's math glossary reads 3 2/5 as san you wu fen zhi er. Both write the whole number to the left of the fraction.唸的順序剛好相反。英文先唸分子（three fourths），中文先唸分母（教育部辭典的例子「三分之五」）。帶分數：OpenStax 把 1 3/5 唸成 one and three-fifths；國教院的數學名詞把 3 2/5 唸作「三又五分之二」。兩邊都把整數寫在分數的左邊。
  • Taiwan's order: unit fractions by folding paper in Grade 2 (N-2-10, continuous quantities only); same-denominator fractions in Grade 3 (N-3-9), where the notes say not to use the terms proper and improper fraction and not to teach mixed numbers yet; those terms, mixed numbers, and adding with the same denominator in Grade 4 (N-4-5); equivalent fractions and “simple” unlike denominators, where one denominator is a multiple of the other, also in Grade 4 (N-4-6); and reducing, expanding, and common denominators in Grade 5 (N-5-4).台灣的順序：二年級用摺紙認識單位分數（N-2-10，限連續量）；三年級是簡單同分母分數（N-3-9），備註寫明不出現「真分數」「假分數」的名詞，也不含帶分數；四年級才引入這些名詞、帶分數和同分母加減（N-4-5），同一年級還有等值分數和「一分母為另一分母之倍數」的簡單異分母分數（N-4-6）；約分、擴分、通分到五年級（N-5-4）。
  • Finding a common denominator is taught differently. The Common Core's Grade 5 example, 2/3 + 5/4 = 8/12 + 15/12 = 23/12, comes with the general rule a/b + c/d = (ad + bc)/bd, which multiplies the denominators. Taiwan's N-5-4 note says the opposite: multiplying the denominators directly is not encouraged. OpenStax teaches the least common denominator.找公分母的方法，兩邊教法不同。美國共同核心課程標準五年級的例子 2/3 + 5/4 = 8/12 + 15/12 = 23/12，後面附了一般式 a/b + c/d = (ad + bc)/bd，也就是分母直接相乘；台灣 N-5-4 的備註則寫明「通分不鼓勵以分母直接相乘」。OpenStax 教的是最小公分母。
  • Models and words. U.S. materials talk about “fraction tiles” (OpenStax) and require that compared fractions “refer to the same whole” (Common Core 3.NF.A.3.d). Taiwan's curriculum lists fraction circles and starts with continuous quantities. This page uses strips of equal length so that “the same whole” is always visible; the Common Core limits Grade 3 to denominators 2, 3, 4, 6, and 8.教具與用語。美國教材說 fraction tiles（OpenStax），並要求比較的分數「指的是同一個整體」（CCSS 3.NF.A.3.d）。台灣課綱列的教具是分數圓形圖，而且先從連續量開始。這一頁用一樣長的條子，讓「同一個整體」一直看得見；美國三年級把分母限在 2、3、4、6、8。

Classroom Activity 1 · 課堂活動一

Paper Strip Fractions 紙條分數

You need · 準備材料

  • Five paper strips of the same length for each student, about 24 cm long每人五條一樣長的紙條，約 24 公分
  • Ruler, pencil, scissors, and two colors尺、鉛筆、剪刀、兩種顏色

Steps · 步驟

  1. Keep one strip whole. Fold the second in half, and mark the third into thirds with the ruler (every 8 cm). Mark the fourth into sixths (every 4 cm).第一條保持完整。第二條對摺成兩半；第三條用尺每 8 公分畫一道，分成三等分；第四條每 4 公分畫一道，分成六等分。
  2. Color one half and one third. Lay them end to end on top of the whole strip. Is the total more or less than the whole?塗一個二分之一和一個三分之一，頭尾相接放在完整的那一條上面。合起來比一整條多還是少？
  3. Use the sixths strip to measure: how many sixths cover the half? How many cover the third? How many cover both together?用六等分的那一條去量：二分之一要幾個六分之一才蓋得住？三分之一呢？兩個合起來呢？
  4. On the fifth strip, mark fifths (every 4.8 cm) and color two fifths. Compare it with the half. What does that tell you about 1/2 + 1/3 = 2/5?第五條每 4.8 公分畫一道分成五等分，塗五分之二。拿它跟二分之一比。這告訴你 1/2 + 1/3 = 2/5 對不對？

Folding paper is how Taiwan's Grade 2 begins (N-2-10), and the strips keep the same whole in view, as the Common Core asks (3.NF.A.3.d). Choosing 24 cm makes halves, thirds, fourths, sixths, and eighths come out in whole centimeters; fifths do not, which is itself a good discussion point.
摺紙正是台灣二年級認識分數的起點（N-2-10）；紙條也讓「同一個整體」一直看得見，符合美國 CCSS 3.NF.A.3.d 的要求。選 24 公分，是因為二、三、四、六、八等分都剛好是整數公分；五等分就不是，這本身就是很好的討論題。

Classroom Activity 2 · 課堂活動二

Is It Reasonable? 這個答案合理嗎？

You need · 準備材料

  • Cards with fraction sums, some right and some wrong, such as 1/2 + 1/3 = 2/5, 1/4 + 1/4 = 1/2, 2/5 + 1/2 = 3/7, and 1/3 + 1/6 = 1/2寫著分數加法的卡片，有對有錯，例如 1/2 + 1/3 = 2/5、1/4 + 1/4 = 1/2、2/5 + 1/2 = 3/7、1/3 + 1/6 = 1/2
  • A number line from 0 to 2 on the board, with 1/2 and 1 marked黑板上一條 0 到 2 的數線，標出 1/2 和 1

Steps · 步驟

  1. Without calculating, decide for each card: could this be right? Use 1/2 and 1 as landmarks.先不要算，每一張卡片都判斷：這有可能是對的嗎？用 1/2 和 1 當路標。
  2. Explain each “no” in one sentence, such as “The answer is smaller than one of the parts.”每一個「不可能」都用一句話說明理由，例如「答案比其中一個加數還小」。
  3. Check the “maybe” cards with strips or by finding a common denominator.「可能」的卡片，再用紙條或找公分母驗算。

This is the reasoning the Common Core asks for in Grade 5: “recognize an incorrect result 2/5 + 1/2 = 3/7, by observing that 3/7 < 1/2.” Students who learn to ask “Is it reasonable?” catch the add-the-bottoms mistake on their own.
這正是美國 CCSS 五年級要的推理：「看出 2/5 + 1/2 = 3/7 是錯的，因為 3/7 < 1/2」。學生學會問「這合理嗎？」，就能自己抓出分母加分母的錯。
