Big idea · 一句話看懂

Some jobs are too important to wait for you to decide. Your brainstem runs them for you, by squeezing or jolting the muscles that move air.
有些工作太重要，不能等你決定。腦幹替你處理——靠的是擠壓或抽動那些負責推動空氣的肌肉。

Unit 26: Why Do You Sneeze, Hiccup, and Yawn?

You are sitting quietly in class when, without warning: Achoo! You did not decide to sneeze. Your body did it for you. A sneeze, a hiccup, and a yawn are all things your body does on its own. Actions like these are called reflexes. Why does your body have them, and how do they work?

你正安安靜靜地坐在教室裡，突然毫無預警地：哈啾！你並沒有「決定」要打噴嚏，是身體替你做的。打噴嚏、打嗝、打呵欠，都是身體自己會做的事。像這樣的動作，叫做反射。身體為什麼要有這些反射？它們又是怎麼運作的？

A reflex needs no thinking. A message travels along a nerve to the brainstem, the stalk at the bottom of your brain. The brainstem answers at once by sending orders to your muscles. It is the same part that keeps you breathing all night. Most of the muscles in this lesson are breathing muscles, and the biggest is the diaphragm, a dome of muscle under your lungs. When it pulls down, air rushes in.

反射不需要思考。訊息沿著神經傳到腦幹——大腦最下方的那一段；腦幹立刻回應，向肌肉發出命令。讓你整個晚上持續呼吸的，也是同一個地方。這一課裡出場的肌肉，大部分都是呼吸用的肌肉，其中最大的一條是橫膈膜：肺下面一片圓頂狀的肌肉。它往下一拉，空氣就衝進來。

A sneeze is a cleaning job. When dust or pollen tickles the inside of your nose, nerve endings there send a message to the brainstem. You take a deep breath, a gate in your throat shuts for an instant, your chest squeezes, and then the air blasts out, carrying the dust with it. One sneeze can spray about 40,000 tiny droplets, and they can fly several meters. That is why you cover a sneeze with your elbow or a tissue.

打噴嚏是清潔工作。灰塵或花粉讓鼻子裡面發癢的時候，那裡的神經末梢向腦幹發出訊息。你深深吸一口氣，喉嚨裡的一道門關上一瞬間，胸部用力一擠，然後空氣猛力噴出去，把灰塵一起帶走。一個噴嚏可以噴出大約四萬顆小飛沫，而且能飛好幾公尺遠——所以打噴嚏要用手肘或衛生紙遮住。

A hiccup is a mistake in the rhythm. Your diaphragm suddenly jerks down, and air rushes in. A tiny moment later, the gate in your throat slams shut, and the air hitting it makes the sound: hic! Eating too fast or drinking soda can start hiccups. Babies even hiccup before they are born. Nobody is sure what hiccups are for.

打嗝是節奏出了錯。橫膈膜突然往下一抽，空氣衝進來；極短的一瞬間之後，喉嚨裡的那道門「啪」地關上，空氣撞上去，就發出那個聲音：嗝！吃得太快或喝有氣泡的飲料，都可能引起打嗝。寶寶甚至在出生以前就會打嗝了。沒有人確定打嗝到底有什麼用。

A yawn is the slow one. Your jaw opens wide, you take a long breath in, your muscles stretch, and then you breathe out fast. People once thought a yawn brings in extra oxygen, but tests do not support that idea. It may help to cool the brain or to wake you up. And yawns are catching! From about the age of six, seeing someone yawn can make you yawn too. Dogs and chimpanzees catch yawns as well.

打呵欠是慢的那一個。下巴大大地張開，你長長地吸一口氣，肌肉伸展一下，然後很快地把氣呼出去。以前的人以為打呵欠是為了多吸一點氧氣，但是實驗並不支持這個說法。它也許能幫大腦降溫，或是讓你清醒一點。而且呵欠會傳染！大約從六歲開始，看到別人打呵欠，你也會跟著打。狗和黑猩猩也會被傳染。

Here is one more reflex, and you are doing it right now: blinking. Each blink spreads tears across your eye and wipes it clean, and it lasts only about a tenth of a second. A grown-up blinks about 17 times a minute. But when people read or stare at a screen, they blink only 4 or 5 times a minute, and their eyes get dry and tired. Try the blink counter below the model with a partner.

還有一個反射，你現在就正在做：眨眼。每眨一次眼，就把淚水抹過整個眼球、把它擦乾淨，而且只花大約十分之一秒。大人每分鐘大約眨 17 次眼。可是，人在閱讀或盯著螢幕的時候，每分鐘只眨 4、5 次，眼睛就會變得又乾又累。找一位同伴，用模型下方的眨眼計數器試試看。

Questions · 閱讀理解

What is a reflex?

Something your body does on its own, without thinking. A message goes to the brainstem, and the brainstem sends orders to the muscles at once.

What happens in your body when you hiccup?

The diaphragm jerks down and air rushes in, and a moment later the gate in the throat slams shut. That makes the “hic” sound.

Why do your eyes get tired when you look at a screen for a long time?

Because you blink much less, only 4 or 5 times a minute instead of about 17, so your eyes dry out.

Each one has its own speed and its own job 每一種都有自己的速度、自己的工作

You can hold your breath or hold back a blink for a little while, but not for long. The brainstem always takes the job back.
你可以憋一下氣，或是忍住一下不眨眼，但是撐不久。腦幹一定會把工作接回去。

How often do I blink? 我多久眨一次眼？

Work with a partner. One of you is the counter, and the other just acts normally. Use the blink counter below the model. Then swap.
兩個人一組。一個人負責數，另一個人照平常的樣子就好。用模型下方的眨眼計數器，數完再交換。

Myth vs. Fact · 常見迷思

Four things people get wrong 四個常見的誤會

✗ Myth · 迷思You cannot sneeze with your eyes open.打噴嚏的時候，眼睛不可能是張開的。

✓ Fact · 事實Your eyes usually close by reflex, but it is possible to sneeze with them open.眼睛通常會反射性地閉上，但是張著眼睛打噴嚏是做得到的。

✗ Myth · 迷思A yawn means you need more oxygen.打呵欠是因為需要更多氧氣。

✓ Fact · 事實Tests do not support that idea. Scientists still do not agree on what a yawn is for.實驗並不支持這個說法。打呵欠到底有什麼用，科學家到現在還沒有共識。

✗ Myth · 迷思A scare, holding your breath, or a glass of water will always stop hiccups.嚇一跳、憋氣或喝一杯水，一定可以止住打嗝。

✓ Fact · 事實No cure has been proved to work. Most hiccups simply stop on their own.沒有哪一種方法被證實有效。大部分的打嗝會自己停下來。

✗ Myth · 迷思A sneeze only reaches as far as your hand.噴嚏最遠只會噴到手掌那麼遠。

✓ Fact · 事實The droplets can travel several meters. Cover your sneeze with your elbow or a tissue, and wash your hands.飛沫可以飛好幾公尺遠。打噴嚏要用手肘或衛生紙遮住，然後洗手。

In Chinese · 中文怎麼說

Bless You! Someone Is Thinking of You Bless you！有人在想你

When someone sneezes, English speakers say “Bless you!” It is an old habit, and people say it today simply to be polite. Chinese speakers have a playful saying instead: 一想二罵三感冒 (yī xiǎng, èr mà, sān gǎnmào). One sneeze means someone is thinking of you, two mean someone is scolding you, and three mean you have caught a cold! The words for these reflexes are fun, too. A yawn is 打呵欠 (dǎ hēqian) in Mandarin and 哈唏 (hah-hì) in Taiwanese, and both sound like a yawn. English “hiccup” and Mandarin 嗝 (gé) both copy the sound of a hiccup. Try saying them: does your mouth make the shape of the reflex?
有人打噴嚏的時候，說英文的人會說 Bless you!（祝福你）。這是很古老的習慣，現在的人說它只是出於禮貌。說中文的人則有一句好玩的俗話：「一想二罵三感冒」——打一個噴嚏是有人在想你，兩個是有人在罵你，三個就是感冒了！這些反射的名字也很有趣：國語的「呵欠」和台語的「哈唏」，聽起來都像在打呵欠；英文的 hiccup 和中文的「嗝」，都是在模仿打嗝的聲音。唸唸看：你的嘴巴是不是做出了那個反射的形狀？

Classroom Activity 1 · 課堂活動一

Does a Yawn Spread? 呵欠會傳染嗎？

You need · 準備材料

  • A short video of people or animals yawning一段人或動物打呵欠的短片
  • A tally sheet for the teacher老師用的一張記錄表

Steps · 步驟

  1. Do not tell the class what the experiment is about. Just say you will watch a short video.先不要告訴全班這個實驗在做什麼，只說要看一段短片。
  2. Play the video. The teacher quietly counts how many students yawn during it and in the two minutes after.播放影片。老師悄悄數一數，影片播放時和之後的兩分鐘裡，有幾位學生打了呵欠。
  3. Now tell the class the secret, and type the two numbers into the tool on this page.現在把祕密告訴全班，再把兩個數字輸入這一頁的工具。
  4. Did reading the word “yawn” just now make anyone yawn?剛才讀到「呵欠」這兩個字，有沒有人因此打了呵欠？

Teaching tip: count yawns for the class as a whole, not by name. Not everyone catches yawns, and that is normal: in studies, only some of the people watching yawn. The real lesson is about method: why did we keep the purpose secret until the end?
教學提示：只數全班一共幾個，不要記名字。不是每個人都會被呵欠傳染，這很正常——研究裡也只有一部分的觀看者會跟著打。真正值得討論的是方法：為什麼要等到最後才說出實驗的目的？

Classroom Activity 2 · 課堂活動二

Find Your Diaphragm 找到你的橫膈膜

You need · 準備材料

  • A book一本書
  • A place to lie down, or a chair一個可以躺下的地方，或一張椅子

Steps · 步驟

  1. Lie on your back and put the book on your belly, just below your ribs.仰躺，把書放在肚子上、肋骨的正下方。
  2. Breathe in slowly through your nose. What does the book do?用鼻子慢慢吸氣。書怎麼了？
  3. Breathe out. What does it do now?呼氣。現在它又怎麼了？
  4. Sit up and say “ha!” sharply with a hand on the same spot. What do you feel?坐起來，把手放在同一個地方，短促地說一聲「哈！」。你感覺到什麼？

Teaching tip: the book rises as the diaphragm pulls down and pushes the belly out, and it sinks as the diaphragm relaxes. The sharp “ha!” is a small jolt of the same muscle, a little like half of a hiccup. Students who play wind instruments or sing may already know this muscle well.
教學提示：橫膈膜往下拉、把肚子往外推，書就升高；橫膈膜放鬆，書就下降。短促的「哈！」是同一條肌肉的小小一抽，有點像半個打嗝。吹管樂器或唱歌的學生，可能早就很熟悉這條肌肉了。

⚕ Health note · 健康提醒This lesson is for learning about the body. It is not medical advice. Cover sneezes and coughs with your elbow or a tissue, and wash your hands afterward. Hiccups almost always stop on their own. If they go on for days, or if you often have trouble breathing, tell an adult and see a doctor.本課是認識身體的教材，不是醫療建議。打噴嚏和咳嗽要用手肘或衛生紙遮住，之後記得洗手。打嗝幾乎都會自己停；如果連續好幾天都不停，或是常常覺得呼吸困難，請告訴大人，並且去看醫生。
