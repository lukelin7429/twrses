Big idea · 一句話看懂

A joint is a place where bones meet. Its shape decides how it moves: a hinge swings one way, a ball in a socket turns every way, and a pivot twists.
關節是骨頭相接的地方，它的形狀決定它怎麼動：鉸鏈只往一個方向擺，球窩往哪裡都能轉，樞軸則是扭轉。

Unit 5: How Do Your Joints Move?

Try walking across the room without bending your knees. You will waddle like a penguin. Now try eating lunch without bending your elbows. Every time you move, bones change their angle at places where two bones meet. These places are called joints, and your body has several hundred of them, from your jaw to your smallest toe.

試試看不彎膝蓋走過教室，你會像企鵝一樣搖搖擺擺。再試試看不彎手肘吃午餐。你每動一下，骨頭都會在兩塊骨頭相接的地方改變角度。這些地方叫做關節，從下巴到最小的腳趾，你全身有好幾百個關節。

Not every joint moves. The bones of your skull are joined by wavy seams called sutures, which lock them together like puzzle pieces to protect your brain. A newborn baby's skull still has soft spots between the bones so that the head can grow, but they close by about the age of two. The joints between the bones of your spine move only a little each, but together they let you bend and twist. The joints that move the most are in your arms and legs.

不是每個關節都會動。頭顱骨之間由彎彎曲曲的接縫——骨縫——連在一起，像拼圖一樣卡緊，保護大腦。新生兒的頭顱骨之間還有軟軟的囟門，讓頭可以長大，但大約兩歲就會閉合。脊椎骨之間的關節每一節只能動一點點，合起來卻讓你能彎腰、扭身。動得最多的關節，在手臂和腿上。

Inside a moving joint, the ends of the bones never grind against each other. Each end is covered with cartilage, a smooth, rubbery layer that is more slippery than ice sliding on ice. The whole joint is wrapped in a capsule filled with a little slippery liquid called synovial fluid, which works like oil in a machine. Strong straps called ligaments hold the bones together, so they can move without coming apart.

在會動的關節裡，骨頭的末端從來不會直接互相磨擦。每個末端都包著一層軟骨，光滑又有彈性，比冰在冰上滑還要滑。整個關節外面包著一層關節囊，裡面有一點點滑滑的液體，叫做滑液，作用就像機器裡的潤滑油。強韌的帶子——韌帶——把骨頭綁在一起，讓它們能動卻不會分開。

The shape of a joint decides how it moves. Your knee and elbow are hinge joints: like a door, they swing one way and back. Your shoulder and hip are ball-and-socket joints: a round ball at the top of the arm or thigh bone sits in a cup, so the limb can swing forward, backward, out to the side, and even in a circle. At the top of your neck, a pivot joint lets your head turn to say “no.” And at the base of your thumb is a saddle joint, which rocks two ways and lets your thumb touch the tip of every other finger.

關節的形狀決定它怎麼動。膝蓋和手肘是鉸鏈關節：像一扇門，只能往一個方向擺、再擺回來。肩膀和髖部是球窩關節：手臂或大腿骨頂端的圓球卡在一個杯狀的窩裡，所以四肢可以往前、往後、往旁邊擺，甚至畫圓。頸部頂端有一個樞軸關節，讓你能轉頭說「不」。拇指根部則是鞍狀關節，可以朝兩個方向搖，讓拇指碰得到其他每一根手指的指尖。

There is always a trade-off between freedom and strength. Your shoulder has a shallow cup, which gives it the widest range of any joint in the body, but it is also the large joint that most often slips out of place. Your hip has a deep cup that holds the ball tightly. It cannot move as freely, but it can carry your whole weight when you run and jump. Your knee, the largest joint in the body, has two rubbery cushions inside and several strong ligaments to keep it steady.

自由和穩固之間，總是要取捨。肩膀的窩很淺，讓它成為全身活動範圍最大的關節，但它也是最常脫臼的大關節。髖關節的窩很深，把球緊緊包住；它不能動得那麼自由，卻能在你跑跳時撐起全身的重量。膝蓋是全身最大的關節，裡面有兩塊有彈性的軟墊，還有好幾條強韌的韌帶讓它保持穩定。

Joints stay healthy when you use them. Moving squeezes fluid in and out of the cartilage, which keeps it fed, and strong muscles around a joint help protect it. Warm up before sports, and never force a joint past where it wants to go. And the popping sound when someone cracks their knuckles? It is a tiny gas bubble forming in the joint fluid. Now make a simple tool to measure how far your own joints can bend.

關節要常常用才會健康。活動會把液體擠進、擠出軟骨，為它帶來養分；關節周圍強壯的肌肉也能保護它。運動前要暖身，也絕對不要硬把關節扳到超過它能到的地方。那折手指時的「啪」一聲呢？那是關節液裡形成的一個小小氣泡。現在做一個簡單的工具，量一量你自己的關節能彎多少度。

Questions · 閱讀理解

Why do the bones in a moving joint not grind against each other?

The ends of the bones are covered with smooth, slippery cartilage, and the joint is filled with synovial fluid that works like oil.

What is the difference between how a hinge joint and a ball-and-socket joint move?

A hinge joint swings in only one direction and back, like a door. A ball-and-socket joint can move in every direction, even in a circle.

Why can the hip carry more weight than the shoulder, even though the shoulder moves more freely?

The hip has a deep cup that holds the ball tightly, so it is strong and stable. The shoulder has a shallow cup, which lets it move more but makes it less stable.

Measure Yourself · 親身測量

Measure your own range of motion 量一量自己的活動範圍

Make a simple joint-angle meter, then work with a partner to measure how far your knee and elbow bend. Compare your numbers with the typical ranges shown on the sliders in the model.
做一個簡單的關節角度計，和同伴一起量膝蓋和手肘能彎幾度，再和模型滑桿上的一般範圍比一比。

  1. 1Make the meter · 做角度計Join two cardboard strips at one end with a brass fastener, and tape a paper protractor at the joint.兩條紙板用雙腳釘在一端接起來，在接點貼一張紙量角器。
  2. 2Line it up · 對準Hold the fastener on your partner's elbow, one strip along the upper arm and one along the forearm.把雙腳釘對準同伴的手肘，一條紙板沿著上臂、一條沿著前臂。
  3. 3Bend and read · 彎曲、讀數Have your partner bend as far as is comfortable, never forcing it, and read the angle. Then measure the knee.請同伴在舒服的範圍內彎到最多（不要硬扳），讀出角度。再量膝蓋。

Myth vs. Fact · 常見迷思

Four things people get wrong 四個常見的誤會

✗ Myth · 迷思Cracking your knuckles causes arthritis.折手指會造成關節炎。

✓ Fact · 事實The pop is a gas bubble forming in the joint fluid. Studies have not found that knuckle cracking causes arthritis, though it can annoy the people around you.那一聲是關節液裡形成的氣泡。研究並沒有發現折手指會造成關節炎——雖然可能會讓旁邊的人覺得很煩。

✗ Myth · 迷思In a joint, bone rubs directly on bone.關節裡是骨頭直接磨骨頭。

✓ Fact · 事實Smooth cartilage covers the bone ends, and slippery fluid fills the space. Bones only grind when the cartilage has worn away, which is a sign of disease.骨頭末端包著光滑的軟骨，中間還有滑液。只有軟骨磨損了，骨頭才會互相磨擦，那是生病的徵兆。

✗ Myth · 迷思Double-jointed people have extra joints.「雙關節」的人多了一些關節。

✓ Fact · 事實They have the same joints as everyone else. Their ligaments are looser, or their joint shapes are a little different, so they can bend farther.他們的關節和大家一樣多，只是韌帶比較鬆，或關節形狀稍微不同，所以能彎得比較多。

✗ Myth · 迷思Stiff joints should be kept still.關節僵硬就應該少動。

✓ Fact · 事實Gentle movement keeps joints healthy by moving fluid through the cartilage. The exception is an injury: then follow what a doctor says.溫和的活動能讓液體在軟骨裡流動，讓關節保持健康。例外是受傷的時候：那就要聽醫生的。

Remember It · 記憶口訣

Joints in a sentence 一句話記住關節

Door, joystick, doorknob門、搖桿、門把

Hinge = door, ball-and-socket = joystick, pivot = doorknob. Name the object, and you know the move.
鉸鏈＝門，球窩＝搖桿，樞軸＝門把。說出那個東西，就知道它怎麼動。

Can it draw a circle?畫得出圓嗎？

If a limb can draw a circle in the air from that joint, it is a ball-and-socket. A hinge can only draw an arc.
如果四肢能從那個關節在空中畫圓，就是球窩關節；鉸鏈只畫得出一段弧。

Shoulder for freedom, hip for strength肩膀要自由，髖部要穩固

Same kind of joint, different cups: shallow for reaching, deep for carrying weight.
同一種關節、不一樣的窩：淺窩用來伸展，深窩用來承重。

Thumbs up for the saddle為鞍狀關節按個讚

Your thumb's saddle joint lets it face the other fingers. That is why you can grip a pencil and pick up a coin.
拇指的鞍狀關節讓它能和其他手指面對面，所以你才能握筆、撿起硬幣。

In Chinese · 中文怎麼說

A Gate and a Bamboo Node 「關」與「節」

The Chinese word for joint, 關節 (guānjié), joins two pictures. 關 is a gate or a mountain pass, a narrow place that things must move through. 節 is the node on a bamboo stem, where one section meets the next, just as one bone meets another. The same 節 appears in 季節 (season) and 節日 (festival), marking where one stretch of time meets the next. And when something is the key point of a problem, Chinese calls it 關鍵 or says you have found the 節骨眼, the “joint-bone eye.”
中文的「關節」由兩個意象組成：「關」是關口、隘口，東西必須通過的狹窄地方；「節」是竹子的竹節，一段接著下一段的地方，就像一塊骨頭接著另一塊。同一個「節」也出現在「季節」「節日」裡，標示一段時間接到下一段。事情的要緊處，中文說「關鍵」，或說抓到了「節骨眼」。

Classroom Activity 1 · 課堂活動一

The Taped-Thumb Challenge 黏住拇指大挑戰

You need · 準備材料

  • Paper tape or masking tape (gentle on skin)紙膠帶（不傷皮膚）
  • A pencil, a button-up shirt or jacket, a few coins一支鉛筆、一件有扣子的衣服或外套、幾枚硬幣
  • A timer一個計時器

Steps · 步驟

  1. Time yourself writing your name, doing up one button, and picking up three coins.計時：寫自己的名字、扣一顆扣子、撿起三枚硬幣。
  2. Ask a partner to gently tape your thumb against the side of your hand so it cannot move.請同伴用紙膠帶輕輕把你的拇指貼在手掌側邊，讓它不能動。
  3. Try the same three jobs again and time them.再做一次同樣的三件事，計時。
  4. Remove the tape. Which job was hardest without your thumb? Why?撕下膠帶。沒有拇指時哪一件最難？為什麼？

Teaching tip: keep the tape loose and take it off after a few minutes. Afterward, look at the thumb in the 3D model and press the circle button: its saddle joint is what lets it face the other fingers.
教學提示：膠帶不要貼太緊，幾分鐘後就撕掉。做完再看 3D 模型裡的拇指，按「畫圓圈」：正是鞍狀關節讓它能和其他手指面對面。

Classroom Activity 2 · 課堂活動二

Joint Hunt: Hinge, Ball, or Pivot? 關節大搜查：鉸鏈、球窩，還是樞軸？

You need · 準備材料

  • A worksheet with three columns: hinge, ball-and-socket, pivot一張分三欄的學習單：鉸鏈、球窩、樞軸
  • Space to move可以活動的空間

Steps · 步驟

  1. Move one joint at a time: jaw, neck, shoulder, elbow, wrist, finger, hip, knee, ankle.一次只動一個關節：下巴、脖子、肩膀、手肘、手腕、手指、髖部、膝蓋、腳踝。
  2. For each one, ask: can it draw a circle? Can it twist? Does it only swing one way?每一個都問自己：能畫圓嗎？能扭轉嗎？只能往一個方向擺嗎？
  3. Write each joint in a column. Some may not fit neatly; mark them with a question mark.把每個關節寫進一欄；不太好歸類的就打個問號。
  4. Compare with a partner and check your answers with the cards on this page.和同伴比對答案，再用這一頁的卡片檢查。

Teaching tip: the wrist and ankle are good puzzles. They move in two directions but cannot twist much, because many small gliding joints work together. Accept good reasoning even when the answer is “it's a mix.”
教學提示：手腕和腳踝是很好的謎題：它們能朝兩個方向動，卻幾乎不能扭轉，因為是許多小滑動關節一起工作。只要理由說得通，「是混合型」也是好答案。
