Big idea · 一句話看懂

Your body fights germs with three walls: skin and mucus keep most germs out, white blood cells attack the ones that get in, and your body remembers each germ so it can win faster next time. That memory is how vaccines work.
身體用三道防線對抗病菌：皮膚和黏液擋住大部分，白血球攻擊闖進來的，身體還會記住每一種病菌，下次贏得更快——疫苗就是靠這份記憶。

Reading · 英文閱讀

Unit 12: How Does Your Body Fight Germs?

Germs are tiny living things, such as bacteria and viruses, that are too small to see. Most bacteria are harmless, and many, like the ones in your gut, even help you. But some can make you sick. Your body is attacked by germs every day, and it wins almost every time, usually without you noticing. It does this with three walls of defense.

病菌是像細菌和病毒這樣的小生物，小到眼睛看不見。大部分細菌是無害的，很多甚至對你有幫助，例如腸道裡的細菌；但有些會讓你生病。你的身體每天都受到病菌攻擊，而且幾乎每次都贏，通常你根本沒發現。它靠的是三道防線。

The first wall keeps germs out. Your skin is a tough, dry barrier that most germs cannot get through. Your nose, mouth, and throat are lined with sticky mucus that traps germs, and tiny hairs sweep the mucus away so you can swallow or sneeze it out. Tears and saliva contain substances that kill bacteria, and your stomach is filled with strong acid. Germs get in mainly through cuts, or through your eyes, nose, and mouth, often from your own hands.

第一道防線把病菌擋在外面。皮膚是一道堅韌、乾燥的屏障，大部分病菌穿不過去。鼻子、嘴巴和喉嚨裡有黏黏的黏液，能黏住病菌，細細的纖毛再把黏液掃走，讓你吞下去或打噴嚏噴出來。眼淚和口水裡有能殺死細菌的物質，胃裡則裝滿了強酸。病菌主要是從傷口，或從眼睛、鼻子和嘴巴進來，常常就是被自己的手帶進來的。

If germs get past the first wall, the second wall attacks right away. Bacteria can double every 20 minutes, so speed matters. Damaged cells send out alarm signals, and blood vessels nearby widen and leak fluid. That is why a cut turns red, warm, swollen, and sore. Then white blood cells squeeze out of the blood vessels and hunt the germs, swallowing them whole. A single drop of blood carries hundreds of thousands of white blood cells. A fever can help too, because many germs grow more slowly when your body is warmer.

病菌如果闖過第一道防線，第二道防線馬上出動。細菌每 20 分鐘就能多一倍，所以速度很重要。受傷的細胞發出警報，附近的血管變寬、滲出液體，所以傷口會紅、熱、腫、痛。接著白血球從血管裡擠出來追捕病菌，把它們整個吞下去。一滴血裡就有幾十萬個白血球。發燒也能幫忙，因為很多病菌在體溫比較高時長得比較慢。

The third wall is the smartest. Special white blood cells study the invader and make antibodies, Y-shaped proteins that fit only that one germ, like a key fits a lock. Antibodies stick to the germs, making them easier to catch and destroy. The first time your body meets a new germ, making the right antibodies can take a week or more, so you may feel sick for days.

第三道防線最聰明。特別的白血球會研究入侵者，製造抗體：一種 Y 字形的蛋白質，只配得上那一種病菌，就像一把鑰匙只開一把鎖。抗體黏在病菌上，讓它們更容易被抓住、被消滅。身體第一次遇到新的病菌時，做出對的抗體可能要一個星期以上，所以你可能會不舒服好幾天。

But your body remembers. After the fight, memory cells stay on guard for years. If the same germ comes back, they make antibodies in a day or two, and the germ is often gone before you feel sick at all. Vaccines use this memory. A vaccine shows your body a harmless piece or a weakened form of a germ, so your body learns to recognize it without having to get sick first.

但是身體會記得。打完這一仗，記憶細胞會守候好幾年。同一種病菌再來的時候，它們一兩天就能做出抗體，病菌常常在你感覺不舒服之前就被消滅了。疫苗就是利用這份記憶：疫苗讓身體看到病菌無害的一小部分，或是減弱過的病菌，讓身體不必先生病，就學會認出它。

You can help your three walls. Wash your hands with soap for at least 20 seconds, especially before eating and after using the toilet. Cover coughs and sneezes with your elbow. Clean cuts and keep them covered. Sleep well and eat well, because a tired body fights germs less well. And remember that antibiotics kill bacteria but do nothing against viruses like colds and flu. Now try the germ calculator and the handwashing timer below the model.

你也可以幫助這三道防線。用肥皂洗手至少 20 秒，尤其是吃東西前和上完廁所後。咳嗽、打噴嚏時用手肘遮住。傷口要清潔，並且蓋好。睡飽、吃好，因為疲累的身體比較打不贏病菌。還要記得：抗生素能殺細菌，但對感冒、流感這些病毒完全沒用。現在試試模型下方的病菌計算機和洗手計時器。

Questions · 閱讀理解

What are the three walls your body uses against germs?

First, skin and mucus keep germs out. Second, white blood cells attack germs that get in. Third, antibodies target each germ, and the body remembers it.

Why does a cut turn red, warm, and swollen?

Damaged cells send alarm signals, and nearby blood vessels widen and leak fluid so that white blood cells can get to the germs.

Why do you often not get sick the second time the same germ comes back?

Memory cells remember the germ and make antibodies in a day or two, so the germ is gone before you feel sick.

Myth vs. Fact · 常見迷思

Four things people get wrong 四個常見的誤會

✗ Myth · 迷思All bacteria are bad for you.細菌都是壞的。

✓ Fact · 事實Most bacteria are harmless, and trillions of helpful bacteria live in your gut, where they help digest food and crowd out harmful germs.大部分細菌是無害的；腸道裡住著好幾兆個有益的細菌，幫忙消化食物，還把有害的病菌擠走。

✗ Myth · 迷思Antibiotics cure colds.抗生素能治感冒。

✓ Fact · 事實Colds are caused by viruses, and antibiotics work only on bacteria. Taking them when they are not needed helps bacteria learn to resist them.感冒是病毒引起的，抗生素只對細菌有效。不需要時吃抗生素，反而讓細菌學會抵抗它。

✗ Myth · 迷思A fever is always bad and should be stopped.發燒一定是壞事，要馬上退燒。

✓ Fact · 事實A fever is often part of how your body fights germs. A doctor or a parent decides when medicine is needed, especially if the fever is very high or lasts a long time.發燒常常是身體對抗病菌的一部分。要不要吃藥由醫生或家長決定，尤其是燒得很高或燒很久的時候。

✗ Myth · 迷思If you can't see dirt, your hands are clean.手看起來不髒，就是乾淨的。

✓ Fact · 事實Germs are far too small to see. Clean-looking hands can still carry them, so wash before you eat and after using the toilet.病菌小到看不見，看起來乾淨的手也可能帶著病菌；吃東西前、上完廁所後都要洗手。

In Chinese · 中文怎麼說

Escaping the Plague 免疫：免除疫病

The Chinese word for immunity, 免疫 (miǎnyì), means “escaping the plague”: 免 is to avoid, and 疫 is an epidemic. English immunity comes from the Latin immunis, “free from a duty,” so an immune person was free from the sickness. The old saying 病從口入 (bìng cóng kǒu rù), “sickness comes in through the mouth,” is why Taiwanese students learn to wash their hands before every meal, and why the five steps 濕、搓、沖、捧、擦 are posted next to so many sinks.
中文的「免疫」，意思是「免除疫病」：免是避開，疫是流行病。英文 immunity 來自拉丁文 immunis，「免除義務」，所以 immune 的人就是不必生那場病的人。老話說「病從口入」，所以台灣的學生從小就學會飯前洗手，很多洗手台旁邊也貼著「濕、搓、沖、捧、擦」五個字。

Classroom Activity 1 · 課堂活動一

The Pepper Germ Test 胡椒粉病菌實驗

You need · 準備材料

  • A shallow plate of water and some ground black pepper一個裝水的淺盤和一些黑胡椒粉
  • Liquid soap or dish soap洗手乳或洗碗精

Steps · 步驟

  1. Sprinkle pepper all over the water. The pepper is the “germs.”把胡椒粉撒滿整個水面，胡椒粉就是「病菌」。
  2. Dip a clean finger into the middle. What happens?用乾淨的手指點一下中間，發生了什麼事？
  3. Now put a drop of soap on your fingertip and dip it in again.在指尖沾一滴肥皂，再點一次。
  4. Where did the pepper go? Why?胡椒粉跑到哪裡去了？為什麼？

Teaching tip: soap breaks the surface tension of the water, so the pepper rushes away. In real handwashing, soap also breaks up oils on your skin and lifts germs off so the water can rinse them away. That is why rinsing with water alone is not enough.
教學提示：肥皂破壞了水的表面張力，所以胡椒粉一下子散開。真正洗手時，肥皂也會分解皮膚上的油脂，把病菌拉起來，讓水把它們沖走——所以只用清水沖是不夠的。

Classroom Activity 2 · 課堂活動二

The Handshake Chain 握手傳播遊戲

You need · 準備材料

  • A little glitter, flour, or washable paint一點亮粉、麵粉或可洗的顏料
  • Soap, water, and paper towels肥皂、水和擦手紙

Steps · 步驟

  1. One student puts a little glitter on one hand. That is the “germ.”一位同學在一隻手上沾一點亮粉，那就是「病菌」。
  2. Shake hands down a line of five or six students.沿著一排五、六位同學依序握手。
  3. Check everyone's hands, then their desks, pencils, and faces. Where did the glitter end up?檢查每個人的手，再看看桌子、鉛筆和臉：亮粉跑到哪裡去了？
  4. Everyone washes with soap. Who got it all off?大家用肥皂洗手，誰洗得最乾淨？

Teaching tip: germs travel the same way, and the last student in line still gets some. Ask how many students touched their face without noticing. Washable materials only, and keep glitter away from eyes.
教學提示：病菌就是這樣傳出去的，排到最後的同學一樣會沾到。問問大家有幾個人不知不覺摸了臉。請只用可以洗掉的材料，亮粉不要碰到眼睛。
