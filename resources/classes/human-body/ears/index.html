How Do Your Ears Hear?耳朵怎麼聽見聲音？

Sound is air that shakes. Follow it through your eardrum and three tiny bones into a snail-shaped tube, and find the highest note you can still hear.

Big idea · 一句話看懂

Sound is air shaking. Your eardrum catches the shaking, three tiny bones pass it on, and the cochlea turns it into nerve signals: high sounds near its entrance, low sounds deep inside.
聲音是空氣在振動：鼓膜接住振動，三塊小骨頭把它傳下去，耳蝸再把它變成神經訊號——高音在入口附近，低音在最裡面。

Unit 9: How Do Your Ears Hear?

Clap your hands. The clap pushes the air, and the push travels outward in waves at about 340 meters per second, fast enough to cross a soccer field in a third of a second. When the waves reach you, the air in your ear shakes back and forth, and your ears turn that shaking into sound. Fast shaking sounds high, like a bird. Slow shaking sounds low, like a drum. Scientists count the shakes in hertz: one hertz means one shake every second. Young people can hear from about 20 to 20,000 hertz.

拍一下手。這一拍推動了空氣，這股推力以每秒大約 340 公尺的速度一波波往外傳，三分之一秒就能跑過一座足球場。聲波到達你身邊時，耳朵裡的空氣前後振動，耳朵再把這種振動變成聲音。振動得快，聽起來就高，像鳥叫；振動得慢，聽起來就低，像鼓聲。科學家用「赫茲」計算振動的次數：一赫茲就是每秒振動一次。年輕人大約聽得到 20 到 20,000 赫茲。

The part of your ear that you can see is shaped to catch sound and send it into the ear canal, a tube about 2.5 centimeters long. At the end of the canal is the eardrum, a thin, tight skin about as wide as your little fingernail. When sound waves hit it, the eardrum shakes, just like the skin of a drum. The canal also makes earwax, which traps dust and germs and slowly moves out on its own. That is why doctors say you should never push cotton swabs into your ears.

耳朵露在外面的部分，形狀剛好能接住聲音，把它送進耳道。耳道是一條大約 2.5 公分長的管子，盡頭是鼓膜：一片又薄又緊的皮膜，大約和你的小指指甲一樣寬。聲波打到鼓膜時，鼓膜就像鼓皮一樣振動。耳道也會製造耳垢，耳垢能黏住灰塵和病菌，還會自己慢慢往外移，所以醫生說絕對不要把棉花棒往耳朵裡塞。

Behind the eardrum is a tiny space filled with air, and in it are the three smallest bones in your body: the hammer, the anvil, and the stirrup. They pass the shaking along like a lever and make it about twenty times stronger. The stirrup is only about 3 millimeters long, smaller than a grain of rice. A thin tube connects this space to the back of your nose. When your ears pop in an elevator or on an airplane, that tube has just opened to let air in or out.

鼓膜後面是一個裝著空氣的小空間，裡面有全身最小的三塊骨頭：鎚骨、砧骨和鐙骨。它們像槓桿一樣把振動傳下去，還讓它增強大約二十倍。鐙骨只有大約 3 毫米長，比一粒米還小。有一條細細的管子把這個空間連到鼻子後面；搭電梯或坐飛機時耳朵「啵」一聲，就是這條管子打開，讓空氣進出。

The stirrup pushes on the cochlea, a tube rolled up like a snail shell and filled with liquid. Inside are about 15,000 hair cells, each topped with tiny hairs that bend when the liquid moves. High notes bend the hairs near the entrance of the cochlea, and low notes travel all the way to its tip. The bending makes nerve signals, which race along the hearing nerve to your brain. Each ear sends signals to both sides of the brain, and by comparing your two ears, your brain can tell which direction a sound came from.

鐙骨推動耳蝸。耳蝸是一條捲得像蝸牛殼的管子，裡面裝滿液體，有大約一萬五千個毛細胞，每個頂端都有細細的毛，液體一動，毛就會彎。高音讓耳蝸入口附近的毛彎曲，低音則一路傳到耳蝸的尖端。毛的彎曲產生神經訊號，沿著聽神經衝向大腦。每隻耳朵都把訊號送到大腦的左右兩邊，大腦比較兩隻耳朵聽到的，就能分辨聲音從哪個方向來。

Next to the cochlea are three loops filled with liquid, called the semicircular canals. They do not hear at all. Instead, they help you keep your balance. When you turn your head, the liquid in the loops sloshes and bends more hair cells, telling your brain which way you moved. If you spin around and stop suddenly, the liquid keeps swirling for a moment, so your brain thinks you are still turning. That is why you feel dizzy.

耳蝸旁邊有三個裝著液體的圓環，叫做半規管。它們完全不負責聽，而是幫你保持平衡。你轉頭時，圓環裡的液體晃動，讓更多毛細胞彎曲，告訴大腦你往哪個方向動。如果你轉圈圈又突然停下來，液體還會繼續轉一下，大腦就以為你還在轉，所以你會覺得頭暈。

Hair cells cannot grow back once they are damaged, so loud sounds can harm your hearing for life. Loudness is measured in decibels. Talking is about 60 decibels, and busy traffic is about 85. Sounds that loud or louder can harm your hearing if you listen for a long time. Firecrackers at a temple festival can be louder than 120 decibels, so cover your ears or keep your distance. With earphones, keep the volume low and take breaks. Now try the hearing test below the model and find the highest note you can hear.

毛細胞一旦受傷就長不回來，所以太大的聲音可能讓聽力永遠受損。聲音的大小用「分貝」來量：說話大約 60 分貝，車多的馬路大約 85 分貝；這麼大聲或更大聲的聲音，聽久了就可能傷害聽力。廟會的鞭炮可能超過 120 分貝，要摀住耳朵或離遠一點。戴耳機時把音量調小，並且記得休息。現在試試模型下方的聽力測驗，找出你聽得到的最高音。

Measure Yourself · 親身測量

Find the highest note you can hear 找出你聽得到的最高音

The hearing test below the model plays short, quiet notes, starting at 8,000 hertz and climbing higher. Do it in a quiet room with your device's speakers, not earphones.
模型下方的聽力測驗會播放短短的、小聲的音，從 8,000 赫茲開始一路往上。請在安靜的房間用裝置的喇叭做，不要戴耳機。

  1. 1Set the volume · 調好音量Play the 1,000 Hz test note and turn your device's volume down until the note is soft but clear. Do not use earphones.播放 1,000 赫茲的試聽音，把裝置的音量調小，調到聽起來輕輕的但清楚。不要戴耳機。
  2. 2Climb the notes · 一路往上Tap “I hear it” for each higher note. When you cannot hear a note anymore, tap “I can't.” Your score is the last note you heard.每聽到一個更高的音，就按「聽得到」；聽不到時按「聽不到」。最後一個聽得到的音，就是你的成績。
  3. kHzCompare · 比一比Compare with your classmates and with adults. Young people can often hear above 17,000 Hz, and the top note goes down as people get older. Some speakers cannot play the very highest notes.和同學、大人比一比。年輕人常常聽得到 17,000 赫茲以上，年紀越大，聽得到的最高音越低。有些喇叭本身放不出最高的音。

Myth vs. Fact · 常見迷思

Four things people get wrong 四個常見的誤會

✗ Myth · 迷思You should clean inside your ears with cotton swabs.應該用棉花棒把耳朵裡面清乾淨。

✓ Fact · 事實Earwax cleans itself out. Cotton swabs can push the wax deeper or even hurt the eardrum. Just wash the outside of your ears.耳垢會自己往外移。棉花棒反而可能把耳垢推得更深，甚至戳傷鼓膜。洗耳朵外面就好。

✗ Myth · 迷思Only old people lose their hearing.只有老人才會聽力變差。

✓ Fact · 事實Loud sounds can damage hair cells at any age, and they never grow back. That is why it matters to protect your hearing now.太大的聲音在任何年紀都可能傷害毛細胞，而且毛細胞長不回來，所以現在就要保護聽力。

✗ Myth · 迷思In space, you could hear a rocket roar.在太空中也聽得到火箭的轟隆聲。

✓ Fact · 事實Space is almost empty, with no air to shake, so sound cannot travel there. Astronauts talk to each other by radio.太空幾乎是空的，沒有空氣可以振動，所以聲音傳不過去。太空人是用無線電互相說話的。

✗ Myth · 迷思Your voice sounds the same to you as it does to everyone else.你聽到的自己的聲音，和別人聽到的一樣。

✓ Fact · 事實You hear your own voice through the air and also through the bones of your head, which makes it sound deeper. That is why your voice sounds strange in a recording.你聽自己說話，除了經過空氣，還會經過頭骨傳進來，所以聽起來比較低沉；這就是錄音裡的聲音聽起來怪怪的原因。

Remember It · 記憶口訣

Your ears in a sentence 一句話記住耳朵

Hammer, anvil, stirrup鎚骨、砧骨、鐙骨

Think of a blacksmith and a horse rider: the hammer hits the anvil, and the rider's foot rests in the stirrup. That is their order from the eardrum inward.
想像鐵匠和騎士：鎚子敲在鐵砧上，騎士的腳踩在馬鐙裡。這就是它們從鼓膜往裡排的順序。

High at the door, low at the end高音在門口，低音在盡頭

High notes bend hair cells near the entrance of the cochlea; low notes travel all the way to the tip.
高音讓耳蝸入口附近的毛細胞彎曲；低音一路走到尖端。

3 seconds = 1 kilometer3 秒＝1 公里

Count the seconds between the lightning and the thunder, then divide by 3. That is about how many kilometers away the storm is.
數一數閃電到雷聲之間有幾秒，再除以 3，大約就是雷雨離你幾公里。

60/60 for earphones耳機 60/60

A common rule of thumb: no more than 60 percent of the volume, for no more than 60 minutes at a time.
常見的簡單原則：音量不超過 60%，一次不超過 60 分鐘。

In Chinese · 中文怎麼說

Sharp Ears, Bright Eyes 耳聰目明

In Chinese, 聰明 (cōngmíng) means smart, but the two characters began with the senses: 耳聰目明, ears that hear clearly and eyes that see clearly. Look closely, and 聰 has 耳, the character for ear, on its left side. Confucius said that by sixty, his ears were “in tune” (六十而耳順): he could listen to anything calmly. And 耳濡目染, “what the ears soak up and the eyes take in,” means learning from what you hear and see every day, which is a great way to learn English, too.
中文的「聰明」是指頭腦好，但這兩個字原本講的是感官：「耳聰目明」，耳朵聽得清楚、眼睛看得明白。仔細看，「聰」字的左邊就是「耳」。孔子說自己「六十而耳順」，到了六十歲，聽什麼都能心平氣和。還有「耳濡目染」：每天聽到、看到的東西，不知不覺就學會了——這也是學英文的好方法。

Classroom Activity 1 · 課堂活動一

Paper Cup Telephone 紙杯傳聲筒

You need · 準備材料

  • Two paper cups兩個紙杯
  • 3 to 5 meters of string, and two toothpicks3 到 5 公尺長的線和兩根牙籤

Steps · 步驟

  1. Poke a small hole in the bottom of each cup. Push the string through and tie it to a toothpick inside the cup.在兩個杯底各戳一個小洞，把線穿過去，在杯子裡綁在牙籤上。
  2. Walk apart until the string is tight. One person talks into a cup, and the other listens.兩個人往後退，把線拉直。一個人對著杯子說話，另一個人聽。
  3. Now let the string go loose. Can you still hear?再把線放鬆，還聽得到嗎？
  4. Pinch the string in the middle while your partner talks. What happens?同伴說話時，用手指捏住線的中間，會發生什麼事？

Teaching tip: sound is shaking, and a tight string carries the shaking from one cup to the other. The bottom of the cup works like an eardrum. A loose or pinched string cannot pass the shaking along, just as a damaged eardrum or stiff ear bones make hearing harder.
教學提示：聲音就是振動，拉緊的線把振動從一個杯子傳到另一個杯子，杯底就像鼓膜。線鬆了或被捏住，振動就傳不過去，就像鼓膜受傷或聽小骨變僵硬時，聽力會變差。

Classroom Activity 2 · 課堂活動二

Where Did That Sound Come From? 聲音從哪裡來？

You need · 準備材料

  • A blindfold, or just closed eyes一條蒙眼布，或直接閉上眼睛
  • A chair in the middle of the room教室中間的一張椅子

Steps · 步驟

  1. One student sits in the chair with eyes closed.一位同學閉上眼睛坐在椅子上。
  2. Classmates take turns snapping their fingers from different places around the chair. The student points to where each sound came from.其他同學輪流在椅子周圍不同的地方彈手指，坐著的同學指出聲音從哪裡來。
  3. Now the student covers one ear with a hand and tries again.接著讓坐著的同學用手摀住一隻耳朵，再試一次。
  4. Count how many times they pointed the right way, with two ears and with one.算一算用兩隻耳朵和一隻耳朵時，各指對了幾次。

Teaching tip: your brain finds a sound's direction by comparing the two ears: the sound reaches the nearer ear a tiny bit sooner and a little louder. With one ear covered, the guesses usually get much worse. Sounds straight in front or straight behind are the hardest.
教學提示：大腦比較兩隻耳朵來判斷方向：聲音會比較早、比較大聲地到達比較近的那隻耳朵。摀住一隻耳朵後，通常會猜錯得多。正前方和正後方的聲音最難分辨。

⚕ Health note · 健康提醒This lesson is for learning about the body. It is not medical advice. Do not put anything into your ear canal. If you have ear pain, ringing in your ears, or trouble hearing, tell an adult. Keep earphones at a low volume, and cover your ears near firecrackers.本課是認識身體的教材，不是醫療建議。不要把任何東西放進耳道。如果耳朵痛、耳鳴，或聽不清楚，請告訴大人。戴耳機時音量要小，靠近鞭炮時要摀住耳朵。
