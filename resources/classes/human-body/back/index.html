Big idea · 一句話看懂

Your spine is a stack of bones with soft discs between them. Its gentle curves work like a spring, and it stays healthy when the load is light, close, and balanced.
脊柱是一塊一塊疊起來的骨頭，中間夾著軟軟的椎間盤。它微微的彎度像彈簧一樣；背的東西輕一點、貼近一點、兩邊平均，它就能保持健康。

Unit 23: Why Does Your Back Need Care?

Reach behind you and run a finger down the middle of your back. Do you feel a row of bumps? Each bump is the back of one bone of your spine. The spine is the tower that holds you up, from your hips to your head, all day long.

把手伸到背後，用手指沿著背的正中間往下摸。有沒有摸到一排一粒一粒的突起？每一粒都是一塊脊椎骨的後端。脊柱就是那座把你撐起來的塔，從屁股到頭，撐一整天。

The tower is not one bone but many. Each one is called a vertebra. There are seven in your neck, twelve in your upper back, and five in your lower back. Below them, five more are joined together into one bone called the sacrum, and at the very bottom is the little tailbone. Altogether a baby starts with about 33, and 24 of them stay separate so that you can bend and twist.

這座塔不是一根骨頭，而是很多塊。每一塊叫做一節脊椎骨：脖子有七節，上背有十二節，下背有五節。再往下，有五節合在一起變成一塊叫做薦骨的骨頭，最底下是小小的尾骨。嬰兒一開始總共大約有 33 節，其中 24 節一直保持分開，所以你才能彎腰、轉身。

Between the separate bones lie 23 soft cushions called discs. A disc has a tough ring on the outside and a soft gel in the middle, a little like a jelly doughnut. The discs let your spine bend, and they soak up the bumps when you run and jump. Do you remember that you are a little taller in the morning than at night? During the day your discs are squeezed, and at night, when you lie down, they fill up again.

分開的骨頭之間，夾著 23 片軟墊，叫做椎間盤。椎間盤外面是一圈堅韌的環，中間是軟軟的膠，有點像包果醬的甜甜圈。它們讓脊柱可以彎，也在你跑跳的時候吸收震動。還記得你早上比晚上高一點點嗎？白天椎間盤被壓扁，晚上躺下來，它們又重新吸飽水。

Look at a spine from the side, and you will see that it is not straight. It curves in at the neck, out at the upper back, and in again at the lower back. A newborn baby has only one long curve. The neck curve forms when the baby learns to hold up its head, and the lower curve forms when the child learns to walk. These curves make the spine stronger and springier, like the bends in a spring.

從側面看脊柱，你會發現它不是直的：脖子那裡往前凹，上背往後凸，下背又往前凹。剛出生的嬰兒只有一個長長的彎；脖子的彎，是寶寶學會把頭抬起來的時候出現的；下背的彎，是孩子學會走路的時候出現的。這些彎讓脊柱更強壯、更有彈性，就像彈簧的彎。

The spine has a second job. Every vertebra has a hole in it, and the holes line up to make a long tunnel. Inside the tunnel runs your spinal cord, the thick bundle of nerves that carries messages between your brain and your body. You met it in the lesson about the brain. The bones of your spine are its armor.

脊柱還有第二個工作。每一節脊椎骨上都有一個洞，這些洞排成一條長長的隧道。隧道裡面就是脊髓——那一大束在大腦和身體之間傳遞訊息的神經，你在大腦那一課見過它。脊椎骨就是它的盔甲。

So how do you take care of your back? Keep the load light: Taiwan's Ministry of Education suggests that a school bag should weigh no more than one eighth of your body weight. Wear both straps, and pull them snug so that the bag sits high and close. Put the heaviest books next to your back. Lift your phone or book up instead of dropping your head. And move! Your back likes changing position much more than it likes sitting still. Now check your own school bag with the tool below the model.

那麼，要怎麼照顧自己的背呢？讓負擔輕一點：教育部建議，書包的重量不要超過體重的八分之一。兩邊肩帶都背，並且拉緊，讓書包又高又貼身；最重的書放在最靠近背的地方。把手機或書本舉高，而不是把頭低下去。還有——要動！比起一直坐著不動，你的背更喜歡常常換姿勢。現在用模型下方的工具，檢查一下你自己的書包吧。

Questions · 閱讀理解

What lies between the separate bones of your spine, and what does it do?

Soft cushions called discs. They let the spine bend, and they soak up bumps.

What are the two jobs of the spine?

It holds your body up, and it protects the spinal cord inside it.

Name three ways to take care of your back when you carry a school bag.

Keep the bag light (no more than one eighth of your body weight), wear both straps and pull them snug, and put the heaviest books next to your back.

Five parts of the spine, from top to bottom 脊柱的五段，從上到下

Why a stack and not one long bone? One bone could not bend. A stack with cushions in between can bend a little at every level, and all those little bends add up to touching your toes.
為什麼是一疊，而不是一根長骨頭？一根骨頭沒辦法彎。一疊骨頭、中間夾著軟墊，每一層都能彎一點點；這些一點點加起來，你就摸得到腳趾了。

Use the bag check below the model. You need a scale. The numbers stay on your screen: nothing is saved or sent anywhere. The tool looks only at the bag.
用模型下方的書包檢查工具。你需要一台體重計。數字只留在你的螢幕上，不會儲存，也不會傳到任何地方。這個工具只看書包。

  1. ⚖️Weigh the bag · 秤書包Put your packed school bag on the scale. If the scale cannot read something so light, stand on it with the bag and without it, and subtract.把裝好的書包放到體重計上。如果東西太輕、體重計量不出來，就先背著書包站上去，再放下書包站上去，兩個數字相減。

Myth vs. Fact · 常見迷思

Four things people get wrong 四個常見的誤會

✗ Myth · 迷思A healthy spine is perfectly straight.健康的脊柱是筆直的。

✓ Fact · 事實Seen from the side, a healthy spine has curves. They make it stronger and springier.從側面看，健康的脊柱是有彎度的。這些彎讓它更強壯、更有彈性。

✗ Myth · 迷思The spine and the spinal cord are the same thing.脊柱和脊髓是同一個東西。

✓ Fact · 事實The spine is bone. The spinal cord is a bundle of nerves that runs inside it.脊柱是骨頭；脊髓是在它裡面的一束神經。

✗ Myth · 迷思It does not matter how you carry a bag, only how heavy it is.書包怎麼背都沒關係，只要不重就好。

✓ Fact · 事實Both matter. The same bag pulls harder when it hangs low or from one shoulder.兩件事都重要。同一個書包，背得太低或只背一邊，拉扯的力量都更大。

✗ Myth · 迷思Sitting very still and very straight is the best thing for your back.坐得直挺挺、一動也不動，對背最好。

✓ Fact · 事實Your back likes to move. Change your position often, and get up and stretch.你的背喜歡動。常常換姿勢，站起來伸展一下。

In Chinese · 中文怎麼說

Backbone and Dragon Bone 脊梁與龍骨

In English, to say that someone is brave and will not give in, you say she “has backbone.” Chinese uses the same picture: a person who stands up for what is right is said to 挺直腰桿 (tǐngzhí yāogǎn), “straighten the back,” and the people a country depends on are called its 脊梁 (jǐliang), its spine. In Taiwanese, the spine has an even grander name: 龍骨 (lêng-kut), the “dragon bone.” The long central beam at the bottom of a ship is also called 龍骨 in Chinese, and “keel” in English. A ship and a person are built the same way: one strong line down the middle, and everything else hangs from it.
英文說一個人勇敢、不肯屈服，會說她 has backbone（有脊梁骨）。中文用的是同一個畫面：為對的事站出來，叫「挺直腰桿」；國家所倚靠的人，叫做「脊梁」。台語給脊柱取了一個更氣派的名字：「龍骨」。船底正中間那根長長的大梁，中文也叫龍骨，英文叫 keel。船和人的造法是一樣的：正中間一條結實的主幹，其他的一切都掛在它上面。

Classroom Activity 1 · 課堂活動一

Build a Spine 疊一條脊柱

You need · 準備材料

  • Eight empty thread spools or thick slices of a cardboard tube八個空線軸，或切成厚片的紙筒
  • Eight pieces of kitchen sponge with a hole in the middle八片中間有洞的菜瓜布海綿
  • A piece of string, 50 centimeters long一條 50 公分長的繩子

Steps · 步驟

  1. Thread the spools onto the string with nothing between them, and tie the ends. Try to bend the tower.只把線軸串在繩子上，中間什麼都不放，兩端打結。試著把這座塔彎一彎。
  2. Now take it apart and build it again, with a piece of sponge between every two spools.現在拆開重新串一次，每兩個線軸之間夾一片海綿。
  3. Bend the new tower. What is different?把新的塔彎一彎。有什麼不一樣？
  4. Press down on the top. What happens to the sponges?從上面往下壓。海綿怎麼了？

Teaching tip: hard pieces alone grind against each other and hardly bend. Hard and soft together bend smoothly and spring back. Pressing down shows the discs being squeezed, which is why we are a little shorter by evening. The string stands for the spinal cord.
教學提示：只有硬的零件，會互相摩擦、幾乎彎不動；硬的和軟的交替，就彎得順，也彈得回來。往下壓可以看到「椎間盤」被壓扁——這就是我們到了傍晚會矮一點點的原因。那條繩子代表脊髓。

Classroom Activity 2 · 課堂活動二

The School Bag Survey 書包大調查

You need · 準備材料

  • A bathroom scale or a luggage scale一台體重計或行李秤
  • A chart on the board黑板上畫一張圖表

Steps · 步驟

  1. Weigh every school bag in the class. Weigh only the bags, not the students.把全班的書包都秤一秤。只秤書包，不秤人。
  2. Mark each weight with an X on the chart. No names are needed.每個重量在圖表上畫一個 X，不必寫名字。
  3. What is the lightest bag? The heaviest? What weight is the most common?最輕的書包多重？最重的呢？哪一個重量最常見？
  4. Empty one heavy bag together. What is in it that could stay at school?一起把一個很重的書包倒出來看看：裡面有哪些東西可以留在學校？

Teaching tip: weigh the bags only, and keep the chart anonymous. Children do not need to share their body weight with anyone; they can use the tool on this page privately at home. The useful discussion is about the bags: water bottles, books for other days, and what the class could keep in the classroom.
教學提示：只秤書包，圖表保持匿名。孩子不需要告訴任何人自己的體重；想算比例的話，可以回家自己用這一頁的工具。值得討論的是書包本身：水壺、別天才用到的課本，還有哪些東西全班可以一起放在教室。

⚕ Health note · 健康提醒This lesson is for learning about the body. It is not medical advice, and it is not about anyone's weight: the tool looks only at the bag. If your back, neck, or shoulders hurt often, or if you feel numbness or tingling in your arms or legs, tell an adult and see a doctor.本課是認識身體的教材，不是醫療建議，也不是在談任何人的體重：工具只看書包。如果你的背、脖子或肩膀常常痛，或是手腳覺得麻麻、刺刺的，請告訴大人，並且去看醫生。
