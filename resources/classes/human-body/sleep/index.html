Unit 15: Why Do You Need Sleep?

You will spend about a third of your life asleep. It can seem like a waste of time: you lie still, your eyes are closed, and nothing happens. But sleep is not like switching off a light. Your brain stays busy all night, and your body uses the quiet hours to do jobs it cannot do well while you are running around.

你的一生大約有三分之一的時間在睡覺。這聽起來好像很浪費：躺著不動、眼睛閉著，什麼事都沒發生。但是睡覺並不像關燈。你的大腦整晚都在忙，身體也利用這段安靜的時間，做那些你跑來跑去時做不好的工作。

Sleep comes in stages. First comes light sleep: your muscles relax, and your heartbeat and breathing slow down. Next comes deep sleep, when your brain waves turn big and slow and it is very hard to wake you. Then comes REM sleep. REM stands for rapid eye movement, because your eyes dart around under your eyelids. In REM sleep your brain is almost as busy as when you are awake, and you have your most vivid dreams. Your big muscles are switched off, so you do not act your dreams out. Scientists in Chicago first described REM sleep in 1953.

睡眠分成幾個階段。先是淺睡：肌肉放鬆，心跳和呼吸變慢。接著是深睡，腦波變得又大又慢，很難把你叫醒。然後是快速動眼期，英文叫 REM（rapid eye movement），因為眼球會在眼皮底下快速轉動。這個階段大腦幾乎和醒著時一樣忙，最鮮明的夢就在這時候。你的大肌肉被關掉了，所以不會把夢裡的動作真的做出來。1953 年，芝加哥的科學家首次描述了快速動眼期。

The stages repeat in cycles, and in an adult one cycle takes about an hour and a half. You go through several cycles each night, but they are not all the same. Most of your deep sleep comes in the first few hours. The REM periods start short and grow longer toward morning. So if you go to bed too late, you lose deep sleep, and if you get up too early, you lose dreams.

這些階段會一輪一輪地重複，大人的一輪大約一個半小時。每晚你會經過好幾輪，但每一輪並不一樣：深睡大多集中在前幾個小時；快速動眼期一開始很短，越接近早上越長。所以太晚睡，少掉的是深睡；太早起，少掉的是夢。

What is all this for? One job is memory. Sleep helps your brain turn what you learned today into long-term memories. That is why a good night's sleep before a test helps more than staying up late to study. Another job is growing. Your body releases growth hormone during deep sleep, which helps you grow and helps your body repair itself. Sleep also helps you fight germs: people who do not sleep enough are more likely to catch colds.

這一切是為了什麼？第一件事是記憶：睡眠幫助大腦把今天學到的東西變成長期記憶。所以考試前好好睡一覺，比熬夜念書更有用。第二件事是長大：身體在深睡時分泌生長激素，幫助你長高，也幫助身體修補自己。睡眠還幫你對抗病菌：睡不夠的人比較容易感冒。

How does your body know when to sleep? You have a body clock deep inside your brain, and it is set by light. When your eyes see daylight, the clock knows it is day. In the evening, as it gets dark, your brain starts to release a hormone called melatonin, which tells the body that night has come. Bright light late in the evening, including the light from screens, can stop your brain from releasing melatonin. Then you do not feel sleepy, even when your body needs rest.

身體怎麼知道什麼時候該睡？你的大腦深處有一個生理時鐘，它是用光來對時的。眼睛看到日光，時鐘就知道現在是白天。到了傍晚，天色變暗，大腦開始分泌一種叫做褪黑激素的激素，告訴身體：晚上到了。深夜的強光——包括螢幕的光——會讓大腦停止分泌褪黑激素，於是身體明明需要休息，你卻不覺得想睡。

How much sleep do you need? More than adults do. Sleep doctors say children from 6 to 12 years old need 9 to 12 hours every night, and teenagers need 8 to 10. You can help your body clock. Go to bed and get up at the same time every day, even on weekends. Turn off screens at least 30 minutes before bedtime. Keep your room dark, quiet, and cool, and skip tea and cola in the evening. Now use the sleep calculator below the model: how many hours did you sleep last night?

你需要睡多久？比大人多。睡眠醫學專家說，6 到 12 歲的孩子每晚需要 9 到 12 小時，青少年需要 8 到 10 小時。你可以幫生理時鐘一個忙：每天在同樣的時間上床、起床，週末也一樣；睡前至少 30 分鐘關掉螢幕；房間保持黑暗、安靜、涼爽；晚上不要喝茶和可樂。現在用模型下方的睡眠計算機算一算：你昨晚睡了幾個小時？

Questions · 閱讀理解

What happens in your brain and body during REM sleep?

Your eyes dart around, your brain is almost as busy as when you are awake, and you have your most vivid dreams. Your big muscles are switched off.

Why do you lose deep sleep if you go to bed too late?

Because most deep sleep comes in the first few hours of the night.

How can light from a screen keep you awake?

Bright light late in the evening can stop the brain from releasing melatonin, the hormone that tells the body night has come.

Myth vs. Fact · 常見迷思

Four things people get wrong 四個常見的誤會

✗ Myth · 迷思When you sleep, your brain switches off.睡覺的時候，大腦就關機了。

✓ Fact · 事實Your brain stays busy all night. In REM sleep it is almost as active as when you are awake.大腦整晚都在忙。快速動眼期時，它幾乎和醒著時一樣活躍。

✗ Myth · 迷思Some people never dream.有些人從來不做夢。

✓ Fact · 事實Almost everyone dreams every night. We forget most dreams unless we wake up in the middle of one.幾乎每個人每晚都會做夢。除非剛好在夢的中途醒來，否則大部分的夢都會忘記。

✗ Myth · 迷思Staying up late to study helps you remember more.熬夜念書可以記得更多。

✓ Fact · 事實Sleep is when your brain turns the day's lessons into long-term memories. Cut the sleep, and you cut the saving.睡覺時，大腦才把當天學的東西變成長期記憶。少睡了，存檔也就少了。

✗ Myth · 迷思Watching videos in bed is a good way to get sleepy.躺在床上看影片，比較容易想睡。

✓ Fact · 事實Bright light late in the evening can stop your brain from releasing melatonin. Turn screens off at least 30 minutes before bedtime.深夜的強光會讓大腦停止分泌褪黑激素。睡前至少 30 分鐘就把螢幕關掉。

In Chinese · 中文怎麼說

A Night, an Inch 一暝大一寸

Taiwanese grandparents say 一暝大一寸 (chi̍t-mî tōa chi̍t-chhùn) to a sleeping baby: “one night, one inch bigger.” Nobody grows an inch in a night, but the saying holds a truth that science found much later: the body releases growth hormone during deep sleep. Mandarin has 早睡早起身體好, “early to bed and early to rise is good for your health,” and English has an old rhyme that says almost the same thing: “Early to bed and early to rise makes a man healthy, wealthy, and wise.”
台灣的阿公阿嬤會對睡著的寶寶說「一暝大一寸」：睡一晚，長大一寸。沒有人真的一個晚上長一寸，但這句話裡藏著科學很久以後才發現的道理：身體在深睡時分泌生長激素。華語說「早睡早起身體好」，英文也有一句幾乎一樣的老話：“Early to bed and early to rise makes a man healthy, wealthy, and wise.”（早睡早起，使人健康、富有又聰明。）

Classroom Activity 1 · 課堂活動一

My Sleep Diary 我的睡眠日記

You need · 準備材料

  • A chart with seven rows: bedtime, wake-up time, hours of sleep, and “How do I feel?” (1 to 5)一張七列的表格：幾點睡、幾點起床、睡了幾小時，以及「精神如何？」（1 到 5 分）
  • A pencil next to your bed床邊放一枝鉛筆

Steps · 步驟

  1. Every morning for one week, write down when you fell asleep and when you woke up.連續一個星期，每天早上寫下你幾點睡著、幾點醒來。
  2. Work out the hours. Give yourself a score from 1 (very sleepy) to 5 (wide awake) at 10 o'clock in the morning.算出睡了幾小時。早上十點時，幫自己的精神打分數：1 分（很想睡）到 5 分（非常清醒）。
  3. Make a bar graph of your seven nights. Draw a line at 9 hours.把七個晚上畫成長條圖，在 9 小時的地方畫一條線。
  4. On which days did you feel best? What was different about the night before?哪幾天精神最好？前一晚有什麼不一樣？

Teaching tip: this is good practice with time: the hours cross midnight, so students cannot simply subtract. Keep the results private or anonymous, because bedtimes depend on family schedules that children do not control. Look for patterns in the whole class instead of comparing students.
教學提示：這是練習時間計算的好機會——時數會跨過半夜，不能直接相減。結果請保密或匿名，因為就寢時間常常取決於家裡的作息，不是孩子能決定的。請看全班的趨勢，不要比較個別學生。

Classroom Activity 2 · 課堂活動二

The Screens-Off Experiment 關螢幕實驗

You need · 準備材料

  • Your sleep diary from Activity 1活動一的睡眠日記
  • A book, drawing paper, or a quiet game for the last half hour of the day一本書、圖畫紙，或是一個安靜的遊戲，給睡前半小時用

Steps · 步驟

  1. For three nights, do what you usually do before bed. Record how long it takes you to fall asleep, and your morning score.前三個晚上，照平常的方式度過睡前時間。記下你花多久才睡著，以及隔天早上的精神分數。
  2. For the next three nights, turn off all screens 30 minutes before bedtime and do something quiet.接下來三個晚上，睡前 30 分鐘關掉所有螢幕，做一件安靜的事。
  3. Compare the two sets of nights.比較這兩組晚上。
  4. Was there a difference? What else might explain it?有差別嗎？還有什麼原因可能造成這個差別？

Teaching tip: this is a real experiment with a real weakness, and it is worth saying so: three nights is a small sample, and students know which nights are which. Ask them how they would make the test fairer. Send a note home first, so that families can join in.
教學提示：這是一個真正的實驗，也有真正的弱點，值得直接告訴學生：三個晚上的樣本很小，而且學生自己知道哪幾晚是實驗組。問問他們：要怎麼讓這個實驗更公平？請先發一張通知單回家，讓家人一起參與。

⚕ Health note · 健康提醒This lesson is for learning about the body. It is not medical advice. Everyone's sleep is a little different. If you often cannot fall asleep, if you snore loudly, or if you feel very sleepy in class most days, tell an adult. Do not take anything to help you sleep unless a doctor says so.本課是認識身體的教材，不是醫療建議。每個人的睡眠都有一點不一樣。如果你常常睡不著、打呼很大聲，或是大部分的日子上課都很想睡，請告訴大人。除非醫生說可以，不要吃任何幫助睡眠的東西。
