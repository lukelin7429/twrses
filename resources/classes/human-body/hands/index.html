Why Are Your Hands So Clever?手為什麼這麼靈巧？

Each hand has 27 bones, but the muscles that bend your fingers are up in your forearm. Pull the “strings” of a real 3D hand, make a fist and a pinch, and test what your thumb can do.
每隻手有 27 塊骨頭，可是彎手指的肌肉卻在前臂。在 3D 裡拉拉真實手骨的「線」，握拳、捏東西，再測測你的大拇指有多厲害。

Big idea · 一句話看懂

Your fingers have no muscles of their own. Muscles in your forearm pull on long tendons, like strings on a puppet, and your thumb can touch every other finger.
手指裡面沒有肌肉：前臂的肌肉拉著長長的肌腱，像拉木偶的線；而大拇指碰得到其他每一根手指。

Reading · 英文閱讀

18

Unit 18: Why Are Your Hands So Clever?

Wiggle your fingers and watch the back of your hand. Do you see thin cords moving under the skin? Now hold your forearm with your other hand and wiggle again. You can feel muscles working near your elbow, far away from your fingers. Your hand is one of the cleverest tools in nature, and it works a little like a puppet.

動一動手指，看看你的手背。有沒有看到皮膚底下有細細的條索在動？現在用另一隻手握住前臂，再動一動手指：你會感覺到靠近手肘的地方有肌肉在用力，離手指好遠。手是大自然裡最靈巧的工具之一，而它運作的方式有點像提線木偶。

Start with the bones. One hand has 27 of them: 8 small bones in the wrist, 5 in the palm, and 14 in the fingers. Each finger has three bones, but the thumb has only two. With so many bones and joints, your hand can wrap around a ball, fit into a glove, or flatten out to clap.

先從骨頭說起。一隻手有 27 塊骨頭：手腕 8 塊小骨頭，手掌 5 塊，手指 14 塊。每根手指有三節骨頭，只有大拇指是兩節。有這麼多骨頭和關節，手才能包住一顆球、套進手套，或是攤平了拍手。

Now the surprise. There are no muscles in your fingers. The muscles that bend and straighten them are in your forearm, and they are joined to your finger bones by long, strong cords called tendons. When a forearm muscle gets shorter, it pulls its tendon, and the tendon pulls the finger, like the string of a puppet. That is why your fingers can be thin and quick: the heavy engines are kept somewhere else. The palm does have some small muscles of its own, for spreading the fingers and for moving the thumb.

接下來是驚喜：手指裡面沒有肌肉。負責彎曲和伸直手指的肌肉在前臂，靠著又長又結實的條索連到指骨上，這些條索叫做肌腱。前臂的肌肉一縮短，就拉動它的肌腱，肌腱再拉動手指——就像木偶的線。所以你的手指才能又細又靈活：笨重的引擎放在別的地方。手掌裡倒是有一些自己的小肌肉，負責把手指張開，以及移動大拇指。

The thumb is the star. At its base is a joint shaped like a saddle, which lets the thumb swing across the palm. It can touch the tip of every other finger. This is called an opposable thumb. Try it now: thumb to index finger, middle finger, ring finger, and little finger. Without that move, you could not hold a pencil, turn a key, or button a shirt.

大拇指是主角。它的根部有一個形狀像馬鞍的關節，讓大拇指能橫過手掌，碰到其他每一根手指的指尖——這叫做「可以對掌的拇指」。現在就試試：大拇指碰食指、中指、無名指、小指。少了這個動作，你就沒辦法握鉛筆、轉鑰匙，或是扣鈕扣。

Your hand has two main ways to hold things. In a power grip, all the fingers wrap around something and squeeze, as when you hold a bat or carry a bucket. In a precision grip, the thumb presses against the fingertips, as when you pick up a coin or thread a needle. Your hand switches between the two without your having to think about it.

手拿東西主要有兩種方式。「力量型的握法」是所有手指包住東西、用力握緊，例如握球棒或提水桶。「精細型的握法」是大拇指抵著指尖，例如撿硬幣或穿針。你的手會在這兩種握法之間自動切換，根本不用你去想。

A hand is not only for holding. Your fingertips have more nerve endings packed into them than almost any other part of your body, so they can feel a single hair or read the dots of braille. Most people use one hand more than the other: about nine people in ten are right-handed. Left-handed or right-handed, your hands learn from practice. Now tape down your thumb and try the thumb challenge below the model.

手不只是用來拿東西。指尖裡擠滿了神經末梢，比身體幾乎任何其他地方都密，所以摸得出一根頭髮，也讀得出點字。大多數人比較常用其中一隻手：大約每十個人裡有九個是右撇子。不管你是左撇子還是右撇子，手都是越練越巧的。現在把大拇指貼起來，試試模型下方的拇指挑戰。

Questions · 閱讀理解

How many bones are in one hand, and where are they?

There are 27: 8 in the wrist, 5 in the palm, and 14 in the fingers.

Where are the muscles that bend your fingers, and how do they reach the fingers?

They are in the forearm. Long cords called tendons join them to the finger bones.

What is the difference between a power grip and a precision grip?

In a power grip, all the fingers wrap around something and squeeze. In a precision grip, the thumb presses against the fingertips.

Myth vs. Fact · 常見迷思

Four things people get wrong 四個常見的誤會

✗ Myth · 迷思Your fingers are full of muscles.手指裡面都是肌肉。

✓ Fact · 事實There are no muscles in the fingers. Forearm muscles move them by pulling on tendons, and small muscles in the palm help.手指裡沒有肌肉。前臂的肌肉拉著肌腱來移動它們，手掌裡的小肌肉從旁幫忙。

✗ Myth · 迷思The thumb is just a short finger.大拇指只是比較短的手指。

✓ Fact · 事實The thumb has two bones instead of three, its own muscles, and a saddle joint that lets it swing across the palm.大拇指只有兩節骨頭（不是三節），有自己的肌肉，還有一個鞍狀關節，讓它能橫過手掌。

✗ Myth · 迷思Left-handed people are very rare.左撇子非常少見。

✓ Fact · 事實About one person in ten is left-handed. In a class of thirty, that is likely to be about three students.大約每十個人就有一個是左撇子。一個三十人的班級裡，大概會有三位。

✗ Myth · 迷思Strong hands come from big hands.手大，握力才會大。

✓ Fact · 事實Grip strength comes mostly from the forearm muscles that pull the tendons. That is why climbers have strong forearms.握力主要來自拉動肌腱的前臂肌肉，所以攀岩的人前臂都很結實。

In Chinese · 中文怎麼說

Ten Fingers, One Heart 十指連心

Chinese says 十指連心 (shí zhǐ lián xīn), “the ten fingers are joined to the heart”: hurt one finger, and the whole person feels it. Science agrees in its own way, because fingertips are packed with nerve endings. A skillful person is 手巧 (shǒu qiǎo), “hand-clever,” and when a job goes smoothly, people say 得心應手, “what the heart wants, the hand does.” Chinese even measures with the hand: 一把米 is a handful of rice that your fingers close around, and 一捧水 is the water you can carry in two cupped hands. English counts in hands too: a “handful,” a “pinch” of salt, and a horse's height is still measured in “hands.”
中文說「十指連心」：傷了一根手指，整個人都感覺得到。科學用自己的方式同意這句話——指尖擠滿了神經末梢。手藝好的人叫「手巧」；事情做得順，就說「得心應手」：心裡想的，手就做得到。中文甚至用手來量東西：「一把米」是手指握得住的米，「一捧水」是兩手合起來捧得住的水。英文也用手來數：a handful（一把）、a pinch of salt（一小撮鹽），而馬的身高到今天還是用 hands（掌寬）來量。

Classroom Activity 1 · 課堂活動一

String Hand 拉線手

You need · 準備材料

  • Thin cardboard, five drinking straws, and tape薄紙板、五根吸管和膠帶
  • Five pieces of string, each about 40 centimeters long, and scissors五條大約 40 公分長的棉線，和剪刀

Steps · 步驟

  1. Trace your hand on the cardboard and cut it out. Fold each finger gently where your own joints are.在紙板上描出自己的手並剪下來。在每根手指上，照自己關節的位置輕輕摺出摺痕。
  2. Cut the straws into short pieces and tape one piece on each part of every finger, with gaps at the folds. Tape a long piece on the palm below each finger.把吸管剪成小段，每一節指節貼一段，摺痕的地方留空；每根手指下方的手掌上各貼一段長的。
  3. Thread a string through the straws of each finger, and tape its end at the fingertip.每根手指穿一條線過那些吸管，線頭用膠帶固定在指尖。
  4. Hold the wrist and pull the strings one at a time. Can you make a fist? A pinch? A thumbs-up?握住手腕，一條一條地拉線。你能讓它握拳嗎？能捏嗎？能比讚嗎？

Teaching tip: the straws are the bones, the strings are the tendons, and the student's own pulling hand is the forearm muscle. Notice that a string can only pull: to open the hand again, something else has to pull the other way. That is why the back of your hand has its own set of tendons.
教學提示：吸管是骨頭，棉線是肌腱，學生拉線的那隻手就是前臂的肌肉。請注意：線只能拉，不能推——要把手重新張開，得有別的東西往反方向拉。這就是手背另外有一組肌腱的原因。

Classroom Activity 2 · 課堂活動二

Which Hand? 哪一隻手？

You need · 準備材料

  • Paper and a pencil for each student每個學生一張紙和一枝鉛筆
  • A tally chart on the board黑板上的劃記表

Steps · 步驟

  1. Write your name with the hand you usually use. Time it.用平常慣用的那隻手寫名字，並計時。
  2. Now write it with your other hand. Time it again, and compare the two.換另一隻手再寫一次並計時，比較這兩次。
  3. On the board, tally how many students write with the right hand and how many with the left.在黑板上劃記：班上有幾個人用右手寫字，幾個人用左手。
  4. Is your class close to nine in ten? Practice with your other hand for a week. Does it get faster?你們班接近十分之九嗎？用另一隻手練習一個星期，有沒有變快？

Teaching tip: left-handed students should never be made to switch. Use the activity to show that the “other” hand is not weak, only less practiced: the muscles and tendons are the same, and the difference is in the brain's practice. A small class may be far from nine in ten, and that is a good chance to talk about small samples.
教學提示：絕對不要要求左撇子的學生改用右手。請用這個活動讓學生明白，「另一隻手」並不是比較弱，只是比較少練習：肌肉和肌腱都一樣，差別在大腦練得多不多。小班級的比例可能離十分之九很遠——這正是討論「樣本太小」的好機會。

⚕ Health note · 健康提醒This lesson is for learning about the body. It is not medical advice. Use paper tape for the thumb challenge, keep it loose, and take it off if anything hurts or tingles. If a finger is swollen, will not bend, or hurts after a fall, tell an adult.本課是認識身體的教材，不是醫療建議。拇指挑戰請用紙膠帶，貼鬆一點；如果會痛或覺得麻，就馬上撕掉。如果手指腫起來、彎不了，或是跌倒之後會痛，請告訴大人。
