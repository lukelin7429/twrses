Your skeleton is a living frame. It holds you up, protects your organs, gives your muscles something to pull on, and makes new blood cells every day.

Unit 1: Why Do We Need a Skeleton?

Imagine for a moment that every bone in your body disappeared. You could not stand, sit up, or even hold up your head. You would slump to the floor like a pile of laundry. Some animals, such as jellyfish and octopuses, get along fine without bones, but they live in water, which helps hold them up. On land, a large animal needs a strong frame inside it. Your frame is your skeleton: about 206 bones, from the tiny ones deep inside your ears to the long thighbone in each leg.

想像一下，你身上的每一塊骨頭突然都消失了。你沒辦法站、沒辦法坐起來，連頭都抬不起來，整個人會像一堆換下來的衣服一樣癱在地上。有些動物，像水母和章魚，沒有骨頭也活得好好的，但牠們住在水裡，水會幫忙撐住身體。在陸地上，體型大的動物身體裡需要一副堅固的支架。你的支架就是骨骼：大約 206 塊骨頭，從耳朵深處的小骨頭，到每條腿裡長長的股骨。

The first job of the skeleton is support. Your spine, a stack of 24 small bones called vertebrae resting on a larger bone called the sacrum, holds up your head and body like the pole of a tent. Your pelvis passes that weight to your legs, and your legs carry it down to your feet. The second job is protection. Your skull is a hard helmet around your brain. Your ribs form a cage around your heart and lungs, and your vertebrae make a bony tunnel that shields your spinal cord, the bundle of nerves that carries messages between your brain and the rest of your body.

骨骼的第一個工作是支撐。你的脊柱由 24 塊叫做椎骨的小骨頭疊成，底下坐在一塊比較大的薦骨上，像帳篷的營柱一樣撐起頭和身體。骨盆把這些重量傳到雙腿，雙腿再一路傳到腳底。第二個工作是保護。頭顱骨是包住大腦的硬安全帽；肋骨圍成一個籠子，保護心臟和肺；椎骨則連成一條骨頭隧道，守護脊髓——那一束在大腦和全身之間傳遞訊息的神經。

The third job is movement. Bones cannot move by themselves, but muscles are attached to them, and when a muscle gets shorter, it pulls on a bone. The places where two bones meet are called joints. Some joints, like your elbow and knee, bend in one direction, like a door on its hinges. Others, like your shoulder and hip, are ball-and-socket joints that let you swing an arm or a leg in a circle. Your hands are especially clever. Each one has 27 bones, which is why you can write, button a shirt, and pick up a peanut with chopsticks.

第三個工作是運動。骨頭自己不會動，但肌肉附著在骨頭上，肌肉一縮短，就會拉動骨頭。兩塊骨頭相接的地方叫做關節。有些關節，像手肘和膝蓋，只往一個方向彎，就像門繞著鉸鏈開關；有些關節，像肩膀和髖部，是球窩關節，讓你可以把手臂或腿甩成一個圓。你的手特別靈巧：每隻手有 27 塊骨頭，所以你才能寫字、扣扣子，還能用筷子夾起一顆花生。

The fourth and fifth jobs are hidden inside. Many bones are filled with a soft tissue called marrow. Red marrow, which in adults is found mostly in flat bones such as the ribs, hips, and vertebrae, makes new blood cells, hundreds of billions of them every day. They include red blood cells that carry oxygen, white blood cells that fight germs, and platelets that help a cut stop bleeding. Bones also store minerals, especially calcium. When your heart and nerves need more calcium than your food provides, your bones release some into your blood.

第四和第五個工作藏在骨頭裡面。許多骨頭裡充滿一種柔軟的組織，叫做骨髓。紅骨髓在成人身上主要位於扁平的骨頭裡，例如肋骨、髖骨和椎骨，它負責製造新的血球，每天多達幾千億個：有運送氧氣的紅血球、對抗病菌的白血球，還有幫助傷口止血的血小板。骨頭也儲存礦物質，特別是鈣。當心臟和神經需要的鈣比食物提供的還多時，骨頭就會釋放一些鈣到血液裡。

It is easy to think of bones as dead, dry sticks, like the skeleton that hangs in a science classroom. But the bones inside you are alive. They have blood vessels and nerves, which is one reason a broken bone hurts so much, and they are always rebuilding themselves. Some cells break down old bone while others build new bone, so over about ten years, most of an adult's skeleton is replaced. A newborn baby has around 300 bones and bony parts. As a child grows, many of them join together, which is why an adult has only about 206.

我們很容易把骨頭想成死掉的乾柴，就像掛在自然教室裡的那副骨架。但你身體裡的骨頭是活的：它們有血管和神經，這也是骨折會那麼痛的原因之一；而且骨頭一直在重建自己。有些細胞負責拆掉舊骨頭，另一些細胞負責蓋新骨頭，所以大約十年之間，成人的骨骼大部分都會換新。剛出生的嬰兒大約有 300 塊骨頭和骨質部分；孩子長大的過程中，其中許多會癒合在一起，所以成人只剩大約 206 塊。

Because bone is alive, it responds to how you treat it. When you run, jump, and play, the extra force tells your bones to grow thicker and stronger. Astronauts who spend months in space, where their bones carry almost no weight, lose bone and must exercise every day to slow the loss. Your bones reach their greatest strength by your late twenties, and much of it is built while you are still in school. So move every day, and eat foods with calcium, such as milk, tofu, small dried fish, and dark green vegetables. Then try this: count the bones in one of your hands.

因為骨頭是活的，它會回應你怎麼對待它。當你跑、跳、玩耍，多出來的力量會告訴骨頭：要長得更粗、更強壯。在太空待上好幾個月的太空人，骨頭幾乎不用承受重量，骨量就會流失，所以他們每天都得運動來減緩流失。骨骼在二十多歲後段達到最強壯的時候，而其中很大一部分是在求學階段打下的基礎。所以每天都要動一動，也要吃含鈣的食物，例如牛奶、豆腐、小魚乾和深綠色蔬菜。然後試試看：數一數你一隻手有幾塊骨頭。

Questions · 閱讀理解

Why can a jellyfish live without bones, when a large land animal cannot?

The jellyfish lives in water, which helps hold its body up. On land, nothing holds a large body up, so it needs a strong frame inside it.

Name the three parts of the skeleton that protect organs, and say what each one protects.

The skull protects the brain, the ribs protect the heart and lungs, and the vertebrae protect the spinal cord.

Why does an adult have fewer bones than a newborn baby?

A newborn has around 300 bones and bony parts, and many of them join together as a child grows, leaving about 206 in an adult.

Myth vs. Fact · 常見迷思

Four things people get wrong 四個常見的誤會

✗ Myth · 迷思Bones are dead and dry, like sticks.骨頭是死的、乾的，像木棍一樣。

✓ Fact · 事實Living bones have blood vessels and nerves, and cells inside them are always tearing down old bone and building new bone.活的骨頭有血管和神經，裡面的細胞一直在拆掉舊骨頭、蓋新骨頭。

✗ Myth · 迷思Babies have fewer bones than adults.嬰兒的骨頭比大人少。

✓ Fact · 事實It is the other way around. A newborn has around 300 bones and bony parts, and many join together as the child grows.剛好相反。新生兒大約有 300 塊骨頭和骨質部分，長大的過程中許多會癒合在一起。

✗ Myth · 迷思Bones are solid all the way through.骨頭從外到內都是實心的。

✓ Fact · 事實Most bones have a hard outer shell and a light, honeycomb-like inside, often with marrow in the spaces. That makes them strong but not too heavy.大多數骨頭外層是堅硬的殼，裡面是像蜂巢一樣輕巧的結構，空隙裡常常有骨髓。這樣既堅固又不會太重。

✗ Myth · 迷思Once you are grown, your bones stop changing.長大以後，骨頭就不會再改變了。

✓ Fact · 事實Adult bones keep rebuilding themselves all through life. Exercise makes them stronger, and months without using them, as in space or in a cast, makes them weaker.成人的骨頭一輩子都在重建。運動讓它們更強壯；好幾個月不用它們（像在太空，或打石膏的時候）就會變弱。

Bones in a sentence 一句話記住骨頭

Breakfast at 7, lunch at 12, dinner at 5早餐 7 點、午餐 12 點、晚餐 5 點

27 + 27 + 26 + 26 = 106：你全身一半以上的骨頭都在手和腳。

Everyday names for big bones大骨頭的俗名

Femur = thighbone, patella = kneecap, clavicle = collarbone, scapula = shoulder blade, sternum = breastbone. Doctors use the first names; everyone else uses the second.
femur＝thighbone（股骨）、patella＝kneecap（髕骨）、clavicle＝collarbone（鎖骨）、scapula＝shoulder blade（肩胛骨）、sternum＝breastbone（胸骨）。醫生用前面那個名字，一般人用後面那個。

In Chinese · 中文怎麼說

Sinews and Bones in Chinese 中文裡的「筋骨」

Chinese often talks about bones together with 筋 (jīn), the sinews that tie muscles and bones together. Someone healthy and strong has good 筋骨, and a serious injury is 傷筋動骨, “hurting the sinews and moving the bones.” A common saying, 傷筋動骨一百天, says such an injury takes a hundred days to heal. It is a saying, not a medical rule, but it makes the same point doctors do: bones heal, but slowly.
中文常把骨頭和「筋」（連接肌肉與骨頭的韌帶、肌腱）放在一起講：身體健壯叫「筋骨好」，嚴重的傷叫「傷筋動骨」。俗話說「傷筋動骨一百天」，意思是這種傷要一百天才會好。這是俗語，不是醫學規則，但它和醫生說的是同一件事：骨頭會癒合，只是很慢。

Classroom Activity 1 · 課堂活動一

Why Are Bones Shaped Like Tubes? 骨頭為什麼是管子的形狀？

You need · 準備材料

  • Three sheets of the same printer paper三張一樣的影印紙
  • Tape膠帶
  • A stack of light books or notebooks一疊輕的書或筆記本

Steps · 步驟

  1. Fold one sheet into a flat strip, fold one into a square column, and roll one into a round tube. Tape each one so it keeps its shape.一張摺成扁平的長條，一張摺成方柱，一張捲成圓管，各用膠帶固定。
  2. Stand each one on its end and gently place books on top, one at a time.把它們一一立起來，在上面一本一本輕輕放書。
  3. Count how many books each shape holds before it bends.記錄每種形狀在彎掉之前撐得住幾本書。
  4. Ask: the same paper, the same weight. Why does the round tube hold the most? Where in your body are the tube-shaped bones?問問自己：同樣的紙、同樣的重量，為什麼圓管撐得最多？你身上哪些骨頭是管子的形狀？

Teaching tip: then turn the 3D model to the legs and arms. The long bones are hollow tubes filled with marrow, strong for their weight like the paper tube.
教學提示：做完再把 3D 模型轉到手臂和腿。長骨就是裡面裝著骨髓的空心管，跟紙管一樣，用很少的重量換來很大的強度。

  • A jar with a lid, and enough white vinegar to cover the bone一個有蓋的罐子，和足夠蓋過骨頭的白醋
  • A second clean bone to compare另一根洗乾淨的骨頭，用來對照

Steps · 步驟

  1. Try to bend the bones. They are hard and stiff.試著把骨頭折彎：它們又硬又直。
  2. Put one bone in the jar, cover it with vinegar, and close the lid. Leave the other bone dry.把一根骨頭放進罐子，倒醋蓋過，蓋上蓋子；另一根保持乾燥。
  3. After three days, pour out the vinegar and add fresh vinegar. Wait about three more days.三天後把醋倒掉、換新的醋，再等大約三天。
  4. Rinse the bone and try to bend it again. Compare it with the dry bone. What did the vinegar take away?把骨頭沖乾淨，再試著折彎，和乾燥的那根比一比。醋帶走了什麼？

Teaching tip: the vinegar dissolves the calcium, leaving the soft, bendy framework behind. Bones need both: calcium for hardness and the framework for toughness. Wash hands after handling raw chicken.
教學提示：醋溶掉了鈣，留下柔軟、可以彎曲的骨架纖維。骨頭兩樣都需要：鈣讓它堅硬，纖維讓它有韌性、不易斷。碰過生雞肉之後要洗手。

⚕ Health note · 健康提醒This lesson is for learning about the body. It is not medical advice. If you think you have hurt a bone, tell an adult and see a doctor.本課是認識身體的教材，不是醫療建議。如果覺得骨頭受傷了，請告訴大人並就醫。
