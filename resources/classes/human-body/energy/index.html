Reading · 英文閱讀

21

Unit 21: Where Does Your Energy Come From?

Have you ever felt weak and grumpy just before lunch, and then much better after eating? Food is your fuel. A car burns gasoline, and a phone runs on its battery. You run on rice, noodles, eggs, vegetables, and fruit. But how does a bowl of rice turn into a kick of a ball?

你有沒有過這種經驗：午餐前全身沒力、脾氣也不好，吃完飯就好多了？食物就是你的燃料。汽車燒汽油，手機靠電池，而你靠的是飯、麵、蛋、青菜和水果。可是，一碗飯是怎麼變成踢球的那一腳的呢？

First, your body breaks the food down. You learned in the lesson about lunch that digestion starts in your mouth and goes on in your stomach and small intestine. Rice, bread, and noodles are mostly starch, and starch is made of long chains of sugar. Your body cuts the chains into single pieces of a sugar called glucose. Glucose is small enough to pass through the wall of the small intestine and into your blood.

首先，身體要把食物分解。你在午餐那一課學過，消化從嘴巴開始，在胃和小腸繼續。飯、麵包和麵條大部分是澱粉，而澱粉是由一長串的糖接起來的。身體把這些長鏈剪成一個一個的糖，這種糖叫做葡萄糖。葡萄糖夠小，可以穿過小腸的壁，進到血液裡。

From the intestine, the blood goes first to your liver. The liver is your sugar bank. When a lot of sugar arrives, a small organ behind your stomach, the pancreas, sends out a signal called insulin. Insulin tells your liver and your muscles to take sugar out of the blood and store it. Hours later, when no food is coming in, the liver lets its stored sugar back out, a little at a time. That is how your blood sugar stays steady between meals, and even while you sleep.

離開小腸的血，會先流到肝臟。肝臟是你的糖銀行。當很多糖一起送到時，胃後面一個小小的器官——胰臟——會送出一種叫做胰島素的訊號。胰島素告訴肝臟和肌肉：把血液裡的糖拿出來存好。幾個小時後，沒有食物進來了，肝臟再把存起來的糖一點一點放回血液。這就是為什麼兩餐之間、甚至你睡覺的時候，血糖都能保持穩定。

The blood carries the glucose to every cell. Inside the cell, glucose meets the oxygen you breathed in, and the cell “burns” the sugar slowly, without a flame, to get energy. What is left over is water and carbon dioxide, which you breathe out. So eating and breathing are two halves of one job. Your muscles use this energy to move. Your brain is a big user, too: an adult's brain takes about a fifth of the energy the body uses at rest. And your body uses energy all night just to stay warm and keep your heart beating.

血液把葡萄糖送到每一個細胞。在細胞裡，葡萄糖遇上你吸進來的氧氣，細胞就把糖慢慢地「燒」掉——沒有火焰——換成能量。剩下來的是水和二氧化碳，二氧化碳再被你呼出去。所以吃東西和呼吸，其實是同一件工作的兩半。肌肉用這些能量來運動；大腦也是用電大戶：大人休息的時候，大腦就用掉全身大約五分之一的能量。而身體整個晚上都在用能量，只為了保持溫暖、讓心臟繼續跳。

Not every food gives up its sugar at the same speed. In a sweet drink, the sugar is already dissolved, so it rushes into your blood. Your blood sugar shoots up, a lot of insulin follows, and soon the sugar is put away and you feel hungry again. A meal with rice, an egg, and vegetables is broken down slowly, so the sugar arrives little by little, and the energy lasts all morning.

不是每一種食物都用同樣的速度把糖交出來。含糖飲料裡的糖本來就是溶開的，所以一下子就衝進血液：血糖飆高，大量的胰島素跟著出動，沒多久糖就被收走，你又餓了。有飯、有蛋、有青菜的一餐會被慢慢分解，糖一點一點地到，能量可以撐一整個早上。

Your body needs more than sugar, of course. It needs protein to build muscles, calcium for bones, and many other things, and no single food has them all. That is why Taiwan's Health Promotion Administration draws a plate with six food groups: whole grains, protein foods, vegetables, fruit, dairy, and nuts and seeds. It even has a rhyme, and one line says that you should have as much rice as vegetables. Now use the plate tool below the model: which groups did you eat today?

當然，身體需要的不只是糖。它需要蛋白質來造肌肉、需要鈣來長骨頭，還需要很多別的東西，而沒有任何一種食物樣樣都有。所以國民健康署畫了一個盤子，上面有六大類食物：全穀雜糧、豆魚蛋肉、蔬菜、水果、乳品、堅果種子，還編了口訣，其中一句是「飯跟蔬菜一樣多」。現在用模型下方的餐盤工具看一看：今天你吃到了哪幾類？

Questions · 閱讀理解

What does your body turn rice, bread, and noodles into?

Into a sugar called glucose, which is small enough to pass into the blood.

What does the liver do with sugar?

It stores sugar when a lot arrives, and it lets the sugar back out into the blood, a little at a time, when no food is coming in.

Why does a meal with rice, an egg, and vegetables give longer-lasting energy than a sweet drink?

Because the meal is broken down slowly, so the sugar arrives little by little. The sugar in a drink is already dissolved and rushes in all at once.

Myth vs. Fact · 常見迷思

Four things people get wrong 四個常見的誤會

✗ Myth · 迷思A sweet drink gives you energy that lasts.喝含糖飲料，可以讓你有力氣很久。

✓ Fact · 事實The sugar arrives fast and is put away fast. A meal that is digested slowly keeps you going much longer.那些糖來得快，也被收得快。慢慢消化的一餐，才能讓你撐得久。

✗ Myth · 迷思You only use energy when you move.只有在動的時候才會用到能量。

✓ Fact · 事實Your brain, your heart, and keeping warm all use energy, every minute, even while you sleep.大腦、心臟，還有保持體溫，每一分鐘都在用能量——連睡覺的時候也是。

✗ Myth · 迷思Everything you eat is used right away.吃下去的東西，馬上就全部用掉了。

✓ Fact · 事實Your liver and muscles store sugar after a meal, and the liver gives it back for hours afterward.飯後，肝臟和肌肉會把糖存起來；接下來的好幾個小時，肝臟再把它放回來。

✗ Myth · 迷思Fat in food is always bad for you.食物裡的油脂一定對身體不好。

✓ Fact · 事實Your body needs some fat. That is why “My Plate” includes a teaspoon of nuts and seeds.身體需要一些油脂，所以「我的餐盤」裡有一茶匙的堅果種子。

In Chinese · 中文怎麼說

Full First, Then Strong 吃飽了才有力氣

Chinese has a word for the strength you feel in your body: 力氣 (lìqi). Parents say 吃飽了才有力氣, “eat your fill, and then you will have strength,” and science agrees: no fuel, no energy. An old saying goes even further: 民以食為天, “to the people, food is heaven.” Notice the greeting, too. For generations, people in Taiwan have said hello with 吃飽了沒？ or, in Taiwanese, 食飽未？ (chia̍h-pá-bōe), “Have you eaten yet?” English speakers ask “How are you?” Both are really asking the same thing: is your body all right?
中文用「力氣」來說身體裡感覺到的那股勁。爸媽常說「吃飽了才有力氣」，科學也同意：沒有燃料，就沒有能量。古話說得更重：「民以食為天。」再看看打招呼的方式：好幾代以來，台灣人見面就問「吃飽了沒？」，台語說「食飽未？」；說英文的人則問 How are you?（你好嗎？）。兩種問法，問的其實是同一件事：你的身體還好嗎？

Classroom Activity 1 · 課堂活動一

Our Class Breakfast Chart 全班的早餐圖

You need · 準備材料

  • Six columns on the board, one for each food group黑板上畫六欄，每一類食物一欄
  • One small sticky note for each student每個學生幾張小便利貼

Steps · 步驟

  1. Think about what you had for breakfast today. No names are needed.想一想你今天早餐吃了什麼。不必寫名字。
  2. Put a sticky note in the column of every food group that was in your breakfast.你的早餐裡有哪幾類食物，就在那幾欄各貼一張便利貼。
  3. Look at the chart. Which group has the most notes? Which has the fewest?看看這張圖：哪一類的便利貼最多？哪一類最少？
  4. Plan one breakfast for the class that covers at least four groups.為全班設計一份早餐，至少要包含四類食物。

Teaching tip: keep it anonymous, and do not comment on any one child's breakfast. What a child eats in the morning depends on the family's time and money, not on the child. Talk about the class pattern, and about small changes that are possible, such as adding a piece of fruit.
教學提示：請保持匿名，也不要評論任何一個孩子的早餐。孩子早上吃什麼，取決於家裡的時間和經濟，不是孩子自己能決定的。請談全班的整體情況，以及做得到的小改變，例如多加一份水果。

Classroom Activity 2 · 課堂活動二

Chew the Rice 嚼一嚼白飯

You need · 準備材料

  • A spoonful of plain cooked rice for each student每個學生一湯匙白飯
  • A clock with a second hand有秒針的時鐘

Steps · 步驟

  1. Put the rice in your mouth. What does it taste like at first?把飯放進嘴裡。一開始是什麼味道？
  2. Chew slowly for one full minute without swallowing.慢慢嚼滿一分鐘，不要吞下去。
  3. What does it taste like now?現在是什麼味道？
  4. Where did the new taste come from?新的味道是哪裡來的？

Teaching tip: the rice turns a little sweet, because saliva begins to cut the starch chains into sugar. This is the first step of the journey in the 3D model, and students can taste it. Check for food allergies first, as in every tasting activity.
教學提示：飯會變得有一點甜，因為口水開始把澱粉的長鏈剪成糖——這正是 3D 模型裡那趟旅程的第一步，而且學生可以親口嚐到。和所有試吃活動一樣，請先確認有沒有人對食物過敏。

⚕ Health note · 健康提醒This lesson is for learning about the body. It is not medical advice, and it is not about anyone's weight or shape. Bodies come in many sizes, and children should not go on diets unless a doctor says so. If you often feel dizzy, very thirsty, or very tired, tell an adult.本課是認識身體的教材，不是醫療建議，也不是在談任何人的體重或身材。每個人的身體本來就不一樣，除非醫生說要，小朋友不應該節食。如果你常常頭暈、非常口渴，或是非常疲倦，請告訴大人。
