Unit 10: What Does Your Skin Do?

Your skin is your body's biggest organ. Spread out flat, an adult's skin would cover about 2 square meters, about the size of a single bed. It is waterproof, it stretches, and it repairs itself when you get a scrape. Most importantly, it is a wall that keeps germs, dirt, and harmful chemicals outside your body, and it keeps the water inside your body from drying out.

皮膚是身體最大的器官。把一個大人的皮膚攤平，大約有 2 平方公尺，差不多是一張單人床那麼大。它防水、有彈性，擦傷了還會自己修補。最重要的是，它像一道牆，把病菌、髒東西和有害的化學物質擋在身體外面，也讓身體裡的水分不會乾掉。

Skin has three layers. The top layer, the epidermis, is thinner than a sheet of paper on most of your body. Its outside is made of flat, dead cells that flake off all the time, and new cells from below replace the whole layer about once a month. Under it is the dermis, a thicker, stretchy layer that holds blood vessels, nerves, sweat glands, and the roots of your hairs. At the bottom is a layer of fat that works like a cushion and a warm blanket.

皮膚有三層。最上面一層是表皮，身體大部分地方的表皮比一張紙還薄。表皮的外面是扁平的死細胞，一直在剝落，下面長出來的新細胞大約一個月就把整層換新。表皮下面是真皮，比較厚、有彈性，裡面有血管、神經、汗腺和毛髮的根。最底下是一層脂肪，像坐墊也像保暖的毯子。

Skin also helps control your temperature. You have between 2 and 4 million sweat glands. On a hot day, they pour sweat onto your skin, and as the sweat evaporates, it carries heat away. The blood vessels near the surface also widen to let heat out, which is why you look red after running. When you are cold, those blood vessels narrow, and tiny muscles pull your hairs upright, giving you goosebumps.

皮膚也幫忙控制體溫。你有兩百萬到四百萬個汗腺。大熱天時，汗腺把汗水送到皮膚上，汗水蒸發時把熱帶走。靠近表面的血管也會變寬讓熱散出去，所以跑步之後臉紅紅的。覺得冷的時候，這些血管會變窄，小小的肌肉把毛拉直，就起了雞皮疙瘩。

Your skin is full of sensors. Some feel a light touch, some feel a hard press, and others feel heat, cold, itching, and pain. Your fingertips and lips have the most, which is why you read with your fingertips when you feel for something in the dark. Your fingertips also have ridges called fingerprints. They formed before you were born, and no two people have the same ones, not even identical twins.

皮膚裡到處都是感受器：有的感覺輕輕的碰觸，有的感覺用力的按壓，還有的感覺熱、冷、癢和痛。指尖和嘴唇最多，所以在黑暗中找東西時，你會用指尖去摸。指尖還有一條條的紋路，叫做指紋。指紋在你出生以前就形成了，每個人都不一樣，連同卵雙胞胎也不同。

Sunlight is good for skin in small amounts, because skin uses it to make vitamin D, which you need for strong bones. But sunlight also carries ultraviolet light, or UV, which you cannot see. To protect itself, skin makes a dark substance called melanin, and a tan is a sign that your skin has already been harmed by UV. Too much UV causes sunburn, and over many years it can lead to wrinkles and skin cancer.

少量的陽光對皮膚有好處，因為皮膚會用它製造維生素 D，那是骨骼強壯需要的。但陽光也帶著看不見的紫外線。為了保護自己，皮膚會製造一種深色的物質，叫做黑色素；曬黑其實表示皮膚已經被紫外線傷害了。紫外線太多會曬傷，長年下來還可能造成皺紋和皮膚癌。

Scientists measure the strength of UV with the UV index. When it is 3 or higher, you need protection, and in Taiwan it often goes above 10 on summer days around noon. A simple test: if your shadow is shorter than you are, the sun is strong. Wear a hat, cover up, look for shade, and use sunscreen. Now check how strong the sun is in Changhua today with the tool below the model.

科學家用「紫外線指數」來量紫外線的強度。指數 3 以上就需要防護；在台灣，夏天中午前後常常超過 10。有個簡單的測試：如果你的影子比你的身高短，太陽就很強。戴帽子、穿長袖、找陰涼的地方、擦防曬乳。現在用模型下方的小工具，看看今天彰化的太陽有多強。
Read aloud · 課文朗讀

Questions · 閱讀理解

What are the three layers of the skin?

The epidermis on top, the dermis in the middle, and a layer of fat at the bottom.

How does sweat help you cool down?

Sweat comes out onto the skin, and as it evaporates, it carries heat away from your body.

Why is a tan not a sign of healthy skin?

Skin makes melanin to protect itself from UV, so a tan shows that the skin has already been harmed by UV.

Three layers and their helpers 三層皮膚和它們的幫手

把皮膚想成一棟房子：表皮是屋頂，真皮是牆壁，裡面藏著水管和電線，皮下脂肪則是地板下的隔熱層。

Myth vs. Fact · 常見迷思

Four things people get wrong 四個常見的誤會

✗ Myth · 迷思You can't get sunburned on a cloudy day.陰天不會曬傷。

✓ Fact · 事實Most UV passes through light clouds. You can get burned on a cloudy day, especially around noon in summer.大部分的紫外線能穿過薄雲。陰天一樣會曬傷，尤其是夏天的中午前後。

✗ Myth · 迷思A tan means your skin is healthy.曬黑代表皮膚很健康。

✓ Fact · 事實A tan is your skin's way of protecting itself after UV has already damaged it.曬黑是皮膚在被紫外線傷害之後，努力保護自己的結果。

✗ Myth · 迷思Sweat itself smells bad.汗本身就很臭。

✓ Fact · 事實Fresh sweat has almost no smell. The smell comes from bacteria on your skin breaking the sweat down.剛流出來的汗幾乎沒有味道；臭味是皮膚上的細菌分解汗水產生的。

✗ Myth · 迷思Goosebumps keep people warm.雞皮疙瘩能讓人保暖。

✓ Fact · 事實In furry animals, raised fur traps a layer of warm air. People have so little body hair that goosebumps help only a little.毛很多的動物把毛豎起來，可以留住一層暖空氣；人的體毛太少，雞皮疙瘩只能幫一點點忙。

Remember It · 記憶口訣

Your skin in a sentence 一句話記住皮膚

Short shadow, strong sun影子短，太陽強

If your shadow is shorter than you are, the sun is high and its UV is strong.
影子比身高短，表示太陽很高，紫外線很強。

Slip, slop, slap穿、擦、戴

A famous sun-safety rule from Australia: slip on a shirt, slop on sunscreen, slap on a hat.
澳洲有名的防曬口號：穿上衣服、擦上防曬乳、戴上帽子。

Epi means on topEpi 就是「在上面」

The epidermis sits on top of the dermis. Derma is Greek for skin.
epidermis（表皮）在 dermis（真皮）的上面；derma 是希臘文的「皮膚」。

Sweat cools by leaving汗水離開才降溫

Sweat cools you only when it evaporates. Dripping sweat that just falls off does not help much.
汗水要蒸發才會帶走熱；直接滴下來的汗幫助不大。

In Chinese · 中文怎麼說

Goose Skin, Chicken Skin 雞皮疙瘩

English says goosebumps, and Chinese says 雞皮疙瘩 (jīpí gēda), “chicken-skin bumps.” Both languages compared our cold skin to a plucked bird. The classic 《孝經》 (Classic of Filial Piety) begins with a line many Taiwanese students know: 身體髮膚，受之父母 (shēntǐ fàfū, shòu zhī fùmǔ), “your body, hair, and skin come from your parents,” so taking care of them is the first way to show respect. Hats and sunscreen count!
英文說 goosebumps（鵝的疙瘩），中文說「雞皮疙瘩」：兩種語言都把冷到起疙瘩的皮膚，比作拔了毛的鳥。《孝經》開頭有一句很多台灣學生都背過：「身體髮膚，受之父母，不敢毀傷，孝之始也。」好好照顧自己的身體和皮膚，就是孝順的開始——戴帽子、擦防曬也算喔！

Classroom Activity 1 · 課堂活動一

Fingerprint Detectives 指紋偵探

You need · 準備材料

  • A soft pencil and a sheet of paper一支軟芯鉛筆和一張紙
  • Clear tape and a magnifying glass透明膠帶和一個放大鏡

Steps · 步驟

  1. Rub the pencil hard on the paper to make a dark patch.用鉛筆在紙上用力塗出一塊黑黑的區域。
  2. Rub your fingertip on the patch, then press a piece of tape onto your fingertip.用指尖在黑色區域上抹一抹，再把一段膠帶貼在指尖上。
  3. Peel off the tape and stick it on white paper. Look at it with the magnifying glass.把膠帶撕下來貼在白紙上，用放大鏡看。
  4. Is it a loop, a whorl, or an arch? Count each kind in the class.它是箕形、斗形還是弓形？統計全班每種各有幾個。

Teaching tip: loops are the most common pattern, then whorls, and arches are the rarest. Even identical twins have different fingerprints, because tiny differences in the womb shape the ridges.
教學提示：箕形最常見，其次是斗形，弓形最少。連同卵雙胞胎的指紋都不一樣，因為在媽媽肚子裡的小小差異，就會影響紋路的形成。

Classroom Activity 2 · 課堂活動二

The Sweat Test 汗水實驗

You need · 準備材料

  • Two thermometers, or just your two hands兩支溫度計，或直接用兩隻手
  • A wet paper towel and a fan or a piece of cardboard一張濕紙巾，以及電風扇或一塊紙板

Steps · 步驟

  1. Wrap a wet paper towel around the end of one thermometer. Leave the other one dry.用濕紙巾包住一支溫度計的末端，另一支保持乾的。
  2. Fan both thermometers for three minutes.對兩支溫度計搧風三分鐘。
  3. Read both temperatures. Which one is cooler?讀出兩支的溫度，哪一支比較低？
  4. No thermometers? Wet the back of one hand, then blow on both hands. Which feels cooler?沒有溫度計？把一隻手的手背弄濕，再對兩隻手吹氣，哪一隻覺得比較涼？

Teaching tip: the wet one cools down because evaporating water carries heat away, just like sweat. On a humid day, sweat evaporates slowly, which is why sticky summer days in Taiwan feel so hot.
教學提示：濕的那一支溫度比較低，因為水蒸發時把熱帶走，就像汗水一樣。潮濕的日子汗水不容易蒸發，所以台灣悶熱的夏天特別難受。

⚕ Health note · 健康提醒This lesson is for learning about the body. It is not medical advice. Use shade, hats, and sunscreen when the UV index is 3 or higher. If a sunburn blisters, a rash spreads, a cut gets red, hot, and swollen, or a mole changes shape or color, tell an adult.本課是認識身體的教材，不是醫療建議。紫外線指數 3 以上時，要找陰涼處、戴帽子、擦防曬乳。如果曬傷起水泡、疹子越來越大、傷口又紅又熱又腫，或痣的形狀、顏色改變，請告訴大人。
