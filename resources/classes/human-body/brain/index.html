Big idea · 一句話看懂

Your nerves carry signals in two directions: from your senses to your brain, and from your brain to your muscles. For some emergencies, your spinal cord takes a shortcut and acts before your brain even knows.

    Reading · 英文閱讀

    7

    Unit 7: How Does Your Brain Talk to Your Body?

    Someone tosses you a ball. In less than half a second, your eyes see it, your brain figures out where it is going, and your arms move to catch it. All of that is the work of your nervous system: your brain, your spinal cord, and the nerves that reach every part of your body. An adult brain weighs only about 1.4 kilograms, but it has around 86 billion nerve cells, and it uses about a fifth of all the energy your body burns.

    有人丟給你一顆球。不到半秒，你的眼睛看到它、大腦算出它要往哪裡飛、手臂就動起來接住它。這一切都是神經系統的工作：大腦、脊髓，以及通到身體每個角落的神經。成人的大腦只有大約 1.4 公斤，卻有大約 860 億個神經細胞，而且用掉全身大約五分之一的能量。

    Nerve cells, called neurons, are long and thin, and they pass messages as tiny electrical signals. The fastest signals travel at about 120 meters per second, more than 400 kilometers an hour, which is faster than Taiwan's high-speed rail. Nerves are bundles of these cells, and they carry messages in two directions. Sensory nerves bring news in from your eyes, ears, skin, and other senses. Motor nerves carry orders out to your muscles.

    神經細胞又叫神經元，又長又細，靠微小的電訊號傳遞訊息。最快的訊號每秒大約跑 120 公尺，也就是時速四百多公里，比台灣高鐵還快。神經是一束束的神經細胞，往兩個方向傳遞訊息：感覺神經把眼睛、耳朵、皮膚等感官的消息送進來，運動神經把命令送出去給肌肉。

    Your spinal cord is the main cable. It is about as thick as your finger and about 45 centimeters long, protected inside your backbone, and pairs of nerves branch off it to every part of the body. At the top sits the brain. The large, wrinkly cerebrum handles thinking, the senses, and movement. Its two halves cross over: the left half runs the right side of your body, and the right half runs the left. The cerebellum at the back helps with balance and smooth movement, and the brainstem keeps you breathing and keeps your heart beating without you ever thinking about it.

    脊髓是主幹線。它大約和手指一樣粗、大約 45 公分長，受到脊柱的保護，一對對的神經從它分出去，通到身體各處。最上面是大腦。又大又皺的大腦負責思考、感覺和動作，它的左右兩半是交叉的：左半邊管身體右側，右半邊管身體左側。後面的小腦幫忙維持平衡、讓動作順暢；腦幹則讓你持續呼吸、心臟持續跳動，完全不需要你去想。

    Some messages never wait for the brain. If you touch a hot pan, a signal shoots to your spinal cord, and the spinal cord sends an order straight back: pull away! Your hand moves before you even feel the pain, which arrives a moment later on slower nerves. This kind of automatic, lightning-fast action is called a reflex. When a doctor taps just below your knee and your leg kicks, that is a reflex too, and it shows the doctor that your nerves are working.

    有些訊息根本不等大腦。如果你碰到燙鍋子，訊號會衝到脊髓，脊髓直接回送命令：縮回來！你的手在感覺到痛之前就已經移開了，痛覺要再過一下子，才沿著比較慢的神經抵達。這種自動、閃電般的動作叫做反射。醫生輕敲你膝蓋下方、你的腿自己踢出去，也是一種反射，這讓醫生知道你的神經運作正常。

    Things that need a decision take longer. To catch a falling ruler, your eyes must see it, your brain must decide, and then the order must travel down to your hand. For most people, that takes about two tenths of a second, and during that time the ruler falls about 20 centimeters. Practice can make you a little faster. Being tired or distracted, for example by looking at a phone, makes you slower, which is why it is dangerous to cross the street while texting.

    需要做決定的事就比較慢。要接住一把掉下來的尺，眼睛得先看到、大腦得做決定，命令再一路傳到手。大多數人大約要零點二秒，這段時間裡尺大約掉了 20 公分。練習能讓你快一點；累了或分心（例如看手機）就會變慢，所以邊走路邊滑手機過馬路很危險。

    Your brain changes every time you learn something. Each time you practice a skill, the connections between your neurons get stronger, which is why practice makes things easier. Sleep matters too: while you sleep, your brain sorts and stores what you learned during the day. And because your brain cannot be replaced, wear a helmet when you ride a bike or scooter. Now try the reaction test below the model and see how far your ruler falls.

    你每學一樣新東西，大腦就會改變一點。每練習一次，神經元之間的連結就更強，所以越練越容易。睡眠也很重要：睡覺時，大腦會整理、儲存你白天學到的東西。而且大腦無法替換，所以騎腳踏車或滑板車時要戴安全帽。現在試試看模型下方的反應測驗，看看你的尺會掉多遠。

    Questions · 閱讀理解

    What is the difference between sensory nerves and motor nerves?

    Sensory nerves carry messages from the senses in to the spinal cord and brain. Motor nerves carry orders out to the muscles.

    Why does your hand pull away from a hot pan before you feel the pain?

    It is a reflex: the signal goes to the spinal cord, which sends an order straight back to the arm without waiting for the brain. The pain arrives later on slower nerves.

    Why is it dangerous to cross the street while texting?

    Being distracted slows your reaction time, so it takes longer to see a car, decide, and move out of the way.

    Myth vs. Fact · 常見迷思

    Four things people get wrong 四個常見的誤會

    ✗ Myth · 迷思We only use 10 percent of our brains.我們只用了大腦的 10%。

    ✓ Fact · 事實Brain scans show that every part of the brain is active at some point during the day. Even during sleep, large parts of it are busy.腦部掃描顯示，一天當中大腦的每個部位都會活躍。就連睡覺時，大腦的許多部分也在忙。

    ✗ Myth · 迷思People are either left-brained or right-brained.人分成「左腦型」和「右腦型」。

    ✓ Fact · 事實Each half does some jobs better, but healthy people use both halves together all the time. Studies have not found people who rely on one side more than the other.左右腦各有比較擅長的工作，但健康的人隨時都是兩邊一起用。研究並沒有發現哪些人比較依賴某一邊。

    ✗ Myth · 迷思Every movement you make is decided by your brain.你的每個動作都是大腦決定的。

    ✓ Fact · 事實Reflexes, like pulling away from heat or the knee-jerk, are handled by the spinal cord before the brain even finds out.反射動作，例如碰到燙的東西縮手、膝跳反射，是脊髓處理的，大腦都還來不及知道。

    ✗ Myth · 迷思A bigger brain always means a smarter animal.腦越大的動物越聰明。

    ✓ Fact · 事實A sperm whale's brain is about five times heavier than yours. What matters more is how the brain is built and how its cells are connected.抹香鯨的腦大約比你的重五倍。更重要的是大腦的構造，以及腦細胞怎麼連結。

    Remember It · 記憶口訣

    Your nervous system in a sentence 一句話記住神經系統

    Sense, decide, act感覺、決定、行動

    Senses send news in, the brain decides, and muscles act. A reflex skips the middle step.
    感官把消息送進來、大腦決定、肌肉行動；反射跳過了中間那一步。

    The left runs the right左腦管右邊

    The two halves of the brain cross over. When you write with your right hand, your left brain is giving the orders.
    左右腦是交叉的。你用右手寫字時，下命令的是左腦。

    A reflex is a shortcut反射是捷徑

    Hand to spinal cord and straight back again. The brain gets a copy of the message, but only afterward.
    從手到脊髓，再直接回去。大腦也會收到訊息的副本，只是晚了一步。

    Faster than a bullet train比高鐵還快

    The fastest nerve signals reach about 430 km/h; Taiwan's high-speed rail tops out at about 300 km/h.
    最快的神經訊號約時速 430 公里；台灣高鐵最快約時速 300 公里。

    In Chinese · 中文怎麼說

    Where the Word 神經 Came From 「神經」這個詞從哪裡來

    The word for nerve, 神經 (shénjīng), was made up in Japan in 1774, when the doctor Sugita Genpaku translated a Dutch anatomy book. He joined 神, from 神氣 (spirit), with 經, from 經脈 (the body's channels in Chinese medicine), and the word later spread to Chinese. Everyday Chinese also talks about the brain with 腦筋: to think hard is 動腦筋, “move your brain sinews,” and a brain teaser is a 腦筋急轉彎, “a sharp turn for the brain.”
    「神經」這個詞是 1774 年在日本造出來的：醫師杉田玄白翻譯一本荷蘭解剖書時，取「神氣」的「神」和中醫「經脈」的「經」組成這個詞，後來才傳進中文。日常中文也用「腦筋」講大腦：用心思考叫「動腦筋」，猜謎題叫「腦筋急轉彎」。

    Classroom Activity 1 · 課堂活動一

    The Ruler Drop League 直尺接接樂

    You need · 準備材料

    • A 30 cm ruler for each pair每兩人一把 30 公分直尺
    • A results table on the board黑板上的結果表

    Steps · 步驟

    1. Each student does five catches with the hand they write with and five with the other hand.每個人用寫字的手接五次，再用另一隻手接五次。
    2. Write down the distance each time and find the middle value.每次都記下距離，找出中間值。
    3. Do five more catches while counting backward from 100 by sevens out loud.再接五次，這次一邊大聲從 100 開始每次減 7 倒數。
    4. Compare: writing hand vs. other hand, and focused vs. distracted.比較：寫字的手 vs. 另一隻手、專心 vs. 分心。

    Teaching tip: the distracted round is usually slower, which connects straight to the danger of using a phone while crossing the street. Use the conversion table in the Measure Yourself section, or the on-screen test, to turn centimeters into seconds.
    教學提示：分心的那一輪通常比較慢，正好可以連結到邊滑手機邊過馬路的危險。用「親身測量」段落的換算表，或電腦版測驗，把公分換成秒數。

    Classroom Activity 2 · 課堂活動二

    The Two-Point Touch Test 兩點觸覺測驗

    You need · 準備材料

    • Two blunt pencils held together with a rubber band, or an unbent paper clip with its two ends about 1 cm apart兩支鈍頭鉛筆用橡皮筋綁在一起，或把迴紋針扳開、兩端相距約 1 公分
    • A partner and a blindfold or closed eyes一位同伴，閉上眼睛或蒙眼

    Steps · 步驟

    1. With your eyes closed, your partner gently touches your fingertip with either one point or both points.你閉上眼睛，同伴用一個點或兩個點輕輕碰你的指尖。
    2. Say “one” or “two.” Try ten times and count how many you get right.說出「一點」或「兩點」，試十次，算答對幾次。
    3. Repeat on the back of your hand and on your forearm.在手背和前臂上重複一次。
    4. Where could you tell two points apart most easily? Why might that be?哪裡最容易分辨出兩個點？為什麼？

    Teaching tip: fingertips have many more touch sensors than the forearm, and a bigger area of the brain's touch map is given to them. Press gently, and never use sharp points.
    教學提示：指尖的觸覺感受器比前臂多得多，大腦的觸覺地圖也分給它們比較大的區域。要輕輕地碰，絕對不要用尖銳的東西。

    ⚕ Health note · 健康提醒This lesson is for learning about the body. It is not medical advice. If you hit your head and feel dizzy, sick to your stomach, or very sleepy, tell an adult right away. Wear a helmet when you ride, and keep your phone away while crossing the street.本課是認識身體的教材，不是醫療建議。如果撞到頭之後覺得頭暈、想吐，或特別想睡，請馬上告訴大人。騎車要戴安全帽，過馬路時不要看手機。
