How Do You Remember?你是怎麼記住東西的？

A memory is a path in your brain that gets stronger each time you use it. Follow one in 3D, watch it fade and come back, and test the best way to learn new words.
記憶是大腦裡的一條路，每用一次就更牢固。在 3D 裡跟著一條記憶走，看它變淡又回來，再親自試試哪種方法最能記住新單字。

Big idea · 一句話看懂

You remember what you use. Each time you pull a memory back out, its path grows stronger, and sleep helps to save it.
用過的才記得住。每一次把記憶「拿出來」，那條路就更牢固一點；睡覺則幫忙把它存好。

Unit 28: How Do You Remember?

What did you have for breakfast today? What is your best friend's name? How do you ride a bike? You answered all three in a moment, but each answer was stored in a different way. Remembering feels like one simple thing. It is really several things, and you can get better at all of them.

你今天早餐吃了什麼？你最好的朋友叫什麼名字？腳踏車要怎麼騎？這三個問題你一下子就答出來了，可是每一個答案的儲存方式都不一樣。「記得」感覺上是很單純的一件事，其實是好幾件事——而且每一件你都可以越做越好。

Deep inside your brain, one on each side, lie two small curved parts called the hippocampus. Each is about five centimeters long and shaped a little like a seahorse, and that is what the name means. When something new happens, the sight is handled in one part of your brain, the sound in another, and the meaning in another. The hippocampus ties these pieces together into one memory.

在大腦的深處，左右各有一個小小的、彎彎的部分，叫做海馬迴。每一個大約五公分長，形狀有點像海馬，它的名字就是這樣來的。有新的事情發生時，「看到的」由大腦的一個地方處理，「聽到的」由另一個地方處理，「意思」又在另一個地方。海馬迴把這些片段綁在一起，變成一條記憶。

A new memory is weak. More than a hundred years ago, a German scientist named Hermann Ebbinghaus memorized lists of nonsense words and then tested himself again and again. He found that forgetting is fastest at the start. Much of a new memory can be gone within days, unless you do something about it.

新的記憶是很弱的。一百多年前，德國科學家艾賓浩斯（Hermann Ebbinghaus）背了一串串沒有意義的音節，然後一次又一次地考自己。他發現：遺忘在一開始最快。如果什麼都不做，一條新的記憶可能在幾天之內就不見了一大半。

What can you do? Use it. Every time you pull a memory out of your own head, the path gets stronger. That is why covering the page and testing yourself works better than reading the page again. Reading again feels easy, but your brain does little work. Recalling feels hard, and the hard work is what makes the memory stay. It also helps to spread your practice out: ten minutes on each of three days beats thirty minutes in one night.

那可以怎麼做？用它。每一次靠自己把記憶從腦子裡拿出來，那條路就更牢固一點。所以把書蓋起來考自己，比把同一頁再讀一次更有效。再讀一次感覺很輕鬆，可是大腦沒做什麼事；回想感覺很費力，而正是這份費力，讓記憶留了下來。把練習分散開來也有幫助：三天各練十分鐘，勝過一個晚上練三十分鐘。

Sleep does the rest. While you sleep, your brain replays what you learned, and little by little the memory is moved out to the outer layer of the brain for long-term keeping. You learned in the lesson about sleep that a good night after studying helps you remember. Staying up late to study steals the very time your brain needs to save the work.

剩下的交給睡眠。你睡覺的時候，大腦會把學過的東西重播一遍，一點一點地把記憶搬到大腦的外層，長期保存。你在睡眠那一課學過：讀完書之後好好睡一覺，會記得比較牢。熬夜讀書，偷走的正是大腦用來存檔的時間。

Riding a bike is a different kind of memory. You cannot explain it in words, and you do not forget it for years. Skills like this depend on other parts of the brain, including the cerebellum at the back, and they grow by doing, not by reading. So here is how to learn anything, including English words: use it, spread it out, sleep on it, and do it again. Try the word experiment below the model and see for yourself.

騎腳踏車是另一種記憶。你沒辦法用話把它說清楚，而且過了很多年也不會忘。像這樣的技能，靠的是大腦的其他部分，包括後面的小腦；它是「做」出來的，不是「讀」出來的。所以，不管要學什麼——包括英文單字——方法都一樣：用它、分散開來、睡一覺，然後再來一次。用模型下方的單字實驗，親自試試看吧。

Questions · 閱讀理解

What does the hippocampus do?

It ties the pieces of a new experience (the sight, the sound, the meaning) together into one memory.

Why does testing yourself work better than reading the page again?

Because pulling a memory out of your own head is hard work for the brain, and that work makes the path stronger. Reading again is easy and does little.

How does sleep help you remember?

While you sleep, the brain replays what you learned and moves the memory to the outer brain for long-term keeping.

Measure Yourself · 親身測量

Which way of studying works better for me? 哪一種讀書方法對我比較有效？

Run the word experiment below the model. You will learn two short lists of English words in two different ways and then test yourself on both. Compare your own two scores. This is not a contest, and nobody else needs to see the numbers.
用模型下方的單字實驗。你會用兩種不同的方法學兩張短短的英文單字表，然後兩張都考一次。請比較自己的兩個分數——這不是比賽，數字也不必給別人看。

  1. 👀List A: read · A 表：一直讀Read the first list again and again until the timer ends.第一張表一直讀、反覆讀，讀到計時結束。
  2. 🙈List B: cover and recall · B 表：蓋起來回想Read the second list once. When it hides, write down every word you can. Then check, and do it once more.第二張表只讀一次。它藏起來之後，把想得起來的字都寫下來；對一次答案，再做一次。
  3. ⏳Wait, then test · 等一等，再測驗Do something else for at least five minutes, or come back tomorrow. Then write down both lists from memory and count.先去做別的事至少五分鐘，或是明天再回來。然後憑記憶把兩張表都寫下來，數一數各記得幾個。

Myth vs. Fact · 常見迷思

Four things people get wrong 四個常見的誤會

✗ Myth · 迷思Memory works like a video camera.記憶就像錄影機。

✓ Fact · 事實A memory is rebuilt from pieces each time you recall it, so it can change, and it can be wrong.每一次回想，記憶都是用片段重新拼起來的，所以它會改變，也可能出錯。

✗ Myth · 迷思Reading your notes again and again is the best way to study.把筆記一讀再讀，是最好的讀書方法。

✓ Fact · 事實Testing yourself works better. Close the book and see what you can pull out.考自己的效果更好。把書闔上，看看自己拿得出多少。

✗ Myth · 迷思Studying all night before a test is the way to remember more.考前熬夜讀書可以記得更多。

✓ Fact · 事實Short sessions spread over several days work better, and sleep is when your brain saves what you learned.分散在幾天的短時間練習效果更好；而睡覺，正是大腦把學到的東西存檔的時候。

✗ Myth · 迷思We use only ten percent of our brains.我們只用了百分之十的大腦。

✓ Fact · 事實You use all of your brain. Different parts are busy at different times.整個大腦你都在用，只是不同的部分在不同的時候忙。

In Chinese · 中文怎麼說

Review the Old to Know the New 溫故知新

Two and a half thousand years ago, Confucius gave two pieces of advice that today's memory scientists would cheer. The first is 學而時習之 (xué ér shí xí zhī): “Learn, and practice what you have learned again and again.” The second is 溫故而知新 (wēn gù ér zhī xīn): “Go back over the old, and you will understand the new.” English and Chinese also agree, wrongly, about where memories are kept. In English you learn a poem “by heart.” In Chinese you 記在心裡 (jì zài xīnlǐ), “keep it in your heart.” Both languages chose the heart. Now you know that it is really the brain.
兩千五百年前，孔子說過兩句話，今天研究記憶的科學家聽了都會鼓掌。第一句是「學而時習之」：學了之後，要時常拿出來練習。第二句是「溫故而知新」：把舊的重溫一遍，就會懂得新的。另外，英文和中文還不約而同地搞錯了記憶放在哪裡：英文說把一首詩 learn by heart（用心背下來），中文說「記在心裡」。兩種語言都選了心臟——現在你知道，其實是大腦。

Classroom Activity 1 · 課堂活動一

I Went to the Market 我去市場買了⋯⋯

You need · 準備材料

  • A circle of students學生圍成一圈
  • Nothing else不需要其他東西

Steps · 步驟

  1. The first student says, “I went to the market and bought an apple.”第一位學生說：「I went to the market and bought an apple.」
  2. The next student repeats the sentence and adds one more thing.下一位學生把這個句子重複一遍，再加上一樣東西。
  3. Keep going around the circle. How long can the list get?繞著圈子繼續下去。這張清單可以變多長？
  4. At the end of the day, try to say the whole list again. What happened to it?放學前再試著把整張清單說一次。它怎麼了？

Teaching tip: every turn is a round of recall, so the early items are practiced the most and are remembered best. When someone gets stuck, let the group whisper a hint instead of ending the game: a hint followed by recall is still good practice. Nobody is out.
教學提示：每輪到一個人，就是一次回想，所以越前面的東西被練習得越多、記得越牢。有人卡住的時候，請讓大家小聲提示，不要讓遊戲結束——有提示之後的回想，依然是很好的練習。沒有人出局。

Classroom Activity 2 · 課堂活動二

My Forgetting Curve 我的遺忘曲線

You need · 準備材料

  • Ten new English words十個新的英文單字
  • A sheet of graph paper for each student每個學生一張方格紙

Steps · 步驟

  1. Learn the ten words on Monday and test yourself right away. Mark your score.星期一學這十個字，馬上考自己一次，把分數標上去。
  2. Test yourself again on Tuesday, on Thursday, and on the next Monday, without looking first.星期二、星期四和下星期一各再考一次，考之前不要先看。
  3. After each test, check your answers and study the words you missed.每次考完對答案，把錯的字再學一次。
  4. Join the marks with a line. What shape does it make?把這些點連成一條線。它是什麼形狀？

Teaching tip: because each test is itself practice, most students' lines dip and then climb back, a picture of the lesson in their own handwriting. Keep the graphs private; the point is each student's own line, not a comparison between students.
教學提示：每一次測驗本身就是練習，所以大部分學生的線會先往下、再爬回來——這一課的內容，由他們自己親手畫了出來。圖表請讓學生自己保留；重點是每個人自己的那條線，不是學生之間的比較。

⚕ Health note · 健康提醒This lesson is for learning about the body. It is not medical advice, and the word experiment is not a test of how good anyone's memory is. Everybody forgets: that is how memory works. Compare your scores only with your own.本課是認識身體的教材，不是醫療建議；單字實驗也不是在測驗誰的記憶力好不好。每個人都會忘記——記憶本來就是這樣運作的。分數只和自己的比。
