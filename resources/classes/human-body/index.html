The Human Body · 人體探索

The Human Body 人體探索

Big ideas about how your body works, read in English and explored with 3D models you can turn, and with measurements you take on yourself.
用英文讀懂身體運作的大道理，用可以親手旋轉的 3D 模型看懂，再用自己的身體量一量。

Every lesson starts with a question about your own body. A 3D model lets you turn, take apart, and look inside the answer, and an English reading explains it, with Chinese a tap away.
每一課都從一個關於自己身體的問題開始：先用 3D 模型轉一轉、拆一拆、看進裡面，找出答案；再讀一篇英文短文把道理講清楚，中文翻譯隨點隨看。

Each lesson also has you measure something on yourself, plus vocabulary, comprehension questions, a short quiz, and a classroom activity. These lessons are for learning, not medical advice.
每一課也會請你在自己身上量一量、數一數，並附生字、閱讀理解、小測驗和課堂活動。本系列是教育用途，不是醫療建議。

Lessons · 課程

8 lessons so far 目前 8 課，持續增加中

Lesson 1 · 第一課入門 · Beginner

Why Do We Need a Skeleton?

為什麼我們需要骨骼？206 bones hold you up, protect your organs, and even make your blood. Take a 3D skeleton apart bone by bone.206 塊骨頭撐起身體、保護內臟，還會製造血液；3D 骨架可以一塊塊拆開看。Start the lesson · 開始上課 →💪Lesson 2 · 第二課入門 · Beginner

How Do Muscles Move Your Bones?

肌肉怎麼拉動骨頭？Muscles can only pull, never push, so they work in pairs. Lift a 3D arm and watch the biceps and triceps take turns.肌肉只能拉、不能推，所以總是成對工作；抬起 3D 手臂，看二頭肌和三頭肌輪流用力。Start the lesson · 開始上課 →❤️Lesson 3 · 第三課入門 · Beginner

Where Does Your Blood Go?

血液在身體裡怎麼流動？Your heart sends blood around two loops, one to the lungs and one to the whole body. Tap along with your pulse, and the 3D heart beats with you.心臟把血液送進兩個循環：一個到肺、一個到全身。跟著自己的脈搏點一點，3D 心臟就用你的心跳跳動。Start the lesson · 開始上課 →🫁Lesson 4 · 第四課入門 · Beginner

How Does Air Get into Your Blood?

空氣怎麼進到血液裡？Every breath travels to hundreds of millions of tiny air sacs, where oxygen crosses into your blood. Breathe along with a 3D pair of lungs.每一口氣都會走到幾億個小肺泡，氧氣在那裡進入血液；跟著 3D 的肺一起呼吸。Start the lesson · 開始上課 →🤸Lesson 5 · 第五課入門 · Beginner

How Do Your Joints Move?

關節怎麼活動？A knee swings like a door, but a shoulder can draw a circle. Move six real joints in 3D, then measure how far your own joints bend.膝蓋像門一樣擺動，肩膀卻能畫圓。在 3D 骨架上動動六個真實的關節，再量一量自己的關節能彎多少。Start the lesson · 開始上課 →🍱Lesson 6 · 第六課入門 · Beginner

What Happens to Your Lunch?

午餐吃下去之後去了哪裡？Your lunch takes a trip of several meters and a day or more. Follow one bite through the mouth, stomach, and intestines, and find out where your own lunch is right now.午餐要走好幾公尺、花一天以上的時間。跟著一口飯走過嘴巴、胃和腸子，再看看你自己的午餐現在走到哪裡。Start the lesson · 開始上課 →🧠Lesson 7 · 第七課入門 · Beginner

How Does Your Brain Talk to Your Body?

大腦怎麼指揮全身？Signals race along your nerves faster than a bullet train. Watch them in slow motion, find out why a reflex beats thinking, and test your own reaction time.訊號沿著神經奔馳，比高鐵還快。用慢動作看它們傳遞，弄懂為什麼反射比思考快，再測測自己的反應時間。Start the lesson · 開始上課 →👁️Lesson 8 · 第八課入門 · Beginner

How Do Your Eyes See?

眼睛怎麼看見東西？Light bends inside your eye and paints an upside-down picture at the back. Focus a 3D eye near and far, fix blurry vision with glasses, and find the blind spot in your own eye.光在眼睛裡轉彎，在眼球後面畫出一幅上下顛倒的圖。讓 3D 眼睛看遠看近、戴上眼鏡修正模糊，再找出你自己眼睛裡的盲點。Start the lesson · 開始上課 →
