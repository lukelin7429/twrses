How Do You Speak?聲音是怎麼發出來的？

Your voice starts as a buzz in your throat, and your tongue and lips shape it into words. Watch the vocal folds open and close in 3D, change the pitch and the mouth shape, and feel the difference between “sss” and “zzz.”

Big idea · 一句話看懂

Your voice is made in three steps: air from your lungs, a buzz from your vocal folds, and a shape from your tongue, lips, and jaw.

    Reading · 英文閱讀

    17

    Unit 17: How Do You Speak?

    Put two fingers gently on the front of your throat and say “ahh.” Do you feel a buzz? Now whisper “ahh.” The buzz is gone. That buzz is your voice being made. Your body does it in three steps, and you can remember them with three words: air, buzz, and shape.

    把兩根手指輕輕放在喉嚨前面，說「啊——」。有沒有感覺到震動？現在用氣音悄悄地說「啊——」，震動不見了。那個嗡嗡的震動，就是你的聲音正在被製造出來。身體分三個步驟來做這件事，你可以用三個詞記住：空氣、嗡嗡聲、形狀。

    The first step is air. To speak, you breathe out. Your lungs push air up your windpipe, the tube that you met in the lesson about breathing. Without moving air, there is no voice at all. Try to speak while you hold your breath, and you will see.

    第一步是空氣。說話的時候，你是在吐氣：肺把空氣往上推進氣管——就是呼吸那一課認識的那條管子。沒有流動的空氣，就完全沒有聲音。憋住氣試著說話，你就知道了。

    The second step is the buzz. At the top of your windpipe is your voice box, which is also called the larynx. Inside it are two small folds of soft tissue called the vocal folds, or vocal cords. When you breathe, they stay open, and air passes between them without a sound. When you speak, they close. The air pushes them apart, and they snap back together, again and again, chopping the stream of air into tiny puffs. That is the buzz you felt with your fingers.

    第二步是嗡嗡聲。氣管的頂端是喉頭，英文也叫 voice box（聲音盒）。喉頭裡面有兩片小小的、柔軟的組織，叫做聲帶。呼吸的時候，聲帶是打開的，空氣從中間通過，沒有聲音。說話的時候，聲帶合起來；空氣把它們推開，它們又彈回去，一次又一次，把一整道氣流切成一小口一小口的。這就是你用手指摸到的嗡嗡聲。

    How fast do the vocal folds open and close? Much faster than you can see. In a man's voice, they do it about 125 times every second. In a woman's voice, it is about 210 times, and in a child's voice, more than 300. Faster means a higher sound. To sing a high note, tiny muscles stretch your vocal folds longer and tighter, like a rubber band pulled tight. Children have high voices because their vocal folds are short. As you grow, they get longer, and your voice gets lower.

    聲帶開合得多快？快到你根本看不見。男人說話時，聲帶每秒大約開合 125 次；女人大約 210 次；小孩超過 300 次。越快，聲音就越高。要唱高音的時候，細小的肌肉會把聲帶拉得更長、更緊，就像把橡皮筋拉緊一樣。小孩的聲音高，是因為聲帶短；隨著你長大，聲帶變長，聲音也就變低了。

    The third step is the shape. A buzz is not a word yet. The sound travels up through your throat and your mouth, and you change it by moving your tongue, your lips, and your jaw. Say “ah,” “ee,” and “oo” on one note. The buzz stays the same, but your mouth makes a different shape for each one, and you hear three different vowels.

    第三步是形狀。嗡嗡聲還不是一個字。聲音往上經過喉嚨和嘴巴，你移動舌頭、嘴唇和下巴來改變它。用同一個音高說 ah、ee、oo：嗡嗡聲沒有變，但嘴巴為每一個音擺出不同的形狀，於是你聽到三個不同的母音。

    Some sounds use no buzz at all. Put your fingers on your throat again and say a long “sss,” and then a long “zzz.” Your mouth does exactly the same thing for both. The only difference is that your vocal folds buzz for “zzz” and rest for “sss.” The same is true for “fff” and “vvv.” English is full of pairs like these, and getting the buzz right is a big part of a clear accent. Now try the buzz check below the model.

    有些音完全不用嗡嗡聲。再把手指放在喉嚨上，拉長說 sss，再拉長說 zzz。這兩個音，嘴巴做的事一模一樣；唯一的差別是：說 zzz 的時候聲帶在振動，說 sss 的時候聲帶在休息。fff 和 vvv 也是這樣。英文裡到處都是這種成對的音，把「有沒有嗡嗡聲」做對，是發音清楚的一大關鍵。現在試試模型下方的嗡嗡聲檢查吧。

    Questions · 閱讀理解

    What are the three steps your body uses to make your voice?

    Air from the lungs, a buzz from the vocal folds, and a shape from the tongue, lips, and jaw.

    What do the vocal folds do when you breathe, and what do they do when you speak?

    When you breathe, they stay open. When you speak, they close and open again and again, chopping the air into tiny puffs.

    What is the difference between “sss” and “zzz”?

    The mouth does the same thing for both, but the vocal folds buzz for “zzz” and rest for “sss.”

    Myth vs. Fact · 常見迷思

    Four things people get wrong 四個常見的誤會

    ✗ Myth · 迷思Your voice is made in your mouth.聲音是在嘴巴裡發出來的。

    ✓ Fact · 事實The sound starts in the voice box, in your throat. Your mouth only shapes it.聲音是從喉嚨裡的喉頭開始的，嘴巴只是把它捏出形狀。

    ✗ Myth · 迷思The vocal cords are strings, like the strings of a guitar.聲帶是一條一條的線，像吉他的弦。

    ✓ Fact · 事實They are two soft folds of tissue. That is why scientists now prefer the name “vocal folds.”它們是兩片柔軟的組織，所以科學家現在比較喜歡叫它們 vocal folds（聲褶）。

    ✗ Myth · 迷思To speak louder, your vocal folds have to move faster.要說得更大聲，聲帶就要動得更快。

    ✓ Fact · 事實Faster makes your voice higher, not louder. For a louder voice, your lungs push more air.聲帶動得更快，聲音是變高，不是變大。要大聲，是肺推出更多的空氣。

    ✗ Myth · 迷思“S” and “z” are made in different parts of the mouth.s 和 z 是嘴巴裡不同的地方發出來的。

    ✓ Fact · 事實The tongue and lips are in exactly the same place. Only the buzz is different.舌頭和嘴唇的位置完全一樣，只差有沒有嗡嗡聲。

    In Chinese · 中文怎麼說

    Round Sounds and Clear Words 字正腔圓

    Chinese has a four-character phrase for good speaking: 字正腔圓 (zì zhèng qiāng yuán), “the words are correct and the tone is round.” It comes from the world of traditional opera, where singers train for years to shape every sound. Notice the two halves: 字, the word, is made by the tongue and lips, and 腔, the tone, is made by the voice. That is the same split you learned in this lesson: the shape and the buzz. Chinese adds one more job for the vocal folds. In Mandarin and Taiwanese, the pitch of a syllable changes its meaning, so the folds are busy stretching and relaxing in every word. English uses pitch too, but for feeling and for questions, not to tell one word from another.
    中文用四個字形容說話說得好：「字正腔圓」。這個詞來自傳統戲曲，演員要花很多年練習把每個音發好。注意這四個字分成兩半：「字」靠舌頭和嘴唇，「腔」靠嗓音——正好就是這一課學到的兩件事：形狀和嗡嗡聲。中文還多給聲帶一份工作：華語和台語裡，一個音節的高低會改變它的意思，所以每說一個字，聲帶都忙著拉緊、放鬆。英文也用音高，但用來表達情緒和問句，不是用來分辨不同的字。

    Classroom Activity 1 · 課堂活動一

    Rubber Band Guitar 橡皮筋吉他

    You need · 準備材料

    • An empty tissue box or a plastic cup一個空的面紙盒或塑膠杯
    • Three rubber bands of different thicknesses, and two pencils三條粗細不同的橡皮筋，和兩枝鉛筆

    Steps · 步驟

    1. Stretch the rubber bands around the box, over the opening. Slide a pencil under them at each end.把橡皮筋套在盒子上、跨過開口，兩端各塞一枝鉛筆墊高。
    2. Pluck each band. Which one makes the lowest sound? Which makes the highest?撥一撥每一條。哪一條聲音最低？哪一條最高？
    3. Pull one band tighter and pluck it again. What happens to the pitch?把其中一條拉緊一點再撥。音高有什麼變化？
    4. Press a finger on the middle of a band to make it shorter. What happens now?用手指按住橡皮筋的中間，讓它變短。現在呢？

    Teaching tip: thinner, tighter, and shorter all give a higher note. Vocal folds follow the same rules, and that is why a child's short folds make a high voice. Ask students to watch a band closely as it sounds: the blur is the vibration. The box matters too: pluck a band in the air, and it is much quieter, just as a buzz without a mouth would be.
    教學提示：越細、越緊、越短，音就越高。聲帶也遵守同樣的規則，所以小孩短短的聲帶發出高高的聲音。請學生仔細看發出聲音的橡皮筋：模糊的那一片就是振動。盒子也很重要：在空中撥橡皮筋，聲音小得多——就像只有嗡嗡聲、沒有嘴巴一樣。

    Classroom Activity 2 · 課堂活動二

    Buzz Pairs 成對的音

    You need · 準備材料

    • Word cards in pairs: sip / zip, fan / van, Sue / zoo, fine / vine成對的單字卡：sip／zip、fan／van、Sue／zoo、fine／vine
    • A small mirror for each pair of students每兩個學生一面小鏡子

    Steps · 步驟

    1. Work in pairs. One student reads a card with two fingers on the throat.兩人一組。一個學生把兩根手指放在喉嚨上，念出一張卡片。
    2. Stretch the first sound: “sssip,” “zzzip.” Buzz or no buzz?把第一個音拉長：sssip、zzzip。有沒有震動？
    3. Look in the mirror while you say both words. Does your mouth look different?照著鏡子說這兩個字。嘴巴看起來有沒有不同？
    4. Now your partner says one word of a pair without showing the card. Can you tell which one it was?現在同伴不給你看卡片，只念出一對裡面的其中一個字。你聽得出是哪一個嗎？

    Teaching tip: Mandarin has an “s” and an “f,” but it has no “z” as in “zoo” and no “v,” so many students in Taiwan say “sue” for “zoo” and “fan” for “van.” The finger test gives them something they can feel, not just hear. Praise the buzz, not the accent.
    教學提示：華語有 s 和 f，卻沒有 zoo 的 z，也沒有 v，所以台灣很多學生會把 zoo 說成 sue、把 van 說成 fan。手指測試讓他們有東西可以「摸到」，而不只是用聽的。請稱讚那個震動，不要評論口音。

    ⚕ Health note · 健康提醒This lesson is for learning about the body. It is not medical advice. Be kind to your voice: drink water, and do not shout for a long time. Touch your throat only gently. If your voice stays hoarse for a few weeks, or if it hurts to speak, tell an adult.本課是認識身體的教材，不是醫療建議。請愛護你的嗓子：多喝水，不要長時間大喊。摸喉嚨的時候只要輕輕地放著。如果聲音沙啞了好幾個星期，或是說話會痛，請告訴大人。
