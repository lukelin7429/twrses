Big idea · 一句話看懂

The Earth turns once a day, 15 degrees every hour, so the Sun is overhead at different places at different times. People divide the Earth into time zones so that clocks in one zone agree. Crossing the International Date Line changes the date by one day.
地球一天轉一圈、每小時轉 15 度，所以太陽在不同的時間照到不同的地方的正上方。人把地球分成時區，讓同一區的鐘一致。跨過國際換日線，日期就差一天。

Unit 5: Why Is It Night in New York When It Is Day in Taipei?

Suppose you call a friend in New York at three o'clock on a sunny afternoon in Taipei. Your friend answers sleepily, because in New York it is two o'clock in the morning. You are both talking at the same moment, yet one of you is in the afternoon and the other is in the middle of the night. How can that be?

假設台北一個晴朗的下午三點，你打電話給在紐約的朋友。朋友睡眼惺忪地接起來，因為紐約是凌晨兩點。你們在同一個瞬間說話，一個人在下午、另一個人卻在半夜。這是怎麼回事？

The answer is that the Earth is turning. It spins once a day, from west to east, so the Sun seems to rise in the east and set in the west. At any moment, about half of the Earth faces the Sun and has day, and the other half faces away and has night. Because a full turn is 360 degrees and a day is 24 hours, the Earth turns 15 degrees every hour.

答案是：地球在轉。地球一天自轉一圈，由西向東轉，所以太陽看起來從東邊升起、從西邊落下。任何時候，地球大約有一半面對太陽，是白天；另一半背對太陽，是黑夜。轉一整圈是 360 度，一天是 24 小時，所以地球每小時轉 15 度。

Long ago, every town set its clocks by its own Sun: noon was when the Sun was highest. That worked when people traveled slowly. But when trains began to cross whole countries, every station had a slightly different time, and timetables became confusing. So people agreed on standard time: everyone in a large area uses the same clock time, even if the Sun is not exactly overhead at noon.

很久以前，每個城鎮照自己的太陽來調鐘：太陽最高的時候就是正午。人走得慢的時候這樣沒問題。可是火車開始橫越整個國家以後，每一站的時間都差一點，時刻表就亂了。所以人們約定了標準時間：一大片地區的人都用同一個鐘點，就算太陽不是剛好在正午時最高也沒關係。

Today the world is divided into time zones. The simplest picture is 24 zones, each 15 degrees wide, one hour apart. Time is counted from the prime meridian at Greenwich, using Coordinated Universal Time, or UTC. Taiwan's national standard time is UTC plus 8 hours, and New York uses UTC minus 5 hours in winter and minus 4 in summer. Real time zone borders are not straight lines. They bend to follow the borders of countries and regions, and some places even use half hours.

今天，世界被分成好幾個時區。最簡單的畫法是 24 個時區，每區寬 15 度，相鄰兩區差一小時。時間從格林威治的本初子午線算起，用的是協調世界時（UTC）。台灣的「國家標準時間」是 UTC 加 8 小時；紐約冬天用 UTC 減 5 小時，夏天用 UTC 減 4 小時。真實的時區界線不是直線，會跟著國家和地區的邊界彎曲，有些地方甚至用半小時。

If you keep moving east through the time zones, your clock keeps moving forward. Go all the way around the world and you would gain a whole day. To fix this, people use the International Date Line, which runs roughly along the 180-degree meridian in the Pacific Ocean. When you cross it going west, the date jumps forward one day. When you cross it going east, the date goes back one day.

如果一直往東走、穿過一個又一個時區，你的鐘就要一直往前調。繞地球一整圈，你就多出了一整天。為了解決這個問題，人們用一條國際換日線，大致沿著太平洋上的 180 度經線。往西跨過它，日期往前跳一天；往東跨過它，日期退回一天。

So when it is Monday afternoon in Taipei, it can still be Monday morning in London, very early Monday in New York, and Sunday evening in Los Angeles. Before you call someone far away, do a little geography: find their time zone, count the hours, and make sure you will not wake them up.

所以當台北是星期一下午，倫敦可能還是星期一早上，紐約是星期一清晨，洛杉磯則還是星期日晚上。打電話給遠方的人之前，先做一點地理：找出對方的時區、算算差幾小時，確定不會把人吵醒。

Questions · 閱讀理解

Why does the Earth turn 15 degrees every hour?

A full turn is 360 degrees and it takes 24 hours, and 360 divided by 24 is 15.

Why did people start using standard time instead of each town's own Sun time?

When trains connected faraway towns, every station having its own slightly different time made timetables confusing. Agreeing on one clock time for a large area solved that.

You fly west across the International Date Line on Tuesday. What day is it right after you cross?

Wednesday. Crossing the line going west moves the date forward one day.

Stories Behind the Clocks · 鐘背後的故事

The day of two noons, Greenwich time, Taiwan's clocks, and a lost day 兩個正午的一天、格林威治的時間、台灣的鐘，和消失的一天

Four short stories about how people agreed on what time it is.
四個小故事，講人怎麼對「現在幾點」取得共識。

1883 · Railways · 鐵路

The day of two noons兩個正午的一天

Before 1883, North American railroads ran on many local times. On November 18, 1883, station clocks were reset to a new standard time as noon arrived in each zone. Many towns heard noon twice that day, once by the old Sun time and once by the new standard time, so the Library of Congress calls it the Day of Two Noons.
1883 年以前，北美的鐵路用的是許多不同的地方時間。1883 年 11 月 18 日，各時區到了正午，車站的鐘就被重新調成新的標準時間。很多城鎮那天過了兩次正午：一次照舊的太陽時，一次照新的標準時間，所以美國國會圖書館稱這一天為「兩個正午的一天」。

Greenwich · 格林威治

From GMT to UTC從格林威治時間到協調世界時

Greenwich Mean Time is the average clock time at Greenwich. Today the world's reference is Coordinated Universal Time, UTC, which the Royal Observatory describes as the modern equivalent of GMT, never more than half a second different from it. UTC is kept by comparing atomic clocks around the world; the International Bureau of Weights and Measures in France combines their results.
格林威治標準時間是格林威治的平均鐘點時間。今天全世界的參考時間是協調世界時（UTC）；格林威治皇家天文台說它是格林威治標準時間的現代版本，兩者相差從來不超過半秒。協調世界時是比較世界各地的原子鐘得出來的，由設在法國的國際度量衡局彙整。

Taiwan · 台灣

Taiwan's clocks have changed before台灣的鐘也改過

Taiwan's official time today is called National Standard Time, UTC+8. It is kept by atomic clocks at Chunghwa Telecom Laboratories on behalf of the Bureau of Standards, and dialing 117 plays it. But it has changed. According to the Taiwan Historica archive, from October 1, 1937 to September 21, 1945 Taiwan used Japan's time, one hour ahead. From 1946 Taiwan used summer time in many years, and it stopped in the early 1980s.
台灣現行的標準時間叫做「國家標準時間」，是 UTC+8，由經濟部標準檢驗局委託中華電信研究院用原子鐘維護；撥 117 聽到的報時就是它。但它改過。依國史館臺灣文獻館的文章，1937 年 10 月 1 日到 1945 年 9 月 21 日，台灣用的是快一小時的日本中央標準時間；1946 年起許多年份實施「夏令時間」，到 1980 年代初期停止。

2011 · The Pacific · 太平洋

The day that disappeared消失的那一天

Because each country chooses its own date, the date line can move. At midnight on December 29, 2011, Samoa and Tokelau moved from the eastern side of the date line to the western side, to keep business hours closer to their trading partners in Asia and Oceania. For them, December 30, 2011 never happened.
因為每個國家自己決定日期，換日線是會移動的。2011 年 12 月 29 日午夜，薩摩亞和托克勞從換日線的東邊換到西邊，好讓營業時間和亞洲、大洋洲的貿易夥伴更接近。對他們來說，2011 年 12 月 30 日根本沒有出現。

Myth vs. Fact · 常見迷思

Four things people get wrong 四個常見的誤會

✗ Myth · 迷思The Sun travels around the Earth once a day.太陽每天繞著地球跑一圈。

✓ Fact · 事實The Earth spins toward the east, so the Sun only seems to cross the sky from east to west.是地球往東自轉，太陽才看起來從東往西走過天空。

✗ Myth · 迷思Time zones are straight strips, each exactly 15 degrees wide.時區是一條一條筆直的帶子，每條剛好 15 度寬。

✓ Fact · 事實That is only the idealized picture. Real time zone boundaries bend to follow the borders of countries and regions, and some places use half hours.那只是理想的畫法。真實的時區界線會跟著國家和地區的邊界彎曲，有些地方還用半小時。

✗ Myth · 迷思An international treaty fixed the International Date Line.國際換日線是國際條約規定的。

✓ Fact · 事實According to NOAA, the date line has no legal international status; each country chooses the date it uses. That is why the line zigzags.依美國國家海洋暨大氣總署的說明，換日線沒有國際法律地位，每個國家自己決定用哪一天的日期，所以這條線彎彎曲曲。

✗ Myth · 迷思Exactly half of the Earth is in daylight at every moment.任何時候，地球都剛好一半是白天。

✓ Fact · 事實Slightly more than half is lit, because the atmosphere bends sunlight around the edge. That is also why the line between day and night is fuzzy, not sharp.被照亮的部分比一半多一點，因為大氣會把陽光折到邊緣後面。所以晝夜的交界是模糊的，不是一條清楚的線。

360 degrees in 24 hours means 15 degrees every hour.
24 小時轉 360 度，每小時 15 度。

East is ahead越往東，鐘越快

The Sun reaches the east first, so places to the east are later in the day.
太陽先照到東邊，所以東邊的地方時間比較晚、鐘比較快。

West over the line, add a day往西跨線，加一天

Cross the date line going west and the date jumps forward; going east, it goes back.
往西跨過換日線，日期往前跳；往東跨過，日期退回。

Check before you call打電話前先算

Find the zone, count the hours, and watch for midnight.
找時區、算小時、注意半夜。

Before the Activities · 做活動之前

Working safely 安全提醒

  • Never look straight at the Sun, and never look at it through a lens or a phone camera.絕對不要直視太陽，也不要透過鏡片或手機鏡頭看太陽。
  • If you use a lamp as the Sun, keep your hands and paper away from the hot bulb, and let an adult plug it in.如果用檯燈當太陽，手和紙張不要碰到燙的燈泡，插電交給大人。
  • When you mark shadows outside, stay in the playground where your teacher can see you, and wear a hat in strong sun.到戶外記錄影子時，待在老師看得到的操場，太陽大時戴帽子。
  • Use a pushpin only with an adult's help, and press it into the ball away from your fingers.圖釘要在大人幫忙下使用，壓進球裡時手指要避開。

Classroom Activity 1 · 課堂活動一

Day and Night with a Lamp 用檯燈做白天和黑夜

You need · 準備材料

  • A desk lamp (the Sun)一盞檯燈（當太陽）
  • A globe or a ball with a line drawn around the middle一顆地球儀，或畫了赤道的球
  • Two small stickers, one for Taipei and one for New York兩張小貼紙，一張是台北、一張是紐約
  • A dark room一間暗一點的教室

Steps · 步驟

  1. Put the stickers on the globe: Taipei near 25 degrees north, 121 degrees east; New York near 41 degrees north, 74 degrees west.把貼紙貼在地球儀上：台北大約在北緯 25 度、東經 121 度；紐約大約在北緯 41 度、西經 74 度。
  2. Turn on the lamp and point it at the globe. Which half is day? Which half is night?打開檯燈照著地球儀。哪一半是白天？哪一半是黑夜？
  3. Turn the globe slowly from west to east, counterclockwise when you look down on the North Pole.慢慢把地球儀由西向東轉，從北極往下看是逆時針。
  4. Stop when Taipei faces the lamp. Where is New York? Keep turning until New York faces the lamp.轉到台北正對著檯燈時停下來。紐約在哪裡？繼續轉，直到紐約正對著檯燈。
  5. Count how much of a turn that took. About half a turn means about half a day apart.數一數轉了多少。大約半圈，就表示兩地大約差半天。

Turning the globe, not moving the lamp, is the key idea: the Sun does not race around the Earth; the Earth turns.
重點是轉地球儀、不是移動檯燈：不是太陽繞著地球跑，是地球自己在轉。

Classroom Activity 2 · 課堂活動二

A World Clock for the Classroom 教室裡的世界時鐘

You need · 準備材料

  • Paper plates, one for each city紙盤，每個城市一個
  • Paper hands and brass fasteners紙做的指針和兩腳釘
  • A world map without borders, or the one in this lesson一張沒有國界的世界地圖，或本課的地圖

Steps · 步驟

  1. Choose five cities and find their longitudes. Divide each by 15 and round to get its idealized time zone.選五個城市，查出經度。每個經度除以 15 再四捨五入，就是理想時區。
  2. Make a clock from each plate and write the city and its time zone, such as “Taipei, UTC+8,” under it.用紙盤做時鐘，在下面寫上城市和時區，例如「台北 UTC+8」。
  3. Set the Taipei clock to the time now. Work out each of the other clocks and set them.把台北的鐘撥到現在的時間，再算出其他每個鐘，撥好。
  4. Hang the clocks in order from west to east, the same order as on the map.照地圖上由西到東的順序，把鐘掛起來。
  5. Check a real world clock online. Which ones are different, and why? Look for daylight saving time or half-hour zones.對照網路上真正的世界時間。哪幾個不一樣？為什麼？找找看是不是夏令時間或半小時的時區。

Idealized zones are a starting point. Real time zones follow decisions made by each country, so always check before an important call or flight.
理想時區只是起點。真實的時區是各地自己決定的，重要的電話或班機之前一定要查清楚。
