Geography · 地理

Geography 地理

Maps, places, and people. Geography asks three questions: Where is it? Why is it there? And what difference does that make? This series starts with a globe you can peel flat.
地圖、地方和人。地理問三個問題：在哪裡？為什麼在那裡？那又怎樣？這個系列從一顆可以剝開攤平的地球儀講起。

The stars of this series are the map and the globe. Each lesson answers one question in English and Chinese, with a model you can turn, flatten, or slice, and one or two small activities that work on any phone.
這個系列的主角是地圖和地球儀。每一課用中英文回答一個問題，配一個可以轉、可以攤平、可以切開的模型，再加一兩個手機也能玩的小互動。

The maps here are drawn from public-domain data and show land, sea, landforms, climate, and people rather than borders. Numbers come with their source and year. Every lesson also has a Map English card: eight sentences for saying where things are.
這裡的地圖用公有領域的資料繪製，畫的是陸地、海洋、地形、氣候和人的分布，而不是國界。每個數字都標明來源與年份。每一課還有一張「地圖英語」卡：八句用英文說位置的句子。

3 of 33 lessons are ready so far; more are on the way.
目前完成 3 課（共規劃 33 課），持續增加中。

圓的地球怎麼變成平的地圖、兩個數字怎麼指出任何一個地方、怎麼在紙上畫出一座山，以及怎麼讀懂一張地圖。

Lesson 1 · 第一課入門 · Beginner

Why Is Every Flat Map Wrong?

為什麼每一張平面地圖都是「錯」的？Peel a 3D globe open and flatten it five different ways. Watch equal circles swell and squash, drag Greenland down to the equator, and find out why no flat map can get everything right.把 3D 地球儀剝開，用五種方法攤平。看一樣大的圓怎麼被拉大、壓扁，把格陵蘭拖到赤道，再想想為什麼沒有一張平面地圖能樣樣都對。Start the lesson · 開始上課 →Lesson 2 · 第二課入門 · Beginner

How Do You Find Any Place on Earth?

怎麼找到地球上任何一個地方？Drag a point around a 3D globe and read its latitude and longitude. See the two angles from the center of the Earth, find eight places from their numbers, and learn why one degree is not always the same distance.在 3D 地球儀上拖動一個點，讀出它的緯度和經度。看看從地心量出來的兩個角，照著數字找出八個地方，再想想為什麼「一度」不一定一樣長。Start the lesson · 開始上課 →Lesson 3 · 第三課入門 · Beginner

How Do You Draw a Mountain on Flat Paper?

怎麼在平面上畫出一座山？Fill a tank around a mountain with water, one step at a time, and let the water's edge draw contour lines. Then look from above, read which slopes are steep, and turn any line on the map into a side view.在一座山的四周一層一層灌水，讓水邊畫出等高線。再從正上方看、判斷哪裡陡，把地圖上任何一條線變成側面圖。Start the lesson · 開始上課 →
