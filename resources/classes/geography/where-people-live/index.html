Geography · Unit 2 · Lesson 8 · 地理 單元二 第八課

Where Do People Live?人都住在哪裡？

Cover a 3D Earth with one dot for every million people, then hunt for the crowded places and the empty ones. Find out why water, flat land, and a gentle climate draw people together.
在 3D 地球上，每一百萬人放一個點，找找哪裡擠滿了人、哪裡幾乎沒有人。看看為什麼水、平地和溫和的氣候會把人聚在一起。

Big idea · 一句話看懂

People are spread very unevenly over the Earth. Most people live where there is water, flat land, and a climate that is neither too dry nor too cold. Deserts, high mountains, and the polar zones have very few people.
人在地球上分布得非常不平均。大多數人住在有水、有平地、氣候不太乾也不太冷的地方；沙漠、高山和寒帶住的人非常少。

Reading · 英文閱讀

8

Unit 8: Where Do People Live?

Imagine giving every person on Earth a seat on a giant map. In 2025, the United Nations estimates, there are about 8.23 billion of us. If people were spread out evenly, every piece of land would have its share. They are not. Some places are packed shoulder to shoulder, and enormous areas have almost nobody at all.

想像給地球上的每一個人在一張巨大的地圖上安排一個位子。聯合國估計，2025 年我們大約有 82.3 億人。如果人分布得很平均，每一塊陸地都會分到一份。事實不是這樣：有些地方擠得肩並肩，有些廣大的地方幾乎一個人都沒有。

On the globe in this lesson, each dot stands for one million people. The dots crowd together in South Asia and East Asia, along the great rivers, and near many coasts. More than half of the world's people live in Asia. In the population data used here, about 86 out of every 100 people live north of the equator, partly because most of the world's land is in the Northern Hemisphere.

在這一課的地球上，每一個點代表一百萬人。點擠在南亞和東亞、沿著大河、靠近許多海岸。全世界超過一半的人住在亞洲。在本課用的人口資料裡，每 100 個人大約有 86 個住在赤道以北，部分原因是地球上大部分的陸地在北半球。

Why there? People need water to drink and to grow food. They need land that is flat enough to farm and to build on, and a climate that is neither too dry nor too cold. Rivers and coasts also make it easier to travel and to trade. Where water, flat land, and a gentle climate come together, villages grow into towns, and towns grow into cities.

為什麼是這些地方？人需要水來喝、來種糧食；需要夠平的地來耕種、蓋房子；也需要不太乾、不太冷的氣候。河流和海岸也讓交通和買賣比較方便。水、平地和溫和的氣候湊在一起的地方，村子就長成城鎮，城鎮再長成城市。

Now look at the empty places. Deserts are too dry. High mountains and plateaus are cold, steep, and short of air. In the polar zones, as Lesson 7 showed, the sunlight is always slanted, and the ground may be frozen. The large deserts outlined on the globe cover about 10% of the land, but only about 2% of the world's people live in them, and most of those live along rivers such as the Nile. Land more than 3,000 meters high covers about 3% of the land and holds about 0.5% of the people.

再看看空空的地方。沙漠太乾；高山和高原又冷、又陡、空氣又稀薄；寒帶的陽光永遠是斜的（第七課看過），地面還可能結凍。地球上標出來的幾個大沙漠，大約占陸地面積的 10%，卻只住了全世界大約 2% 的人，而且大多住在尼羅河這樣的河邊。海拔 3,000 公尺以上的地方大約占陸地的 3%，住了大約 0.5% 的人。

Low land matters, too. A 1998 study found that about a third of the world's people lived less than 100 meters above sea level, even though only about a sixth of the land where people live is that low. The population and height data on this page give almost the same answer: about 34% of people on about 14% of the land. A 2016 study estimated that about 40 percent of people live within 100 kilometers of a coast.

地勢低也很重要。1998 年的一項研究發現，世界上大約三分之一的人住在海拔不到 100 公尺的地方，但有人住的土地裡，這麼低的只占大約六分之一。用本頁的人口與高度資料算，答案幾乎一樣：大約 34% 的人住在大約 14% 的土地上。2016 年的一項研究估計，大約 40% 的人住在離海岸 100 公里以內。

To compare places, geographers use population density: the number of people divided by the area of land. Taiwan is one of the crowded places. In 2024, Taiwan had about 646 people per square kilometer, and Taipei City had more than 9,000. But even in Taiwan, people are not spread evenly: most live on the plains and in the basins, and few live high in the mountains.

為了比較不同的地方，地理學家用「人口密度」：人數除以土地面積。台灣是很擠的地方之一：民國 113 年（2024 年），台灣每平方公里大約有 646 人，臺北市超過 9,000 人。但即使在台灣，人也分布得不平均：大多數人住在平原和盆地，住在高山上的人很少。
Read aloud · 課文朗讀

Questions · 閱讀理解

Why do so many people live along rivers, even rivers that flow through deserts?

Rivers give water for drinking and for growing food, and the flat land beside them is easy to farm. Rivers also make travel and trade easier.

Why do so few people live on high plateaus, in deserts, and in the polar zones?

Deserts are too dry, high plateaus are cold with thin air, and the polar zones get only slanted sunlight and are often frozen. In all of them, it is hard to grow food.

Taiwan is crowded, but its high mountains are not. Why might that be?

The mountains are steep, high, and cooler, so it is hard to farm, build, and travel there. Most people live on the plains and in the basins.

Stories Behind the Dots · 點背後的故事

Counting the people of the world 數一數世界上的人


November 15, 2022 · United Nations · 聯合國

The Day of Eight Billion八十億人的那一天

The United Nations chose November 15, 2022, as the day the world's population was projected to reach 8 billion. It explained that people are living longer, thanks to better public health, nutrition, and medicine.
聯合國把 2022 年 11 月 15 日訂為世界人口預計達到八十億的日子，並說明這是因為公共衛生、營養和醫療進步，人活得更久了。

1998 · Cohen & Small · PNAS

Living close to sea level住在靠近海平面的地方

Two scientists compared where people live with how high the land is. As of 1994, they estimated, 33.5 percent of the world's people lived less than 100 meters above sea level, while only 15.6 percent of inhabited land is that low. The middle person, they found, lived at 194 meters.
兩位科學家把人住的地方和土地的高度對照。他們估計，到 1994 年，世界上 33.5% 的人住在海拔不到 100 公尺的地方，但有人住的土地裡只有 15.6% 這麼低。所有人依高度排成一列，正中間那個人住在 194 公尺。

2016 · Kummu and others · 研究

The 60 percent that wasn't不是 60%

For years, people repeated a 1990 estimate that 60 percent of the world lived within 100 kilometers of a coast. A team in 2016 checked again with more detailed population and coastline data and found about 40 percent in 1990 and 39 percent in 2010. Good geography means checking the numbers.
很多年來，大家一直引用 1990 年的估計：世界上 60% 的人住在離海岸 100 公里以內。2016 年一個研究團隊用更細的人口與海岸線資料重新算，1990 年大約是 40%，2010 年是 39%。好的地理學，就是要回頭檢查數字。

2024 · Taiwan · 台灣

646 people per square kilometer每平方公里 646 人

According to the Executive Yuan, Taiwan had about 646 people per square kilometer in 2024. Taipei City, with 272 square kilometers, had 9,164 per square kilometer, the highest of any city or county. About 70 percent of Taiwan's people live in its six special municipalities.
行政院的資料：民國 113 年，台灣每平方公里大約 646 人；臺北市面積 272 平方公里，每平方公里 9,164 人，是人口密度最高的都市。全國大約七成的人住在六個直轄市。

Myth vs. Fact · 常見迷思

Four things people get wrong 四個常見的誤會

✗ Myth · 迷思People are spread out fairly evenly over the land.人在陸地上分布得還算平均。

✓ Fact · 事實Not at all. Some river valleys and plains are packed, while huge deserts, mountains, and icy lands are nearly empty.完全不是。有些河谷和平原擠滿了人，廣大的沙漠、高山和冰天雪地卻幾乎沒有人。

✗ Myth · 迷思Most of the world's people live within 100 kilometers of the sea.世界上大多數人住在離海 100 公里以內。

✓ Fact · 事實An estimate from 1990 said 60 percent, and it was repeated for years. A 2016 study, using more detailed data, found about 40 percent.1990 年有一個估計說是 60%，這個數字被引用了很多年；2016 年一項用更細資料的研究，算出來大約是 40%。

✗ Myth · 迷思Hot places near the equator have few people.赤道附近很熱的地方，人很少。

✓ Fact · 事實Heat is not the problem; dryness is. Warm, wet places with good soil, such as the island of Java, are among the most crowded on Earth.熱不是問題，乾才是。溫暖、潮濕、土壤又好的地方，像爪哇島，是地球上人最擠的地方之一。

✗ Myth · 迷思High land always has few people.地勢高的地方，人一定很少。

✓ Fact · 事實Not in the tropics. There, high land is cool instead of hot, and many people live on it. In the data on this page, tropical land above 2,000 meters covers about 1.1% of the land but holds about 2.4% of the people.在熱帶不一定。熱帶的高地涼爽而不炎熱，住了很多人。用本頁的資料算，熱帶海拔 2,000 公尺以上的地方大約占陸地的 1.1%，卻住了大約 2.4% 的人。

  • Stay where your teacher can see you when you work outside, and drink water on hot days.在戶外活動時，待在老師看得到的地方；天氣熱要記得喝水。
  • Do not stand on chairs or desks to reach anything.不要站到椅子或桌子上拿東西。
  • Count people by looking, not by touching or pushing. Be kind when you talk about crowded or poor places.數人用看的，不要碰或推別人。談到擁擠或貧窮的地方時，說話要尊重。

Classroom Activity 1 · 課堂活動一

How Crowded Is It? 這裡有多擠？

You need · 準備材料

  • A long measuring tape長捲尺
  • Chalk or tape粉筆或膠帶
  • A calculator計算機
  • Paper and a pencil紙和鉛筆

Steps · 步驟

  1. Measure the length and width of your classroom in meters. Multiply them to find the area in square meters.量教室的長和寬（公尺），相乘得到面積（平方公尺）。
  2. Count the people in the room. Divide the number of people by the area: that is the classroom's population density.數一數教室裡有幾個人。人數除以面積，就是教室的人口密度。
  3. Do the same for a part of the playground with the same number of people standing in it.在操場劃出一塊地，讓同樣多的人站進去，再算一次。
  4. Which place is more crowded? Write the two densities side by side.哪裡比較擠？把兩個密度並排寫下來。
  5. Taiwan has about 646 people per square kilometer (2024). Is your classroom more or less crowded than that? (One square kilometer is 1,000,000 square meters.)台灣每平方公里大約 646 人（民國 113 年）。你們的教室比這個擠，還是比較空？（一平方公里是 1,000,000 平方公尺。）

Population density does not count people one by one in each spot. It is an average: the same number can hide crowded corners and empty ones.
人口密度不是一個一個地方去數，而是平均：同一個數字，可能藏著很擠的角落和空空的角落。

Classroom Activity 2 · 課堂活動二

Where Would You Build a Village? 村子要蓋在哪裡？

You need · 準備材料

  • A large sheet of paper一大張紙
  • Colored pencils彩色筆
  • 20 small paper squares or beans20 張小紙片或 20 顆豆子

Steps · 步驟

  1. In a group, draw an imaginary island with a river, a coast, a tall mountain, a dry sandy area, and a forest.分組，畫一座想像的島：有一條河、海岸、一座高山、一片乾燥的沙地和一片森林。
  2. Each square or bean is 1,000 people. Place all 20 where you think people would choose to live.每一張紙片或每一顆豆子代表 1,000 人。把 20 個都放在你覺得人會選擇住的地方。
  3. Write one reason for each crowded spot and each empty spot.每一個擠的地方、每一個空的地方，都寫一個理由。
  4. Compare your island with another group's. Did you crowd people in the same places?和另一組比一比，你們把人放在一樣的地方嗎？
  5. Now look at the 3D globe. Can you find real places that look like your island?再看 3D 地球：你找得到和你們的島很像的真實地方嗎？

There is no single right answer. Good answers explain the reason: water, flat land, climate, or a way to travel.
沒有唯一的正確答案；好的答案會說出理由：水、平地、氣候，或交通。
