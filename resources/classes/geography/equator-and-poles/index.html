Geography · Unit 2 · Lesson 7 · 地理 單元二 第七課

Why Is It Hot at the Equator and Cold at the Poles?為什麼赤道熱、兩極冷？

Shine the same beam of sunlight on a 3D Earth and move it from the equator toward the poles. Watch the bright patch stretch out, and see why slanted sunlight warms the ground less.
把同一束陽光照在 3D 地球上，從赤道往兩極移動，看地上的光斑越拉越長，了解為什麼斜斜的陽光比較不會把地面曬熱。

Big idea · 一句話看懂

Near the equator, sunlight comes down almost straight, so its energy is packed into a small area. Toward the poles, the same sunlight arrives at a slant and is spread over a much larger area, so each square meter gets less. That is the main reason the equator is hot and the poles are cold.
在赤道附近，陽光幾乎是直直照下來，能量集中在一小塊地面；越往兩極，同樣的陽光斜斜地照進來，攤在大得多的面積上，每平方公尺分到的就比較少。這是赤道熱、兩極冷的主要原因。

Unit 7: Why Is It Hot at the Equator and Cold at the Poles?

Stand in the sunshine at noon on a summer day, and you can feel the heat on your skin. Stand in the same sunshine late in the afternoon, and it feels much gentler, even though it is the same Sun. What changed is the angle of the light. The same idea explains why the lands near the equator are hot and the lands near the poles are cold.

夏天中午站在太陽下，你可以感覺到皮膚上的熱；傍晚站在同樣的陽光下，感覺就溫和多了，雖然還是同一個太陽。改變的是光照下來的角度。同樣的道理，可以解釋為什麼赤道附近的地方熱、兩極附近的地方冷。

Try it with a flashlight. Shine it straight down at a table, and you get a small, bright spot. Tilt it, and the light stretches into a longer, dimmer patch. The flashlight gives out the same amount of light both times. When it is tilted, that light is spread over more of the table, so each part of the table gets less.

用手電筒試試看。從正上方照桌子，會照出一個又小又亮的光點；斜著照，光就拉長成一塊比較長、比較暗的光斑。兩次手電筒發出的光一樣多，斜著照的時候，同樣的光攤在比較多的桌面上，所以桌子的每一部分分到的就比較少。

Sunlight reaches the round Earth in the same way. Near the equator, the noon Sun is high and its rays come down almost straight. Toward the poles, the Earth curves away, and the same sunlight arrives at a slant. NASA explains that from the equator to the poles, sunlight meets the Earth at smaller and smaller angles, and “the light gets spread over larger and larger surface areas.” Slanted sunlight also travels a longer path through the air before it reaches the ground.

陽光照在圓圓的地球上也是這樣。在赤道附近，正午的太陽很高，陽光幾乎是直直照下來；越往兩極，地面越往遠處彎，同樣的陽光就斜斜地照進來。NASA 說明：從赤道到兩極，陽光和地面的夾角越來越小，「光攤在越來越大的面積上」。斜射的陽光在到達地面之前，穿過的空氣也比較長。

The Earth's axis is tilted, so the place where the noon Sun is straight overhead moves during the year. In June it is over the Tropic of Cancer, about 23.4 degrees north. In December it is over the Tropic of Capricorn, about 23.4 degrees south. Outside the tropics, the noon Sun is never straight overhead. Beyond the Arctic and Antarctic Circles, there are days in winter when the Sun does not rise at all.

地球的自轉軸是傾斜的，所以一年當中，正午太陽直射的地方會移動。六月時直射北回歸線，大約北緯 23.4 度；十二月時直射南回歸線，大約南緯 23.4 度。在回歸線以外的地方，正午的太陽永遠不會在頭頂正上方。在北極圈、南極圈以內，冬天會有太陽整天都不升起的日子。

These four lines divide the Earth into five zones: the tropical zone between the two tropics, two temperate zones, and two polar zones beyond the polar circles. Real climates are more complicated, because oceans, winds, mountains, and height above sea level all make a difference. But the angle of sunlight is the starting point.

這四條線把地球分成五帶：兩條回歸線之間的熱帶、兩個溫帶，以及極圈以內的兩個寒帶。真實的氣候比較複雜，海洋、風、山脈和海拔高度都有影響；但陽光的角度是起點。

The Tropic of Cancer crosses Taiwan, passing through Chiayi, Hualien, and Penghu. Taiwan's Central Weather Administration says this is why southern Taiwan is tropical and northern Taiwan is subtropical. In June, the noon Sun stands almost straight above the Tropic of Cancer markers. When you feel the summer sun in Taiwan, you are feeling sunlight that is nearly as direct as sunlight can be.

北回歸線穿過台灣，經過嘉義、花蓮和澎湖。中央氣象署說，因為這樣，台灣南部屬熱帶、北部屬副熱帶。六月時，正午的太陽幾乎就在北回歸線標誌的正上方。當你在台灣感受到夏天的太陽，你感受到的，幾乎是最直的陽光了。

Questions · 閱讀理解

Why does a tilted flashlight make a dimmer patch, even though it gives out the same light?

The same light is spread over a larger area, so each part of the table gets less of it.

Why is the noon Sun never straight overhead in London or Reykjavík?

The overhead Sun only moves between the Tropic of Cancer and the Tropic of Capricorn, and both cities are far north of the Tropic of Cancer.

Besides the angle of sunlight, what else can make one place warmer or colder than another at the same latitude?

Oceans, winds, mountains, and height above sea level. For example, high mountains are colder than the lowlands around them.

Stories Behind the Lines · 線背後的故事

People who followed the Sun 跟著太陽走的人


About 276–194 BCE · Eratosthenes · 埃拉托斯特尼

Sunlight at the bottom of a well照到井底的陽光

The Greek geographer Eratosthenes read that a deep well near Syene, in southern Egypt, was lit all the way down at noon once a year, so the Sun had to be straight overhead. In Alexandria, farther north, posts still cast shadows that day. From the difference he estimated the size of the Earth.
希臘地理學家埃拉托斯特尼讀到：埃及南部賽伊尼附近有一口深井，每年有一天正午陽光會照到井底，所以那時太陽一定在正上方。同一天，北邊亞歷山卓的柱子還有影子。他從這個差別估算出地球有多大。

Britannica · 大英百科

Why “Cancer”?為什麼叫「巨蟹」？

At the June solstice the Sun is overhead at the Tropic of Cancer. Long ago, the Sun appeared in the constellation Cancer at that time, which gave the line its name. Today it appears in Gemini, because the direction of the Earth's axis slowly changes.
六月夏至時，太陽直射北回歸線。很久以前，那時太陽出現在巨蟹座，這條線就因此得名（英文叫「巨蟹的回歸線」）。今天那時太陽出現在雙子座，因為地球自轉軸的方向會慢慢改變。

2004 · Chiayi · 嘉義

A line that walks south往南走的線

The Tropic of Cancer is not fixed. A monument in Chiayi City says it moves south by more than 14 meters a year, and that in 1788 it passed near what is now the corner of Boai Road and Shixian Road. Chiayi City's marker, opened on June 21, 2004, is counted as the world's tenth Tropic of Cancer marker.
北回歸線不是固定不動的。嘉義市的碑文說，它每年往南移動 14 公尺多，西元 1788 年通過今天博愛路與世賢路口北端附近。嘉義市的標誌在 2004 年 6 月 21 日落成，是「全球北回歸線標誌第十處」。

Arctic Circle · 北極圈

From one day to six months從一天到半年

On the Arctic Circle, the Sun stays up all day for about one day each June and stays down for about one day each December. Farther north, these stretches grow longer, until at the North Pole there are six months of daylight and six months of night.
在北極圈上，每年六月大約有一天太陽整天不落下，十二月大約有一天太陽整天不升起。越往北，這樣的日子越長；到了北極，是半年的白天和半年的黑夜。

Myth vs. Fact · 常見迷思

Four things people get wrong 四個常見的誤會

✗ Myth · 迷思The poles are cold because they are farther from the Sun.兩極冷，是因為離太陽比較遠。

✓ Fact · 事實The angle of sunlight matters, not the distance. The Earth is actually closest to the Sun in January, during the northern winter.重要的是陽光的角度，不是距離。地球其實在一月離太陽最近，那時北半球是冬天。

✗ Myth · 迷思At the equator, the noon Sun is straight overhead every day.赤道上，每天正午太陽都在頭頂正上方。

✓ Fact · 事實The overhead Sun moves between the tropics during the year. It passes over the equator only around March and September.太陽直射點一年當中在南北回歸線之間移動，只有在三月和九月前後才經過赤道。

✗ Myth · 迷思Slanted sunlight is weaker because the Sun gives out less light.斜射的陽光比較弱，是因為太陽發出的光比較少。

✓ Fact · 事實The Sun sends the same light. Slanted light is spread over more ground, so each square meter gets less.太陽發出的光一樣多；斜射的光攤在比較多的地面上，每平方公尺分到的比較少。

✗ Myth · 迷思Places in the polar zones never see the Sun in summer.寒帶的地方夏天也看不到太陽。

✓ Fact · 事實In summer the Sun can stay up all day and all night there. It is low in the sky, so its light is slanted.夏天時，那裡的太陽可以整天整夜都不落下；只是太陽很低，光是斜的。

Before the Activities · 做活動之前

Working safely 安全提醒

  • Never look straight at the Sun, and never look at it through a lens, binoculars, or a phone camera.絕對不要直視太陽，也不要透過鏡片、望遠鏡或手機鏡頭看太陽。
  • Do not shine a flashlight into anyone's eyes.不要拿手電筒照別人的眼睛。
  • When you measure shadows outside, stay where your teacher can see you, drink water, and wear a hat in strong sun.到戶外量影子時，待在老師看得到的地方，記得喝水，太陽大時戴帽子。
  • Push the stick into soft ground or a pot of sand with care, and do not leave it where someone could trip over it.把棍子小心插進軟土或一盆沙裡，不要放在別人可能絆倒的地方。

Classroom Activity 1 · 課堂活動一

Flashlight and Graph Paper 手電筒和方格紙

You need · 準備材料

  • A flashlight一支手電筒
  • Graph paper方格紙
  • A protractor量角器
  • A pencil and a ruler鉛筆和尺

Steps · 步驟

  1. Darken the room a little. Hold the flashlight straight above the graph paper, about 20 centimeters away.把教室弄暗一點。手電筒拿在方格紙正上方，大約 20 公分高。
  2. Trace the edge of the bright patch and count the squares inside it.沿著亮的光斑描邊，數一數裡面有幾格。
  3. Keep the same distance, and tilt the flashlight to about 30 degrees from the paper. Use the protractor to check.保持一樣的距離，把手電筒傾斜到和紙面大約 30 度，用量角器檢查。
  4. Trace the patch again and count the squares. How many times larger is it?再描一次光斑、數格子。它大了幾倍？
  5. Which patch looked brighter? Write one sentence that connects this to the equator and the poles.哪一個光斑看起來比較亮？寫一句話，把它和赤道、兩極連起來。

The flashlight gives out the same light both times. Only the angle changes, and so does the size of the patch.
兩次手電筒發出的光一樣多，改變的只有角度，還有光斑的大小。

Classroom Activity 2 · 課堂活動二

How High Is the Noon Sun? 正午的太陽有多高？

You need · 準備材料

  • A straight stick about 1 meter long, or a pencil standing in clay一根大約 1 公尺長的直棍子，或插在黏土上的鉛筆
  • A measuring tape捲尺
  • A sunny, flat place一個有陽光的平地
  • A calculator, or the table your teacher gives you計算機，或老師給的對照表

Steps · 步驟

  1. Stand the stick straight up on flat ground. Measure its height above the ground.把棍子直直地立在平地上，量它露出地面的高度。
  2. Close to noon, measure the length of its shadow. Do not look at the Sun; look only at the shadow.接近正午時，量它影子的長度。不要看太陽，只看影子。
  3. Divide the height by the shadow length. Use a calculator's inverse tangent, or your teacher's table, to turn this into the Sun's height in degrees.用高度除以影子長度。用計算機的反正切，或老師給的對照表，換算成太陽高度幾度。
  4. Compare your answer with the 3D model: choose the season and move the beam to your latitude.和 3D 模型比一比：選季節，再把光束移到你所在的緯度。
  5. Repeat once a month. Does the noon shadow get longer or shorter as winter comes?每個月做一次。冬天快到時，正午的影子變長還是變短？

A long noon shadow means a low Sun and slanted sunlight. A short shadow means a high Sun.
正午影子長，表示太陽低、陽光斜；影子短，表示太陽高。
