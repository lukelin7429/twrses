Unit 4: How Do You Read a Map?

Imagine you are standing on a hill above a small town. You can see roofs, roads, a river, and fields. Now imagine rising higher and higher until you are looking straight down. The town looks flat, and everything in it looks small. A map is a drawing of that view from above, made smaller so that it fits on a sheet of paper or a screen.

想像你站在一座小鎮旁邊的山丘上，看得到屋頂、道路、一條河和田地。現在想像你越升越高，直到從正上方往下看。小鎮看起來是平的，每樣東西都變得很小。地圖就是把這個從上往下看的樣子畫下來，再縮小到一張紙或一個螢幕放得下。

A map cannot show things as they really look, so it uses symbols. A blue line can mean a river, a small flag can mean a school, and green with little dots can mean a forest. Different maps use different symbols, so every good map has a legend, also called a key. The legend is a small table that tells you what each symbol means. When you meet a map you have never seen, the legend is the first place to look.

地圖沒辦法把東西照它真正的樣子畫出來，所以用符號。藍色的線可以代表河，小旗子可以代表學校，綠色加上小點點可以代表森林。不同的地圖用不同的符號，所以好的地圖都有圖例。圖例是一張小表，告訴你每個符號代表什麼。遇到一張沒看過的地圖，第一個要看的就是圖例。

The second thing to find is direction. On most maps, north is at the top, south is at the bottom, east is on the right, and west is on the left. Between them are northeast, southeast, southwest, and northwest. But not every map is drawn that way, so look for a north arrow. If the arrow points somewhere else, turn the map in your mind until the arrow points up.

第二件要找的是方位。大多數的地圖上方是北、下方是南、右邊是東、左邊是西；它們之間還有東北、東南、西南、西北。但不是每一張地圖都這樣畫，所以要找北方箭頭（學術名詞叫「指北標」）。如果箭頭指向別的方向，就在心裡把地圖轉一轉，讓箭頭朝上。

The third thing is the scale. The scale tells you how much smaller the map is than the real ground. A scale of 1:25,000 means that 1 centimeter on the map stands for 25,000 centimeters on the ground, which is 250 meters. Many maps also have a scale bar, a short line marked with a distance such as 500 meters. If the map is enlarged or shrunk, the bar changes with it and stays correct, but the number 1:25,000 does not. You can lay a ruler or the edge of a piece of paper along the bar to measure any distance on the map.

第三件是比例尺。比例尺告訴你地圖比真正的地面縮小了多少。比例尺 1:25,000 的意思是：地圖上的 1 公分，代表地面上的 25,000 公分，也就是 250 公尺。很多地圖還有圖形比例尺，是一小段標著距離（例如 500 公尺）的線。地圖被放大或縮小時，這段線會跟著一起變，所以仍然是對的；1:25,000 這個數字就不對了。把尺或一張紙的邊對著它，就能量地圖上任何一段距離。

The same place can be drawn at different scales. A map of a whole country fits on one page only if it is made much smaller, so it shows less detail. A map of one neighborhood can show every building. Mapmakers call the second kind a large-scale map, because the fraction 1/5,000 is larger than 1/500,000. This sounds backward to many people, so it is worth remembering.

同一個地方可以用不同的比例尺來畫。整個國家的地圖要縮得很小才放得進一頁，所以畫得比較粗略；一個街區的地圖可以把每一棟房子都畫出來。製圖的人把後面那一種叫做「大比例尺」地圖，因為 1/5,000 這個分數比 1/500,000 大。很多人覺得這說法剛好反過來，所以值得記住。

With these three things, you can answer real questions. How far is it from the school to the harbor? Use the scale. Which way should you walk from the bridge to reach the lighthouse? Use the north arrow. Is the green patch beside the road a forest or a rice field? Use the legend. Reading a map is not memorizing it. It is asking the map good questions.

有了這三件事，就能回答真正的問題。從學校到港口有多遠？用比例尺。從橋要往哪個方向走才會到燈塔？用北方箭頭。公路旁邊那一片綠色是森林還是稻田？用圖例。讀地圖不是把地圖背起來，而是向地圖問好問題。
Read aloud · 課文朗讀

Questions · 閱讀理解

Why does every good map need a legend?

Different maps use different symbols, so the legend tells you what each symbol means on this particular map.

A map has the scale 1:50,000. Two towns are 6 cm apart on the map. How far apart are they on the ground?

6 × 50,000 = 300,000 cm, which is 3,000 m, or 3 km.

Which shows more detail: a map at 1:5,000 or a map at 1:500,000? Which one is called the large-scale map?

The 1:5,000 map shows more detail, because it covers a smaller area. It is the large-scale map, because 1/5,000 is a larger fraction than 1/500,000.

Stories Behind the Map · 地圖背後的故事

East at the top, Taiwan's map symbols, three norths, and a common mistake 上方是東方的地圖、台灣地形圖的符號、三種北，和一個常見的錯誤

Four short stories about how maps show direction, symbols, and scale.
四個小故事，講地圖怎麼表示方位、符號和比例尺。

Long Ago · 很久以前

When east was at the top上方是東方的時代

North was not always at the top. A world map of 1532 from the tradition of medieval “T-in-O” maps puts east at the top, according to an exhibit at the Newberry Library in Chicago. The same exhibit explains that north probably moved to the top because sailors could rely on the North Star, and, after about 1450, because compasses, which point north, came into wide use.
北方不是一直都在上面。依美國芝加哥紐伯里圖書館的展覽說明，一張 1532 年、屬於中世紀「T-O 地圖」傳統的世界地圖，上方是東方。展覽也說明，北方後來會跑到上面，可能是因為航海的人可以依靠北極星，而且大約 1450 年以後指向北方的羅盤普遍了起來。

Taiwan · 台灣的地形圖

A flag for a school, an anchor for a harbor學校是一面旗，港口是一個錨

Taiwan's 1:25,000 topographic maps, made by the National Land Surveying and Mapping Center, have a legend with dozens of symbols. A school is a small flag on a pole, a harbor is an anchor, and a lighthouse is a small star of rays. The symbols on this page were drawn for this lesson. They look similar, but they are not the official ones.
內政部國土測繪中心的 1/25,000 經建版地形圖，圖例裡有好幾十種符號：學校是一面插在竿子上的小旗子，港口是一個錨，燈塔是一個放射光芒的小星形。本頁的符號是為這一課自己畫的，樣子相近，但不是官方的符號。

Three Norths · 三種北

Which north does a compass find?指北針找到的是哪一個北？

There is more than one north. True north points along a line of longitude to the North Pole. Grid north follows the grid printed on the map. Magnetic north is where a compass needle points, and it changes slowly with place and time. In Taipei a compass points about 5 degrees west of true north, according to NOAA's calculator for October 2026. Each sheet of Taiwan's topographic maps prints a small diagram of the three norths; one sheet from 2020 shows 4 degrees 44 minutes between grid north and magnetic north.
北方不只一種。真北是沿著經線指向北極的方向；方格北是地圖上印的方格的方向；磁北是指北針的針所指的方向，而且會隨地點和時間慢慢改變。依美國國家海洋暨大氣總署（NOAA）的計算器，2026 年 10 月在台北，指北針大約指向真北偏西 5 度。台灣的地形圖每一幅都印著一張小小的「偏角圖」；其中一幅 2020 年的圖，方格北和磁北之間是 4 度 44 分。

In Math Class · 數學課

A mistake the curriculum warns about課綱特別提醒的一個錯誤

Map scales are taught in grade 6 math in Taiwan. The curriculum asks teachers to deal with a common mistake: thinking that a bigger number after the 1 means a bigger drawing. In fact, 1:50,000 draws the same road half as long as 1:25,000. Try it with the ruler on this page by switching between the island and the town.
台灣在國小六年級的數學課教地圖比例尺。課綱特別要老師處理一個常見的錯誤：以為「比例分母愈大，相對邊長也愈大」。其實 1:50,000 畫出來的同一條路，長度只有 1:25,000 的一半。用本頁的尺，在「整座島」和「小鎮」之間切換試試看。

Myth vs. Fact · 常見迷思

Four things people get wrong 四個常見的誤會

✗ Myth · 迷思North is always at the top of a map.地圖的上方一定是北方。

✓ Fact · 事實Most maps today put north at the top, but not all of them. Always check the north arrow. National Geographic's teaching guide lists this as a common misconception.今天大多數的地圖上方是北，但不是全部。一定要看北方箭頭。國家地理學會的教學指引也把這一點列為常見的誤解。

✗ Myth · 迷思A large-scale map shows a large area.大比例尺的地圖，畫的範圍很大。

✓ Fact · 事實It is the other way around. A large-scale map, such as 1:5,000, shows a small area in great detail. The larger the second number, the smaller the scale.剛好相反。大比例尺的地圖（例如 1:5,000）畫的是小範圍、細節很多。比例尺的第二個數字越大，比例尺就越小。

✗ Myth · 迷思A map symbol means the same thing on every map.同一個符號在每一張地圖上的意思都一樣。

✓ Fact · 事實Each map chooses its own symbols. On older U.S. Geological Survey maps, one small symbol stood for both a school and a house of worship; on Taiwan's topographic maps a school is a small flag. That is why every map needs its own legend.每一張地圖自己決定用什麼符號。美國地質調查所的舊地形圖上，學校和教堂共用同一個符號；台灣的地形圖上，學校是一面小旗子。所以每一張地圖都需要自己的圖例。

✗ Myth · 迷思If you enlarge a map on a copier, its scale of 1:25,000 is still right.用影印機把地圖放大，上面寫的 1:25,000 還是對的。

✓ Fact · 事實The number is no longer right, because every distance on the copy has grown. The scale bar grows with the map, so it is still correct. The same is true when you zoom in on a screen.那個數字已經不對了，因為影本上每一段距離都變長了。圖形比例尺跟著地圖一起放大，所以仍然正確。在螢幕上放大地圖也是一樣。

Remember It · 記憶口訣

Reading a map in a sentence 一句話記住怎麼看地圖

Legend, north, scale圖例、北方、比例尺

Three things to find before you read anything else on a map.
看地圖上任何東西之前，先找這三樣。

Never Eat Soggy Waffles北、東、南、西，順時針

North, East, South, West, going clockwise. The first letters spell the English saying.
從北開始順時針轉一圈：北、東、南、西。英文的四個字首剛好是 Never Eat Soggy Waffles。

Big scale, small area比例尺大，範圍小

A large-scale map shows a small area in great detail.
大比例尺的地圖，畫的範圍小、細節多。

The bar never lies圖形比例尺不會騙人

If a map is enlarged or shrunk, the scale bar changes size with it, so it stays right.
地圖放大或縮小時，圖形比例尺跟著一起變，所以永遠是對的。

Before the Activities · 做活動之前

Working safely 安全提醒

  • When you walk around the school to measure, walk, do not run, and stay where your teacher can see you.在校園裡走動測量時，用走的、不要跑，並待在老師看得到的地方。
  • Do not count paces on stairs, near a road, or near the edge of a pond.不要在樓梯、馬路邊或水池邊數步數。
  • Keep the measuring tape low and do not let it snap back toward anyone's face.捲尺拉低一點，收回時不要讓它彈向任何人的臉。
  • If you use a compass, keep it away from phones, magnets, and metal desks, which can pull the needle off.使用指北針時，遠離手機、磁鐵和鐵桌，它們會讓指針偏掉。

Classroom Activity 1 · 課堂活動一

Map Your Classroom 畫一張教室地圖

You need · 準備材料

  • Graph paper with 1 cm squares1 公分方格紙
  • A measuring tape一捲捲尺
  • A pencil, an eraser, and colored pencils鉛筆、橡皮擦和色鉛筆
  • A compass, or a phone compass checked by the teacher一個指北針，或老師確認過的手機指南針

Steps · 步驟

  1. Measure the length and width of the classroom with the tape.用捲尺量教室的長和寬。
  2. Choose a scale so the room fits on the paper, for example 1 square = 50 cm. Write it as a scale: 1 cm = 50 cm, or 1:50.選一個比例尺，讓教室畫得進這張紙，例如一格＝50 公分。寫成比例尺：1 公分＝50 公分，也就是 1:50。
  3. Draw the walls, the door, the windows, and the teacher's desk to scale.照比例畫出牆、門、窗和講桌。
  4. Make up a symbol for each kind of thing, such as desks, cabinets, and plants, and put them in a legend.幫每一種東西想一個符號（例如課桌、櫃子、盆栽），寫進圖例。
  5. Find north with the compass and draw a north arrow. Then trade maps with a partner and find each other's seats.用指北針找出北方，畫上北方箭頭。然後和同伴交換地圖，找出對方的座位。

A classroom map at 1:50 is a very large-scale map: each desk can be drawn. A map of the whole school on the same paper would need a smaller scale and simpler symbols.
1:50 的教室地圖是很大比例尺的地圖，每張桌子都畫得出來。同一張紙要畫整座學校，就得用比較小的比例尺和比較簡單的符號。

Classroom Activity 2 · 課堂活動二

Treasure Hunt by Paces 用步數尋寶

You need · 準備材料

  • A measuring tape一捲捲尺
  • A simple map of the playground drawn by the teacher, with a north arrow老師畫的操場簡圖，上面有北方箭頭
  • A small object to hide, such as a beanbag一個要藏起來的小東西，例如沙包

Steps · 步驟

  1. Measure 10 meters with the tape. Walk it normally and count your steps. Divide 10 by your number of steps to find the length of one of your paces.用捲尺量出 10 公尺，用平常的步伐走過去，數一數走了幾步。10 除以步數，就是你一步的長度。
  2. One team hides the object and writes directions using the map: “From the flagpole, walk 30 paces north, then 12 paces east.”一組把東西藏起來，對著地圖寫下指示：「從旗桿出發，往北走 30 步，再往東走 12 步。」
  3. Mark the hiding place on the map with a symbol and add it to the legend.在地圖上用一個符號標出藏東西的地方，並加進圖例。
  4. Another team follows the directions to find it. Did their paces match yours?另一組照著指示去找。他們的步數和你們的對得上嗎？
  5. Rewrite the directions in meters instead of paces. Which version works for everyone?把指示改寫成公尺，而不是步數。哪一種寫法每個人都能用？

Paces are a scale that changes from person to person. Meters are the same for everyone, which is why maps use them.
步數是一種因人而異的「比例尺」；公尺對每個人都一樣，所以地圖用公尺。
