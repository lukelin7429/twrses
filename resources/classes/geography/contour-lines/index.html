How Do You Draw a Mountain on Flat Paper?怎麼在平面上畫出一座山？

Fill a tank around a mountain with water, one step at a time, and let the water's edge draw contour lines. Then look from above, read which slopes are steep, and turn any line on the map into a side view.
在一座山的四周一層一層灌水，讓水邊畫出等高線。再從正上方看、判斷哪裡陡，把地圖上任何一條線變成側面圖。

Big idea · 一句話看懂

A contour line joins points that are the same height above sea level. Draw one every few meters of height and a flat map can show a mountain: where the lines crowd together, the slope is steep, and where they spread apart, it is gentle.
等高線把海拔一樣高的點連起來。每隔固定的高度畫一條，平面的地圖就能畫出一座山：線擠在一起的地方坡很陡，線分得很開的地方坡很緩。

Unit 3: How Do You Draw a Mountain on Flat Paper?

A map is flat, but the land is not. Some places are high and some are low, and a hiker, a farmer, or a person choosing where to build a house needs to know which is which. How can a flat sheet of paper show a mountain? One clever answer is the contour line.

地圖是平的，土地卻不是。有些地方高、有些地方低，登山的人、種田的人、要選地方蓋房子的人，都得知道哪裡高、哪裡低。一張平平的紙，要怎麼畫出一座山？有一個很聰明的答案：等高線。

A contour line joins points that are the same height above sea level. Imagine an island in the middle of a calm sea. The edge of the water traces a line all the way around the island, and every point on that line is at a height of zero. That line is the coastline, and it is also the first contour line.

等高線把海拔一樣高的點連起來。想像大海中間有一座島，海面很平靜。水邊沿著島繞了一整圈，這條線上的每一個點，高度都是 0。這條線就是海岸線，也是第一條等高線。

Now imagine that the sea rises by 100 meters. The water's edge moves up the mountain and traces a new, shorter loop. Every point on it is 100 meters high. Let the water rise another 100 meters, and another, and each time draw the new edge. When you look down from above, you see rings inside rings. The difference in height between one line and the next is called the contour interval.

現在想像海面升高 100 公尺。水邊往山上移，畫出一圈新的、比較短的線，線上每一點都是 100 公尺高。再升高 100 公尺、再升高 100 公尺，每次都把新的水邊畫下來。從正上方往下看，你會看到一圈套著一圈。相鄰兩條線之間的高度差，叫做等高距。

The spacing of the lines tells you the shape of the land. Where the lines crowd together, the ground rises quickly over a short distance, so the slope is steep. Where the lines are far apart, the ground rises slowly, and the slope is gentle. A small closed ring in the middle marks a summit. Two summits with a low dip between them make a saddle, and lines that bend into a V show a valley.

線和線之間的距離，告訴你土地的形狀。線擠在一起的地方，很短的距離裡地面就升得很高，所以坡很陡；線分得很開的地方，地面升得很慢，坡就很緩。中間一個小小的封閉圓圈是山頂。兩座山頂中間低下去的地方是鞍部，彎成 V 字形的線代表山谷。

Mapmakers make contour maps easier to read in a few ways. Every fourth or fifth line is usually drawn thicker and labeled with its height, so you do not have to count all the lines. Many maps also color the land by height. The height of a mountain is measured from sea level, so countries choose an official starting point. Taiwan's heights are measured from the average sea level at the tide station in Keelung.

製圖的人用幾種方法讓等高線地圖更好讀。通常每四條或五條線就有一條畫得比較粗，並標上高度，不用一條一條數。很多地圖也會照高度幫陸地上色。山的高度是從海平面量起的，所以每個國家都要選一個官方的起點；台灣的高度，是從基隆潮位站量到的平均海平面算起。

With practice, you can read a contour map like a picture. You can tell which path to the top is shorter but steeper, where a river will flow, and what you would see if you cut the mountain in half and looked at it from the side. That side view is called a profile. Contour lines turn the ups and downs of the land into lines that a flat map can hold.

多練習幾次，你就能把等高線地圖當成一幅畫來讀。你看得出哪一條上山的路比較短、但比較陡，河會往哪裡流，以及把山從中間切開、從側面看會是什麼樣子。這個側面的樣子叫做剖面。等高線把土地的高高低低，變成了平面地圖裝得下的線。

Questions · 閱讀理解

Why is the coastline also a contour line?

Every point on the coastline is at the same height: sea level, 0 meters. A contour line joins points of the same height, so the coastline is the 0 m contour line.

On a contour map, two paths lead to the same summit. One crosses many lines that are close together. The other crosses the same lines, but they are far apart. Which path is steeper, and why?

The path across the crowded lines is steeper. It climbs the same height in a shorter distance.

Why do contour lines of different heights never cross each other on a normal map?

One spot on the ground has only one height. If two lines of different heights crossed, the crossing point would need two different heights at once, which is impossible.

Stories Behind the Lines · 線背後的故事

Lines under the water, lines on the land, Taiwan's maps, and the height of Yushan 水下的線、陸上的線、台灣的地形圖，和玉山的高度

Four short stories about how people learned to draw height on paper and how they measure it.
四個小故事，講人怎麼學會把高度畫到紙上，又怎麼量高度。

About 1730 · Under Water · 水下

The first lines measured depth最早的線量的是水深

Some of the first lines of equal height were really lines of equal depth, drawn for boats. Around 1729 and 1730, the Dutch engineer Nicolaas Cruquius published a map of the river Merwede that, according to Utrecht University Library, was the first to use a printed system of depth contours. A few years later the French geographer Philippe Buache drew depth lines on a chart of the English Channel, prepared in 1737 and published in 1752.
最早的一些「等高線」其實是「等深線」，是為了行船畫的。大約 1729 到 1730 年，荷蘭工程師克魯奎烏斯出版了一張默韋德河的地圖；依荷蘭烏特勒支大學圖書館的說明，這是第一張用印刷的等深線系統畫成的地圖。幾年後，法國地理學家布阿許在英吉利海峽的海圖上也畫了等深線，1737 年畫好、1752 年出版。

1700s · On Land · 陸上

From the riverbed to the mountain從河床到山上

Mapmakers then used the same idea for land. In the 1770s the British mathematician Charles Hutton used lines of equal height while working on survey measurements of a Scottish mountain called Schiehallion. In 1791 a map of France by Jean-Louis Dupain-Triel showed the whole country with contour lines 20 meters apart. Today almost every topographic map uses them.
後來製圖的人把同樣的想法用在陸地上。1770 年代，英國數學家哈頓在處理蘇格蘭一座叫希哈林的山的測量資料時，用了等高的線。1791 年，杜潘－特里耶爾的一張法國地圖，用每隔 20 公尺一條的等高線畫出整個國家。今天，幾乎每一張地形圖都用等高線。

Taiwan · 台灣的地形圖

Lines every 10 meters每 10 公尺一條線

Taiwan's 1:25,000 topographic maps, made by the National Land Surveying and Mapping Center, draw an ordinary contour line every 10 meters and a thicker index contour every 50 meters. Where the land is very flat, they add extra lines every 5 meters. On maps with a smaller scale, the lines are spaced farther apart: 20 and 100 meters at 1:50,000.
內政部國土測繪中心製作的 1/25,000 經建版地形圖，每 10 公尺畫一條首曲線、每 50 公尺畫一條加粗的計曲線；地勢很平的地方，再加每 5 公尺一條的間曲線。比例尺越小的地圖，線隔得越開：1/50,000 是 20 公尺和 100 公尺。

Taiwan · 0 公尺從哪裡算

How tall is Yushan, and tall from where?玉山多高？從哪裡算起？

The sea around Taiwan is not exactly the same height everywhere, because of currents such as the Kuroshio. So Taiwan measures every height from one place: the average sea level at the Keelung tide station, worked out from tide records from 1957 to 1991. From that zero, careful leveling surveys found Yushan's main peak to be 3,951.798 m in 2003, 3,952.430 m in 2017, and 3,952.433 m in 2022. The numbers are for the survey mark at the summit; the top of the stone monument there is about 1 meter higher.
台灣四周的海面並不是每個地方都一樣高，因為黑潮等因素會影響海面。所以台灣的高度都從同一個地方算起：基隆潮位站的平均海水面，用 1957 年到 1991 年的潮汐資料算出來。從這個零點出發，國土測繪中心用最精密的水準測量量出玉山主峰：2003 年 3,951.798 公尺、2017 年 3,952.430 公尺、2022 年 3,952.433 公尺。這些數字是山頂三角點的高度；主峰石碑的頂端大約再高 1 公尺。

Myth vs. Fact · 常見迷思

Four things people get wrong 四個常見的誤會

✗ Myth · 迷思A thick contour line marks a cliff or a special place.粗的等高線代表懸崖或特別的地方。

✓ Fact · 事實On many maps every fifth line is simply drawn thicker and labeled, so readers can count heights quickly. It means nothing special about the ground.很多地圖只是每五條線畫粗一條、標上高度，方便讀的人快速數高度，和地面本身沒有特別的關係。

✗ Myth · 迷思Contour lines are real lines you can see on the ground.等高線是地面上看得到的真的線。

✓ Fact · 事實They are imaginary, like the equator. Mapmakers measure heights and draw the lines. Our model shows how a rising sea could trace them.等高線和赤道一樣是想像的線，是製圖的人量了高度之後畫出來的。我們的模型示範的是：如果海面升高，水邊會怎麼把它們描出來。

✗ Myth · 迷思Where there are more contour lines, the mountain is bigger.等高線越多的地方，山就越大。

✓ Fact · 事實The number of lines between two places shows how much height changes. How close together they are shows how steep it is. A map with a smaller contour interval simply has more lines.兩地之間有幾條線，表示高度差多少；線擠得多近，表示有多陡。等高距比較小的地圖，線本來就比較多。

✗ Myth · 迷思Contour lines can cross whenever the land is complicated.地形複雜的時候，等高線可以交叉。

✓ Fact · 事實Each contour line joins points of one height, so lines of different heights never cross. Where a slope is extremely steep, the lines can come so close that they seem to merge.每一條等高線連的都是同一個高度的點，所以不同高度的線不會交叉。坡非常陡的地方，線會靠得很近，看起來像疊在一起。

Remember It · 記憶口訣

Contour lines in a sentence 一句話記住等高線

Same line, same height同一條線，同一個高度

Walk along a contour line and you never go up or down.
沿著一條等高線走，永遠不會上坡也不會下坡。

Close is steep, far is gentle線密坡陡，線疏坡緩

Crowded lines, hard climb. Spread-out lines, easy walk.
線擠在一起，爬得辛苦；線分得很開，走得輕鬆。

Rings in rings, a hilltop圈中有圈是山頂

The smallest ring in the middle is the top.
最裡面那個最小的圈，就是山頂。

The V points uphillV 字尖端朝上游

Where lines cross a stream they bend into a V, and the point of the V aims upstream.
等高線經過河谷時會彎成 V 字形，V 的尖端指向上游。

Before the Activities · 做活動之前

Working safely 安全提醒

  • An adult cuts the potato or the clay with a knife. Students use plastic tools and keep their fingers away from any blade.切馬鈴薯或黏土由大人用刀切；學生用塑膠工具，手指遠離刀刃。
  • Wipe up spilled water right away so that nobody slips, and keep water away from electrical outlets and devices.水灑出來馬上擦乾，免得有人滑倒；水要遠離插座和電器。
  • Use a waterproof marker on the model and wash your hands afterward.在模型上畫線用防水筆，做完要洗手。
  • If you walk a real slope to feel the difference, stay on marked paths and go with an adult.如果到真的坡地上走走、感受坡度的差別，要走在步道上，並且有大人陪同。

Classroom Activity 1 · 課堂活動一

Draw Contour Lines with Water 用水畫等高線

You need · 準備材料

  • A lump of modeling clay shaped into a mountain, or half a potato一團捏成山形的黏土，或半顆馬鈴薯
  • A clear plastic box taller than the mountain一個比山高的透明塑膠盒
  • A ruler, a waterproof marker, and a cup of water一把尺、一支防水筆、一杯水
  • A sheet of clear plastic and a dry-erase marker一張透明片和一支白板筆

Steps · 步驟

  1. Put the mountain in the box. Stand the ruler in a corner of the box.把山放進盒子裡，把尺立在盒子的一角。
  2. Pour in water until it is 1 cm deep. With the marker, trace the water's edge around the mountain.倒水到 1 公分深。用防水筆沿著山邊的水線畫一圈。
  3. Add water to 2 cm, then 3 cm, and so on, tracing a new line each time, until the top is under water.加水到 2 公分、3 公分……每次都畫一圈新的線，直到山頂淹沒。
  4. Pour the water out. Lay the clear sheet over the top of the box and look straight down. Copy the lines onto the sheet.把水倒掉。把透明片蓋在盒子上，從正上方往下看，把線描到透明片上。
  5. Label each line with its height in centimeters. Where are the lines closest together? Feel the mountain there.在每一條線旁邊標上高度（公分）。哪裡的線最擠？用手摸摸那裡的山坡。

This is the same idea as the 3D model: each line was once the edge of the water. A 1 cm interval on a 10 cm mountain gives about ten lines.
這和 3D 模型是同一個道理：每一條線都曾經是水邊。10 公分高的山、每 1 公分一條線，大約會有十條線。

Classroom Activity 2 · 課堂活動二

Cut a Profile 切一個剖面

You need · 準備材料

  • The contour map you drew on the clear sheet, or a printed one你畫在透明片上的等高線圖，或一張印好的圖
  • A strip of paper and a pencil一張紙條和一枝鉛筆
  • Graph paper方格紙

Steps · 步驟

  1. Draw a straight line across the map, from one side of the mountain to the other.在地圖上畫一條直線，從山的一邊畫到另一邊。
  2. Lay the edge of the paper strip along the line. Make a tick wherever a contour line crosses it, and write the height beside each tick.把紙條的邊貼著這條線，每遇到一條等高線就畫一個記號，旁邊寫上高度。
  3. Move the strip to the graph paper. Above each tick, put a dot at the right height.把紙條移到方格紙上，在每個記號的正上方、對應的高度點一個點。
  4. Join the dots with a smooth curve. You have drawn the profile.用平滑的曲線把點連起來，你就畫出了剖面。
  5. Check your profile with the “From Contour Lines to a Side View” map on this page.用本頁的「從等高線到剖面」地圖，檢查你畫的剖面。

Where the ticks on the strip are close together, the profile climbs steeply. This is the same rule as reading the map.
紙條上記號擠在一起的地方，剖面就爬得很陡。和讀地圖是同一個規則。
