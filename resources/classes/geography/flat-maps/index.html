Geography · Unit 1 · Lesson 1 · 地理 單元一 第一課

Why Is Every Flat Map Wrong?為什麼每一張平面地圖都是「錯」的？

Peel a 3D globe open and flatten it five different ways. Watch equal circles swell and squash, drag Greenland down to the equator, and find out why no flat map can get everything right.
把 3D 地球儀剝開，用五種方法攤平。看一樣大的圓怎麼被拉大、壓扁，把格陵蘭拖到赤道，再想想為什麼沒有一張平面地圖能樣樣都對。

Big idea · 一句話看懂

The surface of a ball cannot be laid flat without stretching it. So every flat map has to choose what to keep right: shape, area, distance, or direction. There is no best map, only a map that fits the job.
球的表面不可能不拉扯就攤平。所以每一張平面地圖都要選：形狀、面積、距離、方向，要保住哪一樣。沒有「最好的地圖」，只有「適合這個用途的地圖」。

This way of flattening · 這一種攤法

Plate carrée等距圓柱投影

The simplest way: longitude becomes left–right, latitude becomes up–down, and the grid turns into equal squares. It has been used for about two thousand years.最簡單的攤法：經度直接當左右、緯度直接當上下，格線變成一樣大的正方形。大約兩千年前就有人用了。

  • Shape形狀Stretched sideways away from the equator離赤道越遠，橫向拉得越寬
  • Area面積Too big toward the poles越靠近兩極畫得越大
  • Distance距離Right along north–south lines only只有南北方向的距離是對的
  • Direction方向North is up, but angles are bent上面是北，但角度不準

Mercator projection麥卡托投影

Made in 1569 for sailors. Every small shape keeps its true form, and a straight line on the map is a line of constant compass direction. The price is size: the map grows without limit toward the poles, so it has to be cut off before it reaches them.1569 年為航海設計。每個小地方的形狀都是對的，地圖上的一條直線就是羅盤方向不變的航線。代價是大小：越靠近兩極放得越大、沒有止境，所以地圖畫不到極點。

  • Shape形狀Small shapes stay true everywhere每個小地方的形狀都正確
  • Area面積4 times too big at 60°, far more beyond緯度 60 度放大 4 倍，再往上更多
  • Distance距離The scale changes with latitude比例尺隨緯度改變
  • Direction方向A straight line keeps one compass direction直線就是羅盤方向不變的線

Gall–Peters projection高爾－彼得斯投影

An equal-area map: every region covers the same share of the map as it does of the Earth. To manage that, land near the equator is drawn tall and thin, and land near the poles is drawn short and wide.一種等積投影：每個地區在地圖上占的比例，和它在地球上占的比例一樣。為了做到這一點，赤道附近的陸地被畫得又高又瘦，兩極附近的陸地被畫得又扁又寬。

  • Shape形狀Tall near the equator, flat near the poles赤道附近拉長，兩極附近壓扁
  • Area面積Sizes can be compared fairly大小可以公平比較
  • Distance距離True only along 45° north and south只有南北緯 45 度那兩條線上正確
  • Direction方向North is up, but angles are bent上面是北，但角度不準

Mollweide projection莫爾威投影

Another equal-area map, shaped like an oval, presented in 1805. The middle looks natural. Toward the left and right edges the land leans over, because the lines of longitude curve in to meet at the poles.另一種等積投影，整張圖是橢圓形，1805 年發表。中間看起來很自然；越靠左右兩邊，陸地越歪斜，因為經線要彎進來在兩極會合。

  • Shape形狀Leaning and squeezed near the edges靠近邊緣會歪斜、擠壓
  • Area面積Sizes can be compared fairly大小可以公平比較
  • Distance距離Not true in general一般來說不正確
  • Direction方向North is not straight up near the edges靠近邊緣時，北方不是正上方

Gores (an interrupted map)一瓣一瓣攤開（分瓣地圖）

Cut the globe into twelve strips, like the segments of an orange peel, and lay each one flat. Shapes and sizes stay close to true inside each strip, but the map is torn: neighbors on the globe end up far apart. Printed strips like these are glued onto balls to make real globes.把地球儀切成十二瓣，像橘子皮一樣，一瓣一瓣攤平。每一瓣裡面的形狀和大小都接近正確，但地圖被撕開了：在地球儀上相鄰的地方，在圖上隔得很遠。真正的地球儀，就是把這樣印好的紙條貼到球上做成的。

  • Shape形狀Close to true inside each strip每一瓣裡面接近正確
  • Area面積Sizes are kept大小保住了
  • Distance距離Broken wherever the map is torn地圖裂開的地方距離就斷了
  • Direction方向The gaps break every route裂縫把路線都切斷

Unit 1: Why Is Every Flat Map Wrong?

Peel a tangerine in one piece, then try to press the peel flat on the table. It will not lie flat. It bulges, and if you push harder, it tears. Now think about the Earth. The Earth is round like the tangerine, and a map is flat like the table. Every flat map of the world has to solve the tangerine-peel problem somehow.

把一顆橘子的皮完整剝下來，再試著把它壓平在桌上。它不會乖乖躺平：中間鼓起來，再用力壓就裂開。現在想想地球。地球像橘子一樣是圓的，地圖像桌面一樣是平的。每一張平面的世界地圖，都得想辦法解決這個「橘子皮問題」。

A globe does not have this problem. A globe is a small model of the Earth with the same round shape, so the shapes of the lands, their sizes, the distances between places, and the directions from one place to another can all be right at the same time. But a globe is hard to carry, hard to print in a book, and it never shows you the whole world at once. So people flatten the globe. A map projection is a set of rules for moving every point on the round Earth to a point on a flat sheet.

地球儀沒有這個問題。地球儀是一顆小小的地球模型，形狀同樣是圓的，所以陸地的形狀、大小、兩地之間的距離、從一地到另一地的方向，可以同時都是對的。但地球儀不好攜帶、沒辦法印在書裡，也沒辦法一眼看到整個世界。所以人們把地球儀攤平。地圖投影就是一套規則，把圓圓的地球上的每一個點，搬到一張平面上的某一個點。

Here is the trouble: something has to stretch. In the 1820s the mathematician Carl Friedrich Gauss proved a theorem which shows that the surface of a ball cannot be flattened without changing some of its distances. Mapmakers call this stretching distortion. A flat map can keep shapes right, or keep areas right, or keep some distances or directions right, but it can never keep all of them right everywhere.

麻煩在這裡：一定有地方要被拉扯。1820 年代，數學家高斯證明了一個定理，從這個定理可以知道：球的表面不可能攤平而不改變上面的某些距離。製圖的人把這種拉扯叫做變形。一張平面地圖可以把形狀畫對，或把面積畫對，或把某些距離、某些方向畫對，但不可能在每個地方把這些全部畫對。

The most famous choice was made in 1569 by Gerardus Mercator, who drew a map for sailors. On his map, a straight line always follows one compass direction, so a sailor could draw the route with a ruler. To make that work, the map stretches more and more toward the poles. At 60 degrees of latitude everything is drawn twice as wide and twice as tall, which means four times too large in area. That is why Greenland looks about as big as Africa on a Mercator map. In fact Africa is about fourteen times larger.

最有名的一個選擇，是 1569 年麥卡托做的。他畫了一張給航海的人用的地圖：在這張圖上，一條直線永遠朝著同一個羅盤方向，所以水手可以用尺畫出航線。為了做到這一點，地圖越靠近兩極就拉得越開。在緯度 60 度的地方，每樣東西都被畫成兩倍寬、兩倍高，也就是面積大了四倍。這就是為什麼在麥卡托地圖上，格陵蘭看起來和非洲差不多大。實際上，非洲大約是格陵蘭的十四倍。

Other maps make the opposite choice. An equal-area projection keeps every size right, so Greenland shrinks back to one-fourteenth of Africa. The price is shape: on one well-known equal-area map, lands near the equator look tall and thin. Some people say equal-area maps are fairer for classrooms, because the Mercator map makes places far from the equator look larger than they are. Others answer that the Mercator map was built for steering ships and was never meant for comparing sizes. Each side is describing what its map does well.

另一些地圖做了相反的選擇。等積投影把每個地方的大小都畫對，所以格陵蘭縮回非洲的十四分之一。代價是形狀：在一種常見的等積地圖上，赤道附近的陸地看起來又高又瘦。有人說等積地圖用在教室裡比較公平，因為麥卡托地圖把離赤道遠的地方畫得比實際大；也有人回答，麥卡托地圖本來就是為了讓船照著方向走而設計的，從來不是拿來比大小的。兩邊說的，都是自己那張地圖擅長的事。

So which map is the best? It depends on the job. To hold a compass course at sea, use a Mercator map. To compare the sizes of lands, use an equal-area map. To study the Arctic, use a map with the North Pole in the middle. To see everything right at once, pick up a globe. Every flat map is wrong somewhere, and a good map reader asks two questions: what was this map made for, and what did it give up?

那麼哪一張地圖最好？要看你拿它做什麼。在海上要照著羅盤方向走，用麥卡托地圖。要比較陸地的大小，用等積地圖。要研究北極，用北極在正中央的地圖。想要樣樣都對，就拿起地球儀。每一張平面地圖都有某個地方是「錯」的；會看地圖的人會問兩個問題：這張地圖是為了什麼做的？它犧牲了什麼？

Questions · 閱讀理解

Why can a globe show shapes, sizes, distances, and directions correctly all at once, when a flat map cannot?

A globe has the same round shape as the Earth, so nothing needs to be stretched. A flat map must flatten a round surface, and that cannot be done without distortion, so it has to give up at least one of the four.

On a Mercator map, Greenland looks about as big as Africa. What is really going on?

The Mercator map stretches everything more and more toward the poles. Greenland is far north, so it is drawn many times too large, while Africa lies across the equator and is drawn close to its true size. In reality Africa is about fourteen times larger than Greenland.

Your class wants a wall map to compare the sizes of the continents. A ship's officer wants a chart for steering by compass. Should they buy the same map? Why or why not?

No. The class needs an equal-area map, because it keeps sizes right even though it bends shapes. The officer needs a Mercator chart, because a straight line on it keeps one compass direction even though sizes are wrong. Each map fits a different job.

Map English · 地圖英語

Eight sentences for talking about maps 談地圖時用得到的八句英文

Every lesson in this series has a Map English card: eight sentences for saying where things are and what a map shows. Listen, repeat, then try them with a map or globe in front of you.
這個系列每一課都有一張「地圖英語」卡：八句用英文說位置、說地圖的句子。先聽、再跟著唸，然後對著一張地圖或一顆地球儀自己說說看。

  1. The equator runs through the middle of the map.

    赤道從地圖的正中間穿過。

    runs through: a line crossing a place一條線穿過某個地方

  2. Greenland is near the North Pole.

    格陵蘭在北極附近。

    near: close to靠近

  3. Taiwan is off the coast of East Asia.

    台灣在東亞的外海。

    off the coast of: in the sea, not far from the shore在外海、離岸不遠

  4. Africa lies on both sides of the equator.

    非洲橫跨赤道的兩邊。

    on both sides of: north and south of a line在一條線的兩邊

  5. Greenland looks bigger on this map than it really is.

    格陵蘭在這張地圖上看起來比實際大。

    looks … than it really is: for distortion說地圖的變形

  6. North is at the top of the map.

    地圖的上方是北邊。

    at the top of / at the bottom of在上方／在下方

  7. Australia is in the Southern Hemisphere.

    澳洲在南半球。

    in the Northern / Southern Hemisphere在北半球／南半球

  8. This map keeps the sizes right, but it stretches the shapes.

    這張地圖把大小畫對了，但把形狀拉長了。

Stories Behind the Maps · 地圖背後的故事

A sailor's map, a long argument, the map in your phone, and Taiwan's own grid 水手的地圖、一場爭論、手機裡的地圖，和台灣自己的坐標

Four short stories about people choosing what a map should keep.
四個小故事，講的都是人怎麼選擇一張地圖要保住什麼。

1569 · For Sailors · 給水手的地圖

Mercator's straight lines麥卡托的直線

Gerardus Mercator presented his world map in 1569. Part of its long Latin title means “for the use of navigators.” A course that keeps one compass direction is called a rhumb line, and on his map every rhumb line is straight. Sea charts still use the projection today. Its weakness for wall maps was known from the start: the poles cannot be shown at all, and lands far from the equator look far too large.
麥卡托在 1569 年發表他的世界地圖，那一長串拉丁文標題裡有一段的意思是「供航海者使用」。羅盤方向固定不變的航線叫做恆向線，在他的地圖上，每一條恆向線都是直的。今天的海圖仍然使用這種投影。拿來當掛圖的缺點也從一開始就看得出來：兩極根本畫不出來，離赤道遠的陸地看起來大得太多。

A Long Argument · 一場爭論

Which map belongs on the classroom wall?教室牆上該掛哪一張地圖？

In the 1970s Arno Peters promoted an equal-area map and criticized the Mercator map for making lands far from the equator look too large. In 2017 the public schools of Boston began using a map of this kind. Many mapmakers replied that the Mercator map was designed for navigation, and that the Gall–Peters map bends shapes badly. In 1989 and 1990, seven geographic organizations in North America advised against using any rectangular map as a general world map, and they named both of these. The argument shows that a map is a choice.
1970 年代，彼得斯推廣一種等積地圖，並批評麥卡托地圖把離赤道遠的陸地畫得太大。2017 年，美國波士頓的公立學校開始使用這一類地圖。許多製圖的人則回應：麥卡托地圖是為航海設計的，而高爾－彼得斯地圖把形狀扭曲得很厲害。1989 到 1990 年，北美有七個地理學會與製圖學會建議，一般用途的世界地圖不要用任何長方形的投影，這兩種都點名在內。這場爭論讓人看見：地圖是一種選擇。

In Your Pocket · 口袋裡的地圖

Why online maps are square網路地圖為什麼是正方形的

Most online maps use a form of the Mercator projection, often called Web Mercator. Google's documentation explains that the map is cut off at about 85 degrees north and south, which makes the whole world a square. A square splits neatly into four smaller squares, again and again, and those are the tiles your phone loads as you zoom in. The projection is also good at keeping angles. Zoomed all the way out, it has the same size problem as any Mercator map.
大多數的網路地圖用的是麥卡托投影的一種變化，常被稱為 Web Mercator。Google 的技術文件說明：地圖在南北緯大約 85 度的地方切掉，這樣整個世界剛好是一個正方形。正方形可以整齊地一分為四、再一分為四，這些就是你放大地圖時手機載入的一塊塊圖磚。這種投影也擅長保住角度。把畫面縮到最小時，它和所有的麥卡托地圖一樣，有大小不準的問題。

Taiwan · 台灣

A Mercator map turned on its side一張橫過來的麥卡托地圖

Taiwan's official coordinate system, TWD97, uses a projection called Transverse Mercator. Imagine the Mercator map turned on its side, so that the line of least distortion runs north–south instead of along the equator. Taiwan is a long island running north to south, so this fits it well. According to the National Land Surveying and Mapping Center, the central line for the main island is 121 degrees east, and the zone is only two degrees wide, which keeps the distortion very small.
台灣的官方坐標系統 TWD97，用的是「橫麥卡托投影」。想像把麥卡托地圖橫過來，讓變形最小的那條線不再是赤道，而是一條南北向的線。台灣是一座南北狹長的島，這種投影很適合。依內政部國土測繪中心的說明，台灣本島的中央子午線是東經 121 度，而且一帶只有經度兩度寬（二度分帶），所以變形非常小。

Myth vs. Fact · 常見迷思

Four things people get wrong 四個常見的誤會

✗ Myth · 迷思The map on the classroom wall shows the true sizes of the lands.教室牆上的地圖，畫的就是陸地真正的大小。

✓ Fact · 事實Only if it is an equal-area map. Many wall maps and almost all online maps enlarge the lands that are far from the equator.只有等積地圖才是。很多掛圖和幾乎所有的網路地圖，都把離赤道遠的陸地放大了。

✗ Myth · 迷思The Mercator map is a wrong map, and an equal-area map is the right one.麥卡托地圖是錯的地圖，等積地圖才是對的。

✓ Fact · 事實Each keeps something different. The Mercator map keeps shapes and compass directions. An equal-area map keeps sizes. Each is right for its job and wrong for the other one.它們保住的東西不一樣。麥卡托地圖保住形狀和羅盤方向，等積地圖保住大小。各自在自己的用途上是對的，換到對方的用途上就不對。

✗ Myth · 迷思With a good enough computer, someone will make a flat map with no distortion.只要電腦夠厲害，總有一天能做出完全沒有變形的平面地圖。

✓ Fact · 事實This is a matter of geometry, and computing power cannot change it. It was proved about two hundred years ago that a sphere cannot be flattened without distortion. A map can only choose where to put it.這是幾何的問題，電腦再厲害也改變不了。大約兩百年前就有人證明：球面不可能毫無變形地攤平。地圖只能選擇把變形放在哪裡。

✗ Myth · 迷思On a map, the shortest way between two places is always a straight line.在地圖上，兩地之間最短的路一定是一條直線。

✓ Fact · 事實On the round Earth, the shortest path follows a great circle. On a Mercator map that path usually looks curved, which is why long flights seem to bend toward the poles.在圓的地球上，最短的路沿著大圓走。這條路畫在麥卡托地圖上通常是彎的，所以長途航線看起來會往兩極的方向彎。

Before the Activities · 做活動之前

Working safely 安全提醒

  • Peel the fruit with your fingers. If the peel needs a starting cut, an adult makes it. Students do not use knives.水果用手剝。如果需要先劃一刀才好剝，由大人來劃；學生不拿刀。
  • Use round-tipped scissors, cut away from your other hand, and pass scissors with the handles toward the other person.用圓頭剪刀，剪的時候刀口不要朝向另一隻手；遞剪刀時把握柄朝向對方。
  • Blow up balloons only as far as the teacher shows you, and keep them away from your face when you cut them. Check first whether anyone in the class is allergic to latex.氣球只吹到老師示範的大小，剪的時候離臉遠一點。活動前先確認班上有沒有人對乳膠過敏。
  • Wash your hands after handling the fruit, and wipe up juice so that nobody slips.碰過水果之後洗手，滴在地上的果汁擦乾，免得有人滑倒。

Classroom Activity 1 · 課堂活動一

The Tangerine-Peel Map 橘子皮地圖

You need · 準備材料

  • One tangerine or small orange for each pair每兩人一顆橘子或小柳丁
  • A marker that writes on the peel一支能畫在果皮上的筆
  • A sheet of paper and a heavy book一張紙和一本厚書
  • A pencil一枝鉛筆

Steps · 步驟

  1. Before peeling, draw a line around the middle of the fruit for the equator. Draw four or five circles of the same size on the peel, some near the equator and some near the top.剝皮之前，先在水果的腰上畫一圈當赤道。再在果皮上畫四、五個一樣大的圓，有的靠近赤道，有的靠近頂端。
  2. Peel the fruit with your fingers, keeping the peel in as few pieces as you can.用手把皮剝下來，盡量讓皮保持完整、越少片越好。
  3. Lay the peel on the paper with the outside facing up, and press it flat with the book. Watch where it tears.把皮攤在紙上、外面朝上，用書壓平。看看它從哪裡裂開。
  4. Trace the outline of the flattened peel on the paper. Mark the gaps.在紙上描出壓平後果皮的輪廓，把裂縫標出來。
  5. Look at your circles. Which ones were torn or pulled out of shape? Compare with the orange-peel map in the 3D model.看看你畫的圓，哪些被撕開、被拉得變形了？和 3D 模型裡的「橘子皮地圖」比一比。

The peel tears because it cannot stretch. A printed map does not tear because the mapmaker stretches the picture instead. Ask the class: which would you rather have, gaps or stretching?
果皮會裂開，是因為它不能伸縮。印出來的地圖不會裂開，是因為製圖的人改用「拉扯圖形」來代替。問問全班：你寧願要裂縫，還是要拉扯？

Classroom Activity 2 · 課堂活動二

The Balloon Map 氣球地圖

You need · 準備材料

  • One round balloon for each group每組一個圓形氣球
  • A permanent marker一支油性筆
  • Round-tipped scissors圓頭剪刀
  • Four pushpins or pieces of tape, and a piece of cardboard四個圖釘或幾段膠帶，和一塊厚紙板

Steps · 步驟

  1. Blow the balloon up to the size of a grapefruit and tie it. Draw an equator and six small squares of the same size, some near the equator and some near the knot.把氣球吹到葡萄柚那麼大，打結。畫一條赤道，再畫六個一樣大的小正方形，有的靠近赤道，有的靠近打結的地方。
  2. Let the air out slowly. Cut the balloon open from the knot to the opposite end.慢慢把氣放掉。從打結的地方剪到另一端，把氣球剪開。
  3. Stretch the rubber into a rectangle on the cardboard and fix the four corners with tape or pins. An adult helps with the pins.把橡皮在厚紙板上拉成長方形，四個角用膠帶或圖釘固定。圖釘請大人幫忙。
  4. Measure your squares now. Which ones grew the most? Are they still square?現在量一量那些正方形。哪幾個變大最多？還是正方形嗎？
  5. Open the 3D model and choose the Mercator map. Do the orange circles change in the same places as your squares?打開 3D 模型，選麥卡托地圖。橘色的圓變化的地方，和你的正方形一樣嗎？

Rubber can stretch, so the balloon map has no gaps, but the squares near the edge are pulled much bigger than the ones in the middle. That is distortion you can measure with a ruler.
橡皮可以伸縮，所以氣球地圖沒有裂縫；但靠邊的正方形被拉得比中間的大很多。這就是可以用尺量出來的變形。
