Big idea · 一句話看懂

Two numbers work like an address for any spot on Earth. Latitude tells how far north or south of the equator it is. Longitude tells how far east or west of the prime meridian it is. Both numbers are angles.
兩個數字，就是地球上任何一個地方的地址。緯度說它在赤道的北邊或南邊多遠，經度說它在本初子午線的東邊或西邊多遠。這兩個數字都是角度。

Unit 2: How Do You Find Any Place on Earth?

Imagine a ship far out in the middle of the ocean. There are no streets, no signs, and no buildings, only water in every direction. If the ship needs help, how can the sailors tell anyone where they are? On land we use addresses with street names and house numbers. The ocean has none of these. Luckily, the whole Earth has an address system of its own, and it needs only two numbers.

想像一艘船在大海的正中央。沒有街道、沒有路牌、沒有建築，四面八方只有海水。如果這艘船需要幫忙，船上的人要怎麼告訴別人自己在哪裡？在陸地上，我們用有路名和門牌號碼的地址；大海上這些都沒有。還好，整個地球有一套自己的地址系統，而且只需要兩個數字。

The first number is latitude. Picture a line around the middle of the Earth, halfway between the North Pole and the South Pole. This is the equator. Latitude tells you how far north or south of the equator a place is. It is measured in degrees, from 0 at the equator to 90 at each pole. Lines of latitude are circles that run east and west. They are also called parallels, because they never meet, and they get smaller as they get closer to the poles.

第一個數字是緯度。想像有一條線繞著地球的腰，剛好在北極和南極的正中間，這就是赤道。緯度告訴你一個地方在赤道的北邊或南邊多遠。它用「度」來量，赤道是 0 度，兩極各是 90 度。緯線是東西向的圓圈。它們彼此平行、永遠不會相交，而且越靠近兩極，圓圈就越小。

The second number is longitude. Lines of longitude, also called meridians, run from the North Pole to the South Pole. One of them is chosen as the starting line and is called the prime meridian. It passes through Greenwich, in London. Longitude tells you how far east or west of the prime meridian a place is, from 0 degrees up to 180 degrees. Unlike lines of latitude, all lines of longitude meet at the two poles.

第二個數字是經度。經線（英文是 lines of longitude，也叫 meridians）從北極一路畫到南極。其中有一條被選為起點，叫做本初子午線，它通過倫敦的格林威治。經度告訴你一個地方在本初子午線的東邊或西邊多遠，從 0 度到 180 度。和緯線不一樣的是，所有的經線都在南北兩極會合。

Put the two numbers together and you have an address. Latitude is said first, then longitude. The middle of Taiwan is at about 23.5 degrees north, 121 degrees east. For more exact work, each degree is divided into 60 smaller parts called minutes. One degree of latitude is about 111 kilometers on the ground wherever you are. One degree of longitude is about 111 kilometers at the equator, but it shrinks toward the poles, where all the lines come together.

把兩個數字放在一起，就是一個地址。先說緯度，再說經度。台灣的中間大約在北緯 23.5 度、東經 121 度。需要更精確的時候，每一度再分成 60 個更小的單位，叫做「分」。一度緯度在地面上大約是 111 公里，到哪裡都差不多。一度經度在赤道上也大約是 111 公里，但越往兩極越短，因為所有的經線在那裡聚在一起。

Why does longitude start at Greenwich? The equator is set by the way the Earth spins, so nobody had to choose it. The prime meridian is different. Any line from pole to pole could have been the starting line, and for a long time different countries used different ones. In 1884, delegates from 25 countries met in Washington, D.C., and voted to use the line through the observatory at Greenwich, which most ships' charts already used. The prime meridian is an agreement among people.

經度為什麼從格林威治算起？赤道是地球自轉的方式決定的，不需要誰來選。本初子午線就不一樣了：任何一條從北極到南極的線都可以當起點，而且有很長一段時間，各國用的起點都不一樣。1884 年，25 個國家的代表在美國華盛頓開會，表決採用通過格林威治天文台的那一條線——當時大多數船隻用的海圖，本來就以它為準。本初子午線是人和人之間的約定。

For sailors, latitude was the easy number. In the Northern Hemisphere, the height of the North Star above the horizon is about the same as your latitude. Longitude was much harder, because finding it means knowing the exact time. The Earth turns 15 degrees every hour, so a clock that keeps the time of a known place can tell a ship how far east or west it has sailed. In the 1700s, the English clockmaker John Harrison built clocks that could do this at sea. Today the satellites of GPS give your phone both numbers in a few seconds.

對航海的人來說，緯度是比較容易的那個數字。在北半球，北極星在地平線上的高度，大約就等於你所在的緯度。經度難得多，因為要知道經度，就得知道準確的時間。地球每小時轉 15 度，所以只要有一個鐘一直保持某個已知地點的時間，就能算出船往東或往西走了多遠。十八世紀，英國的鐘錶匠哈里森做出了能在海上辦到這件事的鐘。今天，GPS 衛星幾秒鐘就能把這兩個數字送到你的手機上。

Questions · 閱讀理解

Lines of latitude are called parallels. Why does that name not fit lines of longitude?

Parallel lines never meet. Lines of latitude never meet, but all lines of longitude come together at the North Pole and the South Pole, so they are not parallel.

Why did people have to hold a meeting to choose the prime meridian, but not the equator?

The equator is fixed by the Earth's spin: it is the circle halfway between the two poles. No line of longitude is special in that way, so people had to agree on which one would be zero. In 1884 they agreed on the line through Greenwich.

A place is at 60 degrees north. You walk along its line of latitude until your longitude has changed by one degree. Did you walk more or less than 111 kilometers? Why?

Less. Lines of longitude get closer together toward the poles. At 60 degrees north, one degree of longitude is only half as long as at the equator, about 56 kilometers.

How Long Is One Degree? 一度有多長？

A degree is an angle, and its length on the ground depends on the direction. Slide the latitude from the equator to the pole. The gold box is one degree of latitude tall and one degree of longitude wide, drawn to scale. The numbers treat the Earth as a perfect ball with a radius of 6,371 km; the real Earth is slightly flattened, so real values differ by less than one percent.
「度」是角度，它在地面上有多長，要看是哪個方向。把緯度從赤道拉到極點看看。金色的格子高是一度緯度、寬是一度經度，照比例畫。這裡把地球當成半徑 6,371 公里的正圓球來算；真正的地球稍微扁一點，所以實際的數字會差不到百分之一。

Stories Behind the Lines · 線背後的故事

A vote, a clock, a line across Taiwan, and a line that moved 一場表決、一座鐘、一條穿過台灣的線，和一條移動了的線

Four short stories about how people drew lines on the Earth and agreed on them.
四個小故事，講人怎麼在地球上畫線，又怎麼取得共識。

1884 · A Vote · 一場表決

Twenty-two to one二十二比一

In October 1884, 41 delegates from 25 countries met in Washington, D.C., to choose one starting line for longitude. The vote for Greenwich was 22 in favor and 1 against, with 2 countries not voting. According to the Royal Observatory, Greenwich was chosen because it caused the least disruption: nearly two-thirds of the world's ships already used charts based on it. The same meeting agreed to count longitude in two directions, up to 180 degrees east and 180 degrees west.
1884 年 10 月，25 個國家的 41 位代表在美國華盛頓開會，要選出一條共同的經度起點。表決結果是 22 票贊成格林威治、1 票反對，另有 2 國棄權。依格林威治皇家天文台的說明，選它是因為造成的變動最小：當時全世界將近三分之二的船，用的海圖本來就以它為準。同一場會議也決定，經度往東、往西兩個方向各算到 180 度。

1700s · A Clock · 一座鐘

Carrying the time to sea把時間帶到海上

The Earth turns 360 degrees in 24 hours, which is 15 degrees every hour. So if a ship carries a clock set to the time of a known place, each hour of difference from local noon means 15 degrees of longitude. The hard part was building a clock that stayed accurate on a rolling ship. In 1714 the British Parliament passed the Longitude Act, offering rewards of up to 20,000 pounds for a solution. John Harrison completed his sea watch, now called H4, in 1759. It was tested on a voyage to Jamaica that began in 1761, and again on a voyage to Barbados in 1764.
地球 24 小時轉 360 度，也就是每小時 15 度。所以只要船上有一個鐘，一直保持某個已知地點的時間，它和當地正午每差一小時，就是 15 度的經度。難的是做出一個在搖晃的船上還走得準的鐘。1714 年，英國國會通過《經度法案》，懸賞最高兩萬英鎊徵求解法。哈里森在 1759 年完成他的航海鐘（今天稱為 H4），1761 年起在前往牙買加的航程中測試，1764 年又在前往巴貝多的航程中再測一次。

Taiwan · 台灣

A line that crosses the island, and moves一條穿過台灣、還會移動的線

The Tropic of Cancer, at about 23.5 degrees north, runs across Taiwan. Taiwan's Tourism Administration lists three markers on the main island: in Shuishang Township in Chiayi County, on the Wuhe Terrace in Ruisui Township in Hualien County, and in Fengbin Township in Hualien County. At noon on the June solstice the Sun is straight overhead on this line, and a pole standing upright casts no shadow. The line is not fixed. It is drifting south by a little more than 14 meters a year, and in 1788 it ran through what is now a street corner in Chiayi City.
北回歸線大約在北緯 23.5 度，橫貫台灣。交通部觀光署的資料列出本島的三座北回歸線標：嘉義縣水上鄉、花蓮縣瑞穗鄉舞鶴台地、花蓮縣豐濱鄉。每年夏至正午，太陽直射這條線，可以看到「立竿不見影」。這條線並不是固定的：它平均每年以 14 公尺多的速度往南移，1788 年時通過的地方，是今天嘉義市的一個路口。

Today · 今天

The line at Greenwich has moved 102 meters格林威治那條線，移了 102 公尺

Visitors to Greenwich like to stand on the brass line that marks the historic prime meridian, with one foot in the east and one in the west. But a phone standing on that line does not read exactly zero. Satellite navigation uses a newer reference line, which passes 102.5 meters to the east of the old one. The old line was set by a telescope on the ground; the new one comes from measurements of the whole Earth. A degree of longitude is still a degree, counted from a slightly different zero.
到格林威治的遊客，喜歡站在標示舊本初子午線的那條銅線上，一腳在東、一腳在西。但把手機放在那條線上，讀數並不會剛好是 0。衛星導航用的是一條比較新的參考線，在舊線東邊 102.5 公尺的地方。舊線是用地面上的一架望遠鏡定出來的，新線則來自對整個地球的測量。經度的「度」還是一樣的度，只是零點稍微挪了一點。

Myth vs. Fact · 常見迷思

Four things people get wrong 四個常見的誤會

✗ Myth · 迷思A degree is a fixed distance, like a kilometer.「一度」是固定的距離，就像一公里一樣。

✓ Fact · 事實A degree is an angle. One degree of latitude is about 111 km everywhere, but one degree of longitude is about 111 km at the equator, about 56 km at 60 degrees, and nothing at the poles.「度」是角度。一度緯度到哪裡都大約是 111 公里；一度經度在赤道約 111 公里，在緯度 60 度約 56 公里，到了兩極是 0。

✗ Myth · 迷思The prime meridian is a natural line, just like the equator.本初子午線和赤道一樣，是自然形成的線。

✓ Fact · 事實The equator is set by the Earth's spin. The prime meridian is an agreement: it was chosen by a vote at an international conference in 1884.赤道是地球自轉決定的。本初子午線則是約定：它是 1884 年一場國際會議表決選出來的。

✗ Myth · 迷思Lines of longitude are parallel to each other, like lines of latitude.經線彼此平行，就像緯線一樣。

✓ Fact · 事實Lines of latitude are parallel and never meet. Lines of longitude are farthest apart at the equator and all meet at the poles.緯線彼此平行、永遠不相交。經線在赤道上隔得最開，到了兩極全部會合。

✗ Myth · 迷思If you stand on the line at Greenwich, your phone will show a longitude of exactly zero.站在格林威治的那條線上，手機顯示的經度會剛好是 0。

✓ Fact · 事實Satellite navigation counts longitude from a newer line about 102 meters to the east, so on the historic line a phone shows a tiny bit of west longitude.衛星導航的經度是從東邊大約 102 公尺的一條新線算起的，所以站在舊線上，手機會顯示一點點西經。

Remember It · 記憶口訣

Latitude and longitude in a sentence 一句話記住經緯度

Latitude is like a ladder緯線像梯子的橫桿

The lines lie flat, one above another, and you climb them to go north.
一條一條橫著排上去；往北走，就像往上爬。

Longitude lines are long經線是長長的直線

Every one runs the long way, from pole to pole, and they are all the same length.
每一條都從北極拉到南極，而且每一條都一樣長。

Latitude first, then longitude先緯度，再經度

The same order as in the dictionary: “la” comes before “lo.”
和字典的順序一樣：la 排在 lo 前面。

N and S for latitude, E and W for longitude緯度配南北，經度配東西

A latitude can never be more than 90. A longitude can go up to 180.
緯度最多 90，經度最多 180。

Before the Activities · 做活動之前

Working safely 安全提醒

  • Stretch rubber bands only as far as they need to go, and never aim one at another person. A band that snaps can hurt an eye.橡皮筋只拉到需要的長度，絕對不要對著別人。橡皮筋彈開可能傷到眼睛。
  • Use washable markers on the ball or fruit, and wash your hands before eating anything.在球或水果上畫線用水性筆；吃東西之前先洗手。
  • If you go outside at night to look for the North Star, go with an adult, stay away from roads, and take a flashlight.晚上到戶外找北極星時，要有大人陪同、遠離馬路，並帶手電筒。
  • Never look at the Sun, even to find a direction.絕對不要直視太陽，即使只是為了找方向。

Classroom Activity 1 · 課堂活動一

A Rubber-Band Globe 橡皮筋地球儀

You need · 準備材料

  • A ball about the size of a grapefruit, or an orange一顆葡萄柚大小的球，或一顆柳丁
  • Six rubber bands in two colors六條橡皮筋，兩種顏色
  • A washable marker and two small stickers一支水性筆和兩張小貼紙
  • A globe or world map to check against一顆地球儀或一張世界地圖，用來對照

Steps · 步驟

  1. Put the two stickers on opposite sides of the ball. These are the North Pole and the South Pole.把兩張貼紙貼在球的正對面兩側，當作北極和南極。
  2. Stretch one band around the middle, halfway between the poles. This is the equator, 0 degrees latitude.把一條橡皮筋套在球的腰上，剛好在兩極的正中間。這是赤道，緯度 0 度。
  3. Stretch three bands of the other color so that each one passes over both poles. Space them evenly. You have made six half-circles of longitude, 60 degrees apart. Mark one of them with the marker as the prime meridian.用另一種顏色的三條橡皮筋，每一條都繞過兩極，間隔平均。這樣就有六條半圓形的經線，彼此相隔 60 度。用筆把其中一條標成本初子午線。
  4. Try to add a band for 60 degrees north. Notice that it has to be a much smaller circle than the equator, and that it will not stay on unless the ball is a little rough.試著再加一條北緯 60 度的橡皮筋。你會發現它得是一個比赤道小很多的圓，而且除非球面有點粗糙，否則很難固定。
  5. Find where Taiwan belongs: about a quarter of the way from the equator to the North Pole, and one-third of the way around the ball to the east of your prime meridian. Mark it with a dot.找出台灣該在的位置：從赤道到北極大約四分之一的地方，從本初子午線往東繞大約三分之一圈。點一個點。

Ask the class: which bands are all the same length, and which are not? The longitude bands are all the same. The latitude bands get shorter toward the poles. That is why one degree of longitude shrinks.
問問全班：哪些橡皮筋一樣長，哪些不一樣？經線那幾條都一樣長；緯線那幾條越靠近兩極越短。這就是一度經度會變短的原因。

Classroom Activity 2 · 課堂活動二

Call the Coordinates 報坐標

You need · 準備材料

  • A printed world map with latitude and longitude lines every 30 degrees, one for each pair每兩人一張印好的世界地圖，上面每 30 度有一條經緯線
  • Ten small counters or paper dots for each pair每組十個小圓片或小紙點
  • A folder to stand between the two partners一個可以立在兩人中間的資料夾

Steps · 步驟

  1. Partner A secretly places five counters on the map, each where two grid lines cross.甲同學偷偷把五個圓片放在地圖上，每一個都放在兩條格線的交點。
  2. Partner A reads each position aloud in English, latitude first: “thirty degrees north, one hundred twenty degrees east.”甲同學用英文把每個位置唸出來，先說緯度：「thirty degrees north, one hundred twenty degrees east.」
  3. Partner B listens and places a counter on the same crossing on a second map.乙同學邊聽邊在另一張地圖的同一個交點放上圓片。
  4. Remove the folder and compare the two maps. Score one point for each counter in the right place.把資料夾拿開，比對兩張地圖。每放對一個圓片得一分。
  5. Swap roles. For a harder round, place counters halfway between the lines and say “about fifteen degrees south.”交換角色。想難一點，就把圓片放在兩條線的中間，說「about fifteen degrees south」。

The most common mistake is swapping the two numbers. Remind students that a latitude can never be more than 90, so “one hundred twenty degrees north” cannot exist.
最常見的錯誤是把兩個數字說反。提醒學生：緯度不可能超過 90，所以不會有「北緯 120 度」。
