What Does Taiwan Look Like from Above?從天上看，台灣長什麼樣子？

Turn a 3D model of Taiwan made from real government elevation data, then cut it from west to east. See why the mountains sit east of the middle, the west is wide and gentle, and the east drops steeply to the sea.
轉動一座用政府真實高程資料做的立體台灣，再從西到東切一刀。看看為什麼高山偏在中間的東邊、西邊寬廣平緩、東邊陡陡地落進海裡。

Big idea · 一句話看懂

Taiwan's high mountains run from north to south, a little east of the middle. West of them, the land slopes gently down to wide plains, tablelands, and basins; east of them, it drops steeply to the sea. This shape decides where rivers flow and where people live.
台灣的高山從北到南縱走，偏在中間的東邊。山的西邊緩緩地降到寬廣的平原、台地和盆地；東邊卻陡陡地落進海裡。這個形狀決定了河怎麼流、人住在哪裡。

Unit 9: What Does Taiwan Look Like from Above?

From an airplane or a satellite, Taiwan's main island looks like a long spindle, wide in the middle and narrow at both ends. According to the Executive Yuan, it is 394 kilometers from north to south and 144 kilometers across at its widest. The Tropic of Cancer, from Lesson 7, crosses it near Chiayi.

從飛機或衛星上看，臺灣本島像一個長長的紡錘，中間寬、兩頭窄。行政院的資料說，它南北長 394 公里，東西最寬的地方 144 公里。第七課的北回歸線，就在嘉義附近穿過它。

Most of the island is mountains. The Executive Yuan lists five ranges, from east to west: the Coastal Mountain Range, the Central Mountain Range, the Xueshan Range, the Yushan Range, and the Alishan Range. In the elevation data used on this page, about 31 out of every 100 squares of land are higher than 1,000 meters, and only about 29 out of 100 are lower than 100 meters.

島上大部分是山。行政院列出五條山脈，由東到西是：海岸山脈、中央山脈、雪山山脈、玉山山脈和阿里山山脈。在本頁用的高程資料裡，陸地每 100 格大約有 31 格高於 1,000 公尺，低於 100 公尺的只有大約 29 格。

The highest peak is Yushan. In 2022, surveyors from the National Land Surveying and Mapping Center carried their instruments up the trail and measured its main peak at 3,952.433 meters, give or take 0.046 meters. The government's Taiwan portal counts 268 peaks higher than 3,000 meters.

最高峰是玉山。民國 111 年（2022 年），國土測繪中心的測量人員把儀器沿著步道一路背上山，量得玉山主峰高 3,952.433 公尺，誤差在 0.046 公尺之內。政府的台灣入口網說，台灣有 268 座超過 3,000 公尺的山峰。

The mountains are not in the exact middle. Cut the island from west to east through Changhua: the highest point on that line, about 3,163 meters, is 96 kilometers from the west coast but only 30 kilometers from the east coast. So the east side is about three times as steep as the west side. On the east coast, the mountains drop almost straight into the sea.

山不在正中間。從彰化從西到東切一刀：那條線上的最高點大約 3,163 公尺，離西海岸 96 公里，離東海岸卻只有 30 公里。所以東邊大約比西邊陡三倍。在東海岸，山幾乎是直直地落進海裡。

West of the Alishan Range, the land becomes gentler. From north to south, the Executive Yuan lists the Taipei Basin, the tablelands of Taoyuan, Hsinchu, and Miaoli, the Taichung Basin, the Chianan Plain, and the Pingtung Plain. In the east there is much less flat land: the narrow Huadong Longitudinal Valley lies between the Central and Coastal Mountain Ranges, and the Lanyang Plain opens to the sea in the northeast.

阿里山山脈以西，地勢變得平緩。行政院由北到南列出：臺北盆地、桃竹苗臺地、臺中盆地、嘉南平原和屏東平原。東部的平地少得多：窄窄的花東縱谷夾在中央山脈和海岸山脈之間，蘭陽平原則在東北朝海敞開。

This shape matters. The Central Mountain Range is the divide between the rivers that flow east and those that flow west. Because the west side is wider, its rivers are longer: the Zhuoshui River, Taiwan's longest at 186.6 kilometers, starts high in the mountains and reaches the sea between Changhua and Yunlin. And most of Taiwan's people live on the wide, gentle west side, as Lesson 8 would predict.

這個形狀很重要。中央山脈是東、西部河川的分水嶺。西邊比較寬，河就比較長：台灣最長的濁水溪，主流長 186.6 公里，從高山一路流到彰化和雲林之間入海。而台灣大多數的人住在寬廣平緩的西部——正如第八課會預測的。

Questions · 閱讀理解

Why are the rivers on Taiwan's west side longer than those on the east side?

The main ridge is closer to the east coast, so there is more land between the mountains and the sea on the west. The rivers have farther to go.

Why does a 3D model of Taiwan need vertical exaggeration?

Taiwan is hundreds of kilometers long but only about 4 kilometers high. At true scale, the mountains would look almost flat.

Where do you think most of Taiwan's farms and cities are, and why?

On the plains and in the basins of the west, where the land is low and flat, and rivers bring water.

Stories Behind the Shape · 形狀背後的故事

Measuring an island 量一座島


2022 · Yushan · 玉山

Carrying a level up the mountain把水準儀背上山

In October 2022, surveyors from the National Land Surveying and Mapping Center measured Yushan step by step, from a benchmark at Tataka up the trail to the main peak, using 23 survey points. Their result: 3,952.433 meters, give or take 0.046 meters.
2022 年 10 月，國土測繪中心的測量人員從塔塔加的一等水準點出發，沿著登山步道選了 23 個點，一段一段量到玉山主峰。結果是 3,952.433 公尺，誤差在 0.046 公尺之內。

Executive Yuan · 行政院

394 by 144394 乘 144

The Executive Yuan describes the main island as spindle-shaped, 394 kilometers from north to south and 144 kilometers across at its widest, with a coastline of 1,150.95 kilometers.
行政院說，臺灣本島呈紡錘形，南北縱長 394 公里，東西最大寬度 144 公里，環島海岸線長 1,150.95 公里。

Water Resources Agency · 水利署

The longest river最長的河

The Zhuoshui River starts between the main and east peaks of Hehuan Mountain, at 3,220 meters, and runs 186.6 kilometers to the sea between Dacheng in Changhua and Mailiao in Yunlin.
濁水溪發源於合歡山主峰與東峰之間（海拔 3,220 公尺），主流長 186.6 公里，在彰化縣大城鄉與雲林縣麥寮鄉之間入海。

2026 · Open data · 開放資料

A map made of 20-meter squares用 20 公尺方格拼成的地圖

The Ministry of the Interior shares a height for every 20-meter square of Taiwan as open data. This page averages them into 1-kilometer squares: 36,417 squares of land. In that 20-meter data, the highest point near Yushan is 3,947.46 meters, a few meters below the surveyed summit, because each square is an average.
內政部把臺灣每一個 20 公尺方格的高度，當作開放資料公開。本頁把它們平均成 1 公里的格子：陸地共 36,417 格。在 20 公尺的資料裡，玉山附近最高的一格是 3,947.46 公尺，比實測的主峰矮幾公尺，因為每一格都是平均。

Myth vs. Fact · 常見迷思

Four things people get wrong 四個常見的誤會

✗ Myth · 迷思Taiwan's mountains are right in the middle of the island.台灣的山就在島的正中間。

✓ Fact · 事實They are east of the middle. Through Changhua, the highest point is 96 kilometers from the west coast but only 30 kilometers from the east coast.偏在中間的東邊。通過彰化的那條線，最高點離西海岸 96 公里，離東海岸只有 30 公里。

✗ Myth · 迷思A 3D model shows how steep the mountains really are.立體模型呈現的就是山真正的陡度。

✓ Fact · 事實Most 3D models of Taiwan stretch the heights. This page's model makes them 5 times taller, and its cross-sections 10 times, or the mountains would look almost flat.大多數台灣的立體模型都把高度拉高。本頁的模型放大 5 倍、剖面放大 10 倍，不然山看起來幾乎是平的。

✗ Myth · 迷思A basin is closed in on every side.盆地四面八方都被圍住。

✓ Fact · 事實Most basins have a lower side where a river flows out. In the data, the Taichung Basin is lower to the south than it is to the east, west, and north.大多數盆地有一邊比較低，河從那裡流出去。在資料裡，臺中盆地的南邊比東、西、北三邊都低。

✗ Myth · 迷思The east of Taiwan is nothing but mountains.台灣的東部全都是山。

✓ Fact · 事實There is less flat land in the east, but not none: the Huadong Longitudinal Valley and the Lanyang Plain are home to towns and farms.東部的平地比較少，但不是沒有：花東縱谷和蘭陽平原上都有城鎮和農田。

Before the Activities · 做活動之前

Working safely 安全提醒

  • Use a plastic knife or a piece of string to cut the clay model, never a sharp blade.切黏土模型用塑膠刀或一條線，不要用尖銳的刀片。
  • Keep clay away from your mouth, and wash your hands when you finish.黏土不要放進嘴裡，做完要洗手。
  • Cover the desk with newspaper or a mat before you start.開始之前先在桌上鋪報紙或墊子。
  • When you look at real mountains, stay on marked trails and with an adult; mountain weather can change quickly.去看真的山時，要走在步道上、有大人陪同；山上的天氣變化很快。

Classroom Activity 1 · 課堂活動一

A Clay Taiwan 黏土做的台灣

You need · 準備材料

  • A printed outline of Taiwan, about 30 centimeters long一張大約 30 公分長的台灣輪廓圖
  • Modeling clay in two or three colors兩三種顏色的黏土
  • A piece of string or dental floss一段線或牙線

Steps · 步驟

  1. Look at the 3D model from the south. Notice where the mountains are highest.從南邊看 3D 模型，注意山最高的地方在哪裡。
  2. Press a thin layer of clay over the whole outline for the plains.在整張輪廓上壓一層薄薄的黏土，當作平原。
  3. Add a long ridge of clay for the mountains. Put it closer to the east coast, and make the east side steeper.加上一長條黏土當作高山，放在比較靠東海岸的地方，東邊做得比較陡。
  4. Cut the model from west to east with the string, near Changhua. Look at the cut face from the side.在靠近彰化的地方，用線從西到東把模型切開，從側面看切開的那一面。
  5. Compare your cut with the cut on this page. What did you get right, and what would you change?拿你的切面和本頁的剖面比一比。哪裡做對了？哪裡要改？

Your model is probably taller than the real Taiwan would be at this size. That is vertical exaggeration, and the 3D model on this page does it too.
照這個大小，你的模型多半比真的台灣高很多。這叫做「垂直誇大」，本頁的 3D 模型也這樣做。

Classroom Activity 2 · 課堂活動二

Draw the Cut 畫出剖面

You need · 準備材料

  • Graph paper方格紙
  • A pencil and a ruler鉛筆和尺
  • The “Cut the Island” activity on this page本頁的「切一刀」活動

Steps · 步驟

  1. Choose one line in “Cut the Island.” Write down the highest point and how far it is from each coast.在「切一刀」選一條線，寫下最高點的高度，以及它離兩邊海岸各多遠。
  2. On graph paper, let one square across be 10 kilometers. Mark the two coasts and the highest point.在方格紙上，橫的一格代表 10 公里，標出兩邊的海岸和最高點。
  3. Choose a height scale, for example one square up for every 500 meters. Draw the mountain between the coasts.選一個高度的比例，例如往上一格代表 500 公尺，在兩個海岸之間畫出這座山。
  4. Work out how many times you exaggerated the height. (Hint: compare 10 kilometers across with 500 meters up.)算一算你把高度放大了幾倍。（提示：拿橫的 10 公里和直的 500 公尺比。）
  5. Which side of your drawing is steeper? Does it match the page?你畫的圖哪一邊比較陡？和本頁一樣嗎？

If one square across is 10 kilometers and one square up is 500 meters, the heights are exaggerated 20 times.
如果橫的一格是 10 公里、直的一格是 500 公尺，高度就放大了 20 倍。
