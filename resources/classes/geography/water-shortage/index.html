Why Can Taiwan Run Short of Water When It Rains So Much?台灣雨這麼多，為什麼還會缺水？

Let it rain on a 3D model of Taiwan and follow the raindrops to the sea. See why short, steep rivers and a rainy season only half a year long can leave a rainy island short of water.
在立體的台灣上下一場雨，跟著雨滴一路流到海。看看為什麼又短又陡的河、只有半年的雨季，會讓一座多雨的島也缺水。

Big idea · 一句話看懂

Taiwan gets a lot of rain, but most of it falls in a few months, and its short, steep rivers carry it quickly to the sea. Many people share the water that is left. So even a rainy island has to store water in reservoirs and save it.
台灣的雨很多，但大多下在幾個月裡，又短又陡的河很快就把雨水送進海裡；剩下的水還要分給很多人。所以即使是多雨的島，也得用水庫把水存起來、省著用。

Unit 10: Why Can Taiwan Run Short of Water When It Rains So Much?

Taiwan is a rainy island. According to the Water Resources Agency, it gets about 2,500 millimeters of rain in an average year, 2.6 times the world average. Yet the Water Resources Agency warns that Taiwan's water is hard to store and use. How can a place with so much rain run short?

台灣是一座多雨的島。經濟部水利署說，台灣平均一年大約下 2,500 毫米的雨，是世界平均的 2.6 倍。可是水利署也提醒，台灣的水資源不容易儲存和利用。雨這麼多的地方，怎麼會缺水？

The first reason is timing. About 78 percent of the year's rain falls from May to October, during the plum rains and the typhoon season. In the south the difference is even bigger: in Kaohsiung, about 91 out of every 100 millimeters fall in those six months. From November to April, it hardly rains there at all.

第一個原因是時間。一年的雨大約 78% 下在 5 到 10 月，也就是梅雨和颱風的季節。南部的差別更大：在高雄，每 100 毫米的雨大約有 91 毫米下在這半年；11 月到 4 月，那裡幾乎不下雨。

The second reason is the shape of the land. As Lesson 9 showed, Taiwan's mountains are high and close to the sea, so its rivers are short and steep. The Water Resources Agency explains that after the rain flows into the rivers, most of it runs straight into the sea. Rain that falls on the east side can reach the sea in just a few dozen kilometers.

第二個原因是地形。第九課看過，台灣的山又高又靠近海，所以河又短又陡。水利署說明，降雨匯入河川之後，大多直接奔流入海。落在東邊的雨，只要走幾十公里就到海了。

On the 3D model, every drop of rain follows the land downhill. The main mountain ridge is a divide: rain on one side flows west, and rain on the other side flows east. West of the ridge, the rivers are longer. The Zhuoshui River, Taiwan's longest, runs 186.6 kilometers, while the Xiuguluan River on the east coast is about 81 kilometers long.

在 3D 模型上，每一滴雨都順著地形往下流。主要的山脊是一條分水嶺：落在一邊的雨往西流，落在另一邊的往東流。山脊以西的河比較長：台灣最長的濁水溪長 186.6 公里，東海岸的秀姑巒溪大約 81 公里。

The third reason is people. Taiwan is crowded, as Lesson 8 showed. The Water Resources Agency works out that each person's share of the rain is about 4,000 tons a year, less than one-fifth of the world average. Rain that runs into the sea in a few hours cannot be shared later.

第三個原因是人。第八課看過，台灣人口很稠密。水利署算出，每個人每年能分到的雨水大約 4,000 噸，不到世界平均的五分之一。幾個小時就流進海裡的雨，之後就沒辦法再分給大家用了。

So Taiwan stores water. At the end of 2025 there were 95 reservoirs, 66 of them on the main island. But their measured useful storage is only about three-quarters of what they were designed to hold. Saving water, catching rain, and using water more than once all help a rainy island get through the dry season.

所以台灣要把水存起來。民國 114 年（2025 年）底，台灣有 95 座水庫，其中 66 座在本島。但實測的有效容量，只有當初設計的四分之三左右。節約用水、收集雨水、把水重複利用，都能幫一座多雨的島撐過乾季。

Questions · 閱讀理解

Why can a place with a lot of rain still run short of water?

Most of the rain falls in a few months, short and steep rivers carry it quickly to the sea, and many people share what is left.

Why are the rivers on Taiwan's west side longer than those on the east side?

The main ridge is closer to the east coast, so there is more land, and a longer way down, on the west side.

Besides building reservoirs, what can families and schools do to get through the dry season?

Save water, fix leaks, collect rainwater for plants and cleaning, and use water more than once.

Stories Behind the Water · 水背後的故事

Counting the rain 數一數雨


Water Resources Agency · 水利署

2.6 times the world average世界平均的 2.6 倍

The Water Resources Agency writes that Taiwan, in a monsoon climate zone, averages about 2,500 millimeters of rain a year, 2.6 times the world average, and that in the north the wet season gets about 6.5 parts of the rain to 3.5 in the dry season, while in the south it is about 9 to 1.
水利署說，台灣位在季風氣候區，年均雨量約 2,500 毫米，是世界平均的 2.6 倍；豐水期與枯水期的降雨比，北部約 6.5 比 3.5，南部約 9 比 1。

2020 · Water Resources Agency · 水利署

Less than one-fifth不到五分之一

Because the rain falls in the wet season, the land is steep, and many people live here, each person's share of the rain works out to about 4,000 tons a year, less than one-fifth of the world average.
因為降雨集中在豐水期、地形陡峻，加上人口稠密，換算成每人每年能分配的降雨量大約 4,000 噸，不到世界平均的五分之一。

2025 · Reservoirs · 水庫

Less room than planned比設計時少的容量

At the end of 2025, Taiwan had 95 reservoirs. Their measured useful storage was 1,986.22 million cubic meters, 76.53 percent of the 2,595.42 million they were designed to hold.
民國 114 年底，台灣有 95 座水庫。實測有效容量 1,986.22 百萬立方公尺，是設計有效容量 2,595.42 百萬立方公尺的 76.53%。

1991–2020 · Central Weather Administration · 中央氣象署

Thirty years of rain三十年的雨

The bar charts on this page use the Central Weather Administration's monthly averages for 1991 to 2020. Each number is an average of thirty years, so any single year can be wetter or drier.
本頁的長條圖用中央氣象署 1991 到 2020 年的月平均。每一個數字都是三十年的平均，所以任何一年都可能比較濕或比較乾。

Myth vs. Fact · 常見迷思

Four things people get wrong 四個常見的誤會

✗ Myth · 迷思A rainy place can never run short of water.多雨的地方不可能缺水。

✓ Fact · 事實What matters is how much water can be kept and shared. In Taiwan, most rain falls in a few months and quickly runs into the sea.重要的是能留下多少、分給多少人。台灣的雨大多下在幾個月裡，又很快流進海。

✗ Myth · 迷思Taiwan's rain falls evenly through the year.台灣的雨平均分布在一整年。

✓ Fact · 事實About 78 percent falls from May to October. In Kaohsiung, about 91 percent does.大約 78% 下在 5 到 10 月；在高雄是大約 91%。

✗ Myth · 迷思Rain on the highest mountain always flows to the nearest coast.最高的山上的雨，一定流向最近的海岸。

✓ Fact · 事實It flows downhill, whichever side of the ridge it lands on. A drop on the west side of Yushan travels all the way to the west coast.雨水往低處流，看它落在山脊的哪一邊。落在玉山西側的雨，一路流到西海岸。

✗ Myth · 迷思All of Taiwan's rivers take about the same time to reach the sea.台灣的河，水流到海的路都差不多長。

✓ Fact · 事實In this page's model, the longest river path on the west side is about 151 kilometers, and the longest on the east side about 87 kilometers.在本頁的模型裡，西邊最長的河道大約 151 公里，東邊最長的大約 87 公里。

Before the Activities · 做活動之前

Working safely 安全提醒

  • Wipe up spilled water right away so no one slips.水灑出來要馬上擦乾，免得有人滑倒。
  • Never play near rivers, ditches, or reservoirs after heavy rain; the water can rise very quickly.大雨過後，絕對不要在河邊、水溝或水庫附近玩，水可能漲得非常快。
  • Use only tap water in the activities, and do not drink the water from your model.活動只用自來水，模型裡的水不要喝。
  • Ask an adult before you put anything outside to catch rain, and set it where no one will trip over it.要把東西放到戶外接雨之前先問大人，放在不會絆倒別人的地方。

Classroom Activity 1 · 課堂活動一

A Mountain in a Tray 托盤裡的山

You need · 準備材料

  • A large tray or plastic box一個大托盤或塑膠盒
  • Crumpled newspaper and a sheet of plastic wrap or aluminum foil揉皺的報紙和一張保鮮膜或鋁箔紙
  • A spray bottle or a watering can with water一個噴水瓶或裝了水的澆花器
  • A few drops of food coloring (optional)幾滴食用色素（可以不用）

Steps · 步驟

  1. Pile the newspaper in the tray into a long ridge, closer to one side, like Taiwan's mountains.在托盤裡用報紙堆出一道長長的山脊，偏向一邊，像台灣的山。
  2. Cover it with the plastic wrap or foil and press it down so the valleys show.蓋上保鮮膜或鋁箔紙，往下壓，讓山谷的形狀出來。
  3. Spray “rain” evenly over the whole mountain.在整座山上平均地噴「雨」。
  4. Watch where the water runs. Does it reach the edge faster on the steep side or the gentle side?看水往哪裡流。陡的那一邊和緩的那一邊，哪一邊比較快流到盒子邊？
  5. Now make a hollow on the gentle side with your thumb: a reservoir. How much water can you keep there?用大拇指在緩的那一邊壓出一個凹洞，當作水庫。你能在那裡留下多少水？

This is why reservoirs are built in valleys: they catch some of the water before it runs away.
這就是為什麼水庫蓋在山谷裡：在水流走之前，先接住一部分。

A Class Rain Gauge 全班的雨量筒

You need · 準備材料

  • A clear plastic bottle with straight sides一個直筒的透明塑膠瓶
  • Scissors (an adult cuts the bottle)剪刀（由大人剪瓶子）
  • A ruler and tape尺和膠帶
  • A notebook for the class record一本全班的紀錄本

Steps · 步驟

  1. Have an adult cut off the top of the bottle. Turn the top upside down into the bottle as a funnel.請大人剪下瓶子的上半部，倒過來插進瓶子裡當漏斗。
  2. Tape the ruler to the side, with zero at the bottom of the straight part.把尺貼在瓶子側面，0 對準直筒部分的底。
  3. Put the gauge in an open place, away from roofs and trees.把雨量筒放在空曠的地方，避開屋簷和樹。
  4. Every school day, read the water level in millimeters, write it down, and empty the bottle.每個上學日讀一次水位（毫米），記下來，再把水倒掉。
  5. After a month, add up your numbers and compare them with the bar chart for the nearest weather station.一個月後把數字加起來，和最近的氣象站的長條圖比一比。

Rain is measured in millimeters: how deep the water would be if none of it ran away or soaked in.
雨量用毫米計算：如果雨水沒有流走、也沒有滲下去，會積多深。
