How Many Continents and Oceans Are There?世界有幾大洲、幾大洋？

Color the land of a 3D Earth by continent and the sea by ocean, then switch between different ways of counting them. See why the answer can be seven, six, or five continents, and four or five oceans.
在 3D 地球上，把陸地依大洲上色、海洋依大洋上色，再切換不同的算法。看看為什麼答案可以是七、六或五大洲，四或五大洋。

Big idea · 一句話看懂

Where the land and the water are is a fact of geography. How we divide them into continents and oceans, and what we call each part, is something people agree on. That is why there is more than one way to count.
陸地和海水在哪裡，是地理的事實；怎麼把它們分成大洲、大洋，每一塊叫什麼名字，是人約定的。所以算法不只一種。

Unit 6: How Many Continents and Oceans Are There?

Ask a class how many continents there are, and you may hear more than one answer. Many students say seven. Some say six, and a few say five. Here is the surprise: each of these answers is taught somewhere in the world. Which one is right depends on how you count.

問全班世界有幾大洲，你可能會聽到不只一個答案。很多同學說七個，有人說六個，還有人說五個。令人驚訝的是：這些答案世界上都有地方在教。哪一個對，要看你怎麼算。

A continent is one of the main divisions of land on Earth. But look at a globe, and you will see that the land does not come in neat, separate pieces. Europe and Asia sit on one huge piece of land. Africa is joined to Asia by the narrow Isthmus of Suez, and South America is joined to North America by the narrow Isthmus of Panama. The lines between continents were drawn by people, not by nature. National Geographic calls the line between Europe and Asia an imaginary one.

大洲是地球上陸地的主要分區。可是看看地球儀，你會發現陸地並不是一塊一塊整齊分開的。歐洲和亞洲在同一大塊陸地上。非洲靠著狹窄的蘇伊士地峽和亞洲相連，南美洲靠著狹窄的巴拿馬地峽和北美洲相連。大洲之間的界線是人畫的，不是大自然畫的。國家地理學會就說，歐洲和亞洲之間的那條線是想像出來的。

So there is more than one way to count. By convention, many people count seven continents: Asia, Africa, North America, South America, Antarctica, Europe, and Australia. Some geographers join Europe and Asia into Eurasia, which gives six. In some countries, students learn that North and South America are one continent, America, which also gives six. Join both pairs, and you get five: Eurasia, America, Africa, Antarctica, and Australia.

所以算法不只一種。照慣例，很多人算七大洲：亞洲、非洲、北美洲、南美洲、南極洲、歐洲、澳洲。有些地理學家把歐洲和亞洲合成歐亞大陸，就是六個。有些國家的學生學到南北美洲是同一洲（美洲），也是六個。兩對都合起來，就是五個：歐亞大陸、美洲、非洲、南極洲、澳洲。

The oceans are counted in different ways too. The U.S. National Ocean Service puts it simply: “There is only one global ocean.” All the water is connected, and the names divide it into parts. For a long time, people named four oceans: the Pacific, the Atlantic, the Indian, and the Arctic. Today most countries also count a fifth, the Southern Ocean, which surrounds Antarctica from its coast to 60 degrees south. National Geographic began counting it in 2021, and in 2023 the International Hydrographic Organization approved a resolution recognizing it.

大洋也有不同的算法。美國國家海洋局說得很簡單：「全世界只有一個海洋。」所有的海水都是相連的，名字只是把它分成幾個部分。很長一段時間，人們只命名了四個大洋：太平洋、大西洋、印度洋和北冰洋（也叫北極海）。現在大多數國家還算第五個：南大洋，也就是從南極洲海岸到南緯 60 度、環繞南極洲的海。國家地理學會從 2021 年開始算它，2023 年國際水文組織也通過了承認它的決議。

Whichever way you count, some facts stay the same. About 71 percent of Earth's surface is covered by ocean. Asia is the largest continent, with about 44.6 million square kilometers. The Pacific is the largest ocean: even without the South China Sea, it covers about 161.8 million square kilometers, more than all the land on Earth put together.

不管怎麼算，有些事實不會變。地球表面大約 71% 被海洋覆蓋。亞洲是最大的洲，大約 4,460 萬平方公里。太平洋是最大的大洋：就算不算南海，也有大約 1 億 6,180 萬平方公里，比地球上所有的陸地加起來還大。

And Taiwan? In the seven-continent count, Taiwan is in Asia. Taiwan's Executive Yuan describes the island as lying in the middle of the East Asian island arc, with the Pacific Ocean to its east. Its main island and 21 smaller islands cover about 35,887 square kilometers, so Asia could hold more than 1,200 Taiwans. The next time someone asks how many continents there are, you can answer with a question: “How are you counting?”

那台灣呢？在七大洲的算法裡，台灣在亞洲。行政院的介紹說，台灣位在東亞島弧的中央，東邊面臨太平洋。台灣本島和 21 個附屬島嶼合起來大約 35,887 平方公里，所以亞洲放得下一千二百多個台灣。下次有人問世界有幾大洲，你可以用一個問題回答：「你怎麼算？」

Questions · 閱讀理解

Why can people give different answers to “How many continents are there?” and all be right?

Because the lines between continents are drawn by people. Some counts join Europe and Asia, or North and South America, so the number can be seven, six, or five.

Which two narrow pieces of land join continents together, and which continents do they join?

The Isthmus of Suez joins Africa and Asia, and the Isthmus of Panama joins North America and South America.

Why do people say there is really only one global ocean?

All the oceans are connected, so the water flows from one to another. The names divide one ocean into parts.

Stories Behind the Names · 名字背後的故事

Who decides how to count? 誰決定怎麼算？

There is no single office that decides how many continents there are. Here are four moments when people counted the world in different ways.
世界上沒有一個機關決定有幾大洲。下面是四個人們用不同方式數世界的時刻。

Olympic Charter · 奧林匹克憲章

Five rings, five continents五個環，五個洲

The Olympic Charter says the five interlocking rings represent “the union of the five continents and the meeting of athletes from throughout the world.” The charter does not say which five continents it means.
《奧林匹克憲章》說，五個相扣的環代表「五大洲的團結，以及全世界運動員的相聚」。憲章並沒有說是哪五個洲。

2021 · National Geographic · 國家地理學會

A fifth ocean on the map地圖上的第五個大洋

National Geographic had recognized four oceans since it began making maps in 1915. On June 8, 2021, World Oceans Day, it began recognizing the Southern Ocean as the fifth. The U.S. Board on Geographic Names had already been using the name since 1999.
國家地理學會從 1915 年開始製作地圖，一直只承認四個大洋。2021 年 6 月 8 日世界海洋日，它開始把南大洋算作第五個。美國地名委員會則從 1999 年起就已經使用這個名字。

2023 · IHO · 國際水文組織

A line at 60 degrees south南緯 60 度的一條線

The International Hydrographic Organization publishes the limits of oceans and seas. In May 2023 its Assembly approved a resolution recognizing the Southern Ocean, with its northern limit at 60 degrees south. Argentina asked for its own position to be attached to the record.
國際水文組織出版海洋與海的界限。2023 年 5 月，它的大會通過決議承認南大洋，北界定在南緯 60 度。阿根廷要求把自己的立場附在紀錄上。

2017 · Zealandia · 西蘭大陸

A continent under the sea?藏在海底的大陸？

In 2017, geologists described a 4.9-million-square-kilometer region of continental crust in the southwest Pacific, around New Zealand, and called it Zealandia. About 94 percent of it is under water. Not everyone agrees it should count as a continent.
2017 年，地質學家描述了西南太平洋、紐西蘭周圍一片 490 萬平方公里的大陸地殼，稱它為「西蘭大陸」。它大約 94% 在海面下。不是每個人都同意它應該算一個洲。

Myth vs. Fact · 常見迷思

Four things people get wrong 四個常見的誤會

✗ Myth · 迷思Everyone in the world agrees there are seven continents.全世界都同意有七大洲。

✓ Fact · 事實Seven is common, but six and five are also taught. The number depends on which pieces of land are grouped together.七大洲很常見，但也有人教六大洲、五大洲；數目要看哪些陸地被合在一起。

✗ Myth · 迷思Europe and Asia are separated by the sea.歐洲和亞洲之間隔著海。

✓ Fact · 事實They are one landmass. The usual line runs along the Ural Mountains and the Caspian and Black Seas, and it was drawn by people.它們是同一塊大陸。常見的分界沿著烏拉山、裏海和黑海，是人畫出來的。

✗ Myth · 迷思Each ocean is a separate body of water.每一個大洋都是分開的一片水。

✓ Fact · 事實All the oceans are connected. There is one global ocean, and the names divide it into parts.所有的大洋都是相連的。全世界只有一個海洋，名字只是把它分成幾個部分。

✗ Myth · 迷思The Southern Ocean is brand-new water.南大洋是新出現的海。

✓ Fact · 事實The water was always there. What changed is the naming: the IHO used the name in 1937, dropped it in 1953, and approved a resolution recognizing it in 2023.那片海一直都在，改變的是命名：國際水文組織 1937 年用過這個名字，1953 年拿掉，2023 年又通過決議承認它。

  • Toss the inflatable globe gently and underhand, only to a partner who is ready, and keep the area clear.充氣地球儀要輕輕地由下往上拋，只拋給準備好的同伴，周圍保持淨空。
  • Do not throw the globe near windows, lights, or people who are not playing.不要在窗戶、燈具或沒有參加的人附近丟球。
  • Blow up the globe with a pump if you have one. If you feel dizzy while blowing, stop and rest.有打氣筒就用打氣筒充氣；自己吹氣時覺得頭暈，就停下來休息。

Classroom Activity 1 · 課堂活動一

Sort the Puzzle Pieces 把拼圖分一分

You need · 準備材料

  • A printed world map with no borders, such as one made from the 3D model's flat view or a blank outline map一張沒有國界的世界地圖，例如空白輪廓圖
  • Scissors and glue剪刀和膠水
  • Three large sheets of paper三張大紙

Steps · 步驟

  1. Cut out the big pieces of land. Where land is connected, keep it in one piece.把大塊的陸地剪下來。陸地連在一起的地方，就保持一整塊。
  2. Count your pieces. Is the number seven? Which continents stayed joined together?數一數有幾塊。是七塊嗎？哪些大洲還連在一起？
  3. On the first sheet, group the pieces into seven continents. You will need to cut where people usually draw the lines.在第一張紙上分成七大洲。你需要在大家通常畫線的地方剪開。
  4. On the second sheet, group them into six. On the third, group them into five.在第二張紙上分成六大洲；第三張分成五大洲。
  5. Compare the three sheets. Write one sentence about what changed and what stayed the same.比較三張紙，寫一句話：什麼改變了？什麼沒有變？

The land is the same on all three sheets. Only the grouping changes. That is the big idea of this lesson.
三張紙上的陸地都一樣，改變的只有分組。這就是這一課的核心概念。

Classroom Activity 2 · 課堂活動二

Catch the Globe: How Much Is Water? 接住地球儀：水佔多少？

You need · 準備材料

  • An inflatable globe or a beach ball with a world map一顆充氣地球儀，或印有世界地圖的海灘球
  • A tally sheet with two columns: land and water一張記錄表，分成「陸地」和「水」兩欄

Steps · 步驟

  1. Stand in a circle. Toss the globe gently to a classmate.圍成一圈，把地球儀輕輕拋給一位同學。
  2. The catcher looks at where their right thumb landed: land or water? Make a tally mark.接住的人看右手大拇指落在哪裡：陸地還是水？在記錄表上畫一筆。
  3. Keep going until you have at least 50 catches.一直玩到至少接了 50 次。
  4. Divide the number of water catches by the total. What fraction of your catches landed on water?用落在水上的次數除以總次數。你們有幾成落在水上？
  5. Compare your answer with the number on this page. The more catches you make, the closer you usually get.把你們的答案和本頁的數字比一比。接得越多次，通常就越接近。

This is called sampling: many random tries give a good estimate of the whole.
這叫做「抽樣」：隨機試很多次，就能大致估計整體。
