Geography · Unit 3 · Lesson 11 · 地理 單元三 第十一課

Where Do Taiwan's People Live?台灣的人住在哪裡？

Stand Taiwan's people up as columns on a 3D model of the island. See how almost everyone lives on the low plains and basins in the west, and why the high mountains are nearly empty.
把台灣的人變成一根根柱子，立在立體的台灣上。看看為什麼幾乎所有人都住在西部低平的平原和盆地，高山卻幾乎沒有人。

Big idea · 一句話看懂

Most of Taiwan's people live on the low, flat land in the west: the plains and the basins. Few people live in the high mountains. The shape of the land helps decide where cities, roads, and railways go.
台灣的人大多住在西部又低又平的地方：平原和盆地；高山上住的人很少。地形幫忙決定了城市、道路和鐵路蓋在哪裡。

Unit 11: Where Do Taiwan's People Live?

At the end of October 2026, Taiwan had 23,204,000 registered residents, according to the Ministry of the Interior. That is about 641 people for every square kilometer, on average. But people are not spread evenly. Some places are packed, and some are almost empty.

根據內政部的資料，民國 115 年（2026 年）10 月底，台灣的戶籍人口是 23,204,000 人。平均每平方公里大約住了 641 人。可是人並沒有平均分布：有些地方擠滿了人，有些地方幾乎沒有人。

Look at the cities. The six special municipalities, New Taipei, Taipei, Taoyuan, Taichung, Tainan, and Kaohsiung, are home to almost 70 percent of the people. New Taipei has the most people, about 4 million. Taipei City is the most crowded: almost 8,900 people live on each square kilometer. In Taitung County, the number is about 59.

先看城市。新北、臺北、桃園、臺中、臺南、高雄六個直轄市，住了將近七成的人。新北市人最多，大約 400 萬人；臺北市最擁擠，每平方公里住了將近 8,900 人。在臺東縣，這個數字大約是 59。

On the 3D model, each column shows how many people live on a small square of land. The tall columns stand on the low land. In this model, land below 100 meters covers about 29 percent of the main island, but about 82 percent of the people live there. Land 1,000 meters and higher covers about 31 percent, yet only about 1 person in every 1,000 lives there.

在 3D 模型上，每一根柱子代表一小塊土地上住了多少人。高高的柱子都站在低地上。在這個模型裡，100 公尺以下的地大約占本島的 29%，卻住了大約 82% 的人；1,000 公尺以上的地大約占 31%，每 1,000 個人卻只有大約 1 個住在那裡。

Why? Lessons 9 and 10 give the clues. On low, flat land it is easier to build homes, roads, and factories, to grow food, and to get water from rivers. The Taipei and Taichung basins are flat, with rivers and hills around them. In the model, basins cover only about 5 percent of the land, but about 27 percent of the people live in them.

為什麼？第九課和第十課有線索。在又低又平的地方，比較容易蓋房子、開路、建工廠、種糧食，也比較容易從河裡取水。臺北盆地和臺中盆地都很平，有河流，四周有山丘。在模型裡，盆地只占大約 5% 的土地，卻住了大約 27% 的人。

The plains and basins in the west line up from north to south, and so do the big cities. The high-speed rail follows them: Taiwan High Speed Rail says its line is 350 kilometers long and passes through 11 counties and cities in the west. East of the mountains, Yilan, Hualien, and Taitung counties together cover about 28 percent of Taiwan's land, but only about 4 percent of the people live there.

西部的平原和盆地由北到南排成一長串，大城市也是。高鐵就沿著它們走：台灣高鐵說，它的路線全長 350 公里，經過西部 11 個縣市。山的東邊，宜蘭、花蓮、臺東三個縣加起來大約占台灣土地的 28%，卻只住了大約 4% 的人。

The numbers on the model are an estimate made for the whole world, so they do not match the official count exactly. For the main island, the model counts about 4 percent more people than the household registration. But the pattern is clear: Taiwan's people live where the land is low, flat, and close to water. Few people live in the mountains, but few is not none.

模型上的數字是為全世界做的估計，所以和官方的戶籍人數不會完全一樣：在本島，模型算出的人比戶籍多了大約 4%。但是分布的樣子很清楚：台灣的人住在又低、又平、又靠近水的地方。山上住的人很少，但少不等於沒有。

Questions · 閱讀理解

Why do most of Taiwan's people live on the low land in the west?

The west has wide plains and basins with rivers, where it is easier to build homes, roads, and farms. The high mountains are steep and closer to the east.

New Taipei has the most people, but Taipei City is the most crowded. How can both be true?

New Taipei is much bigger, so it can hold more people in total. Taipei City is small, so its people are packed more closely: more people on each square kilometer.

Some people do live in the high mountains. What might their lives be like, compared with life in a big city?

Answers will vary: fewer neighbors, longer trips to school, shops, and hospitals, mountain roads, and farming on slopes; also quiet, nature, and close communities.

    Stories Behind the Numbers · 數字背後的故事

    Counting Taiwan's people 數一數台灣的人


    2024 → 2026 · Ministry of the Interior · 內政部

    Seven in ten十個人裡有七個

    The Executive Yuan wrote that in 2024 most people lived in the special municipalities, 69.77 percent of the total. With the Ministry of the Interior's figures for the end of October 2026, this page works it out as 69.92 percent.
    行政院國情簡介說，民國 113 年我國人口大部分集中在直轄市，占全國總人口的 69.77%。用內政部 115 年 10 月底的數字，本頁算出來是 69.92%。

    2026 · Ministry of the Interior · 內政部

    8,900 and 598,900 和 59

    At the end of October 2026, Taipei City had about 8,900 people per square kilometer, the most crowded in Taiwan. Taitung County had about 59 and Hualien County about 67, the least crowded.
    民國 115 年 10 月底，臺北市每平方公里大約 8,900 人，是全國最擁擠的；臺東縣大約 59 人、花蓮縣大約 67 人，是最空曠的。

    Taiwan High Speed Rail · 台灣高鐵

    350 kilometers along the west沿著西部的 350 公里

    The high-speed rail runs from Nangang in the north to Zuoying in the south. Its line is 350 kilometers long and passes through 11 counties and cities in the west, the same line of plains and basins where most people live.
    高鐵從北端的南港到南端的左營，路線全長 350 公里，經過西部 11 個縣市，就是大多數人住的那一串平原和盆地。

    2025 · European Commission JRC · 歐盟聯合研究中心

    Two ways to count兩種數法

    The household registration counts the people registered in each place. The population grid on this page is a different kind of number: an estimate, for every square kilometer of the world, of where people live. The two do not match exactly, but they tell the same story.
    戶籍資料數的是登記在每個地方的人；本頁的人口格網是另一種數字：替全世界每一平方公里估計住了多少人。兩者不會完全一樣，但說的是同一個故事。

    Myth vs. Fact · 常見迷思

    Four things people get wrong 四個常見的誤會

    ✗ Myth · 迷思Taiwan's people are spread evenly across the island.台灣的人平均分布在全島。

    ✓ Fact · 事實In this page's model, about 88 of every 100 people live on the most crowded tenth of the land, and more than half of the 1-kilometer squares have fewer than 10 people.在本頁的模型裡，每 100 個人大約有 88 個住在最擁擠的十分之一土地上；超過一半的 1 公里格子，住不到 10 個人。

    ✗ Myth · 迷思Nobody lives in the mountains.山上沒有人住。

    ✓ Fact · 事實Few people do. The model puts about 28,000 people on land 1,000 meters and higher. Lesson 14 looks at where Taiwan's Indigenous peoples live.有，只是很少。模型估計 1,000 公尺以上的地方大約住了 28,000 人。第十四課會看台灣的原住民族住在哪裡。

    ✗ Myth · 迷思Wherever the land is flat, there is a big city.只要是平地，就會有大城市。

    ✓ Fact · 事實Flat land draws people, but much of it is used for other things, such as farms. The coast of Yunlin is low and flat, yet far fewer people live there than in the Taipei Basin.平地會吸引人，但很多平地有別的用途，例如農田。雲林沿海又低又平，住的人卻比臺北盆地少得多。

    ✗ Myth · 迷思Taipei City has the most people in Taiwan.臺北市是台灣人口最多的地方。

    ✓ Fact · 事實New Taipei has the most people (4,033,118 at the end of October 2026). Taipei City has fewer people, but it is the most crowded.人口最多的是新北市（民國 115 年 10 月底 4,033,118 人）。臺北市的人比較少，卻是最擁擠的。

    Classroom Activity 1 · 課堂活動一

    How Crowded Is Our Classroom? 我們的教室有多擠？

    You need · 準備材料

    • A tape measure or a meter stick一個捲尺或一公尺長的尺
    • A calculator一台計算機
    • Chalk or tape to mark a square on the playground粉筆或膠帶，在操場上畫一個正方形

    Steps · 步驟

    1. Measure the length and width of your classroom in meters, and multiply them to find its area.用公尺量教室的長和寬，相乘得到面積。
    2. Count the people in the room. Divide the people by the area: that is the population density of your classroom.數一數教室裡有幾個人。人數除以面積，就是教室的人口密度。
    3. Mark a square of the same area on the playground and spread the class out inside it, then do it again in a square twice as big.在操場上畫一個一樣大的正方形，全班站進去散開；再換一個兩倍大的正方形試試。
    4. Which felt more crowded? Compare with Taipei City (about 8,900 people per square kilometer) and Taitung County (about 59).哪一次覺得比較擠？和臺北市（每平方公里大約 8,900 人）、臺東縣（大約 59 人）比一比。

    Density is people divided by area. The same number of people can feel crowded or roomy, depending on the space.
    密度就是人數除以面積。一樣多的人，空間不同，就會覺得擠或寬敞。

    Classroom Activity 2 · 課堂活動二

    Our Towns on a Height Map 我們的鄉鎮在高度地圖上

    You need · 準備材料

    • A printed map of Taiwan colored by height (for example, a screenshot of the 3D model from above)一張依高度上色的台灣地圖（例如 3D 模型從正上方看的截圖）
    • Small stickers in two colors兩種顏色的小貼紙
    • A class chart一張全班的統計表

    Steps · 步驟

    1. Each student puts one sticker on the town or district where they live, and another on a place they have visited.每個同學在自己住的鄉鎮或區貼一張貼紙，再在去過的地方貼另一種顏色。
    2. Count how many stickers are on low, green land, and how many are in the mountains.數一數有幾張貼紙在低低的綠色地方，有幾張在山上。
    3. Compare your class with the model: about 82 of every 100 people live on land below 100 meters.把全班的結果和模型比一比：每 100 個人大約有 82 個住在 100 公尺以下的地方。
    4. Talk about it: if your class's result is different, why might that be?討論：如果全班的結果不一樣，可能是為什麼？

    A class is a small sample. A small group of people can look very different from the whole island.
    一個班只是很小的樣本。一小群人的結果，可能和全島很不一樣。
