Earth and Weather · Unit 2 · Lesson 9 · 地球與天氣 單元二 第九課

What Are Lightning and Thunder?閃電和打雷是怎麼回事？

A flash, a pause, and then a boom that shakes the windows. What is that light, what makes the noise, and why does the noise always come late? In a 3D model, watch a thundercloud fill with electric charge and let it go, then follow the sound as it travels to your house in real time. Last, learn to tell how far away a storm is by counting.
一道閃光，停了一下，然後是震得窗戶都在抖的一聲巨響。那道光是什麼？聲音是什麼東西發出來的？為什麼聲音總是比較晚到？在 3D 模型裡，看一朵雷雨雲怎麼充滿電、又怎麼把電放掉，再跟著聲音，看它用真實的速度傳到你家。最後，學會用數數的方法，知道雷雨離你多遠。

Big idea · 一句話看懂

Lightning is a giant electric spark. Inside a thundercloud, fast winds pull electric charge apart, with one kind at the top of the cloud and the other kind at the bottom. When the pull between them becomes too strong for the air to hold back, the charge jumps. The spark heats the air so suddenly that the air bursts outward, and that is thunder. Light reaches you at once, but sound is much slower, so the thunder comes later.
閃電是一個巨大的電火花。在雷雨雲裡，強勁的氣流把電分開：一種電在雲頂，另一種電在雲底。當它們之間的拉力大到空氣再也擋不住的時候，電就跳過去了。這個火花把空氣一下子加熱，空氣猛然向外爆開，這就是雷。光立刻就到你眼前，聲音卻慢得多，所以雷聲比較晚到。

Charge in the cloud · 雲裡累積的電

Light · 光
Thunder · 雷聲
Timer · 計時

Strong winds are rising and falling inside the cloud. Positive charge collects at the top, and negative charge collects at the bottom. The negative charge pulls positive charge toward the ground just below it. Air does not let electricity pass easily, so the charge keeps building.雲裡面的氣流猛烈地上上下下。正電聚在雲頂，負電聚在雲底；雲底的負電又把地面上的正電吸引到它的正下方。空氣不容易讓電通過，所以電越積越多。

The pull is now too strong for the air to hold back. A faint path reaches out first. The moment the path is complete, a huge current rushes along it, and it lights up. That is the flash you see.拉力大到空氣擋不住了。先是一條很暗的通路伸出去；通路接通的那一瞬間，一股強大的電流沿著它衝過去，整條亮了起來。這就是你看到的閃電。

The hot air around the lightning burst outward, and the sound is on its way. You have already seen the flash, because light arrived at once. Sound travels only about 340 meters a second, so keep counting.閃電周圍的熱空氣向外爆開，聲音已經在路上了。閃光你早就看到了，因為光立刻就到。聲音每秒只走大約 340 公尺，所以繼續數。

Now the thunder reaches you. The number of seconds you counted tells you how far away the lightning was: about 1 kilometer for every 3 seconds.雷聲現在才傳到你這裡。你數到的秒數，可以告訴你閃電離你多遠：大約每 3 秒是 1 公里。

Unit 9: What Are Lightning and Thunder?

On a hot summer afternoon, the sky can change very quickly. A white cloud grows tall and turns dark at the bottom. Then there is a flash, and a few seconds later, a boom. For thousands of years people wondered what was happening up there. Today we know that it is electricity.

炎熱的夏天午後，天空可以變得很快。一朵白雲越長越高，底部漸漸變黑。接著是一道閃光，幾秒鐘之後，轟的一聲。幾千年來，人們一直在想：天上到底發生了什麼事？今天我們知道，那是電。

A thundercloud is a very busy place. Warm, moist air shoots upward, and cold air and rain fall back down. Inside the cloud, drops of water and bits of ice are thrown around and rub against each other. This pulls electric charge apart. Scientists still do not understand every detail, but the result is clear: positive charge collects near the top of the cloud, and negative charge collects near the bottom.

雷雨雲裡面非常熱鬧。暖溼的空氣往上衝，冷空氣和雨水往下掉。雲裡的水滴和冰粒被拋來拋去、互相摩擦，把電分開了。科學家到現在還沒有弄清楚每一個細節，但結果很清楚：正電聚在雲頂附近，負電聚在雲底附近。

Opposite charges pull on each other. The negative charge at the bottom of the cloud also pulls positive charge toward the ground just below. But air stands between them, and air does not let electricity pass easily. So the charge builds and builds. In some large storms, the push behind it reaches several million volts.

不同的電會互相吸引。雲底的負電，也把地面上的正電吸引到它的正下方。可是它們中間隔著空氣，而空氣不容易讓電通過。所以電就越積越多。在一些大雷雨裡，推動它的電壓可以高達幾百萬伏特。

At last the air cannot hold it back, and the charge jumps. That giant spark is lightning. It can jump inside one cloud, from one cloud to another, or between the cloud and the ground. All over the world, lightning strikes the ground about 100 times every second.

最後，空氣擋不住了，電就跳了過去。這個巨大的電火花就是閃電。它可以在一朵雲裡面跳，可以從一朵雲跳到另一朵雲，也可以在雲和地面之間跳。全世界加起來，閃電平均每秒鐘擊中地面大約 100 次。

Lightning is extremely hot. It heats the air around it to about 30,000 degrees Celsius in an instant. The air swells so fast that it bursts outward, like a small explosion. The sound of that burst is thunder. So thunder is not clouds crashing together. It is the sound of air being heated by lightning.

閃電非常燙。它在一瞬間把周圍的空氣加熱到大約攝氏三萬度。空氣膨脹得太快，猛然向外爆開，就像一場小小的爆炸。這個爆開的聲音就是雷。所以雷聲不是雲撞在一起的聲音，而是空氣被閃電加熱所發出的聲音。

Lightning and thunder happen at the same moment, but they do not reach you together. Light travels about 300,000 kilometers a second, so you see the flash at once. Sound travels only about 340 meters a second. If you count the seconds between the flash and the boom, you can tell how far away the lightning was. Every 3 seconds is about 1 kilometer.

閃電和雷聲是同時發生的，卻不會一起到你這裡。光每秒大約走 30 萬公里，所以閃光你立刻就看到了。聲音每秒只走大約 340 公尺。數一數從閃光到雷聲隔了幾秒，就可以知道閃電離你多遠：每 3 秒大約是 1 公里。

Questions · 閱讀理解

What happens to the electric charge inside a thundercloud?

It is pulled apart. Positive charge collects near the top of the cloud, and negative charge collects near the bottom, until the air cannot hold it back and it jumps.

What makes the sound of thunder?

Lightning heats the air around it so suddenly that the air bursts outward. The sound of that burst is thunder.

Why do you see the lightning before you hear the thunder?

Light travels about 300,000 kilometers a second, so it arrives at once. Sound travels only about 340 meters a second, so it arrives later.

The distance is the number of seconds times 340 meters, the speed of sound given by the Central Weather Administration. Counting tells you how far away the last flash was. It does not tell you where the next one will strike, so it is not a way to decide that you are safe.距離＝秒數 × 340 公尺（中央氣象署採用的聲音速度）。數秒只能告訴你「剛才那一道」有多遠，不能告訴你下一道會打在哪裡，所以不能用它來判斷自己安不安全。

Lightning by the Numbers · 閃電的數字

Six numbers about lightning 六個和閃電有關的數字

Very fast, very hot, and very common.
很快、很燙，而且很常見。

340 ma second · 每秒

The speed of sound in air. Thunder needs about 3 seconds to travel 1 kilometer.聲音在空氣裡的速度。雷聲走 1 公里大約要 3 秒。

300,000 kma second · 每秒

The speed of light. The flash of lightning reaches your eyes at once.光的速度。閃電的光立刻就到你的眼睛。

100a second · 每秒

Around the world, lightning strikes the ground about this many times every second.全世界加起來，閃電平均每秒鐘擊中地面大約這麼多次。

30,000°Chot · 高溫

Lightning heats the air around it to about this temperature in an instant.閃電在一瞬間把周圍的空氣加熱到大約這個溫度。

Millionsof volts · 伏特

In some large storms, the voltage between the cloud and the ground reaches several million volts.在一些大雷雨裡，雲和地面之間的電壓可以高達幾百萬伏特。

1–2hours · 小時

A single thundercloud does not last long. It usually grows, storms, and fades within one or two hours.單獨一朵雷雨雲的壽命很短，從發展、成熟到消散，通常不過一兩個小時。

Lightning Stories · 閃電的故事

The kite, the rod, the summer afternoon, and the giant trees 風箏、避雷針、夏天的午後，還有神木

How people came to understand lightning, and to live with it.
人們怎麼弄懂閃電，又怎麼和它相處。

1752

Is lightning electricity?閃電是電嗎？

Benjamin Franklin thought that lightning was the same thing as the small sparks people made in their experiments, only far bigger. He proposed a way to test it. In May 1752, a scientist in France tried Franklin's idea with a tall iron rod and drew sparks from a storm cloud. Lightning was electricity. Franklin is famous for flying a kite in a storm that same year. Nobody should ever copy that. It was extremely dangerous.
富蘭克林認為，閃電和人們在實驗裡做出來的小火花是同一種東西，只是大得多。他提出了一個檢驗的方法。1752 年 5 月，法國的一位科學家照他的想法，用一根很高的鐵棒從雷雨雲引出了電火花：閃電確實是電。富蘭克林最出名的故事，是同一年在雷雨中放風箏。千萬不要模仿，那是極度危險的事。

Buildings · 建築

How a lightning rod works避雷針是怎麼保護房子的

A lightning rod is a metal rod with its point facing up, fixed to the top of a tall building. A copper wire joins it to a copper plate buried in the ground. When a thundercloud comes near, charge gathers at the point of the rod and has an easy path to the earth. The electricity takes that path, not the path through the building.
避雷針是一根尖端朝上的金屬棒，裝在高大建築物的頂端，下面用銅線連到埋在地下的銅板。雷雨雲靠近的時候，電荷會集中在避雷針的尖端，並且有一條容易通到地下的路。電走的是這條路，而不是穿過建築物。

Taiwan · 台灣

Summer afternoons夏天的午後

In Taiwan, thunderstorms become more common from March and are most common in July and August. In summer they often come at about two or three in the afternoon, after the sun has heated the ground all morning. The rain cools the ground, the warm air stops rising, and the sky often clears again soon after.
在台灣，雷雨從 3 月開始變多，7、8 月最多。夏天的雷雨常常發生在下午兩三點，也就是太陽把地面曬了一整個上午之後。下過雨，地面涼了，熱空氣不再上升，所以短暫的雷雨過後，天氣常常很快又放晴。

Forests · 森林

Protecting the giant trees保護神木

Tall things that stand alone are the easiest targets, and in the mountains that often means very old, very tall trees. On April 7, 1973, lightning struck the cypress forest beside Xianglin Elementary School on Alishan and started a large fire. The Central Weather Administration suggests that the famous giant trees in forest parks should have lightning rods of their own.
孤立而高聳的東西最容易被雷打中；在山裡，那往往就是又老又高的大樹。民國 62 年（1973 年）4 月 7 日，雷擊中了阿里山香林國小旁邊的檜木林，引起一場大火。中央氣象署建議，森林風景區裡的神木，最好也幫它們裝上避雷針。

Myth vs. Fact · 常見迷思

Four things people get wrong 四個常見的誤會

✗ Myth · 迷思Thunder is the sound of clouds crashing together.雷聲是雲撞在一起的聲音。

✓ Fact · 事實Clouds are made of tiny drops and cannot crash. Thunder is air that lightning has heated, bursting outward.雲是很小的水滴，撞不出聲音。雷聲是被閃電加熱的空氣向外爆開的聲音。

✗ Myth · 迷思Thunder happens a few seconds after lightning.先閃電，過幾秒才打雷。

✓ Fact · 事實They happen at the same moment. The sound just takes longer to reach you.它們是同時發生的，只是聲音比較晚傳到你這裡。

✗ Myth · 迷思A big tree is a good place to wait out a thunderstorm.打雷下雨的時候，躲在大樹下最好。

✓ Fact · 事實It is one of the most dangerous places. Tall things that stand alone are the most likely to be struck.那是最危險的地方之一。孤立而突出的東西最容易被雷打中。

✗ Myth · 迷思Lightning always goes from a cloud down to the ground.閃電都是從雲打到地面。

✓ Fact · 事實It also jumps inside a cloud and from one cloud to another.它也會在一朵雲裡面跳，或是從一朵雲跳到另一朵雲。

Be Ready · 防災要點

In a thunderstorm 打雷的時候

  • Check the forecast before you go running, hiking, or to the beach. The chance of being struck is high in the open during heavy rain.要去跑步、登山或到海邊之前，先看天氣預報。大雨中在空曠的戶外，被雷擊的機率相當高。
  • Stay away from tall things that stand alone, such as towers, poles, chimneys, and big trees. Never shelter under a tree.不要靠近孤立而高聳的東西，例如鐵塔、電線桿、煙囪和大樹。千萬不要在樹下躲雨。
  • Do not stand on open high ground.不要站在空曠的高地上。
  • Do not swim or go out in a boat. A person or a boat on the water stands out and is easy to strike.不要游泳，也不要划船。水面上的人和船是很突出的目標，容易被擊中。
  • Keep away from things that carry electricity well: wires, water pipes, and metal objects. Wet clothes and wet walls carry it too.遠離容易導電的東西：電線、自來水管和金屬器具。溼衣服和潮溼的牆壁也會導電。

These points follow the Central Weather Administration and the National Fire Agency: Central Weather Administration, Thunderstorms ↗以下要點依中央氣象署 氣象常識「雷雨」與內政部消防署消防防災館。

Classroom Activity 1 · 課堂活動一

Pull Charge Apart 把電分開

You need · 準備材料

  • A balloon and a dry wool sweater or your own clean, dry hair一個氣球，還有一件乾的毛衣，或是你自己乾淨、乾燥的頭髮
  • A sheet of paper torn into very small pieces一張撕成很小很小碎片的紙

Steps · 步驟

  1. Blow up the balloon and tie it. Hold it over the paper pieces. Does anything happen?把氣球吹起來、綁好。拿到碎紙片的上方：有什麼事發生嗎？
  2. Rub the balloon on the sweater or on your hair about twenty times.把氣球在毛衣或頭髮上摩擦大約二十下。
  3. Hold the balloon just above the paper pieces again. What do they do now?再把氣球拿到碎紙片的正上方。這一次紙片怎麼了？
  4. Try to stick the balloon to a wall. How long does it stay?試著把氣球貼在牆上。它可以黏多久？

Rubbing pulled charge apart, just as the wild winds do inside a thundercloud. This works best on a dry day. On a humid day the charge leaks away quickly.
摩擦把電分開了，就像雷雨雲裡猛烈的氣流做的事一樣。天氣乾燥的時候效果最好；潮溼的日子，電很快就漏掉了。

Classroom Activity 2 · 課堂活動二

Is the Storm Coming or Going? 雷雨是在靠近，還是在離開？

You need · 準備材料

  • A thunderstorm, and a safe place indoors一場雷雨，還有一個安全的室內
  • Paper and a pen紙和筆

Steps · 步驟

  1. Stay indoors. When you see a flash, count the seconds until you hear the thunder.留在室內。看到閃光，就開始數秒，數到聽見雷聲為止。
  2. Write the number down. Divide it by 3 to get the distance in kilometers.把數字記下來。除以 3，就是大約幾公里。
  3. Do the same for five flashes.用同樣的方法記錄五次閃電。
  4. Look at your five numbers. Are they getting smaller or larger? Which way is the storm moving?看看這五個數字：是越來越小，還是越來越大？雷雨正往哪個方向移動？

Do this only from inside a building. Counting is for learning about the storm, not for deciding when to go out.
這個活動只能在建築物裡面做。數秒是用來了解雷雨的，不是用來決定什麼時候可以出門。
