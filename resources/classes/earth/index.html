Earth and Weather 地球與天氣

Taiwan sits where two great plates meet and where typhoons pass in summer. Why does the ground shake? Where does the rain come from? Look inside the Earth, up into the sky, and down a river to the sea.
台灣坐落在兩大板塊相接的地方，夏天又有颱風經過。地為什麼會搖？雨是從哪裡來的？往地球裡面看、往天空看，再順著河流一路看到海。

Every lesson starts with something you have lived through: an earthquake alert on your phone, a typhoon day, a sudden afternoon storm. A 3D model shows what is going on, one idea at a time, and an English reading explains it, with Chinese a tap away.
每一課都從你親身經歷過的事開始：手機上的地震警報、颱風天、突然下起來的午後雷陣雨。先用 3D 模型看清楚發生了什麼事，一課只講一個道理；再讀一篇英文短文把它說明白，中文翻譯隨點隨看。

The lessons are grouped into four units: the ground beneath us, the sky above, water and the sea, and a changing Earth. When a lesson touches something already explained in Astronomy or How Things Work, such as the seasons, the tides, or rainbows, it links there instead of explaining it twice.
課程分成四個單元：腳下的地、頭上的天、水與海、變動中的地球。遇到「天文教育」或「萬物原理」已經講過的東西，例如四季、潮汐、彩虹，就直接連過去，不重講一次。

Every number is checked against a source listed at the bottom of each lesson, starting with Taiwan's own Central Weather Administration. What to do in an earthquake, a typhoon, or a thunderstorm follows official guidance only.
每一個數字都查證過，出處列在每課最下方，並且優先採用中央氣象署的資料。地震、颱風、打雷時該怎麼做，一律只照官方的說明。

1 of 16 lessons is ready so far; more are on the way.
目前完成 1 課（共規劃 16 課），持續增加中。

Why Does Taiwan Have So Many Earthquakes?
