How Is a Tsunami Different from an Ordinary Wave?海嘯和一般的海浪有什麼不同？

The waves at the beach come and go all day and do no harm. A tsunami is something else. Out at sea it is so low that a ship may not notice it, yet it crosses the ocean as fast as a jet. In a 3D cutaway of the sea, compare the two kinds of waves, and follow a tsunami from deep water to the shore as it slows down and rises. Then learn the signs that tell you to move to high ground at once.
海邊的浪整天來來去去，不會傷人。海嘯卻是完全不同的東西。它在外海矮到船上的人可能根本沒發現，卻用噴射機的速度橫越大洋。在海的 3D 剖面裡，比一比這兩種浪，再跟著一道海嘯從深海走到岸邊，看它怎麼變慢、變高。最後，學會哪些徵兆是在告訴你：馬上往高處走。

Big idea · 一句話看懂

An ordinary wave is made by the wind, and it moves only the water near the surface. A tsunami usually begins when the seabed suddenly jumps up or drops down in a great earthquake. That pushes the whole depth of water, from the bottom to the top. In deep water a tsunami is very fast and very low. Near the shore the water is shallow, so the front slows down, the water behind catches up, and the sea piles up high.
一般的浪是風吹出來的，只動到靠近海面的水。海嘯通常是大地震的時候，海底突然抬升或陷落所造成的；這一下推動的是從海底到海面的整層海水。在深海，海嘯跑得非常快，卻非常矮。到了岸邊水變淺，前面慢了下來，後面的水追上來，海水就堆高了。

These are ordinary waves, made by the wind. Only the water near the surface moves, in small circles. A little way down, the water hardly moves at all. The waves break gently on the beach, one after another.這是風吹出來的一般的浪。只有靠近海面的水在動，繞著小小的圈。往下一點點，水就幾乎不動了。這些浪一道接一道，輕輕地打在沙灘上。

The seabed has moved, and the whole depth of water has been pushed. In the deep ocean, this wave travels as fast as a jet plane, but it is very low and very long. A ship above it would hardly feel it.海底動了，整層海水都被推動。在深海裡，這道波跑得和噴射機一樣快，卻很矮、很長。在它上面的船幾乎感覺不到。

The water is getting shallow, so the front of the wave is slowing down. The water behind is still coming fast. It catches up and piles on top, and the wave grows taller and taller.水越來越淺，所以波的前端慢了下來。後面的水還是來得很快，追上來、疊上去，這道波就越來越高。

The sea does not break and fall back like an ordinary wave. It keeps coming, like a river running onto the land, because all the water behind is still pushing. More waves may follow, so the danger is not over.海水不像一般的浪那樣打上來就退回去。它一直湧進來，像一條河流上了陸地，因為後面整片海水都還在推。接下來可能還有好幾波，所以危險還沒有結束。

Unit 12: How Is a Tsunami Different from an Ordinary Wave?

Stand on a beach and watch the waves. Each one rises, curls over, breaks, and slides back. A minute later you have seen ten of them. These waves are made by the wind blowing across the sea. They look strong, but they move only the water near the top. A diver a little way down hardly feels them.

站在沙灘上看浪。每一道浪升起來、捲過去、碎掉，再滑回去；一分鐘裡你就看了十道。這些浪是風吹過海面造成的。它們看起來很有力，其實只動到最上面的水；潛到底下一點點的人，幾乎感覺不到。

A tsunami is not made by the wind. Most tsunamis begin with a great earthquake under the sea. If the seabed suddenly jumps up or drops down, it lifts or lowers all the water above it, from the bottom of the sea to the top. That huge push spreads outward in every direction. A landslide under the sea or a volcano erupting in the sea can do the same thing.

海嘯不是風吹出來的。大部分的海嘯，是從海底的大地震開始的。海底如果突然抬升或陷落，就會把它上面的水全部抬高或放低，從海底一直到海面。這一下巨大的推力，朝四面八方傳出去。海底的山崩、海裡的火山噴發，也可能造成同樣的事。

Out in the deep ocean, a tsunami is hard to notice. It may be less than a meter high, and the distance from one crest to the next can be hundreds of kilometers, so a ship simply rises and falls a little. But it is moving very fast. Its speed depends on the depth of the water. In the open ocean it travels 500 to 1,000 kilometers an hour, about as fast as a jet plane.

在外海的深水裡，海嘯很難察覺。它可能不到一公尺高，而兩個波峰之間的距離可以有好幾百公里，所以船只是稍微升高、又降下來一點點。可是它跑得非常快。它的速度看的是水深：在廣闊的大洋裡，每小時可以跑 500 到 1,000 公里，和噴射客機差不多快。

Everything changes near the land. The water becomes shallow, so the front of the tsunami slows down. The water behind it is still in deeper water and still moving fast. It catches up and piles on top. The tsunami grows from something a ship cannot feel into a wall of water.

一靠近陸地，情況就完全不同了。水變淺，海嘯的前端慢了下來；後面的水還在比較深的地方，跑得還是很快，就追上來、疊上去。海嘯就從船上感覺不到的小起伏，長成一道水牆。

When it reaches the shore, a tsunami does not behave like a beach wave. It does not break and slide back in a few seconds. The whole sea keeps pouring onto the land for minutes, and it carries boats, cars, and pieces of buildings with it. Then it drains back out, just as strongly. There is usually more than one wave.

到了岸邊，海嘯的樣子也不像沙灘上的浪。它不會打上來、幾秒鐘就退回去；而是整片海水持續好幾分鐘往陸地上灌，把船、車子和房屋的碎片一起帶著走。然後它再用同樣大的力量退回去。而且通常不只一波。

Sometimes the sea gives a warning. Before a tsunami arrives, the water may suddenly pull far back from the shore and leave the seabed bare. This does not always happen. So the rule is simple. If you are near the sea and you feel a strong earthquake, or you see the water draw back, or you hear a tsunami warning, do not wait and do not watch. Go to high ground at once.

有時候，大海會先給一個警告：海嘯到達之前，海水可能突然退得很遠，露出海底。但不是每一次都會這樣。所以規則很簡單：在海邊感覺到強烈的地震、看到海水突然退去，或是聽到海嘯警報，不要等，也不要看，馬上往高處走。

Questions · 閱讀理解

What makes ordinary waves, and what makes most tsunamis?

Ordinary waves are made by the wind. Most tsunamis are made when the seabed suddenly moves up or down in a great earthquake.

Why is a tsunami hard to notice in the deep ocean?

In deep water it may be less than a meter high, and its crests are hundreds of kilometers apart, so a ship only rises and falls a little.

Why does a tsunami grow taller near the shore?

The water is shallow there, so the front slows down. The water behind is still moving fast, so it catches up and piles on top.

Work It Out · 算算看

How fast is a tsunami? 海嘯跑多快？

The speed of a tsunami depends on only one thing: how deep the water is. Choose a depth and see. Then make the water shallower, as it is near a coast.
海嘯的速度只看一件事：水有多深。選一個水深看看。然後把水調淺，就像靠近海岸的時候那樣。

The speed is the square root of the depth times the pull of gravity (9.8), the rule given by the Central Weather Administration. A slower tsunami is not a safer one. As it slows down, it grows taller, and even near the shore it is far faster than a person can run.速度＝（重力加速度 9.8 × 水深）開根號，這是中央氣象署採用的公式。海嘯變慢並不代表比較安全：它一邊變慢、一邊變高，而且就算到了岸邊，還是比人跑的速度快得多。

A tsunami travels at about · 海嘯的速度大約是

713 km/h · 公里／時

That is 198 meters every second. · 也就是每秒這麼多公尺。

At this speed it crosses 100 kilometers in about 8 minutes.用這個速度走 100 公里，大約只要這麼多分鐘。

Tsunamis by the Numbers · 海嘯的數字

Six numbers about tsunamis 六個和海嘯有關的數字

All six come from the Central Weather Administration.
這六個數字都來自中央氣象署。

500–1,000km/h · 公里／時

The speed of a tsunami in the deep, open ocean. That is about as fast as a jet plane.海嘯在深而廣闊的大洋裡的速度，和噴射客機差不多。

30–60 cmhigh · 高

In the deep ocean, a tsunami may be no higher than this. Ships do not notice it.在深海裡，海嘯的浪高可能不超過這麼多，海面上的船不會注意到。

500–650 kmlong · 長

The distance from one crest of a tsunami to the next can be this great.海嘯相鄰兩個波峰之間的距離，可以這麼遠。

7.0magnitude · 規模

A tsunami warning is issued when an earthquake of magnitude 7.0 or more, less than 35 kilometers deep, happens in the seas near Taiwan.臺灣近海發生規模 7.0 以上、震源深度淺於 35 公里的地震，中央氣象署就會發布海嘯警報。

30 cmis enough · 就夠了

A tsunami only 30 centimeters deep can carry away a car. From 30 centimeters up, a person may be swept away.只有 30 公分高的海嘯就可以沖走汽車。從 30 公分起，人就有可能被沖走。

6zones · 個分區

The coasts of Taiwan, Penghu, Kinmen, and Matsu are divided into six zones for tsunami warnings.臺澎金馬的沿海地區劃分成 6 個海嘯警戒分區。

Tsunami Stories · 海嘯的故事

Four things worth knowing if you live on an island.
住在島上的人值得知道的四件事。

Word · 字源

A wave in the harbor港口裡的浪

The word tsunami is Japanese, and it means harbor wave. People once called tsunamis tidal waves, but that name is misleading. Tides are caused by the pull of the moon and the sun. A tsunami has nothing to do with them.
tsunami 這個詞來自日文的「津波」，意思是「港口的浪」。以前有人把海嘯叫做「潮汐波」（tidal wave），但這個名字容易讓人誤會：潮汐是月亮和太陽的引力造成的，海嘯和它們一點關係也沒有。

1867

Keelung基隆

On December 18, 1867, a strong earthquake shook northern Taiwan. Records say that the water in Keelung harbor flowed out to sea until the bottom of the harbor lay bare. A moment later, great waves rolled in and carried boats into the town. Of the possible tsunamis in Taiwan's old records, this is the one that scientists are most sure was caused by an earthquake near the island.
1867 年 12 月 18 日，臺灣北部發生強烈地震。記載說，基隆港的海水向外海流出，港內的海底都露了出來；一瞬間，巨浪又捲進來，把船隻沖進市內。在臺灣歷史上幾次疑似海嘯的紀錄裡，這一次最能確定是臺灣近海的地震引起的。

1781

Fish in the fields田裡都是魚

An old record from southern Taiwan, near today's Jiadong in Pingtung, describes a clear day in 1781. Suddenly the sea roared like thunder and rose over the village. People climbed up the bamboo to escape. Soon the water drained away, and the fields and ditches were full of fish and shrimp. Nobody had felt an earthquake, so the tsunami may have come from far away.
一份清代的紀錄寫到臺灣南部（今天屏東佳冬附近）1781 年的一個大晴天：海水忽然像打雷一樣怒吼，漲起來淹過了村子，居民爬到竹子上逃命。沒多久水就退了，田園和水溝裡到處都是魚蝦。當時沒有人感覺到地震，所以這次海嘯可能是很遠的地方的地震引起的。

Science · 科學

Not every earthquake makes one不是每一次地震都有海嘯

An earthquake under the sea does not always cause a tsunami. The seabed has to move up or down, so that it lifts or drops the water above it. If the two sides only slide past each other sideways, a large tsunami is very unlikely. The shape of the coast matters too. In a bay shaped like a funnel, the water is squeezed together and rises much higher.
海底的地震不一定會引起海嘯。海底必須有上下的錯動，才會把上面的水抬高或放低；如果兩邊只是水平地互相錯開，就很難引發大海嘯。海岸的形狀也有關係：在像漏斗一樣的海灣裡，海水被擠在一起，會升得高出許多。

Myth vs. Fact · 常見迷思

Four things people get wrong 四個常見的誤會

✗ Myth · 迷思A tsunami is just a very big beach wave.海嘯只是特別大的海浪。

✓ Fact · 事實A beach wave moves only the top of the sea and is gone in seconds. A tsunami moves the whole depth of water and keeps pouring in for minutes.沙灘上的浪只動到海的表層，幾秒鐘就過去了。海嘯推動的是整層海水，會持續好幾分鐘往岸上灌。

✗ Myth · 迷思You can see a tsunami coming from far out at sea.海嘯從很遠的外海就看得到。

✓ Fact · 事實In deep water it is less than a meter high. It becomes tall only when it reaches shallow water near the shore.在深海裡它不到一公尺高，要到岸邊的淺水才會變高。

✗ Myth · 迷思The sea always pulls back before a tsunami.海嘯來之前，海水一定會先退。

✓ Fact · 事實Sometimes it does, and then you must leave at once. But not every tsunami gives this warning, so do not wait for it.有時候會，這時候一定要馬上離開。但不是每一次海嘯都會先退水，所以不要等這個徵兆。

✗ Myth · 迷思After the first wave, the danger is over.第一波過了，就沒事了。

✓ Fact · 事實A tsunami usually comes as several waves. Stay on high ground until the warning is lifted.海嘯通常有好幾波。要留在高處，直到警報解除。

Be Ready · 防災要點

If a tsunami may be coming 海嘯可能要來的時候

  • If you are at the seaside and feel a strong earthquake or hear a tsunami warning, leave the water and go to high ground at once. Do not wait for anyone to confirm it.在海邊感覺到強烈搖晃的地震，或聽到海嘯警報，要立刻離開水域、往高處避難，不要等待確認。
  • If the sea suddenly pulls back and the seabed shows, never stay to look. You may have only seconds.看到海水突然退去、海底露出來，千萬不可以駐足觀看，可能只剩下幾秒到幾十秒的時間。
  • Go up, or go inland. Look for a hill. If the land is flat, get as far from the shore as you can.往高處，或往內陸。找附近的高地或山坡；如果附近很平坦，就盡量遠離海岸線。
  • If you cannot reach high ground in time, go to the third floor or higher of a strong building. If you are already there, stay, and keep away from the windows that face the sea.來不及到遠處的高地，就找附近 3 樓以上的堅固建築。如果已經在 3 樓以上，就留在原地，並遠離面向海邊的門窗。
  • Do not try to escape by car. Roads jam, and a tsunami only 30 centimeters deep can carry a car away. Go on foot.不要開車逃離。路上會塞車，而且 30 公分高的海嘯就能沖走汽車。用跑的往高處移動。
  • Do not stop on a bridge or at a river mouth to watch.不要因為好奇，停留在河口或橋上觀看。

These points follow the Central Weather Administration: Central Weather Administration, How to Avoid Tsunami Disasters ↗以下要點依中央氣象署 地震百問「應如何避免海嘯災難？」。

Classroom Activity 1 · 課堂活動一

A Wave That Grows 會長高的波

You need · 準備材料

  • A long, clear plastic box or a baking pan, and water一個長形的透明塑膠盒或烤盤，還有水
  • A thin board or a plastic lid to make a slope at one end, and a flat piece of plastic to push the water一片薄板或塑膠蓋，用來在一端做出斜坡；還有一片平的塑膠片，用來推水

Steps · 步驟

  1. Put the board in one end of the box so that it makes a gentle slope, like a beach. Add water until half of the slope is covered.把薄板放在盒子的一端，斜斜地像沙灘一樣。加水，讓斜坡有一半泡在水裡。
  2. At the deep end, blow gently across the surface. Watch the small ripples reach the slope.在水深的那一端，輕輕地朝水面吹氣。看那些小波紋怎麼到達斜坡。
  3. Now put the flat piece of plastic straight down into the deep end and give the water one slow, firm push toward the slope.接著把塑膠片直直地放進水深的那一端，慢慢地、穩穩地朝斜坡的方向推一下。
  4. Watch from the side. How far up the slope does each kind of wave go? Which one moved more of the water?從側面看。兩種波各衝上斜坡多遠？哪一種推動的水比較多？

Blowing moves only the top of the water. Pushing moves all of it, from the bottom up, and that is why it runs so much farther up your beach.
吹氣只動到水的表面；用板子推，動的是從底到頂的整層水，所以它衝上「沙灘」的距離遠得多。

Classroom Activity 2 · 課堂活動二

Where Would We Go? 我們要往哪裡跑？

You need · 準備材料

  • A map of a seaside place that your family visits一張你們家會去的海邊的地圖
  • A pen, and an adult to plan with一支筆，還有一位一起規劃的大人

Steps · 步驟

  1. Mark the beach or harbor on the map.在地圖上把那個海灘或港口標出來。
  2. Look for the nearest high ground, or the nearest strong building with at least three floors. Mark it.找出最近的高地，或是最近的、至少有三層樓的堅固建築，標出來。
  3. Draw the way you would walk there. Do not plan to drive.畫出你們走過去的路線。不要計畫開車。
  4. The next time you visit, look for the tsunami evacuation signs, and walk part of your route.下一次去的時候，找找看海嘯避難的指示牌，並實際走一段你們的路線。

A plan made on a calm day takes only a few minutes. On the day you need it, there will be no time to make one.
在平靜的日子裡做計畫，只要幾分鐘。真正需要它的那一天，是沒有時間現想的。
