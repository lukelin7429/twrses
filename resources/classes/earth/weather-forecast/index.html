Earth and Weather · Unit 2 · Lesson 10 · 地球與天氣 單元二 第十課

How Do We Forecast the Weather?天氣預報是怎麼做出來的？

The forecast says there is a 70% chance of rain tomorrow. Where does that number come from, and what does it really mean? In a 3D model, send a band of rain toward a small town and run the forecast once, then ten times. Watch the ten answers drift apart as you look further into the future. Then learn the exact words that forecasters in Taiwan use for the sky.
氣象預報說明天的降雨機率是 70%。這個數字是怎麼來的？它到底是什麼意思？在 3D 模型裡，讓一條雨帶移向一個小鎮，先把預報算一次，再算十次。看得越遠，十次的答案就分得越開。最後，學會台灣的預報員描述天空時用的那幾個詞。

Big idea · 一句話看懂

To forecast the weather, people first measure what the air is doing right now, all over the world. Then computers use the rules of nature to work out what the air will do next, step by step. But nobody can measure the air perfectly, and small mistakes grow with time. So a forecast for tomorrow can be quite sure, a forecast for next week is less sure, and an honest forecast tells you how sure it is.
要預報天氣，人們先量出全世界的空氣「現在」是什麼狀況，再讓電腦依照自然的規則，一步一步算出空氣接下來會怎麼變。可是沒有人能把空氣量得完全準確，而小小的誤差會隨著時間越變越大。所以明天的預報可以相當有把握，下星期的預報就沒那麼有把握；一份誠實的預報，會告訴你它有多少把握。

Unit 10: How Do We Forecast the Weather?

Every evening, the weather report tells you about a day that has not happened yet. It says how hot tomorrow will be, and whether you will need an umbrella. How can anyone know the future? Nobody can know it for certain. But the air follows rules, and that makes a good guess possible.

每天晚上，氣象報告都在告訴你一個還沒有發生的日子：明天會多熱，要不要帶傘。怎麼可能有人知道未來的事？沒有人能百分之百知道。但是空氣會照著規則走，所以我們可以做出很好的推測。

A forecast begins with measuring. All day and all night, weather stations on the ground measure the temperature, the air pressure, the wind, and the rain. Balloons carry small instruments high into the sky, and hundreds of them are sent up around the world every day. Radar sees where rain is falling, and satellites look down at the clouds from space. Together they give a picture of the air right now.

預報是從「量」開始的。不分白天黑夜，地面上的氣象站一直在量氣溫、氣壓、風和雨。氣球把小小的儀器帶到高空，全世界每天要放好幾百顆。雷達看得到哪裡正在下雨，衛星從太空往下看雲。把這些合起來，就是一張「空氣現在的樣子」。

Next comes the computing. The air follows the rules of nature: warm air rises, wind blows from high pressure to low pressure, and water vapor turns into cloud. Scientists have written these rules as mathematics. A very powerful computer starts with the picture of the air right now and works out what it will be like a few minutes later, and then a few minutes after that, again and again, until it reaches tomorrow and the days after.

接下來是「算」。空氣照著自然的規則走：暖空氣會上升，風從高氣壓吹向低氣壓，水氣會變成雲。科學家把這些規則寫成了數學。一部非常強大的電腦，從「空氣現在的樣子」出發，算出幾分鐘之後會變成什麼樣子，再算出下一個幾分鐘，這樣一遍又一遍，一直算到明天、後天。

But there is a problem. Nobody can measure the air perfectly. There are not enough instruments, and each one is a little bit wrong. These small mistakes do not stay small. They grow as the computer works its way into the future. So a forecast for tomorrow is usually good, and a forecast for next week is much less sure. Scientists think that about two weeks is the limit.

可是有一個問題：沒有人能把空氣量得完全準確。儀器不夠多，而且每一個都有一點點誤差。這些小誤差不會一直那麼小；電腦越往未來算，它們就變得越大。所以明天的預報通常很好，下星期的預報就沒那麼有把握。科學家認為，大約兩個星期就是極限了。

Forecasters have a clever way to deal with this. They run the computer many times, and each time they change the starting picture just a little. If almost every run brings rain, rain is very likely. If only a few runs bring rain, it is less likely. In the end, the forecaster studies all the information and gives the chance of rain as a number.

預報員有一個聰明的辦法來面對這件事。他們讓電腦算很多次，每一次都把起點的資料改動一點點。如果幾乎每一次都下雨，那就很可能會下雨；如果只有少數幾次下雨，可能性就比較低。最後，預報員研判所有的資料，把下雨的機會用一個數字說出來。

Now you can read that number. In Taiwan, a 70% chance of rain for Taipei means there are seven chances in ten that rain will fall in Taipei during that period. It does not mean that it will rain for 70% of the day or on 70% of the city. And if it stays dry on a 70% day, the forecast was not wrong. Three times in ten, that is exactly what should happen.

現在你會讀這個數字了。在台灣，預報台北市的降雨機率是 70%，意思是在那個時段裡，台北有七成的機會出現降雨。它不是說一天裡有 70% 的時間在下雨，也不是說 70% 的地方會下雨。而且，就算 70% 的那一天沒有下雨，預報也沒有錯：十次裡面本來就應該有三次是這樣。

Questions · 閱讀理解

What are the two main steps in making a weather forecast?

First, people measure what the air is doing right now, with stations, balloons, radar, and satellites. Then a computer uses the rules of nature to work out what the air will do next.

Why is a forecast for next week less sure than a forecast for tomorrow?

Nobody can measure the air perfectly, and the small mistakes grow as the computer works further into the future.

What does a 70% chance of rain mean?

It means there are seven chances in ten that rain will fall in that place during that period. It does not tell you how long it will rain or how much of the place will get wet.

Read the Forecast · 讀懂氣象報告

Sunny, cloudy, or overcast? 晴、多雲，還是陰？

Forecasters in Taiwan do not choose these words by feeling. They imagine the whole sky cut into ten equal parts and count how many parts are covered by cloud. Move the slider and see which word they use.
台灣的預報員不是憑感覺選這幾個詞的。他們把整個天空想成十等分，再數一數有幾分被雲遮住。撥動滑桿，看看他們會用哪一個詞。

This follows the Central Weather Administration. Thin cloud and thick cloud both count. When a forecast joins two words, such as sunny at times cloudy (晴時多雲), the first word is the one expected for at least half of the period. The English words are our translations.分法依中央氣象署。不管是薄而透光的雲，還是濃厚的雲，都要算進去。預報把兩個詞連在一起的時候，例如「晴時多雲」，前面那個詞是預計會占一半以上時間的天空狀況。英文是我們的翻譯。

Forecasts by the Numbers · 預報的數字

Six numbers about forecasts 六個和預報有關的數字

From the first idea to the forecast on your phone.
從最早的想法，到你手機裡的預報。

4times a day · 每天

The Central Weather Administration gives the chance of rain for each area four times a day: at 5 and 11 in the morning, and at 5 and 11 in the evening.中央氣象署一天發布 4 次各地的降雨機率：5 時、11 時、17 時和 23 時。

22areas · 區

The daily forecasts cover Taiwan, Kinmen, and Matsu in 22 areas, each with its weather, its high and low temperatures, and its chance of rain.每天的天氣預報把臺灣及金馬分成 22 區，各有天氣、最高最低氣溫和降雨機率。

1922the year · 年

The English scientist Lewis Fry Richardson published the idea of forecasting the weather by calculation, long before there were computers fast enough to do it.英國科學家理查森發表了「用計算來預報天氣」的構想；那時候還沒有夠快的電腦可以做這件事。

1950the year · 年

An early computer called ENIAC made the first computer weather forecast. It needed almost 24 hours to work out a forecast for the next 24 hours.早期的電腦 ENIAC 做出了第一份電腦天氣預報；它花了將近 24 小時，才算出未來 24 小時的預報。

Hundredsa day · 每天

Around the world, hundreds of balloons carry instruments into the sky every day to measure the air high above the ground.全世界每天有好幾百顆氣球把儀器帶上天，去量高空的空氣。

14days · 天

Scientists think this is about as far ahead as the weather can be forecast accurately, even with perfect measurements and a perfect computer.科學家認為，就算有完美的測量和完美的電腦，天氣能準確預報的極限也大約只有這麼遠。

Forecast Stories · 預報的故事

The dream, the slow computer, the butterfly, and the cold wave 一個夢想、一部很慢的電腦、一隻蝴蝶，還有寒流

How forecasting grew up, and one word from the winter forecast.
天氣預報是怎麼長大的，還有冬天預報裡的一個詞。

1922

A hall full of people坐滿了人的大廳

Lewis Fry Richardson believed that the weather could be worked out with mathematics. But there were no computers. So he imagined a huge round hall, like a theater, filled with people. Each person would do the sums for one small part of the world and pass the answer to the next. It was only a dream, but the method he described is the one that computers use today.
理查森相信，天氣是可以用數學算出來的。可是那時候沒有電腦。於是他想像了一個像劇院一樣的巨大圓形大廳，裡面坐滿了人；每個人負責計算世界上的一小塊地方，再把答案傳給旁邊的人。這只是一個夢想，但他描述的方法，就是今天電腦在用的方法。

1950

A forecast that came too late來得太晚的預報

In 1950, an early computer named ENIAC made the first weather forecast by computer. The forecast was for the next 24 hours, and the machine needed almost 24 hours to finish it. By the time the answer came out, the weather had already happened! But it worked, and Richardson, who was still alive, called it an enormous scientific advance.
1950 年，一部叫做 ENIAC 的早期電腦，做出了第一份用電腦算出來的天氣預報。它預報的是未來 24 小時，而機器本身就花了將近 24 小時才算完。答案出來的時候，那天的天氣都已經發生過了！不過這個方法是行得通的。當時還在世的理查森說，這是科學上的巨大進步。

Chaos · 混沌

The butterfly那隻蝴蝶

The scientist Edward Lorenz found that a tiny change at the start of a weather calculation could lead to a completely different result later. To explain it, he used a picture that became famous: a butterfly flapping its wings could, weeks later, change where and when a storm forms. He did not mean that butterflies cause storms. He meant that we can never measure the air well enough to be sure about the far future.
科學家羅倫茲發現，天氣的計算裡，起點只要有一點點不同，後來的結果就可能完全不一樣。為了說明這件事，他用了一個後來很有名的比喻：一隻蝴蝶拍動翅膀，幾個星期之後，可能改變一場風暴形成的時間和地點。他的意思不是蝴蝶會造成風暴，而是我們永遠沒辦法把空氣量得夠準，準到能確定很遠以後的事。

Taiwan · 台灣

When is it a cold wave?什麼時候才叫寒流？

Winter forecasts in Taiwan use three phrases, and each one has a rule. If the lowest temperature in Taipei is expected to fall to 10°C or below, it is a cold wave (寒流). If it is expected to fall to 12°C or below, the forecast says a strong continental cold air mass. Between 12°C and 14°C, it says a continental cold air mass. Above that, it says only that the northeast monsoon is growing stronger.
台灣冬天的預報有三種說法，每一種都有標準。預測台北的日最低溫會降到 10°C 或以下，就叫「寒流」；預測會降到 12°C 或以下，說的是「強烈大陸冷氣團」；在 12°C 到 14°C 之間，是「大陸冷氣團」；如果不會降到 14°C，就只說「東北季風增強」。

Myth vs. Fact · 常見迷思

Four things people get wrong 四個常見的誤會

✗ Myth · 迷思A 70% chance of rain means it will rain for 70% of the day.降雨機率 70%，就是一天裡有 70% 的時間在下雨。

✓ Fact · 事實The number has nothing to do with how long it rains or how much of the area gets wet. It means seven chances in ten that rain will fall there in that period.這個數字和下多久、下多大的範圍都沒有關係。它的意思是：那個時段裡，那個地方有七成的機會出現降雨。

✗ Myth · 迷思The forecast said 20%, and it rained, so the forecast was wrong.預報說 20%，結果下雨了，所以預報不準。

✓ Fact · 事實A 20% chance means that it should rain about two times in ten. This was one of those times.20% 的意思是十次裡面大約有兩次會下雨。這一次剛好就是其中一次。

✗ Myth · 迷思With a fast enough computer, we could forecast the weather for any day next year.只要電腦夠快，明年任何一天的天氣都可以預報。

✓ Fact · 事實The limit is not the speed of the computer. Small mistakes in measuring the air grow with time, and about two weeks is as far as an accurate forecast can go.極限不在電腦的速度。量空氣時的小誤差會隨時間變大，準確的預報大約只能做到兩個星期。

✗ Myth · 迷思Forecasters decide by looking out of the window.預報員是看看窗外的天空來決定的。

✓ Fact · 事實They use measurements from stations, balloons, radar, and satellites, and the results of computer models, and then they add their own judgment.他們用的是氣象站、氣球、雷達和衛星的觀測資料，加上電腦模式算出來的結果，最後再加上自己的研判。

Give the chance說出機率

70% means seven chances in ten.
70% 就是十次裡有七次。

Classroom Activity 1 · 課堂活動一

Keep Score on the Forecast 幫氣象預報打分數

You need · 準備材料

  • A notebook, and the weather forecast for your town一本筆記本，還有你住的地方的天氣預報
  • Ten days十天的時間

Steps · 步驟

  1. Every evening, write down tomorrow's chance of rain for your town.每天晚上，記下明天你住的地方的降雨機率。
  2. The next evening, write down whether it rained that day: yes or no.隔天晚上，記下那一天有沒有下雨：有，或沒有。
  3. After ten days, look at the days when the chance was 70% or more. On how many of them did it rain?十天之後，找出降雨機率在 70% 以上的那幾天：其中有幾天真的下雨了？
  4. Now look at the days when the chance was 30% or less. On how many of them did it rain?再找出降雨機率在 30% 以下的那幾天：其中有幾天下雨了？

You cannot judge a forecast by one day. A good forecaster is one whose 70% days are rainy about seven times in ten. Ten days is only a start, so keep going if you can.
只看一天，沒辦法判斷預報準不準。好的預報，是「說 70% 的日子裡，十次大約有七次真的下雨」。十天只是開始，可以的話就繼續記下去。

Classroom Activity 2 · 課堂活動二

Be the Sky Observer 當一次天空觀測員

You need · 準備材料

  • A place outside where you can see most of the sky一個看得到大部分天空的戶外地點
  • Paper and a pen紙和筆

Steps · 步驟

  1. Look at the whole sky. Never look straight at the sun.看看整個天空。絕對不要直視太陽。
  2. Imagine pushing all the clouds together into one corner. How many tenths of the sky would they cover?想像把所有的雲都推到同一個角落：它們會遮住天空的十分之幾？
  3. Write down your number, and the word that goes with it: sunny, cloudy, or overcast.寫下你的數字，還有對應的詞：晴、多雲，或是陰。
  4. Do this at the same time for five days. Then compare your words with the words in the forecast.連續五天，在同一個時間做一次。然後把你的用詞和預報的用詞比一比。

Two people looking at the same sky often choose numbers that differ by one or two. That is a small example of why no measurement of the air is perfect.
兩個人看同一片天空，常常會差個一兩分。這就是一個小小的例子：對空氣的測量，沒有一種是完美的。
