Earth and Weather · Unit 1 · Lesson 1 · 地球與天氣 單元一 第一課

What Is Inside the Earth?地球裡面有什麼？

The deepest hole people have ever drilled is about 12 kilometers deep. The center of the Earth is 6,371 kilometers down. So how do we know what is in between? Cut the Earth in half in 3D, travel down through its four layers, and see how earthquake waves show us what no one has ever seen.
人類鑽過最深的洞，大約 12 公里深。地球的中心在 6,371 公里底下。那麼中間有什麼，我們是怎麼知道的？在 3D 裡把地球切成兩半，一路往下穿過四層，再看看地震波怎麼讓我們「看見」從來沒有人看過的地方。

Big idea · 一句話看懂

The Earth has four layers. On the outside is a thin, hard crust. Under it is the mantle, thick and solid but able to flow very slowly. Deeper still is the outer core, made of liquid iron and nickel, and at the center is the inner core, a solid ball of iron. No one has been there. We learned it by studying how earthquake waves travel through the Earth.
地球有四層。最外面是又薄又硬的地殼；底下是地函，很厚，是固體，卻能非常緩慢地流動；再往下是外核，是液態的鐵和鎳；最中心是內核，一顆固態的鐵球。沒有人去過那裡。我們是靠研究地震波怎麼穿過地球，才知道這些的。

The crust is the layer we live on. It is hard rock, anywhere from 5 to 70 kilometers thick and about 17 on average. Compared with the whole Earth, it is thinner than the skin on an apple. The deepest hole ever drilled stops at about 12 kilometers.地殼是我們住的這一層。它是堅硬的岩石，厚度從 5 公里到 70 公里不等，平均大約 17 公里。和整個地球比起來，它比蘋果的皮還薄。人類鑽過最深的洞，大約在 12 公里就停了。

The mantle is the thickest layer and makes up most of the Earth. It is solid rock, with temperatures of 2,000 to 3,000°C. Yet over a very long time it flows, slowly, and that slow flow helps move the plates above it.地函是最厚的一層，地球大部分都是它。它是固態的岩石，溫度高達攝氏 2,000 到 3,000 度。可是時間拉得夠長，它會慢慢地流動；這種緩慢的流動，推著上面的板塊移動。

The outer core is liquid: melted iron and nickel, about 2,220 kilometers thick. The moving metal makes the Earth's magnetic field, the one that turns a compass needle.外核是液體：熔化的鐵和鎳，大約有 2,220 公里厚。這些流動的金屬產生了地球的磁場，就是讓指南針轉動的那個磁場。

The inner core is a solid ball of iron about 1,251 kilometers in radius. It is thousands of degrees hot, but the weight of the whole Earth presses on it so hard that the iron cannot melt.內核是一顆固態的鐵球，半徑大約 1,251 公里。這裡有好幾千度，可是整個地球的重量壓在它上面，壓力大到鐵沒辦法熔化。

Listening to the Earth · 聽地球的聲音

An earthquake at the top sends waves through the whole Earth. S waves cannot travel through liquid, so they stop at the outer core. P waves get through, but the core bends them. That leaves a band on the far side, from 103 to 143 degrees away, where instruments record no direct P waves. This shadow zone told scientists that the Earth has a core, and that part of it is liquid.頂端的地震把波送進整個地球。S 波沒辦法穿過液體，所以到外核就停了。P 波穿得過去，可是會被地核折彎。結果在地球的另一側，距離 103 度到 143 度之間，儀器收不到直接的 P 波。這個「陰影帶」讓科學家知道：地球有一個核心，而且其中一部分是液體。

Unit 1: What Is Inside the Earth?

How far down can people dig? The deepest hole ever drilled is in the far north of Russia. Scientists worked on it for many years, and in 1989 it reached 12,262 meters, a little more than 12 kilometers. That sounds deep. But the center of the Earth is 6,371 kilometers below your feet. The hole did not even get through the outer skin.

人類可以往下挖多深？世界上鑽過最深的洞在俄羅斯的極北地區。科學家花了很多年，1989 年鑽到 12,262 公尺，也就是 12 公里多一點。聽起來很深吧？可是地球的中心在你腳下 6,371 公里的地方。這個洞連最外面那層皮都還沒穿過去。

That outer skin is called the crust. It is the hard rock we live on, and it lies under the oceans too. The crust is from 5 to 70 kilometers thick, and about 17 kilometers on average. If the Earth were an apple, the crust would be thinner than the peel.

那層皮叫做地殼。它是我們居住的堅硬岩石，海洋底下也是地殼。地殼的厚度從 5 公里到 70 公里，平均大約 17 公里。如果把地球想成一顆蘋果，地殼比蘋果皮還要薄。

Below the crust is the mantle, the thickest layer by far. It reaches down to 2,900 kilometers and makes up more than four fifths of the Earth. The mantle is solid rock, and it is very hot: 2,000 to 3,000 degrees Celsius. Over millions of years it flows, very slowly, and this slow movement helps push the plates of the crust around.

地殼底下是地函，它是最厚的一層，而且厚得多。它一路延伸到 2,900 公里深，占了地球的五分之四以上。地函是固態的岩石，而且非常熱：攝氏 2,000 到 3,000 度。經過幾百萬年，它會非常緩慢地流動；這種緩慢的移動，幫忙推著地殼的板塊到處跑。

Under the mantle is the core, and it has two parts. The outer core is a deep ocean of liquid iron and nickel. As this metal moves, it makes the Earth's magnetic field. At the very center is the inner core, a ball of iron about 1,251 kilometers in radius. It is even hotter than the outer core, yet it is solid, because the pressure there is so great.

地函底下是地核，分成兩個部分。外核是一片很深的液態鐵和鎳的海洋；這些金屬流動的時候，就產生了地球的磁場。最中心是內核，一顆半徑大約 1,251 公里的鐵球。它比外核還熱，卻是固體，因為那裡的壓力太大了。

But if no one has ever been deeper than 12 kilometers, how do we know any of this? The answer is earthquakes. Earthquake waves travel all the way through the Earth, and they change speed and direction when the rock changes. One kind, the S wave, cannot pass through liquid at all. By recording the waves all over the world, scientists can work out what lies below.

可是，既然從來沒有人到過比 12 公里更深的地方，我們怎麼會知道這些？答案是地震。地震波會一路穿過地球，而且岩石一變，波的速度和方向就跟著變。其中一種叫 S 波，完全沒辦法穿過液體。科學家在世界各地記錄這些波，就能推算出底下有什麼。

In 1909 a scientist named Andrija Mohorovičić found the bottom of the crust this way. In 1936 Inge Lehmann studied earthquake records and discovered that the core has a solid center. Every large earthquake still teaches us something. We have never seen the inside of our planet, but we have learned to listen to it.

1909 年，一位叫莫霍洛維奇的科學家就是用這個方法找到地殼的底部。1936 年，英厄．雷曼研究地震紀錄，發現地核的中心是固體。直到今天，每一次大地震都還在教我們新的東西。我們從來沒有親眼看過這顆行星的內部，但我們學會了怎麼聽它。

Questions · 閱讀理解

What are the four layers of the Earth, from the outside in?

The crust, the mantle, the outer core, and the inner core.

How is the outer core different from the inner core?

The outer core is liquid iron and nickel. The inner core is a solid ball of iron, kept solid by the great pressure.

How do scientists know what is inside the Earth?

They record earthquake waves all over the world. The waves change speed and direction in different kinds of rock, and S waves cannot pass through liquid.

Stories from Below · 來自地底的故事

A very deep hole, the bottom of the crust, a solid heart, and a planet that is a magnet 很深的洞、地殼的底、固態的中心，還有一顆會當磁鐵的行星

Four discoveries about a place no one has visited.
四個發現，說的是一個從來沒有人去過的地方。

1989

The deepest hole最深的洞

On the Kola Peninsula in Russia, scientists set out to drill as deep into the crust as they could. In 1979 their hole became the deepest in the world, and in 1989 it reached 12,262 meters. A later attempt had to stop when the rock turned out to be 180°C, much hotter than expected.
在俄羅斯的科拉半島，科學家想盡可能往地殼深處鑽。1979 年，他們的洞成了全世界最深的；1989 年鑽到 12,262 公尺。後來再往下鑽的時候，發現岩石的溫度高達攝氏 180 度，比原先預期的熱得多，只好停下來。

1909

The bottom of the crust地殼的底

In 1909 the Croatian scientist Andrija Mohorovičić noticed something odd in his earthquake records: two sets of waves from the same earthquake. One set had come straight through the crust. The other had dipped down into faster rock and come back up. He had found the boundary between the crust and the mantle. It is still called the Moho.
1909 年，克羅埃西亞的科學家莫霍洛維奇在地震紀錄裡發現一件怪事：同一個地震，竟然有兩組波。一組直接穿過地殼；另一組往下鑽進傳得比較快的岩層，再繞上來。他找到了地殼和地函的交界，到今天這個交界面還叫做「莫霍面」。

1936

A solid heart固態的中心

Scientists once thought the whole core was liquid. Then the Danish scientist Inge Lehmann studied the records of earthquakes in New Zealand. Some waves seemed to bounce off something deep inside the core. In 1936 she showed that the liquid core has a solid center.
科學家原本以為整個地核都是液體。後來，丹麥的科學家英厄．雷曼研究了紐西蘭地震的紀錄，發現有些波好像被地核深處的某個東西反彈回來。1936 年，她證明了液態的地核裡面有一個固態的中心。

The compass · 指南針

A planet that is a magnet會當磁鐵的行星

Why does a compass needle point north? The answer is in the outer core. Heat escaping from the core keeps the liquid iron and nickel moving, and the moving metal carries electric currents. Those currents make the magnetic field that surrounds the whole Earth.
指南針的針為什麼會指向北方？答案在外核。從地核往外散的熱，讓液態的鐵和鎳不停地流動，流動的金屬帶著電流，這些電流就產生了包住整個地球的磁場。

Myth vs. Fact · 常見迷思

Four things people get wrong 四個常見的誤會

✗ Myth · 迷思The inside of the Earth is all melted rock.地球裡面全都是熔化的岩漿。

✓ Fact · 事實Most of it is solid. The mantle is solid rock and the inner core is solid iron. Only the outer core is liquid.大部分是固體。地函是固態的岩石，內核是固態的鐵，只有外核是液體。

✗ Myth · 迷思People have drilled deep into the Earth.人類已經鑽到地球很深的地方了。

✓ Fact · 事實The deepest hole is about 12 kilometers, less than 0.2% of the way to the center.最深的洞大約 12 公里，連到地心路程的 0.2% 都不到。

✗ Myth · 迷思The center of the Earth is liquid because it is the hottest part.地球的中心最熱，所以是液體。

✓ Fact · 事實It is the hottest part, but it is solid. The pressure is so great that the iron cannot melt.那裡確實最熱，卻是固體。壓力太大了，鐵沒辦法熔化。

✗ Myth · 迷思Scientists are only guessing about what is down there.底下有什麼，科學家只是用猜的。

✓ Fact · 事實They measure it. Earthquake waves recorded all over the world show where the layers begin and end.他們是量出來的。世界各地記錄到的地震波，顯示出每一層從哪裡開始、到哪裡結束。

Classroom Activity 1 · 課堂活動一

An Egg Is a Planet 一顆蛋就是一顆行星

You need · 準備材料

  • A hard-boiled egg for each group, and a plate每組一顆水煮蛋，還有一個盤子
  • A ruler and a knife (for the adult)一把尺，還有一把刀（由大人使用）

Steps · 步驟

  1. Tap the egg gently so that the shell cracks into several pieces but stays on. These pieces are your plates.輕輕敲蛋，讓蛋殼裂成好幾片但還留在蛋上。這幾片就是你的「板塊」。
  2. Ask the adult to cut the egg in half through the shell.請大人連殼把蛋切成兩半。
  3. Look at the cut face. Which part is like the crust, which the mantle, and which the core? Measure how thick each one is.看看切面。哪一部分像地殼？哪一部分像地函？哪一部分像地核？量量看每一層有多厚。
  4. Talk about it: in what ways is the egg a good model of the Earth? In what ways is it wrong?討論：這顆蛋有哪些地方很像地球？又有哪些地方不對？

One thing the egg gets wrong: the yolk is one piece, but the Earth's core has a liquid outer part and a solid center.
這顆蛋有一個地方不對：蛋黃是完整的一塊，可是地球的地核外面是液體、中心是固體。

Classroom Activity 2 · 課堂活動二

What Is in the Box? 盒子裡有什麼？

You need · 準備材料

  • Several closed boxes or cans, each with something different inside: sand, a marble, water in a small sealed bottle, cotton幾個蓋好的盒子或罐子，裡面各放不同的東西：沙子、彈珠、裝在密封小瓶裡的水、棉花
  • Paper and a pencil紙和鉛筆

Steps · 步驟

  1. Do not open the boxes. Shake each one, tilt it, and tap it, and listen carefully.不可以打開盒子。把每一個搖一搖、歪一歪、敲一敲，仔細聽。
  2. Write down what you think is inside each box, and what clue made you think so.寫下你覺得每個盒子裡是什麼，以及是哪個線索讓你這麼想的。
  3. Is it solid or liquid? One piece or many? How can you tell without looking?它是固體還是液體？是一整塊還是很多小塊？不用看，你怎麼知道？
  4. Open the boxes and check. Which were easy? Which fooled you?打開盒子對答案。哪些很容易？哪些把你騙了？

This is how scientists study the Earth. They cannot open it, so they send waves through it and listen to what comes back.
科學家就是這樣研究地球的。他們沒辦法把地球打開，所以讓波穿過去，再聽傳回來的是什麼。
