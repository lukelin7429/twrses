Big idea · 一句話看懂

The Earth's surface is made of huge plates that move a few centimeters a year. Taiwan sits where two of them meet, and one pushes hard against the other. The rock gets stuck, bends, and stores up force. When it can hold no more, it slips all at once. That sudden slip is an earthquake.
地球的表面是由幾塊巨大的板塊組成的，每年移動幾公分。台灣剛好在兩塊板塊相接的地方，其中一塊用力推著另一塊。岩層卡住、被壓彎，把力存了起來；到了撐不住的時候，就一下子滑動。這突然的一滑，就是地震。

Unit 2: Why Does Taiwan Have So Many Earthquakes?

Your phone makes a loud sound. A few seconds later, the lamp begins to swing and the floor moves under your feet. If you live in Taiwan, you know this feeling well. Taiwan records about 40,000 earthquakes every year. Most are too small to notice, but people can feel about 1,000 of them. Why does the ground here shake so often?

手機發出很大的聲音。幾秒鐘之後，燈開始晃，腳下的地板也動了起來。住在台灣的人，對這種感覺再熟悉不過。台灣每年大約測到四萬次地震，大部分小到沒有人注意，但其中大約有一千次是人感覺得到的。這裡的地為什麼這麼常搖？

The answer begins with the Earth's outer shell. It is not one piece. It is broken into huge slabs of rock called plates, and the plates move very slowly, a few centimeters a year. Taiwan sits right where two plates meet: the Eurasian Plate and the Philippine Sea Plate. The Philippine Sea Plate pushes toward the northwest at about 7 to 8 centimeters a year.

答案要從地球的外殼說起。它不是完整的一塊，而是裂成好幾片巨大的岩石板，叫做板塊。板塊移動得非常慢，每年只有幾公分。台灣剛好在兩塊板塊相接的地方：歐亞板塊和菲律賓海板塊。菲律賓海板塊每年大約以 7 到 8 公分的速度往西北方推擠。

If the plates slid past each other smoothly, we would feel nothing. But rock is rough, and the plates get stuck along cracks called faults. The plate keeps pushing while the fault stays stuck, so the rock bends, a little like a plastic ruler. All that time, it is storing up force. At last the rock can hold no more. The fault slips suddenly, and the rock springs back. That is an earthquake.

如果板塊之間可以順順地滑過去，我們什麼都感覺不到。可是岩石很粗糙，板塊會沿著叫做「斷層」的裂縫卡住。板塊繼續推，斷層卻卡著不動，岩層就被壓彎了，有點像一把被折彎的塑膠尺。這段時間裡，它一直在把力存起來。最後岩層撐不住了，斷層突然滑動，岩層彈了回去。這就是地震。

The slip sends out waves through the ground. The fastest ones, called P waves, arrive first and usually shake gently. The S waves come next, and they shake harder. Instruments near the earthquake catch the first waves, and computers quickly send an alert. For places far enough away, the alert can arrive seconds, or even tens of seconds, before the strong shaking.

這一滑，會把波從地底送出去。跑得最快的叫做 P 波，最先到，通常搖得比較輕。接著來的是 S 波，搖得比較厲害。地震附近的儀器收到最早的波，電腦很快算出結果、發出警報。對離得夠遠的地方來說，警報可以比強烈的搖晃早幾秒、甚至幾十秒到。

Most of Taiwan's earthquakes happen in the east, where the two plates meet. Yet earthquakes in the west often do more damage. They tend to be shallow, they happen under the land, and many people live there. A strong earthquake under a crowded plain is more dangerous than a stronger one far out at sea.

台灣的地震大部分發生在東部，也就是兩塊板塊相接的地方。可是西部的地震常常造成比較大的災害：它們通常比較淺、發生在陸地底下，而且那裡住了很多人。一個發生在擁擠平原底下的強震，比遠在外海、規模更大的地震還要危險。

Can scientists tell us when the next big one will come? Not yet. No one can predict the time, place, and size of an earthquake. What we can do is be ready. When the ground shakes, drop down, take cover under a strong table, and hold on. The same push that shakes Taiwan also built its high mountains. Earthquakes are part of living on a young, growing island.

科學家能不能告訴我們下一次大地震什麼時候來？現在還不行。沒有人能預測地震發生的時間、地點和大小。我們能做的是準備好。地開始搖的時候：趴下、躲到堅固的桌子底下掩護、穩住。推著台灣搖晃的這股力量，也把台灣的高山推了起來。住在一座年輕、還在長大的島上，地震就是生活的一部分。

Questions · 閱讀理解

Which two plates meet at Taiwan?

The Eurasian Plate and the Philippine Sea Plate.

What happens along a fault before and during an earthquake?

Before the earthquake, the plate keeps pushing but the fault is stuck, so the rock bends and stores up force. During the earthquake, the fault slips suddenly and the rock springs back.

Why can an earthquake alert arrive before the strong shaking?

Instruments near the earthquake catch the first waves, and the alert is sent out very quickly. In places far enough away, it arrives before the slower, stronger S waves do.

Six numbers from the Central Weather Administration 中央氣象署的六個數字

All six come from the agency that measures Taiwan's earthquakes.
這六個數字都來自負責測量台灣地震的機關。

40,000earthquakes a year · 次地震（每年）

About how many earthquakes Taiwan's instruments record in an ordinary year.平常的年份，台灣的儀器一年大約測到這麼多次地震。

Earthquake Stories · 地震的故事

A bent fence line, a night in 1999, a ring around the ocean, and forty-nine seconds 被扯彎的地面、1999 年的一個夜晚、繞著海洋的一圈，還有四十九秒

Four stories about how we came to understand earthquakes.
四個故事，說的是我們怎麼慢慢弄懂地震。

1906

The idea of the spring「彈簧」的想法

After the great San Francisco earthquake of 1906, the American scientist Harry Fielding Reid studied how the ground along a fault had moved in the fifty years before it. He saw that the land had been slowly bending the whole time. His idea, that rock stores force and then springs back, is called elastic rebound. It is still the main way we explain earthquakes.
1906 年舊金山大地震之後，美國科學家李德研究了斷層兩邊的地面在地震前五十年是怎麼移動的。他發現，這段時間裡地面一直在慢慢彎曲。他的想法是：岩層先把力存起來，再彈回去。這叫做「彈性回彈」，到今天仍然是解釋地震最主要的理論。

1999 · 九二一

September 21, 1:47 a.m.九月二十一日凌晨 1 點 47 分

The Chi-Chi earthquake of 1999 was caused by the Chelungpu Fault in central Taiwan. The ground broke along a line about 100 kilometers long. In some places one side moved as much as 7 meters sideways and 4 meters up. September 21 is now National Disaster Prevention Day, and on that morning phones across Taiwan receive a practice alert.
1999 年的集集大地震是車籠埔斷層引起的。地表沿著一條大約 100 公里長的線裂開，有些地方兩側水平錯開最多 7 公尺、垂直最多 4 公尺。現在，9 月 21 日是國家防災日，那天早上全台灣的手機都會收到一則演練用的警報。

The Pacific · 太平洋

A ring of earthquakes一圈地震帶

If you mark the world's earthquakes on a map, most of them fall along narrow bands. The biggest band runs all the way around the Pacific Ocean: through Japan, the Ryukyu Islands, Taiwan, the Philippines, and Indonesia to New Zealand, and down the west coasts of North and South America. About 90 percent of the world's earthquakes happen along it.
把全世界的地震標在地圖上，會發現它們大多排成一條條細長的帶子。最大的一條繞了太平洋一整圈：經過日本、琉球、台灣、菲律賓、印尼到紐西蘭，再沿著南北美洲的西岸往下。全世界大約 90% 的地震發生在這一圈上。

2016

Forty-nine seconds四十九秒

On February 6, 2016, an earthquake struck Meinong in southern Taiwan. About 12 seconds after it began, the Central Weather Administration's system had worked out where it was. For Taipei, far to the north, that meant 49 seconds of warning before the strong shaking arrived. Close to the earthquake, though, there was no time at all.
2016 年 2 月 6 日，南台灣的美濃發生地震。地震發生後大約 12 秒，中央氣象署的系統就算出了它的位置。對遠在北邊的台北來說，這代表在強烈搖晃到達之前，有 49 秒的預警時間。不過在離地震很近的地方，一點時間都沒有。

Myth vs. Fact · 常見迷思

Four things people get wrong 四個常見的誤會

✗ Myth · 迷思Scientists can tell us when the next big earthquake will come.科學家可以告訴我們下一次大地震什麼時候來。

✓ Fact · 事實Not yet. No one can predict the time, place, and size of an earthquake. That is why being ready matters.現在還不行。沒有人能預測地震發生的時間、地點和大小。所以平常就要做好準備。

✗ Myth · 迷思Strange clouds or hot, still weather mean an earthquake is coming.出現奇怪的雲，或天氣又悶又熱，就是要地震了。

✓ Fact · 事實There is no evidence that weather and earthquakes are linked. So-called earthquake clouds are ordinary clouds, and earthquakes start deep underground.沒有證據顯示天氣和地震有關。所謂的「地震雲」只是普通的雲，而地震是從很深的地底開始的。

✗ Myth · 迷思The alert on my phone predicts the earthquake.手機的警報是在預測地震。

✓ Fact · 事實The earthquake has already started. The alert simply travels faster than the strong waves.地震已經發生了。警報只是跑得比強烈的地震波快。

✗ Myth · 迷思A place with many small earthquakes is safe from big ones.常常有小地震的地方，就不會有大地震。

✓ Fact · 事實Not so. A big earthquake can still happen there. Each step up in magnitude releases about 32 times more energy, so small ones cannot use it all up.不是這樣。那裡還是可能發生大地震。規模每增加 1，放出的能量大約多 32 倍，小地震沒辦法把力全部用掉。

Be Ready · 防災要點

What to do when the ground shakes 地震來了怎麼辦

  • The most important thing is to protect your head and neck. At once, drop down, take cover, and hold on.最重要的是保護頭部和頸部。馬上趴下、掩護、穩住。
  • Get under a strong table and hold on to its leg. If the table moves, move with it.躲到堅固的桌子底下，握住桌腳。桌子移動的話，人跟著桌子一起移動。
  • If you shelter beside a pillar or in a corner instead, watch out for furniture, lights, bookcases, and anything else that could fall.如果是躲在柱子旁邊或牆角，要小心家具、燈具、書櫃和其他可能掉下來的東西。
  • Stay away from sea walls, riverbanks, and cliffs. If you live near the coast, move to high ground in case of a tsunami.遠離海堤、河堤和山崖。住在海邊的人要往高處移動，以防海嘯。

These points follow Taiwan's official guidance: National Fire Agency, Fire Prevention and Disaster Education Hall ↗以上要點依內政部消防署「消防防災館」與中央氣象署「地震百問」的官方說明。

Classroom Activity 1 · 課堂活動一

Stick and Slip 卡住，然後滑動

You need · 準備材料

  • A heavy book, a long rubber band, and a paper clip or string to hook the band to the book一本厚重的書、一條長橡皮筋，還有把橡皮筋勾在書上的迴紋針或線
  • A rough table or a sheet of sandpaper, and a ruler粗糙的桌面或一張砂紙，還有一把尺

Steps · 步驟

  1. Put the book on the rough surface and hook the rubber band to it.把書放在粗糙的面上，橡皮筋勾在書上。
  2. Pull the free end of the rubber band very slowly and steadily. Watch the band stretch while the book stays still.非常慢、非常穩地拉橡皮筋的另一頭。看橡皮筋越拉越長，書卻沒有動。
  3. Keep pulling until the book suddenly jumps. Measure how far it moved, then keep going and count the jumps.繼續拉，直到書突然往前一跳。量量看它移動了多遠，再繼續拉，數一數跳了幾次。
  4. Put a second book on top and try again. Are the jumps bigger or smaller? Do they come more often or less often?上面再疊一本書，重做一次。每次跳得比較遠還是比較近？跳得比較常還是比較少？

Your hand is the plate, the rubber band is the bending rock, and the jump is the earthquake. Could you tell exactly when each jump would happen?
你的手是板塊，橡皮筋是被壓彎的岩層，書跳的那一下就是地震。你有辦法準確說出每一次什麼時候跳嗎？

Classroom Activity 2 · 課堂活動二

The Human Wave 人體地震波

You need · 準備材料

  • Eight or more students standing in a line, each with hands on the shoulders of the person in front八位以上的同學排成一排，每個人把手搭在前面同學的肩膀上
  • A timer, and a desk for each student to practice under afterward計時器；之後每人還需要一張可以躲的桌子

Steps · 步驟

  1. P wave: the last student gently pushes forward and pulls back. Watch the push travel down the line.P 波：最後一位同學輕輕往前推、再拉回來。看這一下怎麼沿著隊伍傳下去。
  2. S wave: the last student sways gently from side to side. Watch the sway travel down the line.S 波：最後一位同學輕輕左右搖。看搖晃怎麼沿著隊伍傳下去。
  3. Time both waves from one end to the other. Which one was faster?兩種波都從頭計時到尾。哪一種比較快？
  4. Now the drill. When the teacher calls “Alert!”, everyone has ten seconds to drop, take cover under a desk, and hold on.接著演練。老師喊「警報！」的時候，每個人有十秒鐘趴下、躲到桌子底下掩護、穩住。

Push and sway gently, and keep your feet still. In the ground, the push wave really is the faster one, so your line of students should show the same thing.
推和搖都要輕輕的，腳不要移動。在地底下，「推」的那種波確實跑得比較快，你們的隊伍應該也會看到一樣的結果。
