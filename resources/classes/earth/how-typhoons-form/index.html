How Does a Typhoon Form?颱風是怎麼形成的？

A typhoon can be hundreds of kilometers wide, yet it starts as nothing more than warm, wet air rising from the sea. In a 3D model, watch a loose patch of cloud grow into a spinning storm with an eye in the middle. Cut it open to see where the air goes, then move it over land and watch what happens. Last, follow the eye of a typhoon as it passes right over your town.
颱風可以有好幾百公里寬，可是它一開始只不過是從海面上升的暖溼空氣。在 3D 模型裡，看一團鬆散的雲怎麼長成一個旋轉的風暴，中間還有一個眼。把它剖開，看看空氣往哪裡走；再把它移到陸地上，看看會發生什麼事。最後，跟著颱風眼，看它從你住的地方正上方通過。

Big idea · 一句話看懂

A typhoon is a huge, spinning storm that is born over a warm tropical sea. Warm, moist air rises, and more air flows in to take its place and begins to circle. When the water vapor in the rising air turns into cloud, it gives out heat, which makes the air rise even faster. So the storm feeds itself as long as it stays over warm water. Over land it loses its supply and grows weaker.
颱風是在溫暖的熱帶海面上誕生的、巨大而旋轉的風暴。暖溼的空氣上升，四周的空氣流進來補位，並且開始繞圈。上升的空氣裡的水氣變成雲的時候會放出熱，讓空氣升得更快。所以只要還在溫暖的海面上，這個風暴就會自己餵飽自己；到了陸地上，補給斷了，它就慢慢變弱。

Now it is a typhoon. As the rising vapor turns into cloud, it gives out heat. The heat makes the air rise faster, so more air rushes in. Over warm sea, the storm keeps growing.現在它是颱風了。上升的水氣變成雲的時候會放出熱；這些熱讓空氣升得更快，於是更多空氣湧進來。在溫暖的海面上，風暴會繼續長大。

Unit 8: How Does a Typhoon Form?

Every summer and fall, people in Taiwan watch the weather report closely. Somewhere far out over the Pacific, a patch of cloud has begun to turn. A few days later it may arrive as a typhoon, with wind strong enough to pull up trees and rain enough to fill a river in hours.

每年夏天和秋天，台灣的人都會特別留意氣象報告。在遙遠的太平洋上，有一團雲開始旋轉了。幾天之後，它可能變成颱風來到這裡，風強得可以把樹連根拔起，雨大得可以在幾個小時裡灌滿一條河。

A typhoon begins over a warm tropical sea. The sun heats the water, and a lot of it turns into water vapor. The air above the sea becomes warm and moist, so it is light, and it rises. Cooler air from all around flows in to take its place, and then it rises too. The air pressure in the middle becomes lower and lower.

颱風是從溫暖的熱帶海面上開始的。太陽把海水曬熱，很多海水變成水氣。海面上的空氣又暖又溼，所以比較輕，就往上升。四周比較涼的空氣流進來補位，然後也跟著上升。中間的氣壓就變得越來越低。

The air does not flow straight to the middle. Because the Earth spins, the air curves, and it begins to circle the center. North of the equator, it turns counterclockwise. Now the storm has a shape: long bands of cloud winding in toward the middle.

空氣並不是直直地流向中間。因為地球在自轉，空氣會轉彎，開始繞著中心打轉。在赤道以北，它是逆時針轉的。這時候風暴有了形狀：長長的雲帶一圈一圈繞向中間。

Then the storm starts to feed itself. As the moist air rises, its water vapor cools and turns into cloud and rain. This change gives out heat. The heat makes the air rise even faster, which pulls in more moist air from the sea. When the wind near the center reaches 17.2 meters a second, which is about 62 kilometers an hour, we call the storm a typhoon.

接下來，風暴開始自己餵飽自己。潮溼的空氣上升的時候，裡面的水氣變冷，凝結成雲和雨；這個變化會放出熱。這些熱讓空氣升得更快，又把更多潮溼的空氣從海面吸進來。當近中心的風速達到每秒 17.2 公尺，也就是每小時大約 62 公里的時候，我們就把這個風暴叫做颱風。

A strong typhoon has a strange thing at its center: an eye. Inside the eye there is almost no wind and no rain, and you may even see the sun or the stars. But right at the edge of the eye is a ring of thick cloud where the wind and rain are the strongest of all. From there outward, they slowly grow weaker.

強的颱風，中心有一個奇特的東西：颱風眼。眼裡面幾乎沒有風，也沒有雨，有時候還看得見太陽或星星。可是就在眼的邊緣，有一圈厚厚的雲，那裡的風雨是整個颱風裡最強的。從那裡再往外，風雨才慢慢變小。

A typhoon cannot live without warm sea. When it moves over land, there is far less water vapor to feed it, and mountains and rough ground break up its shape. It grows weaker, and in the end it fades away. Most typhoons last from four or five days to a little more than ten. That is long enough to cross an ocean, so the safest thing we can do is follow the warnings and get ready early.

颱風離不開溫暖的海。它一到陸地上，能餵它的水氣少了很多，高低起伏的山和地面又破壞它的結構。它越來越弱，最後就消散了。大部分的颱風可以活四、五天，長的有十幾天。這段時間足夠它橫越一片大洋，所以我們能做的最安全的事，就是留意警報、提早準備。

Questions · 閱讀理解

Where does a typhoon begin, and what does it need?

It begins over a warm tropical sea. It needs warm, moist air that rises, so that more air flows in and starts to circle.

Why does a typhoon keep growing stronger over warm sea?

When the water vapor in the rising air turns into cloud, it gives out heat. The heat makes the air rise faster, and that pulls in more moist air from the sea.

What is it like inside the eye, and what is it like at the edge of the eye?

Inside the eye there is almost no wind or rain, and the sky may be clear. At the edge of the eye, the wind and rain are the strongest in the whole storm.

Typhoons by the Numbers · 颱風的數字

Six numbers about typhoons 六個和颱風有關的數字

From the size of the eye to the list of names.
從颱風眼的大小，到名字的清單。

62km/h · 公里／時

When the strongest wind near the center reaches 62 kilometers an hour (17.2 meters a second), the storm is called a typhoon.近中心的最大風速達到每小時 62 公里（每秒 17.2 公尺），這個風暴就叫做颱風。

26a year · 每年

From 1958 to 2025, about 26 typhoons a year formed in the northwestern Pacific and the South China Sea. August had the most.從 1958 年到 2025 年，西北太平洋和南海地區平均每年大約生成 26 個颱風，其中 8 月最多。

45 kmacross · 直徑

The eye of a typhoon is about 45 kilometers wide on average. Small ones are 10 to 20 kilometers, and large ones can reach 100 to 150.颱風眼的直徑平均大約 45 公里；小的只有 10 到 20 公里，大的可以到 100 至 150 公里。

200–300 kmfrom the center · 半徑

On average, the strong winds of a typhoon reach this far from the center. In a large typhoon they reach 400 to 500 kilometers.颱風的暴風半徑平均大約這麼遠；大的颱風可以到 400 至 500 公里。

20–30minutes · 分鐘

When the eye passes over a place, the calm usually lasts only this long. Then the wind and rain come back.颱風眼經過一個地方的時候，平靜通常只維持這麼久，接著狂風暴雨又會出現。

140names · 個名字

Typhoons in this part of the world take their names from a list of 140. Each of 14 countries and regions gave 10.這個地區的颱風，名字來自一份 140 個名字的清單，由 14 個國家和地區各提供 10 個。

Four things people say about typhoons, and where they come from.
四件大家常說的、和颱風有關的事，以及它們的由來。

Word · 字源

Where the word comes from「颱風」這個詞是怎麼來的

Many people think the word came from the Cantonese for “big wind.” Another idea is that it came from Taiwanese. An old book about Taiwan describes how people saw wind and rain whirl in from every side, turning in the air like a sieve, and so they called it “wind sieve.” To this day, the Taiwanese word for a typhoon is hong-thai, with “wind” in front.
一般認為這個詞是從廣東話的「大風」演變來的。另一種說法是從台語來的：清代的《重修臺灣縣志》描寫當地人看見風挾著雨從四面旋轉而來，在空中打轉像篩子一樣，所以叫它「風篩」。直到今天，台語還是把颱風叫做「風颱」。

World · 世界

One storm, many names同一種風暴，不同的名字

Typhoons do not happen only near Taiwan. The same kind of storm forms over other tropical seas, but it has other names. In the western North Pacific and the South China Sea it is a typhoon. In the western Atlantic and the eastern North Pacific it is a hurricane. Over the Indian Ocean it is a cyclone. Scientists use one name for all of them: tropical cyclone.
颱風並不是台灣附近才有。其他的熱帶海面上也有同一種風暴，只是名字不一樣。在北太平洋西部和南海叫做颱風（typhoon）；在大西洋西部和北太平洋東部叫做颶風（hurricane）；在印度洋上叫做氣旋（cyclone）。科學家把它們統稱為「熱帶氣旋」。

Names · 命名

Animals, flowers, and stars動物、花和星星

Since the year 2000, typhoons in this region have taken their names from a shared list. The names are not only people's names. There are animals, plants, stars, places, and figures from old stories. When a typhoon causes a great disaster, its name is taken off the list and replaced. In Taiwan, the Central Weather Administration reports each typhoon mainly by its number, with the name beside it.
從 2000 年開始，這個地區的颱風用一份共同的清單來命名。這些名字不只是人名，還有動物、植物、星象、地名和神話人物。造成重大災害的颱風，名字會從清單上拿掉，換成別的。在台灣，中央氣象署報導颱風的時候以編號為主，國際命名為輔。

Taiwan · 台灣

Wind typhoons and rain typhoons風颱風和雨颱風

Weather forecasters in Taiwan sometimes use two informal names. A “wind typhoon” has strong wind but not much rain. A “rain typhoon” has weaker wind but a great deal of rain. So the strength level in the news does not tell you everything. A typhoon that is called mild can still bring dangerous rain.
台灣的氣象人員有時候會用兩個非正式的稱呼。「風颱風」是風力比較強、雨量比較少的颱風；「雨颱風」是風力不強、雨量卻很大的颱風。所以新聞裡說的強度，並不能告訴你全部的事：被稱為「輕度」的颱風，一樣可能帶來危險的大雨。

Myth vs. Fact · 常見迷思

Four things people get wrong 四個常見的誤會

✗ Myth · 迷思The wind and rain have stopped, so the typhoon is over.風停了、雨也停了，颱風已經走了。

✓ Fact · 事實A sudden calm may be the eye. In about 20 to 30 minutes the storm can return from the opposite direction. A typhoon that is really leaving fades slowly.突然平靜下來，可能是颱風眼正在經過。大約 20 到 30 分鐘以後，狂風暴雨會從相反的方向回來。真正離開的颱風，風雨是慢慢變小的。

✗ Myth · 迷思The strongest wind is in the very middle of a typhoon.颱風的正中央風最強。

✓ Fact · 事實The middle is the eye, which is calm. The strongest wind is at the edge of the eye.正中央是颱風眼，那裡是平靜的。風最強的地方在眼的邊緣。

✗ Myth · 迷思Typhoons happen only near Taiwan.只有台灣附近才有颱風。

✓ Fact · 事實The same storms form over other tropical seas. They are called hurricanes or cyclones there.其他的熱帶海面上也有同樣的風暴，只是在那裡叫做颶風或氣旋。

✗ Myth · 迷思A mild typhoon is nothing to worry about.輕度颱風不用擔心。

✓ Fact · 事實The level describes only the wind near the center. A mild typhoon still has winds of force 8 to 11, and it may bring very heavy rain.強度說的只是近中心的風速。輕度颱風的風一樣有 8 到 11 級，而且可能帶來很大的雨。

Be Ready · 防災要點

Before, during, and after a typhoon 颱風來之前、來的時候、走了以後

  • Before: follow the typhoon news on TV, radio, or the internet, so you know where the storm is.來之前：透過電視、廣播或網路留意颱風消息，知道颱風的最新動向。
  • Before: take down or tie down things that hang or stand outside, such as flower pots on the balcony. Strong wind can turn them into dangerous objects.來之前：把屋外懸掛或擺放的東西（例如陽台的花盆）拿下來或固定好，以免被風吹落傷人。
  • Before: get ready three or four days of food and drinking water, and a flashlight and batteries in case the power goes out.來之前：準備三、四天的食物和飲用水，還有手電筒和電池，以備停電。
  • During: if your home is strong and safe, stay inside. If the wind suddenly drops, it may be the eye, so do not go out.來的時候：住家堅固安全的話，就留在家裡不要外出。風勢突然減弱可能是颱風眼經過，不可以貿然外出。
  • During: never go to the seaside to fish, watch the waves, or play in the water. Stay away from fallen wires, and do not go into a flooded basement.來的時候：千萬不要到海邊釣魚、觀浪或戲水。看到垂落的電線不要碰，也不要進入淹水的地下室。
  • After: go out only when the weather report says the typhoon has moved away. Boil tap water before you drink it.走了以後：確定氣象報告說颱風已經遠離，才可以外出。自來水要煮沸以後才能喝。

These points follow the National Fire Agency: National Fire Agency, Typhoon Preparation ↗以下要點依內政部消防署消防防災館「防颱準備」。

Classroom Activity 1 · 課堂活動一

A Spiral in a Bowl 碗裡的螺旋

You need · 準備材料

  • A large round bowl of water and a spoon一個裝了水的大圓碗和一支湯匙
  • Food coloring, or a little milk, and a dropper or a straw食用色素或一點牛奶，還有滴管或吸管

Steps · 步驟

  1. Stir the water in a circle, counterclockwise, until it is all turning. Take the spoon out.用湯匙以逆時針方向把水攪動，直到整碗水都在轉。把湯匙拿出來。
  2. Look at the surface from the side. Is the middle higher or lower than the edge?從側面看水面：中間比邊緣高，還是比邊緣低？
  3. Put one drop of coloring near the edge and another drop near the middle.在靠近碗邊的地方滴一滴色素，在靠近中間的地方再滴一滴。
  4. Watch the two drops for one minute. What shape does the color make? Which drop goes around faster?觀察這兩滴一分鐘。顏色被拉成什麼形狀？哪一滴繞得比較快？

The bowl shows only the shape of a typhoon: bands that wind toward a low middle. You made it turn with a spoon. A real typhoon is kept turning by the heat of a warm sea.
這碗水只能表現颱風的「樣子」：一條條繞向中間低處的帶子。碗裡的水是你用湯匙攪動的；真的颱風，是靠溫暖海面的熱一直轉下去的。

Classroom Activity 2 · 課堂活動二

Our Typhoon Checklist 我們家的防颱檢查表

You need · 準備材料

  • Paper and a pen紙和筆
  • An adult in your family to walk around the home with you一位家裡的大人，陪你在家裡走一圈

Steps · 步驟

  1. Write down four things to check: a flashlight and batteries, drinking water, food that keeps, and things outside that the wind could move.寫下四樣要檢查的東西：手電筒和電池、飲用水、可以久放的食物，還有屋外會被風吹動的東西。
  2. Walk around your home together. Put a check mark beside each thing that is ready.和大人一起在家裡走一圈。準備好的項目，就在旁邊打勾。
  3. For each thing that is not ready, write down who will take care of it, and when.還沒準備好的項目，寫下由誰負責、什麼時候完成。
  4. Decide together where everyone will stay during a typhoon, and how you will reach each other if the phones are busy.一起決定颱風來的時候大家待在哪裡；萬一電話打不通，要怎麼聯絡彼此。

Do this on a sunny day. Getting ready is easy when nothing is happening, and hard when the wind has already begun.
挑一個晴天來做。風平浪靜的時候準備起來很輕鬆；等風已經起來了，就難了。
