Earth and Weather · Unit 2 · Lesson 6 · 地球與天氣 單元二 第六課

Why Does It Rain?為什麼會下雨？

The water that falls on your umbrella was in the sea not long ago. How did it get into the sky, and why does it fall on one side of a mountain and not the other? In a 3D model, send moist air from the sea up a mountain, watch a cloud form, and find the hot, dry wind on the far side. Then work out how much water one storm really brings.
落在你雨傘上的水，不久之前還在海裡。它是怎麼跑到天上去的？又為什麼只下在山的這一邊，不下在另一邊？在 3D 模型裡，把海上來的潮溼空氣送上一座山，看雲怎麼形成，再找找山的另一邊那股又乾又熱的風。最後算算看，一場雨到底帶來多少水。

Big idea · 一句話看懂

The sun turns water from the sea into a gas we cannot see, called water vapor. Warm air can hold a lot of it. When that air rises, it cools, and cool air cannot hold as much. The extra vapor turns back into tiny drops of water, and millions of them make a cloud. When the drops grow too heavy to float, they fall as rain.
太陽把海裡的水變成一種看不見的氣體，叫做水氣。溫暖的空氣可以容納很多水氣。空氣一上升就會變冷，冷空氣裝不了那麼多，多出來的水氣就變回很小很小的水滴；幾百萬顆小水滴聚在一起，就是雲。水滴大到浮不住的時候，就掉下來變成雨。

Moist air from the sea is pushed up the slope and cools. At the cloud base it reaches its dew point, and the vapor turns into cloud. Rain falls on this side of the mountain, the windward side.海上來的潮溼空氣被推上山坡、變冷。到了雲底的高度，它降到露點，水氣變成了雲。雨下在山的這一邊，也就是迎風面。

This air is almost full of water vapor, so it has to rise only a little before cloud forms. The cloud sits low, and it rains hard on the windward side.這團空氣裡的水氣幾乎已經裝滿了，所以只要稍微上升一點就成雲。雲壓得很低，迎風面下得很大。

This air does not rise high enough. It would have to climb above the top of the hill before any cloud formed, so it crosses with no cloud and no rain. Try a higher mountain, or more humid air.這團空氣升得不夠高。它得爬到比山頂還高才會開始成雲，所以翻過去的時候沒有雲、也沒有雨。換一座高一點的山，或是讓空氣更潮溼，再試試看。

Now the wind comes from the sea on the other side. The same mountain, the same moist air, but the rain has moved to the eastern slope, and the west is dry and warm. This is why the rainy side of Taiwan changes with the season.現在風從另一邊的海上吹來。山還是同一座，空氣一樣潮溼，可是雨跑到東邊的山坡去了，西邊變得又乾又熱。台灣哪一邊多雨會隨著季節改變，就是這個道理。

Unit 6: Why Does It Rain?

Rain seems simple: water falls from the sky. But how did the water get up there? The sky has no pipes and no tanks. The answer is that the water rose by itself, as a gas that you cannot see.

下雨好像很簡單：水從天上掉下來。可是水是怎麼上去的？天上沒有水管，也沒有水塔。答案是：水是自己升上去的，而且是變成一種你看不見的氣體升上去的。

When the sun warms the sea, some of the water turns into water vapor and mixes with the air. Most of the water vapor in the air comes from the oceans. There is never very much of it, only about one part in a hundred on average, but it makes almost all of our weather.

太陽把海水曬暖，一部分的水就變成水氣，混進空氣裡。空氣中的水氣，主要來自海洋。它的量一向不多，平均只占大氣的百分之一左右，可是我們幾乎所有的天氣變化都是它造成的。

Warm air can hold more water vapor than cold air. If air gets about 11 degrees warmer, it can hold about twice as much. This also works the other way. When air cools, it can hold less, and at a certain temperature it is full. That temperature is called the dew point.

溫暖的空氣能容納的水氣，比冷空氣多。溫度大約升高 11 度，能裝的水氣就多一倍。這件事反過來也成立：空氣變冷，能裝的就變少；降到某一個溫度，它就滿了。這個溫度叫做露點。

So the secret of rain is rising air. Air cools as it rises, about 6.5 degrees for every kilometer. When it has cooled to its dew point, the extra water vapor turns into tiny drops of liquid water. A cloud is a huge crowd of these tiny drops. Inside the cloud the drops bump together and grow, and when they are too heavy to float, they fall.

所以，下雨的祕密在於空氣往上升。空氣越往上越冷，每升高 1 公里大約降 6.5 度。冷到露點的時候，多出來的水氣就變成很小很小的水滴。雲，就是一大群這樣的小水滴。在雲裡，小水滴互相碰撞、越變越大；重到浮不住的時候，就掉下來。

What makes air rise? A mountain is one answer. When moist wind from the sea meets a mountain, the air has nowhere to go but up. Clouds form on that side, the windward side, and the rain falls there. By the time the air crosses the top, it has lost much of its water. It sinks down the other side dry and warm.

是什麼讓空氣往上升？山是其中一個答案。從海上吹來的潮溼的風遇到山，空氣無處可去，只能往上爬。雲在這一邊（迎風面）形成，雨也下在這一邊。等空氣翻過山頂，水氣已經掉了大半；它順著另一邊往下沉，又乾又熱。

Taiwan shows this very well. A high wall of mountains runs down the island, and the wind changes with the seasons. In winter the northeast wind brings steady rain to the northeast, while the center and south are mostly sunny. In summer the wind turns, and the south gets most of its rain. The same mountains, the same sea: only the wind has changed.

台灣把這件事表現得非常清楚。一道高高的山脈縱貫全島，而風向隨著季節改變。冬天，東北風替東北部帶來綿綿不斷的雨，中部和南部卻大多是晴天。到了夏天，風向一轉，南部一年大部分的雨都在這時候下。同樣的山、同樣的海，變的只有風。

Questions · 閱讀理解

How does water get from the sea into the sky?

The sun warms the sea, and some of the water turns into water vapor, a gas we cannot see, which mixes with the air.

Why does rising air make clouds?

Air cools as it rises. Cool air cannot hold as much water vapor, so the extra vapor turns into tiny drops of water, and the drops make a cloud.

Why is one side of a mountain often wetter than the other?

Moist wind is forced up the windward side, so clouds form and rain falls there. The air that crosses the top has lost much of its water, so the far side is drier.

How much water is in one storm? 一場雨有多少水？

Rain is measured by how deep the water would be if none of it ran away: 1 millimeter of rain on 1 square meter is exactly 1 liter. Choose how much rain falls in a day and what it falls on. Watch the name that the Central Weather Administration gives to that much rain.

Rain by the Numbers · 雨的數字

Six numbers about rain 六個和雨有關的數字

From a single drop to a whole year.
從一滴雨，到一整年。

1.1%of the air · 的空氣

On average, water vapor makes up only about 1.1% of the atmosphere. It ranges from 0.01% to about 4%.平均來說，水氣大約只占大氣的 1.1%，少的時候 0.01%，多的時候 3% 到 4%。

11°Cwarmer · 升高

Air that is 11°C warmer can hold about twice as much water vapor.空氣的溫度升高 11°C，能容納的水氣大約多一倍。

6.5°Cper kilometer · 每公里

On average, the air gets this much colder for every kilometer you go up.平均每往上 1 公里，氣溫就降低這麼多。

1 kmcloud base · 雲底

In Taiwan, the puffy white clouds of a fine day usually have their bases about one kilometer up.在台灣，好天氣常見的那種一朵朵的白雲，雲底大約在一公里高。

2,600millimeters a year · 毫米（每年）

About how much rain falls on Taiwan in an average year.台灣平均一年的雨量大約是這麼多。

1 Lper square meter · 每平方公尺

One millimeter of rain puts exactly one liter of water on every square meter.1 毫米的雨，剛好在每 1 平方公尺上留下 1 公升的水。

Rain Stories · 雨的故事

The rainy corner, the hot wind, the afternoon storm, and the shape of a raindrop 多雨的角落、火燒風、西北雨，還有雨滴的形狀

Four things about rain that you can check for yourself.
四件和雨有關、你可以自己驗證的事。

Winter · 冬天

The rainy corner多雨的角落

From about November to March, the wind over Taiwan blows from the northeast. It has crossed the sea, so it is full of water vapor, and the first thing it meets is the mountains of the northeast. During these months the northeast gets steady rain, while the central and southern parts of the island are mostly sunny.
大約從 11 月到隔年 3 月，台灣吹的是東北風。這股風剛越過海面，帶著滿滿的水氣，最先碰到的就是東北部的山。這幾個月，東北部陰雨綿綿，中部和南部卻大多是晴天。

焚風

The hot wind火燒風

Air that has dropped its rain on one side of the mountains comes down the other side dry, and it warms as it sinks. The result is a hot, dry wind that people in Taiwan call the fire wind. It happens most often around Taitung, when a typhoon or a low passes north of the island and strong west winds blow across the mountains.
在山的一邊把雨下完的空氣，從另一邊下來的時候是乾的，而且越往下沉越熱。結果就是一股又乾又熱的風，叫做焚風，台灣俗稱「火燒風」。當颱風或低氣壓從台灣北部通過、吹起強勁的西風時，最常在台東一帶發生。

Summer · 夏天

The afternoon storm午後的雷雨

A mountain is not the only thing that makes air rise. On a hot, humid summer afternoon, the ground heats the air above it, and the air shoots upward by itself. Tall, dark clouds can grow from 1 kilometer to 6 kilometers high in about ten minutes, and then the rain pours down.
會讓空氣上升的不只有山。在悶熱潮溼的夏天午後，地面把上面的空氣加熱，空氣自己就往上衝。高聳灰暗的雲，大約十分鐘就可以從 1 公里長到 6 公里高，接著大雨就倒下來。

A raindrop · 雨滴

Not a teardrop不是淚滴的形狀

We draw raindrops with a point on top, but real raindrops never look like that. Small ones are almost perfect balls. Bigger ones are pushed flat on the bottom by the air as they fall, like a hamburger bun. Very big ones break apart into smaller drops.
我們畫雨滴的時候，都會在上面畫一個尖尖的角，可是真的雨滴從來不是那個樣子。小的雨滴幾乎是圓球；大一點的，落下的時候底部被空氣壓扁，像一個漢堡麵包；非常大的，會碎成比較小的雨滴。

Myth vs. Fact · 常見迷思

Four things people get wrong 四個常見的誤會

✗ Myth · 迷思Clouds are made of water vapor.雲是水氣做的。

✓ Fact · 事實Water vapor is a gas, and you cannot see it. A cloud is made of tiny drops of liquid water, or tiny crystals of ice.水氣是氣體，看不見。雲是很小很小的液態水滴，或是很小的冰晶。

✗ Myth · 迷思Rain falls when clouds bump into each other.雲撞在一起就會下雨。

✓ Fact · 事實Rain falls when the drops inside a cloud grow too heavy to stay up.雲裡面的水滴長大到浮不住的時候，才會下雨。

✗ Myth · 迷思Raindrops are shaped like teardrops.雨滴是淚滴的形狀。

✓ Fact · 事實Small raindrops are round, and bigger ones are flattened on the bottom. None are pointed.小的雨滴是圓的，大一點的底部是扁的，沒有尖尖的。

✗ Myth · 迷思A rainy place is rainy because it is near the sea.一個地方雨多，是因為它靠海。

✓ Fact · 事實The sea gives the water, but the air must also rise. Places where moist wind is pushed up a mountain get the most rain.海提供了水，可是空氣還得往上升才行。潮溼的風被山抬高的地方，雨才最多。

Be Ready · 防災要點

When heavy rain is forecast 預報有大雨的時候

  • Check the forecast before you go out. A heavy rain or extremely heavy rain advisory means a lot of water in a short time.出門前先看氣象預報。發布大雨或豪雨特報，代表短時間內會有很多水。
  • Stay away from streams, riverbeds, and steep slopes during and after heavy rain. Water can rise quickly, and rocks can fall.大雨的時候和大雨過後，遠離溪流、河床和陡坡。溪水可能很快暴漲，也可能有落石。
  • For the experiments, use warm water from the faucet, not boiling water, and wipe up spills.做實驗用溫的自來水就好，不要用滾水；潑出來的水要擦乾。

  • A dark piece of paper to hold behind the jar一張深色的紙，用來墊在瓶子後面

Steps · 步驟

  1. Pour warm water into the jar until it is about a quarter full. Wait one minute.把溫水倒進瓶子，大約四分之一滿。等一分鐘。
  2. Put the plate of ice on top of the jar, like a lid.把放了冰塊的盤子蓋在瓶口，當蓋子。
  3. Hold the dark paper behind the jar and watch the space just under the plate.把深色的紙墊在瓶子後面，注意看盤子正下方的空間。
  4. Look at the bottom of the plate after five minutes. What has formed there? Where did it come from?五分鐘後看看盤子的底部。上面出現了什麼？它是從哪裡來的？

The warm water is the sea, the air in the jar is the sky, and the cold plate is the high, cold air. The drops under the plate are your rain.
溫水是海，瓶子裡的空氣是天空，冰冷的盤子是高空的冷空氣。盤子底下的水滴，就是你的雨。

Classroom Activity 2 · 課堂活動二

Make a Rain Gauge 做一個雨量計

You need · 準備材料

  • A straight-sided clear container, such as a jar or a cut plastic bottle一個直筒狀的透明容器，例如玻璃瓶或剪開的寶特瓶
  • A ruler, tape, and a notebook一把尺、膠帶和一本筆記本

Steps · 步驟

  1. Tape the ruler to the outside of the container with zero at the bottom.把尺貼在容器外面，0 的那一頭對齊底部。
  2. Put the container in an open place, away from trees and roofs.把容器放在空曠的地方，不要在樹下或屋簷下。
  3. After each rainy day, read how many millimeters of water are in it, write it down, and empty it.每個下雨天過後，讀一讀裡面的水有幾毫米，記下來，再把水倒掉。
  4. Compare your number with the rainfall reported for your town. Then work out how many liters fell on your classroom roof.把你的數字和氣象報告裡你們鄉鎮的雨量比一比。再算算看，教室屋頂上總共落下了幾公升的水。

The container must have straight sides. If it is wider at the top than at the bottom, it will catch too much rain and your reading will be too high.
容器一定要是直筒的。如果口比底寬，接到的雨水會太多，讀出來的數字就偏高。
