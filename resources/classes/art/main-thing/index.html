Where Should the Main Thing Go?主角該放在哪裡？

A picture has edges, and where you put things inside them changes how the picture feels. Drag shapes around a frame, watch the balance point move, and crop a wide scene in three different ways.
畫面有邊，東西放在邊框裡的哪裡，會改變整張畫給人的感覺。在畫框裡拖動形狀，看重心怎麼移動；再把一片寬寬的風景，用三種方式裁切。

Big idea · 一句話看懂

A picture is a rectangle with edges. Where you place things inside it changes its balance and decides what the viewer looks at first. A big pale shape near the middle can balance a small dark shape far out, like a seesaw. The main thing does not have to be in the center, and it does not have to be on a thirds line either. The empty space around it is part of the picture too.
畫面是一個有邊的長方形。東西放在裡面的哪裡，會改變畫面的平衡，也決定看的人先看哪裡。靠近中間的大而淡的形狀，可以和放得遠遠的小而深的形狀互相平衡，就像蹺蹺板。主角不一定要放在正中央，也不一定要放在三分線上。它四周空著的地方，也是畫面的一部分。

What this model leaves out 這個模型簡化了什麼

Reading · 英文閱讀

7

Unit 7: Where Should the Main Thing Go?

Every picture has edges. A painting has a frame, a photo has borders, and a page in your sketchbook stops where the paper stops. Inside those edges, the artist decides where everything goes. This is called composition. Move the main thing a little to the left, or make the empty space a little larger, and the whole picture feels different.

每一張畫都有邊。油畫有畫框，照片有邊緣，素描本的一頁到紙的邊就停了。在這些邊的裡面，東西要放在哪裡，由畫的人決定。這叫做構圖。把主角往左移一點，或把空白留大一點，整張畫的感覺就不一樣了。

One thing artists think about is balance. Some shapes look heavier than others. A big shape looks heavier than a small one, and on light paper a dark shape looks heavier than a pale one. When the heavy parts are all on one side, the picture seems to tip over. When they are spread out well, it feels steady.

畫家會考慮的一件事是平衡。有些形狀看起來比別的重：大的看起來比小的重；在淺色的紙上，深的看起來也比淡的重。重的部分全擠在同一邊，畫面就好像要倒了；分配得好，畫面就感覺很穩。

There are three main kinds of balance. In symmetrical balance, the two sides are like mirror images. In asymmetrical balance, the two sides are different but still look balanced. A big pale shape near the middle can balance a small dark shape far out, just as a light child at the far end of a seesaw can balance a heavier child sitting near the middle. In radial balance, things spread out from a center, like the petals of a flower.

平衡主要有三種。對稱的平衡，兩邊就像鏡子裡的影像。不對稱的平衡，兩邊不一樣，看起來卻還是平衡的：靠近中間的大而淡的形狀，可以和放得遠遠的小而深的形狀互相平衡，就像蹺蹺板上，坐在最尾端的輕的小孩，可以和坐在靠近中間的重的小孩平衡一樣。放射狀的平衡，東西從一個中心往外排開，像花的花瓣。

Artists also decide what should catch your eye first. This is called emphasis, and the place you look at first is the focal point. An artist can make one area stand out by making it different from everything around it. Scientists who track people's eyes have found that faces and words pull our eyes strongly, even when we are asked to look for something else.

畫家也會決定什麼東西要先抓住你的目光。這叫做強調，你最先看的地方叫做焦點。畫家可以讓某一塊和周圍都不一樣，讓它跳出來。追蹤眼睛的科學家發現，臉和文字特別會吸引我們的目光，就算我們被要求去找別的東西也一樣。

You may have heard of the rule of thirds. Draw two lines across the picture and two lines down it, so that it is cut into nine equal parts, and put the main thing on one of the lines or where two of them cross. The name was first written down in 1797. It is a rule of thumb, not a law. Many fine pictures put the main thing right in the middle.

你可能聽過三分法：在畫面上畫兩條橫線、兩條直線，把它分成一樣大的九格，再把主角放在其中一條線上，或兩條線的交點上。這個名稱最早在 1797 年被寫下來。它是經驗法則，不是定律；很多很好的畫，主角就放在正中央。

Finally, look at what is not there. The empty space around and between things is called negative space, and it has a shape of its own. Chinese calligraphers say that the blank parts of a character should be planned as carefully as the brush strokes. A picture with lots of empty space can feel calm, and a crowded one can feel busy. Neither is wrong. It depends on what the artist wants you to feel.

最後，看看沒有東西的地方。東西四周和中間空著的地方叫做負空間，它也有自己的形狀。中國的書法講「計白當黑」：字裡空白的地方，要像筆畫一樣用心安排。空白多的畫面可以感覺很安靜，擠滿東西的畫面可以感覺很熱鬧。兩種都沒有錯，要看畫的人想讓你感受到什麼。

Questions · 閱讀理解

What is the difference between symmetrical and asymmetrical balance?

In symmetrical balance the two sides are like mirror images. In asymmetrical balance the two sides are different, but the picture still looks balanced.

How can a small shape balance a much bigger one?

Make the small shape darker, so it looks heavier, and put it farther from the middle, like a light child at the far end of a seesaw.

Is the rule of thirds a law that every good picture follows?

No. It is a rule of thumb that was first written down in 1797. Many fine pictures put the main thing in the middle, and studies have not found that the rule makes pictures better.

Stories Behind the Frame · 畫框背後的故事

A rule named in 1797, eyes that follow questions, blank space in calligraphy, and rules put to the test 1797 年取名的規則、跟著問題走的眼睛、書法裡的空白，以及接受檢驗的規則

Four short stories about where people put things in a picture, and how we know what works.
四個小故事：人怎麼決定東西放在畫面的哪裡，以及我們怎麼知道哪樣行得通。

1797 · 英國

A rule gets its name一條規則有了名字

In 1797 the English artist John Thomas Smith wrote about a “Rule of thirds” in a book called Remarks on Rural Scenery. He meant it for landscapes: let the sky take up about two-thirds of the picture, or about one-third, instead of exactly half. Not everyone agreed. In 1845 another writer, George Field, called the rule “neither accurate nor universal.”
1797 年，英國的畫家史密斯（John Thomas Smith）在《鄉村風景評述》這本書裡寫到「三分法」。他指的是風景畫：讓天空大約占畫面的三分之二，或大約三分之一，而不是剛好一半。並不是每個人都同意。1845 年，另一位作者菲爾德（George Field）說這條規則「既不精確，也不普遍」。

1960s · 蘇聯

Eyes that follow the question跟著問題走的眼睛

The Russian scientist Alfred Yarbus recorded where people's eyes went as they looked at pictures. He found that the path of the eyes depended on what the viewer had been asked to do. When people looked at a face, their eyes kept going back to the eyes and the mouth. So there is no single path that every viewer's eyes follow through a picture.
俄國科學家亞布斯（Alfred Yarbus）記錄人看圖時眼睛往哪裡看。他發現，眼睛走的路線要看看的人被要求做什麼。人看一張臉時，目光會一再回到眼睛和嘴巴。所以，並沒有一條每個人的眼睛都會照著走的路線。

Calligraphy · 書法

Count the white as black計白當黑

Chinese calligraphy has a saying, “count the white as black” (計白當黑). The Ministry of Education's dictionary explains that it means arranging the blank spaces within and between characters as carefully as if they were real brush strokes, so that the space is well balanced. The same idea works in a picture. The empty part is something you design.
中國書法講「計白當黑」。《重編國語辭典修訂本》的解釋是：書法上指將字裡行間中的空白處，當做實體筆畫一般安排布置，務使空間調配得當。畫面也是一樣：空著的部分，也是要設計的。

Science · 科學

Putting the rules to the test把規則拿來檢驗

Do the rules really work? In 2014 a team of scientists checked large sets of high-quality photographs and paintings. They found that the rule of thirds seems to play only a minor role, if any. In another study, when people framed a single object facing the viewer, they liked it best at or near the center. Rules can help you start, but your eyes have the last word.
這些規則真的有用嗎？2014 年，一組科學家檢查了大量的優秀照片和畫作，發現三分法似乎只扮演很小的角色，甚至沒有角色。另一個研究發現，當人替一個正對著觀眾的東西取景時，最喜歡把它放在正中央或靠近中央。規則可以幫你起頭，但最後要由你的眼睛決定。

Myth vs. Fact · 常見迷思

Four things people get wrong 四個常見的誤會

✗ Myth · 迷思Never put the main thing in the middle.主角絕對不能放在正中間。

✓ Fact · 事實Many fine pictures do. In one study, when people framed a single object facing the viewer, they liked it best at or near the center. The middle feels steady and still, which is sometimes exactly what the artist wants.很多很好的畫就是這樣放的。有一個研究發現，人替一個正對著觀眾的東西取景時，最喜歡放在正中央或靠近中央。中間給人穩定、安靜的感覺，有時正是畫的人想要的。

✗ Myth · 迷思The rule of thirds is a law of good pictures.三分法是好畫面的定律。

✓ Fact · 事實It is a rule of thumb from 1797. A 2014 study of large sets of photographs and paintings found that it seems to play only a minor role, if any.它是 1797 年留下來的經驗法則。2014 年一個檢查大量照片和畫作的研究發現，它似乎只扮演很小的角色，甚至沒有角色。

✗ Myth · 迷思Famous paintings, like the Mona Lisa, were built on the golden ratio.名畫（例如〈蒙娜麗莎〉）都是照黃金比例畫的。

✓ Fact · 事實The claim about the Mona Lisa is not supported by Leonardo's own writings, and careful tests of whether people prefer the golden ratio have been inconclusive at best.〈蒙娜麗莎〉用了黃金比例的說法，在達文西自己的文字裡找不到根據；仔細檢驗人是否偏好黃金比例的研究，結果最多只能說沒有定論。

✗ Myth · 迷思Empty space is wasted space.空白就是浪費掉的地方。

✓ Fact · 事實Negative space has a shape of its own, and it decides how calm or crowded a picture feels. Chinese calligraphers plan the blank parts as carefully as the strokes.負空間有自己的形狀，也決定畫面感覺安靜還是擁擠。中國的書法家安排空白，就像安排筆畫一樣用心。

Before You Start · 動手之前

Scissors and looking around 剪刀和四處看看

  • Use scissors to cut the viewfinder. If a craft knife is needed for the window, an adult should do that part.用剪刀剪取景框。如果挖窗口需要美工刀，那一部分請大人來做。
  • Carry scissors closed, with the points down, and hand them to someone handle first.拿剪刀走動時要合起來、尖端朝下；遞給別人時握柄朝向對方。
  • When you walk around looking through a viewfinder, stop before you look. Do not walk while your eye is at the window.拿著取景框到處看的時候，先停下來再看。眼睛貼在窗口上時不要走路。
  • Never look at the Sun through the viewfinder or in any other way.不要透過取景框看太陽，用任何方式都不行。

Classroom Activity 1 · 課堂活動一

A Paper Viewfinder 紙做的取景框

You need · 準備材料

  • A piece of card about the size of a postcard一張大約明信片大小的卡紙
  • Scissors and a ruler剪刀和尺
  • Paper and a pencil紙和鉛筆

Steps · 步驟

  1. Cut a window 8 cm wide and 6 cm high in the middle of the card. That is the same 4:3 shape as the frame on this page.在卡紙中間挖一個寬 8 公分、高 6 公分的窗口。這和本頁畫框一樣是 4:3 的形狀。
  2. Choose one thing in the classroom or the schoolyard to be your main thing, such as a plant, a chair, or a friend's backpack.在教室或校園裡選一樣東西當主角，例如一盆植物、一張椅子或同學的書包。
  3. Look at it through the window three ways: with the main thing in the middle, on a thirds point, and very close so that it fills the window.透過窗口用三種方式看它：主角在正中央、主角在三分點上、靠得很近讓主角塞滿窗口。
  4. Make a quick sketch of each one. Draw only the big shapes.每一種都畫一張快速的草圖，只畫大的形狀。
  5. Put a star next to the one you like best. Compare with a classmate who chose the same main thing.在你最喜歡的那一張旁邊畫一顆星。和選了同一個主角的同學比一比。

Moving the card closer to your eye shows more of the scene, and moving it away shows less, just like making the yellow frame bigger or smaller in Crop It.
卡紙拿近眼睛，看到的範圍變大；拿遠一點，範圍變小。這就像在「裁切看看」裡把黃色的框放大或縮小。

Classroom Activity 2 · 課堂活動二

Balance It With Paper 用紙排出平衡

You need · 準備材料

  • White paper for the background一張白紙當底
  • Light gray or pale paper and black paper淺灰或淡色的紙，和黑色的紙
  • Scissors剪刀

Steps · 步驟

  1. Cut one big circle from the pale paper and one small circle from the black paper.從淡色的紙剪一個大圓，從黑色的紙剪一個小圓。
  2. Lay them on the white paper, without glue, in three arrangements: both on one side, mirror-like if you can, and big near the middle with small far out.不用膠水，把它們放在白紙上，排出三種樣子：都放在同一邊；盡量左右對稱；大的靠近中間、小的放得很遠。
  3. For each one, ask three classmates whether it looks balanced. Write down their answers.每一種都問三位同學：看起來平衡嗎？把他們的回答寫下來。
  4. Copy each arrangement into the model on this page. Does the model agree with your classmates?把每一種擺法在這一頁的模型裡排一次。模型和同學的看法一樣嗎？
  5. Now hold the two paper circles in your hands. The black one is much lighter to hold, yet it may look heavier. Visual weight is not the weight of the paper.把兩個紙圓拿在手上。黑色的拿起來輕很多，看起來卻可能比較重。視覺重量不是紙的重量。

The screen shows gray by giving off light, and your paper shows it by reflecting the light in the room. Real paper may look a little different from the gray on the screen, so trust what you see on the desk.
螢幕上的灰色是螢幕自己發出的光，紙上的灰色是紙反射教室裡的光。真的紙看起來可能和螢幕上的灰不太一樣，請相信你在桌上看到的。
