Why Do Drawn Faces Look Wrong?為什麼畫的臉常常怪怪的？

Most people draw the eyes too high. Turn a simple 3D head, switch on its proportion lines, and find out how much head there really is above the eyes.
大多數人畫臉時，都把眼睛畫得太高。轉一轉一顆簡單的 3D 頭像，打開比例線，看看眼睛上面其實還有多大一塊頭。

Big idea · 一句話看懂

When we draw a face, we often draw what we think a face is like, not what we see. The most common slip is putting the eyes too high. On an adult head, the eyes sit about halfway between the top of the head and the chin. Hair hides the top of the skull, which makes the forehead look smaller than it is. Proportions like these are a starting point for drawing, not a rule for how a face should look. Every face is different.
畫臉的時候，我們常常畫的是自己「以為」的臉，而不是眼睛看到的臉。最常見的失誤，是把眼睛放得太高。成人的頭上，眼睛大約在頭頂和下巴的正中間。頭髮蓋住了頭頂，讓額頭看起來比實際小。這些比例是畫畫的起點，不是臉「應該」長什麼樣子的規定。每一張臉都不一樣。

Reading · 英文閱讀

8

Unit 8: Why Do Drawn Faces Look Wrong?

Ask a friend to draw a face from memory. Very often, the eyes end up high on the head, with only a thin strip of forehead above them. The nose and mouth are fine, and each part looks right on its own. Yet the whole face looks a little strange, as if the top of the head had been sliced off.

請朋友憑記憶畫一張臉。很多時候，眼睛會被放在頭的高處，上面只剩下一條細細的額頭。鼻子和嘴巴都沒問題，每個部分單獨看都對，整張臉卻有點怪，好像頭頂被削掉了一塊。

Scientists have tested this. In 2014 two researchers asked many people to draw faces and found that the eyes were placed too high again and again. They even found the same slip in pictures of faces printed in well-known research articles. Knowing a lot about faces does not stop it.

科學家測試過這件事。2014 年，兩位研究者請許多人畫臉，發現眼睛一次又一次被放得太高。他們甚至在著名研究論文裡刊出的人臉圖上，也看到同樣的失誤。對臉懂得很多，也避免不了。

So where do the eyes go? On an adult head, they sit about halfway between the top of the head and the chin. One university drawing guide adds a few more starting points. The bottom of the nose is about halfway between the eyes and the chin. The mouth is about halfway between the nose and the chin. The ears reach from the eyebrows down to the bottom of the nose.

那眼睛到底該在哪裡？成人的頭上，眼睛大約在頭頂和下巴的正中間。一份大學的繪畫講義還給了幾個起點：鼻子底大約在眼睛和下巴的正中間；嘴巴大約在鼻子和下巴的正中間；耳朵從眉毛的高度延伸到鼻子底的高度。

Hair makes this harder to see. It covers the top of the skull and often part of the forehead, so the face seems to start lower than it really does. Hair also adds height on top. When you measure, imagine the shape of the skull under the hair.

頭髮讓這件事更難看出來。頭髮蓋住頭頂，常常也蓋住一部分額頭，所以臉看起來好像從比較低的地方才開始。頭髮還會讓頭頂多出一些高度。量的時候，要想像頭髮底下頭骨的形狀。

Artists have studied proportions for a very long time. The Roman writer Vitruvius divided the face into three equal parts: chin to nose, nose to eyebrows, and eyebrows to hairline. About 1490 Leonardo da Vinci drew his famous Vitruvian Man and wrote measurements beside it. In 1528 the German artist Albrecht Dürer published four books on human proportion, based partly on measuring two to three hundred people.

藝術家研究比例已經很久了。羅馬的作家維特魯威把臉分成一樣長的三段：下巴到鼻子、鼻子到眉毛、眉毛到髮際線。大約 1490 年，達文西畫了著名的〈維特魯威人〉，並在旁邊寫下各種尺寸。1528 年，德國畫家杜勒出版了四冊談人體比例的書，部分根據的是他量過的兩、三百個人。

But no real face follows the rules exactly. When scientists measured the faces of 153 young adults in North America, the old rules did not describe the average face. A baby's head has a very large skull compared with its small face. Proportions are a tool to help you see. The face in front of you is always the final guide.

不過，沒有一張真實的臉會剛好符合這些規則。科學家測量了北美洲 153 位年輕人的臉，發現這些古老的規則都不能代表平均的臉。嬰兒的頭骨和他小小的臉比起來非常大。比例是幫助你「看見」的工具；眼前那張真實的臉，永遠是最後的依據。

Questions · 閱讀理解

Where do the eyes sit on an adult head?

About halfway between the top of the head and the chin.

Why can hair make it hard to place the eyes?

Hair covers the top of the skull and part of the forehead, so the face seems to start lower than it really does, and the head looks taller.

Do real faces follow the proportion rules exactly?

No. When scientists measured real faces, the old rules did not describe the average face. Proportions are a starting point, and every face is different.

Stories Behind the Face · 臉背後的故事

Three equal parts, a man in a circle, three hundred people, and a test of our drawings 三等分的臉、圓圈裡的人、三百個人，以及對我們的畫的一次測驗

Four short stories about how people have measured faces, and what they found.
四個小故事：人怎麼量臉，又發現了什麼。

Ancient Rome · 古羅馬

Three equal parts三等分

The Roman architect Vitruvius wrote that the face can be measured in three equal parts. From the bottom of the chin to the underside of the nostrils is one third. The nose up to a line between the eyebrows is the same. From there to the lowest roots of the hair is also a third, the forehead.
羅馬的建築師維特魯威寫道，臉可以分成一樣長的三段：從下巴底部到鼻孔下方是三分之一；鼻子到兩眉之間的線也一樣長；從那裡到髮根最低的地方，也是三分之一，也就是額頭。

About 1490 · 大約一四九〇年

The Vitruvian Man〈維特魯威人〉

Leonardo da Vinci drew a man standing inside a circle and a square, and wrote measurements around him. One says that from the hairline to the bottom of the chin is a tenth of a man's height. Leonardo started from Vitruvius, but he also corrected some of the ancient measurements, especially for the head.
達文西畫了一個站在圓形和正方形裡的人，並在四周寫下尺寸。其中一條說，從髮際線到下巴底部，是一個人身高的十分之一。達文西以維特魯威為起點，但也修正了一些古代的尺寸，尤其是頭部的。

1528 · 德國

Dürer measures people杜勒量人

The German artist Albrecht Dürer wrote Four Books on Human Proportion, published in 1528, shortly after his death. He did not rely only on old rules. In his own words, he based his figures partly on “two to three hundred living persons.” Even so, the first book alone shows five different types of men and five of women, not one ideal body.
德國畫家杜勒寫了《人體比例四書》，在 1528 年他去世後不久出版。他不只靠古老的規則；用他自己的話說，他的人像部分是根據「兩、三百個活生生的人」。即使如此，光是第一冊就畫了五種男性和五種女性的體型，而不是只有一種理想的身體。

Science · 科學

Testing the old rules, and our drawings檢驗古老的規則，也檢驗我們的畫

In 1985 a team measured the faces of 153 young adults in North America and found that the classical rules do not represent average facial proportions. In 2014 two psychologists asked people to draw faces and found that the eye position was always higher than in reality. Rules can be wrong, and so can our memory of a face.
1985 年，一個研究團隊測量了北美洲 153 位年輕人的臉，發現古典的比例規則並不能代表平均的臉。2014 年，兩位心理學家請人畫臉，發現畫裡眼睛的位置總是比實際高。規則可能是錯的，我們對臉的記憶也可能是錯的。

Myth vs. Fact · 常見迷思

Four things people get wrong 四個常見的誤會

✗ Myth · 迷思The eyes are near the top of the head.眼睛在頭的上方。

✓ Fact · 事實On an adult head they are about halfway between the top of the head and the chin. There is a lot of head above the eyes, much of it hidden by hair.成人的眼睛大約在頭頂和下巴的正中間。眼睛上面還有一大塊頭，很多被頭髮蓋住了。

✗ Myth · 迷思If you know a lot about faces, you will draw them right.對臉很了解的人，就會把臉畫對。

✓ Fact · 事實Researchers found the eyes drawn too high even in face pictures printed in well-known research articles about faces.研究者發現，就連著名的人臉研究論文裡刊出的人臉圖，眼睛也畫得太高。

✗ Myth · 迷思There is one correct set of face proportions.臉的比例有一套「正確答案」。

✓ Fact · 事實Proportions are averages and starting points. Measurements of real people show that faces vary, and the old rules do not even describe the average face.比例是平均值，也是起點。測量真人的結果顯示每張臉都不一樣，古老的規則連平均的臉都描述不了。

✗ Myth · 迷思Children's faces have the same proportions as adults' faces.小孩的臉和大人的臉比例一樣。

✓ Fact · 事實A baby's skull is very large compared with its face. Faces change as people grow, so an adult guide does not fit a baby.嬰兒的頭骨和臉比起來非常大。人長大的過程中臉會改變，所以成人的比例不適用於嬰兒。

Before You Start · 動手之前

Measuring faces kindly 友善地量臉

  • Ask before you measure someone's face, and let them say no. You can always measure your own face in a mirror instead.量別人的臉之前要先問，對方可以拒絕。你也可以改成對著鏡子量自己的臉。
  • Hold the pencil at arm's length and never bring it close to anyone's eyes, including your own.鉛筆要拿在手臂伸直的地方，絕對不要靠近任何人的眼睛，包括你自己的。
  • Talk about drawings, not about people's looks. Every face is different, and no face is “wrong.”討論的是畫，不是別人的長相。每張臉都不一樣，沒有哪張臉是「錯的」。
  • Use a mirror that is not cracked, and hold it with both hands or stand it on the desk.使用沒有裂痕的鏡子，用雙手拿好，或把它立在桌上。

Classroom Activity 1 · 課堂活動一

Measure With a Pencil 用鉛筆量

You need · 準備材料

  • A pencil一枝鉛筆
  • A mirror, or a classmate who agrees to sit for you一面鏡子，或一位同意當模特兒的同學
  • Paper for notes記錄用的紙

Steps · 步驟

  1. Hold the pencil straight up at arm's length, with one eye closed. Line up the tip with the top of the head.閉起一隻眼睛，把鉛筆直直拿在手臂伸直的地方，讓筆尖對齊頭頂。
  2. Slide your thumb down the pencil until it lines up with the chin. That is the height of the head.大拇指沿著鉛筆往下滑，對齊下巴。這一段就是頭的高度。
  3. Keeping your arm straight, check where the eyes are. Are they at the halfway point of that length, above it, or below it?手臂保持伸直，看看眼睛在哪裡：在這段長度的一半、比一半高，還是比一半低？
  4. Do the same for the bottom of the nose and the mouth. Write down what you find.用同樣的方法量鼻子底和嘴巴，把結果寫下來。
  5. Compare with three classmates' notes. Do all the faces match the model on this page?和三位同學的紀錄比一比。每張臉都和這一頁的模型一樣嗎？

With hair, you have to guess where the top of the skull is. Gently pressing the hair flat, on your own head, helps. You will probably find the faces differ a little, and that is the real lesson.
有頭髮的時候，要猜頭骨的頂在哪裡。在自己頭上輕輕把頭髮壓平會有幫助。你大概會發現每張臉都有一點不同，這才是真正要學的事。

Classroom Activity 2 · 課堂活動二

Two Self-Portraits 兩張自畫像

You need · 準備材料

  • A mirror一面鏡子
  • Two sheets of paper and a pencil兩張紙和一枝鉛筆
  • A ruler一把尺

Steps · 步驟

  1. On the first sheet, draw your face from memory, without looking in the mirror.在第一張紙上，不看鏡子，憑記憶畫自己的臉。
  2. On the second sheet, draw an egg shape for the head. With the ruler, mark the halfway line for the eyes, then halve again for the nose and the mouth.在第二張紙上畫一個蛋形當作頭。用尺標出一半的線放眼睛，再對半找出鼻子和嘴巴的位置。
  3. Look in the mirror and draw your face on the second sheet, using the lines as a start and changing them where your own face is different.看著鏡子，在第二張紙上畫自己的臉。以那些線為起點，你的臉哪裡不一樣就照著改。
  4. Put the two drawings side by side. Measure where the eyes are on each one.把兩張畫並排，量量每一張的眼睛在哪裡。
  5. Write one sentence about what changed.寫一句話，說說有什麼改變。

Compare your paper drawings with the head on this page. The screen shows a smooth gray model that gives off light, but your mirror shows a real face lit by the room. Trust the mirror.
把紙上的畫和這一頁的頭像比一比。螢幕上是一顆會發光、光滑的灰色模型，鏡子裡則是被教室燈光照亮的真實的臉。請相信鏡子。
