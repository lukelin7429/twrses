Where Do Paint Colors Come From?顏料的顏色從哪裡來？

Paint is colored powder plus something sticky. Pick a pigment from a stone, an insect, a plant, or a factory, mix it with a binder, and see which paint you get.
顏料是有顏色的粉，加上會黏的東西。從石頭、昆蟲、植物或工廠挑一種色粉，配上一種黏著劑，看看你做出哪一種顏料。

Big idea · 一句話看懂

Paint has two main parts. The pigment is a fine colored powder that does not dissolve. The binder holds the powder together and sticks it to the paper, wood, or wall. Mix the same pigment with gum arabic and you get watercolor; with egg yolk, egg tempera; with linseed oil, oil paint. Pigments come from the earth, from living things, or from factories.
顏料有兩個主要部分。色料是有顏色、不會溶解的細粉；黏著劑把粉黏在一起，再黏到紙、木板或牆上。同一種色粉配阿拉伯膠就是水彩，配蛋黃就是蛋彩，配亞麻仁油就是油畫顏料。色粉的來源有土和礦石、生物，還有工廠。

Unit 9: Where Do Paint Colors Come From?

Open a box of paints and every color looks ready to use. But each one began as a powder. A pigment is a fine colored powder that does not dissolve in water. That is the difference between a pigment and a dye. A dye dissolves, like the color in a cup of tea, but a pigment stays as tiny solid grains.

打開一盒顏料，每個顏色看起來都可以直接用。可是每一種，一開始都是粉。色料是有顏色、不會溶於水的細粉，這就是色料和染料的不同：染料會溶解，像茶水裡的顏色；色料則一直是一顆一顆很小的固體顆粒。

Powder alone will not stay on paper. It brushes off. So painters mix the pigment with a binder, something sticky that holds the grains together and glues them to the surface. The National Gallery in London puts it simply: paint is made by mixing a pigment with a medium that binds the particles together.

光有粉，是留不住在紙上的，一擦就掉。所以畫家把色粉和黏著劑混在一起：一種會黏的東西，把顆粒黏在一起，再黏到表面上。倫敦國家美術館說得很簡單：顏料是把色料和把顆粒黏在一起的媒劑混合而成的。

The binder decides what kind of paint you get. Watercolor uses gum arabic, a gum from acacia trees. Egg tempera uses egg yolk, and most early Italian paintings were made with it. Oil paint uses a drying oil, such as linseed oil. Acrylic paint uses an acrylic polymer. Taiwan's glue painting, 膠彩, mixes pigments with animal glue. The same pile of yellow ochre can become any of these paints.

黏著劑決定了顏料的種類。水彩用阿拉伯膠，一種金合歡樹的樹膠。蛋彩用蛋黃，早期的義大利繪畫大多是用它畫的。油畫顏料用會乾的油，例如亞麻仁油。壓克力顏料用壓克力樹脂。台灣的膠彩畫，是把顏料和動物膠調在一起。同一堆土黃色粉，可以變成上面任何一種顏料。

Where do the pigments come from? Many come from the earth. Ochre is clay colored by iron, and people were already mixing an ochre-rich paste about 100,000 years ago. Ultramarine was ground from a blue stone mined in Afghanistan, and the best of it could be worth more than gold. Azurite, called 石青 in Chinese painting, is a blue mineral that contains copper.

色粉從哪裡來？很多來自土和礦石。赭土是被鐵染色的黏土，大約十萬年前，人類就已經在調製富含赭土的糊狀物。群青是用阿富汗開採的藍色石頭磨成的，最好的群青可能比黃金還貴。中國畫的石青，是一種含銅的藍色礦物。

Other pigments come from living things. Carmine is made from cochineal, small insects that live on cactus plants in Mexico and South America. Indigo comes from the leaves of plants, and Chinese painters call it 花青. Shell white is ground from clam shells. Still other pigments are made in laboratories and factories. Prussian blue was made by accident in Berlin around 1706, and titanium white has been mass-produced since 1916.

也有色粉來自生物。胭脂紅是用胭脂蟲做的，那是一種住在墨西哥和南美洲仙人掌上的小昆蟲。靛藍來自植物的葉子，中國畫叫它花青。蛤粉是用文蛤的殼磨成的。還有一些色粉是在實驗室和工廠裡做出來的：普魯士藍大約在 1706 年於柏林被意外做出來；鈦白則從 1916 年開始大量生產。

Not every old pigment is safe. Lead white contains lead. Vermilion is made from cinnabar, which contains mercury. Gamboge, a yellow tree resin used in Chinese painting, is also poisonous. Over time, safer pigments such as titanium white took the place of some of these. Today the paints made for schools are chosen with safety in mind, but you should still wash your hands and never eat or drink while you paint.

不是每一種古老的顏料都安全。鉛白含鉛；朱砂含汞；中國畫用的藤黃是一種黃色的樹脂，也有毒。後來，比較安全的色粉（例如鈦白）漸漸取代了其中一些。今天給學校用的顏料，選的時候都考慮過安全，但畫畫時還是要洗手，也絕對不要邊畫邊吃東西或喝飲料。

Questions · 閱讀理解

What are the two main parts of paint?

The pigment, a fine colored powder, and the binder, which holds the powder together and sticks it to the surface.

How is a pigment different from a dye?

A pigment does not dissolve. It stays as tiny solid grains. A dye dissolves in a liquid.

What makes watercolor different from oil paint?

The binder. Watercolor uses gum arabic, and oil paint uses a drying oil such as linseed oil. The pigment can be the same.

Stories Behind the Colors · 顏色背後的故事

A workshop 100,000 years old, a blue worth gold, a lucky accident, and glue painting in Taiwan 十萬年前的工作坊、和黃金一樣貴的藍、一場幸運的意外，以及台灣的膠彩畫

Four short stories about where colors have come from.
四個關於顏色從哪裡來的小故事。

100,000 years ago · 十萬年前

The oldest paint workshop最古老的顏料工作坊

In Blombos Cave in South Africa, scientists found two abalone shells that had been used to mix and store a liquid paste rich in ochre about 100,000 years ago. Nobody knows exactly what it was used for. The scientists suggest decoration or protecting the skin.
在南非的布隆伯斯洞穴，科學家找到兩個鮑魚殼，大約十萬年前被用來調製、存放一種富含赭土的糊狀液體。沒有人確定它拿來做什麼；科學家推測可能是裝飾，或保護皮膚。

Middle Ages and Renaissance · 中世紀與文藝復興

A blue worth more than gold比黃金還貴的藍

Ultramarine was ground from lapis lazuli mined in Afghanistan, and it was the most brilliant and stable blue that painters had. The National Gallery in London says the best grades could be worth more than gold, and that patrons would often pay for the exact amount of ultramarine they wanted in a painting.
群青是用阿富汗開採的青金石磨成的，是當時畫家能用的最鮮豔、最穩定的藍。倫敦國家美術館說，最好的等級可能比黃金還貴，訂畫的人常常按照自己想在畫裡用多少群青來付錢。

About 1706 · 大約一七〇六年

A lucky accident in Berlin柏林的一場幸運意外

A paint maker in Berlin, Johann Jacob Diesbach, got a deep blue by accident. Prussian blue is described by the National Gallery as the first totally modern synthetic pigment made by a chemical reaction. He had been trying to make a red. Within a few years Prussian blue was on sale, and painters had a strong blue that was cheap and easy to make.
柏林一位顏料師傅迪斯巴赫，意外得到一種深藍色。倫敦國家美術館說，普魯士藍是第一種完全現代、靠化學反應做出來的合成色料。他原本想做的是紅色。沒幾年普魯士藍就開始販售，畫家有了一種濃烈、便宜又容易做的藍。

Taiwan · 台灣

Painting with glue用膠來畫

In glue painting, 膠彩畫, the pigments must be mixed with glue, which is how it got its name. Many of its colors are ground minerals and shells, such as 石青 from azurite, 石綠 from malachite, and 蛤粉 from clam shells. A Taiwanese scholar notes that Taiwan's glue painting came from Japan.
膠彩畫作畫時，顏料必須和膠水調和，所以叫做膠彩。它的許多顏色是磨碎的礦石和貝殼，例如用藍銅礦做的石青、用孔雀石做的石綠，以及用文蛤殼做的蛤粉。台灣的學者指出，台灣的膠彩畫是從日本傳來的。

Myth vs. Fact · 常見迷思

Four things people get wrong 四個常見的誤會

✗ Myth · 迷思Paint color is just colored water.顏料的顏色只是有顏色的水。

✓ Fact · 事實Most paint color comes from pigment, tiny solid grains that do not dissolve. A binder holds them together and sticks them to the surface.大多數顏料的顏色來自色料，也就是不會溶解的細小固體顆粒；黏著劑把它們黏在一起，再黏到表面上。

✗ Myth · 迷思Natural colors are always safe.天然的顏色一定安全。

✓ Fact · 事實Vermilion comes from a natural mineral that contains mercury, and gamboge is a tree resin, yet both are poisonous. Natural does not mean safe.朱砂來自含汞的天然礦物，藤黃是一種樹脂，但兩者都有毒。天然不等於安全。

✗ Myth · 迷思Each color needs its own kind of paint.每個顏色都需要自己的那一種顏料。

✓ Fact · 事實The same pigment can be made into watercolor, tempera, oil paint, or acrylic. The binder decides the kind of paint.同一種色粉可以做成水彩、蛋彩、油畫顏料或壓克力顏料；決定顏料種類的是黏著劑。

✗ Myth · 迷思All blue paint is made from the same thing.所有的藍色顏料都是同一種東西做的。

✓ Fact · 事實Ultramarine came from a stone, azurite is a copper mineral, indigo comes from a plant, and Prussian blue was made in a laboratory.群青來自石頭，石青是含銅的礦物，靛藍來自植物，普魯士藍是在實驗室裡做出來的。

Before You Start · 動手之前

Powders, paints, and clean hands 粉、顏料，和乾淨的手

  • Adults should do any mixing of powders, because fine dust can be breathed in. Students do not grind or pour powders.粉要由大人來調，因為細小的粉塵可能被吸進去。學生不磨粉，也不倒粉。
  • Keep food and drinks out of the art area, and never eat or drink while you paint.食物和飲料不要放在美術區，畫畫時絕對不要吃東西或喝飲料。
  • Wash your hands with soap and water when you finish. Do not clean your skin with paint thinners.做完要用肥皂和水洗手。不要用溶劑清潔皮膚。
  • Use only paints made for children. Historic pigments such as lead white, vermilion, and gamboge are for reading about, not for touching.只用給兒童使用的顏料。鉛白、朱砂、藤黃這些古老的色料，只能讀，不能碰。

  • Two brushes, paper, and two small dishes兩枝筆刷、紙和兩個小碟子

Steps · 步驟

  1. An adult puts a spoonful of chalk powder in each dish.由大人在每個碟子裡放一匙粉筆粉。
  2. In the first dish, mix the powder with only water. In the second, mix it with egg yolk and a few drops of water.第一個碟子只加水調；第二個碟子加蛋黃和幾滴水調。
  3. Paint a stripe of each on the paper and let both dry.兩種各在紙上刷一條，等它們乾。
  4. When they are dry, rub each stripe gently with a finger. Which one comes off?乾了以後，用手指輕輕擦每一條。哪一條會掉？
  5. Wash your hands, and write one sentence about what the egg yolk did.洗手，再寫一句話說說蛋黃做了什麼。

Anyone allergic to eggs should not handle the yolk. They can watch and do the rubbing test with a clean tissue instead.
對蛋過敏的同學不要碰蛋黃，可以在旁邊看，改用乾淨的面紙做擦拭測試。

Classroom Activity 2 · 課堂活動二

Over the Black Line 蓋過黑線

You need · 準備材料

  • Thick paper and a black permanent marker厚紙和一枝黑色油性麥克筆
  • School watercolor and school poster paint學校用的水彩和廣告顏料
  • A brush and water筆刷和水

Steps · 步驟

  1. Draw a thick black line across the paper and let it dry.在紙上畫一條粗粗的黑線，等它乾。
  2. Paint a stripe of watercolor across the line.刷一條水彩，橫跨過黑線。
  3. Next to it, paint a stripe of poster paint of a similar color across the line.在旁邊刷一條顏色差不多的廣告顏料，也橫跨過黑線。
  4. When both are dry, compare. Which one covers the line better?兩條都乾了以後比一比：哪一條把黑線蓋得比較好？
  5. Compare your results with the model on this page.把結果和這一頁的模型比一比。

The screen makes its colors with light, and your paint reflects the light in the room, so the two will not look the same. Trust what you see on the paper.
螢幕的顏色是用光做出來的，你的顏料則是反射教室裡的光，兩者看起來不會一樣。請相信你在紙上看到的。
