Art · Unit 1 · Lesson 1 · 美術 單元一 第一課

Why Do Paint and Light Mix Differently?顏料和光，混色為什麼不一樣？

Shine a red, a green, and a blue lamp on a white wall and watch the overlaps turn yellow, cyan, magenta, and white. Then swap the lamps for three sheets of paint and see the overlaps get darker.
把紅、綠、藍三盞燈打在白牆上，看重疊的地方變成黃、青、洋紅和白；再把燈換成三片顏料，看重疊的地方越來越暗。

Big idea · 一句話看懂

Light adds: every lamp you switch on sends more light to your eyes, so the mix gets brighter, and red, green, and blue together look white. Paint takes away: every layer absorbs some of the light, so the mix gets darker, and yellow, magenta, and cyan together look almost black.
光是用加的：每多開一盞燈，進到眼睛的光就多一些，所以越混越亮，紅、綠、藍加在一起看起來是白色。顏料是用減的：每多一層顏料，就多吸掉一些光，所以越混越暗，黃、洋紅、青疊在一起接近黑色。

What the paint model leaves out 顏料模型簡化了什麼

  • A screen can only make light. The sheets of paint here are drawn with red, green, and blue light, like everything else on your screen.螢幕只會發光。這裡的顏料色片，和螢幕上其他東西一樣，是用紅、綠、藍三種光畫出來的。
  • The model cuts light into just three parts: red, green, and blue. Real light is a smooth spread of colors, and a real paint absorbs a little or a lot at every point along it.模型把光只切成紅、綠、藍三段。真正的光是連續的一整片顏色，真的顏料在每一段吸收的多寡都不一樣。
  • The model treats paint as thin, clear sheets laid one over another, like colored film or printing ink. In paint that is stirred together, light meets many grains of pigment at different depths before it comes back out, so the result can differ.模型把顏料當成一片一片透明的薄片疊起來，像色片或印刷油墨。攪在一起的顏料裡，光要在不同深度碰到許多色料顆粒才出來，結果可能不一樣。
  • How much each sheet absorbs (about nine tenths of one part of the light, and a little of the other two) are numbers we chose for this page. Nobody measured them from a real paint.每一片吸掉多少光（其中一段大約九成，另外兩段各一點點），是我們為這一頁選的數字，不是從真的顏料量出來的。

Unit 1: Why Do Paint and Light Mix Differently?

Squeeze yellow, magenta, and cyan paint onto a plate and stir them together. The mix turns into a dark gray that is close to black. Now go into a dark room with three lamps, one red, one green, and one blue, and point all of them at the same place on a white wall. That place turns white. Three colors of paint make something close to black, and three colors of light make white. Why are the two results opposite?

把黃、洋紅、青三種顏料擠在盤子上攪在一起，會變成接近黑色的深灰。現在走進一間暗房，拿紅、綠、藍三盞燈，全部照在白牆上的同一個地方，那個地方變成白色。三種顏料混出接近黑的顏色，三種光卻混出白色。為什麼結果剛好相反？

Start with the lamps. A wall in a dark room sends no light to your eyes. Switch on the red lamp, and the wall reflects red light back to you. Switch on the green lamp as well, and the place where the two spots overlap now reflects red light and green light together. More light reaches your eyes, so that place looks brighter, and it looks yellow. This is called additive mixing, because every lamp adds light. Red, green, and blue are called the primary colors of light. Red and green make yellow, green and blue make cyan, and red and blue make magenta. All three together make white.

先看燈。暗房裡的牆不會把任何光送到你的眼睛。打開紅燈，牆就把紅光反射給你。再打開綠燈，兩個光斑重疊的地方同時反射紅光和綠光；進到眼睛的光變多了，所以那裡看起來更亮，而且是黃色。這叫做加法混色，因為每一盞燈都加上一些光。紅、綠、藍叫做色光三原色。紅加綠是黃，綠加藍是青，紅加藍是洋紅；三種全部加在一起是白色。

Why yellow? The answer is in your eyes. At the back of each eye are cells called cones, and there are three kinds. Each kind responds to a wide range of light, but one is most sensitive to long waves, one to medium waves, and one to short waves. Your brain works out a color from how strongly the three kinds respond. A certain mix of red and green light stimulates the cones exactly as much as yellow light does, so your eyes cannot tell the two apart.

為什麼是黃色？答案在你的眼睛裡。眼睛後方有一種叫做視錐細胞的細胞，一共三種。每一種都對很大範圍的光有反應，但一種對長波的光最敏感，一種對中波，一種對短波。大腦根據這三種細胞反應的強弱判斷出顏色。某一種比例的紅光加綠光，刺激視錐細胞的程度和黃光一模一樣，所以眼睛分不出兩者的差別。

Paint does not make light. Paint gets its color from a pigment, and a pigment absorbs some of the light that falls on it and reflects the rest. White paper reflects almost all of the light. A layer of yellow paint absorbs most of the blue light. Magenta paint absorbs mostly green light, and cyan paint absorbs mostly red light. Lay yellow over magenta and both the blue and the green are taken away, so red is what is left. Each new layer takes away more light and the mix gets darker. This is called subtractive mixing.

顏料不會發光。顏料的顏色來自色料，色料會吸收一部分照在上面的光，把其餘的反射出去。白紙幾乎把所有的光都反射出去。一層黃色顏料吸收大部分的藍光；洋紅色顏料主要吸收綠光，青色顏料主要吸收紅光。把黃色疊在洋紅上，藍光和綠光都被拿走，剩下的就是紅色。每多一層，被拿走的光就更多，混出來的顏色也更暗。這叫做減法混色。

Look again and you will see that the two sets fit together. Where two lamps overlap you get yellow, cyan, and magenta, and those are the three colors of paint in the model. Where two of those paints overlap you get red, green, and blue. A color printer uses cyan, magenta, and yellow ink for this reason. Real inks are not perfect, and the three together usually give a dark gray, so printers add black ink as well. A screen works the other way. Each pixel is usually made of three tiny lamps: one red, one green, and one blue.

再看一次，你會發現這兩組顏色剛好互相對應。兩盞燈重疊的地方是黃、青、洋紅，正是模型裡的三種顏料；其中兩種顏料重疊的地方，則是紅、綠、藍。彩色印表機用青、洋紅、黃三種墨水，就是這個道理。真正的墨水並不完美，三種疊在一起通常只是深灰，所以印表機還會加上黑色墨水。螢幕則反過來：每個像素通常由三個小小的燈組成，一個紅、一個綠、一個藍。

In art class you may have learned a different set of primary colors: red, yellow, and blue. That set is older than modern color science, and it is still used in painting and in art lessons today. Printers use cyan, magenta, and yellow. Both sets are in use, and neither can do everything, because no three real paints, inks, or lights can mix every color there is. One more thing: the paint on this page is drawn by a screen, which means it is drawn with light. Try the same mixes with real paint and see how close the model comes.

在美術課，你學到的三原色可能是另一組：紅、黃、藍。這一組比現代的色彩科學更早出現，今天的繪畫和美術課仍然在用；印刷則用青、洋紅、黃。兩組都有人用，也都不是萬能的，因為沒有任何三種真實的顏料、墨水或光，可以混出所有的顏色。還有一件事：這一頁的顏料是螢幕畫出來的，也就是用光畫出來的。請拿真的顏料混混看，看模型有多接近。
Read aloud · 課文朗讀

Questions · 閱讀理解

On a white wall, why does the place where a red spot and a green spot overlap look brighter than the red spot alone?

The overlap reflects both the red light and the green light, so more light reaches your eyes there. Light mixes by adding, and every lamp you add makes the mix brighter.

Yellow paint over cyan paint looks green. Which light did each layer take away?

The yellow layer absorbed most of the blue light and the cyan layer absorbed most of the red light. Green is the part of the light that neither layer took away.

Why can a screen not show you exactly what real paint will do?

A screen can only make light, so it draws paint with red, green, and blue light. The picture follows a simple model, and real paint behaves in more complicated ways. You have to mix real paint to find out.

In Your Eyes · 眼睛裡

Three kinds of cones三種視錐細胞

People normally have three kinds of cone cells, named L, M, and S for the long, medium, and short waves of light they are most sensitive to. Their strongest responses are at about 560, 530, and 420 nanometers. Each kind still responds to a wide range of light, and the ranges overlap. That is why three well-chosen lights are enough to make so many colors.
人通常有三種視錐細胞，依照它們最敏感的長波、中波、短波的光，叫做 L、M、S。它們反應最強的地方大約在 560、530、420 奈米。不過每一種都對很大範圍的光有反應，範圍互相重疊。所以只要三種選得好的光，就能混出很多顏色。

1861 · 一八六一年

A ribbon photographed three times拍了三次的緞帶

In 1861 the physicist James Clerk Maxwell gave a lecture about color at the Royal Institution in London. For it, the photographer Thomas Sutton photographed a tartan ribbon three times: once through a red filter, once through green, and once through blue. Put together again, the three pictures made one color image. The plates of that time hardly responded to red or green light, so the result was far from perfect. It is remembered as the first durable color photograph.
1861 年，物理學家馬克士威（James Clerk Maxwell）在倫敦的皇家研究院做了一場關於顏色的演講。攝影師薩頓（Thomas Sutton）為這場演講，把一條蘇格蘭格子緞帶拍了三次：一次透過紅色濾鏡、一次綠色、一次藍色。三張照片再合在一起，就成了一張彩色影像。當時的感光版對紅光和綠光幾乎沒有反應，所以結果離完美還很遠；後人仍把它當作第一張能保存下來的彩色照片。

In the Printer · 印表機裡

Why printers carry black ink印表機為什麼還要黑色墨水

In theory, cyan, magenta, and yellow ink together absorb all the light. Real inks are not perfect, and the three together usually come out dark gray. Black ink covers those unwanted tints in the dark parts of a picture, keeps fine details sharp, and saves the more expensive color inks when only black or gray is needed. The K in CMYK stands for that black ink.
理論上，青、洋紅、黃三種墨水疊在一起會吸掉所有的光。真正的墨水並不完美，三種疊起來通常只是深灰。黑色墨水可以蓋掉畫面暗處那些不該有的色偏、讓細節保持銳利，而且只需要黑或灰的地方，就不必用比較貴的彩色墨水。CMYK 的 K 指的就是這種黑色墨水。

In Your Class · 教室裡

Not everyone sees the same colors每個人看到的顏色不完全一樣

Some people's cones work differently, and reds and greens can be hard for them to tell apart. The U.S. National Library of Medicine says that among people of Northern European ancestry this affects about 1 in 12 males and 1 in 200 females, and fewer in almost all other groups studied. A study of school students in Kaohsiung from 2019 to 2021 found it in 3.46% of the boys and 0.14% of the girls. That is why this page writes a name or a letter beside every color.
有些人的視錐細胞運作方式不同，紅色和綠色對他們來說不容易分辨。美國國家醫學圖書館的資料說，在北歐血統的族群裡，大約每 12 位男性、每 200 位女性有一位如此，其他被研究過的族群幾乎都比較少。2019 到 2021 年高雄市中小學生的一項研究裡，男生是 3.46%，女生是 0.14%。所以這一頁的每一個顏色，旁邊都寫了名稱或字母。

Myth vs. Fact · 常見迷思

Four things people get wrong 四個常見的誤會

✗ Myth · 迷思Red, yellow, and blue are the only real primary colors.只有紅、黃、藍才是真正的三原色。

✓ Fact · 事實Several sets are in use. Light is mixed from red, green, and blue. Printing uses cyan, magenta, and yellow. Painters have long used red, yellow, and blue. No set of three real paints, inks, or lights can mix every color.通行的說法不只一套。光用紅、綠、藍來混；印刷用青、洋紅、黃；畫家長久以來用紅、黃、藍。沒有任何三種真實的顏料、墨水或光能混出所有的顏色。

✗ Myth · 迷思The more colors you mix, the brighter the result.混進去的顏色越多，結果就越鮮亮。

✓ Fact · 事實That holds for light, where each lamp adds more. With paint it runs the other way: each paint absorbs more of the light, so the mix gets darker.光是這樣，因為每一盞燈都多加一些光。顏料正好相反：每一種顏料都多吸掉一些光，所以越混越暗。

✗ Myth · 迷思A yellow patch on a screen sends out yellow light.螢幕上黃色的地方，發出的是黃光。

✓ Fact · 事實On a typical screen, the red and green parts of each pixel are lit and the blue part is dark. No part is yellow. That mix stimulates your cones the way yellow light does, so you see yellow.在一般的螢幕上，是每個像素的紅色和綠色部分亮著、藍色部分不亮，沒有任何一個部分是黃色的。這樣的組合刺激視錐細胞的方式和黃光一樣，所以你看到黃色。

✗ Myth · 迷思A shadow is always gray or black.影子一定是灰色或黑色的。

✓ Fact · 事實A shadow is a place that one light cannot reach. If other lights still reach it, you see their mix. Under red, green, and blue lamps, the shadow that blocks only the red lamp is cyan.影子是某一道光照不到的地方。如果別的光還照得到，你看到的就是那些光混出來的顏色。在紅、綠、藍三盞燈下，只擋住紅燈的影子是青色的。

Remember It · 記憶口訣

Color mixing in a sentence 一句話記住混色

Light adds, paint takes away光是加，顏料是減

More lamps, brighter. More paint, darker.
燈越多越亮，顏料越多越暗。

RGB makes white, CMY makes almost black紅綠藍變白，青洋紅黃接近黑

Three lights add up to white. Three paints take away nearly all the light.
三種光加起來是白色；三種顏料幾乎把光全部拿走。

Each paint eats one light一種顏料吃掉一種光

Yellow absorbs blue, magenta absorbs green, and cyan absorbs red.
黃吸收藍，洋紅吸收綠，青吸收紅。

Two of one set make one of the other這一組的兩個，混出那一組的一個

Red and green light make yellow. Yellow and magenta paint make red.
紅光加綠光是黃；黃色顏料加洋紅顏料是紅。

Before You Start · 動手之前

Lamps and paint 燈和顏料

  • Do not look straight into a bright lamp or flashlight, and do not point one at anyone's eyes. Look at the wall, not at the lamp.不要直視很亮的燈或手電筒，也不要拿它照別人的眼睛。看牆面，不要看燈。
  • Do not use a laser pointer for these activities. Taiwan's Bureau of Standards says a laser pointer must never be aimed at a person, least of all at the eyes, and that children under 14 should not use one.這些活動不要用雷射筆。經濟部標準檢驗局提醒：雷射筆不可對準人，尤其是眼睛，並禁止 14 歲以下兒童使用。
  • Keep food and drinks away from the paint. Do not put paint, brushes, or painted fingers in your mouth, and wash your hands when you finish.食物和飲料不要放在顏料旁邊。顏料、畫筆和沾了顏料的手指都不要放進嘴巴；畫完要洗手。
  • Before the room goes dark, clear the floor and decide where everyone will stand. Move slowly, and switch the room lights back on before you tidy up.關燈之前，先把地板清空，並決定每個人站在哪裡。慢慢走動；收拾之前先把教室的燈打開。

Classroom Activity 1 · 課堂活動一

Three Flashlights 三支手電筒

You need · 準備材料

  • Three flashlights of the same kind三支同一款的手電筒
  • Red, green, and blue cellophane (or color filters) and rubber bands紅、綠、藍三種玻璃紙（或濾色片）和橡皮筋
  • A white wall or a large sheet of white paper, in a room you can make dark一面白牆或一大張白紙，以及一間可以弄暗的教室
  • A pencil or a finger to make shadows一支鉛筆或一根手指，用來做影子

Steps · 步驟

  1. Cover each flashlight with one color of cellophane and hold it on with a rubber band. Use two layers if the color looks pale.每支手電筒各包一種顏色的玻璃紙，用橡皮筋固定。顏色太淡就包兩層。
  2. Darken the room. Shine each flashlight on the wall by itself and say its color.把教室弄暗。每支手電筒先單獨照在牆上，說出它的顏色。
  3. Overlap red and green. Before you look, say what you expect. Then try green with blue, and red with blue.讓紅光和綠光重疊。看之前先說出你的預測。再試綠加藍、紅加藍。
  4. Overlap all three. Move a flashlight nearer or farther to change how bright its spot is, until the middle looks as white as you can get it.三種光全部重疊。把某一支手電筒拿近或拿遠來改變光斑的亮度，直到中間盡量接近白色。
  5. Hold a pencil close to the wall in the white spot. Count the shadows and name their colors. Which lamp is blocked in each one?在白色光斑裡，把鉛筆拿到靠近牆面的地方。數一數有幾個影子，說出它們的顏色。每個影子裡，被擋住的是哪一支手電筒？

Cellophane is not a perfect filter, and flashlights are not equally bright, so your white may look a little pink or a little blue. That is normal. Compare what you see with the model on this page, and write down what is the same and what is different.
玻璃紙不是完美的濾色片，每支手電筒的亮度也不一樣，所以你的「白色」可能偏粉紅或偏藍，這很正常。把看到的結果和這一頁的模型比較，記下哪些一樣、哪些不一樣。

Classroom Activity 2 · 課堂活動二

The Screen Against Real Paint 螢幕和真的顏料比一比

You need · 準備材料

  • Watercolors: a yellow, a magenta or rose red, and a cyan or sky blue水彩：黃色、洋紅（或玫瑰紅）、青色（或天藍）各一
  • The red and the blue from an ordinary paint box, to compare一般顏料盒裡的紅色和藍色，用來對照
  • A brush, clean water, a plate, and white paper畫筆、清水、調色盤和白紙
  • This page, with the model switched to Paint這一頁，並把模型切換到「顏料」

Steps · 步驟

  1. In the model, look at the overlap of yellow and magenta. Write down the color name it shows. This is the prediction.在模型裡看黃色和洋紅重疊的地方，把它顯示的顏色名稱寫下來。這是「預測」。
  2. Paint a wide stripe of yellow on the paper and let it dry. Paint a stripe of magenta across it. Look at the square where they cross.在紙上畫一道寬寬的黃色，等它乾。再橫著畫一道洋紅。看兩道交叉的那一格。
  3. Do the same for yellow with cyan, for magenta with cyan, and for all three. Hold the paper beside the screen each time.黃加青、洋紅加青、三種全加，各做一次。每次都把紙拿到螢幕旁邊比較。
  4. Make a table with three columns: the mix, what the model showed, and what the paint did. Mark each row as close or not close.畫一張三欄的表：混了什麼、模型顯示什麼、顏料實際的結果。每一列註明「接近」或「不接近」。
  5. Now repeat the crossings with the ordinary red and blue in place of magenta and cyan. Which pair gives the cleaner green? The cleaner purple?把洋紅和青換成一般的紅色和藍色，再做一次交叉。哪一組混出來的綠比較乾淨？紫色呢？

Your results will not match the screen exactly, and they should not. The model leaves things out, every brand of paint is different, and a screen makes its colors with light. Finding where the model is wrong is the point of this activity.
你的結果不會和螢幕一模一樣，也不應該一樣：模型省略了一些事，每個牌子的顏料都不同，而且螢幕是用光來顯示顏色的。找出模型哪裡不準，正是這個活動的目的。
