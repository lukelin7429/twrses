Art · Unit 3 · Lesson 10 · 美術 單元三 第十課

How Are Watercolor, Oil Paint, and Ink Different?水彩、油畫、水墨有什麼不同？

One apple, painted three ways side by side. Step through watercolor, oil paint, and ink, and see why each one is painted in a different order.
同一顆蘋果，用三種方式並排畫出來。一步一步看水彩、油畫和水墨，看看為什麼每一種的畫法順序都不一樣。

Big idea · 一句話看懂

Watercolor, oil paint, and ink differ in how they treat light and water. Watercolor is semi-transparent, so the white of the paper gives the lights, and painters often work from light to dark. Oil paint covers well and dries slowly, so lighter paint can go over darker paint and changes are easy. Ink is one black, made into many grays with water, on paper that drinks it in, so a stroke cannot be taken back.
水彩、油畫和水墨，差在它們怎麼處理光和水。水彩是半透明的，亮的地方靠紙的白，畫家常常由淺畫到深。油畫顏料遮蓋力強、乾得慢，淺色可以蓋在深色上，也容易修改。水墨只有一種黑，靠水調出許多種灰，畫在會吸水的紙上，所以一筆下去就收不回來。

Unit 10: How Are Watercolor, Oil Paint, and Ink Different?

Give three painters the same apple. One uses watercolor, one uses oil paint, and one uses Chinese ink. Their pictures will look different, but the bigger difference is in how they work: what they paint first, what they paint last, and what they can change.

給三位畫家同一顆蘋果：一位用水彩，一位用油畫顏料，一位用中國的水墨。他們的畫看起來會不一樣，但更大的不同，在於他們怎麼畫：先畫什麼、最後畫什麼，以及什麼可以修改。

Watercolor is semi-transparent. Each layer lets the layers and the paper beneath it show through. So the white of the paper becomes the light in the picture, and white areas are often simply left unpainted. A pale color comes from adding more water, not white paint. Many watercolor painters work from light to dark, laying one thin wash over another.

水彩是半透明的。每一層都讓底下的層和紙透出來，所以紙的白就成了畫裡的光，白色的地方常常乾脆不塗。淡的顏色是靠多加水，不是加白色顏料。許多水彩畫家由淺畫到深，把一層薄薄的顏色疊在另一層上面。

Oil paint covers well and dries slowly. A thin layer may be dry to the touch in a few days, and a thick one takes longer. Because it stays wet, the painter can blend colors and make changes. One traditional way is to work from the darkest colors to the lightest, and a light color can be painted right over a dark one.

油畫顏料遮蓋力強，而且乾得慢。薄薄的一層可能幾天就摸起來乾了，厚的就要更久。因為顏料一直是濕的，畫家可以調和顏色、修改畫面。有一種傳統的畫法是從最暗的顏色畫到最亮，而淺的顏色可以直接蓋在深色上面。

Chinese ink painting uses black ink, made mostly of soot and glue, and water. With one black, the painter makes many grays by changing how much water is in the brush. Chinese painters speak of ink having five colors, meaning its many tones, from dark to pale and from dry to wet.

中國的水墨畫用黑墨和水；墨主要是用煙灰和膠做的。只有一種黑，畫家靠改變筆裡的水量，調出許多種灰。國畫講「墨分五色」，指的是墨色從濃到淡、從乾到濕的各種變化。

Ink painting is usually done on xuan paper, which is very absorbent. Raw xuan paper drinks in water so quickly that ink spreads and blurs. Once a stroke is painted, it cannot be changed or erased. That is why ink painting asks for so much planning and practice before the brush touches the paper.

水墨畫通常畫在宣紙上，宣紙非常會吸水。生宣紙吸水吸得很快，墨一畫上去就會暈開。筆畫一落下，就改不了也擦不掉。所以畫水墨之前，要先想好、多練習，才讓筆碰到紙。

None of these is better than the others. Each one invites a different way of thinking. A watercolor painter plans where to keep the white, an oil painter can build up and change the picture over many days, and an ink painter decides each stroke before making it. Try all three, and see which way of thinking suits you.

這三種沒有哪一種比較好，每一種都帶來不同的思考方式。水彩畫家要先想好哪裡留白；油畫家可以花很多天慢慢堆疊、修改；水墨畫家要在下筆之前就決定好每一筆。三種都試試看，看看哪一種想法最適合你。
Read aloud · 課文朗讀

Questions · 閱讀理解

In watercolor, where does the white in a picture usually come from?

From the paper itself. White areas are often left unpainted.

Why can an oil painter make changes more easily than an ink painter?

Oil paint dries slowly and covers well, so it can be blended or painted over. An ink stroke on xuan paper cannot be changed or erased.

How does an ink painter make light and dark grays with only black ink?

By changing how much water is in the brush. More water gives lighter ink, and less water gives darker ink.

Stories Behind the Brush · 筆背後的故事

Leaving the paper white, waiting days for paint to dry, five colors of ink, and paper that drinks 留白的紙、等好幾天才乾的顏料、墨分五色，以及會喝水的紙

Four short stories about how each medium shapes the way a painter works.
四個小故事：每一種媒材怎麼影響畫家的做法。

Watercolor · 水彩

The paper is the light紙就是光

The Tate in London explains that because watercolor is semi-transparent, the white of the paper gives the washes of color a natural glow, and white areas are often just left unpainted. Some artists even cover parts of the paper for a while to keep them white as they paint around them.
倫敦的泰德美術館說明，因為水彩是半透明的，紙的白讓一層層的顏色自然地發亮，白色的地方常常乾脆不塗。有些畫家甚至會先把紙的某些地方暫時遮住，讓它在四周上色時保持白色。

Oil paint · 油畫

Time to change your mind有時間改變主意

Oil paint dries by reacting with the air, not by losing water. The Tate says a thin layer can be tack-free in a few days, while thicker layers take longer, and the paint keeps hardening for decades. That slow drying lets a painter develop a picture gradually and make corrections.
油畫顏料乾，是靠和空氣起反應，不是靠水分蒸發。泰德美術館說，薄薄的一層幾天後就不黏手，厚的要更久，而且顏料會在幾十年裡持續變硬。乾得慢，讓畫家可以慢慢發展畫面、隨時修正。

Ink · 水墨

Five colors of ink墨分五色

Chinese painters say ink has five colors. The Ministry of Education dictionary explains that the five may be listed as burnt, thick, heavy, light, and clear, or as thick, light, dry, wet, and black. Either way, the idea is that one black ink, used well, can show all the tones of a thing.
國畫講「墨分五色」。《重編國語辭典修訂本》說，五色指焦、濃、重、淡、清，或濃、淡、乾、溼、黑等濃淡乾溼不同的墨色；「墨分五色」就是用墨色的濃淡乾溼，生動地表現物象的色調。

Paper · 紙

Paper that drinks會喝水的紙

Xuan paper first came from Xuancheng in Anhui. Raw xuan paper absorbs water so well that ink on it blurs, while treated xuan paper is sized with alum, which makes it stiffer and less absorbent. Painters choose the paper for the effect they want.
宣紙原產於安徽宣城。生宣紙很會吸水，墨畫上去會暈開；熟宣紙在製作時加了礬，比較硬，也比較不吸水。畫家依照想要的效果來選紙。

Myth vs. Fact · 常見迷思

Four things people get wrong 四個常見的誤會

✗ Myth · 迷思To make a watercolor lighter, add white paint.要讓水彩變淡，就加白色顏料。

✓ Fact · 事實In transparent watercolor, a paler color comes from more water, so more of the paper's white shows through. Mixing in white is a different, opaque way of painting.在透明水彩裡，淡的顏色是靠多加水，讓更多紙的白透出來。加白色是另一種不透明的畫法。

✗ Myth · 迷思Oil paint dries in an hour.油畫顏料一個小時就乾了。

✓ Fact · 事實A thin layer may take a few days to stop being sticky, thick layers take longer, and the paint keeps hardening for decades.薄薄一層可能要幾天才不黏手，厚的要更久，而且顏料會在幾十年裡持續變硬。

✗ Myth · 迷思Ink painting is just black and white.水墨畫只有黑和白。

✓ Fact · 事實One black ink makes many grays, depending on how much water is used. Chinese painters speak of ink having five colors.同一種黑墨，看水量多少，可以變出許多種灰。國畫講「墨分五色」。

✗ Myth · 迷思In ink painting, you can fix a mistake by painting over it.畫水墨畫錯了，再塗一層蓋過去就好。

✓ Fact · 事實On absorbent xuan paper, a stroke soaks in at once. Once it is painted, it cannot be changed or erased.在會吸水的宣紙上，筆畫馬上就吸進去了。一落筆，就改不了也擦不掉。

Before You Start · 動手之前

Paint, ink, and clean hands 顏料、墨，和乾淨的手

  • Oil painting often uses solvents such as turpentine and paint thinner. Children should not use them, so this lesson's activities use only watercolor and ink.畫油畫常會用到松節油、稀釋劑這類溶劑。兒童不應該使用，所以這一課的活動只用水彩和墨。
  • Ink stains clothes and skin. Wear an old shirt or an apron, and cover the table.墨會弄髒衣服和皮膚。穿舊衣服或圍裙，桌上先鋪好。
  • Never put brushes, paint, or ink in your mouth, and keep food and drinks away from the painting table.不要把筆、顏料或墨放進嘴裡；食物和飲料遠離畫桌。
  • Wash your hands with soap and water when you finish. Do not use solvents to clean your skin.畫完用肥皂和水洗手。不要用溶劑清潔皮膚。

Classroom Activity 1 · 課堂活動一

One Apple in Watercolor, One in Ink 一顆水彩蘋果，一顆水墨蘋果

You need · 準備材料

  • A real apple, or a picture of one一顆真的蘋果，或一張蘋果的照片
  • Watercolor paper, school watercolors, and a brush水彩紙、學校用的水彩和筆
  • Xuan paper (or thin, absorbent paper), black ink in a dish, and water宣紙（或很薄、會吸水的紙）、盛在碟子裡的墨，以及水

Steps · 步驟

  1. Look at the apple and find its brightest spot. On the watercolor paper, decide where that spot will be and do not paint it.看著蘋果，找出它最亮的地方。在水彩紙上決定那個點的位置，不要塗它。
  2. Paint a pale red wash over the rest of the apple. Let it dry, then add a second wash on the shadow side.在蘋果其他地方塗一層淡淡的紅。等它乾了，再在暗面疊第二層。
  3. On the xuan paper, make three test strokes: a lot of water, some water, and very little water.在宣紙上試畫三筆：水很多、水一些、水很少。
  4. Paint the ink apple with those three tones, from the palest to the darkest.用這三種深淺畫水墨蘋果，從最淡畫到最濃。
  5. Put the two apples side by side. Write down one thing that was easy and one thing that was hard in each.把兩顆蘋果並排。各寫下一件容易的事、一件困難的事。

Compare your apples with the ones on this page. The screen makes color with light, and your paper and ink reflect the light in the room, so they will look different. Trust what you see on the paper.
把你畫的蘋果和這一頁的比一比。螢幕用光做出顏色，你的紙和墨則是反射教室裡的光，看起來會不一樣。請相信你在紙上看到的。

Classroom Activity 2 · 課堂活動二

How Fast Does It Spread? 暈開得多快？

You need · 準備材料

  • One sheet of xuan paper and one sheet of ordinary drawing paper一張宣紙和一張普通的圖畫紙
  • Black ink, water, and a brush墨、水和筆
  • A ruler and a clock or timer一把尺，和時鐘或計時器

Steps · 步驟

  1. Load the brush with watery ink and touch it to the xuan paper for one second.筆沾上加了水的墨，在宣紙上點一下，停一秒。
  2. Do the same on the drawing paper.在圖畫紙上做一樣的事。
  3. After one minute, measure how wide each spot has grown.過一分鐘，量量每一個點變得多寬。
  4. Repeat with thick ink that has very little water.換成水很少的濃墨，再做一次。
  5. Which paper spreads ink more? Does the amount of water matter?哪一種紙讓墨暈得比較開？水的多少有沒有影響？

Your results will depend on your paper and your ink. Some xuan paper is treated to absorb less. Write down the kind of paper you used.
結果要看你用的紙和墨。有些宣紙經過處理，吸水比較少。請記下你用的是哪一種紙。
