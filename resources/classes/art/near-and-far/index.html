How Do Painters Show Near and Far?畫家怎麼畫出遠和近？

Five identical trees stand at five distances. Switch four cues on and off, overlap, size, position, and air, and watch a flat row of trees turn into a deep landscape.
五棵一模一樣的樹，立在五個不同的距離。把四種線索開開關關：重疊、大小、位置、空氣，看一排平平的樹怎麼變成有深度的風景。

Big idea · 一句話看懂

A painter has four simple cues for distance besides perspective lines. Near things cover far things. Far things are drawn smaller. On the ground, far things sit higher up the picture, toward the horizon. And far things look paler and bluer, because of the air between. Use none of them and the picture is flat. Use all four and it looks deep.
除了透視線，畫家還有四種簡單的線索可以表現遠近：近的東西蓋住遠的；遠的東西畫得小；在地面上，遠的東西畫得比較高、靠近地平線；遠的東西看起來比較淡、比較藍，因為中間隔著空氣。一種都不用，畫面是平的；四種都用，畫面就有了深度。

What this model leaves out 這個模型簡化了什麼

  • The way the trees fade with distance follows a curve we chose. Real air depends on the weather, the moisture and dust in it, and the light. Distant things are usually bluish, and at sunrise or sunset they can turn reddish.樹隨著距離變淡的方式，照的是我們自己選的一條曲線。真實的空氣要看天氣、空氣裡的水氣和灰塵，還有光線。遠處的東西通常偏藍；日出或日落的時候，也可能偏紅。
  • Twenty-seven meters of clear air would hardly change a tree's color. The model squeezes the effect of several kilometers into a short distance so that you can see it.27 公尺的乾淨空氣，幾乎不會改變一棵樹的顏色。模型把好幾公里才有的效果，壓縮到很短的距離裡，你才看得出來。
  • The trees are flat cut-outs of one color, with no light and shade and no small details. Far things also lose detail, and the model does not show that.這些樹是單一顏色的平面剪影，沒有明暗，也沒有細節。遠處的東西還會失去細節，這一點模型沒有表現出來。
  • The position cue here works for things standing on flat ground below your eye level. Clouds and birds above your eye level do the opposite: the far ones sit lower, toward the horizon.這裡的「位置」線索，適用於站在平地上、比眼睛低的東西。比眼睛高的雲和鳥正好相反：遠的畫得比較低，同樣是靠近地平線。

Reading · 英文閱讀

6

Unit 6: How Do Painters Show Near and Far?

Draw five trees in a row, all the same size, all the same green, all standing on one line. Your picture is flat. Nothing in it says which tree is close and which is far. Yet a landscape painter can make you feel that one hill is a short walk away and another is a day's journey. Painters do it with a small set of cues, and you already read every one of them without thinking.

畫五棵樹排成一排，一樣大、一樣綠，站在同一條線上。你的畫是平的，畫面上沒有任何東西說明哪一棵近、哪一棵遠。可是風景畫家卻能讓你覺得，這座山走幾步就到，那座山要走上一整天。畫家靠的是少少幾種線索，而每一種你其實早就會讀了，連想都不用想。

The first cue is overlap. If one tree hides part of another, the hidden one must be behind. Overlap tells you the order of things, though it cannot tell you how far apart they are. The second cue is size. When you know two trees are about the same size, the one that looks smaller seems farther away. This is the rule you met in Lesson 5: twice as far, half as tall.

第一種線索是重疊。如果一棵樹擋住另一棵的一部分，被擋住的那一棵一定在後面。重疊告訴你東西的前後順序，但說不出它們相隔多遠。第二種線索是大小。當你知道兩棵樹差不多一樣大的時候，看起來比較小的那一棵，就顯得比較遠。這是第五課遇過的規則：兩倍遠，一半高。

The third cue is position on the picture. Look at a field or a floor. The ground near your feet is at the bottom of your view, and the ground far away is higher up, close to the horizon. So a tree whose foot is drawn higher on the picture looks farther off. This works for things on the ground, below your eye level. Clouds above you do the opposite.

第三種線索是在畫面上的位置。看看一片田野或地板：腳邊的地面在視野的最下面，遠處的地面比較高，靠近地平線。所以一棵樹的底部畫得越高，看起來就越遠。這只適用於地面上、比眼睛低的東西；頭頂上的雲正好相反。

The fourth cue is the air itself. Air scatters sunlight, and that scattered light is added to everything you see through it. The more air lies between you and a hill, the paler the hill looks, and the less contrast it has. Scattered sky light usually has more blue in it, so far hills usually look bluish. Artists call this aerial perspective, or atmospheric perspective.

第四種線索是空氣本身。空氣會散射陽光，而這些被散射的光，會疊加在你透過空氣看到的每一樣東西上。你和一座山之間的空氣越多，山看起來就越淡，對比也越低。被散射的天空光通常藍色的成分比較多，所以遠山通常看起來偏藍。藝術家把這叫做空氣透視，也叫大氣透視。

Leonardo da Vinci wrote about it in his notebooks about five hundred years ago. He called it aerial perspective, because, he said, the atmosphere lets us tell how far away different buildings are. His advice to painters was plain. Paint the nearest building in its real color, and make the more distant ones less defined and bluer.

大約五百年前，達文西在筆記裡寫過這件事。他把它叫做空氣透視，因為他說，靠著大氣，我們才分得出不同的建築各有多遠。他給畫家的建議很直接：最近的建築畫它本來的顏色，比較遠的就畫得模糊一點、藍一點。

Chinese landscape painters thought about distance too. In the eleventh century the painter Guo Xi described three kinds of distance in a mountain scene: looking up from the foot of a mountain to its peak, looking from the front of a mountain to what lies behind, and looking from a near mountain across to far ones. All these painters, east and west, were solving the same puzzle. The paper is flat, and the world is not.

中國的山水畫家也思考過遠近。十一世紀的畫家郭熙說，山有三種「遠」：從山下仰望山頂，從山前看到山後，從近山望向遠山。東方和西方的這些畫家，解的是同一道難題：紙是平的，世界卻不是。
Read aloud · 課文朗讀

Questions · 閱讀理解

Overlap shows that one tree is behind another. What can it not tell you?

It cannot tell you how far apart the two trees are. Overlap gives only the order, which one is in front.

Two trees are the same real size, but one is drawn half as tall as the other. What does that tell you?

The smaller one is farther away. If they really are the same size, it is about twice as far from your eye.

Why do far hills usually look paler and bluer than near ones?

Air scatters sunlight, and that scattered light is added between you and the hills. More air means more added light, so the hills look paler. The scattered light usually has more blue in it, so they look bluish.

Stories Behind the Distance · 遠近背後的故事

Leonardo's blue mountains, Guo Xi's three distances, size that means importance, and the air above your town 達文西的藍色遠山、郭熙的三遠、代表地位的大小，和你頭頂的空氣

Four short stories about how people have noticed distance and put it into pictures.
關於人怎麼注意到遠近、又怎麼把它放進畫裡的四個小故事。

About 1500 · 大約一五〇〇年

Leonardo names it達文西替它取了名字

In his notebooks Leonardo da Vinci wrote: “There is another kind of perspective which I call Aerial Perspective, because by the atmosphere we are able to distinguish the variations in distance of different buildings.” He also asked why far mountains look blue. He argued that the air itself is not blue: it takes on that color, he wrote, from particles of moisture that catch the rays of the sun.
達文西在筆記裡寫道：「還有另一種透視，我稱它為空氣透視，因為靠著大氣，我們才分得出不同建築遠近的差別。」他也問過遠山為什麼是藍的。他認為空氣本身並不是藍色的；他寫道，空氣是因為其中的水氣微粒截住了陽光，才帶上那種顏色。

Northern Song · 北宋

Guo Xi's three distances郭熙的三遠

The painter Guo Xi wrote that a mountain has three distances. Looking up from the foot to the peak is high distance. Looking from the front of the mountain to what lies behind is deep distance. Looking from a near mountain to far ones is level distance. A Taiwanese education encyclopedia notes that one Chinese landscape often holds all three at once, because it does not keep to a single viewpoint.
郭熙在《林泉高致》裡說：「山有三遠：自山下而仰山顛，謂之『高遠』；自山前而窺山後，謂之『深遠』；自近山而望遠山，謂之『平遠』。」《教育大辭書》指出，中國山水畫不採單點透視，所以一個畫面裡往往同時具備三遠的空間表現。

Ancient Egypt · 古埃及

When bigger meant more important畫得大，是因為地位高

Size has not always stood for distance. In the art of ancient Egypt, people of higher status were sometimes drawn larger than those of lower status. This is called hierarchical proportion. Egyptian artists did use simple overlapping to show which figure was nearer. A cue is a habit shared by the artist and the viewer, and different times have used the same cue for different jobs.
大小並不是一直都代表遠近。在古埃及的藝術裡，地位高的人有時畫得比地位低的人大，這叫做階級比例。不過埃及的畫師確實用簡單的重疊，來表示哪一個人物比較近。線索是畫的人和看的人共有的習慣；不同的時代，會拿同一種線索做不同的事。

Science · 科學

Why the far hills fade遠山為什麼會變淡

Molecules of air, and larger particles such as water vapor and smoke, scatter light. This scattered sky light is laid like a thin veil over the light coming from a distant hill, and it lowers the hill's contrast. Sky light usually has more short-wavelength light in it, which is also why the sky looks blue, so distant things usually look bluish. On a hazy day the veil is thicker, and the hills fade sooner.
空氣的分子，還有水氣、煙塵這些比較大的微粒，都會散射光。這些被散射的天空光，像一層薄紗蓋在遠山傳來的光上面，讓遠山的對比降低。天空光裡短波長的光通常比較多（這也是天空看起來是藍色的原因），所以遠處的東西通常偏藍。霧霾重的日子，這層薄紗比較厚，山也就更快淡掉。

Myth vs. Fact · 常見迷思

Four things people get wrong 四個常見的誤會

✗ Myth · 迷思Far hills look blue because air is blue.遠山是藍的，因為空氣是藍色的。

✓ Fact · 事實Air scatters sunlight, and the scattered light, which usually has more blue in it, is added between you and the hills. At sunrise or sunset the added light can be reddish. Leonardo already argued that the air itself is not blue.空氣會散射陽光，被散射的光通常藍色成分比較多，疊加在你和遠山之間。日出或日落時，疊加的光也可能偏紅。達文西當年就主張，空氣本身並不是藍色的。

✗ Myth · 迷思Anything drawn higher on the picture is farther away.畫在畫面上越高的東西，就越遠。

✓ Fact · 事實That holds for things on the ground, below your eye level: far ground is nearer the horizon. Above your eye level it is reversed. A far cloud sits lower than a near one, and it too is nearer the horizon.這只適用於地面上、比眼睛低的東西：遠處的地面比較靠近地平線。比眼睛高的東西正好相反：遠的雲比近的雲低，同樣是比較靠近地平線。

✗ Myth · 迷思A bigger figure in a picture is always a nearer one.畫裡比較大的人物，一定比較近。

✓ Fact · 事實Only when the artist is using size for distance. In ancient Egyptian art, a larger figure was often a more important person. You have to know which habit the picture follows.只有在畫的人用大小來表現遠近的時候才是這樣。在古埃及的藝術裡，畫得比較大的往往是地位比較高的人。要先知道這張圖用的是哪一種習慣。

✗ Myth · 迷思Without perspective lines, a picture cannot look deep.沒有透視線，畫面就不可能有深度。

✓ Fact · 事實Overlap, size, position, and air need no ruler and no vanishing point. Landscapes with no straight lines at all can look very deep. The model on this page has no perspective lines in it.重疊、大小、位置和空氣，都不需要尺，也不需要消失點。完全沒有直線的風景，也可以看起來很深。這一頁的模型裡就沒有任何透視線。

Before You Start · 動手之前

Looking far, cutting paper 看遠方，剪紙

  • Never look at the Sun, not even through a window or through haze. Look at hills and buildings away from it.絕對不要看太陽，隔著窗戶或隔著霧霾也不行。請看離太陽遠一點的山和房子。
  • Look from a safe place: a classroom window, a balcony with a railing, or the playground. Do not lean out or climb on anything to see farther.在安全的地方看：教室的窗邊、有欄杆的陽台，或操場。不要為了看得更遠而探出身體或爬到東西上面。
  • Carry scissors closed, with the points down, and hand them to someone handle first.拿剪刀走動時要合起來、尖端朝下；遞給別人時握柄朝向對方。
  • Looking far is good for your eyes. Rest them for ten minutes after every thirty minutes of close work.看遠方對眼睛有好處。用眼每 30 分鐘，就休息 10 分鐘。

Classroom Activity 1 · 課堂活動一

Count the Layers Out of a Window 從窗戶數一數有幾層

You need · 準備材料

  • A window or a high place with a long view of hills, buildings, or rows of trees一扇窗戶或一個高處，看得到一層一層的山、房子或成排的樹
  • Paper and a pencil紙和鉛筆
  • This page, with the model's table showing這一頁，模型的表格開著

Steps · 步驟

  1. Find a view with at least three layers, such as trees close by, buildings farther off, and hills behind them.找一個至少有三層的景：例如近處的樹、遠一點的房子、更後面的山。
  2. Draw one box for each layer, nearest at the bottom. In each box write what the layer is.每一層畫一個格子，最近的放在最下面。在格子裡寫下那一層是什麼。
  3. For each layer, write one word for its color and one word for how sharp its edges are.替每一層寫下一個形容顏色的詞，和一個形容邊緣清不清楚的詞。
  4. Mark every place where one layer covers part of another.標出每一個「這一層蓋住另一層」的地方。
  5. Compare your notes with the model. Do your far layers get paler? Bluer? Look again on a hazy day and on a clear day.把你的紀錄和模型比一比。你看到的遠處，有沒有變淡？有沒有變藍？霧濛濛的日子和晴朗的日子各看一次。

You may find that your far layers are gray or brownish and hardly blue at all. That is a real result. The color of the haze depends on the weather, on what is in the air, and on where the Sun is. Write down what you see, and do not write what the model says you should see.
你可能會發現，遠處那幾層是灰的或偏褐色，幾乎不藍。這是真實的結果：霧霾的顏色要看天氣、空氣裡有什麼，還有太陽在哪裡。請寫下你看到的，不要寫模型說你「應該」看到的。

Classroom Activity 2 · 課堂活動二

A Landscape in Four Layers of Paper 用四層紙做一張風景

You need · 準備材料

  • Four sheets of paper in one color family, from dark to very pale, or white paper and one watercolor同一個色系、由深到很淺的四張紙；或白紙加一種水彩
  • A sheet of pale blue or white paper for the sky一張淡藍或白色的紙，當作天空
  • Scissors and glue剪刀和膠水

Steps · 步驟

  1. If you are using watercolor, paint four strips with more and more water, so that they go from dark to very pale. Let them dry.如果用水彩，就畫四條紙帶，水越加越多，讓顏色由深到很淡。等它們乾。
  2. Cut a row of hills from each sheet. Make the palest hills the smallest and the darkest hills the largest.從每一張紙上剪出一排山。最淡的剪得最小，最深的剪得最大。
  3. Glue the palest row highest on the sky paper. Glue each darker row a little lower, so that it covers the foot of the row behind.把最淡的那一排貼在天空紙上最高的地方。越深的貼得越低，並蓋住後面那一排的底部。
  4. Stand back. Which of the four cues did you use? Write them on the back.退後幾步看。四種線索你用了哪幾種？寫在背面。
  5. Now make a second picture that breaks one cue, for example with the palest hills in front. Show both to a classmate and ask which looks deeper.再做第二張，故意破壞其中一種線索，例如把最淡的山貼在最前面。把兩張都拿給同學看，問哪一張看起來比較深。

Compare your paper landscape with the picture on this page. The screen makes its pale colors with light, and your paper makes them with pigment or with more water, so the two will not match exactly. What matters is the order from dark to pale.
把你的紙風景和這一頁的畫比一比。螢幕的淡色是用光做出來的，你的紙是用色料或加水做出來的，兩者不會完全一樣。重要的是由深到淡的順序。
