Art · Unit 1 · Lesson 2 · 美術 單元一 第二課

How Do Colors Work Together?顏色怎麼搭配？

Pick a color on the wheel and watch one drawing change its mood as its colors change. Then find out why two squares of the very same gray can look different.
在色相環上選一個顏色，看同一張圖換了配色之後感覺怎麼變；再看看兩塊一模一樣的灰色，為什麼看起來會不一樣。

Big idea · 一句話看懂

You never see a color by itself. Neighbors on the color wheel are close to each other and look like one family. Colors on opposite sides differ the most and make each other stand out. Even the same gray looks lighter or darker depending on what is around it.
我們從來不會單獨看到一個顏色。色相環上相鄰的顏色彼此接近，看起來像一家人；對面的顏色差最多，放在一起會互相襯托。就連同一個灰色，也會因為周圍的顏色不同，看起來比較淺或比較深。

Model · 模型

A Color Wheel and One Drawing 一個色相環，和同一張圖

Click a step on the wheel to make it the main color, marked with a star. Then choose how the other colors are picked: its neighbors, the color straight across, or three colors spaced evenly. The drawing on the right takes those colors. You can also change the wheel itself, from the painter's wheel to the screen wheel. Every step has a number, and the colors in use are named on the right, so you never have to tell them apart by eye alone.
點色相環上的一格，把它設成主色（有星號的那一格）。再選其他顏色怎麼挑：它的鄰居、正對面的顏色，或平均分開的三個顏色。右邊那張圖會換上這些顏色。色相環本身也可以換：畫家的色相環，或螢幕的色相環。每一格都有編號，用到的顏色名稱列在右邊，不必只靠眼睛分辨。

Colors in this scheme · 這個配色的顏色

    How far the main color is from the others · 主色和其他顏色差多少0

    Black and white are 100 apart on this scale. Two vivid colors can be farther apart than that.在這個尺度上，黑和白相差 100；兩個很鮮豔的顏色可以差得比這更多。

    The twelve steps · 十二格的名稱

      What this model leaves out 這個模型簡化了什麼

      • The twelve swatches on the painter's wheel are screen colors chosen to stand for the names. They are light from your screen, not paint, and no paint was measured to make them.畫家的色相環上那十二個色塊，是為了代表各個名稱而選的螢幕顏色。它們是螢幕發出的光，不是顏料，也不是從任何顏料量出來的。
      • A wheel shows only hue. It cannot show how light or dark a color is, or how vivid or dull. Those need more room than a flat circle has.色相環只表示色相，沒辦法表示一個顏色有多亮多暗、多鮮豔或多混濁；那需要比一個平面的圓更多的空間。
      • Twelve steps is a choice. Newton's circle had seven colors, Goethe's had six, and other wheels have more.分成十二格是一種選擇。牛頓的色環有七個顏色，歌德的有六個，也有分得更細的色相環。
      • The number for how far apart two colors are comes from one formula. It is an estimate, and people's eyes do not all agree with it.兩個顏色「差多少」的數字來自一條公式，只是估計；每個人眼睛的感覺不一定和它一樣。

      So test what you see here with colored paper or paint. Both classroom activities show how.所以請拿色紙或顏料來驗證這裡看到的結果；做法在兩個課堂活動裡。

      Unit 2: How Do Colors Work Together?

      Cut two small squares from the same sheet of gray paper. Lay one on black paper and the other on white paper. Now they do not look the same. In 1810 the German writer Goethe noted that a gray shape on a black background looks much brighter than the same shape on white. Nothing happened to the paper. Your eyes and brain judge every color against the colors around it. This effect is called simultaneous contrast, and it is why artists think about colors in groups.

      從同一張灰色的紙上剪下兩個小方塊，一塊放在黑紙上，一塊放在白紙上。現在它們看起來不一樣了。1810 年，德國作家歌德記下：灰色的圖形放在黑色的底上，看起來比同一個圖形放在白色的底上亮得多。紙並沒有改變。眼睛和大腦判斷每一個顏色，都是拿它和周圍的顏色比較。這個現象叫做同時對比，也是藝術家總是把顏色一組一組來考慮的原因。

      To plan those groups, artists bend the colors into a circle called a color wheel. Each place on the wheel is a hue, such as red, orange, or blue. Isaac Newton put the first color wheel in his book Opticks in 1704. In 1766 the English artist Moses Harris wrote about a wheel built on red, yellow, and blue, with orange, green, and purple between them. Painters still use a wheel like that one. Screens use another wheel, built on red, green, and blue.

      為了安排這些組合，藝術家把顏色彎成一個圓，叫做色相環。環上的每一個位置是一個色相，例如紅、橙、藍。牛頓在 1704 年出版的《光學》裡畫出了第一個色環。1766 年，英國藝術家哈里斯（Moses Harris）寫到一個以紅、黃、藍為基礎的色環，中間是橙、綠、紫。畫家到今天還在用類似的色相環。螢幕用的是另一個，以紅、綠、藍為基礎。

      Colors that sit next to each other on the wheel are called analogous colors. Red, red-orange, and orange are one example. Neighbors have a lot in common. A picture that uses an analogous color scheme looks like one family of color, with little contrast between its parts.

      在色相環上相鄰的顏色叫做類似色，例如紅、紅橙、橙。鄰居之間有很多共同點。用類似色來配色的圖，看起來像同一家的顏色，各部分之間的對比很小。

      Colors on opposite sides of the wheel are called complementary colors. On the painter's wheel the pairs are red and green, yellow and purple, and blue and orange. Side by side they give a strong contrast. The French chemist Michel Eugène Chevreul studied this at the Gobelins tapestry works in Paris, where people had complained about the black dye. He found that the dye was good. The black only looked weak and reddish when deep blues surrounded it. A color, he wrote, seems to shift toward the opposite of its neighbor. He published his findings in 1839.

      在色相環上位置相對的顏色叫做互補色。在畫家的色相環上，這幾對是紅和綠、黃和紫、藍和橙。它們放在一起，對比很強。法國化學家謝弗勒（Michel Eugène Chevreul）在巴黎的戈布蘭織毯廠研究過這件事：當時有人抱怨那裡的黑色染料不好。他發現染料本身沒有問題，只是黑色被深藍色包圍的時候，看起來又淡又偏紅。他寫道：一個顏色看起來會偏向旁邊那個顏色的對面。他在 1839 年發表了這些發現。

      Which color is the opposite of red? It depends on the wheel. On the painter's wheel the answer is green. On the screen wheel the pairs are red and cyan, green and magenta, and blue and yellow. Both wheels are useful. One grew out of mixing paint and the other fits mixing light, as you saw in Lesson 1. When someone says two colors are complementary, it is fair to ask which wheel they mean.

      紅色的對面是哪一個顏色？要看是哪一個色相環。在畫家的色相環上，答案是綠色。在螢幕的色相環上，這幾對是紅和青、綠和洋紅、藍和黃。兩個色相環都有用：一個是從混顏料發展出來的，另一個適合混光，就像第一課看到的那樣。有人說兩個顏色互補的時候，可以問一句：是哪一個色相環？

      Artists also call the hues from red through yellow warm, and the hues from blue-green through blue-purple cool. People have used these words since at least the late 1700s, but they do not agree on exactly where the line falls. You may also hear that warm colors excite people and cool colors calm them. Researchers who reviewed the studies say it is too early to make strong claims like that. The rules for matching colors are guides. They are not laws, and tastes differ from person to person and from culture to culture. Use the wheel to find ideas, and then trust what you see.

      藝術家也把紅到黃的色相叫做暖色，把藍綠到藍紫的色相叫做冷色（也叫寒色）。這兩個詞至少從十八世紀後期就有人用了，不過交界線畫在哪裡，大家的說法並不一致。你可能也聽過「暖色讓人興奮、冷色讓人平靜」。整理過相關研究的學者說，現在要下這麼肯定的結論還太早。配色的規則是參考，不是定律；每個人、每個文化的喜好都不一樣。用色相環找靈感，然後相信自己看到的。

      Questions · 閱讀理解

      Two squares cut from the same gray paper look different on black and on white. What changed, and what did not?

      The squares did not change. They are the same gray. What changed is the background, and our eyes and brain judge a color against the colors around it.

      Why can two people both be right when one says the opposite of red is green and the other says it is cyan?

      They are using different color wheels. On the painter's wheel, built on red, yellow, and blue, green is across from red. On the screen wheel, built on red, green, and blue, cyan is across from red.

      A friend says, "Blue always makes people calm." What would you say?

      It is a common saying, but researchers who reviewed the studies say it is too early for strong claims like that. People and cultures differ, so it is better to say that blue is usually called a cool color.

      • Which pairs of colors count as complementary depends on the color model you use. With red, yellow, and blue as primaries, the pairs are red and green, yellow and purple, blue and orange.       Stories Behind the Wheel · 色相環背後的故事

      A circle of seven colors, a black dye, an old observation, and a third direction 七個顏色的圓、一種黑色染料、一個很老的觀察，和第三個方向

      Four short stories about how people learned to arrange colors and to notice how they change each other.
      關於人怎麼排列顏色、又怎麼注意到顏色會互相影響的四個小故事。

      1704 · 一七〇四年

      Newton bends the rainbow into a circle牛頓把彩虹彎成一個圓

      Isaac Newton's book Opticks, published in 1704, contained the first color wheel. He set the seven colors of the spectrum into a circle, in segments of unequal size that followed the pattern of a musical scale. His circle showed only the colors of the spectrum. Later wheels added the purples that join the red end to the violet end.
      牛頓 1704 年出版的《光學》裡有第一個色環。他把光譜的七個顏色排成一個圓，每一格大小不一，照的是音階的規律。他的色環只有光譜上的顏色；後來的色相環才補上把紅端和紫端接起來的那些紫紅色。

      1839 · 一八三九年

      Chevreul and the black dye謝弗勒與黑色染料

      In 1824 the chemist Michel Eugène Chevreul was put in charge of the dye works at the Gobelins factory in Paris, after complaints about its colors. He found some dyes at fault, but the black that people criticized most was first-rate. Cloth dyed with it looked weak and reddish only when deep blues or purples lay around it. He called the effect simultaneous contrast and wrote a book about it in 1839.
      1824 年，化學家謝弗勒在外界抱怨顏色有問題之後，接掌巴黎戈布蘭工廠的染坊。他發現有些染料確實不好，但被批評得最兇的黑色其實是上等的；用它染的布，只有在周圍是深藍或深紫的時候，才顯得又淡又偏紅。他把這個效果叫做同時對比，並在 1839 年寫成一本書。

      About 1,000 Years Ago · 大約一千年前

      An old observation一個很老的觀察

      The oldest known scientific note on this effect comes from the physicist Ibn al-Haytham in the eleventh century. He described spots of paint that looked almost black on a white background and paler than their true color on a black one. Goethe wrote down the same thing about gray shapes in 1810. People have been surprised by it for a very long time.
      目前所知最早寫下這個現象的科學文獻，出自十一世紀的物理學家海什木（Ibn al-Haytham）：他描述顏料的小點放在白色的底上看起來幾乎是黑的，放在黑色的底上又比原來的顏色淡。歌德在 1810 年也對灰色的圖形寫下同樣的觀察。人們為這件事感到驚訝，已經很久很久了。

      After 1900 · 一九〇〇年之後

      Munsell adds two more directions孟塞爾多加了兩個方向

      A flat wheel shows hue and nothing else. In the first decade of the twentieth century the American painter Albert Munsell built a system on three properties of color: hue, value (how light), and chroma (how strong). His hue circle has five principal hues: red, yellow, green, blue, and purple. Lesson 3 goes up and down the direction that the wheel leaves out, from light to dark.
      一個平面的色相環只能表示色相。二十世紀的頭十年，美國畫家孟塞爾（Albert Munsell）用顏色的三個性質建立了一套系統：色相、明度（多亮）和彩度（多鮮豔）。他的色相環有五個主要色相：紅、黃、綠、藍、紫。第三課會沿著色相環沒有畫出來的那個方向走：從亮到暗。

      In the Curriculum · 對應課綱

      Where this lesson fits 這一課對應的課綱

      The visual arts curriculum does not name the color wheel, complementary colors, or warm and cool colors. These are the closest entries. The links to this lesson are our reading of them.
      視覺藝術課綱的條文沒有點名色相環、互補色或寒暖色。下面是最接近的條目；它們和這一課的連結，是本教材的解讀。

        色彩感知、造形與空間的探索。

        Noticing that a color changes with its surroundings is part of sensing color.察覺一個顏色會隨周圍改變，是色彩感知的一部分。

      Myth vs. Fact · 常見迷思

      Four things people get wrong 四個常見的誤會

      ✗ Myth · 迷思The opposite of red is green, and that is the only answer.紅色的對面就是綠色，只有這一個答案。

      ✓ Fact · 事實It depends on the wheel. On the painter's wheel, built on red, yellow, and blue, green is across from red. On the screen wheel, built on red, green, and blue, the color across from red is cyan.要看是哪一個色相環。在以紅、黃、藍為基礎的畫家色相環上，紅色對面是綠色；在以紅、綠、藍為基礎的螢幕色相環上，紅色對面是青色。

      ✗ Myth · 迷思If two patches look different, they must be different colors.兩塊顏色看起來不一樣，就一定是不同的顏色。

      ✓ Fact · 事實The same gray looks lighter on black and darker on white. The patches are equal. What differs is the background, and the eye judges a color against what is around it.同一個灰色，放在黑色上看起來比較淺，放在白色上看起來比較深。兩塊其實一樣，不一樣的是底色；眼睛判斷顏色，是拿它和周圍比較。

      ✗ Myth · 迷思Science has proved that warm colors excite people and cool colors calm them.科學已經證明暖色讓人興奮、冷色讓人平靜。

      ✓ Fact · 事實Warm and cool are names artists use for two sides of the wheel. A review of the research on color and feelings says it is too early for firm claims, and one well-known early claim could not be repeated by other researchers.「暖」和「冷」是藝術家對色相環兩側的稱呼。一篇整理顏色與心理研究的回顧文章說，現在要下定論還太早；早期一個很有名的說法，後來別的研究者也無法重複出來。

      ✗ Myth · 迷思There are rules for which colors match, and breaking them is wrong.顏色怎麼配有一定的規則，不照規則就是錯。

      ✓ Fact · 事實Color schemes are guides that many artists find useful. How pleasing a combination looks also depends on the person, the place, and the culture, so no formula can decide it for everyone.配色的方法是很多藝術家覺得好用的參考。一組顏色好不好看，也和看的人、場合與文化有關，所以沒有一條公式能替所有人決定。

      Before You Start · 動手之前

      Eyes, scissors, and paint 眼睛、剪刀和顏料

      • Rest your eyes. Taiwan's Health Promotion Administration advises ten minutes of rest after every thirty minutes of close work, and at least 120 minutes outdoors each day.讓眼睛休息。國民健康署建議：用眼每 30 分鐘休息 10 分鐘，每天戶外活動 120 分鐘以上。
      • Carry scissors closed, with the points down, and hand them to someone handle first. Cut away from your other hand.拿剪刀走動時要合起來、尖端朝下；遞給別人時握柄朝向對方。剪的時候，另一隻手不要放在刀口前面。
      • Keep food and drinks away from the paint. Do not put paint or brushes in your mouth, and wash your hands when you finish.食物和飲料不要放在顏料旁邊。顏料和畫筆不要放進嘴巴；畫完要洗手。
      • Cover the desk before you paint, and let each paper dry flat before you stack it.上色之前先把桌面鋪好；每一張紙都平放晾乾之後再疊起來。

      Classroom Activity 1 · 課堂活動一

      The Same Gray, Twice 同一張灰紙，剪兩塊

      You need · 準備材料

      • One sheet of medium gray paper一張中等深淺的灰色紙
      • One sheet of black paper and one of white paper黑紙和白紙各一張
      • Colored paper in yellow and blue黃色和藍色的色紙
      • Scissors, a ruler, and a pencil剪刀、尺和鉛筆

      Steps · 步驟

      1. Cut four squares, three centimeters on each side, from the same sheet of gray paper.從同一張灰紙上剪下四個邊長三公分的正方形。
      2. Lay one square in the middle of the black paper and one in the middle of the white paper. Put the two papers side by side.一塊放在黑紙中央，一塊放在白紙中央，再把兩張紙並排。
      3. Ask five classmates which square looks darker. Write down each answer.問五位同學：哪一塊看起來比較深？把每個人的答案記下來。
      4. Now slide the two squares until they touch. Do they still look different? Write down what you see.把兩塊灰色推到互相碰在一起。它們看起來還不一樣嗎？把看到的寫下來。
      5. Repeat with the other two squares on yellow and on blue paper. Then compare your paper results with the squares on this page.用另外兩塊在黃紙和藍紙上再做一次。最後把紙上的結果和這一頁螢幕上的方塊比一比。

      Not everyone sees the effect equally strongly, and paper and screens do not behave the same way. Record what people actually say, including anyone who sees no difference.
      不是每個人看到的效果都一樣明顯，紙和螢幕的表現也不相同。請照實記下大家說的，包括說「看不出差別」的人。

      Classroom Activity 2 · 課堂活動二

      One Drawing, Three Color Schemes 一張圖，三種配色

      You need · 準備材料

      • Three copies of the same simple line drawing, such as a house and two hills同一張簡單的線稿三份，例如一間小屋和兩座山丘
      • Colored pencils, crayons, or watercolors with at least twelve colors色鉛筆、蠟筆或水彩，至少十二色
      • This page, with the painter's wheel showing這一頁，並把色相環切換到畫家的色相環

      Steps · 步驟

      1. On the wheel, pick a main color. Write down its number and name, and the names of its two neighbors.在色相環上選一個主色，寫下它的編號和名稱，以及兩個鄰居的名稱。
      2. Color the first copy with those three analogous colors. Find the closest pencils or paints you have.用這三個類似色替第一張上色；從手邊的筆或顏料裡找最接近的。
      3. Color the second copy with your main color and the color across the wheel from it.第二張用主色和它在色相環正對面的顏色上色。
      4. Color the third copy with three colors spaced evenly around the wheel.第三張用色相環上平均分開的三個顏色上色。
      5. Pin up all three. From across the room, which one do you notice first? Hold each copy beside the screen. Where do your colors differ from the model's?把三張都貼起來。從教室另一頭看，最先注意到哪一張？再把每一張拿到螢幕旁邊：你的顏色和模型的顏色，哪裡不一樣？

      Your pencils will not match the screen, and that is expected: the wheel on this page is made of light, and its swatches only stand for the color names. There is no single right answer about which copy looks best. Ask your classmates and count the votes.
      你的筆和螢幕的顏色不會一樣，這是正常的：這一頁的色相環是光做的，色塊只是代表那些顏色的名稱。哪一張最好看，沒有唯一的答案；問問同學，數一數票數。
