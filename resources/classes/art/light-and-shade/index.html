Art · Unit 1 · Lesson 3 · 美術 單元一 第三課

How Does Shading Make a Circle Look Like a Ball?明暗怎麼把圓形變成球？

Move one lamp around a ball and watch the light side, the dark side, and the shadow follow it. Then take the shading away and see the ball turn back into a flat circle.
拿一盞燈繞著一顆球移動，看亮面、暗面和影子跟著它變；再把明暗拿掉，看球變回一個平面的圓。

Big idea · 一句話看懂

An outline can only tell you that a shape is a circle. Shading tells you that it is a ball. The surface is lightest where it faces the lamp and grows darker as it turns away, and the shadow falls on the side away from the lamp. Light, shade, and shadow all follow the light source.
輪廓只能告訴你這個形狀是圓的；明暗才能告訴你它是一顆球。表面朝著燈的地方最亮，越轉離燈就越暗，影子落在背向燈的那一邊。亮面、暗面和影子，全部跟著光源走。

Point at the ball · 指著球

  • The lamp, which only marks where the light comes from
    燈：只是標出光從哪裡來
  • The dashed line: the direction of the light
    虛線：光的方向
  • The small dot: the spot whose gray is read out on the right
    小圓點：右邊讀出的是這一點的灰

The grays are screen values from 0 (black) to 255 (white). L* is a measure of lightness from 0 to 100. The ball and the table are drawn straight from the numbers, with no other lighting added.
灰色是螢幕的設定值，從 0（黑）到 255（白）。L* 是明度的量，從 0 到 100。球和桌面直接照這些數字畫出來，沒有再加別的光影。

What this model leaves out 這個模型簡化了什麼

  • The lamp is treated as a light very far away, so only its direction matters. A real lamp close to a ball lights it a little differently, and its shadow spreads out.模型把燈當成非常遠的光，所以只看方向。真正的燈離球很近的時候，照出來的樣子會有一點不同，影子也會往外張開。
  • The reflected light under the ball comes from one simple rule. The model does not follow light as it bounces around a real room.球底下的反光只用一條簡單的規則算出來；模型沒有真的去追蹤光在房間裡來回反射。
  • The edge of the shadow is drawn with one fixed softness. Real shadow edges are sharper near the object and softer farther away, and softer still when the lamp is large.影子的邊緣用固定的柔和程度來畫。真正的影子，靠近物體的地方比較清楚，越遠越模糊；燈越大，邊緣也越柔和。
  • The ball is plain gray and a little shiny. Real things have color and texture, and a matte ball has no bright highlight.這顆球是單純的灰色，表面有一點光澤。真實的東西有顏色和質感；表面不反光的球，就沒有明顯的亮點。

So set up a real ball and a real lamp and compare. Classroom Activity 1 shows how.所以請拿真的球和真的燈來比一比；做法在課堂活動一。

Reading · 英文閱讀

3

Unit 3: How Does Shading Make a Circle Look Like a Ball?

Draw a circle on a sheet of paper. It could be a coin, a plate, or a hole. Nothing in the outline tells you which. Now make one side of the circle light and let it grow darker little by little toward the other side, and add a dark patch on the ground beside it. Suddenly the circle is a ball. The outline did not change. What you added is shading, and shading is how a flat drawing shows that a thing is round.

在紙上畫一個圓。它可能是一枚硬幣、一個盤子，或是一個洞；光看輪廓，分不出是哪一個。現在讓圓的一邊亮，往另一邊一點一點變暗，再在旁邊的地面上加一塊暗暗的形狀。忽然之間，圓變成了一顆球。輪廓並沒有改變，你加上去的是明暗；平面的圖畫，就是靠明暗來表現一樣東西是圓鼓鼓的。

Shading copies what light does. Put a ball on a table and switch on one lamp. The part of the ball that faces the lamp gets the most light. As the surface curves away, the same light is spread over more of it, so it looks darker. Where the surface turns its back on the lamp, no direct light arrives at all. Every gray on the ball depends on one thing: how squarely that spot faces the light.

明暗是照著光的行為畫出來的。把一顆球放在桌上，打開一盞燈。球面朝著燈的部分，得到的光最多。表面越彎離燈，同樣多的光要攤在更大的面積上，所以看起來比較暗。到了背對燈的地方，就完全沒有直接照到的光了。球上每一處的灰，只看一件事：那一點有多正對著光。

Artists have names for the parts. The highlight is the brightest spot, where a shiny surface sends the lamp's light straight to your eye. The light side shades into the halftone, the middle grays. Then comes the core shadow, a soft dark band that begins where the direct light ends. Beyond it the shadow side is often a little lighter again. That is reflected light, which bounces up from the table onto the ball.

藝術家替這幾個部分取了名字。亮點是最亮的一小塊，有光澤的表面在那裡把燈光直接反射到你的眼睛。亮面慢慢過渡到半色調，也就是中間的灰。再過去是最暗的那一圈（英文叫 core shadow），一道柔和的暗帶，從直接的光結束的地方開始。過了這一圈，暗面常常又稍微亮一點，那是反光：光從桌面彈上來，照到球上。

The ball also blocks light from reaching the table, and that makes a cast shadow. A cast shadow always points away from the lamp. Raise the lamp and the shadow gets shorter. Lower the lamp and it stretches out, the way your own shadow does late in the afternoon. A small lamp gives a shadow with a crisp edge. The wider the light source, the more blurred the shadow becomes.

球也擋住了照向桌面的光，於是桌上有了投影，也就是影子。影子永遠指向燈的反方向。把燈舉高，影子變短；把燈放低，影子拉長，就像傍晚時你自己的影子。小小的燈照出邊緣清楚的影子；光源越寬，影子就越模糊。

You do not need hundreds of grays to draw a ball. How light or dark a color is, is called its value, and a value scale is a row of even steps from white to black. In the Munsell system, value runs from 0 for black to 10 for white. In 1907 the teacher Denman Ross described a scale of nine values. With only five steps of pencil gray, placed in the right places, a circle already looks round.

畫一顆球，不需要幾百種灰。一個顏色有多亮或多暗，叫做它的明度；明度階是從白到黑、一階一階間隔相等的一排灰。在孟塞爾系統裡，明度從 0（黑）到 10（白）。1907 年，美術教師羅斯（Denman Ross）描述過一套九階的明度。其實只要五階的鉛筆灰，放在對的位置，一個圓看起來就已經是圓鼓鼓的了。

Painters have used light and shade this way for centuries. The Italian word chiaroscuro means light-dark, and it describes clear contrasts of tone that suggest the volume of the things in a painting. Leonardo da Vinci and Caravaggio are famous for it. You can see the same thing in the night sky. The Sun always lights half of the Moon, like one lamp shining on one ball, and what we call the phases are our changing view of that sunlit half.

幾百年來，畫家一直這樣運用明與暗。義大利文 chiaroscuro 的意思是「明—暗」，指的是用清楚的明暗對比來表現畫中物體的體積；達文西和卡拉瓦喬都以此聞名。同樣的事，在夜空中也看得到：太陽永遠照亮月球的一半，就像一盞燈照著一顆球；我們說的月相，就是從地球上看到的那一半亮面一直在改變。
Read aloud · 課文朗讀

Questions · 閱讀理解

A drawn circle and a drawn ball have the same outline. What makes one of them look round?

Shading. The ball is lightest where it faces the light and gets darker as its surface turns away, and it has a cast shadow beside it. The flat circle is one even gray.

You see a ball whose shadow is very long and points to the right. Where is the lamp?

On the left, and low. A cast shadow points away from the lamp, and a low lamp makes a long shadow.

Why is the shadow side of a ball on a white table usually not completely black?

Light bounces off the table and other things nearby and reaches the shadow side. Artists call this reflected light.

Stories Behind the Shadows · 明暗背後的故事

A word for light-dark, a smoky edge, a ball in the sky, and nine grays 「明—暗」這個詞、煙霧一樣的邊緣、天上的一顆球，和九階灰

Four short stories about how people have used and studied light and shade.
關於人怎麼運用、怎麼研究明與暗的四個小故事。

A Word from Italy · 來自義大利的詞

Chiaroscuro明暗對比法

Chiaroscuro is an Italian term that literally means light-dark. The National Gallery in London uses it for clear contrasts of tone that suggest the volume and modeling of the things in a painting, and names Leonardo da Vinci and Caravaggio as artists famed for it. In Caravaggio's late paintings, faces and hands are lit against a setting that is mostly dark. That stronger kind has its own name, tenebrism.
chiaroscuro 是義大利文，字面的意思是「明—暗」。倫敦國家美術館用這個詞指清楚的明暗對比，用來表現畫中物體的體積與立體感，並舉達文西和卡拉瓦喬為以此聞名的畫家。卡拉瓦喬晚期的畫裡，臉和手被照亮，周圍大部分是暗的；這種更強烈的做法另有一個名稱，叫暗色調主義（tenebrism）。

Leonardo · 達文西

Painting without lines不用線條來畫

Look at the edge of the ball in the model. There is no line around it. One gray simply meets another. Leonardo da Vinci advised painting without lines. The soft, smoky way he blurred his contours is called sfumato: figures emerge from a dark background through gradual changes of tone, with no harsh outlines.
看看模型裡那顆球的邊緣：它周圍並沒有一條線，只是一種灰碰到另一種灰。達文西主張畫畫「不用線條」。他把輪廓弄得柔和、像煙霧一樣的畫法，叫做暈塗法（sfumato）：人物靠著一點一點變化的明暗，從暗暗的背景裡浮現出來，沒有生硬的輪廓線。

In the Sky · 天上

The Moon is a ball lit by one lamp月球就是一盞燈照著的一顆球

The Sun always lights half of the Moon while the other half stays dark. The line between lunar day and night is called the terminator, and long shadows there make craters and mountains easy to see. The dark part of a crescent Moon is sometimes faintly visible too. NASA calls that earthshine: sunlight that reflects off the Earth onto the Moon. It is the same reflected light you see under the ball.
太陽永遠照亮月球的一半，另一半是暗的。月面上白天和黑夜的分界線叫做明暗界線（terminator）；那裡的影子很長，坑洞和山脈特別容易看清楚。彎彎的月亮暗的那一部分，有時候也隱約看得見。NASA 把它叫做地照（earthshine）：陽光先從地球反射到月球上。這和你在球底下看到的反光，是同一回事。

1907 · 一九〇七年

Nine grays, or eleven九階灰，或十一階

How many steps should a value scale have? In A Theory of Pure Design, published in 1907, the American teacher Denman Ross set out a scale of nine neutral values, with white the highest and black the lowest. Albert Munsell's system, from the same years, numbers value from 0 for black to 10 for white. The number of steps is a choice. Seeing the steps clearly is the skill.
明度階應該分成幾階？美國美術教師羅斯在 1907 年出版的《A Theory of Pure Design》裡，列出一套九階的中性明度：白最高、黑最低。同一個年代，孟塞爾的系統把明度從 0（黑）編到 10（白）。分幾階是一種選擇；能把每一階看清楚，才是真正要練的功夫。

Myth vs. Fact · 常見迷思

Four things people get wrong 四個常見的誤會

✗ Myth · 迷思To make a drawing look real, you need a strong, dark outline.要畫得像真的，輪廓線要畫得又黑又清楚。

✓ Fact · 事實An outline shows only the shape of the edge. A ball looks round because of its shading and its cast shadow. Real objects have no line around them at all.輪廓線只表現邊緣的形狀。球看起來圓鼓鼓的，靠的是明暗和投影。真實的物體周圍，其實根本沒有一條線。

✗ Myth · 迷思The darkest part of a ball is the part farthest from the lamp.球上最暗的地方，就是離燈最遠的地方。

✓ Fact · 事實The darkest part is often a band just past the edge of the direct light, called the core shadow. Farther around, reflected light from the table makes the surface a little lighter again.最暗的地方，往往是直接的光剛結束的那一圈（core shadow）。再往後，桌面彈上來的反光又讓表面稍微亮了一點。

✗ Myth · 迷思A shadow is solid black.影子是全黑的。

✓ Fact · 事實A shadow is a place that direct light does not reach. Light reflected from other surfaces usually still gets in, and when the light source is wide, the edge of the shadow is blurred.影子是直接的光照不到的地方。別的表面反射過來的光通常還是照得進去；光源比較寬的時候，影子的邊緣也是模糊的。

✗ Myth · 迷思Pencil lead is made of lead.鉛筆芯是鉛做的。

✓ Fact · 事實Pencil lead is not lead. Most pencil cores are made of graphite powder mixed with clay. That is still no reason to put a pencil in your mouth.鉛筆的「鉛」不是鉛。大多數鉛筆芯是石墨粉加黏土做成的。不過這不代表可以把鉛筆放進嘴巴。

Remember It · 記憶口訣

Light and shade in a sentence 一句話記住明暗

Face the light, be light朝著光，就亮

A surface is lightest where it faces the lamp and darker as it turns away.
表面朝著燈的地方最亮，越轉離燈越暗。

Shadows point away影子指向另一邊

The cast shadow always lies on the side away from the lamp.
投影永遠落在背向燈的那一邊。

Low lamp, long shadow燈越低，影子越長

High lamp, short shadow. Think of noon and late afternoon.
燈越高，影子越短。想想正午和傍晚。

Dark has light in it暗裡面還有亮

Look for reflected light on the shadow side before you fill it in black.
把暗面塗黑之前，先找找裡面的反光。

Before You Start · 動手之前

Lamps, pencils, and a dark room 燈、鉛筆，和暗暗的教室

  • Do not look straight into the lamp, and do not point it at anyone's eyes. Look at the ball and the shadow.不要直視燈，也不要拿燈照別人的眼睛。看球和影子就好。
  • A lamp bulb can get hot. Let an adult move it, or wait until it has cooled. Keep the cord out of the way so nobody trips.燈泡可能會燙。請大人移動它，或等它涼了再碰。電線收好，免得有人絆倒。
  • Pencil lead is graphite, not lead, but pencils still do not belong in your mouth. Wash the graphite off your hands when you finish.鉛筆芯是石墨，不是鉛，但鉛筆還是不該放進嘴巴。畫完把手上的石墨洗掉。
  • Before the room goes dark, clear the floor. Rest your eyes too: ten minutes after every thirty minutes of close work.關燈之前先把地板清空。也要讓眼睛休息：用眼每 30 分鐘休息 10 分鐘。

Classroom Activity 1 · 課堂活動一

A Real Ball and a Real Lamp 真的球，真的燈

You need · 準備材料

  • A plain, light-colored ball, such as a table tennis ball or a foam ball一顆素色、淺色的球，例如桌球或保麗龍球
  • A desk lamp or a flashlight, in a room you can make dim一盞檯燈或一支手電筒，以及一間可以弄暗的教室
  • A sheet of white paper and a sheet of black paper to stand the ball on白紙和黑紙各一張，用來墊在球下面
  • This page, with the model set to Ball這一頁，模型設在「球」

Steps · 步驟

  1. Put the ball on the white paper. Dim the room and shine the lamp on the ball from the upper left.把球放在白紙上。把教室弄暗，從左上方用燈照球。
  2. Find the lightest spot, the darkest band, and the cast shadow. Point to each one and say its name.找出最亮的地方、最暗的那一圈和投影。一個一個指出來，說出名稱。
  3. Set the lamp in the model to about the same place. What is the same on the screen and on your desk? What is different?把模型裡的燈調到差不多的位置。螢幕上和桌上，哪些一樣？哪些不一樣？
  4. Look at the shadow side near the table. Now slide the black paper under the ball in place of the white. Does that part get darker?看球的暗面靠近桌面的地方。把白紙換成黑紙墊在球下面，那個地方有沒有變暗？
  5. Move the lamp lower, then higher. Measure the shadow each time with a ruler and write the numbers down.把燈放低，再舉高。每一次都用尺量影子的長度，把數字記下來。

Your shadow will not match the model exactly. The model treats the lamp as very far away, and a real lamp is close and has some width, so its shadow spreads and its edge is soft. A matte ball may have no bright highlight at all. Finding these differences is the point.
你的影子不會和模型一模一樣。模型把燈當成非常遠，真的燈很近、又有寬度，所以影子會張開、邊緣是柔和的；表面不反光的球，可能根本沒有明顯的亮點。找出這些差別，正是這個活動的目的。

Classroom Activity 2 · 課堂活動二

Five Grays, One Ball 五階灰，畫一顆球

You need · 準備材料

  • A soft pencil, such as a 2B, and white drawing paper一支軟的鉛筆（例如 2B）和白色的圖畫紙
  • An eraser and a ruler橡皮擦和尺
  • The ball and lamp from Activity 1, or the model set to Pencil steps with 5 steps活動一的球和燈，或把模型設在「分階」、五階

Steps · 步驟

  1. Draw a row of five boxes. Leave the first one white and make the last one as dark as your pencil will go.畫一排五個格子。第一格留白，最後一格塗到這支鉛筆能塗的最黑。
  2. Fill the three boxes between them so that each step is the same size. This is your value scale.把中間三格塗上灰，讓每一階的差距一樣大。這就是你的明度階。
  3. Draw a light circle. Mark where the light comes from with a small arrow outside it.輕輕畫一個圓，在圓的外面用小箭頭標出光從哪裡來。
  4. Shade the ball using only your five grays: the lightest toward the arrow, the darkest in a band on the far side, and a slightly lighter gray below that band.只用你的五階灰替球畫上明暗：最亮的朝著箭頭，最暗的在另一側畫成一圈，那一圈下面再用稍微亮一點的灰。
  5. Add the cast shadow on the side away from the arrow. Hold your drawing beside the screen and compare.在背向箭頭的那一邊畫上投影。把你的畫拿到螢幕旁邊比一比。

Squint at the ball until the small details blur. Squinting makes the big areas of light and dark easier to sort. If your drawing looks flat, the usual reason is that the darkest gray is not dark enough.
瞇起眼睛看球，直到小細節變模糊；這樣比較容易分出大塊的亮和暗。如果畫出來扁扁的，最常見的原因是最暗的那一階不夠暗。
