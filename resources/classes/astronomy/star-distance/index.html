How Far Away Are the Stars?星星離我們多遠？

Hold up your thumb and blink: it jumps. Earth plays the same trick with its orbit to measure the stars. Then find out when tonight's starlight set out, from the year you were born back to the Ming dynasty.
伸出拇指、輪流閉一隻眼，拇指會跳動。地球用繞太陽的軌道玩同一招，量出星星的距離。再看看今晚的星光是哪一年出發的：從你出生那年，一路到明朝。

Big idea · 一句話看懂

Stars are so far away that we measure them in light-years. As Earth goes around the Sun, a nearby star shifts back and forth a tiny bit against the faraway stars behind it. This shift is called parallax, and the farther away the star, the smaller the shift.
星星遠到要用「光年」來量。地球繞著太陽走，近的星星會在更遠的星空前面微微來回移動，這叫做「視差」；星星越遠，移動得越少。

Unit 10: How Far Away Are the Stars?

Look up on a clear night, and all the stars seem to sit on the same dark dome, as if they were the same distance away. They are not. Some stars are close neighbors of the Sun, and others are hundreds of times farther. The distances are so huge that kilometers are no help, so astronomers use the light-year: the distance light travels in one year, about 9.46 trillion kilometers. A light-year is a distance, not a time. Proxima Centauri, the nearest star after the Sun, is about 4.2 light-years away.

晴朗的夜晚抬頭看，所有的星星好像都貼在同一個黑色的圓頂上，跟我們一樣遠。其實不是。有些星是太陽的近鄰，有些卻遠了好幾百倍。這些距離大到用公里來量已經沒有意義，所以天文學家用「光年」：光走一年的距離，大約 9.46 兆公里。光年是距離，不是時間。除了太陽以外最近的恆星是比鄰星，大約在 4.2 光年外。

How can you measure the distance to something you can never visit? Try this. Hold up your thumb at arm's length and look at it with one eye. Then blink and look with the other eye. Your thumb seems to jump against the background. This jump is called parallax. Bring your thumb closer to your face, and it jumps more; move it farther away, and it jumps less. Your brain uses the small difference between your two eyes to judge how far away things are.

一個永遠去不了的地方，要怎麼量它有多遠？試試看：把拇指伸直到手臂那麼遠，先用一隻眼睛看，再眨眼換另一隻眼睛看。拇指好像在背景前面跳了一下。這一跳就叫做「視差」。把拇指移近臉，它跳得更多；移遠一點，它就跳得少。你的大腦就是用兩隻眼睛之間的這一點點差異，來判斷東西有多遠。

Astronomers use the same trick, but with two eyes much farther apart. Every six months, Earth reaches the opposite side of its orbit, about 300 million kilometers from where it was. Seen from these two places, a nearby star shifts a tiny bit against much more distant stars in the background. Measure the angle of that shift, draw a triangle, and you can work out the distance. The angle is tiny: even for Proxima Centauri, it is like the width of a coin seen from more than five kilometers away. Farther stars shift even less.

天文學家用的是同一招，只是兩隻「眼睛」離得遠多了。每隔半年，地球就走到軌道的另一邊，離原來的位置大約 3 億公里。從這兩個地方看，一顆近的星會在背景中遠得多的星星前面，移動一點點。量出這個位移的角度，畫一個三角形，就能算出距離。這個角度非常小：就算是比鄰星，也只像從 5 公里外看一枚硬幣的寬度。更遠的星，移動得更少。

For more than 2,000 years, people looked for this shift and could not find it. Some ancient Greek thinkers even took this as proof that Earth does not move. The shift was simply too small for their eyes. At last, in 1838, the German astronomer Friedrich Bessel measured the parallax of a faint star called 61 Cygni and found it was about 10 light-years away. Today we know it is 11.4. In 1989, the European satellite Hipparcos began making precise measurements from above the blurry air, for about 118,000 stars, and its successor, Gaia, has measured nearly 1.5 billion.

兩千多年來，人們一直在找這個位移，卻找不到。有些古希臘的思想家甚至把它當成「地球不會動」的證據。其實只是位移太小，肉眼看不出來。直到 1838 年，德國天文學家貝索量到一顆暗星「天鵝座 61」的視差，算出它大約在 10 光年外；今天我們知道是 11.4 光年。1989 年，歐洲的依巴谷衛星（Hipparcos）開始在模糊的大氣層之上做精確測量，量了大約 11.8 萬顆星；它的接班人蓋亞（Gaia）已經量了將近 15 億顆。

Because light needs time to travel, looking at the stars means looking into the past. The light from Sirius that reaches you tonight left it about eight and a half years ago. Vega's light left 25 years ago, and Betelgeuse's light began its journey around 500 years ago, during China's Ming dynasty. The poet Li Bai wrote that people today cannot see the Moon of ancient times, yet this same Moon once shone on the ancients. Starlight turns his idea around: tonight you can see light that set out while people of the past were alive.

光走路需要時間，所以看星星就是在看過去。今晚到達你眼睛的天狼星光，大約是 8 年半前出發的；織女星的光是 25 年前出發的；參宿四的光大約 500 年前、中國的明朝時就踏上了旅程。李白寫過「今人不見古時月，今月曾經照古人」。星光正好倒過來：今晚你看到的光，出發時，古時候的人還活著。

Parallax is the first step on a ladder of distances. It works well for nearby stars, but for very distant ones the shift is too small to measure well, so astronomers use other clues, such as how bright a star really is. Even some famous bright stars, like Deneb, still have uncertain distances. Use the list below to find out when tonight's starlight left home, and look for your own birthday star: a star whose light set out in the year you were born.

視差是量距離這座「梯子」的第一階。它很適合量近的星，但星太遠時位移小到量不準，天文學家就改用別的線索，例如星星本身到底有多亮。就連天津四這樣有名的亮星，距離到今天還說不準。用下面的清單查查今晚的星光是哪一年出發的，也找一找你自己的生日星：它的光，就在你出生那年出發。

Questions · 閱讀理解

What is a light-year?

It is the distance light travels in one year, about 9.46 trillion kilometers. It measures distance, not time.

Why do astronomers measure a star's position six months apart?

Six months apart, Earth is on opposite sides of its orbit, about 300 million kilometers apart. Looking from two places so far apart makes the star's shift as big as possible.

Who first measured the distance to a star, and when?

The German astronomer Friedrich Bessel, in 1838. He measured the parallax of the star 61 Cygni.

Distance and Time in the Sky 天上的距離與時間

七夕 · Qixi

Fifteen Light-Years Apart牛郎織女相隔 15 光年

盈盈一水間，脈脈不得語。——〈迢迢牽牛星〉（古詩十九首）"Just one clear stream between them, they gaze and cannot speak." (Nineteen Old Poems)

In the story, the Cowherd (Altair) and the Weaver Girl (Vega) meet once a year on a bridge of magpies. In space they are about 15 light-years apart, so even a message sent at the speed of light would take 15 years to arrive.
故事裡，牛郎（牛郎星）和織女（織女星）每年在鵲橋相會一次。在太空中，兩顆星相隔大約 15 光年：就算用光速傳訊息，也要 15 年才收得到。

李白 · Li Bai

Light from the Past今月曾經照古人

今人不見古時月，今月曾經照古人。——李白〈把酒問月〉"People today do not see the Moon of old times, yet today's Moon once shone on the people of old." (Li Bai)

Starlight turns the poem around. The light you see tonight from Betelgeuse set out about 500 years ago, during the Ming dynasty. The farther the star, the older its light.
星光把這首詩倒過來：今晚看到的參宿四星光，大約 500 年前、明朝時就出發了。星越遠，光越古老。

1838 · Bessel

The First Star Measured第一顆量到距離的星

Friedrich Bessel watched 61 Cygni for more than a year and found a parallax of about 0.3 arcseconds, smaller than the width of a hair seen from 30 meters away. It was the shift people had looked for since ancient Greece, and the first distance ever measured to a star.
貝索觀測天鵝座 61 一年多，量到大約 0.3 角秒的視差，比從 30 公尺外看一根頭髮還細。這是從古希臘就有人在找的位移，也是人類第一次量出到恆星的距離。

周髀 · Zhoubi

Measuring the Sky with Triangles用三角形量天

The ancient Chinese book Zhoubi Suanjing compared the shadows of poles in different places and worked out that the Sun was 80,000 li high. Using two viewpoints and a triangle was the right idea. The answer was wrong only because the book assumed Earth was flat.
古代的《周髀算經》比較不同地方的竿影，推算出「日高八萬里」。用兩個觀測點加上三角形，方法是對的；答案錯，只是因為書裡假設大地是平的。

Myth vs. Fact · 常見迷思

Four things people get wrong 四個常見的誤會

✗ Myth · 迷思A light-year is a measure of time.光年是時間單位。

✓ Fact · 事實A light-year is a distance: how far light travels in one year, about 9.46 trillion kilometers. It uses time to describe length, just like saying a store is "ten minutes away."光年是距離：光走一年的路程，大約 9.46 兆公里。它是用時間來描述長度，就像說「商店在十分鐘路程外」。

✗ Myth · 迷思All the stars are the same distance away, stuck on a dome.所有星星都一樣遠，貼在同一個圓頂上。

✓ Fact · 事實They only look that way because they are too far away for our eyes to judge depth. In Orion alone, Bellatrix is about 250 light-years away, Betelgeuse about 500, and Rigel about 860.看起來是這樣，只是因為它們太遠，眼睛分不出遠近。光是獵戶座：參宿五大約 250 光年，參宿四約 500 光年，參宿七約 860 光年。

✗ Myth · 迷思The brightest stars are the closest ones.越亮的星，離我們越近。

✓ Fact · 事實Within 20 light-years, Hipparcos measured 74 stars, and only 17 of them can be seen without a telescope. Most of the Sun's neighbors, including Proxima Centauri, are dim red dwarfs. Deneb shines brightly even though it is more than 1,000 light-years away, because it gives off tens of thousands of times more light than the Sun.20 光年內，依巴谷衛星量到 74 顆星，肉眼看得到的只有 17 顆。太陽的鄰居大多是暗淡的紅矮星，比鄰星也是。天津四在 1,000 多光年外仍然很亮，因為它發出的光是太陽的好幾萬倍。

✗ Myth · 迷思Many of the stars we see have already burned out.我們看到的星星，很多早就熄滅了。

✓ Fact · 事實Starlight is old, but not that old. Almost every star you can see without a telescope is within a few thousand light-years, and stars shine for millions or billions of years. Their light left them only recently, as stars go, so nearly all of them are still shining.星光很古老，但沒那麼古老。肉眼看得到的星，幾乎都在幾千光年以內，而恆星能發光幾百萬到幾十億年。以恆星的一生來說，它們的光才剛出發不久，所以幾乎都還亮著。

比鄰星、天狼星、織女星大約在 4.2、8.6、25 光年外。

Classroom Activity 1 · 課堂活動一

Measure Parallax with a Pencil 用鉛筆量視差

You need · 準備材料

  • A pencil and a tape measure一枝鉛筆、一條捲尺
  • Ten sticky notes numbered 1 to 10, stuck in a row on a wall about 10 cm apart十張寫上 1 到 10 的便利貼，在牆上排成一列，每張相隔約 10 公分
  • A worksheet with three columns: pencil distance, number of notes it jumps, and notes一張學習單，分三欄：鉛筆距離、跳過幾張便利貼、備註

Steps · 步驟

  1. Stand 3 meters from the wall. Hold the pencil upright at arm's length, in front of the sticky notes.站在離牆 3 公尺的地方，把鉛筆直立、手臂伸直，擋在便利貼前面。
  2. Close your left eye and note which number the pencil covers. Switch eyes and count how many notes it jumps.閉上左眼，記下鉛筆擋住哪個號碼；換眼睛看，數數它跳過幾張。
  3. Repeat with the pencil at half an arm's length, then as close as you can still see it clearly. Record each jump.把鉛筆移到半個手臂遠，再移到最近還看得清楚的地方，各做一次，記下跳了幾張。
  4. Discuss: when the pencil is twice as close, what happens to the jump? (It roughly doubles.) Astronomers use the same rule to turn a shift into a distance.討論：鉛筆近了一倍，跳的距離怎麼變？（大約變兩倍。）天文學家就是用這個規律，把位移換算成距離。

Teaching tip: your eyes are about 6 cm apart, while Earth's "eyes" are 300 million kilometers apart. Then open the 3D model and compare the shift of Proxima Centauri with Betelgeuse.
教學提示：我們的兩眼相距約 6 公分，地球的兩隻「眼睛」相距 3 億公里。接著打開 3D 模型，比較比鄰星和參宿四的位移。

Classroom Activity 2 · 課堂活動二

Find Your Birthday Star 找出你的生日星

You need · 準備材料

  • The birthday-star finder on this page本頁的生日星查詢
  • The sky map in Lesson 5 (tonight's sky over Changhua) or a printed star map第五課的星空圖（今晚彰化的星空），或一張印出來的星圖
  • Paper and colored pencils for a class timeline做全班時間軸用的紙和彩色鉛筆

Steps · 步驟

  1. Type your birth year into the finder. Write down your star's name, its distance, and the best month to see it.在查詢框輸入出生年份，記下你的星的名字、距離，以及最適合看它的月份。
  2. Find the star on the Lesson 5 sky map or a star map, and mark it.在第五課的星空圖或星圖上找到這顆星，做個記號。
  3. Make a class timeline: put each student's star on it, along with the year its light set out.做一條全班的時間軸：把每個人的星，連同光出發的年份，貼上去。
  4. Discuss: why will you need a different star next year? (Next year you will be a year older, so you need a star one light-year farther away.)討論：為什麼明年要換一顆星？（明年你大一歲，要找再遠一光年的星。）

Tip: distances have small errors, so any star within about half a light-year of your age counts. Parents and grandparents can find theirs too; the finder goes out to 100 light-years.
提示：距離有小小的誤差，跟你歲數相差半光年以內都算數。爸媽和爺爺奶奶也能找自己的生日星：查詢範圍到 100 光年。
