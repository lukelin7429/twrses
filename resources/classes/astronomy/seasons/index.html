Astronomy · Lesson 3 · 天文教育 第三課

Why Do We Have Seasons?為什麼會有四季？

It is the tilt, not the distance. Follow Earth around the Sun on real dates, and watch Taiwan's days grow long and short.
原因是傾斜，不是距離。跟著真實日期繞太陽一圈，看台灣的白天一天天變長、變短。

Big idea · 一句話看懂

Seasons come from the tilt of Earth's axis, not from its distance to the Sun. The tilt gives each hemisphere a higher Sun and longer days in turn.
四季來自地軸的傾斜，不是地球離太陽的遠近。傾斜讓南北半球輪流得到更高的太陽和更長的白天。

Unit 3: Why Do We Have Seasons?

Ask a room full of people why summer is hot, and many will say it is because Earth is closer to the Sun in summer. It sounds reasonable, but it cannot be right. Earth is actually closest to the Sun in early January, in the middle of winter in Taiwan. And when it is summer in Taiwan, it is winter in Australia, even though both countries are the same distance from the Sun. The real reason for the seasons is not distance. It is a tilt.

問一屋子的人夏天為什麼熱，很多人會說：因為夏天地球離太陽比較近。聽起來很合理，但它不可能是對的。地球其實在 1 月初離太陽最近，那時台灣正是冬天。而且台灣過夏天的時候，澳洲正在過冬天，兩個國家離太陽的距離明明一樣。四季真正的原因不是距離，而是一個傾斜。

Earth spins around an imaginary line through the North and South Poles, called its axis. The axis is not straight up and down compared with Earth's path around the Sun. It is tilted by about 23.5 degrees. Just as important, the axis keeps pointing in the same direction in space all year, toward the North Star, Polaris. So as Earth travels around the Sun, the Northern Hemisphere leans toward the Sun for half of the year and away from it for the other half.

地球繞著一條穿過南北極的假想線自轉，這條線叫做地軸。跟地球繞太陽的軌道相比，地軸不是直立的，而是傾斜了大約 23.5 度。同樣重要的是：地軸整年都指向太空中的同一個方向——北極星。所以地球繞太陽運行時，北半球有半年傾向太陽、另外半年背向太陽。

Leaning toward the Sun does two things. First, the Sun climbs higher in the sky. When sunlight comes from high overhead, it strikes the ground straight on, and its energy is concentrated on a small area. When the Sun is low, the same sunlight is spread across a much larger area, like a flashlight beam shining on a table at a slant. Second, the days get longer. In Changhua, the Sun is up for more than 13 and a half hours in late June but only about 10 and a half hours in late December. More hours of stronger sunlight add up to summer.

傾向太陽會帶來兩件事。第一，太陽在天空中爬得更高。陽光從高處照下來時，是正面打在地上，能量集中在一小塊面積上；太陽低的時候，同樣的陽光攤在大得多的面積上，就像手電筒斜斜照在桌上一樣。第二，白天變長了。在彰化，六月下旬太陽在天上超過 13 個半小時，十二月下旬卻只有大約 10 個半小時。更長的時間、更強的陽光，加起來就是夏天。

Four dates mark the turning points. Around June 21, the summer solstice, the Northern Hemisphere leans most toward the Sun, and at noon the Sun stands straight overhead at the Tropic of Cancer, about 23.5 degrees north of the equator. The Tropic of Cancer runs right through Taiwan, through Chiayi and Hualien, so on that day a pole standing there at noon casts almost no shadow. Around December 21, the winter solstice, the Sun is overhead at the Tropic of Capricorn, far to the south. Halfway between, around March 20 and September 23, come the equinoxes, when day and night are almost equal everywhere on Earth.

有四個日子是轉折點。6 月 21 日前後是夏至，北半球最傾向太陽，正午太陽直射北回歸線，也就是赤道以北約 23.5 度的地方。北回歸線正好穿過台灣的嘉義和花蓮，所以那天正午，豎在那裡的竿子幾乎沒有影子。12 月 21 日前後是冬至，太陽直射遙遠南方的南回歸線。兩者中間的 3 月 20 日和 9 月 23 日前後，是春分和秋分，那時全世界的晝夜幾乎一樣長。

Long before anyone knew why, Chinese farmers tracked the seasons with great care. The traditional calendar divides the Sun's yearly path into 24 solar terms, each about 15 days long, with names like Grain Rain, White Dew, and Frost's Descent. The solstices and equinoxes are four of them. Because the solar terms follow the Sun rather than the Moon, they fall on nearly the same dates every year, which made them a reliable guide for planting and harvest. In 2016, UNESCO added them to its list of the Intangible Cultural Heritage of Humanity.

早在有人知道原因之前，中國的農民就非常仔細地觀察季節。傳統曆法把太陽一年走過的路分成二十四個節氣，每個大約十五天，有穀雨、白露、霜降這樣的名字；夏至、冬至、春分、秋分就是其中四個。因為節氣跟著太陽走、不是跟著月亮走，每年幾乎落在同樣的日期，所以成了播種與收成可靠的指南。2016 年，聯合國教科文組織把二十四節氣列入人類非物質文化遺產代表作名錄。

One last puzzle: if the Sun is highest in June, why are July and August the hottest months in Taiwan? Oceans and land take weeks to warm up, the way a pot of water takes a while to boil after you turn on the stove, so the warmest weather lags behind the strongest sunlight. The same delay makes January and February colder than December. And in the Southern Hemisphere, everything is reversed: in Australia, many families spend Christmas at the beach.

最後一個謎題：如果太陽在六月最高，為什麼台灣最熱的是七、八月？因為海洋和陸地要花好幾週才會熱起來，就像鍋子裡的水，開了爐火還要等一陣子才會沸騰；所以最熱的天氣，會落後最強的陽光一段時間。同樣的延遲，也讓一、二月比十二月更冷。而在南半球，一切都反過來：在澳洲，很多家庭是在海灘上過聖誕節的。

Questions · 閱讀理解

Why can't Earth's distance from the Sun be the cause of the seasons?

Earth is closest to the Sun in early January, during winter in Taiwan, and the two hemispheres have opposite seasons even though they are the same distance from the Sun.

What are the two ways that leaning toward the Sun makes the weather warmer?

The Sun climbs higher, so its light is concentrated on a smaller area, and the days are longer, so there are more hours of sunlight.

Why are July and August hotter than June in Taiwan, even though the Sun is highest in June?

Oceans and land take weeks to warm up, so the warmest weather lags behind the strongest sunlight.

Myth vs. Fact · 常見迷思

Four things people get wrong 四個常見的誤會

✗ Myth · 迷思Summer is hot because Earth is closer to the Sun.夏天熱是因為地球離太陽比較近。

✓ Fact · 事實Earth is closest to the Sun in early January, and its distance changes by only about 3 percent all year. In the model, look for the diamond marked Closest.地球在 1 月初離太陽最近，而且整年的距離只差約 3%。在模型裡找找標著「近日點」的菱形。

✗ Myth · 迷思In summer, the noon Sun is straight overhead everywhere.夏天的正午，到處都是太陽直射。

✓ Fact · 事實The Sun can be straight overhead only between the two tropics. Taiwan sits right on the Tropic of Cancer, so its June noon Sun is almost overhead. In Paris, the noon Sun never climbs higher than about 65 degrees.只有南北回歸線之間的地方，太陽才可能直射。台灣剛好在北回歸線上，六月正午太陽幾乎在頭頂；在巴黎，正午太陽最高也只到大約 65 度。

✗ Myth · 迷思Earth tips toward the Sun in summer and tips back in winter.地球夏天往太陽傾、冬天再倒回來。

✓ Fact · 事實The axis does not move. It points at the North Star all year. What changes is where Earth is in its orbit, so the same tilt faces toward the Sun in June and away from it in December.地軸根本沒有動，整年都指向北極星。改變的是地球在軌道上的位置：同一個傾斜，六月朝向太陽、十二月背向太陽。

✗ Myth · 迷思On the equinox, day and night are exactly 12 hours each.春分、秋分那天，白天和黑夜剛好各 12 小時。

✓ Fact · 事實Almost, but not quite. The air bends sunlight over the horizon, and the Sun is a disk rather than a point, so in Changhua the equinox day lasts about 12 hours and 7 minutes.差一點點。大氣會把地平線下的陽光折上來，太陽又是一個圓盤而不是一個點，所以彰化在春分、秋分的白天大約是 12 小時 7 分。

Remember It · 記憶口訣

Seasons in a sentence 一句話記住四季

Tilt, not distance是傾斜，不是距離

If distance caused the seasons, both hemispheres would have summer at the same time. They never do.
如果是距離造成四季，南北半球應該同時過夏天；但它們從來沒有。

Higher Sun, longer day太陽越高，白天越長

Summer brings two gifts at once: stronger sunlight because the Sun is higher, and more of it because the day is longer.
夏天同時帶來兩樣東西：太陽比較高，陽光比較強；白天比較長，陽光的時間也比較多。

The Chinese seasons start early節氣的季節提早開始

In the solar terms, spring begins at Start of Spring in early February, about six weeks before the equinox. That is why the Lunar New Year is also called the Spring Festival.
在節氣裡，春天從 2 月初的立春就開始了，比春分早了大約六週。這也是為什麼農曆新年又叫「春節」。

Opposite hemispheres, opposite seasons南北半球，季節相反

When Taiwan eats tangyuan on the winter solstice, Sydney is having its longest day of the year. Try switching the place in the model.
台灣在冬至吃湯圓的時候，雪梨正在過一年中白天最長的一天。在模型裡切換地點試試看。

Classroom Activity 1 · 課堂活動一

Flashlight and Graph Paper 手電筒與方格紙

You need · 準備材料

  • A flashlight一支手電筒
  • A sheet of graph paper一張方格紙
  • A pencil and a ruler鉛筆和尺

Steps · 步驟

  1. Hold the flashlight straight above the paper, about 30 centimeters away, and trace the bright circle. This is the noon Sun in summer.把手電筒垂直舉在方格紙正上方約 30 公分處，描出亮圈的輪廓——這是夏天正午的太陽。
  2. Keep the same distance but tilt the flashlight to a low angle, and trace the bright shape again. This is the Sun in winter.保持同樣的距離，但把手電筒斜斜地放低，再描一次亮區——這是冬天的太陽。
  3. Count the squares inside each shape. The same light now covers many more squares.數一數兩個形狀裡各有幾格：同樣的光，現在蓋住了多得多的格子。
  4. Ask: which shape would feel warmer on your hand? Why?問問自己：哪一個亮區照在手上會比較熱？為什麼？

Teaching tip: turn off the room lights so the edges are easy to trace. Compare the result with the Noon sunlight panel in the 3D model.
教學提示：把教室的燈關掉，亮區的邊緣比較好描。做完再對照 3D 模型裡的「正午陽光」小畫面。

Classroom Activity 2 · 課堂活動二

Walk a Tilted Earth Around the Lamp 帶著傾斜的地球繞燈走一圈

You need · 準備材料

  • A lamp with the shade removed (the Sun)一盞拿掉燈罩的檯燈（太陽）
  • A ball with a pencil pushed through it (Earth and its axis)一顆插著鉛筆的球（地球與地軸）
  • A sticker to mark Taiwan一張標示台灣的小貼紙

Steps · 步驟

  1. Put the sticker about a quarter of the way from the equator to the top of the ball.把貼紙貼在球上赤道到頂端大約四分之一的位置——這是台灣。
  2. Tilt the pencil a little and point its top at one wall of the room. That wall is the North Star.把鉛筆稍微傾斜，讓頂端指向教室的某一面牆——那面牆就是北極星。
  3. Walk slowly in a circle around the lamp, always keeping the pencil pointed at the same wall.繞著檯燈慢慢走一圈，鉛筆的頂端始終指向同一面牆。
  4. Stop at four places. Where does the top half of the ball get the most light? Where does it get the least?在四個地方停下來看：球的上半部在哪裡被照得最多？在哪裡最少？

Teaching tip: the most common mistake is turning the pencil to face the lamp. Keeping it pointed at the same wall is the whole point, and exactly what the real axis does.
教學提示：最常見的錯誤是把鉛筆轉去朝向檯燈。讓它始終指向同一面牆，正是整個活動的重點，也正是真實地軸的行為。
