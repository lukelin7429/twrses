The Sun rises due east only around the equinoxes. Stand in Changhua and watch the sunrise point slide along the horizon for a whole year, then step inside a 4,000-year-old sunrise calendar at Taosi.

Big idea · 一句話看懂

Because Earth's axis is tilted, the Sun's daily path shifts north and south during the year, so the place where it rises slides along the horizon: about 26 degrees north of east in June and 25 degrees south of east in December in Changhua. Ancient people used this swing as a calendar.
因為地軸傾斜，太陽每天走的路一年之中南北移動，它升起的地方就沿著地平線來回滑動：在彰化，六月從東偏北約 26° 升起，十二月從東偏南約 25° 升起。古人就拿這個擺盪當日曆。

Unit 16: Why Does the Sun Rise in a Different Place Every Day?

Where does the Sun rise? Most people say "in the east," and that is almost right. But if you watch the sunrise from the same window every week and compare it with a landmark, such as a tall building or a mountain, you will notice something surprising. The Sun comes up in a slightly different direction each day. In summer it rises north of east, and in winter it rises south of east.

太陽從哪裡升起？大多數人會說「東方」，這幾乎是對的。但如果你每個星期都從同一扇窗戶看日出，拿一個地標（像是一棟高樓或一座山）來比對，你會發現一件令人驚訝的事：太陽每天升起的方向都稍微不一樣。夏天它從東偏北升起，冬天從東偏南升起。

In Changhua, the sunrise point swings to about 26 degrees north of east in June and about 25 degrees south of east in December. That is more than 50 degrees along the horizon, the width of five fists held at arm's length. The Sun rises due east only around the spring and fall equinoxes, in March and September. The sunset point swings the same way in the west.

在彰化，日出點六月擺到東偏北約 26°，十二月擺到東偏南約 25°，沿著地平線總共超過 50°，差不多是手臂伸直時五個拳頭的寬度。太陽只有在三月和九月的春分、秋分前後，才從正東升起。西邊的日落點也照同樣的方式擺動。

Why does this happen? Earth's axis is tilted by about 23.5 degrees. As Earth travels around the Sun, the Sun's daily path across our sky shifts north for half the year and south for the other half. Each day the Sun follows a circle across the sky, and the place where that circle meets the horizon is where the Sun rises. When the whole circle moves north, the sunrise point moves north too.

為什麼會這樣？地軸傾斜了大約 23.5°。地球繞著太陽走，太陽每天在我們天空中走的路，半年往北移、另外半年往南移。太陽每天沿著天上的一個圓走，這個圓和地平線相交的地方，就是太陽升起的地方。整個圓往北移，日出點也跟著往北移。

The sunrise point does not move at the same speed all year. Near the equinoxes, it moves almost one Sun-width every day. Near the solstices, it slows down, seems to stand still for several days, and then turns around. The word solstice comes from Latin words meaning "the Sun stands still." The Chinese names, 夏至 and 冬至, mean that summer and winter have reached their limit.

日出點不是一年到頭都用同樣的速度移動。春分、秋分前後，它每天移動將近一個太陽的寬度；接近夏至、冬至時，它慢下來，好幾天看起來幾乎不動，然後掉頭往回走。英文 solstice 來自拉丁文，意思是「太陽停住」；中文的「夏至」「冬至」，意思是夏天、冬天走到了盡頭。

Ancient people noticed this swing and used the horizon as a calendar. At Taosi in Shanxi, China, archaeologists found the remains of an observatory more than 4,000 years old: a half circle of thirteen rammed-earth pillars. Standing at the center, an observer could watch the Sun rise through the gaps between the pillars. On the winter solstice it rose in the second gap, and on the summer solstice in the twelfth. Knowing the date helped people decide when to plant and when to harvest.

古人注意到這個擺盪，就把地平線當成日曆。在中國山西的陶寺，考古學家發現一座四千多年前的觀象台遺跡：十三根夯土柱排成半圓。站在中心，觀測者可以看著太陽從柱子之間的縫隙升起：冬至從第二道縫升起，夏至從第十二道縫升起。知道日期，就能決定什麼時候播種、什麼時候收成。

You can see the swing in a city, too. When a long, straight street points to where the Sun sets, the Sun can hang right at the end of the street between the buildings. In New York this is called Manhattanhenge, and in Taiwan it is called 懸日, the "hanging Sun." Because the sunset point passes each direction twice a year, once going and once coming back, there are two henge seasons. 在城市裡也看得到這個擺盪。一條又長又直的街道如果對準太陽落下的方向，太陽就會剛好掛在兩排大樓之間的街道盡頭。在紐約這叫「曼哈頓懸日」，在台灣叫「懸日」。因為日落點一年會經過每個方向兩次（去一次、回來一次），所以一年有兩段懸日季。看看下面：今天彰化的日出，以及一個告訴你太陽哪天會對準一條街的查詢工具。

Questions · 閱讀理解

Does the Sun always rise due east? Explain.

No. It rises due east only around the equinoxes in March and September. In summer it rises north of east and in winter south of east; in Changhua the swing is about 26 degrees north and 25 degrees south.

Why does the sunrise point move during the year?

Because Earth's axis is tilted. As Earth goes around the Sun, the Sun's daily path across the sky shifts north and south, so the place where the path meets the horizon moves too.

How did the pillars at Taosi work as a calendar?

An observer stood at the center and watched the Sun rise through the gaps between thirteen pillars. Each gap matched a time of year: the Sun rose in the second gap on the winter solstice and in the twelfth on the summer solstice.

Around the World · 世界各地

Calendars on the Horizon 地平線上的日曆

Long before printed calendars, people in China, England, and many other places watched where the Sun rose and set to keep track of the year.
早在印刷的日曆出現之前，中國、英國和世界許多地方的人，就靠觀察太陽從哪裡升起、落下來掌握一年的時間。

陶寺 · Taosi

A 4,000-Year-Old Sunrise Calendar四千多年前的日出日曆

In 2003, archaeologists at Taosi in Xiangfen, Shanxi, uncovered a half-circle platform with the bases of thirteen rammed-earth pillars. Seen from a marked spot at the center, the Sun rose over Taer Mountain through the second gap on the winter solstice, the seventh around the equinoxes, and the twelfth on the summer solstice. Many scholars see it as an early root of the twenty-four solar terms. Choose Taosi in the 3D model to try a simplified version.
2003 年，考古學家在山西襄汾陶寺發掘出一座半圓形的夯土台基，上面有十三根夯土柱的基礎。從中心的觀測點看出去，冬至太陽從第二道縫、春秋分前後從第七道縫、夏至從第十二道縫，在東邊的塔兒山上升起。許多學者認為這是二十四節氣的早期源頭。在 3D 模型裡選「陶寺」，可以試試簡化的版本。

尚書 · Book of Documents

Greeting the Rising Sun寅賓出日

乃命羲和，欽若昊天，曆象日月星辰，敬授人時。——《尚書．堯典》"He ordered Xi and He to follow the vast heaven with care, to chart the Sun, the Moon, and the stars, and to give the people the seasons." (Book of Documents)

The same chapter sends an official to the east to "respectfully greet the rising Sun" and to arrange the spring farm work. Some archaeologists think Taosi may have been the capital of the legendary Emperor Yao, but this is still debated.
同一篇還派官員到東方「寅賓出日，平秩東作」：恭敬地迎接日出，安排春天的農事。有些考古學家認為陶寺可能就是傳說中堯的都城，不過這還在爭論中。

巨石陣 · Stonehenge

Stones That Frame the Solstices對準至日的巨石

In England, the great stones of Stonehenge were raised about 4,500 years ago. Its main axis lines up with the sunrise on the summer solstice and the sunset on the winter solstice, and crowds still gather there to watch. Stonehenge is about 51 degrees north, so its sunrise point swings much farther than Changhua's: almost 40 degrees each way.
英國巨石陣的大石頭大約在 4,500 年前立起。它的主軸對準夏至的日出與冬至的日落，到今天仍有大批人去那裡看。巨石陣在北緯約 51°，日出點擺得比彰化寬得多：往南往北各將近 40°。

懸日 · Henge

Manhattanhenge and Taipei's Hanging Sun曼哈頓懸日與台北懸日

The astrophysicist Neil deGrasse Tyson gave Manhattanhenge its name: the days when the setting Sun lines up with New York's street grid. Taiwan's Central Weather Administration now forecasts 懸日 every year. In 2026, the Sun set at the end of Zhongxiao East–West Road in Taipei from April 30 to May 2 and again from August 10 to 12. Myth vs. Fact · 常見迷思

Four things people get wrong 四個常見的誤會

✗ Myth · 迷思The Sun always rises exactly in the east.太陽一定從正東方升起。

✓ Fact · 事實Only around the equinoxes. In Changhua, the sunrise point swings from about 26 degrees north of east in June to about 25 degrees south of east in December.只有春分、秋分前後才是。在彰化，日出點從六月的東偏北約 26°，擺到十二月的東偏南約 25°。

✗ Myth · 迷思The sunrise point moves the same amount every day.日出點每天移動的距離都一樣。

✓ Fact · 事實It moves fastest near the equinoxes, almost one Sun-width a day, and nearly stops for several days around the solstices before turning back.春分、秋分前後移得最快，每天將近一個太陽寬；冬至、夏至前後好幾天幾乎不動，然後才往回走。

✗ Myth · 迷思In winter the Sun rises farther south because Earth is farther from the Sun.冬天太陽從比較南邊升起，是因為地球離太陽比較遠。

✓ Fact · 事實Earth is actually closest to the Sun in early January. The swing comes from the tilt of Earth's axis, which shifts the Sun's daily path north and south.其實地球在一月初離太陽最近。擺盪來自地軸傾斜，它讓太陽每天走的路往北、往南移動。

✗ Myth · 迷思A street can have a henge only once a year.一條街一年只會有一次懸日。

✓ Fact · 事實The sunset point passes each direction twice, once going north and once coming back, so a street that lines up with the sunset gets two henge seasons, like Taipei's in May and August.日落點每個方向都會經過兩次（往北一次、回來一次），所以對準夕陽的街一年有兩段懸日季，像台北的五月和八月。

Classroom Activity 1 · 課堂活動一

Make a Sunset Calendar 做一個日落日曆

You need · 準備材料

  • A window or a safe spot outside that faces west, with buildings or mountains as landmarks一扇朝西的窗戶或戶外安全的地點，看得到大樓或山當地標
  • A long sheet of paper for drawing the skyline一張長紙，用來畫天際線
  • Colored pens and a calendar彩色筆和一份月曆

Steps · 步驟

  1. Draw the western skyline you see, with every landmark.畫下你看到的西邊天際線，每個地標都畫上去。
  2. Once a week at sunset, stand in exactly the same spot and mark where the Sun goes down. Write the date next to the mark.每週一次，在日落時站在完全相同的位置，標出太陽落下的地方，旁邊寫上日期。
  3. After two months, connect the marks. Which way is the sunset point moving?兩個月後，把標記連起來：日落點正往哪個方向移動？
  4. Compare with the chart beside the 3D model. Did your marks move the way it predicts?和 3D 模型旁邊的圖比較：你的標記是不是照它預測的方向移動？

Safety tip: never stare at the Sun, even at sunset. Glance to see where it is, then look at the skyline.
安全提示：即使在日落時也不要直視太陽。瞄一眼看它在哪裡，就把目光移回天際線。

Classroom Activity 2 · 課堂活動二

Build a Mini Taosi 做一個迷你陶寺觀象台

You need · 準備材料

  • A paper plate and 13 clothespins or folded paper strips一個紙盤和 13 個曬衣夾（或摺好的紙條）
  • A flashlight一支手電筒
  • This page's 3D model, set to Taosi本頁的 3D 模型，地點選「陶寺」

Steps · 步驟

  1. Stand the 13 clothespins in a half circle on the east side of the plate, leaving 12 gaps. Mark the center as the observing spot.把 13 個曬衣夾立在紙盤東半邊排成半圓，留下 12 道縫，在中心標出觀測點。
  2. In the 3D model, choose Taosi and press Winter solstice, Spring equinox, and Summer solstice. Write down which gap the Sun rises in each time.在 3D 模型選「陶寺」，依序按冬至、春分、夏至，記下每次太陽從第幾道縫升起。
  3. Hold the flashlight low at the edge of the table as the rising Sun. Move it until its light shines through gap 2, then gap 7, then gap 12 onto the center.把手電筒當成剛升起的太陽，低低地拿在桌邊，移動它，讓光依序穿過第 2、第 7、第 12 道縫照到中心。
  4. Discuss: why would farmers 4,000 years ago want a calendar like this?討論：四千年前的農夫，為什麼需要這樣的日曆？

Teaching tip: the real half circle was about 20 meters across, so each gap was a narrow slit. The narrower the gaps, the more exact the calendar.
教學提示：真正的半圓直徑約 20 公尺，每道縫都很窄；縫越窄，日曆越準。
