Astronomy · 天文教育

Astronomy 天文教育

Big ideas about the sky, read in English and explored with 3D models you can turn in your hands.
用英文讀懂天空的大道理，再用可以親手旋轉的 3D 模型親眼看懂。

Every lesson starts with a question you can check for yourself by looking up. A 3D model lets you move the Sun, Earth, and Moon and see the answer, and an English reading explains it, with Chinese a tap away.
每一課都從一個抬頭就能驗證的問題開始：先用 3D 模型親手移動太陽、地球和月亮，看出答案；再讀一篇英文短文把道理講清楚，中文翻譯隨點隨看。

Each reading comes with vocabulary, comprehension questions, a short quiz, and a classroom activity that needs nothing more than a lamp and a ball.
每篇文章都附生字、閱讀理解、小測驗，以及一個只需要一盞燈、一顆球就能在教室做的活動。

Lessons · 課程

12 lessons so far 目前 12 課，持續增加中

Lesson 1 · 第一課入門 · Beginner

Why Does the Moon Change Shape?

月亮為什麼會有圓缺？The Moon is always half lit. What changes is how much of that lit half we can see — explore it in 3D.月亮永遠是一半亮的，改變的只是我們看得到多少亮面。用 3D 模型親手轉轉看。Start the lesson · 開始上課 →Lesson 2 · 第二課入門 · Beginner

Why Isn't There an Eclipse Every Month?

為什麼不是每個月都有日食月食？Solar and lunar eclipses, the Moon's tilted orbit, and eclipse seasons, with a 3D model that runs on real dates.日食、月食、傾斜的月球軌道與「食季」；3D 模型照真實日期運轉，算得出下一次日月食。Start the lesson · 開始上課 →Lesson 3 · 第三課入門 · Beginner

Why Do We Have Seasons?

為什麼會有四季？It is the tilt, not the distance. Follow Earth around the Sun on real dates, and watch Taiwan's days grow long and short.原因是傾斜，不是距離。跟著真實日期繞太陽一圈，看台灣的白天一天天變長、變短。Start the lesson · 開始上課 →Lesson 4 · 第四課入門 · Beginner

How Does the Moon Make the Tides?

月亮怎麼造成潮汐？Two bulges, two tides a day, and why new and full moons bring the biggest tides, on a real-time 3D Earth.兩個鼓起、一天兩次滿潮，以及為什麼新月和滿月會漲大潮；3D 地球照真實時間運轉。Start the lesson · 開始上課 →Lesson 5 · 第五課入門 · Beginner

Why Do the Stars Change with the Seasons?

為什麼每個季節的星空不一樣？Real stars on a 3D sky, Earth's night side pointing at a new part of space each season, and tonight's sky over Changhua.真實亮星組成的 3D 天球：地球的夜側每一季對著不同方向的星空；再看看今晚彰化的天上有什麼。Start the lesson · 開始上課 →Lesson 6 · 第六課入門 · Beginner

How Do You Find North at Night?

夜裡怎麼找北方？Find the North Star with the Big Dipper, see why it never moves, and measure your latitude with your fist, from Changhua to the North Pole.用北斗七星找到北極星，看懂它為什麼不動；再用拳頭量出你的緯度，從彰化一路到北極點。Start the lesson · 開始上課 →Lesson 7 · 第七課入門 · Beginner

Why Do Planets Wander Across the Sky?

行星為什麼會在星空中遊走？Watch Earth overtake Mars on real orbits, see why planets loop backward among the stars, and find tonight's planets over Changhua.在真實軌道上看地球超車火星，看懂行星為什麼會在星空中倒退繞圈；再看今晚彰化的天上有哪些行星。Start the lesson · 開始上課 →Lesson 8 · 第八課入門 · Beginner

How Did People Tell Time by the Sun?

古人怎麼用太陽看時間？Turn a stick into a clock, tilt it toward the North Star to make a sundial, and find out why a Changhua sundial can be 19 minutes off your watch.把一根竿子變成時鐘，再讓它斜斜指向北極星做成日晷；看看彰化的日晷為什麼會和手錶差到 19 分鐘。Start the lesson · 開始上課 →Lesson 9 · 第九課入門 · Beginner

How Big Is the Solar System?

太陽系有多大？Shrink the Sun to a basketball and Earth becomes a sesame seed 26 meters away. Fly out to Neptune on true distances, and race a flash of sunlight across the solar system.把太陽縮成一顆籃球，地球就是 26 公尺外的一粒芝麻。照真實距離飛到海王星，再跟著一道陽光跑過整個太陽系。Start the lesson · 開始上課 →Lesson 10 · 第十課入門 · Beginner

How Far Away Are the Stars?

星星離我們多遠？Hold up your thumb and blink: it jumps. Earth plays the same trick with its orbit to measure the stars. Then find out when tonight's starlight set out, from the year you were born back to the Ming dynasty.伸出拇指、輪流閉一隻眼，拇指會跳動。地球用繞太陽的軌道玩同一招，量出星星的距離。再看看今晚的星光是哪一年出發的：從你出生那年，一路到明朝。Start the lesson · 開始上課 →Lesson 11 · 第11課入門 · Beginner

Where Do Meteor Showers Come From?

流星雨是怎麼來的？A shooting star is a grain of comet dust burning up 100 kilometers overhead. Ride with Earth as it plows through a comet's trail on the same dates every year, and plan your night for the next shower over Changhua.流星是一粒彗星碎屑，在 100 公里高空燒掉的光。跟著地球每年在同一天穿過彗星留下的碎屑帶，再規劃你看下一場流星雨的夜晚。Start the lesson · 開始上課 →Lesson 12 · 第12課入門 · Beginner

Why Are Stars Different Colors?

星星為什麼有不同的顏色？Red Betelgeuse and blue-white Rigel sit in the same constellation. Heat a star from 2,000 to 30,000 degrees and watch its color change, then sort 580 real stars by color into the famous H-R diagram.紅色的參宿四和藍白的參宿七在同一個星座裡。把一顆星從 2,000 度加熱到 30,000 度，看它怎麼變色；再把 580 顆真實的星依顏色排排站，排出有名的赫羅圖。Start the lesson · 開始上課 →
