Why Do the Stars Change with the Seasons?為什麼每個季節的星空不一樣？

Real stars on a 3D sky, Earth's night side pointing at a new part of space each season, and tonight's sky over Changhua.
真實亮星組成的 3D 天球：地球的夜側每一季對著不同方向的星空；再看看今晚彰化的天上有什麼。

Big idea · 一句話看懂

At night we look out from the side of Earth that faces away from the Sun. As Earth travels around the Sun, that night side points toward different stars, so each season has its own constellations.
夜晚，我們是從地球背對太陽的那一側往外看。地球繞著太陽走，這一側就對著不同的星星，所以每個季節都有自己的星座。

Unit 5: Why Do the Stars Change with the Seasons?

On a winter evening in Taiwan, Orion stands high in the south, with the three stars of his belt in a neat row. Look for him on a summer evening, and he is gone. In his place, the curling tail of Scorpius hangs low over the southern horizon, with the red star Antares at its heart. About 1,300 years ago, the poet Du Fu wrote that friends who never meet are like Shen and Shang, two groups of stars that are never in the sky together. Shen is the Chinese name for Orion's main stars, and Shang is Antares. Why do they take turns like that?

在台灣的冬夜，獵戶座高掛在南方天空，腰帶上的三顆星排得整整齊齊。到了夏天的晚上再去找他，他卻不見了；取而代之的，是天蠍座彎彎的尾巴低掛在南方地平線上，紅色的心宿二就在牠的心臟位置。大約 1,300 年前，詩人杜甫寫道：朋友難得相見，就像天上的參與商，從來不會同時出現。「參」是中國對獵戶座主要亮星的稱呼，「商」就是心宿二。它們為什麼要這樣輪流登場？

First, the stars never go away. They are in the sky during the day as well, but sunlight scattered by the air makes the daytime sky so bright that it hides them. Night is simply the side of Earth that faces away from the Sun. When your part of the world turns into that side, the glare is gone, and you can finally see out into space. So the stars you see at midnight are the stars in the direction opposite the Sun.

首先，星星從來沒有消失。白天它們一樣在天上，只是陽光被空氣散射，讓白天的天空亮得把星星都蓋住了。所謂夜晚，就是地球背對太陽的那一側。當你住的地方轉到這一側，刺眼的陽光不見了，你終於能看進太空。所以午夜看到的星星，就是太陽對面方向的星星。

Earth also travels around the Sun, once a year. As it moves along its orbit, the night side points in a slowly changing direction, about one degree a day. In winter, it faces the part of space where Orion is. Six months later, Earth is on the other side of the Sun. Now the night side faces Scorpius, while Orion lies behind the Sun, lost in daylight. The stars have not moved; we have. The constellations along the Sun's yearly path are called the zodiac constellations, and the Sun passes in front of a new one about every month.

地球同時也繞著太陽轉，一年一圈。它沿著軌道前進時，夜側指的方向也慢慢改變，大約一天一度。冬天，夜側對著獵戶座所在的那片太空；半年後，地球跑到太陽的另一邊，夜側改對著天蠍座，而獵戶座則躲到太陽背後，淹沒在白天的陽光裡。星星沒有動，是我們在動。沿著太陽一年路線排列的星座，叫做黃道星座；太陽大約每個月換到一個新的黃道星座前面。

You can notice this shift a little every night. A star rises about four minutes earlier each evening. That adds up to about two hours a month, so a star that rises at 11 p.m. this month rises around 9 p.m. next month. After a full year, Earth is back where it started, and the same stars return at the same time of night. That is why farmers in ancient China and Egypt could use the stars as a calendar, telling them when to plant and when to expect the rains or the floods.

每天晚上都能察覺到一點點這種移動：同一顆星每晚大約提早四分鐘升起，一個月累積下來大約兩小時。所以這個月晚上 11 點升起的星，下個月大約 9 點就升起了。整整一年後，地球回到原來的位置，同樣的星星又在夜裡同一個時刻回來。這就是為什麼古代中國和埃及的農民能把星星當作曆法，知道什麼時候該播種、什麼時候雨季或洪水要來了。

Different cultures connected the same stars into different pictures. The constellations astronomers use today come mostly from ancient Greece. Ancient Chinese astronomers divided the sky into the Three Enclosures around the North Pole and the Twenty-Eight Lunar Mansions along the path of the Moon. The two stars in the story of the Cowherd and the Weaver Girl, Altair and Vega, sit on either side of the Milky Way, the "Silver River." They shine high overhead on summer evenings, around the time of the Qixi Festival on the seventh day of the seventh lunar month.

不同的文化，把同樣的星星連成不同的圖案。今天天文學家使用的星座，大多來自古希臘。中國古代的天文學家則把天空分成北極周圍的「三垣」，以及沿著月亮路線排列的「二十八宿」。牛郎織女故事裡的兩顆星——牛郎星（河鼓二）和織女星——分別在銀河的兩岸。夏天的晚上，它們高掛頭頂，差不多就是農曆七月初七七夕前後。

A constellation is a pattern, not a family. Its stars only look close together because they lie in the same direction. Betelgeuse, Orion's red shoulder, is roughly 550 to 700 light-years away, while Rigel, his bright foot, is about 860 light-years away. The light you see from Betelgeuse tonight left it at least 500 years ago, during the Ming dynasty or even earlier. Stars do drift, but so slowly that the constellations will look the same for your whole life. So step outside tonight and look up: the sky you see belongs to this season.

星座只是一個圖案，不是一家人。星座裡的星看起來靠在一起，只是因為它們在同一個方向上。獵戶座紅色的肩膀參宿四，距離我們大約 550 到 700 光年；他明亮的腳參宿七，則大約 860 光年遠。今晚你看到參宿四的光，至少在 500 年前就出發了——那時還是明朝，甚至更早。星星其實也在移動，但慢到你一輩子看到的星座都不會變樣。所以今晚走到戶外抬頭看吧：你看到的，正是屬於這個季節的星空。

Questions · 閱讀理解

Why can't we see the stars during the day?

They are still in the sky, but sunlight scattered by the air makes the daytime sky so bright that it hides them.

Why is Orion easy to see on winter evenings but not on summer evenings?

In summer, Earth is on the other side of the Sun, so Orion lies behind the Sun. It is up during the day and lost in the daylight.

About how much earlier does a star rise each month, and why?

About two hours, because a star rises about four minutes earlier each night as Earth moves along its orbit.

參商 · Shen and Shang

Two Friends Who Never Meet參商不相見

人生不相見，動如參與商。——杜甫〈贈衛八處士〉"In life we seldom meet, just like Shen and Shang." (Du Fu, To the Hermit Wei Ba)

Shen is Orion's main stars, and Shang is Antares in Scorpius. They sit almost opposite each other in the sky, so one is rising about when the other is setting. The Zuo Zhuan, a history more than 2,000 years old, tells of two quarreling brothers who were sent to live far apart, one to watch Shang and the other to watch Shen.
參是獵戶座的主要亮星，商是天蠍座的心宿二。它們在天上幾乎正對面，一個升起時，另一個差不多正要落下。兩千多年前的《左傳》記載，高辛氏的兩個兒子閼伯與實沈天天爭吵，只好被分開：一個去主管商星，一個去主管參星。

七夕 · Qixi

The Cowherd and the Weaver Girl牛郎織女

Altair, the Cowherd, and Vega, the Weaver Girl, sit on either side of the Milky Way. In the legend, magpies build a bridge so they can meet once a year on Qixi. In reality they are about 15 light-years apart, so even a message sent at the speed of light would take 15 years to arrive.
牛郎星（河鼓二）和織女星隔著銀河相望。傳說每年七夕，喜鵲搭起鵲橋讓他們相會。實際上兩顆星相距大約 15 光年，就算用光速傳訊息，也要走 15 年。

四象 · Four Symbols

Twenty-Eight Mansions二十八宿與四象

The Moon takes about 27 days to go once around the sky, so Chinese astronomers marked 28 "mansions" where it stays night after night. Seven mansions make each of four animals: the Azure Dragon of the east, the Black Tortoise of the north, the White Tiger of the west, and the Vermilion Bird of the south. Shen belongs to the White Tiger, and Antares is the Dragon's heart.
月亮大約 27 天在天上繞一圈，古人就沿著它的路線劃出 28 個「宿」，像是月亮每晚住宿的地方。每七宿組成一隻神獸：東方青龍、北方玄武、西方白虎、南方朱雀。參宿屬於白虎，心宿二就是青龍的心臟。

三垣 · Three Enclosures

A Sky Shaped Like an Empire天上的朝廷

Around the North Pole lies the Purple Forbidden Enclosure, the emperor's palace in the sky; Beijing's Forbidden City is named after it. The Supreme Palace Enclosure is the government, and the Heavenly Market Enclosure is a busy marketplace. In all, the traditional Chinese sky has 283 star groups, while astronomers today divide the sky into 88 constellations.
北極周圍是紫微垣，天帝的宮殿，北京的紫禁城就是照它命名；太微垣是朝廷，天市垣是熱鬧的市集。傳統中國星空一共有 283 個星官，而現代天文學把天空分成 88 個星座。

Myth vs. Fact · 常見迷思

Four things people get wrong 四個常見的誤會

✗ Myth · 迷思The stars disappear during the day.白天星星就不見了。

✓ Fact · 事實They are always there. Sunlight scattered by the air lights up the whole sky and hides them. During a total solar eclipse, when the Sun is covered, bright stars appear in the middle of the day.星星一直都在。陽光被空氣散射，照亮整片天空，把星星蓋住了。日全食時太陽被遮住，大白天也看得到亮星。

✗ Myth · 迷思The constellations change with the seasons because the stars move.星座隨季節改變，是因為星星在移動。

✓ Fact · 事實The stars stay put; Earth moves. As it goes around the Sun, our night side faces a different direction. The stars do drift, but so slowly that Orion looks almost exactly the way it did for Du Fu.星星沒動，是地球在動：地球繞太陽走，夜側就對著不同方向。星星其實也在移動，只是慢到我們今天看到的獵戶座，跟杜甫當年看到的幾乎一模一樣。

✗ Myth · 迷思The stars in a constellation are close to each other.同一個星座的星星彼此靠得很近。

✓ Fact · 事實They only lie in the same direction. Rigel is about 860 light-years away, but Bellatrix, Orion's other shoulder, is only about 250. Seen from another star, Orion would not look like a hunter at all.它們只是在同一個方向上。參宿七大約 860 光年遠，獵戶座的另一個肩膀參宿五卻只有約 250 光年。從別的恆星看過來，獵戶座根本不像個獵人。

✗ Myth · 迷思On your birthday, the Sun is in front of your horoscope sign.生日那天，太陽就在你的星座前面。

✓ Fact · 事實Usually it is not. The horoscope signs were fixed about 2,000 years ago, and Earth's slow wobble has shifted the sky by almost a whole constellation since then. On October 1 the sign is Libra, but the model shows the Sun in front of Virgo. The Sun also passes through Ophiuchus, a 13th constellation that horoscopes leave out.通常不是。星座運勢的「星座」是大約兩千年前定下的，地軸緩慢的晃動（歲差）讓天空至今偏移了將近一整個星座。10 月 1 日在運勢上是天秤座，模型裡的太陽卻在室女座前面。太陽還會經過運勢裡沒有的第 13 個星座：蛇夫座。

Remember It · 記憶口訣

Find your way around the seasons 一句話記住四季星空

Four minutes a night, two hours a month每晚早 4 分鐘，每月早 2 小時

A star rises about 4 minutes earlier each night. If Orion rises at 11 p.m. tonight, a month from now it will rise around 9 p.m.
同一顆星每晚大約提早 4 分鐘升起。如果今晚獵戶座 11 點升起，一個月後大約 9 點就升起了。

Winter hunter, summer scorpion冬看獵戶，夏看天蠍

Orion and Scorpius sit almost opposite each other in the sky, so they take turns: the Shen and Shang of Du Fu's poem.
獵戶座和天蠍座在天上幾乎正對面，只能輪流登場，這就是杜甫詩裡的參與商。

Look opposite the Sun太陽對面，整夜可見

Whatever constellation is opposite the Sun is up all night. In early October the Sun is in Virgo, so Pisces, across the sky, is up from dusk to dawn.
跟太陽正對面的星座整夜可見。十月初太陽在室女座，對面的雙魚座就從黃昏一直掛到天亮。

Three triangles and a square三個大三角，一個四邊形

Winter: Betelgeuse, Sirius, Procyon. Spring: Arcturus, Spica, Denebola. Summer: Vega, Altair, Deneb. Autumn has a square instead: the Great Square of Pegasus.
冬：參宿四、天狼星、南河三。春：大角星、角宿一、五帝座一。夏：織女星、牛郎星、天津四。秋天則是一個四邊形：飛馬座大四邊形。

Classroom Activity 1 · 課堂活動一

Walk Around the Lamp 繞著燈走一圈，看見四季星座

You need · 準備材料

  • A lamp without a shade, in the middle of the room (the Sun)一盞沒有燈罩的燈，放在教室中央（太陽）
  • Four paper signs: Pegasus, Orion, Leo, Scorpius四張紙牌：飛馬座、獵戶座、獅子座、天蠍座
  • Tape to put the signs on the four walls膠帶，把紙牌貼在四面牆上

Steps · 步驟

  1. Tape the signs on the four walls, going counterclockwise around the room: Pegasus, Orion, Leo, Scorpius.把四張紙牌依逆時針方向貼在四面牆上：飛馬座、獵戶座、獅子座、天蠍座。
  2. One student is Earth. Stand between the lamp and the Orion wall, with your back to the lamp. Your face is on the night side. Which constellation do you see? It is winter.一位同學當地球，站在燈和獵戶座那面牆之間，背對著燈：你的臉就在夜側。你看到哪個星座？這是冬天。
  3. Now turn and face the lamp. That is your daytime sky. Scorpius, behind the lamp, is lost in the glare.現在轉身面向燈，這是白天的天空。燈後面的天蠍座被強光淹沒，看不到。
  4. Walk a quarter of the way around the lamp, counterclockwise, and stop with your back to the lamp again. Name the season and the constellation at each stop: spring and Leo, summer and Scorpius, autumn and Pegasus.沿逆時針方向繞燈走四分之一圈，每停一次都背對著燈。說出每一站的季節和星座：春天獅子座、夏天天蠍座、秋天飛馬座。
  5. At any stop, turn slowly in place. The sign in front of you rises on one side and sets on the other: that is one night.在任何一站原地慢慢轉一圈：眼前的紙牌從一側「升起」、從另一側「落下」，這就是一個晚上。

Teaching tip: afterward, switch the 3D model to Night side and play it at 20 days a second. It is the same walk, with the real stars.
教學提示：活動結束後，把 3D 模型切到「看夜側」，用「每秒 20 天」播放，就是同樣的一圈，只是換成真實的星空。

Classroom Activity 2 · 課堂活動二

Same Time, Two Weeks Later 兩週後的同一時間

You need · 準備材料

  • A spot with a clear view to the south or west that you can find again一個看得到南方或西方天空、下次還找得到的位置
  • The Tonight's Sky list on this page本頁的「今晚彰化的星空」清單
  • Paper, a pencil, and a clock紙、鉛筆和時鐘

Steps · 步驟

  1. At 8:30 p.m., stand at your spot, such as the school gate or a corner of your balcony. Pick one bright star from the Tonight list.晚上 8 點半站在你選的位置（例如校門口或陽台一角），從「今晚」清單裡挑一顆亮星。
  2. Sketch the star together with rooftops, trees, or poles. Measure its height with your fist at arm's length: one fist is about 10 degrees.把這顆星和屋頂、樹、電線桿一起畫下來。手臂伸直用拳頭量它的高度：一個拳頭大約 10 度。
  3. Two weeks later, stand in the same spot at exactly the same time and sketch it again.兩週後，在同一個位置、同一個時間，再畫一次。
  4. Compare the two sketches. The star has moved about a fist and a half toward the west, because it now arrives at each spot about an hour earlier.比較兩張圖：這顆星往西移了大約一個半拳頭，因為它現在每到一個位置，都比兩週前早了大約一小時。
  5. Predict: where will it be at 8:30 p.m. two months from now? Check your guess with the 3D model.預測看看：兩個月後的晚上 8 點半，它會在哪裡？再用 3D 模型驗證你的答案。

Safety tip: stargaze with an adult, and never look straight at the Sun. On a cloudy night, the sky chart in the 3D model shows the same view.
安全提示：觀星要有大人陪同，絕對不要直視太陽。遇到陰天，3D 模型旁的星空圖一樣看得到同樣的景象。
