Play two notes at once and see their waves line up. Hear the beats when two notes are almost the same, and build major and minor chords with three keys.

Sound stays off until you press the button, and it starts quiet. If you hear nothing on an iPhone, check the silent switch.按下按鈕之前不會發出聲音，而且一開始很小聲。如果在 iPhone 上聽不到，請檢查靜音開關。

Big idea · 一句話看懂

When two notes sound together, a simple frequency ratio makes the combined wave repeat quickly, and many listeners hear that as blended. Notes that are almost, but not exactly, the same make beats. Three notes stacked in thirds make a chord.
兩個音同時響時，簡單的頻率比會讓疊起來的波很快重複，許多人聽起來就覺得融在一起。幾乎相同、但不完全相同的音會產生拍音。三個音按三度疊起來，就是和弦。

Unit 8: Why Do Some Notes Sound Good Together?

An interval is the difference in pitch between two sounds. You can also say it as a frequency ratio. Two notes an octave apart have a ratio of 2 to 1. A perfect fifth is 3 to 2, a perfect fourth is 4 to 3, a major third is 5 to 4, and a minor third is 6 to 5. You met 2 to 1 and 3 to 2 on the string in lesson 1.

音程是兩個聲音之間音高的差距。你也可以用頻率比來說：差一個八度的兩個音，頻率比是 2 比 1；完全五度是 3 比 2，完全四度是 4 比 3，大三度是 5 比 4，小三度是 6 比 5。2 比 1 和 3 比 2，你在第一課的弦上就遇到過。

An interval’s name counts the notes it covers, with both ends included. From C to E is a third, because C, D, and E are three notes. In semitones, a minor third is 3, a major third is 4, a perfect fourth is 5, and a perfect fifth is 7. A perfect fifth is at the start of “Twinkle, Twinkle, Little Star.” A piano is not exactly in these ratios: its fifth is 700 cents, but 3 to 2 is about 701.96 cents, and its major third is about 14 cents wider than 5 to 4.

音程的名字是數它涵蓋的音，頭尾都算。C 到 E 是三度，因為 C、D、E 是三個音。用半音數來說：小三度是 3、大三度是 4、完全四度是 5、完全五度是 7。〈小星星〉一開始就是完全五度。鋼琴並不完全照這些比例：它的五度是 700 音分，而 3 比 2 約是 701.96 音分；它的大三度比 5 比 4 寬約 14 音分。

Musicians call a stable combination of notes a consonance, a point of rest. They call a tense combination a dissonance, which seems to want to move on. Consonance is often associated with simple ratios. Look at the lab: when two notes are an octave apart, the combined wave repeats as fast as the lower note. For a fifth it lines up again every 1/110 of a second. For a half step the pattern takes much longer, and the two notes seem to rub against each other.

音樂家把穩定的音的組合叫協和，是休息、到達的地方；把緊張的組合叫不協和，好像想要往前走。協和常常和簡單的比連在一起。看看實驗室：兩個音差八度時，疊起來的波重複得和低音一樣快；五度每 1/110 秒重新對齊一次；半音的圖樣要花久得多，兩個音聽起來好像在彼此摩擦。

When two notes are almost the same, you hear something else. The sound gets louder and softer, again and again, and this is called beats. The beat rate is the difference between the two frequencies: 220 Hz and 224 Hz make 4 beats per second. Slow beats are heard as the loudness changing, and faster ones, above about 20 per second, as a rough, fluttering sound. Piano tuners listen for beats to know when two strings are in tune. These beats are not the beat of a song, which you learned about in lesson 3.

當兩個音幾乎相同時，你會聽到另一種現象：聲音一次又一次地變大、變小，這叫拍音。拍音的速度就是兩個頻率的差：220 赫茲和 224 赫茲會產生每秒 4 次的拍音。慢的拍音聽起來是音量在變；每秒超過約 20 次，就變成粗糙、顫動的聲音。調琴師聽拍音來判斷兩條弦準不準。這裡的拍音，和你在第三課學的歌曲的「拍」不是同一件事。

A chord is two or more notes sounding at the same time. A triad has three notes stacked in thirds: the root, then a third above, then a fifth above. A major triad is a major third and a perfect fifth above the root, so from C it is C, E, G. In just intonation its ratio is 4 : 5 : 6. A minor triad has a minor third instead, so from C it is C, E♭, G. Only the middle note is different, by one half step. Major and minor triads are the most common triads in Western music.

和弦是兩個或更多的音同時響。三和弦有三個音，按三度疊起來：根音、上面的三度音、再上面的五度音。大三和弦是根音上面有大三度和完全五度，從 C 開始就是 C、E、G；在純律裡它的比是 4 : 5 : 6。小三和弦用小三度，從 C 開始是 C、E♭、G。只有中間的音不同，差一個半音。大三和弦與小三和弦是西方音樂裡最常用的三和弦。

Is the pleasure of consonance built in, or learned? Researchers still debate it. In a 2016 study, people of the Tsimane’, an Amazonian society with little exposure to Western music, rated consonant and dissonant chords as equally pleasant. But they still disliked rough sounds. Dissonance is not simply bad, either. Dissonances often play a decisive role in making music pleasant. So listen with open ears, and let your own listening tell you what you find beautiful.

協和的愉悅是天生的，還是學來的？研究者還在爭論。2016 年的一項研究裡，奇曼內人（Tsimane’，亞馬遜雨林裡很少接觸西方音樂的社群）認為協和與不協和的和弦同樣悅耳；但他們還是不喜歡粗糙的聲音。不協和也不只是「不好」：不協和常常是讓音樂變得悅耳的關鍵。所以請用開放的耳朵去聽，讓你自己的聆聽告訴你，你覺得什麼美。

Questions · 閱讀理解

Why is the interval from C to E called a third?

Because the name counts the notes it covers, with both ends included: C, D, E are three notes.

What do you hear when you play 220 Hz and 224 Hz together, and how fast is it?

You hear beats, the loudness rising and falling. The rate is the difference between the frequencies, 224 − 220 = 4 times per second.

What is the difference between a major triad and a minor triad?

Only the middle note. A major triad has a major third (4 semitones) above the root, and a minor triad has a minor third (3 semitones). Both have a perfect fifth (7 semitones).

Stories Behind the Notes · 音符背後的故事

A name, a legend, a rough sound, and different ears 一個名字、一個傳說、一種粗糙的聲音，和不同的耳朵

A Name · 名字

Why “perfect”?為什麼叫「完全」？

Perfect intervals, such as the fourth, fifth, and octave, are called perfect because they were traditionally considered perfectly consonant. Taiwan’s standard translations use the word 完全, as in 完全五度, for perfect fifth. The name does not mean that every other interval is imperfect, or that a perfect interval is always the best choice.
完全音程，例如四度、五度和八度，叫「完全」，是因為傳統上它們被認為是完全協和的。台灣的標準譯名用「完全」兩個字，例如 perfect fifth 是完全五度。這個名字不表示其他音程就是不完美，也不表示完全音程永遠是最好的選擇。

A Legend · 一個傳說

Pythagoras and the hammers畢達哥拉斯與鐵錘

According to legend, Pythagoras heard blacksmiths’ hammers ringing and discovered that musical notes could be turned into numbers. But no authentic writings of Pythagoras survive, and modern scholars usually ascribe these discoveries to the later Pythagorean Philolaus. The tuning built from fifths is named after Pythagoras, but its system dates back to ancient Mesopotamia.
依傳說，畢達哥拉斯聽到鐵匠的錘子敲擊聲，發現音可以變成數字。但畢達哥拉斯本人沒有留下可靠的著作，現代學者通常把這些發現歸給後來的畢達哥拉斯學派的菲洛勞斯。用五度疊出來的音律以畢達哥拉斯命名，但這個系統可以追溯到古代美索不達米亞。

A Rough Sound · 粗糙的聲音

Beats on purpose故意製造的拍音

Beats are not always a mistake. Many pipe organs have “celeste” stops that intentionally produce beating, and in some musical traditions people consider the rough, fluttering sound an attractive part of the timbre. Whether a sound is pleasant depends on the style of music and the listener.
拍音不一定是錯誤。許多管風琴有「天使音栓」（celeste），故意製造拍音；有些音樂傳統認為，這種粗糙、顫動的聲音是迷人的音色的一部分。一個聲音好不好聽，要看音樂的風格與聽的人。

Different Ears · 不同的耳朵

What the Tsimane’ rated奇曼內人的評分

In a 2016 study published in Nature, the Tsimane’, a native Amazonian society with minimal exposure to Western culture, rated consonant and dissonant chords and vocal harmonies as equally pleasant. They did show Western-like dislike for acoustic roughness. Bolivian town and city dwellers preferred consonance, less strongly than US residents did. The authors concluded that culture has a dominant role in shaping these responses, and other researchers still argue for a biological part.
2016 年發表在《自然》期刊的一項研究中，奇曼內人（Tsimane’，亞馬遜地區、很少接觸西方文化的原住民社群）認為協和與不協和的和弦與人聲和聲同樣悅耳；但他們和西方人一樣不喜歡粗糙的聲音。玻利維亞城鎮的居民比較喜歡協和，程度比美國居民輕。作者的結論是文化在塑造這些反應上占主導地位；也有其他研究者主張其中有生物性的成分。

Myth vs. Fact · 常見迷思

Four things people get wrong 四個常見的誤會

✗ Myth · 迷思Dissonant means bad.不協和就是不好聽。

✓ Fact · 事實A dissonance is a tension that wants to move on. Dissonances often play a decisive role in making music pleasant.不協和是一種想要往前走的緊張。不協和常常是讓音樂悅耳的關鍵。

✗ Myth · 迷思A piano plays exactly 3 to 2 for a fifth.鋼琴彈出的五度就是剛好 3 比 2。

✓ Fact · 事實A piano’s fifth is 700 cents, and 3 to 2 is about 701.96 cents. It is very close, but not the same. Its major third is about 14 cents wider than 5 to 4.鋼琴的五度是 700 音分，3 比 2 約是 701.96 音分，非常接近，但不完全一樣。它的大三度比 5 比 4 寬約 14 音分。

✗ Myth · 迷思Everyone hears consonance the same way.每個人聽到的協和都一樣。

✓ Fact · 事實Researchers still debate how much is built in and how much is learned. One study found a group who rated consonant and dissonant chords as equally pleasant.研究者還在爭論有多少是天生的、有多少是學來的。有一項研究發現，有一個社群認為協和與不協和的和弦同樣悅耳。

✗ Myth · 迷思Pythagoras discovered the ratios from blacksmiths’ hammers.畢達哥拉斯是聽鐵錘發現比例的。

✓ Fact · 事實That is a legend. No authentic writings of Pythagoras survive, and scholars usually ascribe the discoveries to Philolaus.那是傳說。畢達哥拉斯沒有留下可靠的著作，學者通常把這些發現歸給菲洛勞斯。

Protect Your Ears · 保護耳朵

Before you listen 聽之前

  • Start with the volume low and turn it up a little at a time. When two or three notes sound together, the total is louder than one note, so be careful.先把音量調小，再一點一點調大。兩三個音一起響時，總音量比一個音大，要特別小心。
  • With headphones, keep the volume at no more than 60% of maximum, and use personal audio devices for less than one hour a day, as the World Health Organization suggests.戴耳機時，音量不要超過最大音量的 60%，個人音響裝置每天使用少於一小時，這是世界衛生組織的建議。
  • The rough, beating sounds on this page are not harmful at a low volume, but if a sound bothers you, stop it. You do not have to listen to a sound that feels unpleasant.本頁粗糙、起伏的聲音在小音量下沒有害處；但如果某個聲音讓你不舒服，就停下來。你不必勉強聽讓你不舒服的聲音。

Classroom Activity 1 · 課堂活動一

Two Voices, One Note 兩個聲音，一個音

You need · 準備材料

  • Pairs of students兩人一組
  • A keyboard or this page to give the starting note鍵盤或本頁，用來給起始音

Steps · 步驟

  1. Both students sing the same comfortable note together, as steady as they can.兩位同學一起唱同一個舒服的音，盡量穩。
  2. One student slowly slides the pitch a tiny bit higher, then back. Everyone listens for the pulsing, which is beats.其中一位慢慢把音高滑高一點點，再滑回來。大家聽那種一起一伏，就是拍音。
  3. Find the exact moment when the pulsing disappears. That is when the two notes match.找到起伏消失的那一刻。那就是兩個音對上的時候。
  4. Now try to sing a fifth apart. One student holds the note while the other finds the note that sounds blended.再試試相差五度。一位同學唱住音，另一位找出聽起來融在一起的音。

Singing in tune with another voice is hard at first, so let students try several times and praise the effort. Stay in a comfortable range.
一開始要和別人唱得準很難，讓同學多試幾次，肯定他們的努力。待在舒服的音域裡。

  • Paper and pencils紙和鉛筆

Steps · 步驟

  1. Play a major triad and a minor triad on the same root, one after the other. Write down words that describe each one.在同一個根音上，先後彈一個大三和弦和一個小三和弦。把形容它們的詞寫下來。
  2. Count the half steps from the lowest note to the middle note. Write 4 or 3 next to each word list.數最低音到中間音有幾個半音。在每份詞彙旁寫下 4 或 3。
  3. Move both chords to a new root and play them again. Do the words still fit?把兩個和弦移到新的根音再彈一次。這些詞還適用嗎？
  4. Share your lists. Did everybody pick the same words? Why might people differ?分享你們的詞彙。每個人選的詞一樣嗎？為什麼人們可能不一樣？

Accept every honest description. The activity is not a test of the “right” feeling.
每個誠實的形容都接受。這個活動不是在測驗「標準」的感覺。
