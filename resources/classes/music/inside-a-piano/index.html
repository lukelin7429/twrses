Big idea · 一句話看懂

A piano key does not pluck the string. A chain of levers throws a felt hammer at the string and lets it go, so the string can ring freely until the key comes up and the damper falls back.
鋼琴的鍵不是直接去撥弦。一串槓桿把毛氈槌子扔向弦，再讓它放手，弦才能自由地響，直到琴鍵放開、制音器落回去。

Unit 10: What Happens Inside a Piano?

A piano is a keyboard instrument that makes sound when its keys are pressed, which activates an action where hammers strike strings. A modern piano usually has 88 keys: 52 white and 36 black, from A0 to C8. In the Hornbostel–Sachs system it is a chordophone, because strings are what vibrate, and it is played by striking. The name piano is short for pianoforte, from an Italian phrase for a harpsichord with soft and loud.

鋼琴是一種鍵盤樂器：按下琴鍵，啟動擊弦機，讓槌子敲弦而發聲。現代鋼琴通常有 88 個鍵：52 個白鍵、36 個黑鍵，從 A0 到 C8。在霍恩博斯特爾—薩克斯系統裡，它是弦鳴樂器，因為振動的是弦，演奏方式是敲擊。piano 這個名字是 pianoforte 的縮短，來自一句義大利文，意思是「有弱也有強的大鍵琴」。

That name matters. On a harpsichord a note sounds equally loud no matter how hard you press the key. The piano changed that. The harder you press, the faster the hammer moves and the louder the sound. From pianissimo to fortissimo, the hammer speed changes by almost a factor of a hundred. The harder blow is also brighter: the contact between the hammer and the string is shorter, and a shorter contact puts more high partials into the sound.

這個名字很重要。在大鍵琴上，不管你多用力按鍵，一個音都一樣大聲。鋼琴改變了這件事：按得越用力，槌子越快，聲音越大。從 pp 到 ff，槌子的速度差了將近一百倍。用力的一擊也比較亮：槌子和弦接觸的時間比較短，接觸越短，聲音裡的高泛音就越多。

Between your finger and the string is the action, a mechanical assembly that turns the pressing of a key into the rapid motion of a hammer. It amplifies a small movement of the key into a longer, faster movement of the hammer. In a grand piano the rising key lifts a part called the wippen, the wippen carries a jack, and the jack pushes the hammer up toward the string. At the same time, the key lifts the damper, a felt block that normally rests on the string and keeps it still.

在你的手指和弦之間，是擊弦機：一套把按鍵變成槌子快速動作的機械裝置。它把琴鍵一個小小的動作，放大成槌子更長、更快的動作。在平台鋼琴裡，上升的琴鍵抬起一個叫 wippen 的零件，wippen 帶著傳動桿（jack），傳動桿把槌子往弦的方向推。同時，琴鍵也抬起制音器（damper）——一塊平常壓在弦上、讓弦保持不動的毛氈。

Here is the clever part. The hammer must strike the string but not stay on it, because continued contact would damp the sound. So just before the hammer reaches the string, the jack is tripped and slips away. This is called let-off, and it frees the hammer. The hammer flies on by its own momentum, strikes, and rebounds, and a back check catches it. The contact lasts only about 4 thousandths of a second at pianissimo, and under 2 at fortissimo.

巧妙的地方在這裡：槌子必須敲到弦，但不能留在弦上，因為持續接觸會悶住聲音。所以在槌子碰到弦之前，傳動桿被絆開、滑走，這叫脫離（let-off），它讓槌子自由。槌子靠自己的慣性飛過去、敲弦、彈回，再由一個 back check 把它接住。接觸的時間在 pp 時只有約 4 毫秒，在 ff 時不到 2 毫秒。

A piano has one string for each note in the bass, two for each note in the tenor, and three for each note in the treble. They are steel wire, and the bass strings are wound with copper. A string alone is not loud, because a thin string moves little air. Its vibrations go through a bridge to a large soundboard that moves a lot of air. When you let go of the key, the damper falls back on the string, and the sound stops. The strings of a modern grand pull with a total of more than 20 tons.

鋼琴的低音每個音有一條弦，中音每個音有兩條，高音每個音有三條。弦是鋼絲，低音弦外面纏著銅線。一條弦自己不大聲，因為細細的弦推不動多少空氣。它的振動經過琴馬傳到一大塊共鳴板，共鳴板才推動大量的空氣。你放開琴鍵，制音器就落回弦上，聲音就停了。現代平台鋼琴所有弦加起來的拉力可以超過 20 噸。

The pedals change what the dampers and hammers do. The right pedal, the damper pedal, lifts all the dampers, so notes keep ringing after you release the keys. It does not make notes louder, even though it is sometimes called the loud pedal. On a grand piano the left pedal shifts the whole action so the hammers hit two of the three strings, and that changes the timbre as well as the loudness. And the strings are a little stiff, so their overtones are slightly sharp. That is why tuners tune octaves slightly wider than 2 to 1.

踏板改變制音器和槌子的動作。右踏板，制音踏瓣，會把所有制音器抬起，所以放開琴鍵後，音還會繼續響。它不會讓音變大聲，雖然有時被叫做「大聲踏板」。在平台鋼琴上，左踏板把整個擊弦機往旁邊移，槌子只敲三條弦中的兩條，音量和音色都改變了。還有，弦有一點僵硬，所以它的泛音會稍微偏高。這就是為什麼調音師把八度調得比 2 比 1 稍微寬一點。

Questions · 閱讀理解

Why must the hammer leave the string right after it strikes?

Because if the hammer stayed on the string, the contact would damp the vibration and stop the sound. The jack slips away at let-off, so the hammer rebounds and the string rings freely.

Does the damper pedal make notes louder?

No. The pedal lifts all the dampers so the strings keep vibrating after the keys are released. It sustains the sound, but it does not make it louder.

What do piano tuners do to octaves, and why?

They tune octaves slightly wider than the exact 2 to 1. The overtones of a stiff piano string are slightly sharp, so the notes are stretched to match them.

Stories Behind the Notes · 音符背後的故事

A name, a second keyboard, iron and felt, and a wider octave 一個名字、第二個鍵盤、鐵和毛氈，和更寬的八度

Four short stories about how the piano came to be.
關於鋼琴怎麼變成今天這個樣子的四個小故事。

A Name · 名字

Soft and loud弱和強

Piano is short for pianoforte, from an Italian phrase meaning a harpsichord with soft and loud. A 1700 inventory of the Medici family lists an instrument of new invention that produces soft and loud, built by their instrument maker Bartolomeo Cristofori. Dates differ in other sources: Taiwan’s dictionary says about 1709. The three Cristofori pianos that survive today date from the 1720s.
piano 是 pianoforte 的縮短，來自一句義大利文，意思是「有弱也有強的大鍵琴」。1700 年美第奇家族的一份清冊，列了一件「新發明、能發出弱與強」的樂器，由他們的樂器師克里斯托弗里（Bartolomeo Cristofori）製作。別的資料說法不同：台灣的辭典說大約 1709 年。今天還留存的三架克里斯托弗里鋼琴，是 1720 年代的。

The Second Keyboard · 第二個鍵盤

Not the first to answer your touch不是第一個回應手指力道的

The piano was not the first keyboard that could change its sound with touch. The clavichord could, so Wikipedia calls the piano the second keyboard instrument that allows gradations of volume and tone. The harpsichord could not: a note sounds equally loud no matter how hard the key is pressed. The piano’s name says what it added: soft and loud.
鋼琴不是第一個能用手指力道改變聲音的鍵盤。擊弦古鋼琴（clavichord）就可以，所以維基百科說鋼琴是第二個能有音量與音色漸變的鍵盤樂器。大鍵琴做不到：不管多用力按，一個音都一樣大聲。鋼琴的名字說出了它加上的東西：弱與強。

Iron and Felt · 鐵和毛氈

Stronger frames, softer hammers更強的框、更軟的槌

The fortepiano changed by adding a cast iron frame, which allowed much greater string tensions. The first full iron frame for grand pianos was patented in 1843. Felt for the hammers was first used in pianos by Jean-Henri Pape in 1826. Felt has to be soft enough, because a hard hammer would create loud, very high harmonics. Today the strings of a modern grand pull with a total tension of more than 20 tons.
古鋼琴（fortepiano）改變的方式之一，是加上鑄鐵框，讓弦可以承受大得多的張力。第一個全鐵框平台鋼琴的專利是 1843 年。琴槌用毛氈，是帕普（Jean-Henri Pape）1826 年第一個用在鋼琴上。毛氈要夠軟，因為硬的槌子會產生又大聲又很高的泛音。今天現代平台鋼琴的弦，總拉力超過 20 噸。

A Wider Octave · 更寬的八度

Tuning the piano為鋼琴調音

Pianos are usually tuned to a modified version of equal temperament. Because the overtones of a stiff string run progressively sharp, octaves are tuned slightly wider than the exact 2 to 1. How much they are stretched varies from piano to piano, and a longer piano with longer strings has lower inharmonicity. The inharmonicity is not just a fault: it is important for tones to sound piano-like.
鋼琴通常調成「修正過的平均律」。因為僵硬的弦，泛音越高越往上偏，所以八度要調得比精確的 2 比 1 稍微寬一點。拉寬多少，每架鋼琴不一樣；弦比較長的大鋼琴，不諧波性比較低。不諧波性不只是缺點：它是讓聲音聽起來像鋼琴的重要部分。

Myth vs. Fact · 常見迷思

Four things people get wrong 四個常見的誤會

✗ Myth · 迷思The right pedal makes the notes louder.右踏板讓音變大聲。

✓ Fact · 事實It lifts all the dampers so the notes keep ringing after you let go. It does not make them louder, even though it is sometimes called the loud pedal.它把所有制音器抬起，放開琴鍵後音還繼續響。它不會讓音變大聲，雖然有時被叫做「大聲踏板」。

✗ Myth · 迷思The left pedal always moves the hammers closer to the strings.左踏板一定是讓槌子靠近弦。

✓ Fact · 事實That is how it works on an upright piano. On a grand piano it shifts the whole action sideways, so the hammers hit two of the three strings, and the timbre changes too.直立式鋼琴是這樣。在平台鋼琴上，它把整個擊弦機往旁邊移，槌子只敲三條弦中的兩條，音色也會改變。

✗ Myth · 迷思The hammer stays on the string after it hits.槌子敲下去之後會停在弦上。

✓ Fact · 事實It would damp the sound. The hammer rebounds, and it touches the string for only a few thousandths of a second.那會悶住聲音。槌子彈回來，只接觸弦幾毫秒。

✗ Myth · 迷思Every note on a piano has three strings.鋼琴每個音都有三條弦。

✓ Fact · 事實There is one string for each note in the bass, two in the tenor, and three in the treble.低音每個音一條弦，中音兩條，高音三條。

Before you listen 聽之前

  • Start with the volume low and turn it up a little at a time. A loud touch on this page starts with a sharp attack.先把音量調小，再一點一點調大。本頁用力的一擊，一開始有尖銳的起音。
  • With headphones, keep the volume at no more than 60% of maximum, and use personal audio devices for less than one hour a day, as the World Health Organization suggests.戴耳機時，音量不要超過最大音量的 60%，個人音響裝置每天使用少於一小時，這是世界衛生組織的建議。
  • Do not open a real piano or put your hands inside it unless a teacher is with you. Fingers can be hurt by the parts, and the strings are under very high tension.沒有老師在場，不要打開真的鋼琴，也不要把手伸進去。零件會夾傷手指，而且弦的張力很大。

Classroom Activity 1 · 課堂活動一

A Lever on Your Desk 桌上的槓桿

You need · 準備材料

  • A ruler一把尺
  • An eraser or a pencil to use as a pivot橡皮擦或鉛筆當支點
  • A small crumpled paper ball一個小紙球

Steps · 步驟

  1. Put the ruler on the pivot, closer to one end. Place the paper ball on the short end.把尺放在支點上，靠近一端。把紙球放在短的那一端。
  2. Press the long end down slowly by a small amount. What does the short end do?把長的那一端慢慢壓下去一小段。短的那一端怎麼動？
  3. Press quickly. Does the ball fly? Compare how far the two ends move.快一點壓下去。紙球會飛嗎？比較兩端各移動多遠。
  4. Talk about it: how is this like the action of a piano? What does the lever change: the distance, the speed, or both?討論：這和鋼琴的擊弦機像在哪裡？槓桿改變的是距離、速度，還是兩者？

Use a soft paper ball and aim away from faces. A lever can make a small movement longer and faster, which is what the piano action does.
用軟的紙球，並朝向沒有人臉的方向。槓桿可以把小動作變得更長、更快，這正是鋼琴擊弦機做的事。

Classroom Activity 2 · 課堂活動二

Listen to the Damper 聽聽制音器

You need · 準備材料

  • A piano or a keyboard with a sustain pedal, or this page鋼琴、有延音踏板的電子琴，或本頁
  • Paper and pencils紙和鉛筆

Steps · 步驟

  1. Play one note and hold the key down. Listen to how the sound slowly fades, and write what you hear.彈一個音，按著不放。聽聲音慢慢變小，把聽到的寫下來。
  2. Play it again and let go quickly. What changes at the moment you let go?再彈一次，很快放開。放開的那一刻有什麼改變？
  3. Play it again with the damper pedal down, and let go of the key. What happens now?再彈一次，踩著制音踏瓣，放開琴鍵。這次怎麼了？
  4. Draw a timeline of what the key, the hammer, and the damper do in each case.在每種情況下，為琴鍵、槌子和制音器畫一條時間線。

Do not open the piano, and keep fingers away from the strings. Sounds with the pedal down can get louder together, so keep the volume comfortable.
不要打開鋼琴，手指也不要靠近弦。踩著踏板時聲音會疊在一起變大，音量要放在舒服的程度。
