Big idea · 一句話看懂

A note is not one shake but a mix of many shakes at once: the fundamental and its harmonics. The pitch you hear comes from the fundamental. Which harmonics are strong, and how the note begins and fades, give it its tone color.
一個音不是只有一種振動，而是很多種振動同時混在一起：基音和它的諧波。你聽到的音高來自基音；哪些諧波強、這個音怎麼開始又怎麼消失，決定了它的音色。

Unit 2: Why Does a Piano Sound Different from a Violin?

Play middle C on a piano, and then play the same note on a violin. The pitch is the same. The loudness can be the same too. Yet you would never mix them up, even with your eyes closed. Something about the sound is different. Musicians call it tone color, or timbre (say "TAM-ber").

在鋼琴上彈中央 C，再在小提琴上拉同一個音。音高一樣，音量也可以一樣。可是就算閉著眼睛，你也不會把它們搞混。聲音裡有某個地方不一樣。音樂家把它叫做「音色」，英文是 tone color 或 timbre（唸成「坦伯」）。

A well-known standard from the American National Standards Institute describes timbre as the quality that lets a listener tell that two sounds are different, even when they have the same loudness and the same pitch. So timbre is not pitch, and it is not loudness. It is the third thing.

美國國家標準學會有一份很有名的標準，把音色描述成：就算兩個聲音的響度和音高都一樣，聽的人還是能分辨它們不同的那種性質。所以音色不是音高，也不是音量，它是第三件事。

To see where timbre comes from, look inside one note. A string does not shake in only one way. It shakes as a whole, and at the same time in two halves, in three thirds, and so on. Each way of shaking is called a harmonic. The whole-string shake is the fundamental, or the first harmonic. The second harmonic has exactly twice the frequency, and the third has three times, and so on. The second harmonic is also called the first overtone.

要看出音色從哪裡來，得看進一個音的裡面。一根弦不是只有一種振動方式。它整根在振動，同時也一半一半地振動、三分之一三分之一地振動，依此類推。每一種振動方式叫一個「諧波」。整根弦的振動是「基音」，也就是第一諧波；第二諧波的頻率剛好是兩倍，第三諧波是三倍，依此類推。第二諧波也叫做第一泛音。

You do not hear these harmonics as separate notes. You hear one note, and its pitch is the fundamental. But the mix matters. If the harmonics are strong, the sound is bright and buzzy. If only a few are present, the sound is round and soft. The mix of harmonics changes the shape of the sound wave, even though the repeating rate, and so the pitch, stays the same.

你不會把這些諧波聽成一個個分開的音。你聽到的是一個音，音高就是基音。但是混合的比例很重要：諧波強，聲音就明亮、有點嗡嗡的；只有幾個諧波，聲音就圓潤柔和。諧波的混合會改變聲波的形狀，可是重複的快慢不變，所以音高也不變。

Where does the mix come from? Partly from how you start the string. Helmholtz, a German scientist who wrote a famous book on sound in 1863, noticed that a string struck in its middle loses its second partial tone. In the model you can check this: pluck exactly in the middle and every even harmonic disappears, because a node sits right where you pluck. Pluck near the end and many high harmonics are strong. Partly it comes from the instrument itself. A clarinet, for example, mostly makes the odd harmonics.

這個混合從哪裡來？一部分來自你怎麼讓弦動起來。亥姆霍茲是一位德國科學家，1863 年寫了一本關於聲音的名著，他發現弦在正中間被敲時，第二個分音就消失了。你可以在模型裡檢查：剛好在正中間撥，所有偶數諧波都不見了，因為節點剛好在你撥的位置。靠近一端撥，很多高諧波就很強。另一部分來自樂器本身，例如單簧管主要發出奇數諧波。

Time matters too. A piano string is struck by a hammer, so the note starts at once and then fades. A bowed violin string can start more gently and keep going. Reference works on timbre note that if you remove the beginning of a piano or trumpet note, it becomes harder to tell which instrument it is. Our model uses only simple recipes, so it cannot sound like a real piano or violin. Real instruments have many more harmonics and many more details.

時間也很重要。鋼琴的弦是被琴槌敲響的，所以聲音一開始就出現，然後慢慢消失；用弓拉的小提琴弦可以輕輕地開始、持續下去。談音色的參考資料提到，如果把鋼琴或小號的音一開始的那一瞬間拿掉，就比較難聽出是哪一種樂器。我們的模型只用簡單的配方，所以不可能聽起來像真的鋼琴或小提琴；真的樂器有多得多的諧波和細節。

Questions · 閱讀理解

Two notes have the same pitch and the same loudness, but they sound different. What do we call that difference?

It is called timbre, or tone color. Timbre is the quality that tells two sounds apart when their pitch and loudness are the same.

A note has a fundamental of 200 Hz. What are the frequencies of its first four harmonics?

The harmonics are whole-number multiples of the fundamental: 200 Hz, 400 Hz, 600 Hz, and 800 Hz. The first one is the fundamental itself.

Why do the even harmonics disappear when you pluck a string exactly in the middle?

Every even harmonic has a node exactly in the middle of the string, a point that does not move. If you pluck right there, those harmonics cannot start. The odd harmonics have their biggest swing in the middle, so they are strong.

Same Mix, Different Start 同樣的混合，不同的開始

A note also has a shape in time. Attack is how fast it starts, decay is how fast it fades, and sustain is how much is kept. Compare a struck or plucked note, which starts at once and fades, with a bowed note, which starts gently and keeps going. The harmonics are exactly the same in both.
一個音在時間上也有形狀：起音是它多快響起來、衰減是它多快變小聲、維持是它保留多少。比較敲擊或撥奏的音（一開始就響、然後慢慢消失）和拉奏的音（輕輕開始、一直持續）。兩個的諧波完全一樣。

Stories Behind Tone Color · 音色背後的故事

A scientist, a tube, a hammer and a bow, and a word 一位科學家、一根管子、一把槌子和一張弓，和一個詞

Four short stories about how people came to understand the color of sound.
關於人怎麼一步步看懂聲音的「顏色」的四個小故事。

A Scientist · 一位科學家

Helmholtz and the partial tones亥姆霍茲與分音

The German scientist Hermann von Helmholtz published a book on the sensations of tone in 1863. In it he studied the partial tones of strings, tubes, and voices. He noted that when a string is struck in its middle, the second partial tone disappears. The English translation by Alexander Ellis appeared in 1875.
德國科學家亥姆霍茲（Hermann von Helmholtz）在 1863 年出版了一本談聽覺與音的名著，書裡研究了弦、管和人聲的分音。他指出，弦在正中間被敲時，第二個分音就消失了。亞歷山大・艾利斯（Alexander Ellis）的英文譯本在 1875 年出版。

A Tube · 一根管子

Why the clarinet is different單簧管為什麼不一樣

A clarinet is a tube that is almost closed at one end by the reed. Such a tube mostly reinforces the odd harmonics, so a clarinet makes mainly the odd members of the series. The even ones are weak, not completely missing. Helmholtz found the same by ear, up to the seventh partial, and found that instruments with conical tubes, such as the oboe, reinforce all of them.
單簧管是一根一端幾乎被簧片封住的管子。這樣的管子主要加強奇數諧波，所以單簧管主要發出奇數的那幾個；偶數的很弱，但不是完全沒有。亥姆霍茲用耳朵也發現了同樣的事，一直到第七個分音；他還發現雙簧管這類圓錐形管的樂器，會把所有的諧波都加強。

A Hammer and a Bow · 一把槌子和一張弓

Two ways to start a string讓弦動起來的兩種方法

A piano string is struck by a felt-covered hammer, so the sound begins at once and fades. A bow does something different: the string clings to the bow, is carried along, and then suddenly slips back. This stick-and-slip motion can keep the string going. A piano string is also a little stiff, so its higher partials come out slightly above exact whole-number multiples.
鋼琴的弦是被包著毛氈的琴槌敲響的，所以聲音一開始就出現，然後慢慢消失。弓的做法不一樣：弦黏在弓上被帶著走，然後突然滑回來。這種「黏住、滑開」的運動可以讓弦一直振動下去。鋼琴的弦還有一點硬，所以它的高分音會比剛好的整數倍稍微高一點。

A Word · 一個詞

Tone color, 音色, KlangfarbeTone color、音色、Klangfarbe

Helmholtz called it Klangfarbe, German for “tone color,” and English adopted both “tone color” and “timbre.” In Chinese, the dictionary of the Ministry of Education also lists 音品 and 音質 for the same idea. Chinese players use words for it too. The erhu, for example, is described as having a beautiful timbre that ranges from soft to strong.
亥姆霍茲把它叫做 Klangfarbe，德文就是「聲音的顏色」；英文後來用 tone color 和 timbre 兩個詞。在中文裡，教育部辭典把同一個概念也寫作「音品」「音質」。演奏者也用詞彙來形容它，例如二胡被形容為音色優美、表現力強，剛柔多變。

Myth vs. Fact · 常見迷思

Four things people get wrong 四個常見的誤會

✗ Myth · 迷思Two instruments that play the same note make exactly the same sound wave.兩種樂器彈同一個音，發出的聲波一模一樣。

✓ Fact · 事實They share the same repeating rate, so the same pitch. But the mix of harmonics and the way the note starts and fades are different, so the wave shapes differ.它們重複的快慢一樣，所以音高一樣；可是諧波的混合、以及音怎麼開始和消失都不同，所以波形不一樣。

✗ Myth · 迷思You can hear the harmonics as separate notes.你可以把諧波一個一個聽成分開的音。

✓ Fact · 事實Normally you hear one note, and its pitch is the fundamental. The harmonics change the color of that note. It takes practice, or special tools, to pick them out.平常你只聽到一個音，音高就是基音；諧波改變的是這個音的顏色。要把它們一個個挑出來，需要練習或特別的工具。

✗ Myth · 迷思A violin's sound is a sawtooth wave.小提琴的聲音就是鋸齒波。

✓ Fact · 事實A bow drives the string with a motion that looks like a sawtooth, and synthesizers use sawtooth waves as one ingredient for bowed sounds. But the sound of a real violin is far more complicated than any simple recipe.弓帶動弦的運動看起來像鋸齒，合成器也拿鋸齒波當做拉弦聲音的材料之一；但真的小提琴的聲音，比任何簡單的配方都複雜得多。

✗ Myth · 迷思The beginning of a note does not matter for telling instruments apart.要分辨樂器，一個音開頭那一瞬間不重要。

✓ Fact · 事實It matters a lot. If you remove the attack from a piano or trumpet note, it becomes harder to identify, because the hammer strike or the first blast of breath is very characteristic.非常重要。如果把鋼琴或小號的音一開始的那一瞬間拿掉，就比較難辨認，因為槌子敲弦的聲音或第一口氣的聲音，都是那種樂器很有特色的部分。

Remember It · 記憶口訣

Tone color in a sentence 一句話記住音色

Same pitch, different mix音高一樣，混合不一樣

The fundamental sets the pitch. The mix of harmonics sets the color.
基音決定音高，諧波的混合決定顏色。

Harmonics: 1×, 2×, 3×, 4×諧波：1 倍、2 倍、3 倍、4 倍

Each harmonic is a whole-number multiple of the fundamental.
每個諧波都是基音頻率的整數倍。

Pluck in the middle, lose the evens正中間撥，偶數就不見

A node sits where you pluck, so the even harmonics cannot start.
節點剛好在撥的位置，偶數諧波動不起來。

Color is the mix and the start顏色是混合，也是開始

Harmonics, attack, and decay together make a tone color.
諧波、起音、衰減，合起來才是一個音色。

Protect Your Ears · 保護耳朵

Before you listen 聽之前

  • Start with the volume low and turn it up a little at a time. The sound bar on this page starts at 30%.先把音量調小，再一點一點調大。本頁的聲音列一開始是 30%。
  • The mixes on this page are scaled so that the wave never gets taller than the pure tone. A bright mix with many harmonics can still sound sharp. If it hurts, stop and turn the volume down.本頁的混合都會縮放，讓波形不會比純音更高；但諧波很多的明亮混合聽起來還是可能刺耳。如果不舒服，就停下來、把音量調小。
  • With headphones, keep the volume at no more than 60% of maximum in a quiet place, and use personal audio devices for less than one hour a day, as the World Health Organization suggests.戴耳機時，在安靜的地方不要超過最大音量的 60%，個人音響裝置每天使用少於一小時，這是世界衛生組織的建議。
  • In the classroom activities, do not shout or blow into anyone's ear. Sing or hum at a comfortable volume.課堂活動中，不要對著別人的耳朵大叫或吹氣。用舒服的音量唱或哼就好。

Classroom Activity 1 · 課堂活動一

Pluck in the Middle, Pluck Near the End 在中間撥，在靠近一端撥

You need · 準備材料

  • The rubber-band box from lesson 1, or a guitar or ukulele if your class has one第一課的橡皮筋盒子；班上有吉他或烏克麗麗也可以
  • A ruler一把尺
  • A sheet of paper for words一張寫形容詞的紙

Steps · 步驟

  1. Pluck one string right in its middle. Listen, and write a word for how it sounds: round, bright, hollow, thin, buzzy…在弦的正中間撥一下。仔細聽，寫下一個形容它的詞：圓、亮、空、薄、嗡嗡……
  2. Pluck the same string about one tenth of the way from the end. Is the pitch the same? Is the color the same?在離一端大約十分之一的地方撥同一根弦。音高一樣嗎？顏色一樣嗎？
  3. Repeat three times each, and take turns with a partner, who listens with eyes closed and says which one was plucked near the end.各重複三次，兩人輪流；另一個人閉著眼睛聽，說出哪一次是靠近一端撥的。
  4. Compare with the model: it says that plucking near the end gives more high harmonics.和模型比對：模型說，靠近一端撥，高諧波比較多。

The difference is small on a rubber band, so listen carefully and try more than once. If your partner cannot tell, that is a result too. Keep the rubber band away from your face.
橡皮筋上的差別很小，所以要仔細聽、多試幾次。如果同伴聽不出來，那也是一個結果。橡皮筋要離臉遠一點。

Classroom Activity 2 · 課堂活動二

Who Is Singing? Same Note, Different Voices 是誰在唱？同一個音，不同的聲音

You need · 準備材料

  • A pitch from this page: play the C4 key in lesson 1 or the whole string從本頁取一個音：在第一課彈 C4 鍵或整根弦
  • Four or five volunteers, or a recorder or ukulele if you have them四、五位自願的同學；有直笛或烏克麗麗也可以
  • Paper and pencil for each listener每位聽的同學一張紙和一支筆

Steps · 步驟

  1. Everyone listens to the pitch from the page. Each volunteer, one at a time, sings or plays that same pitch for two seconds, behind the others.大家先聽頁面上的音。每位自願的同學一次一位，站在大家後面，把同一個音唱或吹兩秒。
  2. Listeners keep their eyes closed and write down who it was, and one word for the sound.聽的人閉著眼睛，寫下是誰，再寫一個形容聲音的詞。
  3. Now start the note in two ways: very gently, and with a sharp “t” or “d” at the front. Does that change how easy it is to know the sound?再用兩種方式開始這個音：很輕柔地開始，和在前面加一個清脆的「ㄊ」或「ㄉ」。這樣有沒有改變辨認的難易？
  4. Talk about it: pitch was the same, so what told you apart?討論：音高是一樣的，那是什麼讓你分辨出來的？

Each person's voice has its own timbre, just as every instrument does. This is a good chance to practice words for sound in English: bright, dark, warm, soft, sharp, round.
每個人的聲音都有自己的音色，就像每一種樂器一樣。這也是練習用英文形容聲音的好機會：bright、dark、warm、soft、sharp、round。
