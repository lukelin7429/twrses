Big idea · 一句話看懂

A note is high when its string shakes fast and low when it shakes slowly. Cut the vibrating part of a string to half its length and it shakes twice as fast, so the note rises by one octave.
弦振動得快，音就高；振動得慢，音就低。把會振動的那一段縮短一半，振動就快一倍，音剛好高一個八度。

Unit 1: Why Are Some Notes High and Some Low?

Stretch a rubber band around a shoebox and pluck it. You hear a note, and if you touch the band lightly, you can feel it shaking. Now press the band against the box with one finger, so that only half of it is free to move, and pluck it again. The note sounds higher. The rubber band is the same and your pluck is the same. Only one thing has changed: how much of the band can shake.

把一條橡皮筋套在鞋盒上撥一下，你會聽到一個音；輕輕碰著橡皮筋，還能感覺到它在抖動。現在用一根手指把橡皮筋壓在盒子上，讓只剩一半能自由活動，再撥一次，音變高了。橡皮筋沒換，撥的力氣也一樣，只有一件事不同：能振動的部分有多長。

Musicians call how high or low a note sounds its pitch. Pitch depends on how fast something vibrates, which means how many times it shakes back and forth in one second. That number is called the frequency, and it is measured in hertz (Hz). One hertz is one vibration per second. The faster the vibration, the higher the pitch. The unit is named after the German physicist Heinrich Hertz.

音樂家把一個音聽起來有多高或多低，叫做「音高」。音高取決於東西振動得多快，也就是一秒鐘來回抖動幾次。這個次數叫「頻率」，單位是「赫茲」（Hz）。一赫茲就是每秒振動一次。振動越快，音高越高。這個單位是以德國物理學家海因里希・赫茲（Heinrich Hertz）命名的。

Be careful not to mix up pitch and loudness. A big, strong shake makes a loud sound, while a fast shake makes a high sound. These are two different things. A string can swing widely but slowly, which gives a loud, low note. It can also swing only a little but very quickly, which gives a quiet, high note.

要小心，不要把音高和音量搞混。大力、大幅度的抖動讓聲音大，快速的抖動讓聲音高，這是兩回事。一根弦可以擺得很寬但很慢，發出又大聲又低的音；也可以只擺一點點但很快，發出小聲又高的音。

On a string instrument, the length of the part that vibrates helps decide the pitch. If two strings are made of the same material and are equally tight, the shorter one vibrates faster, so it sounds higher. A violin player presses a finger on a string, and this makes the part that can shake shorter. The double bass has longer strings than the other instruments of the violin family, and its notes are very low.

在弦樂器上，會振動的那一段有多長，會影響音高。如果兩根弦的材料相同、拉得一樣緊，短的那一根振動得比較快，所以音比較高。小提琴手用手指按住一根弦，就是讓能振動的那一段變短。低音大提琴的弦比提琴家族的其他樂器長，音非常低。

There is a neat rule behind this. When the string is the same, the frequency goes up exactly as the length of the vibrating part goes down. Half the length gives twice the frequency. A note with exactly double the frequency sounds like the same note, only higher, and musicians call the gap between the two notes an octave. In our model, the whole string vibrates 261.63 times every second. Press it at the middle and the frequency is 523.25 Hz. The two notes are C4 and C5, one octave apart.

這背後有一條簡單的規則：同樣的弦，會振動的那一段變短多少，頻率就等比例變高。長度一半，頻率就是兩倍。頻率剛好是兩倍的音，聽起來像同一個音、只是更高，音樂家把這兩個音的距離叫「八度」。在我們的模型裡，整根弦每秒振動 261.63 次；在正中間按住，頻率就變成 523.25 赫。這兩個音是 C4 和 C5，相差一個八度。

Other simple fractions work too. Two thirds of the string makes a note that forms a musical fifth with the whole string, and three quarters makes a fourth. The ancient Greeks studied these whole-number ratios on a one-string instrument called a monochord. A modern piano is tuned a little differently, though. It divides every octave into twelve equal steps, so its notes come very close to these simple ratios but are not exactly the same. For two thirds of a string, the difference is only about two cents, and a cent is one hundredth of a piano step. Your ear can hardly tell, but the numbers can.

其他簡單的分數也行得通。弦長的三分之二發出的音，和整根弦構成音樂上的「五度」；四分之三則構成「四度」。古希臘人在一種叫「單弦琴」（monochord）的樂器上研究這些整數比。不過現代鋼琴的調法稍有不同：它把每個八度分成十二個相等的階梯，所以鋼琴的音很接近這些簡單比例，卻不完全一樣。弦長三分之二的音，差距只有大約兩音分；一音分是鋼琴一個階梯的百分之一。耳朵幾乎聽不出來，數字卻看得出來。

Questions · 閱讀理解

How can you make a rubber band sound higher without changing the band?

Make the part that can shake shorter, for example by pressing a finger on it, or stretch the band tighter. The shorter or tighter band vibrates faster, so the pitch is higher.

Why can't we see a guitar string shake 261 times a second, and what does the model do about it?

It shakes too fast for our eyes. The model slows the shaking down about 174 times, so the whole string shakes 1.5 times per second and the other lengths are slowed by the same amount. That way you can still see which one is faster.

A friend says, "The higher note must be the louder note." What would you say?

Pitch and loudness are different. Pitch depends on how fast the string vibrates. Loudness depends on how big the vibration is. A quiet note can be high, and a loud note can be low.

Stories Behind the Notes · 音高背後的故事

A legend, a one-string instrument, a pitch standard, and a prince 一個傳說、一把單弦琴、一個標準音，和一位王子

Four short stories about how people learned to measure and agree on pitch.
關於人怎麼量出音高、又怎麼對音高取得共識的四個小故事。

A Legend · 一個傳說

Pythagoras and the blacksmiths畢達哥拉斯與鐵匠鋪

A famous story says that Pythagoras walked past a blacksmith's shop, heard the hammers ring with pleasing notes, and found that their weights were in simple ratios. The oldest full version we have was written down in the first or second century AD. It cannot be true as told, because hammers do not make notes in proportion to their weight. The simple ratios are real, but they belong to string lengths.
有個有名的故事說，畢達哥拉斯經過鐵匠鋪，聽到鐵錘敲出好聽的音，發現鐵錘的重量成簡單的比例。我們看得到最早的完整版本，是西元一、二世紀寫下來的。照故事那樣講是不可能的，因為鐵錘敲出的音高並不和重量成比例。簡單的整數比是真的，但它們屬於弦的長度。

An Instrument · 一件樂器

The monochord單弦琴

A monochord is a single string stretched over a box with a movable bridge, very much like our model. The Greeks used it to measure the musical concords. Their results were whole-number ratios: the octave is 2 to 1, the fifth is 3 to 2, and the fourth is 4 to 3. The movable bridge is the same idea as your finger on a violin string.
單弦琴是在箱子上拉一根弦、再加一個可以移動的琴橋，和我們的模型很像。希臘人用它來量音樂上的「協和音程」，得到的是整數比：八度是 2 比 1、五度是 3 比 2、四度是 4 比 3。可移動的琴橋，和你的手指按在小提琴弦上是同一個道理。

A Standard · 一個標準

Why concert A is 440為什麼標準音是 440 赫

Pitch used to be different from place to place and from century to century. A law passed in France on February 16, 1859, set the A above middle C at 435 Hz. In May 1939, delegates met at Broadcasting House in London and agreed on 440 Hz. The International Organization for Standardization took it up as a recommendation in 1955 and formalized it as ISO 16 in 1975. Even now not every orchestra follows it.
過去各地、各個世紀的音高都不一樣。法國在 1859 年 2 月 16 日通過法律，把中央 C 上面的 A 定為 435 赫。1939 年 5 月，各國代表在倫敦的 BBC 廣播大樓開會，同意採用 440 赫。國際標準化組織（ISO）在 1955 年把它當作建議採用，1975 年正式定為 ISO 16。直到現在，也不是每個樂團都照著用。

A Number · 一個數字

Zhu Zaiyu and 1.0595朱載堉與 1.0595

Zhu Zaiyu was a prince of the Ming dynasty who spent thirty years on the mathematics of tuning. In 1584 he described equal temperament: each of the twelve steps in an octave multiplies the frequency by the same number, about 1.0595. Some people think his work may have reached Europe, but there is no evidence for that. Equal temperament has been the main tuning system of Western music since the twentieth century.
朱載堉是明朝的王子，花了三十年研究調音的數學。1584 年，他說明了十二平均律：一個八度裡的十二個階梯，每一階都乘上同一個數，大約是 1.0595。有人認為他的研究可能傳到了歐洲，但沒有證據。二十世紀以來，十二平均律是西方音樂最主要的調音系統。

Myth vs. Fact · 常見迷思

Four things people get wrong 四個常見的誤會

✗ Myth · 迷思A higher note is a louder note.音越高，就越大聲。

✓ Fact · 事實Pitch and loudness are different properties of a sound. Pitch depends on how fast something vibrates. Loudness depends on how strongly it vibrates. A tiny, fast shake gives a quiet, high note.音高和音量是聲音不同的性質。音高看振動得多快，音量看振動得多用力。很小、很快的抖動，會發出小聲又高的音。

✗ Myth · 迷思Pythagoras found the notes by listening to blacksmiths' hammers.畢達哥拉斯是聽鐵匠敲鐵錘才發現音階的。

✓ Fact · 事實The story is a legend, and it would not work: the pitch of a hammer is not proportional to its weight. The simple ratios are real for strings, and the Greeks studied them on a monochord.這是傳說，而且照故事那樣做行不通：鐵錘敲出的音高和重量並不成比例。簡單的整數比在弦上是真的，希臘人就是用單弦琴研究它們。

✗ Myth · 迷思Two thirds of a string gives exactly the note you find on a piano.弦長三分之二，剛好就是鋼琴上那個音。

✓ Fact · 事實It is very close, but not exact. Two thirds of a C4 string gives 392.44 Hz, and the piano's G4 is 391.99 Hz, about two cents lower. The piano splits the octave into twelve equal steps instead.非常接近，但不完全一樣。C4 的弦取三分之二，得到 392.44 赫；鋼琴的 G4 是 391.99 赫，低了大約兩音分。鋼琴是把八度分成十二個相等的階梯。

✗ Myth · 迷思If something shakes, you can always hear it.只要東西在抖，你就一定聽得到。

✓ Fact · 事實People hear frequencies from about 20 Hz to 20,000 Hz. Vibrations below 20 Hz are called infrasound, and those above 20,000 Hz are called ultrasound. We cannot hear them, although some animals can.人聽得到的頻率大約是 20 赫到 20,000 赫。低於 20 赫叫次聲波，高於 20,000 赫叫超聲波；我們聽不見，有些動物卻聽得見。

Remember It · 記憶口訣

Pitch in a sentence 一句話記住音高

Fast shake, high note抖得快，音就高

Slow shake, low note. Pitch follows the speed of the vibration.
抖得慢，音就低。音高跟著振動的快慢走。

Half the string, one octave up弦短一半，高八度

Half the length, double the frequency.
長度一半，頻率兩倍。

Double the hertz, same note, higher赫茲加倍，同名更高

220 Hz, 440 Hz, 880 Hz: each is the same note, one octave up.
220 赫、440 赫、880 赫：每個都是同名的音，一個比一個高八度。

Pitch is speed; volume is size音高看快慢，音量看大小

How fast the string shakes sets the pitch. How far it swings sets the volume.
弦抖得多快決定音高，擺得多遠決定音量。

Protect Your Ears · 保護耳朵

Before you listen 聽之前

  • Start with the volume low and turn it up a little at a time. The sound bar on this page starts at 30%. Never begin with the volume at maximum.先把音量調小，再一點一點調大。本頁的聲音列一開始是 30%。千萬不要一開始就把音量開到最大。
  • With headphones, set a comfortable volume in a quiet place and keep it to no more than 60% of maximum. The World Health Organization also suggests using personal audio devices for less than one hour a day.戴耳機時，在安靜的地方調到舒適的音量，不要超過最大音量的 60%。世界衛生組織也建議，每天使用個人音響裝置的時間少於一小時。
  • The World Health Organization recommends a safe level of 80 decibels for 40 hours a week, and the louder the sound, the shorter the safe time. Music through headphones at full volume is about 94 to 110 decibels.世界衛生組織建議的安全聆聽量是每週 40 小時、80 分貝；聲音越大，安全的時間越短。耳機開到最大音量的音樂，大約是 94 到 110 分貝。
  • Do not put your ear against a speaker or hold a high tone for a long time. The hearing-range slider plays for three seconds at most and is kept quiet on purpose. If a sound hurts, stop and turn the volume down.不要把耳朵貼在喇叭上，也不要長時間聽很高的音。聽力範圍的滑桿最多只播三秒，而且刻意壓得很小聲。如果聲音讓你不舒服，就停下來、把音量調小。

Classroom Activity 1 · 課堂活動一

A Rubber-Band Guitar 橡皮筋琴

You need · 準備材料

  • An open box, such as a tissue box with no lid, or a shoebox一個沒有蓋子的盒子，例如面紙盒或鞋盒
  • Three rubber bands of the same thickness三條一樣粗的橡皮筋
  • A pencil to use as a bridge, and a ruler一支當琴橋的鉛筆，和一把尺
  • Safety glasses, or keep your face to the side護目鏡，或把臉轉到旁邊

Steps · 步驟

  1. Stretch the three rubber bands around the box, a few centimeters apart. Slide the pencil under them at one end to make a bridge.把三條橡皮筋套在盒子上，彼此隔幾公分。在一端把鉛筆塞到橡皮筋底下，當作琴橋。
  2. Pluck one band and listen. This is your whole string.撥一條橡皮筋，聽聽看。這就是你的「整根弦」。
  3. Press the band against the box at its middle with one finger and pluck the free half. Is the note higher or lower?用一根手指把橡皮筋在正中間壓在盒子上，撥另一半。音變高還是變低？
  4. Measure the part that can shake with the ruler. Try a half and three quarters. Write down the length and whether the note is higher or lower.用尺量出會振動的那一段。試試一半和四分之三。記下長度，以及音是比較高還是比較低。
  5. Now change one more thing: pull one band tighter. Does the pitch go up or down? Change only one thing at a time.再改變另一件事：把一條橡皮筋拉得更緊。音高是升還是降？一次只改變一件事。

A shorter band and a tighter band both sound higher. Our model keeps the tension fixed, so that only the length changes. Do not stretch a band until it snaps, because it can fly back at your face.
橡皮筋變短、或拉得更緊，音都會變高。我們的模型固定張力，只改變長度。不要把橡皮筋拉到快斷，它彈回來可能打到臉。

Classroom Activity 2 · 課堂活動二

Tapping the Water Cups 敲水杯

You need · 準備材料

  • Five identical plastic cups (plastic is safer than glass)五個一模一樣的塑膠杯（塑膠比玻璃安全）
  • Water and a spoon水和一支湯匙
  • A marker and a sheet of paper for your table一支簽字筆和一張記錄用的紙

Steps · 步驟

  1. Line up the cups. Pour a different amount of water into each one, from almost empty to almost full.把杯子排成一排，每個杯子倒入不一樣多的水，從幾乎沒有到幾乎滿。
  2. Tap each cup lightly with the spoon. Which one sounds highest, and which sounds lowest?用湯匙輕輕敲每個杯子。哪一個最高、哪一個最低？
  3. Put the cups in order from the lowest note to the highest. Write the order on your paper.把杯子照音高從低到高排好，把順序寫在紙上。
  4. Predict first, then test: if you blow across the top of a bottle with water in it instead of tapping, will the order stay the same?先預測，再測試：如果不用敲的，改成對著裝了水的瓶口吹氣，順序會不會一樣？

Tapping makes the cup and the water vibrate. Blowing across a bottle makes the air inside vibrate. Different things vibrate, so the order of the notes can change. Which part is shaking in each case?
敲的時候，是杯子和水在振動；對著瓶口吹氣，是瓶子裡的空氣在振動。振動的東西不一樣，所以音的順序可能會不同。每一種情況裡，是哪個部分在抖？
