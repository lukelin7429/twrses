Big idea · 一句話看懂

A beat is a steady pulse that cuts time into equal steps. Strong and weak beats come back in a repeating pattern, which groups them into bars of two, three, or four. The speed is counted in beats per minute.
拍是穩定的脈搏，把時間切成一樣長的格。強拍和弱拍照著一個圖樣一直回來，把拍分成二拍、三拍或四拍的小節。速度則用每分鐘幾拍來數。

Unit 3: What Is a Beat?

Think of a song you love and tap your foot along with it. Everyone in the room taps at the same speed, and nobody had to count. What you are tapping is the beat. A beat is the basic unit of time in music, the pulse, and it keeps going whether the melody has a long note or a short one.

想一首你喜歡的歌，跟著它用腳打拍子。房間裡每個人敲的速度都一樣，而且沒有人需要數。你敲的就是拍。拍是音樂裡最基本的時間單位，是脈搏；不管旋律是長音還是短音，它都一直走下去。

Beats come one after another, each the same length, like the ticks of a clock. We say how fast the beats are with the word tempo, which is Italian for “time.” Tempo is measured in beats per minute, or BPM. At 60 BPM there is exactly one beat every second, like the second hand of a clock. At 120 BPM the beats are twice as fast. For comparison, the resting pulse of a person aged ten or older is 60 to 100 beats per minute, counted in the same unit.

拍一個接著一個，每一拍一樣長，像時鐘的滴答。我們用「速度」（tempo，義大利文是「時間」的意思）來說拍有多快，單位是每分鐘幾拍，簡稱 BPM。60 BPM 就是每秒剛好一拍，像時鐘的秒針；120 BPM 就快一倍。作為對照，十歲以上的人靜止時的脈搏是每分鐘 60 到 100 下，用的是同一種單位。

But beats are not all the same. Some feel stronger and some weaker, and the pattern keeps coming back. A set of beats from one strong beat to the next is called a bar, or a measure. The first beat of the bar is the downbeat, and it is usually the strongest. The pattern of strong and weak beats is the meter.

可是拍不全是一樣的。有些感覺比較強、有些比較弱，而且這個圖樣一直回來。從一個強拍到下一個強拍之間的拍，合成一組，叫做一個小節（measure，也叫 bar）。小節的第一拍是強拍（downbeat），通常最強。強弱拍的圖樣叫做拍子（meter）。

The most common patterns are twos, threes, and fours. In two beats, the pattern is strong, weak, like a march step. In three beats, it is strong, weak, weak, and you count one-two-three. In four beats, it is strong, weak, medium, weak. Written music shows this with a time signature. In 3/4, the top number says there are three beats in each bar, and the bottom number says that a quarter note gets one beat.

最常見的圖樣是二拍、三拍和四拍。二拍子是強、弱，像行進的腳步；三拍子是強、弱、弱，數成一二三；四拍子是強、弱、次強、弱。樂譜用「拍子記號」（拍號）把它寫下來。在 3/4 裡，上面的數字說每小節有三拍，下面的數字說以四分音符為一拍。

Chinese traditional music has its own names for the same idea. The strongest beat is the 板 (bǎn), struck on a wooden clapper. The weaker beats are the 眼 (yǎn), marked with a drumstick. “One 板 and one 眼” means two beats in a bar, and “one 板 and three 眼” means four. In nanguan music, the beats are counted in 撩拍, with its own clapper called the 拍板.

中國傳統音樂對同一件事有自己的名字。最強的拍叫「板」，用拍板敲出來；比較弱的拍叫「眼」，用鼓簽標出來。「一板一眼」是一小節兩拍，「一板三眼」是一小節四拍。南管音樂則用「撩拍」來數拍，並且有自己叫做「拍板」的樂器。

A metronome keeps the beat for you. The mechanical kind has a rod that swings like a pendulum and a weight that slides up the rod to make it slower. A German inventor named Johann Maelzel patented one in 1815, after an earlier design by Dietrich Winkel. Beethoven was soon the first notable composer to write metronome numbers in his music. Even so, the old Italian words such as allegro and andante are only rough guides, and different books give them different numbers.

節拍器可以替你保持拍子。機械式的節拍器有一根像鐘擺一樣擺動的桿子，和一個沿著桿子往上滑就會變慢的重錘。德國發明家約翰・梅爾策爾（Johann Maelzel）在 1815 年取得專利，在這之前已有迪特里希・溫克爾（Dietrich Winkel）的設計。貝多芬很快就成為第一位把節拍器數字寫進樂譜的知名作曲家。即使如此，allegro、andante 這些舊的義大利文，也只是大略的指引，不同的書給它們的數字也不一樣。

Questions · 閱讀理解

A song is played at 120 BPM. How long is one beat, and how many beats are there in 10 seconds?

One beat lasts 60 ÷ 120 = 0.5 seconds. In 10 seconds there are 10 ÷ 0.5 = 20 beats.

What does the time signature 3/4 tell you?

The top number, 3, means there are three beats in each bar. The bottom number, 4, means that a quarter note gets one beat. The pattern is usually strong, weak, weak.

A friend says, “Tempo and beat are the same thing.” How would you explain the difference?

The beat is the steady pulse itself, one step of equal length after another. Tempo is how fast those beats go, usually counted in beats per minute.

Stories Behind the Beat · 拍子背後的故事

Four short stories about how people keep and name the beat.

A Clapper · 一塊拍板

板 and 眼 in Chinese music中國音樂裡的板與眼

In traditional Chinese opera and music, the strongest beat of a bar is the 板, struck with a wooden clapper. The weaker beats are the 眼, marked with a drumstick. So “one 板 and three 眼” is a bar of four beats. The phrase also became an everyday saying for someone who does things by the book. In nanguan music, the beat is called 撩拍 instead, kept with a clapper made of several wooden pieces.
在中國傳統戲曲與音樂裡，小節裡最強的拍叫「板」，用拍板敲出來；比較弱的拍叫「眼」，用鼓簽標出來。所以「一板三眼」就是一小節四拍。這句話後來也變成一句日常的俗語，形容人做事一絲不苟。南管音樂則改稱「撩拍」，用好幾片木板做成的拍板來打拍子。

A Pendulum · 一個擺錘

How a mechanical metronome works機械節拍器怎麼運作

A mechanical metronome uses an upside-down pendulum: a rod with a fixed weight at the bottom and a weight you can slide. Slide it up and the rod swings slower. Slide it down and it swings faster. Most are adjustable from about 40 to 208 beats per minute. Dietrich Winkel built a version in Amsterdam in 1814, and Johann Maelzel added a numbered scale and patented it in 1815.
機械節拍器用的是倒過來的擺：一根桿子，下面有固定的重錘，另外還有一個可以滑動的重錘。往上滑，擺得比較慢；往下滑，擺得比較快。大多數可以調到大約每分鐘 40 到 208 拍。迪特里希・溫克爾 1814 年在阿姆斯特丹做出一種版本，約翰・梅爾策爾加上有數字的刻度，並在 1815 年申請專利。

A Number · 一個數字

Beethoven and M.M.貝多芬與 M.M.

Maelzel knew Beethoven, and Beethoven became the first notable composer to write specific metronome marks in his music. This was in December 1815, in the corrected score of his Cantata op. 112. Since then, a mark such as M.M. = 60, for Maelzel’s Metronome, has told a player exactly how fast to go: sixty beats per minute.
梅爾策爾認識貝多芬，而貝多芬成為第一位把明確的節拍器數字寫進樂譜的知名作曲家。那是 1815 年 12 月，在他的清唱劇作品 112 的修正稿上。從那時起，像 M.M. = 60（M.M. 是梅爾策爾節拍器的縮寫）這樣的標記，就明確告訴演奏者要多快：每分鐘六十拍。

Some Words · 幾個詞

Tempo, allegro, andante, prestoTempo、allegro、andante、presto

Tempo is the Italian word for “time.” Well-known Italian speed words include allegro, which means “cheerful,” andante, “walking pace,” and presto, “quickly.” People often give each word a range of BPM numbers, but the books do not agree, and one reference calls the numbers “very rough approximations.” The speed words tell you a feeling and a rough speed, and a metronome mark tells you the exact one.
Tempo 是義大利文的「時間」。有名的義大利文速度詞包括 allegro（輕快、愉快）、andante（走路的速度）和 presto（很快）。人們常給每個詞一個 BPM 的範圍，但不同的書意見不一，有一份參考資料還說這些數字只是「非常粗略的近似」。速度詞告訴你一種感覺和大略的快慢，節拍器的標記才告訴你精確的數字。

Myth vs. Fact · 常見迷思

Four things people get wrong 四個常見的誤會

✗ Myth · 迷思Beat and rhythm are exactly the same thing.拍和節奏是完全一樣的東西。

✓ Fact · 事實The beat is the steady pulse that you would tap your foot to. Rhythm is the pattern of long and short sounds that moves over it, and it can be much more complicated than the beat.拍是你會用腳去打的穩定脈搏；節奏是在它上面進行的長短聲音的圖樣，可以比拍複雜得多。

✗ Myth · 迷思All beats have the same strength.每一拍的力度都一樣。

✓ Fact · 事實In most music some beats are stressed and some are unstressed. The pattern of strong and weak beats coming back again and again is what we call meter.大多數音樂裡，有些拍是重的、有些是輕的。強弱拍一再回來的圖樣，就叫拍子（meter）。

✗ Myth · 迷思A faster song has more beats in each bar.越快的歌，每小節的拍數越多。

✓ Fact · 事實The number of beats in a bar and the speed are two different things. A song in three beats can be slow or fast. The top number of the time signature counts beats in a bar. The BPM counts beats in a minute.每小節幾拍和速度是兩件事。三拍子的歌可以慢也可以快。拍號上面的數字數的是每小節的拍數，BPM 數的是每分鐘的拍數。

✗ Myth · 迷思“Allegro” means exactly 120 BPM.「Allegro」就是剛好每分鐘 120 拍。

✓ Fact · 事實The speed words are rough guides. Two common references give different ranges for allegro, and even the order of two of the words, largo and adagio, is the opposite of what it was in the Baroque period. Only a metronome mark gives an exact number.速度詞只是大略的指引。兩份常見的參考資料給 allegro 的範圍就不一樣，連 largo 和 adagio 兩個詞誰快誰慢，現在也和巴洛克時期相反。只有節拍器標記才給精確的數字。

In 3/4, the top number says three beats, the bottom number says quarter notes.
3/4 裡，上面說三拍，下面說以四分音符為一拍。

Before You Start · 開始之前

A few reminders 幾個提醒

  • The lights and dots flash with the beat, up to three times a second at 180 BPM. For some people with photosensitive epilepsy, lights flashing at about three to sixty times a second can trigger a seizure. If you or anyone in your class is sensitive, switch the flashing off with the switch under the model. The sound and the pendulum still work.燈和圓點會跟著拍子閃，180 BPM 時每秒最多三次。對患有光敏感性癲癇的人來說，大約每秒三次到六十次的閃光可能引發發作。如果你或班上有人是敏感的，請用模型下方的開關把閃爍關掉；聲音和擺錘仍然會動。
  • Start with the volume low and turn it up a little at a time. The sound bar on this page starts at 30%.先把音量調小，再一點一點調大。本頁的聲音列一開始是 30%。
  • With headphones, keep the volume at no more than 60% of maximum, and use personal audio devices for less than one hour a day, as the World Health Organization suggests.戴耳機時，音量不要超過最大音量的 60%，個人音響裝置每天使用少於一小時，這是世界衛生組織的建議。
  • When you tap or clap in class, use your hands or a soft pad. Do not hit a phone screen or a desk hard, and do not clap close to anyone’s ears.在課堂上敲或拍手時，用手或軟墊就好。不要用力敲手機螢幕或桌子，也不要在別人耳朵旁邊拍手。

Classroom Activity 1 · 課堂活動一

Your Pulse and the Metronome 你的脈搏和節拍器

You need · 準備材料

  • A clock or timer with seconds有秒針或秒數的時鐘或計時器
  • Paper and pencil紙和筆
  • This page, for the metronome本頁的節拍器

Steps · 步驟

  1. Sit quietly for a minute. Put two fingers on the inside of your wrist, gently, and find your pulse.安靜坐一分鐘。把兩根手指輕輕放在手腕內側，找到你的脈搏。
  2. Count the beats for 15 seconds and multiply by four. That is your pulse in beats per minute.數 15 秒的下數，乘以四，就是你每分鐘的脈搏數。
  3. Set the metronome to a number close to yours. Listen: is your pulse faster or slower than the clicks?把節拍器調到接近你的數字。聽聽看：你的脈搏比喀喀聲快還是慢？
  4. Jump up and down for 30 seconds and count again. What changed? Compare your numbers with the 60 to 100 that is normal at rest.原地跳 30 秒，再數一次。有什麼改變？把你的數字和靜止時正常的 60 到 100 比一比。

This is not a medical test. Everyone's pulse is a little different. Do not press hard on your neck. Use your wrist.
這不是醫療檢查，每個人的脈搏都有一點不一樣。不要用力壓脖子，用手腕就好。

Classroom Activity 2 · 課堂活動二

Human Metronome: 板 and 眼 人體節拍器：板與眼

You need · 準備材料

  • A desk or a pair of wooden blocks for the strong beat一張桌子或一對木塊，當作強拍
  • Your hands, for the weak beats你的雙手，當作弱拍
  • The metronome on this page本頁的節拍器

Steps · 步驟

  1. Set the metronome to 80 BPM and four beats in a bar. Listen to the high click, the strong beat.把節拍器調到 80 BPM、每小節四拍。聽那個比較高的喀，就是強拍。
  2. Strike the desk on the strong beat, the 板, and tap your knee on every other beat, the 眼. That is “one 板 and three 眼.”在強拍（板）敲桌子，其他每一拍在膝蓋上輕拍（眼）。這就是「一板三眼」。
  3. Switch to two beats in a bar: “one 板 and one 眼.” Then to three beats. Which one feels like a march? Which one feels like a waltz?換成每小節兩拍：「一板一眼」。再換成三拍。哪一種像行進？哪一種像圓舞曲？
  4. Take turns being the metronome for the class: one student keeps the 板, and the class keeps the 眼.輪流當全班的節拍器：一位同學打「板」，全班打「眼」。

Keep the volume of the metronome low, and use the soft part of your hand. Tapping softly lets everyone hear the clicks.
節拍器音量調小，用手掌軟的部分輕拍，這樣大家才聽得到喀喀聲。
