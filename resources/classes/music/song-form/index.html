Big idea · 一句話看懂

A song is built from repetition and contrast. A section that comes back is repetition; a new section is contrast.

Unit 17: How Is a Song Put Together?

Song structure is the arrangement of a song. It is typically sectional, which means a song uses repeating forms. In musical form, the first statement of a musical idea is called A. Later contrasting sections are labeled B, C, D, and so on. Musical form shows the importance of two principles: repetition and contrast.

歌曲結構（song structure）是一首歌的編排。它通常是分段的，也就是一首歌使用重複的形式。在曲式裡，一個音樂想法第一次出現叫 A，之後對比的段落依序叫 B、C、D 等。曲式顯示了兩個原則的重要：重複與對比。

A verse of a song is a repeated sung melody where the words change from use to use. Each verse usually employs the same melody, while the lyrics usually change. A song structure in which all verses are sung to the same music is called strophic. Many folk and popular songs are strophic, including ballads and hymns.

歌曲的一段「主歌」（verse）是一段重複演唱的旋律，歌詞每次都不同。每一段主歌通常用同一條旋律，歌詞則通常每段不同。所有詩節都用同一段音樂來唱的歌曲結構，叫做分節歌（strophic）。許多民謠和流行歌是分節歌，包括敘事歌謠與聖詩。

The chorus, or refrain, is the part of the song that repeats at least once, both musically and lyrically. It usually retains the same set of lyrics every time its music appears, and it is normally the most memorable part. Verse–chorus form has two main sections that often contrast in melody, rhythm, harmony, and loudness. The two words chorus and refrain are often used interchangeably.

副歌（chorus 或 refrain）是歌曲裡至少重複一次的部分，音樂與歌詞都重複。每次它的音樂出現，歌詞通常都一樣，也通常是最容易記住的部分。主歌—副歌的形式有兩個主要段落，兩者常在旋律、節奏、和聲與力度上形成對比。chorus 和 refrain 這兩個詞常常互換使用。

A bridge is a contrasting section that prepares for the return of the original material. It is a device used to break up the repetitive pattern of the song and keep the listener’s attention. The verse and the chorus are usually repeated throughout a song. The intro, the bridge, and the outro are usually used only once.

橋段（bridge）是一個對比的段落，為原來的材料回來做準備。它是用來打破歌曲的重複模式、保持聽眾注意力的手法。主歌和副歌通常在整首歌中重複出現。前奏、橋段和結尾通常只用一次。

An introduction generally has just music and no words. An outro is a way of finishing or completing the song. Pop songs may have an introduction and an ending, but these parts are not essential to the identity of most songs. The dictionary also lists the interlude: music inserted between the main sections of a song.

前奏一般只有音樂、沒有歌詞。結尾（outro）是結束或完成一首歌的方式。流行歌可以有前奏和結尾，但這些部分對大多數歌的特色並不是必要的。辭典還列了間奏：插在歌曲主要段落之間的音樂。

There is no single order. Song form terminology is not standardized, and not every song has both a verse and a chorus. Some songs have only verses. Others, in the thirty-two-bar form, have an A section that shares the same melody three times with a contrasting B section. This is why we use the letters A, B, and C in this lesson, and not names. Can you hear what comes back and what is new?

沒有唯一的順序。歌曲形式的術語並沒有標準化，也不是每首歌都同時有主歌和副歌。有些歌只有主歌。有的是三十二小節形式：A 段用同一條旋律三次，加上一個對比的 B 段。所以這一課用字母 A、B、C，不用名稱。你聽得出什麼回來了、什麼是新的嗎？

Questions · 閱讀理解

What is the difference between repetition and contrast?

Repetition is when a section comes back; contrast is when a new, different section appears.

Does every song have both a verse and a chorus?

No. Some songs have only verses, and song form terminology is not standardized.

Why do we use the letters A, B, and C in this lesson?

Because names like verse, chorus, and bridge are used differently in different songs; letters just show what comes back and what is new.

Stories Behind the Notes · 音符背後的故事

Letters, two words, a bridge in Chinese, and a round 字母、兩個詞、中文的橋段，和輪旋曲

Four short notes about words and forms.
關於詞彙與曲式的四個小註解。

Letters · 字母

A, B, C, and no standard namesA、B、C，沒有標準名稱

Musical form uses letters. The first statement of an idea is A, and the contrasting sections that follow are labeled B, C, D, and so on. Song form terminology is not standardized, so one writer’s chorus may be another’s refrain. Taiwan’s dictionary defines 曲式 simply as the way a piece of music is built.
曲式用字母。一個想法的第一次出現是 A，接著對比的段落依序標為 B、C、D 等。歌曲形式的術語並沒有標準化，所以一位作者說的 chorus，可能是另一位說的 refrain。台灣的辭典把「曲式」簡單定義為樂曲的構成方式。

Two Words · 兩個詞

Chorus and refrain副歌與疊句

Taiwan’s list of music terms gives 副歌 for the pop-music chorus, and 疊句 or 副歌 for refrain. The dictionary’s 疊句 is a poetry word: a sentence used again and again in a poem, song text, or play. English Wikipedia adds that repeats of a refrain may use different words, and what makes a refrain recognizable is its melody.
台灣的樂詞對照表，把通俗音樂的 chorus 對成「副歌」，refrain 對成「疊句」或「副歌」。辭典裡的「疊句」是詩詞用語：詩詞、戲曲、文章中重複使用的句子。英文維基百科補充說，refrain 的重複可以換字，讓它容易認出來的是旋律。

Bridge · 橋段

A bridge, and a different 橋段橋段，不是 bridge

In Chinese, 橋段 means a plot device in a story, a comic, or a film. It is not the bridge of a song. Taiwan’s list of music terms gives 過渡樂段 for the music word bridge. We keep the English word bridge in this lesson, and we tell you when the Chinese word means something else.
中文的「橋段」是小說、漫畫、電影裡的表現手法，不是歌曲的 bridge。台灣的樂詞對照表，把音樂的 bridge 對成「過渡樂段」。這一課保留英文的 bridge，並在中文的詞意思不同時告訴你。

Rondo · 輪旋曲

A long-lived pattern一個很老的樣式

The dictionary describes the rondo, also called 迴旋曲, as a piece with one main theme and several inserted sections. The main theme usually appears three times and alternates with the inserted sections, in the general form A–B–A–C–A. It shows the same idea as our blocks: one section keeps coming back, and new ones come between.
辭典描述輪旋曲（也叫迴旋曲）是有一個主要主題和若干插入段的曲子。主題通常出現三次，與插入段輪流變換，一般形式是 A－B－A－C－A。它和我們的色塊是同一個想法：一個段落不斷回來，新的段落插在中間。

Myth vs. Fact · 常見迷思

Four things people get wrong 四個常見的誤會

✗ Myth · 迷思Every song has a verse and a chorus.每首歌都有主歌和副歌。

✓ Fact · 事實Pop and rock songs often have both, but some songs have only verses.流行與搖滾歌曲常常兩者都有，但有些歌只有主歌。

✗ Myth · 迷思The bridge always comes after the second chorus.橋段一定在第二次副歌之後。

✓ Fact · 事實It usually does in many songs, but not always. A bridge is a contrasting section that prepares for the return of the original material.許多歌通常如此，但不一定。橋段是為原來材料回來做準備的對比段落。

✗ Myth · 迷思The chorus always has exactly the same words.副歌的歌詞一定一模一樣。

✓ Fact · 事實It usually does. But repeats of a refrain may use different words; the melody is what makes it recognizable.通常如此。但 refrain 的重複可以換字，讓它容易認出來的是旋律。

✗ Myth · 迷思Intro, bridge, and outro each appear in every song.每首歌都有前奏、橋段和結尾。

✓ Fact · 事實They are not essential to the identity of most songs, and they are usually used only once.它們對大多數歌的特色並不是必要的，而且通常只用一次。

Protect Your Ears · 保護耳朵

Before you listen 聽之前

  • Start with the volume low and turn it up a little at a time. When a song plays for a while, your ears can get tired without you noticing.先把音量調小，再一點一點調大。歌曲播放一段時間後，耳朵可能累了你卻沒發現。
  • With headphones, keep the volume at no more than 60% of maximum, and use personal audio devices for less than one hour a day, as the World Health Organization suggests.戴耳機時，音量不要超過最大音量的 60%，個人音響裝置每天使用少於一小時，這是世界衛生組織的建議。
  • Take a quiet break between games. Your ears like it.每個遊戲之間讓耳朵安靜休息一下。耳朵會喜歡。

Classroom Activity 1 · 課堂活動一

Color-Block a Song You Know 把你會唱的歌塗成色塊

You need · 準備材料

  • A short song your class already knows一首全班已經會的短歌
  • Colored paper or crayons彩色紙或蠟筆

Steps · 步驟

  1. Sing the song together and notice where the music comes back.全班一起唱，注意音樂在哪裡回來。
  2. Give each section its own color. Same music, same color.給每個段落一種顏色。一樣的音樂，一樣的顏色。
  3. Line up the colored blocks in order. What letters do you get?把色塊依序排好。你得到什麼字母？
  4. Compare with a friend. Did you hear the sections in the same places?和同學比一比。你們聽到的段落在同樣的地方嗎？

There may be more than one right answer. Let students explain what they heard.
答案可能不只一個。讓學生說明他們聽到了什麼。

Classroom Activity 2 · 課堂活動二

Make Two Blocks 做兩個色塊

You need · 準備材料

  • Hands for clapping or classroom instruments拍手或教室樂器
  • Paper and a pen紙和筆

Steps · 步驟

  1. In pairs, make a short rhythm of eight beats. This is block A.兩人一組，做一個八拍的短節奏。這是 A 色塊。
  2. Make a second rhythm that sounds different. This is block B.再做一個聽起來不同的節奏。這是 B 色塊。
  3. Perform them as A A B A. Then try A B A B.表演成 A A B A，再試 A B A B。
  4. Ask the class: which did you like better? Why?問全班：你比較喜歡哪一個？為什麼？

Keep each block the same length so the changes are easy to hear.
每個色塊保持一樣長，變化才容易聽出來。
