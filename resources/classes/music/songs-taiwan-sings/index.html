Big idea · 一句話看懂

A song has people behind it: a composer, often a lyricist, and singers. On the five-note scale, almost any tune you write sounds smooth, because none of its notes are a half step apart.
一首歌背後有人：作曲家、常常還有作詞的人，和唱歌的人。在五聲音階上，你寫的旋律幾乎都聽起來順，因為它的音之間沒有半音。

    Unit 20: Who Wrote the Songs Taiwan Still Sings?

    A pentatonic scale is a musical scale with five notes per octave. Starting on C, the major pentatonic scale is C, D, E, G, and A. Pentatonic scales were developed independently by many ancient civilizations, and they are still used in various musical styles today. The black keys on a piano keyboard make a pentatonic scale. In Taiwan’s dictionary, the five tones 宮, 商, 角, 徵, and 羽 are the five notes of the old Chinese scale. We tested our own notes by computer: no two of the notes C, D, E, G, and A are a half step apart.

    五聲音階是每個八度裡有五個音的音階。從 C 開始，大調五聲音階是 C、D、E、G、A。五聲音階是許多古老的文明各自獨立發展出來的，到今天仍用在各種音樂風格裡。鋼琴的黑鍵組成的也是一個五聲音階。在台灣的辭典裡，宮、商、角、徵、羽五個音，是古代中國音階的五個音。我們用電腦測試過自己的音：C、D、E、G、A 當中，任何兩個音都不是相差半音。

    Teng Yu-hsien was a Hakka composer, born on July 21, 1906, in the Longtan area of Taoyuan, in Japanese-ruled Taiwan. He died on June 11, 1944. At fifteen he entered the Taipei Normal School, a teacher-training school, and studied music there. He played the cello, the violin, the piano, and the mandolin. In 1925 he became a teacher at a public school. He later studied the theory of composition at a music college in Tokyo, and he returned to Taiwan in 1930.

    鄧雨賢是客家作曲家，1906 年 7 月 21 日出生在日本統治下的台灣，桃園龍潭一帶。他在 1944 年 6 月 11 日去世。十五歲，他進了臺北師範學校，在那裡學音樂。他會拉大提琴、小提琴，也會彈鋼琴和曼陀林。1925 年，他成為一所公學校的老師。後來他在東京的音樂學校學作曲理論，1930 年回到台灣。

    In 1933, when he was 28, a Columbia record company invited him to be its full-time composer, and in the same year he wrote songs such as “Longing for the Spring Breeze” and “Worry on a Moonlit Night”. Four of his best-known songs, “Rainy Night Flower”, “Longing for the Spring Breeze”, “Worry on a Moonlit Night”, and “Four Seasons Red”, are grouped together under the name 四月望雨. In 1940 he left the record industry and went back to teaching, at a school in Xinzhu.

    1933 年，他 28 歲，古倫美亞唱片公司請他當專任作曲家，同一年他寫了〈望春風〉、〈月夜愁〉這些歌。他最有名的四首歌合稱「四月望雨」：〈雨夜花〉、〈望春風〉、〈月夜愁〉、〈四季紅〉。1940 年，他離開唱片業，回去教書，在新竹的一所學校。

    The Hakka Affairs Council says he left behind nearly 100 songs, many of which continue to be sung today. “Longing for the Spring Breeze” was voted the most popular 100-year-old song in Taiwan. Chinese Wikipedia describes the melody of this song as using the pentatonic gong mode. But who wrote the words? The words of “Longing for the Spring Breeze” were written by Li Lin-chiu, and the words of “Rainy Night Flower” by Chou Tien-wang. A song often has a composer and a lyricist, who are different people.

    客家委員會說，他留下近一百首歌，其中許多到今天還有人唱。〈望春風〉被選為台灣最受歡迎的百年老歌。中文維基百科描述這首歌的旋律採用五聲的「宮」調式。但詞是誰寫的？〈望春風〉的詞是李臨秋寫的，〈雨夜花〉的詞是周添旺寫的。一首歌常常有作曲家和作詞的人，他們是不同的人。

    “Rainy Night Flower” began as a children’s song called “Spring”. It was written in 1933 for Taiwanese children by the writer Liao Han-chen, and he gave it to Teng to set to music. Many of Teng’s songs were later banned, and some were rewritten into Japanese. A record of a Japanese popular song that was thought to be lost was rediscovered by a collector in 2007. An asteroid, 255989 Dengyushian, was named in his honor.

    〈雨夜花〉一開始是一首叫〈春天〉的兒歌，是作家廖漢臣 1933 年為台灣的孩子寫的，他交給鄧雨賢譜曲。鄧雨賢的許多歌後來被禁，有些被改寫成日文。一張以為已經遺失的日本流行歌唱片，2007 年被一位收藏者重新發現。有一顆小行星 255989 Dengyushian 用他的名字命名。

    Why do we not play his tunes or print his words here? Taiwan’s copyright law protects a work during the author’s life and 50 years after death, and the term ends on the last day of that year. By this rule, a composer who died in 1944 has been out of copyright since 1995, but the lyricists lived longer, so their words are still protected. Recordings and arrangements have their own terms. A composer’s moral rights, such as the right to be named and to keep a work from being distorted, continue even after death. So this lesson tells you the facts and the song names, and you write your own tune.

    為什麼我們這裡不播他的旋律、也不印他的歌詞？台灣的著作權法保護一件作品在作者生存期間，與死後五十年，期間以那一年的最後一天為止。依這條規則，1944 年去世的作曲家，作品在 1995 年就已經不在保護期內，但作詞的人活得比較久，所以歌詞還在保護期內。錄音與編曲各有自己的期間。作曲家的人格權，例如署名的權利、不被歪曲作品的權利，在他死後仍然繼續。所以這一課告訴你事實和歌名，旋律你自己寫。

    Stories Behind the Notes · 音符背後的故事

    A teacher, two names on a song, hard years, and whose song it is 一位老師、一首歌上的兩個名字、艱難的年代，和歌是誰的

    Four short stories about Teng Yu-hsien and his songs. We only write what the sources say.
    關於鄧雨賢與他的歌的四個小故事。我們只寫來源說的。

    A Teacher · 老師

    From Longtan to the classroom從龍潭到教室

    Teng Yu-hsien was born in the Longtan area of Taoyuan and entered the Taipei Normal School at fifteen. He played the cello, the violin, the piano, and the mandolin. He became a teacher at a public school in 1925, left the record industry in 1940, and went back to teaching in Xinzhu. In 2014 a picture book about him was written so that children could get to know him.
    鄧雨賢出生在桃園龍潭一帶，十五歲進了臺北師範學校。他會拉大提琴、小提琴，也會彈鋼琴和曼陀林。他 1925 年成為一所公學校的老師，1940 年離開唱片業，回新竹教書。2014 年有人寫了一本關於他的繪本，讓孩子認識他。

    Two Names · 兩個名字

    Music and words曲與詞

    On the songs “Longing for the Spring Breeze” and “Rainy Night Flower”, the music is by Teng Yu-hsien, and the words are by two other people: Li Lin-chiu and Chou Tien-wang. “Rainy Night Flower” began as a children’s song called “Spring”, written in 1933 by Liao Han-chen, and Teng set it to music. So behind one song there may be several people, and each of them has a name that should be remembered.
    〈望春風〉和〈雨夜花〉這兩首歌，曲是鄧雨賢寫的，詞是另外兩個人寫的：李臨秋和周添旺。〈雨夜花〉一開始是一首叫〈春天〉的兒歌，1933 年廖漢臣寫的，鄧雨賢為它譜曲。所以一首歌背後可能有好幾個人，每一個人的名字都值得記得。

    Hard Years · 艱難的年代

    Banned songs, and a lost record被禁的歌，與一張失落的唱片

    Many of the songs Teng composed were banned, and some were rewritten into Japanese. This happened in the years when Taiwan was under Japanese rule. Years after his death, in 2007, a record of a Japanese popular song that had been thought lost was rediscovered by a collector. Music can be lost, and it can be found again.
    鄧雨賢作曲的許多歌曲曾被禁，有些被改寫成日文。這發生在台灣被日本統治的年代。他去世多年後，2007 年，一張以為已經遺失的日本流行歌唱片，被一位收藏者重新發現。音樂可以遺失，也可以被重新找到。

    Whose Song? · 歌是誰的

    The copyright rule著作權的規則

    Taiwan’s law says a work is protected for the author’s life and 50 years after death, and the term ends on the last day of that year. Using this rule, a composer who died in 1944 is out of copyright from 1995, while a lyricist who died in 1979 is protected through 2029, and one who died in 1988 through 2038. Recordings and performances have their own term, counted from publication. If you ever want to use a song, ask a teacher or an expert first.
    台灣的法律說，一件作品在作者生存期間與死後五十年受保護，期間到那一年的最後一天為止。用這條規則算，1944 年去世的作曲家，從 1995 年起不在保護期內；1979 年去世的作詞者，保護到 2029 年；1988 年去世的，保護到 2038 年。錄音與表演有自己的期間，從發表後算。如果你想使用一首歌，先問老師或專家。

    Myth vs. Fact · 常見迷思

    Four things people get wrong 四個常見的誤會

    ✗ Myth · 迷思A song has just one author.一首歌只有一個作者。

    ✓ Fact · 事實Often one person writes the music and another writes the words. In “Longing for the Spring Breeze”, they were Teng Yu-hsien and Li Lin-chiu.常常是一個人寫曲，另一個人寫詞。〈望春風〉是鄧雨賢和李臨秋。

    ✗ Myth · 迷思Teng Yu-hsien wrote only four songs.鄧雨賢只寫了四首歌。

    ✓ Fact · 事實Four songs are grouped together as his best known. The Hakka Affairs Council says he left behind nearly 100 songs.有四首歌合稱他最有名的。客家委員會說，他留下近一百首歌。

    ✗ Myth · 迷思When a work is out of copyright, you can do anything with it.作品過了著作權期間，就可以隨便使用。

    ✓ Fact · 事實Moral rights, such as the right to be named and to keep a work from being distorted, continue after death. Recordings and lyrics have their own terms.人格權，例如署名的權利與不被歪曲作品的權利，在死後仍然繼續。錄音與歌詞各有自己的期間。

    ✗ Myth · 迷思The pentatonic scale belongs to one country.五聲音階屬於某一個國家。

    ✓ Fact · 事實Many ancient civilizations developed pentatonic scales independently, and they occur in many musical traditions.許多古老的文明各自獨立發展出五聲音階，它出現在許多音樂傳統裡。

    Protect Your Ears · 保護耳朵

    Before you listen 聽之前

    • Start with the volume low and turn it up a little at a time.先把音量調小，再一點一點調大。
    • With headphones, keep the volume at no more than 60% of maximum, and use personal audio devices for less than one hour a day, as the World Health Organization suggests.戴耳機時，音量不要超過最大音量的 60%，個人音響裝置每天使用少於一小時，這是世界衛生組織的建議。
    • When you play the half-step notes F and B, they may sound sharper than the rest. Lower the volume if they bother you.播放相差半音的 F 和 B 時，它們可能比其他的音更刺耳。如果覺得不舒服，就把音量調小。

    Classroom Activity 1 · 課堂活動一

    Write a Five-Note Tune 寫一段五個音的旋律

    You need · 準備材料

    • A xylophone, a keyboard, or bars with only C, D, E, G, and A木琴、鍵盤，或只有 C、D、E、G、A 的音條
    • Paper and a pen紙和筆

    Steps · 步驟

    1. Work in groups. Choose eight notes from C, D, E, G, and A. Write them down.分組。從 C、D、E、G、A 選八個音，寫下來。
    2. Play your tune. Try to end on C. Does it feel finished?彈你的旋律。試著結束在 C。有結束的感覺嗎？
    3. Bring back a small pattern. Can your friends hear it?讓一個小圖樣回來。同學聽得出來嗎？
    4. Swap tunes with another group, and play each other’s tunes.和另一組交換旋律，彼此演奏。

    There is no wrong tune on five notes. Talk about what each tune does.
    五個音上沒有錯的旋律。討論每一段旋律做了什麼。

    Classroom Activity 2 · 課堂活動二

    Two Names on a Song 一首歌上的兩個名字

    You need · 準備材料

    • A song your family or class likes你的家人或班上喜歡的一首歌
    • A teacher, a family member, or a library to help you look up the names老師、家人或圖書館，幫你查名字

    Steps · 步驟

    1. Choose a song. Find out who wrote the music and who wrote the words.選一首歌，查是誰作曲、誰作詞。
    2. Write the names and the song’s title on a card. Do not copy the words.把名字和歌名寫在卡片上。不要抄歌詞。
    3. Ask: how long ago did they live? Is the song still protected?問：他們生活在多久以前？這首歌還在保護期內嗎？
    4. Share the cards. Say thank you to the people who made the song.分享卡片。對寫這首歌的人說謝謝。

    A teacher or a librarian can help you check. Do not record or share a song without asking.
    老師或圖書館員可以幫你確認。沒有問過，不要錄音，也不要分享歌曲。
