Big idea · 一句話看懂

Music is written differently in different times. The same tune can get a different accompaniment, loudness, and tempo, and that is a big part of what the names Baroque, Classical, and Romantic point to.
不同的時代，音樂的寫法不同。同一段旋律可以有不同的伴奏、力度和速度，而這正是「巴洛克」「古典」「浪漫」這些名稱主要指的東西。

Period · 時期

What is typical of this style · 這種寫法的典型特徵

  • A continuous bass line, called basso continuo, with a chord instrument such as a harpsichord or organ.不停的低音，叫通奏低音（持續低音），由大鍵琴或管風琴等能彈和弦的樂器擔任。
  • Loudness changes in steps: piano, then forte. This is called terraced dynamics, and a harpsichord cannot vary its loudness by touch.力度一階一階地換：弱，然後強。這叫階梯式力度；大鍵琴不能用手指的力道改變音量。
  • Ornaments, such as trills and mordents, were often added by the performers. We add one short ornament.裝飾音，例如顫音與迴音，常由演奏者自己加上。我們加了一個短短的裝飾音。
  • Our own simplification: short, bright, plucked-sounding notes, as on a harpsichord.我們自己的簡化：短而亮、像撥出來的音，像大鍵琴。
  • A clear melody over a subordinate chordal accompaniment: a lighter, clearer texture.一條清楚的旋律，在比較次要的和弦伴奏上面：比較輕、比較清楚的織度。
  • Alberti bass: the notes of the chord in the order lowest, highest, middle, highest.阿貝提低音：和弦的音依「最低、最高、中間、最高」的順序彈。
  • Balanced phrases that are brief, typically four bars long. Our tune is one four-bar phrase.平衡的樂句，很短，通常是四小節。我們的旋律就是一個四小節的樂句。
  • The piano, or fortepiano, replaced the harpsichord as the main keyboard instrument. The smooth swell in loudness is our own choice.鋼琴（或古鋼琴）取代了大鍵琴，成為主要的鍵盤樂器。力度平順的起伏是我們自己的選擇。
  • A wider range of dynamics, for example from ppp to fff.更寬的力度範圍，例如從 ppp 到 fff。
  • Increased chromaticism: harmony that uses more notes from outside the key. We borrow one chord from the minor key.更多的半音化：和聲用更多調外的音。我們借了一個小調的和弦。
  • Rubato, a slight speeding up and then slowing down of the tempo, for expression. The word is older than the Romantic era.彈性速度：為了表情，稍微加快、再放慢。這個詞比浪漫時期還早。
  • Individual, emotional, dramatic music, and a modern piano with a more powerful, sustained tone. The wide arpeggios are our own simplification.個人的、情感的、戲劇性的音樂，與音量更大、延音更長的現代鋼琴。寬廣的分解和弦是我們自己的簡化。

Unit 13: How Did Western Music Change Over 400 Years?

The words “classical music” can mean all of the Western art music tradition, from the Middle Ages to today. But there is also a narrower Classical period. In this lesson we look at three periods that are often named: the Baroque, roughly 1600 to 1750, the Classical, roughly 1750 to 1820, and the Romantic, roughly the nineteenth century. The dates are only approximate, and the books disagree about them.

「古典音樂」這幾個字，可以指整個西方藝術音樂的傳統，從中世紀到今天。但也有比較窄的「古典時期」。這一課我們看三個常被提到的時期：巴洛克，大約 1600 到 1750 年；古典，大約 1750 到 1820 年；浪漫，大約是十九世紀。年代只是約略的，各書的說法也不一樣。

Much Baroque music has a basso continuo: a bass line that keeps going, played by a bass instrument together with an instrument that can play chords, such as a harpsichord or an organ. Performers often added ornaments, such as trills and mordents. A harpsichord cannot change its loudness by how hard you press the key, so the dynamics were often terraced, a step from piano to forte. But not all of it: later composers, such as Vivaldi, used more varied dynamics.

許多巴洛克音樂有通奏低音：一條不停的低音，由低音樂器和一件能彈和弦的樂器（例如大鍵琴或管風琴）一起演奏。演奏者常常加上裝飾音，例如顫音和迴音。大鍵琴不能用按鍵的力道改變音量，所以力度常常是階梯式的，從弱一階跳到強。但不是全部：後來的作曲家，例如韋瓦第，用了更多變化的力度。

Classical music tends to have a lighter, clearer texture. It is mainly homophonic, with a clear melody over a chordal accompaniment. The phrases are balanced and brief, typically four bars long. A common accompaniment is the Alberti bass, which plays the notes of a chord in the order lowest, highest, middle, highest. The symphony and the string quartet became prominent, and the piano gradually replaced the harpsichord.

古典音樂的織度比較輕、比較清楚。它主要是主音音樂（homophony）：一條清楚的旋律，在和弦的伴奏之上。樂句平衡而短，通常是四小節。常見的伴奏是阿貝提低音，把和弦的音依「最低、最高、中間、最高」的順序彈。交響曲和弦樂四重奏變得重要，鋼琴也逐漸取代了大鍵琴。

Romantic composers sought to make music that was individual, emotional, and dramatic. They used a wider range of dynamics, for example from ppp to fff, and more chromatic harmony. The orchestra grew, with as many as 120 players for the works of Wagner and Mahler. The modern piano, with its more powerful and more sustained tone, took over from the fortepiano. And the inspiration was not only love: it could be nature, literature, or poetry.

浪漫時期的作曲家想寫個人的、情感的、戲劇性的音樂。他們用更寬的力度範圍，例如從 ppp 到 fff，與更半音化的和聲。樂團變大了，華格納與馬勒的作品用到多達 120 位演奏者。現代鋼琴音量更大、延音更長，取代了古鋼琴。而靈感不只是愛情：可以是自然、文學或詩。

The periods overlap, and many composers do not fit just one. Bach (1685–1750) is a late Baroque composer. Mozart (1756–1791) is a Classical composer. Chopin (1810–1849) is an early Romantic composer. Beethoven (1770–1827) was mentored during the Classical period, and his style was a key driver of the transition to Romantic music. Schubert (1797–1828) belongs to the late Classical and early Romantic eras.

這些時期互相重疊，許多作曲家也不只屬於一個。巴哈（1685–1750）是巴洛克晚期的作曲家。莫札特（1756–1791）是古典時期的作曲家。蕭邦（1810–1849）是早期浪漫的作曲家。貝多芬（1770–1827）在古典時期受教，他的風格是推動轉向浪漫音樂的關鍵。舒伯特（1797–1828）屬於古典晚期與浪漫早期。

The tune on this page is our own, and the three versions are only teaching imitations of some typical features. Real composers varied a great deal. One musicologist points out that there is too much diversity within the music of the time to make sweeping generalizations. So treat the names as helpful handles, and listen to real music with open ears.

這一頁的旋律是我們自己寫的，三個版本只是對一些典型特徵的教學式模仿。真正的作曲家變化很大。一位音樂學者指出，那個時代的音樂內部差異太大，不能一概而論。所以把這些名稱當作方便的把手，然後用開放的耳朵去聽真正的音樂。

Questions · 閱讀理解

What is an Alberti bass?

An accompaniment figure that plays the notes of a chord in the order lowest, highest, middle, highest. It is often used in the Classical era.

Why were Baroque dynamics often “terraced”?

A harpsichord cannot vary its loudness by touch, so a change in loudness came as a step, from piano to forte. But later Baroque composers used more varied dynamics.

Why is Beethoven hard to put in just one period?

He was mentored in the Classical period, and his style drove the transition to Romantic music. Some call him a transitional composer.

Stories Behind the Notes · 音符背後的故事

A word with two meanings, a keyboard that changed, a bigger orchestra, and a bridge 一個有兩個意思的詞、一件改變的鍵盤、變大的樂團，和一座橋

Four short stories about how Western music changed.
關於西洋音樂怎麼變的四個小故事。

A Word · 一個詞

“Classical” twice兩個「古典」

In Taiwan’s dictionary, 古典音樂 has two senses. The first is art music rooted in the Western tradition, from about the eleventh century to the present. The second is the music of the Classical period, 1750 to 1820, valuing form, reason, and objectivity, and setting the model for the symphony, the sonata, and chamber music. So the word “classical” has to be read from the context.
在台灣的辭典裡，「古典音樂」有兩個意思。第一個是根植於西方傳統的藝術音樂，從大約十一世紀直到現在。第二個是古典時期 1750 到 1820 年的音樂，注重形式、理性與客觀，建立了交響曲、奏鳴曲和室內樂的典範。所以「古典」這個詞，要看上下文才知道指哪一個。

A Keyboard · 一件鍵盤

From harpsichord to piano從大鍵琴到鋼琴

A harpsichord plucks its strings and gives no expressive control over individual notes. The piano can continuously vary dynamics by touch, so it slowly took over in the Classical period. In the nineteenth century a cast iron frame allowed much greater string tension, and the modern piano, with a more powerful and sustained tone, took over from the more delicate fortepiano. The harpsichord did not vanish at once: Classical composers still used it in concerts.
大鍵琴是撥弦，對單一個音沒有表情的控制。鋼琴可以用手指力道連續改變力度，所以在古典時期慢慢取代了它。十九世紀，鑄鐵框讓弦可以承受大得多的張力，音量更大、延音更長的現代鋼琴，取代了比較纖細的古鋼琴。大鍵琴不是一下子消失的：古典時期的作曲家在音樂會上還是常常用它。

A Bigger Orchestra · 變大的樂團

From about 40 to over 100從約 40 人到一百多人

Baroque orchestras were not standardized: one orchestra of Bach had up to 18 players, while Corelli’s in Rome had between 35 and 80 for daily performances. The typical Classical orchestra was around 40. The size of the orchestra grew throughout the eighteenth and nineteenth centuries, to as many as 120 players for Wagner and Mahler.
巴洛克的樂團沒有標準：巴哈的一個樂團多達 18 位演奏者，而柯雷利在羅馬的樂團，平日演出有 35 到 80 位。古典時期的典型樂團大約 40 人。樂團的規模在十八和十九世紀不斷成長，到華格納和馬勒時，多達 120 位演奏者。

A Bridge · 一座橋

Beethoven, in between貝多芬，在中間

Beethoven was mentored during the Classical period, and his musical style was a key driver of the transition to Romantic music. Some people count his Third Symphony as the first symphony of the Romantic era, though that is contested. Schubert, who was born in 1797, is also described as a composer of the late Classical and early Romantic eras. Real music does not stay in boxes.
貝多芬在古典時期受教，他的音樂風格是推動轉向浪漫音樂的關鍵。有些人把他的第三號交響曲算成浪漫時期的第一首交響曲，不過這有爭議。1797 年出生的舒伯特，也被描述為古典晚期與浪漫早期的作曲家。真正的音樂不會待在盒子裡。

Myth vs. Fact · 常見迷思

Four things people get wrong 四個常見的誤會

✗ Myth · 迷思Baroque music ended exactly in 1750, when Bach died.巴洛克音樂在 1750 年巴哈去世時剛好結束。

✓ Fact · 事實1750 is a convenient end date. The sources admit appreciable disagreement, and there was a short transition, the galant style, in between.1750 只是方便的結束年份。資料承認各家的說法有明顯的差異，中間還有一段短短的過渡期，「加朗」風格。

✗ Myth · 迷思“Classical music” means only the music of Mozart’s time.「古典音樂」只指莫札特那個時代的音樂。

✓ Fact · 事實The term can include all Western art music. The narrower Classical period is roughly 1750 to 1820.這個詞可以包括所有西方藝術音樂。比較窄的古典時期，大約是 1750 到 1820 年。

✗ Myth · 迷思Romantic music is all about love.浪漫音樂都是在寫愛情。

✓ Fact · 事實It is often inspired by nature, literature, poetry, or the fine arts. “Romantic” here is tied to the Romanticism movement, not only to romance.它常常受自然、文學、詩或美術啟發。這裡的「浪漫」連著浪漫主義這場運動，不只是愛情。

✗ Myth · 迷思Rubato was invented by the Romantic composers.彈性速度是浪漫時期的作曲家發明的。

✓ Fact · 事實The term rubato existed even before the Romantic era. Romantic players used it a great deal for expression.「rubato」這個詞在浪漫時期之前就有了。浪漫時期的演奏者為了表情，用得很多。

旋律在和弦上、短樂句、阿貝提低音。

Romantic: wide and personal浪漫：寬廣而個人

Big dynamics, more chromatic harmony, rubato.
大的力度、更半音化的和聲、彈性速度。

The dates are approximate年代只是約略的

Composers do not stay in boxes.
作曲家不會待在盒子裡。

Protect Your Ears · 保護耳朵

Before you listen 聽之前

  • Start with the volume low and turn it up a little at a time. The Romantic version gets much louder in the middle.先把音量調小，再一點一點調大。浪漫的版本在中間會變得大聲很多。
  • With headphones, keep the volume at no more than 60% of maximum, and use personal audio devices for less than one hour a day, as the World Health Organization suggests.戴耳機時，音量不要超過最大音量的 60%，個人音響裝置每天使用少於一小時，這是世界衛生組織的建議。
  • In a concert hall, you may hear very loud passages. If something is too loud, covering your ears for a moment is fine.在音樂廳裡，你可能會聽到非常響的段落。如果太大聲，暫時摀住耳朵也沒關係。

Classroom Activity 1 · 課堂活動一

Three Ways to Play One Tune 一段旋律，三種彈法

You need · 準備材料

  • A simple tune everyone knows, such as a short nursery song大家都會的簡單旋律，例如一首短短的童謠
  • A keyboard or xylophone, or just voices and clapping鍵盤或木琴，或只用聲音和拍手

Steps · 步驟

  1. Play or sing the tune plainly, one time. Everyone listens.平平地彈或唱一次旋律。大家聽。
  2. Version 1: play every note short and bright, and play the loudness in steps: loud, loud, soft, soft.版本一：每個音彈得短而亮，力度一階一階：大聲、大聲、小聲、小聲。
  3. Version 2: add a steady pattern underneath: low, high, middle, high. Make the loudness grow and fade smoothly.版本二：下面加一個穩定的圖樣：低、高、中、高。讓力度平順地漸強再漸弱。
  4. Version 3: play slowly, slow down at the end of each phrase, and make big swings from very soft to very loud.版本三：慢慢彈，每個樂句結尾放慢，從很弱到很強，大幅度地起伏。
  5. Talk about it: which one felt the most like a story? Which was the most tidy? Why?討論：哪一個最像在說故事？哪一個最整齊？為什麼？

There is no right answer for how each one feels. Let students use their own words.
每一個版本給人的感覺沒有標準答案。讓學生用自己的話說。

Classroom Activity 2 · 課堂活動二

A Class Timeline 班級時間軸

You need · 準備材料

  • A long strip of paper一長條紙
  • Colored pens彩色筆
  • The timeline on this page本頁的時間軸

Steps · 步驟

  1. Mark the years 1600, 1700, 1800, and 1900 along the strip.在紙條上標出 1600、1700、1800、1900 年。
  2. Draw the three periods as colored bars. Let them overlap where the sources disagree.把三個時期畫成彩色長條。各來源不同的地方，讓它們重疊。
  3. Add the composers from this page with their years of birth and death.把本頁的作曲家和他們的生卒年加上去。
  4. Ask: who lived in two periods? What changed in the instruments and the orchestra?問：誰橫跨兩個時期？樂器和樂團改變了什麼？

Let students argue about where to draw the lines. That is the point.
讓學生為界線畫在哪裡爭論。那就是重點。
