Big idea · 一句話看懂

Voices can be layered in different ways: all together, one after another, over a held note, or moving in parallel. People in Taiwan sing in all of these ways, and some songs belong to a time and a place, so we describe them and we do not copy them.
聲部可以用不同的方式疊起來：一起唱、一個接一個、在拉長的音上、或平行移動。台灣各地的人用這些方式唱歌，有些歌屬於特定的時間與場合，所以我們描述它們，不複製它們。

Unit 15: What Music Grew in Taiwan?

Many kinds of music grew in Taiwan, and people sing them in different languages. Taiwanese folk songs can be sorted by ethnic group and language: songs of the indigenous peoples in their own languages, Hakka folk songs mainly in Hakka, and Hokkien folk songs mainly in Taiwanese Hokkien. The Council of Indigenous Peoples recognizes 16 indigenous peoples officially. In this lesson we ask one question about all of them: how can voices be layered?

台灣長出了許多種音樂，人們用不同的語言唱。台灣民謠可以依族群與語言分類：原住民族用自己語言唱的歌、主要用客語的客家民謠，和主要用台灣閩南語的閩南語民謠。原住民族委員會官方認定的原住民族有 16 族。這一課對它們問同一個問題：聲部可以怎麼疊？

There are some useful words. In unison, two or more parts sound the same pitch, or pitches an octave apart. In a round, each voice sings the same melody, but each begins at a different time. A drone is a note or chord that is sounded continuously throughout most or all of a piece. Polyphony is a texture of two or more simultaneous lines of independent melody. Heterophony is the simultaneous variation of a single melodic line.

有幾個有用的詞。齊唱（unison）是兩個以上的聲部唱同一個音高，或相差八度的音高。輪唱（round）是每個聲部唱同一條旋律，但各自在不同的時間開始。持續音（drone）是在一首曲子的大部分或全部時間裡，持續發聲的一個音或和弦。複音（polyphony）是兩條或以上同時進行、各自獨立的旋律線所形成的織度。異音（heterophony）是同一條旋律線同時出現變化。

Several indigenous peoples of Taiwan are known for polyphonic singing. The Amis, Bunun, Paiwan, Rukai, and Tsou are known for their polyphonic vocals, and each has a unique variety. The Council of Indigenous Peoples says Amis songs use lead-and-answer singing, chanting, and polyphonic counterpoint. The Bunun style called eight-part harmony starts with four parts, and the “eight” comes from overtones that appear at a high register. It is sung on vowels, without words.

台灣有好幾個原住民族以複音歌唱聞名。阿美族、布農族、排灣族、魯凱族和鄒族以複音的人聲聞名，而且各有獨特的類型。原住民族委員會說，阿美族的歌謠有領唱與答唱、吟唱，以及複音對位。布農族稱為八部合音的唱法，開始時只有四部，「八」來自高音域出現的泛音。它用母音來唱，沒有歌詞。

Some songs belong to a time and a place. According to Chinese Wikipedia, Amis ritual music is mostly sung only at specific ceremonies. The Council of Indigenous Peoples says the sacred ancestral songs of the Thao are not to be sung casually. Taiwan’s 2007 Act for the protection of indigenous traditional intellectual creations lists music and songs, and a right holder may license their use. That is why this lesson describes the ways of singing, and does not copy any song.

有些歌屬於特定的時間與場合。依中文維基百科，阿美族的祭儀音樂多半只在特定儀式的特定場合吟唱。原住民族委員會說，邵族的祖靈祭歌謠具有神聖性，平日禁止隨意吟唱。台灣 2007 年的《原住民族傳統智慧創作保護條例》把音樂與歌曲列入，權利人也可以授權他人使用。所以這一課只描述唱法，不複製任何一首歌。

Han traditions layer sound in other ways. Nanguan is an old kind of music popular in Fujian, Taiwan, and Southeast Asia. The singer holds clappers, and the accompaniment is led by the pipa, with the xiao, sanxian, erxian, and a set of four boards. It is described as typically slow and gentle, and as heterophonic. Beiguan is another Han tradition in Taiwan; its name is paired with Nanguan, and “north” also stands for a lively, high-pitched style.

漢人的傳統用別的方式疊聲音。南管是流行於福建、台灣與東南亞的古樂。歌者拿拍板，伴奏以琵琶為主，再配洞簫、三弦、二弦、四塊板。它被描述為通常緩慢、柔和，並且是異音的。北管是台灣另一種漢人傳統，名稱與南管相對，「北」也用來表示熱鬧高亢的風格。

Hakka mountain songs are short in form with free rhythm, and are popular in mountain villages. The main tunes are the old mountain song, the mountain-song-zi, and the pingban. The tunes are fixed, and the words can be changed and improvised. Shange has traditionally been sung while people work, particularly while picking tea. Taiwanese opera developed in Taiwan after folk arts came from southern Fujian, and its tunes were taken from folk songs.

客家山歌形式短小、節奏自由，流行於山村。主要的調子是老山歌、山歌子和平板。曲調固定，歌詞可以變化、即興。山歌傳統上在工作時唱，特別是採茶的時候。歌仔戲是閩南的民間藝術傳到台灣後發展出來的，曲調採自歌謠小調。

Questions · 閱讀理解

What is the difference between a unison and a round?

In unison, voices sing the same pitch at the same time. In a round, each voice sings the same melody but begins at a different time.

Is the Bunun “eight-part” style really eight parts?

It starts with four parts, and the “eight” comes from overtones at a high register.

Why does this lesson not play real songs?

Some songs are sung only at the right time and place, and the music of recognized works can be licensed, so we describe how people sing and do not copy their songs.

Peoples · 族群

Sixteen recognized peoples官方認定的十六族

The Council of Indigenous Peoples lists 16 peoples: Amis, Atayal, Paiwan, Bunun, Puyuma, Rukai, Tsou, Saisiyat, Tao (Yami), Thao, Kavalan, Truku, Sakizaya, Seediq, Hla’alua, and Kanakanavu. Some scholars say these 16 groupings do not reflect social entities and are administrative categories. Each people has its own languages, songs, and customs.
原住民族委員會列出 16 族：阿美、泰雅、排灣、布農、卑南、魯凱、鄒、賽夏、雅美（達悟）、邵、噶瑪蘭、太魯閣、撒奇萊雅、賽德克、拉阿魯哇、卡那卡那富。有學者說這 16 個分類並不反映社會實體，是行政上的分類。每一族有自己的語言、歌與習俗。

Right Time · 對的時間

Sung on the right occasion在對的場合才唱

Amis ritual music is mostly sung only at the specific ceremonies. The Thao sacred ancestral songs are not sung casually. Under the 2007 Act, music and songs are named among traditional creations; only works that are recognized and registered receive protection, and a right holder may license use. Education use of published works is allowed, with the source stated.
阿美族的祭儀音樂多半只在特定儀式的特定場合吟唱。邵族的祖靈祭歌謠不隨意吟唱。依 2007 年的條例，音樂與歌曲列在傳統智慧創作裡；只有經認定並登記的才受保護，權利人可授權他人使用。已發表的創作為教育而使用是允許的，並要註明出處。

Four or Eight · 四部還是八部

Four parts, eight voices of sound四部，聽起來像八部

The Bunun eight-part style is often named that way, but it is not eight parts in the vocal sense. It starts with four parts, and overtones make it sound like eight. The number of singers is also not eight: Chinese Wikipedia says it can go up to twelve people. Only two Bunun subgroups sing it, so not all Bunun do.
布農族的八部合音常被這樣稱呼，但並不是聲樂中的八部。它從四部開始，因為出現泛音而聽起來像八部。歌者的人數也不是八個：中文維基百科說可以增加到十二人。只有布農族的兩個群體唱它，所以不是所有布農人都唱。

Fixed Tune, New Words · 曲調固定、歌詞變

Hakka mountain songs客家山歌

Hakka mountain songs have three main tunes: the old mountain song, the mountain-song-zi, and the pingban. The tunes are fixed, and the words can be changed freely and improvised. Pingban is also called the “improved tune”. Chinese Wikipedia notes the figure “nine accents and eighteen tunes” is generally taken as a figure of speech, not an exact count.
客家山歌有三大調：老山歌、山歌子和平板。曲調固定，歌詞可以隨意變化、即興創作。平板又稱「改良調」。中文維基百科說，「九腔十八調」一般認為是虛詞，不是精確的數字。

Myth vs. Fact · 常見迷思

Four things people get wrong 四個常見的誤會

✗ Myth · 迷思The Bunun eight-part style has eight vocal parts.布農族的八部合音有八個聲部。

✓ Fact · 事實It starts with four parts. Overtones at a high register make it sound like eight.它從四部開始。高音域出現的泛音，讓它聽起來像八部。

✗ Myth · 迷思Songs of Taiwan’s indigenous peoples are free for anyone to record and reuse.原住民族的歌誰都可以隨意錄音、重複使用。

✓ Fact · 事實Some songs are sung only at the proper occasion. Recognized, registered works are protected, and a right holder may license their use.有些歌只在適當的場合唱。經認定並登記的創作受保護，權利人可以授權他人使用。

✗ Myth · 迷思All indigenous peoples in Taiwan sing the same way.台灣所有原住民族都用同一種方式唱歌。

✓ Fact · 事實Each has its own variety. For example, the eight-part style is sung by only two Bunun subgroups.各族有自己的類型。例如八部合音只有布農族的兩個群體唱。

✗ Myth · 迷思“Harvest festival” is the name every Amis community uses.「豐年祭」是所有阿美族部落自己的名稱。

✓ Fact · 事實Chinese Wikipedia says the local names do not carry the meaning of harvest, and that the term was given from outside.中文維基百科說，各地的稱呼幾乎都不帶有「豐收」的意思，這個名稱是外來的。

Protect Your Ears and Voice · 保護耳朵與聲音

Before you listen and sing 聽與唱之前

  • Start with the volume low and turn it up a little at a time. Four voices together are louder than one, even with our mixing.先把音量調小，再一點一點調大。四個聲部在一起，即使經過我們的混音，還是比一個聲部大聲。
  • With headphones, keep the volume at no more than 60% of maximum, and use personal audio devices for less than one hour a day, as the World Health Organization suggests.戴耳機時，音量不要超過最大音量的 60%，個人音響裝置每天使用少於一小時，這是世界衛生組織的建議。
  • When you sing along, stay in a comfortable range. If your throat hurts, stop and drink some water.跟著唱的時候，留在舒服的音域。如果喉嚨痛，就停下來喝點水。

Classroom Activity 1 · 課堂活動一

Four Ways to Sing One Song 一首歌的四種唱法

You need · 準備材料

  • A short song or rhyme your class already knows (not a ceremonial song)一首全班已經會的短歌或童謠（不是祭儀歌曲）
  • A space where groups can stand apart可以讓各組分開站的空間

Steps · 步驟

  1. Everyone sings it together: unison.全班一起唱：齊唱。
  2. Divide into three groups. Group 1 begins; Group 2 enters when Group 1 finishes the first line; Group 3 enters after Group 2: a round.分成三組。第一組開始；第一組唱完第一句，第二組進來；第二組之後，第三組進來：輪唱。
  3. A few singers hum one low note all the way through while the rest sing: a drone.幾位歌者從頭到尾哼一個低音，其他人唱歌：持續音。
  4. Let one group sing the same rhythm a little higher or lower, if they can find it: parts.讓一組用同樣的節奏唱得稍高或稍低（找得到的話）：分部。
  5. Talk about it: which sounded thickest? Which was easiest to follow?討論：哪一種聽起來最厚？哪一種最容易跟著唱？

Choose a song with a clear, steady beat. A round needs a tune that can be sung one after another.
選節拍清楚穩定的歌。輪唱需要可以一個接一個唱下去的旋律。

Classroom Activity 2 · 課堂活動二

Ask Before You Share 分享之前先問

You need · 準備材料

  • A family member, teacher, or community member who is willing to talk一位願意聊聊的家人、老師或社區裡的人
  • Paper and a pen紙和筆

Steps · 步驟

  1. Ask: what songs does your family or community sing? When are they sung?問：你的家人或社區唱哪些歌？什麼時候唱？
  2. Ask: are there songs that are only for certain times or places?問：有沒有只在特定時間或地點唱的歌？
  3. Ask: how would you like others to learn about them?問：你希望別人怎麼認識它們？
  4. Write down what you learned, and do not record or share it without asking.把學到的寫下來；沒有問過，不錄音也不分享。

Listening comes first. Say thank you, and give the person’s name and community when you share what they told you, if they agree.
先聽。說謝謝；對方同意的話，分享時註明是誰、哪個社區說的。
