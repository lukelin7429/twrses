Music · 音樂

Music 音樂

Music is sound that people have shaped. This series starts with a single vibrating string and climbs, one question at a time, to scales, instruments, and the music of many places.
音樂是人把聲音一點一點塑造出來的東西。這個系列從一根振動的弦講起，一次回答一個問題，一路講到音階、樂器，以及各地的音樂。

Every lesson has a model you can touch and a sound you can make yourself. All of the sounds are made by your browser, one tone at a time, so there are no recordings and nothing to download.
每一課都有可以動手的模型，和你自己能發出的聲音。所有的聲音都由瀏覽器一個音一個音合成，沒有任何錄音，也不需要下載。

Music words come from many languages. Where a term comes from Italian, you will see the Italian word beside the English and Chinese. Please start with the volume low: the series starts quiet and you can turn it up.
音樂的詞彙來自很多種語言。有義大利文來源的術語，會把義大利文和英文、中文並列。請先把音量調小：系列的聲音一開始很小聲，你可以再慢慢調大。

1 of 21 lessons is ready so far; more are on the way.
目前完成 1 課（共規劃 21 課），持續增加中。

Unit 1 · 單元一

Four Sides of Sound 聲音的四個樣子

Pitch, tone color, beat, and the words for loud and fast: the four things you notice first when you listen to any music.
音高、音色、拍子，以及形容大聲小聲、快慢的詞：聽任何一段音樂時，最先注意到的四件事。

Why Are Some Notes High and Some Low?
