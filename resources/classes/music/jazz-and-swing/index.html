Unit 14: What Makes Jazz Sound Like Jazz?

Jazz is a music genre that originated in the African-American communities of New Orleans, Louisiana, in the late 19th and early 20th centuries. But jazz did not begin as a single musical tradition in New Orleans or elsewhere. It is difficult to define, because it includes a wide range of music. Jazz is characterized by swing and blue notes, complex chords, call and response, polyrhythms, and improvisation.

爵士樂是十九世紀末到二十世紀初，在美國路易斯安那州紐奧良的非裔美國人社群中形成的音樂類型。但爵士樂並不是從紐奧良或別處的某一個傳統開始的。它很難定義，因為它包含很廣的音樂。爵士樂的特色是搖擺與藍調音、複雜的和弦、呼應（call and response）、多重節奏，以及即興。

In swing rhythm the pulse is divided unequally. The first note of each pair of eighth notes is lengthened, and the second is shortened. The first note is often understood to be twice as long as the second, a triplet feel, a ratio of 2:1. But in practice the ratio is often much more subtle. In jazz, the swing ratio typically lies somewhere between 1:1 and 3:1, and can vary considerably. It tends to be wider at slower tempos and narrower at faster ones.

在搖擺的節奏裡，脈動被不平均地切開。每一對八分音符的前一個被拉長，後一個被縮短。人們常把前一個理解成後一個的兩倍長，也就是三連音的感覺，比例 2:1。但實際上，比例常常細微得多。在爵士樂裡，搖擺比例通常落在 1:1 到 3:1 之間，變化很大。速度慢時比例傾向變大，速度快時傾向變小。

A typical jazz group has a rhythm section, which provides the underlying rhythm, harmony, and pulse. The standard rhythm section is piano, bass, and drums. A soloist, or a horn section of woodwinds and brass, plays the melody on top. The bass often plays a walking bass line: unsyncopated notes of equal value, usually quarter notes, like the regular steps of someone walking.

一個典型的爵士樂團有節奏組，提供底下的節奏、和聲與脈動。標準的節奏組是鋼琴、貝斯和鼓。獨奏者，或是由木管與銅管組成的管樂組，在上面演奏旋律。貝斯常常彈行走低音（walking bass）：沒有切分、音值相等的音，通常是四分音符，像一個人規律的腳步。

Improvisation is one of jazz’s defining elements. Jazz improvisation is the spontaneous invention of melodic solo lines or accompaniment parts in a performance. It is usually done over a chord progression, so it is not random. For example, the twelve-bar blues is based on the I, IV, and V chords of a key. And not all jazz is improvised: swing-era big bands relied more on arrangements that were written or memorized, and the soloists improvised within them.

即興是爵士樂的決定性元素之一。爵士即興是在演出中，當場創造旋律的獨奏線條或伴奏聲部。它通常在和弦進行上進行，所以不是亂彈。例如十二小節藍調，是建立在一個調的 I、IV、V 和弦上。而且不是所有爵士樂都是即興的：搖擺時代的大樂團比較倚賴寫好或背熟的編曲，獨奏者在編曲裡即興。

Two more ingredients are blue notes and call and response. A blue note is sung or played at a slightly different pitch from standard, typically between a quartertone and a semitone. Call and response is a succession of two distinct phrases that works like a conversation in music: one musician offers a phrase, and a second answers. Syncopation places rhythmic stresses where they would not normally occur.

另外兩個要素是藍調音與呼應。藍調音是用和標準音高稍微不同的音高唱或奏出，通常相差四分之一音到一個半音之間。呼應是兩個不同樂句相連，像音樂裡的對話：一位樂手提出一個樂句，另一位回答。切分音是把節奏的重音放在平常不會出現的地方。

The tune and the band on this page are our own simple model, built to show one idea. Real players vary a great deal, and not all jazz swings: free jazz explored playing without regular meter or beat. Louis Armstrong (1901–1971) and Duke Ellington (1899–1974) are two of its famous figures. Ellington once said of the music, “It’s all music.”

這一頁的旋律和樂隊是我們自己的簡單模型，用來展示一個想法。真正的樂手變化很大，也不是所有爵士樂都搖擺：自由爵士探索沒有規則拍子與節拍的演奏。路易斯・阿姆斯壯（1901–1971）和艾靈頓公爵（1899–1974）是它著名的人物。艾靈頓曾說到這種音樂：「這全是音樂。」

Questions · 閱讀理解

What does “swing” do to a pair of eighth notes?

It lengthens the first note and shortens the second. The beat itself stays steady.

Is jazz improvisation random?

No. It is usually done over a chord progression, so it follows the harmony.

Does every jazz piece swing?

No. Free jazz explored playing without regular meter or beat.

Origins · 起源

Not one place, not one day不是一個地方、一個日子

Jazz is said to have originated in the African-American communities of New Orleans in the late 19th and early 20th centuries. But the Jazz article also says it did not begin as a single musical tradition in New Orleans or elsewhere. A single birthday for jazz is a convenient story, not a fact.
一般說爵士樂是十九世紀末到二十世紀初，從紐奧良的非裔美國人社群發展出來的。但同一篇條目也說，它並不是從紐奧良或別處的某一個傳統開始的。給爵士樂一個生日，是方便的故事，不是事實。

A Word · 一個詞

“Swing” twice兩個「搖擺」

The word swing has two main uses in music. One is the technique we heard: lengthening and shortening the notes in each pair. The other is a style of jazz, “swing music”, that developed in the United States in the late 1920s and early 1930s and was dominant from 1935 to 1946. Taiwan’s dictionary describes 搖擺樂 as emphasizing the weak beats of the bar.
「swing」在音樂裡有兩個主要用法。一個是我們聽到的技巧：把每一對音的前一個拉長、後一個縮短。另一個是一種爵士樂的風格「搖擺樂」，在 1920 年代末到 1930 年代初的美國形成，1935 到 1946 年最盛行。台灣的辭典描述搖擺樂強調小節裡的下半拍或弱拍。

The Band · 樂隊

Who plays what誰彈什麼

The rhythm section, piano, bass, and drums, provides the underlying rhythm, harmony, and pulse. The core elements are usually the drum kit and bass. In a swing band, the classic rhythm section was a quartet of electric guitar, piano, double bass, and drums. As bebop evolved, smaller groups dropped the guitar.
節奏組，鋼琴、貝斯和鼓，提供底下的節奏、和聲與脈動。核心通常是鼓組和貝斯。搖擺樂團的經典節奏組是電吉他、鋼琴、低音提琴和鼓的四重奏。隨著咆勃（bebop）發展，較小的團體把吉他拿掉了。

The Taichung Jazz Festival is an annual jazz music festival held in the West District of Taichung. According to Wikipedia, it started in 2003 and is held in October. We could not find a source for other facts about jazz in Taiwan, so we leave them out.

Myth vs. Fact · 常見迷思

Four things people get wrong 四個常見的誤會

✗ Myth · 迷思Jazz was born in one place on one date.爵士樂在一個地方、一個日子誕生。

✓ Fact · 事實It grew out of African-American communities in New Orleans, but it did not begin as a single tradition in one place.它從紐奧良的非裔美國人社群發展出來，但並不是在一個地方、從一個傳統開始的。

✗ Myth · 迷思Jazz is all improvised, and so it is random.爵士樂全是即興，所以是亂彈。

✓ Fact · 事實Improvisation is usually done over a chord progression. Swing-era big bands relied more on arrangements that were written or memorized.即興通常是在和弦進行上做的。搖擺時代的大樂團比較倚賴寫好或背熟的編曲。

✗ Myth · 迷思Swing is exactly a 2:1 triplet feel.搖擺剛好就是 2:1 的三連音。

✓ Fact · 事實2:1 is how swing is often understood. In practice the ratio is often more subtle, typically between 1:1 and 3:1, and it varies.2:1 是人們常用來理解搖擺的方式。實際上比例常常細微得多，通常在 1:1 到 3:1 之間，而且會變。

✗ Myth · 迷思Jazz standards were all written by jazz composers.爵士標準曲都是爵士作曲家寫的。

✓ Fact · 事實Not all jazz standards were written by jazz composers.不是所有爵士標準曲都是爵士作曲家寫的。

Protect Your Ears · 保護耳朵

Before you listen 聽之前

  • Start with the volume low and turn it up a little at a time. The hi-hat is a bright, high sound, so it can feel sharper than it is loud.先把音量調小，再一點一點調大。hi-hat 是明亮的高音，會比它的音量讓人覺得更尖。
  • With headphones, keep the volume at no more than 60% of maximum, and use personal audio devices for less than one hour a day, as the World Health Organization suggests.戴耳機時，音量不要超過最大音量的 60%，個人音響裝置每天使用少於一小時，這是世界衛生組織的建議。
  • At a live concert, stand away from the speakers, and give your ears a quiet break afterward.在現場音樂會，離喇叭遠一點，結束後讓耳朵安靜休息一下。

Classroom Activity 1 · 課堂活動一

Straight, Then Swung 先平均，再搖擺

You need · 準備材料

  • A short, familiar rhyme or nursery song一首短短的、大家熟悉的童謠
  • Your hands for clapping用來拍手的雙手

Steps · 步驟

  1. Clap a steady beat, four counts, and keep it going all the time.拍一個穩定的拍子，數四拍，一直保持。
  2. Say the rhyme with two equal syllables on each beat: “ti-ti, ti-ti”.把童謠念出來，每一拍兩個一樣長的音節：「ti-ti, ti-ti」。
  3. Say it again, but make the first syllable longer and the second shorter: “long-short, long-short”. Do not change the clapping.再念一次，但把前面的音節拉長、後面的縮短：「長—短，長—短」。拍手不要改。
  4. Talk about it: what changed, and what stayed the same?討論：什麼變了？什麼沒變？

The beat stays the same; only the words move. If the clapping speeds up, start again.
拍子不變，只有字在動。如果拍手跟著變快，就重來。

Classroom Activity 2 · 課堂活動二

A Class Rhythm Section 班級節奏組

You need · 準備材料

  • A desk or drum for the “bass drum”一張桌子或鼓，當作「低音鼓」
  • Classroom percussion, such as a tambourine or blocks教室打擊樂器，例如鈴鼓或木塊
  • One singer or player as the soloist一位歌者或演奏者當獨奏者

Steps · 步驟

  1. One group plays steady quarter notes, like a walking bass: one note on every beat.一組彈穩定的四分音符，像行走低音：每一拍一個音。
  2. A second group plays only on beats 2 and 4.第二組只在第二拍和第四拍敲。
  3. The soloist invents a short phrase over it, and the group answers: call and response.獨奏者在上面創造一個短樂句，大家回答：呼應。
  4. Swap roles and try again.換角色，再來一次。

Let the soloist use only two or three notes at first. A small set of notes makes improvising easier.
一開始讓獨奏者只用兩、三個音。音少一點，即興比較容易。
