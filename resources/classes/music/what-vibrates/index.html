Big idea · 一句話看懂

Every instrument sounds because something vibrates. We group instruments by what vibrates: a string, a column of air, a membrane, or the body of the instrument itself.

Unit 9: How Do Instruments Make Sound?

Every sound starts with something that vibrates. Musical instruments can be grouped by what it is. A well-known system was devised by Erich von Hornbostel and Curt Sachs and published in 1914. It uses four families: chordophones, where a string vibrates; aerophones, where air vibrates; membranophones, where a stretched membrane vibrates; and idiophones, where the body of the instrument itself vibrates. In Taiwan they are 弦鳴樂器, 氣鳴樂器, 膜鳴樂器, and 體鳴樂器.

每個聲音都始於某個東西在振動。樂器可以依「是什麼在振動」來分組。有一套著名的系統，由霍恩博斯特爾（Erich von Hornbostel）和薩克斯（Curt Sachs）提出，1914 年發表。它分成四個家族：弦鳴樂器，是弦在振動；氣鳴樂器，是空氣在振動；膜鳴樂器，是拉緊的膜在振動；體鳴樂器，是樂器本身在振動。在台灣的標準譯名是弦鳴樂器、氣鳴樂器、膜鳴樂器和體鳴樂器。

A string’s pitch depends on three things. The shorter the string, the higher the pitch. The tighter the string, the higher the pitch. And the lighter the string, the higher the pitch. A string alone barely moves the air, so most string instruments have a body. The soundboard moves a greater volume of air than the thin string, and that makes the sound louder. The body does not add energy. The string is still where the sound comes from. Strings can be plucked, bowed, or struck.

弦的音高取決於三件事：弦越短，音越高；弦越緊，音越高；弦越輕，音越高。弦自己幾乎推不動空氣，所以大多數弦樂器有琴身。共鳴板比細細的弦推動更多的空氣，聲音就變大聲。琴身不會增加能量；聲音的來源還是弦。弦可以撥、可以用弓拉，也可以敲。

In a wind instrument the air inside a tube vibrates, and the player starts it in one of three ways: by blowing across an edge, as in a flute, by making a reed vibrate, as in a clarinet, or by buzzing the lips, as in a trumpet. The pitch depends on the length of the tube. Finger holes, valves, and slides change the effective length. A tube that is closed at one end makes only the odd harmonics, 1, 3, 5, and so on, and sounds an octave lower than an open tube of the same length. A clarinet behaves roughly like that, and a flute behaves like an open tube.

管樂器是管子裡的空氣在振動，演奏者用三種方式讓它開始：吹過邊緣，如長笛；讓簧片振動，如單簧管；或用嘴唇嗡嗡振動，如小號。音高取決於管子的長度，按音孔、活塞和滑管會改變有效長度。一端封閉的管子只有奇數諧波 1、3、5……，基音比同長度的開管低一個八度。單簧管大致像這樣；長笛則像開管。

A drum has a stretched membrane. When you tighten the head, the pitch goes up. A circular membrane does not vibrate in whole-number multiples. For an ideal drumhead the overtones are about 1.59, 2.14, 2.30, and 2.92 times the lowest frequency. That makes a drum sound different from a string, and many drums have no clear pitch. But some, such as the timpani, are tuned to definite pitches with a pedal that changes the tension of the head.

鼓有一張拉緊的膜。把鼓皮拉緊，音就變高。圓形的膜不是整數倍地振動：理想鼓皮的泛音大約是最低頻率的 1.59、2.14、2.30、2.92 倍。這讓鼓聽起來和弦不同，許多鼓沒有明確的音高。不過有些鼓，例如定音鼓，用踏板改變鼓皮的張力，可以調到確定的音高。

In a marimba or a xylophone, the bar itself vibrates. A free bar has overtones at about 2.76, 5.40, and 8.93 times its lowest frequency, which are not whole numbers either. Makers tune a marimba bar by taking wood from the middle of its underside, which lowers the pitch. The shorter the bar, the higher the pitch. A bell is a body too. A well-tuned bell has a hum, a strike tone, a tierce, a quint, and a nominal.

在馬林巴琴或木琴裡，是琴鍵那根棒本身在振動。兩端自由的棒，泛音大約是最低頻率的 2.76、5.40、8.93 倍，也不是整數倍。製作者調馬林巴琴鍵的音，是從琴鍵底面中間削掉木頭，音會變低。棒越短，音越高。鐘也是體鳴樂器。調好音的鐘有低音（hum）、敲擊音（strike）、小三度音（tierce）、五度音（quint）和高八度音（nominal）。

So the family is not about the material. A saxophone is usually made of brass, but it is a woodwind, because a reed starts the vibration. Our ears also use more than the overtones to tell instruments apart. Researchers say the timbre depends mainly on the frequency spectrum, but also on the attack and on how the sound changes in time. The sounds on this page are our own simple recipes, so real instruments sound richer.

所以家族和材料沒有關係。薩克斯風通常是銅做的，但它是木管樂器，因為開始振動的是簧片。我們的耳朵也不只靠泛音來分辨樂器。研究者說，音色主要取決於頻率頻譜，但也取決於起音和聲音隨時間的變化。本頁的聲音是我們自己的簡單配方，所以真樂器聽起來更豐富。

Questions · 閱讀理解

Why is a saxophone a woodwind, even though it is usually made of brass?

Because a vibrating reed starts the sound. The family depends on how the vibration is started, not on the material.

What happens to a string’s pitch when you make the string half as long?

The frequency doubles, so the pitch goes up one octave. This is the 2 to 1 ratio from lesson 1.

What makes the overtones of a drum different from those of a string?

A string’s overtones are whole-number multiples, 1, 2, 3… times the lowest. A drumhead’s overtones are not: about 1, 1.59, 2.14, 2.30, 2.92.

Stories Behind the Notes · 音符背後的故事

A system, eight sounds, a flute with a skin, and a carved bar 一套系統、八音、一支有膜的笛，和一根削過的琴鍵

Four short stories about how people have grouped and built instruments.
關於人們怎麼把樂器分組、怎麼製作樂器的四個小故事。

A System · 一套系統

Four families, then a fifth四個家族，後來又多一個

The Hornbostel–Sachs system was first published in 1914. It builds on an earlier classification by Mahillon, who divided instruments by the sound-producing material: an air column, a string, a membrane, or the body of the instrument. In 1940 Sachs added a fifth group, the electrophones, for instruments that use electricity.
霍恩博斯特爾—薩克斯系統在 1914 年首次發表。它建立在馬約（Mahillon）更早的分類上：他依發聲的材料把樂器分成空氣柱、弦、膜和樂器本身。1940 年薩克斯加上第五類，電鳴樂器，給使用電的樂器。

Eight Sounds · 八音

Classifying by material依材質分類

Ancient China had its own system, called the eight sounds: metal, stone, silk, bamboo, gourd, clay, hide, and wood. Taiwan’s dictionary gives examples: bells are metal, chimes stone, the zither silk, flutes bamboo, the sheng gourd, the xun clay, the drum hide, and the zhu and yu wood. It sorts by what an instrument is made of, which is a different principle from the four families.
中國古代有自己的系統，叫八音：金、石、絲、竹、匏、土、革、木。台灣的辭典舉例：鐘屬金、磬屬石、琴瑟屬絲、簫笛屬竹、笙屬匏、塤屬土、鼓屬革、柷敔屬木。它依樂器的材料分類，和四個家族的原則不同。

A Flute with a Skin · 有膜的笛

The bangdi梆笛

Taiwan’s dictionary describes the bangdi, a bamboo flute played sideways, as having one blow hole, one membrane hole, and six finger holes. So an instrument that is played by blowing has a hole made for a membrane. The erhu has a membrane too, but in another place: it is a bowed string instrument, with a python skin stretched over its resonator.
台灣的辭典描述梆笛是橫吹的竹笛，有一個吹孔、一個膜孔、六個按音孔。也就是說，這支用吹的樂器，有一個為膜而開的孔。二胡也有膜，但在別的地方：它是擦弦樂器，在琴筒上蒙著蟒皮。

A Carved Bar · 削過的琴鍵

Tuning by taking wood away削掉木頭來調音

A marimba maker tunes a bar by taking wood from the middle of its underside, which lowers the pitch. On a vibraphone, the arch ground under the bar makes the first overtones line up in consonant intervals: for an F3 bar, the bar gives F3, its first overtone is F5, and its second is A6. A glockenspiel, by comparison, has no arch at all, and a xylophone has a shallower one.
馬林巴琴的製作者，從琴鍵底面中間削掉木頭來調音，音會變低。在顫音琴上，琴鍵底下磨出的弧度，讓前幾個泛音排成協和的音程：以 F3 的琴鍵為例，基音是 F3，第一個泛音是 F5，第二個是 A6。相較之下，鋼片琴完全沒有弧度，木琴的弧度比較淺。

Myth vs. Fact · 常見迷思

Four things people get wrong 四個常見的誤會

✗ Myth · 迷思A drum has no pitch.鼓沒有音高。

✓ Fact · 事實Some drums, such as the timpani, are tuned to definite pitches. Many other percussion instruments, like snare drums and cymbals, have no pitch you can name.有些鼓，例如定音鼓，是調到確定音高的。很多其他打擊樂器，像小鼓和鈸，沒有可以說出名字的音高。

✗ Myth · 迷思The body of a violin makes the sound.小提琴的琴身發出聲音。

✓ Fact · 事實The string is where the sound starts. The body vibrates too, and it moves more air, which makes the sound louder, but it adds no energy.聲音是從弦開始的。琴身也會振動，它推動更多的空氣，讓聲音變大聲，但它沒有增加能量。

✗ Myth · 迷思Woodwinds are made of wood and brass instruments of brass.木管樂器是木頭做的、銅管樂器是銅做的。

✓ Fact · 事實The family depends on how the vibration starts. A saxophone is usually brass but is a woodwind, because a reed vibrates. A wooden cornett is a brass instrument, because the player’s lips vibrate.家族取決於振動怎麼開始。薩克斯風通常是銅做的，卻是木管樂器，因為振動的是簧片；木製的短號是銅管樂器，因為振動的是演奏者的嘴唇。

✗ Myth · 迷思A wind instrument just blows air.管樂器只是把空氣吹進去。

✓ Fact · 事實Blowing is only the start. The player uses an edge, a reed, or buzzing lips to make the air inside the tube vibrate.吹氣只是開始。演奏者靠邊緣、簧片或嘴唇的振動，讓管子裡的空氣振動。

Protect Your Ears · 保護耳朵

Before you listen 聽之前

  • Start with the volume low and turn it up a little at a time. The drum and bar sounds on this page start with a sharp attack, so be careful with the volume.先把音量調小，再一點一點調大。本頁鼓和棒的聲音一開始有尖銳的起音，音量要特別小心。
  • With headphones, keep the volume at no more than 60% of maximum, and use personal audio devices for less than one hour a day, as the World Health Organization suggests.戴耳機時，音量不要超過最大音量的 60%，個人音響裝置每天使用少於一小時，這是世界衛生組織的建議。
  • Real drums, cymbals, and bells are much louder than this page. Do not put your ear close to them when they are played.真的鼓、鈸和鐘比本頁大聲得多。有人敲打時，不要把耳朵靠近它們。

Classroom Activity 1 · 課堂活動一

Sort the Sounds 把聲音分家族

You need · 準備材料

  • Safe, ordinary things: a rubber band, a ruler, a drinking straw, a plastic bottle, a paper cup, a spoon, a glass jar安全的日常物品：橡皮筋、尺、吸管、塑膠瓶、紙杯、湯匙、玻璃罐
  • Four sheets of paper labeled string, air, membrane, body四張紙，分別寫著弦、空氣、膜、體

Steps · 步驟

  1. In small groups, make a sound with each object. Gently: pluck the rubber band, blow across the top of the bottle, tap the stretched surface of a cup or jar lid, tap the spoon on a jar.小組輪流用每個東西發出聲音。要輕：撥橡皮筋、對著瓶口吹、輕敲繃緊的杯面或罐蓋、用湯匙輕敲玻璃罐。
  2. Ask: what is vibrating? Put each object on the sheet for its family.問：是什麼在振動？把每一個東西放在它那一家的紙上。
  3. Find an object that could fit two families. Argue for each side, then decide what starts the vibration.找一個可能屬於兩個家族的東西。為兩邊辯論，再決定是什麼開始振動。
  4. Share your sorted sheets with another group. Did you put anything differently?把分好類的紙給另一組看。你們有沒有放得不一樣的？

Keep everything gentle and keep glass on the table. Do not stretch rubber bands near anyone’s face.
動作要輕，玻璃放在桌上。不要在別人臉旁邊拉橡皮筋。

Classroom Activity 2 · 課堂活動二

The Rubber-Band Guitar 橡皮筋吉他

You need · 準備材料

  • An open box or a plastic container一個開口的盒子或塑膠盒
  • Rubber bands of different thickness不同粗細的橡皮筋
  • A pencil to use as a bridge一支鉛筆當琴馬

Steps · 步驟

  1. Stretch several rubber bands over the box. Pluck each one and listen. Which sounds higher?把幾條橡皮筋套在盒子上。每一條撥一撥，聽聽看。哪一條比較高？
  2. Slide the pencil under the bands to make them shorter. What happens to the pitch?把鉛筆塞到橡皮筋下面，讓振動的部分變短。音高怎麼變？
  3. Stretch one band tighter. What happens now?把一條橡皮筋拉得更緊。這時候會怎樣？
  4. Write down what makes the pitch go higher. Compare with the “How can you make it higher?” sliders on this page.寫下什麼讓音高變高。和本頁「怎麼讓它變高」的滑桿比較。

Use thin, new rubber bands and do not stretch them far, so they do not snap. Keep the box low and away from your face.
用細細、新的橡皮筋，不要拉得太長，免得彈斷。盒子放低，離臉遠一點。
