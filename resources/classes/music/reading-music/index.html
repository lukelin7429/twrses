Reading · 英文閱讀

5

Unit 5: How Do You Read Music?

Writing needs letters, and music needs a way to write too. A composer who writes a tune on paper must tell a player which notes to play, and how high or low each one is. Over many centuries, people found a simple idea: put the notes on a ladder. The higher a note sits on the page, the higher its pitch.

寫文字需要字母，寫音樂也需要一種寫法。作曲家把旋律寫在紙上，要告訴演奏的人彈哪些音，每個音有多高或多低。經過許多個世紀，人們找到一個簡單的辦法：把音符放在一座梯子上。音符在紙上畫得越高，音高就越高。

That ladder is the staff. It is made of five horizontal lines with four spaces between them. A note can sit on a line, or in a space. We count the lines from the bottom: the first line is the lowest. In British English the staff is usually called a stave.

這座梯子就是五線譜（staff）。它由五條水平的線和線與線之間的四個間組成。音符可以在線上，也可以在間裡。線從下面數起：第一線是最低的。英國英文通常把五線譜叫做 stave。

But which note is which line? That is the job of the clef, the symbol at the beginning. The treble clef is also called the G clef. Its curl goes around the second line, and tells you that this line is G4, the G above middle C. The bass clef is also called the F clef. Its two dots sit above and below the fourth line, and tell you that this line is F3. These shapes began as the letters G and F, and became stylized over time.

可是哪一條線是哪個音？這是開頭那個符號——譜號（clef）的工作。高音譜號也叫 G 譜號，它的捲曲圈住第二線，說明這條線是 G4，也就是中央 C 上面的那個 G。低音譜號也叫 F 譜號，它的兩個點夾住第四線，說明這條線是 F3。這些形狀原來是 G 和 F 兩個字母，後來慢慢變成了圖案。

Once you know one line, you know them all. Notes go up in alphabetical order, through the letters A to G, and then start again. On the treble clef the lines, from the bottom, are E, G, B, D, F, and the spaces are F, A, C, E. Many students remember them as “Every Good Boy Does Fine” and “FACE.” On the bass clef the lines are G, B, D, F, A, and the spaces are A, C, E, G. If a note is above or below the staff, we add a short extra line, called a ledger line.

只要知道一條線，其他的就都知道了。音依字母順序往上走，A 到 G，然後再從頭開始。高音譜號從下往上的五條線是 E、G、B、D、F，四個間是 F、A、C、E。很多學生用「Every Good Boy Does Fine」和「FACE」來記。低音譜號的線是 G、B、D、F、A，間是 A、C、E、G。如果音在五線的上方或下方，就加一小段短線，叫做加線（ledger line）。

Middle C shows how clefs work together. It sits one ledger line below the treble staff, and also one ledger line above the bass staff. It is the same note, the same C4, written in two places. In Taiwan, many schools sing notes with the names Do, Re, Mi, Fa, Sol, La, Si. The English letter names C, D, E, F, G, A, B go with them. A letter name is repeated in every octave, because notes an octave apart share a name.

中央 C 最能看出兩個譜號怎麼合作。它在高音譜號的下方加一線上，也在低音譜號的上方加一線上。同一個音、同一個 C4，寫在兩個地方。在台灣，很多學校用 Do、Re、Mi、Fa、Sol、La、Si 唱音，英文字母音名 C、D、E、F、G、A、B 和它們對應。字母名稱在每一個八度裡重複，因為相差八度的音共用同一個名字。

The names Do, Re, Mi came from a hymn. In the eleventh century in Italy, a music teacher named Guido of Arezzo named the notes of a six-note pattern after the first syllables of the lines of a Latin hymn, “Ut queant laxis.” Later, “Ut” was replaced by the easier “Do.” Some systems keep Do fixed on C, which is called fixed do. Others move Do to the first note of any key, which is called movable do. Taiwan’s school music also uses numbers, 1 to 7, in a notation called jianpu. It writes the same notes as the staff, and both appear in the curriculum.

Do、Re、Mi 這些名字來自一首讚美詩。十一世紀的義大利，音樂老師圭多（Guido of Arezzo）用拉丁文讚美詩〈Ut queant laxis〉每一行的第一個音節，替一組六個音命名。後來「Ut」被比較好唱的「Do」取代。有的系統讓 Do 固定在 C 上，叫固定唱名法；有的系統讓 Do 跟著任何調的第一個音走，叫首調唱名法。台灣的學校音樂也用數字 1 到 7 的記譜法，叫簡譜。它寫的是和五線譜同樣的音，兩種寫法都出現在課綱裡。
Read aloud · 課文朗讀

Questions · 閱讀理解

Why do we need a clef at the beginning of a staff?

The five lines and four spaces are only positions. A clef names the note on one line, and then all the other lines and spaces follow from it.

A note sits on the first line of a treble staff. Which note is it? What about the first line of a bass staff?

On the treble staff the first line is E4. On the bass staff the first line is G2. The same position means different notes in different clefs.

Is middle C in the treble clef a different note from middle C in the bass clef?

No. It is the same note, C4, 261.63 Hz. In the treble clef it is written on a ledger line below the staff, and in the bass clef on a ledger line above it.

Stories Behind the Staff · 五線譜背後的故事

A hymn, a ladder, two letters, and a row of numbers 一首讚美詩、一座梯子、兩個字母，和一排數字

Four short stories about how music came to be written down.
關於音樂怎麼被寫下來的四個小故事。

A Hymn · 一首讚美詩

Where Do, Re, Mi came fromDo、Re、Mi 從哪裡來

In eleventh-century Italy, the music theorist Guido of Arezzo named the six notes of a pattern after the first syllable of each line of a Latin hymn to St. John: ut, re, mi, fa, sol, la. Each line of the hymn begins one step higher than the last, so singers could remember each note by its line. Later “ut” was changed to the easier “do.” Sources disagree about who changed it and when, so we only say that it happened.
十一世紀的義大利，音樂理論家圭多（Guido of Arezzo）用一首獻給聖約翰的拉丁文讚美詩，每一行的第一個音節，替一組六個音命名：ut、re、mi、fa、sol、la。這首詩每一行的開頭都比上一行高一階，歌者就可以靠那一行詩記住那個音。後來「ut」被改成比較好唱的「do」。各處資料對誰改的、什麼時候改的說法不一，所以我們只說有這回事。

A Ladder · 一座梯子

A staff that grew from four lines to five從四線長成五線

The modern use of staff lines is attributed to Guido of Arezzo, whose four-line staff is still used in chant books today. Five-line staves appeared in Italy in the 13th century. Staves with four, five, and six lines were all used as late as about 1600. The five-line staff we know became the standard only after that.
現代使用的譜線，歸功於圭多，他的四線譜到今天仍用在聖歌集裡。五線譜在十三世紀出現在義大利。一直到大約 1600 年，四線、五線、六線的譜都還有人用。我們熟悉的五線譜，是在那之後才成為標準。

Two Letters · 兩個字母

Clefs are letters in disguise譜號是變了形的字母

At first, a staff had a letter written at its beginning to show which line it named. Over time the shapes of these letters became stylized. The treble clef comes from G, and its curl circles the G line. The bass clef comes from F, and its two dots sit around the F line. There is also a third kind, the C clef, used for the viola and some other instruments.
一開始，五線譜的開頭是直接寫一個字母，表示那一條線的名字。隨著時間，這些字母的形狀變得圖案化。高音譜號來自 G，它的捲曲圈住 G 線；低音譜號來自 F，它的兩個點夾住 F 線。還有第三種，C 譜號，中提琴和一些其他樂器會用到。

A Row of Numbers · 一排數字

Jianpu, the numbered notation簡譜，用數字寫的音樂

Instead of a staff, numbered notation writes the notes of a scale as the numbers 1 to 7, which stand for Do, Re, Mi, Fa, Sol, La, Si. A dot above a number means an octave higher, and a dot below means an octave lower. Rousseau presented a similar idea to the French Academy of Sciences in 1742, and the Galin–Paris–Chevé system developed it. It came to China by way of Japan around the turn of the twentieth century.
簡譜不用五線，而是把音階的音寫成數字 1 到 7，它們代表 Do、Re、Mi、Fa、Sol、La、Si。數字上方加一點是高八度，下方加一點是低八度。盧梭在 1742 年向法國科學院提出類似的想法，後來由加林—帕里斯—謝韋（Galin–Paris–Chevé）體系發展它。它大約在十九世紀末二十世紀初，經由日本傳到中國。

Myth vs. Fact · 常見迷思

Four things people get wrong 四個常見的誤會

✗ Myth · 迷思A position on the staff always has the same note name.五線譜上的同一個位置，永遠是同一個音名。

✓ Fact · 事實The clef decides. The first line is E4 in the treble clef, and G2 in the bass clef. Without a clef, a staff is only a set of positions.由譜號決定。第一線在高音譜號是 E4，在低音譜號是 G2。沒有譜號，五線譜只是一組位置。

✗ Myth · 迷思Middle C is a different note in the treble and bass clefs.中央 C 在高音譜號和低音譜號是不同的音。

✓ Fact · 事實It is the same note, C4. It sits on a ledger line below the treble staff, and on a ledger line above the bass staff.它是同一個音，C4。它在高音譜表下方的加線上，也在低音譜表上方的加線上。

✗ Myth · 迷思Do always means the note C.Do 永遠就是 C 這個音。

✓ Fact · 事實Only in fixed do. In movable do, Do is the first note of whatever key the music is in. The curriculum names “solfège” but does not choose between them.只有固定唱名法是這樣。首調唱名法裡，Do 是這首音樂所在調的第一個音。課綱只寫「唱名法」，沒有在兩者之間選一個。

✗ Myth · 迷思Do, Re, Mi were made up to teach children.Do、Re、Mi 是為了教小朋友才編出來的。

✓ Fact · 事實They come from the first syllables of the lines of a Latin hymn, and they were in use in the eleventh century in Italy. Teachers have used them to teach singing for about a thousand years.它們來自一首拉丁文讚美詩每一行的第一個音節，十一世紀的義大利就在用。老師用它們教唱歌，已經差不多一千年。

Classroom Activity 1 · 課堂活動一

A Floor Staff 地板上的五線譜

You need · 準備材料

  • Five long strips of masking tape or five ropes五條長長的紙膠帶或五條繩子
  • A large card with a clef drawn on it一張畫了譜號的大卡片
  • Cards with the letters C D E F G A B寫了 C D E F G A B 的卡片

Steps · 步驟

  1. Lay the five strips on the floor, about one big step apart, to make a staff. Put the treble clef card at the left end.把五條帶子鋪在地上，每條相隔大約一步，做成五線譜。把高音譜號的卡片放在左端。
  2. One student stands on a line or in a space. The class says the note name. The student with the keyboard, or the page, plays it to check.一位同學站在一條線上或一個間裡。全班說出音名。拿著鍵盤或用本頁的同學彈出來核對。
  3. Move the clef card to a bass clef card. Does the same place have the same name?把譜號卡片換成低音譜號的卡片。同一個位置還是同一個名字嗎？
  4. Let a student stand one step above the top line. How many ledger lines does that note need?讓一位同學站在最上面那條線再往上一格的地方。這個音需要幾條加線？

Walk, do not run, and keep the strips taped down so nobody slips.
用走的，不要跑，帶子要貼牢，免得有人滑倒。

  • A keyboard, recorder, or the page on this screen一個鍵盤、直笛，或這個螢幕上的頁面

Steps · 步驟

  1. Draw five lines on the paper, evenly spaced, and write a treble clef on the left.在紙上畫五條間隔均勻的線，並在左邊寫一個高音譜號。
  2. Put the coins on the lines and spaces to make a short tune, and write the letter names under them.把硬幣放在線和間上，做成一小段旋律，並在下面寫出字母音名。
  3. Play the tune on the page or an instrument, from the letters. Does it sound the way you pictured it?照著字母，在頁面或樂器上把旋律彈出來。聽起來和你想的一樣嗎？
  4. Swap papers with a partner and play each other’s tunes.和同伴交換紙，彼此演奏對方的旋律。

Use only notes between the lowest and the highest line at first. Add a ledger line only after you can read the staff itself.
一開始只用最低線到最高線之間的音。等你會讀五線以後，再加加線。
