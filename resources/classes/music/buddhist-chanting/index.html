Unit 16: What Is Buddhist Chanting?

In Chinese Buddhism, chanting is called fanbai. The Taiwan dictionary says it means, in China, chanting short verses or songs of praise. Chinese Wikipedia says it gradually took on a specific melody and rhythm to make reciting easier. The melodies are traditionally passed on by ear from one generation to the next, and the chanting book usually does not mark the melody. In this lesson we look only at the sound and the rhythm.

在漢傳佛教裡，唱誦叫做梵唄。台灣的辭典說，在中國，它指唱頌短偈或歌讚。中文維基百科說，它為了方便誦念，慢慢形成了特定的旋律與節奏。旋律傳統上是一代一代用耳朵傳下去的，課誦本通常也不標示旋律。這一課我們只看聲音與節奏。

When a group chants, many people sing the same melody at the same time. In music this is called unison. Someone leads. The dictionary says the leader, the weina, starts the chant and leads the group by striking the small bell, the yinqing, in the morning and evening sessions. Those sessions are held every day, and everyone in the temple takes part.

一群人唱誦的時候，很多人同時唱同一條旋律。音樂上這叫齊唱（unison）。有人帶領。辭典說，帶領的維那在早晚課時起腔，並敲引磬領眾。這樣的早晚課每天都有，全寺的人一起參加。

A temple has many ritual instruments, called faqi. The dictionary lists instruments such as the fish, the cymbals, and the bell. The wooden fish has two shapes. One is a long, straight fish that hangs in a corridor, to call people to a meal or to gather them. The other is round, and it sits on a table when chanting. The round one is hollow, and the hollow sound changes with its size, material, and the hollow inside. Chinese Wikipedia says a bigger one has a lower pitch.

寺院裡有很多法器。辭典列出的有魚、鐃、鈸、磬等。木魚有兩種形狀。一種是挺直的魚形，掛在走廊上，用來叫人吃飯或聚集大家。另一種是圓的，誦經時放在桌上。圓的是中空的，中空的聲音隨它的大小、材質和裡面的空腔而不同。中文維基百科說，木魚越大，音高越低。

How is the wooden fish used? English Wikipedia says that in Chan, Seon, Zen, and Thien Buddhism, the wooden fish serves to maintain rhythm during chanting. The mallet often has a rubber-coated tip, which gives a muffled but clear sound. A wooden fish often rests on a cushion, to prevent unpleasant knocking sounds. That is why our model uses a short, quickly fading knock, once on every beat.

木魚怎麼用？英文維基百科說，在禪宗（Chan、Seon、Zen、Thien）裡，木魚用來維持唱誦的節奏。木槌的前端常包著橡膠，敲出來悶悶的、但很清楚。木魚常放在軟墊上，避免敲出難聽的碰撞聲。所以我們的模型用短而快速消失的敲擊，每一拍一下。

The small bell, the yinqing, is a small bowl-shaped copper bell with a wooden handle, used to get everyone’s attention. A bigger bowl-shaped bell, the qing, is struck during chanting and ceremonies. Be careful with the word qing. It also names an ancient hanging chime of stone or metal. The dictionary lists the two as different instruments that share a character.

引磬是小小的碗形銅鈴，附木柄，用來引起大家的注意。比較大的碗形的磬，則在誦經和法會時敲。要小心「磬」這個字。它也是古代用石頭或金屬做的懸掛樂器的名字。辭典把兩者列成不同的樂器，只是共用一個字。

How do many voices breathe? A singer cannot hold a note forever, so there must be breaths. If everyone breathes at the same time, there is a small gap in the sound. A technique for choirs, called staggered breathing, has each singer breathe at a different time, so that no breath gaps can be heard. We use it in our model to show the idea. We do not say that any chanting group does it. Chanting is not one sound either: it varies by tradition, with some more monotone and some more melodic.

很多人怎麼換氣？一個人不能一直拉著一個音，所以一定要換氣。如果大家同時換氣，聲音就會有一個小空隙。合唱團有一種技巧叫輪流換氣（staggered breathing），每個人在不同時間換氣，這樣就聽不到換氣的空隙。我們的模型用它來展示這個想法。我們並沒有說任何唱誦的團體這樣做。唱誦也不是只有一種聲音：因傳統而不同，有些比較接近單調，有些比較有旋律。

Questions · 閱讀理解

What does the wooden fish do in a group chant?

English Wikipedia says that in Chan, Seon, Zen, and Thien Buddhism, it serves to maintain rhythm during chanting.

What does a leader use the small bell for?

The dictionary says the leader strikes the small bell to start the chant and lead the group.

Why does our model use “breathe in turn”?

To show the choir technique of staggered breathing: no breath gaps can be heard. We do not claim that chanters use it.

Stories Behind the Notes · 音符背後的故事

A word, two fish, two bells, and two styles 一個詞、兩種魚、兩種磬、兩種風格

Four short stories about sound and instruments. We leave the teachings to the teachers of each tradition.
關於聲音與樂器的四個小故事。教義留給各傳統的老師來講。

A Word · 一個詞

Fanbai, passed on by ear梵唄，用耳朵傳下去

The dictionary describes fanbai as singing short verses or songs of praise. Chinese Wikipedia says the specific melodies came to be shaped to make reciting easier, and that the chanting book usually gives no melody marks. English Wikipedia says the melodies are traditionally passed on only by ear from generation to generation. Traditional accounts of where it began are contested, so we give no date.
辭典把梵唄描述為唱頌短偈或歌讚。中文維基百科說，特定的旋律是為了方便誦念而形成的，課誦本通常不標示旋律。英文維基百科說，旋律傳統上只用耳朵一代傳一代。關於它從哪裡開始，傳統的說法有爭議，所以我們不寫年代。

Two Fish · 兩種魚

Hanging and sitting掛著的、坐著的

The dictionary says the wooden fish has two shapes. One is a long straight fish, hung in temple corridors to call people to a meal or to gather them. The other is a round fish, placed on a table when chanting. A different instrument, the fish drum, is a bamboo tube covered with fish skin, used in storytelling. It is not the temple wooden fish.
辭典說木魚有兩種形狀。一種是挺直的魚形，掛在寺院走廊上，用來叫人吃飯或聚集大家。另一種是圓形的，誦經時放在桌上。另一件樂器魚鼓，是一端蒙魚皮的竹筒，用在說唱。它不是寺院的木魚。

Two Bells · 兩種磬

One character, two instruments一個字，兩種樂器

The dictionary lists two meanings for the character qing. One is an ancient hanging chime made of jade, stone, or metal, shaped like a carpenter’s square. The other is a hollow copper instrument shaped like a bowl, struck in temples. English Wikipedia’s article on standing bells adds that the word, which historically meant a lithophone used in state rituals, was more recently applied to this type of bell.
辭典列出「磬」的兩個意思。一個是古代用玉石或金屬做的懸掛樂器，形狀像曲尺。另一個是寺院裡敲的碗形中空銅製樂器。英文維基百科的「站立式鈴」條目補充說，這個詞歷史上指國家儀式用的石磬，後來才被用在這一種鈴上。

Taiwan · 台灣

Two styles heard in Taiwan台灣聽得到的兩種風格

Wikipedia says that in Taiwan, the styles of fanbai can mainly be divided into two types. The Gushan style is faster-paced, with more ornamentation around the core notes. The Haichaoyin style is slower, relaxed, and steady, with a simpler melody. Wikipedia does not cite a source for this, so we treat it as a second-hand description. We could not find an official record about fanbai.
維基百科說，在台灣，梵唄的風格主要分成兩種。鼓山調速度較快，骨幹音上加花比較多。海潮音速度比較舒緩平穩，旋律比較簡單。維基百科沒有為這段話列出處，所以我們把它當作第二手的描述。我們也找不到關於梵唄的官方紀錄。

Myth vs. Fact · 常見迷思

Four things people get wrong 四個常見的誤會

✗ Myth · 迷思All Buddhist chanting sounds the same.所有佛教的唱誦聽起來都一樣。

✓ Fact · 事實The intonation and style vary by tradition, some more monotone and others more melodic. Even in Taiwan, two styles are named.音調與風格因傳統而不同，有的比較接近單調，有的比較有旋律。光在台灣，就有兩種風格。

✗ Myth · 迷思The word “qing” always means a stone chime.「磬」一定是石磬。

✓ Fact · 事實The dictionary lists two meanings: an ancient hanging chime, and a hollow copper bowl-shaped instrument struck in temples.辭典列了兩個意思：古代的懸掛樂器，和寺院裡敲的碗形中空銅製樂器。

✗ Myth · 迷思A wooden fish is a drum made from fish skin.木魚是用魚皮做的鼓。

✓ Fact · 事實The wooden fish is a hollow wooden instrument. The fish drum, a different instrument, is covered with fish skin.木魚是中空的木製樂器。魚鼓是另一種樂器，才蒙著魚皮。

✗ Myth · 迷思Singing bowls are ancient chanting tools.頌缽是古老的唱誦工具。

✓ Fact · 事實English Wikipedia says the manufacture and use of bowls specifically for “singing” is believed to be a modern phenomenon.英文維基百科說，專門做來「唱」的缽，其製作與使用被認為是現代的現象。

Protect Your Ears and Voice · 保護耳朵與聲音

Before you listen and hum 聽與哼之前

  • Start with the volume low and turn it up a little at a time. A knock is short and sharp, so it can feel louder than it is.先把音量調小，再一點一點調大。敲擊又短又尖，會讓人覺得比實際更大聲。
  • With headphones, keep the volume at no more than 60% of maximum, and use personal audio devices for less than one hour a day, as the World Health Organization suggests.戴耳機時，音量不要超過最大音量的 60%，個人音響裝置每天使用少於一小時，這是世界衛生組織的建議。
  • When you hum a long note, stay in a comfortable range and breathe whenever you need to. Stop if you feel dizzy.哼長音的時候，留在舒服的音域，需要就換氣。覺得頭暈就停下來。

Classroom Activity 1 · 課堂活動一

One Beat, One Breath 一個拍子、一口氣

You need · 準備材料

  • A desk and a soft object, such as a folded cloth, to knock on一張桌子和一個軟的東西，例如折起來的布，用來敲
  • A classroom clock or a metronome教室的時鐘或節拍器

Steps · 步驟

  1. One student knocks a slow, steady beat on the cloth, one knock every second.一位學生在布上敲一個緩慢穩定的拍子，每秒一下。
  2. The rest of the class hums one low note for six beats, then breathes for two beats. Everyone breathes at the same time.其他人哼一個低音六拍，再換氣兩拍。大家同時換氣。
  3. Try again, but let each small group breathe at a different time. Can you make the sound never stop?再來一次，但讓每一小組在不同時間換氣。你們能讓聲音不中斷嗎？
  4. Talk about it: what was hard? What did the steady knock help with?討論：什麼最難？穩定的敲擊幫了什麼忙？

Keep the knock soft. The knocker must not speed up or slow down, even when the hum changes.
敲得輕一點。敲的人不能加快或放慢，即使哼的聲音變了。

Classroom Activity 2 · 課堂活動二

Listening With Respect 尊重地聆聽

You need · 準備材料

  • Paper and a pen紙和筆
  • A teacher or a guest who is willing to talk about their own community’s music一位願意聊聊自己社區音樂的老師或來賓

Steps · 步驟

  1. Ask: how do people in your community behave when they listen to music at a special event?問：你們社區的人在特別的場合聽音樂時，怎麼表現？
  2. Write three ways to show respect when listening to music from a culture that is not your own.寫下三種聽別的文化的音樂時表示尊重的方法。
  3. If you visit a temple or another place of worship, follow the guidance of the people there.如果你去寺院或其他的宗教場所，聽那裡的人的指引。
  4. Share your list with the class.和全班分享你的清單。

Do not record or share someone’s music without asking them first.
沒有先問過，不要錄別人的音樂，也不要分享。
