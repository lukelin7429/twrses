Big idea · 一句話看懂

A scale is a ladder of notes from low to high. The steps between the notes are whole steps and half steps, and the pattern of those steps decides which scale it is.

Unit 7: What Is a Scale?

A scale is a series of notes arranged from low to high, from one note up to the same note an octave higher. The first note is called the tonic. The English word scale comes from the Latin scala, which means ladder, and that is a good picture: every note is a rung, and you climb from the tonic to the top.

音階是一串由低到高排好的音，從一個音一直排到高八度的同一個音。第一個音叫主音。英文的 scale 來自拉丁文的 scala，意思是梯子，這是很好的畫面：每個音是一格橫木，你從主音往上爬。

The distance between neighboring rungs comes in two sizes. A half step, also called a semitone, is the distance between two neighboring keys on a piano. A whole step is two half steps. In the equal temperament you met in lesson 1, each half step is a frequency ratio of 2 to the power of 1/12, so two half steps multiply the frequency by about 1.12. On the piano, E to F and B to C are half steps, because there is no black key between them.

相鄰兩格之間的距離有兩種大小。半音（semitone）是鋼琴上相鄰兩個鍵的距離；全音是兩個半音。在你第一課認識的十二平均律裡，每個半音的頻率比是 2 的 1/12 次方，所以兩個半音會把頻率乘上大約 1.12。在鋼琴上，E 到 F、B 到 C 是半音，因為它們之間沒有黑鍵。

A major scale has a pattern of seven steps: whole, whole, half, whole, whole, whole, half. That is W W H W W W H. Start on C and you only need white keys: C D E F G A B C. The two half steps fall between the third and fourth notes, and between the seventh and eighth. In equal temperament, the notes of a major scale are 0, 200, 400, 500, 700, 900, 1100, and 1200 cents above the tonic.

大調音階有七個步階的圖樣：全、全、半、全、全、全、半，也就是 W W H W W W H。從 C 開始，只要用白鍵：C D E F G A B C。兩個半音落在第三、四音之間，和第七、八音之間。在十二平均律裡，大調音階各音比主音高 0、200、400、500、700、900、1100、1200 音分。

The pattern is what makes the scale, not the starting note. Start on D and keep W W H W W W H, and you must use F♯ and C♯. A sharp raises a note by a half step. A flat lowers it. A natural minor scale has a different pattern: W H W W H W W. Compared with a major scale from the same tonic, its third, sixth, and seventh notes are a half step lower. From A, it uses only white keys: A B C D E F G A.

決定一個音階的是圖樣，不是起點。從 D 開始、維持 W W H W W W H，就必須用 F♯ 和 C♯。升記號把音升高一個半音，降記號把音降低一個半音。自然小調音階的圖樣不同：W H W W H W W。和同一個主音的大調比，它的第三、第六、第七個音各低一個半音。從 A 開始，只用白鍵：A B C D E F G A。

Many people hear major scales as brighter and minor scales as more serious or sad. Taiwan’s dictionary says a major key usually sounds brighter than a minor one. But research suggests this link is at least partly learned, and it also depends on tempo and context. So try it yourself: play the same tune in both, and describe what you hear in your own words.

許多人聽大調覺得比較明亮，聽小調覺得比較嚴肅或悲傷。台灣的辭典說，大調的色彩通常比小調明朗。不過研究認為，這種連結至少有一部分是學來的，也和速度與情境有關。所以請你自己試試：同一段旋律用兩種音階各彈一次，用你自己的話形容聽到的。

Not every scale has seven notes. A pentatonic scale has five notes in an octave. The major pentatonic scale uses the first, second, third, fifth, and sixth notes of a major scale: C D E G A. The five black keys on a piano also make a pentatonic scale. Pentatonic scales are found in many cultures. In Chinese music, the five notes are named gong, shang, jue, zhi, and yu, and each of them can be the tonic of its own mode.

不是每個音階都有七個音。五聲音階在一個八度裡有五個音。大調五聲音階用大調音階的第一、二、三、五、六音：C D E G A。鋼琴上的五個黑鍵，也組成一個五聲音階。許多文化都有五聲音階。在中國音樂裡，這五個音叫宮、商、角、徵、羽，每一個音都可以當自己那個調式的主音。

Questions · 閱讀理解

In the C major scale, which neighboring notes are a half step apart?

E and F, and B and C. Those are the pairs with no black key between them, and they are the third and fourth notes, and the seventh and eighth notes of the scale.

Why does a major scale that starts on D need black keys?

Because the pattern W W H W W W H must stay the same. Starting on D, the third note has to be F♯, and the seventh note has to be C♯, so two black keys join in.

Are the black keys the only pentatonic scale on a piano?

No. They make one pentatonic scale, F♯ major pentatonic. The white keys C D E G A also make a pentatonic scale, and so do many other groups of five.

Stories Behind the Notes · 音符背後的故事

A ladder, five tones, a cut pipe, and a world of five notes 一座階梯、五個音、一根被截短的管，和世界各地的五個音

Four short stories about how people have named and built scales.
關於人們怎麼為音階命名、怎麼建構音階的四個小故事。

A Ladder · 一座階梯

Scale means ladderscale 就是梯子

According to Wikipedia, the English word scale comes from the Latin scala, which literally means ladder. Taiwan’s dictionary calls 音階 a translation of the English word scale. Each note is a rung, and the steps between the rungs are not always the same size.
依維基百科，英文 scale 來自拉丁文 scala，字面意思是「梯子」。台灣的辭典說「音階」是英文 scale 的意譯。每個音是一格橫木，而橫木之間的距離並不總是一樣大。

Five Tones · 五音

Gong, shang, jue, zhi, yu宮、商、角、徵、羽

Taiwan’s dictionary lists the five tones of ancient Chinese music as 宮, 商, 角, 徵, and 羽, and cites the Zuo Zhuan and the Rites of Zhou. A reference entry adds that each of the five tones can serve as the tonic, which gives five different pentatonic modes. They are often matched with Do Re Mi Sol La, but only roughly, because any of the five can be the tonic.
台灣的辭典把中國古代音樂的五音列為宮、商、角、徵、羽，並引用《左傳》和《周禮》。一則辭書條目補充：這五個音每一個都可以當主音，所以有五種不同的五聲調式。它們常被對應到 Do Re Mi Sol La，但只是大致對應，因為五個音都可以當主音。

A Cut Pipe · 一根被截短的管

Subtract a third, add a third三分損益

Ancient Chinese texts, including the Guanzi and the Lüshi Chunqiu, describe making notes by cutting a length into three parts and either removing one part or adding one. Start with 81. Remove a third and you get 54. Add a third to that and you get 72. Taiwan’s reference entry says this works out the same, mathematically, as the method of Pythagoras in ancient Greece. Removing a third leaves 2/3 of the length, the same 2 : 3 you saw in lesson 1.
中國古代文獻，包括《管子》和《呂氏春秋》，記載把一段長度分成三份，再減去一份或加上一份來生出音。從 81 開始，減去三分之一得到 54；再加上它的三分之一得到 72。台灣的辭書條目說，這在數理上和古希臘畢達哥拉斯的方法相通。減去三分之一，剩下 2/3 的長度，就是第一課看過的 2 比 3。

Five Notes Worldwide · 世界各地的五個音

Pentatonic songs you may know你可能聽過的五聲音階歌曲

Wikipedia says pentatonic scales were developed independently by many ancient civilizations. It names the hymn “Amazing Grace” and the Scottish tune of “Auld Lang Syne” as pentatonic melodies. The Chinese folk song “Mo Li Hua” (Jasmine Flower) uses a five-note scale that is common in Chinese music. Try playing them on the black keys.
維基百科說，許多古老的文明各自發展出五聲音階。它舉出聖詩〈Amazing Grace〉和蘇格蘭曲調〈Auld Lang Syne〉是五聲音階的旋律；中國民歌〈茉莉花〉用的是中國音樂裡很常見的五個音的音階。你可以試著用黑鍵彈彈看。

Myth vs. Fact · 常見迷思

Four things people get wrong 四個常見的誤會

✗ Myth · 迷思Major is happy and minor is sad.大調就是快樂，小調就是悲傷。

✓ Fact · 事實Many listeners hear major as brighter and minor as more serious, but studies suggest the link is at least partly learned, and it depends on tempo and context as well.許多聽眾覺得大調較明亮、小調較嚴肅，但研究認為這種連結至少有一部分是學來的，也和速度與情境有關。

✗ Myth · 迷思The black keys are the pentatonic scale.黑鍵就是五聲音階。

✓ Fact · 事實The black keys make one pentatonic scale. The white keys C D E G A make another, and there are many more.黑鍵組成的是其中一個五聲音階。白鍵 C D E G A 也是一個，還有很多別的。

✗ Myth · 迷思A scale must have eight notes.音階一定有八個音。

✓ Fact · 事實A major scale has seven different notes, and the eighth is the tonic again. A pentatonic scale has five.大調音階有七個不同的音，第八個是再回到主音。五聲音階只有五個。

✗ Myth · 迷思A half step is a note that is half as long.半音就是長度只有一半的音。

✓ Fact · 事實A half step is about pitch, not length. It is the distance between two neighboring piano keys, and a whole step is two half steps.半音說的是音高，不是長短。它是鋼琴上相鄰兩個鍵的距離，兩個半音是一個全音。

Protect Your Ears · 保護耳朵

Before you listen 聽之前

  • Start with the volume low and turn it up a little at a time. The sound bar on this page starts at 30%.先把音量調小，再一點一點調大。本頁的聲音列一開始是 30%。
  • With headphones, keep the volume at no more than 60% of maximum, and use personal audio devices for less than one hour a day, as the World Health Organization suggests.戴耳機時，音量不要超過最大音量的 60%，個人音響裝置每天使用少於一小時，這是世界衛生組織的建議。
  • When you sing or hum a scale, stay in a range that is comfortable. Do not push your voice too high or too loud, and stop if your throat hurts.唱或哼音階時，待在舒服的音域裡。不要把聲音勉強唱得太高或太大聲；喉嚨痛就停下來。

Classroom Activity 1 · 課堂活動一

The Human Scale 人體音階

You need · 準備材料

  • Eight students, or a row of eight chairs八位同學，或排成一排的八張椅子
  • Two sticky notes marked W and H for each gap每個空隙兩張標了 W 和 H 的便利貼
  • A keyboard, a xylophone, or this page鍵盤、木琴或本頁

Steps · 步驟

  1. Students stand in a row and each is one note of C major, from C up to the next C.同學排成一列，每個人是 C 大調的一個音，從 C 到上一個 C。
  2. Between each pair of neighbors, place a W or an H sticky note, following W W H W W W H.在每兩位相鄰的同學之間貼上 W 或 H 的便利貼，依照 全 全 半 全 全 全 半。
  3. Sing the scale up, with each student singing only their own note. Notice where the half steps are.把音階唱上去，每位同學只唱自己的那個音。注意半音在哪裡。
  4. Now ask the group to start on G. Who needs to change? (Hint: one student has to become F♯.)再請大家改從 G 開始。誰要換？（提示：有一位同學必須變成 F♯。）

Let students step forward as they sing, and take the half steps as smaller steps. Then the pattern can be seen and felt.
讓同學唱的時候往前一步，半音的地方走小一點的步子。這樣圖樣看得見，也感覺得到。

Steps · 步驟

  1. Write the tune as scale numbers. Play it in a major scale.把曲子寫成音階的數字。用大調音階演奏。
  2. Lower the third note by a half step and play it again. Does it sound different? How would you describe the change?把第三個音降低一個半音再彈一次。聽起來不一樣嗎？你會怎麼形容這個改變？
  3. Try the pentatonic scale by skipping the fourth and seventh notes of the major scale.試試五聲音階：跳過大調音階的第四和第七個音。
  4. Talk it over: what words did different students use? Is there a right answer?討論一下：不同同學用了哪些詞？有標準答案嗎？

Accept every honest description. Listeners often hear a scale differently, and that is part of what the activity shows.
每個誠實的形容都接受。不同的人聽音階的感覺常常不一樣，這也是這個活動要呈現的。
