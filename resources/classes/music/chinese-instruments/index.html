Big idea · 一句話看懂

A Chinese orchestra sorts its instruments by how they are played: blown, bowed, plucked, or struck. Each way sets something different vibrating, and the sounds you hear come from that.
國樂團把樂器依演奏方式分組：吹、拉、彈、打。每一種方式讓不同的東西振動，你聽到的聲音就從那裡來。

Unit 12: What Do Chinese Instruments Sound Like?

A Chinese orchestra, called a guoyuetuan in Taiwan, was developed in the twentieth century. It is based on the structure and principles of a Western symphony orchestra, but it uses Chinese instruments. It grew out of the Jiangnan sizhu ensembles in the 1920s. Its instruments are grouped by the way they are played: blown, bowed, plucked, and struck. In Chinese these are 吹, 拉, 彈, and 打.

國樂團（在台灣叫國樂團，英文用拼音 guoyuetuan）是二十世紀發展出來的。它以西洋交響樂團的結構與原則為基礎，但使用中國的樂器。它從 1920 年代的江南絲竹合奏發展而來。樂器依演奏方式分組：吹、拉、彈、打。

The erhu is a Chinese two-stringed bowed instrument. One end of its small sound box is covered with python skin, and the bow hair passes between the two strings instead of over them. There is no fingerboard, so the player presses the strings with the fingertips in the air. The two strings are commonly tuned a fifth apart. The erhu is not a violin, even though people sometimes call it the Chinese violin.

二胡是一種中國的兩條弦的拉弦樂器。它小小的琴筒一端蒙著蟒皮，弓毛是從兩條弦之間穿過去，不是在弦的上面。它沒有指板，演奏的人用指尖在空中按弦。兩條弦常常相隔五度定弦。二胡不是小提琴，雖然人們有時叫它「中國的小提琴」。

The pipa is a plucked instrument with a pear-shaped body and four strings. Taiwan’s dictionary says a pear-shaped, four-stringed pipa came to the Central Plains in the fourth century from the western regions. The pipa has been played in China for almost two thousand years. In the Chinese word, “pí” is to strike outward with the right hand, and “pá” is to pluck inward.

琵琶是一種彈撥樂器，半梨形的音箱、四條弦。台灣的辭典說，半梨形、四弦四柱的琵琶，在西元四世紀從西域傳入中原。琵琶在中國已經彈了將近兩千年。在這個詞裡，「pí」是右手向外彈，「pá」是向內挑。

The dizi is a transverse bamboo flute. A bangdi has one blow hole, one membrane hole, and six finger holes. The membrane hole is between the blow hole and the finger holes. It is covered with a thin membrane, the dimo, which makes the sound louder and brighter, with a buzzing quality. The bangdi is shorter and higher, with a clear, bright tone. The qudi is longer and mellower, and about a fourth lower.

笛是橫吹的竹笛。梆笛有一個吹孔、一個膜孔、六個按音孔。膜孔在吹孔和按音孔之間，蒙著一層薄膜，叫笛膜，它讓聲音變大、變清脆明亮，帶著嗡嗡的質感。梆笛比較短、比較高，音色清脆明亮；曲笛比較長，音色比較柔和，音大約低四度。

A drum, a gu, is a skin stretched over a hollow wooden body, played with the hands, sticks, or mallets. A gong, a luo, is a flat bronze disc struck with a mallet. In Chinese opera, the percussion section is particularly important, and a pair of gongs is an essential part of the orchestra. The overtones of a drumhead and of a gong are not whole-number multiples, as you saw in lesson 9.

鼓是把皮蒙在中空木桶上的樂器，用手、木棒或鼓槌來敲。鑼是銅製的淺平圓盤，用槌敲。在戲曲裡，打擊特別重要，一對鑼是樂隊的重要部分。鼓皮和鑼的泛音不是整數倍，這在第九課你看過了。

A lot of Chinese music is built on five notes, called gong, shang, jue, zhi, and yu. Any of the five can be the tonic. The guqin was said to have had five strings for these five notes, and the guzheng is traditionally arranged in a pentatonic scale. But this is a tendency and not a rule: some pieces use seven notes. In Taiwan, two old traditions are nanguan, with a pipa held across the lap, and beiguan, with suona, drums, and gongs.

許多中國音樂是用五個音來寫的，叫宮、商、角、徵、羽，每一個音都可以當主音。古琴傳說原本有五條弦，代表這五個音；古箏傳統上也是按五聲音階排列。不過這是傾向，不是規則：有些曲子用七個音。在台灣，有兩種古老的傳統：南管，琵琶橫抱在膝上彈；和北管，用嗩吶、鼓和鑼。

Questions · 閱讀理解

Why does the erhu’s bow stay between the two strings?

The horsehair bow passes between the two strings, so one stroke of the bow rubs both strings, and the player chooses which one by pressing and angling the bow. It is never separated from the strings.

What does the membrane on a dizi do?

It is a thin membrane over the membrane hole. It makes the sound louder and brighter, and gives the tone a buzzing quality.

Is “blown, bowed, plucked, struck” an ancient way of sorting Chinese instruments?

No. It is the grouping of the modern Chinese orchestra, from the twentieth century. The older Chinese way is the eight sounds, which sorts by material: metal, stone, silk, bamboo, gourd, clay, hide, wood.

Stories Behind the Notes · 音符背後的故事

Four short stories about Chinese instruments and the music of Taiwan.
關於國樂器和台灣音樂的四個小故事。

A Modern Orchestra · 現代的樂團

From Shanghai to Taipei從上海到台北

The Chinese orchestra is a twentieth-century development. In 1921 Zheng Jinwen founded the Great Unity Music Society in Shanghai and grew the ensemble to 35 players. The Broadcasting Chinese Orchestra, founded in Nanjing in 1935, is considered the first Chinese orchestra, and it moved to Taiwan in 1949. The Taipei Chinese Orchestra was founded in 1979, and the National Chinese Orchestra began in 1984 as an experimental orchestra at a school, and has had its present name since 2012.
國樂團是二十世紀發展出來的。1921 年，鄭覲文在上海成立大同樂會，把合奏團擴大到 35 位演奏者。1935 年在南京成立的中央廣播電臺國樂團，被認為是第一個國樂團，並在 1949 年隨政府遷到台灣。臺北市立國樂團成立於 1979 年；臺灣國樂團 1984 年從國立藝專的實驗國樂團開始，2012 年起改用現在的名稱。

A Membrane · 一片膜

Why does a flute have a skin?為什麼笛有一層膜？

Most flutes have no membrane at all. The dizi has a special hole, the mo kong, covered by a thin shaving of reed or bamboo called the dimo. Taiwan’s dictionary says it enlarges the volume and makes the tone clear and bright. English-language sources add that the sound gets a buzzing, nasal quality. So a dizi is an air column instrument with a vibrating membrane in it.
大多數的笛根本沒有膜。笛有一個特別的孔，叫膜孔，蓋著一層薄薄的蘆葦或竹子的膜，叫笛膜。台灣的辭典說，它讓音量變大、音色清脆明亮。英文的資料補充，聲音多了一種嗡嗡的、帶鼻音的質感。所以笛是一件空氣柱樂器，裡面有一片會振動的膜。

A Lute on the Lap · 橫抱的琵琶

Nanguan南管

Taiwan’s dictionary describes nanguan as ancient music that has been popular in Fujian, Taiwan, and Southeast Asia since the Song and Yuan dynasties. The singer holds clappers, and the accompaniment is led by the pipa, with the dongxiao vertical flute, the sanxian, the erxian, and clappers. The pipa is held horizontally across the lap. Nanguan came to Taiwan with immigrants from Fujian, and it became popular in places such as Lukang. UNESCO listed it in 2009.
台灣的辭典說，南管是宋元以後流行於福建、臺灣及東南亞地區的古樂。歌者拿著拍板，伴奏以琵琶為主，再配上洞簫、三弦、二弦和拍板。琵琶是橫抱在膝上彈的。南管隨著福建的移民傳到台灣，在鹿港等地很盛行。2009 年被聯合國教科文組織列入名錄。

A Street Band · 廟會的樂隊

Beiguan北管

Beiguan is a traditional music that was widespread in Taiwan. It usually uses two suona, a woodblock, large and small gongs, drums, cymbals, and also a pipa. Most of the lively street bands that join temple festivals are beiguan bands, and there are the most clubs in Changhua and Yilan. The name beiguan, “northern,” is relative to nanguan from southern Fujian. It was probably brought to Taiwan in the years of the Qianlong and Jiaqing emperors, though where it came from in Fujian is not certain.
北管是曾經在台灣很流行的傳統音樂。通常使用兩支嗩吶、梆子、大鑼小鑼、鼓、鈸，還有琵琶。大多數在廟會裡熱鬧高亢的音樂陣頭，是北管樂團；彰化和宜蘭的館閣最多。「北管」的「北」，是相對於來自閩南的南管。它大約在乾隆、嘉慶年間傳入台灣，不過它在福建的來源並不確定。

Myth vs. Fact · 常見迷思

Four things people get wrong 四個常見的誤會

✗ Myth · 迷思The erhu is a Chinese violin.二胡就是中國的小提琴。

✓ Fact · 事實It has two strings and no fingerboard, and the bow passes between the strings. It is a spike fiddle, sometimes called the Chinese violin, but it is a different instrument.它有兩條弦、沒有指板，弓從兩條弦之間穿過。它是一種插在琴筒上的提琴，有時被叫做「中國小提琴」，但它是另一種樂器。

✗ Myth · 迷思The guqin and the guzheng are the same.古琴和古箏是同一種。

✓ Fact · 事實The guqin has seven strings and no bridges, and it is very quiet. The guzheng has movable bridges and many more strings, 21 to 25.古琴有七條弦、沒有琴馬，聲音很小。古箏有可以移動的琴馬、弦多得多，有 21 到 25 條。

✗ Myth · 迷思The dizi is 9,000 years old.笛有 9000 年的歷史。

✓ Fact · 事實Simple flutes without the membrane hole are that old. The dizi as we know it dates roughly to the 5th century BC.沒有膜孔的簡單笛子才有那麼老。我們今天知道的笛，大約可以追溯到西元前五世紀。

✗ Myth · 迷思Chinese music uses only five notes.中國音樂只用五個音。

✓ Fact · 事實Many pieces are built on five notes, but it is a tendency, not a rule. Some use seven notes, and modern music can be quite chromatic.許多曲子建立在五個音上，但這是傾向，不是規則。有些用七個音，現代的音樂也可以很半音化。

Protect Your Ears · 保護耳朵

Before you listen 聽之前

  • Start with the volume low and turn it up a little at a time. The drum and the bright flute sounds can be sharp.先把音量調小，再一點一點調大。鼓和明亮的笛聲可能很尖銳。
  • With headphones, keep the volume at no more than 60% of maximum, and use personal audio devices for less than one hour a day, as the World Health Organization suggests.戴耳機時，音量不要超過最大音量的 60%，個人音響裝置每天使用少於一小時，這是世界衛生組織的建議。
  • Real gongs, drums, and suona are very loud up close. Stay back and ask your teacher about ear protection if you play them.真的鑼、鼓和嗩吶近距離聽非常響。要離遠一點；如果要演奏它們，請問老師有沒有護耳的辦法。

Sort the Instruments 把樂器分類

You need · 準備材料

  • Cards with names: erhu, pipa, dizi, dongxiao, guzheng, drum, gong寫了名字的卡片：二胡、琵琶、笛、洞簫、古箏、鼓、鑼
  • Four sheets: blown, bowed, plucked, struck四張紙：吹、拉、彈、打

Steps · 步驟

  1. In small groups, put each card on the sheet that shows how the instrument is played. Use the lesson to check.小組把每張卡片放在代表演奏方式的那張紙上。用這一課來核對。
  2. Ask for each one: what vibrates? A string, the air, or a skin?每一件都問：是什麼在振動？弦、空氣，還是皮？
  3. Find the instruments that sort differently in the eight sounds, by material.找出用八音（依材質）來分，會分到不同地方的樂器。
  4. Share: which instruments did you know already? Which are new?分享：哪些樂器你本來就知道？哪些是新的？

Photos or videos from the teacher help, but the lesson itself does not use any recordings.
老師準備的照片或影片會有幫助，但這一課本身沒有用到任何錄音。

Classroom Activity 2 · 課堂活動二

A Five-Note Tune 五音旋律

You need · 準備材料

  • A keyboard, or this page鍵盤或本頁
  • Paper and pencils紙和鉛筆

Steps · 步驟

  1. Play only the five notes on this page, or the five black keys of a piano. Everything you play will sound fine together.只彈本頁的五個音，或鋼琴的五個黑鍵。你彈的任何音放在一起都會很順耳。
  2. Write a short tune of eight notes, and end on the first note, the gong.寫一段八個音的短旋律，結束在第一個音，宮。
  3. Play it for a partner. Can your partner clap the rhythm back?彈給同伴聽。同伴能把節奏拍回來嗎？
  4. Try ending on a different note. How does the feeling of ending change?試試結束在不同的音。結束的感覺怎麼變？

Let students use their own words for how an ending feels.
讓學生用自己的話，說結束的感覺怎麼樣。
