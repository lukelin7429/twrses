Unit 4: How Do You Tell a Musician “Louder” or “Faster”?

Imagine you are a composer, and the musician who will play your piece is far away, or lives a hundred years after you. You can write the notes. But how loud should they be? How fast? Notes alone only tell the player which pitch and how long. To say the rest, composers add signs and words.

想像你是一位作曲家，要演奏你作品的人在很遠的地方，或是在你之後一百年才出生。你可以把音寫下來，可是要多大聲？多快？光靠音符，只能告訴演奏的人音高和長短。要說明其餘的事，作曲家就加上記號和字。

There are two questions. How loud is the music? That is called dynamics. How fast is it? That is called tempo. For loudness, composers use letters. The letter p stands for piano, which means “soft.” The letter f stands for forte, which means “loud.” The word mezzo means “half,” so mp is a medium soft and mf is a medium loud. Doubling a letter makes it stronger: pp is pianissimo, very soft, and ff is fortissimo, very loud.

有兩個問題。音樂有多大聲？這叫做「力度」（dynamics）。音樂有多快？這叫做「速度」（tempo）。說明大聲小聲，作曲家用字母：p 是 piano，意思是「弱」；f 是 forte，意思是「強」。mezzo 是「一半」，所以 mp 是中弱、mf 是中強。字母重疊就更強：pp 是 pianissimo，很弱；ff 是 fortissimo，很強。

These signs are always relative. A p never means a precise amount of loudness. It only says that this part should be quieter than the parts marked f. In fact, a tuba playing mezzo piano can be louder than a guitar playing forte. Each player decides what soft and loud are for the instrument and the room.

這些記號永遠是相對的。p 絕對不是指一個精確的音量，它只是說這一段應該比標 f 的段落安靜。事實上，大號吹 mezzo piano，可能比吉他彈 forte 還大聲。什麼叫弱、什麼叫強，要由演奏者依樂器和場地來決定。

Loudness can also change gradually. Crescendo means “growing,” and it is drawn as two lines that open to the right, like <. Diminuendo means “dwindling,” and it is drawn as two lines that close to the right, like >. These signs are sometimes called hairpins. In Chinese they are 漸強 and 漸弱.

大聲小聲也可以慢慢改變。crescendo 是「增長」，畫成向右打開的兩條線，像 <；diminuendo 是「逐漸減少」，畫成向右合起來的兩條線，像 >。這兩個記號有時被叫做「髮夾」。中文是「漸強」和「漸弱」。

For speed, the words are also Italian. From slow to fast, a few well-known ones are largo (“broad”), adagio (“at ease”), andante (“walking”), moderato (“moderate”), allegro (“lively”), and presto (“quick”). To change the speed, ritardando means slowing down, and accelerando means speeding up. Such words give a feeling and a rough speed. A metronome mark, which you met in the last lesson, gives the exact number.

速度的詞也是義大利文。由慢到快，幾個有名的是 largo（寬廣）、adagio（從容）、andante（走路的速度）、moderato（中等）、allegro（輕快）和 presto（很快）。要改變速度，ritardando 是漸慢、accelerando 是漸快。這些詞給的是一種感覺和大略的速度；你在上一課認識的節拍器標記，才給精確的數字。

Why Italian? The practice grew up in the 17th and 18th centuries, when most composers were Italian. Later, composers also wrote in French, German, and English. And why is a piano called a piano? It is short for pianoforte, “soft and loud.” Early in the 1700s, the keyboard instrument made by Cristofori could play both, depending on how hard the player pressed the key. The harpsichord and the organ could not do this.

為什麼是義大利文？這個做法是在十七、十八世紀發展起來的，當時大多數的作曲家是義大利人。後來作曲家也用法文、德文和英文。那鋼琴為什麼叫 piano？它是 pianoforte 的縮寫，意思是「弱與強」。十八世紀初，克里斯托弗里（Cristofori）做出的這種鍵盤樂器，可以依彈奏的人按鍵多用力，彈出弱和強。大鍵琴和管風琴就做不到。

Questions · 閱讀理解

What is the difference between dynamics and tempo?

Dynamics are about how loud the music is. Tempo is about how fast it is. They are separate, so a melody can be soft and fast, or loud and slow.

Why can't a sign such as f be given one fixed loudness number?

Dynamic signs are relative. They only say how one part compares with another. The same sign can mean a different volume in different pieces, on different instruments, and in different rooms.

A player sees mf, then a < sign, then f. What happens to the music?

The music starts moderately loud, then gets gradually louder, until it reaches loud. The notes and the speed stay the same.

    Stories Behind the Signs · 記號背後的故事

    A name, a language, a tuba, and two words that swapped 一個名字、一種語言、一支大號，和兩個對調的詞

    Four short stories about the words musicians use for loud, soft, and fast.
    關於音樂家用來說大聲、小聲、快的那些詞的四個小故事。

    A Name · 一個名字

    The piano is “soft and loud”鋼琴就是「弱與強」

    The piano takes its name from the Italian words for soft and loud. An inventory kept by the Medici family of Florence shows that Bartolomeo Cristofori had a piano by 1700. Cristofori called it a keyboard “of piano and forte.” Unlike the harpsichord, it changed its volume with the touch of the player, and the harder you press the key, the louder the sound.
    鋼琴（piano）的名字來自義大利文的「弱」和「強」。佛羅倫斯的梅迪奇家族的財物清冊顯示，克里斯托弗里（Bartolomeo Cristofori）在 1700 年已經有一架鋼琴。他把它叫做「有弱有強的」鍵盤樂器。它和大鍵琴不一樣，音量會隨彈奏的人的觸鍵而變，鍵按得越用力，聲音就越大。

    A Language · 一種語言

    Why Italian?為什麼是義大利文？

    Italian is typically used because it was the language of most composers when these words became common. The practice developed in the 17th and 18th centuries. Later, composers wrote speed words in their own languages too. Beethoven was one of the first German composers to do so, but only a little. Later still, Schumann and Mahler wrote more and more of their instructions in German.
    會用義大利文，是因為這些詞開始普遍的時候，大多數作曲家說的就是義大利文。這個做法在十七、十八世紀發展起來。後來作曲家也用自己的語言寫速度詞。貝多芬是最早用德文的德國作曲家之一，但用得很少。後來舒曼和馬勒，更是把越來越多的指示寫成德文。

    A Tuba · 一支大號

    Soft and loud depend on the instrument弱和強要看樂器

    Dynamic markings are always relative. Because of that, a tuba playing mezzo piano will likely be louder than a guitar playing forte. A high instrument such as the piccolo can sound loud even when it is not very strong. This is why a conductor listens to the whole group before deciding what “soft” means.
    力度記號永遠是相對的。所以大號吹 mezzo piano，很可能比吉他彈 forte 還大聲。像短笛這種高音樂器，就算不是真的很用力，也可能聽起來很響。這就是為什麼指揮要先聽整個團，才決定「弱」是什麼意思。

    Two Words · 兩個詞

    When largo and adagio swappedlargo 和 adagio 對調的時候

    Words for speed can change their meaning. A modern largo is slower than an adagio. In the Baroque period it was the other way around, and largo was the faster one. Allegretto changed too. This is one more reason why a metronome number is the only exact answer, and why the speed words give only a feeling.
    速度的詞，意思也會改變。現代的 largo 比 adagio 慢，但在巴洛克時期剛好相反，largo 比較快。allegretto 的意思也變過。這就是為什麼只有節拍器的數字才是精確的答案，而速度詞只給一種感覺。

    Myth vs. Fact · 常見迷思

    Four things people get wrong 四個常見的誤會

    ✗ Myth · 迷思Forte means a fixed number of decibels.Forte 就是固定幾分貝。

    ✓ Fact · 事實Dynamic markings are always relative. A forte never means a precise level of loudness. It only says to play louder than the soft parts of the same piece.力度記號永遠是相對的。forte 不是精確的音量，只是說要比同一首曲子裡弱的部分大聲。

    ✗ Myth · 迷思Playing louder also means playing faster.越大聲，就是越快。

    ✓ Fact · 事實Loudness and speed are separate. Dynamics change how loud, and tempo changes how fast. A melody can be quiet and quick, or loud and slow. Special words, ritardando and accelerando, change the speed.音量和速度是分開的。力度改變多大聲，速度改變多快。旋律可以又小聲又快，也可以又大聲又慢。改變速度要用另外的詞：ritardando 和 accelerando。

    ✗ Myth · 迷思Allegro means an exact number of beats per minute.Allegro 就是精確的每分鐘幾拍。

    ✓ Fact · 事實The numbers for the speed words are only very rough approximations, and different books give different ranges. A metronome mark is the only way to write an exact number.速度詞的數字只是非常粗略的近似，不同的書給的範圍也不同。只有節拍器標記，才能寫出精確的數字。

    ✗ Myth · 迷思A louder note is just the same note with the volume knob turned up.大聲的音，只是把同一個音的音量鈕轉大。

    ✓ Fact · 事實On an acoustic instrument the player changes the sound. On a piano, the harder you press a key, the harder the hammer hits the string and the louder the sound. The note’s character changes a little, too.在傳統樂器上，是演奏的人改變聲音。鋼琴上，鍵按得越用力，槌子打弦越用力，聲音就越大；音的性格也會稍微改變。

    Protect Your Ears · 保護耳朵

    Before you listen 聽之前

    • Start with the volume low and turn it up a little at a time. The sound bar on this page starts at 30%.先把音量調小，再一點一點調大。本頁的聲音列一開始是 30%。
    • The loudest sign here, fortissimo, is never louder than the loudest sound on the other pages. But if you turn the volume up to hear a soft sign, then a loud sign will be much louder. Move to a quieter room instead of turning the volume up.這裡最強的記號 fortissimo，也不會比其他頁面最大的聲音更大。但如果你為了聽清楚弱的記號而把音量調大，強的記號就會大聲很多。請改到比較安靜的地方，不要一直調大音量。
    • With headphones, keep the volume at no more than 60% of maximum, and use personal audio devices for less than one hour a day, as the World Health Organization suggests.戴耳機時，音量不要超過最大音量的 60%，個人音響裝置每天使用少於一小時，這是世界衛生組織的建議。
    • In the classroom, a crescendo with voices or clapping should stay in a comfortable range. Never clap or shout close to anyone's ears.在課堂上，用聲音或拍手做漸強時，要維持在舒服的範圍。千萬不要在別人耳朵旁邊拍手或大叫。

    Classroom Activity 1 · 課堂活動一

    Human Crescendo 人體漸強

    You need · 準備材料

    • The whole class, in a circle or in rows全班，圍成圓圈或排成幾列
    • One student as the conductor一位當指揮的同學
    • Cards with p, mf, f, <, and > written on them寫了 p、mf、f、<、> 的卡片

    Steps · 步驟

    1. Everyone claps lightly together on a steady beat. The conductor shows the card p, then mf, then f. The class matches it.大家跟著穩定的拍子輕輕一起拍手。指揮依序出示 p、mf、f 的卡片，全班跟著調整。
    2. Now the conductor shows < and slowly raises the hands. The class gets louder, little by little, and stops before it is too loud.接著指揮出示 <，慢慢舉高雙手。全班一點一點變大聲，在太大聲之前停下來。
    3. Try > the same way, and then try a crescendo that ends in silence.用同樣的方式試試 >，再試一個結束在安靜裡的漸強。
    4. Next, keep the loudness and change only the speed: slower, then faster. Which dial did you turn?再來，保持音量、只改變速度：先慢、再快。你轉的是哪一個旋鈕？

    Clap with soft hands, not hard hands. The loudest sound should still be comfortable, and the conductor decides when to stop.
    用軟軟的手拍，不要用力拍。最大聲也要覺得舒服，由指揮決定什麼時候停。

    Classroom Activity 2 · 課堂活動二

    Say It With Signs 用記號說一句話

    You need · 準備材料

    • A short sentence, for example: “I did not say that.”一句短句，例如：「我沒有那樣說。」
    • Paper and pencil紙和筆

    Steps · 步驟

    1. Say the sentence softly, as if it is a secret. Say it loudly, as if you are far away. Write p or f above it.用很小聲、像祕密一樣說這句話。再用很大聲、像在很遠的地方一樣說。在句子上面寫 p 或 f。
    2. Say it so that it gets louder word by word, and draw a < under it. Then say it getting softer, and draw a >.說的時候一個字比一個字大聲，在底下畫 <。再說成越來越小聲，畫 >。
    3. Say it slowly and then fast. Which words did you pick for speed, adagio or allegro?慢慢說、再快快說。你為速度選了哪個詞，adagio 還是 allegro？
    4. Swap sentences with a partner. Read the signs and perform it exactly as written.和同伴交換句子。照著記號，把它一模一樣地唸出來。

    You just did what a composer does: you wrote down how to say something, and someone else can perform it. Use a comfortable speaking volume and keep your voice from straining.
    你剛剛做的就是作曲家做的事：把「怎麼說」寫下來，讓別人也能照著表演。用舒服的說話音量，不要喊到喉嚨用力。
