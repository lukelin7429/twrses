How Long Is a Note?一個音要多長？

Fill a bar with note blocks until it is exactly full, then see it on a staff and hear it. Find out why every note is half as long as the one before it.
用音符積木把一小節剛好填滿，再看它寫在五線譜上、聽它播出來。看看為什麼每個音符都是前一個的一半長。

Sound stays off until you press the button, and it starts quiet. If you hear nothing on an iPhone, check the silent switch.按下按鈕之前不會發出聲音，而且一開始很小聲。如果在 iPhone 上聽不到，請檢查靜音開關。

Big idea · 一句話看懂

Note lengths keep getting cut in half: a whole note, then two half notes, four quarter notes, eight eighth notes. A time signature says how many beats fit in a bar, and a bar must be filled exactly.
音符的長度一路對半分：一個全音符，是兩個二分音符、四個四分音符、八個八分音符。拍號說每小節裡放得下幾拍，而一小節要剛好填滿。

Unit 6: How Long Is a Note?

Last time you learned where to put a note on the staff to show how high it is. But a note also needs to say how long it lasts. The answer is in its shape. A note value tells us the relative length of a note by the look of its head, whether it has a stem, and whether it has flags or beams.

上一課你學到在五線譜上把音放在哪裡，可以表示音有多高。可是一個音也要說明它持續多久。答案就在它的形狀裡。「音符時值」是靠符頭的樣子、有沒有符桿、有沒有符尾或符槓，來說明音符的相對長度。

The idea is easy to remember: each note is half as long as the one before it. A whole note is a hollow oval with no stem. Two half notes, which have a stem, fit in the time of one whole note. Four quarter notes, with filled heads, fit in the same time. Eight eighth notes, which have one flag each, and sixteen sixteenth notes, which have two, do too. In the United States we use these names. In British English they are called semibreve, minim, crotchet, quaver, and semiquaver.

這個想法很好記：每個音符都是前一個的一半長。全音符是沒有符桿的空心橢圓；兩個有符桿的二分音符，剛好等於一個全音符的時間；四個符頭是實心的四分音符，也是同樣的時間；八個各有一條符尾的八分音符，和十六個各有兩條符尾的十六分音符，也一樣。美國用這些名稱；英國英文則叫 semibreve、minim、crotchet、quaver、semiquaver。

How long is a quarter note in seconds? It depends on the tempo. A note value is relative. At 60 beats per minute, a quarter note lasts one second. At 120 beats per minute, it lasts half a second. In music we count beats, and a quarter note is one beat when the bottom number of the time signature is 4. So a half note gets two beats, and a whole note gets four.

四分音符有幾秒長？要看速度。音符時值是相對的。每分鐘 60 拍時，四分音符長一秒；每分鐘 120 拍時，長半秒。在音樂裡我們數拍，拍號下面的數字是 4 的時候，一個四分音符就是一拍。所以二分音符是兩拍，全音符是四拍。

A dot after a note makes it longer by half of its own value. A half note is two beats, so a dotted half note is two plus one, three beats. A dotted quarter note is one and a half beats. There are rests, too. A rest is the absence of sound for a set length of time, and each kind is named for the note value it matches. A quarter rest lasts as long as a quarter note, and it is silent.

音符後面加一個點，就會多出它自己一半的長度。二分音符是兩拍，所以附點二分音符是二加一，共三拍；附點四分音符是一又二分之一拍。也有休止符：它是一段固定長度的沉默，每一種都用它對應的音符時值來命名。四分休止符和四分音符一樣長，只是沒有聲音。

Now we can understand the time signature. In 4/4, the top number says there are four beats in each bar, and the bottom number says that a quarter note is one beat. So a bar of 4/4 can hold one whole note, or two half notes, or four quarter notes, or a half note and two quarter notes. In 3/4, it holds three beats. The notes of a bar must add up exactly. That is the puzzle that composers solve in every bar.

現在我們可以懂拍號了。在 4/4 裡，上面的數字說每小節有四拍，下面的數字說以四分音符為一拍。所以一個 4/4 的小節，可以放一個全音符、或兩個二分音符、或四個四分音符，或一個二分音符加兩個四分音符。在 3/4 裡，放三拍。一小節裡的音符，加起來要剛剛好。這就是作曲家在每個小節裡都要解的謎題。

Notes with different lengths make rhythm. Taiwan’s school music also uses jianpu, the numbered notation, and it has its own signs for length. A short line after a number makes it longer, a line under a number makes it half as long, and a circle is a rest. The same ideas of halves and halves again are there too. Whichever way it is written, the relative length is what matters, and the tempo decides how many seconds it lasts.

長短不同的音符組成節奏。台灣的學校音樂也用簡譜（數字記譜），它有自己表示長短的記號：數字後面加一條短橫線，就變長；數字底下加一條線，就變成一半；圓圈則是休止符。同樣是一半再一半的想法。不管怎麼寫，要緊的是相對的長短，速度才決定它是幾秒。
Read aloud · 課文朗讀

Questions · 閱讀理解

How many eighth notes fit in one half note?

Four. A half note is two beats, and an eighth note is half a beat, so 2 ÷ ½ = 4.

A bar in 3/4 has a dotted half note. What else fits in the bar?

Nothing else. A dotted half note is 3 beats, and a bar of 3/4 holds 3 beats, so it is full.

Is a quarter note always one second long? Why or why not?

No. A note value is relative. A quarter note is one beat, and how long a beat lasts depends on the tempo. At 60 BPM it is one second, and at 120 BPM it is half a second.

Stories Behind the Notes · 音符背後的故事

Names, a dot, a silence, and a row of numbers 名字、一個點、一段安靜，和一排數字

Four short stories about how music writes down length.
關於音樂怎麼把長短寫下來的四個小故事。

Names · 名字

Whole, half, quarter: a German loan全、二分、四分：從德文借來的

The American names are translations of German ones. The American whole note is a calque of the German ganze Note, and the quarter note follows the German Viertelnote. The British names are older and come from other places. Crotchet is from an Old French word for a little hook, named after the shape of the note, and minim comes from the Latin for very small.
美式名稱是從德文翻譯來的。美式的 whole note 是德文 ganze Note 的直譯，quarter note 也來自德文 Viertelnote。英式名稱比較古老，來自別的地方：crotchet 來自古法文的「小鉤子」，是依音符的形狀命名；minim 來自拉丁文的「很小」。

A Dot · 一個點

A dot, and a double dot一個點，和兩個點

A single dot adds half of the note's value. A second dot adds half of that again, so a double-dotted note lasts 1¾ times its basic value. For instance, a double-dotted half note is 2 + 1 + ½ = 3½ beats. Double dots were not used until the middle of the 18th century.
一個點加上音符時值的一半。第二個點再加上那一半的一半，所以複附點音符的長度是原時值的 1¾ 倍。例如複附點二分音符是 2＋1＋½＝3½ 拍。複附點直到十八世紀中葉才有人用。

A Silence · 一段安靜

Rests: counted, not played休止符：要數，但不演奏

A rest is the absence of sound for a defined length of time, and each kind is named for the note value it matches. There are rests for every note value, from the whole rest to the thirty-second rest. One special case: when a whole bar is silent, a whole rest is used, whatever the time signature is. So a whole rest does not always mean four beats.
休止符是一段有明確長度的沒有聲音，每一種都用它對應的音符時值命名。從全休止符到三十二分休止符，每一種時值都有休止符。有一個特別的情況：整個小節都沒有聲音時，不管拍號是什麼，都用全休止符。所以全休止符不一定是四拍。

A Row of Numbers · 一排數字

Length in jianpu簡譜怎麼寫長短

In jianpu, each number is worth a quarter note. A dash after a number makes it twice as long, like a half note. A line under a number cuts it in half, like an eighth note. A rest is written with a circle. The time signature is written as two numbers, such as 3/4, in the same way as on the staff.
在簡譜裡，每個數字相當於五線譜的四分音符。數字後面加一條短橫線，長度加倍，相當於二分音符；數字底下加一條線，長度減半，相當於八分音符；休止符用圓圈寫。拍號和五線譜一樣，寫成兩個數字，例如 3/4。

Myth vs. Fact · 常見迷思

Four things people get wrong 四個常見的誤會

✗ Myth · 迷思A whole note always lasts four seconds.全音符永遠是四秒。

✓ Fact · 事實A note value is relative. A whole note is four beats when a quarter note is one beat, and the number of seconds depends on the tempo. At 60 BPM it is four seconds. At 120 BPM it is two.音符時值是相對的。當四分音符是一拍時，全音符是四拍，幾秒要看速度：60 BPM 是四秒，120 BPM 是兩秒。

✗ Myth · 迷思A rest means nothing happens.休止符就是什麼都不發生。

✓ Fact · 事實A rest is silent but it takes time. It keeps the rhythm going, and the player must count it.休止符沒有聲音，但它占時間。它讓節奏繼續走，演奏的人必須數它。

✗ Myth · 迷思A whole rest is always four beats.全休止符永遠是四拍。

✓ Fact · 事實A whole rest is used for a whole silent bar, in any time signature. In 3/4 a whole rest means three beats.全休止符用來表示整個沒有聲音的小節，不管什麼拍號。在 3/4 裡，全休止符是三拍。

✗ Myth · 迷思A dot just makes a note longer by one beat.附點只是讓音符多一拍。

✓ Fact · 事實A dot adds half of the note's own value. For a half note that is one beat, for a quarter note it is half a beat, and for an eighth note it is a quarter of a beat.附點加上音符自己時值的一半：二分音符加一拍、四分音符加半拍、八分音符加四分之一拍。

Protect Your Ears · 保護耳朵

Before you listen 聽之前

  • Start with the volume low and turn it up a little at a time. The sound bar on this page starts at 30%.先把音量調小，再一點一點調大。本頁的聲音列一開始是 30%。
  • With headphones, keep the volume at no more than 60% of maximum, and use personal audio devices for less than one hour a day, as the World Health Organization suggests.戴耳機時，音量不要超過最大音量的 60%，個人音響裝置每天使用少於一小時，這是世界衛生組織的建議。
  • When you clap or tap rhythms in class, use soft hands or a pad, and do not clap close to anyone’s ears.在課堂上拍或敲節奏時，用軟軟的手或墊子，不要在別人耳朵旁邊拍手。

Classroom Activity 1 · 課堂活動一

Fraction Clapping 分數拍手

You need · 準備材料

  • Paper strips, each one the same length, for the whole class全班每人一條一樣長的紙條
  • Pencils鉛筆
  • The page, for a steady beat本頁的節拍

Steps · 步驟

  1. Fold the strip in half, then in half again, and again, like folding paper into halves, quarters, and eighths. Mark the folds.把紙條對摺、再對摺、再對摺，就像把紙摺成二分之一、四分之一、八分之一。把摺痕標出來。
  2. Let the whole strip be one bar of four beats. Clap one clap for the whole strip, then two claps for halves, four for quarters, and eight for eighths, all in the same four beats.把整條紙條當作四拍的一小節。整條拍一下，二分之一各拍一下共兩下，四分之一共四下，八分之一共八下，全都在同樣的四拍裡。
  3. Write the claps on the strip as notes: whole, half, quarter, eighth.把拍手的次數在紙條上寫成音符：全音符、二分音符、四分音符、八分音符。
  4. Now clap a pattern of your own that fills the strip. Your partner reads it back.再拍一個你自己的圖樣，填滿紙條。同伴把它讀回來。

Second graders already learn halves, quarters, and eighths of folded paper in math class, so the same idea is a good start for note values.
二年級的數學課就用摺紙認識二分之一、四分之一和八分之一，所以同樣的想法是學音符時值的好開頭。

Classroom Activity 2 · 課堂活動二

Rest Statues 休止符雕像

You need · 準備材料

  • An open space in the classroom教室裡一塊空地
  • Cards with notes and rests: quarter note, quarter rest, half note, half rest寫了音符和休止符的卡片：四分音符、四分休止符、二分音符、二分休止符
  • The metronome from lesson 3 at 80 BPM第三課的節拍器，速度 80 BPM

Steps · 步驟

  1. Everyone steps in place on every beat. The teacher shows a card.大家在每一拍原地踏步。老師出示一張卡片。
  2. For a note card, clap once and hold the clap for the length of the note. For a rest card, freeze like a statue for the length of the rest.看到音符卡，就拍一下手，並保持到音符那麼長。看到休止符卡，就像雕像一樣靜止，停那麼久。
  3. Show a row of four cards, one for each beat, and perform them together.出示一排四張卡片，每張一拍，大家一起表演。
  4. Let students make their own row for a 4/4 bar, and check that it adds up to four beats.讓學生做自己的一排 4/4 小節，並檢查加起來是不是四拍。

Keep the stepping light and stay in your own space. The quiet of a rest is part of the music, so listen to it.
踏步輕輕的，待在自己的位置。休止符的安靜也是音樂的一部分，要聽它。
